The petitioner's date of grant of selection grade on completion of 18 years was changed from February 1997 to November 1999, which violated the principles of natural justice.
The selection grade was granted as per notification of year 1992 by counting entire service, but the change was made as per subsequent notification of 1998.
No notice or opportunity was given, leading to the order being unsustainable and antithesis of arbitrariness.
[Paras 16, 16, 18, 19 and 23] B.
The petitioner stands retired, and since no fault is on the part of the petitioner, it would be just and proper to allow him to retain such a benefit.
Moreover, when the recovery was ordered without giving notice or an opportunity of hearing.
[Para 30] C. The petitioner was rightly granted selection grade as per notification of 1992, and the subsequent revision on the basis of a subsequent amendment cannot be sustained as it cannot be applied retrospectively to deprive petitioner of the benefit already granted to him.
Hence revision quashed.
