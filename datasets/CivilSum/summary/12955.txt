According to the Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39(d) and 226, there should be equal pay for equal work.
While the Grade I Sanitary Inspector post is solely promotional, the petitioners in this case are not promotees to the post, nor was there any such gradation under the service regulation of the Municipality.
The employer has the authority to make decisions about the constitution of the post, fixation of pay scales, gradation of different posts, and earmarking of different feeder posts.
As such, the writ petitioners are not entitled to claim identical Grade I pay since their cases are not identical.
