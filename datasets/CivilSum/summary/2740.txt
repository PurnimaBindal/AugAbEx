WP No. 1214 of 1993 and WP No. 1215 of 1993 involved compensation for non-payment of minimum wages, and the Labour Commissioner had ordered Mining Corporation to pay compensation.
The Corporation did not dispute their liability to pay wages, but there was some delay in payment, which had been rectified before the proceedings.
The authority was not justified in imposing compensation under Section 20(3) of the Minimum Wages Act, and the petition was allowed.
