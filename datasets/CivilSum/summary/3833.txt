Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Stop-gap appointment - The petitioner was continuously employed in a temporary vacancy on a stop-gap basis from 1971 until 1998 when he was selected for a regular position.
It was determined that his past service could be counted for computation of retirement benefits and addition of increments, according to the decision of the Supreme Court in RK Tandon's case.
The petitioner is entitled to increments from the date of his initial appointment, but benefits will only be paid from the date of filing the petition.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Pay Scale - The petitioner was appointed as a member of the faculty on a regular basis after being selected.
The petitioner sought the benefit of the pay scale on the basis of equal pay for equal work.
It was held that when the petitioner was appointed as a lecturer and was performing the duties of a lecturer, he couldn't be denied the benefit of the pay scale available under rules.
