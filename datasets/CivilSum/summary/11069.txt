Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 54 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 2(d) - Deputationist - Disciplinary proceedings - Petitioner, working for a Co-operative Society, was sent to work for the Co-operative Land Mortgage Bank - After returning, the Registrar Co-operative Societies charged the petitioner with irregularities committed while on deputation under Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Section 54 of the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act covers past and present individuals who had been managing the affairs of the society and whose acts caused losses to the society - When specific provisions are available, the general provisions should not be applied - Therefore, the charge-sheet issued to the petitioner under the Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules was illegal as the matter was covered by the special provisions of the Act.
