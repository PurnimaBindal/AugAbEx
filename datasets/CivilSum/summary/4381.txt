In the appointment of teachers as per the U.P.
State Universities Act, (Presi.
Act 10 of 1973), Section 31(8)(a), if the Executive Council disagrees with the recommendations of the Selection Committee, the matter must be referred to the Chancellor.
It has been determined that the principles of natural justice must be followed in this process.
When the Chancellor exercises jurisdiction under Section 31(8)(a) of the Act, he or she has quasi-judicial powers and must provide a speaking order.
