Constitution of India, Article 226 - Suspension - Continuance of - A police inspector caught accepting a bribe of Rs. 50,000 is being prosecuted in court and placed under suspension.
The government circular limits the period of suspension to 6 months, extendable for three months during investigation.
However, the petitioner cannot invoke this circular as he is already charged with an offense.
The suspension order remains valid and has not been revoked by any authority.
Therefore, the State Government's decision to continue the petitioner's suspension during the trial is justified.
[Paras 5, 7 and 8]
