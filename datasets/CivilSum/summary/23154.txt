According to the Himachal Pradesh State Electricity Board Recruitment and Promotion (Regulations) for Ministerial Employees of the Board, 1972, Regulations 5 and 8 read with Appendix `A' and `B', promotion as Head Clerk was permissible from UDCs as well as from Stenographers junior scale.
A petitioner promoted from Stenographer junior scale cannot be deprived of their right to be considered for promotion to the post of senior scale stenographer, as that would go against Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, since the petitioner accepted their promotion in the clerical line, they could not re-open the matter, which had already been disposed of by the court long back.
The tribunal was not justified in entertaining the application for the cause of action that arose 25 years ago and directing the Board to treat the petitioner as having been promoted from the date when the Board was not in existence, especially when State Government and the affected person in the seniority were not party before it.
It would not be in the interest of justice to disturb the entire cadre management at such a belated stage at the instance of a retired person.
