The 1987 Punjab Prisons State Service (Class-II) Rules, Rule 8(1)(b)(iii) comply with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution as it does not violate the requirement that promotions must be based on relevant experience.
[Para 7]
The suitability of candidates for a post is a decision that must be made by the Rule making authority and not the Courts.
[Para 9]
The promotion avenue provided to members of the ministerial establishment based on experience and observations does not breach Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 12]
The pay scales of various posts were revised in accordance with the recommendations of the Pay Commission.
The Government did not overlook any recommendations for any cadre.
[Para 13]
