Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(c)(2) - 33 employees of Punjab State Electricity Board were transferred to Bhakra Beas Management Board - The employees who accepted a regular appointment on fresh scale of pay were not given protection of pay from their previous work-charged positions - For those who did not receive retrenchment compensation, the Labour Court determined that their pay should be protected based on their previous service in the Electricity Board - The Court held that employees who accepted a new appointment in the Board cannot have their pay fixed based on their previous service.
