As per the Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part-I, Regulation 173, the disease of "Unspecified Psychosis" is equivalent to "Psychosis and Psychoneurosis" cited in annexure III to Appendix II in Regulation 173.
The term 'Psychosis' stands for mental illness, whereas 'Neurosis' refers to a mental state resulting in unreasonable fears, high anxiety and behavior.
The Regulations lists the disease "Unspecified Psychosis" as one of the affected diseases by stress and strain; thus, a person invalidated from Army service due to "Unspecified Psychosis" is eligible for disability pension.
