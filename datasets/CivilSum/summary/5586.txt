Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, Rules 918(2) and 131 - Compulsory retirement - Approval - The State Government had already provided approval before the order dated 331994 was passed.
However, the court set aside the order of compulsory retirement on the grounds that it was not passed by the competent authority.
A valid order for compulsory retirement was passed by the Director General of Police on 9.6.1995 after considering the petitioner's entire conduct during his tenure.
As the approval was obtained before the order on 331994, it remains in force and the competent authority can pass a valid order for compulsory retirement.
There are no grounds to interfere with this decision.
