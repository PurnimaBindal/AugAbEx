Constitution of India, Articles 21, 300-A - Daily wager - Arrears of salary - Claim of - Respondent worked for 11 years 6 months - Tribunal directed to consider case of respondent for regularization and ensure payment of arrears of salary - Petitioner claims service of respondent has been terminated and work is now being taken through contractor - However, in absence of evidence of termination of appointment, the status conferred by appointment order continues and the story of engagement by contractor is an attempt to overreach process of law and order of Tribunal - Therefore, Tribunal's directions are upheld.
