Madhya Pradesh Vishwavidyalaya Adhiniyam, 1973, Section 59 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 14 Rule 2(b) Jurisdiction/Bar of jurisdiction of Civil Court - If there is a question of bar of jurisdiction, the Court must make a decision based on the provisions of Order 14 Rule 2 (b) - The Court is required to consider the law in force on the date the suit was filed - In a dispute regarding the appointment of Deputy Registrar, the jurisdiction of the Civil Court was barred at the time the suit was filed - The dispute must be referred to the tribunal - The question of jurisdiction will pertain to the law that was in effect at the time of filing of the suit - It won't matter if the cause of action had arisen before the enactment.
