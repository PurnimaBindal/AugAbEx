Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Rule 2.3 - Punjab Land Revenue Manual, Rule 3.56-A - Pension - Patwaries - Patwaries who retire on or after August 1, 1959 are entitled to both pension and "reward" and will retire at age 55 without possibility of extension.
According to the rule, "Besides," if the incumbent works beyond age 55 and becomes eligible for retirement after 181959, he/she cannot be denied the benefit.
If the Patwari dies, his/her widow is entitled to family pension.
