Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Absorption - Golden Hand Shake Scheme - Allegation that it violates equality - Corporation (PUNWAC) facing financial losses - Ordered to remove excess staff - Introduces Golden Hand Shake Scheme - It is argued that this scheme violates the equality clause - It was held that there is no obligation on the employees to opt for the scheme so it cannot be said that they are being deprived of their rights - The scheme cannot be invalidated - Furthermore, the petitioners cannot demand that they should not be retrenched or remain in employment with the Corporation - This is because they can always approach the Labour court if they feel that they have been unjustly dismissed.
