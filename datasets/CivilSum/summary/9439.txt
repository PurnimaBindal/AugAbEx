Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Pay scale - The petitioner, who was appointed as a JBT teacher and performed the duties of SS Mistress throughout, is entitled to the pay scale of SS Mistress.
This is because the employer cannot expect an employee to perform at a higher post without providing the corresponding pay scale for duties and responsibilities.
The Principal of the school issued a certificate showing that the petitioner performed the duties of SS Mistress.
