Civil Procedure Code, Order 17 Rule 3 - Closing of evidence - Last opportunity - The last opportunity is intended to serve as a warning to parties in order to emphasize the importance of providing evidence and to make the necessary efforts to complete it by the specified date - The petitioner failed to complete his evidence within the three years given despite being granted the last opportunity by the court - Parties cannot be permitted to delay the proceedings based on their whim - Defendants cannot delay proceedings to avoid incurring a decree against them - Thus, the trial court's decision to strike off the petitioner's evidence was reasonable.
