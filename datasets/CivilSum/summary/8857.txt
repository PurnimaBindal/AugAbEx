Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - Reference - Jurisdiction of the appropriate Govt. - When there is an industrial dispute, the Govt. is required to make a reference - It is not within the Govt.'s purview to evaluate the matter's merits - Issues of disputes regarding whether the workers did not come to join duties per settlement/compromise or were not allowed to join by the management must be determined by the Court - As a consequence, the Govt. cannot refrain from making a Section 10(1) reference based on such a disputed question of fact.
