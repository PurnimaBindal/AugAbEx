Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - Re-assessment of disability - Petitioner, a former Indian Army member, was granted a disability pension of 100% due to tuberculosis.
However, his disability was eventually reduced to less than 20%, and his appeal was also rejected.
The court held that without consulting a higher Medical Board, the Accounts Branch cannot disregard medical experts' opinions.
As a result, the petitioner must be evaluated by a Re-survey Medical Board, which will determine any arrears owed to him.
