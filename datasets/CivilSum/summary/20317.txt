Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 2, Rule 2 - Payment of Wages Act, Section 15 - Res judicata/Back Wages - An employee filed a declaratory suit claiming promotion to the post of Driver `C' in the Railways and received a declaratory decree for promotion - However, the employee did not claim any relief for back wages in the suit - Later, the employee applied under Payment of Wages Act for back wages - Jurisdiction of Payment of Wages Act under Section 15 to compute back wages on promotion is in question - As per Order 2 Rule 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, the employee is debarred from claiming back wages since the declaration for promotion was already granted and there was no relief for payment of back wages in the original suit - Therefore, the authority under the Payment of Wages Act has no jurisdiction to entertain the claim for back wages.
