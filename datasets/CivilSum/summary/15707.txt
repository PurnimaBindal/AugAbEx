The Voluntary Retirement Scheme was introduced in two phases, with separate packages.
The object of both schemes is identical, however, there is discrimination between the terms of one package and the other.
The denial of the benefit of notice pay and other terminal benefits to petitioners, that is second batch optees, on the ground that they continued in service till a date later than the cut off date due to delay in administrative formalities of the corporation, is discriminatory.
The financial difficulty of the appellant corporation in giving effect to the Judgment of learned single Judge is not a valid and legal ground to deny the benefits to which petitioners are entitled, as allowed to the first batch of optees.
There is no illegality in the order of the learned Single Judge.
