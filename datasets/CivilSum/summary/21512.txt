Manipur Home Guards Act, 1966, Sections 4(4) and 8 - Manipur Home Guards Rules, 1981, Rules 3, 7 and 8 - Regularisation - The Home Guards was established as a reserved force to be used in emergency situations and is not considered a service like the police, para-military force or army.
Members do not have the right to continue until the age of 55 and their initial appointment is for 3 years.
It is at the discretion of the Commandant to reappoint a member after the initial 3 years.
