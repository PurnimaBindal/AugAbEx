Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission - Engineering Course - A claim for admission against one of the seats reserved for 'Punjabis settled in other states' - The University had omitted Code 13 used for the category of persons of "Punjabi origin settled in other States" in the year 1994 - However, this does not render an eligible person as ineligible for being considered - In fact, every person who does not fall in any other categories from 2 to 12 shall have a right to be considered, subject to their eligibility for the seats reserved for the category of Punjabis who have settled in other States - The petitioner's father has been issued a certificate by the Executive Magistrate, Chandigarh, indicating that they are a permanent resident of the place - The petitioner is also considered a domicile of Punjab - Despite this, they were excluded from consideration against the seats reserved for the domiciles of Punjab on the unfair grounds that they had passed the 10+2 examination from a school in Chandigarh and that the code indicated in the application form relates to the general category - The court has directed the respondents to consider the petitioner's claim for admission to Engineering Course(s).
