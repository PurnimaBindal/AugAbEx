Constitution of India, Article 226 - ROPA Rules 1990 - Age of retirement - Petitioner, a Teacher, was appointed by the State Electricity Board in its school.
All employees of the Board retire at the age of 58 years.
The petitioner's contention is that they are entitled to retire at 60 years as per the ROPA Rules, 1990, which stipulate the retirement age for teaching staff.
However, the petitioner is strictly bound by the terms and conditions as stipulated by the Board, and thus no relief can be granted.
