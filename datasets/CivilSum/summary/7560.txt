The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, as well as Section 25-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, state that 240 days in a calendar year must be completed.
The management did not raise any issues regarding the condition of Section 25-B or the jurisdiction of the Labour Court during the proceedings or in the writ petition.
As a result, the award cannot be interfered with based on oral arguments during the writ petition stage.
