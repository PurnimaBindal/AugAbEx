Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc Appointment/Regularisation - A writ petitioner who was not considered for regularisation of services in accordance with earlier executive instructions is entitled to be considered for regularisation in accordance with the earlier instructions and found entitled to all consequential benefits, even if their services were later regularised in accordance with subsequent instructions.
Such regularisation in accordance with later instructions would not take away their right to be considered for regularisation in accordance with the earlier instructions, as this would impact their seniority.
