Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Termination - Reference - Delay/Laches - The petitioner was terminated on 23978 due to a criminal case against him and his absence from joining duty after being released on bail.
The demand notice was issued 11 months after the F.I.R. was quashed and the petitioner took no action to challenge the award of the Labour Court.
As a result, the petitioner's case has become stale with the Labour Court's decision to reject his reinstatement and consequential relief upheld.
