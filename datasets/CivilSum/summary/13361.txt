Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absence from duty - Dismissal - Enquiry Officer - Disciplinary authority - The enquiry officer recommended that the petitioner's balance salary during suspension be forfeited and a warning be given to him, however, the punishing authority dismissed the petitioner without providing any reasons for deviating from the recommendation of the enquiry officer.
When the disciplinary authority proposes to overturn a finding made by an enquiry officer against a delinquent employee, they must provide tentative reasons in the show cause notice to allow the employee to defend themselves.
The order of dismissal against the petitioner is unlawful.
[Paras 5 and 6]
