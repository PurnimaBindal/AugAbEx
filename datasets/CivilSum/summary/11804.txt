Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Classification - The difference in revised pay scales between Distt.
Sports officers and Coaches in Schools of Sports is justified based on various considerations.
Coaches working in Schools of Sports receive substantial facilities such as rent-free furnished accommodation, free food during term with students, free units of electricity upto some limit and education of children at concessional rates.
If converted into monetary value, these facilities may provide more benefits to the petitioners than to those working as Sports Officers.
The Petitioners are not at a loss or facing any discrimination as they refuse to forego the existing benefits if pay scales become equal.
Classification upheld.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Higher pay scale - Coaches are not eligible for promotion as no such provision exists in their service.
The higher pay scales are prescribed for Coaches who have completed more than 10 or 20 years of satisfactory service to address the issue of stagnation.
Therefore, Coaches are not entitled to claim promotion.
