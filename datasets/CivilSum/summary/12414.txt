According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, higher qualifications should result in a higher pay scale.
Petitioners are claiming a higher pay scale for their D.P.E.
positions from the dates they acquired their higher qualifications which were prescribed under the National Fitness Corps Programme by the Central Government.
A policy letter dated 2111968 of the Central Government and a Supreme Court judgment in Ram Partap Yadav's case support their claim.
The Government has been directed to verify the particulars of the qualifications and fix their salary in the higher pay scale within 6 months.
