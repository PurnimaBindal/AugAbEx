Delay of 22 years in initiating an industrial dispute will render it stale and non-existent.
A criminal trial for the petitioner's charges of misappropriation resulted in acquittal in 2002, and conciliation proceedings were initiated thereafter.
Despite 22 years having elapsed since the petitioner's dismissal from service, the Government referred the dispute to the Labour Court.
However, a delay of such a significant duration without adequate explanation justifies the dismissal of the reference as stale.
