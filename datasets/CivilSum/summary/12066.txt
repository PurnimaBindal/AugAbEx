Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation of post - A Data Entry Operator was appointed as a petitioner on a contractual basis - His services were extended for eight years without any interruption - He was initially appointed for a period of three months only - He willingly signed the agreement with complete knowledge of the job's terms and conditions - Since the position of a data entry operator was never sanctioned, the court cannot compel the state to generate jobs that no longer exist.
