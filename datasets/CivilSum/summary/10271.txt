Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Quashing of order of Suspension - Allegations of malafide made against Shri Om Parkash Chautala, Chief Minister - The disciplinary authority is authorized to place an employee under suspension pending enquiry and the courts typically do not meddle with such orders of suspension.
However, if the suspension is arbitrary or malafide, it may be challenged.
The writ jurisdiction under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, especially the writ in the nature of certiorari, which only provide supervisory jurisdiction, cannot scrutinize matters of evidence.
