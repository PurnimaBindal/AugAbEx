Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Cancellation of Caste Certificate based on a challenge to genuinity - Appointment and promotion was secured by the petitioner because of a certificate obtained from a Tehsildar stating that he belonged to the ST category - Joint Collector issued a notice to the petitioner to show cause why his certificate should not be cancelled - Petitioner requested certain documents but was refused - Cancellation of Certificate was ordered but the order lacked clarity and did not comply with the principles of natural justice - Order was set aside and the issue remitted for fresh consideration.
[Paras 15, 24, 25 and 26]  B.
Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Cancellation of Caste Certificate based on fraud or misrepresentation - If the certificate is to be cancelled, it must be established that the certificate was obtained through fraudulent or misrepresentation means - The petitioner is only entitled to documents that were relied on by the competent authority for the proceedings and not any other documents.
