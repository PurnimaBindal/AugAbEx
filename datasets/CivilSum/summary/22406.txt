Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 4K and 6N - Termination of service - Industrial dispute raised after 10 years of termination of service - Respondent worked only for 2 years - Award of reinstatement but without back wages - In the present situation, the workman should have been given compensation rather than re-instatement - Therefore, the re-instatement decision is canceled - ON FACTS- Respondent has already received over Rs. 7 Lakhs under an interim order from the High Court as an interim measure - Respondent has already received sufficient compensation for his illegal termination of employment - There is no need to pay any additional amount - However, the amount that has already been paid does not need to be recovered.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Illegal termination of service - Reinstatement with back wages is the normal rule - However, compensation may be awarded instead of reinstatement depending on the facts and circumstances of the case - There is no hard and fast principle regarding this matter now.
