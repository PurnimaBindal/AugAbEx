Constitution of India, Article 226 - Military Service - Benefit of - Petitioner is seeking the benefit of his past military service for increment computation, seniority, and pension.
As there has been no response in the matter yet, the Court found no justification for further adjourning the case.
The Pleadings of the petitioner which has not been controverted are accepted to be true on the ground of traverse.
Hence, the Court has directed to grant the petitioner the benefit of military service rendered by him within a month.
[Paras 5 & 6]
