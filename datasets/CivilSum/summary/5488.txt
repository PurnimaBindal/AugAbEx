According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Section 11 Explanation IV of Civil Procedure Code, 1908, the principle of constructive res judicata applies to writ proceedings.
Thus, a party cannot file a second writ petition claiming relief that he ought to have claimed in the earlier writ petition as it would be barred.
[Para 19]

Even though the Code of Civil Procedure is not applicable to writ proceedings under Article 226 of the Constitution, the principle of constructive res judicata is applicable to it.
[Para 19]

If the petitioner's grievance is the non-compliance of court directions by respondents, filing a second writ petition under Article 226 for enforcement of orders passed in earlier writ petition is not an appropriate remedy.
Instead, the appropriate remedy would be to seek relief under provisions of Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 or by invoking Article 215 of the Constitution.
[Para 22]

In the exercise of its jurisdiction to punish, a court in contempt jurisdiction also has the power to enforce orders passed by it.
[Para 25]
