Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Agricultural Marketing Board (Class-II) Rules, 1988 - Petitioners, who work as Head Clerks in different Market Committees, questioned the provisions of the Rules when 43 posts of Secretaries of Market Committees had fallen vacant prior to the Rules' promulgation on May 2, 1988.
The filling of these vacant positions must be guided by the criterion that was in force at that time.
The Rules shall only apply to the vacancies that occurred on or after May 2, 1988.
Therefore, the court directed that the 43 vacancies of Secretaries of Market Committee which occurred prior to May 2, 1988, be filled up in accordance with the provisions of the rules or criterion which was in force at that time.
[Paras 5 and 6]
