Punjab University Calender Vol. III, Part 3, Rule 38 - Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation Class III Service Rules, 1980, Rule 10 Note-2 - Seniority - Clerical cadre - 1980 rules state that the seniority for clerks and computors will be determined based on length of service in their respective cadres.
Note 2 to rule 10 provides that the solitary post of computor will be included in the clerical cadre, and the entire experience of an employee as a computor should be considered towards their seniority as a clerk since the posts are interchangeable.
Qualification differences between the two positions do not matter because of this integration.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Stagnation - Article 16 of the Indian Constitution guarantees the right to explore chances of promotion for public servants, as promotion is necessary for efficiency in public service.
Keeping an individual in the same position for their entire career is inequitable and unjust, as it prevents growth and positive attitude towards achieving excellence.
[Para 11] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Integration of posts - Qualifications - The differences in qualifications between the post of clerk and computor are immaterial and do not matter, because the posts are interchangeable under Note 2 to Rule 10 in the Clerical Cadre.
