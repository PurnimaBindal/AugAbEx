Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-H - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 77 - Unauthorized absence - Dismissal from service - Delinquent must be served charge-sheet on fourth occasion of misconduct in terms of Bipartite Settlement - Penalty of censure imposed on three occasions - Enquiry not necessary when delinquent admits facts and misconduct is not disputed - Charge-sheet cannot be said to be invalid where minor misconduct persists and censure or warning has been administered as per settlement - Award set aside and matter remanded to consider afresh.

Effect of a decision of issue not referred - It is well established that industrial court derives its jurisdiction from a reference - Therefore, a tribunal transgresses its jurisdiction when it decides an issue that has not been referred.
