Technical Service Rules of I.C.A.R.
Rules 31, 51 and 7.1 - Promotion - Fitment and promotion are different - One cannot immediately move from Grade T2 of Category I to Grade II-3 of Category II for promotion based only on qualifications - One can only claim promotion to Category II Grade T-II-3 if they previously held a position in the next lower Grade T-I-3 of Category I - Rule 7.2 pertains only to promotions from Category I to Category II for those with necessary qualifications and is not related to the induction addressed in Rule 51.
[Paras 4 and 5]  B.
Words and Phrases/Promotion - Promotion means appointment to another higher post in the same service in higher category carrying higher grade.
