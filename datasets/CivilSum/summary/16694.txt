The Constitution of India, Articles 226 & 227 - Pension - Retiral Benefits - Release of - The petitioner retired on 3152002 on superannuation, and 25% of pension and retiral benefits were withheld due to a show cause notice issued on December 7, 1998, regarding certain material handed over to the petitioner while in service.
The petitioner replied on December 12, 1998, and no final decision was made on the show cause notice and representation dated February 27, 2003.
However, a charge-sheet is necessary for the initiation of a departmental enquiry, which has not been issued yet.
Thus, there is no justification for withholding the petitioner's pension, and the respondents must release the remaining pension and retiral benefits with interest at 9% pa. [Paras 2, 5 and 6]
