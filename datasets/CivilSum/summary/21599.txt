Constitution of India, Articles 14, 133 and 41 - Appointment - Compassionate appointment - Damodar Valley Corporation's land acquisition - Displaced persons eligible for absorption in the Corporation's service - The Corporation must expeditiously appoint such persons without age limit objections due to pending litigation, subject to medical fitness and eligibility based on the High Court's categorization - Findings of fact regarding categorization must not be interfered with on appeal.
