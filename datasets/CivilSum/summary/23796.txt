Uttar Pradesh Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Regulations 101 to 107 - Compassionate appointment is an exception to the general rules and should not interfere with the right of other eligible persons to seek employment.
If all vacancies for Class III posts are filled by dependents of persons who died in harness, it could violate the right to equality in employment.
Therefore, if a Class III post is not available, the dependent will be appointed to a Class IV post and a supernumerary post in Class IV will be created for that purpose.
