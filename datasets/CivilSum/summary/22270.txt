Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311- State Bank of India Officers (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service) Order, 1979, Paragraph 19(1) - Compulsory retirement - Branch Manager of Bank - An Order of compulsory retirement was appropriately given to a Branch Manager of a bank, upon review of his performance, which found it not satisfactory and his initiative average - There cannot be any fault found with the order of compulsory retirement, and the court cannot substitute its own judgment to that of the Reviewing Committee by re-examining an employee's service record.
