Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of Service - Daily wagers - Regularization was done w.e.f.
181987 - Writ petitions were filed in the year 1999 - The respondents sought regularization of their services from the date of initial appointment with all consequential benefits - However, no explanation was given for the delayed approach - The High Court allowed the writ petition along with other cases, citing a Supreme Court decision from 2004(2) SCC 433 - Nonetheless, the Supreme Court had previously held that the orders impugned in those appeals were not sustainable because the writ petitions were filed after a long lapse of time - The High Court, unfortunately, misread the Supreme Court's judgment while granting relief - Consequently, the orders of the High Court have been vacated.
