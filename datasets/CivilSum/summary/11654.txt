Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Family pension - The petitioner, who is the adopted minor son of the deceased pensioner, was denied family pension.
However, the petitioner was able to obtain a succession certificate as required by the respondents and also provided an adoption deed.
As the adopted son of the deceased, the petitioner is entitled to family pension.
The court issued directions to grant pensionary benefits, including pension, to the petitioner in accordance with the rules.
