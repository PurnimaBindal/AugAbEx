Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Service with UGC - Pay scale - The UGC made a decision on 12121995 regarding the revision of pay scales of Joint Secretary and Additional Secretary.
The petitioners are seeking implementation of this decision.
Approval from the Union of India is not required since UGC is not a public sector undertaking.
The Union of India must assist UGC in implementing the decision.
[Paras 29 to 32]
