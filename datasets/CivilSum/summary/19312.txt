Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment - Probation - Termination - Petitioner appointed as Clerk-cum-Steno typist (Punjabi) - During probation services terminated - Petitioner challenged order of termination - Respondents argued that petitioner lacked knowledge of Stenography in English, and so her services were rightfully terminated - The court held that the petitioner was not regularly appointed as per the letter of appointment, and only worked for three months, which does not warrant any right to claim regularization - The order of termination is without any error, and the petition is dismissed.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
