In relation to pension retirees, the State's continuing obligation, specifically with respect to the adverse effects caused by a drop in rupee value and increase in prices, will not ipso facto apply to PF retirees.
It cannot be concluded that Nakara ruled that the State's obligation to PF and pension retirees should be the same.
Furthermore, no classification based on an imaginary definition of obligation that includes all Government retirees was made in Nakara, and therefore cannot serve as the basis for classification in this case.
Nakara is not relevant to this case.
