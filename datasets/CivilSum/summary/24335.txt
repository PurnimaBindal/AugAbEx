Constitution of India, Article 32 - Selection - Appointment - Recommendations - Policy - The policy adopted by the Indian Government to reopen recommendations for the selection and appointment of High Court Judges is questionable.
The policy allows for reconsideration only if there is a change in the Chief Justice of the High Court or Chief Minister.
It is suggested that any reconsideration should be referred to the Chief Justice of India for review, even if the matter requires reconsideration for reasons other than personal.
