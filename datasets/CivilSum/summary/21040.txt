The Oil and Natural Gas Commission Act, 1959, Sections 15 and 32 saw a `Draft Superannuatory scheme' drawn up for appellant employees, which underwent several revisions before being ultimately approved on 1891991 by the Central Govt.
While such benefit under Draft Superannuatory Scheme cannot be granted to a sole individual like the respondent, the respondent who retired on 3061991 was nevertheless given the benefit of the retrospective effect to finally approved scheme.
