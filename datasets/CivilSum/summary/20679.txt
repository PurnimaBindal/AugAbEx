Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.4(2) - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(i)(a) - Qualifying service - Forfeiture of service - Pension - If an employee is penalized with forfeiture of service, the reduction in pay should equal the amount of increments the employee would have received during those years - The forfeiture of service should not be considered a break in service for the purpose of calculating qualifying service for pension or superannuation - Employees cannot claim retention to make up for the forfeited period of service to reach 25 years qualifying service for compulsory retirement.
