Constitution of India, Article 14 - Evidence Act, Section 115 - Appointment - The offer of appointment of a candidate against a particular post at a particular place is a term and condition of his appointment - The offer of appointment must be either accepted as a whole or refused - Failure to comply with the terms and conditions of appointment letter will result in appointment cancellation - Petitioners appointed as lecturers in the Medical College at Srinagar and ordered to join within 21 days - Their request to join at Jammu Medical College due to fear of terrorism cannot be considered- Appointments are as per specific requirements at a particular place and for specific posts.
