According to Rajasthan Evaluation Service Rules, 1979, Rule 26 and Rule 10, determination of vacancies must be done within twelve months of the last determination in an appropriate cyclic order corresponding with the proportion set out in the rules.
The number of vacancies for each year is based on the vacancies anticipated during the following twelve months.
A plea to carry unfulfilled vacancies to the next year was rejected, and the Court dismissed the writ petition.
