Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 311 - Ad hoc employee/Regularisation of services/Termination - Appointment as Lower Division Clerk on ad hoc basis - Services terminated later on the ground of mala fide and favouritism - Despite revocation of order of their regularisation, six other employees in similar circumstances allowed to continue on ad hoc basis - Appellant's termination illegal - It would only operate as revocation of regularisation and relegate appellant to the position of an ad hoc employee - Appeal accepted.
