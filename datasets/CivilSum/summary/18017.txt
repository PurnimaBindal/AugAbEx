Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979, Rules 3, 4, 6 and 7 - Pay fixation - The petitioner, who was promoted in an officiating capacity as Additional District & Sessions Judge from his substantive post of Senior Sub Judge, should have his pay fixed on the appointed date based on holding charge on each post.
He is entitled to all benefits on his substantive post of Senior Sub Judge as well as Additional District Judge from the appointed date.
Fixing the pay of juniors drawing lower pay scales higher than the petitioner's without justification is arbitrary and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
As such, the petitioner is entitled to have his pay fixed higher than that of his juniors based on the pay drawn on the appointed date with all other consequential benefits and arrears of pay.
