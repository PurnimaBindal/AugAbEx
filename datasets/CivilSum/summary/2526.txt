Section 1 (3) (b) of the Employee Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 defines the term "Commercial Establishment" in Entry No. 11, which is inclusive of establishments to which the Act is applicable.
This includes establishments of brokers, advertisers or commission agents, even if there is no actual purchase, sale or storage of goods.
The word "Purchase, Sale or Storage" is not meant to be understood simultaneously, but disjunctively.
An establishment that has engaged in purchase only is enough to fall under the provisions of the Act.
