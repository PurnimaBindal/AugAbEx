Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Jurisdiction - Bar - Division Bench in appeal reserving all questions raised by appellant in writ petition to enable him to agitate the same before High court in case the petitioner was aggrieved by decision of specific authority before which he was asked to make representation - Petitioner filing petition after rejection of representation - It was determined that observations of single Judge in earlier writ petition cannot prevent the petitioner from raising said questions in subsequent petition filed after the rejection of representation.
