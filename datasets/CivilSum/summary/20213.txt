Railway Establishment Code, Rule 320 - Railway Manual, Rule 219(g) - Seniority - Relative seniority of employees in an Intermediate Grade - The criteria for selection as Law Assistants must be based on Rule 219(g) of the Railway Manual - Respondents, who are from different units, are not entitled to preferential 15 marks for seniority - Therefore, the non-selection of the appellant was arbitrary and the CAT's order was overturned - Respondents are to follow proper selection procedures and make appointments in accordance with the law.
