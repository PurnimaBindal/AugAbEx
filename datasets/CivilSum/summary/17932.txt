Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural Justice/Enquiry Report - The delinquent officer must receive a copy of the Enquiry Report before major punishment is imposed, according to the principles of natural justice.
Failure to provide a copy of the Enquiry Report violates these principles and renders the order illegal.
The Inquiry Officer's findings are distinct from those of the punishing authority, and the delinquent has the right to contest any errors in the findings if they are provided with a copy of the report.
To construct an effective hearing, the accused must be given all the material against them, which is required to contest the charges.
Without receiving the inquiry report, the petitioner was not given an effective hearing, violating the principle of natural justice.
