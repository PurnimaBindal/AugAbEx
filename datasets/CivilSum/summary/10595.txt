Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Dismissal/Competent authority - The Deputy Inspector General dismissed the petitioner from service without being appointed by the Appointing Authority, making the order null and void.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Departmental enquiry/Confidential enquiry - A regular enquiry is required for the findings to be sustainable and the dismissal order illegal without one.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162(1) - Dismissal - Discrimination - The order of dismissal against one, while reinstating the other equally situated employee, was in violation of Articles 14/16 of the Constitution and unsustainable.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1629 - Appeal/Hearing/Natural justice - The order of the Appellate Authority is unsustainable as the petitioner was not given the opportunity of a hearing before it was dismissed.
