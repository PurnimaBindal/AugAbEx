The case involves the Indian Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 17, Indian Bank Officers' Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 37, and Central Civil Services Pension Rules, Regulation 21.
The pensionary benefits are being debated here, particularly the inclusion of leave that was taken by the Respondent at the time of loss of pay for calculating the pensionary benefits.
The bank's rejection of the representation, High Court's approval of the writ petitions, and the interpretation of Regulation 17 of the Pension Regulations vs Regulation 37 of the Service Regulations are some of the other issues detailed in the case.
The application of Service Regulations and Pension Regulations in different areas is also highlighted in Para 10.
The Respondents obtained leave on medical grounds without pay, and the court held that the employee of the Bank would be entitled to the benefit of the proviso appended to Regulation 17 of the Pension Regulations if there is a direction by the sanctioning authority.
If no such direction is issued, the competent authority would not grant leave for the purpose of pensionary benefits.
