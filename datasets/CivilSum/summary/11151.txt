Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment & Service) Regulations, 1981, Regulation 17.1 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(r) (ii) - Superannuation age/Retirement - In the absence of specified superannuation age, employees have the right to continue serving as long as they remain medically fit - The Payment of Gratuity Act pays gratuity only on superannuation and does not cover situations of deemed superannuation - However, employees are entitled to continue beyond superannuation age if rules are framed to that effect - Retired employees preserving their superannuation date under the Payment of Gratuity Act receive all benefits on the date of application of new rules.
