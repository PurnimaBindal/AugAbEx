Rule 5 of Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 deals with Efficiency Bar/Stoppage of increment/performance and states that stopping an employee at the efficiency bar does not constitute any penalty.
Passing a speaking order or giving notice is not necessary.
However, an employee can be stopped at the efficiency bar only if he is found unfit to cross it.
Disallowing an employee to cross the efficiency bar because of poor performance or if he is found unfit to discharge the duties of the post is not arbitrary.
[Para 3]

As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, if a junior employee is given special increments for having higher qualifications, then the senior employee cannot claim the same benefit only on the basis of his seniority.
The senior employee must also possess equal qualifications to receive special increments.
