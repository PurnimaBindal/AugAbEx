According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, natural justice should be followed in cases of dismissal from service.
If a person is accused of being biased against the delinquent in question and instrumental in creating evidence against him, then the disciplinary authority should not appoint that person as the inquiry officer.
The enquiry officer should also provide a detailed report with proper reasoning, and failure to do so can result in the violation of natural justice and the punishment order being invalidated.
