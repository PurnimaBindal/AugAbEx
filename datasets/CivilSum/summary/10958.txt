Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Reservation - The petitioner was admitted to GMC, Patiala as a candidate of the Border Area Category, for which 2% seats had been reserved - Later, she was shifted to Faridkot as a candidate of general category - The challenge was made as to the shifting - Relying on Navreet Kaur Sandhu's case, it was held that the shifting of the petitioner cannot be sustained - Once a candidate is admitted to the reserved category, there is no occasion for taking them into a general category seat which became vacant later on.
