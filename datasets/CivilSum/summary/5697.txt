Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 311 - Disciplinary Action - Double Jeopardy - Petitioner, a Grade I Inspector in the Oriental Insurance Company, was accused of issuing two cover notes in excess of his authority and against the interest of the company, which resulted in disciplinary proceedings against him as well as a criminal case and two civil suits.
The criminal court acquitted the petitioner and the civil court dismissed the petition against him.
Despite these judicial decisions in the petitioner's favour, the High Court had rejected his writ petition on the basis that the issues involved in the criminal case, civil suits and departmental enquiry were different.
However, the court of appeal held that the issues were in fact the same, as were the documents relied upon in all the proceedings.
In light of this, it was deemed improper to force the petitioner to face a departmental enquiry after he had already been cleared by the competent courts.
The judgment was therefore quashed and the appeal allowed.
