Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Ad hoc appointee who has been working for 12 years has to make way for candidates chosen for regular appointment.
However, if vacancies are still available, the Government may also consider the case of the ad hoc appointee for appointment to one of those vacancies, subject to the approval of the selection agency.
The Government may also relax the age limit, if necessary.
[Paras 3 and 4]
