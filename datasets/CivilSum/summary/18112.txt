Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Unemployment Allownace for the Educated Unemployed Scheme, 2005 dated 17112005, Clause V - Claim of unemployment allowance - Challenge to recovery of amount paid - The petitioner, who is 10+2 qualified and registered with Employment Exchange since 18102002, had to renew the registration in October 2005 with a grace period up to November 2005.
Although the petitioner renewed her name on 11112005, payment of allowance was stopped and recovery was ordered on the ground that she failed to renew registration by 1112005.
The scheme, effective from 1112005, allows for a grace period, thus the petitioner would be deemed to be registered w.e.f.
1112005 even if she applied for renewal on 101105.
The policy, though issued on 171105, is given effect from 11105.
Therefore, the respondents rightly granted unemployment allowance to the petitioner from November 2005 to March 2006.
Recovery is quashed and directions are given to pay "unemployment allowance" according to the policy.
[Paras 4 and 5]
