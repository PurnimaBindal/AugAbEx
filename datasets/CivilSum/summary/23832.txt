According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, courts and tribunals can only interfere with punishment as being excessively shocking if a finding is recorded that the punishment awarded by the disciplinary authority was shockingly disproportionate.
A teacher is expected to uphold a higher standard of honesty and integrity, and if they commit forgery either themselves or with the help of others to obtain benefits of a higher pay scale, it is not only a serious misconduct but also a serious criminal offense.
In the case of punishment of dismissal being imposed, it cannot be said to be shockingly disproportionate to the gravity of misconduct.
Therefore, the orders of the Tribunal and High Court have been set aside and the punishment of dismissal is upheld.
