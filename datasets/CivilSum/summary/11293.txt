J&K Police Manual, Rule 187 - Termination - Unauthorised absence - A probationary constable was terminated due to unauthorised absence as the petitioner was deemed unlikely to become a good police officer.
The petitioner tried to justify their absence on health grounds.
The order of termination was made in accordance with Rule 187 of Police Rules.
The order of termination was challenged after three years, but since the vires of Rule 187 were not challenged initially, the petitioner cannot challenge the order of termination.
[Paras 7 and 8]
