According to Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Haryana Women Development Corporation is classified as an industry because it functions to uplift women and provide training and loans to weaker sections of society, similar to the Women, Child and Nutrition Department of the Government of Rajasthan, which was previously challenged in court.
[Paras 5-7] B.
The petitioner-Corporation's classification as an industry was not determined in the Labour Court due to lack of evidence or arguments presented; if the petitioner wanted to make a distinction based on activities, this would require factual consideration and evidence, which cannot be brought up for the first time in a writ jurisdiction.
