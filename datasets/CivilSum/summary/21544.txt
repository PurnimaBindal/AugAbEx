Constitution of India, Article 309 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 3 and Rule 4 - Ex-serviceman - Allowance for military service pertaining to increments and seniority on appointment in Government service - Military personnel that joined during the first emergency declared during the Indo-China War and continued during the second emergency (Indo-Bangla war) can only have their service from the first emergency considered for receiving benefits.
