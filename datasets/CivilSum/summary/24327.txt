MP Development Authority Services (Officers and Servants) Recruitment Rules, 1987 - Daily wagers and ad hoc employees cannot claim regularisation or permanency against regular posts, as their appointments are in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and their service conditions are not determined.
Regularisation is only to remove an irregularity.
[Paras 16 and 17]  B. MP Development Authority Services (Officers and Servants) Recruitment Rules, 1987 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wagers and ad hoc employees cannot claim regular pay scales equal to those holding regular posts unless their services are legally regularised against regular posts.
[Para 35]  C. MP Development Authority Services (Officers and Servants) Recruitment Rules, 1987 - Project workers - The services of daily wagers and ad hoc employees of a project are automatically terminated on completion of the project.
[Paras 30 and 32]  D. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - In the absence of any reference of demand, pleadings, and evidence, the Labour Court has no jurisdiction to grant any relief regarding regularisation of service.
[Paras 28 and 30] E. Evidence Act, Section 115 - Adverse inference - Adverse inference can only be drawn if the court directs production of a document/record, but despite admitting possession of such a document, the party concerned fails to produce it.
The non-production of appointment letters by the management is not sufficient grounds to draw an adverse inference.
