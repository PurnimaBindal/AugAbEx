Union Bank of Employees Provident Fund Rules, Rules 17 and 18 - Union Bank Employees Gratuity Fund Rules, Schedule A Clause 3 - Gratuity - Forfeiture of - Natural Justice - Petitioner's dismissal due to fraudulent acts in regard to loan disbursal constitutes misconduct - Prior to forfeiture, Bank must assess the financial loss incurred due to misconduct - If loss is not determined, Bank cannot forfeit employer's contribution to petitioner's PF - Absence of opportunity to refute the proposed forfeiture violates natural justice principles.
