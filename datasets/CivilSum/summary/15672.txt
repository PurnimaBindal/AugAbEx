Punjab Education Class II (School Cadre) Rules, 1995 - Recruitment - Advertisement - The petitioner's candidature for the post of science Mistress Mustien (Medical) was rejected on the grounds that she did not possess the qualifications advertised - It was held that the prescribed qualifications under statutory rules must take precedence over those in the advertisement - Government and its functionaries are not allowed to make recruitments outside of the qualifications laid out in statutory rules.
