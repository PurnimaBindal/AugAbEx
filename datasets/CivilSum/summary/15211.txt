Punjab Police Rules, 1959, Rule 1221 - Absence from duty - Discharged from service - Natural justice - The petitioner's admission of absence and submission of a reply to the show-cause notice indicates no violation of natural justice rules.
[Para 7]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Natural Justice - When a petitioner is discharged from service due to absence from duty, it cannot be claimed that natural justice rules were breached, as the petitioner was issued a show-cause notice and the admission of absence was mentioned in their reply.
