The appellant claimed that respondents violated Rule 8 of the Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, by not informing him of his entitlement to assistance from a co-employee during the inquiry, thereby depriving him of an opportunity to defend himself.
However, it is worth noting that the appellant is a literate individual who admitted his guilt during the investigation.
Moreover, the reference to Rule 8 of the said Rules is not applicable in this case since this point was not raised before the lower court, and the appellant could not demonstrate how he was prejudiced if he did not receive assistance from a co-employee.
As a result, the appeal has been dismissed.
