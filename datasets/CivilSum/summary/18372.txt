Tamil Nadu Electricity Board Standing Orders, Order 19(i) - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Double Jeopardy - Conducting disciplinary enquiry due to employee's negligence - Management can recover the loss sustained by them regardless of the punishment imposed for the misconduct, but recovery proceedings should not be considered as double jeopardy - Board cannot determine the amount of loss without giving the guilty employee the opportunity to defend themselves.
