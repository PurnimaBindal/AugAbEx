Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Government Employee Rules, 2003, Rule 3(d)(i)(ii) - Compassionate appointments - A classification based on length of service of regular, ad hoc, temporary, and daily wage employees is permissible when made with a rational basis and object to be achieved within the scope of Articles 14 and 16 - Rule 3(d)(i)(ii) of Haryana Rules for compassionate appointments is not unconstitutional or ultra vires of Article 14 - The classification made under the Rules does not result in an arbitrary or illegal denial of family pension or benefits of compassionate appointment - The objective of the Rules is to provide support to the family of a deceased employee when there is no other source or means for subsistence as an exception to the normal rule of equality in public recruitment under Article 14.
