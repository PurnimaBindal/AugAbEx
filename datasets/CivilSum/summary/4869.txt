Service Matter - Petition against reinstatement of employee dismissed - No evidence provided to support claims made - Lack of information on misfeasance or malpractice or how petitioners are socially inclined or serving public interest - Public interest litigation intended for underprivileged and weak, not for personal vendettas - Based on law established by Apex court, writ is an abuse of legal process and wastes judicial resources - Petition is rejected.
