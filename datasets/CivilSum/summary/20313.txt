Advocates Act, 1961, Section 38 - Supreme Court Rules, 1966, Order 5 - Advocate - Professional misconduct - The Disciplinary Committee of the Bar Council of India suspended an advocate's licence for two years after finding him guilty of professional misconduct - The advocate had obtained a Mukhtarnama of his client in the name of his junior and transferred the client's property to his father via a registered sale deed - The client was unaware of the contents of the documents - The punishment of suspension is not excessive considering the severity of the advocate's misconduct.
