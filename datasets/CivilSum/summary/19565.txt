Constitution of India, Articles 311, 300-A and 226/227 - Voluntary retirement - Pension - After serving for twenty years, a Sepoy in CRPF has the right to voluntary retirement.
If an impediment arises, which can prevent them from serving the force, a medical board may be constituted.
The petitioner's request to be invalidated was only made after the medical board had already conducted a medical examination.
A more beneficial request to the petitioner should have been accepted, based on the rules, however, the one that was accepted suited the respondents more.
The Union of India was directed to voluntarily retire the petitioner from the date of their request and pay them the pension applicable to someone who has completed twenty years of service.
