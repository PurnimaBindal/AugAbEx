Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, Rule 14 - Forfeiture of pension - Misrepresentation - Scheduled Caste - Petitioner retired as Income Tax Officer after getting appointment in the quota of Scheduled Castes by submitting a forged certificate - Show-cause notice issued and proved that he did not belong to Scheduled Caste - Forfeiture of pension is a warranted punishment.
[Paras 1, 3 and 4]
