The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(a) and 10(1) were applied when the Appropriate Government refused to make a reference of Industrial Dispute.
The petitioner/union sought direction for the regularisation of workers, who worked in the State of Haryana for respondent No. 2.
The Conciliation Officer (Respondent No. 1) passed an order that it was not a fit case for reference because the establishment was situated in Haryana.
The judge held that Respondent No. 1 had not performed any adjudicatory function and has acted within its domain.
The decision was based on admitted facts, and it concluded that Respondent No. 1 was not the "appropriate government" and therefore incompetent to make a reference.
Section 10(1) of the Act confers jurisdiction to form an opinion before it decides to make or refuse to make a reference.
