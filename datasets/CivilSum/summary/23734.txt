Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Qualification - While the principle of "equal pay for equal work" is desirable, it may not always be easy to apply as there may be varying educational or technical qualifications for the same job title.
Therefore, it is best to leave the evaluation of job positions for pay scales to experts and accept their evaluations unless there are mala fides.
For example, the modes of recruitment, qualifications, and promotion differ for Lower Division and Upper Division Assistants in the Secretariat and in the Directorate of Information of State of UP, which justifies different pay scales.
The High Court's direction for the grant of equal pay for equal work was not justified.
