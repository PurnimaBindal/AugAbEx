UCO Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 18 - Punishment - Quantum of - Interference by Court - A chief cashier in UCO Bank was charged with misappropriating Rs. 6,630/- and was given a penalty of withholding two increments of pay, later upgraded by the reviewing authority to removal from service.
The reviewing authority followed the proper procedure prescribed under Reg.
18 by assigning reasons for differing with the disciplinary authority and giving the respondent the opportunity to meet the same before enhancing the penalty.
The punishment cannot be considered an "outrageous" defiance of logic.
The appeal of the respondent was allowed and the present appeal by Bank is dismissed.
[Paras 3 and 5]
