Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - SAIL Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1977, Rule 25.0 - Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry proceedings can be quashed if natural justice has been violated - In this case, enquiry was conducted against petitioner on basis of vague charges and principles of natural justice were violated at various stages - Relevant documents were not supplied to the petitioner and he was not allowed to defend himself - As a result, the disciplinary proceedings were vitiated and the enquiry report cannot be sustained - The decision of Single Judge to quash the charge sheet and enquiry report and order reinstatement with full back wages, was affirmed.
[Paras 31, 35 and 36]
