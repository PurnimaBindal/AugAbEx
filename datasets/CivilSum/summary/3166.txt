Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 59(2) - Show cause notice - Punishing Authority disagreements with Enquiry Officer's report - Provided the Punishing Authority creates reasoning for not agreeing with the report, the delinquent can persuade the authority otherwise - The enquiry report must be given to the delinquent with an opportunity to change the Punishing Authority's decision - This is essential under natural justice - Giving a hearing by the appellate authority will not fix this defect - The order is cancelled - The Punishing Authority must proceed according to law.
