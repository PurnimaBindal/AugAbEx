In accordance with Section 12 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, an appellant can receive compensation for the death of a workman, with liability falling on the Corporation as the principal employer, regardless of whether or not the workman was engaged by the respondent-contractor.
The Corporation, as the principal employer, has the right to recover any compensation paid to claimants from the respondent-contractor through indemnity, and it is important to note that the liability of the principal-employer cannot be waived through agreement.
