According to the Rajasthan Agriculture Produce Market Service (Pension) Rules, 1995, Rule 10(2) and the Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 179, Rajasthan government employees are entitled to pension benefits calculated from their initial appointment date rather than when CPF was first deducted.
However, Rule 10(2) of the Rajasthan Agriculture Produce Market Service (Pension) Rules, 1995 contradicts Rule 179 RSR and unfairly discriminates by ignoring pre-CPF deduction service.
This violates Articles 14 and 16 and has been removed.
