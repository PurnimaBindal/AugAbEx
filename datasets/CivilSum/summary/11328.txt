According to Section 18(9) of the UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Boards Act, 1982 (as amended by UP Act No. 24 of 1992), recruitment and appointment will take effect from the date of appointment and not from the date of vacancy.
[Para 4]
The vacancy of a Lecturer occurred on 22.1.1992 and was intimated to the Commission on 941992.
The appointment on the basis of selection by the Committee of Management was made on 20.12.1992, after the incorporation of the amendment in Section 18 of the Act by UP Act No. 24 of 1992.
The selection could only be made according to the amended provisions prevailing at the time of selection, which was on 20.12.1992.
Thus, there was no illegality in not granting approval to the selection and directing to follow the amended provisions of the Act.
