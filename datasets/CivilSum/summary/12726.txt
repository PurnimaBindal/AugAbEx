Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 4 - Suspension - The petitioner was facing a criminal case and sought quashment of FIR.
The presentation of challan was stayed, but the suspension order did not provide any reasoning for the suspension.
Since no charge sheet was issued, the suspension was deemed purposeless and against the rules, resulting in the suspension order being quashed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
