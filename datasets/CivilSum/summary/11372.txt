Punjab Privately Managed Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 18(1) - Disciplinary proceedings - If the punishing authority disagrees with the findings of the enquiry officer, it must provide reasons for the disagreement and record its own findings based on evidence for each article of charges - Non-compliance with this mandatory provision will render the punishing authority's order null and liable to be quashed.
[Paras 6 & 7]  B Punjab Privately Managed Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 18(2) - Disciplinary proceedings - After imposing any penalty, the punishing authority must give the employee a copy of the enquiry report - Failure to comply with this rule will render the punishing order invalid and unsustainable.
[Para 7]  C Punjab Privately Managed Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 18(2)(b) - Disciplinary proceedings - The employee must receive notice of any proposed penalty and be given an opportunity to represent themselves - Failure to comply with this rule will render the punishing order invalid and unsustainable.
[Para ]  D. Punjab Privately Managed Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 18(2)(b) - Disciplinary proceedings - An order of retrospective termination from service is not sustainable.
