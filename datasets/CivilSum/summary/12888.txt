According to Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulation 179, a petitioner who has been invalidated out of service due to disability and has rendered the necessary amount of service is entitled to disability pension.
In this case, the petitioner had served for 17 years and 143 days and was found to be 80% disabled by the Invalidating Board with his disability being aggravated by his military service.
As a result, the petitioner is eligible for disability pension at the rates set by the Central Government.
However, arrears are limited to the three years preceding the filing of the writ petition.
