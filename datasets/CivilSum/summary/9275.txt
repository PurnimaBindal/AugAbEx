Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 21 - The Executing Court is unable to go behind the decree - In writ jurisdiction, the Executing Court is restricted to the mandamus issued by the Court - The judgment-debtor may only challenge the relief granted in the appropriate forum - Even if the judgment has been complied with, the judgment holder still deserves additional benefits - The Executing Court must ensure compliance with the Court's mandate, despite the non-binding nature of the Contempt of Courts Act proceedings.
