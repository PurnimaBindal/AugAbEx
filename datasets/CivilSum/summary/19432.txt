The Dharwad Agricultural University Statutes, governed by the University of Agricultural Sciences Act, 1963, provided employees the option to choose between the Pension and Provident Fund schemes.
If employees opted for the Pension Scheme within three months of the statutes' promulgation on 28121988, they would be entitled to all the benefits associated with the Pension Scheme.
The petitioner, who rightfully opted for the Pension Scheme within the specified time frame, is thus entitled to pension and other related benefits offered by the respondent-University.
[Para 8] B.
The term "promulgation" refers to the date on which the statutes are made public and known to the general public, not the date on which they come into force.
