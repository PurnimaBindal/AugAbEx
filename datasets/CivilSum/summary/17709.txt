According to Section 115 of the Evidence Act, 1872, the recognition of a certificate creates an estoppel.
The petitioner was refused an appointment solely on the basis that the Varanasi Sanskrit Vishwa Vidyalaya from which he obtained his matriculation was not recognized.
However, the same university was later recognized under a different name, Sampoorna Nand Sanskrit Vishwa Vidyalaya.
The authorities cannot use inconsistent arguments or act in a contradictory manner once the university has been recognized under the new name.
The petitioner should be considered for appointment based on this.
