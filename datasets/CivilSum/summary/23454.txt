Admission/Education/Estoppel - Fraud - Students who obtain admission through fraudulent means by misrepresenting their social status cannot expect the principle of promissory estoppel or equity to apply against the authorities.
The court is unlikely to show any sympathy or exercise equitable jurisdiction in favor of such students.
In this particular case, the petitioners secured admission in a B.D.S.
course on the basis of a false Social Status Certificate, which was discovered midway through their studies, and as such, their admission was rightly cancelled.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15, and 16 - Reservation - Social Status Certificate - Procedure for issuance - Admission and appointments obtained through false SC/ST certificates have the unfortunate effect of depriving genuine SC/ST candidates of opportunities.
Sometimes, even the parents of the students are involved in the fraudulent act.
Therefore, it is crucial to scrutinize certificates issued at an early stage with utmost promptitude and streamline the procedure for issuing such certificates so that states can follow the suggested protocol.
