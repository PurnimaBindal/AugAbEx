For Respondent :- BG Neema, Advocate.
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Promotion of Officer of Indian Army - While an Officer does not have a right to promotion, they have the right to fair and just consideration - Petitioner was unfairly denied promotion to the post of Lieutenant General - Selection Board considered irrelevant annual confidential reports which were not required to be considered thereby invalidating the selection process - Special Selection Board directed to decide on petitioner's promotion.
[Paras 19, 22, 25, 28 and 31]
