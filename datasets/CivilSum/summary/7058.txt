Rajasthan Class IV Service (Recruitment and other Service Conditions) Rules, 1963, Rule 27 - Temporary appointee - Claim of regularisation - Show cause notice for termination - Challenge as to - Petitioner claimed that they were not covered under the scheme for regularisation due to lack of eligibility qualification and was serving as a cook since 1987 - The Schedule I appended to Rules empowers the appointing authority to relax the qualification requirements if sufficient candidates aren't available - It was held that although the eligibility conditions should be adhered to, some variance would be permissible in exceptional circumstances, particularly when the State Government has been given the power to make such relaxation and has already done so in similar cases - The vast experience of working on such a post should be taken to have made up for the deficiency of eligibility conditions - The show cause notice was quashed, and the respondents were directed to consider the case for regularisation according to the law.
