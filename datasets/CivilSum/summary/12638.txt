According to the Constitution of India, under Articles 226 and 227, a Naib Subedar in the Army was denied an extension of service due to being granted rigorous imprisonment under Section 39(b) of the Army Act when he was a Supper rank in 1984.
However, a clarification dated 13.6.2007 to the policy dated 3191998 states that any punishment in a lower rank will not prevent an extension in the rank of JCO.
Therefore, the petitioner's case should be considered for extension since he is set to retire on 31.5.2008.
