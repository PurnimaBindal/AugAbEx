Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960.
Regulations 7/LIC of India, Recruitment of (Class III and Class IV) Staff Instructions, 1979, (as amended by circular dated April 11, 1980, Para 9) - The selection of 25% candidates who have passed Matriculation and above for the post of peon is considered valid according to instructions.
[Para 1]
Interview marks - Selection - Nature of post - In the selection process for the post of peon, the use of interview alone is appropriate and any amount of marks kept for interview cannot be considered illegal or arbitrary.
