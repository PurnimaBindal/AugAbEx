According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, Rule 10 of Punjab Educational Service (School and Inspection) (Class-II) Rules, 1976 states that promotion is possible.
However, when petitioners claimed promotion to Class-II service after having worked as Lecturers for about 14 years, their claim was denied by the Director on the grounds that they were no longer lecturers once they became Headmasters.
In accordance to the decision made in Baldev Raj Preenja v. State of Punjab, 1997(1) RSJ 571, the respondents are now required to reclassify the petitioners as Lecturers and to consider their claim of promotion.
