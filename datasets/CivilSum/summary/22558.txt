Punjab Police Rules, Rules 12.21, 1624 - Discharge from service - Discharge simplicitor - When an employee is discharged simplicitor, no aspersions or stigma should be cast upon them.
Instead, the employee should be told that they are being discharged due to unsatisfactory work.
If there are any concerns, a proper enquiry should be held under Rule 1624.
The High Court's order that the discharge was simplicitor is unsustainable.
