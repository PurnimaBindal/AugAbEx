According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the government declined the reference on the grounds that the workman worked for only 199 days and the termination did not fall under the definition of retrenchment.
However, it was improper since the workman also alleged unfair labor practices on the part of management.
The government cannot adjudicate on this matter, and it is the job of the Industrial Tribunal or Labor Court to determine this dispute.
The appropriate government should pass fresh orders on the demand notice, as per AIR 1985 SC 860.
