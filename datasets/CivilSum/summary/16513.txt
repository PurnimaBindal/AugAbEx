Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Claim for a retrospective date - Petitioners were recruited as Conductors in Nigam Transport - Nigam Transport disbanded in 1995 - Municipal Corporation allowed petitioners to be absorbed as a Clerk and they started working from 1.1.1999 - It is held that the petitioners are not entitled to seniority either from the date of their recruitment as conductors or from the date they were working as Clerk while holding posts of Conductors - The order rejecting their claim is free from any legal errors.
