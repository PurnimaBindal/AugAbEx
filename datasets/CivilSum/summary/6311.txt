Constitution of India, Article 226 - LIC of India Recruitment (of Class III and Class IV Staff) Instructions, 1993, Regulation 21(iii) - Compassionate Appointment - Death of an employee in service does not automatically entitle their family to claim employment for any of its members - Petitioner's two brothers were employed at the time of their father's death and were members of the family, and still are - No need to hold an oral enquiry to determine whether the elder brother was separated from the family - Consideration for compassionate appointment is not a guaranteed right - Respondent's rejection of petitioner's request for compassionate appointment was not illegal.
