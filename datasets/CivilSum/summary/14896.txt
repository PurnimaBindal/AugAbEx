Constitution of India, Article 16 - Canara Bank Officers Service Regulations, 1979, Proviso to Regulation 19(1) - Retirement - Recruitment - The definition of recruitment should be broadened to include appointment on contract basis - Two different ages of retirement can be set based on the cut off date of an appointment - The retirement age of officers appointed after 19.7.1969 should be fixed at 60 years in accordance with government guidelines - The petitioner, who was appointed as a Probationary Officer on 1871969, is entitled to retire at the age of 60 - Absorption into service is dated back to the date of confirmation, which is 1871969.
