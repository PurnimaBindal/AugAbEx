Under Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a workman is terminated in an illegal or arbitrary manner, he must be reinstated with full back wages.
However, if a workman is terminated for participating in an illegal strike, he cannot find employment elsewhere.
Even if the employer tries to delay the adjudication process, the workman must fight for his rights.
If there is no evidence that the workman had gainful employment, he must receive the benefit of full back wages.
