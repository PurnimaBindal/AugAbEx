Under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the post of Assistant Professor in Civil Engineering requires candidates to possess a Bachelor or Post Graduate degree in Civil Engineering.
The appellant in this case did not possess this degree, and thus was not shortlisted for an interview.
However, the authorities have been directed to reconsider his case in light of his long and satisfactory service and explore the possibility of posting him in a different department where his specialisation can be effectively used.
[Paras 11, 12, and 13]
