Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Dying in harness - Adopted son of deceased employee claiming appointment on compassionate ground - The appellant did not provide any evidence showing that a dependent of the deceased can be appointed on compassionate grounds - Compassionate appointments are only granted to the spouse, son, or unmarried daughter - The fact that the appellant was given a succession certificate as an adopted son is not sufficient for him to be granted a compassionate appointment.
