Under Section 17B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (XIV of 1947), the Labour Court is authorized to award a subsistence allowance to a workman.
It is important to note that despite the employer-company's manufacturing activities being halted, the workman cannot be denied relief under Section 17B The only situation in which a High Court or Supreme Court could refuse to pass an order for payment of benefits accrued under Section 17B is if they are satisfied that the workman in question is receiving adequate remuneration during any such period or part thereof.
