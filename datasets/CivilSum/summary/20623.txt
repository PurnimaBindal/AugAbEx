Constitution of India, Article 14 - The right to justice means speedy justice.
In a progressive society, the judiciary must be active and deliver justice as quickly as possible.
Both the Bench and the Bar have a duty to help people access justice promptly.
Only their collective efforts can bring about a change.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - The Orissa District Police Ministerial Officers (Method of Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1995 - Police Act, 1861, Sections 7 and 8 - The Ministerial Staff/cadre and Field Staff/cadre have different emoluments/rights to promotion in the Orissa Police Department.
They have equivalent executive ranks, but special pay and house rent benefits are available to ministerial ranks, who are designated "Police Officers (Ministerial)" after Resolution dt. 7.9.1974.
Not all police officers are eligible for consideration for promotion to higher ranks in the office of DIG/IG/DGP after the resolution.
There is a difference in terms of appointment and qualifications, and posting and transfers are no longer interchangeable between the two cadres.
The recruitment Rules for two levels have two sets of rules.
The distinction between the two sets of officers has always been made, and the distinction was made even clearer after the framing of the Rules.
There was no fusion of the two erstwhile cadres at any time.
