Article 226 of the Constitution of India discusses the powers and duties of Appellate Authority.
In a case where a petitioner was punished with stoppage of three increments with cumulative effect after an enquiry, on appeal, the Appellate Authority rejected the appeal without rendering its findings on all factual aspects.
The Revisional authority went into merits and confirmed the order of the appellate court while also ordering a fresh enquiry in respect of the charges that were not proved.
However, the impugned orders show that there was no proper appreciation of facts by authorities below and are thus liable to be quashed.
Consequently, the matter has been remanded to the Appellate Authority for reconsideration of facts and for passing fresh orders.
[Paras 5 and 9]
