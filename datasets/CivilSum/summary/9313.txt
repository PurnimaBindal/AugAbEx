The Employees State Insurance Corporation Act, 1948, Section 2(22) includes D.A./T.A.
within the definition of wages.
However, employers have been known to avoid this rule by labeling D.A./T.A.
as commission to drivers and conductors.
But this commission falls within the definition of wages and the employer is liable to pay employer's contribution.

Assessments made without hearing the employer cannot be sustained under the Employees State Insurance Corporation Act, 1948, Section 45 and 45A Therefore, the assessment in question was quashed.
