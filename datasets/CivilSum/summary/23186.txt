Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Back wages - If an Industrial Tribunal is asked to decide on the justification of a worker's termination and grant appropriate relief, including whether the worker should be reinstated with full or partial back wages or none, the Tribunal can determine all incidental questions arising from the reference, including the question of whether back wages should be granted - Failure to grant back wages amounts to denial of compensation - Back wages are deemed to be denied if not granted.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33-C(2) and 10(1) - Scope of - Back wages - If a Labour Court is asked to decide on the validity of a worker's termination and if not valid, what relief the worker can claim, the Court has the power to determine whether the worker is entitled to back wages, but not if they weren't granted in the award of reinstatement under Section 10 - The principle is that if a claim is made but not granted, it is deemed to be declined.
