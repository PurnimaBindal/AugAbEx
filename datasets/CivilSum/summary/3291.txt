Constitution of India, Article 14 - Reservation - Seats reserved for University employees and their dependents cannot be deemed to lapse after striking down of reservation - Challenge is only to reservation and not to creation of additional seats - University cannot deny fruit of victory to succeeding parties - Reservation deemed bad.
[Para 5]
Admission - Reserved seats for University employees and their dependents held to be invalid - All plaintiffs in civil suits and writ petitions must be considered in one category - Respondents directed to grant admission in current academic year.
