Pharmacy Council of India Act, 1948, Section 12 - All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Section 10 - Technical Education Institutions/approval - The Board of Technical Education is not authorized to grant approval for technical institutions after the All India Council for Technical Education became a statutory body under the Act - Technical institutions cannot be started without the council's prior approval - The Pharmacy Council of India, All India Council for Technical Education, and Board of Technical Education are distinct and independent bodies - There is a difference between approval and affiliation of institutions - The State Govt. or the Pharmacy Council cannot grant approval/disapproval for technical institutions - Permission granted by the Board of Technical Education's Chairman is irrelevant - Students admitted to an unapproved institute cannot take the examination - This has been followed in 1990 DLT 544, AIR 1991 Mad.
141, and JT 1991(2) SC 343.
[Paras 11, 14, and 15] B All India Council for Technical Education 1987, Section 10 - Inspection/Disapproval - Approval for technical education institutions is mandatory under the Act - However, before disapproving an institution, the council must grant an opportunity for inspection to satisfy the Act's conditions - Natural justice principles must be followed.
