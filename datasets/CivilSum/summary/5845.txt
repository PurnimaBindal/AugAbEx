Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rules 189-A, 189-B and 315 - Disciplinary proceedings - Retirement - Disciplinary proceedings after retirement - Payment of pension and gratuity - A charge-sheet that is issued one day before the superannuation date can allow the government to withhold gratuity payments until proceedings conclude.
However, if proceedings last for longer than two years, 90% of the gratuity must be released.
The court is not permitted to demand that authorities make payments in opposition to established rules.
[Paras 21 to 27 and 40]
