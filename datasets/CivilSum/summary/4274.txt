The Punjab Service of Engineers Class-II (Public Works Department, Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1941, Rule 3(i)(c) - Promotion - Decree and diplomas - Promotion of Junior Engineers to the post of S.D.O.
- Eligibility - A.M.I.E.
decree holders are eligible for promotion to the next higher post, while diploma-holder Junior Engineers are not.
Therefore, the diploma-holder J Es.
cannot claim the same right to promotion and quota in the higher cadre as AMIE decree holders, as classification based on qualifications has been approved by the Supreme Court.
Although the rules provide promotion for diploma-holders based on exceptional cases or merit relaxation, this is not a sufficient ground for providing a quota for them if they are otherwise ineligible.
Additionally, they cannot challenge the quota provided for AMIE decree holders.
