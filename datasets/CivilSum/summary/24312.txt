According to Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an employee who has been proved to have committed misconduct, such as collecting fares without issuing tickets, can be dismissed from service.
However, the court may not direct reinstatement and must refrain from interfering with findings of misconduct, though it may order that back wages be forfeited until the employee is reinstated.
Misappropriation of public funds is such a serious misconduct that may render an employee unfit for public employment.
[Para 10]

Under Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a roadways conductor who is found guilty of misappropriating funds can be dismissed from service.
The court cannot consider the punishment disproportionate, as the question at hand is not just about misconduct, but also about loss of confidence.
An employee who is deemed guilty of misappropriating employer funds should not be retained in service.
