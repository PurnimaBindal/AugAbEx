Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Section 10 - Admissions/Education - Technical Education - According to the Act, any new institution must obtain approval before starting - Admissions to the first year were made prior to approval being issued by the Central Council - Admissions to the second year were made without approval - Although the Central Council dropped the penal proceedings for the benefit of the institution, admissions made to the second year against the terms of approval are illegal and cannot be upheld - It is the court's duty to enforce discipline and not allow deviations from the Act - Students should have known that the institution did not have approval for admission.
