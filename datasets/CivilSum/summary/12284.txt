In the realm of Education, selecting students for admission can include an interview, and requiring 15% of marks to come from interviews is not unreasonable.
However, interviews cannot be used to unfairly select lower-achieving candidates or exclude those with higher merit in the written test.
If it's discovered that interviews were marked in a way that favored certain candidates at the expense of merit, that selection can be reversed.
In this particular case, the Institution was directed to create a seat and admit the petitioner who was excluded due to arbitrary interview marking, despite his high merit in the written test.
