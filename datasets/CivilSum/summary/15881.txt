Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Petitioners challenged the admission of Respondents with lower merit in an entrance examination of Post-Graduate Degree/Diploma Courses in Medicine from among HCMS - The selection process was challenged when the government change the criteria midway by issuing a memo - Court held that the conditions mentioned in the prospectus control the admission - Following the memo ignored the merit of entrance test entirely - Admission given to respondent not upheld - Authorities directed to make admission strictly on the basis of position in the merit list as per the prospectus.
