Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Maharashtra Ground Water Service Class I (Recruitment) Rules, 1976, Rule 3(1) - Appointment to the post of Deputy Director in Maharashtra Ground Water Service, Class I - The Rules prescribe 10 years experience after prescribed academic qualifications - This requirement of 10 years experience is applicable to appointments other than by promotion - For promotion, a different interpretation should apply - The appellant, being the senior-most candidate, will be entitled to the post in either case since the clause of experience is omitted from the current rules.
