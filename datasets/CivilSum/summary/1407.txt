Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Sections 17B and 33(2)(b) - Dismissal from Service - Industrial Tribunal approval required - If the Industrial Tribunal refuses to approve the dismissal order, then the workman will continue to be employed - Refusing the order is considered an award under Sections 2(b) and 2(k) - In case the employer challenges the Tribunal's order, the workman is entitled to his wages, including arrears from the date of dismissal, as per Section 17B - Future wages to be paid on a monthly basis.
