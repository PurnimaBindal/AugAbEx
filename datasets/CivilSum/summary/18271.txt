Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226- Promotion - Departmental proceedings - Post of Deputy Superintendent, Jail - The petitioner contended that his claim was not considered despite his juniors being promoted.
However, a review of the petitioner's service record revealed reports of an Average or Below Average rating for the past 16 years.
Furthermore, he underwent departmental proceedings in 1984 whereby censure was imposed as a punishment.
Government instructions dated 1341972 stipulate that an incumbent must have obtained at least 50% with a Good or Better than Average record to be considered for promotion.
Thus, the petitioner did not meet the required criteria for promotion, and the respondents were justified in not promoting him when his juniors were promoted.
