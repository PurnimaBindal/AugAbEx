Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Reservation in admissions - Fixing quotas for different categories in admissions to Post-graduate Diploma/Degree MDS courses - 50% seats reserved for `in-service' candidates and remaining 50% for `non-service' candidates - Such classification for allotment of seats to different categories for a particular object cannot be termed as reservation - Candidates of other categories cannot claim any right to the seats allotted to another category even on the basis of their merit - Mere use of the word `reservation' does not necessarily apply the entire mechanism underlying the constitutional concept of a protective reservation for BC or S.C./S.T.
citizens - Classification and categorisation is upheld.
