Whether the non-furnishing of documents asked for by the delinquent-employee prejudiced their case in a disciplinary enquiry must be pointed out by the delinquent who must show the relevance of every document.
The Tribunal is also bound to make a finding on the non-supply of any relevant documents and whether this has prejudiced the case of the delinquent-employee.
As the Tribunal did not make any such finding, the case has been remitted for rehearing.
