Road Transport Corporation Act, 64 of 1950, Section 45 - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Enquiry - Principle of natural justice - Employee regularly attending enquiry - Employer absent on 9 consecutive occasions - Employee absent only once - Order of dismissal passed ex parte - Such order violates principle of natural justice - Dismissal order nullified - Employer directed to conduct fresh enquiry.
