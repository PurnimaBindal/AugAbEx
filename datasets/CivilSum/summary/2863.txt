Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - High Court may refuse to exercise its writ jurisdiction on the ground of delay and laches, if no explanation is provided and the petitioner would not be entitled to relief - Court must assess reasonable delay based on each case's facts and circumstances and may restrict relief on that ground - Appellate court should be cautious of interfering with writ court's satisfaction that delay was justified.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 208 - resignation - Pension - Mere resignation does not result in forfeiture of qualifying service for pension, but depends on the circumstance of each case - Employee who properly applied to join other service and was permitted to will not forfeit service by resigning and is entitled to pension benefit for qualifying service.
[Paras 8-10,12-15]
Pension - Pension is not a favor, but a statutory right and deferred salary for service rendered.
