National Cadets Corps Act, 1948, Section 9 - National Cadet Corps (NCC) Rules, 1948, Rule 16(iii) proviso - Appointment as Commission in National Cadet Corps (NCC) under the Scheme framed under Rule 16 - The Government is not obligated to allow appointees up to the age of 45 years - Appointment under the Scheme is not considered an appointment under Section 9 of the Act, and the word "otherwise" refers to any members of the corps other than staff of any university or school.

National Cadets Corps Act, 1948, Section 9 - National Cadet Corps (NCC) Rules, 1948, Rule 16(iii) proviso - Appointment as Commission in National Cadet Corps (NCC) - Once an appointee under the Scheme accepts their appointment with full knowledge of the terms and conditions, they are estopped from challenging the same or making any other claims, particularly when the terms and conditions are in line with the Scheme, which has not been challenged.
