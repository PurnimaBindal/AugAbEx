The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Sections 25F and 25H, state that if a workman has been retrenched and has been paid all the dues as per Section 25F, the private employer is not obligated to re-instate them.
However, the workman has the right to be recalled under Section 25H if the employer employs someone like them in the future.
