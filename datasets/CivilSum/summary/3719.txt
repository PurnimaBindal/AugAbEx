In the Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(e) is discussed regarding a deceased manager who made a salary of more than Rs. 1000 per month and whether or not they are covered under the term "employee".
The single judge dismissed the entire claim, but it was held that the essence of gratuity is past service and not present.
As a result, the appellant is entitled to receive gratuity for their service rendered prior to their promotion as a manager.
The appeal was allowed.
