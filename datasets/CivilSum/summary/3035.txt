Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1, 2 and 4 - Injunction - An application for interim injunction has been filed against defendants-employees to restrain them from holding demonstrations, shouting slogans, and other similar activities - The dispute that led to this injunction is regarding service matters and service conditions between the employer and employees, which has remained unresolved for four years - The court has granted interim injunction subject to the condition that the defendants-employees must not hold demonstrations etc. near the plaintiff's premises, and the plaintiff must co-operate and join in any lawful proceedings to settle the dispute.
