Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Contractual Appointment through proper channel - Clause in appointment letter stating services can be terminated at any time without prior notice is unconstitutional and violates the protection guaranteed by the Constitution - Government is taking measures to regularize appointments - State would not be disadvantaged if the petitioners are permitted to continue working on their appointments with the termination clause removed until regular appointees join their positions as per the rules - Petitioners are entitled to receive at least the minimum of the pay scale along with dearness allowance.
