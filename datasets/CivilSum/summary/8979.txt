Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (Conduct and Discipline) Regulations, 1971 - Regulation 25 - Disciplinary proceedings - When considering revocation of dismissal after a certain number of years, the Court must examine the soundness of the challenge presented to the validity of orders - Technical infirmity in proceedings justifies a de novo enquiry - Court has discretion to order reinstatement with or without backwages - Advisability of restarting proceedings at a late point in time considered - Backwages and de novo enquiries should be granted cautiously to bear in mind use of public time and money - Constitution of India, Article 226.

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (Conduct and Discipline) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 25 - Disciplinary enquiry - Punishment - The gravity of punishment should not be based on previous misconduct without employee's opportunity to dispute the record - Previous misconduct should not form the basis of a harsh punishment unless employee had the opportunity to show cause - Past record should be brought to the notice of the employee when charges are held established.
