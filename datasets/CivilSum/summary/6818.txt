The petitioner in this case was found guilty of drinking alcohol during duty, which has been regarded as a grave act of misconduct under Rule 16.2(1) of the Punjab Police Rules of 1934.
Consequently, the decision to dismiss the petitioner from service was upheld.
[Para 3]

When the petitioner appeared before the Enquiry Officer regarding charges levelled against him under Rule 16.2(1) of the Punjab Police Rules, he was given the opportunity to hear a summary of the charges against him and was handed a copy of the same and a list of witnesses by the Enquiry Officer.
Although he was not cross-examined, he was only explained the nature of the allegations.
As a result of the impartial and proper departmental enquiry, the charges against the petitioner were proven.
[Paras 2 and 5]
