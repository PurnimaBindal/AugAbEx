University admission to a five-year course that requires some papers to be completed and others to be considered supplementary.
Candidates who cannot pass supplementary subjects will be granted provisional admission to the next year.
Same procedure applied in later years.
Regulations state if a candidate granted provisional admission and fails to pass subsidiary papers, their second or final year exam will be cancelled.
Candidates allowed to appear for subsidiary papers prior to final results.
If they pass subsidiary papers before final results are declared, they are eligible to receive a final result.
Candidates are allowed to participate in the examination process, with rules that allow for two interpretations.
The interpretation that favours the candidate is accepted.
