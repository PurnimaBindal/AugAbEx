Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984 - Promotion - Refusal of - Based on petitioner's not having passed test as per 1984 Rules - Though a junior was promoted in 1986, petitioner granted seniority from 1983 - Decided that the order from 1986 links to seniority list of 1983 - Therefore, petitioner is eligible for promotion as he is senior.
