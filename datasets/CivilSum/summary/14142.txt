Constitution of India, Article 311 - Unauthorized absence - Termination thereof - The services of a police constable were terminated due to his willful absence from duty on two occasions, despite his claim of his mother's illness not being substantiated - It was held that the punishment was proportionate to the appellant's misconduct as he displayed insincerity and negligence towards his duty - The court cannot question the authority's decision to terminate the services based on the misconduct - Accordingly, the appeal was dismissed.
