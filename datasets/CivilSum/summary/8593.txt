Army Act, Section 192 - Army Regulations, 1950, Regulation 333(C)(c) - Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 125 - Criminal Court and Civil Court - When an Army personnel marries multiple times, and if a matter is pending before a Civil Court, the decision of the Civil Court must be awaited before passing an order of removal from service under Regiment 333(C)(c).
In the instance of the case, the matter was pending before the Criminal Court under Section 125 of the Criminal Procedure Code.
The Court will be deemed as a Civil Court for the purpose of Regiment 333(C)(c), and the removal of the Army personnel from service without waiting for the decision of the Court is set aside.
