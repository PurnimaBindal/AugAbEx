Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paras 173 and 173-A Appendix II - Entitlement Rules, Rule 7(b) - Disability pension - Non-release of disability pension is not an acceptable reason for military administration to retain the benefit - Would be an injustice towards the members if the government takes advantage of such situations.

Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paras 173 and 173-A, Appendix II - Entitlement Rules, Rule 7(b) - Disability pension - It is a mistake to consider Schizophrenia as a constitutional disease that cannot be associated with stress and strain of military service.
It does not disentitle those discharged with the disease from receiving disability pension.
