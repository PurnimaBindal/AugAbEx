Sections 10 to 14 and Schedule Parts I and II of the Nursing Council Act (48 of 1947) state that the establishment of nursing colleges/institutions does not require prior permission or recognition from the Indian Nursing Council.
However, individuals who obtain recognised qualifications from non-compliant training institutions are not eligible to register themselves as nurses, midwives, auxiliary nurse-midwives, etc., in a State other than where the institution is located.
