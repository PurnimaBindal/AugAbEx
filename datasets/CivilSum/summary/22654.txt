Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Determination of Employer Employee relationship - The burden of proving that a claimant was employed by a specific management lies on the person making the claim - However, the level of proof needed varies depending on the case and is ultimately a question of fact - All relevant material presented by the claimant and the management must be taken into account before making a determination.
