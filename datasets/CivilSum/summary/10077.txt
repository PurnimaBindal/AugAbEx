Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Dismissal from service - Petitioner obtained a loan of Rs. 51,700/- for purchase of house - Authorities charged petitioner with misappropriation of loan amount and unauthorised absence - However, petitioner did not misappropriate funds as the loan was taken from his own account - Charge of unauthorised absence not substantiated - Moreover, petitioner suffered from serious illness and was unable to attend office - Therefore, order of dismissal is overturned.
[Paras 8 to 11]
