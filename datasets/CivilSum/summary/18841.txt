Panjab University Regulations, Regulation 12 - Punjabi University Ordinance for MBBS examination, Regulation 26(B)(i) - Grace marks - Non-grant of - According to Punjabi University Regulations, failing candidates can be declared pass if they receive 1% of total marks, including practical and internal assessment marks.
Panjab University Regulations state that a candidate who takes full subjects can receive 1% of total marks in one or more subjects as grace marks.
The University's decision to not award grace marks to deserving candidates is deemed unsustainable.
Petitioners are entitled to grace marks and universities must comply.
