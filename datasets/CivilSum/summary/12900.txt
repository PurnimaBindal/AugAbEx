Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - ROPA Rules, 1998 - Compassionate appointment - Discrimination - The provision for compassionate appointment is available to teachers working in primary schools, but not to the wards of teaching/non-teaching members of staff working in recognized non-government secondary schools - The petitioner cannot claim discrimination and seek a mandamus from the writ Court - As there is no provision for compassionate appointment to the wards of teachers working in recognized non-government secondary schools in the State, the petitioner cannot allege discrimination - The order of Director rejecting the application for approval of appointment on compassionate grounds is considered proper.
