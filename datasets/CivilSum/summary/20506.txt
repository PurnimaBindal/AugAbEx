Central Civil Services (Temporary) Services Rules, 1965 - The respondent retired on 13111980 upon reaching the age of superannuation - She was not eligible for a liberalized pension in 1980 because she had not worked for 20 years - The Fourth Pay Commission recommended the granting of pro rata pension for those who have completed 10 years qualified service for pension for the first time with effect from 111986 - The respondent argued that the recommendation should not be applied retrospectively - However, it was held that the recommendation was only effective from 111986 and not from the date when the respondent retired on 13111980, which means that she was not entitled to the pension under the new recommendation.
