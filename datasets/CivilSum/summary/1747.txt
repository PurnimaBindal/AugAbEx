Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 1998 - Promotion - Selection Criteria - Seniority-cum-merit - For promotion from clerk to JMGS-I, the competent authority can determine a minimum standard and mode of assessment of an employee's merit - Assessment can be based on performance appraisal, service record, and interview, and a minimum mark can be set for promotion by seniority-cum-merit - In this case, the petitioner received only 54 marks, while at least 55 marks were required for eligibility - The last selected candidate had scored 62 marks - Therefore, it was right not to promote the petitioner to the JMGS-I position.
[Paras 5 and 8]
