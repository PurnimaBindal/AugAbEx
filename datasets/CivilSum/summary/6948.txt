The West Bengal Schools (Recruitment of Non-teaching Staff) Rules, 2005, Rule 8(5) mandates employers to obtain eligible candidates' names from employment exchange.
The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 affirm the right to participate in selection processes.
However, if the petitioner's name is not sponsored by employment exchange as per statutory recruitment rules, they cannot take part in the selection process.
As the requisition was sent according to statutory rules, the petitioner's plea is dismissed.
