The order of compulsory retirement of any official cannot be based on extraneous considerations like better talent available in the department, as per Constitution of India, Article 311.
This order was quashed.
[Para 5] B.
Before passing the order of compulsory retirement as per Constitution of India, Article 21, 311, the principles of natural justice need not be followed.
Also, provisions of Article 21 need not be observed in such cases.
