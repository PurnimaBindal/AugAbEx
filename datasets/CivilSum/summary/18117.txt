The entitlement of fixed medical allowance for an employee is not a sufficient ground to refuse reimbursement in the case of a serious ailment such as cancer.
The meager amount of fixed medical allowance cannot be considered enough for the necessary treatment of serious ailments like cancer.
[Para 5] B.
The bills for the treatment of cancer by reputed government hospitals such as PGI, Bombay Hospital, and Bombay Hospital & Medical Research Center cannot be alleged to be ingenuine.
[Para 5] C. The mere fact that the patient was taken to a hospital other than AIIMS to which PGI referred is not sufficient to refuse reimbursement.
The authority cannot be too technical and rigid to reject the claim in the urgency of the situation to save a life.
The value of life and practical approach must be prioritized over money and technical considerations.
The reimbursement must be at least equal to the rates of recognized hospitals for that purpose.
