Constitution of India, Article 136 - Education - Admission - The respondents submitted a writ petition asking for admission to the MBBS Course - The Single Judge directed the university to give admission to the respondents in the next session if admission in the current course was not feasible - The university's appeal was dismissed by the Division Bench - The university filed a special leave petition - Respondent No. 3 withdrew from litigation during the pendency of the SLP as he had completed his Agricultural Science studies and was unable to join the MBBS Course - Other respondents did not participate despite being served - Only one respondent, through a written statement sent by post, sought direction to the University to grant admission to him in the Post Graduate Medical Clinical Course - The matter, deemed of academic interest, was left open without any expression of opinion regarding the interpretation placed by the High Court - The appeal was dismissed as infructuous.
