Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Section 22(1) - Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952, Para 32-B - Recovery of damages from Sick Industry - Employees' Provident Funds are payable regardless of the health of the undertaking - They are an essential part of employees' right to life under Article 21 of the Constitution - Recovery of Employee's Provident Funds is not covered by Section 22(1) of the Sick Industries Act, 1995 - Parliament protected only against damages under Section 14-B in the case of a sick industry with an approved scheme - Legal action for Employee's Provident Funds recovery is permissible against sick industries.
