Punjab Civil Services, Volume I, Part I, Rule 8.21 - Leave encashment - Individuals who retire on their date of superannuation or through premature retirement under the rules are entitled to leave encashment - No differentiation exists between those who retire on superannuation and those who do so through premature retirement - Preventing those given premature retirement from receiving the benefit is arbitrary and illegal.
[Paras 3 and 4]
Punjab Civil Services, Volume I, Part I, Rule 8.21 - Punjab Civil Services (Pre-mature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Premature retirement does not constitute a penalty or stigma - It merely follows rules permitting such retirement - Denial of leave encashment to individuals given such retirement is entirely irrational and discriminatory.
[Para 4]
Pre-mature/compulsory retirement/instructions contrary of rules - In a case where the government's instructions deny leave encashment benefits for those given premature retirement, the Labour Court can override them - Such instructions are already deemed illegal and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution - Labour Court can rightly grant the benefit of leave encashment, despite the instructions.
