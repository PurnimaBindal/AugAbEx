Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Gujarat Secondary Education Act, 1972, Section 36(3) - Criminal Proceeding - Deemed suspension - Petitioner, who was in judicial custody for over 48 hours, claimed that he should be deemed suspended during that period and thus entitled to suspension allowance - No official order of suspension was passed but salaries were withheld on "no work no pay" principle - Tribunal correctly rejected claim for subsistence allowance.
