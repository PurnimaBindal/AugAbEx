Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Petitioner was terminated from service on 2283 - The petitioner's right to continue in service up to the age of 65 years was given in accordance with the rules, as they were granted a three-year extension from 10381 - In 1983, the petitioner filed a petition challenging the termination - The length of time that has passed since then, 16 years, makes it inappropriate to dismiss the petition on the grounds that the petitioner decided to close the chapter regarding their termination - If it is determined that the petitioner had the right to work up to the age of 65 years and that their termination was unlawful, it would be inappropriate to non-suit the petitioner on such hyper-technical grounds - Therefore, the respondents are required to pay the petitioner's salary from 2283 to 9384, treating them as if they were still in service until the age of 65 years.
