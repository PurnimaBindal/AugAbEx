Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Discrimination - Necessary parties - Estopped - Post of Deputy Director of Planning (Area Planning and Development Programme) - Petitioner was unjustly deprived of promotion to the said post - In contrast, his juniors, who never held the feeder post of Special Officer, Small Enterprise Programme and did not have Doctorate Degree in Economics, were promoted to the impugned post - This is a clear case of unfair exercise of power, arbitrary discrimination, and victimization of petitioner - Additionally, the norms set down by Calcutta Metropolitan Development Authority (C.M.D.A.)
were not followed - It is also important to note that non-joinder or misjoinder of parties must be pleaded at the initial stage - Since the appellant failed to raise this objection before the trial Judge at the initial stage, they cannot now wait to point out any alleged irregularities.
