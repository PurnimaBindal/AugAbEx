Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularization - Post of Scientific Assistant - Petitioner started as LDC and was afterwards appointed as Scientific Assistant on a purely temporary basis - Notification of appointment clearly stating the temporary nature of post - Petitioner accepted the terms and conditions of service without protest - The petition to seek regularization was dismissed by the Single Judge - Court held that since petitioner was not appointed in accordance with the rules, his service could not be regularized because he had worked for some time - The petitioner has no enforceable right arising from such an appointment - Recruitment cannot be made a mode of regularization.
[Paras 9 and 10]
