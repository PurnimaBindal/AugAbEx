Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 (as amended in 2001), Sections 13(3) and 25 - Medical Council Screening Test Regulations, 2002, Regulation 3 - Registration as medical practician - Screening test for registration - Internship for those with medical degrees from Russian institutions after reports of erosion of educational standards - Govt. of India does not recognize such degrees for permanent registration - Students who passed before 1532002 and those after must qualify the Screening Test Regulations, 2002 before registration - Amendment and cut off date make Executive policy legislative - Compliance with provisions of Section 13(4A) of the Act - Regulations fix 1532002 a cut off date.
