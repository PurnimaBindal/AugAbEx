Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Posts of Primary Teacher - The petitioner failed to secure appointment based on her original marks of 478/800 in the B.Ed.
examination.
She later applied for a paper improvement which increased her marks by 9 to attain 487/800.
However, she did not present the revised mark sheet or submit another application before the deadline.
As a result, her improved marks were not considered for appointment.
The court held that the petitioner's carelessness and inaction were to blame for her failure to secure the desired result.
The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 8 and 9]
