Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Joint Entrance Test - Admission - Clause 14 of the advertisement for admission to various diploma courses in Engineering states that candidates not reporting on time shall lose their right to priority in merit - However, no specific time for reporting was mentioned in Clause 14 - Interpreting this clause as written would render it unworkable - Therefore, a candidate appearing for counselling before others does not necessarily have a preferential right to admission over those with higher merit.
