Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 20(3) - Minimum wages - The employer, who was the proprietor of a hotel, denied paying minimum wages to his employees by paying them a salary of Rs. 105000 without giving them a salary slip and taking signatures on a blank voucher.
A representation was made to the Labour Inspector who directed the employer to pay minimum wages, but the employer challenged the order contending that he could not appear before the authority as he was out of station.
However, the grounds shown by the employer were not convincing, and no interference was called for.
[Paras 7, 8 & 12]
