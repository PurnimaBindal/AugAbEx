Haryana Govt.'s Regularisation Policy dated 7/1831996 states that Daily Wage Employees can be regularised after a year of service, but with a break in service not exceeding one month at a time, only if the person has worked for a minimum of 240 days in that year.
If the condition is met, the employee is entitled to continuous service and can be regularised after completing the prescribed period.
