Under the UP Higher Education Service Commission Act, the power to make appointments of teachers in colleges has been over-ridden, which means that the management of the college, the Vice-Chancellor, and even the State government are unable to appoint a teacher or principal on deputation as per Section 31(12) of the Service Commission Act.
Appointing a Principal on deputation in violation of the Act is illegal.
This has been established by various cases such as Om Prakash v. Swaroop Singh Tomar, Dr. Krishna Sinha v. UP Higher Education Service Commission, Dr. Man Singh v. Committee of Management, and Gadde Venkateswara Rao v Govt. of Andhra Pradesh.
