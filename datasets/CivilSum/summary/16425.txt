Article 311(2)(d) of the Constitution of India details the process for dispensing with the regular enquiry against a government servant before termination, requiring the punishing authority to provide cogent reasons for the decision and demonstrate that it is not reasonably practicable to hold the enquiry.
Failure to follow this procedure can render the punishment invalid.
