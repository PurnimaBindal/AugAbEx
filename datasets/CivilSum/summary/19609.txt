The Impugned order that modified the terms of appointment for the petitioner, who was initially employed as an oilman on temporary work charge basis and later re-employed on daily wage basis, is deemed illegal and set aside.
[Para 3]

The petitioner's appointment as oilman against a vacancy that arose after one oilman was promoted to boilerman was questioned given that the sanction for boilerman was only for five posts, against which six persons were senior to petitioner, making the petitioner's appointment dubious.
Further, the junior most person had to be reverted as oilman due to this foul play, and the Administration of PGI was given two options: terminate the petitioner's services by giving him a month's notice or allow him to continue his duties as a daily wage worker from where he was picked up.
The court upheld the change in service conditions for petitioner and his case would be considered for work charge appointment as an oilman when such a post arises.
Until then, he shall remain on the job on a daily wage basis.
[Paras 2, 3, and 4]
