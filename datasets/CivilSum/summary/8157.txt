Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 27(1)(a) - Appointment of Administrator - Appointment of Supervisor by the Administrator - The administrator's term is limited to one year, during which time they must organize new elections before their term expires.
They are not authorized to appoint a supervisor after their term has ended, and doing so is in violation of the Act and invalid.
