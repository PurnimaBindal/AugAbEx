Rules 39-A, 39-B, 50, 51, 52, and 53 of the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972 deal with pensions, gratuities, family, and nominations.
An unmarried government servant's nomination becomes invalid once he is married.
A government servant's widow is entitled to the family pension and gratuity if her husband dies.
Extra rules to the Hindu Succession Act are included.
[Paras 13, 16, 22, and 23]

Rule 19.3 of the Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme, 1980 outlines family and nomination criteria, pertaining specifically to unmarried government servants; their nominations become invalid once they marry.
Members with families may nominate only other members of their family; the definition of family includes wives/widows but excludes married male subscribers' mothers.
Only widows are entitled to collect the group insurance scheme's amount.
The Act to Hindu Succession is described.
[Paras 17, 18, 22, 23, and 24]

Rule 10 of the General Provident Fund (Central) Service Rules, 1960 defines the term "Family," stating that it is the wife/widow who is entitled to the deceased husband's GPF amount.
A nomination made before marriage becomes void after marriage.
Additional rules pertaining to the Hindu Succession Act are included.
