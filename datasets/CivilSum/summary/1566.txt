Teaching experience and postgraduate degrees are important qualifications for appointment as a principal of B.Ed.
College.
A teacher can continue as a student of postgraduate courses if the management can adjust their teaching and study hours without any interference and impact upon each other.
There is nothing wrong with this.
The experience of a teacher in teaching while doing his Ph.D cannot be ignored merely because he was continuing his studies also.
Despite the requirements for a few classes in the first semester of MPhil, which can be easily adjusted for a teacher, no regular attendance is required in the second semester since it relates to dissertation and is a matter only between the student and the guide.
A teacher can achieve the requisite teaching experience while serving as a lecturer in a College of Education and continuing with their studies in MPhil.
Once Management knowingly adjusted a teacher's timing for their study, it cannot be said that the teacher was concealing their studies, especially when their excellence in teaching was exhibited.
