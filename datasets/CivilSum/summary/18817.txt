According to Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, the petitioner who served in the army for 14 months and was subsequently discharged with 100% disability is entitled to receive disability pension.
The nature of his duties clearly indicate that the disability suffered by him is attributable to his army service.
Therefore, the respondents are directed to release his disability pension for a period of 38 months prior to the institution of the writ petition.
