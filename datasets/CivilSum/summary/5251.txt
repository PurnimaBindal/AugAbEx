As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 6, an employee who has been dismissed following a guilty verdict in an enquiry but later acquitted of the identical charges in a criminal case cannot assume that the acquittal will automatically absolve him/her of the dismissal.
To determine the validity of the domestic enquiry, the labour court will consider if it was fair, reasonable, and in line with the principles of natural justice.
