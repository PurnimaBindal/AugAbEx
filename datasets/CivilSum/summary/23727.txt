The Recruitment Rules of 1981 for the Assistant Conservator of Forests (Gujarat Forest Service Class II) do not mention the fixation of seniority.
The State Government was thus within its rights to establish a principle for seniority fixation via an administrative resolution dated January 31, 1992.
Since no inter se seniority of directly recruited and promoted officers had been determined before this resolution, provisional and final seniority lists did not contain any names of directly recruited officers.
As a result, the Single Judge correctly deemed these lists invalid and thus promotee officers cannot claim promotional rights based on said lists.
Therefore, the judgment of the Single Judge has been restored.
