The duty of the authorities under the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous provisions Act, 1982, Sections 86 and 11 - Recovery of Debts due to Banks and Financial Institutions Act, 1993, Sections 2(g), 17, 29 and 34 - is to investigate the objections of the petitioners' Banks as to the sale of disputed property.
In order to realize certificate dues from two companies, the EPF organization issued a notice for the sale of premises which were then attached by the Recovery officer.
However, the Banks claimed that buildings mortgaged in their favor entitled them to notice and hearing before the sale of such property.
The objections of the petitioner were rejected on the grounds of maintainability but it was held that the petitioner has the right to be heard before the sale of disputed property until objections have been adjudicated.
