Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulation 6(3) - Disciplinary proceedings - Challenge regarding statement of allegations - Although the statement of allegations was not furnished as required, substantial compliance with Regulation 6(3) was achieved as the articles of charge contained the necessary information - Furthermore, since no prejudice was demonstrated, there was no negative effect.
[Para 31]

Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulations 6(10), 6(11), 6(12) - Disciplinary proceedings - Refusal to allow defence witnesses - Since petitioner failed to show the relevance of the witnesses he wished to examine, the regulation was complied with when records which were relevant were filed - It should be noted that the law mandates a fair hearing and not the fairest possible hearing - Additionally, employees have the right to summon relevant records.
[Paras 33 and 34]

Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulations 6(10), 6(11), 6(12) - Disciplinary proceedings - Appointment of enquiry officer - There is no merit in the plea that an enquiry officer was appointed without considering the reply to the charge-sheet since the right of the delinquent officer is only affected after the enquiry is completed.
[Para 35]

Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977 - Disciplinary proceedings - Failure to call material witness - The charge, which relates to giving undue advantage to a firm, is admitted by the delinquent officer who disclosed that his wife entered into partnership with such person, with a 90% share and that such person retired from the partnership after three months - Given these facts, examining such witnesses would not have justified fair play in action and hence, examining such witness was irrelevant.
[Paras 37 and 38]

Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulation 6(7) - Disciplinary proceedings - Assistance by legal practitioner - Assistance of a legal practitioner cannot be insisted by the petitioner because it is not the case of presenting officer being a lawyer.
[Para 42]

Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulation 6(17) - Disciplinary proceedings - Questioning of delinquent - Since delinquent led no evidence, there was no need to question the delinquent regarding the circumstances appearing against him - Furthermore, since no prejudice was demonstrated, there was no negative effect.

Disciplinary proceedings - Writ jurisdiction - Scope of - The role of a High Court while exercising power under Article 226 is to determine if the delinquent got a fair and adequate hearing within broad parameters and if the material on record justifies the findings and not to reappreciate the evidence or dissect enquiry microscopically.
