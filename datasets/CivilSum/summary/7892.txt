Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Service of Engineers and Research Officers (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1954, Rule 7-A - Deputation - Discrimination - In accordance with the established law, Government employees cannot be seconded without their explicit approval - Once an individual has been selected for a post - whether through direct recruitment or promotion or due to a post upgrade - they are considered a member of one cadre and must be treated equally - Assistant Engineers who have been promoted and those who have been recruited directly are both part of the same group and cannot be differentiated based solely on their recruitment status with regard to deputation.
