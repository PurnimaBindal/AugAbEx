Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal proceedings - If the criminal court did not draw up a charge sheet against the employee, the Respondent- Management is at liberty to proceed with the disciplinary proceedings against the writ petitioner in accordance with the law.
Even if the charges framed in the domestic enquiry and the charges mentioned in the FIR are identical, continuing with disciplinary proceedings is not unlawful.
