Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 11-A - Retrenchment - Compensation instead of termination - Labour Court ordered reinstatement with full back wages for a production worker employed at the rate of Rs. 050 per hour plus allowances.
However, a writ petition against the award was dismissed.
The court did not consider the merits of the case, but emphasized industrial peace and harmony and the interest of justice.
Therefore, instead of reinstatement, the worker was awarded a compensation of Rs. 150 lacs.
