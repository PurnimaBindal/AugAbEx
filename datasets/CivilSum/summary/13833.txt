Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Fixation of Pay - Reduction - Stepping up - The petitioner joined services in 1976 while the sixth respondent joined in 1981.
The sixth respondent's pay has been refixed twice, resulting in a higher pay than the petitioner.
The petitioner's request for stepping up his pay was rejected.
It was held that a senior person in the same grade cannot receive lower pay.
The authority directed to step up the petitioner's pay to the level of the sixth respondent.
