Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - APSRTC Employees (Service) Regulations, 1964, Regulation 6(B) - Resignation - A bus conductor in APSRTC resigned on health grounds on 182001, and APSRTC accepted the resignation with immediate effect, while forfeiting the security deposit in lieu of the one-month notice period.
The petitioner contested an election to the post of Gram Panchayat on 1482001, after submitting the resignation, but being defeated in the election.
The petitioner sought to withdraw the resignation on 3182001 on the plea that he was entitled to withdraw the resignation anytime before the expiry of the notice period.
Only after petitioner requested, was the resignation accepted by APSRTC.
The respondents could have taken no exception to accepting the resignation.
[Paras 8 and 9] Khaja Masiuddin v Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corpn., 2000 (2) ALD 370.
