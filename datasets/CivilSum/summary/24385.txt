Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16(4) - Fraud - Issuing and receiving benefits as freedom fighters on forged certificates and medals is a fraud on the public and a serious disrespect towards actual freedom fighters - The writs in public interest litigation, upon a report from an enquiry committee on alleged fraud, cannot be dismissed by looking at only a few petitions but rather each one must be examined based on its own facts - Fraud defined - Case remanded.
[Paras 7 to 11]
