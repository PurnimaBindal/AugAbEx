Under the Employees State Insurance Act of 1948, Sections 2(12), 44, 45-A and 45-B, and the Employees' State Insurance Regulations of 1950, Regulations 11, 12 and 32, the burden of proof for non-payment of contribution lies with the petitioner factory, which must prove that it did not employ more than 20 persons during the relevant period.
However, if the factory cannot prove this, it will be directed to pay contributions on actual wages paid to employees during that period.
