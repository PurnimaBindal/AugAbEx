Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recovery - Natural justice - The authorities cannot recover excess amounts paid to employees for more than 10 years without affording them an opportunity to address the issue - Employees acquire civil rights under the pay fixation order - The recovery cannot take place without giving notice and opportunity of hearing to the concerned employee - The order of recovery is quashed with the liberty to proceed again after giving notice and opportunity of hearing.
