Article 226 of the Constitution of India pertains to absence from duty by an employee on probation who is absent at the time the probationary period ends.
The principle of deemed confirmation does not exist, and this is distinguished from the 1968(3) SCR 1 case.
[Para 8]
The State Bank of India Officers (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service) Order, 1979 outlines rules for probationary or trainee officers, stating that if the competent authority finds their training to be satisfactory and does not issue a termination order, they are deemed to have been confirmed.
However, it is important to note that the employer must allow the employee to continue in their post even after the maximum period of probation has ended, which is consistent only with the fact of their confirmation on the job.
