Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Maintainability of reference - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - The Labour Court must base its decision on the case presented by the parties - The management did not object to being considered an industry and the petitioners as workmen - The Labour Court erred in declining the reference as not maintainable when no such issue was framed - The award is set aside and the case is remanded for a decision on merits to be made promptly.
