Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punishment/Dismissal from service/Absence from duty - The plaintiff was punished with dismissal for absencing himself from duty while in Police Lines for security of the Chief Minister and over-staying his leave.
The District Court considered the punishment of dismissal too severe and instead substituted it with compulsory retirement and prospective pensionary benefits due to the plaintiff's long service.
The Supreme Court, in Ashok Kumar's case 1988(2) SLR 209, deemed the judgment of the District Judge fair and just, and did not see any reason to interfere.
