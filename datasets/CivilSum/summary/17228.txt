Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Increase in fee - Petitioner enrolled in a BE Computers Course reserved for NRI/NRI sponsored candidates - Petitioner deposited the required amount for the 1996-97 session - Petitioner now objects to being charged more for the 1997-98 academic year - Director Technical Education sanctioned a fee of 5000 US dollars per annum for NRI candidates, so the petitioner cannot insist on being charged the same amount as the previous year.
