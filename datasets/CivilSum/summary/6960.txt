Constitution of India, Article 311 - Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rule 14(ii) - Dispensing of enquiry - Removal from service - Employee who assaulated senior officer - Order of removal passed without holding any enquiry - Natural justice not followed - It was not demonstrated that it was not reasonably practicable to hold enquiry - Order of removal was invalidated.
