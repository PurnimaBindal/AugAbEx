Constitution of India, Articles 25 and 26 - Appointment - "santhikaran" or poojari - As long as the appointed Santhikarna is well-versed, properly trained and qualified to perform the pooja in a manner conducive to the worship of the particular deity, no valid or legally justifiable grievance can be made in a Court of law, regardless of their pedigree.
[Para 18] B Custom and usage - No usage can be accepted or upheld in Court if found to be in derogation of the law of the land, opposed to public policy or social decency, or violates human rights or the specific mandate of Constitution law made by parliament, regardless of any proof of their existence in pre-constitutional days.
