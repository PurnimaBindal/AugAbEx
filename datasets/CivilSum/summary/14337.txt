Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gratuity - A petitioner who served as a Lecturer in a private aided institution for more than 33 years was denied gratuity - Instead, the petitioner was informed that the Rs. 25,000/- already paid as Ex-Gratia Grant should be treated as the college management's share of gratuity - The court directed Respondent No. 3 to disburse the full amount of gratuity within three months.
