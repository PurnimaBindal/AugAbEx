Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28 and Schedule IV, Item 1 - Dismissal from service - An employee who was dismissed from service for being abusive filed a complaint with the Labour Court.
While the complaint was pending, the employee reached the superannuation age.
The Labour Court found that the punishment of dismissal was excessive and awarded full back-wages.
It was noted that the employee had seven other misconducts to their credit, and the order of the Labour Court was modified.
The respondent was directed to pay the employee 50% backwages from the date of dismissal until the retirement date.
