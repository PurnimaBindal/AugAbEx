Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Compassionate appointment - Public service recruitment - All appointments in public services must be made through open invitation of applications and merit - No other method of appointment or consideration is allowed - Only in cases of pure humanitarian reasons, where an employee dies in harness and leaves his family stranded and penniless, can a compassionate appointment be made - The goal is to support the family in their time of need, not to offer them a job - Therefore, the Government must consider the situation and assess the resources available to the family before deciding to offer a compassionate appointment.
[Paras 1 and 2]
