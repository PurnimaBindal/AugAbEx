Kerala Khadi and Village Industries Board Regulations, 1967, Clause 16 - Cancellation of appointment - The appellants' appointment was cancelled due to a mistake made during the appointment process.
The Commission was misled by a certificate issued by Chavara Block Khadi Producers Industries Co-operative Society, which falsely stated that it was certified by Khadi and Village Industries Commission.
The appellants are not responsible for this false certificate.
Cancelling their appointment after seven years of service would be a grave injustice to them.
[Paras 3 and 7]
