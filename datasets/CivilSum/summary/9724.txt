Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(3)(a) & (b), 7A - Application of Act - Provident Fund Inspector assessed employees to be above 20 - The contention that only 14 employees are regular and service of rest is utilized subject to exigencies is disputed - The duration for which additional hands worked is irrelevant and the normal regular requirement of the establishment reflecting its financial capacity and stability is to be seen - It cannot be held that provisions of Act will not apply when casual workers are regularly employed month after month and year after year.
