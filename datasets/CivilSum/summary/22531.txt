Central Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3(1) (i), 3(1)(ii) and 3(1)(iii) - Income-tax Officer who performs quasi-judicial functions while completing assessment in an irregular manner and confers undue favour upon assessees, is liable to disciplinary action by the Government - There is no immunity to an authority from disciplinary proceedings especially with respect to matters that are decided in the exercise of quasi-judicial functions - If there are any issues, the legality of the order of the authority can be questioned in appeal or Revision under the Act.
