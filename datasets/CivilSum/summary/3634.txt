The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Schedule II, Item 6, and Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act of 1946, Section 2-A(1) dictate the age of retirement.
Certified Standing Orders of employer do not specify the age of retirement, and it is contended that the Model Standing Orders under the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946 must be the governing principle for the age of superannuation.
However, until the modification of the certified Standing Orders to comply with the amended Model Standing Orders, the amended Model Standing Orders cannot come into effect.
Letters of appointment of some workmen have provided the age of retirement as 55 years, while others retired only upon completion of 60 years of age.
It is argued that isolated cases of such retirement could not establish a customary concession or long practice in respect of retirement age.
