Constitution of India, Article 16 - Redesignation - Sustainability - Cancellation of redesignation without notice - When a post is redesignated to two posts higher, a vested right is obtained by the petitioner, and as a result, principles of fair play, equity and natural justice require providing an opportunity to be heard before removing such a vested right - Request is granted.
