Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I Part I, Rule 4.4(a) - Haryana Health Department Non-Medical Gazetted Officers Statistical (Group B) Service Rules, 1995, Rule 9 - Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation (Group A) Service Rules, 1963, Rule 13 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(1) - Assistant Director and Statistical Officer - The Court affirmed that the posts occupied by the Petitioners cannot be compared with the post of Deputy Economic and Statistical Adviser and Research Officer as they belong to different departments, have distinct duties, qualifications and hierarchical structures.
Moreover, the judiciary cannot make a comparison between two different departments purposes for equal work and equal pay.
Petitioners have already benefitted from state-issued instructions that removed the parity anomaly, hence there is no violation of Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution.
[Paras 11, 13, 14, 15 and 16]
