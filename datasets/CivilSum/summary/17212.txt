According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, delay or laches can be an issue.
In this case, a seniority list was created in 1982 and effectively implemented.
Respondent No. 2 was promoted in 1986 while the petitioner was promoted in 1991.
The seniority list was challenged for the first time in 1996, but the petition was barred due to delay.
[Para 4] B.
As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner was taken in as a fresh recruit as of June 3, 1981, while respondent was a direct recruit since July 21, 1979.
Respondent was duly promoted in July 1986, while the petitioner was promoted in May, 1991.
As a result, respondent was rightfully considered senior to the petitioner.
