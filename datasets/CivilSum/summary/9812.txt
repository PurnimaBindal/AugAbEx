Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 150 - Pensionary Benefits - The husband of the petitioner retired while on unauthorised leave for around 14 years, thus pensionary benefits were not paid for the unauthorised absence.
The respondents are now directed to pay the pensionary benefit for the unauthorised leave, which will be calculated based on the "average pay" that would have been drawn prior to retirement.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
Bihar Service Code, 1952, Rule 76 - Pensionary Benefits - Although the petitioner's husband was absent for 14 years without authorization, his service was not terminated automatically as Rule 76 of the Code has been declared invalid.
The petitioner is therefore eligible for pensionary benefit of her husband who retired whilst on leave without authorisation.
