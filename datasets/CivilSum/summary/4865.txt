Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Conductor - Disciplinary proceeding - Charge of taking fare and not issuing tickets - Order of dismissal - Denial of approval by Industrial Tribunal - The Tribunal and Single Judge both concluded appellant failed to prove charge - There is nothing in statement of witnesses to show respondent collected fare - Evidence like tallying of cash can be used to prove fare was collected - No reason to interfere with award.
