Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4, 17, 19, 28 - Compromise - A complete bar exists to reaching an agreement or compromise where a worker may relinquish their right to compensation from their employer - An agreement that reduces the statutory amount of compensation is void - The Commissioner cannot allow liable individuals to avoid liability under the Act simply by reaching a compromise for a smaller amount.
[Paras 9 and 10 to 12] B Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 15 - Limitation Act, Section 5 - Appeal - An appeal under the Workmen's Compensation Act filed after a 19-day delay under the bona fide belief that the limitation is 90 days, as in appeals under the Motor Vehicles Act for compensation claims arising out of an accident involving a motor vehicle - A mistake by a counsel cannot be used to make a party suffer - A bona fide mistake is considered a sufficient cause, and the delay has been condoned.
