According to the Syndicate Bank Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1976 and the Syndicate Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 6, a CBI officer who is a public servant can serve as Presenting Officer in a domestic enquiry.
[Para 11] B.
During a domestic enquiry, the principles of natural justice must be adhered to.
In this case, statements and documents against the petitioner were not proved, and witnesses did not testify in support of the charges.
Despite this, the disciplinary authority accepted the report.
The petitioner was greatly prejudiced, and the removal order was quashed.
