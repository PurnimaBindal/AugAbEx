The members of West Bengal Higher Judicial Service are treated the same as members of the Indian Administrative Service in all matters according to a government order.
Thus, any changes in the service conditions of the Indian Administrative Service would also apply to the West Bengal Higher Judicial Service.
When the retirement age of Indian Administrative Service officers increased from 58 to 60 years, the retirement age of members of West Bengal Judicial Service increased accordingly.
Therefore, the High Court cannot recommend compulsory retirement of judicial officers in Bengal before they reach the age of 60 years.
[Paras 9, 10 and 11]

The High Court's simple recommendation to retire a judicial officer on the grounds of their efficiency did not fulfill the conditions required in Rule 75(2)(a)(a) of the West Bengal Service Rules, Part-I.
It cannot be said that the provisions of the Rule were invoked by the High Court nor did the government exercise its discretion.
Thus, the order of retirement was deemed illegal.
[Para 12]
