Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Pay scale - Revision of pay scale - Fixation in revised pay scale - Option/Re-option - Cut off date - Petitioners exercised re-option within allowed extended time - Dispute is whether arrears resulting from re-option of Higher grades are admissible from date of effect of re-option or from date of filling of re-option - Decided, Date of effect of re-option and not date of actual exercise of re-option was intended - Grant of benefits should be based on relevant date of effect of re-option - Benefits granted should not be taken back retrospectively.
