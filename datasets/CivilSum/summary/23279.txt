The Electricity (Supply) (Himachal Pradesh Amendment) Act, 1990, amended the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, Section 5(6) on superannuation by instituting a retirement age for the Chairman.
The Government's policy automatically ceases on an individual holding office under Section 5(6) once they turn 65.
This applies to both the Chairman and other Board members.
The amendment is legal due to the same phraseology in Article 102 and 191 of the Constitution.
The State's policy was to introduce a superannuation age of 65 for the Chairman of the State Electricity Board under Section 3 of the Electricity (Supply) (Himachal Pradesh Amendment) Act, 1990, relating to a single person.
There is nothing unlawful about this section.
The Court cannot question the wisdom of policy nor attribute malicious intent to the legislature according to Article 14 of the Constitution and the interpretation of statutes.
A writ petition challenging the validity of the Act, which prescribes the age of superannuation, without including the newly appointed Chairman, is still valid under Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 1 Rule 10.
The Act challenged because the respondent stopped holding office as Chairman of the State Electricity Board after retirement upon turning 65.
