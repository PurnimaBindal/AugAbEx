Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986, Section 3 - Constitution of India, Articles 39(e), 39(f), 41 and 47 - Dealing with the issue of Child Labour - The Supreme Court directed State Governments to follow the legislative intent behind the enactment and ordered offending employers to compensate with Rs. 20,000 for every child employed in the Child Labour Rehabilitation Fund.
[Paras 28-31]
