Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Calcutta Metropolitan Development Authority (Service) Regulations, Sections 17 and 18 - Seniority - Petitioner, who entered service as a Technical Assistant, was not granted seniority based on their date of joining but on the basis of merit - The authority decided that promotion should be based on merit-cum-seniority - It was found that the Petitioner was entitled to notional seniority from the date of their joining before the provisional gradation list was published in 1981 - However, the promotional seniority granted to other candidates does not need to be disturbed.
