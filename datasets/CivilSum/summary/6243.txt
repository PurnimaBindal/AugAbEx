Fundamental Rules, Rules 56(j)(i) - Order of compulsory retirement - Challenge as to - Petitioner challenges Review Committee's decision on the ground that it acted in a mechanical manner without applying mind properly - Also, petitioner received adverse remarks one year before the order was passed - Out of the last nine ACRs, four are average and two are adverse - Court held that the Committee considered ACRs and career profile while deciding to retire the Petitioner and therefore, the order is not arbitrary, illegal or without basis.
[Paras 20 to 22]

Fundamental Rules, Rules 56(j)(i) - Order of compulsory retirement - Scope of judicial review - Judicial review's scope is limited to verifying the compliance with the parameters specified in Baikunthnath Dass's case and Umed Bhai Patel's case - If necessary, courts can examine original records to verify that the competent authority had considered relevant documents while concluding that public servant had outlived his utility.
