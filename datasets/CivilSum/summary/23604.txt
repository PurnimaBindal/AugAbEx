According to Section 2(22) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, overtime wages paid to an employee by appellants would be considered as "wages" under this section.
Even if the original contract of employment doesn't include stipulation for payment of the overtime wages, such remuneration paid towards the overtime work cannot be excluded from the definition of "wages".
[Para 5] B.
As per the same section, any remuneration paid or payable for overtime work is considered as part of the wages.
Therefore, the employer is required to pay contribution under the Act and comply accordingly.
