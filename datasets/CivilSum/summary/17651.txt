Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 124 and 126 Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services Regulations, Article 128 - Jammu and Kahsmir Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956 Unathorised absence - Termination of services - The grounds for action against respondent were not clearly defined, no formal charge or charges were communicated along with statement of allegations or other facts to be considered while passing termination order - The charge sheet could not be served upon respondent due to his unavailability - Order of termination cannot be passed without conducting an inquiry as laid down under Section 33 of Rules, 1956 - No inquiry conducted in this case - Therefore, the order of termination was nullified.
