Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Scope of - Enquiry - Fairness of enquiry - If an enquiry held by the employer is deemed unfair or improper, the employer has the right to establish the justification and fairness of the action taken by presenting fresh evidence.
If such permission is granted, evidence from the original enquiry cannot be used to justify the impugned action.
If the employer fails to provide fresh evidence, the workman is entitled to relief and is not obligated to provide evidence to support their claim.
