Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Sub-station Attendants - Claim of promotion as Junior Engineer (Sub-Station) - The Recruitment Policy has been amended to fill 20% posts of JE's by promotion on a seniority-cum-merit basis from subordinate technical staff of all cadres with three years' experience - 48 Junior employees from different technical cadres promoted as JE's field, Sub-station, Test, Carrier - It was contended that from the 20% quota, promotion could only be made to the post of JE (Field) - However, the court held that promotions against the 20% Direct Quota posts from amongst subordinate staff could not only be made to the post of JE (Field) since AFM/SSA/LM/ASSA/ALM/SA/LA/MM etc. are feeder cadres to the post of JE (Field), JE (Sub-Station), JE (Test), JE (Carrier).
