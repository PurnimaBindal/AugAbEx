Constitution of India, Article 14 - Suspension allowance for Bank employees during Criminal Proceedings and departmental proceedings after acquittal in Criminal Proceedings - Third Bipartite Settlement between Banks and employees after Sastry Award and Desai Award - Para 5 of Third Bipartite Settlement - Sub-clause (iii) of para 5 applies to cases where investigation is carried out for the purpose of criminal prosecution by an outside agency like police or CBI for departmental proceedings - Full pay and allowances are payable as subsistence allowance for such cases where the outside agency decides not to prosecute the employee, even when departmental proceedings follow the acquittal by a criminal court - The person who is acquitted cannot be placed in a worse position than the one who was proceeded against through the outside agency and concluded not to be prosecuted - Criminal trials cannot be considered as part of departmental action.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
