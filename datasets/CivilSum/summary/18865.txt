Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 9-A - Writ Petition - Alternative Remedy - Petitioner, employees union seeking to prevent respondent-Corporation from implementing new promotion policy and issuing notifications - Contending that this action violates the mandatory provisions of Section 9-A of the Industrial Disputes Act - Petitioner aims to enforce rights as laid out in the Industrial Disputes Act - Essentially, the appropriate recourse for the petitioner is to initiate an industrial dispute under the Industrial Disputes Act which represents an alternative remedy - Thus, the writ petition is not maintainable.
