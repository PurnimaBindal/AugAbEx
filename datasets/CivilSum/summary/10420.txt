According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, daily wage employees who work in regular departments or establishments of the government cannot receive a salary rate below the regular pay scale for the same position.
These workers are entitled to the same salary as their regular counterparts holding the same position.
Previous judgments of the Supreme Court do not address employees who work in the same organization as regular employees.
The Supreme Court's decision in State of Punjab v. Devinder Singh, states that daily wage workers in permanent government departments cannot be denied the regular pay scale of the position.
