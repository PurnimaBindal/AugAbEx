Under Punjab Civil Services Rules article 226, MP Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, and Rule 9(1), promotion to Head teachers requires one increment of higher responsibility for promotees.
However, this does not apply in cases where the worker was charge-sheeted after their retirement and has not caused any monetary loss to their employer.
As such, there is no obligation to impound 50% of their pension and the order of punishment and chargesheet should be quashed.
The employer should instead pay the petitioner's full pension.
[Paras 6, 7, 9 and 10]  B Similarly, under the same rules of MP Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, and Rule 9(2)(b), charges relating to events that take place more than 4 years before institution of departmental proceedings are considered stale.
For instance, if an employee was charge-sheeted after retirement for submitting a false certificate in 1961, departmental proceedings should not take place as it is outside the 4-year window.
Thus, Rule 9(2)(b) applies here.
