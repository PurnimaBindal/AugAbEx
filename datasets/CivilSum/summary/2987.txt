Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointments - Selection - Direction to appoint - Directions to reconsider - The judicial review power cannot extend to acting as if it were the executive, as the power to appoint or promote an officer belongs to the executive - If there is a dispute on facts, even if the court feels the petitioner was entitled to appointment, the writ court must direct the competent authority to reconsider the case of the aggrieved person rather than directing promotion or appointment.
[Paras 10 to 12]
