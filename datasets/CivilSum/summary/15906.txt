The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 21, and 311 outline the necessity of suspending employees who habitually accept illegal gratification in the public interest, even though the suspended employee still receives a suspension allowance and cannot complain about the violation of Articles 14 and 21 by being deprived of the right to livelihood.

The Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules of 1960, Rules 10 and 34, and the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 21, and 309 and 311 provide for the right of appeal only against an order passed by the authority lower than the Government, with a substituted right of review provided when the Government orders.
Therefore, even if the delinquent is not entitled to appeal, he can still use the review process to challenge the suspension order.
[Para 14]  D The Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules of 1960 prescribe the manner in which the Government must issue an order and how it should be authenticated by the Secretaries.
The amended Rules of the Government of Kerala are not contrary to Rule 10 of the Kerala Civil Services (C.C.A.)
Rules.
