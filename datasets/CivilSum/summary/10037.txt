Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous - Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7A to 7C and 7Q - According to the authority's order dated June 27, 1994, the petitioner had to pay the determined amount; however, a second proceeding was initiated under Section 7A and a final order was made determining due payments from July 1987 to February 2004.
The authority gave a fresh decision on the direction of the writ court and passed the impugned order directing the establishment to pay statutory dues along with interest under Sections 7A and 7Q.
The continuing offence principle had no application to proceedings under Section 7A as the provisions of Section 7C did not have any retrospective effect.
The impugned order was set aside.

Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 47A and 7C - Money due from employer - Escaped amount - The order of the authority directing the establishment to pay outstanding statutory dues along with interest was simply said that there were a large number of employees who did not pay statutory dues.
However, the names and other particulars of those employees were not disclosed.
Therefore, the order of authority considered to be one non-speaking to the extent possible.
