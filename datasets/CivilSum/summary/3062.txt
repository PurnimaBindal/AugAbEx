Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 17 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 - LPA challenging dismissal of writ petitions under Article 226 on basis of availability of alternative remedy under Section 17 of Payment of Wages Act - Held that availability of alternative remedy does not automatically lead to dismissal of petition.
