Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - Challenge by Appellants Fire Foreman (Regular) of transfer to Varanasi and that six senior officers were not transferred - AAI formulated FCS and promoted six officers from Fire and Rescue Operators to Fire Foreman (FCS) - Nature of duties between Fire Foreman (FCS) and Fire Foreman (Regular) are different and cannot be combined - Two categories are distinct even for transfer purposes - AAI treated Fireman (Regular) and Fire Foreman (FCS) as different classes, which is not illogical - Appeal rejected.
