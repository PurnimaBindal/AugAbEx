Army Regulations Regulation 173 - Disability pension - An employee is entitled to disability pension if no mention is made regarding the disease or disablement at the time of entry in service.
Presumption would be that the disability occurred during the course of service and was on account of the stress and strains of the army service.
A speaking order is required to be passed if the disability pension is to be denied.
The delay in approaching the court is irrelevant.
[Paras 28 and 29]
