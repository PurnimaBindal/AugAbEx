Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Termination - Selection of Petitioner as 'Commission Agent' in Railways - While accused of a criminal case, the petitioner was selected as the fittest person to act as Commission Agent.
It was found that the petitioner had a legitimate expectation to get the order of appointment in such a post following the withdrawal of his offer due to finding himself involved in criminal case.
The Railway authorities cannot terminate or withdraw his appointment simply because of this reason, and they have to respect the principle of presumed innocence until proven guilty by a competent court of law.
Because if the petitioner is acquitted from the accusation, he will lose such job for no fault of his.
[Para 30]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Termination - Petitioner selected commission Agent - While the petitioner was accused in an offence under section 498 A read with section 306, IPC, the Railway authorities tried to terminate his services by falsely accusing him of involvement in burning a lady, which was untrue.
However, the order of termination was set aside by the court.
