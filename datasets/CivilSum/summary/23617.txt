An autonomous body receiving grants from the government and having its own Memorandum of Association and rules for internal management will not be considered a State, as government control is only confined to the appropriate utilization of funds.
[Paras 1, 5, and 9]

Whether an autonomous body can be considered a State depends on whether the government operates behind a corporate veil while carrying out activities and functions of vital public importance.
