Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 33-C (2) - Ex parte order - A conductor was charged with not issuing tickets and was issued a chargesheet-cum-show cause notice.
Upon claiming wages under section 33-C(2) of the Act, the petition was disposed of ex parte by the labour court.
The management then filed an application to set aside the ex parte order, but it was refused due to territorial jurisdiction.
A writ petition was filed and the court held that the ex parte order should be set aside and the case should be transferred to a labour court with jurisdiction.
The labour court should have examined the petitioner or allowed them to file relevant documents instead of solely relying on the petition.
The court further stated that in the interest of justice, the ex parte order must be set aside and the management should be given another opportunity to file a counter.
[Paras 4 and 6]
