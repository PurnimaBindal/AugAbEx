Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medical reimbursement - Treatment from recognised hospital - Reimbursement of medical expenses - The approved limit for CABG treatment is Rs. 89,000/-, but a recognised hospital charged Rs. 2 lac and over and above approved rates - Petitioner still entitled to full reimbursement - Recovering excess amount from the hospital falls under the jurisdiction of the respondents - Defendants ordered to pay remaining amount with 6% interest within 2 months.
[Paras 23 and 24]
