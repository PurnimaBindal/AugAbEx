The Scheduled Castes and Schedules Tribes Orders (Amendment Act) 1976 and Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 state that the Civil Court cannot declare a caste to be a Scheduled Caste.
As such, an individual who is born a Christian cannot claim to be a Scheduled Caste solely because their father was born as one and had converted to Christianity before the individual's birth.
This is in accordance with the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 341 and 366 (24).
