Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 8 & 9 - Prevention of Corruption Act, Section 5 - Criminal Proceedings/Acquittal on benefit of doubt/Departmental proceedings - Even if an employee is acquitted from corruption charges in criminal proceedings due to benefit of doubt, departmental proceedings can still be initiated.
The standard of proof required in criminal proceedings is not the same as the one required in departmental proceedings.
