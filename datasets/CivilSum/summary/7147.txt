According to the Gujarat Public Services (Class I and Class II) Recruitment Rules, Rule 4, minimum qualifying marks for the Viva Voce test must be fixed for appointment.
The qualifying standard for Viva Voce is significantly lower than that for the written test.
The commission is authorized to fix minimum qualifying marks for both written and Viva Voce tests, and the selection process is not affected.
[Paras 10-13]
