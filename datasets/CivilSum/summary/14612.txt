Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education/Admission - Domicile certificates - Domicile certificates issued by the Sub- Divisional Magistrate (Civil) and Sub-Divisional Officer (Civil) after due verification of facts from the Naib Tehsildar are deemed valid - Authorities issuing the certificates were not informed of any doubts regarding the authenticity of the certificates, and certificates were neither cancelled nor challenged - Petitioners who completed half of the term of the course after obtaining provisional admission cannot be denied admission on the grounds of invalid domicile certificates when persons below in merit to them have already been admitted  - It is unfair to deprive them of the benefits of their studies.
