In accordance with UP Intermediate Education Regulations, Regulation 3, determining factors for inter se seniority are not the date of joining the post, but rather the date of approval of appointment by approving authority.
When dates of approval are the same, the person who is older in age will receive precedence in the matter of inter se seniority pursuant to Regulation 3(1)(bb).
[Paras 11 and 16]
The seniority list previously prepared for the purpose of rotational representation of teachers in Committee of Management does not meet the requirements of Regulation 3 for the purpose of promotion.
The petitioner called upon the Committee of Management to draw a fresh seniority list under Regulation 3(2) and cannot now claim that the earlier seniority list did not require any review under Regulation 3(2).
