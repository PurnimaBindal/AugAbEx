Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pay scale - Petitioners previously received a fixed salary of Rs. 2000/- for working on a World Bank project (TOPIC), which only lasted for two years - Afterwards, they were given the role of Junior Clerk even though their designation was Planning and Recovery Assistant - Although the petitioners argued for the pay scale of Rs. 2200-4000, they were not automatically entitled to this scale simply because they held equivalent qualifications and designations - Their job title alone could not be used for higher pay - The court dismissed the petition.
