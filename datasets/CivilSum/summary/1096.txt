Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 - Admission - MBBS course - Evaluation of answers - Petitioner appeared for EAMCET exam with a defective paper book - Extra time was not granted but Petitioner answered all questions - Petitioner wants re-examination of his paper, particularly the questions he got wrong - Court holds that fundamental rights of Petitioner under Articles 14 and 21 were not violated just because of a mistake - Compensating with extra marks may be appropriate in certain cases - Court cannot order a recheck of wrongly answered questions - Such evaluation method is not viable - The appeal is allowed.
