Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Selection - Appointment - Termination - Petitioner applied for selection as Junior Engineer in response to the advertisement but received direct appointment letter purported to based on recommendations of selecting agency - Appointment deemed invalid - Termination considered appropriate even after completion of probation period without inquiry - Additionally, it was ruled that hiding such occurrences would not be allowed - State Government was instructed to refer the matter to CBI.
