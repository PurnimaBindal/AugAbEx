Constitution of India, Article 226 - Review - Disciplinary proceedings against respondent quashed on appeal - FCI and State ordered to pay all benefits with interest - FCI files application alleging State responsible for payment - State's request for review denied as appeal was already disposed of - State solely responsible for paying interest - State cannot blame FCI for delay and must pay full interest owed.
