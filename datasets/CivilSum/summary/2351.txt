Central Reserve Police Force Act, Section 11 - Punishment - Dismissal from service - The claim that no major penalty could be imposed under section 11 of the Act, which deals with minor punishments, because the action in question was taken by the respondents - Dismissal is one of the punishments listed in section 11 - The title 'Minor Punishment' in section 11 is inaccurate - Typically, the High Court will not interfere with a punishment unless it is deemed to be unacceptable to the court's sense of morality.
