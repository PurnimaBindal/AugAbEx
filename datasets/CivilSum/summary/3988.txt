Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner, who was enrolled in the Army on 281944, was invalided out of service due to disease 'Trench Foot' after 9 years of service.
The Respondent-CCDA (P) exceeded its jurisdiction and authority by reducing the petitioner's disability, despite him undergoing one medical board and two re-survey medical boards - all of which defined his disability as over 20%.
The order denying the petitioner's disability pension has been quashed and respondents have been directed to reconsider and release said pension.
