Constitution of India, Articles 38 and 226 - Reliefs for families affected by terrorism - Dependents of terrorist victims entitled to subsistence allowance - Policy must provide both subsistence allowance and employment - Petitioner's deceased husband left two teenaged daughters with her - Daughters cannot be denied subsistence allowance because petitioner is employed - They are entitled to a joint subsistence allowance of Rs. 1000/- per month.
