Delhi University Act, 1922, Section 23 - Admission/Entrance Test - The decision to hold an admission test and eliminate the sports quota came from the Academic Council of the University based on a report by experts in legal education.
Neither the Committee nor any other authority was required to provide reasons or take notes for this decision.
