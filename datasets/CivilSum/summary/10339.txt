Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Appointment of respondent sponsored by Employment Exchange - The appellant terminated the services of the respondent without giving an opportunity of hearing - The Labour Court awarded reinstatement and continuity in service to the respondent - The award was challenged - The respondent was appointed on a permanent basis - Termination of services without assigning any reason is invalid - The appeal was dismissed.

Constitution of India Article 226 - Appeal - Additional plea - The only objection is that prior approval of the Registrar was not obtained for appointment and Registrar had not fixed the cadre strength - The special officer did not record any reasoning in the impugned order - The appointment was on a permanent basis - The appeal was dismissed.
