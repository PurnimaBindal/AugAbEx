Letters patent Rules, Clause X - New plea - Promotion - The appellant cannot raise a plea in LPA that was not raised before the Single Judge.
The petitioner argued that from 1964 to 1967, passing a test was not necessary for promotion, so they should have received notional promotion from that time.
This fact was disclosed by the Railways in an affidavit, but the petitioner did not have the opportunity to plead or counter it before the Single Judge.
The appropriate time to raise such facts is before the Single Judge, not in LPA for the first time.
Unless the Single Judge has explicitly considered the plea in their judgment.
