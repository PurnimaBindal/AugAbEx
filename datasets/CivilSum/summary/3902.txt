Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - The Industrial Tribunal can determine if a workman claiming regularization as Mason in proper pay-scale from the date of their initial appointment is entitled to regularisation, and can also examine if the workman was entitled to proper pay-scale.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 39(d) and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule III, Item I - The principle of equal pay for equal work applies to both daily rated workers and regular employees.
As the respondent-Workman has been working as a Mason on adhoc basis for 12 years and waiting for regularisation, and meanwhile performing the same duties as regular Mason, the Tribunal has directed to pay the respondent-Workman equal salary to the minimum salary paid to regular employees from the date of award.
