Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Death of workman - Denial of employment - The management presented evidence such as attendance register, ESI returns and production incharge testimony - Despite this, the Commissioner approved the claim based on the address of the deceased in the hospital as c/o the Company, admitting his presence in the factory and the address of the management given in the ration card - The wife of the deceased accepted that he was well known to the Director of the Company - However, there was no proof of wage payments - Therefore, under the circumstances, the Commissioner had erred in determining employee relationship - The factory's address in the hospital or ration card cannot prove the deceased's status as a workman - The appeal was granted.
