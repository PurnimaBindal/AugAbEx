The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 and the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.2(1) (as applicable in Haryana) pertain to the recruitment under sports quota for the position of Constable and the discharge of candidates on the basis of lower gradation certificates.
After considering the appeals of other constables in similar situations, it was held that the petitioner, who holds State level and Inter-University participation certificates, should be reinstated without being told to try an alternative remedy since the facts are clear.
Thus, the respondent is directed to reinstate the petitioner along with all consequential benefits provided to candidates who were reinstated.
