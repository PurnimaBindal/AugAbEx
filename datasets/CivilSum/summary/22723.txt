Constitution of India, Article 311 - Burden of proof - Misconduct - The burden of proof in disciplinary proceedings is determined by the nature of the charges and the explanation provided by the delinquent.
If the charge is that the delinquent made false notations while recommending a loan, the High Court was wrong to place the burden of proof on the employer instead of on the delinquent, according to the inquiry officer.
