According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 226, teachers employed in recognised private schools are entitled to receive pay at the same rate as teachers employed in government schools.
The respondent has been directed to adjust the scale of pay for petitioners to equal that of government-employed teachers.
[Para 15 and 18]
As stated in Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, claims for arrears of revised pay scale can only be made for a certain period of time.
The petitioner raised their demand for arrears on the eve of their resignation, making the claim for earlier years highly belated and barred by limitation.
However, the petitioner is entitled to arrears of the revised scale for the three years prior to the filing of the writ petition.
[Paras 16 and 17]
