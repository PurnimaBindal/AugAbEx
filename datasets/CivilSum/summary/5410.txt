As per Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 7.3 and 75 [applicable to Haryana], an employee who has been dismissed and subsequently reinstated is entitled to back wages.
In the case of the petitioner's acquittal in a criminal case, they were reinstated in service, but the intervening period was not treated as on duty and back wages were denied.
However, it was held in Para 8 that according to Rule 75, the petitioner is entitled to full salary and allowances for the period of suspension and dismissal.
