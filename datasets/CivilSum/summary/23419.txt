Sikkim Government Establishment Rules, 1974, Rule 4(4), Article 371-F, Articles 14, 16 and 309 of the Constitution of India - Appointments/Classification - Rule 4(4) gives preference to local candidates and provides them special protection - Non-local candidates' services can be terminated, as permitted under Article 371-F(K) - Rule found to be valid and not inconsistent with the Constitution of India.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15, 16 and 309 - Classification - Appointments/Discrimination - The Constitution prohibits discrimination between equals - However, classification based on an intelligible differentia is permissible for ensuring equality guaranteed by the Constitution - Such a classification groups together persons or things that share common characteristics.
