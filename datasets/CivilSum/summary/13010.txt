Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Voluntary Retirement Scheme (VRS) - Petitioners who opt for VRS are released from the respondent-Company's service - Those opting for VRS are paid their dues, excluding their training periods - Respondents argue that VRS of 1990 and 2001 are not the same - Held, petitioners are entitled to VRS of 1990 and not any other scheme.
