Constitution of India, Article 226 - Group Insurance Scheme - Petitioner's claim for the release of Rs. 1 lac on account of the death of her husband was rejected - The transferee office claimed that the scheme was not renewed for the benefit of the deceased member - The Drawing and Disbursing Officer failed to give a note in the deceased's last pay certificate that he was a member of the Insurance Scheme - The mistake was noticed by the department and they suggested that a sympathetic view be taken and grant of Rs. 1 lac be given to petitioner from Benevolent fund as a special case - It was held that the Master is responsible for the acts of his servant - The Life Insurance Company cannot be held liable, but the official respondents cannot escape their liability for negligence - Thus, they are directed to release the amount of Rs. 1 lac with interest at the rate of 9% per annum.
