According to State and Subordinate Service Rules (Kerala Rules), Rule 3(c) in the Constitution of India, if an advice for appointment is made under a mistake, as outlined in the case of the appointment of a Peon in the Co-operative Department, the Commission has the power to cancel the advice for appointment.
In this particular incident, the appellant's name was included only in the supplementary list and was therefore mistakenly advised for the position instead of 'S'.
However, the Commission realised their mistake and gave the appellant an opportunity before cancelling the advice for appointment.
