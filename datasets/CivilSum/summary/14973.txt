Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Family Pension Scheme, 1964 - Pension Regulation for Army, 1940 (Part II) Para 318 - Petitioner claims revised War Injury pension and family pension for husband invalidated from Army service on 2341946 and receiving war injury pension until his death on 251088 - Petitioner relies on Army Instruction 51 of 1980 and Family Pension Scheme, 1964 - Adjudged that husband was not entitled to pension as he served for less than 10 years, not drawing any pension at time of death, with no family pension admissible - Jangi Inaam benefit applicable to one life only - Instructions of 51 of 1980 apply to armed forces personnel who were in service on 111964 or later, like the 1964 scheme.
