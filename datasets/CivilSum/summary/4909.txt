According to the Andaman and Nicobar Islands (Panchayats) Regulations, 1994, Regulation 17 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j) and 17-B, the Gram Panchayat falls under the definition of an industry in Section 2(j) of the ID Act and the Pradhan is considered as the employer of all lawful employees of the Panchayat under Section 2(j) of the ID Act and Regulation 17 of the Regulations.
