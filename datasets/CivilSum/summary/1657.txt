Labor Court has the authority to proceed ex parte and to set aside ex parte proceedings if sufficient cause is demonstrated under Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rules 22 and 24(b), Industrial Disputes (Punjab) Rules, 1958, Rules 22 and 24, and Civil Procedure Code Order 9 Rule 13.
Labor Court also has the power to consider an application for setting aside an ex parte decision on sufficient grounds 30 days after the award's publication.
As a result, if sufficient cause is demonstrated justifying the non-appearance, the High Court may refer the case to the Industrial Tribunal for fresh adjudication.
