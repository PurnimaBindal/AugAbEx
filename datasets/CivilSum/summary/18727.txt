According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rule 5.32-A(c), if a notice for voluntary retirement is given after evading the service of notice of compulsory retirement issued on valid grounds, the employee cannot offer for voluntary retirement to forestall the action.
The employee cannot be deemed to have voluntarily retired on expiry of statutory period of notice if he does not reply or decline.
