Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Sections 11, 12 - Dismissal - The authorities mentioned in Section 11 have the power to dismiss or suspend a member of the Force who is found guilty, and punishment in (a) to (e) may be in addition to or in lieu thereof, even if the delinquent is not prosecuted for more heinous or less heinous crimes under Section 9 or Section 10.
Neither Section 11 nor Section 12 implies that a person may not be dismissed if he is not convicted or sentenced under the Act.
[Para 24] B Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Sections 9, 10, 11 - Punishment - There is a significant difference in the procedure for awarding punishment for offences under Sections 9 and 10 and for awarding punishment for misconduct under Section 11.
There is also a distinction between the procedure for punishment of dismissal provided under Section 12 and the punishment of dismissal and other punishment provided under Section 11 of the Act.
