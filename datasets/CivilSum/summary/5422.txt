Constitution of India, Article 226 - Canara Bank Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations, Regulation 6 - Canara Bank Officers Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 17 - When the Appellate Authority in appeal filed by a delinquent officer against order of compulsory retirement imposed by Disciplinary Authority reduces the penalty, it mustn't issue directions that interregnum period between date of compulsory retirement and date of reinstatement shall not be reckoned for any purpose, as Appellate Authority is not authorised under Regulations to issue such directions after reducing penalty imposed by Disciplinary Authority.
As a result, order made by Appellate Authority regarding treating interregnum period from date of compulsory retirement from service till date of reinstatement as period not spent on duty was set aside.
[Paras 15 and 40]
