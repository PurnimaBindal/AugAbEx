The petitioner was appointed as Programmer on an ad hoc basis and was posted to the position of System Analyst-cum-Programmer.
The post of System Analyst-cum-Programmer was filled through regular appointment while the post of Programmer was still vacant.
The petitioner's services could not be terminated until the post he was appointed to was no longer vacant.
