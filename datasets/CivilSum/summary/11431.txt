Civil Procedure Code, Order 21 Rule 32 - Execution - Successive execution - The executing court cannot sustain an order to implement a decree by sealing bank accounts of the authority when the second execution application is vague, lacking material particulars, and fails to indicate which part of the decree was left unpaid and the amount remaining for arrears of salary.
Additionally, the JD's plea that the earlier execution application satisfied the decree raises further complications.
