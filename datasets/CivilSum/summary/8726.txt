The Presiding Officer of the Central Government Labour Court granted the workmen's claim under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 for the difference in wages between their acquisition of temporary status and their appointment on regular pay scales.
However, since the claims were disputed and not settled by adjudication or recognized by the employers, it was not possible to compute the benefit under Section 33-C(2).
As a result, the application under Section 33-C(2) was not maintainable and the awards made by the court were set aside.
The decretal amount, which the workmen had already received several years ago, was very meagre and would not be recovered from them.
Thus, the writ petition was allowed.
