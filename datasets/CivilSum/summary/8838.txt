Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Section 5(2) of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 2006 state that a person who has been convicted of a criminal charge can be dismissed from their position as a public servant, regardless of the appeal or suspension of sentence.
The conviction remains effective until it is set aside by the appellate court or the court of revision.
Thus, the dismissal of the petitioner from service due to his conviction stands valid.
[Para 8] B Proviso (a) to Article 311(2) refers to a conviction resulting from a criminal charge based on the conduct of the person convicted.
A conviction recorded by a criminal court can be taken as the basis for punishment without any further inquiry, regardless of whether any enquiry was conducted by the respondent authority.
The conviction remains effective until it is set aside, and during the pendency of an appeal against the conviction, the conviction is still alive.
The conviction can be made the basis for dismissal or removal of a public servant from service.
