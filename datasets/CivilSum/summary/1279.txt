General Rules of Recruitment and Promotion of Academic and Non-Academic Staff of NIOS, 2002 - Promotion - Reversion - Petitioner was not eligible for promotion due to a lack of requisite length of service, which did not account for his deputation as an Accounts Officer.
However, after being permanently absorbed, he was entitled to include his service as a deputationist for the purpose of determining his length of service.
The decision of the Supreme Court in the case of K Madhavan supports this.
