Punjab School Education Board (Provident Fund) Regulations, 1970 - Punjab School Education Board (Employees Pension, Provident Fund and Gratuity) Regulations, 1991, Regulations 5.1, 6 - Retirees from Board - Petitioners who were directly recruited as Assistants with a required seven years of experience as Clerk in Government department will be entitled to the benefit of past service to be included in qualifying service for the purpose of pension since their experience on the post of clerk was an integral part of the essential qualification.
