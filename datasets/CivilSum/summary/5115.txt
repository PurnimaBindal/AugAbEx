The Labour Court ordered reinstatement with continuity and backwages for a claim of difference of wages under settlement, but the Petitioners argue that the respondent was not named nor had they submitted a declaration at the time of the settlement.
However, since they never disputed or denied the respondent's right before the Labour Court and even admit to deleting their signature on the declaration, it is too late for the Petitioners to dispute the respondent's right.
[Paras 4 and 5]

The issue of backwages calculation is not a question of law but of fact, and since the Petitioners did not raise any dispute regarding the calculation earlier, they cannot be permitted to raise the issue for the first time in the High Court.
[Paras 11]

The claim is in relation to difference in wages, and the respondent quantified the amount due by filing an affidavit.
It is not necessary for the pleading to include evidence in support of the contention, and the proof is to be produced in course of evidence.
Thus, the quantification cannot be said to be against the law in any manner.
[Paras 8 and 9]
