Punishment - Quantum of punishment - The power to impose punishment rests with the punishing authority, and the court should not usually interfere with the punishment imposed.
[Para 17] B.
Constitution of India, Art. 14 - Double jeopardy - Punishment - Recovery - The imposition of a penalty to make up for the loss caused by the appellant's negligence cannot be considered a punishment, and therefore there is no issue of double jeopardy.
