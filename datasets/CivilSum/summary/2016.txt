Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Administration of Justice - Conditions of service of Judicial officers and Judges - The administration is responsible for providing Court rooms, Bar Room, Library, Canteen, parking, and chambers for lawyers, an Administrative block in Court complexes, and all facilities for litigants - It is crucial that adequate funds are allocated and utilized for the judiciary and not allowed to lapse - Despite previous directions, construction work at various court complexes has not commenced - A new directive has been issued to start work within two months and complete it within two years, with sufficient funds allocated for this purpose.
