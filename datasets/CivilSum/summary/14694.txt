"Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Section 3 (as applicable in Haryana) - Premature retirement/Compulsory retirement/Adverse reports/Latest confidential reports - The order of premature retirement passed against the petitioner on the basis of a struck down instruction to have 70% good reports, without considering overall service record, has been deemed arbitrary - An employee who may have had average reports in their initial stage of service, but has improved and earned good or above reports in the recent past, should not be prematurely retired and cannot be seen as a poor performer - The action has been quashed.
