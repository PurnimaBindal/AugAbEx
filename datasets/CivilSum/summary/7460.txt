Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Grant of bonus marks - The circular does not cover Inter College tournaments as State level but in accordance with Umesh Jangid's case, bonus marks shall be given for District/State/National levels - Inter College tournaments cannot be treated as District level - The petitioner is entitled to bonus marks and appointment if he comes in merit.
