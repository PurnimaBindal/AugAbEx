Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab State Cooperative Agricultural Service Societies Rules, Rule 14 - Termination - Respondent No. 3 not afforded participation in inquiry and denied the opportunity to cross-examine - No provision made for respondent No. 3 to review enquiry report - No show cause notice issued before impugned termination order - Discrepancies found between charges outlined in memorandum of charges compared to reasoning provided in enquiry report - Failure to adhere to proper enquiry procedure - Therefore, petitioner's termination order deemed illegal - Decision of appellate authority to reinstate respondent No. 3 confirmed and upheld by revisional authority.
