Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 2(f) - Employees - Apprentices - Employers have the burden to prove that employees listed in the wage register are in fact only apprentices and not eligible for provident fund benefits - If an employer fails to make an effort to prove this, then those employees will be treated as employees eligible for provident fund benefits.
[Paras 9 and 11]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7(a) - When employers are given notice to determine eligibility and contribution of their employees, they have ample opportunity to provide relevant evidence and records to show that their employees should not be covered under the Act - If they do not take advantage of this opportunity and choose to challenge the notice in a writ petition, their petition will be dismissed as not maintainable.
