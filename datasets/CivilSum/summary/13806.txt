Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer policy - Cancellation of transfer - Employees do not have an inherent right to choose their place of posting - The transfer policy is merely a guideline that is not mandatory and does not create any legal right - Transfer of an employee is not only an incident but a condition of service - The Court cannot typically interfere with a transfer order unless it is shown to be tainted by mala fide or in violation of a statutory provision or issued by an incompetent authority.
