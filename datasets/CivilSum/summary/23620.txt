The Constitution of India, Articles 23, 32, 39(c), 41 and 42 along with the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976 and the Inter-state Migrant Workmen (Regulation of Employment & Conditions of Service) Act deal with the issue of Bonded Labour.
The stone quarry bonded labourers work under inhumane conditions, facing a lack of drinking water, school and medical facilities, which is the responsibility of the State Government to maintain or enforce.
Untrained workers are forced to carry out blasting operations with explosives.
The Haryana Government must enforce labour laws to improve their working conditions, providing necessary facilities such as drinking water, healthcare and schooling, as the workers are not in a negotiating position and not sufficiently organised to force their demands.
