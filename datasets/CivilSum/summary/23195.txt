Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25F(a), (a) & (c) - Retrenchment compensation - The offer of retrenchment compensation must be given simultaneously with the process of retrenchment to the concerned workmen or it will vitiate the retrenchment.
However, the non-receipt of compensation by the workers will not vitiate the offer of compensation.
Advance notice and information regarding the amount of retrenchment compensation to the cashier is enough to comply with the offer of compensation.
[Paras 9, 10, 13, 15] B.
Retrenchment compensation - The purpose of retrenchment compensation is not only to reward the previous service of the employee but also to provide sustenance for the period during which the worker is searching for employment.
[Para 9] C Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25F(a), (a) & (c) - Retrenchment compensation - The fulfillment of clauses (a) and (b) of Sec. 25F is mandatory, but for the clause (c), it is only directory.
