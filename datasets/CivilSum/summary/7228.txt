The Labour Court directed the management to pay compensation under Section 25-N of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, after determining that they fell under the definition of an Industrial Establishment carrying out a manufacturing process as per Section 2(k) of the Factories Act.
The management argued that there was no direct evidence of a manufacturing process, but the Court found that the evidence, including a statement from a Senior Clerk regarding the establishment's strength and repair work, supported their conclusion.
Therefore, the Court did not see any reason to interfere with its findings.
