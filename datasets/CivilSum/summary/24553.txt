Section 7(A) of the Employees Provident Fund Act and the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 deals with determination of money due from an employer.
In a case where the Commissioner of Provident Fund called upon the Corporation to deposit the contribution payable under the Act, the Corporation had appointed contractors for execution of works.
The Corporation objected the Commissioner's order as the contractors possessed the relevant employee lists and were not notified or made parties in the proceedings.
However, the High Court dismissed the writ against the Commissioner's order.
Later, it was held that it is the legal duty of the Commissioner to exercise all their powers to gather evidence before coming to a conclusion and failure to do so is a jurisdictional error.
Hence, the order of the Commissioner and that of the High Court was reversed and the appeal was allowed.
