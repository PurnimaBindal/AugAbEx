The Legal Service Rules of 1957, Rules 4(d) and 6-A, pertain to the appointment of an Additional Secretary in the Ministry of Law.
A legal services member is the respondent who is unsatisfied with not being considered for the post of Additional Secretary.
The petitioner's argument is that as a deputationist, she has no right to be considered for such a post.
However, Rule 6-A permits appointment to a duty post through deputation and Rule 4(d) defines a member of legal service as including a person appointed to a duty post.
As such, there can be no interference.
