Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1953, Rule 17(b) - Disciplinary proceedings - Delay - A first charge memo against a Prison Warder regarding ill-treatment of a prisoner was canceled, resulting in the petitioner receiving three promotions.
Nonetheless, a second charge memo reiterating the same charge was issued after 12 years due to a change in government, and it was held unsustainable on the grounds of an unexplained and inordinate delay.
