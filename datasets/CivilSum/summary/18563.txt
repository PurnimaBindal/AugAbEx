Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab State Instructions dt. 22.12.1994 and dt. 29.12.1994 - Promotion - Failure to pass the type test resulted in petitioner's promotion not being considered - Employees must pass the type test within a year of being promoted as per the instructions - Petitioners, who were junior to those promoted, have the right to be considered for promotion.
