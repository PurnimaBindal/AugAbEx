It was declared that Secretaries of different Gram Panchayats constituted under the Act, 1973 are not holders of civil posts under the Government of West Bengal and are not entitled to the benefits, privileges, emoluments, pay etc. of government servants.
The Secretary of Gram Panchayat is not considered a government servant nor do they hold a civil post.
As such, there is no relationship of master and servant between the State and the Secretary.
The petition was subsequently dismissed.
