The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 309, along with Section 14(1) of the Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, pertain to regularisation.
The petitioner, who was initially appointed in an ad hoc capacity, did not follow the recruitment rules outlined in the proviso appended to Article 309 of the Constitution.
As a result, they have no enforceable rights arising from this illegal appointment.
The recruitment rules stipulate equal consideration for all eligible candidates, and an individual who is employed via a backdoor method cannot claim permanence merely because they have been employed for a period of time.
Petitioner has no legal right to claim regularisation of their service.
