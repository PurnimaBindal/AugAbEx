According to Section 2(22)(c) of the Employees State Insurance Act (XXXIV of 1948), the payment of 'travelling batta' to drivers and conductors should not be considered as 'wages.'
Any amount disbursed to employees to cover additional expenses of work cannot be a component of 'wages.'
The 'travelling batta' is similar to other allowances such as 'washing allowance', 'meals allowance', and 'travelling allowance.'
