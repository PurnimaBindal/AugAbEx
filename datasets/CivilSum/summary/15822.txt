Section 4-A of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Section 95 of the Motor Vehicles Act, stipulate that the insurer is directed to pay 6% interest, and the penalty of 25% pertains only to amounts over Rs. 83,51625.
The Motor Vehicles Act, Section 95 clearly states that the company shall not be liable to cover any liability except death or bodily injury arising under the Workmen's Compensation Act.
The penalty is not imposed for bodily injury but for the default committed by the employer.
As per Section 4A, the amount of penalty imposed is on account of the personal fault or default of the insured employer to pay the compensation within the permissible time.
Thus, the insurer is liable only to pay interest and not a penalty.
