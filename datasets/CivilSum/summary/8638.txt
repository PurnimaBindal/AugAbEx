For Rajasthan: Mr. MR Singhvi, Advocate.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Unsanctioned post - Ad hoc appointment - No provision for ad hoc appointment to the post of Professor and Associate Professors under the University Rules - Appointment can be made on an ad hoc basis for a fixed term against an unsanctioned post – No right to claim continuation till the post is sanctioned and regular appointments are made - Termination upheld.
