According to the Apprentices Act, 1961, Sections 2(aa) and 20(2), and the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2), an apprentice who is absorbed into the service is still considered an apprentice and is not entitled to all the benefits of the ID Act.
Their remedy for any disputes with the employer is only under the Apprentices Act, since a particular remedy provided in a specific legislation must be resorted to.
