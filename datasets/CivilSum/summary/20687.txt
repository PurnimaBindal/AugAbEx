The Appellant had claimed Seniority in the Gradation list, but his past services were not taken into account and he was placed Junior in Seniority List.
The court held that his services as a Senior Auditor in Revenue Department should be taken into account for determining his seniority.
The Labor Department is directed to determine the seniority of the Appellant in Cadre by considering his service in Revenue Department.
After considering him for promotion to any post, it was found that he is suitable for national promotion.
His retiral benefits must be re-calculated on that basis.
The appeal is allowed.
