The litigant is expected to approach the writ Court in a timely fashion, within six months or one year of the cause of action, under Articles 14 and 226 of the Indian Constitution.
However, the petitioner in this case boarded out of the Army in 1944 and first made a representation and served a legal notice in 1992, before finally filing a writ petition in 2005.
Due to the significant delay and laches, the writ petition is likely to be dismissed.
