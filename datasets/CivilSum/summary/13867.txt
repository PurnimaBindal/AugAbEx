The Constitution of India in Articles 161(4) and 239 discusses Reservation and its Extension to Other States.
A question arises whether someone who holds an SC/ST certificate from a State can receive notification from Union Territory of Delhi on 2182003.
The validity of these notifications is also in question.
The verdict cited in (2005)3 SCC 1 addresses this issue and concludes that the notifications dated 2182003 and 2782003 are valid.
