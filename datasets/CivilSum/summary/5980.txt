In accordance with the Civil Procedure Code, 1909, Order 5 – Rule 20 (As before amendment), the court must record in writing its personal satisfaction regarding the defendant's evasion of service before allowing substituted service/service through notice in newspaper.
After that, it is crucial for the plaintiff to provide evidence that the notice was published in a newspaper circulating normally in the relevant locality where the defendant is believed to be.
Since neither the court found the defendant had evaded service nor the circulation of the newspaper in the normal way was proved, a decree rendered against the plaintiff is null and void.
Regardless, the defendant has the right to pursue the suit against the plaintiff from the point at which the proceeding was initiated ex parte in the lawsuit.
(1964 Madras 415, 1935 Lahore 129, 1970 RCR 722) [Paras 5 and 6].
