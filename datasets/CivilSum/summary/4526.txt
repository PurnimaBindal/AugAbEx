The Haryana School Education Act of 1995 and the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 511 and 616, along with the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act of 1995, Sections 2(t), 47 are relevant to the case of a permanently incapacitated teacher being denied a pension.
The teacher's service after the government took over the school is the only period counted, and since no order of superannuation was passed, the petitioner cannot be considered retired from service until the date of superannuation.
As per Section 47 of the 1995 Act, the petitioner is entitled to a salary with 8% interest from the date of relieval until the date of retirement.
The petitioner shall be paid salary until his retirement in June 2007, and respondents shall pass an order of retirement/pension as per the law upon the petitioner attaining the age of superannuation.
