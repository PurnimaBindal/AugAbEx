"Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay fixation - Pay scale - The State Government instructed that the petitioner should be treated on the college side for the purpose of pay fixation while working as Assistant Director (Examination) Public Instructions, Punjab.
This entitled him to the scale admissible to those on the college side.
The order that fixed his pay treating him as on the school side has been nullified.
[Para 3]"
