Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rule 1221 - Limitation Act, Article 113 Termination of Service/Void order/Declaratory suit - Article 113 of the Limitation Act is a catch-all provision that applies in situations not covered by specific provisions of the Limitation Act - Individuals who wish to challenge a termination of services order on the grounds of illegality must do so within three years of the order - Any suit filed beyond this three-year period will be subject to the defense of being barred by limitation.
