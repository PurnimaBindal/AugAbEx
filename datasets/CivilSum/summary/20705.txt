Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - The dependent of a deceased individual may be considered for appointment on compassionate grounds, taking into account special circumstances related to the father's death - Even if the applicant's brother is employed, if he is not supporting the family and residing separately, the applicant may be eligible for compassionate appointment - In this case, the applicant has already been employed for more than five years during the appeal process, and it would not be appropriate to uproot him from his position.
