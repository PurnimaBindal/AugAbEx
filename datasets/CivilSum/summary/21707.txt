Constitution of India, Article 16 mandates that the first vacancy be reserved for Scheduled Tribes candidates.
In the event that no such candidate is available, the vacancy may be allocated to the candidate at the top of the merit list, as per government instructions.
This procedure is considered valid.
A claim by a Scheduled Caste candidate that they should be granted the vacancy in the absence of a Scheduled Tribes candidate was rejected.
[Paras 7 and 8]
