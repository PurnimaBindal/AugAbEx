Constitution of India, Article 14 - Parity in pay scale - Petitioner employed as a Projectionist in Respondent Council under the Ministry of Industry - Petitioner seeks pay scale parity with Projectionists employed in Doordarshan under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting - The scale is denied based on the premise that the work of Projectionist in Doordarshan requires more skill and responsibility - The Court ruled that the Petitioner cannot be equated with the Projectionist of another department where work needs expertise, specialization, and more care - Furthermore, the Petitioner did not demonstrate that he performed identically qualitative and quantitative duties.
[Paras 15 and 17]
