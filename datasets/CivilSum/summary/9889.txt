Constitution of India, Article 14 - Demobilised Service Rules - Rules 5(1) and 6 - Two-fold contentions of writ petitioners/respondents - Firstly, the Punjab Government took a policy decision to grant military service benefits to selected candidates against general and open vacancies despite their Army service - Secondly, similarly situated doctors granted short service commission were given the benefit of military service under the state government's post-emergency policy decision - State offered no satisfactory explanation proving discrimination and violation of Article 14 - Single Judge allowed military service benefits rendered by writ petitioners/respondents towards pay/seniority under rules 5(1) and 6 - Appeal dismissed.
