Assistants Grade I in Food Corporation of India transferred from Ministerial Cadre to the Accounts Cadre on permanent basis have the right to receive transfer with retrospective effect if their work and conduct during their stay in Accounts Cadre had been found to be satisfactory.
The petitioners' transfer was cancelled with retrospective effect on the grounds that he was not qualified for appointment in Accounts Cadre, which invalidated the impugned order.
[Para 6]
Formation of Accounts Cadre without natural justice a totally arbitrary classification between petitioner and others who were absorbed in the Accounts Cadre on the basis of their qualification.
The Deputy Manager (Personnel) had recommended the petitioner's case, which had been duly accepted.
Acceptance of the respondent's claim would deny the opportunity to the petitioner and would introduce a totally arbitrary classification.
[Para 9]
The petitioner has the right to be included in the Accounts Cadre in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Natural justice - Formation of Accounts Cadre.
The name of petitioner was cancelled with retrospective effect and deleted from zonal seniority list without giving any opportunity to the petitioner to express his opinion.
The impugned order was quashed because it cancelled the order of permanent absorption from the Accounts Cadre without providing the petitioner a chance to make a representation.
