Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Petitioners claimed to be considered for promotion above their juniors - Decree was passed stating that if qualifications were met, petitioners were to be promoted from the date their juniors were promoted - Execution application dismissal by Trial Court was incorrect - Respondents ordered to consider the case of petitioners from date of their juniors' promotion, with the condition that petitioners must meet the required qualifications.
