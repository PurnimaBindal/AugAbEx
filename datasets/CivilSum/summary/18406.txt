Punjab National Bank (Employees Pension Regulations) Regulations 21 and 56 - Qualifying service for pension - An employee's suspension period will only count as qualifying service if he/she was fully exonerated of the charge and the suspension was held to be wholly unjustified.
Resort to Regulation 56 and CCS Pension Rules cannot be taken if there is no doubt that the employee was not exonerated.
The petitioner is not entitled to count the period of suspension towards qualifying service.
