Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 13.4(2) and (3), 131 and 1318 - Officiating promotion- Promotion as Head Constables - Reversion - Constables of Haryana Commando Force promoted as Head Constables of Commando Force under Rule 13.4(2) and (3) without following Rule 131 and not made by the competent authority cannot be considered promotions under Rule 131.
Officiation period cannot be treated under Rule 1318 to treat them as regularly appointed Head Constables.
They cannot benefit from Rishal Singh v. State of Haryana, JT 1994(2) SC 157 judgment as such promotions are fortuitous and do not confer any right.
