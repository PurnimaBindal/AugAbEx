Railway Service Conduct Rules, 1966, Rule 18 sub-rule 3 - Misconduct - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner cannot be held guilty of any misconduct for failing to declare assets bought by his adult son with money from his grandfather.
The rule does not make it mandatory for the petitioner to declare such purchases.
Orders imposing penalty of major punishment by way of compulsory retirement from service against petitioner have been set aside and the petitioner is entitled to all benefits of his services.
