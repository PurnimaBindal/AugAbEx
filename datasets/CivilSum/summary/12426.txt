Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39 - Equal Pay for Equal work - Even though Artists in Agriculture and Artists in Public Relations Department of the State are entitled to equal pay for equal work principle, it cannot be considered in this case as their duties are different and qualification criteria are dissimilar - Thus, Artists of Agriculture Dept.
cannot demand the same pay scale as the Artists of Public Relations Department.
