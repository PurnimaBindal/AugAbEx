Under Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Voluntary Resignation - A labourer was suspended after being caught in a case of theft of the company's property.
He admitted his guilt in writing and voluntarily resigned, requesting that no criminal case be pursued against him.
No police action was taken against him, but he later claimed that his admission was made under threat and not voluntarily.
He raised an industrial dispute to report him back to duty, but the Labour Court rejected the reference.
The court found no infirmity in the Labour Court's findings and therefore dismissed the appeal.
