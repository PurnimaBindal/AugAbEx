Army Act, Section 39(a) - Army Rules, Rules 95, 129 - Absence without leave - Dismissal - The petitioner challenges that they were not given adequate opportunity to defend themselves, witnesses were examined covertly, and that they were not provided with the necessary documents - However, the record shows that no application for documents was filed after recording of evidence - No explanation was given for the admitted absence - The court concludes that no rules or principles of natural justice were breached - The petitioner's unauthorized absence was a repeated act of indiscipline within a year - Maintaining discipline is crucial for armed forces - The petitioner did not fulfill their onus in the case of the admitted unauthorized absence, and therefore the court cannot accept their contentions.
