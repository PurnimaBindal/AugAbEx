Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Revaluation - The petitioner failed two subjects in the second semester examination and requested a re-evaluation of their answer sheets.
An interim order from the court permitted the petitioner to take part in the third semester examination.
Following re-evaluation, the petitioner passed in one subject and was deemed eligible to appear in the 3rd semester.
As a result, the university was ordered to declare the results, and the petitioner's admission into the 3rd semester was affirmed.
The writ was accepted.
