Constitution of India 1950, Articles 31A and 300A - Pension Regulation for Army, 1961 Regulation 173 and Appendix II, Rules 2, 3, 4, and 7 - Pension - Disability Pension - Claim - Military Service must be the contributing factor for disability - Disability must be attributable to Military Service - Claimants entitled to reasonable doubt - If the disease is not noted at recruitment, it is deemed to have arisen due to Military Service - In this particular case, the disease was noticed while the petitioner was in service, thus entitled to disability pension.
