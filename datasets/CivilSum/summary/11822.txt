Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311(1) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934 - Show cause notice - The Superintendent of Police (SP) was well within their rights to revise their opinion on the nature of punishment to be awarded to the petitioner after the show cause notice proposing punishment for stoppage of increments was issued but before any final decision was taken.
This was not an act without jurisdiction, as the final decision had not yet been made.
Therefore, the court should not interfere before a decision is made by the competent authority.
[Paras 7 and 8]  B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934 - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311(1) - Natural Justice - Show cause notice - The SP, Jind had the jurisdiction to issue a show cause notice proposing dismissal from service to the petitioner, as they are a coordinate authority in rank to the SP, Ambala.
The court dismissed the petition, but directed the competent authority to give the petitioner a fresh notice and opportunity to be heard if they make a request.
