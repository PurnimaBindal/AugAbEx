Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Delay and laches - Limitation - Although there is no prescribed limitation under the Industrial Disputes Act, it does not mean that an outdated claim must be accepted.
The procedural laws like estoppel, waiver and acquiescence and doctrine of Acceptance Sub silentio are applicable to Industrial Disputes Act proceedings.
[Paras 10, 13 to 15] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Res judicata - Withdrawing a writ petition to exercise other alternate remedy will not create a fresh cause of action.
All objections on which the writ petition could have been dismissed will remain open, technical and merit-based.
[Paras 9 and 14] C. Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 215 - Judgments and precedents - A decision stands as an authority of what it decides and not what can logically be deducted therefrom.
