The challenge of the reinstatement of the respondent is made under Sections 11-A and 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
In the Labour Court award dated 1682000, it was found that the respondent was assigned the work of Typist for a limited period after completing her apprenticeship and was later assigned census work with a clear term of abolition of the post after the census work was over, which she admitted and knew.
However, upon termination, the respondent received an award of reinstatement for having worked for 240 days.
This award of the Labour Court is quashed.
