Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part I, Para 7(b) of the Appendix - Defence Medical Regulations, Para 423 - Disability Pension - In case a casual connection can be established between disablement or death and military service, eligibility for disability pension arises - If there was no possibility of such disease at the time of induction into military service nor any findings have been recorded by the Medical Board to the effect that it was not possible to detect such disease at the time of enrollment, a disease that leads to the discharge or death of an individual will be deemed to have ordinarily arisen during service.
Hence, the individual will be entitled to disability pension and in case of death, his family for family pension accordingly.
