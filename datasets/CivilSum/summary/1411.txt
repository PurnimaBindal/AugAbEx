Article 226 of the Indian Constitution covers the appointment and selection process, including the minimum cut-off marks for financial analyst positions.
While eight posts were reserved for Scheduled Caste candidates, the petitioner only scored 101 out of 300 and did not meet the minimum cut-off mark of 125 for SC/ST candidates set by the Board Chair.
The notification did not specify any minimum cut-off marks, and there is no evidence the Selection Board Chair had the authority to set them.
Thus, selection based on such cut-off marks is invalid.
[Para 5] B.
The viva voce exam is worth 100 marks out of the total 300, which was deemed excessive in terms of reservation.
Therefore, selection based on this criterion is not valid, and even if a candidate did not raise this issue in their affidavit, they can still do so during the hearing.
