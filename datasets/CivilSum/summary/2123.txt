The cross-objections filed for the aggrieved part of an order in an appeal under the Workmen's Compensation Act cannot be entertained or adjudicated upon by the High Court since the Enactment does not provide for it.
A CMA under Section 30 should have been filed, and the provisions of Civil Procedure Code only apply as in Rule 41 of the Rules of 1924.
