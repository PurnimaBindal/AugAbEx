According to Sections 2(22) and 75 of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, wages that are classified as production incentive must be paid as additional remuneration to employees at intervals not exceeding two months.
If incentive is paid quarterly beyond two months intervals, it is not covered under the first part or last part of Section 2(22) of ESI Act.
The court cannot add the word `payable' or any other similar expression to the word `paid' used in Section 2(22) and it cannot be considered as part of `wages' under the ESI Act.
