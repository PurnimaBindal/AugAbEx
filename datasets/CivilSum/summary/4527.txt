Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal proceedings - Petitioner charged with receiving illegal gratification - Prayer for stay of departmental proceedings pending criminal trial - No prejudice to petitioner if departmental proceedings continue - Standard of proof in both proceedings entirely different - Departmental proceedings cannot wait endlessly till conclusion of criminal trial - Charges serious - Stay of proceedings would only serve the interest of accused - Departmental proceedings cannot be stayed.
