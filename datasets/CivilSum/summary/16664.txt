Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules (1958), Rule 17 - Disciplinary proceedings - The Excise Department's practice of setting quotas for registering criminal cases is deemed unreasonable - This is because officers may falsely implicate innocent people in order to avoid punishment - The punishment of stoppage of increment without cumulative effect for failing to meet quota is considered void.
