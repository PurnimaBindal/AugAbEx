According to Section 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Retrenchment means the termination of employment, but in this case, there is no order of termination or preventative actions on the part of the employer.
There are also no facts to support the claim that the workman sought to report on duty and was prevented by the employer from doing so.
Therefore, the act of terminating the services of the workman cannot be substantiated, and the case is ultimately one of abandonment of services.
As such, the workman cannot be considered as retrenched.
