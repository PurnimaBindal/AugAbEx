Enrolment Rules of Bar Council of Tamil Nadu, Rule 8(a) (as inserted in 2006) - Enrolment of an Advocate - Age - The age factor has no relevance in maintaining standards of the profession, and hence, Rule 8(a) is beyond the rule-making power of the State Bar Council and held to be arbitrary, void and unconstitutional.
[Paras 13 and 14] B. Enrolment Rules of Bar Council of Tamil Nadu, Rule 8(A) (as inserted in 2006) - Enrolment of an Advocate - Transfer of enrolment to another State Bar council - Bar to transfer - Age bar to transfer at 45 years age to maintain standards of the profession - The explanation added to Rule 8(A) inserted to Tamil Nadu Bar Council Enrolment Rules in 2006 providing bar to transfer, held to be void and unconstitutional.
[Paras 15, 16 and 17] C. Enrolment Rules of Bar Council of Tamil Nadu, Rule 9A - Enrolment as an Advocate - Age bar at 45 years age to maintain standards of the profession - The age factor, in the absence of any concrete material with statistics, has no relevance in maintaining standards of profession - Legal profession is distinct in its nature compared to other professions adversely affected by such age factor - Rule 9A of Tamil Nadu Bar Council held to be void and unconstitutional.
[Paras 13 and 14 and 16] D All India Bar Council Rules, Rule 9 - Enrolment as an Advocate - Age Bar at 45 years age to maintain standard of the profession - The Bar Council of India has no power to impose unreasonable restrictions - Any Rule made imposing such unreasonable and arbitrary conditions can be struck down by the Court in exercise of its jurisdiction under Article 226, 32 and 141 of the Constitution of India.
