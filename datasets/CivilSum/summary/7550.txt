Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 & 2(k) - Industrial dispute - Resignation - Withdrawal of resignation before acceptance - An employee asked for permission to withdraw their resignation submitted under depression - The employer refused permission, alleging that it was already accepted - Whether the resignation was accepted before the request for withdrawal or not is a matter of investigation that requires evidence - The refusal to refer the dispute without citing any reasons is unacceptable.
