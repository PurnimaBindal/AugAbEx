The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 address Promotion and Seniority, with court-issued directions dictating that the case of the private respondent for promotion must be considered alongside all eligible candidates in accordance with seniority-cum-merit.
The Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution are violated when eligible candidates are not considered together.
[Paras 10 to 13]  B Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, Delay and Laches must be taken into account when considering promotion and a Person who is ignored while considering his juniors for promotion may file a writ petition for every promotion of a junior that violates seniority rule.
Refusal to dismiss a Writ Petition due to delay or laches is possible if it follows the promotion of the last person and is filed immediately.
