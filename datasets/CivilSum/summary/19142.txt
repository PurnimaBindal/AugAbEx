According to Punjab Services Medical Attendance Rules, 1940, Rule 2(3) for out-door patients, the employer is solely responsible for the employee's good health, physical and mental condition to ensure that they are able to perform their duties.
If an employee is not reimbursed for the treatment of a fatal disease, then the Medical reimbursement will lose its purpose.
The treatment of daily medications that an employee cannot survive without will be considered indoor treatment.
Furthermore, follow-up treatment after the indoor treatment/operation is regarded as part of indoor treatment.
[Para 4] B.
The scope and applicability of Punjab Services Medical Attendance Rules, 1940, Rule 2 for cooperative society employees are similar to that of State Govt. employees.
The authorities of the Cooperative Bank are required to reimburse the entire bill of medicines, including out-door treatment.
