Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Excise and Taxation Department (Group B) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 5(ii) - Age bar in promotions - The age limit of 54 years for promotion to Assistant Excise and Taxation Officer from Assistant and Senior Scale Stenographers, as prescribed by Rule 5(ii) of Haryana Excise and Taxation Department (Group B) Service Rules, 1988, is illegal and arbitrary.
There is no connection between fixing such an age limit and achieving any objective.
Additionally, it gives way to juniors to be promoted over their qualified and otherwise eligible seniors.
As a result, Rule 5(ii) of Haryana Excise and Taxation Department (Group B) Service Rules, 1988 is struck down to that extent.
