Rajasthan Engineering Subordinate Service (Pubilc Health Branch) Rules, 1967, Schedule - For the position of Junior Engineer, the candidate should hold a diploma, and the petitioner was eligible to apply for this position - However, they were not able to qualify in the interview, and as a result, they were not given an appointment - The record of the selection proceedings shows that people who did not score any marks in the interview were chosen for positions in other categories - There were no specific rules or regulations prescribing particular passing marks in the interview, and no conditions were put in place that demanded candidates to be successful during the interview - The petitioner should, therefore, be treated on the same level as people who have been appointed in other categories and is entitled to be appointed to the position of Junior Engineer Mechanical (Diploma).

The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discriminatory treatment is prohibited, and equals must be treated as equals - Any differential treatment must be based on principles that distinguish one class from another, and these principles must have a rational relation to the objective pursued - The objective of appointing eligible and meritorious candidates to the position of Junior Engineer Mechanic is critical, and those who do not score well in interviews must be treated equally - If a person in one category is appointed despite not securing marks during their interview, then another person in another category cannot be denied appointment on that same basis.
