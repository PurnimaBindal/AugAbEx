The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, discuss temporary appointment and breaks in service.
When a temporary employee is appointed until a regular appointee is selected, their appointment automatically ends when the regular person is appointed.
However, the petitioner may still work in the interest of the institution without a valid order of extension.
In this case, a regular appointment letter was issued after 16 days, and the period without a valid appointment will not count towards seniority.
However, it will count for Provident Fund, gratuity, and allotment of housing, in addition to the period of temporary appointment.
