As per Bihar Shops and Establishments Act, 1953, Sections 26(2) and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j), an establishment under the Bihar Act is defined as one carrying on any business, trade or profession, or any work incidental or ancillary thereto.
The Industrial Disputes Act considers any business, trade undertaking, manufacture or calling of employers, and any calling, service, employment, handicraft, or industrial occupation or avocation of workmen as industry.
However, if there exists an organised activity between an employer and employees to impart education, it may be considered an industry, but not a profession, trade or business for the purposes of Article 19(1)(g) of the Constitution and would not fall within the scope of establishment under the Act.
It is important to note that the definition of 'establishment' under the Bihar Act is not as wide as the definition of 'industry' under the ID Act.
