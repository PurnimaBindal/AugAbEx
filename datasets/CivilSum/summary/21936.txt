Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 16(4) - Orissa Reservation of Vacancies in Posts and Services (for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes) Act, 1975 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Tribes - The Reservation Act does not apply to the State Electricity Board since a Board is not included in the definition of the general word `State.'
A formal decision of Board is necessary for applying the Act and prior to that, employees had no legal rights under the Act.
