According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, aided schools and colleges are required to provide rural area allowance to teachers working within a certain area.
Government instructions mandate that teachers working within 10 miles of the International Border and in recognized institutions are entitled to receive house rent and rural area allowances at the same rate as Punjab Government employees.
This policy also applies to privately managed institutions, and they are not permitted to withhold this benefit.
In this case, respondents are instructed to pay rural area allowance to the petitioners at the same rate as government teachers.
