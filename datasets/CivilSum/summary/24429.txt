Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), 2(ra) and 25-B, G & H - Industrial Disputes Act Central Rules, 1957, Rules 3, 78 - Workman - Termination of service - Workman worked for 87 days as messenger - No appointment letter issued - Workman accepted amount under Section 33-C(2) as full and final satisfaction of claim - Dispute raised after one year alleging violation of Section 25-H - Violation of Section 25-G was not pleaded or referred to Tribunal - Under the circumstances, termination was not violative of Section 25-G - Non-raising of issue was a factor which Tribunal should have taken into consideration in weighing evidence - Since Section 25-H proceeds on assumption that retrenchment was validly made, High Court's view that termination was invalid under Section 25-H cannot be sustained - Amount paid to respondents shall not be recovered.
