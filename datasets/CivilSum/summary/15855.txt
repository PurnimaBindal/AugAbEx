Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules 1975, Rules 3(2), 2(3)(a), (b) and (c) and Rule 4 - Premature retirement - Voluntary retirement - Pension and gratuity - If an employee has ongoing disciplinary proceedings involving grave misconduct, they cannot claim voluntary premature retirement as a right - The authority has the discretion to accept or refuse voluntary retirement in such cases where misconduct is likely to affect retiral benefits - The authority must either accept or decline the request within the notice period, and failure to do so results in deemed acceptance - The employee need not be under suspension for the authority to decline the request.
