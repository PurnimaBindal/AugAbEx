Punjab Police Service Rules, 1959, Rules 2(b), 2(f), 6 and 10, and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - The post of Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP), police services - Absorption - Suitability - Respondent No. 4 (R4) served as an Assistant Commandant in the CRPF on compassionate grounds.
After being deputed to the State Police Service in 1993, R4 was approved for absorption in 1998.
R4 was adjudged suitable for a higher rank too.
Challenge to R4's appointment and deputation was dismissed because it had no merit.
