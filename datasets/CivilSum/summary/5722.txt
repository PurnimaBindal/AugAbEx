Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Section 5 - Workman charged with misconduct and dismissed from service after a domestic inquiry.
Industrial Tribunal declined approval due to alleged incident occurring outside factory premises, but it was held that the incident was connected to a previous incident and thus constituted misconduct.
Order of the Tribunal refusing approval was set aside and dismissal order was directed to be approved.
