The provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act still apply even if an entity has voluntarily adopted the Central Civil Services Rules, according to Section 33A and 33(2)(b) of the Act.
Although a reference order was made in 1989 challenging the applicability of the Industrial Disputes Act, the petitioner's participation in proceedings before the Tribunal from 1989 to 1996 without a satisfactory explanation for the delay in challenging the order, resulted in the dismissal of the petition on grounds of laches and merits, according to the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965.
Disputes before the Tribunal must be adjudicated and disposed within six months, per the Secretarial Civil Services Rules, 1995.
