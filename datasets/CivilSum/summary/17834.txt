Petitioners who are employed as Linemen on workcharge basis have claimed a revision in their pay scale with effect from the date on which regular Linemen were granted revised pay scale.
They argue that they perform exactly the same duties as regular Linemen and have no difference in qualifications, duties or responsibility.
They have asked to be placed equal to regular Linemen in the matter of pay scale.
[Paras 7 & 9] B Petitioners who were appointed/promoted as workcharged linemen between 1979-1985 were denied a higher pay scale which they are still entitled to receive.
The respondents argue that the petitioners are now estopped from making grievances, but it is held that denial of scale of pay furnishes a continuing cause of action, and the employee suffers discrimination every day when they are denied something which is being given to others and is actually their due.
The writ petition cannot be dismissed on account of delay.
