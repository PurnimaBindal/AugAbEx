Admissions to engineering courses include free and payment seats.
Due to irregularity in advertisement for admission, some meritorious candidates were left out and had to be re-advertised.
When more meritorious candidates applied, those who had previously been granted free seats were no longer eligible and had to switch to payment seats.
They cannot claim their free seats as a right based on an irregularity of the advertisement, particularly since all criteria remained consistent and no candidates of lesser merit were awarded free seats.
