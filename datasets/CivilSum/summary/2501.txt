An employee convicted of a crime, such as section 302 of the Indian Penal Code, is not entitled to automatic reinstatement upon filing an appeal.
The fact that the employee has earned a remission or has an interim order suspending their sentence does not give them the right to resume their employment.
The employer has the right to terminate the relationship between master and servant, but an inquiry must be conducted beforehand.
This inquiry should consider the conduct that led to the conviction and determine whether it involves moral turpitude.
Para 4

The Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, section 389 states that an employee who has been convicted of a crime may receive a remission of their sentence.
Para 14

The question of whether an offence under section 302 involves moral turpitude or not depends on the circumstances under which the offence was committed.
