The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Daily wagers regularization - Proper appointments process and procedure are followed, including fair publications - After a reasonable duration, daily wagers must be regularized and cannot be deprived of the benefits of being a regular employee indefinitely.
[Paras 20, 26, 27, 31]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(ra) and 2(oo) - Industrial Employment Standing Orders Act, 1946, Clause A - Regularisation of daily wage workers - Unfair labor practices by employers include keeping the contingent workers for a long time, offering them appointments periodically, and abruptly stopping them from taking on duty - Employers are to blame for the poor financial and physical conditions of the workers after such treatment - Employers are not allowed to deprive their employees and their families from the benefits available to regular employees - Appropriate directions are issued for their regularization of services.
[Paras 28 to 31]
