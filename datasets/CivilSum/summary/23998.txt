Under the Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Excise Service Rules, 1983, 3(g), 3(j) and 21(1), the petitioner was ad hoc promoted to the Excise Inspector position in February 1972, and was eligible for substantive promotion under the Rules.
No explanation is given as to why the petitioner was not considered for substantive promotion for several years.
As a result, the petitioner should be deemed to have been promoted as of February 1972, with the benefit of seniority.
