Constitution of India, Article 16 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, Regulation 5, Instruction No. 4(a) and (b) - Appointment on compassionate ground - A petitioner has applied for compassionate appointment seven years after attaining the majority despite rules stating that the application must be made within one year - The Authority is directed to offer the appointment - Rules and instructions are favorable to provide appointment to the minor ward of the deceased.
