Prasar Bharti (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990, Section 11(1) - Transfer - Absorption - Deputation - Absorption of Central Government employees in the Corporation transferred under Section 11(1) - Deputation is not applicable under Section 11 of the Act - However, the transferred employee can choose whether to become an employee of the Corporation - The option has no prescribed limitation - Writ petition expressing unwillingness to be an employee was treated as option - Authority directed to consider option and prescribe time for it.
