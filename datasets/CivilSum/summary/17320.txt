Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Compensation - The appellant filed a claim for compensation of Rs. 5,00,000/- stating that he had received multiple injuries on vital parts of his body - However, the evidence on record does not support this claim - Additionally, the fact of his employment with the respondent has also not been established - Therefore, the appellant's claim for compensation cannot be sustained.
