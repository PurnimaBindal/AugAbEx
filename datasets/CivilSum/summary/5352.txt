J & K State Legislature Member Pension Act, 1984 (Amended Act 13 of 1997) - Pension - Court order - Misinterpretation - Contempt of Court - The court ordered that the petitioner was entitled to pension from the date he became the member of Legislature.
The respondent granted his claim of pension from the date of judgment, contending that pension was released as per advice of Law department who interpreted the judgment.
However, it was held that this was a case of misinterpretation and not willful or deliberate disobedience of court order, thus the contempt proceedings cannot be attracted and are dropped accordingly.
[Paras 5, 6, 7 and 9]
