According to Section 11 of the Medical Council Act, 1956, a challenge regarding appointment as an Assistant Professor on the ground of the course not being recognized by the MCI is invalid.
This is because of an implied consent by the State Government during the appointment of the respondent No.6 and the judgment in Dr. Arun Kumar Agrawal's case.
The MCI and the Medical Council of Madhya Pradesh have been directed to ensure that in the future, no candidate is allotted a seat in a postgraduate course that is not recognized by the MCI.
The petition has been dismissed.
