Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Family Pension - Delay and laches - Claiming family pension late - Limiting claim of family pension to only 38 months before filing writ petition - Decision; not reasonable - Employer must calculate and give family pension to dependents of deceased staff member - If employer wrongfully denies claim of family pension to uneducated and poor dependents of deceased person, petitioners' claim cannot be restricted due to employer's fault.
