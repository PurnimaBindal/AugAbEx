Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981, Sections 62 and 21 - Supersession of Pollution Board - Removal of Chairman of the Board - Supersession and removal of the Chairman only justifiable in the event of the Board working against the government's policies, causing problems for the state government and growth of Cottage Industries, and the Chairman adopting an adamant attitude - No mala fide on the part of the government, especially when the board was its own creation - Removal of the Chairman was justified.
[Paras 5 and 6] B. Mala fide - Removal of the Chairman not based solely on the Central Minister's complaint, hence, no mala fide attributed to the government - Complaint only served as a consideration for the removal of the Chairman - Removal of the Chairman was justified.
[Para 7] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981, Section 62(1)(a) and (b) - Notice - Natural justice - Notice required for superseding the Board under Section (a) due to defaults but not for superseding it under Clause (b) for reasons mentioned - Legislature deliberately excluded notice and opportunity from Clause (b) of Section 62(1) of the Act - Govt. action cannot be set aside for want of notice and opportunity to the Board.
