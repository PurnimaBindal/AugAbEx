As per the government instructions dated 2761988 for CRPF, when a person becomes completely incapacitated, he is entitled to lump sum grant of Rs. 25,000/- plus monthly recurring payment of Rs. 400/- pm for 20 years or until death.
The petitioner, who is completely and permanently incapacitated, is entitled to receive the same benefits.
The instructions clearly state that length of service being less than 10 years cannot be a reason to deny the benefit.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to Rs. 25,000/- as lump sum grant and monthly payment of Rs. 400/-.
In addition to that, directions have also been issued to issue an appointment letter in favour of the petitioner's wife.
