The constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 and 16 are in play in the case of the petitioner.
They have undergone an amendment subsequent to the declaration of the merit list in the prospectus, and the petitioner has been equally benefited and not suffered any prejudice.
Institutional weightage/concessions are acceptable, and the amendment is of clarificatory in nature and does not change the basic merit criteria.
The petition is dismissed.
