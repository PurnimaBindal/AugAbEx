Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act and Order 9 Rule 13 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908 govern the setting aside of ex parte orders for compensation.
In this case, an ex parte order was passed for the payment of compensation to a workman who suffered an injury during the course of employment.
The petitioner firm refused to accept service of summons and no reason was given for not approaching the court immediately.
The contention that the petitioner firm only became aware of the ex parte order upon receiving notice regarding its execution was found to be incorrect.
The court ruled that the ex parte order was not illegal and the application to set it aside was barred by limitation due to insufficient cause for delay.
