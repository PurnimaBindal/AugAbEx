The fact that the goods were hypothecated with the bank and needed appropriate handling is not disputed in the Termination case.
It was the owner of the goods who made this arrangement, and while it is feasible that the bank manager may have recommended the individual, it does not change the fact that the watchman would have become an employee of the bank.
As a result, the tribunal's conclusion that there is a master and workman relationship in this instance cannot be upheld, and the tribunal's award reinstating the claimant with service continuity and backwages is revoked.
