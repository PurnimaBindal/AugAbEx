According to the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(j) and 10(1)(c), the Directorate of Punjab State Lotteries, which runs various lotteries, is considered an industry under the definition of 'Industry' in the Act.
The running of lotteries is a commercial activity.

The termination of a workman's services after six years of continuous service without providing retrenchment compensation and notice pay is in violation of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
The termination is deemed illegal and has to be cancelled.
The Labour Court's awards are upheld.
