Madhya Pradesh Non-Gazetted Class III Education Service (Non- Collegiate Service) Recruitment and Promotion Rules, 1973, Rule 10(3) and Proviso (as amended by 1993 amendment) - Delegation of Powers - The rule making authority reserves the right to prescribe criteria and procedure for selection in special circumstances.
Therefore, excessive delegation does not arise as the authority gives itself power to prescribe the necessary criteria and procedure.
[Para 13]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - The selection exercise is held as seriously distorted when the selected and appointed candidates aren't made a party to the tribunal's proceedings.
[Para 12]

Constitution of India, Article 309 - Recruitment Rules - According to this article, if the Government prescribes higher technical qualifications as per job requirements, then no objection can be raised against the dispensing of the present criteria and procedure for such criteria is not deemed unfair.
[Paras 15, 17 and 18]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Recruitment - The selection through employment exchanges without advertising the vacancies in the open market cannot be deemed unfair or violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution if no eligible candidate challenged the same and applications invited were only from employment exchanges.
[Para 19]
