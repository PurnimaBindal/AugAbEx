Bhudeb Bhattacharjee, Advocate discusses appointment and seniority of unqualified teachers who subsequently acquire necessary qualifications.
If initial appointment was not made according to rules, subsequent regularization of service does not entitle employee to benefit of intervening service for seniority.
The petitioner could not be appointed on a substantive post due to being under-qualified and her service was approved purely on a temporary basis with the condition that it could come to an end and she shall be appointed on a substantive post as soon as she passes B.A.B.T.
However, when a person is appointed on a temporary or ad hoc basis having requisite qualifications and in accordance with the law, but cannot be appointed on a substantive post due to want of vacant posts, their seniority should be given from the initial date of appointment.
