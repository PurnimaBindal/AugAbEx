Kerala Civil Service Rules, Part II, Rule 24 - Prevention of Corruption Act, Section 2(c)(iii) - Government servant - Deputation - Disciplinary proceedings - Repartriation - The government can repatriate a government servant sent on deputation to any government department or its instrumentality as long as their lien has not been terminated and has the power to take disciplinary action against them during their deputation and even suspend them.
