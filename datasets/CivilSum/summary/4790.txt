The delinquent in disciplinary proceedings under Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India was not given a chance to present evidence in their defence, causing definite and sufficient prejudice.
The punishment is thus held to be violative of natural justice.
[Paras 12, 15, 18, 20, 43]
When a delinquent is not holding a pensionable post and retired, disciplinary proceedings cannot continue.
The Civil Services Regulations Article 351A does not apply, and no other provisions allow for continuation.
[Paras 30, 36, 43]
