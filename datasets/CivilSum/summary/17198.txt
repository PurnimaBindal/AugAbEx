Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) and 2(k) - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner seeking compassionate appointment - Faced rejection from the employer on the basis of not being a workman under section 2(s) of the ID Act - Industrial dispute raised - The expression "person" in section 2(k) of the ID Act is comprehensive enough to encompass such individuals - Decision to reject the reference is reversed.
