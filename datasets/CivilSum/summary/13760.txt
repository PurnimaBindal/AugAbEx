Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-I, Part-I, specifically Rules 12 and 14 - PCMS-II (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1943 - Eligibility for maternity leave - Female candidates who are found to be 12 weeks pregnant on initial entry to Government service must be declared temporarily unfit and are not recruited to service on an ad hoc basis until their confinement is over, according to the instructions.
- The petitioner obtained her appointment by lying under oath and claiming to be only 10 weeks pregnant when in fact she was 12 weeks pregnant at the time of entry.
As a result of her fraud, she was able to take maternity leave.
- The decision to treat her maternity leave as extraordinary leave is not illegal.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
