Appointment - Part-time service - Training Pay and allowances - Subsequent course of events - Vicarious liability - Estoppel - As a part-time craft teacher, the petitioner later promoted to a full-time craft teacher and selected to undergo teacher's training course - The petitioner is entitled to receive full pay and allowances during the training period, without the requirement to appoint a substitute - The post the petitioner occupied as a full-time teacher was converted to a part-time role, but she is still entitled to full pay and allowances during the training period - The principle of estoppel does not apply.
[Paras 5 and 6] B. Part-time service - Pay fixation - Part-time teachers who are promoted to full-time teachers are not eligible for the benefit of part-time service when it comes to fixing their pay.
