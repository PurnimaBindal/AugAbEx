Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 18, 19, 29 - Violation of settlement - Management to be prosecuted for violation of settlement - Petitioner's application dismissed by Labour Court on the basis of notice issued under section 19(2) of Act - Settlement under Act does not become invalid solely due to issuance of notice under section 19(2) - Even after expiry of term, settlement remains effective between parties until a new settlement or award is in place - Directions given to initiate legal proceedings against respondent under Section 29 of Act.
