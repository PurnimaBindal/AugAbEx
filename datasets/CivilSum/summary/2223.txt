Constitution of India, Article 116 - AP Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules (1991), Rule 20 - Departmental enquiry - Appointment of Enquiry Officer - According to Service Rules, the disciplinary authority must frame charges before appointing an Enquiry Officer.
The Enquiry Officer's role begins after the charges have been framed by the disciplinary authority.
The Enquiry Officer must also wait to receive the delinquent's written statement of defence before proceeding.
Without jurisdiction, an incompetent Enquiry Officer cannot issue a charge memo.
