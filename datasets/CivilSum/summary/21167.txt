The Supreme Court cannot review factual findings regarding completion of 240 days of service by a workman appealing a retrenchment decision under Article 136 of the Constitution, according to the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F.
[Para 6]
While violation of the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F cannot be excused, reinstatement is not always necessary as the Tribunal can award reasonable compensation if the workman is unwilling to return under the same terms.
[Paras 7 and 8]
