The petitioner was promoted to Head Constable based on their exceptional performance in sports as per Punjab Police Rules, Rules 135, 138 and 1318.
However, the petitioner was not an upgraded constable and did not meet the basic criteria under Rule 135 for promotion.
Additionally, only sportsmen representing the national team in different games are eligible for special consideration under Rule 138, making the petitioner ineligible for promotion.
The order to revert the petitioner to the post of constable was justified and legal.
[Paras 2, 3, 12, 13, 18 and 19]
