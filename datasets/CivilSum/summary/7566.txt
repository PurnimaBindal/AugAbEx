According to Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 Section 2(12) and General Classes Act, 1897, Section 8, the definition of "manufacturing process" under section 2(k) of Factories Act, 1948 does not include the process of storing milk in coolers for sale.
However, if such amendment in definition of 'manufacturing process' under Factories Act is not adopted in definition of "factory" under ESI Act, some employees will not get benefits, which would result in discrimination.
Therefore, the demand by corporation is in accordance with the law and both acts supplement each other.
