Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Rules, 1994, Rules 17 & 20 - J&K Law Officers (Subordinate) Service Recruitment Rules, 1990, Rules 5 and 9, Schedule II(b) - When promoting to Senior Scale Stenographer, relaxation of standards for Schedule Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates according to Rule 17 of Reservation Rules is only possible if the posts are reserved for these categories; 1990 Rules do not have provisions for that - Standard of eligibility and qualifying speed test, set at a minimum of 80:40 words per minute, must be applied to all candidates regardless of category to maintain efficiency and responsibility of the posts.
