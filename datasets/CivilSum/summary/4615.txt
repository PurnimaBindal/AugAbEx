Rajasthan Transport Subordinate Service Rules, 1963, Schedule I, Clause 3, Condition Nos. 3 and 4, Qualification for the post of Motor Vehicle Sub-Inspector - Possession of driving licence for direct recruitment quota as compared to the non-essential condition for promotional quota amounts to discrimination.
Despite enhancing the incumbents' competency, the qualification for direct recruitment must be equally essential for the promotional quota.
It is for the government to consider amending the qualification for direct recruitment relative to the promotional quota for the post of Sub-Inspector Motor Vehicles.
The petition is dismissed.
[Para 14] B Motor Vehicles Act, Section 213, Sub-section (4) - Direct recruitment of Motor Vehicles Sub-Inspector - Appointment - Qualification - Prescribed qualifications are required under the law and can be changed or modified through rules.
No vested right is violated by having modified qualifications.
The government is responsible for specifying the qualifications and there is no flaw in the rules for rejecting petitioners who do not satisfy prescribed qualifications when submitting applications.
The petition is dismissed.
