Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punishment - Stoppage of increment - The petitioner was punished with stoppage of four annual grade increments with cumulative effect due to causing an accident out of carelessness - Enquiry found the petitioner guilty of negligent driving - Punishing authority considered past satisfactory record and awarded lenient punishment - The punishment is not disproportionate to the offence - No interference.
