Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part I, Section III, Regulations 48, 173 and 184 - Disability pension - To determine the attributability of disability of Army Service, the authority must consider both direct and circumstantial evidence even with casual connection between disability and service.
The petitioner, despite suffering internal orthopedical injury in his knee during the 1965 war, continued to fight and was granted special Short Service Commission in 1972, deployed in Indo-Pak war and released in 1974 with his disability having been reported to be 40% to 50%.
The authority is directed to grant disability pension with arrears and 12% interest from the date of entitlement.
[Paras 17 and 18]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part I, Section III, Regulations 48, 173 and 184 - Disability pension - The Court cannot substitute its decision to that of the authorities, but the entire relevant material and documents available on record must have been taken into consideration and a speaking order provided with reasons.
The decision may be interfered with if the test of reasonableness is not met.
The authority must provide the reasons for its decision, fulfilling the principles of natural justice.
