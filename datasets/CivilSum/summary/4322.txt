Constitution or India, Article 14 - Punjab Secretariat Service Building and Roads Branch (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1943, Section 2(e) and section 9 - Seniority/Field Service/Head Office Staff - Service conditions of field staff and head office staff are governed by different service rules - A member from field service who is transferred to head office on compassionate grounds is not allowed to claim seniority over clerks working in the head office - Seniority should be recorded from the date of joining head office service only and the benefit of field service cannot be claimed.
[Para 9]  B Constitution of India, Article 309 - Punjab Secretariat Service Building and Roads Branch (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1943, section 2(e) and section 9 - Seniority - The seniority of members of service holding posts in the same scale of pay is fixed from the date of confirmation - If the date of joining and confirmation is the same, rule 9(ii) will apply - Any instructions running counter to the rules for determining inter se, seniority have to be ignored and cannot be pressed into service to challenge the seniority fixed according to the rules.
