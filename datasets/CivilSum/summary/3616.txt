The Bank is not prevented from discontinuing the policy of allowing Head Peons to stay overnight at certain branches/office premises, since no condition of service was conferred on the Head Peons to retain the occupation of such space.
A writ court cannot allow the petitioners to reiterate the submissions before the Industrial Tribunal or the Labour Court which they have failed to establish before the Conciliation Officer.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
