Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that equal pay should be provided for equal work.
However, since Matric and Non-matric Workshop Instructors differ in qualifications, they are placed in different pay scales.
Non-matriculates can receive the pay scale equal to Matriculates only after they pass a trade test of skill.
The difference is considered valid, as mere long service does not grant the employee a right to demand pay parity.
It is the responsibility of the claimant to demonstrate equality.
