Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Dismissal from service - The petitioner was unable to join the transferred post due to his illness - Despite this, disciplinary proceedings were initiated against him and he was dismissed from service - After the petitioner's death, it was discovered that the issue of his ailment was not taken into consideration during the dismissal process - As a result, the dismissal order was overturned and the writ petitioner was deemed to be in service as of the date of his death with 50% back wages - The daughters of the original petitioner are eligible to apply for compassionate appointment.
