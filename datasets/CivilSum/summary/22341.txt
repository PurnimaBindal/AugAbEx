Constitution of Indain, Article 14, Indian Military Nursing Service Ordinance, 1943, Sections 10 and 11 - Dress Code for Military Nursing Service - As part of the Armed Forces, Military Nursing Service is treated differently under a special code and is not subject to the Army Act.
The Chief of Army Staff has the discretion to prescribe the Dress Code, which is purely an administrative function.
There is no need for intervention from the court, as a special body has already been constituted to determine the Dress Code and has considered all objections and provided full opportunity to all concerned.
Therefore, even under Article 14 of the Constitution, there is no scope left for court intervention.
