According to Pension Regulations for the Army, Part-I of 1961, Paragraph 173 - Army Order 27/81, paragraph No. 2(f), individuals can claim for Disability Pension.
However, if there is no record of the person's enrollment date or medical condition prior to entering service, the claim may be denied.
Additionally, if it can be shown that the disease could have been detected earlier, the claim can be denied.
In this particular case, it was not denied that the disease had been aggravated by an unsuccessful operation at the Base Hospital, so the direction was given to Government to consider the claim for disability pension again.
