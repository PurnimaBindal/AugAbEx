Constitution of India, Articles 215 and 141 - Judgments and precedents - The administration of justice depends on consistency - This consistency can only be achieved by following the rule of finality and respecting precedents and principles of stare decisis - These rules are based on public policy and are necessary to prevent chaos in the administration of justice.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion Seniority - Senior employees do not have an automatic right to promotion - They have a right to be considered for promotion - Courts cannot mandamus promotion, only direct authorities to perform their duty to consider employees for promotion.
