Indian Forest Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1968, Rule 3(2)(c), Explanation 4 - Indian Forest Service (Cadre) Rules, 1966, Rule 9 - Seniority - Only Officers with a certificate of the State Government in terms of Explanation 4 to Rule 3(2)(c) of Seniority Rules, 1968 and approval for officiation in a cadre post beyond period of 6 months from the Central Government and the Union Public Service Commission can claim seniority on account of long officiation in a cadre post.
Non-cadre and non-Select-List Officers are not entitled.
