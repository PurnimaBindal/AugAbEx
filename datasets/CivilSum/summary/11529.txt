Legal Services of Authorities Act, 1987, Sections 5, 6 and 20 - Award of Lok Adalat - Parties voluntarily submitted to the jurisdiction of Lok Adalat - No application for recalling the case from Lok Adalat filed - The Adalat's award treated as a settlement executable like a decree under section 20.
[Paras 3 & 4] ORDER MM Kumar, J (Oral) - An application was filed under Sub-Sections 5 and 6 of Section 20 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, seeking to decide the appeal on merit and set aside the Lok Adalat's order dated 28052001, which the Act treats as a decree under Section 21(1).
[Para 3] The State counsel did not deny that the order was a settlement and had no compulsion attached.
[Para 3] The parties submitted voluntarily to Lok Adalat's jurisdiction.
[Para 3] The application was filed after the decision went against the appellant.
[Para 4] Refer to Section 20, Sub-sections 5 and 6 of the Act to understand no award and seek remedy in court.
[Para 4].
