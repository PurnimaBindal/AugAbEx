Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Claim for - Delay and laches - Petitioner's claim for seniority was rejected by the CAT for being belated - The petitioner claimed seniority from 1980 instead of 1986 - The writ petition was filed in 1996, but the seniority list was already finalized in 1992 - Even though it was contended that the seniority list was finally prepared in 1996, the argument was rejected - The tribunal's decision to deny the petitioner relief was appropriate.
