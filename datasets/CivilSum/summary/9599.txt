Promotion from the post of Assistant Engineer to the post of Executive Engineer is governed by Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
Petitioner received an order of deemed promotion as Assistant Engineer with retrospective effect, giving him the right to be treated as holding the post from that retrospective date and gaining experience for promotion as Executive Engineer.
However, promotion and consideration were withheld due to a status quo order passed in some writ petitions, and after dismissal of those writ petitions, the non-consideration of the petitioner for promotion is arbitrary and contrary to equality clause, violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
