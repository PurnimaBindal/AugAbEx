According to Sections 2(s) and 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, an individual is considered a "workman."
Even though the tribunal claimed the respondent was a "workman" under Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the evidence on record states that he was an officer.
As a result, the termination of the respondent's services was illegal and the Tribunal had no right to solve the dispute.
The Tribunal's decision is overturned.
