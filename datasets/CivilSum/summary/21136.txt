Validity of power of sub-delegation under the Employees' State Insurance (Central) Rules, 1950, Rule 16(2) - While delegation of powers or duties under the Rules or Regulations is allowed, sub-delegation of powers delegated to the Director General by the Standing Committee U/s 94-A is not permissible.
[Paras 15 to 18] B. Initiation of disciplinary proceedings - Any superior authority can be a controlling authority and can initiate disciplinary proceedings, even if not competent to impose penalty or make appointment.
[Para 13] C. Delegation of powers - While sub-delegation of delegated powers is not permissible, statutory powers can be further delegated to the extent the statute allows.
In the absence of any such provision, competent authority must exercise the powers personally.
