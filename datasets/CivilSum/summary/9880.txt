The Food Corporation of India's Staff Regulations from 1971, Regulations 56 and 57 (which were amended on 16101987) make a distinction between the Disciplinary and Punishing Authority.
The amendment to Regulation 56 means that an authority inferior to the appointing authority cannot impose a major penalty.
However, this does not affect the authority's ability to initiate disciplinary proceedings or issue a charge sheet.
Pursuant to the amended Regulation 57(2), the disciplinary authority competent to impose minor penalties can also launch proceedings for major penalties specified in Regulations 54(v) to (ix).
The exception is that no authority below the rank of the appointing authority or the authority specified in Appendix 2 for that purpose may impose an order that imposes major penalties.
In the case of Assistant Manager (Quality Control), the Sr.
Regional Manager is the controlling authority and the Zonal Manager is the punishing authority for major penalties.
The charge sheet is issued, and the order appointing the Inquiry Officer to impose a major penalty for the Category III employee is not without jurisdiction.

Article 311(1) of the Indian Constitution defines the competent authority that may serve as the punishing authority.
When there are no specific rules governing the authority to initiate disciplinary action, any controlling authority of an employee, even one subordinate to the appointing or punishing authority, may issue a charge sheet and initiate proceedings, on the condition that the punishment order is not below the rank of the appointing authority.
