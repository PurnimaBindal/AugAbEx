Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Himachal Pradesh Higher Judicial Services Rules, 1973, Rule 9(1) - Appointment/cut off date - The notice for post of Additional District Judge must invite applications by July 15, 1998 from those aged between 35 and 45 years old as of January 1, 1998, with no less than 7 years of practice at the bar - The notice's cut off date is not opposed to Rule 9(1) of the Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1973 - The rule does not permit those over 45.
There is no violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The petition is dismissed.
