Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Suspension - The order of suspension should not be made arbitrarily, as it could cause harm and suffering to the person concerned unnecessarily.
However, the suspension in itself is not a punishment.
In this particular case, a charge-sheet was issued to the petitioner on allegations of shortages in the stock of coal under their control while working as Area General Manager and subsequently appointed as Additional Chief Mining Engineer.
The petitioner is required to explain the charges of shortage in the stock.
The continuance of the petitioner on the post may cause embarrassment and hindrance to a fair and free inquiry.
Therefore, the suspension in this case is justified and not arbitrary.
The order has been upheld.
[Paras 11 to 14]
