Advocates Act, 1961, Section 24-A - Enrolment as Advocate - Disqualification - The Bar Council of Karnataka disqualified the petitioner from becoming an advocate due to his pre-enrolment training while working as Divisional Commercial Supdt.
where he was found guilty in a Domestic Enquiry for holding assets disproportionate to his known income.
As legal profession is a noble one, those with a bad service track cannot be enrolled with the Bar Council.
The writ petition was rejected.
