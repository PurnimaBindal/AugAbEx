Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 25-F - Fixed term appointment - Despite the continuation of work after repeated extensions on fixed term appointment, the provisions of Section 25-F cannot be evaded by simply stating that the appointment was for 89 days on daily wages.
[Para 2]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Retrenchment - Termination due to the transfer of a regular incumbent to the post - Such termination falls within the ambit of the definition of retrenchment described as "for any reason whatsoever."
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Industry - Estoppel - If the management has not raised any objection regarding its status as an "industry" before the labour Court, then it is not permissible for them to object for the first time in a writ petition.
