The Rules for Emergency Commissioned Officers and Short Service Commissioned Officers provide that military service includes the training period of released ECO and SSCOs, which is counted towards their deemed date of appointment.
There is no restriction on the benefit of military service only being applicable to the first appointment in civil service, and the benefit extends to all appointments.
As the petitioner was not appointed in excess of the reserved quota for ECOs and SSCOs, they cannot be denied the benefit of the Rules based solely on the ground of a second appointment in Civil.
