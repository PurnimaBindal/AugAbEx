Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Liability to make payment of gratuity - The law stipulates that only the employer is liable to pay gratuity to employees and does not provide for any other entity to be held responsible - Regardless of whether or not the government is providing grant-in-aid to the management, the liability still rests on the employer - Thus, the State cannot be directed to compensate for the payment of gratuity.
