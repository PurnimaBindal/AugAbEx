Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India pertain to the qualifications and recognition of school courses.
The examination of Prathma conducted by the Hindi Sahitya Samelan in Allahabad has been recognized by Panjab University and the Punjab School Education Board, but not by the Chandigarh Administration as equivalent to Matric.
Therefore, the petitioner, an employee of the Chandigarh Administration, cannot receive any benefit from it unless the Administration recognizes it.
The court does not have the power or expertise to direct the recognition of any degree or course.
The petitioner, who possesses the qualification of Prathma, was held to be unqualified as Matric, and the reversion was upheld.
