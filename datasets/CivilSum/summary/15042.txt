Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Gratuity - Alleged withholding of gratuity following retirement - Gratuity payment withholdment without charge sheet or show cause notice - Imposing a charge sheet for inquiry is obligatory - No gratuity can be detained following retirement in relation to accusations associated with the date after retirement - Respondents ordered to release unpaid amount of gratuity within 30 days.
[Paras 1-3]
