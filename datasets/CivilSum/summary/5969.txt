Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19 - Bank of India Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 - Termination/Competent Authority - The Bank of India Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, which are of statutory nature and have been framed under Section 19 of the Banking Companies Act, 1970, provide protection for employees under their service conditions.
Under the regulations, the Zonal Manager cannot pass an order for termination of a specific category of employees, when the General Manager is the competent authority for dealing with their service conditions, otherwise it would be null and void.
The principles of natural justice and the provision of General Clauses Act as well as statutory and non-statutory service rules and regulations are also effective to protect employees from indiscriminate exercise of jurisdiction.
[Paras 8 and 10]

Competent authority/Jurisdiction/Termination - A subordinate authority having no jurisdiction cannot pass a termination order, nor can management remedy this defect by passing a new order and getting it signed by the competent authority.
[Para 12]
