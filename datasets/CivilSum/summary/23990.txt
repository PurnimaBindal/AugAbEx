The Constitution of India, Article 225 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B, are referenced in relation to Letters Patent Appeal.
The issue at hand is whether an LPA against an order made by a Single Judge under Section 17-B of the ID Act is maintainable under Clause 10 of the Letters Patent of Patna before a Division Bench of the High Court.
The decision was that the Single Judge's order made under Section 17-B of the ID Act constitutes a "judgment" as per Clause 10 of the Letters Patent of Patna, and therefore, it is appealable.
As a result, the case was remanded to the High Court to decide the LPA on its merits in accordance with the law.
