Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4 Scheduled IV (as amended) - General Clauses Act, Section 6(c) - Amendment of Act - Accident that occurred on 14.8.1973 will not be affected by the amendment in the Schedule IV to Section 4 that was made enforceable from 1.10.1975 - The computation of compensation under the amended Schedule was deemed illegal, and claimants will receive compensation as per the unamended Schedule.
[Para 9] B Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A - Penalty - Delay in payment - Employers who fail to deposit compensation within a month from the date of the accident are liable to pay a penalty at the rate of 50% and 6% interest.
