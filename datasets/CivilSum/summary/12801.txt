Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Discharge from Air Force services due to medical reasons - Petitioner, who was declared 100% disabled because of a service-related illness, was also declared fit for civil services at the age of 54, which caused his dependents to lose out on the opportunity of compassionate appointment - The petitioner was granted only Rs. 2000/- per month pension, which he must use to support his mother, two daughters, and son - Although the authorities promised a job for the petitioner's son when he turned 18, they refused to provide him with one later on - The authorities' actions were considered arbitrary, and they were ordered to appoint the petitioner's son on compassionate grounds.
