Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Compassionate appointment - Earnings of other family members or relatives - Applicants cannot claim compassionate appointment as a right, but it cannot be denied on the grounds of the earnings of other family members or relatives unless it's shown that the earnings were contributing to the survival of the applicant - The Tribunal is not authorized to direct the appointment of any person to a specific post or to generate a post for compassionate appointment.
The only direction to consider the applicant for any post is deemed acceptable.
[Paras 9 and 11]
