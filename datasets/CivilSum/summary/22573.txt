Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Industry - Burden of proof - A respondent appointed a clerk on a purely temporary basis in the Forest Department in the State of Gujarat, but his service terminated after 1-1/2 years.
The contention that the service was terminated without complying with the provisions of Section 25F was made, but there was no assertion of fact that the establishment to which he had been appointed is 'an industry.'
The High Court committed a serious error of law in holding that the provisions of the Act apply to the appointment in question.
