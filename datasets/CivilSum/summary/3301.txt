Res judicata/Constructive res judicata/Charge-sheet/Bias - The petitioner is prohibited from raising the plea of defective charge-sheet and biased Disciplinary Authority as he had already filed a writ petition complaining that he was not being allowed assistance of a lawyer to defend him in the domestic enquiry and this plea was dismissed earlier.
The Court considers this plea barred by constructive res judicata.
[Para 7]

Enquiry/Admission/Natural Justice - The petitioner admitted his guilt and participated in the enquiry, which was held properly according to the rules.
The court refrains from interfering with the proceedings as well as quantum of punishment if it can be awarded on the allegations levelled against the employee.
[Para 7]
