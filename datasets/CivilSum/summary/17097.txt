Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Petitioner's request for compassionate appointment declined due to existing employment of one of the deceased employee's sons and non-precarious family condition - Terminal dues of Rs. 4,35,217/- were paid to petitioner on her husband's death and she earned a monthly income of Rs. 4253/- - Taking into account these factors, the application was rightfully rejected - No intervention is warranted.
