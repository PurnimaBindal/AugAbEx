The Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 142, and the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 235, 236 and 482, dealt with in the context of law relating to making remarks against a subordinate judicial officer in a judgment, with detailed elaboration.
[Paras 14, 15, 10 and 11]  B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 142 and 136, Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 482, 235, 236, outlined the remedy a concerned judicial officer can approach, in case of disparaging remarks made by court of superior jurisdiction against subordinate judicial officer, and tests for expunging any passage from an order or judgment.
[Paras 10, 11 and 14]  C. The Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 142, and Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 482, in relation to the unbecoming conduct of subordinate judicial officer while deciding a case, prescribed that High Court must avoid judicial pronouncement on the conduct of judicial officer, but draw attention of Chief Justice separately for appropriate action.
