Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Loan repayment - Liability of employer to repay after salary deductions - Interest - Employer had to deduct loan instalment from employee's salary, but did not deposit the amount with bank - Despite repeated representations and notices, no action was taken by the employer - It was held that the employer is liable for delayed payments and any interest charged by the bank should be paid by the employer and not the employee.
