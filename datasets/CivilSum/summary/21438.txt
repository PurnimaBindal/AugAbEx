Contempt Petition Nos. 30506 I.A. Nos. 1115 of 1993.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Bihar Inter University Board Act, 1981 - Promotion - Seniority and merit - The time bound promotion scheme cannot be equated with ordinary promotion under which merit is considered along with seniority by a duly constituted committee or commission.
In the time bound promotion scheme, seniority and merit are evaluated by a Screening Committee and Selection Commission which is not an empty formality, and promotion is not automatic upon completion of required length of experience in the lower post.
