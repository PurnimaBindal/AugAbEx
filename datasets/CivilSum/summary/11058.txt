Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 4 - Preliminary inquiry - The absence of preliminary inquiry does not invalidate proceedings taken by employer in regular inquiry as it is not a precondition.
[Para 5]

Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 4 - Natural justice - Departmental Inquiry - Delinquent did not attend inquiry proceedings after noting down the date - Ex parte inquiry does not violate natural justice.
[Para 5]

Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 4 - Natural justice - Enquiry Report - Non-supply of - Inquiry report need not be supplied unless it can be shown that non-supply caused prejudice to the case, and this alone does not vitiate the departmental proceedings.
[Para 5]

Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 4 - Disciplinary proceedings/Subsistence Allowance - Non-payment of subsistence allowance during enquiry - Omission to pay subsistence allowance does not vitiate proceedings unless it can be shown that it resulted in prejudice to the case.
[Para 5]

Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 4 - Dismissal with retrospective effect - Resolution of Managing Committee for dismissing the teacher must be approved by DPI - Approval alone does not amount to passing an order of dismissal with retrospective effect.
[Para 5]
