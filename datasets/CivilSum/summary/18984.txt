Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 48-A and 49(2)(b) - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 19 - Pensionary benefits to persons who have resigned from service - The petitioners were denied pensionary benefits because they resigned instead of retiring in accordance with the C.S.C.
(Pension) Rules.
However, according to Rule 49(2)(b) of the C.C.S.
(Pension) Rules, individuals who retire or resign under Rule 19 of the B.S.F.
Rules are entitled to proportionate pension.
