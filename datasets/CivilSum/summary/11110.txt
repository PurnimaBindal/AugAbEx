Punjab State Leather Development Corporation Ltd. - Accounts Assistants seeking equal pay - Respondents argued that the duties of Accounts Assistants were of lower responsibilities than other positions in Punjab Government, making parity unattainable under law - The Third Pay Commission's report did not support parity - The writ petition was dismissed with no order as it costs.

The petitioners were required to prove that their counterparts were equal in every regard in order to demonstrate the application of the doctrine of equal pay for equal work.
