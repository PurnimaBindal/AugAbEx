Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 21(1)(b) - Delay and laches - Writ Petition challenging the orders of Central Administrative Tribunal - If filed after more than two years, it is liable to be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches as the Administrative Tribunals Act sets the reasonable time for challenging the orders of the Tribunal to be six months or at the best one year if sufficient cause for delay is shown.

Medical Institutions Regulations, 1998, Regulation 3 - Residency Scheme, 1992 - Promotion to the post of Professor of Microbiology - Eligibility - Experience - Under the Residency scheme, a Resident is a person who is doing Postgraduation and earlier was described only as a M.D.
Student - With the introduction of Residency Scheme, 1992, the period of post graduation as teaching/professional experience will be considered as experience of Resident for promotion to the post of Professor.
