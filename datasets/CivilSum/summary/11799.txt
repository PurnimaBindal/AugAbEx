According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Corporation had been supplying weavers with materials to produce carpets, while also providing market facilities for the same.
However, the weavers were not supervised in any way and the Corporation did not have any sort of master and servant relationship with them.
When the Corporation began incurring losses and could no longer provide the materials necessary to the weavers, it was not considered as retrenchment under the Act since the weavers did not meet the definition of "Workmen".
