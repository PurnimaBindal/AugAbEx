According to Army Act, Sections 2(1)(c) and 2(2), Indian Reserve Forces Act, 1888, Section 5, and Defence Service Regulations, Regulation 206, persons who belong to Reserve Forces will be subject to the provisions of Army Act unless retired or discharged.
The plea that Defence Regulations will apply cannot be accepted, and the Act will prevail in case of conflict between Act and Rules.
Dismissal by Brigade Commander, in this case, was justified.
[Paras 9, 10 and 11]

As per Army Act, Section 20(3), Pension Regulations for Army, Regulation 113, and Defence Service Regulation, Regulation 203(b), a petitioner is required to complete 10 years in colour and 10 years in reserve service for pension eligibility.
Since the petitioner failed to attend the mandatory reservist training, he was declared deserter and dismissed under Section 20(3).
Thus, he is not entitled to pension as he failed to complete entire length of service, and a person dismissed from service is not entitled to pension.
[Paras 14 to 16]
