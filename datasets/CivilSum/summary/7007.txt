According to Section 2(K) of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a Youth Hostel cannot be classified as an industry.
There is no commercial activity or business involved, and hence, it does not meet the criteria for being an industry.

As per Section 6-N of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an employee working on a temporary daily wage basis is not entitled to the benefit of this section, even if they have worked for over 240 days.
Section 6-N only applies to workmen properly appointed on sanctioned posts in any industry.
