According to Punjab Finance (Treasuries and Accounts Department) (State Services Class III) Rules, 1979, Rule 8(1) and Constitution of India, Article 16(4), the policy of reservation of vacancies in favour of Scheduled Castes cannot be applied to a single cadre post as it amounts to 100% reservation.
However, the reserved point must be carried forward and in the next recruitment year, even if there is only a single vacancy, it must go to the Scheduled Caste category.
The petitioner's claim is justified and the petition is allowed as per Paras 12, 13, and 15 of the mentioned text.
