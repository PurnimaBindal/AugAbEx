Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11-A - Gujarat Industrial Disputes Rules, 1966, Rule 26 - Reference - The Labour Court does not have the authority to dismiss a reference due to a workman's default.
Instead, the Labour Court is responsible for making a decision on the reference based on its merits.
It would be unfair for a workman to suffer due to the fault of their advocate, and thus, one more opportunity should be given to the workman.
