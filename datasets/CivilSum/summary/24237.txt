Constitution of India, Article 229(2) - Calcutta High Court Services Rules, 1998 - Validity - Pay scales - Committee of Judges on pay scales of High Court employees made recommendations - State Government approval is necessary - State is responsible for funds - Consensus between parties is needed - Rules framed by Chief Justice of India carry importance and should be approved unless there is a very good reason not to - Ordered for a Special Pay Commission to be constituted consisting of judges and administrators to report within six months.
