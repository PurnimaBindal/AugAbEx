Haryana State Central Co-operative Banks' Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1975, Rule 3.1 - Appointments in the Bank - The Administrative Committee, that is constituted in adherence with the bye-laws of the Bank, holds the power to appoint staff and implement the Rules.
A resolution passed by the directors for making appointments by a committee other than the Administrative Committee is in breach of the Rules and bye-laws, and appointees by such committee are unlawful.
