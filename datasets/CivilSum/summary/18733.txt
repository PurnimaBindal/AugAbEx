Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 277 - Promotion - Cancellation of promotion - Petitioner was promoted to the position of clerk by the Market Committee based on their perception of him being a staff member - Demotion of petitioner without proper opportunity of hearing - The argument that Mandi Board had not granted approval for this promotion is not valid as the petitioner was treated as an employee of the Market Committee - Order cancelling petitioner's promotion is quashed - Petitioner shall work as a clerk and shall be paid their salary for the position of the Clerk from the date of their appointment.
