Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226 - Revised pay scale - The petitioners in this case were appointed as Electricians/Refrigerator Mechanics for Technical Posts, with a Matric and 2 years ITI Diploma qualification.
The Govt. made a policy decision to grant a revised pay scale from 121981, which was further revised from 151990.
It was argued that this policy was applicable to common categories of posts in other departments, and not to work charged employees in PWD (B&R), but this contention was not sustainable based on the Amar Singh case.
As a result, consequential relief of recovery of arrears was granted, but only for a restricted period of 38 months.
