Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 9-A - Notice of change - In the case of discontinuing a mailing section without introducing new or better methods, no notice of change is required before termination as it does not constitute rationalization.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 9-A - Rationalization - Rationalization refers to organizing work scientifically to achieve more efficiency and cost-effectiveness by replacing old methods with new ones.
[Para 13] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(1) - Termination during pendency of conciliation proceeding - If the demands were not accepted during conciliation proceedings, Section 33(1) will not apply and the employer may alter work conditions, provided it does not affect the conditions applicable before the start of the proceedings.
