Rules for Recruitment - According to the Punjab Development and Panchayat Class-II (Service) Rules, 1974, the recruitment agency cannot waive a prescribed qualifying test in favor of an interview.
The court may find it arbitrary and unreasonable to exclude the written test if the documents suggest inconsistency and obscurity.
[Paras 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15]

Court Jurisdiction - If the court finds that the recruitment process is inconsistent with the prescribed rules, it may declare the action arbitrary and unreasonable.
[Para 15]

Definition of Arbitrariness - The definition of arbitrariness varies based on the facts and circumstances of a particular case.
[Para 1]

Selection Criteria - While oral interviews play a significant factor in the selection process, exclusive selection based on interview leaves room for suspicion and manipulation.
[Paras 9 and 13]
