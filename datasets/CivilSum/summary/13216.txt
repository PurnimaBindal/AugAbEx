Punjab Civil Service (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 12 - Contempt - The Hon'ble Division Bench's operative portion of the order clearly stated that the excessive amount drawn by the petitioner on the basis of his option cannot be recovered.
However, the later part of the Hon'ble Division Bench's direction was only a signal to the respondents to relax the petitioner's case by invoking Rule 12.
The respondent-authority's order that relaxation is not allowed is a direct infringement of the operative portion of the Hon'ble Division Bench's order.
The respondent-authority has been given one more chance to comply with the directions of the Hon'ble Division Bench.
The petitioner is free to approach the Court with a contempt petition again if the orders are not followed.
