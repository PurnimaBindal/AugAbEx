Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Diplomate of National Board Course - Change in examination policy - Plaintiffs nearing the end of their training - The change in policy should not be unreasonable according to the Wednesbury sense - It has been held that there is no public interest in postponing the examination simply because two students enrolled three years ago - In the field of education and medicine, every instrumentality of the State must act reasonably and not arbitrarily or in breach of solemn assurances, even if it requires some financial outlay - Furthermore, the policy matter can be altered by the Board for the next years.
[Paras 14 to 17]
