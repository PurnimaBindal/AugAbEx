The pay scales of Veterinary Assistant Surgeons were equated to those of PCMS Class II doctors following a representation made by the Veterinary Assistant Surgeons.
However, the Order of Single Judge was challenged on the grounds that they did not pass any order equating the pay scale of Veterinary Surgeons with that of Medical Officers.
The appellants had already decided to equate the pay scales of Veterinary Assistant Surgeons and PCSMS Class-II doctors and could not have taken a different stand or made discrimination that could not be justified in a court of law.
The Order of Single Judge was not found to require any interference as it was based upon the facts and proper appreciation of the provisions of the law.
The appeal was dismissed in accordance with Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X.
