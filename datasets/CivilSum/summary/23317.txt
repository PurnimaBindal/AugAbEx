Constitution of India, Articles 310 and 311 - Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 33 - Appointments by ministers/Temporary appointments/Contractual appointments - Appointments made purely at the discretion of the minister and are co-terminus with the tenure of the minister's establishment - Despite being shown as direct and purely temporary appointments, the order of appointment must be read as a whole - Such appointments that do not follow the established rules can be made by the state under Article 310(2) of the Constitution and end when the minister's tenure is terminated - Rule 33 of the Bombay Civil Services Rules does not apply.
