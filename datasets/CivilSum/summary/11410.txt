According to Sections 114(e) and 27 of the Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, a Co-operative Society has the authority to pass a resolution terminating an employee's services.
However, under Section 27, the Deputy Registrar holds the power to rescind such a resolution.
Additionally, Section 114(e) stipulates that the Registrar is empowered to hear an appeal.
