Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16 and 18 - Removal from service - Charge sheet and statement of allegations - These fail to provide details about the alleged misconduct of the petitioner, leaving them without material particulars.
The petitioner is reinstated.
[Para 8]

Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16 and 18 - New Charges - Removal from service - Natural justice - The petitioner is found guilty of a new charge without being given the chance to defend himself against it.
The order of removal from service is quashed.
[Para 15]

Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1958 Rules 16 and 18 - Removal from Service - Evidence - The disciplinary authority did not objectively examine the evidence and wrongfully found the petitioner guilty, based on mere suspicion.
The petitioner is directed to be reinstated with pay and allowances.
