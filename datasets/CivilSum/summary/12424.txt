Constitution of India, Articles 311, 16 and 14 - Revision of pay scales - Disparity - The petitioners requested a writ petition in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to grant them the revised pay scale of Rs. 480-760 w.e.f.
121981 identical to Electricians having two years ITI certificate course, and to grant further revised pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 w.e.f.
111986, as others in all the departments have been given the grade of Rs. 480-760 under the pay scale revision.
It was held that petitioners are entitled to be placed within the scale of Rs. 480-760/- w.e.f.
121981, or the initial date of appointment, whichever is later, and in the scale of Rs. 1200-2040 w.e.f.
111986.
[Paras 3 and 4]
