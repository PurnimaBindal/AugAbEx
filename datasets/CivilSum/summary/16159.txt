Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Police Training College Manual, Clause 310 - Unfair means/Disqualifying from taking examination/Dismissal/Natural justice - Clause 310 of the Manual implies that candidates must have been caught using unfair means - Any decisions regarding disqualification must be made in compliance with natural justice - A verdict of guilt cannot be handed without presenting the delinquent with the appropriate material for rebuttal - Any order enforcing disqualification that results in a penal consequence is subject to the principles of natural justice - Lack of hearing opportunities before passing the order deems it liable for cancellation.
