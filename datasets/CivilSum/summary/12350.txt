According to the PWD Code Rules, Rule 1101(2), a dismissed SDO in the PWD department was reinstated after their dismissal order was set aside on 21297, with the passing of a departmental examination on 30698.
The passing of the examination would be considered from the first time the incumbent had the chance to take it.
Because the petitioner's juniors had been promoted in 1996, the petitioner now has the right to be considered for promotion from the date their juniors were promoted.
As a result, the petitioner's pay must be calculated with consideration of the examination being passed by the petitioner in their first attempt to take it.
