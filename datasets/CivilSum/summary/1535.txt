Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 25F - Reinstatement - Denial of back wages - The tribunal must grant back-wages with reinstatement where termination is illegal and not the fault of the workman.
[Para 8] B Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 25F - Legal Representatives of workman - Ability to claim backwages - Legal Representatives may be granted compensation even after the workman’s death during an industrial dispute, as a claim for backwages is not a personal cause of action.
