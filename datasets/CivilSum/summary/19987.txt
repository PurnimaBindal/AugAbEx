Punjab State Electricity Board Ministerial Services (Class III) Regulations, 1985, Regulation 13 - Time bound promotional pay scale - Scheme for time-bound promotion to a higher pay scale formulated by the Board falls within its power under Section 79(c) of the Electricity Act and Regulation 13 of the Regulations - The Board can prescribe employee pay where not specifically governed by the Act or Rules under Regulation 13.

Punjab State Electricity Board Ministerial Services (Class III) Regulations, 1985, Regulation 13 - Time bound promotional pay scale - Employees are not entitled to double benefit from the Scheme - If a junior employee is fixed in a higher pay by virtue of the Scheme, the senior employee may receive an upgrade in his pay since a junior cannot earn more than a senior in normal circumstances.
