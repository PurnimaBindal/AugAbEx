Article 311 of the Constitution of India concerns punishment, enhancement of punishment, and natural justice.
According to this article, if an authority proposes to enhance the punishment, they must give the delinquent an opportunity to be heard.
In this case, the petitioner was given an opportunity to represent against the proposed enhanced punishment.
However, the punishment order violated the principles of natural justice.
[Para 8]  B Article 311 of the Constitution of India states that opportunity of hearing is a must before any order of punishment is passed.
However, the petitioner was neither supplied with the enquiry report nor given any time to represent against the proposed punishment.
Therefore, the order of punishment was illegal and violative of the principles of natural justice.
