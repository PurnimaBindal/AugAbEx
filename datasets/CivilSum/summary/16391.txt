Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Retirement age - Enhancement - The petitioner, who served as a Lt. General in AFMS, was required to retire at 60 years old according to the government's decision to increase the age of retirement for army personnel.
Nevertheless, the petitioner was excluded from the purview of the circular letter, which was an arbitrary and unconstitutional decision as per Article 14.
While the retirement age of officers including Lt. General in AFMS was extended from 60 years to 62 years, the petitioner was left out and had already retired.
The court ordered the respondents to allow the petitioner to resume duties and work until May 31, 2000, with payment of salary and allowances starting from May 31, 1998.
