Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Estoppel - Benefit of past service on new assignment in other Govt. Deptt.
- If a Govt. servant's selection and appointment is conditional upon resignation from their earlier position and the benefit of their past service is protected for the purposes of length of service, then the authority cannot deny this benefit after the resignation has been accepted - The resignation is considered to have been submitted with all benefits consequent thereof - Even if the orders accepting the resignation were unclear, the authority is still estopped from taking different stands.
[Paras 11, 13 and 15]
