Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rules 133, 134 - Disciplinary action - Judicial review - The decision of the authority must be supported by reasons to exclude any possibility of bias, arbitrariness, discrimination, extraneous consideration, perversity and unreasonableness.
The payment of subsistence allowance for overstayal of leave bars the possibility of charging the employee with unauthorised absence for the same period.
The disciplinary authority is required to record reasons in support of its conclusions even in the absence of requirement by statute.
[Paras 9 to 18 and 28 to 32, 42 to 44]

Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rules 135, 139, 143 - Disciplinary action - Judicial review - The authority may be corrected by courts when its actions violate the rule of law.
If the charge sheet is not served, Rules 135 and 139 mandate the reinstatement of the employee.
[Paras 44]

Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rules 143, 156 - Disciplinary action - Overstayal of leave during suspension - Removal from service - The CRPF officer may be removed from service even for the charge of absence during suspension.
It is for the disciplinary authority to consider the facts and reasons shown by the delinquent.
Failure to consider valid applications for leave extension, supported by medical certificates of a spouse, invalidates the action.
Suspension and subsequent removal orders are quashed, and the employee will be reinstated and awarded consequential relief and 50% back wages.
[Paras 35, 42 to 44 and 48]
