Constitution of India, Articles 310, 311 and 226 - Date of birth - Date of superannuation - Delay - Estoppel - The petitioner provided a certificate of his own date of birth and validated it by signing each page of his service book where his date of birth is recorded, which he did annually - Despite submitting three different certificates showing a different date of birth as compared to the one he provided at the time of entry into service, the authorities did not rely on them - The conclusions drawn by the competent authority regarding the facts are reasonable and do not require intervention - However, a directive was issued to calculate his pension and other benefits.
