Constitution of India, Article 311 - After completing the initial probationary period, a probationer may continue working, but this does not mean automatic confirmation.
[Para 9]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - A probationer may be terminated for unsuitability for the position, but the principles of natural justice should be followed, including providing evidence and informing the employee of any issues.
If the termination order is arbitrary and against these principles, it may be quashed.
In this case, the petitioner was not informed about any defects in their work or performance, and no material in their service record indicated such issues.
Therefore, the termination order was deemed arbitrary and quashed.
