Bombay Police (Punishment & Appeals) Rules, 1958, Rule 3(1)(a-1) - Compulsory retirement - Misconduct - The petitioner, a female constable, had a quarrel with her nephew's wife, which was unrelated to her job.
She was acquitted in the criminal case related to this incident, and her involvement did not constitute any misconduct.
Therefore, imposing the extreme penalty of compulsory retirement is unjustified.
