Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952), Section 7-A - Enquiry by an Authority under Section 7-A - If the employer or employee fails to produce the required record, the Authority may pass the order on basis of material on record - However, the Authority under the ESI Act cannot order detention in civil prison for failure to produce records.
