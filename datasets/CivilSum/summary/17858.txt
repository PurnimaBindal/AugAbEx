The petitioner, a government servant who joined the Army, is entitled to count the period of military service towards seniority, increments, promotion, and pension.
Rule 5 virtually incorporates the concept of Next Below Rule known under the PCS Rules Benefits admissible under the Statutory rules given to petitioner.
The entire period of service rendered by the petitioner in the Army is treated as if it had been spent in the service of the Deptt of Coop.
Societies.
Petitioner's pay, increment, and seniority have been fixed on these premises.
The underlying idea behind Instructions is that a temporary Govt. employee who had joined the Army and in whose case the lien has not been retained, should be absorbed on a post for which he is qualified on his return from the Army.
The petitioner was actually absorbed on the post which he was holding immediately prior to his joining the Army.
The question of absorbing him on any other post for which he may have fulfilled the qualifications did not arise.
