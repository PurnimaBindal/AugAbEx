Constitution of India, Article 227 - Dismissal - Reasonable opportunity - A delinquent employee is entitled to a copy of the enquiry report before the disciplinary authority reaches a conclusion about his guilt or innocence.
Failing to provide the report deprives the employee of his reasonable opportunity to state his case before the authority and is therefore unjust.
The Labour Court found that the enquiry was neither fair nor proper and set aside the dismissal order.
The petition is dismissed but with no interference.
[Paras 2, 4 and 5]  B.
Natural Justice - Enquiry Report - Non-supply of - Prejudice - An employee is entitled to see the enquiry report before the disciplinary authority imposes a penalty that could lead to the employee's dismissal.
This is based on principles of natural justice as the employee does not get any right to place his view point before any punishment is decided to be imposed upon him if he does not receive a copy of the report.
The Court found that the employee's right to receive a copy of the report was essential for fairness and when the management failed to provide a copy, it resulted in failure of justice.
Constitution of India, Article 227 is followed according to 1993(5) SLR 532.
[Paras 4 and 5]  C. Enquiry report - The right of an employee to receive a copy of the enquiry report is an integral part of the departmental enquiry.
