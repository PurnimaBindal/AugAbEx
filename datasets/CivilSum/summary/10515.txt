The appointment of a Lecturer against a leave vacancy must be made in accordance with relevant rules and regulations as well as adherence to equality clauses in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Respondents are required to give all eligible persons equal consideration by advertising the post and considering all applicants.
The petitioner has no right to claim subsequent regularisation of service based on her appointment against a leave vacancy that occurred in either 1987 or 1994.
[Para 4] B.
A person appointed as a Lecturer against leave vacancy after the issue of the circular dated 841991 does not acquire the right to be regularised in service, even if they were appointed on the recommendation of a duly constituted Selection Committee.
The petitioner has no legal right to be regularised in service as of 3131994.
