Constitution of India, Article 226 - Petitioner No. 1 requested the respondent to grant affiliation for starting Law College at Faridabad and to make admissions for the academic Session 2000-2001 - Petitioner received "Approval" from the Bar Counsel of India to begin the Law College and "No Objection Certificate" from Government of Haryana, as desired by the Respondent - However, the required affiliation was not granted.
[Paras 1, 2, 3 and 4] The Second Inspection Committee, by considering all aspects in accordance with guidelines provided by the MD University, recommended granting provisional affiliation.
The Executive Council's approach is not constructive and positive, and the impugned order does not reflect a reasonable cause for rejecting the request for affiliation of the Law College.
The plea that the Academic Council has taken a decision not to grant affiliation to any private or Govt Institution is not seriously accepted.
Therefore, the petition is allowed and the impugned order dated August 07, 2000 projected to have been passed by the Vice Chancellor is quashed.
