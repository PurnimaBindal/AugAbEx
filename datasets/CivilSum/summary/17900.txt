According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311, along with the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, the Punjab Municipal Corporation Services (Recruitment & Condition) Rules, 1978, Rule 11 and the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1970, a demotion must be ordered by the Government's competent authority.
As such, a demotion order made by the Commissioner of the Corporation is not sustainable.
