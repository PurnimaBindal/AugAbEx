The Industrial Disputes Act, 1948, Section 25-F - Retrenchment/Termination/Loss of confidence/Enquiry - The workman was retrenched by the management on the grounds of repeated embezzlement of money - The management had lost confidence in the workman - The retrenchment order was not preceded by an enquiry, therefore, amounting to punishment without enquiry - However, the management was able to successfully prove its case of loss of confidence before the Labour Court - As a result, no injustice was done to the petitioner and as per Article 226 of the Constitution, the petition was dismissed.
