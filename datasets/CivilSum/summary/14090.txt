Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - A.C.Rs.
- Gradation in ACRs - Downgrading - Natural justice - Administrative instruction required in recording ACRs and grading - Officials must be notified if their performance is lower than previous and reporting must include reasoning - Reviewing authority cannot reduce grading without seeing work for at least 90 days and providing reasonable opportunity - AIR 1996 SC 1661 followed.
