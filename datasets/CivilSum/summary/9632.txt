The action of the Superintendent of Police in not sending information to the District Magistrate despite the absence of any record showing that the petitioner is accused of having committed the criminal offence of which the accused officer has been accused is not violative of Rule 1638(1), as the rule compels the Superintendent of Police to send information only when a police officer commits an offence "in connection with his official relations with the public," which is not evidenced in this case.
[Para 7]  B It is not necessary for a Superintendent of Police to report every criminal offence committed by a police officer to the District Magistrate, as per Rules 1638(1) and 1624(ii) of the Punjab Police Rules of 1934, and information to be supplied only when the officer commits an offence "in connection with his official relations with the public."
[Para 8]  C A police officer who allegedly commits a criminal offence may face departmental action and prosecution in a criminal court, as per Rule 1624(ii) of the Punjab Police Rules of 1934.
[Para 8]  D The provisions of Rule 1628 of the Punjab Police Rules of 1934, Section 7 do not violate the provisions of Section 7 and fall within the rule making authority's powers.
