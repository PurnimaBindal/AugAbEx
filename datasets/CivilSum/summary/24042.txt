Under the amended Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, if a Central Government company or undertaking, a specified controlled industry, or any industry carried out by or under the authority of the Central Government is included, then the Central Government will be the `appropriate Government'.
The Government of the State in which that establishment situates will be the `appropriate Government' otherwise.
B.
A notification under Section 10(1) of the CLRA Act prohibiting employment of contract labour must be issued by the appropriate Government after consulting the Central Advisory Board or the State Advisory Board.
The notification dated 9.12.1976 issued by the Central Government is not valid as it does not meet the mandatory requirements of Section 10.
C. Neither Section 10 nor any other provision of the CLRA Act provides for automatic absorption of contract labour when a notification is issued prohibiting employment of contract labour.
The principal employer cannot be required to order absorption of the contract labour working in the concerned establishment.
D. The criteria for determining if the Central Government is the appropriate Government within the meaning of the CLRA Act is that the industry must be carried out only or under the authority of the Central Government.
This is a separate concept from the one under Article 12 of the Constitution.
E. While a workman engaged in an establishment would have a direct relationship with the principal employer as a servant of master, contract labour cannot always be treated as direct workmen of the principal employer.
