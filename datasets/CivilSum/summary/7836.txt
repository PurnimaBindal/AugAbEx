Civil Procedure Code 1908, Section 87B - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19(1)(F), 363, 366(22) - Rulers - Special Treatment - The constitutionality of special treatment given to Rulers of Indian States by Section 87B of Code is upheld as they form a distinct class and discrimination between ex-Rulers and citizens is justified considering their historical and legislative background.

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Temporary Injunction - Grant of - Prior to the grant of a temporary injunction, the court must be convinced of the prima facie existence of the right and its infringement, irreparable harm, and balance of convenience, and such actions should be seen as a supplement, with the court refraining from making determinations on the merits of the case.
