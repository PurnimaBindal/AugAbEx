Article 21 and 136 of the Constitution of India - A Police Officer exceeding his rights by handcuffing an undertrial - A High Court order for an adverse entry in the officer's service record - The officer must be given an opportunity of hearing before making the adverse entry.
[Para 4]

Articles 21, 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - A Police Officer handcuffing and parading an undertrial - High Court ordering the Officer to pay Rs. 10,000 to the prisoner - Officer not personally liable as he acted within official limits - State instructed to pay the damages.
