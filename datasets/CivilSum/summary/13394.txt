Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Admission - B.Tech.
Course - Whether regulations introduced after students' admission are applicable to them?
- New regulations introduced from the academic year 1994-95 - Some candidates admitted in 1993-94 were kept back in terms of new regulations - It was ruled that candidates admitted before the academic year 1994-95 will be treated according to earlier regulations.
