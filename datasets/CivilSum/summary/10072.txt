Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Life Insurance Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 39(1) and (2) - Discipline proceedings - Removal from service - The enquiry office did not supply the documents relied upon by the delinquent nor allowed him to inspect them - The order of removal was unreasoned and non-speaking - The order violates the principles of natural justice and Regulation 39(1) of the Life Insurance Corporation Regulations - The impugned order has been declared illegal.
