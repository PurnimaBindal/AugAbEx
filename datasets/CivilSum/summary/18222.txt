Constitution of India, Article 14 - Penalties, Appeal and Review Rules, Part-II of the MD University Service and Conduct Rules, Clauses2(A)(ii) - The major penalty under the rules includes stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect.
If the punishing authority intended to impose a minor penalty, stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect would be improper.
Therefore, the penalty was reduced to stoppage of two increments without cumulative effect.
