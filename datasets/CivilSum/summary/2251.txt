According to the Constitution of India and the All India Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefits) Rules, 1958, Rule 16(3), an IAS Officer can be compulsorily retired in public interest after they turn 50, based on recommendations from a High Level Committee.
In this case, a Review Committee found that the petitioner was inefficient and indifferent, and therefore unfit to continue in service.
The petitioner had already completed enough years of service to receive full pension benefits under Rule 16(3).
Compulsory premature retirement of a government servant is allowed if it is deemed necessary in the public interest.
(Paras 8, 9 and 10)
