Orissa General Financial Rules, Rule 65 - Age - The respondent submitted a horoscope indicating their date of birth as 27-11-1928 and signed an acceptance of that date in the service roll.
A representation was made in 1986 claiming a different date of birth, but it was rejected.
Even if the alternative date of birth (27-11-1938) was assigned, the respondent would still have been overage by 5 years at the time of joining service.
Therefore, the rejection of the representation was not illegal and did not go against principles of natural justice.
Personal hearing was not required for dismissal of the representation.
