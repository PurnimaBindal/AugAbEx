The Army Group Insurance Scheme, 1979, Rules 10, 11 and 43 and the Insurance Act, 1938 specify the entitlements of various parties in case of an employee's death - As per Rule 43, the parents of the deceased can only claim the amount under the scheme if the employee passed away without leaving behind the wife/husband and children - Rule 10 mandates that the nomination be made in favour of the spouse or children if the employee is married, thereby excluding the parents' claim - The Insurance Act, 1938 does not apply to the Army Group Insurance Scheme, and hence parents cannot claim as class I heirs.
