Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The appellant, who worked as a conductor, failed to issue tickets to passengers who paid Rs. 13 - A fair trial was conducted during the inquiry, and the appellant received a full opportunity to defend his case - The checking staff recorded statements of passengers who did not receive tickets, and the appellant did not deny the fact that the checking took place - The appellant's fault was established - The order of termination was not illegal.
