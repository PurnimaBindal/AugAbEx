Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Challenged - Petitioners contested the selection process for the position of Learner Binders as it was not based on candidates' qualifications and appeared arbitrary - Interviews completed within two days which was not enough time for each candidate to demonstrate their abilities and intellect - The selection process was a farce and candidates were not given a fair chance to prove themselves - Court quashed the selection and directed for a fresh and lawful selection process to be conducted.
[Paras 8 to 10]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Quasi-judicial - Members of the Selection Committee are responsible for performing the quasi-judicial function of selecting candidates - Focus should be on the candidates' merit and suitability for the job - Merit must be judged on the basis of efficiency - Selection process must be fair and transparent and the conduct of committee members should not be questionable - If members of the Selection Committee have pre-determined their choice of candidates, the selection process would be a sham and would go against the principles of impartiality and merit.
[Paras 8 and 9]
