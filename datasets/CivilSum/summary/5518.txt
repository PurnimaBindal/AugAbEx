The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, outline the criteria for appointment and eligibility qualifications.
In the case of a candidate for the position of Lecturer in Women Polytechnic, the petitioner did not meet the proscribed qualification of a three years diploma course, which resulted in the rejection of their candidacy.
Despite obtaining an interim order by misleading the Court and relying on superseded rules, the petitioner is not entitled to equitable relief if statutory provisions are clear.
Furthermore, no order should be passed in favor of a litigant in misplaced sympathy, as it can supersede public interest.
The process of selection was challenged after a delay of seven months, and therefore, no relief can be granted.
