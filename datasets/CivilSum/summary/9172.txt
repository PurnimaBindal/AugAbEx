Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Seniority - When considering for promotion, the Promotion Committee must consider candidates six times the posts available for promotion.
If the petitioner does not fall within the consideration zone, then they cannot raise any legitimate grievance against the promotions made.

Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Findings of fact - The findings of the lower appellate court are based on the appreciation of evidence on record and have not been shown to be perverse or contrary to evidence.
Therefore, there is no reason to upset the findings of fact.
