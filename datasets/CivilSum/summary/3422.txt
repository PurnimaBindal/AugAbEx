The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 and the Service Selection Board Act of 1991, Section 14, outline the appointment and selection process for the post of Master.
The petitioner in this case did not rank high in merit and therefore was not selected for the position.
The petitioner's challenge to the allocation of 20 marks for the viva voce and the failure to constitute a Selection Committee in accordance with Section 14 of the Act cannot be entertained since they took part in the selection process and are therefore estopped from challenging the criteria for selection.
The formula used to ascertain the percentage of marks applied uniformly to all and the members of the Selection Committee, which consist of two members from the Board and two experts, cannot be contested.
