Constitution of India, Articles, 14 and 16 - The Punjab Co-operative Audit Department (Ministerial) Class III Service Rules 1972, Rules 5 and 10(f) - Appointment by promotion - Seniority in the feeder cadre - Ad hoc service received in another department cannot be taken into consideration - Instructions allowing ad hoc service to count if appointed by selection through regular selection agency and completed more than one year in another Government Department before regularization in the present department, subject to good work and conduct during that period - Efficiency and experience on some other post in another department may not be mandatory for the current role - Promotion by adjusting seniority benefit given for ad hoc service in other Department is deemed illegal - Private respondent's promotion and seniority nullified.
