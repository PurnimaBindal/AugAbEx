Respondent No. 2 Mr. VK Kaushal Advocate argued that there are specific regulations and laws that dictate the selection and approval of appointments for the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board.
Specifically, Section 11 of the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board (Services) Regulations, 1982 states that the selection made by the high powered selection committee cannot be refused approval lightly by the government.
There must be compelling reasons before a conclusion can be drawn that the selection was irregular.
Furthermore, in the case of appointments, the approval of the Ministry of Finance is required only when new posts are created, not for appointments against the posts already in existence.
Finally, it is stated that selecting more candidates than the number of posts requisitioned does not necessarily warrant the rejection of the entire selection.
