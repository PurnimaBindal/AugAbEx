As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the concept of equal pay for equal work must be followed.
In the case of the petitioners who were appointed as Diesel Pump Attendants in the pay scale of 350-500 in the Haryana Roadways, it was noted that the qualifications and pay scales of the Diesel Pump Attendants and Battery Attendants were identical, and that the duties of both were alleged to be equivalent.
However, the Battery Attendants were given a pay scale of Rs. 420-700 later on while the petitioners did not receive any such hike.
Since the facts were uncontroverted, the petitioners were also entitled to the pay scale of Rs. 420-700 from the same date.
