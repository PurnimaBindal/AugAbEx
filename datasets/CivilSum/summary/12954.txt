Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Panchayat (Gram Panchayat Administration) Rules, 2004, Rule 48(3) - Appointment - A petitioner and other candidates underwent a written test for the position of "panchayat karmee" - During the test, a candidate named `G' achieved the first rank, while the petitioner secured the second position - Subsequently, `G' was found ineligible according to Rule 48(3) of the West Bengal Panchayat (Gram Panchayat Administration) Rules - The authority cancelled the candidature of `G' and initiated a fresh recruitment process - However, the order to cancel the entire selection process was deemed arbitrary and illegal - The authority was directed to reconstruct the panel and appoint the first empanelled candidate.
