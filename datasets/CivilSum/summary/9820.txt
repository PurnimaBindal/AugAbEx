According to Section 3 Entry.
21 of Schedule of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, an establishment rendering services can be classified as a shop.
A photo studio also falls under this definition.
According to the definition of "shop" under the Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1960, any premises where services are provided to customers or where business is conducted falls under this category.
Since the petitioners are providing services, their establishment can be referred to as a "shop".
