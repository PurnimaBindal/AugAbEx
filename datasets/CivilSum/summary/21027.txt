Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of service - Temporary employee - Probation - The appellant, who was given temporary appointment and put on probation for one year, had deficiencies in her service pointed out after an evaluation of her work, and was given an opportunity to show improvement.
Because of no improvement, her services were terminated by giving one month's notice.
The plea that her services couldn't be terminated without affording opportunity of hearing, and without complying with the provision of Article 311, was held to be not sustainable.
As a temporary employee, the employer had the right to terminate her services at any time during probation and otherwise as well.
