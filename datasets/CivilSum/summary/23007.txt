Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 142 - Indian Railway Medical Department (Assistant Medical Officers, Class II) Recruitment Rules, 1977 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - Determination of seniority in a cadre must be based on the rules when they exist, or else the principles enunciated in Direct Recruits Engineering Officers case should be followed by the Supreme Court.
Seniority cannot be counted for those ad hoc appointees who did not regularize through UPSC examination as per Government of India letter dated 2151966, even after being regularized pursuant to orders of Supreme Court in AK Jain's case.
The decision in Dr. Haque's case "we direct that seniority of direct recruits - Both outsiders and insiders should be determined according to the dates of their regular appointments through UPSC" cannot be the guiding principle.
There is no inconsistency between the judgments in Dr. Haque's case and that in the decision of that Tribunal in Dr. Srinivasulu's case nor any infirmity in grant of benefit of ad hoc service in Dr. Lalita Rao.
