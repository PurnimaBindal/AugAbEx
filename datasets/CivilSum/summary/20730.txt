Constitution of India, Articles 98(2) and 98(3) - Lok Sabha Secretariat (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1955, Rule 4 - Recruitment/Deputation - According to Rule 4(1)(b), deputation is one of the ways in which recruitment can be done - It is within the Speaker of Lok Sabha's authority to depute IAS officers to high positions and to engage experienced officers on a contract basis - The 70% quota for in-service officers' promotion is reasonable and in the best interest of the service, but it only applies from the date that the Rules come into effect - Officers that were brought in before that date will be replaced by promotions for in-service officers as they become fit for promotion in their 75% quota.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9] B Lok Sabha Secretariat (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1955, Rule 5 - Promotion - Qualifications - Experience - The rule-making authority has the power to set conditions for promotion - To ensure competence and efficiency in the service, the promotion of IAS officers to higher positions requires 17 years of service - The rule is not arbitrary, ultra vires, or a violation of Article 14 of the Constitution.
