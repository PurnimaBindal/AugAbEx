The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 14A - Lien/Substantive appointment on other post - No more than one employee can be substantively appointed to the same permanent post at the same time, and an employee cannot be substantively appointed to a post on which another employee holds a lien - A government employee will no longer have lien on a post upon being substantively appointed to another permanent post.
[Paras 20 and 21]
The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice - Selection Grade - Revocation of Selection Grade without giving the opportunity to be heard is a violation of the principles of natural justice.
[Paras 22 to 24].
