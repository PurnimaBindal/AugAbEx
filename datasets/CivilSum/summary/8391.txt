Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - Award of - When a member of the armed forces is on casual leave for a certain purpose, the leave is considered to be on duty - However, if they are involved in an accident while on casual leave and are medically invalidated, the injuries are considered to be a result of army service - The respondents are instructed to provide the petitioner with a disability pension.
