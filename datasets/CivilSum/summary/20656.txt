Ex gratia payment - Scheme for granting Ex-gratia payment to Special Protection Group (SPG) Personnel - The appellant received a permanent partial disablement due to a motor accident when traveling from staff quarters to the South Block for official duty in the SPG vehicle provided for that purpose - The accident occurred during his employment and performing actual VIP security duty - The concerned authorities must follow the same liability test as that of the Workmen's Compensation Act and treat the circular liberally instead of taking a rigid and pedantic approach - The expression "actual VIP security duty" in the circular has the meaning same as "in the course of employment" in the Workmen's Compensation Act - The appellant is entitled to an ex-gratia payment of Rs. 50,000 in accordance with the circular dated 13.6.1986.
