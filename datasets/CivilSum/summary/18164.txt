According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, as well as the West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education (Examination) Regulations of 1999 and the WB Board of Secondary Education Act of 1963, a petitioner who failed to appear in all papers of an annual examination cannot question a subsequent circular that changed the structure of the supplementary examination, which the petitioner claims he was entitled to take according to past practice.
This is because the petitioner had already taken the examination conducted in accordance with the circular.
[Paras 6 and 9]
