Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Probation - Termination - Services of Petitioners appointed on probation by selection committee terminated - Promoters argue that termination order is a punishment and one month's notice was not given - Respondents allege that petitioners caused indiscipline and were appointed on temporary post - Alleged misconduct of petitioners was a motive, not foundation of termination order, thus making it a case of termination similicitor - No enquiry was held or findings given, so impugned order cannot be said to be punitive or casting stigma - According to the court, it's not mandatory to hold a regular inquiry in such cases - Petition dismissed accordingly.
