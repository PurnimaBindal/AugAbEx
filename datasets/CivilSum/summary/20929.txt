As per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sch.
2 Item 3 and Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 5, Rule 15, termination can occur after natural justice is followed.
A notice should be served, and in case a workman is absent without informing, disciplinary action can be taken.
In a specific case where a workman was arrested in a murder case and his wife received a notice regarding regular inquiry, she was allowed to participate in the proceeding.
There was no violation of the principle of natural justice, and the termination was held proper.
Moreover, as the respondent had put in twelve years of service, the appellant was ordered to pay an ex-gratia amount of Rs 3 lac.
