The validity of increasing the reservation quota in promotion from 18% to 21% in favor of scheduled castes under the UP Public Services (Reservation for SC, ST, and BC) Act, 1994, has been concluded by the Supreme Court, according to Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226.
The petitioner has little room for argument on this matter as previous court decisions have deemed reservation to be valid, leading to the dismissal of the petition.
