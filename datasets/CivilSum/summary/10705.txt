As per the appointment order, the probation period for the appellant was a minimum of two years, and during this period, the appellant was given increments.
However, the probation was later extended by one year.
The Medical Council of India Rules do not stipulate automatic confirmation, and the period of two years cannot be considered the maximum period.
The note that was relied upon is merely a self-appraisal.
It was held that there should be a positive order of confirmation, and for inference of deemed confirmation, there must be a specific rule prohibiting the continuation of services of an employee on probation beyond a specific year.
