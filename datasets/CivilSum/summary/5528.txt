Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Misconduct - Departmental enquiry - The petitioner was dismissed after being found guilty of consuming liquor while on duty and being absent on multiple occasions - The enquiry was properly conducted with opportunity of hearing given before dismissal - The High Court cannot act as an appellate Court - Consuming liquor and absenting from duty constitute a grave misconduct for a member of a disciplined force.
