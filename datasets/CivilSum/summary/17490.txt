Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - Haryana Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations, 1972, Regulation 9(A)(I) - The petitioner, who previously served as a Superintendent of Police in the State of Haryana, sought voluntary retirement and was later appointed as a Member and then as Chairman of the Haryana Public Service Commission.
He claimed pension under Regulation 9(A)(I) of Haryana Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations, 1972, which was rejected.
However, the court held that Regulation 9(A)(I) does not have a condition against granting pension to those who previously held a post in the Service of the State Government.
Thus, the petitioner is entitled to the pension under the said Regulation along with 12% interest per annum from the date pension became due.
