Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - The petitioner was initially appointed as Branch Manager with the company, but was later transferred to perform the duties of an Assistant Divisional Officer due to his unsatisfactory performance.
It was held that this transfer did not amount to a reduction in rank since both posts were part of the same cadre.
The employer has the right to determine the suitability of its officers and can post them to an equivalent position if their performance is not up to the mark.
