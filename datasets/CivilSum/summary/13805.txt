Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment on daily wages basis - It is not necessary that compassionate appointment always results in regular appointment - As per rules, ordinarily one would not be entitled to a job unless regular vacancies are available and duly advertised - Compassionate appointment is not a right and the goal is not to provide a job post to a deceased family member - If the petitioner was offered a job on a daily wage basis, no injustice would have been done to her for which a direction to offer regular appointment may be sought from the writ court.
