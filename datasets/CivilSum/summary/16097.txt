Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-F and 2 (00) - Retrenchment - Natural Justice Misconduct - Termination on the basis of absence without leave is not retrenchment simplicitor - Any termination without adhering to principles of natural justice will not be justified and be liable to be quashed.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punishment/Absence from duty/Quantum of punishment - Termination - Two-day absence from duty is too short a period for termination, according to the 1982(1) SCC 645 judgment.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Misconduct/Promotion- Absence from duty - Termination on account of absence from duty for two days while working on the lower post is not justified when the employee has been promoted - Any mistake before the promotion cannot be taken into account for subsequent termination due to absence without leave - Order quashed.
