Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Tamil Nadu Statistics Service Rules, Rules 3 and 8 - Promotion - Enquiry - Double jeopardy - Stoppage of increments without cumulative effect - Promotions during the period of continuation of punishment of stoppage of increments - If promotion is made on the basis of merit and ability, the employee is not entitled to promotion during the period of continuation of punishment - Granting promotion during such period would reward misconduct - Excluding the petitioner from consideration during the punishment period does not violate Articles 14, 16, and 21 or constitute double jeopardy - Promotions cannot be granted with retrospective effect even after punishment has expired.
