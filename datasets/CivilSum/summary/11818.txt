Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 13(1)(b) - Suspension - Criminal proceedings - Rule 13(1)(b) permits the suspension of an employee with criminal proceedings pending against them, even by a subordinate authority designated by the government.

Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Competent Authority - Suspension - The burden of proof falls on the person disputing the competence of the authority ordering suspension - The presumption of validity regarding official acts done in the regular course is rebuttable - Sufficient evidence must be provided to defeat the presumption under section 114 of the Evidence Act for the order to be invalidated.
