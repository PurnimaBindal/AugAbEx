Section 3(5) of the Haryana Private Colleges (Taking Over of the Management) Act, 1978 states that an election of a college's governing body is valid if it is held in the presence of the government's nominee, who must also inform the government about the validity of the election.
Although the university was also informed to send its nominees, it failed to do so.
Despite these circumstances, the governing body's election is deemed invalid, as per the government's instructions, and the state is obligated to turn over the college and its property to the appellant.
