Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 5 and 8 - Punishment - Doctrine of Proportionality - Removal from service - Delinquent charged with absence from duty - Despite 22 years of unblemished service, punishing authority failed to consider service record when imposing punishment - Court deems punishment of removal from service and deprivation of pension and retiral benefits to be arbitrary and unconscionable - Punishment of removal from service overturned, replaced with compulsory retirement.
