SK Bhanot, DAG, Punjab, argues that Constitution of India, Article 14 and 311, are violated in regards to charge sheet and departmental proceedings.
The charge sheet was issued in 1995 for alleged irregularities committed in 1982 and came to notice in 2/1984.
No explanation was given for the delay.
The respondent believes that the delinquent would not be able to recall the events from 14 years ago and would be handicapped in their defense.
Therefore, it would not be just and fair to allow belated proceedings.
As a result, the charge-sheet has been quashed and the petition has been allowed.
