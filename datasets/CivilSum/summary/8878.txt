Plaintiff, a peon who was convicted of a criminal case for 10/15 days with a fine of Rs. 300, was terminated without any show cause notice or any opportunity given to him.
The authority did not consider any other form of punishment and did not use their own discretion in determining whether it was suitable to retain him.
This order of termination is invalid, and the appeal of the State was dismissed.
