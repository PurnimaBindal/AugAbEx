Jammu and Kashmir State Board of School Education Act, 1975, Section 13(4) - Placement of post of legal Assistant from grade of 6500-10500 to 5500-9000 - Additional post of Senior legal Assistant in grade of 6500 -10500 created - Committee recommends pay scale of 6500-10500 for post of Legal Assistant - The petitioners' designation changed from Senior Assistant to Legal Assistant based on their performance in legal section and to enable them to produce record and appear in court in absence of counsel.
Even though it was argued that only professional Degree in law is required, the court held that since the petitioners were directed to perform duties of legal Assistants and their designation was changed, the respondents cannot turn around and claim that their degree is not professional.
The order is violative of principle of equality, is discriminatory, and will deprive the petitioners of their right to have equal pay for equal work.
As such, the order is set aside.
[Paras 10, 12, 13]
