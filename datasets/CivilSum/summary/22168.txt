The Railway administration sets a specific cutoff mark for unreserved, OBC, SC, and ST candidates in the appointment of a 'Gangman'.
The candidates must have the requisite educational qualification and must pass the written test, interview, and physical test.
The fixation of the cutoff mark was rational and within the appellants' jurisdiction to decide.
Candidates who participated in the selection process cannot question the procedure.
[Paras 10, 11, and 18]

Unfilled vacancies designated for reserved category candidates do not guarantee appointment irrespective of general category candidates' qualification and performance.
General candidates must also fulfill the eligibility clause, including the cutoff mark.
It is up to the appellant to decide whether to dereserve or carry forward the posts.
[Paras 13 and 15]
