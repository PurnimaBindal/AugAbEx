Punjab Civil Service Rules.
Volume II, Appendix I - Family Pension Scheme 1964, Clauses 3 & 4 - Petitioner was denied family pension on the ground that the deceased did not complete five years of service.
However, the rules do not mention any such condition for the grant of pension.
The court held that the rejection is liable to be nullified as the petitioner satisfies all requirements.
[Para 7] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Res judicata - The court dismissed a previous petition by the mother of the petitioner, giving the petitioner liberty to claim pension.
The court held that the earlier petition would not operate as res judicata, as permission to make a claim was granted to the petitioner by a Division Bench, and the current petition was filed by a different person.
[Para 8] C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - The court held that in matters concerning pension, the cause of action is a continuous one, and delay and laches cannot be pleaded to defeat the cause of pension.
