According to Section 126-A of the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, regarding Retiral benefits, the Head Office has a legal obligation to assess government dues two years before the retirement date but no later than eight months prior to superannuation.
The respondents have been instructed to release the gratuity and other retiral benefits that are owed to the petitioner, regardless of whether they are empowered to recover the amount by refixation of salary after the petitioner has superannuated.
