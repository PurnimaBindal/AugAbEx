The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 govern the concept of natural justice.
If a departmental candidate from another department is directly appointed, they must be repatriated after four years of completing their probation period without extension.
If charges are leveled against them without holding any inquiry, the action of the punishing authority is not considered legal or fair.
The order of the authority is quashed, and a cost of Rs. 5000/- is imposed.
