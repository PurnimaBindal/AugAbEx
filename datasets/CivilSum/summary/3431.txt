The Haryana Financial Commissioner's Office (Group B) Service Rules, 1986, Rule 9 states that promotion from Stamp Auditor to Chief Stamp Auditor is based on seniority-cum-merit and a senior person cannot be ignored without proper reason.
The competent authority must give reasons for ignoring the senior in preference to the junior and this situation only arises if the senior is found ineligible for promotion based on their service record.
This rule is in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
