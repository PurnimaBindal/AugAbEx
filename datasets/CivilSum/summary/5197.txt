Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Labour Court - Jurisdiction - Whether Labour Court can interfere with order of punishment when misconduct stands proved in departmental enquiry - The Labour Court now has the jurisdiction to assess whether the punishment given was necessary for the type of misconduct attributed to the workman, following the insertion of Section 11A of the Act.
[Paras 10, 13, 14]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award - Writ jurisdiction - Scope of - Single Judge setting aside order of Labour Court without concluding that there is error apparent on face of record - The High Court should not sit in appeal and make its own conclusions, except when the finding is perverse, not founded on material on record or is found on irrelevant consideration or by ignoring relevant considerations.
[Paras 4, 22]
