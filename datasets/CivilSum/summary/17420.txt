Assistant Linesmen and Sub Station Attendants are ineligible for appointment to the posts of Junior Engineer Grade II (Electrical), according to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
However, they are still eligible for promotion to higher positions such as Linemen and Sub Station Operators, with further avenues for advancement.
As a result, the recruitment process for Junior Engineers was limited to the higher category of linemen, which excludes the promotion pathways of Assistant Linemen and Meter Readers.
Appeals were dismissed.
