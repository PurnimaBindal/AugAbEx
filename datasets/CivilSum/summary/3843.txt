Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Rules under Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Act, 1930, Rule 4A - Extension of service - Rule 4A allows for an extension of service to a teacher appointed by a board after the age of 60 up to the age of 65, provided the teacher is fit and alert - an amendment in 1992 imposes a condition that if a teacher opts for the pre-1986 pay scale, extension can be given on a yearly basis, whereas, if a teacher opts for the 1990 scale, he or she will have to retire at the age of 60 years - the division bench held that those who opt for the new scale may retain it and go beyond 60 years of age on extension until 65 years - in 1998, the department passed orders directing the teachers concerned to withdraw the option of the scale of pay - the petitioners claim to enjoy the revised pay scale and also the right of extension of service up to 65 years.
They want to have it both ways and are challenging the changes they previously agreed to - the state's attempt to bring parity in the terms of service for both groups of teachers cannot be considered unreasonable - the petition is dismissed.
