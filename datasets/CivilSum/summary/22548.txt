Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Admission - A petition was filed seeking Suspension of Proceedings of University which has nominated two Representatives to serve on Selection Committee for admissions to College in Question.
The High Court granted an interim stay and an appeal was made against it.
The Supreme Court declined to interfere with the High Court orders but it made it clear that admissions would be made strictly in accordance with the ratio laid in (1993) 4 SCC 112.
