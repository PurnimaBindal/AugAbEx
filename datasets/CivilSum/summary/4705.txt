Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - FCI (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 54 - Double jeopardy - Misconduct - Delinquent employee causing financial loss - Stoppage of increments and recovery of amount of loss as punishment for misconduct - Recovery of amount not considered punishment for double jeopardy - Employer has the right to impose punishment and recover the loss simultaneously.
