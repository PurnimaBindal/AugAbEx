Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2K and 2A - Dismissal - Alternative remedy - Petitioner, who is a workman, is aggrieved by dismissal order - Raising industrial dispute in Writ Petition - Court holds that an efficacious alternative remedy is available to the Petitioner under Industrial Disputes Act - Petitioner must avail the remedy under the Act before invoking extraordinary Writ Jurisdiction under Article 226 - The forum created under the Act should be approached when seeking relief against termination on the ground of violation of Section 25-F. [Paras 7, 12 to 15]
