The appointment made without any application by the petitioner and without following the selection procedure does not allow him to hold the post, and it is not only violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution but is fraudulent.
[Para 47]
The right to consideration for appointment/promotion is not just a statutory right but a fundamental right according to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution.
[Paras 27]
According to Article 226 of the Constitution, the court can use its writ jurisdiction to enforce the performance of a statutory duty by public bodies, provided that the person can prove they have a legal right to insist on such performance.
The person's right must exist as a precondition to invoke the writ jurisdiction.
