UP State Universities Act, 1973 (As amended by UP State Universities (Amendment) Act, 1992), Sections 13, 31(1) and (10) - Appointment - Selection - Substantive appointments as lecturers - Ad hoc appointees - Eligibility for substantive appointment as lecturer requires a good academic record and attainment of certain marks in Bachelor Degree and Intermediate Examination, as well as passing a qualifying comprehensive test - Selection process includes advertising of the posts - Executive Committee of the University was correct in not recommending ad hoc appointees for substantive appointment as lecturers.
