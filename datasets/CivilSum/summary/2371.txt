In accordance with the Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16, a circular formulated by the State cannot be considered a statutory rule and therefore any appointment in violation of the recruitment rules would be considered void.
Casual workers cannot be described as full-time employees and back-door appointments cannot be encouraged.
The petition of the DDT spray workers seeking regularization was rejected on the grounds that as per their engagement letter, they could not be described as casual workers.
The petition was henceforth dismissed.
