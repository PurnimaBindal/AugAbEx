According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, specifically Sections 25, 25H, and 25G, and the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 78, termination of a daily wage worker's services while retaining his juniors constitutes a breach of the relief of reinstatement, regardless of the worker not having completed 240 days of work.
In cases where violations of sections 25G and 25H occur through the retention of retrenched workers' juniors, the completion of 240 days of service is not necessary under the First Come Last Go principle.
However, employers cannot invoke this provision without complying with Rule 78, and regardless, the workman is entitled to recall on priority.
