Constitution of India, Article 14 - Personnel of Communication Wing of Border Security Force - Claim of parity of pay and rank structure - Delhi Police Communication Wing personnel - He noted that the BSF and Delhi Police wardens operators have totally different rules, promotional avenues, and eligibility requirements - The basic qualification for direct entry as ASI also varied - The court believed that Article 14 applies only when parties are of the same class, and as these are two different organisations, there can't be any questions of parity between the two - Additionally, the court opined that pay scales and parity are executive functions and that it is not proper for the court to encroach into executive domain.
