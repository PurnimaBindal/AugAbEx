Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Andhra University Act, Section 35A/Superannuation - Teachers of privately managed colleges - Initially, the age of superannuation was fixed at 60 years at the time of appointment - The government later reduced the retirement age to 55 years - This order applies prospectively - Petitioners have the right to continue working until the age of 60 years.
