Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regulations of Navy Part-2, Regulations 118 and 119 - Discharge from service of Navy due to conviction in murder case - On appeal, petitioner acquitted of the murder charge and conviction and sentence set aside - Cases must be evaluated on their own facts - Petitioner was falsely accused of the crime - Petitioner to be reinstated into service with continuity of service, full back wages, and consequential benefits.
