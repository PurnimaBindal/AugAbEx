According to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Regulation 25 of the Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation Servants (Conduct and Discipline) Regulations, 1971, an employee's past conduct may be taken into consideration when imposing a punishment for a proven charge, even if the employee was not given an opportunity for a hearing in that specific case.
In this case, the appellant workman had been involved in similar incidents 18 times before and was found guilty of a charge relating to the non-issuance of tickets to nine passengers.
Therefore, the punishment of dismissal based on the appellant's past conduct is justified under Regulation 25 of the KSRT Servants (C&D) Regulations and cannot be considered improper.
