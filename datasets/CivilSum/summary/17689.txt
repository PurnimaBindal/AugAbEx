Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Two courses - Examinations - Result - The petitioner was enrolled in both a B.Ed.
course and an MPhil course during the same year.
No provision restricts a candidate from sitting for two university exams held in a year.
However, the University has the option to make regulations if administrative or academic reasons support such restrictions.
Absent a specific regulation, the University should not cancel a candidate's outcome.
As a result, the University has directed completing her MPhil exam and providing her a degree if she has passed it.
