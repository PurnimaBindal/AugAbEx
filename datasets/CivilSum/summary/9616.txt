Examination/Education - Re-evaluation of papers - A student argues that an examiner awarded varying marks for identical answers to specific questions in one set of answer books - Student requests re-evaluation by different examiner(s) - Such requests must not be rejected without reason - However, officials denied the student's request without explanation - Consequently, the university must have the answers to the mentioned questions re-evaluated by PGI's professor within one month (citing 1987 SC 294).
