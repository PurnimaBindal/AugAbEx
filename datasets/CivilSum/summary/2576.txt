Karnataka State University Act, 1976, Section 49(4), and Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Tribes - University not to restrict the applications while advertising the posts - Board of Appointment must comply with the reservation at the time of preparing the selection list.
[Para 7]
Article 16(4) and 335 of the Constitution of India - Reservation - Excessive reservation and its permissibility - Attempting to fill reserved vacancies in already advertised vacancies is not excessive reservation; it is in line with the policies of reservation, however, Reservation more than 50% is not permissible.
[Para 33]
Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Reservation - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Tribes/Discrimination - Recruitment against posts reserved categories withholding those meant for open categories amounts to discrimination violating Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution - Therefore, reservation has to be worked out, out of a common recruitment.
[Paras 32 to 35]
Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Tribes - Reservation made Cadrewise - In situations that require filling one post, there should be no reservation.
[Para 33]
Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India - Reservation - Roster - 100 point Roster - Where cadrewise reservation is present and smaller numbers of posts are available - To fulfill the Policy, it is reasonable to maintain a roster - Scheme of 100-point roster is not practical - Fixing the roster points for the minimum available cadre posts is reasonable.
