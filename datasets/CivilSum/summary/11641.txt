Constitution of India, Article 226 - Petitioner who was married to a Scheduled Tribe member had obtained a Scheduled Tribe Certificate after marriage and subsequently obtained a job under reserve category.
However, the petitioner's husband's certificate of caste was cancelled, and the petitioner was removed from service after proceedings.
The tribunal rejected all contentions raised by the petitioner and held that she had no right to obtain a job claiming to be a Scheduled Tribe at the cost of somebody else.
The petitioner also ceased to be a Scheduled Tribe member once her husband's certificate was cancelled by competent authority.
Furthermore, the appointment obtained by the petitioner through fraud could be cancelled, and thus the petition was dismissed.
