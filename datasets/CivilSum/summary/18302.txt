Haryana State Industrial Security Force Act, 2003 (21 of 2003), Section 3(1) - Repeal by Haryana State Industrial Security Force (Repeal) Act, 2005 (5 of 2005) - The Government decided to dissolve illegally raised Haryana State Industrial Security Force Battalions immediately - As a result of the Act being repealed, all positions were eliminated, and the petitioners' services were terminated - The Authorities' action in repealing the Act does not violate the petitioner's fundamental rights under Article 21 of the Constitution of India or the Directive Principles of State Policy, as contained in Articles 38 and 41 of the Constitution of India, since the petitioners have lost their employment rights under the Act and have been deprived of their right to life.
