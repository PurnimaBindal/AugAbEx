Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - and Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act of 1970, Section 1(4) - Employment through unregistered contractor - Absorption of contract labour in service - Unregistered contractors retain their status, and their labour remains separate from the principal employer's work - Labour appointed through such contractors do not fulfil the 240 days service criteria of the principal employer - Reinstatement awarded is held to be illegal and unjustified.
[Paras 21 & 22]
