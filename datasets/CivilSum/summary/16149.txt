Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Family pension - Hindu Marriage Act - Naib-Subedar in Indian Army from 1901 to 1924 - Death in 1927 leaving behind two-widows - Claim of family pension and Victoria Cross Allowance by both the widows - Rule used to deny junior widow benefits deemed to be irrational and unfair - Deceased was legally permitted to have two wives prior to the 1955 Hindu Marriage Act.

Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Family pension - Naib-Subedar in Indian Army from 1901 to 1924 died in 1927 leaving behind two widows - Both widows applied for pension and Victoria Cross Allowance - Only senior widow received payment before dying in 1929 - Junior widow entitled to family pension and Victoria Cross Allowance dating back to 1929, as well as all other benefits owed with interest on arrears.
