Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Selection - For post of UDC - Petitioner was denied promotion due to reduction in number of posts from 14 to 11 and subsequent change in bench mark criteria for remaining vacancies - Petitioners' names excluded for ranking `good' while respondents' grades were changed from `good' to `very good' - Decision to grade respondents as `very good' lacks justification - Court concludes that petitioners were wrongly and illegally denied promotion and are entitled to it.
