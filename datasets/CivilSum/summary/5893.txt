A.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25F(b) and 25-FFF - Closure/Retrenchment/Completion of work - If an employer's undertaking is set up for construction purposes and closes within two years, Section 25FFF(2) exempts them from paying compensation to their workmen.
However, if the construction is not completed within two years, the employer must give both notice and compensation under Section 25F [Paras 19 and 20] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-N, 25-F, 25-G - Establishment/Closure/Sister concerns - If there is a functional integrality of work in two units and the establishment remains viable, employees of one unit can claim lien in another unit.
However, if there is no functional integrality of work in the two units, workmen of one unit cannot be taken onto the rolls of the other.
Hence, the worker of a smaller unit with less than 100 workers will not benefit from the clubbing of two units for purposes of Section 25-N, 25-G and 25-F. Common accounts for provident funds and ESI will not be enough to consider the two units as one.
[Paras 26 to 28] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 25-FFF(2), 25-N Closure/Retrenchment/Bonafide closure - If the establishment is closed after the completion of construction work, then Section 25-FFF(2) and 25-F are applicable but not Section 25-N Permission from Government will not be necessary.
[Paras 29 and 30] D. Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-G - Seniority - If there is no similar and interchangeable post in the sister concern or in the same unit, there can be no seniority list for a single workman under Section 25-G Even appointing one workman during closing time for a short period for similar work does not come under Section 25-G. [Paras 31 and 33] E. Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - Retrenchment compensation - If a workman avoids taking the compensation tendered by registered post along with the notice for retrenchment, an offer or tender made to the workman by any other recognised media is valid under Section 25-F(b).
The workman cannot allege non-compliance with Section 25-F(b).
[Paras 34 to 37] F. Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-FFF - Closure/Victimisation/Completion of work - Retrenchment following the completion of work is not victimisation.
The employer cannot be mandated to retain a workman when no work is left.
