Fundamental Rules, Rule 22(1)(a)(i) - Pay fixation in promotional scale - Non-functional higher grade - According to FR 22(1)(a)(i), an employee is entitled to fitment in a higher pay scale only when being fitted into the time-scale of a higher post for the first time.
An employee who has already received the benefit of a higher scale due to a non-functional higher grade will not be entitled to the same benefits again upon promotion to a functional grade if there are no available vacancies.
The order of the Tribunal to refix the respondent's pay and grant him the benefit of stepping up under FR 22(1)(a)(i) has been quashed.

Higher pay scale/Non-functional higher grade - Non-functional higher grades are given to officials who have completed a particular tenure to avoid frustration due to stagnation.
This benefit is given until their normal turn for functional promotion comes.
