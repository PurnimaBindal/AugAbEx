Rule 9 of the Haryana Revenue (Group-B) Service Rules, 1988 regarding appointment to the post of Tehsildar provides that the position may be filled through various sources, including transfer, but only to the extent of 20%.
The petitioners have not provided evidence that a Superintendent from the office of the Commissioner was ever considered as part of this 20% quota.
As no challenge has been made to the provisions of this rule, the petition is dismissed.
