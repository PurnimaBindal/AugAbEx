Punjab Excise and Taxation Subordinate Service Rules, 1943, Rule 7 - Reservation/Reasonable classification/Qualifications - In promoting Excise and Taxation Inspectors, the classification and reservation will be based on qualification, which is justifiable on the grounds of administration efficiency.
It is the responsibility of any individual questioning the classification's justification to prove its unreasonability.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Res judicata/Constructive res Judicata - Any unresolved contention remains open and can be later resolved, there will be no construction res judicata.
