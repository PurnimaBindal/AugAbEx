Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), Sections 3 and 4 - Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Sections 167 and 147 - Negligent driving of a tractor causing death of an employee of owner of tractor - Liability of Insurance Company is limited to the liability arising under Workmen's Compensation Act (1923) qua the employees specified in proviso to Section 147 - Prior adjudication of such liability under Workmen's Compensation Act not necessary - AIR 2000 Karnataka 1 (FB), No longer good law.

Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Sections 146 and 147 - Policy of insurance required to be taken in terms of Section 146 need not cover the liability in respect of the death or bodily injuries arising out of and in the course of the employment of every employee of the person insured under the policy - However, such liability must be covered in respect of three classes of employees of the insured, including an employee engaged in driving the vehicle covered by the policy, an employee engaged as a conductor of the vehicle or for examining the tickets on the vehicle if the vehicle insured under the policy is a public service vehicles and an employee who is being carried in the vehicle covered by the policy if such vehicle is a goods carriage.
