Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Adhoc promotion/Regularisation/Seniority - Ad hoc promotions made without following procedure - Promotees allowed to remain in position for an extended period - Despite repeated representations, non-confirmation of ad hoc employees - Official cannot be held responsible for the status of ad hoc employee indefinitely - Seniority cannot be based solely on the date of confirmation or approval - Officiating period should be considered in seniority determination.
