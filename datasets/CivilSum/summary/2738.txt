Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Compulsory retirement - The order of compulsory retirement based on the evidence found on record of the petitioner misbehaving with lady employee while on duty and instigating fellow persons to do the same is justified - The fact that delinquent was acquitted in the criminal case would not be beneficial during the departmental enquiry - There is no reason for interference.
