Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Natural Justice - Annual confidential reports - If an administrative authority is found to have violated the principles of natural justice, the court should not necessarily quash the whole process and grant relief, but rather remit the matter to the authority to proceed afresh from that stage in accordance with law and adjudicate upon the matter after giving proper opportunity to the person concerned and taking into consideration all the material available and made available by him - this includes downgrading of ACRs without affording any opportunity to the employee concerned.
