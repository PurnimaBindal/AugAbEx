Constitution of India, Article 311 - Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Rule 10 - Misconduct - Disciplinary proceedings - The fact that a government servant falls short of attaining the highest standards of efficiency in their official duties cannot automatically be assumed to be misconduct but may simply amount to inefficiency - Misconduct under the Service Rules cannot be found every time an officer falls short of expected standards - The sales tax officer in question was charged only with regards to failure to revise the assessment of certain assessees and the charges were not serious enough to warrant suspension - The charges against the petitioner were also common in every office and department due to heavy workload, and hence do not qualify as miscondcut requiring disciplinary action.
