Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226/227 - Appointment on compassionate ground - Ex gratia scheme - An application for employment on compassionate grounds must be made within three years of the death of the employee and cannot be granted as a matter of right after that time - Yet Board's instructions issued in 1985 and 1986 not to entertain late applications would cause hardship in cases where the deceased employee leaves behind minor children - Such instructions are arbitrary and violate Article 14.
