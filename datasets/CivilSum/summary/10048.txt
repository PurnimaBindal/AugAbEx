The payment of gratuity act, 1972, Section 7(3-A) specifies how to determine the amount of gratuity in August 1986, and whether interest can be claimed under Section 7(3-A).
The Controlling Authority resolved the case by stating that sub-section 3-A of Section 7 had not yet been added to the payment of gratuity act, and therefore interest was not payable on the gratuity amount.
