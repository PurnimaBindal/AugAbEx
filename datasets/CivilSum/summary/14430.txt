Punjab Police Rules, Rule 16.2(2) - Acquittal - Reinstatement - A dismissed constable who was convicted and later acquitted after an appeal requested reinstatement - The Appointing Authority was instructed to review their case per Rule 16.2(2) - If investigation into the petitioner's behavior is required by the Authority according to rules, it is at their discretion - If entitled to reinstatement, the petitioner will receive all relative benefits.
