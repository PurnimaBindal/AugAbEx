Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Rule 15 - Proceedings for imposing major penalty - Imposition of minor punishment without enquiry - Not permissible - The disciplinary authority cannot take a short cut by not following the procedure as it may result in denial of opportunity to the Petitioner to prove her innocence, especially when she denied the allegations.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Non-speaking orders - Lack of sustainability - Failure to address the specific allegations raised by the Petitioner - Non-application of mind - Orders are arbitrary and illegal - Therefore, cannot be sustained.
