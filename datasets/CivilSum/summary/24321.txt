Constitution of India, Article 14 - Bombay High Court (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rules 7 & 8 - Natural justice - Every Rule must incorporate principles of natural justice even if it does not intend to offer an opportunity - Personal hearing may not always be mandatory - Issuing a detailed show-cause with opportunity to respond fully against all charges and findings is enough to comply with natural justice.
