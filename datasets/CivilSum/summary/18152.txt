The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(6)(a) states that the gratuity of an employee can be forfeited by the employer in case of financial loss due to employee misconduct or negligence.
The Discipline and Appeal regulations of Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulations 56 and 54(ix) also justify the forfeiture of gratuity in case of negligence.
In a case where the loss is due to the employee's negligence, even if no specific order is passed, the entire amount of gratuity can be forfeited.
The Rules stand satisfied if the termination order mentions the fact of loss.
