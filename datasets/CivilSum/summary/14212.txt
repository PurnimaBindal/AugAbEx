Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 151, Order 47 Rule 1 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Misconduct - Removal - Review - The workman was accused of demanding money from the Assistant Cashier in cash room and abusing and threatening him, while in a drunken state with a conductor.
It has been ruled that the petitioner cannot be granted the benefit of Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act by replacing the punishment with a lighter one.
