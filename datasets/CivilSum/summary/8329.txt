Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rule 78 - Pay - Drivers of Railway Protection Force claiming higher scale of pay - Pay fixed under statutory Rules - Promoters in higher scale of pay must be given equal pay for equal work - A statutory Rule cannot be supplanted by a circular letter - The court cannot fix any scale of pay under Article 226, particularly when the matter had been considered by an expert committee - The court must also consider the serious impact of such a direction, as it will apply to all drivers of the Railway Protection Force working across India.
