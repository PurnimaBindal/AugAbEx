Constitution of India, Article 16 and 227 - Transfer - Higher Pay Scale - Conditional Grant of - The petitioner was transferred as Sr.
Clerk (Accounts) with the condition that if he failed to resume duty at the place of transfer, he would not be entitled to the benefit of higher pay scale - As the petitioner did not join in pursuance of the transfer order, he was not entitled to the higher pay scale - The order of the tribunal was upheld.
