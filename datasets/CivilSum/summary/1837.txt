Under Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, workmen will not be paid for days they did not work due to wrong entries in attendance registers.
The authority was justified in rejecting the salary claim of a workman who did not work for a period of time due to such entries.

The High Court's jurisdiction under Article 227 of the Constitution is not appellate, but supervisory.
The Court cannot interfere with the lower court's factual findings unless they are totally perverse or without any supporting evidence.
