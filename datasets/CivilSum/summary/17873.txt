Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 532-B (As amended by Haryana Govt. vide Notification dated 2931983) - Voluntary retirement/Qualifying service - To be eligible for retirement under Rule 532-B, the employee must have completed 20 years of qualifying service - Retirement approval acts as confirmation that the employee met the required service length - Authorities cannot later argue that the employee did not qualify for pension benefits - The petitioner is entitled to pension and benefits per Clause 3 of Rule 532-B - Evidence Act, Section 115.
