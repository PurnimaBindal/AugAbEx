Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection - Non-approval of - Petitioner selected by Madrasha but rejected by District Inspector of Schools - Reason being violation of Recruitment Rules - Director of School Education upheld decision - However, subsequent list was sent to correct mistake and Petitioner was ultimately selected from list of 20 - Held, both lists should be considered as one and single - Recruitment Rules not violated - Order of District Inspector of Schools quashed - Petition disposed accordingly.
