The AP Medical and Health Service Special Rules, 1982 - Rules 8 & 9- Conditions of service - Promotion - Being considered for promotion is a condition of service that can only be regulated by a rule made under Article 309 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 16] B.
According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the recommendations of the Medical Council of India cannot override the rules framed under Article 309 regarding promotion.
[Para 14] C. Promotion - Qualifications - All eligible candidates must be taken into account while choosing those who will be promoted.
