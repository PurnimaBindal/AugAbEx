Constitution of India, Article 311 - Temporary - Appointment/Termination/Work and Conduct - An Assistant Project Officer was appointed on a temporary basis, with a condition that services could be terminated without notice if the work and conduct were not satisfactory.
The petitioner was found to be irregular in attending to duties and insubordinate, leading to the termination of her services.
The High Court's order was set aside in favor of the appeal, resulting in the dismissal of the writ petitioner.
