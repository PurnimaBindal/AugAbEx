Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - The petitioner filed a petition to be declared promoted from the same date as his juniors, along with consequential reliefs of arrears and increments, after being ignored in promotions.
The petitioner died soon after and his widow was added in his place.
The respondents argued that the petitioner did not receive promotions due to adverse remarks in his ACRs, but these remarks were not communicated to the petitioner.
The allegations of adverse remarks could not be considered against the petitioner.
Additionally, the respondents did not provide any reasons for the non-grant of increments.
The petitioner was found entitled to the reliefs claimed, including notional promotions, arrears of increments, and more.
The respondents were directed to consider these issues from the date of promotion of the petitioner's juniors and the arrears accrued were restricted to three years and two months prior to the original petition's filing.
Therefore, the petition was allowed.
