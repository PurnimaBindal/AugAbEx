Railway Protection Force Rules, Rule 153, 154 and 155 - Natural Justice - Departmental Enquiry - The Enquiry Officer presented a report and the disciplinary authority provided the Petitioner with a copy of it.
When the Petitioner submitted a representation, the disciplinary authority did not provide them with the result but rather issued a notice ordering the Enquiry Officer to conduct further inquiry.
It was held that the disciplinary authority should have recorded reasons and provided due intimation to the Petitioner before referring the matter back to the Enquiry Officer.
Further, the purpose of the additional inquiry was not disclosed, which did not give the Petitioner a fair opportunity to defend themselves.
Thus, the disciplinary authority’s entire procedure was flawed and the impugned notice was quashed.
[Paras 9, 21, 22 and 23]
