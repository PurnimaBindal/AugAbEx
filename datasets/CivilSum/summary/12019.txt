The Court cannot interfere with transfers from one department to another if they are not against any Rule or law or alleged to be mala fide, according to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 10]
The services of daily wage workers cannot be regularized against regular posts without following the proper procedure for appointment, even if they have worked for some time, as stated in Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India.
If the employer has been regularizing such employees under some regularisation policy in a phased manner subject to availability of posts, the petitioners must wait for their turn.
[Para 13]
Section 9A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, requires notice to workmen for changes in service conditions, but it does not cover the mere transfer of a workman from one post to another and is not violative of the fourth schedule of the Act.
