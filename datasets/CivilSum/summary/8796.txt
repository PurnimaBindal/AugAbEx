Civil Procedure Code, Order 5 Rule 20 - Service of notice - Natural Justice - Ex parte order - Report of process server - In order for ex parte proceedings to be directed against a party, a report of the Process server, in the form of an affidavit, must be on record - Absence of a report of process server along with no affidavit stating that substituted service was effected - Repeated reports by postal authorities stating premises were closed - Insufficient service - Malafide not found in petitioner/defendant's explanation - Denial of fair trial if proper service is not provided - Violation of principles of natural justice - Valid and proper service necessary before proceeding with ex parte judgement - Ex parte judgement quashed, direction given to proceed afresh in accordance with law.
