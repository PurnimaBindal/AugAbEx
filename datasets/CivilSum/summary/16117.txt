The Rules of Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Patwari State Services, Class III, 1955, Rule 11, and Articles 226 and 14 of the Indian Constitution mention that certain people from a different department have been given accommodations in the petitioner's department even though they had not passed the Patwar examination.
This action is seen as discriminatory because the petitioner had passed the examination and therefore was more qualified than those who were granted an accommodation.
This has resulted in shunting the petitioner out and adjusting others, which is arbitrary.
[Para 4]
Moreover, the Indian Constitution's Articles 14, 16, and 226 indicate that since the petitioner has been in service ever since he came to occupy the post of Irrigation Booking Clerk/Canal Patwari, and 47 other persons have been regularized in service even though they did not pass the Canal Patwar Examination, the petitioner is also entitled to regularization in service and all consequential benefits.
