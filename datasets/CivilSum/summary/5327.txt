Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Regularization - Rejection - Continuity in service - Petitioner requests regularization of service in accordance with government policy - Argument states that Petitioner does not meet the requirements because he has not completed 3 years of service with 240 working days each year and without a break of more than a month at a time - Petitioner obtained an award from the Labour Court which treated the period of his termination, which is currently in dispute, as continued service - This period has been considered a break of more than 30 days - It is held that since the award is not contested, the period of termination should be counted as duty - Respondent is directed to consider the Petitioner's case for regularization - Petition is granted accordingly.
