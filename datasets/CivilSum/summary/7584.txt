Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 124 - Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations, Rules 5(ii), 37 - Age cut off date - Legality - It is held that setting a cut off date with reference to the beginning of the calendar year in which the notification inviting application was issued is not unreasonable, and cannot be challenged by saying that the cut off date should be with reference to the year of examination or last date of receiving application - Therefore, the Petitioner is not eligible due to being underage.
[Paras 8 to 11] B Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations, Rules 37 and 5(ii) - General Rules, Rule 37 - Applicability of General Rules - Dispute regarding cut off date - When Rules specific to the relevant services fix the cut off date, any General Rule on the matter does not apply.
