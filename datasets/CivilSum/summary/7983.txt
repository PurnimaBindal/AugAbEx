According to Sections 2(g) and 2(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Commission Agents who work on commission basis without any fixed wage do not fall under the definition of a workman and do not have an employer and employee relationship with the dairy owner.
They are free to run the agency through any helper, agent, or employee and are not required to maintain attendance or personal presence.
This arrangement is considered a purely commercial agreement, not employment.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot use Section 10 of the ID Act to refer to the Labour Court.
