APSRTC Employees (Recruitment) Regulations, 1966, Regulation 12(2)(i) [as amended] - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45-Amended regulation - The amendment of Regulation 12(2)(i) incorporated a reservation of 33-1/3% for women in all direct recruitment posts after obtaining permission of government on 9/7/1977 - This provision is applicable with effect from the date and not retrospectively - Originally, a notification was issued on 7/9/1993 by APSRTC for recruitment to certain posts - It was cancelled and a fresh notification was issued on 5/9/97, with provision for women reservation pending approval of government for amending the Recruitment Regulations - The notification dated 5/9/97 was challenged only by three petitioners - The entire selection process was completed in furtherance of notification dated 5/9/97 - The court issued directions to appoint the three writ petitioners in the post applied for, if their names appeared in the select list prepared pursuant to the earlier Notification of 1993 - Remaining vacancies by made available to candidates who applied for appointment pursuant to the fresh notification of 1997.
