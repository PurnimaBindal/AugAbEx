As per the Haryana Education Code, a Sanskrit teacher may be entitled to a special certificate and its resulting benefits upon obtaining a higher qualification like a Diploma (Honours) in Sanskrit (Shastri), as per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
A previous DB decision in CWP No.5355 of 1992, Nathu Ram Sharma v. State of Haryana.
D/d.
16.9.1992, has already tackled this matter, and therefore, the petition is granted.
