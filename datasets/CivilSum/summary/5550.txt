West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973, Sections 60 and 106 - Recruitment and Leave Rules for the West Bengal Primary School Teachers, Rule 14(b) - Compassionate appointment - The retirement age of teachers under the Rules is 60 years, but if a teacher is incapacitated after reaching the age of 58 years, his dependents cannot claim compassionate appointment under Rule 14(b) of the Rules.
[Paras 11 and 12] B Judgments and precedents - Judgment `Per-icuriam' - A "judgment per incuriam" issued by the court while ignoring or being inconsistent with the law cannot be considered a binding precedent.
