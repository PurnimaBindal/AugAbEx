As per Section 2(s)(iv) of the Industrial Disputes Act, a Legal Assistant cannot be considered as fulfilling any managerial or supervisory roles, since their work entails providing legal counsel to the management when required.
Thus their employment should not be classified as being in a supervisory capacity, and therefore the Legal Assistant can be regarded as a workman under Section 2(s) of the Act.
This interpretation has been affirmed by the 1985(1) PLR 153 case as well.
