Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Abuse of power - Bad faith - Administrative needs - Transferring an employee from Calcutta to Mumbai during mid-session to accommodate another officer is deemed to be without administrative basis - Accusation of bad faith by petitioner holds strong - Transfer order violated transfer guidelines - Order nullified.

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Fairness doctrine - Administrative law - Writ court jurisdiction - Transfer is an integral attribute of service and an individual does not have the right to stay in a particular post - The Writ Court will investigate the matter if the transfer order was solely for the sake of accommodating another officer and lacked administrative needs - The administration must have cogent reasons to issue a Transfer Order - Arbitrary use of Transfer Order is not permitted - The administration must exercise power with due regard for fairness and reasonableness - The public interest must be considered in a Transfer Order for the order to be valid under the scrutiny of the Writ Court.
