Rule 244(2) of the Rajasthan Service Rules and Rule 53(1) of Rajasthan Civil Services Pension Rules allows for compulsory retirement of an Addl.
District Judge.
The decision to retire the petitioner was made based on an assessment of their overall performance in service, and it was deemed to be in the public interest.
This decision was not arbitrary or malicious and aimed to improve efficiency in service.
[Para 13]

Compulsory retirement based on an overall assessment of the service record of the petitioner cannot be deemed illegal simply because they were promoted or granted a selection grade in the meantime.
This decision was based on an assessment of the entire service record and entries from an earlier period cannot be ignored.
[Para 12]

The Court cannot question the sufficiency of material for arriving at a subjective satisfaction regarding compulsory retirement.
The Court can only ensure that the decision was made based on material on record and can be challenged on the grounds of mala fide conduct from the concerned authority.
There was sufficient material on record for the appropriate authority to arrive at a subjective satisfaction to retire the petitioner in the public interest.
The decision was not arbitrary or without any basis.
[Paras 5 and 10]
