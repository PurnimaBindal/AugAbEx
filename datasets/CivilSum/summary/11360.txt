Section 439 of the Criminal Procedure Code, along with the Prevention of Corruption Act of 1988's S. 13(1)(c), were invoked in a misappropriation case involving Rs. 778 lacs worth of wheat stock.
The case hinged on documental evidence, as there seemed to be no possibility of the accused fleeing from prosecution.
As a result, the accused was granted bail, citing the 1997 (1) R.C.R.
391 (Delhi) case as a precedent.
