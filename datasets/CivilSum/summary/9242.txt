According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the jurisdiction of the Labour Court/Chargesheet/Domestic enquiry is relevant to a workman who has been dismissed from service without being issued a charge-sheet.
In this case, the absence of a separate charge-sheet is not significant.
The Labour Court must assess whether the workman was guilty of the alleged misconduct once the entire case is before them.
Even if the management seeks permission to prove the allegations, which are declined, the Labour Court ought to have framed issues on the pleadings and decided about the workman's guilt to avoid harassing the parties.
