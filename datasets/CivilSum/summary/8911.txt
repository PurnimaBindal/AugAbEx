Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Rajasthan (Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying While in Service) Rules, 1975, Rules 2(f), 3 and 5 - Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956, Sections 6, 7 and 11 - Compassionate appointment - Adoption - The petitioner was adopted by the deceased, an unmarried individual who executed a Will in the petitioner's favour after performing necessary ceremonies - The appointment was initially refused on the basis of the invalidity of adoption by an unmarried person - However, the court established the validity of the adoption and the petitioner's entitlement to claim appointment on compassionate grounds and receive all retiral benefits of the deceased.
