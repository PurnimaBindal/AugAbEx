Constitution of India, Article 14 - AP Professional Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admission into under Graduate Professional courses through Common Entrance Test) Rules, 1993, Rules 7(1), Rule 5(2)(a) - Admission - Application for - Requirement of - The petitioner was unable to submit his admission form due to illness despite achieving fifth rank in EAMCET (Medical) - The petitioner is not permitted to submit his admission form as the specified time has passed - It is argued that making an application is a mere formality - Rule 7(1) mandates the submission of a second application - It has been held that solely submitting an application to appear in the entrance examination does not establish the right to admission - As it is not disputed that counselling is necessary and cannot be done suo moto, admission without counselling is not an option - Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
