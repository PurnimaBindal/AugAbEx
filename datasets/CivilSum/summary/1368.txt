The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, allows for the Fixation of a total of 250 marks - 150 marks for written tests and 100 for interviews.
The 100 marks are divided into five sub-categories, and prescribing the mark distribution in this manner does not violate Article 16.
The fact that the candidate knew the process and criteria was taken into account, and therefore the candidate cannot make a grievance that they were wrongly ignored.
