According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 136, a writ of quo warranto may be applied to assess the validity of an appointment.
After almost a decade, a writ of quo warranto was used to question the appointment of a private aided school's Principal, but regardless of whether or not the writ is appropriate, the Principal's role is not of such public significance that the Court should interfere.
