As per the Constitution of India, Article 226 and the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F, termination of fixed-period work-charge employees cannot be considered retrenchment if it adheres to the terms of their contract.
The petitioners' termination was in line with their employment contract, and thus, it cannot be considered a violation of Section 25-F Since they were not offered retrenchment compensation before their employment termination, no relief can be granted against the order of termination.
