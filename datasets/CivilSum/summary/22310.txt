The Constitution of India's Articles 15(4) and 16 state that reservation will be given to Central Government servants, settlers of pre and post 1942 in a State, and those 10 years old on the basis of Article 14 and not under Article 15(4).
However, the quota fixed for Tribals falls under Article 15(4).
[Para 5]
According to Articles 15(4), 330, and 332 of the Indian Constitution, reservation cannot be claimed proportionate to the population of a particular community as it was only available for limited purposes, such as representation in the Lok Sabha and State Legislature.
[Paras 28, 29 and 30]
While determining the factual position in an enquiry to fix quotas for different categories and classes, relevant facts should be considered while irrelevant facts should be avoided.
This cannot be reviewed under Article 226 of the Constitution, however, it should be reviewed periodically to ensure members of reserved categories are able to get sufficient representation or still require some reserved quota.
