Constitution of India, Articles 16, 319(1) and 321 - Equal Pay for Equal Work - Respondents are required to pay wages to the contingent paid staff who have worked for about eight years at rates equivalent to the minimum pay received by regularly employed workers in the corresponding cadre.
[Para 5] The principle of equal pay for equal work is also applicable when seniors and juniors perform the same function.
[Para 3] Directions have been issued to pay workmen employed as contingent paid staff at the rates equivalent to the minimum pay in the pay scale of regularly employed workers.
