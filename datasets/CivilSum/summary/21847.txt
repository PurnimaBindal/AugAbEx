As per the Punjab University Act, 1947, Section 21 and Regulations of Punjab University, Regulation 102, the Registrar of the University is authorized to file appeals without any decision from the Senate of the University.
The actions taken by the Registrar are later ratified by the competent authority through resolution, which relates back to the time when the action was taken.
Despite ratification, the Registrar is responsible and has statutory powers to represent the University in legal proceedings.
In a recent case, it was held that a refusal to examine a matter on its merits would not serve the ends of justice, and therefore, the matter was remanded to the first Appellate Court for a decision on its merits.
