Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Natural Justice/Preliminary Inquiry - Statements recorded in the preliminary Inquiry - The inspection of records was allowed even if copies were not supplied - No objection or demand before the inquiry officer - The delinquent was allowed cross-examination of the witness - Enquiring Officer and punishing authority are within their rights to rely on the statements recorded in the preliminary enquiry as no prejudice or loss was caused.
