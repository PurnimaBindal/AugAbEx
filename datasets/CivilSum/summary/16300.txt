Clause 13 on page 363 of the 1986 Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar (Vol. 11) has been amended by the Education Admission Amendment, which took effect after gaining approval in October 1990.
Although the petitioner was admitted in August 1990, the amendment does not affect the admissions made before it came into force.
[Para 7]
The Prospectus for 10+2 examinations does not define the requirement of 50% marks as only in +2 examination.
The percentage is calculated from the average of total marks obtained in all three 10+2 examinations, not just +2.
To pass the 10+2 examination, the candidate must pass all three examinations.
[Paras 7 and 9]
According to clauses 13 of Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar 1986 (Vol. II) and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a candidate who obtains much higher marks in the aggregate of all three 10+2 examinations cannot be considered ineligible for admission to the BAMS course when other candidates who have only 50% marks in other courses equivalent to 10+2 are eligible.
