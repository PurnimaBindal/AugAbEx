Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Termination - Approval - Failure to comply with conditions - If full one month's pay is not given, approval may be refused - Without approval, termination is incomplete - If payment less than one month's wage is offered, Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act will apply - If approval is not granted due to non-compliance with conditions, the employee's relationship will be restored and they will be entitled to all service benefits.
