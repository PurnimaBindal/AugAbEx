Constitution of India, Article 16 - Voiding termination of the Head Master of a school - Request for payment of pay arrears and terminal benefits - Argument that employee worked in another school during the period of removal - Since the employee had already reached the age of superannuation, he cannot be reappointed - It was held that merely working in another school did not disqualify the petitioner from receiving relief in the form of a judgment on his suitability for appointment in his earlier school, although it could be a factor in denying him actual pay arrears on the principle of no work, no pay - Directions were given to regard the employee as having continued in service and to pay all pension benefits and arrears of pension with interest at a rate of 65%.
