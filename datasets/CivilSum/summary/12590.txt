University teachers occupying University-provided accommodation cannot claim to be tenants in writ jurisdiction as it has to be adjudicated through appropriate proceedings, according to Constitution of India, Article 226.
[Para 5]
University teachers are entitled to pension, provided by the Constitution of India, Article 226, even if they do not vacate the University-provided accommodation on retirement.
The pension is a right and cannot be withheld as it does not depend on anyone's discretion or as a bounty.
[Paras 6 and 7]
