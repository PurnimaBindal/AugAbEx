Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 14(i) - Subsistence allowance payable to an employee under suspension - The rule requires the employee to submit a certificate every month stating that they are not employed or doing business, etc. - The only question to be decided is whether this "every month" stipulation is mandatory - Since subsistence allowance is paid at the end of each month, the stipulation is necessary - However, requiring the certificate every single month may not be mandatory, and could be seen as merely a suggestion.
