Constitution of India, Articles 311 - Central Reserve Police Force Act (66 of 1949) Section 18 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1949, Rule 27(ii) - Dismissal - Absence without leave - The service of the petitioner was terminated due to overstaying sanctioned leave, despite the contention that a registered letter was sent to extend the leave along with a medical certificate for the petitioner's wife.
However, it was found that the punishment awarded was not proportionate to the gravity of the charges.
Therefore, it was deemed that the punishment given was harsh and arbitrary, and hence, it needs to be replaced with a minor punishment.
Accordingly, the petition was allowed.
