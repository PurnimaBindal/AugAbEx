Constitution of India, Article 311 - Enquiry - An accountant with Market Committee - After issuing a charge-sheet, a sub-committee was appointed to investigate the allegations against the accountant.
The sub-committee submitted its report, and the Market Committee imposed punishment.
However, the Marketing Board nullified the Market Committee's resolution.
The Market Committee was ordered to conduct a de novo enquiry, but the subsequent enquiry was invalid due to the fact that there was no unlawfulness in the previous enquiry.
