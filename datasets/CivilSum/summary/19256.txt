In accordance with Article 14 of the Constitution of India, ad hoc promotion may result in reversion.
In this case, an ad hoc promotion from Demonstrator to Sr. Lecturer-cum-Manufacturing Chemist was made, but the rules did not provide for such a post at the time.
The inclusion of the post was under consideration, though the petitioner was ultimately reverted to their original position.
Despite this, it is not illegal or arbitrary, and the petitioner is entitled to the salary of the higher post during the period they were performing its duties.
A directive was given to pay the difference in salary.
