Constitution of India, Articles 341, 342 and 16 - Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes - Right of migratories to claim reservation - State-wise reservation - Reservation for Scheduled Castes is specific to each state - The classification of a particular caste or tribe for its inclusion in the list of Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribe in a given State would depend on the nature and extent of disadvantages and social hardships suffered by that caste or tribe in that State - A Scheduled Caste person who migrates to another State is not eligible for reservation benefits in the new State.
