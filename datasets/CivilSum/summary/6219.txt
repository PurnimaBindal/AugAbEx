According to Section 3 of the Andhra Pradesh Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admission and Prohibition of Capitation Fees) Act, 1983, professional educational institutions may reserve 4% of seats for the children of Ex-servicemen and Defence Personnel without being in violation of the law, and the rules' phrase "to the extent indicated" does not preclude determining a reservation under 4%.
