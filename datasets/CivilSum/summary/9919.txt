The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) (bb) and 25-F are related to termination, reference, and reinstatement.
A Labour Court found that workmen were appointed for 89 days, given breaks of one or two days, and then re-appointed.
The Management did not comply with provisions of Section 25-F before terminating their services, which was deemed unjustified.
The workmen worked for more than 240 days preceding the date on which their services were terminated.
References were decided in favour of the workmen, directing their reinstatement with continuity of service and full back wages from the dates of the demand notice.
The findings recorded by the Labour Court are supported by evidence and do not require interference.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
