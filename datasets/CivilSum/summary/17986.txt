Orissa Aided Educational Institutions Employees Common Cadre and Inter-transferability Rules of 1979 state that qualifications are necessary for appointment, but in some cases, the requirement of minimum qualifications may be relaxed at the time of appointment.
It cannot be used as an excuse later that the official was not qualified and hence could not have been appointed.
Once the qualifications have been relaxed, they remain so forever.
[Para 6] B Orissa Aided Educational Institutions Employees Common Cadre and Inter-transferability Rules of 1979 have an amendment in eligibility criteria brought in 1983.
According to the amendment, the respondent was not eligible for promotion as Reader.
However, as the promotion was made in 1981, the amendment cannot be applied to the earlier appointment.
