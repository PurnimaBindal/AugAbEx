Constitution of India, Article 226 - Removal - Principles of Natural Justice - A railway servant who was chargesheeted and removed from service is challenging the removal order, citing that certain documents that should have been given for inspection were not given and that the removal was made by someone higher than the appointing authority - The court held that taking action against an employee based on confidential documents that form the foundation of the removal order shows complete misunderstanding about the disciplinary procedure to be followed - The principles of natural justice were violated by not giving any credence to the appeal and the order of removal was set aside - The Appellate Authority is directed to give the petitioner a fair hearing and pass appropriate orders within two months.
[Paras 5, 6, 9 and 11]
