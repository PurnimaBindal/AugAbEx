The Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India were at issue in the case of fourteen charge-sheeted watchmen.
The petitioner's dismissal from service was questionable given that other charge-sheeted watchmen received minor punishment.
The appellate authority was tasked with issuing a speaking order, but failed to address all of the petitioner's arguments.
The orders were ultimately quashed and left for the authorities to reissue after the petitioner has been heard.
