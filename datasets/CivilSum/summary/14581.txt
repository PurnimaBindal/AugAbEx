Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25 F - Retrenchment - Petitioners who were working as Mining Mates and Blasters in Haryana Minerals Limited were subject to retrenchment when certain stone mines ceased to operate.
The retrenchment was carried out in accordance with the provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act and a joint seniority list of all the employees in various mines was maintained in a joint category-wise manner.
The retrenchment was considered justified as there were no junior individuals remaining in their respective category after the retrenchment.
