Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Discrimination/Pension - Criteria for granting Pension - Pension worked out as 30/60 of average emoluments for one set of employees retiring only on completing the qualifying service while it is worked out as 33/66 of average emoluments for those retiring at superannuation stage - Both resulting in 50% of the emoluments - Scheme based on a rational basis with no discrimination.
