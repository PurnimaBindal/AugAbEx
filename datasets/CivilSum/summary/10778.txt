Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 310 - Pre-mature retirement - Adverse report - Punishment - The competent authority must assess the employee's capability and fitness for retention based on available material before dismissing them - No pre-mature retirement can be based on stale adverse entries or punishments if the authority preferred to retain the employee at that time - Later improvements cannot be ignored - Order of pre-mature retirement without any adverse entries in the preceding 14 years service record cannot be justified for public interest or extraneous considerations - Settled principles of pre-mature retirement noted.
