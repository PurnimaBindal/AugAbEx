Constitution of India, Articles 309, 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Compulsory retirement - Although the petitioner received unfavorable reports in previous years, no such reports were received in the six years prior - It cannot be genuinely concluded that removing the petitioner from service before their superannuation would be in the public's best interest - It was decided that the order retiring the petitioner just four months prior to their superannuation was arbitrary.
