Constitution of India, Articles 311 - West Bengal Co-operative Societies Act, 1983, Section 147 - West Bengal Co-operative Societies Rules, 1987, Rule 48(f) - Dismissal - Disciplinary proceedings - The respondent faced grave charges of misappropriation of public money and disciplinary proceedings were initiated against him.
However, the respondent chose not to participate in the enquiry and avoid placing any defence - In the current case, it would be unfair to grant the respondent any premium for his recalcitrance and non-cooperation which led to the employer society having to issue the order of dismissal - Hence, the court finds no grounds to interfere with the order of dismissal.
