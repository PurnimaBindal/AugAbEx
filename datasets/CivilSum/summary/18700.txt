Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Reduction of pay - Appellant agreed to move to Hyderabad for a higher post and pay but no other Accounts Clerk was willing to do so - After three years, Appellant requested to be transferred back to Gujarat due to personal issues - Appellant was transferred to his desired location on a higher pay scale, but was later placed in a lower pay scale without a hearing - Corporation argued that the mistake was rectified, but the court found that Management cannot unilaterally reduce pay after accepting a request for transfer - Since there is no restriction on employee transfer, the Management's action was arbitrary and unlawful - The Single Judge's decision was set aside.
