The administrator upgraded five posts of Chief Metropolitan Magistrates to the rank of Delhi Higher Judicial Service, and included them in the service pursuant to Rules 16, 2, and 4 of the Delhi Higher Judicial Service (Recruitment) Rules.
The appointees to those posts in the service from Delhi Judicial Service must be held to have been born in the service from the date of their appointment.
The upgraded posts of Chief Metropolitan Magistrates were born in the cadre of Delhi Higher Judicial Service, and therefore, the incumbents appointed against those posts would not ordinarily be deprived of their benefit accruing from such appointment unless such appointments can be termed 'fortuitous or stop-gap.'
The appointment to a service and posting thereafter are two different concepts, and therefore, the subsequent posting against some posts born in the service will not deprive the appointees from the benefits of continuous appointment against such posts.
Once the post is upgraded and included in the Higher Judicial Service, the appointees cannot be deprived of the benefit of their entire service from appointment.
