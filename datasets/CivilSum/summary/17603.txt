Constitution of India, Article 226 - Nagpur University Ordinance No 55, Clauses 16 and 18 - Admissions/Education - Illness - Provision of second chance due to illness for admission to MBBS - Candidates who failed to avail the chance due to illness must be given an additional chance with proper evidence and conclusion of their inability to avail the chance - Authorities must develop a proper procedure to determine and conclude this fact.
[Paras 5 and 6]
