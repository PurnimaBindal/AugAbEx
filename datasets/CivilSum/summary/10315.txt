Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Rule 9.14(1)(d) - Criminal proceedings - Departmental proceedings - An employee's retiral benefits cannot be withheld on account of proceedings pending against him unless those proceedings are pending on the date of his retirement.
Since mere registration of an FIR on allegations of corruption without any further proceedings or charges being framed for 4 years cannot be used as a basis for withholding an employee's pension or other retiral benefits.
