Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Workman - Award of reinstatement - State challenges - Denial of back wages, continuity in service - Workmen engaged on temporary or casual basis against non-sanctioned post and engagement was connected with project work - Completion of 240 days service - Mere completion of 240 days service does not confer the right to continue in service; sanctioned posts must be filled following Recruitment Rules - Workmen entitled to compensation of Rs. 50,000 each and not to reinstatement.
