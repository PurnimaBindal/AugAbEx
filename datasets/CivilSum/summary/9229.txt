After Section 11-A was incorporated into the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Labour Tribunals now have a wider scope of interference in the findings and punishments given by departmental authorities.
They can consider evidence, justification, perversity of findings and punishments, as well as adequacy thereof.
In cases where the enquiry is defective or vitiated, the management can even establish an employee's misconduct before the Tribunal itself.
The Tribunal can discharge the findings of an enquiry officer if they are found to be perverse, and the rulings of 1973(1) SCC 813 and 1982(1) SLJ 255 are followed, while those of 1969 FLR 249 are no longer applicable.
