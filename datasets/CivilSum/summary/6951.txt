A Major General was denied promotion to the post of Lieutenant General and considered but ultimately not selected.
The petitioner claimed their profile was not properly and correctly assessed and their figurative assessment was made against policy decision.
The court found the denial of a second mandatory look for NDC was wrongful and illegal, and directed a thorough change in the entire profile of the petitioner.
The reconsideration of their claim was also directed, and the order of non-selection for promotion was quashed.
Additionally, the rejection of complaints filed by the petitioner regarding this issue was found to violate the Rule of 'Audi Alteram Partem' due to the vital document not being supplied to them, thus vitiating the rejection.
[Paras 66, 74-78]
