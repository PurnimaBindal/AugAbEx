According to Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India, the determination of pension should include the qualifying service.
This means that the respondent, who worked in the Construction Division of the Railway as a casual workman, should have 50% of his temporary service included in the determination of his pension and gratuity upon completion of 120 days working as a casual employee.
Even though the petitioner was confirmed only on a lower post after his retirement, this cannot be used as a ground for denying his pension in the pay scale drawn by him on a higher post where he had worked for 10 years.
