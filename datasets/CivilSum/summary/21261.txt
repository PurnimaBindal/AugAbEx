In accordance with the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 136, the Government Engineering College, Bhatinda with its existing staff was handed over to an autonomous body.
However, some employees decided to file a writ petition for their absorption in government departments.
The High Court directed the government to absorb all of them in government institutions on a permanent basis.
However, the state government was aggrieved and filed an appeal.
It was settled law that a government servant on the abolition of the post is either dismissed from service or dealt with according to policy.
This is similar to the situation when the government decides to hand over its institution to a private body.
Only 14 respondents insisted on their absorption in government service.
The rest of them agreed to be absorbed in the institution taken over by the autonomous body, and the order was made accordingly.
[Para 3]
In regard to the claim of the respondents for absorption in government service, the court determined whether it was possible for the government to allow the respondents to retain their status as government servants in any vacancy in any department.
The government stated that it was prepared to absorb them on the posts in the same scale of pay.
Consequently, the court directed the government to absorb the respondents in any equivalent post to which rules of recruitment would permit.
Necessary directions were made accordingly.
