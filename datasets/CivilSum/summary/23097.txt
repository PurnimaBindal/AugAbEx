Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Reinstatement - Back wages - The granting of relief of reinstatement and back wages is not an automatic process and Industrial Courts must balance the relevant factors in such situations.
ON FACTS, the Respondent was employed for a short period of time in two different spells from 1987 to 1990 and from 1995 to 1997.
Although the award of reinstatement with full back wages was made, the Industry being a sick unit, compensation in the amount of Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rs. Two lakhs only) is instead directed to be paid.
