The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, dictate rules regarding the change in date of birth for employees.
The petitioner cannot claim a change in date of birth now, as they have already taken advantage of early entry into service.
Additionally, correction of date of birth is not governed by any statutory rules.
It is worth noting that having accrued over 36 years of service credit is sufficient for securing a comfortable retirement.
