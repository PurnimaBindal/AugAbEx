Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Departmental Enquiry/Natural Justice - The delinquent must be allowed to cross-examine witnesses and to have access to prosecution evidence when allegations are denied.
The findings arrived at must be based on a sufficient record of evidence and grounds.
All charges and other adverse circumstances must be disclosed to the delinquent.

Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Departmental Enquiry/Natural justice - Cross-examination - Documents that are not properly attested, or which lack cross-examination, cannot be relied upon.
Denying the delinquent the right to cross-examine and produce evidence vitiates proceedings, while the opportunity to cross-examine is valuable.
Relevant material must be included in the evidence, and any documents produced must be properly verified with appropriate witnesses being called if necessary[Paras 8 to 10] C Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Natural justice - Departmental proceedings - The failure to consider the delinquent's evidence in his defense is a significant flaw in the inquiry which causes prejudice to the delinquent.
