Sections 11A and 44 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Schedule IV Items 5, 6, 9 and 10 of the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971 deal with disciplinary proceedings and misconduct.
The Industrial Court made an erroneous ruling by ignoring the fact that the workman had been charge-sheeted under Standing Order 23, and subsequently concluding that punishment of dismissal could not have been imposed for habitual absenteeism.
As a result, the order of the Industrial Court has been invalidated.
