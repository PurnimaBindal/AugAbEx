Central Government Health Scheme - Medical reimbursement - Central Government pensioners - The petitioner received a fixed medical allowance of Rs. 100/- per month for day-to-day medical expenses and did not have access to CGHS facility in Ludhiana, where he resides.
After receiving medical treatment for heart ailment in the Escort Hospital, he is entitled to reimbursement of medical expenses equal to the rates of AIIMS New Delhi.
The state cannot deny reimbursement of the expenditure incurred by a government servant, and every rule and regulation should be in favor of the employee.
The state cannot be indifferent in such matters.
[Para 7] B Medical Reimbursement of medical expenses - Retired Govt. servant - Head of Departments must take responsibility for the delay in reimbursement of medical expenses.
