Central Civil Services (Extraordinary Pension) Rules, 1974, Rule 9 - Extraordinary pension - Claim for extraordinary pension - The petitioner developed epilepsy during service and suffered its aggravation due to exigencies of service - While the disease was not directly caused by service, it was aggravated by the stress and strain of service conditions as per column 6 - The contention that the petitioner can claim only if the disease is mentioned in Schedule I-A is not valid - The list in Schedule I-A is only illustrative and the use of the conjunction 'other' suggests that other diseases could also be included - Hence, the petitioner is entitled to extraordinary pension.
[Paras 4 and 6]
