Civil Appeal No. 10251 of 2003 (@ SLP(C) No. 4445/02) A Assam Secondary Education Act, 1961, Section 24 - Assam Board of Secondary Education Regulations for Conduct of Examination, Regulation 8 - Scope of - Correction of date of birth - The process for correction of date of birth in certificates and entries falls under the power to make regulations and is part of the examination process and publishing of results.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Article 14 - Assam Secondary Education Act, 1961, Section 24 - Assam Board of Secondary Education Regulations for Conduct of Examination, Regulation 8 - Correction of date of birth - Limitation for - A person has only a limited right provided by the statute or rules to correct an entry in a certificate issued by a statutory educational institution and within the prescribed scope.
There is no general right to correct records based on information provided by the candidate.
[Para 9] C Constitution of India, Article 14 - Assam Secondary Education Act, 1961, Section 24 - Assam Board of Secondary Education Regulations for Conduct of Examination, Regulation 8 - Delay and laches - Discretion is defeated by delay and limitation destroys any remedy.
Equity principles deny discretionary relief and policy principles extinguish remedies on expiry of limitation.
Correction of educational certificates is not possible after the prescribed period of limitations in the rules.
