Constitution of India, Article 136 - Pension - Respondent-employees of work-charged establishment - The Administrative Tribunal directed appellants to give pension to the respondent-employees after notional absorption in regular service, as the appellants neither contested the application nor filed a counter-affidavit.
The appellants filed an appeal against the Tribunal's judgment, arguing that the posts occupied by respondents were not brought over to the regular establishment as they were not of a permanent nature, and therefore, they were not eligible to receive pension.
The Supreme Court did not interfere with the matter, as the respondents, being unrepresented employees, were prima facie entitled to the benefit of a notional absorption unless the appellant could demonstrate otherwise.
The question of law was left open to be decided in the appropriate case.
