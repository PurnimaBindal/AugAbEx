Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-N - Closure - Retrenchment by way of closure - In order to retrench employees in terms of Section 25-N, the management must follow the mandatory procedure and receive approval from the government.
Approval granted under the condition that temporary employees would be retrenched without following the mandatory procedure or taking into consideration the financial condition of the management is illegal and violative of the ID Act and Article 43 of the Constitution.
Employees cannot be termed as temporary in order to make their retrenchment easier.
