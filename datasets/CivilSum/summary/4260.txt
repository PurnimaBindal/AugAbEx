A Rajasthan Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Service Rules, Rule 11, Schedule, Item No. 2 - Petitioners challenged the derecognition of the STC/NTC certificates as equivalent to BSTC - The syllabus of State Trade Certificate (Tailoring and Cutting) Course do not match the BSTC course - Craft subject is not available in Primary Schools under Panchayat Samitis - Rule does not allow indirect equivalence or application of mathematical formulas - The Government's action must be in conformity with some standard and norms which are not arbitrary, irrational or irrelevant - Letters of the administration declaring STC, NTC and STC certificates as equivalent to Basic School Training Certificate declared null and void - Any such certificates cannot be treated equivalent to Basic School Training Certificate - Reference answered in negative - DB decision in CWP 1458/88, Babulal v. State approved.

Constitution of India, Article 215 - Binding effect of judgments of larger benches on the smaller benches and those of the higher courts on the subordinate courts - A Division Bench cannot disregard or ignore the decision of another Division Bench unless the same is overruled by a Full Bench or the Supreme Court.
