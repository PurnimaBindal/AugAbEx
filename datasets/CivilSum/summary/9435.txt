The withdrawal of an additional increment given on promotion falls under the definition of an order causing civil consequences.
To pass an order of this nature, rules of natural justice must be observed.
The petitioner was not given an opportunity to be heard, resulting in a reduction of salary and recoveries from salaries.
The impugned orders have been quashed, with liberty to pass fresh orders in accordance with the law after giving the petitioner due opportunity of hearing.
[Para 5] B.
In the absence of an opportunity to be heard, an order that causes adverse civil consequences cannot be passed.
The withdrawal of an additional increment given on promotion without observing the rules of natural justice violates this principle.
