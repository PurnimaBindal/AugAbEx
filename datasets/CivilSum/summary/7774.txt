Under the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 11A, 25F, retrenchment occurred when a workman who had been periodically appointed for 12 years refused to join during the pendency of the appointment despite an offer from management.
The management contended that the appointment was a one-time event for a specific term.
However, the workman was entitled to full back-wages until the offer was presented and afterward, 50% of back-wages until the age of superannuation, as per the ruling.
