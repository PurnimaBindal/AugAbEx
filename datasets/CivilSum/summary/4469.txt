Constitution of J&K, Section 103- Disability pension- A petitioner was hit by a Tipper while riding a scooter with his wife to attend a religious ceremony which resulted in his discharge from the service due to injury sustained - Disability pension cannot be denied to petitioner on the basis of injury not related to military service, which is improper.
- During casual leave, an employee is considered to be on active duty - Petitioner is entitled to receive disability pension.
