Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Promotion - Reservation - Out of the three available posts for promotion, one post was given to a candidate from the general category and the second to a scheduled caste candidate.
The third post was given to the appellant, a scheduled caste candidate, by treating it as a carry forward vacancy.
However, the court held that it was not appropriate to assign the third post to a reserved candidate.
