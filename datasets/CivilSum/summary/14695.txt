Army Act, 1950, Section 19 and Section 2 - Army Rules, Rule 14 - Dismissal - Removal from service - Retirement - Dismissal from service after retirement from military service - An individual can only be dismissed or removed from service if they are currently employed - Once an individual has retired after reaching the age of superannuation, they are no longer considered employed - Therefore, they cannot be dismissed or removed from service.
