According to the UP Basic Education Act, 1972, Section 9 and the UP Basic Education (Teachers) Service Rules, 1981, Rule 22, only trained Assistant Teachers are eligible for promotion to the post of Headmaster.
When the 1981 Rules came into force, the date of appointment as Trained Assistant Teacher in a substantive capacity is to be seen for promotion, not the date of appointment as untrained Assistant Teacher.
The writ petitioners had become trained Assistant Teachers by the date the Rules came into force, so only their appointment as a Trained Assistant Teacher is relevant.
