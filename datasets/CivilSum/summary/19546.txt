Appointment to the position of Assistant Engineer (Civil) in the Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board is governed by the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
The Board rejected the candidates appointed by the selection committee and no previous records of the candidates were available to the Board for consideration.
The Board has been directed to reconsider the case and appoint the petitioner if he is found to have better qualifications than those appointed, and to create a supernumerary post if necessary.
[Paras 9 and 10] B.
The petitioner's challenge to the selection was timely, but he withdrew the petition with permission to file anew on the same cause of action.
The second petition was filed without undue delay.
Therefore, the petition cannot be dismissed on the grounds of delay and laches in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 [Para 9].
