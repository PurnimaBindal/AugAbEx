The delay in initiating proceedings does not justify ignoring the seriousness of allegations of bribe involving a member of a selection committee, as per Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 5(v) - Selection - Bribe - Departmental proceedings.
Article 226 and 311 of the Constitution of India were also considered.
[Para 8]
The fact that the delinquent was one of several members of the selection committee does not negate the finding of the enquiring authority based on evidence proving that he accepted a bribe with a promise to select.
As per Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 5(v) - Selection - Bribe, a member of the selection committee holds an influential position.
[Para 9]
In cases involving selection, bribe, and departmental proceedings, the scope of writ jurisdiction under Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 5(v), and Article 226 and 311 of the Constitution of India, is limited to ensuring that the principles of natural justice were followed during the enquiry and that the delinquent was not prejudiced.
The high court cannot be an appellate court.
