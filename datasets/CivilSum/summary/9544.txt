According to Section 10(i) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the services of a petitioner were terminated, and a reference was declined by the Joint Secretary of the Labour Department.
However, the question of whether the workman had completed the necessary length of service is solely within the purview of the Labour Court to determine.
Therefore, the Joint Secretary cannot decline the reference on that basis.
The Joint Secretary of the Labour Department has been instructed to refer the matter under Section 10(i) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
