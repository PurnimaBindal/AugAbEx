Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner, who joined the army in 1971 after passing a Medical Board, fell victim to Neurosis in 1979.
Despite being approved for disability pension by a Medical Board, the respondent-authorities denied him the same on the grounds that the disease was not related to military service.
The petitioner's case is similar to that of B.
Sergeant Satnam Singh Bajwa and he has been held entitled to disability pension.
