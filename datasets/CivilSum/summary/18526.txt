The Haryana Civil Services (Assured Career Progression) Rules, 1998, Rule 11 and Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution of India state that an employee who chooses to forego a functional promotion while drawing their pay in any ACP scale will no longer be able to draw their pay in that scale and will instead receive pay according to the functional pay scale for their current position.
