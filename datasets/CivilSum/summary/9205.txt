Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 102 and 103 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Dismissal of workman by Co-operative Society - Dismissed workman has the right to appeal to Registrar of Co-operative Societies, but this does not prevent them from seeking reference to Labour Court under Industrial Disputes Act via the State Govt. - Order of Registrar Co-operative Societies cannot be considered as final decision for res judicata in Labour Court in reference under Section 10 of Industrial Disputes Act - Overruling of 1991(1) PLR 624.
[Para 7]  B Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, Section 128 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Section 128 of Co-operative Societies Act deemed ultra vires - Workman cannot be denied remedies under Industrial Disputes Act.
