Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16(5) - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Assistance of Legal Practitioner - The petitioner requested to be assisted by a legal practitioner during a departmental enquiry.
The authority offered him the assistance of a departmental representative, which he refused.
The departmental nominee for prosecution was not a legal practitioner nor a Prosecuting Inspector at the relevant time, and the charges were not of such a nature that the employee could not defend himself or with the assistance of a departmental representative.
No prejudice was caused to the petitioner due to not permitting the assistance of a legal practitioner, and therefore the dismissal order cannot be quashed on this account.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Departmental enquiry/Evidence/Affidavits - During the enquiry, the delinquent produced some affidavits of other persons in his defence without producing the deponents for cross-examination.
Such affidavits were not taken into consideration and there was no illegality.
