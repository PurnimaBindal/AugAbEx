The Rajasthan Technical Education Service Rules, 1973, Rule 2 regarding the Appointment and Selection of Lecturers is challenged by the petitioners citing Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India and the amendment made to the schedule of Rules.
It is left to the discretion of the selection committee or recruitment board to decide the criteria for selection to a particular post.
Since the petitioners failed in the selection process, they cannot question the validity of the amendment after having participated in it.
The petition is dismissed.
