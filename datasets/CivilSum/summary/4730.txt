Constitution of India, Articles 4 and 16 - Indian Electricity Act, 1910 - Electricity Supply Act, 1948, Section 5(2) - DMC Act, Section 511-B(2)(i) - Delhi Electric Supply Undertaking (DESU) was reconstituted into a State Electricity Board, with the Lt.
Governor of Delhi being delegated powers under the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948 and Indian Electricity Act, 1910 - Notification dated Feb 24, 1997 redesignated certain posts of DESU as Chairman and Members of the Board, but two Member posts were left vacant - Petitioner, who was AGM (Technical and Generation) was not redesignated despite the other incumbents being automatically designated to their new posts - Impugned notification on Feb 27, 1997 deprived the petitioner of the benefits of being a Member - Court held that once a legal right had accrued in his favour, it cannot be taken away with retrospective effect, and directed the respondent to redesignate the petitioner as Member (Technical) of the Board.
