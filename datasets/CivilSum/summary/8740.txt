Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-(C)(2) - Waiting mileage allowance - Payment for waiting mileage - When workmen are on duty and certain trains are cancelled, they are entitled to waiting allowance - Even if no duty is performed, the workmen remain on duty and therefore deserve the allowance - It is clear under Section 33-(C)(2) of the Act that the waiting allowance is ascertainable.
