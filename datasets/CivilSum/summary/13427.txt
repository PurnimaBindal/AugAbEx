According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, there is a claim for higher pay scale and upgradation of post of Editor to Assistant Director (Media), but there are no promotional avenues provided, nor is there any provision for a higher pay scale.
However, the government has allowed five biennial increments to incumbents of such posts.
It is up to the State Government to decide whether there should be promotional avenues, and at the time of joining, the petitioner knew that there were no promotional chances.
Accepting the petitioner's claim could also have repercussions in other departments.
