Industrial Disputes Act, (14 of 1947), Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Workman - Termination on expiry of contract - It is deemed that the temporary employment of a workman will expire at the end of their contracted period.
As a result, the provisions of Section 25-F are not applicable due to the provisions stated in Section 2(oo)(bb).
