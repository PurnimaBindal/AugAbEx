Punjab Agricultural University Act and Statutes, 1982, Chapter IV, Statute 4(2) - Appointment as Professor - Pay grade - The petitioner cannot claim that receiving pay in the grade of Professor while working as National Fellow entitles him to appointment as Professor in the service of the University.
Additionally, the fact that the petitioner had worked in the field of extension and done good work does not entitle him to claim an implied appointment as Professor.
[Para 7]  B Estoppel - Seniority list - Since the petitioner did not challenge the seniority list issued in June 1988, in which he was placed at serial No. 5, he is not entitled to claim that he was wrongly placed in the seniority list or that his service as National Fellow was not recognized for the purpose of assigning seniority in the cadre of Professors.
[Para 7]  C Punjab Agricultural University Act and Statutes, 1982, Statute 4(2)(iii) - Composition of Selection Committee - The discretion of the Vice-Chancellor is implied in the provision, which requires that the Selection Committee has to consist of three experts, including two outside experts, one of whom must represent ICAR.
If there already exists a list of experts approved by the Board of Management of the University, and the Vice-Chancellor exercises discretion to nominate one person from the list, the action cannot be declared contrary to the statute.
The fact that the Vice-Chancellor did not send an intimation to ICAR to send one person as its representative does not make the composition of the Selection Committee bad.
