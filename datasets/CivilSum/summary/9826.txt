The Executing Court dismissed the execution application of the petitioner on the grounds that the service rendered after the emergency cannot be considered for determining seniority.
The question of whether the petitioner is entitled to his entire military service starting from 29121962 to 4121973 or just for the period when the first emergency was in force from 26101962 to 10.1.1968 was deliberated upon.
The held decision was that the petitioner is only entitled to the benefit of military service when the emergency was actually in force, and he would only be entitled to the benefit of increment/seniority from 29121962 to 10.1.1968, and no other period.
