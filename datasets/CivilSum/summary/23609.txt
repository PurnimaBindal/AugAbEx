Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.P Palika (Centralised) Service Rules, Rules 21 Article 40(2) - Seniority - Regularisation - The ad hoc employees who were regularised w.e.f.
17.5.1985 could not be granted seniority above direct recruits prior to that date according to statutory rules, and the government was not authorized to treat them as regularly appointed from an earlier date - The High Court's dismissal of the petition of direct recruits was wrong as it left them at the mercy of the government, which was attempting to defeat their legitimate claim.
