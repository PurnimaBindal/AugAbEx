Sections 2(f) and 2(e) of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 define the terms 'Employer' and 'Employee', respectively, while also establishing the Liability of employer.
FCI, who acquired work through contractors for loading and unloading, did not have the labor working for them directly, and the Court was unable to determine the existence of any agreement between FCI and the Union representing the workers that would classify FCI as the employer of said workers, with regard to the definitions of 'employer' and 'employee'.
As a result of this, the High Court had no right to enforce FCI making contributions or holding them accountable under the Act, prompting a proper inquiry to be conducted.
