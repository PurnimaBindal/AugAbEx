Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Ad hoc service - Daily wager - Haryana Govt. mandates that ad hoc employees, who have completed two years of service by 3111996 and are still in service, be regularised - Petitioner was a daily wager from 1990, became ad hoc from 151995, and completed over two years of service by 3111996 - Two years of ad hoc service is not a requirement, only two years of service and ad hoc status on the appointed date are necessary as per instructions - The petitioner cannot be deprived of regularisation benefits from the policy's inception date.
