Rajasthan Municipal (Subordinate and Ministerial) Service Rules, 1969 - Rajasthan Municipal Service Rules, 1963, Rule 7 - Promotion - Right of consideration - Deputation - In the event that there are eligible candidates within the Municipal Council, the position of Asstt.
Accounts Officer cannot be occupied by deputation from government employees - The post of A.A.O.
is to be filled entirely through promotion adhering to the Rules - The government is not permitted to fill it up with direct recruitment either - As a constitutional authority with complete autonomy, any interference by the government would violate constitutional provisions.
