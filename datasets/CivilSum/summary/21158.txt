Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Assistant Engineers (Akashwani and Doordarshan Group `B' Posts) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 1985, Rule 2(1)(a) - Qualifications - Degree and diploma - While Tribunal's decision was that degree and diploma-holders enter the Engineering Assistants cadre through different channels and have different minimum qualifications, the decision was held to be wrong, and all Engineering Assistants are considered to belong to one single cadre, and no difference can be made on the basis of degree and diploma - Even during promotions, no distinction is made based on educational qualifications - The new policy in Rule 2(1)(a) and (b) of Amendment Rules aims to balance the benefits arising from merit and long service-based experience and is not discriminatory or arbitrary - Amendment is deemed as valid.
