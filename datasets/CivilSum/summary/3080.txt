Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - In cases where a workman challenges the validity of a domestic enquiry conducted against them resulting in punishment, the issue of the enquiry's validity should be decided as a preliminary issue.
Additionally, when a worker has raised an industrial dispute against their dismissal due to an improper enquiry, it is their responsibility to prove the illegality or invalidity of the enquiry.
Thus, the workman must provide evidence first to establish the charges against them, not the management.
The order of the Central Government Industrial Tribunal (CGIT) directing the Bank to lead evidence first is not valid and has been set aside.
[Paras 6, 9 &10]
