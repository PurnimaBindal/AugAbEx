Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Lower School Course - Dispute on facts - Petitioner was initially informed that no admission test for List B-1 was to be held in January 1995 - However, it was later found that a test had been held and successful candidates were sent for the lower school course - The petitioner may be eligible for relief if he can prove that he had no notice of the test, but he is able to take the test held every year including the upcoming one in January 1997 - If he passes and is selected, he may make a representation to the authorities.
