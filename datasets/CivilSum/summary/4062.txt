Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Admission - Combined Medical Test for admission to various medical courses - In the year 1997, serious complaints of corruption, bungling and manipulation in conducting the test arose; key answers were not published - Fairness in evaluation and secrecy of test must be maintained - Candidates have a right to know the manner in which they were marked, which is protected under Article 19(1)(a) of the Constitution - System to avoid manipulations in examination to be established and maintained.
