Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Payment of Gratuity (Amendment) Act, 1998, Section 4(3) - Gratuity Amount - Reduction in Amount - Limitation in the amount of gratuity by amendment with retrospective effect - Retrospectively implementing an amendment that reduces the amount of gratuity takes away a benefit already available to employees under the existing rule - Ruled as arbitrary, discriminatory and in violation of the rights guaranteed under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
