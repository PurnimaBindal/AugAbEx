Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Service of Engineers Class II (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1941, Rules 4 and 9, Punjab Service of Engineers Class II P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rule 5 - Validity of Rules - Officiating Sub Divisional Engineers do not fall under the definition of Class II Service under Rule 2(5) of Class I Rules - Temporary Engineers included within the definition - Petitioners claimed discrimination - Temporary Engineers require an Engineering degree, whereas officiating S.D.Os.
do not - Petitioners are distinct from Temporary Engineers - Writ dismissed - Rules are valid.
