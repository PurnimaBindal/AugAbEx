Regulation of the Medical Services of Armed Forces Act, Regulation 423(e) - Pension Regulation for Army, Regulation 173 - Bikaner Army Pension and Gratuity Rules, Rules 42 and 43 - Grant of disability and special family pension - Invalidation of service due to disability - If a disease has caused an individual's discharge or death, it will generally be considered to have occurred during service if there was no indication of it at the time of acceptance into the Armed Forces.
However, if it is determined by medical opinion that the disease could not have been detected before being accepted into service, it will not be considered to have arisen during service.
