UP Food and Civil Supplies (Supply Branch) Ministerial Service Rules 1979 - Seniority - Promotion - Seniority lists for each category of posts in the service are to be maintained separately for each division to avoid confusion and imbalance within the service - To restore balance, it is suggested that orders be recalled and set aside and that all relevant rules and norms be kept in mind when making promotions with retrospective effect from the date a vacancy arises for an incumbent according to the State Level Seniority List - However, those promoted prior to 111985 by the UP Regularisation of Ad hoc Promotions (on posts within purview of PSC) Rules, 1988 need not be disturbed and those promoted with retrospective effect will be entitled to all pecuniary benefits of such promotion without having to refund any benefits previously enjoyed.
