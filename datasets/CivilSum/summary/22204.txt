Employees Provident Funds Act, 1952, (Act No. 10 of 1998), Section 16(1)(d) - Infancy protection - Amendment to existing rights - Retroactivity - Company granted protection for three years from establishment set up - Despite repeal of provision for infancy protection, appellant entitled to protection for three years from establishment set up.
(i) Language of statutory provision determines scope of retrospectivity.
Appeal is allowed.
[Paras 17 and 20]
Interpretation of Statutes - Principles explained - (i) Provision affecting only future rights is not retrospective in operation.
(ii) Retrospective operation is not intended unless expressed or implied by necessary implication.
(iii) Statute or section not to have larger retrospective operation than its language renders necessary.
(iv) Every statute is prima facie prospective.
[Paras 17 and 18]
Words and Phrases - "Provisions which touch a right in existence at the passing of the statute are not to be applied retrospectively in the absence of express enactment or necessary intendment."
