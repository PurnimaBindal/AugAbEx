Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Termination of a daily wage labourer - The labourer pleaded violation of both Sections 25-F and 25-G, but no violation of section 25-F was found due to lack of proof of completion of 240 days in any year prior to his discharge.
However, the evidence regarding another person appointed in his place leads to a conclusion of violation of section 25-G without any cross-examination on that point.
The appeal was allowed to that extent, and the matter was remanded to the Labour Court to decide the issue of violation of section 25-G. [Paras 8 and 15] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Failed challenge of termination of a daily wage labourer before both the Labour Court and HC and present LPA - The respondent raised a preliminary objection that LPA was not maintainable as the appellant invoked Art. 227 of the Constitution, whereas a writ can only be issued under Art. 226.
However, the writ was sought under Art. 226 of the Constitution of India.
The appeal was partly allowed, and the matter was remanded to the Labour Court for a fresh decision.
