Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion Seniority - The appellant was considered for promotion but later ignored due to poor service record.
Subsequently, juniors were found suitable and promoted.
When the appellant was later promoted, he cannot claim seniority over those promoted earlier and before the next promotion.
The juniors, once promoted, become senior.
[Para 9]

Limitation Act, Article 113 - Suit for declaration/promotion - The petitioner was ignored for promotion, while juniors were promoted.
According to Article 113 of the Limitation Act, the petitioner has only three years to challenge such an order.
This follows AIR 1991 SC 2219.
[Para 10]
