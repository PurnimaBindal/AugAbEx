Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 84 and 68 - Quasi-judicial order/Irregularity in quasi-judicial orders - Quasi-judicial authorities are protected from disciplinary action when they exercise their power bona fide - However, when the exercise of power is tainted by mala fides or arbitrariness, no immunity against departmental action is available.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 22(b) - Enquiry - Disciplinary proceedings after retirement - Disciplinary proceedings initiated during employment will not lapse because of the employee's retirement and may continue after retirement if authorized by the Rule making authority.
[Para 6] C. Constitution of India, Article, 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 11, Rule 22 - Enquiry Officer - Appointment of Enquiry Officer - Reasons - The competent authority must indicate in the appointment order of the Inquiry Officer if the reply to the show cause notice has been considered and found unsatisfactory - The omission to record reasons is not fatal to the pending enquiry.
