Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Admission - Eligibility - Admission to B.Ed.
- Admission granted upon passing Joint Entrance Test - Order necessitating the completion of an additional Bridge Course for matriculate + three years degree Court (14 years) before admission - Completion of Bridge Course a requirement for admission - Cancellation of admission justified if condition not fulfilled within requisite time - University's relaxation in time limit also deemed acceptable - If Bridge Course completed within the extended time, respondents will announce result.
