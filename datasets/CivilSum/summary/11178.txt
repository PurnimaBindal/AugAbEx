According to Constitution of India, Article 191(1)(a) and the Punjab Gram Panchayat Act, 1952, Section 6(5)(2), holding an 'office of profit' disqualifies a person from contesting an election as a member of the Legislative Assembly or as a Panch or Sarpanch in the State of Punjab.
The petitioners, who held 'offices of profit' under the State of Punjab as employees of Punjab State Tractors Ltd, P.S.E.B.
and Pepsu Road Transport Corporation, were rightfully disqualified from contesting election as a Sarpanch or Panch.
The instructions dated 27121992 were also not illegal or ultra vires of the Act.
[Paras 6 and 7]
The Punjab Gram Panchayat Act, 1952, Section 6(5)(g) allows the State Govt. to issue instructions, which were issued in line with the clause.
The mere mention of a wrong provision in the instructions does not make them illegal.
