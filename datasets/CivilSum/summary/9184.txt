Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Criminal proceedings - Suspension - Departmental proceedings - Revival of suspension order - If a criminal case against an employee ends in discharge, their suspension order automatically ceases to be operative and they are deemed to be in employment - The revival of a suspension order on the basis of an independent charge after discharge by a Special Court is not permissible - Such an order has been set aside.
[Paras 5, 12 and 13] It is important to note that when a government servant is suspended pending a criminal investigation or trial, the order of suspension automatically ceases to be operative as soon as criminal proceedings end in acquittal or discharge of the government servant.
