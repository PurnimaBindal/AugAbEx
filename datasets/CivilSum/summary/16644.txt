Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - School Lecturers recruitment - Advertisement issued, followed by a Carrigendum after a fresh requisition - The selection process conducted, and select list sent to State Govt. by the State Public Service Commission - Selection deemed not open for interference - State Govt. empowered to grant age relaxation to over-aged candidates in case of fresh selections due to corrigendum.
