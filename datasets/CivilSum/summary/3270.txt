Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Compulsory retirement - Calcutta Port Trust Employees' (Classification, Control and Appeal) - Regulations, 1987, Regulations 7 and 8A(1)(ii) - The Disciplinary Authority failed to proceed with an open mind after the order of remand by the appellate authority.
He only reiterated the earlier order passed by his predecessor-in-office and effectively gave it a rubber stamp.
As a result, the impugned order of compulsory retirement was quashed, and the petitioner is entitled to full back-wages and reinstatement in the same post from which he was compulsorily retired.
