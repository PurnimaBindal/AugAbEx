Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 226 - Pension - Revised pay scale - Pensioners who retired on or after 31121995 are entitled to the benefits of revised pay scales which came into effect from 1.1.1996 - According to 2005(2) ALD 1 (FB), a Government servant retiring on the last day of the month will become a pensioner with effect from the first day of the succeeding month and entitled to all pension benefits - Such pensioners should be considered for pension as per revised scales.
