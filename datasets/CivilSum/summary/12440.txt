Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Post Graduate Technical Apprentices - In 1976, 84 candidates were selected, including the petitioners, but there was a delay in issuing appointment letters due to character and antecedent verification.
By 1978, all candidates, except for the petitioners and 16 others, were appointed as GTA.
After a representation by the petitioners and those appointed in 1977, a deemed date of eligibility for appointment as Asst.
Engineer was given to them w.e.f.
June 1, 1978, the same date as those who joined earlier.
While Petitioners cannot be appointed to the post of Asst.
Engineer prior to May, 1979, they are eligible for the fixation of pay w.e.f.
June 1, 1978.
[Paras 2 and 4 to 7]
