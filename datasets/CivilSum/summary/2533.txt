Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(g) and 10(1) - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 - Clarification on relationship of employee and employer - In cases where workers are employed through a contractor as contract labour, the Labour Court can investigate on the establishment of an employee-employer relation based on facts and evidence - If the workmen were allowed to continue in employment with the principal employer after the expiry of contract with the contractor and direct control/payment of salary was made by the principal employer, a direct relationship of employee and employer is established under Section 2(g) of the ID Act - Termination of services without complying with the provisions of the Act warrants reinstatement of the workman.
