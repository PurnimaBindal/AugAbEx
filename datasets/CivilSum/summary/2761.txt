According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner was advised to undergo bye-pass heart surgery, which was then completed at Escorts.
However, the petitioner was only reimbursed at the rates of AIIMS due to a condition imposed upon granting permission.
In 1996(1) SLR 260, the Apex Court ruled that such a condition was illegal and the petitioner was entitled to receive full reimbursement.
[Paras 4 and 8]
