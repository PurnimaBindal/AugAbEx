According to Rajasthan Forest Subordinate Service Rules, 1963, Rule 2 (a), the PCCF is the appointing authority of Rangers Grade-I and has power to transfer them under Rule 20 of Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951.
[Para 7]

Administrative Instructions issued by State Govt. regarding allocation of duties and responsibilities between PCCF & CCF are merely administrative instructions and the guideline for seeking prior approval of State Govt. for transfer of Ranger - Grade-I (Gazetted), who has not completed two years' tenure at one place is merely administrative in nature and has no statutory force.
Such guidelines also do not confer any vested right to the appellant to challenge his transfer.
It can be held that for want of seeking approval of State Govt, the impugned transfer order is not unauthorized or without jurisdiction.
[Para 15]

There is no evidence of political interference in the impugned transfer order.
Simply because the order was made by the PCCF in compliance with a certain UO note of the Minister for Forest and Environment, it cannot be assumed that the then Minister for Enger.
& Power Deptt.
had influenced the then Forest Minister.
The impugned order of transfer is neither mala fide nor under political interference.
