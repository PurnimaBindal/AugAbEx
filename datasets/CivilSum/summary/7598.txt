Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Employees' Pension Scheme 1974 - Notice of the Scheme to employees/pensioners - Options - Estoppel - According to the specific terms of the Railway Employees' Pension Scheme 1974, individual communication to every pensioner was necessary for exercising option and right to change over to pension scheme from Railway Provident Fund.
The failure of the authorities to comply with these terms cannot allow them to argue that the "awareness" of the scheme was sufficient notice to a retiree to exercise their option.
