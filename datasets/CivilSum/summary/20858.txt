Indian Police Service (Probation) Rules, 1954, Rule 10 - Indian Police Service (Cadre), Rules, 1954 Rule 5 - Probationary training - Exemption from probationary training - A probationer in the IPS can be granted exemption from training if they want to improve their chances of being selected for a better service - This exemption itself is a significant factor in treating them as a member of the Indian Police Service - An exempted probationer must be treated as a probationer of the same batch for all practical purposes, including cadre service, and included in the common list of probationers of that batch.
