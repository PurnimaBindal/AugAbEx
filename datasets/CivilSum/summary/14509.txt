According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 226/227 regarding arrears of pay, if an employee is promoted retroactively, they cannot be denied the pay and allowances they would have received if they had actually been promoted on the later date.
In this case, the respondents have been ordered to pay the petitioners their arrears of pay with interest at a rate of 15% per annum from their deemed dates of promotion.
Additionally, because the petitioners have already retired, the respondents must revise their pension to include these benefits.
