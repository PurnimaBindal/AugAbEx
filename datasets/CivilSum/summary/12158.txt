As per Rule 58 of the Employees State Insurance Rules from 1950, in case of a workman's death due to an employment injury, the son and daughter above 18 years are not eligible for benefits.
The widow, being the sole dependent, will receive 3/5 share of the full benefit rate but will not be entitled to the full benefit rate.
One dependent of the deceased insured workman will not receive the full rate of dependent benefits if there are no other dependents, as stated in paragraphs 3 and 4.
