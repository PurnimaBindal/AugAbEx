Haryana School Education Board Matriculation Examination Regulations, Regulation 8 - Examination - Eligibility - According to the regulation, individuals must have passed their 8th standard examination from a school affiliated to CBSE to be eligible to appear as a private candidate for matriculation in Haryana Board.
Additionally, candidates who have passed their 8th class with at least a two-year gap are also allowed to appear.
The Court held that the petitioner was not eligible to take the examination as a private candidate because he did not pass the 8th class examination from the Haryana School Education Board.
Therefore, there was no interference.
[Paras 3 and 4]
