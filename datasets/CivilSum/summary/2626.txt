A.
Education - Admission - Absence - The booklet of syllabus which has no statutory force and can be amended states that the authority is only competent to relax the shortage in attendance up to 10% for students.
However, in appropriate cases, if a satisfactory explanation is given, the authority can exercise its discretion to condone the absence and shortage of attendance beyond 10%.
Petition was granted.
[Para 9] B Natural justice - Admission - Cancellation of - Absence - The student's name was struck off without any notice and without an opportunity to explain the reason of absence.
If the student had been given notice, they could have explained that they were seriously ill and unable to attend classes.
The non-compliance of the principles of natural justice caused serious prejudice to the student and violated their right to complete the course.
The action of the authority must be nullified.
