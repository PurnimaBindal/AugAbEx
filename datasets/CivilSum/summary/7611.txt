The Army Act, 1951, Section 8 and Air Force Pension Regulations, Regulation 520 state that in the event of an officer's death while not on active duty, the family must receive a pension.
The report of the Court of Inquiry is no longer considered merely recommendatory and must be accepted by the sanctioning authority, unless the Government of India has sufficient reason to differ.
The opinion of the Brigade Commander cannot be disregarded and the death cannot be labeled as not attributable to service solely based on the government's disagreement.
