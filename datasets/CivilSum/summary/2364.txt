Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rule 153(8) - Purposive interpretation - Appellant contested the constitutionality of Rule 153(8) of RPF Rules - Rule 153 details the procedure for major disciplinary action - A charged member can seek aid from a force member or a friend, but cannot cross-examine the witness - Court holds that the statute should follow the principles of natural justice and fair play - Having the right to be represented by a friend/member is not part of audi alteram partem doctrine and would cause serious harm - As such, part of Rule 153(8) is found unconstitutional, and appeal allowed accordingly.
