Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 141 - Uttar Pradesh Junior High Schools (Payment of Salaries of Teachers and Other Employees) Act, 1978, Sections 10 and 13-A - Payment of salaries to private primary school teachers - The State government is not responsible for paying salaries to high school-level, privately-managed school teachers - A previous court decision regarding the Payment of Salary Act, 1978 and entitlement of salaries to teachers of primary sections in recognised schools requires reconsideration, as the court overlooked the separation of education into primary and junior high school levels - The legislature provides a distinction between "junior basic schools" and "junior high schools," treating them as two separate entities - The Act does not include primary sections or separate primary schools - The matter is to be referred to a larger bench.
