Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Bihar Finance Service Rules, 1953 - Seniority - Merger of senior and junior cadre - Retrospective effect of instruction - Individuals in the junior cadre who were merged into senior cadre will receive seniority from the date of the merger, not the date of appointment in the junior cadre, as stated in the Rule providing seniority fixation in the cadre from appointment to the merged cadre - Retroactive executive instructions that undermine the rights delineated in the Rules are illegal and in violation of Articles 14, 16, and 309 of the Constitution; even rules enacted under Article 309 of the Constitution cannot diminish vested rights unless explicitly specified.
