According to Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, the son of the owner of a vehicle who is working as a driver for his father and was involved in an accident while carrying some relatives is eligible for compensation.
The mother of the deceased driver, who was dependent on her son's income, is claiming compensation.
The vehicle's policy covered the risk of the driver, and thus the insurance company is responsible for indemnifying the owner.
