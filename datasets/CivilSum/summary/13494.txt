Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Andhra Pradesh Electricity Board Employees Service Regulations, (Part I), Regulation 33(b) - Writ Jurisdiction - Discretion - Increments - Leave - Extraordinary leave - The Electricity Board has power to count extraordinary leave for increments if leave is taken due to illness or reasons beyond the employee's control - This power is discretionary and must be exercised reasonably and in good faith - A Regional Medical Board confirmed the petitioner's illness during his leave - The orders impeding the petitioner's increments are quashed and respondents must approve periodical increments for the extraordinary leave period.
