Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo) and 2(oo) (bb) and Section 25F - Contract of service - Fixed term appointment - Clause (bb) of Section 2(oo) covers fixed term appointments and thus, Section 25F does not apply unless there is evidence of misuse of power or mala fide motive, or that the appointment was a sham - Without such evidence, termination of a fixed term contract cannot be deemed illegal.
