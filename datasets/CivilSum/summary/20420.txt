Constitution of India, Article 311 - Post of Head of Department became vacant - A subordinate Officer was declared as the Head of Department until a regular incumbent was appointed - Dismissal of an employee resulting from disciplinary inquiry made by the said officer was deemed valid - The argument that the said officer was not appointed as Read of the Department but was only declared Head of Department and thus could not exercise the powers of Read of Department was not valid.
[Paras 8, 9, 10 and 11]
