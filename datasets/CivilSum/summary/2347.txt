Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 10(1) - Defense Counsel - The tribunal cannot interfere with an enquiry unless the rules, regulations, or settlement provide for defence counsel to be engaged.
In this case, since the charges were simple, the tribunal exceeded its jurisdiction by interfering with the enquiry.
[Paras 7, 8]
Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 10(1) - Enquiry - The non-supply of documents to the workman has violated the principles of natural justice and has occasioned the failure of enquiry.
The tribunal acted without jurisdiction by failing to hold an enquiry that was vitiated on this count.
