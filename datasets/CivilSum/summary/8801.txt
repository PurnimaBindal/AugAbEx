Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F(a) and (b) - Termination of service - Failure to comply with mandatory conditions and relevant precedents under Section 25-F renders the termination order invalid ab initio - Affected workers are entitled to reinstatement with continuity of service and back wages - Respondents instructed to reinstate petitioners with all commensurate benefits, including service regularisation from the date of their initial appointment.
[Paras 4 and 7]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25F - Termination - Natural justice - Alternative remedy - Termination order passed against established principles of natural justice and accepted procedure - Non-compliance with Section 25F(a) and (b) - Writ remedy available in cases where the impugned order passes in violation of natural justice - Petitioners need not seek alternative remedy - Determined, Availability of another remedy cannot prevent exercising the writ jurisdiction.
