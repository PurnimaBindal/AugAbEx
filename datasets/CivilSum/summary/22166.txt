The terms and conditions of Compassionate Appointment for dependents of deceased coal mine employees were governed by the National Coal Wage Agreement V, including Clauses 932 and 9.5.0.
A settlement was in place, which was binding and had no period of limitation.
The appellant's first application was rejected due to being a minor at the time, but upon reaching majority, filed an application only to have it rejected again due to not being eligible for employment and delayed filing.
However, the action of the respondent was deemed unfair, unreasonable, and not done in good faith.
Additionally, a public sector undertaking such as this was expected to act fairly and reasonably as per the Constitution of India, under Article 12.
