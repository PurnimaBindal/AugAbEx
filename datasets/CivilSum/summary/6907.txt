Constitution of India, Article 16 - Legality of Compulsory Retirement - A charge was made against the petitioner for falsely stating his date of birth - despite this, the inquiry report was not provided to the petitioner and no show cause notice was given before imposing any penalty - therefore, the compulsory retirement order against the petitioner is a violation of the Rules and is set aside.
