The petitioner in this case sought appointment against seats reserved for the backward class.
However, due to poor performance at the time of the interview, the petitioner was not selected.
The Commission is not obligated to recommend any unsuitable candidate simply because he or she belongs to a backward class.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]

The Constitution of India Article 14 sets forth certain qualification requirements to be considered for a position.
The qualifications that are taken into account are those obtained until the last date of submission of the application.
In this case, the petitioner had obtained a M Phil.
qualification later than that date.
As a result, the Commission was right in not considering the petitioner for the position of Lecturer (College Cadre).
