Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 103 and 114(k) - Award - Appeal - Petitioner was accused of embezzlement, and an arbitrator was appointed to determine the liability of the petitioner regarding the embezzled amount.
However, the findings of the arbitrator cannot be used in departmental proceedings on charges of misconduct.
The appellate authority cannot rely on fresh evidence to interfere with the findings of punishing authority.
The fact that the amount held to be due towards the petitioner by the arbitrator was deposited after the punishment order does not invalidate the order.
The appellate authority acted illegally by setting aside the termination order and directing the petitioner's reinstatement.
Therefore, the order of the appellate authority is quashed.
