Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 311 and Industrial Disputes Act's Section 11-A govern the quantum of punishment, including removal from service.
A conductor was found guilty of failing to punch the tickets he issued to passengers, a charge he repeated five times, leading to his removal from service.
The Labour Court ordered his reinstatement as a fresh entrant, but a Single Judge granted him 50% back wages and continuity of service for the triviality of the charges.
However, the Appellate Bench modified the order by solely directing reinstatement without back wages, due to the proven charge.
