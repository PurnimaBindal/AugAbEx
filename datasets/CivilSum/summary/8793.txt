The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and the Sales Promotion Employees (Conditions of Service) Act, 1976, are in question regarding whether a dispute raised by a sales representative can be tried under the former.
The Industrial Disputes (Amendment) Act, 1982, is also applicable, with its Section 24 Omitting Section 6(2) of the 1976 Act.
However, a notification dated 2181984 shows that the Central Government has not given effect to Section 24 of the Amending Act, 1982, which means that Section 6(2) of the 1976 Act is still in force.
The petitioner argues that sales promotion employees should be treated as workmen by virtue of the deeming clause incorporated in Section 6(2) of 1976 Act, and that the award of the Labour Court is flawed and needs to be quashed.
