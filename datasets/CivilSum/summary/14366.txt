According to the Panjab University Act, 1947 and Panjab University Calendar Volume-I, Chapter 8-A, delay in affiliation of colleges is due to the University needing to conduct inspections and enquiries before granting affiliation.
It is important for both the University and college to take action for affiliation in a timely manner to avoid adverse effects on students.
However, the University cannot be forced to grant affiliation if it is not possible for students to complete their courses at the college in question.
The University is directed to take up the matter for the next year's session.
