Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - A writ petition was filed in 1993 without challenge to the punishment order passed in Dec 1988 but contesting the non-grant of special increments on that basis.
The writ was amended in 1997 without any reasonable explanation for the eight-year delay to contest the punishment order, and was therefore considered belated.
