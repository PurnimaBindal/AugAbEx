According to Sections 79(4) and 80(5) of the Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966, a rural allowance is provided.
When the plaintiff was transferred from the Beas Construction Board to the Bhakra Beas Management Board due to the state's reorganisation, their service conditions changed, and the payment of rural allowance was stopped.
The order to halt payment was found to be unlawful, and the decree and judgments that restrained the Board from doing so were upheld.
