According to Kerala Service Rules, Part I, Rules 31, 32, and 33, past service in aided schools cannot be counted towards increments in government service, as that provision is not permitted by the rules.
Although the Petitioners had previously relied on a government order that allowed for benefits of past service, that order has since been revoked.
Therefore, it is held that the Petitioners do not have any right to the benefits they seek and cannot claim discrimination just because the government made an error by issuing the order.
