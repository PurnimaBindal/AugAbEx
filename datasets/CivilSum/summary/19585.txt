Admission/Education/Sportsmen quota in admissions - If an admission notice includes the condition that incomplete or late applications will not be accepted, a candidate who has not provided their option for admission in the sports quota and has not supplied their bio-data of sports grading cannot be considered for the reserved seats of the sports quota because their application was incomplete.
[Paras 3 and 4] B. Admission/Education/Merit - The action of a college granting admission to a candidate against a reserved seat of sports quota who did not indicate their choice for that category is improper.
However, if the candidate admitted had higher merit than the petitioner, their admission cannot be nullified without justification, and the petitioner alone cannot be considered for the solitary seat without also calling in other candidates higher in merit than the petitioner.
The college has been directed to consider all candidates higher in merit along with the petitioner.
