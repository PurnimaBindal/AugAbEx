The Circular dated 2811987 of The State Bank of India rules that any member of Award Staff who wishes to contest an election must first obtain permission.
Permission is subject to the public and administrative interest and can result in compulsory resignation from the bank.
The Circular complies with the Constitution of India, Articles 19(1)(a), (b) as well as Section 9-A of the Industrial Disputes Act.
