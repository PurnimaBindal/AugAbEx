Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Selection - Appointment - The mere selection of candidates does not guarantee an appointment.
Candidates can only stake a claim in case of discrimination where a junior in the select list is appointed ahead of them.
Public authorities cannot be forced to make appointments beyond their requirement and have discretion to make limited appointments to avoid burdening public expenditure.
In cases where public interest is at odds with private interest, private interest must give way to the public interest.

Constitution of India, Articles 136 & 226 - Res judicata - The non-disclosure of dismissal of an earlier SLP does not need to be disclosed if the SLP is dismissed without a speaking order at the special leave stage.
Such a decision does not constitute the law under Article 141 nor does it operate as res judicata.
[Para 22] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Recruitment - Requisition - The appointing authority has the power to reduce or withdraw its requisition at any time and is not obligated to make all appointments as per the requisition.
The authority cannot be compelled to make appointments beyond their requirement.
