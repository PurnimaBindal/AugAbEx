Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 relates to retrenchment and mandates that if a workman is employed for more than 280 days during the twelve months preceding his termination, he is entitled to the benefits of the said Act.

According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a workman has worked for more than 240 days in the calendar year before his disengagement, he cannot be retrenched until the conditions mentioned in Section 25-F are fulfilled by the employer.
[Para 6]
Section 25-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 pertains to deemed continuous service and stipulates that if a workman has worked for 240 days during a period of 12 calendar months preceding the date with reference to which the calculation is to be made, he would be deemed to have been in continuous service for a period of one year.
