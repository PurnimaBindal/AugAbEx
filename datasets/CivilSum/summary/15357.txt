Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Revised qualifications for the post of Part-time Lecturer in Sanskrit - Application of - Appointment on part-time basis is considered independently from the appointment for regular selection - Petitioner's part-time appointment did not create a right to claim continuance in service - Approval granted by the University for part-time Lecturer is not final for all times to come - Declining approval for appointment due to non-fulfilment of revised qualifications is fully justified as the petitioner was never regularised.
