Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Disputes related to promotion and seniority are a recurring cause of action that financially affects the person every month.
The grievance of the writ petitioner was that he was deprived of his right of promotion, and the appropriate procedure had not been followed while wrong criteria were applied.
The Tribunal wrongly rejected the application on the ground of bar of limitation and threw it away.
The petitioner was not claiming promotion by disturbing the rights of those who had superseded him.
The application ought not to have dismissed on the ground of want of necessary party, and there was no need for hearing juniors.
The Respondents were directed to consider the petitioner's promotion while following appropriate circulars, and if found entitled, the employer would grant promotion w.e.f.
the date the other persons, including juniors, were given promotion.
The order passed by the Tribunal was dismissed.
[Paras 12,15,17,18,19 and 27]
