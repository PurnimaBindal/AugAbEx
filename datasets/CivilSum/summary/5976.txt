Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 115 - Revisional jurisdiction - The Petitioner received a decree in their favour, but the Respondent State was allowed to correct their mistake and the Petitioner was not deemed eligible for inclusion in the PES Class II (School and Inspection) Grade - The decree in favour of the Petitioner still stands compliant - The Petitioner's petition under Article 32 of the Constitution was also dismissed - As a result, the revision petition has been dismissed due to lack of merit.
