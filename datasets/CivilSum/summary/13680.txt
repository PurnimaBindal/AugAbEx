Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 128 and 12.21 - Probationary appointment/Probation/Discharge/Stigma - In case a probationer's work and conduct are deemed unsatisfactory, he/she may be discharged as per the terms of the appointment - The statement regarding the petitioner's inadequacy for appointment in the Punjab Police is merely explanatory, not stigmatizing - The order does not impugn the petitioner's professional prospects.
