Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Punjab Police Service Rules, 1959, Rules 7 and 14 - Appointment - Eligibility conditions - Physical standard - Special considerations for relaxation - The appellant was selected for the post of D.S.P.
but was found deficient in height for 120 cms.
However, due to meritorious services rendered by his brother during terrorism in the State and as per Rule 7, the Govt. of Punjab relaxed the condition for special consideration.
The relaxation policy was not attributed to any favouritism and has to be construed liberally in order to achieve implementation of good policy.
