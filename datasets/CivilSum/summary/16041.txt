Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - Admissions - Education - The petitioner possesses certificates that affirm her father was boarded out due to disability incurred while in military service - The petitioner's application for admission to MBBS courses under the quota reserved for the dependent children of disabled JCOs during Military service was not considered - The respondent whose father was released in 1944 and died in 1978 was granted admission - Deceased father cannot be presumed to have died due to a disease attributed to service - Admission granted to respondent is quashed, and direction provided for admission to be granted to the petitioner.
[Paras 4 and 5]
