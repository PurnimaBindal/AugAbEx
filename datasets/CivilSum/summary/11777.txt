The Punjab Government is permitted to issue instructions for reserving seats for deputing Constables/Head Constables/Asst.
Sub Inspectors of reserved categories for various school courses in order to achieve the object of reservation in promotions.
Such instructions do not violate any provision of the Constitution of India or the Punjab Police Rules, and are necessary to ensure that persons belonging to reserved categories get promoted.
The Government is well within its rights to reserve seats for police officials belonging to these categories in order to achieve the required qualifications for promotion.
The instructions dated 3031979 were not the subject-matter before the Full Bench in Sarbjit Singh's case, and in any case, are not ultra vires of Article 16(4) or inconsistent with Punjab Police Rules.
