Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India relate to Promotion and Selection.
The case in question pertains to the selection of Senior Technical Assistant (Drilling) in Geological Survey of India Department through Departmental Promotion Committee.
As per the rules, the D.P.C.
was required to meet every year to fill up the vacancies that occurred during that year.
However, if the D.P.C.
was unable to meet for any reason, the vacancies during that year cannot be bunched together.
Only those who were eligible at the relevant time for the vacancies occurring during a particular year were entitled to be considered for selection.
Therefore, the D.P.C.
has been directed to consider the petitioners accordingly.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
