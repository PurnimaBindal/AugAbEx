Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Constructual Employment - Petitioner appointed on contract basis - The petitioner's contention that he is entitled to continue up to the age of 60 years as a permanent employee is unfounded - The employment of the petitioner has been contractual in nature and is governed by the terms of the employment contract - After the expiry of the contract period, the petitioner's services have been terminated - There is no legal obligation on the respondent authority to renew the contract of service which expired by efflux of time in terms of the contract - The petitioner has also failed to provide any evidence of rules or regulations governing his service.
