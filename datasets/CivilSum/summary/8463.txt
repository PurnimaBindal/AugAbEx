The Constitution of India, through Articles 14, 16 and 226, pertains to the concept of misconduct in relation to the case of an individual who applied for a loan under Indian Airlines Housing Loan Regulations.
The loan, worth Rs1,23,000, was granted to the petitioner; however, the petitioner then sold the lodging later on without management permission.
The charge sheet was issued as a violation of the terms of the loan granted and as a result guilty of misconduct.
The Supreme Court pointed out that there is no rule stating that an employee's violation of rules and regulations would constitute misconduct.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot be charged against by the first respondent.
