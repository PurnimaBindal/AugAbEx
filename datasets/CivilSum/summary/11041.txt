Constitution of India, Article 16 - Cadre bifurcation - Diversion of an existing cadre into more than one must be based on rational criteria - Categorisation of employees depends on last posting - Assistants cannot be categorized in the bifurcated cadre without last option - Employer cannot arbitrarily categorize employees in different cadres - Option must be given to all employees to get categorized in one of the cadres - Categorization decided by order of seniority if all employees cannot be accommodated in their desired cadre.
