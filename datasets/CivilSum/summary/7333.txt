Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9, Rule 13 - Ex parte order - Setting aside of - The ex parte order should be set aside since the appellant's advocate joined judicial services without informing the appellant, and the appellant cannot be held accountable for the mistake made by the advocate.
