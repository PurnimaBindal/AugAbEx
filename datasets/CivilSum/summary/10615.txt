The petitioner challenged an order of termination from service in the case concerning Constitution of India, Article 311 and West Bengal Municipal Act, 1993, Section 61.
The petitioner received a show cause notice about the utilization of the money.
An Enquiry Committee was formed on finding his reply unsatisfactory.
Unfortunately, the petitioner did not receive a copy of the enquiry report and his comments were also not available before disciplinary authority.
As a result, gross injustice was caused to the petitioner by non-observance of principles of natural justice.
[Paras 23 and 24] B.
In the case dealing with Constitution of India, Article 311 and West Bengal Municipal Act, 1993, Section 61, the petitioner was dismissed from service due to failure to submit detailed accounts about money advanced to him.
Though he sought 15 days' time to submit detailed accounts, his failure to do so did not amount to an admission of guilt.
Thus, the order of his dismissal was not proper.
As a result, his reinstatement in service was ordered with consequential benefits.
