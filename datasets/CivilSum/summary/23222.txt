According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 309, the Regularisation Policy states that any appointments made without advertising the vacancies in open to the general public or through employment exchanges are considered illegal and irregular.
Rules or executive instructions cannot be made under Article 162 or 309 of the Constitution for regularising such backdoor entries.
This would be in violation of the rules under Article 309 of the Constitution and go against equality clauses mentioned in Articles 14 and 16.
