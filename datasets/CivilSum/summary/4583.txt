A bank employee was dismissed after an enquiry was conducted, but principles of natural justice were not followed.
The tribunal did not make its award until it was directed to do so by the High Court, and the award was passed on the same day that the High Court's order was served.
The proceedings before the enquiry officer and tribunal were therefore vitiated, and an order of reinstatement was passed without back wages.
