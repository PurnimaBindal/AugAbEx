Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Sections 9, 10(3) and 15 - Privately managed Schools - Schools Tribunals - During the pendency of an appeal regarding the termination of a private school teacher's service before the Director, Schools Tribunal is constituted - The appeal is transferred to the Tribunal and decided more than one year after the prescribed period of three months - The order of the Tribunal is not vitiated due to the want of jurisdiction to decide the appeal after the three-month period - When an exclusive forum is established by statute, the jurisdiction of other authorities is ousted by necessary implication.
[Paras 21, 26, and 29]  B. Bombay Primary Education Rules, 1949, Rule 9(1)(2) - Termination - Enquiry - Privately-managed Schools - The services of a permanent teacher are terminated without holding an enquiry - The Tribunal correctly rejected the termination.
