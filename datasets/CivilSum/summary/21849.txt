The Tribunal has suggested that a separate plan for workmen's regularization be made by the Reserve Bank to address the Ticca Mazdoors/daily rated employees.
The situation was further examined by the Apex Court on the issue of standards in different sectors of the country, concluding that workmen must be regularised with effect from 31594 according to the issued settlement.
Consequently, orders have been given that the members represented by Reserve Bank Workers Organisation for regularization are appropriately processed according to the directions given in C.A.
No. 7407/1994 determined on 28.11.95.
