Constitution of India, Articles 16, 41 and 310 - Rajasthan Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Act, 1959 - Government servants - Age relaxation in promotions - Members of State Civil Service who work in Panchayat Raj Institutions, including teachers in Panchayat Samitis, are entitled to age relaxation for promotions and appointment to the posts of Lecturer, as are other State Government employees.
