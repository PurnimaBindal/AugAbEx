Constitution of India, Article 226 - Examination/Education - Leakage of question papers - Copying - Examination of School Education Board - The cancellation of the exam was justified due to mass copying and a report from the Naib Tehsildar and paper valuers indicating that teachers did not object to cheating.
The High Court was not justified in overturning this decision and ignoring the report on technical grounds.
It is challenging for the Board to differentiate between innocent and guilty students in cases of such widespread cheating.
