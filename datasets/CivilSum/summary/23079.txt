Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Service Law - If the State's actions were not bona fide and/or unfair, the High Court could issue a writ of or in the nature of Mandamus.
[Para 14]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Madhya Pradesh Non Gazetted Class III Education Service (Non Collegiate Service), Recruitment and Promotion Rules, 1973, Rule 10(3) - Recruitment of Assistant Teachers - `Operation Black Board' - The State cannot make any appointment in absence of a Select List, unless the action is found to be unfair, unreasonable or mala fide.
[Paras 13 and 19]

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 26 - Service Law - Writ jurisdiction - Ordinarily, the writ court cannot act on the basis of sympathy alone and selected candidates do not have a legal right in this regard for recruitment to the post of Assistant teacher.
