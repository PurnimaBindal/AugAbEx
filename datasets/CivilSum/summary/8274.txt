Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Schedule II, items 1(a) and (b) and Schedule IV, Items 9 and 10 - Respondent-employer did not maintain statutory records or pay minimum wages - After workmen joined petitioner's Union, they were not all allowed to resume duty - The relationship between the respondent and workmen clearly established employer-employee - Thus, it was held that the respondent-employer committed unfair labour practices - Respondent-employer was ordered to cease and desist from engaging in unfair labour practices and allow workmen to resume duty with 50% of back wages, continuity of service and consequential benefits.
