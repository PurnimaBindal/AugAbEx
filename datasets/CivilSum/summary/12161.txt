Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Indian Administrative Service (Cadre) Rules, 1954, Rules 6, 8 and 7 - Deputation - Transfer on deputation - Posting against non-cadre post - Cadre officers can be deputed to a non-cadre post without their consent, but such a decision has to be made fairly and without bias - Cadre officers possess the right to choose to remain in their cadre posts if there is no pressing need for their transfer - It would be unfair to force a cadre officer to take deputation to a non-cadre post if non-cadre officers are already occupying cadre posts for a long duration or if some officers have the responsibility of two cadre posts - The number of available non-cadre posts does not obligate the government to fill all of them with cadre officers.
