According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, there may be seasonal workers appointed on daily wages for specific seasons.
However, a workman who was not appointed for sugar crushing work which is considered to be seasonal cannot be held as a seasonal worker.
In this case, the Morinda Co-operative Sugar Mill case principles are not applicable and those laid down in State of UP v. Rajendra Singh Butola will support the case.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5] B Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that in conciliation proceedings, if a conditional offer of reinstatement is made to the workman before the Conciliation Officer, the workman cannot be obligated to accept it.
This does not create any bar to reference.
