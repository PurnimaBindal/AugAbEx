Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Para 25 of Chapter II - Correction of date of birth - Permission - Application made after 20 years of service - Petitioner who joined the army in 1938 sought to change date of birth from 1920 to 1924 - It was held that the petitioner's claim was contradicted by his own conduct, as he could not have joined the army without being 18 years of age - Additionally, representation could not be made after two years of joining service as per rules.
