Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gujarat University Act (Bombay Act 50 of 1949), Section 58 (as amended by Gujarat Act 9 of 1983) - Alternative remedy - Writ petition contesting election of members to "Court" of Gujarat University - Is not maintainable due to the mandatory provision of Section 58, which bars any dispute related to election of university 'Authority' to be referred to State Government.
