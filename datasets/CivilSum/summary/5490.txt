According to the Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 8(2), minority aided schools are exempt from the provisions of Section 8(2) in order to provide full independence and autonomy to such institutions, as opposed to Article 19(1) of the Constitution of India, which imposes reasonable limitation.
The rights bestowed upon the minority educational institution, whether aided or unaided, is unbounded and unrestricted, and cannot be revoked solely because it is an aided school.
