Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Termination - Absence from duty - An enquiry must be held by the Government as given in the mandatory requirements of the law - The order of termination is void if such mandatory provisions are not followed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Limitation Act, 1963 - Relief Act, 1963, Section 34 - Termination/Void order/Suit for declaration can be specified - There is no limitation period for challenging a void order - A declaratory suit is not required to set aside a void order - A void order can be ignored.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Remand - Back wages are granted on reinstatement - If the employee was reinstated on a technical ground and the absence was deliberate, back wages cannot be granted - The period of past service can be counted towards seniority, fixation of pay and pension, etc. [Para 7]
