A. Punjab State Education Class-III (School Cadre) Service Rules, 1978, Rules 5 and 9(1) read with Appendix `B' entry 3 - Qualifications - B.Ed.
is not a requirement for the posts of Agriculture Master, Block Primary Education Officer, Commerce Master and Physical Training Masters - It was deemed illegal to advertise B.Ed.
as one of the essential qualifications for such posts as it was not included in the Rules.
However, the recommendations of the Selection Committee including those without B.Ed.
qualification were not deemed illegal.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Article 309 - Instructions - Government cannot issue administrative instructions laying down additional qualifications to those already prescribed in the Rules as it would amount to amending the Rules which cannot be done through administrative instructions.
[Paras 6 to 8] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Recruitment - Advertisements for posts with additional qualifications not in accordance with the recruitment Rules are illegal, and the selection of candidates who are qualified under the Rules cannot be upheld because some qualified candidates may not have applied due to the additional qualification requirement.
The authorities are directed to issue a fresh advertisement in accordance with the Rules and hold a new selection process.
