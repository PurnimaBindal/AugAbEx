According to the Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 311, a petitioner was not technically reverted from the post of Director Animal Husbandry to that of Joint Director, and as such an undertaking was made to refund any excess amount required.
The outcome of a Writ petition dismissed as infructuous did not require the petitioner to refund any excess pay as an order stipulated, and the undertaking did not imply that any future refund would be necessary if it were determined that the petitioner was not as senior as someone else.
The impugned order was quashed and the gratuity deduction for the petitioner annulled.
