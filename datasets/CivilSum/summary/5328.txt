The admission of the petitioner was granted provisionally, after the condition of eligibility was relaxed and there was no misrepresentation on the part of the statement.
The cancellation of admission at this stage is not appropriate, as the decision of the government and the university altered the condition of the brochure.
The authorities should not be permitted to cancel the admission of the petitioner at this stage.
[Para 12] B.
When authorities grant provisional admission by relaxing the conditions of admission, they cannot later withdraw that condition.
The conditions of the brochure are binding on the candidates and have the force of law.
The authorities cannot withdraw the condition, especially when the student has taken two semesters worth of examinations.
[Para 6] C. When there is a change in policy, the authorities are required to notify the candidates of the consequences.
The students must not suffer any avoidable prejudice to their academic courses.
