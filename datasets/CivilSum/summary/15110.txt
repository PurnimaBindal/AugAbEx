Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Contractual appointment - A Sanskrit Teacher was appointed on a contracted basis for 89 days and relieved in July 1997.
The teacher claims appointment on the basis of an earlier appointment for 89 days in 2006.
However, it was held that there is no legal right to claim reappointment, following the 2006 4 SCC 1 case.
