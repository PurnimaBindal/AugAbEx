The Indian Railway Traffic (S&T Deptt.
Class-I) Services Rules, Rule 4(b) states that 40% of the promotion quota is reserved for Class II Officers to be promoted to Class-I However, if there is a shortage of direct recruits or officers in Group `A', the rule provides for variation in the promotion percentage.
This variation can be either upward or downward and should be based on rational and relevant considerations.
The provision for variation is equivalent to the power of relaxation and is meant to provide leeway in administrative interest and unforeseen contingencies.
The Union of India did not commit any illegality in appointing 127 Group `B' officers of S&T Department of Railways to the junior scale of Group `A' and the Tribunal erred in interpreting the rule by stating that 38 officers had to be promoted on a regular basis against future vacancies.
