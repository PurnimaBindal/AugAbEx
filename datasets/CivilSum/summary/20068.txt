Recruitment as a Delhi Police Constable includes a verification of character and antecedents by the local police, even if the candidate passes the physical endurance, written, and interview exams.
The respondent in this case was provisionally selected, but it was subsequently discovered that they had been involved in IPC offences under Sections 304 and 324/34.
The respondent's name was rejected, but their case was subsequently heard by a tribunal who allowed their application for appointment to the constable post, arguing that they had been discharged or acquitted.
The appeal overturned the tribunal's decision, stating that the view was incorrect.
