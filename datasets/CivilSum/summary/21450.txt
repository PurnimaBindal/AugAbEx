Bihar Regional Development Authority Act, 1981, Section 6(3) - Appointment - Appellants were employed on a daily wage basis - The Commission was correct to reject the government's request for the Public Service Commission's concurrent for regularization.

The High Court was correct in not issuing a mandamus for the regularization of services that violated the rules and no mandamus or direction will be given to break the law.
