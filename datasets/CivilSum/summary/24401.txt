Civil Procedure Code, Order 47, Rule 1 - Review - The scope of review jurisdiction in exercise of power of judicial review of the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution is quite broad - The Court must not be reluctant to review its own earlier order when an error is detected on the face of the record and the interest of justice so demands in appropriate cases - The Court must not overlook the materials on record that may justify the petitioner's claim on proper consideration - If the applicant is able to satisfy the court as to why he was not able to produce relevant documents at the time of passing the order sought to be reviewed, and the documents go to the root of the matter, the Court should consider the same on merits even if they were not available on record at the time.
