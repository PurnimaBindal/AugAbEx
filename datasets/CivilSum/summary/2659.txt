Mr. Suryanarayana Rao, Advocate, is mentioned in No. 2.
Sections 21 and 22-A of the Chartered Accountants Act 1949, along with Regulations 67(1) and 67(3) of the Chartered Accountants Regulations Act 1968, pertain to a complaint made by an Articled clerk of a Chartered Accountant.
The report submitted after inquiry shows that the requests of the petitioner Chartered Accountant to drop further proceedings regarding the complaints of the Articled clerk were rejected.
It is held that the Council's substantial powers under Section 21 read with Regn.
12 are not affected by the preliminary inquiries under Section 67, and only the Council can make the final decision.
