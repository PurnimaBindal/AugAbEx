The Constitution of India under Article 16 and Delhi School Education Rules Rule 47(2) states the protection of salary for Assistant teachers who have been declared surplus and transferred to another primary school.
However, the petitioners are only being paid 95% of their salary.
Petition for full payment of salary is being made as Rule 47(2) clearly mandates the protection of salary.
The petitioners are entitled to full salary and allowances as per Paras 5 to 7.
