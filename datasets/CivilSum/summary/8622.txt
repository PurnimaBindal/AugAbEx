Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 3 and 4 - Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules, 1982, Rules 9(3) - Military service benefit - The benefits of 1965 Rules were taken away by 1982 Rules prospectively - People who joined Civil Service after the 1982 rules came into force are not entitled to the benefits under the 1965 Rules irrespective of their service during the 1962 emergency - The petitioner was not promised that he would get the benefits proclaimed under 1965 rules when he joined military before 1965 - The benefits would have been available on joining Civil Service, but until then the Rules stood repealed - The Government has the right to withdraw the concessions - The petitioner who joined Civil service after coming into force of 1982 Rules, is not entitled to any benefit under 1965 Rules.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 9(3) - Discrimination - Classification - The 1982 Rules created a classification between those who joined Civil Services before 12 February 1982 and those who joined after - The two groups will form class apart - There is no discrimination between similarly placed persons - Therefore, there is no question of discrimination - Rule 9(3) of the 1982 Rules cannot be considered discriminatory.
