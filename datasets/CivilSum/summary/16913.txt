Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25N - Retrenchment - Due to a worldwide recession resulting in a decrease in production and lack of orders, management requested permission to retrench some of the junior-most employees.
Relevant documents were presented as evidence, but an issue was raised regarding whether the workers union had the right to object to them and be heard.
According to Section 25N(3), employees must also be given an opportunity to be heard in addition to the employer, and the workers' interest must be considered.
Therefore, the impugned orders were quashed since they only provided a reasonable opportunity to the management and not the employees.
[Paras 6.3 and 64]
