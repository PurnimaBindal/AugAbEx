Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Mandamus - The petitioner filed a petition as they were not promoted even though they were senior to Respondent No.2, who was posted as Engineer-in-Chief on 1.11.1983 instead - The court allowed the petition and directed the respondents to consider the matter afresh - The petitioner was then promoted and posted as Officer on Special Duty in the grade of Engineer-in-Chief by an order dated 1101984, while Respondent No.2 was posted as Engineer-in-Chief on 1101984 - Petitioner argues that they should have been promoted as Engineer-in-Chief w.e.f.
1.11.1983 in the cadre post and not against the post temporarily created from 1.11.1983 to 3091984 - The court directed that for all practical purposes, the petitioner should be treated as promoted w.e.f.
1.11.1983.
