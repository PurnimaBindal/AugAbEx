Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Qualification Relaxation - Subsequent Promotion - Despite lacking the necessary qualification, the petitioners were promoted as UD Clerks due to a lack of qualified candidates - Respondent Nos. 2 to 11 were shown as junior to the petitioners - However, respondent No. 1 was promoted to the post of Superintendents in violation of the seniority list - It was held that the relaxation of qualification cannot be restricted to a particular stage and promoting respondents No. 2 to 11 as Superintendents in preference to the petitioner in violation of the seniority list was not justified.
