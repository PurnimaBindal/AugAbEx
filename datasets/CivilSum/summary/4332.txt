Constitution of India, Article 14 - Regularisation of services/Qualification/Experience - If an employee has extensive experience on a post, it can be deemed equivalent to basic qualifications, even if they were lacking those qualifications at the time of entry into service.
As long as the employee did not misrepresent or conceal any qualification-related facts during their temporary appointment and has been allowed to continue in service for a significant period of time, the authorities cannot object to their lack of qualifications while considering their case for regularization or confirmation on that post or terminating their services on that ground.
The Supreme Court decisions in AIR 1990 SC 371 and 1991(2) RSJ 66 are followed.
