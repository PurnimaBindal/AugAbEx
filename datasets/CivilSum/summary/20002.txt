Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226 - Interest - In order for interest to be granted on equitable considerations, it must be examined whether there are any special circumstances that justify such a grant, despite the absence of a legal provision - The respondent was awarded selection grade (non-functional) in the post of DC of income-tax from January 1, 1986 - In 1992, the respondent received a payment as a result of the selection grade being granted - The respondent now seeks interest on this amount from January 1, 1986 until the end of March 1992 - The tribunal provided the respondent with an interest rate of 12% per annum for that period - However, there are no circumstances in favour of the respondent that would require interest to be granted from January 1, 1986 - Since the departmental enquiry is still ongoing and the respondent has not been absolved of the charges against him, granting interest would be wholly unfair - The decision is set aside.
[Paras 9 and 10]
