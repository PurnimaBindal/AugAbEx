According to Haryana School Education Act, 1995, Sections 8(2) and 25, and Haryana School Education Rules, 2003, Rule 87, the termination of employees during probation from aided schools requires prior approval from the Education Department.
Applying old rules that have been repealed under Section 25 of the 1995 Act is not permissible, and such a contradictory stand cannot be taken by State Authorities.
Even if such a stand is taken, the court is not bound to accept it and the termination order cannot be sustained without prior approval.
[Paras 8 to 10]
