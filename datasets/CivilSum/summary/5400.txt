Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Reinstatement - The Labour Court ordered the 1st respondent be reinstated without back wages as it found the termination to violate Section 25-F.
The issue of irregularities in the respondent's attendance was not raised during the proceedings before the Labour Court and therefore cannot be raised in a writ petition.
The Labour Court considered the evidence and directed reinstatement, and the High Court cannot reexamine the evidence under Article 226.
The award of the Labour Court was upheld.
