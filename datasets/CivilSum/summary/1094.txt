Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Last drawn wages - Payment of full wages to workman pending proceedings in Higher Court - Whether equal to minimum wages - There is conflict regarding whether to pay the last drawn wages or the current minimum wages.
Payment is treated as a subsistence allowance that is not recoverable even if management succeeds.
The difference between the two wages must be deposited in a fixed deposit until a final decision is made.
[Paras 5 and 8]
