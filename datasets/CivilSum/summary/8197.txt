Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act - Withdrawal of resignation - Scope of - A workman who volunteered to join duty but was not permitted to may be entitled to wages for the period after volunteering.
No calculation or decision is necessary.
If there is no order from a competent authority preventing payment of the wages, withholding the amount of the wages without justification is not permitted, and as a result, the award is not beyond the jurisdiction of the Labour Court under Section 33-C(2) of the Act.
