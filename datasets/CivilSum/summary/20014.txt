The Accountant General's Manual, Para 320 - Seniority - Inter se seniority - Promotion - Seniority of Divisional Accountants - Divisional Accountants must pass the Divisional Accounts test to be promoted to Grade I - Paragraph 320 specifically states that the date of passing the Divisional Accounts test determines inter se seniority - Therefore, inter se seniority should be determined based on the date the respective candidate passed the Accounts test.
