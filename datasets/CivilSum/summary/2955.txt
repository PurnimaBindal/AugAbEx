According to the National Council for Teachers Education Act 1993, private individuals cannot be appointed on deputation unless both the parent and borrowing department are governmental, semi-governmental, or a statutory department.
A petitioner who applied for the post of Regional Director in the Council, who was working as Principal in a private institute, was appointed on deputation without it being mentioned in the advertisement that the post was on deputation.
The petitioner was subsequently terminated from their former employer and requested to be either relieved or made regular.
The petitioner contended that their non-relieving gave them the right to continue, but the department was bound by the terms of the ad.
The court held that no civil status could be conferred on a private individual who was appointed on deputation and allowed the petitioner to retain the job.
[Paras 22, 31, 46, 48 and 49]
