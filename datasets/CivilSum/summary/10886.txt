The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, as well as the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1)(d), covers the issue of the legality of a strike.
A financial commissioner was asked to determine whether a strike by a certain group of workers was legal or not.
One party claimed that there was a strike whereas the other party claimed there was not.
The dispute over the fact that a strike had occurred or not meant that a specific issue should have been classified.
The case was referred to the financial commissioner for consideration of the issue.
