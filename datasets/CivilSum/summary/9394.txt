The cancellation of admission for medical courses based on a lower score obtained by petitioners who were admitted on September 30, 2006, than other candidates who took the PMET, was deemed unacceptable in the absence of complying with natural justice principles.
It was also mentioned that there were candidates on the waiting list who had obtained a higher PMET score that could have taken their place.
These circumstances refer to Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Indian Constitution.
[Paras 10-12, 14-15, and 19]
