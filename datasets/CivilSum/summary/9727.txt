According to Section 124 of the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir and Rule 14 of Jammu and Kashmir (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules (1956), an ad hoc appointee for Sainik Welfare cannot claim continuation of service beyond nine months in violation of Rule 14.
Even if the appointee continued in terms of an interim direction of the Court, this does not grant him the right to continue beyond the two-month appointment period as the post was already filled up.
[Paras 6 and 9]
