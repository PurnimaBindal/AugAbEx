According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44, promotion of J.B.T.
teachers as Head teachers requires giving one increment of higher responsibility to promotees.
[Para 2]  B.
The appointment of Respondent No. 3 was done illegally and unconstitutionally as the authorities did not advertise the post to enable all eligible persons to apply and compete for the position.
As per the principle of "ACTLIS LEGITIMT NON RECIPLIM MODLIM", when doing anything in a particular sanctioned manner, it cannot be done differently.
The order of appointment for Respondent No. 3 has been quashed and the respondents have been directed to fill the post afresh as per the law.
