Employees of an instrumentality of State can avail constitutional remedy under Articles 32, 136, and 226 when there is no statutory remedy available to them, according to the Constitution of India's Articles 226, 32, and 136 and Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act.
While citizens have both the remedy of reference under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act and the fundamental right to judicial review, these remedies will not apply to employees holding a Civil Post governed by statutory rules.
Instead, such employees are entitled to remedy under the Administrative Tribunals Act and are excluded from the protection of Section 10 of the ID Act, as explained in Paragraphs 9 to 11.
