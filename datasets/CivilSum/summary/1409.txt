Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I Rule 7(3) (i) - Reinstatement/Back Wages/Arrears of service- If the court declares the order of dismissal or removal from service as illegal and unwarranted, then the employee is entitled to the consequential relief of recovery of pay.
[Para 10]

Fundamental Rule 54(3)(b) - Bar Council of India Rules, Rule 51 - Gainful Employment/Advocate - A practising lawyer can claim his salary after getting a declaration in his favour even if there is no evidence of earnings from the profession of Advocate.
[Paras 10 and 11]

Civil Procedure Code, Section 11, Order 2, Rule 2 - Res judicata/Matter in issue - Unless a matter was directly and substantially in issue for adjudication and not heard and decided finally, any court observation on a subsequently raised matter will not bind as res judicata - Previous suit for declaration of plaintiff's dismissal from service as illegal did not address arrears of salary - The court's observation that he was not entitled to arrears of salary does not bar subsequent litigation for recovery of salary arrears.
[Para 8]
