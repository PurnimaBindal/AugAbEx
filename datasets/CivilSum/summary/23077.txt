Casual Labourers (Grant of Temporary status and Regularization) Scheme of Government of India, 1993, Clause 4 - Seasonal/Casual labourers - Minimum pay scale to be paid to those granted 'Temporary Status' - Scheme effective from September 1, 1993 - Only casual labourers who were employed on the scheme's commencement date are eligible for temporary status - It is not a blanket policy for all casual workers - However, the Union Government may introduce a scheme when necessary - High Court's view that this is an ongoing scheme is not defensible.
