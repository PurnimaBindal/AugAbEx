Rajasthan Engineering Subordinate Service (Public Health Branch) Rules, 1967, Rule 28 - Vires of the Rule - Seniority - Petitioner disputed his seniority placement below those who earned a Degree after he received his Diploma and contested the legality of Rule 28 - The petition was dismissed as the Supreme Court had already passed a judgment on a similar issue in Vijay Singh Deora and others - The validity of Rule 28 was not the subject of the previous case, which only addressed the non-compliance of seniority during application of the Rule - The High Court's decision was overturned and the case was sent back to be reconsidered.
