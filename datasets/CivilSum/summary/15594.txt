The Constitution of India, Articles 226/16 - Overseas Employment and Export Promotion Corporation - offering voluntary retirement scheme to employes with retirement compensation at the rate of three months' salary for each completed year of service and Gratuity - A petitioner who accepted the offer brought a case where it was held that the benefits under the scheme cannot be denied by subsequently modifying the terms - The court issued a direction to pay Gratuity amount and retirement compensation - The petition was allowed.
[Paras 10 and 11]
