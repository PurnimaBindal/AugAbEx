Central Ground Water Board (Scientific Group A Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1995, Rule 7(2)(b) - Promotion - Alleged discrimination in promotion of departmental candidates and deputationists - Promotions were declined due to insufficient experience - Tribunal granting relief to respondents was outside its jurisdiction - Mandamus cannot be issued contrary to Act and Rules - The argument that deputationists and departmental candidates form one class of officers is irrelevant in the absence of a challenge to the vires of rules.
[Paras 11 and 12] B Court decision - Precedent - Illegal decisions cannot be perpetuated - Decisions contrary to law and rules do not become precedents - A decision becomes a precedent when it decides the law in accordance with Act and Rules.
