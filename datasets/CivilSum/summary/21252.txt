The Constitutional rules of India state that a ratio of 1:1 must be prescribed for promotion to the post of Excise Preventive Officer from Excise Guard between those who have the qualification of SSLC and those who do not - This rule must be considered reasonable, as per the decision of T.R.
Kothandaraman v. Tamil Nadu Water Supply & Drainage Board - The impugned judgment has been set aside - Appeal allowed.
[Paras 1, 5 to 7] B.
The rule providing a ratio of 1:1 for promotion gives both categories an equal opportunity, to some extent, but may result in some non-SSLC Excise Guards getting promoted later to a post meant for SSLC qualified Excise Guards - The senior most non-SSLC Excise Guards are likely to be promoted next, as that would fulfill the vacancy meant for such an incumbent - This means that the chance of promotion for non-SSLC Excise Guards is only postponed, not denied - According to settled law, promotion cannot be claimed as a right under Article 16, since no incumbent has a right to be promoted - Thus, change of promotion is not covered within the right visualised by Article 16.
