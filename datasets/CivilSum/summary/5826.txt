According to Sections 34 and 35 of the Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, the Road Transport Corporation is obligated to follow the directions issued by the State Government, even with regards to temporary and ad hoc appointments of drivers and conductors.
When the Government imposed a ban on recruitment, the Corporation had to comply and halt recruitment until the ban was lifted.
Temporary and ad hoc appointments were necessary to carry on the Corporation's affairs during the intervening period, but these employees would need to step aside for those appointed based on merit.
Even though there may have been a legitimate expectation of continued service by some, there is no crystallized right so relief cannot be claimed.
The Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation has the power to make temporary appointments under Regulation 19 when it is deemed necessary for administrative purposes.
