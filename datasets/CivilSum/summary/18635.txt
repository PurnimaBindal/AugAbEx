West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973, Section 106 - Rules Regulating Recruitment and Leave of Teachers in Primary Schools in West Bengal, 1991, Rule 14(b) - Interpretation of - Compassionate appointment - The petitioner made an application for compassionate appointment in favour of his daughter, along with seeking premature retirement at least 21 days and two years before he attained age of superannuation as per Rule 14(b).
However, the respondent-council denied the appointment on the ground that the teacher was declared permanently disabled within 2 years before his superannuation.
The court held that this decision was not sustainable and the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of compassionate appointment in favour of his ward as per Rule 14(b).
