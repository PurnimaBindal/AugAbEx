Industrial Employment Standing Orders Act, 1946, Section 2 - Certifying Officer - Even after being transferred from Chandigarh to Hyderabad, the Certifying Officer retains jurisdiction over the matter - He certified standing orders that reduced the retirement age from 60 to 58 years - This order is valid despite an earlier withdrawn application from the Management and despite one of the unions not being heard - The concept of collective bargaining applies and previously employed individuals are entitled to benefits.
