According to the Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173(A) and 179, Army Service Pension Rules, Annexure II, Para 7, Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards 1982, Rules 27(c), disability pension is determined based on the percentage of disability.
In the case of "POLIOMYELITIES EFFECTS OF," the petitioner had been placed in category "EEE" after several medical board and re-survey medical board assessments.
CCDA(P) is not authorized to ignore the findings of the Medical Board and reduce disability without proper justification or give an opportunity for further medical examination.
Due to the arbitrary rejection of the claim, the authority has been directed to pay arrears of the entire disability pension from his entitlement date.
