Under Rule 9.18(1) of Punjab Police Rules, CSR Volume I, Part I, Rule 3.26(d), and CSR Vol-II, Rule 5.32(1)(c), the appointing authority has complete authority to retire any Government employee at the age of 55 without giving reasons.
If an employee's integrity is found to be doubtful, it is not in the state's best interest to retain such an individual in public service.
Further, the questioned order is not stigmatic, resulting in the rejection of the petition.
