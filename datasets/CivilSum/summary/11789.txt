Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Recognition of school and certificate - Admission to B.Ed.
Course - The appellant's certificate was not recognized, but was countersigned by Education Department officials and accepted by University authorities - The appellant was allowed to join and complete the course and sit for exams - The School authorities and Education Department officers are to blame for making the University believe the certificate's genuineness - The University cannot shift the blame to the appellant for not verifying the document upon admission - The petitioner's result should be declared and if there was fraud, it should be investigated further with equity.
