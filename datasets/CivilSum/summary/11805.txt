Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Military pension - Permissibility of Deduction - The petitioners were re-employed in Central Industrial Security Force with new pay terms and no condition attached for deduction of Military Pension.
Moreover, they were receiving Military Pension previously - The action of deducting enhanced military pension retrospectively from the pay of the petitioners is illegal and quashed - Respondents are directed to re-fix their pay by ignoring military pension and refrain from making any recovery of alleged arrears - Any recovered amount will be refunded to petitioners within three months.
[Paras 3,4 and 5]
