Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 309 - West Bengal Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Vacancies in Service and Posts) Act, 1976, Section 5(a) - Promotion - Reservation - Roster System - Seniority - The petitioners belonging to the reserved category must be considered independently using the roster system as mandated by the State Government.
They cannot be clubbed with the general category candidates.
Additionally, seniority of an employee should be counted from the date of permanent appointment and not from the date of confirmation.
[Paras 9, 14, 18, and 23]
