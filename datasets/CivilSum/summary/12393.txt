Constitution of India, Article 309 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 43(b) - Border Security Force (Seniority, Promotion and Superannuation of Officers) Rules, 1975, Rule 9 - The petitioner claims for an extension of service up to the age of 58 years, but the retirement age of the petitioner's category is 55 years.
The extension can only be granted in exceptional circumstances.
The court cannot act as a superior administrative authority and this matter falls within the purview of the executive authorities.
The decision will be taken by administrative authorities and judicial review is not permissible.
