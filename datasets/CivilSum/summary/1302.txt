The Railway Protection Force Act, 1979 along with the Probation of Offenders Act, 1958, Section 12, state that a convicted person can receive the benefit of probation.
However, in the case of a constable in the Railway Protection Force who received probation but was later dismissed from their position after an inquiry, the order of dismissal due to conviction does not qualify as a disqualification according to Section 12 of the Act.
