The petitioner in a case involving the Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 226, had their pension delayed due to not submitting the required two sets of pension papers.
However, the petitioner only submitted one set, causing a lapse on their part.
The pension was still released to the petitioner within four months, and the court determined there was no inordinate delay on the part of the respondents that would make the state liable for payment of interest.
As a result, the appeal was dismissed.
