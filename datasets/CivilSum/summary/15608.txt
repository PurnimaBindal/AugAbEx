Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Seniority - Promotion - The petitioner, a former Major in the Army, contends that respondents 5 to 28 have been senior to him unfairly and respondents 29 to 31 were promoted to Lt. Col. above him.
Petitioner seeks to surpass those respondents in seniority and obtain promotion to Lt. Col.
The court ruled in favor of the petitioner, recommending that he be treated as if he were promoted to Lt. Col. on the same day as respondents 29 to 31, with his pension based on that pay.
The petitioner's relief is limited to being treated as if he were retiring as Lt. Col. and having his pension and other retirement benefits calculated accordingly.
