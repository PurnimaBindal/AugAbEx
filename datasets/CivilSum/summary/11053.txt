Promotion from the post of Executive Engineer to that of Superintending Engineer in Delhi Development Authority - Persons in the consideration zone - Cannot be ignored from consideration for promotion mere on proposed/contemplated departmental proceedings unless a charge sheet is issued to him - The authority has no jurisdiction to invoke the power of sealed cover promotion till the charge sheet is issued - Pending proceedings cannot exist until a charge sheet is issued.
[Paras 10 and 11]

Promotion withheld illegally by resorting to sealed cover promotion procedure - Employee held entitled to full arrears of salary from the date his promotion order was put under sealed cover and his juniors were promoted, more so when the person is not found guilty of the charge - Doctrine of "No work no pay" does not apply where the employee was not allowed to work on the entitled post.
[Paras 11 to 13]
