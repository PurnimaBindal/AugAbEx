Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A - Compensation - Interest - Compensation is payable from the date of the accident, not from the date of determining the amount.
Liability to pay compensation arises when personal injury to the workman occurs from the accident - In case of delay in payment, the workman is entitled to receive interest at the rate of 6% per annum from the date of the accident until the amount is paid.
