Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of service element of disability pension - An individual who was discharged from Army due to tuberculosis was granted a disability pension, which was later stopped following a reassessment of their disability at less than 20%.
However, the appellant was directed to pay the service element of the pension in light of previous court decisions.
The pension would be paid retroactively from 38 months prior to the date of the suit, and would also include interest at a rate of 8% per year.
