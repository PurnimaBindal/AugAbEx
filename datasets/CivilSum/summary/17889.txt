As per the instructions of the Punjab Government, reservations for the handicapped are applicable under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The reservation at points 11, 43, and 71 in the roster have been set at 1% each for the blind, deaf, and orthopaedically handicapped.
Despite 20 available positions, no handicapped candidate was appointed.
The petitioner was placed at the 71st reserved point, which can be interchanged with other categories of handicapped if no person of that category is available at a given roster point.
As the 11th point was not given to any handicapped, the petitioner has the right to be considered against it with seniority.
The respondent-department has been directed to conduct a reconsideration of the petitioner against the 11th point.
