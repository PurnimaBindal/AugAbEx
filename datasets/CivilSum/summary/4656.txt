Goa University Statutes, Section 15 Note 6 and Section 4315 - Constitution of India, Article 226 Appointment/Offer of Appointment/Offer of appointment/Contract of service - The petitioner was selected and offered employment, and after he accepted, he resigned from his previous job.
The offer of employment cannot be withdrawn, and once accepted, the contract of employment becomes valid even if the performance is postponed.
Additionally, objections to qualifications cannot be raised after selecting a candidate, and offers of appointment cannot be withdrawn on this ground.
[Paras 10 to 12]
