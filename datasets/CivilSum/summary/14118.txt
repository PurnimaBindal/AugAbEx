Per the Punjab Govt. to National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Section 4 and 4-A (as incorporated by Hy.
Amendment dt. 13.8.1979 & 27.4.1982), the benefit of military service is not available on only the first appointment and not towards ad hoc appointments.
Rule 4-A, which was incorporated by Hy.
amendment, was deemed unconstitutional and subsequently reincorporated, thus rendering it invalid as well; therefore, the benefit was not available for ad hoc appointments, but it is available on regular appointments.
