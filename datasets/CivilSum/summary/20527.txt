Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 37 and 37-A - Pension - Commuted pension - Restoration of pension after commuted amount of pension has been adjusted - Rule 37-A itself makes a clear distinction between one-third portion of pension that can be commuted without conditions and two-thirds of pension to be received as terminal benefits with conditions attached - Petitioners who commuted their one-third portion of pension under Rule 37-A are entitled to benefits as given in the 'Common Cause case' since, after 15 years, those who opted for one-third commutation and have become entitled to restoration of pension are treated the same as the petitioners.
