Industrial Tribunal Act, 1947, Section 15 - Exparte Award - Recalling of - An exparte award was made in favor of a workman, stating he is entitled to all dues as admissible to him w.e.f.
the date of his termination.
A notification was published upon the service of notice in the claim petition.
The management-petitioner did not appear on the behalf.
The petitioner later moved the Tribunal against the impugned award, 34 days after publication of the notification without any justifiable reason.
Moreover, there was a gap of 3 years and 4 months in moving to the Tribunal for recalling the exparte award.
The order of the Tribunal to reject the application for recalling the exparte award is proper.
[Paras 6 and 8]
