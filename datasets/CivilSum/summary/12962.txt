Constitution of India, Article 16 - Equation of posts - A petitioner appointed as Constable in the BSF eventually drew a salary in the pay scale of Rs. 825-1200 as Lance Naik - The petitioner was later appointed as Staff Car Driver with CAT in the pay scale of Rs. 950-1500 - The petitioner claimed seniority with effect from the date he commenced his duties as a Driver in BSF in September 1972 - However, the post of Constable cannot be considered equivalent to that of Staff Car Driver, neither in rank nor in status or for that matter in terms of pay scale - It was held that the petitioner cannot be granted seniority from September 1972 and the Central Administrative Tribunal was justified in granting seniority to the petitioner only with effect from 6101985.
