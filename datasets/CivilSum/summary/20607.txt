In admissions for medical courses, territorial and institutional reservation has resulted in some students who moved to a different state and do not meet the criteria for their home-state to miss out on opportunities for career advancement.
To balance the fairness, qualified students who have migrated from their home-state and were accepted in the all India quota should have the chance to compete in their home-state as well.
This inclusion would not give them an unfair advantage, as the competition is on an all India basis, and would promote uniformity nationwide.
The benefit of removing a small disadvantage for a small section of society outweighs any potential disturbance to the balance between different demographics.
