Dismissal from Duty - Absence from Duty - The petitioner, a Constable in Commando Police, was absent from duty for a period of 244 days.
He produced a certificate claiming that he was ill during that time, but it was found to be false and bogus.
A detailed enquiry was conducted and the petitioner was given ample opportunity to be heard.
The enquiry found that absence from duty in a disciplinary force constitutes misconduct and therefore the impugned order of dismissal was not illegal.
[Paras 5 & 6] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Enquiry - Dismissal from service - The court cannot sit in appeal of the findings of the inquiry officer as accepted by the punishing authority, appellate authority, and revisional authority.
