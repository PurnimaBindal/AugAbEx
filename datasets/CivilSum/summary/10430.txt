Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Constitution of J&K, Section 126 - Departmental Enquiry - A petitioner was dismissed from service without any inquiry, despite not being found guilty by any court of law for any alleged activity prejudicial to the security of the State.
The gravity of his alleged activities did not justify taking away the constitutional guarantee of an inquiry before removing him from service.
The interests in the security of the State are different from the magnitude of the law.
The government order was quashed, and the disciplinary authority is required to initiate fresh departmental proceedings.
[Paras 7 and 9]
