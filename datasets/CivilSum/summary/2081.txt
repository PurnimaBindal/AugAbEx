Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 29-A - Jurisdiction - Termination of services - If an appeal was already filed before a High Court having jurisdiction to entertain, it is not required to be transferred to the Administrative Tribunal, even if it was filed before the establishment of the tribunal.
Appeal cannot come into force on 22186 if it was not pending on the date when the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, came into force.
