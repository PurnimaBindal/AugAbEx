Article 30(1) of the Constitution of India and Sections 3 and 4 of the Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Act, 1974 - Minority institutions - Educational institutions established and administered by linguistic or religious minority which are protected by Article 30(1), cannot be made to surrender the fundamental right of this protection on the mere ground that they receive grant-in-aid from Govt. No amount of consent or acquiescence can make provision of the Statute applicable to a subject when application of it is violative of Article 30(1).
