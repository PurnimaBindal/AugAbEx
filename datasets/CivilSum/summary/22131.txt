Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25F - Any termination, retrenchment or absence from duty of a workman except those that are expressly excluded will be classified as retrenchment under Section 2(oo) of Industrial Disputes Act - This includes striking a workman's name from the rolls due to absence from duty.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - To ensure fairness and justice, employees must be given a reasonable opportunity to present their case before their services are terminated.
A proper enquiry is required before terminating services.
Any rule or procedure that violates Articles 14 and 21 must be struck down.
Orders that affect the employee's livelihood and career must only be passed while adhering to principles of natural justice.
