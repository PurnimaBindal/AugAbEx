The Rajasthan Subordinate Courts Ministerial Establishment Service Rules, 1986, Rule 19(iii) - Recruitment - Anticipated and future vacancies - Appointments exceeding clearly advertised vacancies must come from a select list except for anticipated vacancies for the recruitment year - New vacancies from subsequent events cannot be filled without advertising and reselecting and the rule only allows names not appointed in one year to be struck off.
[Paras 8, 9, 11, 12, 13] B Rajasthan Subordinate Courts Ministerial Establishment Service Rules, 1986, Rule 19(iii) - Recruitment - Waiting list - Appointments exceeding anticipated and future vacancies are not allowed - The waiting list candidate cannot claim appointment for anticipated vacancies unless offered against an existing vacancy.
