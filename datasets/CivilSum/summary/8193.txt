Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for Equal work - Driving Allowance - The petitioner was functioning as a Driver-Constable from the beginning of joining as Constable and was allowed to draw driving allowance by the Superintendent of Police - However, the benefits were later withdrawn on the grounds that the appointing authority of Driver-Constable was the Assistant Inspector General - There was no indication of any deficiency on the part of the petitioner and he should receive the benefits of driving allowance and time scale including graded pay scale as per rules.
