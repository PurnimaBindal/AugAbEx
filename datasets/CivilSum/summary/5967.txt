Under the Industrial Dispute Act of 1947, Section 25-F, if the services of a workman are terminated after completing 240 days of service with one month's notice without simultaneous payment of Retrenchment Compensation, simply sending the Retrenchment Compensation by money order later on does not meet the requirements of the Section.
Neither is this considered an exceptional circumstance for the workman to be denied back wages.
In situations like this, the High Court can intervene under Article 226 of the Constitution to provide the workman with appropriate relief.
