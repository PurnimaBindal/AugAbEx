Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - A delinquent who is awarded major punishment without being given the opportunity to put forth a defense, cross-examine witnesses, and review documents, and based on vague charges is in violation of the principles of natural justice.
As a result, a court may be justified in nullifying the punishment order.

Civil Service Regulations (relating to pension), Regulation 315-A - Natural justice - The government has the authority to withhold or reduce pensions on the basis of financial irregularities.
