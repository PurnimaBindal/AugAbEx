Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Government declined reference by delving into merit of the dispute - Government acted beyond its jurisdiction to consider petitioner not a workman especially when petitioner is a messenger in the bank - The impugned order declining to make a reference to labour Court is set aside and case is remanded to take decision according to law - Writ Petition is allowed.
[Paras 4, 11 and 12]
