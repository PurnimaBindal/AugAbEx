All India Institute of Medical Sciences Regulations, 1958, Regulations 30 and 35 - Fundamental Rules 56(j) - Premature retirement - Tenure post - Direct recruitment fills tenure post - Tenure post refers to a term during which an office is held, and it is a condition of holding the office - Appointment of a person to a tenure post starts when they join and ends on completion of the tenure unless curtailed on justifiable grounds - A person holding a tenure post does not superannuate, but rather leaves the office on completion of the tenure - Therefore, premature retirement is not applicable.
