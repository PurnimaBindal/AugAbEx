The Civil Procedure Code, Order 7 Rule 11 and Order 6 Rule 16 dictate how a plaint can be rejected or pleadings struck out, and also govern partial rejection of pleadings.
When deciding whether to reject a plaint under Order 7 Rule 11, the Court is limited to the averments made in the plaint and the documents filed with it, not considering the defendant's written statement or documents filed by them.
The Court cannot partially reject pleadings under Order 7 Rule 11 as it can under Order 6 Rule 16, and must consider the cumulative effect of the pleadings and documents in support of the plaint and cause of action.
