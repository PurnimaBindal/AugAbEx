Constitution of India, Article 14 - Seniority - Date of joining - Common cadre - The cooperative department created a common cadre and seniority list upon the amalgamation of various posts.
The petitioner became junior due to a change in the date of appointment and filed representations.
The Registrar ordered the determination of seniority based on the date of appointment at par with the clerks, but the order was not complied with without justification.
The court directed compliance with the Registrar's orders and the fixing of the petitioner's seniority accordingly within four months.
