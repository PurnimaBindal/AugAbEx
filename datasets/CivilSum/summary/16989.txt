Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Section 42 - Petitioner initially appointed on daily wages for regular posts, then regularised but later treated as daily wager, due to the review of regularisation policy by Government of Punjab, Department of Personnel.
Although the alternative remedy of filing revision under Section 42 of Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961 was not availed, the petitioner was allowed liberty to file a representation for regularisation and its decision should be made within 3 months.
