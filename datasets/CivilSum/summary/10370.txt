UP Public Service Commission Act, 1989, Section 2 and Constitution of India Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Benefit of reservation - Earlier Government order dated 19.2.81 stipulated that all reserved category candidates would be selected on merit, excluding reserved vacancies - At the time of selection, this order was in operation - Subsequent Government order clarifies that reserved category candidates in open competition, on the basis of merit, without availing of any prescribed relaxation, will be adjusted against general vacancies - Subsequent order does not supersede or amend earlier order - Selection made as per subsequent order is not improper.
