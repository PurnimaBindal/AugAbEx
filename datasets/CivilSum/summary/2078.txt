Constitution of India, Article 311 - De novo enquiry - A Health Officer in Calcutta Municipal Corporation - Disciplinary proceedings - Disciplinary Authority disagreed with the Enquiry Officer's findings - De novo enquiry directed - Calcutta Municipal Corporation did not have any particular rule or regulation allowing a de novo enquiry - The Disciplinary Authority is fully responsible for bringing witnesses to support its case, and not doing so would not affect the enquiry proceedings and its conclusions based on the evidence presented - Order is flawed.
