Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Back wages - Labor Court ordered reinstatement with continuity of service for workman terminated due to embezzlement - Workman served for 19 years prior to termination - Award of back wages did not automatically follow even if termination was illegal - Workman needed to prove he was not gainfully employed - Decision to set aside award of back wages.
