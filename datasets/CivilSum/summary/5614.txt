Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 311 - Misconduct - Departmental Enquiry - Dismissal - Reinstatement - Back wages - S.T.
bus conductor dismissed for non-issuance of tickets to nine passengers - Misconduct proved in departmental enquiry - Passengers' statements produced by checking inspector and signatures taken on the day of the incident - Contention regarding their non-availability for cross-examination dismissed - Guilt found by enquiry officer accurate - Reinstatement with 50% backwages granted - Previous work record considered in imposing excessive punishment - Impugned award reversed and back wages set aside - Reinstatement with continuity of service upheld.
