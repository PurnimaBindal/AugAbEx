Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6 - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal - Enquiry report - The concerned workman must receive a copy of the enquiry report and be given a reasonable opportunity to be heard - If these requirements are not met, the dismissal order may be set aside by the tribunal, making its award legal and valid.
