Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Appointment/Merit List - One selection list must be prepared and appointments made in accordance with the merit list - Only if a candidate's antecedents are incomplete can their merit be disregarded - Otherwise, such action violates Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 3] B Delay and laches - Petitioner ranked at No. 5 on the merit list from selections made in 1985 - A candidate below her was allowed to join in 1991 - The petitioner received part of the cause of action in 1991 - Thus, the petition can't be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
