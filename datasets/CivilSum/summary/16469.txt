The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, 309 and 226, discusses the revision of pay scales and the cut-off date of revision.
The State has the power to set different dates for implementation of revised pay scales for different categories of employees.
However, it cannot prescribe different dates for a person in a particular cadre.
If the order for fixing or revising pay scales appears arbitrary or unjust, the Court can rectify the order using its jurisdiction under Art. 226 of the Constitution.
