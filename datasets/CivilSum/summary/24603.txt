The Reorganised Bombay State Assistant Overseers and Deputy Engineers Seniority Lists Rules, 1978; the Reorganised Bombay State Assistant Engineers and Executive Engineers Seniority Lists Rule, 1981; the Maharashtra Service of Engineers (Regulation of Seniority and Preparation and Revision of Seniority Lists for Specified Period (Rule, 1982; and the Maharashtra Service of Engineers Class II (Regulation of Seniority and Preparation and Revision of Seniority Lists for Specified Period) Rules, 1983 apply to the Cadre, where posts held by permanent Deputy Engineers as well as officiating Deputy Engineers under the State of Maharashtra belong to single cadre of Deputy Engineers - Article 16 and Article 309 of the Indian Constitution [Para 47].
Executive instructions may prescribe a quota for recruitment from different sources if the rules are silent on the subject - Quota rule [Para 47].
If appointments are made from more than one source, it is permissible to fix the ratio for recruitment from different sources, the rules issued in this regard must ordinarily be followed strictly - Quota rule [Para 47].
If it becomes impossible to adhere to the existing quota rule, it shall get substituted by an appropriate rule to suit the needs of the situation.
In case this quota rule has not been followed for a number of years, it has broken down.
If appointees are made from one source in excess of quota, but following the procedure prescribed by the rules, they should not be pushed down below the appointees from the other source inducted in the service at a later date - Quota rule [Para 47].
If the rules permit the authorities to relax the provisions relating to the quota, ordinarily, it should be presumed that there was such relaxation when there is a deviation from the quota rule - Quota rule [Para 47].
If the quota rule is prescribed by an executive instruction, and does not continue to follow for many years, the inference is that the executive instruction has ceased to remain operative - Quota rule [Para 47].
