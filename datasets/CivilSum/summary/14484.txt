Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rules 7 and 5 (as applicable in Haryana) - Appointment to HCS (Executive Branch) from Register A-I meant for Naib Tehsildars - Duration of Training Period - Whether it Counts as Government Service or Not - The Petitioner was considered as a candidate under training while in training - He only received his appointment as N-Tehsildar after completing his training and obtaining a Kanungo certificate from the Director of Land Records - His training did not involve performing the functions of a revenue officer - The period of training could not count towards the requirement of continuous government service under Rule 7(1)(a)(i) since the relationship of master and servant was established only after his appointment and posting as N-Tehsildar.
