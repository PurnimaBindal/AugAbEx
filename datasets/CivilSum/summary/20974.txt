MP Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rule 9 - Suspension - During disciplinary enquiry - If a charge-sheet is not served within 90 days and the delinquent employee is at fault, then they are not entitled to be reinstated.
Simply not serving a charge-sheet does not automatically make disciplinary proceedings illegal.
In this particular case, the employee himself avoided service.
It is important to note that the delay in serving the charge-sheet does not mean a delay in initiating disciplinary proceedings.
