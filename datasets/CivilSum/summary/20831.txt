Madhya Bharat Panchayat Rules, Samwat 2008, Rule 150 - Regularisation - The appellants, who were temporarily appointed as LDS in 1985 and continued in service up to 1992 after short breaks, were terminated in 1992.
Although there were no rules prescribed for their appointment, the appointment was made after following a general procedure approved by the Collector.
However, the approval of the Collector was not given when the Panchayat passed a resolution seeking regular appointment of the appellants.
Thus, the appointment of the appellants cannot be considered regular.
But, a direction is given that when regular appointment on said posts are made, the cases of appellants will be considered along with other applicants, waiving age bar.
[Paras 1, 4 and 5]
