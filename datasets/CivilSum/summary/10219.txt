Constitution of India, Article 226 - Evidence Act, Section 108 - Untraceable employee - Retiral benefits - Entitlement to - It has been stated that a legal presumption of death can be drawn for employees who have been missing for more than 7 years, as per Section 108 of the Act.
Due to this, the employee's family will be eligible for benefits that they would have received if the employee had actually passed away.
