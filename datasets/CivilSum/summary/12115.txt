Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Parity in pay Scale - Petitioners who are working as storekeepers in ITI Punjab are seeking for pay parity with clerks and storekeepers of other departments.
Before the revision, the petitioners were drawing higher pay scale than clerks.
In the absence of any evidence to show a drastic change in the nature of duties of storekeepers after the revision, it is clear that such an anomaly cannot be justified.
Therefore, the respondent is directed to re-fix Petitioners' pay and pay arrears.
[Paras 6, 7 and 11]
