High court directed the maintenance of separate cadres of clerks and typists despite their amalgamation, and a petitioner was allegedly reverted from the post of Assistant due to amalgamation and joint seniority.
This reversion violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 5]
A petitioner was promoted to the position of officiating Assistant until the position was abolished or a senior staff member was reverted, but their reversion became a necessary consequence of the amalgamation of the Clerks and Typists cadres and creation of a joint seniority list.
This act of reversion is illegal.
