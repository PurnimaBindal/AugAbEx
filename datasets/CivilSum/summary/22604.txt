State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, 1975, Rules 20-A, 20, 20-B (as introduced w.e.f.
141997) - Disciplinary proceedings after superannuation - An employee can be given an extension for a maximum of two years after the normal age of superannuation is reached - During the extension period, if a dismissal order is passed, the employee cannot protest - The bank has the discretion to continue the service of an employee for the purpose of disciplinary proceedings - The High Court is mistaken in deeming the dismissal order invalid - The High Court's decision is overturned.
