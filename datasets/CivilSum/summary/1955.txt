Delhi Road Transport Authority (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulations, 1952, Regulation 9(a)(i) - A workman working on probation had their probation extended and service terminated without any given reason.
The Industrial Tribunal found the termination to be proper and valid.
After the workman appealed in CWP, a Single Judge set aside the award of the IT and ordered retrenchment within the meaning of Section 2(oo) of ID Act.
As the Appellant did not comply with the provision of Section 25- F of ID Act, the order of termination is void ab-initio and inoperative.
While reinstatement is not guaranteed, compensation has been awarded in lieu of reinstatement and back wages.
