Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 Reinstatement/Back wages/Termination - If a termination is deemed illegal and non-existent, a workman should be reinstated with full back wages - Deductions on wages can only be made if the management proves the workman's gainful employment during their termination period - There should be no reduction solely based on the previously stated charge leading to termination - The petitioner has a right to full back wages - As such, the petition is granted.
