According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, seniority of Petitioner changed from the date of temporary appointment on post to the date of regular appointment.
The contention that seniority cannot be changed due to continuous service rendered is not valid.
Petitioner was legally appointed only on selection for the regular post, hence seniority would be counted from that date.

The seniority of the Petitioner, according to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, was earlier changed by authorities.
The contention that opportunity of hearing was not given is not valid.
The principles of natural justice are not violated simply because the mistake committed by authorities earlier were sought to correct through a speaking order.
