Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - The petitioners had been employed as daily wages workers for a specific period, with no posts advertised for filling up on a regular or daily wage basis, and no requisition sent to Employment Exchange for engaging persons on a daily wage basis - Consequently, no selection process was conducted, and no relevant rules were followed regarding filling up the vacancy - These are evident cases of back-door entries - Petitioners cannot claim regularisation without undergoing a selection process in accordance with rules.
