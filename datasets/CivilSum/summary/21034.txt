Constitution of India, Article 16 - Tamil Nadu Civil Service (CCA) Rules, Rule 1(b) - Promotion - The respondent, a Deputy Tehsildar, committed misconduct while on deputation, causing financial loss to the corporation.
He was charge sheeted in 1985 and in 1987, while disciplinary proceedings were still pending, the panel of names for promotion to the post of Tehsildar was prepared.
The incumbent was considered but found unfit and therefore not included in the panel.
The tribunal made a factual error in stating that the respondent's name was considered but omitted from the panel, as he was duly considered but found unfit.
Thus, his right to be considered for promotion was not denied to him.
