Constitution of India, Article 226 - Annual Confidential Reports - Evaluation - Officers shall receive an ACR that reflects their potential for promotion or demotion based on their conduct in an official capacity - Punctuality, dedication, and ability to follow instructions and provide guidance are critical considerations - Any unfair or misleading comments that disadvantage the officer violate the constitutional rights of the individual and must be restrained.
[Paras 5 and 6]   B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Annual Confidential Reports - Promotions - Criteria - For positions that do not require technical knowledge, personnel should not be excluded from consideration for promotion on frivolous grounds following the principles of natural justice - Seniority combined with merit-based ranking is the standard for advancement.
