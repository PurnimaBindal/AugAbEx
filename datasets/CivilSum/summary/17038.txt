State Bank of Patiala (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 13 & 14 - Addition to qualifying service under Draft Pension Scheme is not valid - Pension benefits cannot be claimed under a draft scheme - Rule making authority has the discretion to limit additional benefits - Proviso to the Rule restricting addition of previous service to 5 years is constitutional - Retirees before 1196 are not entitled to benefits under these Regulations.
