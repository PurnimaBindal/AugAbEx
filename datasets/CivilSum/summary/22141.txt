Orissa Ministerial Service (Method of Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Lower Division Assistant in the Office of the Department of Secretariat) Rules, 1951 - Constitution of India, Article 142 - Challenge as to seniority - Res judicata - Despite a decision by the Tribunal to supersede the Gradation list of 1990 and restore that of 1984 instead, a subsequent circular by the state in 1992 made a separate right for the petitioner to contest government action on unrelated causes.
The Tribunal subsequently denied the petitioner's application due to the principle of res judicata and a lack of distinct cause of action.
The High Court upheld this decision.
While the Supreme Court's judgment did not dispense with the principle of res judicata, it may be presumed that the Court made its ruling in accordance with the law.
The intention of the Court was not to allow the questioning of matters already decided upon.
Therefore, the appeal was dismissed.
