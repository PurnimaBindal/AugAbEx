The State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules 1975, Rules 48 and 49, state that financial irregularities in banking services would fall under the term 'misconduct,' including transactions made fraudulently.
[Paras 12 and 13]
Dismissal from service due to misconduct would require the appellate authority to follow natural justice and give a personal hearing, along with a speaking order on the appeal.
