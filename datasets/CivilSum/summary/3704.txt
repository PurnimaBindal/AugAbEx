Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Higher pay scale - Recommendations of Third Pay Commission - The petitioner received a higher pay scale only after the High Court intervened - During an earlier writ petition, recommendations of Third Pay Commission had been implemented - The petitioner was denied the higher pay scale as per Third Pay Commission on the grounds that it was ordered after the recommendations had been issued - The Court held that the petitioner was entitled to the next higher pay scale as per Third Pay Commission, as his claim for selection grade had been accepted retroactively - He could not be deprived of the placement in the next higher grade solely because his claim had been accepted after the issuance of the Third Pay Commission's recommendations.
