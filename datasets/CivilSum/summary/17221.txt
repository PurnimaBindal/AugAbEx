Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reversion from the post of Helper to Baildar - The petitioner claims that being asked to perform the job of helper after being appointed as Baildar on a daily wage basis is equivalent to a reversion, but the post of helper is not considered a higher or promotional post.
There is no reversion when petitioner is again asked to perform the job of Baildar as it was simply a redesignation due to administrative exigency.
Therefore, the writ petition is not maintainable.
[Paras 4 and 5]
