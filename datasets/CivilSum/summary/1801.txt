Haryana Compassionate Assistance to Dependants of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003 stated that the appointment of the petitioner on compassionate grounds has been cancelled due to being contrary to statutory Rules.
The cancelation is legal since the appointment was void ab-initio.
Compassionate appointment is designed to soothe the distressful situation created by the employee's death, not to give job for a job.
[Paras 5, 7]

The appointment of petitioner on compassionate grounds was cancelled, and no notice was given to the petitioner.
Since the appointment was void, the cancellation was legitimate and did not infringe on the petitioner's legal rights.
Even if a show-cause notice was given, it would not legitimize an illegal appointment.
[Para 7]

Rules made under Article 309 of the Constitution would prevail over executive instructions that are contrary to Rules.
Executive instructions can only supplement but not replace Rules.
[Para 8]

The benefits illegally granted to one party by authorities would not create a legal right that could be enforced by issuing a writ for the other party's benefit.
