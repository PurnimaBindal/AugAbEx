The Madhya Pradesh Industrial Relations Act requires that Presiding Officers of Labour Courts are appointed through selection by a committee.
While compulsory questions not related to industrial disputes are not grounds for challenging the selection process, the court may consider whether an interview is held after a written test has been taken.
Academic aspects of the examination are left for the authorities appointing candidates to decide, and the court does not have the power to substitute its view.
[Paras 9 to 12]

The appointment to public service requires both a written test to measure intellect and an interview to assess the candidate.
The interview should be conducted by a committee chaired by a Judge of the High Court.
Judicial review is not allowed in such cases.
[Para ]
