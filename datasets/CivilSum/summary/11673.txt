Payment of Gratuity Act, Section 7(7) - Appeal - Limitation - Delay in filing appeal - The Payment of Gratuity Act is a special statute, without provision for condonation of delay, but the Appellate Authority may have jurisdiction to condone delay beyond the initial sixty-day limit if sufficient cause is shown, by extending that period by up to an additional sixty days.
However, the Appellate Authority cannot go beyond this time limit.
