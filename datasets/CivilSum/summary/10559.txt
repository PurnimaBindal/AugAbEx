The A.A.G Varinder Paul Singh is the Respondent in the case, according to Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8.
The Petitioner received a charge-sheet in 1991 for alleged actions dating back to 1974.
The department couldn't provide a satisfactory reason for the delay of 17 years, making the delay itself fatal.
As a result, the charge-sheet was quashed, following the JT 1990(2) SC 54.
