Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 2(b) - Civil Contempt meaning - For contempt to be punishable, it must be a wilful disobedience to any judgment, decree, direction, order writ or other process of Court.
The Court must be convinced that the contemner's disobedience was both wilful and intentional before punishing them for noncompliance of a court order.
[Para 9]

Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 2(b) - Order of Court allowing enhanced pay scales to employees - The Court must be aware of the fact that the order will create a liability of Rs. 28 Crores on the State in the form of arrears.
If it is established that the disobedience was due to compelling circumstances, the Court may not find the case of contempt made out, and the State paid Rs. 20 Crores.
AIR 1981 SC 2026 was considered in this case.
[Paras 9, 10, and 11]
