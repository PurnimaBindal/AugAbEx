The General Officer Commanding has the authority to convene General Court Martial and is considered a competent authority under Section 109 of Army Act, 1950 by virtue of a Note.
[Para 10]
Court Martial Proceedings must be completed within three years of the date of commission of offence known to the Army authorities according to Section 122 of Army Act, 1950.
[Para 11]
A Writ Court cannot intervene with the findings of Court Martial through the re-appreciation of evidence or provide a second opinion.
Only the violation of statutory provisions of the Act or rules can be a ground for interference by the Writ Court pursuant to Army Act, 1950 Section 109 and Constitution of India Article 226.
