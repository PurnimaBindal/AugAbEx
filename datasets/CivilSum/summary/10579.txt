Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Although the principle of equal pay for equal work is not explicitly declared as a fundamental right, it is considered a fundamental right due to Article 39(d) of the Directive Principles of State Policy - Rural, district, and inter-state level duties cannot differ based on mode of recruitment or place of posting - Those in similar positions in Haryana's District Public Relations Department and Rural Community Theatres must receive equal pay scales.
