Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Education Class III (School Cadre) Rules, 1955, Rules 6 and 9 - Promotion - Classification between male and female teachers - Seniority must be determined for persons holding 'the same class of posts' - If Rules 6 and 9 of Appendix 'A' to the Rules are construed harmoniously, the seniority of Masters in the Men's branch should be determined separately from the Mistresses in Women's branch - Promoting a woman mistress recruited later than a Master against the vacancy of the women's branch does not violate Article 14 or 16 of the Constitution or discriminate against seniors in the Men's branch - Masters can only raise grievances if a junior Master is promoted without considering the case of a senior in their branch - The allotment of vacancies in the ratio of 2:1 to Men's and Women's branches is not unfair or arbitrary.
