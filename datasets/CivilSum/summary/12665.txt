The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 266, deal with the appointment of the Head of Department.
As per the bye-laws of the university, the Director had discretion to appoint the Head of Department for a period he deems fit.
The decision taken by the Director not to continue the petitioner as Head was specifically considered and approved by the Board of Governors.
It was held that the decision cannot be regarded as arbitrary or whimsical, as it was approved and based on relevant rules.
[Paras 5 to 7]
