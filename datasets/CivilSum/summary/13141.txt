The Constitution of India and the Education Code provide provisions for wilful absence and dismissal of employees.
Article 81(d) of the Code allows employees to give an explanation to the provisional view of loss of lien taken by competent authority and provides for an appeal.
These provisions were complied with and it was held that employees of Sangathan cannot seek recourse to Article 311.
While it was contended that clause 81 violated the equality clause and principles of natural justice, it was deemed to be just, fair and reasonable.
