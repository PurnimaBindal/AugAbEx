Kerala Buildings (Lease and Rent Control) Act, 1965, Section 11(4)(i) - Tenant could be evicted for subletting without landlord's written consent - This is applicable even when the subletting occurred before the landlord purchased the premises - The fact that the sub-tenant had been in possession for a long time doesn't imply landlord's consent.
