Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Section 33(4)(1) - Reversion - Enquiry - Suspension - The Board's resolution was annulled because the petitioner's case was not on the committee's meeting agenda.
The punishment of stoppage of two increments also cannot be sustained due to no findings that the Sub-Committee was entitled to pass a fresh resolution; therefore, the committee had no jurisdiction in ordering a de novo inquiry.
Furthermore, since the inquiry was not justified, the impugned order passed on that finding cannot be sustained.
[Paras 2 and 5]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Principles of natural justice must be followed before depriving a person of his salary during suspension - Decision to deny full salary without giving an opportunity to show cause against it violates natural justice principles and cannot be sustained.
