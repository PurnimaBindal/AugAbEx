Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Father of the petitioner was a special assistant in SBI who voluntarily retired on medical grounds and is now receiving pension and financial benefits of Rs.3 lakhs - It is stated that compassionate appointment is not an automatic right and can only be given in cases where the family is found to be in a distressed condition due to the death or retirement on medical grounds of an employee, and cannot be given on a priority basis to qualified unemployed persons in poverty - [Para 8]
