According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 311 and 226, the termination of employment of the petitioners who were appointed as Safai Karamcharis has been deemed illegal.
The government refused to approve the appointments on the basis that MC expenditure exceeded the prescribed norms and that new posts had not been sanctioned.
However, provision had been made in the budget prior to the petitioners' employment and they had been appointed to existing posts.
The ground for rejection that their case was for the creation of a new post was non-existent then.
The government later conceded that available posts should be filled in, making the termination order invalid.
