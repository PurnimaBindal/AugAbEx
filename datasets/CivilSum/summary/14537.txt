Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 256B and 256C Delay and laches - Pension - The age of superannuation is fixed at 58 years and the maximum benefits of pension are made available after the completion of 30 years of service.
The government has been applying the rules under the revised formula to all employees, regardless of their date of retirement, for the past 20 years.
The petitioners are unhappy about the notification of the revised formula dated 291985 that states that the maximum benefits will be calculated on the completion of 33 years of service.
The court is not willing to interfere after the lapse of 20 years, particularly since the government is already providing the benefit.
