Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Admission - Reservation of Seats - The state government decided that Army Institute of Law should conduct a separate entrance test for the Army category and a Common Entrance Test for the 12 seats reserved for the Civil category.
However, the Institute contended that holding two separate entrance tests was not justified.
The court held that a centralized and single window procedure for admissions was in the larger interest and welfare of the student community and ordered that all seats in the petitioner institute be filled through a single Common Entrance Test.
