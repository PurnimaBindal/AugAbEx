The petitioner was selected for the post of Scientific Engineer (Group B) and the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 were applicable to their appointment, selection, and regularization.
However, the state of Haryana did not issue an appointment letter on the pretext that the post had been taken out of the purview of HPSC The state then proceeded to regularize the services of an ad hoc appointee against the post who was not selected by HPSC The petitioner argued that the state's action was unjustified and unfair, and that the ad hoc appointee cannot be given precedence over the regular selectees.
The Piara Singh case was cited, but the petitioner argued that the ad hoc appointee was not found fit and was rejected by HPSC Despite this, the state authorities were attempting to provide a back door entry to the appointee.
The court allowed the petition with the cost of Rs. 5,000/-.
