Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 17, 17-A, 20 and 33-C(2) and Rule 23 - Ex parte award - Despite being served with the notice, management did not appear for the proceedings - Ex parte award was passed under Section 33-C(2) - No application was filed for condonation of delay or to set aside the award before the labour court within stipulated time - Rule 23 empowers Labour Court to set aside the ex parte decisions within 15 days of such orders as a Civil Court - Section 17-A makes the award enforceable after thirty days of its publication - After the expiry of this statutory period, Labour Court has no power to impede the implementation of the award.
