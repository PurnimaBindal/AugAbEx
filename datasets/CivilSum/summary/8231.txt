Under the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 1(6), 7(2)(b), and 9(2) proviso, employees who draw wages of Rs. 1,600/- or more a month are not subject to the Act.
If an employer imposes penal wage cut on such employees under the Act, it is illegal and beyond the employer's jurisdiction.
Proceedings initiated by the employer to recover wages from such employees are non-est.
The employer has no authority or jurisdiction to take action under the Act against employees drawing wages of Rs. 1,600/- or more per month.
