According to Kurukshetra University Act, 1986, Section 11(5) - Admission - Notification, there were two vacant seats for M.Sc.
Zoology after admission was closed, but no procedure was prescribed to fill them up in the Handbook.
The Vice-Chancellor issued two notifications to facilitate admission, but the High Court found no emergency to issue them.
The Vice-Chancellor could not use emergency powers to allow admission of those who were absent on specific dates, making them ineligible.
The notification went against the University rule and High Court's judgment was correct with no errors.
