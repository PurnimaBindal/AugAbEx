Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Removal from service - Approval - Jurisdiction of Tribunal - The scope of Section 33(2)(b) is limited to granting approval, not permission to remove - The Tribunal only has the power to grant or refuse approval, and not modify the order passed - The High Court cannot modify the scope of the order beyond what was granted by the Tribunal under Section 33(2)(b).
