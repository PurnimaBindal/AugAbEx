Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 25 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 100 - Pay Scales - Assistant teachers who were recruited in 1962 without meeting the minimum educational qualifications must receive fitment in pay scales under the 4th pay scales under the 5th Pay Commission, due to the management having waived the objection regarding minimum qualification - The management is obligated to release benefits under the 5th Pay Commission.
[Para 17]
Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 25 - In case of denial of fitment to teachers in 5th Pay Commission pay scales, teachers may file a suit in Civil Court due to the statute lacking a machinery to resolve the dispute - The Civil Court has jurisdiction over the matter.
