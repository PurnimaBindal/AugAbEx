Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Quantum of punishment - Compulsory retirement - Gravity of misconduct - Petitioner, who was working as UDC and in charge of Copying Agency of the Court, was found guilty of serious misconduct of tampering with the judicial records, which adversely affected the credibility of judiciary - The punishment of compulsory retirement with all consequential benefits cannot be considered excessively severe, particularly since the wrongdoer was given ample opportunity to defend himself during inquiry proceedings.
