Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39(d) and 226 - `Equal pay for Equal work' - A welder at the Ranjit Sagar Dam, who was asked to teach as a stop gap measure until teachers arrived from the Education Department, was not entitled to receive the salary scale of a teacher, as he was not appointed as one.
He could only be paid the salary of a welder, which was his substantive post, even if he performed the job of a teacher.
This was the reason the court dismissed the petition.
