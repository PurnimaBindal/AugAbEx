Constitution of India, Article 311 - Union Bank of India, Vigilance Manual Volume 1, Para 233 - Disciplinary enquiry - Enquiry Officer - Union Bank of India, Vigilance Manual Volume 1, Para 233 states that the Enquiry Officer should be of a sufficiently senior rank and not suspected of any prejudice or bias against the accused.
However, it does not imply that the Officer selected should be senior to the delinquent Officer.
[Para 4] The Delinquent Officer must establish that there is a real likelihood of bias in the mind of the Judge, due to the alleged circumstances.
A real likelihood implies a possibility of a substantial degree.
In order to prove bias, strong circumstances have to be shown, as vague suspicion cannot be made a ground to believe the allegation of bias.
[Para 6] The Delinquent Officer does not have the right to take assistance of a Counsel as a matter of right.
