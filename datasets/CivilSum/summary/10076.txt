According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India and Regulation 30(b) of the Bardhaman Gramin Bank (Staff) Service Regulations, 1981, employees may have their promotions withheld if they have been punished by the department in departmental proceedings.
The petition of the employee claiming a promotion was dismissed and the court clarified that promotion is not a guaranteed right of the employee.
Regulation 30(b) does not prevent the stopping of promotions.
