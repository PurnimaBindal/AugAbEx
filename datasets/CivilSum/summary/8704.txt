Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Quantum of Punishment - Termination - As long as the inquiry conducted by the management is deemed to be fair, the decision to terminate the employee is justified - If it is determined that the worker has committed fraud by embezzling public funds, they forfeit their right to continue their employment in the public sector - In this specific case, a conductor intentionally issued tickets of a lesser denomination in order to embezzle funds, and the Labour Court should not have interfered with the punishment imposed by invoking Section 11-A - The ruling of the Labour Court has been overturned.
