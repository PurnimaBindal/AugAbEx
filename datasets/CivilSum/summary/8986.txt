Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 14 and 10 - Evidence - Additional evidence - In a domestic enquiry held by the management, the workman was not afforded an adequate opportunity during the enquiry and the enquiry was found to be improper - The management did not make any request to prove the charges and give evidence in support of the findings - Without any request from the management, the Labour Court has no authority to grant permission to introduce new or additional evidence.
