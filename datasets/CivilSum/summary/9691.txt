Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Departmental Enquiry - Natural Justice - Penalty of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect - Recovery of pecuniary loss caused - Considered a major penalty - Despite the plaintiff responding to the charge-sheet and denying charges, the appointed enquiry officers did not record any evidence - Even if the plaintiff was absent from the proceedings, an ex parte enquiry was still required - However, no ex parte enquiry report was provided and punishing authority did not provide any reasons for imposing the penalty - As a result, the penalty order is deemed illegal, null and void and a breach of natural justice.
[Paras 5 & 6]
