Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300A - Appellant requests to count past service from 1960 to 1968 in Agricultural Department towards pension qualifying service - Respondent, in mercy petition, allowed subject to depositing benefits received and 6% interest - Appellant accepted and received benefit - In civil suit, appellant claims 18% interest on arrears, but trial court grants 9% interest - Held, trial court's decision is just and fair, no interference necessary - Appeal denied.
