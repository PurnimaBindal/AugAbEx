The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 4(1) and 2(r) states that an employee must retire on superannuation and receive gratuity based on their actual date of birth.
If an employee continues to work beyond the age of superannuation by providing false information, they will not be entitled to claim gratuity for that period.
This is because the service rendered during that time is not considered qualifying service, due to the employee's dishonesty.
In a case where an employee obtained incorrect gratuity by providing false information about their date of birth, an employer obtained the correct information from the school authority.
As a result, the employee is not entitled to claim gratuity for the period beyond their superannuation.
The petition in this case has been granted.
