Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 15 - Seniority - The regulations include two seniority lists, one prior to the confirmation of an officer and the other after confirmation.
Seniority after confirmation is based on the date of confirmation as per rule 15.
The significance of seniority before confirmation diminishes after confirmation, unless the confirmation date is deemed incorrect.
The court cannot question the validity of the confirmation orders of the respondents as Assistant Engineers and Assistant XENs since they were not challenged in the writ petition.
