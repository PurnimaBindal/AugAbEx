Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311-A and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Compulsory retirement - Adverse reports - Service record - The Government has the discretion to retain a Government employee beyond the age of 55 years, but this discretion is subject to Rules or instructions - If executive power is exercised arbitrarily and influenced by external factors without sufficient evidence to support conclusions, court has the right to review such orders - Petitioner had a good service record and was retired prematurely without any basis for the decision - No evidence provided to support the impugned order - The judgment of the single judge was upheld and the appeal of the Government was dismissed.
