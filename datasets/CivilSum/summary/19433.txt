Response: "According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Seniority is determined based on rank assigned in the merit list, regardless of regularisation of service.
In preparation of seniority list, it is important to comply with principles of natural justice and authorities may rectify errors/mistakes.
Revised seniority list reflects ranking given in merit list and is deemed correct, even though the original seniority list was based on an incorrect position.
[Paras 8, 9, and 10]"
