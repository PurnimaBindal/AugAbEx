Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Family Pension - Parents - Parents are entitled to family pension and should not be excluded or denied benefits under any rules.
The exclusion of parents from the concept of family is arbitrary and lacks rationale.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Family Pension - Qualifying service - Ad hoc/temporary service - Regularisation of services - Adhoc conductors who complete two years of service should be regularised - After regularisation, the term beyond two years of adhoc service is considered regular - Upon the death of a conductor's son after rendering more than one year of service following two years of adhoc service, the conductor is entitled to family pension.
