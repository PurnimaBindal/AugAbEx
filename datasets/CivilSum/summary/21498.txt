The validity of a suspension order, under the All India Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1969 - Rule 3(1), is subject to the fulfillment of certain conditions by the government.
It is not necessary for the order to include a recital of condition fulfillment, assuming that the condition has indeed been fulfilled.
An order may only be challenged on the ground that it did not fulfill a statutory pre-condition if it's specifically pleaded that the condition did not exist.
The burden of proof is on the challenger to prove the non-existence of the condition.
If the order is challenged on the ground that it did not fulfill the required conditions, it is up to the authority that passed the order to demonstrate the presence of such conditions at the time of order passing.
