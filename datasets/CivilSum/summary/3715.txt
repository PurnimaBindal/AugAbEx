As per Regulation 22(A)(1) of the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, an employee can withdraw the notice of voluntary retirement with approval from the competent authority before the intended date of retirement.
The only grounds for denial of permission is if administrative set up is affected.
Hence, the rejection of withdrawal due to the acceptance of an earlier offer is against the law and is quashed.
[Paras 15, 16 and 17]

The High Court upheld the order of voluntary retirement and quashed the same.
The court also ensured that the petitioner was given all consequential benefits.
However, since the petitioner filed the petition after two years and ten months of the rejection of his request to withdraw, the number of representations made thereafter had no statutory meaning.
If reinstated, the petitioner will be entitled to backwages from the date of filing of the petition.
[Para 19]
