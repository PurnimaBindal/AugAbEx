The Indian Constitution, in Articles 14 and 16, determines that promotions become effective from the date of being granted, not from the date of vacancy or creation of the post.
The date of occurrence of vacancy does not matter in this regard.
[Paras 7 and 10]  B The Indian Constitution, in Articles 14 and 16, indicates that the appellant was promoted as General Manager on 29111996 and cannot claim promotion w.e.f.
13.3.1996 with consequential benefits.
Such relief cannot be granted.
