Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education, Act 1963, Section 28(2) - Refusal of school upgradation by authorities thrice - Distinguish between recognition and upgradation of Institution - Upgradation case is superior to recognition case - Grounds for recognition cannot be grounds for upgradation - No discrimination allowed when a decision has already been made for one district - Board ordered to allow upgradation after completion of all requirements.
