Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Merit list of other categories will be operated even if not operating merit list of ST category - Operating merit list of ST category is required according to normal rules, administrative ban cannot impede operation.
Ban cannot prejudice rights of parties, it applies to all appointments and not few.
