Civil Procedure Code, Section 24 - Transfer of matrimonial dispute - Priority is given to the convenience of the woman in handling transfer petitions - A wife who resides with her parents in Panchkula with her young child and no earning capabilities is totally dependent on her parents - A petition under Section 9 of Hindu Marriage Act was submitted in Karnal - It has been instructed to be shifted to Panchkula.
