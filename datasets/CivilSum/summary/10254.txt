Constitution of India 1950 - Articles 226/227 14 and 16 - Daily wagers unable to regularise their services claiming absence due to uncontrollable reasons - Court decides in favor of the petitioner, and rules that he is entitled to regularisation and all consequential benefits shall be paid from the date when the writ was instituted.
Tek Chand's case used as reference.
