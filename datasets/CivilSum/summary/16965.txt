Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Compassionate appointment - Daily wagers - Regularisation of services - State Govt. instructs to regularise daily wagers in service after 5 years - While dependents of daily wagers are not entitled to compassionate appointment, if daily wager completed over 5 years and was not formally regularised, they are not considered mere daily wagers and their dependents are entitled to compassionate appointment - Daily wagers who die in harness after 5 years of service are deemed to be in regular service, thus their dependents are entitled to compassionate appointment.
