Employees Provident Fund Scheme, 1952, Para 30 - Employees Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 6 - Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Sections 2(1)(d) and 2(1)(o) - Service - Consumer - An employee member of the Provident Fund Scheme is considered a 'consumer' and PF Commissioner renders 'service' under the Scheme for 'consideration' - The administrative charges paid by the employer for the membership of its employee are towards 'consideration' for the 'services' being rendered under the Scheme - Thus, the employee is the 'user' of such services and is a 'consumer' within the definition of 'Consumer' in Section 29(1)(d) of the 'Services' within the definition of Section 2(1)(o) of the Consumer Protection Act - This interpretation is necessary to avoid defeating the objects of the Acts and the Scheme.
