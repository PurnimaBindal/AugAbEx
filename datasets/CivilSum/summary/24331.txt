Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Gauhati High Court (Appointment, Conditions of Service & Conduct) Rules, 1967, Schedule I Class- II(B) - Promotion - Posting - Merely being posted to a higher-paying, superior position cannot be considered a promotion without appropriate processes being followed to assess all eligible candidates - Moreover, such a posting does not grant any right to claim seniority in a higher role, particularly when the establishment has independent administrative wings responsible for posting and transfers - The Gauhati High Court, which has additional benches, has posted Stenographers Grade I as Private Secretaries to the Hon'ble Judges without any specific selection or promotion process - This posting does not give the candidates claiming for seniority in higher cadre over those who are senior in the lower cadre any legal right.
