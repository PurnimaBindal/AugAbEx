Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 13 - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental Proceedings - Criminal Proceedings - Merely dropping criminal proceedings for want of sanction under the Act does not serve as grounds to quash departmental proceedings against an employee.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 13 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 14 - Natural Justice - Procedure in departmental proceedings - There is no definitive rule on whether evidence collected in a criminal inquiry that wasn't in compliance with the Act can be considered - The punishing authority and the inquiry officer must ensure no prejudice is caused to the accused and that principles of natural justice are strictly followed by taking into account evidence obtained in an investigation of a criminal offence not authorised by law, while adhering to the prescribed procedure for the enquiry.
