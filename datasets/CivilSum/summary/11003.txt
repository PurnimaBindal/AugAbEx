Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Seniority - Post in super-time scale of the accounts clerks cadre - Only one post in the cadre with a higher grade.
Granting promotion to the selection grade scale would not constitute promotion under the law.
Rules of the reservation did not apply to selection grade/super-time scale in the same cadre.
Petitioner cannot benefit from the decision of the Apex Court nor the reservation guidelines.
Respondent No7, senior to petitioner, was rightly granted super-time scale.
Impugned order is upheld.
