Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Compulsory retirement - Qualifying service - Judicial Officer - The order of compulsory retirement can be passed with a future date and in this case, the order was passed after the petitioner completed 25 years of service - The order would come into effect from the date of its receipt.
[Paras 3 and 4] B Mala fide - Administrative orders of reversion etc. that have already been challenged and upheld by the court cannot be used as grounds to assert mala fide of the authority passing the order of compulsory retirement.
[Para 5] C. Constitution of India, Article 311 - Compulsory retirement - Public interest - The Full Court of the High Court considered ten entries adverse to the reputation and integrity of the petitioner (a judicial officer) and suggested the State retire him - It can't be claimed that the order was not based on any material of public interest.
