Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 2(ra) Schedule Fifth, Item No. 5 - Unfair Labour Practice - Ad hoc appointments - The employer is allowed to make ad hoc arrangements until regular appointments are made, and is instructed to make regular appointments before the ad hoc appointees complete 240 days of employment in order to avoid legal complications, which cannot be considered an unfair labour practice.
To establish an unfair labour practice, there must be a specific allegation of improper motive supported by evidence to enable the court to make a finding.
While orders of reinstatement with back wages for the workers cannot be sustained, in this case, as the workers have already been reinstated, reinstatement is not intervened but the remaining back wages will not be paid.
