Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1946, Sections 42(4) and 85 - Refusal to do Begari work by workman - Employer offering petitioner work as Begari which was a lower type of work than the helper position - Industrial Court concluded that the workman refused the offered work and had no right to claim employment in any particular section - Labour Court's order to pay 50% back wages was set aside - Industrial Court could not interfere with Labour Court's findings unless work of Begari was equivalent to helper work - Petition granted.
