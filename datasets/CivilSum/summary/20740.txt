The appellant-petitioner is seeking employment for his son under compassionate appointment, stating that he was declared unfit for light duty in accordance with the 1992 policy circular issued by the Haryana government.
According to the medical certificate provided by the appellant-petitioner, he is unable to perform light duty, but is not categorized as 'Nakara' or no use.
The 1992 policy allows for extreme cases of blindness or absolute unfit for government jobs.
Although the appellant-petitioner has been deemed unfit for light duty, he is not qualified as disabled or 'Nakara'.
Hence, the appellant is not entitled to demand employment for his son.
[Paras 6 and 7]
