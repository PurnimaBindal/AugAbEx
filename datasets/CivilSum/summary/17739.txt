The Punjab State Electricity Board is within its rights to consider candidates who passed ITI on a regular basis for direct recruitments to the posts of Sub-Station Attendants, instead of those who attended evening classes.
The training quality and competitiveness of regular students is better than that of evening class students, and the petitioners do not possess the necessary National Apprenticeship Electrician Certificate.
Therefore, the Board's decision cannot be considered arbitrary or unfair.
The petition is dismissed with parties left to bear their own costs.
