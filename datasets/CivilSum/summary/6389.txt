Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Seniority - While an employee does not have a vested right of promotion, they have the right to be considered for promotion based on their seniority - Chances of promotion is not a condition of service and is not guaranteed - A person can only be considered for promotion when a vacancy arises - In this case, the petitioner was not prejudiced as no person junior to them was promoted before them.
[Paras 10 & 11]
