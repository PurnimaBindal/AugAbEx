Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 311 - Acquittal - Reinstatement - Retirement - Disciplinary proceedings - Double jeopardy - After being dismissed from service due to a conviction in criminal court, the appellant was acquitted by the Sessions Judge on grounds of merit and lack of sanction.
He was reinstated and the period during which he was suspended was treated as time on duty.
The appellant was retired at the age of superannuation and more than 11 years have passed since then, making it unjustified to initiate disciplinary proceedings.
