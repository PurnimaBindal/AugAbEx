Constitution of India, Article 311 - Voluntary retirement - Withdrawal of - Petitioner argues that their application for withdrawal of voluntary retirement was pending prior to the effective date, but the respondents claim that the necessary order had already been passed and benefits were accepted - Court holds that failure to pass an order on the withdrawal application invalidates the voluntary retirement process - Employees have the option to withdraw their proposal even after acceptance before the retirement date.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Voluntary retirement - Withdrawal of - Petitioner claims that respondent-Corporation did not conduct an exit interview before accepting voluntary retirement, but the respondent argues that the Petitioner did not appear for two scheduled interviews due to prior leave, suggesting intent - Court rules that failure to hold the exit interview will not impact or invalidate the Voluntary retirement process.
