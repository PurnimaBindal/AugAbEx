The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 mandate a reservation for disinherited groups.
Persons in the "creamy layer" are not entitled to that reservation by adopting someone from a lower income group to prove eligibility.
Such action would undermine the purpose of safeguarding those in impoverished groups.
A lower income certificate is required.
A tehsildar is not qualified to validate the adoption's legitimacy, but certifies only based on facts presented and validated by him/her.
