Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Annual Confidential Reports - Recording of ACRs - Administrative instructions dated 351960 as further clarified by Circular dated 12.6.1990 of the Punjab Govt. - The Minister in-charge can call for the ACRs and personal files of any official and give his remarks of personal knowledge only in case of wide difference between reporting and reviewing/accepting authorities - Downgraded and upgraded ACRs of officers can be interfered with by the Minister incharge and Chief Minister in certain cases.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Annual Confidential Reports - Recording of ACRs for short intervals - Administrative instructions dated 351960 as further clarified by Circular dated 12.6.1990 of the Punjab Govt. - The period of three months is regulatory not mandatory applicable to Reporting and Reviewing/ Accepting Authority as per rules and instructions -Short intervals does not mean that no officer will be competent to record ACRs for those intervals.
