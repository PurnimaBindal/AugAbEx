The question of whether the Food and Supplies Department falls within the definition of Industry is a matter of fact.
The petitioners failed to raise this before the Labour Court, disentitling them from doing so before the High Court.
Adjudicating on this matter is beyond the scope of the High Court's jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
