According to Sections 2(s) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, a workman's termination of service can only be counted as 240 days by the tribunal in the year of termination.
However, it was held that the tribunal should have counted backwards to the preceding 12 months to accurately reckon the 240 days of service in a calendar year.
The approach taken by the tribunal was deemed perverse and unjustified, and the matter has been remanded back to the tribunal for a fresh decision.
