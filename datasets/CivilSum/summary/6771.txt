MP Medical and Dental Post-Graduate Entrance Examination Rules, 1997, Rule (X)(iii) reject an application to convert a Post Graduate Diploma Course in Anaesthesia into a Course of MD (Anaesthesia) citing Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
According to Rule (X)(iii), a candidate assigned to a particular course cannot change their subject.
Such a change is considered seeking admission in a different post-graduate course in the same subject.
[Paras 10, 20 and 24]
