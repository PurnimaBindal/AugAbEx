Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Education/Domicile certificate/Reservation for dependents of deceased/disabled defence personnels - Medical courses - A student applying for MBBS courses must hold and maintain required eligibility qualifications until the last date for admission applications.
Respondent No. 4 gained admission against a reserved seat for dependent children of deceased/disabled defence personnel by using false information to obtain a domicile certificate.
Haryana Government allows for an Army individual settled after retirement and drawing his pension in Haryana to be eligible for a domicile certificate, and their children are allowed for admission.
Respondent No. 4's father transferred his account to a bank in Gurgaon but was not able to draw his pension before the last date of admission.
Further inquiry by the authorities revealed that the certificate was issued on erroneous facts.
As respondent No. 4 was not entitled to the domicile certificate on the date of admission, her admission should be cancelled, and the petitioner who submitted the correct domicile certificate on time earned the right to admission against that seat.
However, respondent No. 4 has already completed three years of the course, and the petitioner was allowed provisional admission, so the admission of both may continue and be regularized.
