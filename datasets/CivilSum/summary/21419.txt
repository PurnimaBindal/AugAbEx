Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311(2) - Dismissal - Employee found sleeping during office hours - Instead of submitting explanation he filed criminal report against Chairman - He was also sent a registered letter demanding him submit an explanation of the charge sheet - He never replied and the order of dismissal was eventually passed - The tribunal dismissed the suit as the departmental remedies were not exhausted.
The High Court set aside the order of dismissal because the observation made by the tribunal failed to take into account that despite repeated requests, the employee failed to reply to the charge sheet and did not inspect the record when asked to do so.
However, the order of HC was set aside and the dismissal order was upheld.
