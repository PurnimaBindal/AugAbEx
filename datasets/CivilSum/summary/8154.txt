Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 2 Rule 2 - Alternative remedies - Doctrine of choice - Withdrawal of suit without permission to file fresh or avail other remedy - The worker has the option to pursue either the general law's suited remedy or the industrial dispute remedy against wrongful termination of their services, but they cannot switch midway without court permission.
If the suit is withdrawn without permission to seek alternate remedy, claim under Industrial Disputes Act will not be feasible.
