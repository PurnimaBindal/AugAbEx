Punjab State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Services (Common Cadre) Rules 1967, Rule 1.6(d) - Delegation of Powers - Disciplinary action against officials below the rank of Assistant Secretary can be taken by the Managing Director with a resolution passed by the Administrative Committee.
The powers delegated to the Managing Director do not cease to operate on the expiry of the Committee's term unless the resolution is specifically rescinded or superseded.
Actions taken by the Managing Director in the interest and affairs of management of the Federation will be upheld.
[Para 13] B. Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, Section 26(ID) - Administrator - Powers to appoint an Administrator of the Markfed delegated to the Additional Registrar of Co-operative Societies.
Even if there was some defect in the Administrator's appointment, actions taken by him in his official capacity in the public interest and affairs of management would be valid.
[Paras 15, 17 and 19] C. Punjab State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1967, Rule 2.17 - Appeal - On the appointment of Administrator of the Federation, the powers of the Board of Directors vested in the Administrator.
The Administrator is competent to hear and decide the appeal against the orders of the Committee.
