Although the principles of the Civil Procedure Code are not fully enforceable in industrial adjudication, the principles of Section 11 regarding res judicata must be observed.
This includes the concept of constructive res judicata.
If a writ petition on the same matter and for the same relief is already dismissed on its merits, the industrial court cannot accept the same claim.
Parties cannot use different forums for the same cause of action.
[Paras 10 & 11]
