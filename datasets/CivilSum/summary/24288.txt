The termination of a driver in the Roadways Department on the basis of ill health due to a defect in eyesight falls under the definition of "ill health" as per clause (c) of Section 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
The termination is not illegal for not complying with Section 25F [Paras 7 and 8]
The loss of required vision to work as a driver or sub-normal eyesight does not fall under the definition of employment injury or occupational disease in the Employees State Insurance Act, Section 2(8), Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3(2), and Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(c).
A special compensation scheme is provided for such cases.
