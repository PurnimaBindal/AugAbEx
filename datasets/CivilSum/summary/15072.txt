Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Conviction - Termination - Acquittal in appeal - Regardless of conviction for any type of offence, an employee cannot be terminated - Any reluctance of the employer to take action is irrelevant - The termination order passed after 16 months, just before the date of acquittal by the appellate court, due to a dispute concerning a private matter raked up by a rival party is not justified.
