Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Penalty of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect - Suspension - Natural justice - The court stayed the suspension order as it was passed without giving any opportunity to the petitioner and directed the matter to be re-considered.
The order to stop two annual grade increments with cumulative effect was cancelled and a new show cause notice was given to the petitioner after the consideration of two charge sheets.
The Director's warning order was also set aside.
The Special Secretary to the Govt. in the Health Department was directed to decide the matter after considering the enquiry reports and giving a fresh show cause notice to the petitioner.
