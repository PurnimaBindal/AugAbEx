According to Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, disobedience of orders is not permitted.
In a case where the contemners failed to reimburse a treatment bill within the stipulated time, a reply filed on behalf of them was found to be unsatisfactory.
There was no valid reason given for the delay in payment and non-compliance of orders for various similar cases.
The officials of the government have demonstrated callousness and irresponsible behavior by not carrying out the court's orders despite repeated directions.
This act of contempt of orders of the Court is prima facie due to mere callousness and irresponsibility; thus, the contemners were directed to pay 18% interest on the amount for the period of delay.
This interest will be recoverable from them personally as the public exchequer should not be burdened for the officials' callousness.
