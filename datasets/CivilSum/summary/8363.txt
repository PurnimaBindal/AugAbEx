According to the Constitution of India, Article 226 and the CRPF Act, 1949, Section 11(1) and CRPF Rules, 1955, Rule 27(a), those who have been found to have purchased and possessed bottles of IMF Liquor without proper authorization and intend to smuggle it to a dry state, will face dismissal and punishment.
The sentence imposed cannot be reviewed judicially under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
