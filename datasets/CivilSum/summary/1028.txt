Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11 - Quantum of punishment - Discretion of the High Court and Labour Court to interfere with the quantum of punishment - While courts usually do not interfere with the quantum of punishment given by the punishing authority, if the punishment is found to be shockingly disproportionate, courts may provide relief - In a specific case, the petitioner was punished with dismissal for 18 months of absence, while another employee guilty of 4 years of absence was allowed to retain their job - The authority has been instructed to reconsider the punishment and award a lesser one due to the difference in the yardsticks used to award punishment.
