Jammu and Kashmir Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1961, Section 8-A, 8A(3) - Notice for deposit of contribution - The respondents did not conduct any inspection and thus violated the provisions of Section 8-A(3) of the 1961 Act by not providing a reasonable opportunity of hearing before determining the amount payable.
As a result, the order has been quashed.
