Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - House Rent Allowance - Writ Petitions disposed of with the directions to State Govt. to examine cases of all affected employees, pass appropriate detailed orders without separate representations or writ petitions - No recovery of alleged excess HRA amount shall be made from petitioners or other affected persons, reducing HRA amount shall remain stayed as per notification dated 3081988 - If representations are decided against petitioners or other affected persons, amounts drawn by them on the basis of this order shall be recoverable by adjustment in future.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
