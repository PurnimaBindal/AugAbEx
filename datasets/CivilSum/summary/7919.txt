The petitioner challenged their removal and the departmental proceedings citing statutory violations, bias, malafide, and non-compliance with natural justice principles.
The authority made the decision to conduct an inquiry before receiving a reply, and the petitioner was acquitted in a criminal case based on identical evidence.
However, the conclusions in the report contradicted the evidence on record, suggesting a biased and unfair attitude from the authority.
Adverse inference can be drawn against the authority, and the findings have become perverse.
Additionally, the rule relied upon from initiation of proceedings until completion was declared ultra vires, rendering the petition allowed accordingly.
