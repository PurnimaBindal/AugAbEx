According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, as well as the Andhra Pradesh Education Act of 1982, specifically Section 3, teaching staff working in an educational institution under a private management cannot be transferred to a different private institution.
The statute does not allow for the accommodation of a retrenched employee by another educational institution, so the powers of superintendence do not come into play.
While individuals cannot claim appointment as a right, they are entitled to consideration in accordance with the rules.
Thus, the aforementioned proceedings are considered illegal and without jurisdiction.
