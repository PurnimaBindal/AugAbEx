Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 26 - Voluntary Retirement Scheme for employees of Punjab Cooperative Societies - Voluntary Retirement - Retiral benefits - Disciplinary proceedings - Recovery proceedings - Employers are not obligated to grant voluntary retirement - If an employer accepts an employee's request for voluntary retirement despite pending or contemplated disciplinary proceedings, the retiree cannot be denied retiral benefits - Any recovery proceedings pending against the retiree may still be enforced after retirement, but this cannot be used as a reason to deny retiral benefits.
