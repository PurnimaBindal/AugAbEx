The petitioners, District Dairy Development Officers, argue that they possess a degree in Dairy Science which the State itself considers equivalent to a degree in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry.
They claim equality with employees who hold the latter degrees and perform the same duties on the same post.
The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, dictate pay parity and prohibit discrimination.
The Respondent-State is not justified in creating a classification that provides a different pay scale to employees with degrees in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry and those with equivalent degrees in Dairy Science.
The impugned order is therefore discriminatory and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
