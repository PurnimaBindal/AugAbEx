Pension - Benefit of Pension Scheme - Cut off date - The petitioner retired from service in 1977, prior to the enforcement of the pension scheme.
The scheme became applicable from 1782.
The petitioner had received Contributory Provident Fund on his retirement.
However, the petitioner is claiming that people in similar situations were given pension in light of the judgment in Harbans Lal & Ors v. State of Haryana & Ors. 1986(2) SLJ 290.
Although the judgment in Harbans Lal was overruled by Full Bench in Sher Singh Ghuman's case, it has been implemented, followed, and applied to a large number of employees.
Therefore, it would be unfair to single out the petitioner for not receiving the benefit of the Pension Scheme, whereas others who received the benefit based on the judgment.
Hence, the case of the petitioner should also be considered for granting the benefit of Pension Scheme.
