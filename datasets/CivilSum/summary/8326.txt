Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Proposal to retain retiring school teachers till end of academic session to uphold continuity in teaching on contract basis - Appointments on contract basis made - Almost all the direct recruits have since been appointed - Teacher whose contract was due to come to an end approached Central Administrating Tribunal - Tribunal gave an order of status quo - Finally ordered that applicants shall be allowed to continue in present posts till regular candidates appointed - Petitioners contended they do not require the service of additional teachers and can manage their affairs without the services of respondents - Held, if they could manage their affairs without the services of respondents, there was no reason for seeking to retain retired teachers - Services of respondent are required - Order of Tribunal proper.
[Paras 31, 33 and 34]   B.
Constitution of India - Order by Central Administrative Tribunal - Can Tribunal advise how direction is to be complied with?
- Decided that this is not within the domain of Judicial review - Once a direction is given, how it is to be adhered to or worked out is to be decided by the concerned authority to whom the direction is given - Impugned direction quashed.
