Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Age bar - Petitioner had the opportunity to appear before the Selection Committee on 25.2.1993 - Selection was postponed until 1996 - The petitioner was denied benefits simply because they had become overage - The court held that aging is a natural process and the petitioner should not be denied benefits solely because the selections were delayed - Furthermore, the petitioner approached the Court in time - The respondents were directed to consider the petitioner's name for appointment.
