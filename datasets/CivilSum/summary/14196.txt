As per Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, the workman who had their service terminated after one and a half years must be reinstated and receive back wages.
Despite the respondent's claim that the petitioner had abandoned their service, they failed to show proof of delivering a notice and did not produce the necessary documentation to support their argument of the petitioner not working for 1-1/2 years.
The court ruled that the workman had rendered a short period of service and directed that the back wages be at 50% as the petitioner was to be reinstated.
The writ petition was allowed.
