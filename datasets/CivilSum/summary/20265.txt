Junior Work Inspectors in the P.W.D.
Electricity Department have requested to be granted an equal pay scale as Senior Work Inspectors due to "Equal Pay for Equal Work" outlined in Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d).
However, Senior Work Inspectors and Junior Work Inspectors were classified under different posts with different pay scales since 1971, thus there is no justification to provide Junior Work Inspectors with a higher pay scale.
