According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, it is necessary to maintain pay scale and revise it as needed.
This is especially important for employees who are transferred between different departments, like the STAs in the Irrigation Department of Punjab who were later transferred to the Agriculture and Forests Department.
Denying these employees the revised pay scale that they are entitled to based on their work is discriminatory and violates the doctrine of "equal pay for equal work."
The petitioners are directed to receive the revised pay scale with arrears from the date of notification.
