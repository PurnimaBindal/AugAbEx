Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, canteen employees who work as contract labor should receive equal pay for equal work, have a relationship with their employer, and be entitled to regularisation.
When the maintaining of a canteen is a statutory obligation of an establishment under the Factories Act and Rules, the employees of such a canteen are legally considered in the employment of the principal employer.
They have the right to claim regularisation, obtain equal pay for equal work and be absorbed in the permanent strength of the regular staff of the establishment in regular pay scale.
