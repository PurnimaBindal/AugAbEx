The mere admission of an appeal where a decision of a single bench is under challenge cannot serve as a precedent if another decision in a similar matter of anomaly in pay scales has already been confirmed by the Supreme Court.
[Para 6] B.
If the court finds an anomaly in the pay scales of two equally placed groups of employees, the judgment must be implemented for all concerned and delay in implementation or non-implementation cannot be used to dismiss the petitioners who were not party to that petition.
