Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 31 - Criminal case - Acquittal - Departmental proceedings - Double jeopardy - Any double jeopardy rule being taken recourse does not and cannot arise since some of the charges are completely different - The standard of proof required for a departmental enquiry may not be "beyond all reasonable doubt" - In a criminal prosecution, all the witnesses except for the complainant have turned hostile - Therefore, the question of staying all departmental proceedings due to the acquittal in the criminal case does not and cannot arise.
