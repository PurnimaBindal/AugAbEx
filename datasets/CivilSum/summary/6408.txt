Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Payment of damages for use of flat - A highly qualified Doctor working in Defence Service is allotted residential accommodation according to entitlement - Respondent No. 1 asks the petitioner to vacate Defence Pool accommodation and move to higher accommodation - Damages imposed on petitioner from 5195 until vacating flat in November, 1997 - Petitioner challenges penal rate of rent - Court holds respondents did not follow Principle of Natural Justice and acted unreasonably - Impugned order is quashed.
[Paras 32, 33 and 34]
