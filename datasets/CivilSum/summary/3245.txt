As per the Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 105(3-A)(6)(iii) and Central Reserve Police Force (2nd Amendment) Rules, 1985, promotion to the post of Assistant Commandants requires experience, and experience on various Gazetted posts in the Indo-Tibetan Border Police is also acceptable.
[Paras 27, 28 and 29] B.
Those who are aggrieved by an order promoting a junior over their head should bring the matter to the court within a reasonable time as per Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
The court may refuse the exercise of extra-ordinary powers under Article 226 unless they provide a cogent explanation for the delay.
[Paras 22 to 24] C. Eligibility for promotion should be based on qualifications and not mere seniority, as per the Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 105(3-A), and the selection for the competent authority should not be limited to only those officers who fall within the consideration zone up to the number of posts.
