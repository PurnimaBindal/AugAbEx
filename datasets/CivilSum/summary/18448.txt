Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 118, 123 - Primary teacher - Breach of code of conduct - Charge sheet - Three charge sheets related to the same charges were issued, but earlier two were dropped due to a defect in the constitution of the Disciplinary Committee that passed them - No enquiry was conducted - Therefore, it is not necessary to have a de novo enquiry.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Plea of mala fide - Ground that since Petitioner had raised issue pertaining to wage revision with government official, disciplinary action is mala fide - However, when all the teachers included had raised the pay revision issue, why would the school single out Petitioner?
Hence, the action cannot be termed malafide.
[Para 13] C Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 123 - Charge on teacher to enter examination hall - Whether a misconduct - Held, when the teacher was not deputed as invigilator and prima facie had no authority to enter examination hall, such a charge would constitute a misconduct under Rule 123 of the 1973 Rules.
