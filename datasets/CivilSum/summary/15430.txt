Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Test for promotion - Opportunity for promotion must be offered to similarly situated employees - An employee cannot be penalized for being deprived of a promotional examination due to no fault of their own, such as exigencies of service - Petitioners, who were on election duty, were unable to take the test and cannot be ignored - Directions have been issued to provide them with an opportunity - AIR 1989 SC 1133 has been followed.
