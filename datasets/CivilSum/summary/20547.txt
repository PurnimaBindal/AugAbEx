The Working Journalist and Other Newspaper Employees [Conditions of Service] and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955, Sections 11, 2(d), 104(4) were amended by the Working Journalists and other Newspaper Employees [Conditions of Service] and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) Act, 1989.
The Wage Board Report, known as the Bachawat Award, has been accepted and put into action through an order.
To legally enforce the award, it must be modified by extending the limitation of upgradation to newspaper establishments in clauses 1A to V. The award is not in violation of the petitioners' rights under Articles 19(1)(a) and 19(1)(g) of the Constitution.
After the amendment of the relevant sections, the old provisions can no longer be used to contest the grouping of the All India Establishments.
The modified award is to be implemented by all establishments, and the employees will receive wages starting from 111988.
The newspaper establishments of all classes will benefit from part 6(2) of Section 1 of part I of Chapter IX and shall not be stepped up by more than two classes.
