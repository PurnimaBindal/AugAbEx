Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment on compassionate ground deemed after 26 years of death of employee - Petition dismissed - Determined: (1) Appointment on compassionate ground is not a means of joining the service, but is only meant to aid surviving family members overcome sudden financial crises created by the death of the breadwinner - After such a large delay of over 26 years, it cannot be concluded that the situation created by the death of the petitioner's father is continuing.
(2) This ruling will not prevent the petitioner from receiving the respondent Board's offered financial assistance of Rs. 3,00,000/-.
