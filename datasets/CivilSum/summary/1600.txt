Navy Pension Regulations, 1964, Regulation 79(1)(i) - Petitioner was denied pensionary benefits due to lack of requisite qualifying service - Service below age of 17 years is excluded from total service for pension eligibility - Petitioner claimed to have rendered 15 years and 2 months of service - Prescribed qualifying service for pension is 15 years - Respondents calculated his service at 14 years and 24 days - Court held that employer cannot deny benefits after accepting regular service - Petitioner entitled to pensionary benefits and service rendered as a "boy" in Indian Navy must be counted towards qualifying service.
