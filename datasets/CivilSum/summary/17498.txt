According to Punjab Civil Services Rules (Vol. II), Rules 532A, individuals who resign from service are not eligible for pensionary benefits.
The petitioner requested voluntary retirement, but it was rejected as ineligible.
Despite the rejection, he resigned, but he cannot claim pension and other retiral benefits by alleging that his request for retirement was wrongly declined.
As a result, he is not entitled to pension, gratuity, etc. under any rule or instructions.
