Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Absence from Duty - Dismissal - Reinstatement - The dismissal of a workman for remaining absent from duties for 62 days with a previous record of unauthorised absence for 68 days resulting in the stoppage of two annual increments was justified.
The disciplinary authority had taken past conduct into consideration, and the charge was serious.
The Labour Court was not justified in directing reinstatement with full back wages and holding the punishment to be disproportionate.
[Paras 6, 11, 12 and 15]
