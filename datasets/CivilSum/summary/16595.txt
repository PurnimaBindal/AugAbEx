The Employment Exchange Compulsory Notification of Vacancies Act, 1959, Section 4 states that Health Assistants (Seasonal) can be appointed for a period of 3-1/2 months.
The petitioner in this case did not receive an interview despite being issued a call letter by the respondents.
The respondents were found to be negligent in their duties, although the selected candidate had already been relieved.
The petition was dismissed, but directions were given to consider the petitioner for future selections.
