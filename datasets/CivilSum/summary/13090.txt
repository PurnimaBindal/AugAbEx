The Bipartite settlement stipulates that the department cannot conduct an inquiry for one year from the date of the offence if the offence for which the delinquent is prosecuted is the same as that charged in the misconduct charge-sheet.
In this case, the charges levelled against the petitioner were different from those for which he was being prosecuted.
Thus, Clause 194 of the Bipartite Settlement does not apply, and there is no obstruction to initiating departmental proceedings against the delinquent.
