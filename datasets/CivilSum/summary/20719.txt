Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sch.
2 Item 6 - Direct and temporary/ad hoc appointees' seniority in regular service - Seniority of direct appointees in regular service starts from the date of appointment, not from the confirmation date after completing the probation period - The seniority of ad-hoc/temporary employees regularised during the probation period cannot be higher than regular appointees - Only after they are absorbed in regular service, the seniority of those regularised will begin.
