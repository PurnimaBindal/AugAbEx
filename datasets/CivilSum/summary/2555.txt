Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Air Force Act, 1950, Section 110 - Delegation of authority - Conveyance of decision to hold General Court Martial by a subordinate authority does not amount to delegation of authority, as decision was taken by competent authority.
[Paras 5 to 8]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Court Martial Proceedings - Objection as to Competent Authority - Objection cannot be raised for the first time in writ jurisdiction if it was not raised before the authorities.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction/Court Martial Proceedings - High Court cannot re-examine evidence to determine correctness of findings of Court Martial.
[Para 15]
Air Force Act, 1950, Section 110 - Air Force Rules, 1969, Rules 102 and 103 - Counsel Fee - General Court Martial - Authorities are not obligated to pay counsel fees for the accused to defend themselves, and inability to pay fees does not invalidate proceedings.
[Para 20]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Air Force Act, 1950, Section 153 - Court Martial - Hearing - Natural justice - There is no provision for accused to make a representation or be heard before the confirmation of their sentence.
