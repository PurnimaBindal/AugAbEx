As per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 regarding eligibility, experience is required for the appointment as Vocational Master in Automobile Engineering.
However, the petitioner's objection, that he had not gained three years of experience after obtaining his Diploma in Automobile Engineering, is overridden by the fact that he had passed the Diploma before the job was advertised.
In accordance with the decision made in Swaran Singh v. State of Punjab, the petitioner is qualified for appointment and the respondents must consider him for the post.
