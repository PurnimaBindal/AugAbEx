According to Sections 14 and 22 of the Minimum Wages Act, to claim overtime, the employee must work on a day in excess of the number of hours constituting a normal working day and a minimum rate of wages should be established under the Act.
Additionally, the overtime U/s 14 is payable only to those employees who are receiving a minimum rate of wage as prescribed under the Minimum Wages Act.
However, employees of the Municipality who are getting much more than the wages fixed under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 under the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Service (Scales of Pay and Allowances) Rules, 1967 are not qualified for overtime U/s 14 of the Act.
