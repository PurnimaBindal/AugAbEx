Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Municipal Employees (Pension and Provident Fund) Rules, 1994, Rules 2(j) and 8 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 3.17 - Retiral benefits - The Municipal Corporation has been directed to count the work charge period of every employee for the purpose of qualifying service for retiral benefits.
If the period counted qualifies the employee for pension, it must be paid to him.
The decision in AIR 1988 Punjab & Haryana 265 (FB) has been followed.
