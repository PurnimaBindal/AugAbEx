Interpretation of Sections 42 and 64 of the Insurance Act of 1938 regarding the appointment of surveyors and loss assessors, and the competency of the Controller to appoint unqualified and unlicenced persons.
It was found that, according to Section 64 UM of the Act, an insurer may employ any person to survey a loss, as long as the claim is worth less than Rs. 20,000/- and the person is not disqualified, although the term "disqualified" only refers to as defined in Section 42(4) of the Act.
It was also determined that sub-Section (6) of Section 64 does not restrict the insurer's right to settle claims through its own officers.
