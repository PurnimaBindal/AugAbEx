UP District Office (Collectorate) Clerk Grade Service Rules (1980), Rules 5, 16 and 17 - Promotion/Reversion - Promotion from Stenographer, Grade II to Stenographer- Grade I - Promoted candidate was the only suitable option - Reversion made without hearing the promoted employee - Reversion deemed illegal - Order quashed.
[Paras 12 and 14]  B.
'Suitable' and 'Past conduct reflected in the character roll' are not equivalent terms - A person can have a positive character roll but not be fit for promotion - Conversely, a person may be qualified but their past conduct disqualifies them for promotion - Interpretation should be context-appropriate.
