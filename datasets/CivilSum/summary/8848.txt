Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c) - Termination - Industrial Dispute - The Labour Court found that the workman's appeal upheld the order of termination, therefore reference u/s 10(1)(c) was not maintainable - The Court also noted that the remedy under the Act and the one the workman took were alternative - As the workman chose to appeal, he could not raise an industrial dispute - The reference was thus decided in favor of the employer, but due to the lack of decision on merits, the case was remanded to the Labour Court for further proceedings.

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Res Judicata - Even when a departmental authority's decision is made in appeal, it does not operate as res judicata when proceedings are initiated under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 for adjudication - Followed 1993(1) PLR 424.
