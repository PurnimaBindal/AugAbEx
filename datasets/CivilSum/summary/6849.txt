Constitution of India, Articles 311, 309 - Change in date of birth - Permissibility - Matriculation certificate to be treated as correct - Review of determination of date of birth allowed under Clause (B) of instruction No. 76 - Petitioner's correct date of birth recorded in Identity card and submitted in Seva Abhilekh records - Service records mention age but no explanation provided for how date of birth was determined - Entries in service register cannot nullify requirements under instruction No. 76 - Matriculation certificate acknowledged in Identity Card by respondents - Petitioner's claim for correction of date of birth reasonable and not raised at fag end of service - Direction given for necessary correction.
