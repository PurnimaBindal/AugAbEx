An advocate who works in the office of Advocate General is not considered a whole time employee of the government and is eligible to contest and hold office in Bar Council elections.
The Rules state that advocates who are in full time employment of corporations, banks, and other institutions are ineligible to participate in the elections.
An affidavit must be provided by practicing advocates to declare that they are not regularly employed and not disqualified under the Advocate Act.
Rule 49 of the Bar Council of India Rules, as amended, is not in violation of the Constitution of India.
The names of the Advocate Generals cannot be removed from electoral rolls as they are not comparable to those advocates who are in employment of corporations, banks, etc. and are not considered full time government employees.
These Law Officers are advocates and cannot be excluded from the definition of the term.
