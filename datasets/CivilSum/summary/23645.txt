Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Junior lecturers working in private aided colleges must receive equal pay for equal work as those working in Govt.
Colleges of Kerala State and those working in the Calicut University - However, Junior Lecturers of pre-degree classes in two Colleges of U.T.
of Lakshadweep affiliated to the Calicut University are being paid lower scale of pay - No difference in the duties and responsibilities of the two has been pointed out - Administrator of U.T.
has recommended for equal pay scales for such Junior Lecturers - Prescribing different pay scales for them is not permissible - Junior Lecturers teaching pre-degree classes cannot be paid lower scale of pay based on only that ground.
