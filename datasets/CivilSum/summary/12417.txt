Haryana Education Code, Article 67 - Revised scales - Appointment against unsanctioned post - Under Article 67 of Haryana Education Code, grant-in-aid for the appointment against unsanctioned post is inadmissible, as determined in Ajay Kumar Sexena's case.
Department is not required to pay the person appointed against unsanctioned posts without its approval.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rules 8, 9 and 10 - Pay and allowances - Concealment of material facts - Misrepresentation at the time of appointment - Requisite qualification not fulfilled - An appointment who does not satisfy the necessary qualification for the post is deemed illegal and is against the rules.
Such individuals are not given the privilege of revised scales of allowances.
The decision to withhold the allowances is not in violation of the rules or principles of natural justice.
Only those who meet the qualifications are eligible for revised scales.
