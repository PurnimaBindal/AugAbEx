The son of a deceased applicant for a post as Assistant Teacher filed a timely application for compassionate appointment after his father died in harness.
However, the District Compassionate Committee rejected the petitioner's prayer based solely on the grounds of delay, without considering other factors and without properly assessing the petitioner's case.
The petitioner filed within the stipulated period, and as a result, the decision of the compassionate committee was invalidated.
