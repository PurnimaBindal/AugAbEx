The Maharashtra State Transport Employees Service Regulations consist of Clause 49 of the 1956 Settlement and Clause 19 of the 1985 Settlement, which deal with the benefits and time scale of pay applicable to daily wage workers and time scale workers.
According to Clause 49, those who complete 180 days of continuous service are eligible for benefits, but it is unclear whether this clause has been superseded by Clause 19, which provides for the absorption of employees who have also completed 180 days of continuous service.
The court held that both clauses operate in different fields and that there is a difference between the status of employees and benefits accruing to workmen.
Therefore, there is no question of supersession.
Despite the cancellation of Clause 49, workmen who have been working since 1978 are entitled to benefits provided they satisfy all conditions prescribed in Resolution No. 8856 and Clause 19 of the 1985 settlement.
