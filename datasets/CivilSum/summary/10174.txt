Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311(1) - Challenge to Charge Sheet - Argument that the officer lacked power and jurisdiction to issue and appoint an inquiry officer - Claiming that the appointing authority was the Executive Committee, not the Director - However, Article 311(1) does not require that the authority in power to dismiss or remove should initiate or conduct the inquiry, only that the civil servant cannot be dismissed by a subordinate officer - Therefore, the challenged orders are legal and valid.
