Constitution of India, Article 16 - Constitution of India, Articles 162 and 226 - Regularisation of services of employees - State Government and court cannot regularize services of employees appointed in contravention of statutory rule or constitutional mandate - Article 162 or any Act cannot be used for directing regularisation of services - Court cannot grant interim relief to illegal appointees - Article 226 cannot be used for such purposes - Indirect orders for direct violations are not allowed.
2004(4) SCT 728 (SC) followed.
