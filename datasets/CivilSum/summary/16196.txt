Constitution of India, Article 309 in combination with Article 187(3) - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1969 - Equating of posts/ pay scales - When the government equates one post to another, the increment in pay scale of one post necessarily results in proportional increase in pay scale of the other post - State has not governed the recruitment and terms of secretarial staff using any law, only rules set by the Governor after consultation with the Speaker govern these terms - Senior Translator post has been equated to Assistant Grade A, while Junior Translator post to a clerk - Future revisions of pay scale rules cannot displace or modify rules decreed under Article 309 and 187(3) - Senior Translators Grade II are entitled to same pay scale as Senior Assistants, as well as all subsequent pay advances.
