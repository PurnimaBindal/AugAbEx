Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 162 - Appointment on compassionate ground - Executive instructions issued by the Govt. or the Board are intended to provide only immediate relief to the family of the deceased employee - Refusing relief to a widow due to a late application causes discrimination against the entire family.
The instructions are arbitrary and violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 162, 14 and 16 - Employment - Appointment - Executive instructions - A widow applies for employment of a minor child who is neither educated nor eligible for employment.
Granting of employment will be deferred until the child has received sufficient education and attained majority.
The interpretation of the instructions allows for the consideration of the candidate despite their eligibility while interpreting the instructions as excuse for late application would save them from discrimination under Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Para 6] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 162 - Employment on compassionate ground - Executive instructions - A petitioner's father died in 1982 when petitioner was ineligible for government jobs.
After becoming eligible, the petitioner submitted an application, which was rejected.
Such an interpretation of the instructions would result in extreme hardship to the petitioner, and therefore, the impugned order has been quashed, and the respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's claim for the post for which he has applied and is eligible in accordance with the law within three months.
