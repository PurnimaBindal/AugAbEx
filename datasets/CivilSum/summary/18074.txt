Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Pension - Retirement benefits - Issuance of charge sheet/Departmental proceedings - Withholding Retirement benefits on Retirement - Gratuity - Unnecessary delay in payment - The State Government can initiate departmental proceedings before or after retirement however, it cannot withhold retirement benefits.
The State can only proceed to recover after some amount is found due against the retiree.
Payment of all retirement benefits is the right of the retiree, and any delay in payment entitles them to interest at a rate of 12%.
