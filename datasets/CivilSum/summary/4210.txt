A. Madhya Pradesh Panchayati Raj Adhiniyam, 1993 - A policy decision was made for appointing Panchyat Karmis and Panchyat Sachiv.
It is not tenable to argue that appointments should be made only based on marks obtained in the educational examination.
Several other factors have to be taken into consideration, and the assessment made by the Panchayat should not be interfered with.
The decision should be left to the higher authority or permitted to be agitated in departmental appeals.
[Paras 8 and 19] B.
Merit system refers to a system of appointing employees wherein appointments are made based on merit rather than political considerations.
[Para 9] C Appointments to the office of Panchayat Karmis under Article 16 of the Constitution of India - It is made clear that the selection made by Panchayat should not be normally interfered with.
