Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension & Gratuity - Cut off date - Revision of pension and gratuity on revision of pay scales and dearness allowance - Gratuity is a one-time payment, and the transaction is considered closed and cannot be revisited at a later stage - It is calculated based on the emoluments being drawn by the retiree on the date of retirement - Payment of pension is also determined by the rules existing on the date of retirement - Retirees of earlier dates are not automatically entitled to benefit from subsequent amendments of rules or instructions prescribing a cut off date - The cutoff date is necessary for the state to meet its financial liability.
[Paras 5, 7, 8 & 10]
