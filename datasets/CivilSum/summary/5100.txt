Constitution of India, Article 311 - State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958 (Kerala), Rule 9(a)(i) - Provisional promotion granted, but employee continued in service for a few years and later was reverted for unsatisfactory work - Reversion does not constitute a reduction in rank, although there may be a loss of emoluments - Continuation in a post does not confer any rights for confirmation.
