Constitution of India, Article 32 - Admission - MBBS/BDS course - All India quota - The scheme framed in Sharwan Kumar's case stipulates the process of allotment of 15 percent all India quota - The II round of counselling was supposed to be concluded by a specific date - There were vacancies arising after the II round of counselling - Rejecting the plea for a III round of counselling, the court opined that there is no provision for III round of counselling under the scheme - Altering the scheme when seats are found vacant could lead to an endless process until all seats under All India Quota are filled up.
