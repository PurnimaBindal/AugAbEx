Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Unless equal work is established, the claimants of equal pay for equal work cannot apply the doctrine of equal pay and will not receive a writ to pay on that basis.
[Para 21]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Temporary or ad hoc appointments without following the regular process and procedure for selection as per rules will not result in a right to continue after the expiry of the term of appointment and no direction can be issued to reappoint or reinstate them.
They are free to contest in the regular selection.
