Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension/Reduction of pension - The petitioner has been receiving monthly pension at the rate of Rs. 271/- per month, but is alleging that he should be paid a monthly pension of Rs. 406/- per month as he has received commuted pension.
However, the petitioner has failed to provide any substantial evidence to support his claim.
As a result, the petition has been dismissed.
[Para 2]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - Availability of - The petitioner is claiming interest at the rate of 18% per month on commuted pension, but this is a disputed matter and there is an alternative and equally efficacious remedy available to the petitioner to approach a civil court by filing a regular suit.
This Court cannot exercise its extra-ordinary jurisdiction under Article 226 to decide such a dispute.
This Court can normally deal with only those cases where a party alleges violation or infringement of its fundamental, legal or other right and seeks enforcement of that right.
[Paras 3 and 4]  C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Recovery of money - In this case, recovery of money is at issue and disputed questions of facts are involved.
To entertain a petition like this on the grounds that civil court proceedings will take a long time, would defeat the system of civil jurisprudence and frustrate the working of the civil courts.
Matters like recovery of money give rise to disputed questions of facts, which form the subject-matter of issues for trial in the civil court proceedings.
