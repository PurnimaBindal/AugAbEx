According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(aaa) and 25F, the term "monthly wages" refers to the average wages earned by an employee.
The principle of construing meaning of words and terms used in a statute states that the words of a statute must be given their ordinary meaning and the natural and ordinary meaning of the words should not be departed from.
There can only be a different meaning if the legal context in which the words are used requires it.
With regard to Section 2(aaa), the language used is entirely clear and unambiguous.
Thus, the terms "monthly wages" or "average pay" used in other statutes like the Payment of Gratuity Act cannot be imported into the definition of wages used in Sections 2(aaa), 25F, and 25B of the ID Act for the purpose of computing the amount of retrenchment compensation.
