Bombay Civil Service Rules, 1959, Rule 161 - Compulsory retirement - An order of premature retirement is not a penalty and is not subject to the provisions of Article 311 of the Constitution.
The order must be bona fide, reasonable, and not arbitrary.
[Paras 8 and 11]

Bombay Civil Service Rules, 1959, Rule 161 - Compulsory retirement - In cases where an employee's integrity is doubtful but there is not enough evidence to take disciplinary action, he may be ordered to retire prematurely in the interest of the public.
[Para 21]
