Handicap allowance - Govt. policy - Pay scale - In a suit seeking a declaration that the plaintiff was entitled to be regularised in the classical and vernacular teacher cadre as a Sewing Teachress and receive all benefits, privileges and rights enjoyed by other similarly situated Classical and Vernacular Teacheresses, the defendants admitted the plaintiff's handicapped status and her entitlement to the handicap allowance under government policy, as set out in a government circular dated 18th April, 1973.
The courts below erred in holding that the plaintiff was not entitled to the benefit of the same pay scale as enjoyed by the Classical and Vernacular Teacheresses.
The plaintiff is entitled to the revised pay scale and increments, in addition to the handicap allowance.
[Paras 11 to 15]
Admitted facts - Need not be proved.
[Para 11]
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 8 Rule 5 - Admitted fact - Handicap allowance - Defendants' denial of State for the fact in the plaint with regard to handicap allowance was evasive and not in accordance with the provisions of Order 8 Rules 4 and 5, which require a categorical denial of a fact.
Therefore, it can be inferred and concluded that the defendants admitted the plaintiff's handicapped status and entitlement to the handicap allowance as per Government policy.
