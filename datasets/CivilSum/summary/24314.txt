In accordance with the Evidence Act, Section 115 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10, adverse inference can be drawn if a record is not produced.
However, it can only be drawn if the workman claims completion of 240 days within the specific period for which the record was not produced.
If the workman makes a general assertion of completion of 240 days, but the record shows that he was never in 240 days continuous service over two years, then no adverse inference can be drawn.
