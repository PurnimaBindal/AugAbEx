The Tamil Nadu State Judicial Service (Cadre and Recruitment) Rules, 1995 allow for the screening of applications for the viva voce examination based on cut off marks, which is a reasonable method to manage the large number of candidates.
These candidates are not denied their right to consideration nor is Article 14 of the Constitution violated.

In the case of exigencies, recruitment can be made through a viva voce test, with the consultation of the High Court, and this method of selection is lawful.
