According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India and the Baba Farid University of Health Sciences Prospectus for admissions to Medical/Dental colleges, the term "preference" means priority only, and does not exclude a candidate from being considered for other available courses.
They are still equally entitled to be considered against other courses if not granted the preference.
[Paras 11, 12, 13, 18 and 19] B.
The term "option" is not a substitute for the term "preference".
When an individual makes an option, he makes a choice in favor of one to the exclusion of others.
However, mere preference does not amount to exclusion from others.
[Para 11] C. In the case of Admissions, preference is given to those with superior merit.
Mere preference given by a candidate does not mean that candidates of lower merit can be admitted to the exclusion of those with superior merit.
[Para 15] D. An honest opinion expressed while advising an institute cannot be the basis of a legal analysis.
