Constitution of India, Article 226 - HUDA Service Regulation, 1989 - Promotion - Petitioners cannot be promoted to Junior Engineer (Civil) from Surveyors as per Statutory HUDA Regulations because they do not provide for such promotion - Authorities and Govt. must provide promotion channels for different categories of employees to Junior Engineer (Civil) posts - Court cannot compel respondents to promote petitioners to Junior Engineer (Civil) posts against statutory regulations.
