Central Civil Services Pension Rules, 1972, Rule 17(1)(b) - Contractual service - Counting with permanent service - Petitioner seeks counting entire service for pensionary benefits - Petitioner must refund monetary benefits of contract service within three months of re-engagement as per Rule 17 - Petitioner's argument that rules don't apply since appointment in 1966 is not tenable - Petitioner became confirmed incumbent in 1976 - Since Petitioner did not exercise option as per Rule 17(1)(b) within three months of re-engagement, contractual service cannot be added for pensionary benefits.
