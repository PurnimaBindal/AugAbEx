An employee is not allowed to bequeath their family pension through a Will, as family pension is not considered an estate.
Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 54(7)(a) and Succession Act, 1925, Sections 63(c) and 276 are referenced to emphasize this point.
If a government employee passes away, the legally wedded wife who is listed as the nominee will be entitled to the family pension, regardless of any Will the employee may have executed.
It is further stated that the deceased employee can bequeath other benefits such as provident and gratuity, but not family pension.
The Will must be attested by at least two witnesses who have seen the testator sign the Will.
Lastly, it is repeated that family pension cannot be bequeathed through Will, but other pensionary benefits like provident fund, gratuity, and retrial dues can be bequeathed.
References include Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 54(7)(a), Succession Act, 1925, Sections 63(c), 276, and Evidence Act, Section 68.
