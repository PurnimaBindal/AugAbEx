Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 5 - Enquiry proceedings - Pre-mature retirement as punishment - Principles of natural justice - Prosecution witnesses were not listed with the delinquent, non-listed witnesses were examined, effective cross-examination was not possible, and enquiry completed within an hour on an adjourned date without proper notice to the delinquent, hence the violation of natural justice clear - The enquiry report and punishment order cannot stand.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry - The enquiry was conducted at 445 PM on the same day instead of the scheduled time of 11 AM, leading to inadequate time for the delinquent to meet the charges - Such conduct by the Enquiry Officer supports the allegations against him, and hence the enquiry report cannot be sustained.
