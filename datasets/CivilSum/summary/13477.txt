The Punjab Service of Engineers Class-II P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1941 - Seniority - Promotion - Despite being senior to the defendants in a civil suit, the petitioner was given a later date of promotion than them - The claim made by the respondents that the petitioner's dispute has been fully redressed and that he has been given rightful seniority cannot be supported - The petitioner's claim is also not considered irrelevant.

(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1941 - Seniority - Promotion - The petitioner had retired from service while his case for promotion was pending - While he may not be able to enjoy actual promotion, granting him any benefits might still result in some monetary gain - His retirement is not reason enough to deny him relief - The respondents are directed to reconsider the petitioner's claim for ante-dated promotion from the date of promotion of a person junior to him.
