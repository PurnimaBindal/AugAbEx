Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Discrimination - The selection process for Teacher Grades II and III - Candidates of urban areas were granted ten bonus marks while candidates of rural areas were granted fifteen marks as per state govt policy.
As a result, the petitioners who belong to an urban area obtained fewer marks compared to those belonging to rural areas.
The policy contradicts the doctrine of equality and rationality, thus it is not acceptable.
