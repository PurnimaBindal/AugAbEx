"The selection policy of the Corporation states that the post of Director should be filled by outside candidates first, and internal candidates should only be considered if there are no excellent outsider candidates available, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
The Selection Committee initially interviewed the internal applicants while keeping the options open to choose better candidates from external sources as well.
Then the committee called the outsiders for interviews and allowed the internal applicants to participate in the interviews too.
The selection committee prepared a panel of selected candidates based on the interviews of both internal and external applicants, and this action was not in violation of the policy or Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 11, 12, and 14]"
