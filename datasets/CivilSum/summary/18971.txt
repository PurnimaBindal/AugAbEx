The petitioner's fresh option certificate for extension of two years Army service was rejected under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
The petitioner filed a non-option certificate on 16.7.97 to exercise his first option, which the Screening Committee considered and concluded was filed on 14.12.98.
As the petitioner's exercise of the second option on 10299 was after the Screening Committee concluded the non-option certificate in view of clause 6(b) of Administrative guideline became conclusive, he cannot contend contrary to clause 6(b).
Therefore, no interference under Article 226 is warranted.
