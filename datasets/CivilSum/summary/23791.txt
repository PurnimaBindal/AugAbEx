Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 7 - Promotion - Consideration - The petitioner was not included in the select list for appointment to the Haryana Civil Service for the years 1980, 1982 and 1983.
However, his service record was considered after expunging of adverse entries, but the High Powered Committee found him unsuitable.
There was no lack of consideration or inclusion of irrelevant or inadmissible aspects.
The petitioner only had the right of consideration, and since his name was considered, he cannot grieve against his non-selection.
[Paras 4 and 8]
