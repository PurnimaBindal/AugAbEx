Civil Procedure Code Section 11 and Industrial Disputes Act 1947 Section 10(1) - Res judicata - Refusal to make reference - The government's refusal to make a reference under Section 10 of the ID Act does not serve as a grant or availment of the industrial dispute remedy and does not operate as a res judicata or estoppel to a subsequent civil suit for the same cause.
