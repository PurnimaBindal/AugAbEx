The PGI Medical Technologists Association demanded interest on delayed payment of arrears of salary following a revision in pay scales.
However, the association disclaimed the acceptance of the revised pay scales and went on strike, putting forth a fresh set of demands.
The Home Secretary of the Chandigarh Administration referred the matter to the Labour-cum-Industrial Tribunal in Chandigarh for adjudication.
Therefore, the demand for grant of interest at this stage is premature.
The writ petition has no merit and is dismissed with costs.
[Paras 10, 12 and 15] B Article 226 of the Constitution of India states that a writ of mandamus can only be issued when the petitioner can demonstrate a legal right to the performance of a legal duty imposed by the Constitution of India or by a statute or common law, or by rules or orders having the force of law.
