Constitution of India, Article 16 - Recovery from pension gratuity etc. of an employee whether can be made - The order for recovery of Rs. 89463/- from the gratuity of the petitioner, whose pay was refixed, has been set aside - Recovery cannot be imposed in the absence of any fraud or misrepresentation by the employee - Referring to 1995(1) SCT 668 (SC).
