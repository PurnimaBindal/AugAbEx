Army Act (46 of 1950), Ss.
38, 106 - Family pension - Deserter - A soldier was declared a deserter but then his status was changed to missing and later back to deserter.
The authorities failed to find evidence that the soldier did not intend to desert his unit.
Steps were not taken to find him or know his whereabouts.
It cannot be assumed that the soldier did not intend to return to his services and therefore deserted the unit.
In addition, the Army authorities did not consider the aspect of presumption of death.
Denying family pension to the soldier's wife on the ground of her husband's deserter status is improper.
