Constitution of India, Article 16 - The petitioner was chosen by the Government for the role of female attendant - However, due to police verification and other related documents being delayed, the petitioner was rejected - The fault was not that of the petitioner's - It is unfair to deny the petitioner the appointment as a form of punishment - The petition is granted and the Government is ordered to provide the petitioner with the appointment.
