Haryana Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Vol. II Rule 5 - Family pension - For a work charge employee, if regularisation of service is done after eight years and the employee dies within one year of regularisation of service, the whole service is regularised and the wife is entitled to family pension.
1990(2) PLR 82 is considered as reference.
