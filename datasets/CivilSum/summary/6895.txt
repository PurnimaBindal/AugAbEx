Under the Employees Provident Fund Scheme of 1952 and Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, the termination of workmen's service must comply with Section 25 regulations.
In this case, the Labour Court found that the termination of service was in violation of Section 25 and constituted retrenchment.
The Court ordered the petitioner's reinstatement with 75% backwages and determined that the workshop and Foundry Unit were interdependent.
The petitioner contended that the mere fact that the Foundry Unit was working did not mean the workmen were retrenched.
However, the Court found that the petitioner failed to prove that the units were separate.
As such, the found fact cannot be interfered with in writ jurisdiction.
The closure occurred in 1984, and since the case is being decided in 2008, the respondents must not have remained idle for such a long period.
Therefore, backwages are restricted to 25%.
