Entitlement Rules to Casualty Pensionary Awards to the Armed Forces Personnel, 1982, Rules 17(b), (c) and 27(c) - Assessment of disability - It is not justifiable for any other authority to conduct a reassessment and reduce the disability of a patient below the eligible limits, especially without examining the patient, once the duly constituted Medical Board has made an assessment of the disability after examining the patient.
