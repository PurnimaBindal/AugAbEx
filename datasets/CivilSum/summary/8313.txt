Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Writ jurisdiction - A private organisation, Charity Eye Hospital, is the respondent - It must be demonstrated that the organization is fulfilling a public duty in order to be held accountable to writ jurisdiction - Despite the Supreme Court's categorization of running a hospital as an industry, the organization running the hospital cannot be said to be fulfilling a public duty - Thus, the respondent is not subjected to writ jurisdiction.
