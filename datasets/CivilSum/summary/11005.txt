Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rule 16 - Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - The filling of vacancies must be done by calling names from the Employment Exchange, even provisional vacancies - The wording "or in such other manner as may be deemed fit" - Services obtained through the exchange are seen as fair - There is no arbitrary effort in this process - This procedure is Constitutional.
[Paras 10 & 11]
