Punjab Police Rules, 1934 (as applicable to Haryana) Rule 13.7 - Training School Course - Petitioner was mostly out of Haryana during the course and received no communication or notice - Presumption is that job information could not reach him - Lower School Course is critical for the constable's career and passing it determines his future - The authorities must refer the petitioner in the next session of the course and treat him along with those who already passed, giving him all consequential benefits of service.
