Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 11-A and 17-A - Coal Mines (Nationalization) Act, 1973, Section 14 - Writ jurisdiction - Workmen must not delay case with laches - Despite a delay in action for industrial disputes from first respondent workmen in 1971 that was not addressed until 1988, the Tribunal directs the re-appointment of the first respondent with prospective effect and no back wage; however, first respondent is not in employment since 1971 and is guilty of delay and laches.
In the interest of justice, directing the implementation of this award is necessary.
