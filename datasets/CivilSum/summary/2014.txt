The Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 39 and 40 state that a demand notice may be issued for the deposit of contributions for a relevant period.
However, the applicant-appellant has a statutory duty to deposit both the employer's and employee's contribution under Ss.
39 and 40 of the Act with the Corporation even without receiving a demand notice.
A code number is allotted to factories or establishments for the purpose of correspondence and document retrieval.
The Act and Regulations do not specify that a factory or establishment is not under a statutory obligation to deposit contributions until the code number is allotted.
