Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection - The tribunal made the right decision in upholding the respondents' appointment to certain posts, as they had successfully passed all stages of selection and were not involved in any unlawful or illegal activity.
There is no reason to interfere with the decision made by the tribunal, particularly given the current challenging economic climate in which many individuals are experiencing unemployment.
