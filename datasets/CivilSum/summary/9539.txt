Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Recruitment - Rejection of unsigned application - Petitioner sought recruitment as Science Mistress - Petitioner argued that short-listing process was arbitrary - However, the petitioner's application was deemed legally non-existent due to being unsigned - Accordingly, the petitioner's candidacy was rightly rejected based on this issue.
