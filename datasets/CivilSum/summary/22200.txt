Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Termination - Natural justice - Appointing authority - If the appointing authority lacks the power to make an appointment after their own services have been terminated, the appointment order is deemed null and void, and the principles of natural justice do not apply - Any argument that the appointees should have received notice or been made a party to the court case invalidating their appointments would be pointless.
