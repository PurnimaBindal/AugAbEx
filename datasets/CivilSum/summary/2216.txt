Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Dismissal from service - Approval of - An application for the approval of the dismissal of a workman was previously dismissed as infructuous; however, a fresh inquiry was conducted and the workman was ultimately dismissed due to grave misconduct.
An application was subsequently moved for the revival of the earlier application seeking approval of the dismissal, arguing that the Tribunal lacked the power to review the earlier order.
The court held that the application seeking revival should be revived and restored, and decided in accordance with the provisions of Section 33(2)(b) after giving the parties concerned an opportunity to be heard.
[Paras 17 & 18]
