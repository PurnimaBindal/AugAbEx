Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rule 9 - Promotion - Departmental enquiry - Promotion to the post of Chief Engineer - The petitioner was passed over for promotion on the basis of ongoing departmental proceedings against him, but was later exonerated.
After a second charge-sheet was issued, the petitioner should have been considered for promotion immediately upon exoneration and the respondents cannot argue the second charge-sheet as sufficient reason for overlooking the petitioner's promotion.
The court has directed the competent authority to immediately consider the petitioner for promotion to the post of Chief Engineer.
