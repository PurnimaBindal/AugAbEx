Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - De novo enquiry/Appeal/Department Enquiry - If an order of de novo enquiry is passed during the pendency of appeal, the appeal automatically becomes infructuous and should be dismissed as such - The order of de novo enquiry passed cannot be considered as an interim order which can fizzle out automatically - Service of the worker may be terminated on the basis of earlier enquiry report, but the enquiry report of the second enquiry will continue to exist and the first enquiry report becomes non-existent - Labour Court quashed the termination on the basis of non-existing enquiry report, and the awarded judgment is legally valid.
