According to Constitution of India, Articles 110 and 226 and Evidence Act, 1872, Section 35, the date of birth and retirement can be determined by examining entries in the School register or a certificate issued by school authorities.
However, the School Register alone has no evidentiary value.
In this case, the Petitioner initially submitted a Medical Certificate to authenticate his age at the time of service regularization and later requested to use the School certificate instead.
However, since the Petitioner did not specify any reason or occasion for the sudden change, the School certificate cannot be relied upon.
