Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Arrears of salary - Over payment - Recovery - Where there was no mis-representation or fraud on the part of the employee, and arrears have been paid to the employee based on the interpretation of the judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court, the employee cannot be blamed for over payment.
Recovery cannot be made from the employee and even equity demands that recovery should not be made if the employee has already retired from the service.
