Article 226 of the Indian Constitution deals with retiral benefits and mandates interest for delayed payment.
The jurisdiction of lok adalat is concerned in this matter.
However, if the parties did not settle when the matter was before the lok adalat, the decision to award interest to the petitioner by the lok adalat is not appropriate.
[Paras 7, 8 & 9]

The Constitution of India, Article 226 states that if there is a delay in payment of retiral benefits, interest should be awarded.
The fact that the payment was delayed is in dispute, making it a contested question of facts.
Therefore, the petitioner must seek remedies from other appropriate forums, including the civil court.
[Para 10]
