Andhra Pradesh Engineering Service, Special Rules - GOMs.
No. 54, Irrigation (Service IV-2) dated 15.2.1983 - This rule's validity is under question regarding the retrospective seniority benefit given to Junior Engineers.
The High Court ruled that the rule violated seniority rights under the Constitution's Articles 14 and 16.
The Supreme Court posed the issue for determination and referred the matter to a Larger Bench.
