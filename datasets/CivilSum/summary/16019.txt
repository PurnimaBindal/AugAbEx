Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 14-B And 17-B - Explanation of the distinction between the term "employer" used in Section 14-B and the one occurring in Section 17-B - The Act's provisions indicate that a period or stage before any establishment transfer is contemplated by Sections 14-B, 15(2), and 17(5) - By contrast, Section 17B of the Act contemplates a stage after the transfer.
