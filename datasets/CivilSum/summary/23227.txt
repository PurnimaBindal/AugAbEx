Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority/Ad hoc appointmnet/ Ad hoc promotion/ Alligibility/Seniority - Promoting a professor to Senior Scale Professor on an ad hoc basis before attaining eligibility - The promotee is eligible for the pay and other allowances for the period worked but cannot claim seniority on that basis - This period cannot be included while determining seniority.
