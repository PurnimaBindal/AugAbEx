Under Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the employer petitioner was unable to demonstrate that the workman respondent had been employed and provided with sufficient payment throughout the writ petition's pendency.
The workman filed an affidavit stating that he had not been involved in any organization since his dismissal from work.
As a result, the employer must pay the workman the full salary he was previously receiving during the writ petition's pendency.
