The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 162 and 309, provide for rules making powers and the exercise of such powers through executive instructions.
It is important to note that the power under Articles 162 and 309 are distinct and Article 309 is a rule making power that can be applied retrospectively.
On the other hand, Article 162 does not provide for any rule making power and is limited to issuing administrative instructions.
