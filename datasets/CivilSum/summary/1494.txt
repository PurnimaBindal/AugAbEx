Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Constitution of India, Article 20(2) - Removal after disciplinary proceedings - Acquittal in criminal case - At the conclusion of a criminal case, a person's acquittal would not impact the result of a departmental enquiry or punishment.
[Paras 15 and 19] Delay and laches in filing a writ petition against a removal order can be deemed unjustifiable and unreasonable if there is no satisfactory explanation for the delay.
