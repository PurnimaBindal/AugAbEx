Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Selection - Appointment - Test - A physically handicapped person, the Petitioner, took the test but did not appear in the eligible candidates list.
Respondent No. 7, a dwarf, was appointed under the physically handicapped category despite not falling under the category in the strict sense.
The Respondent university is directed to treat him as appointed against the General category.
Post reserved for physically handicapped persons is to be readvertised and appointment made after fresh selection.
[Paras 11 and 13]  Locus standi - Those who fail in selection can challenge illegal appointments of others.
