Air Force Act, 1950, Section 189 Chapter III, IV - Air Force Rules, 1969 - Refusal of Service Extension - Sustainment - Refusal predicated on appraisal of performance and usefulness - Court holds that respondent's discretion to consider performance of petitioner is appropriate - High Court is not allowed to reassess suitableness unless there is evidence of bad faith or arbitrary behavior.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Non-extension of service - Violation of principles of natural justice - The court determines that the respondents' decision based on assessment of suitability and desirability does not infringe upon natural justice principles because petitioner has no vested right to force engagement.
