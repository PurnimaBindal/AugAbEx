According to the Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Act, 1971, Sections 2(d) and 3, if a Principal of a Senior Secondary School's services are terminated and the District Education Officer declines approval, the school can file an appeal under Section 3 of the Act.
However, if no stay is granted by the Appellate Authority, the school must follow the order of the DEO until it is set aside or stayed.
As per the directive, the school must take the petitioner on duty.
