According to Section 2(12) of the Employees Insurance Act, 1948, the owner of a petrol pump who employs six people on the premises and precincts of the petrol pump and three people in a separate office cannot combine these two groups to meet the minimum ten employee requirement for insurance coverage.
[Para 4] B.
The Petrol Pump, in this case, is considered a factory under Section 2(12) of the Employees Insurance Act, 1948, as pumping is done using power and the manufacturing process is conducted there.
