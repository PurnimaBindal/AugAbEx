According to Punjab Civil Service Rule, Volume I, Part 1, Rule 326 and Volume II, Rule 5.32, an employee who has crossed the efficiency bar and earned 6 good and 4 average reports cannot be regarded as a non-performing worker.
Additionally, the instruction mandating for 70% good reports during the last decade has already been invalidated.
Consequently, the petitioner's premature retirement order has been nullified with expenses, and the respondents have been directed to reappoint the petitioner in a three-month duration.
