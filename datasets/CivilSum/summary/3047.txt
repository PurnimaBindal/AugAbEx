Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulations 58 and 59 - Departmental proceedings - Failure to provide a delinquent with an enquiry report constitutes a denial of reasonable opportunity - If the punishing authority wants to differ with the findings of an enquiry officer, a show cause notice accompanied by a copy of the report and reasons for differing must be granted under the regulations and Article 311(2) of the Constitution, as well as to comply with principles of natural justice - Non-supply of an enquiry report to a government servant in this situation certainly causes prejudice to a government servant - In such situations, the punishment order is null and void.
