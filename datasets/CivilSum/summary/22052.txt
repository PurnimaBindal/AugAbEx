For admission to post-graduate medical courses through the All India Competitive Examination, candidates are evaluated on the basis of their merit, preferences, and eligibility.
When applying for the All India Quota, candidates must identify their preferred medical colleges and subjects, and final results will be announced based on merit.
A Selection Committee will contact the top 150 candidates a day, in order of merit, and ask them to indicate their choice of college and subject.
From 1994 onwards, a counselling system will be in place for admissions.
