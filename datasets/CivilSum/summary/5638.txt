Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Speaking order - In order to avoid arbitrary and unjust actions, every State action must include reasons for the decision.
Reasoning provides an essential element in the administration of justice and an indispensable part of a sound judicial review system.
Furthermore, the authority cannot use admission of guilt to avoid providing elaborate reasons for the conclusion if the dispute is not over.
