According to the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunity, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, in Section 39, there is no provision for the reservation of seats, but the word "posts" can be used instead.
This is in accordance with the legislative intent as seen in Sections 32 and 33.
Government educational institutions or aided institutions can reserve 3% of posts for persons with disabilities, and the procedural aspects for employment mentioned in other provisions of Chapter VI apply to Section 39 as well.
