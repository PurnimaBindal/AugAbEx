An employee who is convicted in a case under the Prevention of Corruption Act can be dismissed without a show cause notice or an inquiry.
This is according to the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, Sections 5(1)(d) and 5(2); as well as Constitution of India, Article 311(2).
The cases of AIR 1985 SC 1416 and 1995(2) SCT 717 are referred to in support of this.
