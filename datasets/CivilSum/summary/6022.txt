Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Delay/laches - The petitioner's services were terminated with an order dated 14585.
The petitioner filed a petition challenging the termination order on 8.7.93, however, there was no explanation provided for the inordinate delay in filing the writ petition.
The petitioner can be held guilty of laches and delay and hence cannot invoke the extraordinary jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.

This is because the official is entitled to know the imputation of the charges.
