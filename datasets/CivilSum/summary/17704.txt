Punjab Market Committee (Class III) Service Rules, 1989, Rule 10 - Appointment - Seniority - After both petitioner and Respondent No. 3 were regularly appointed on the same day, the one who is older in age has been considered as senior to the other.
This decision was found to be neither arbitrary nor unfair according to Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
[Para 5] B Punjab Market Committee (Class III) Service Rules, 1989, Rule 10 - Seniority - The seniority of service members who have been appointed purely on an ad hoc basis or on a daily wage rate before the commencement of the Rules must be determined when they are regularly appointed, taking into account the date of such regular appointment.
Therefore, the period of work performed on a daily wage or ad hoc basis cannot be considered.
[Para 4] C Punjab Market Committee (Class III) Service Rules, 1989, Rule 10 - Appointment - Regularisation - The committee was investigating the regularisation of ad hoc employees.
As the petitioner and respondent No. 3 were appointed on an ad hoc basis and joined on the same date, their services have been treated as regular from that date.
The committee was not faced with the need to regularise the services of persons working on a daily wage basis.
The petitioner's claim to be deemed regular from the date of appointment on the daily wage rate cannot be upheld.
