As per the Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, under Sections 4-A and 10, a notice to the owner of the vehicle/employer is enough and there is no requirement to give notice to the Insurance Company.
If the employer was aware of the accident, a notice under Section 10 is presumed.
Despite the owner of the vehicle failing to meet the statutory obligation, the Insurance Company cannot avoid liability under the Act.
However, if the Insurance Company was not unwilling to fulfil its liability, the conduct can be taken into account while calculating the percentage of the penalty to be imposed.
