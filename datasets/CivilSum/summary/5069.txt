According to Sections 82, 45A and 75(1)(g) of Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, manufacturing companies are not liable to pay ESI contribution for employees of their transport contractors or their drivers, as they are not considered employees of the manufacturing company.
They do not work in the company premises or are under their control.
[Paras 10 and 16]
Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(9) and 2(13) specify the definition of `employee', stating that they must work on the premises of the factory or be under the supervision of the principal employer.
