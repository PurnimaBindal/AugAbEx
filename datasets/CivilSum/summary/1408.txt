According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules, the plaintiff was integrated into the judicial service of PEPSU on 1 September 1948 and was appointed as Sub Judge I Class, Sangrur on 29 August 2005.
After the merger of PEPSU with the State of Punjab on 1 November 1956, the plaintiff was governed by PCS Rules, and the retirement age was raised from 55 to 58.
Therefore, he retired on superannuation on 30 April 1980 after completing 58 years of age.
The plaintiff lost the right to sue when the gradation lists for the period from 1 January 1966 onwards were published as it was communicated and acknowledged by him on 3 July 1971.
As a result, the civil suit filed by him on 25 September 1990 for a declaration that he could not be retired earlier than 30 April 1982 is beyond limitation and dismissed.
A suit for the enforcement of rights or obligations is a civil suit that this Court has jurisdiction to try.
