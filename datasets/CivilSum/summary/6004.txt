For the selection and appointment of Munsiff to J&K Civil Service (Judicial), the eligibility criteria state that applicants must have three years of actual practice at the bar at the time of submitting their application.
The petitioner's application was received before the cut-off date of February 8, 2002, and although he completed his three years of experience on February 4th, his application was rejected for having less than three years of bar practice.
However, this rejection goes against the stipulation of the advertisement notice and the respondents have been instructed to consider the petitioner's merit in the selection process.
