Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Approval of termination - Denial by Tribunal - Workman arrested on 11121991, was bailed out on 28121991, informed DTC of arrest on 6.1.1992, SHO wrote letter to DTC on 12121991 which was received by DTC on 811992 i.e. two days after letter by workman was received - Tribunal finds no intention of workman to conceal fact of arrest - Charge sheet does not mention of letter received from workman - DTC could not prove charge after having reserved right to lead additional evidence - Workman deemed to be in service for all effects and purposes from date of termination.
