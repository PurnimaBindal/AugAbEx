Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - M.P. Sanvida Shikshak Bharati Prakriya Niyam, 2001, Rule 6(5)(6) - Termination of services - Appointment to the post of Sanvida Shala Shikshak Grade III - According to the rules, candidates who marry before the age of 21 and have more than two children are not eligible - Despite being married and having five children, the respondent was given the appointment - However, upon complaint, the respondent's services were terminated - The respondent was deemed disqualified - The termination of the respondent's services before the reply to the notice was received was deemed appropriate.
