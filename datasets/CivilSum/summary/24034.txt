Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - Appointment as Lecturer - University/UGC qualifications apply - Punjabi University, Patiala amended qualifications for lecturer appointment - Candidate applied based on amended qualifications, but not eligible due to not clearing NET - Candidate cannot argue that University did not adopt UGC qualifications or did not notify its own - Once amended qualifications circulated, candidate cannot dispute University's power to amend them - NET remains compulsory for lecturer appointment even for candidates with Ph.
D. - Introduction of NET does not exempt it as a requirement.
