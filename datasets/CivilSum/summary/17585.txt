Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Retiral benefits - Re-fixation of pay - Natural justice - When an employee's pay is re-fixed, they have the right to be heard - The petitioner's pay was reduced and the Department claims that a substantial amount has to be recovered - The respondents were required to give the petitioner a reasonable opportunity before reducing his pay - The petitioner was not given any such opportunity - The order by which the petitioner's pay was re-fixed has been quashed - The case has been remitted to the respondents for a fresh decision after hearing the petitioner - The respondents have been directed to release the petitioner's pay, gratuity, leave encashment and other retiral benefits within one month.
