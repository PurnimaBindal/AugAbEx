The Appellant, a retired employee of the Respondent Trust, claimed for pension and gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.
The Appellant provided a Notification dated July 16, 1972 to prove that the Trust Estate was treated as an Establishment and provisions of the Employees Provident Funds and Family Pension Fund Act, 1952 were applied to the Trust Estate.
Due to the provisions of these acts, the Appellant would also be entitled to gratuity.
The Appeal was granted.
