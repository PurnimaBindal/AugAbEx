Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Charge-sheet - Dismissal - Allegations of delinquent consuming liquor while on duty and being absent from duty for one day - Delinquent provides reasonable explanation for absence - Punishment should match severity of misconduct - One-day absence is not grave enough for dismissal from service.
1992(1) SCT 123 (P&H) cited.
