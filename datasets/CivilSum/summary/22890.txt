While probation in a certain grade does not guarantee promotion, all eligible permanent employees must be considered for promotion with seniority being a factor.
[Para 20]
Seniority is only taken into account when merit and ability are roughly equal for promotions under the Kerala Local Fund Audit Service Special Rules, Rule 3.
[Para ]
Promotion should come from current government servants and not outsiders under Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 27(c) and 28.
[Para 22]
Once becoming permanent, leave without allowance during probationary periods in the entry grade is not a factor in promotion under Kerala Local Fund Audit Service Special Rules, Rule 3 and Kerala Service Rules, Appendix XIIA, Paragraph 4 & 5.
