Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bombay State Reserve Police Force Rules 1959, Rules 56, 57 and 59 - Seniority - Determination - To avoid inequities against those who were unable to pass examinations due to invalid reasons and seniority must be based on earlier cadre, not solely on the date of exam passing.
[Para 7]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government action - Validity - The court presumes that the government's actions are reasonable and serve the public interest.
Hence, to show the lack of reasonableness or public interest or violation of statutory provisions, the challenging party bears a greater burden.
