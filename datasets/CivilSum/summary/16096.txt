Punjab Universities Calendar (Volume II), 1988, Regulations 4 and 5 - Unfair means - Examination - Use of unfair means in examination - Possession of incriminating material in examination can be deemed inadvertent or mala fide depending on each case's facts - The candidate's intention must be inferred from various circumstances - The court cannot intervene in this matter - If a candidate appears to be trying to hide his notes on a small piece of paper or makes awkward movements when looking at them, it suggests mala fide possession - Disqualifying a petitioner for two years based on the committee's inference of mala fides is lawful under Regulation 5.
