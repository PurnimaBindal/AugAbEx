Indian Railway Establishment Code, Rules 2544(g) - "Running allowance" counted towards average emoluments for calculating pension - Pension increased post recommendations of 5th Pay Commission - Inclusion of running allowance benefit cannot be reconsidered for future computation - Benefit of running allowance should only be considered once for pension calculation and it is not necessary to consider it again for any future calculation if it was included during the time of employee's retirement.
