Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Standing Orders for Workmen engaged in Forest Department of the Rajasthan, Chapter - III Clause - 3 - Quasi-permanent workers - The criteria for declaring workers quasi-permanent must be followed, including giving reasons while ignoring some workers and including others.
Failure to do so is hostile discrimination and violates the Constitutional Philosophy of equal treatment with all Government Servants in the service of State.
When there is apparent discrimination, the official responsible is held liable for causing illegal loss and must compensate the affected person without affecting the state exchequer.
