Constitution of India, Article 226 - Certified Standing Orders, Clause 21-A - Semiconductor Complex Ltd Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, Rule 26 - Suspension during criminal proceedings - The Standing Orders mandate that a conscious decision be made to suspend an employee - There isn't an automatic suspension order - The employer may opt to mark the employee absent from duty or suspend them - A writ court cannot demand that the employee be suspended.
