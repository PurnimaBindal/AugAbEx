Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Dismissal from service - Petitioner employed in a defense Canteen allegedly involved in corruption and creating fake bills for liquor bottles - Dismissal order passed after a preliminary investigation showed evidence of wrongdoing - Charge-sheet comprising two charges against the petitioner issued 2-1/2 months later - No departmental inquiry conducted - The law establishes that an employee cannot be dismissed without undergoing a departmental inquiry - Dismissal order revoked.
