Constitution of India, Article 226 - Concealment of facts - Relevancy of facts - Litigants with unclean hands, concealment of facts, and misstatements of facts are not entitled to a hearing on the merits - It is the responsibility of the litigant to present all relevant facts to the court - In this case, the petitioner filed a second petition without disclosing that the earlier petition had been dismissed - The petitioner is guilty of concealing material facts - As a result, the petition is dismissed and the petitioner is ordered to pay Rs. 3,000 in costs.
[Paras 1, 7, and 8]
