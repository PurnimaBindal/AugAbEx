UCO Bank Employees (Pension) Regulations, 1993, Regulations 14 and 22 - Pension - Retirement - Resignation - The petitioner resigned from the Bank to start his own business with no impact on his service career, and was relieved accordingly.
The Pension Regulations were established and applied in 1994, requiring ten years of qualifying service in the Bank on the retirement date, and retirement/deem to be retired prior to 1111993.
Although there is a difference between resignation and retirement, the petitioner served the Bank for over 28 years without any blemish on his record.
As a result, he claimed resignation not voluntary retirement, as Regulations were not yet in place.
According to paragraphs 7 and 8, the petitioner is entitled to pension under the Regulations, and the Bank's appeal was dismissed.
