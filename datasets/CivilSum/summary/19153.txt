Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(3) - Compensation - Enhancement of compensation - Due to a 20% permanent disability sustained by the appellant, they have lost 100% earning capacity - Because the nature of the injury sustained by the appellant is not specified in Schedule 1 of the Act, 15309 is the relevant factor for a person aged 50 years - The Commissioner did not consider relevant statutory provisions when determining compensation - As a result, the appellant is entitled to compensation of Rs. 24000/-.
