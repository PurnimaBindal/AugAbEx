Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Gratuity - While the Gratuity Act serves as a comprehensive code to tackle gratuity-related matters, a Labour Court's directive for payment of gratuity as part of its award and all subsequent benefits does not harm the management.
Furthermore, under settled law, an order of the court cannot be overturned on the basis of lack of jurisdiction unless an initial objection was made, and it is demonstrated that such a lack of jurisdiction has caused significant harm to the aggrieved party, leading to a failure of justice.
