ESI Act 1948, Section 2(22) - Incentive Amount - Whether it constitutes Salary - Employer paying incentive amount under `Quality Incentive scheme' - The scheme is designed to encourage employees to achieve quality and quantity of work as well as to maintain a positive relationship between employer and employees - ESIC included the contribution amount in wages, but it was held that the payment under the scheme does not constitute a condition of service that requires compliance with Section 9A of the Act - Therefore, ESI Corporation is not liable to recover contribution from the Respondent, as the payment does not fall within the definition of wages - The appeal is dismissed accordingly.
