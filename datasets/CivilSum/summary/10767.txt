The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, dictate pensionary benefits and military service benefits.
In this case, an ex-serviceman was re-employed as a Technician in the Department of Tele-communications.
The confirmation letter showed that not only the petitioner, but 86 other employees were notified through their respective heads of departments of their confirmation and availability of option for counting military service towards pensionary benefits.
The confirmation order of all these employees was duly circulated through HODs.
However, the petitioner failed to exercise the option within the prescribed period.
As a result, the order declining the petitioner the benefit of counting of military service cannot be interfered with in writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 13 and 14]
