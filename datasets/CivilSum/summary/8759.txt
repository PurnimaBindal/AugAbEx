Constitution of India, 1950 Article 14 - Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Sections 10(m) and 12 - Central Reserve Police force Rules, 1955, Rule 28 - Conviction - Dismissal - The order of dismissal should not be given without proper consideration, as it shows non-application of mind, and should only be passed for serious charges - Punishment of confinement in the court room until the court rises - Order of dismissal passed in routine manner shows non-application of mind - Order quashed.
[Para 17] B Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 28 - The Commandant is required to transmit records to the Deputy Inspector General - An appeal against the order of dismissal passed by the Commandant lies with the appellate authority.
