Under the Employees State Insurance Act of 1948, injuries listed in Schedule 1 are not exhaustive since injuries to the human body can be so varied that they cannot be fully classified or codified.
The contention that there is no reference to ear injuries in Part II of Schedule 1 of Section 2(15-A) and 2(15-B) and that no disablement benefit can be awarded is not valid.
[Paras 6 & 7] B The Medical Board and Medical Appellate Tribunal established under the Employees' State Insurance Act of 1948 can provide reports on employee injuries.
Reports given by private medical practitioners are not admissible.
