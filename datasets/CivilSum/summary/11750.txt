Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recruitment - Qualification - The recruitment for Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector included the requirement of one year of work experience after acquiring basic qualification.
The petitioner passed the diploma examination on 6891 and worked in a workshop from 1691 to 30.6.92 according to his experience certificate.
However, work experience should be considered from the date of publication of the diploma examination result.
As the petitioner lacks one year of work experience, he is not qualified for the post and the deletion of his name from the short list is not arbitrary.
[Paras 13 and 24]
