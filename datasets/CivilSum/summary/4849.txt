Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 1(3)A - Judicial review - Gratuity - Eligibility for - The workman who failed to establish that the establishment had more than 10 employees at any point in time is not eligible for gratuity.
The authority's conclusion that the Act is not applicable cannot be challenged through writ jurisdiction.
The Controlling Authority and Appellate Authority both arrived at their conclusions based on evidence on record, and the High Court cannot interfere with findings of fact solely because an alternate conclusion is possible.
