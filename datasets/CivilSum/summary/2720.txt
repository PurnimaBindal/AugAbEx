Constitution of India, Article 311 - Contractual appointee - Termination of service - Respondent caught in drunken condition, was handed over to police, placed under suspension and charge-sheeted - The termination was considered punitive by the tribunal.
However, the order did not specify any misconduct and cannot be considered as punitive.
The employer has the right to keep records of employee misconduct during the contract period and can refuse to renew the contract at the end.
