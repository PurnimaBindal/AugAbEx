The Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act, 1965, and the Rajasthan Cooperative Societies Rules, 1966, state that a manager cannot return to their position.
However, the petitioner was selected and recruited by respondent No. 2 Bank and was later confirmed in their position.
Despite completing their training, the Petitioner was not allowed to join their duties by Samiti and was denied being an employee of the Bank.
The impugned order was passed without providing notice or an opportunity of hearing or enquiry into misconduct.
As neither Bank nor Samiti is willing to declare the petitioner's cessation of employment, the impugned order cannot be sustained.
As a result, the petitioner must be considered in continuous service, and a directive is given to provide them with a suitable posting and pay emoluments.
