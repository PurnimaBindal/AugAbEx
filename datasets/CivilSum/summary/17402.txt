Constitution of India, Articles 31-A and 300-A - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 208 and 244 - Voluntary retirement - Pension - Resignation - Petitioner seeks voluntary retirement after 25 years of service.
Retirement denied, but resignation accepted.
Pensionary benefits denied.
It is held that if the public servant's resignation is due to age, it cannot be deemed a resignation requiring forfeiture of service.
The petitioner's resignation is not due to any inefficiency due to age or misconduct, but due to age and domestic difficulties.
Such resignation does not entail forfeiture of past service.
The respondents were mistaken in denying the pension benefits, and they are directed to consider the petitioner's case for the grant of pensionary benefits in accordance with the law.
