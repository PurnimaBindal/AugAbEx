The 1st respondent had given his date of birth as 12.6.27 and mentioned his age as 25 years in an application, based on which a certificate was issued by the manager of the company.
His service record, provident fund booklet and annual reports all contained the same date of birth.
It is implausible to believe that he was not aware of his date of birth for all these years.
Additionally, he failed to dispute his date of birth when other employees were correcting theirs.
The 1st respondent's claim that he intimated the company of a different date of birth in 1953 was not supported by evidence and therefore, he cannot contest his date of birth at the end of his career.
