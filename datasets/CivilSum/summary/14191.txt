Constitution of India, Article 14 - Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 16(6), Chapter IV, Statute 4 - Appointment to post of Head of Department - Selection - Recommendations of Selection Committee to the Board - There is no Personal Committee under this statute.
The VC must select the Head of Department based on the panel of two names in order of merit as per recommendations made by the Selection Committee, which is a body of experts.
The Board cannot reject these recommendations without reasonable cause and sufficient reasons, and they have no jurisdiction to appoint a candidate who was not found suitable and recommended by the Selection Committee.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 16(6), Chapter IV, Statute 4 - Appointment to post of Head of Department - Selection - Recommendation of Panel of two candidates by Selection Committee to the Board - If the Selection Committee finds only one candidate suitable and recommends only a single candidate, the selection and recommendations cannot be deemed invalid for lack of a panel of two candidates.
