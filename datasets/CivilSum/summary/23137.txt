Civil Procedure Code, Order 21 Rule 1 and Section 115 - Execution proceedings - Jurisdiction of executing court - Interest - Executing court's scope limited to order or decree under execution - Order of promotion with all consequential benefits doesn't include interest on arrears of salary due to promotion - Interest not part of decree - Executing court overstepping its jurisdiction by awarding interest - High Court only allowed to correct mistakes affecting the ultimate justice in revision - Duty of High Court to correct excessive jurisdiction - High Court order upholding setting aside of executing court's interest-based order.
