Constitution of India, Articles 71, 161 and 226 - Penal Code, 1860, Sections  302/34 - Murder - Termination of services - Restoration of civil rights - portion of sentence - Reinstatement in service - According to the ruling, the Governor's remission of Appellant's sentence under Article 161 of the Constitution does not affect his conviction and sentence, and does not entitle him to restoration of civil rights as in the case of a pardon under Article 161.
The difference between remission and pardon is explained.
