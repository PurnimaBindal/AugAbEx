Andhra Pradesh Industries Service Rules, Rules 1, 2 and 6 - Direct recruitment - Promotion - Substantive vacancies - Promotion to the permanent posts of Assistant Director Sericulture - The State Govt. can carry out direct recruitment for the permanent posts of Assistant Director Sericulture if there are more than five permanent posts/vacancies available, and 17 existing substantive vacancies in the cadre that have been continuously present for over a decade must be treated as such.
Thus, it cannot be claimed that only three vacancies were open for direct recruitment.
The Government has the option to fill any of these substantive posts via direct recruitment so long as the ratio in Rules is maintained.
