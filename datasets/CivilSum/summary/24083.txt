Promotion to the post of Employment Officer with a pay scale above Rs. 760 under the West Bengal Services (Revision of Pay and Allowance) Rules, 1970, requires consultation with the Public Service Commission as per the West Bengal Service Commission (Exemption from Consultation) Regulation, 1955, Section 3A.
The approval of the Commission is necessary as this post is not excluded from its purview.
B Under the West Bengal Services (Determination of Seniority) Rules, 1981, Rule 6(2), ad hoc promotion cannot be counted towards seniority unless it is in accordance with the relevant statutory provisions of Recruitment Rules, and consultation by the Governor and prior approval of the Public Service Commission are obtained.
The recommendation of the Public Service Commission is necessary for such promotions.
