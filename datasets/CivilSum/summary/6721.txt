Rule 9.18(2) of Punjab Police Rules, 1934 and Rule 532 of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, state that only the Deputy Inspector General of Police can be deemed as the appointing authority for the post of SI This means that the Senior Superintendent of Police does not have the power to compulsorily retire an SI As such any order passed by an SP for compulsorily retiring an SI would have no jurisdiction and cannot be upheld.
[Para 12]
Rule 9.18(1) of Punjab Police Rules, 1934 says that the Competent Authority has the power and discretion to assess the service record of an employee and decide whether he is fit to be retained or liable to be retired in the public interest.
Any order passed without such exercise by the Competent Authority, but merely on directions of IG Police and decision taken by Officers Committee, would be vitiated by non-application of mind of the Competent Authority.
