Definition of Workman - The nature of the work of a lady demonstrator for Sewing Machine, who was redesignated as a sales representative after confirmation and whose work was primarily dealing with customers and explaining the value of the product, cannot be excluded from the definition of a 'workman' as per Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
[Para 18] B.
Termination - The termination of a lady demonstrator for Sewing Machine, who was accused of misconduct without any enquiry, cannot be considered as a termination under contract of service and amounts to an unfair labor practice under Section 2(oo) (bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
[Para 21] C. Minimum Wages - Since the respondent worked not only as a demonstrator of Sewing Machines but also as a clerk, she is entitled to minimum wages applicable to her as a clerk as per Section 20 of the Minimum Wages Act, even if her primary role was that of a demonstrator.
