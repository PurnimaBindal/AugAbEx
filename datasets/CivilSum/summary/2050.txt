Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental enquiry - Bias must be inferred from several factors, including whether the delinquent has a reasonable apprehension of bias and there is a real likelihood of bias on the part of the Presiding Officer.
If the delinquent requests a change of enquiry officer due to bias, and there is any likelihood of bias and other officers are available as substitutes, the enquiry officer must be changed.
[Para 9]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Assistance of advocate in departmental enquiry - The enquiry officer has discretion to allow the delinquent to take assistance of an advocate in a departmental enquiry, which must be exercised judicially.
The request for legal assistance must not be declined unreasonably, and when charges are serious and could affect the delinquent's career and livelihood, they must be allowed legal assistance.
[Paras 12-16]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Charges in departmental enquiry - Charges must be linked to specific incidents and have a correlation between the individual charge and the statement of allegations.
Charges that are general and not specific to any particular incident are considered vague.
[Paras 14-16]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Doctrine of merger - A punishment order based on an illegal and invalid enquiry is non est and cannot be given effect to.
Such an order cannot be confirmed in the eye of the law, and the doctrine of merger has no relevance to such situations.
