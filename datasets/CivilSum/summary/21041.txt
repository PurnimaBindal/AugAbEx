Constitution of India, Article 16 - Himachal Pradesh PWD Subordinate Services Class-II Junior Engineer (Electrical) (Technical) Recruitment Rules, 1979, Rule 4 - Promotion - Reservation - If a person has been promoted against a reserved post and a rival claim is made stating that the post falls under the reserved quota, the Government cannot cancel the promotion of a reserved category candidate on the grounds of unavailability of posts in the reserved quota after a writ petition has been dismissed.
This is because they have already made a conscious decision to promote the person under the rules and instructions.
The order of reversion has been quashed.
