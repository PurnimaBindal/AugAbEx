The function of the Selection Committee is administrative, not judicial or adjudicatory, and it is not required to give reasons for selecting candidates in the absence of a statutory requirement.
However, procedural fairness must be followed.
The Selection Committee consisting of experts holding high status, acts by taking interviews of candidates, making assessments of their relative merits, and recommending a panel of names for appointment.
ILR (1982) 1 Karnataka 523, Reversed.
[Para 7]
The fair procedure is the main requirement in administrative action, including in the Selection Committee's function.
While not required to give reasons for the decision, the Selection Committee cannot be an exception to the principle of procedural fairness.
They must make decisions reasonably and without being guided by extraneous or irrelevant considerations.
