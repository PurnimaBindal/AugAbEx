According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F, termination of an employee is a matter to be decided by the Labour Court.
The government has no power under Section 10 to make an inquiry and conclude that the employer complied with Section 25-F The government's only consideration is whether an industrial dispute exists between the workman and employer.
In this case, the petitioner's services were terminated in 1994 and he claims that it was wrongful termination.
Therefore, there is an industrial dispute between the petitioner and his employer.
The State Government's refusal to refer the dispute on the grounds that the petitioner had received benefits under Section 25-F cannot be sustained under the law.
It is directed that the government must refer the dispute to the Labour Court for adjudication.
This ruling is in accordance with Article 226 and 227 of the Indian Constitution.
