Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination/Misconduct/Absence from duty/Abandonment of duty - Differentiates between termination of service based on absence from duty versus abandonment of duty by the employee - Absence from duty is considered misconduct - If an allegation of absence from duty is made, the employer must conduct an enquiry and provide the employee with a charge-sheet - Failure to conduct an enquiry and provide a charge-sheet violates principles of natural justice and results in a void and vitiated termination.
AIR 1966 SC 492 precedent is followed.
