Constitution of India, Articles 32, 226 and 22 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Delay and laches - Court not required to decide delayed disputes on merits - Neglect of a party for a long time must be taken into account - Government's reference to court only forms an opinion of apprehension of dispute, not a decision on merits - Delay may waive a person's right to a remedy - Delay may also render it inequitable, unjust, and unfair to grant relief.
Patiala Central Coop.
Bank Ltd v. Presiding Officer' Labour Court etc overruled.
