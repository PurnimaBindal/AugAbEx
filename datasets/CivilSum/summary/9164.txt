Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A - M.P. Industrial Relations Act, 1960, Sections 31(3) and 61 - Right to encash earned and casual leave - Petitioner cannot encash casual leave as it lapses within the same year and cannot be accumulated - Both Industrial Court and Labour Court denied relief of encashment and dismissed the case because the petitioner did not claim encashment of earned leave within the stipulated time - Orders under Article 226 not to be interfered with - Petition dismissed.
