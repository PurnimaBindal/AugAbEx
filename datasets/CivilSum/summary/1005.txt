Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226 - Punjab High Court Rules and Orders (applicable to Delhi), Volume IV Para 30 - Reports of Third and Fourth Central Pay Commissions of 1973 and 1986 - Dress allowance - Outfit allowance - Judicial officers must wear dress as prescribed by High Court while performing their duties - Delhi Judicial Service officers should be provided with uniform and allowances for clothing maintenance - The officers should receive initial dress allowance and thereafter, given a monthly amount to maintain their dress which will be enough to purchase further dress from it, without any extra cost of dress every three or five years - Ordered to pay Rs. 5500/- as the amount of directed monthly dress allowance with arrears.
