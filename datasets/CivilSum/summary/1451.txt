Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol.
I, Part-1, Rule 3, 26(d), and Vol. II, Rule 532-A(c) - Judicial Officers - Premature Retirement - Judicial Review of Subjective Satisfaction of the Competent Authority - Courts whose jurisdiction is guided by the Indian Constitution's Article 226 cannot inquire into whether sufficient materials exist for the competent authority to be convinced or not.
Only a single red flag is enough to satisfy this requirement.
The court may consider concerns of unethical conduct if they exist.
[Paras 7 to 9]
The punitive retirement of judicial officers should not be regarded as a punishment.
It does not cause injury.
It's primary function is to maintain government efficiency by identifying corrupt officials or those who are incapable of retaining their job.
[Para 8]
A negative entry made in personnel reports cannot be compared to punishment, which necessitates an investigation and an opportunity to be heard.
[Para 9]
The basis for doubting the moral integrity of a judicial official cannot be verified with certainty by positive testimony, and it can only be proved through bias and favoritism.
