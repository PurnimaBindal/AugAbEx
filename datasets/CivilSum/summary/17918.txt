Constitution of India, Article 14 - Ad hoc service/Regularisation of services/Reimbursement of Medical bills - Medical reimbursement cannot be granted to ad hoc employees appointed for a short period; however, ad hoc employees allowed to continue for a lengthy period are eligible for the concession.
It has been instructed that ad hoc employees regularised into service will also be entitled to medical reimbursement.
After regularisation, the period of ad hoc service should be considered for service benefits such as seniority, pension, gratuity and medical reimbursement.
The petition has been approved.
