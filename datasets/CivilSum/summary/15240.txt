The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Death of an employee - The daughter of a deceased employee born from a second marriage is entitled to compassionate appointment, even though the second marriage was void - The child born through the second wife, even though such marriage was void, shall be seen as a legitimate child.
