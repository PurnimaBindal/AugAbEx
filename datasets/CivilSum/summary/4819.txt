Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo) (bb) and 25-F - Disciplinary proceedings - Termination of services - Unauthorised absence - Workman, who fell sick, admitted to Hospital belonging to the employer itself.
The company is not entitled to the benefit of Section 2(oo) (bb) of Industrial Disputes Act.
Reliance on stipulation of terms of service is not tenable with regard to unauthorised absence giving rise to the presumption of abandonment of service on part of the employee.
[Paras 2 and 14] B.
Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo) (bb) and 25-F - Disciplinary proceedings - Termination of services - Unauthorised absence - Termination of workman's services was bad in law as no enquiry was held regarding the charge and the employer followed no procedure.
The workman is entitled to reinstatement with back wages, and the award of the Tribunal was upheld.
