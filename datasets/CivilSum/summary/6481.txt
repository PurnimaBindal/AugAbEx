Constitution of India, Article 16 - Evidence Act (1 of 1872), Section 108 - Compassionate appointment - A worker absent for 7 years and whose employment has been terminated - A wife seeking compassionate appointment on the assumption that her husband is dead - Section 108 of the Evidence Act does not presume the date and time of death - The wife should obtain a certificate from a competent court or authority certifying her husband's death.
