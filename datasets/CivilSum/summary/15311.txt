According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 423 for Pensionary Benefits, employees can count the period of ad hoc and temporary service towards gratuity and pension if there was a break in service beyond their control.
Rule 4.23 automatically condones interruption between two periods of service, except for cases involving resignation, dismissal, removal from service, or participation in a strike.
The petitioners did not willfully absent themselves from duty before their appointment on a regular basis, and the break in service was a unilateral act of the State Government.
Therefore, they should count the days of their actual ad hoc service towards qualifying for pensionary benefits.
