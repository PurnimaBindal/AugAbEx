The lower age of retirement for air hostesses, with an option for ground duties beyond 50 years up to 58 years, was incorporated through negotiations between air hostesses and their association, and is a binding service condition.
The condition cannot be questioned on the basis of Section 5 of Equal Remuneration Act and is otherwise compliant with the Act.
C Air hostesses can be assigned ground duties after retirement from flying duties at the age of 50 years, under directives from the central government, in compliance with the terms and settlements agreed by parties.
The directives are not in violation of the Constitution's Articles 14, 15 and 16.
