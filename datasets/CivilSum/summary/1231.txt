Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Management Rules, 1999 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 72 - Rules for Reservation & Concessions for Physically Handicapped, Ex Serviceman, Sportsman & Compassionate Appointments, Rule 228 - Reservation in employment for disabled candidates - The respondent claims to be a deaf candidate but does not meet the definition of Rule 228 of the Rules, and is above the maximum age limit - Thus, the respondent is not eligible and the selection is set aside.
