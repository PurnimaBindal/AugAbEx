Constitution of India, Articles 25 and 26 - Sikh Gurdwara Act, 1925, Section 69, Rule 4 - Sikh Gurdwara Judicial Commission, Sections 132, 142, 69 - Dismissal - Appeal - The order of dismissal of petitioners, who are employees of SGPC and the services are controlled as per Sikh Gurdwara Act, is challenged in writ petition as illegal and against service Rules framed by SGPC - The court held that the petitioner must challenge the order of Executive Committee of SGPC before Sikh Gurdwara Judicial Commission and not in writ jurisdiction of this court - The very object to swiftly punish any act of employees against religious tenets of Sikh community will be frustrated if petitioners are allowed to invoke writ jurisdiction in this matter - Therefore, petition not maintainable.
