According to the Army Act, Sections 63 and 47, and the Constitution of India, Article 226, the territorial jurisdiction is crucial.
If an Army Officer, dismissed while posted in Dehradun, is settled in Patiala and receives a notice from the Govt. of India requesting to show cause his entitlement to pensionary benefits, the mere service of notice at Patiala would not be enough to challenge the order of dismissal or the General Court Martial proceedings.
1993(2) Recent Revenue Reports 349 (DB) has been relied on.
