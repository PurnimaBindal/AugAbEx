Under Section 3 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Section 70 of the Co-operative Societies Act, 1959, and Rule 18(4) of the Co-operative Societies Rules, 1960, if an employee of a Co-operative Society files a claim for gratuity, proceedings must be initiated under the provisions of the Payment of Gratuity Act and any dispute cannot be raised under the Co-operative Societies Act.
