The National Emergency (Concession) Rules of the Punjab Government, 1965, Rule 4, which allows for the regularization of military service during the period of emergency, applies to the petitioner, who served in the Indian Air Force from June 12, 1956, to August 12, 1974, as an airman.
The petitioner is entitled to benefits such as the ante-dating of his service and the payment of arrears from 1.4.77 instead of 1101980, in keeping with the military service that he rendered.
[Para 6]
The expression 'military service' in the National Emergency (Concession) Rules of 1965, Rules 2 and 4, which apply to those who render service during the period of emergency, is broad and encompasses all soldiers who serve during this period.
The rules are still in effect and will continue to apply to any future periods of emergency.
[Para 5]
It is not necessary to amend the National Emergency (Concession) Rules to enable the state government to grant benefits to eligible employees every time the emergency is proclaimed.
The expression 'military service' is comprehensive and an enabling provision has been made to facilitate the application of these rules at any time in the future when the need arises.
