The University had decided to follow the grades set by the Haryana Government for pay scales.
The pay scales granted to petitioners were not suggested to be lower than those sanctioned by the Haryana Government.
The University's Committee recommendation was considered but not accepted.
The State Government is responsible for the financial burden of salaries and has to consider its resources before accepting recommendations.
Hence, the University cannot be blamed for seeking approval from the Government.
Non-teaching employees of Kurukshetra University are not entitled to claim parity of pay with employees of Panjab University.
Kurukshetra University Act, Section 12-A states that the University cannot create non-teaching posts or revise the pay scale of non-teaching employees without the approval of the Government.
The University sought approval from the State Government, and it was declined.
The respondents cannot be faulted for refusing parity of pay with employees of Panjab University.
