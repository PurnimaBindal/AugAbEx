According to Articles 311 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a candidate who is recruited despite not possessing the required qualifications on the date of recruitment does not acquire any rights to the post.
Termination of his service without a hearing is legal, and acquiring qualifications after his appointment would not benefit him.
[Para]
As per UP Basic (Teachers) Services Rules, 1981, Rule 8, and Section 19 of the UP Basic Education Act, 1972, the appointment of a candidate who did not have the required qualifications on the date of recruitment does not entitle him to the post.
Furthermore, acquiring qualifications after appointment does not provide any benefits to the employee.
Cancellation of appointment without a hearing is legal, and the candidate has no right over the post.
[Paras 11, 19, 20, 21]
