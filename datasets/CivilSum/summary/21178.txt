Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Termination of service - No stigma attached to termination order for temporary government employee accused of defalcation of public money - Mere acquittal does not automatically entitle reinstatement - Competent authority has the power to terminate services or conduct an enquiry before directing reinstatement - A direction for reinstatement would allow the employee to misappropriate public money with impunity - High Court erred in directing compensation as it would amount to a premium for misconduct.
[Paras 5 and 6]
