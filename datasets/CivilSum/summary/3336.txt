Pensionary Benefits - An order to permanently withhold benefits must obtain sanction of the President under Section 9(2)(b)(1) of the Pension Rules; a subsequently passed order is not illegal.
A contention that such an order could not be passed against a retiree is untenable.
[Para 14]
Jurisdiction - The Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 21, imposes a limitation; the order under challenge was passed in 1976, which is nine years before the tribunal came into existence.
Since no steps were taken to challenge that order in any court of law, it attained finality.
Therefore, the tribunal has no jurisdiction to entertain a petition.
[Para 13]
Limitation - Filing of repeated representations does not extend limitation unless there is statutory sanction for filing such representation, according to the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 21.
