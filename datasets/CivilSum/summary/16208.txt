According to Punjab Government Instructions dated 2931965, Temporary State Government Employees who were absorbed in posts under the State Government after release from service in GREF were entitled to benefit of service in GREF.
If an employee is appointed afresh in the same department after joining various assignments in other departments and does not return to the post held by him prior to his proceeding to service in GREF, he is entitled to be treated in the same manner as new entrants under the Punjab Government instructions dated 571963.
The employee is entitled to the benefit of service rendered in GREF, and 1990 (11) RSJ 1966 does not apply.
[Para 10] B.
Even if the Department allowed the claim of an employee, but did not implement the decision for years, and later declined the same claim without superseding the orders of the Department, employees can still continuously pursue their claims.
Any wrong can be undone at any time, and injustice can be remedied at any time.
There is no deadline by which the benefit must be provided to an employee.
Moreover, the Department cannot claim that if the benefit was denied, even wrongfully, then that mistake must be perpetuated, and the relief must be declined.
The Department did not even implement an order that was passed 20 years earlier, so the claim cannot be declined on the grounds of delay and laches.
