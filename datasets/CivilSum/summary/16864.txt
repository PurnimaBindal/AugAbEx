According to the Jammu & Kashmir Army Rules, Rule 13(III)(i), along with the Jammu & Kashmir Pension Regulations, the authority must fulfill certain conditions of discharge, such as completing pensionable qualifying service and providing military pension.
Rule 13 (III)(i) should be applied, not sub-rules (ii) or (v).
If a person has not been asked for the option to extend their service and has not requested for discharge, any orders for discharge are invalid.
In such cases, Regulations 123, 124, and 125 must be invoked to give the benefit of non-qualifying service.
Any mistakes by the authority cannot be made to the detriment of the wrongly discharged individual.
