Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Market Committee Class-III Service Rules, 1989, Rule 8(2) - Promotion from Class-IV posts to Clerical cadre - Reservation for Class-IV posts to Clerical cadre - Reservation for Class-IV cadre employees - Electrician - If other employees holding posts not mentioned in the Appendix were considered and promoted to Clerical posts in the quota reserved for Class-IV employees, an employee's lack of mention in the Class-IV posts Appendix as an Electrician would not disqualify them from receiving similar treatment - To ignore one employee from receiving such treatment would violate the doctrine of equality enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
