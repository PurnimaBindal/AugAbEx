Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Rule of harness - The claim of the petitioner for compassionate appointment was rejected because his elder brother is already employed, his mother is receiving family pension upon the death of his father, and the family possesses agricultural land.
The petitioner's contention that the department could not prove the possession of the land and that the father had sold it during his lifetime did not help his case, except for the possibility of funds being available with the family upon the sale of the land.
The tribunal made no mistake in rejecting the petitioner's candidature.
