Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 2.2(b) - Premature Retirement/Enquiry after retirement - Compulsorily retiring an official involves dropping or closing all pending or contemplated proceedings against him that may form the background motive to the decision - Recovery proceedings on the accusations regarding 8 years back ordered after premature retirement - If there had been any substance in the accusations, a regular enquiry under the Rules should have been initiated instead of ordering compulsory retirement - Charges ordered to be dropped.
[Para 4] B Pension/pre-mature retirement/Punishment - Dismissal or removal is a penalty resulting in the loss of benefits already obtained - An officer who is compulsorily retired is entitled to all retirement benefits - There is no element of charge or imputation in the case of compulsory retirement.
