Section 38 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 1986, and the Bureau of Indian Standards (Recruitment to Administration, Finance and Other Posts) Regulation, 1988, describe promotion to the post of Director (Administration).
In this case, the petitioner is working as Dy.
Director (Printing-cum-Production) and is claiming promotion.
However, the petitioner is from Technical Support Service (TSS), whereas candidates considered for promotion to the post of Director (Administration) should be from the group of Administration and Finance (A&F).
Although there was an omission by mistake in putting the "(A&F)" suffix in the Regulation 1990, this does not allow persons from a different cadre to become eligible for the cadre of A&F.
Therefore, the petitioner is not eligible for promotion based on the amended Regulation 1990, and their petition is dismissed.
