Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, PWD (Building & Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 152.
- Increments - Departmental examination - It is mandatory for an officer to pass his departmental examination within three years of his appointment or within the extended period.
Failure to pass will result in no future grade increments until the examination is passed.
Once the exam is passed, all increments will be released and paid retrospectively.
Arrears cannot be claimed for the period the exam was not passed.
[Paras 7 & 8].
B Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, PWD (Building & Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 2(2) & 2(12) - Appointment to the service - A person appointed to an ex-cadre post or an officiating appointment against a cadre post does not have a right to become a member of the service as per Clause (12) of Rule 2.
This is separate from a person who has already become a member of the service.
[Paras 8 to 10].
C Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, PWD (Building & Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 9.16 - Recovery and adjustment of Government dues - Government dues can be recovered from an employee's death-cum-retirement gratuity.
Overpayment of pay and allowances or leave salary would count as Government dues.
[Paras 12 & 13].
