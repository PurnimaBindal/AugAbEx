Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.25 - Misconduct - Collection of money - Petitioners collected money to meet legal expenses for filing a joint writ petition - Court deemed this not to be misconduct since every citizen has the liberty to approach the court of law - Charge-sheet quashed - Petition granted with costs.
