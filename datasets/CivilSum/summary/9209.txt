Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Bihar and West Bengal (Transfer of Territories) Act, 1956, Section 41(4) - Superannuation age - Transfer - An employee of Govt. of Bihar who initially served as a village level worker and later opted to serve under Govt. of West Bengal cannot be legally disadvantaged in terms of the age of superannuation, which would be 60 years as per the initial appointment.
The employee is entitled to continue in service until the age of 60 years.
[Paras 1 and 14]
