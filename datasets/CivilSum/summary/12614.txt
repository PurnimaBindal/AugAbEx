Punjab Kanungo Service (State Service Class III) Rules, 1976, Rule 9(2) - Promotion/Supersession/Adverse reports - The petitioner's promotion from Patwari to Kangungo in 1981 was denied due to uncommunicated adverse reports from 1967-68 and 1970-71, as well as a confidential report of censure from that same period.
However, during that period, the petitioner was rated as average and had no records of doubtful integrity.
Moreover, uncommunicated and stale entries cannot be used as a basis for denial of promotion or given premature retirement.
[Paras 6 and 7]
