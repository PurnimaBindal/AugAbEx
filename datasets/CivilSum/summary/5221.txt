Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981 - Punjab Educational Service (Schools and Inspection) (Class-II) Rules, 1976, Rule 9, Appendix "B" - Qualification of Teachers and Principals - Qualifications listed in Appendix "B" to 1976 Rules are only applicable to those seeking recruitment in Punjab Government Schools, and not to Aided School teachers and Principals.
An aided post of Principal in a privately managed aided school is governed by Security of Service Rules, 1981, which provide different qualifications and experience.
Merely offering "grades and qualifications as per Government" in the advertisement for an aided post does not make the 1976 Rules applicable to such posts.

Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) (First Amendment) Rules, 1998 - Scope and applicability - The Revised Pay Scales are not applicable to the aided posts of private school teachers and principals, and are limited to government employees only.
