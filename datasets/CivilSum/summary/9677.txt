The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300, along with Pension Regulation for Army Part II, 1961, Para 82, describe that the pension of Army retirees may be suspended if they are convicted of a serious offence.
In this case, the petitioners who retired as Hawaldars from the Army had their pension suspended by the respondents for the duration of their sentence under Sections 323/148/149 IPC.
Para 82 of the Army Pension Regulations permits the withholding of pension during the period a pensioner is sentenced, until their release from jail.
The Court finds no illegality in the respondent's decision to stop the petitioners' pension during the period of their sentence.
