The scope of interference in departmental proceedings under Article 226 of the Constitution is limited.
The High Court cannot act as an appellate court to re-examine the evidence and will only interfere if the delinquent had no opportunity to present their case.
If the disciplinary action is based on relevant evidence and proper opportunity was given, the writ court cannot intervene.
[Para 8] B The Appellate Authority under Rule 40 of the General Insurance (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1975 is the General Manager.
If the General Manager passes the punishment order, the memorandum can still be addressed to the Chairman-cum-M.D.
