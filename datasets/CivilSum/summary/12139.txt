The circular dated 17.1.1998 issued by the DSE, Rajasthan defines the classification of Sports Certificates for selection under sports quota.
According to this classification, State level tournaments only include those held within the State amongst Universities.
Whereas, tournaments amongst Universities from different States are considered National level tournaments.
Therefore, Petitioner who participated in inter-State tournament cannot be graded as having received a State level Certificate, but is entitled to marks as per National level Certificate.
[Paras 11 to 18] B Petitioner was wrongly awarded marks as a State level player instead of a National level player due to the incorrect grading of Sports Certificates.
The mere expiry of merit list is not a valid ground to deny the petitioner the right to which he is entitled to and offer it to someone else.
