According to Section 4 and Schedule IV of the Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, if a compromise on compensation for the death of a son is made before the Commissioner for an amount less than the minimum prescribed by the statute, it is considered a complete bar for the workman to relinquish their right to compensation from the employer.
Any agreement or compromise that removes or reduces the liability of any person to pay compensation is null and void.
