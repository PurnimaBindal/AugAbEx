Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Pension and Provident Fund) Amendment Rules, 2001, Rules 6 and 17(3) - Applicability of Rule - The petitioner who retired on 3182003 and was previously governed by the 1999 rule while working in Punjab, was later appointed in Haryana and governed by the 2001 rules at that time.
If the petitioner made contributions towards EPF but they were not accounted for due to an official lapse, the employee cannot be held responsible.
The petitioner would be entitled to the benefit of their entire service in both states.
