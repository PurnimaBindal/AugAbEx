Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979, Rule 7(i) Proviso (ii) - Punjab Privately, managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1979, Section 7 - Pay Scale - Parity of pay scale of employees of the Privately Managed Recognised Schools and corresponding posts in the schools run by the State Government should be maintained to avoid discrimination - In cases where the scales of pay are lower, the Managing Committee must ensure that they are brought at par with the employees of State Government - It is mandatory to grant an extra increment to seniors if their juniors get a higher pay in government schools - Private school employees should not be treated differently - The petitioner should be given the benefit of the increment as claimed.
