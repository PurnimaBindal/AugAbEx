Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Section - Appointment - Date of joining - Right to arrears of salary - The petitioner, appointed as Agricultural Supervisor, was unable to report for duty due to communication gap and unserved registered letters - The petitioner is entitled to receive pay scale and seniority as per similar employees from the date he should have reported for duty i.e. 16121993, along with arrears of salary from the said date.
