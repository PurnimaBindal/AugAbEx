Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rules 518 & 5.19 - Discharge of disabled employee on medical ground - If an employee consents to be retained in service on light duty and thereby relinquishes the right to compensation under the Workmen's Compensation Act, the employee cannot be discharged on medical grounds under Rule 518 without first being assessed for disability.
Rule 518 does not provide for premature retirement on the basis of medical unfitness, and an order of discharge under that rule would be without jurisdiction.
[Paras 9 & 10]  B Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights of Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 - Prohibition from discharge on account of disability - Section 47 legally prohibits dispensing with the services of an employee due to acquired disability, thus an order of discharge on this ground would be illegal.
