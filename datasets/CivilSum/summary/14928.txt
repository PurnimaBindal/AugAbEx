Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Natural justice - Application of mind - A writ petition challenges the government's order of refusing permission to open a new school.
This order was in a cyclostyled form and no opportunity was given for the petitioner to be heard.
The order's cyclostyled format is evidence of an absence of careful consideration in certain situations.
The denial of an opportunity to be heard constitutes a breach of the principle of natural justice.
The impugned order should be set aside because it is insufficient and inadequate.
The order must be comprehensive and not be brief or careless.
The foundation for a new order must be established by supported material and data.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
