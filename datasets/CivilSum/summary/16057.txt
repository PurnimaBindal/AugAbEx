Article 128 of the Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations does not permit termination of services without an inquiry into alleged unauthorised absence.
A simple notice alleging unauthorised absence cannot justify termination of a government servant's job.
The Constitution of India and Section 126 of J&K Constitution protect a Govt. servant's service, and proper procedures must be followed before imposing any punishment for misconduct.
The order impugning the petitioner's alleged unauthorised absence is in violation of various laws and rules.
The major punishment of removal from service cannot be imposed without an inquiry into the alleged misconduct.
The order of dismissal has been quashed.
J&K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Section 33, Constitution of J&K, Section 126, Constitution of India, Articles 311, 14, 16 and 21 are relevant.
