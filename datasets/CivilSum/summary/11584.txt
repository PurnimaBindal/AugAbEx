The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 state that the selection of Taxation Inspectors has been quashed and the Petitioner, who resigned from a previous position to become a Taxation Inspector, was denied reinstatement due to the earlier post not being vacant.
Other positions were available, but the Petitioner is now over-age for any civil post.
The court directed the Respondent to consider the Petitioner's claim for appointment to a suitable position.
