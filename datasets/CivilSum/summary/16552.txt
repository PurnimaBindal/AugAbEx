According to Rule 4(iii) of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, and Rule 4.3 of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Military service cannot be counted towards pension if they have already received four increments as a benefit.
However, it is the responsibility of the competent authority to grant necessary permission regarding counting of military service for pension purposes.
Nonetheless, the maximum qualifying period of service for pension is 33 years.
