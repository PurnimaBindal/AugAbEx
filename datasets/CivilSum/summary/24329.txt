The West Bengal Service (Revision of Pay and Allowance) Rules, 1961 regarding equal pay for equal work between Surveyors and Amins cannot be met as there is no functional or qualifications equality between the two positions.
The nature of duties and required qualifications for both positions are different, as Surveyors have more technical duties that require the use of sophisticated instruments whereas Amins' tasks require simple equipment and minimal technical training.
As such, Amins can only be entitled to pay scale No. 6 and promotions to higher scales according to their qualifications and experience.

Judgments and precedents are subject to the concept of sub silentio and per incurium, wherein a court's decision may not be considered a binding precedent if it did not consider relevant rules and earlier precedents.
In such cases, subsequent courts can reach a different conclusion if these issues are brought to their attention, and the Supreme Court has the power to correct such anomalies under Article 142 of the Constitution.
