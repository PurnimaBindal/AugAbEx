According to Gujarat Housing Board Services Classifications and Recruitment Regulations, 1981 Regulations 3, as well as Gujarat Housing Board Act, 1961 Sections 74(c) and 82, Appointment/Eligibility/Deputation criteria must be met in order to be appointed as Housing Commissioner (Technical)/Superintending Engineer.
The Rule stipulates that Executive Engineers of the Housing Board can be appointed as Superintending Engineer by deputation from the State Government's Building and Communication Department only when there is no eligible candidate for promotion within the Housing Board.
Hence, appointing someone by deputation without considering the eligible Executive Engineers of the Board violates the rules and is illegal.
