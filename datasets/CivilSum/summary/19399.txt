The Rules of the Regional Engineering College (Kurukshetra) Society state that candidates can only be shortlisted up to six times the number of vacancies available, but no guidelines on shortlisting were provided.
The authority in charge of shortlisting had already determined the eligibility and merits of all candidates and the shortlisting process cannot be arbitrary or go against the Constitution.
The selection was upheld and the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 6 to 9]

The College advertised only two vacancies, but one vacancy was substituted for an ad hoc appointment.
The appointment of five candidates based solely on the recommendation of the selection committee was improper without advertising the additional vacancies.
All eligible candidates have the right to compete for future vacancies.
