Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Reversion - Natural justice - Petitioner was initially shown as senior in the seniority list of Inspectors and AFSOs before promotion to DFSO but was later reverted as his seniority was deemed to be wrongly fixed.
- Revisions to seniority cannot be made that would disadvantage an employee without giving them a chance to be heard - The revision of seniority and consequent reversion were found to be a violation of natural justice and were therefore quashed.
