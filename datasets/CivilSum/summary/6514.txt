According to Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 173, 178, 179, and 48 along with Appendix II Entry 7(b), disability pensions will be granted if the disability is attributed to military service.
Even if the initial Medical Board did not mention it, if the release Medical Board later found a disability, it would be considered attributable to or aggravated by military service.
Additionally, there is no authority that can review the Medical Board's findings.
