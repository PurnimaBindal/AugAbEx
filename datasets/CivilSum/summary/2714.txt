Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 30 - Admission in Private Medical Colleges - Academic fees - Fixation of - The State Govt. has the authority to fix academic fees for the Private Medical and Dental Colleges for free seats in MBBS and BDS Courses - Govt. of MP fixed the fee for academic year 2003-2004 at Rs. 38,500/- per annum for the entire Course in line with Unni Krishnan's case - The students are obligated to pay the fee.
