Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(j) states that an organization can only be considered an industry if it produces and provides services for the satisfaction of human needs and wants.
Even though the Physical Research Laboratory operates systematically with the help of its employees, it is not an industry because it is not involved in any economic ventures or commercial enterprises.
It is an institution that discharges government functions under the Government of India.
The appeal has been allowed.
