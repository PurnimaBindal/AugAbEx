Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 29 - Vth Bipartite Settlement - Syndicate Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 1995 - Pension - Circular dated 4111995, Clause 17(a) - Pension scheme - The management retired the respondent as a punishment for unauthorised absence, which is not covered by the Circular dated 4111995.
However, as the respondent had put in 20 years of service and comes within the pension scheme, she is entitled to pension.
The decision of management deeming her to have voluntarily retired is misconceived.
