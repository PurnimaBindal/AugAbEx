Constitution of India Articles 226 and 227 - Selection - Petitioner was not selected because his experience certificate was not accounted for and because he did not study English during BA level - Court ordered the respondents to produce original application form and other documents - The respondents failed to do so, and it was held that an attested certificate on record showed that petitioner had worked in the school from 1988-91 and had studied English during BA level - The failure to produce the original application form raised a presumption against the respondents - Petition allowed and respondents instructed to reconsider the issue.
