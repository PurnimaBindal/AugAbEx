Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - The petitioner's father was absent from 31573 and did not receive any salary or emoluments - No service benefit was provided to the petitioner's father after 31573 until his death on 23185 - The petitioner's father did not claim any service benefits during his lifetime after 1973 - Even after his death, no claim was made to consider him in service and give him service benefits until his death - It was appropriately assumed that the petitioner's father was not continuing in service at the time of his death - It cannot be said that the whole family faced a financial crisis due to the death of the petitioner's father - There is no error in rejecting the petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment.
