Constitution of India, Articles 311, 226 and 227 - Termination - Reinstatement - After the respondent's services were terminated, the Presiding Officer found that no enquiry had been held and that the termination order was passed by an incompetent authority - The order of termination was set aside - 25% backwages were awarded with reinstatement - The Municipal Corporation challenged the award - As the respondent was appointed by the General Manager of Punjab Roadways and sent on deputation to the Municipal Corporation, Ludhiana, and there was no evidence that his services had been transferred, only the General Manager could have terminated his services - No grounds exist to interfere with the Presiding Officer's order regarding backwages - The respondent must be awarded a lump sum of Rs. 20,000/- and must return to duty immediately.
