Civil Procedure Code, Order 7 Rule 11 - Suit against Foreign State - Consent of Foreign State - Implied waiver - Court may go behind certificate of consent in special cases - Contract between employer and employee provided for application of Dutch Civil Law except for some limited purpose - Choice of law does not necessarily exclude jurisdiction - Provision enabling the application of Indian local laws for a particular purpose cannot constitute waiver - Consent under Section 86 can be challenged collaterally.

[Paras 15, 16 and 17]  C. Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - Based on rule of public policy - Finality of decisions of competent court - Parties not vexed twice over same issue.
