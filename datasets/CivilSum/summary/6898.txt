Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(2)(b), Schedule 2, Item 6 - Termination - Reinstatement with backwages - A plea that backwages cannot be granted because the workman did not request reinstatement upon dismissal of the approval application has been rejected - Neither the filing of the approval application nor its dismissal was communicated to the workman or the labour court - Accepting the plea would reward dubious conduct by the petitioner - The workman will be entitled to backwages.
