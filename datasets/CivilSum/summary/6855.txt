Article 226 of the Constitution of India, 1950 and Rule 5(1) of the Central Civil Services (Temporary Services) Rules, 1965 state that a temporary employee's termination of service requires an enquiry to be conducted, which was not done when the Petitioner was terminated on 441997 under Rule 5(1) of 1965 Rules.
The Petitioner shall be reinstated following an appellate authority order to set aside the previous termination order from 3.1.1996 with instructions to conduct an enquiry which resulted in his reinstatement vide order dated 2531997.
[Para 25] B.
Since the Petitioner was not employed since his termination on 441997, it is just and appropriate to award 50% backwages as per the facts and circumstances.
