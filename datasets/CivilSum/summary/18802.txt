Haryana State Electricity Board Conduct Regulations, 1984, Rule 21 - Disciplinary proceedings - Conduct of an employee - Strikes - Press statements - Speeches - Audio tapes of speeches - Gate meetings and dharnas - Denial of the delinquent - Proof of - Gate meeting speeches and audio recorded tapes played before the delinquent but denied must be proved by producing the individuals who heard them - Press statements cannot be proven by simply producing a copy of the newspaper without examining the reporter who recorded them - Enquiry Officer did not conduct the enquiry fairly, judicially and charge-wise - Order of punishment based on misconceptions without evidence is liable to be quashed.
[Paras 15 to 17]

Haryana State Electricity Board Conduct Regulations, 1984, Rule 21 - Disciplinary proceedings - Discrimination - The State authority proceeding differently against the similarly situated persons charge-sheeted with the same allegations of misconduct - Petitioner singled out to be proceeded against dropping the proceeding against others - Petitioner held to be discriminated.
