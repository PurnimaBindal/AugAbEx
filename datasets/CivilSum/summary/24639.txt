The Karnataka State Civil Services (Regulation of Promotion, Pay and Pension) Act, 1974, Section 3, along with Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311, follows seniority rules.
In a case of an Assistant Director temporarily promoted to Deputy Director and a Direct recruit who later joined and was confirmed as Deputy Director, a subsequent notification of regular promotion for the promotee with retrospective effect was not considered justified.
The Direct recruit was entitled to seniority in this case, in accordance with Paras 7 and 8.
