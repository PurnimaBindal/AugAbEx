The petitioner was given two chances to reappear for her examination.
But for the first chance, she failed to clear her paper.
Then, her examination form for the second chance was rejected because she had not submitted her migration certificate.
However, the petitioner claims that she had already submitted the certificate with her admission form.
She argues that she wouldn't have been allowed to sit for the first exam had she not produced the certificate.
Consequently, the university has been instructed to permit the petitioner to take the exam.
