According to Punjab Agricultural University Act 1961, Sections 22 and 25, past service rendered by the petitioner with the State of Pepsu between the period of 19.7.1955 to 19.12.1962 should be counted for the purpose of pension, firstly by Punjab Agricultural University and subsequently by ICAR.
Although benefits for past services were not sent to ICAR by the Punjab Government, the petitioner cannot be deprived of such benefits.
This was supported by the fact that the Tribunal dismissed the OA on the ground of jurisdiction.
