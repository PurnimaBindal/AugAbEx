Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Punjab State Co- operative Housing Federation Service Rules, 1976, Rule 31 - Those who were in service during the period in which the pay scale was revised must receive a revision in their pay scale, even if they had resigned or left the job before the order revising pay scales was passed.
This entitles the individual to receive arrears and the difference in pay from the date of joining service to the date of resignation.
