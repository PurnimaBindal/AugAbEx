The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 as well as the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-G were invoked in a case where the petitioner was terminated and the Principle of Last come first Go was violated.
Although the labor court ordered reinstatement with 25% back wages, this was challenged in court.
It was found that there were no allegations of any kind of wrongdoing in appointing the workman and that the writ petition lacked any merit.
