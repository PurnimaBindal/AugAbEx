Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 22 - Fatal accident - Death of driver - Insurance Company is liable to pay compensation - The Insurance Company cannot escape from indemnifying the insured once it is established that the driver had a valid driving license and the vehicle was insured at the time of the accident.
[Para 5]

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Appeal - Only substantial questions of law can be considered - The appeal under Section 30 is maintainable only if it involves a significant question of law.
