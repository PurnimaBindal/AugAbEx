According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the power of an authority to conduct domestic enquiry is not taken away by the fact that an accused was acquitted, nor is its discretion fettered in any way.
Departmental proceedings and criminal proceedings can proceed simultaneously, but in cases where complicated questions of law and facts are involved, departmental proceedings can be stayed pending disposal of the criminal case.
The standard of proof in departmental proceedings is one of preponderance of probabilities, while in a criminal case, the prosecution must prove the charge beyond reasonable doubt.
