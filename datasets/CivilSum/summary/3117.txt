The Constitution of India, Article 14 pertains to the validity of Selection Commission constitutions and appointments based on such constitution - Selection of the petitioners as College Principals by a reconstituted Selection Commission was declared invalid due to illegality in the reconstitution and subsequent selections - However, non-challenged appointments made on the basis of the reconstitution and subsequent selections while the Commission was allowed to function by an interim order of another SLP, will be saved by the doctrine of saving and de facto - Such appointments will be considered valid.
