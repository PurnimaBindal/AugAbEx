According to Sections 101 and 102 of the Evidence Act, 1872 and Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, it is the responsibility of the workman to prove his claim for overtime allowance.
The workmen have failed to provide any supporting evidence for their claim, and the only evidence available is the time cards provided by the management, which only prove the entry and exit time of the workman, but not necessarily that he was in fact working during that period.
