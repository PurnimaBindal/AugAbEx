Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 10 - Stopping one annual increment - Minor penalty - Delinquent employee should get the opportunity to present his case before the concerned authority - The decision to stop one increment permanent was deemed illegal and void because the punishing authority did not examine the case with an open mind.

Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 10 - Inquiry Charge-sheet - Stopping one annual increment - Show cause notice - Penalty imposed was deemed illegal and void because the enquiry officer had a pre-determined mind and the penalty was based on an illusory opportunity given through a show cause notice.
