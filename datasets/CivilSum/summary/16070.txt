Punjab Forest Service, Class II, Rules, 1941, Rule 4(b) stipulates that class II posts from Forest Rangers can be filled through promotion and direct recruitment within the fixed quotas.
The promotion quota is fixed at 33-1/3% of the total 13 posts, with 4 posts given to promotees not exceeding the quota specified in Rule 4(b).
If there is no direct recruit available for a post, diverting the post to promotees will not be illegal as well as not doing so from the alternative source can cause serious public mischief.
[Para 6] B.
A rule providing promotion quota up to a particular extent, upheld in a writ petition, will bar subsequent writ petitions challenging the promotions within the quota for promotees on that very ground.
[Para 7] C. The promotion made with retrospective effect on the post falling to the promotion quota in Punjab Forest Service, Class-II, Rules, 1941, Rule 4(b), will not be illegal.
