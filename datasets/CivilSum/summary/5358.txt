Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Termination - Petitioner, an Assistant Teacher, had services terminated on account of a judgement by Division Bench which invalidated Petitioner's appointment - Petitioner claimed that despite the cancellation of appointment, the panel's selection and approval were still valid since they were never interfered with - The court ruled that this argument was not valid - Earlier court orders stipulated a freeze on teaching and non-teaching staff appointments until office-bearers of the Managing Committee were elected - An order made in breach of a court order is a nullity and cannot be upheld - The invalidated appointment necessarily nullifies all prior actions - Petition dismissed.
