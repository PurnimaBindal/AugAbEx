Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Advertisement - Application - Ambiguity - Date of application - Advertisement inviting applications by a certain date - Unclear whether the application must reach by the specified date or be dispatched by the applicants by that date - Petitioner submitted application on time but it arrived a day late - Orders to be made based on case merits since the Commission had barely commenced collection of applications - Commission advised to specify in its future advertisements that applications must reach the Commission by the specified date or that applications received after the closing date will not be entertained.
