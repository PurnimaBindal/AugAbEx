Haryana Panchayats Department State Service, Group C, Rules 1979, Rule 12 - Seniority - Determination of - Method - According to the provisions, direct recruitment for posts of clerks must be carried out by the Board.
Inter se seniority should be determined by the length of continuous service on any post in the cadre.
When two persons compete for a post, the selection of the person placed higher in the order of merit determines seniority.
[Para 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Panchayats Department State Service, Group C, Rules 1979, Rule 12 - Seniority - Determination of - The January 4, 1983 notification only applies to persons whose services were regularized, and does not supersede the pre-existing statutory rules that govern service.
It cannot be said that seniority of persons appointed by regularization vis-a-vis those who have been directly recruited shall be determined in accordance with clause (vi) and not according to provisions of statutory rules.
[Paras 7, 8, 9, 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Panchayats Department State Service, Group C, Rules 1979, Rule 12 - Seniority - Inter se seniority - The person who is higher in order of merit has to be senior to the one who is below him.
Rejected candidates cannot be senior to those who are selected.
[Para 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Panchayats Department State Service, Group C, Rules 1979, Rule 12 - Regularisation - Seniority - The regularisation of services of persons who were rejected in open competitive selection cannot give them seniority over those who were found to be meritorious and have been selected.
