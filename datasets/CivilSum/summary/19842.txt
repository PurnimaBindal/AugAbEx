Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Haryana Harijan Kalyan Nigam Ltd Employees Service Bye-Laws, Bye-Laws 6.1 and 62 - Charge-Sheet - Show-cause notice - Absence of evidence - Termination - Departmental Enquiry - Employee issued charge-sheet - Enquiry Officer appointed - Only the statement of the accused employee was recorded and findings were given against him, which held the charges were proven - The prosecution failed to present any evidence to support the charges - The accused employee is only required to refute the evidence presented against him - Recording of his statement for establishing charges against him is not permissible - Enquiry proceedings violated Rule 7 and are thereby void - Termination is invalidated.
