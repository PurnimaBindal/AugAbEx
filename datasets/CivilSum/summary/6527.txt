According to Section 2(n) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, compensation for workmen is applicable when the deceased was doing maintenance personally under the supervision of Electricity Board officials, even if the employment was of a casual nature.
If the employment is for the purpose of the employer's trade or business, one is considered a workman.
Therefore, the deceased is considered a workman.
