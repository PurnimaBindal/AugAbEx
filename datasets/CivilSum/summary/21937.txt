Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Reservation - Year of recruitment - De-reservation - To promote candidates from SC, ST or BC categories, general candidates will not be considered for promotion to the same post.
Vacancies will not be thrown open to the general category unless reserved candidates are not available, or dereservation is done.
It is not compulsory for the government to fill the vacancy as soon as it arises.
Recruitment year is the year in which recruitment takes place.
The same criteria will apply to fill the reserved vacancy.
Dereservation will be considered only at the end of the third recruitment year provided that reserved candidates are not available, are found not eligible for promotion, or are carried forward for three consecutive recruitment years.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Reservation - Dereservation - Rule of carry forward for three successive years - Since no candidate belonging to the SC category was promoted for three years, the vacancy becomes available to general candidates.
