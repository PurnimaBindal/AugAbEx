Constitution of India, Article 14 - University Grants Commission Act, 1956, Section 26-C - Service Regulation of 1983 - The Central Government has established a principle that employees who have rendered ad hoc service for five years or more must be given five years service benefit for the purpose of regularisation - UGC followed this principle and regularised all ad hoc service/appointments/promotions - UGC promises that promotions will be made as per 1983 regulations, and strict compliance is unnecessary.
