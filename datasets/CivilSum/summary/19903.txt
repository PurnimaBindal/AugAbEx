Constitution of India, Article 226 and 311 - Natural justice - Pay - Notice - The petitioner was suspended, but the suspension was later revoked, with the period of suspension being treated as period on duty - However, the petitioner was denied pay over and above subsistence allowance without any notice or opportunity - While the competent authority had discretion in this matter, such discretion must be exercised judiciously - As a result, the petitioner was ultimately allowed full salary.
