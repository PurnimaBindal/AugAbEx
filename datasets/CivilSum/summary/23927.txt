According to Sections 10(14), 17 and 2(24) of the Income Tax Act, taxable salary income includes allowances such as House Rent allowance, Dearness allowance and CCA.
Income tax must be paid at the point when the income is earned, regardless of its destination or utilization.
The Fundamental Rules for payment of allowances are immaterial for determining taxability.
Equity and hardship cannot be considered when interpreting tax law.
The words "income" and "salary" have no connection with profits of the employer, and "profits" only refers to any advantage or gain received by the employee.
