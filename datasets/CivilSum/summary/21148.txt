According to Criminal Judicial Services Rules, Rule 12 and Kerala State Service Rules, Rule 28(a), the appellant who was temporarily promoted to the post of Magistrate Ist Class but couldn't pass the prescribed examination during the promotion period is not entitled to benefits under the first proviso to Rule 28(a) of State Service Rules.
As the officers junior to the appellant who passed the examination during their promotion are empanelled for Magistrate Ist Class, it is not illegal to fix the appellant's seniority before his juniors.
The appellant only passed the examination during an extended period of probation.
