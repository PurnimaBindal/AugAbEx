According to the Constitution of India, UP Rajya Karamchari Kalyan Nigam is considered a "State" under Article 12 and therefore falls under the jurisdiction of the High Court under Article 226.
To determine whether a body falls under the definition of "State" under Article 12, the multiple tests include the nature of financial, functional and administrative control of the State over it and whether it is mainly dominated by the State Govt. and subject to regular supervision.
