Pepsu Road Transport Corporation Conditions of Appointment and Service Regulations, 1981, Appendix (B) item No. 2 Clause (g) - Competent authority - Appointing and punishing authority - The Deputy General Manager is both the appointing and punishing authority under the Rules, rendering the Depot Manager's order of dismissal invalid and without jurisdiction.
