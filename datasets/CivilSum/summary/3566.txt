The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, and the Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 state that a suit for declaration that an order of dismissal or termination is wrongful, illegal and ultra vires must be filed within three years from the date of order.
The Hon'ble Apex Court in 1992 (6) SLR 739 held this.
A suit filed in April 1998 challenging an order from March 21, 1980 was beyond the period of limitation.
Therefore, the suit was dismissed for the right reasons.
