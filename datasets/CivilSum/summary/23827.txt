Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Selection post - Seniority - Appointment to the posts of `Private Secretary' for Executive Directors to the National Airport Authority from amongst the Stenographers Grade I and II with 20 years service as Stenographers Grade I, II and III - Selection is to be made on the basis of written and oral test - Appointment is based on merit regardless of seniority or being in Stenographer Grade I or II - The person who stands first in the test is appointed - The petitioner does not have the right to be appointed solely on the basis of her seniority in Stenographers Grade I as she was found unsuitable in the test - Selection is not based on seniority-cum-suitability.
