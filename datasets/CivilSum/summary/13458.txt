Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioner appointed as Private Secretary on casual basis without following regular process of selection claiming pay scale admissible to stenograghers in regular Grade-II which exclusively is a promotion post by selection not for direct recruitment - The petitioner has been deemed not entitled to the regular pay scale of Steno Grade-II.
