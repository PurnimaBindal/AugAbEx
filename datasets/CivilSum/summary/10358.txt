Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bombay State Financial Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1954, Regulations 25, 26 and 40 - There is no provision for continuation of disciplinary proceedings after the employee superannuates in the Regulations - Any continuation of such proceedings is illegal - Withholding of Retirement benefits is also not sustainable - Respondent's only option is to either amend the Regulation or sue the superannuated delinquent employee for recovery of pecuniary loss.
[Paras 9 and 10] B Bombay State Financial Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1954 - Payment of Gratuity Regulations, Regulation 7 - Recovery of pecuniary loss by deducting or withdrawing amount from gratuity - This power cannot be exercised in the absence of holding a disciplinary enquiry - Additionally, the continuation of disciplinary proceedings after retirement is illegal - Regulation 7 is inapplicable.
