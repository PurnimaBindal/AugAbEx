Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Haryana Labour Department (Group C) Service Rules 1982, Rule 9(g)(i) - Vires of Rule 9(g)(i) - Promotion to the post of Labour Inspector - The post of Draftsman serves as a feeder cadre for the post of Cartographer - Promotion signifies a step towards advancement to a higher position, grade or honour - Thus, Rule 9(g)(i), which includes the post of Cartographer for promotion to the post in the same pay scale as the Labour Inspector, should be amended.
