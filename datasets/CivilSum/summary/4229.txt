The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Sections 2(oo), 25-F, and 25-G, concerns retrenchment.
The termination of an employee in accordance with the stipulation in the appointment letter that their services will be terminable upon the arrival of regular appointees is allowed.
In this case, the workman's appointment was extended twice for three months each time, still with the same stipulation.
The workman was terminated when regular appointees arrived, alongside others who were considered but not selected.
As such, the termination was not a retrenchment within the meaning of Section 2(oo), and the provisions of Section 25F and 25G were not applicable.
Consequently, the Labour Court's award to reinstate the workman was quashed.
