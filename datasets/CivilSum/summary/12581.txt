Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Government instructions of July, 1957 allowing all JBT teachers to receive Master's grade upon acquiring qualifications of BT/BEd - Later, government issued instructions on 391990 specifying that only JBT Teachers appointed as Masters would be eligible for Master's Grade - Court held that these instructions applied only to future appointments - Teachers who obtained higher qualifications prior to 391990 were entitled to Masters Grade regardless of their appointment status - Instructions dated March, 1990 would only have prospective effect and would not invalidate the previously accrued rights of petitioners.
The verdict was based on the reliance of AIR 1987 SC 1621.
