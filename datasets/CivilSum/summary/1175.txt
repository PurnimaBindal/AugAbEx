Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion on the post of JE - PSEB fixed quota of 6733% for Test Inspectors based on diploma and non-diploma holders - Petitioners challenged it for discrimination due to common cadre and seniority - Court held that educational qualification classification is not unconstitutional - Petition dismissed as it lacks merit, with no order for costs.
