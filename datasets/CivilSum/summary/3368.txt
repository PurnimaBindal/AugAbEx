The Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 53 and 61 clarify the applicability of the Workmen's Compensation Act.
If a deceased employee is covered under the Employees' State Insurance Act, the said sections bar any claim to be made under provisions of Workmen's Compensation Act.
Therefore, a petition made under the Workmen's Compensation Act is not maintainable.
