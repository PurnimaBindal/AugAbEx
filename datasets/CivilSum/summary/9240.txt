According to Schedule 2, Item 3 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the termination of service for an apprentice proof-reader in a newspaper establishment is allowed if the petitioner did not perform their duties with due care and to a satisfactory standard.
The labour court found evidence of unsatisfactory performance, and therefore, the order to terminate the petitioner's apprenticeship is valid and not subject to interference.
