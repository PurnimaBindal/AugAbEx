Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 20 and 33-C (2) - Request for revised pension arrears - The petitioner's reversion was overturned by the labor court, entitling him to arrears of pay and promotional incentives - Despite being due, the petitioner was not given his arrears of pension and gratuity upon reaching retirement age - As a result, the respondents have been ordered to pay the petitioner's arrears of pension, gratuity, and other retiral benefits based upon his last salary.
