Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Apprentices Act, 1961, Section 22 - Apprentices - Right of apprentices to preferential appointment - Employers are not obligated to provide regular jobs to apprentices-trainees, however, they are required to give preference to trained apprentices over direct recruits when filling job openings - Trained apprentices are also to be given age relaxation for the duration of their apprenticeship if needed - The training institute must keep a record of trained apprentices to ensure they are given preference based on seniority - Adhering to this process satisfies the requirements of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Apprentices Act, 1961, Section 22 - Apprentices - Posts for apprentices - Apprenticeships do not constitute a post, and instead are for training purposes where apprentices receive a stipend according to the rules.
