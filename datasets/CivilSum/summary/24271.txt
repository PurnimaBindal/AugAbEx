Constitution of India, Entry 66 of List I and Entry 25 of List III - Technical Higher Education - The States retain the right to set higher standards for admission to engineering colleges, provided these standards are complementary or supplementary to AICTE's standards - Prescribing standards for education is beneficial for its quality - AICTE's standards are not necessarily permanent, as States may prescribe higher qualifications for candidates - These standards should be realistic and achievable - Higher minimum marks can enhance the quality of education - Admission to engineering colleges is not guaranteed solely by qualifying CET.
