Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State Co-operative Milk Producers Federation is not considered a State within the definition of Article 12 of the Constitution of India, meaning writ jurisdiction does not apply - State Government has no financial control over the federation, nor any functional and administrative control, and the federation is independent in all aspects of business - Federation cannot be defined as an agency of the State Government.
