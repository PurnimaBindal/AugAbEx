Tamil Nadu State Judicial Service Rules, Rules 20 and 2 - Seniority - Direct recruits - It was held that direct recruits cannot be placed above those who were already working on a regular basis as District Munsiff on 8101988, when the rules were amended.
Rule 20's third proviso does not take away the seniority of those appointed to services under Rule 2(b)(i) before 3111988.
[Paras 25, 30 and 31]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Tamil Nadu State Judicial Service Rules, Rule 20 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - In an earlier writ petition, the validity of Rule 20 was challenged and upheld.
In a subsequent writ petition questioning seniority, it was questioned whether Rule 20 was correctly observed while fixing seniority.
The earlier decision would not operate as res judicata.
[Paras 24 and 32]
