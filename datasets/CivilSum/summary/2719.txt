Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Industrial Dispute - Delay and laches - Referring to Industrial dispute after termination without any reasonable cause and after 20 years have passed, with no explanation for such a delay - Demand to make a reference rejected on the grounds of this unreasonable delay - Such rejection cannot be considered unjustified - After such a long period of time, it cannot be assumed that any dispute or threat to industrial peace remains.
[Paras 22 to 24]
