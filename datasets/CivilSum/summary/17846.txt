According to Punjab Education Service Class-III (School Cadre) Rules, 1955, Rule 7, the Director of Public Instructions is not authorized to issue instructions that supplement the Rules or to frame the Rules themselves.
Any changes to the qualifications required for appointment can only be made by amending the Rules.
Despite this, Executive instructions substituted the qualifications for BA, B.Ed.
with a combination of subjects.
As this change was made by an incompetent authority, it cannot be enforced.
As per 1970 SLR 505, the petitioner is entitled to promotion as a Master without the condition of subject combination.
[Paras 6 and 7]
