Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Admission - Examination - Despite not being admitted under the NRI category, petitioner expected to receive a roll number for examination - It was discovered that the mistake had been made by the college who failed to include the petitioner's name among the submitted NRI candidates - The petitioner should not be penalized for the college's mistake - The court ordered the regularization of the petitioner's admission as an NRI candidate and to declare his result for the 1st year examination.
