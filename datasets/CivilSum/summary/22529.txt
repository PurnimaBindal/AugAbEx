According to the Army Act, 1950, Sections 40(a) and 48, Regulation 113(a), a non-commissioned officer who is dismissed under the act is not eligible for pension and gratuity.
However, the President may have discretion to grant service pension at a rate equal to what the individual would have received if discharged on the same day.
In this case, the respondent is not entitled to pensionary benefits.
