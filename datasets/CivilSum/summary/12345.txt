Punjab Police Rules, 1934 (as applicable in Haryana), Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Gravest misconduct - The punishment authority must provide reasons for deprivation of pension, which is a civil right.
It is not a bounty, but an ex gratia granted by the government to those who spent their lives in service to the country.
Charge of refusal to acknowledge patrol duty orders is not considered as gravest misconduct, according to the explanation in the rule.
The punishment order does not indicate application of mind and is therefore not sustainable.
[Paras 6 to 11]
