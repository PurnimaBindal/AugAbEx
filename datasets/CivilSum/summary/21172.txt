According to Section 24(3)(aa) of the Advocates Act, 1961, one cannot be enrolled as an advocate simply because he or she is appearing before a Sales Tax Authority without a degree in law.
The Bombay Sales Tax Act or rules framed under it may permit appearance before a Sales Tax Authority without a law degree, but this does not entitle a person to practice law as specified in Clause (aa) of Sub-section (3) of Section 24.
