Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 5.9(b) outlines provisions for appointments with fixed terms, including termination before the expiration of the term with notice pay.
The rule specifically addresses situations where a person appointed on a fixed term is required to be removed before the expiration of the term.
The rule provides justifiable grounds for curtailing the tenure post with specific power given to the Appointing Authority.
It is important to note that sections 7, 7-C, and 8 of the Industrial Disputes Act do not apply for curtailing the fixed term of a temporary employee, but rather apply for the retention of such an employee after the fixed term expires.
The Rule has statutory force and was recently upheld in a case where a petition was dismissed.
