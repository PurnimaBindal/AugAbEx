The Circular issued on 1521987 permitted the regularisation of class III ad hoc employees, who had completed two years service on 141986, with the exception of teachers.
The court found sufficient grounds to direct the respondents to consider the petitioners' service regularisation cases in line with the directives issued in the 1521987 Circular.
While not applicable under the Circular from 311983, there is no reason to deny deserving petitioners' relief merely because they have not adequately articulated their prayers.
The court ordered that the petitioners' service be regularised from 1111986.
