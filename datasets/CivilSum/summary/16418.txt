Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - NCC Rules, 1948, Rules 23, 24 and 28 - Lecturer - Challenge to transfer of private respondent and order directing to handover charge - Petitioner argued that transfer of respondent-5 is not valid and order to handover charge is violative of 1948 Rules - However, court held that transfer of respondent-5 has nothing to do with 1948 Rules and that respondent-5 is fully qualified to be given charge of NCC wing as an Associate NCC Officer, while petitioner was not selected as such and therefore has no right over and above respondent No. 5.
