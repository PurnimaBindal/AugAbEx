According to Rule 19 of the Border Security Force Rules, 1969, BSF Constables who resign before completing 20 years of qualifying service are not eligible for pensionary benefits.
This was the case with a petitioner who resigned after 12-1/2 years of service.
Although pensionary benefits were granted based on equity to some BSF personnel who had already been receiving pension for a long period, the petitioner could not claim pension as a matter of right.
As a result, the writ petition was dismissed.
