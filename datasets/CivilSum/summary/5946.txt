Allotment of Government residences to dependents of retired Government employees is regulated by the Government Residence (Chandigarh Administration Common Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972.
The Constitution of India, Article 14 mandates non-discrimination.
Dependents of retired Government employees who have lived with retired Government employees for six months are eligible for a Government quarter.
The six-month period was extended to three years since 2821989.
The categorisation of retiring government employees based on their date of retirement is unjust and arbitrary.
