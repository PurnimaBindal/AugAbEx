The liability of pension and other terminal benefits of the employees of the DISCOMS extends to cases of voluntary retirement, and Rule 48-A of CCSP Rules protects their right to apply for voluntary retirement.
[Paras 72 & 73]
The GNCT is not liable to own the liability of pension and other terminal benefits under the Special Voluntary Separation Scheme, and there can be no estoppel against the GNCT regarding this.
[Para 83]
Those who had opted for the Scheme of DISCOMS as per the conditions of service existing as on 01.07.2002 are outside the purview of Rule 48-A of CCSP Rules, and the liability to fund the payment of terminal benefits of such VRS/SVSS optees is that of the DISCOMS.
[Para 86]
The liability for payment of terminal dues to VRS optees by DISCOMS can be made through IPGCL mode or the Trust can pay the dues and residual pension after DISCOMS pays the additional cost.
