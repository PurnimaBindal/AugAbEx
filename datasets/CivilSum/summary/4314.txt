Constitution of India, Articles 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 9(1) - Departmental enquiry - De novo Inquiry - If a punishing authority believes that a defect exists in an inquiry's findings into allegations against a civil servant, then the authority may only refer the matter back to the same enquiry officer for further inquiry on that point, and a fresh de novo inquiry cannot be ordered.
However, if the inquiry was previously declared vitiated based on irregularities in the procedure for imposing a penalty, this decision cannot prevent the state from initiating another inquiry into the same subject matter.
An objection was raised that the earlier inquiry officer was completely biased against him, making it necessary to appoint a different officer to hold a fresh inquiry de novo.
