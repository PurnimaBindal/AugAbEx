Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Objective is to assist surviving members in coping with the aftermath of bread earner's death and easing the hardship - Applicant filed application after 17 years since employee's death - It seems apparent that the family was able to manage somehow during this time and had some means of subsistence - The claim was correctly dismissed by the Tribunal for lacking in merit.
