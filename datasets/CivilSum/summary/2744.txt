Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Gujarat Civil Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rules 9 and 10 - Departmental Enquiry - Natural Justice - The punishment of reduction in rank for a period of five years on the advice of Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC) - It is improper to withhold the advice of GPSC from the petitioner, which formed an essential material for the punishment.
