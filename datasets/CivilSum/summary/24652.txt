Constitution of India, Article 30(1) - Bihar State Madarasa Education Board Act, 1982, Section 7(2)(4) - Education Board - It was found that Section 7(2)(4) of the Act, which allowed the Board to dissolve the Managing Committee of an educational institution, violated Article 30 of the Constitution.
As a result, the order was given accordingly.
[Paras 6 & 8] B Bihar State Madarasa Education Board Act, 1982, Sections 3 and 10 - Education Board - It was determined that the constitution of the Board would not violate the constitutional rights of minorities under Article 30 of the Constitution if the majority of the Board's members and its Chairman were not from the minority community.
Therefore, the order was given accordingly.
