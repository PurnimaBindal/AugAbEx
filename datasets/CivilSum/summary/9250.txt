Constitution of India, Article 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25FF - Dismissal - Petitioner employed by respondent No. 2, who leased the undertaking to respondent No. 1 - Respondent No. 1 dismissed petitioner during the lease period - Labour Court directed respondent No. 2 to reinstate the petitioner without awarding back wages - Agreement of lease revoked during the pendency of appeal - Industrial Court ordered respondent No. 1 to pay back wages from the date of dismissal until revocation of lease and retrenchment compensation as per the law - Upheld.
