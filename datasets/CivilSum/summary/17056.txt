Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory Retirement - Absence from duty - Petitioner was absent from duty due to sickness - No evidence that petitioner did not follow medical rules - The respondent did not claim that the sick leave certification was fake - Petitioner notified superiors of sickness through certificate of posting - Once period of absence was regularized as leave without pay, petitioner cannot be considered absent without permission - Order for compulsory retirement as punishment must be overturned.
