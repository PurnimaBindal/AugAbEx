The J & K Daily Rated Workers/Work charge Employees Regularisation Rules, 1994, Rule 8 stipulates that employees' services are to be regularised as and when they complete seven years of service, with the word "years" used in plural.
In this case, the petitioner had worked as a daily worker for three years and was later re-employed as a casual labourer, but the respondents only took note of the re-employed service and not the previous service, which was deemed invalid.
[Para 9] B The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F provides that if the services of a workman who has completed more than 240 days of service are terminated without compliance, the workman is entitled to reinstatement.
In this case, the petitioner had completed over 240 days of continuous service when his services were terminated without complying with Section 25F, making reinstatement a valid option.
