Constitution of India, Articles 15(4), 16(4) - J & K Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 1 - Scheme for absorption - Court found that the Rules of 1994 for the scheme of absorption of Instructors of Non-formal Education Centres in 3% of teacher posts do not contain any reservation.
The circular in question only serves as a means of absorption and its scope is entirely distinct from the Rules that provide for reservation.
