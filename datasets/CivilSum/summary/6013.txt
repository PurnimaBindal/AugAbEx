Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226 and 227 - Promotions on ORP ranks that defeat the purpose and intent of regular promotion are not legal - In this case, promotions on ORP ranks resulted in the petitioner being reverted - Held, these promotions are not sustainable in law.
[Para 9]
Police Act, Section 12 - Adhoc promotion to police officer due to family members being killed by terrorist - ORP rank - Not legal - Held, promotions on ORP ranks that defeat the purpose and intent of regular promotion are not sustainable in law.
[Para 9]
Punjab Police Rules, Rule 13.8 and Constitution of India, 1950 - Pension - Petitioner worked on a Higher post on ORP rank and later reverted - The department is required to count the period of service on the higher post towards calculation of the petitioner's pension - Held, the petitioner has functioned as Head Constable and was paid accordingly, so this service must be counted.
