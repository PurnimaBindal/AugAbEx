Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution dictate promotion and prohibit discrimination.
The income tax department's ministerial service consists of two sub-groups: (1) L.D.C.s, UDCs, Head Clerks, and Supervisors (Grade II and Grade I) and (2) Stenographers (Ordinary grade) and Stenographers (Senior grade).
The sub-groups can seek promotion within their respective groups.
The Tax Assistant cadre is an intermediary cadre between UDC and Head Clerks.
Writ petitioners who were stenographers claimed the posts of Tax Assistant, which were likely to be filled up from a distinct group; therefore, they were not justified in alleging discrimination.
