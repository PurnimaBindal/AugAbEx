Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II Rules 6.17 and 16-B and 834 - Family Pension - Exclusion of parents of unmarried deceased employees from the definition of 'Family' under Rule 617 whereas they have been allowed to remain entitled to benefits as dependents of deceased as members of his 'Family' under Rule 16-B and 834 - The purpose of the rule relating to family pension is to provide means of sustenance to the members of the family of deceased employee - No reasons shown for excluding parents from the definition of 'Family' - Every action of executive and legislature as well should meet the test of reasonableness contemplated Under Art.14 of the Constitution - Parents cannot be excluded from the definition of 'Family' - Rule 617 of the Pb.
Civil Services Rules, Vol. II so far it excludes parents from the 'Family' is ultra vires and cannot be sustained to that extent - Rule struck down - Parents held entitled to the benefits as members of 'Family' of deceased - State directed to make the payment with 12% interest on the arrears.
