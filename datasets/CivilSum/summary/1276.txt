Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(OO)(bb) - Retrenchment - Contract workers appointed for a specified period exceeding 240 days through agreement with a contracting body - Termination at the end of the period does not amount to retrenchment - Even if the work was of a regular nature, engaging personnel on a contract basis is not prohibited and termination does not require the formalities of retrenchment, provided the contract had a specific duration.
[Paras 22, 26 and 27]
