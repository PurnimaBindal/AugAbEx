The High Court has granted parity in pay scales for Actors, Stage Masters, Harmonium Masters, and Tabla Masters of Drama Parties employed as Artists in other departments of the State Government of Haryana with their counterparts in Rural Community Theatre, under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, which state that equal pay must be given for equal work.
However, the State Government submitted an additional affidavit regarding the difference in service conditions, and the petitioners did not submit a counter-affidavit despite specific orders and an opportunity to do so.
As a result, the orders of the High Court granting pay scale parity were set aside.
