Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 268-H - Cut off date - Pension/Retiral benefits - The government made a policy decision to introduce a liberalized pension scheme for government servants in service on or after February 29, 1964.
The court stated that such policy decision was not justiciable unless it was wholly capricious, arbitrary, and whimsical, which was not the case.
The court also determined that the cut-off date mentioned in Rule 268-H was not arbitrary and capricious and was not in violation of Article 14.
However, the court called on the state government to consider reviewing the question of antidating the benefits under Rule 268-H to retirees before February 29, 1964, as the government is fully aware of the hard realities faced by such retirees.
