Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical fitness - Appointment on the post of Bank Clerk - An applicant who passed the written and viva voce tests was deemed unfit for the post due to a serious eye defect discovered during a medical examination.
Although a specialist Doctor gave a contrary opinion, the High Court decided in favour of the applicant in the writ jurisdiction, and the Bank absorbed the applicant on an alternate post due to a humanitarian approach.
It was held that the High Court's decision to determine medical fitness in writ jurisdiction against the opinion of the specialist Doctor was inappropriate.
