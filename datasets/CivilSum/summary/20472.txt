Constitution of India Article 16 - Back wages cannot be denied on reinstatement - Central Administration Tribunal allowed back wages in a similar case - Reasons provided by Tribunal are irrelevant and based on weak grounds - Consequential order should be of equal significance in both cases, unless exceptional circumstances are mentioned by the Tribunal - Denial of back wages by the Tribunal was arbitrary and unreasonable - Tribunal's order overturned.
