According to Punjab Civil Services Rules of Volume I - Part I - Rule 4.9, a person's ad hoc service period in another department will not be counted towards increments if they are re-appointed on an ad hoc basis in a different department.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to receive benefits of increments on the basis of their ad hoc service in another department because they were not appointed by the SSSB on an ad hoc basis in the earlier department nor were they appointed by the Board in the subsequent department on regular basis.
This is based on information in Paragraphs 7, 8, and 9.
