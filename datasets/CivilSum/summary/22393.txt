The Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 24, Jammu and Kashmir Police Manual, Regulation 382, 384(2), and 390(2), and Jammu and Kashmir Police (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1984, Rules 5, 14, and 20, state that officiating promotions to the position of Sub Inspectors must be granted to both direct recruits and promotees.
The Respondents' names were entered in List E on 2541978, and they were promoted on an officiating basis on 19.5.1979, with retrospective effect from 2541978.
The Appellants were appointed on 25.4.1979 as direct recruits in the position of Sub Inspectors.
Private respondents were employed as ASI at the time.
It has been held that the retrospective promotion was wrong in law and violated the Rules since a government must not show favoritism to one set of employees, thereby jeopardizing the legitimate claim of another set.
The appeal has been allowed.
