According to Rule 249 of Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume III, a Dak Runner is entitled to daily allowance, incidental charges, and wages for overtime incurred while performing their duties between different places.
The plaintiff, who had been appointed as a Dak Runner in 1952 and had been performing his duties without any objections, was not entitled to any relief because the lower appellate court declined his case, as there was no competent authority declaring that the rule was applicable to employees.
The trial court made an error in law by relying on Rule 249.
