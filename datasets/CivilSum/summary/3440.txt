Regarding Recruitment for Assistant District Attorneys, there were 1500 applications for 74 available positions.
The time spent determining the comparative merit by conducting interviews was deemed not illegal.
[Para 6]
In regards to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, the applicants for the post of Assistant District Attorney cannot challenge the legality of the screening test as they took the test without making any representation on the grounds of a shortage of time for preparation.
Their decision to take the test was made with full knowledge, therefore, they are estopped from challenging its legality.
