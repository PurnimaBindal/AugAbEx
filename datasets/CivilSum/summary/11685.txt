Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pay scale - Senior scale - Discrimination - Whether State can deviate from its own policy and criteria laid down by Government of India to favor some employees with the benefit of senior scale - Held that deviation from own declared policy may be allowed on a rational basis, but it must be applied equally to all similarly situated employees - State cannot choose to favor one group of employees over others in deviation from its own policy.
[Paras 10 and 11]
