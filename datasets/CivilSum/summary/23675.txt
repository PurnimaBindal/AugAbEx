According to Section 28 of the Delhi School Education Act, 1973 and Rule 47(4) of the Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, selection grade based on seniority cannot be denied to an employee who is senior and better qualified than another employee given selection grade.
In addition, an employee's seniority cannot be forfeited because they were declared surplus, provided they have no adverse entries on their character rolls and no disciplinary proceedings are pending.
The appellant in this case was eligible for placement in selection grade.
