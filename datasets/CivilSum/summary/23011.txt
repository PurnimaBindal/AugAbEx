Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Seniority/Cadre Post/Ex-cadre post/Regularisation - In Chadha's case AIR 1986 SC 638, it was decided that the expression "posts" includes both cadre and ex-cadre posts when it comes to counting the entire period of service in Grade IV posts for seniority of promotees - The word 'Post' refers to an appointment to any job or position - The seniority of the promotees should be based on continuous length of service regardless of whether they worked against the cadre post or ex-cadre post - The Supreme Court's decision does not use the expressions 'Cadre Post' or 'Ex-cadre Post' and is clear and easy to understand, needing no further explanation or interpretation.
