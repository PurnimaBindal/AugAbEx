According to Section 2(q) of the Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, the designation "civil post" cannot be determined solely by the source of the employee's salary.
Once the competent authority appoints an employee on an adhoc, part-time, fixed salary, or contractual basis to fulfill a constitutional obligation, the employee is considered to hold a civil post and is entitled to apply under section 19 of the Act.
This applies to lecturers appointed by the Chandigarh Administration under relevant rules.
