Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code (5 of 1908), Section 11 and Order 9, Rules 9 and 8 - Evidence Act (1 of 1872), Section 115 - A fresh writ petition on the same cause of action is not maintainable after dismissal for non-appearance of petitioners - While dismissal does not operate as res judicata, the rule of estoppel should still be applied and parties are responsible for the presence of their counsel during hearings.
