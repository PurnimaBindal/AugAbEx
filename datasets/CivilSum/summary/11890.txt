Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Delhi Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 12(1) (c) - Whether Depot Managers in Delhi Transport Corporation have the power to take disciplinary action (including removal, dismissal, and reduction in rank) against Class-III/Class-IV employees such as drivers and conductors - It has been held that Depot Managers' power to initiate disciplinary action and issue show cause notices is a valid exercise of statutory powers based on Resolutions passed by DTC Board and derived from provisions of the Act - The initiation or taking of disciplinary action by Depot Managers cannot be found lacking in power or authority - D.R.T.A.
