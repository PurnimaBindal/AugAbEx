Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment compensation - Compensation must be paid before or along with the notice of retrenchment; otherwise, subsequent acts of sending the amount of compensation will not validate the illegality.
The order of retrenchment will be void and liable to be quashed.
[Para 7]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Delay - In case of a demand notice against illegal retrenchment given after a delay of 4 years, there is no prescribed limitation for raising an industrial dispute.
The reference or relief cannot be declined due to delay, but it may affect only back wages.
[Para 7]
