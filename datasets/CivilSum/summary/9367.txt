According to Sections 441 and 446 of the Criminal Procedure Code, if an accused is convicted by a trial court and then runs away just before the judgment is pronounced, the surety bond will be forfeited.
Even if the accused was present in court for some time before disappearing, the duty of the surety is to ensure their continuous presence until the proceedings conclude.
Therefore, the trial court was correct in forfeiting the surety bond.
