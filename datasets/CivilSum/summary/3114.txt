Under the Advocates Act, 1961, Section 5, and the Bar Council of Delhi Rules, 1963, Rules 126, 127, 129, and 130, it is a question of whether the removal of the petitioners from the post of Honorary Secretary should be interfered with.
Rules do not envision the institution of an honorary secretary, and therefore, such an honorary secretary would not be bound by the provisions of Rule 129(2).
If an appointment is contrary to the rules, any claim to said office cannot be sustained, and the appointee should no longer be treated as holding such office.
Moreover, the appointment of the second respondent was bad in law, and so the enforcement committee meeting called before it was even constituted is quashed.
Finally, only the secretary appointed under Rule 126 could have convened a meeting of the council and its committees, making the notice issued by him quashed.
