Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorized Occupant) Act, 1971, Section 4 - Official accommodation - Penal rent - SBI Circular dated 19121995 authorizes two months for vacating official accommodation, with provision for extension to six months - Penal rent is only applicable after eight months - Employees are allowed reasonable time to arrange for alternative accommodation.
