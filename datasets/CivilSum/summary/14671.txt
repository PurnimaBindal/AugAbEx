Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rules 7 and 8 - Show cause notice - Minor Penalty - Stoppage of two annual grade increments with cumulative effect - Representation - Opportunity of hearing - The punishing authority considered the representation and did not proceed under rule 7.
They decided that a minor punishment under rule 8 would suffice for the situation.
A show cause notice was issued as to why the minor penalty should not be imposed.
The punishment was inflicted on the individual and upon appeal, the Appellate Authority disposed of the case after affording an opportunity of hearing to the petitioner.
There was no violation of principles of natural justice and the actions of the respondents were in accordance with the Rules.
