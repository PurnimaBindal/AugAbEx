The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, and the Tea Act of 1953's Section 3(1) provide for promotion of employees.
The Central Government's Office Memorandum from February 8th, 2002 applies, but only if relevant rules are amended.
A petitioner was selected for a post based on merit-cum-seniority, and when this is the criterion for promotion, seniority loses its significance.
Thus, reversion of the petitioner from the promotional post and substitution with respondent No7 based on seniority alone cannot be justified.
The authority that issued the impugned order acted illegally, and the order has been quashed.
