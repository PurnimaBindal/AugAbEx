Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner had been assessed at 40% disability by two Medical Boards.
The medical advisor who had reduced the disability did not examine the petitioner.
It has been held that the opinion of the advisor cannot be given precedence over that of the Medical Board, unless there is evidence to the contrary.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to receive disability pension benefits at a rate of 40%.
