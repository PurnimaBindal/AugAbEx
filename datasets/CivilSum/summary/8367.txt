According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, an ex parte award can be set aside if a petitioner can prove that they were not properly served with summons.
In this case, a petitioner filed an application for setting aside an award, claiming that they were not properly served with summons, but this contention was proven to be incorrect.
Additionally, the petitioner failed to show sufficient cause for their non-appearance, and the Labour Court was therefore justified in passing the ex parte award.
