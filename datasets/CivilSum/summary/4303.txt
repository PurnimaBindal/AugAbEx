Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Abandonment/Termination/Retrenchment - The workman was terminated due to his abandonment of the job without giving any intimation.
This amounts to termination and is considered as retrenchment under Section 2(oo) of the Act.
The compliance with the provisions of Section 25-F is required to be ensured.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Reinstatement - Back wages - If the provisions of Section 25-F are not complied with, the termination of services cannot be upheld and the workman must be given reinstatement along with continuity of service.
The workman must also be awarded a lump sum of Rs. 30,000/- as back wages for the period he remained out of job until the date of his reinstatement.
[Paras 8 and 9]
