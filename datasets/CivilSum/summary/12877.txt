Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 and 3 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 13(2) - Mercy petition - There are no provisions for filing a mercy petition or representation under the Rules after appeal and revision - Filing such a petition would not extend the limitation for challenging orders passed by the competent authority in accordance with the Rules - A writ petition filed after an unexplained inordinate delay would be dismissed.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Department proceedings - Dismissal from service - Quantum of punishment - The "Wednesbury" principle states that a decision will be deemed unreasonable if it is based on irrelevant material or considerations or ignores relevant material, leading to an absurd conclusion that no sensible person could have reached.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental and Criminal proceedings - Acquittal in criminal proceedings - Even if the employee is acquitted in criminal proceedings, if the competent authority holds a proper inquiry in accordance with the Rules and records findings of guilt against the employee, subsequent acquittal in criminal proceedings does not invalidate the punishment order.
