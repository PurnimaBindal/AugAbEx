Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - The son of a deceased government servant who was a minor when his father passed away applied for a compassionate appointment after becoming an adult - However, his application was dismissed as appointments cannot be sought more than 10 years after the death of the breadwinner.
This decision was supported by cases from 1994(3) SCT 174 (SC) and 1996(2) SCT 778 (SC).
