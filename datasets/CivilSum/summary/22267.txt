Recruitment and Promotion Rules of Class II Posts (Gazetted) in the School and Inspection Cadre, in the Department of Education, 1980 - Promotions - There was a dispute between Headmasters and School Lecturers regarding promotional posts, including promotions to the posts of Class II Officers and further promotions to the posts of Deputy Directors Education and Joint Directors - Subsequently, the State Government began entering into ad hoc agreements with employees without proper authorization - These agreements, with the exception of those creating additional posts of Deputy Director of Education, were invalidated - The State Government was instructed to follow the 1980 Rules for promotions to Class II Officers and establish a common seniority list for such officers.
