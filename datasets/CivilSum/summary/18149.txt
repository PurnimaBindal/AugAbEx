Constitution of India Articles 226 and 227 - Removal - Reinstatement on appeal - The petitioner seeks to treat the interim period as on duty - The order of removal was passed without affording opportunities for hearing, show cause notice/charge-sheet/taking reply or holding enquiry - The petitioner was not exonerated from the charge - The intervening period from date of removal till reinstatement shall be deemed to be as on duty for all intents and purposes.
