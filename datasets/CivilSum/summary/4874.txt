Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.-II, Rule 22(b) in relation to Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India - Criminal and departmental proceedings against an employee acquitted of corruption - An honorable acquittal recorded by the criminal court, even if granted by the benefit of doubt, must be respected - The employee cannot be penalized by forfeiting his benefits of pay and allowances or by initiating fresh departmental proceedings on the same charges and evidence - Before a retired employee can be prosecuted, a grave misconduct or great financial loss caused by him has to be established - If the proceedings were not initiated before the retirement, they cannot be initiated afterwards.
[Paras 5, 7 & 8]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.I, Rule 73 and Rule 22 of Vol.II in relation to Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India - Suspension of a retired/retrenched employee accused in criminal and departmental proceedings after acquittal - The employer cannot treat the suspension period as non-duty period to forfeit the employee's pay and allowance - The authority has to follow the comprehensive procedure laid down in Rule 73 to determine how the period of suspension is to be treated - After retrenchment, the employer-employee relationship comes to an end, and the authority has no jurisdiction to proceed against a retrenched employee.
[Paras 9 & 10]
