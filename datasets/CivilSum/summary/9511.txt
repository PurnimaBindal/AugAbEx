Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Service (Appointment and Confirmation) Rules, 1979, Rule 5(6) - Confirmation - Seniority - Departmental examination - Passing of departmental examination is a pre-condition for confirmation - Private respondent granted higher pay without passing the examination - Petitioners who passed the examination paid lower pay - Petitioners entitled to confirmation and higher emoluments than private respondents - Petitioners' position in gradation list cannot be lower than that of private respondents - Seniority determined from date of initial appointment, not automatic or fortuitous circumstance.
[Paras 2 and 3]
