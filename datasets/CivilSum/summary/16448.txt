According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, Rule 17 of the Punjab Department of Public Works (Building & Roads) Junior Engineers (Class-III) Service Rules, 1993, and Rule 5(vii) of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, the appellate authority is not authorized to issue an original punishment order.
Any punishment order issued by an appellate authority would be legally invalid.
