Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1221, 162 - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal - Appellant disputed the order of discharge on the ground of being punitive - Contention that no inquiry was conducted and provisions of Article 311 of Constitution were not complied with - Respondents argued that the services were terminated under Rule 1221 due to acts of misconduct - Learned Single Judge dismissed the Petition - Contention that even though the order does not contain express words casting stigma, the same should be treated as punitive based on finding of guilt - Held, Learned Single Judge overlooked the fact that before passing the order, an inquiry was conducted into the allegation of misconduct and a show cause notice proposing dismissal was also given - Hence, it is sufficient to draw an inference that the termination order is given by way of punishment - Order quashed being punitive and passed in non-compliance of Rule 162 and Article 311 of Constitution - Appeal allowed accordingly.
