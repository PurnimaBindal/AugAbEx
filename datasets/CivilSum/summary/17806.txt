Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Dismissal of probationer during probation period or for failure to complete probation period satisfactorily - Dismissal not based on work and conduct during probation being unsatisfactory or any extraneous factor - Termination order issued as per the terms of appointment letter - Order not punitive and deemed legal by the court - Petition rejected.
