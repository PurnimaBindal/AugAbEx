The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Section 25-B and 25-N(6), covers lay off and voluntary retirement schemes.
In this case, the respondents were casual employees of a Government Company seeking the benefits of a voluntary retirement scheme that had been agreed upon between the company and trade unions.
Although the respondents were casual employees and not covered by the scheme, the Government decided to pay them an ex-gratia amount of Rs. 5000/-.
Despite this, the respondents are not entitled to the benefits of the scheme.
