Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Rules, 1978, Rule 3(4) - Competence of Enquiry Officer - According to the rules, only the managing committee has the power to appoint an enquiry authority, but if the managing committee decides not to do it themselves, then they can appoint someone else - The grounds of respondent were that the enquiry officer was not appointed by the managing committee - No record of the order appointing an enquiry officer - When rules are violated, the order of termination cannot be valid - No legitimate reason to interfere with the District Judge's order.
