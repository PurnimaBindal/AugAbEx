The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay scale revision - A unified cadre, including school and college lecturers, and a joint seniority list were considered.

The court held that the state's action, which denies equality of opportunity in employment under the state, was arbitrary and ex facie violative of Article 16 of the Constitution.
