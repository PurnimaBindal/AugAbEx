The Bihar Chaukidari Manual, along with Govt. memo No. 359 dated 17190, and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, fixed the cut-off date as 1190 for the Govt.'s declaration of Chaukidars and Dafadars as class IV employees of the State Govt.
This decision was deemed justified.
[Para 7]
The Govt.'s policy decision to classify Chaukidars and Dafadars as class IV employees led to multiple benefits for them, including better service conditions and pay scales.
The petitioner cannot challenge the conditions contained in Govt. memo No. 359 after benefiting from its monetary and other advantages.
[Paras 8 and 9]
