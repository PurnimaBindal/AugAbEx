The petitioner is seeking appointment to the post of Headmaster, which they are qualified for according to the Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Rule 44(1), as well as Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India.
However, the management argues that the petitioner cannot claim such appointment since they have permanently relinquished their claim for promotion.
The court held that there cannot be permanent relinquishment to the post of Headmaster and the management's stand cannot be sustained.
The respondents are directed to pass orders regarding the petitioner's claim.
