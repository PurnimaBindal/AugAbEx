Punjab Forest Subordinate Service (Executive Section) Rules 1944, rules 12 and 13 - Seniority - Fixation of seniority of members of service - The government has been fixing the seniority of members of the service based on the merit list created by the training college upon completion of training by candidates.
However, the criterion used by the government has been criticized as wrong and baseless.
On the other hand, the seniority of service members established in accordance with Rule 13 is exceptional.
