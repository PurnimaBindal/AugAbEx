Chandigarh Co-operative Financing Institutions Service Rules, Rule 3 - Appointment - Creation of posts - Approval of Registrar - Only the Bank's Board has the authority to determine the number of substantive posts and must secure approval from the Registrar Co-operative - Any appointments made by the MD without approval for such posts are deemed illegal.
[Paras 5 & 7] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Chandigarh Co-operative Financing Institutions Service Rules, Rule 3 - Appointments - Selection - Advertisement of posts - Any available vacancies for public employment must be advertised or requisitions sent to the Employment Exchange to allow eligible candidates to apply and ensure that the best candidates are selected - Any appointments made by the MD without following such procedure are deemed wholly illegal, violating both Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and basic principles of fairness.
[Paras 5 to 8] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural Justice - Probation - Termination during probation - In cases where no sanctioned posts are available, termination of employment during probationary periods is allowed, but probationary periods cannot be allowed to lapse into confirmed employment - Orders for termination without notice or hearing in line with the appointment order are deemed valid.
[Para 8] D. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Discrimination - Appointments - Illegal appointments - Terminating illegal appointments will not be challenged solely on the grounds of discrimination, even as other similarly appointed persons are retained - Repeating a wrong does not make it right and should not be allowed.
[Para 9] E Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Illegal appointments - Orders cannot prejudice those not listed in the petition challenging illegal appointments.
[Para 9] F Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Chandigarh Co-operative Financing Institutions Service Rules, Rule 3 - Sanction of posts - Approval - Termination of persons appointed without sanction and approval of posts - Any subsequent creation, sanction, or approval of posts by competent authorities will not retroactively invalidate terminations before such posts exist.
