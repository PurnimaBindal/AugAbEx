Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 11 and 11-A - Misconduct/Enquiry - In case a workman is dismissed on the grounds of misconduct without a proper enquiry, then the order will only relate to the date of the Labour Court's award.
However, if the workman is found guilty of misconduct, he will be entitled to full back wages till the date of the Labour Court's award, but would not be eligible for any retrenchment compensation.
The decisions of AIR 1980 SC 1896 and AIR 1990 SC 2174 have been followed.
