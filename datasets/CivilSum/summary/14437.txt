Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1980, Rules 4(2) & (3) - Charge sheet - According to the Rules, the Managing Committee must draw up the charges and cannot leave it to an individual - The Managing Committee must pass orders to issue a charge sheet, while it can be served by the ministerial staff - Any charge not discussed by the Committee in the meeting cannot be included in the charge sheet - Issuing such an individual charge sheet is against Rules 4(2) & (3), and can be quashed.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Bias - It is a fundamental principle that impartiality is necessary in judgment - When there is a conflict between one's interest and duty, that person cannot participate in the proceedings - Witnesses cannot sit in judgment and hold whether the charges were approved by the Managing Committee - This is a clear case of bias, and the person being charged has the right to be apprehensive about it.
