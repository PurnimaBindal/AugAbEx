Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Delay and laches - Petitioner was superseded on 2591985, and his representation against the same was rejected on 9.10.1986.
He filed the present writ petition in May 1988, when he was about to retire.
However, the petition was dismissed as being belated and on account of delay and laches.
Furthermore, the petitioner did not satisfy the requirement of having 70% good or better category reports during the 10 years preceding his promotion.
