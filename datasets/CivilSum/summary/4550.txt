According to Section 4-A(3) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, if there is a default in depositing the compensation amount awarded by the Commissioner, a penalty of 50% of the awarded amount is imposed along with interest.
The Insurance Company in this case was not held liable to pay the penalty of Rs. 46,400/-, but only liable to pay the compensation amount of Rs. 92,841/- and Rs. 24,128/- towards interest.
