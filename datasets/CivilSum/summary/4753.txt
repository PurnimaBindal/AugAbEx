A.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Syndicate Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 4 - Stopping of increment with cumulative effect - Imposing the accumulation of withholding of increments of pay constitutes a major penalty, which requires a regular inquiry to be held in advance - This withholding results in a reduction to a lower stage in a time-scale of pay and falls within the scope of Regulation 4(e) of major penalty - The authority cannot impose a major penalty under the guise of passing an order for minor penalty - Kulwant Singh Gill v. State of Punjab, 1990(6) SLR 73 : 1991(2) SCT 30 (SC) lays down the law on this matter.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Syndicate Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 - Regulation 4 - Stoppage of increment with cumulative effect - Rule provides for the competent authority to order the stoppage of increments to be done with or without cumulative effect - The actual effect of the order must be observed - If the stoppage of increments results in a decrease in an employee's pay to a lower stage in the time scale of the post held by him, the penalty prescribed under Regulation 4(e) will be deemed to have been imposed.
