Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Deputation/Lien - An employee on temporary promotion through deputation must maintain his lien on his parent department's substantive post until it is specifically terminated; he cannot claim any seniority in his deputation department.
One cannot hold two substantive positions simultaneously.
Regardless, he will receive all rights and promotions due in his parent department in accordance with regulations.
