The Uttar Pradesh Water Supply and Sewerage Act, 1945, Sections 15, 31(1) 37, 89 and 71, as well as the Uttar Pradesh Jal Nigam Engineers (Public Health Branch) Service Regulations, 1978, Regulation 31, relate to the age of retirement of employees.
The appointed date for the purposes of the Act was 18-6-1975, and the service conditions of the employees would be the same as those applicable to the employees of the State Government under the rules.
The State Government enhanced the age of superannuation of government servants from 58 years to 60 years, which would equally apply to the employees of the Nigam.
If an employee has not been allowed to continue after completing the age of 58 years by virtue of erroneous decision taken by the Nigam, they would be entitled to payment of salary for the remaining period up to the age of 60 years.
