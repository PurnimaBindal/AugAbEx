Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3 and the Constitution of India, Article 227, a claim for compensation was allowed by the Respondent in an ex-parte proceeding.
However, it has been contended that the Respondent did not implead the insurer even though the vehicle was insured, and that the Commissioner did not consider the aspect of the vehicle being insured when rejecting the Petitioner's prayer.
It has been held that the Petitioner's plea of not being served cannot be ignored, as the Petitioner's liability is to be indemnified by the insurer due to a valid policy.
As a result, the matter has been directed to be reheard and the petition has been disposed of accordingly.
