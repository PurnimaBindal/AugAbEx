According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Compassionate Appointments are given to those in financial crisis due to the sudden death of an earning family member, without the right to a particular post.
In a case where a petitioner's father, a DSP, died during service, the petitioner was given compassionate appointment as "Head Constable."
The petitioner objected, claiming that similarly placed private respondents were given compassionate appointments as Inspector and ASI.
However, it was held that the appointment of the petitioner was appropriate and in accordance with the law, as only posts in class III and IV can be offered on compassionate grounds after examining the financial condition.
Furthermore, claims of appointment at the rank of ASI or above as a matter of right and not to relieve financial difficulties are not legitimate.
If some private respondents were wrongly appointed, it would not justify committing another wrong.
[Paras 5, 6, 10, 13]
