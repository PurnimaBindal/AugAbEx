To understand the legislative intent, Courts can take into consideration the ostensible purpose and object of legislature because a mere mechanical interpretation of the words has the potential to render the legislation otiose.

The requirements of Section 3(d) of the Dentists Act, 1948 are quite liberal and only demand the existence of one or more Universities in a state that grants a recognized dental qualification.
C. The doctrine of 'reading down' must be applied to interpret Section 3(d) of the Act to equate the Academic Committee of AIIMS with the Senate of a University, and the Governing Body as the Court of the University for the purposes of Section 3(d).
D The Council had acted in haste and adopted a narrow approach while construing Section 3(d) of the Act.
The communication by the Council was contrary to the mandate and spirit of the Govt.'s letter Dated 11072005, and thus, the action of the Council cannot be sustained.
E. To treat AIIMS as a deemed University, the doctrine of reading down needs to be applied and AIIMS's Academic Committee needs to be equated with the Senate of a University and its Governing Body as the Court of the University for the purpose of Section 3(d) of the Dentists Act, 1948.
