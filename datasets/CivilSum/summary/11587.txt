Constitution of India, Article 16 - Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Act, 1930, Section 6 - Regularisation - Petitioner appointed on a temporary basis against a permanent vacancy - Seeks regularization - Respondents argue that the Petitioner was not appointed following recruitment rules, therefore regularization cannot be granted - Petitioner has completed 19 years of service - The Recruitment Rules, notifications, and Government circulars cannot be interpreted to mean that authorities have no obligation when sanctioned posts remain vacant for several years - Authorities must fulfill their obligation and cannot argue that irregularities are not allowed by law - Failure to regularize the petitioner's service not only deprives him of his employment benefits but also exposes him to the risk of losing his job at a later stage in life - The authorities are ordered to regularize the petitioner's service.
