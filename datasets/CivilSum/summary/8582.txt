According to Rule 36 of Rajasthan State Road Transport Employee Standing Orders from 1965, a conductor was found carrying ten passengers without a ticket and a full enquiry was held resulting in a punishment of two annual grade increments being imposed after the report from the enquiry officer.
The punishment was challenged in Labour Court and resulted in a modified award, but the Corporation was dissatisfied and filed instant writ petitions.
The Labour Court assumed that a copy of the enquiry report was mandatory under the Standing Orders before imposing punishment which was not done, but it was held that the petitioner needed to prove how he was prejudiced as such averment was not valid.
