Kurukshetra University Calendar, Volume I, 1989, Statute 29 - General Rules for Examination, Clause 4.1 - Diploma in Computer Application - The petitioner's result was withheld pending an inquiry into their complaint against the Chairman of the Department.
However, the allegations in the complaint do not violate moral principles and the action taken by the Vice-Chancellor was not authorized by the rules.
As a result of the court's ruling, the petitioner's result was declared pass, and the proceedings against them were quashed.

Kurukshetra University Calendar, Volume I, 1989, Statute 29 - General Rules for Examination, Clause 4.1 - The exercise of power under Statute 29(c) requires that the person have been conferred a degree or a Diploma which is required to be withdrawn, and that they stand convicted of a serious offense according to the academic council's opinion.
Neither of these requirements is satisfied in the petitioner's case.
The complaint against the Chairman of the Department does not qualify as an immoral act and the action taken by the Vice-Chancellor is not authorized by the rules.
The proceedings against the petitioner were quashed.
