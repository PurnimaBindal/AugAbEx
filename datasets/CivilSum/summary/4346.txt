The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(j), 10, and 25-F state that Industrial Development Centres offer technical guidance, testing, and certification for product evaluation using national and international standards.
Consequently, Industrial Development Centre is regarded as an industry.
The illegal termination of service, violating Section 25-F, mandates reinstatement without back wages, as per the order.
