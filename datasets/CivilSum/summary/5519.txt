Constitution of J. and K., Article 126 corresponding to Article 311 of the Constitution of India - J & K Civil Services Regulations (Volume - I), Article 108-b(ii) - Termination of services - Reinstatement - Back wages - Pecuniary benefits - Denial of - The terminated Constables in the Police Department were reinstated in service but without any pecuniary benefits after no evidence was provided to support the allegations of negligence against them.
The Impugned order debarring the petitioners from claiming pecuniary benefits was set aside.
