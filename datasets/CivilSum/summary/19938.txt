As per Section 18(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a dispute relating to dearness allowance is pending and employees opt for voluntary retirement, and the unit is subsequently closed, the pending dispute cannot be considered settled and still survives.
Therefore, the High Court's order that all disputes were settled is erroneous.
The closure of the unit or voluntary retirement of all workmen does not automatically end a validly pending industrial dispute unless the employer can prove that the grievance was included and settled in the agreement.
