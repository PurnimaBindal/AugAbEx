Constitution of India, Articles 112(3) 221, Second schedule, Part D, Para 11(b)(i) - High Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1954, Section 2(1)(c)(i) - Pensionery Benefits - A High Court Judge who retired in 1981 and then served in other positions like commission of inquiry and Commissioner of Payments until 1988, was determined to only be eligible for a pension for the period of service rendered as a High Court Judge which constituted a charge to the consolidated fund of India.
Any subsequent service rendered according to orders by the state government would not be charged to the consolidated fund.
