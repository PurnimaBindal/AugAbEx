Haryana Education Rules, 2003, Rule 91(5) - Medical allowance - Teachers in privately aided schools claiming parity with government college teachers for medical allowance - Rule 91(5) clarifies that leave encashment, LTC, bonus, and medical reimbursement benefits are at the discretion of the managing committee and the State cannot release any grant-in-aid on that account - The observations in Champa Devi's case (2002)10 SCC 78 are in line with Rule 91(5) - Therefore, teachers and science masters working in privately managed schools cannot claim medical allowance as per the circular that only grants a fixed medical allowance to Haryana Government employees.
