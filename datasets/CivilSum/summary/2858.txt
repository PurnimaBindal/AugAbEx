The Indian Forest Services (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1966, Regulation 5 states that the committee shall consider all relevant assessments in the ACRs of the candidates for the purpose of giving them grading for promotion.
However, the committee is not required to consider the entire service record which is impractical.
The committee considered five years of record prior to the date of vacancies, and this action was not arbitrary or unreasonable.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
The courts cannot interfere with the assessment made by the Departmental Promotion Committee unless any malafide is made out.
Neither the Central Administrative Tribunal nor the High Court can sit as a Court of appeal over the selection made by the expert committee, as established in AIR 1990 SC 434.
