Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pension - Family pension - The Haryana Government Order dated 3111988 and Notification dated January 31, 1992 that prescribes a new method for fixing family pension is unfair and arbitrary to people who drew basic pay ranging from Rs. 1501/- to Rs. 7060/- because applying this method leads to a reduction of their family pension.
It is unjust for the rate of family pension to decrease if salaries are increased to meet inflation.
The Government is ordered to re-fix the family pension rates to avoid any reduction.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11]
