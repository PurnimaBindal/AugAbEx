The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Seniority - The Panchayat Samiti employees had a district cadre in which they could claim seniority within their own district - Respondent No. 3, who was initially appointed as a Clerk in Panchayat Samiti Guru Har Sahai, was a part of Ferozepur district even after Faridkot district was carved out - No option was given to employees before allocating the district, and it was clear from the affidavit that Respondent No. 3 had been submitting representations from time to time for his transfer to the district cadre Ferozepur - He was eventually transferred to Ferozepur after his representation - It was held that the respondent was entitled to seniority from his original date of appointment.
