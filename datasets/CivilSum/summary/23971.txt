Constitution of India, Article 226 - Uttar Pradesh Regularisation of Ad hoc appointments (on posts outside the purview of the Public Service Commission) Rules, 1979 - Illegal appointments - Appointments made by the District Judge found to be illegal/irregular made in gross violation to the Rules and procedure prescribed for that purpose - Regularisation Rules, 1979 do not aim to regularise the service of illegal and irregular recruits - Illegal appointees are not allowed to seek the discretionary jurisdiction of the Court under Article 226 of the Constitution.
