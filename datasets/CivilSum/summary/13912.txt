According to Aircraft Rules, 1937, Rule 160, a petitioner who passes all qualifying tests but submits an application for a license after two years of passing the test may have their application rejected on the basis of delay.
However, in this case, there is no evidence presented to show how the license could be declined on the grounds of a late application.
The authority is therefore directed to pass a fresh order and consider the claim of the petitioner while also showing the relevant basis for rejecting their claim.
