Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Sections 10(1)(2) and 24-B - Consumer Protection (Pb.)
Rules 1987, Rules 3(5) - The State Government is responsible for appointing the disciplinary and controlling authority of District Forum members.
The State Commission only has administrative and superintendent powers over District Forum members and cannot recommend disciplinary action or issue chargesheets.
The State Commission can propose action to the appointing authority regarding District Forum members.
[Paras 15, 20, 21, 22, 23, and 24]
Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Section 24-B - The State Commission has administrative and superintending powers over District Forum members in accordance with Section 24-B of the Act.
If a member of the adjudicating forum previously acted as an advocate for a party, the State Commission can withdraw their work and ask for an explanation.
No judge may hear a case in which they participated to the benefit of a party.
[Paras 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19]
