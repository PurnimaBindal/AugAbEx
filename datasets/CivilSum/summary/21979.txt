Demobilized Indian Armed Forces Personnel Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Technical (Medical and Engineering) Service Rules, 1968, Rule 3(e) - Seniority - Inter-se Seniority - Although respondents were allowed to count the period of service rendered in Army during emergency after joining in civil service according to Punjab Government circulars of 1964 and 1966, there is no provision in the statutory rules for giving seniority to individuals who joined after the emergency was lifted and therefore cannot be included within the definition of 'Released Indian Armed Forces Personnel' in Section 3(e) of the 1968 Rules.
