Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Sections 25-F and 25-H Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 77 - Retrenchment compensation not paid simultaneously at the time of retrenchment - The workman is instructed to collect the retrenchment compensation from the Accountant, but the Accountant does not pay the same to the workman, nor is there any attempt made to send the amount by post - No presumption can be drawn that the workman did not accept the retrenchment compensation - Furthermore, the seniority list was not displayed on the notice board as required under Rule 77 for the purpose of Section 25-H - As there was no proper compliance of the provisions of the law, the retrenchment was illegal - The workman is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages - Therefore, the award of the labour court was quashed.
