Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Lecturer in University - Maternity Leave after joining - Deferment of joining date - Petitioner, who was earlier deemed "temporary unfit" due to pregnancy at the time of joining, relied upon a circular by Haryana State stating that it is not compulsory to label a pregnant candidate as "Temporarily unfit" before appointment against posts without elaborate training - University's position not justified in light of the aforementioned instructions - Petitioner entitled to all consequential benefits and treated as if she had joined service on 22704 instead of 18102004.
