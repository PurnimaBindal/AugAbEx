The appellant did not produce witnesses to prove the composite charge and confession letters, claiming that he issued delivery orders without adequate security under compulsive situation as per his superior's directives.
It was held that had those witnesses been examined, the petitioner could have been absolved of charges by proving his stand.
Hence, the proceeding cannot be sustained since it was conducted in violation of the principles of natural justice and settled principles of law.
[Paras 16, 18 and 21]
The punishment of an officer who was dismissed from service has been modified.
In view of the violation of the principles of natural justice in the disciplinary proceedings, the proceedings have been set aside.
Since the officer has reached the age of superannuation, it would not be just to remand the matter back to the authorities at this stage.
A direction has been given to grant 50% backwages along with pecuniary benefits as if the officer had been in service.
The punishment has been modified accordingly.
