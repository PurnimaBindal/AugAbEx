In accordance with Article 222 of the Constitution of India, the power of transfer is exercised to serve a public purpose and promote "public interest" to improve the administration of justice throughout the country.
The Judges' Case-II held that this inherent guideline is to ensure that there is no possibility of arbitrariness while exercising the power of transfer in accordance with the recommendation of the Chief Justice of India.
However, judicial review is not excluded.
[Paras 13, 15, 16, and 18] B.
The validity of a transfer of a High Court Judge under Article 222 can be challenged only by the transferred judge himself and not by anyone else.
If any other court in the country must decide on such a matter, it would consider the option of requesting the Supreme Court to withdraw the case to this Court for decision to avoid any embarrassment.
