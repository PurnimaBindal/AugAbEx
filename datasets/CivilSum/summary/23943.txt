According to the Navy Act, 1957, Sections 11 and 16- Navy (Ceremonial, Conditions of Service and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations, 1963, Regulations 268(2) & (3) - Service Pension and Gratuity Pension Regulations (Navy), 1964 - Navy Order No. STR 17/94 - Contract Act, Sections 18, 19 and 30, a sailor who is re-engaged in accordance with the Navy Regulations is not entitled to claim discharge during the period of re-engagement.
The entitlement to seek discharge at the end of the period of engagement is also subject to exceptions.
A sailor cannot claim release as a matter of right and the authority is not bound to accept the request.
On the other hand, if an option for re-engagement was given misled by misrepresentation on the part of the authority, the sailor is entitled to withdraw the option and seek cancellation of re-engagement and release.
Re-engagement for a period more than 3 years prescribed by Naval order is void and the sailor is entitled to seek his discharge unless he is re-enrolled in accordance with Regln.
268(3)(b).
Furthermore, discrimination in refusing to discharge by the authority while allowing others amounts to discrimination and Navy Order 17/94 cannot override the Act or regulations.
