In accordance with the Constitution of India, Article 311 and the State Bank of India (Supervisory Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50(5), it is imperative that natural justice is observed during disciplinary proceedings against Bank employees.
The Disciplinary Authority cannot rely on material that has not been provided to or shown to the employee in question.
The order may be invalidated if it is shown that the reliance was not only on irrelevant material but also on material that could not have been considered.
