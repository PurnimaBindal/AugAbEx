According to Section 5(4)(a) of the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, the Chairman of the Tribunal may be either a Judicial or an Administrative Member.
However, if the Chairman is a Judicial Member, he will act as a Judicial Member on the Bench, and if he is an Administrative Member, he will act only as an Administrative Member.
The Chairman can act as such on different Benches at different locations as well.
