The petitioner for the post of Assistant Teacher (Physical Training) in terms of service conditions claims confirmation, however, the appointment letter only specified a temporary period of one year and then a further period of one year.
The absence of salary, allowances, initial salary, and retirement benefits in the appointment letter leads to the conclusion that deemed confirmation cannot be inferred, and the petitioner has no right to be appointed permanently, having been appointed against a temporary vacancy.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226, along with the Code of Regulations for Anglo Indian and Other Listed Schools, 1993, Clause 21 are all pertinent documents in this case.
