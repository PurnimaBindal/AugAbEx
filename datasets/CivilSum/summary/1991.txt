Punjan Police Rules, 1934, Rule 163 - Constitution of India - Articles 226, 227 - Head Constable - Confirmation - The petitioner faced two criminal cases and was not confirmed as Head Constable while his juniors were confirmed.
The petitioner was later acquitted of both criminal cases, but his representation for confirmation was denied on the grounds that an appeal against his acquittal was pending.
The department then initiated an enquiry against the petitioner, but a civil court granted an injunction to stop the enquiry.
Although the petitioner's relief in the present petition was not granted, the respondents were directed to reconsider the petitioner's case in accordance with Rule 163.
