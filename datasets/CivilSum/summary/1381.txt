Clause X of Letters Patent and Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 pertain to the right of casual laborers to receive wages at the rate they were being paid before termination of services.
According to Section 17-B, employees are entitled to full wages last drawn by them, inclusive of any maintenance allowance, until the proceedings against the award are disposed of.
This entitlement ends only if the worker secures employment elsewhere.
The impugned order of the Single Judge has been set aside and the respondent must receive wages at the rate at which he was being paid prior to his termination.
