The Constitution of India - Articles 226/227, 14 and 16 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Seniority - Respondent No. 2 became senior to the petitioner after obtaining benefit of military service through Civil Writ Petition - The petitioner challenged the relief given to respondent No. 2 through the present Writ Petition - Held, petitioner cannot challenge the judgment of this Court by filing a Writ Petition - Remedy open to him is either to file a Review Petition before the High Court or to file a special leave petition before the Supreme Court.
