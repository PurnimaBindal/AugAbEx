Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Government Employees (Conduct) Rules, 1966, Rules 3 and 11 - Official secrets/official documents - The act of producing notings from departmental files in court by a Government Servant is deemed improper and such notings do not create any right in favour of an employee.
Only orders passed by the competent authority can create a right.
[Para 10] B Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II P.W.D., Rules 1965, Rule 125 - Seniority - The seniority of the petitioner can only be counted in Chandigarh Administration as per the rules, and not in various organisations of Punjab Government, as claimed by the petitioner.
The benefit of earlier service can only be claimed in the parent department and not while on deputation with Chandigarh Administration.
