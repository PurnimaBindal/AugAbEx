The Industrial Tribunal has the power to interfere with the quantum of punishment under Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, but it must be exercised judiciously.
The Tribunal can only do so if it finds the punishment to be highly disproportionate to the degree of guilt and is not satisfied with the findings of the Management's inquiry.
[Para 20]
The Industrial Tribunal found the punishment of dismissal to be disproportionate, and substituted it with the stoppage of one year's increment.
The High Court was wrong to set aside the Tribunal's decision, as it had correctly appreciated the evidence and substituted the punishment in a fair and proper inquiry.
