Constitution of India, Article 136 - Examination - Result - Withholding of - Two Lakh applications for Upper subordinate service preliminary Examination - Provisional admission cards issued on the condition that if defects found in applications, the result will not be declared - Result of two applicants-respondents withheld due to found defects - High Court ordered to declare result without explanation - Respondents did not appear to defend High Court verdict - Judgment of High Court overturned as it was not necessary to send the case back for reconsideration.
