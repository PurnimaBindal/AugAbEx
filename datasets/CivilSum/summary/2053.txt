Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - MP Industrial Relations Act, 1960 - Misconduct - Conductor in M.P.
Roadways - The worker was terminated on the basis of conjectural findings that passengers could use the already issued tickets for other passengers.
The Labour Tribunal's findings must be rejected.
[Paras 14 and 15]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Superintending power of the High Court on all the subordinate courts - The High Court has remanded the case to the Labour Court with comments on the findings recorded by it, and the Labour Court cannot criticize the comments of the High Court.
[Paras 12, 13 and 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - The past record of service of the delinquent was commented upon and taken into consideration while upholding the punishment, and the petitioner was not informed.
Therefore, the orders must be quashed.
[Paras 15 and 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Legal representatives - Legal representatives of the employee can be brought on record and benefit granted to them in a petition for service benefits and reinstatement.
