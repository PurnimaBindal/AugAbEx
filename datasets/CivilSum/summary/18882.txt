Constitution of J. and K., Section 124 - J and K Civil Services Regulations, Schedule 1-C, Entry No. 98 - Transfer - The Major Head of Departments were delegated with power to transfer all officers having maximum pay scale exceeding Rs. 1000/- in year 1966.
As a result, Assistant Surgeons whose pay scale did not exceed Rs. 1000/- in year 1966 were covered.
With subsequent rules, revised pay scale had provisions to regulate the effect of revision of pay scale on delegated powers in respect of transfer.
Therefore, the Director has the power to order the transfer of Assistant Surgeons with a pay scale of Rs. 8000- 13500.
[Paras 10 and 11]
