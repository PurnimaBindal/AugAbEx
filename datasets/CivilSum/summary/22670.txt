Orissa Education (Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers and Members of Staff of Aided Educational Institutions) Rules, 1974 - Aided educational institution - Appointment - Non-approval by authorities - The respondent argues to have been appointed as the 'fourth peon' by the institution; however, the state officials have not approved the appointment, as it is beyond the prescribed standard.
The claim that the post is promotional and carries a higher pay scale is also made.
The management must move the authorities for the approval of the promotional appointment and simultaneously recommend the appointment to the Class IV post as per law, as the appointment to the post of 'Daftary' was to be made by promoting persons holding the Class IV post.
[Paras 11 and 12]
