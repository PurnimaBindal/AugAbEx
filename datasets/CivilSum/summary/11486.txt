According to Section 103 of the Constitution of J and K, Rule 1 of J. and K. Medical (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules (1970), and Regulation 65 of J and K Civil Service Regulations, the post of Chief Medical Officer can only be filled through promotion.
However, the petitioner, who was not eligible for the promotion to this post, was posted as Chief Medical Officer as a stop-gap arrangement.
The petitioner cannot seek mandate for his continuation against said post since stop-gap arrangement doesn't confer any rights.
[Paras 13 to 15]
