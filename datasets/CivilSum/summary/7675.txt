Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Voluntary abandonment of service - Absence from duty - Show cause notice issued to the employee - Order of termination not invalid despite no inquiry held or opportunity of hearing afforded - Failure to respond to notice and provide explanation forfeits plea of natural justice - Explanation and material must be submitted to authority in order for plea to be considered.
