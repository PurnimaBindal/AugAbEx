According to the rules, transfer orders cannot be disputed unless there is evidence of wrongful intent or if it is a punitive transfer.
The petitioner was originally appointed as a Research Assistant for a project located in Jammu and was transferred to Delhi on compassionate grounds because of her husband's medical problem.
She was later assigned to Jammu again because the HRD ministry wanted the project to be completed quickly.
The transfer order was not malicious, and no issues were found with the transfer order.
