Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Workman - Termination of service - Allegation of remaining absent and misbehaviour - Contentions regarding Section 11A of 1947 Act not being properly appreciated by labour Court - It was determined that the threatening attitude of the workman towards a senior officer could not be overlooked - Allowing such behaviour to go unpunished would cause discipline to collapse within the institution - Furthermore, the petitioner was found to have absented on 17 occasions between August to October 1981 - Thus, the punishment did not exceed what was appropriate.
