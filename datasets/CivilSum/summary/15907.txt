Constitution of India, Articles 14, 12 and 226 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertaking) Act, 1970, Section 9 - Special Allowances/Writ jurisdiction - The Central Government has not made any provisions for granting benefits to Bank employees that are available to Government employees - Service conditions of Nationalised Bank employees cannot be compared with Central Government employees - Writ of mandamus cannot enforce the payment of special allowance to Bank employees equal to that of Government employees.
