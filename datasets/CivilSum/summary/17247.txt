Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP Intermediate Education Regulations, Regulation 35 - Dismissal - Approval of - Manager issued a charge-sheet against the Principal - Committee of Management did not approve the charge-sheet - Dismissal order passed without approval can be voided - The petitioner had retired, preventing reinstatement - The petitioner is still entitled to all applicable benefits as if he had remained Principal until superannuation.
