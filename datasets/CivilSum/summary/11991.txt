According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II Part I, Rule 3.17-A(g), work charge employees are not entitled to pensionary benefits for their family members.
This is based on the ruling from the Apex court in Surji Devi's case, which stipulates that the service of a work charged employee can only be considered once they become regular.
Therefore, the petitioner, who opted to be a work charged employee and benefited from the extended service period, cannot now demand pension.
