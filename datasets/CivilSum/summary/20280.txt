There is a presumption in favor of the administration, that its powers are exercised in good faith and for the public benefit under Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Mala fides - It would be on individuals to prove malafide exercise of power by providing sufficient material - However, it is not an easy burden to discharge.

Allegations of bias towards a candidate were made against the Chairman of the selection committee because of his close relationship with the candidate and proximity to the Secretary under the Constitution of India Articles 14,16, and 226 - Selection - Mala fides - It was held that if such allegations were accepted, no administration would be safe.
