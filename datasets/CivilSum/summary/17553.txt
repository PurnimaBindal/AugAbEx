According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume-II, Rule 2(B), pension recovery can only be made if there is evidence of Govt. servant causing pecuniary loss to the Government.
A retired Govt. servant who was accused of taking a bribe of Rs. 500 while working as Asstt.
Registrar, Cooperative Societies, was allowed to retire voluntarily pending an inquiry.
The Government cannot reduce his pension due to misconduct unless there is a pecuniary loss to the Government.
[Paras 2 and 3]
