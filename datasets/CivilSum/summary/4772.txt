Per the Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, a permanent lecturer at a State Govt. aided college claimed retiral benefits with interest.
The High Court's judgment in KC Sharma's case directed the management of the college to pay the gratuity amount within one month, regardless of aid from the state, along with interest at a rate of 9%.
No further directions are necessary since Provident Fund and Contributory Provident Fund were already paid.
Any other retiral benefit due to the petitioner will be paid in accordance with KC Sharma's case judgment.
