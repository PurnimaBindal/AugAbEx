The Petitioner, who had suffered 26% disability due to an accident, was assigned light duty as the Incharge of the counter but retired on medical grounds on 10122002.
The Court held that the Government has the right to exempt certain establishments from Section 47(2) of the Persons with Disabilities Act, 1995, which deals with compulsory retirement.
The exemption had been granted to drivers and conductors of Roadways by notification dated 27.6.2005.
However, the exemption cannot be applied retrospectively to the detriment of the Petitioner who was already retired in 2002.
Therefore, the Respondents have been directed to reinstate the Petitioner with all consequential benefits and assign duties that are commensurate with the Petitioner's physical capability.
