The petitioner in a military case sought discharge to avoid charges, but the court determined it was not a discharge under Clause IV of Rule 13 of Army Rules, and the writ petition was dismissed.
The petitioner then filed a review application, alleging a mistake or error on the face, but the court found that none of the points raised or grounds taken in the review application satisfied the requirements of Order 47, Rule 1 of C.P.C. and the review application was dismissed.
