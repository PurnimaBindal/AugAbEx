According to Section 2(k) of the Punjab Lokpal Act, 1996, a member of a selection committee who is posted as the Dy.
Director (Establishment) office of the Director Public Instructions (Education) Punjab is not classified as a "Public Man."
Consequently, the jurisdiction of the Lokpal under the said Act does not extend to entertaining complaints against such committee members.
Therefore, any recommendations made by the Lokpal regarding such complaints will be unenforceable due to lack of jurisdiction.
