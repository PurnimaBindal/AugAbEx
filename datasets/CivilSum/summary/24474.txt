Rajasthan Panchayat Samiti and Zila Parishad Act, 1959 - Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Rules 1996 - Termination of service - Post of Primary School Teacher - Qualification - Appointment on temporary basis - To be considered qualified, teachers are required to possess a Short Training Certificate - The respondent in this case did not possess the necessary qualification - Merely being allowed to continue in service due to an interim order from the High Court does not validate their claim to the post or render the order of termination bad in law - The court held that the employee had no legal right to continue in service - Therefore, the order of termination was not illegal.
[Para 13] B.
Termination of service - Qualification - Without the required educational qualification, an employee cannot claim a right to continue in the post - The fact that an interim order was passed by the High Court does not confer a legal right upon the employee - Citing 2006(1) SCT 610.
