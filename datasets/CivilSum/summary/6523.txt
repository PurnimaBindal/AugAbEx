According to the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 103, Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 141, Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 19, and Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 49(2)(b), an individual with 10 years of qualifying service is entitled to a grant of pension in accordance with Rule 49(2)(b) of CCS (Pension) Rules.
Pension cannot be denied to a person whose resignation was accepted before retirement age or after the necessary years of service had been completed.
The rejection of the petitioner's claim for pension has been set aside, and the respondents have been instructed to reconsider the petitioner's case.
[Paras 19 and 20]
The Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 103, states that a claim for payment of pension is recurring every month, and therefore the plea of delay is not sustainable.
