Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 5.32 (a), (b) (c) and 16.12 - Pension - Voluntary retirement - Ex-employees must complete a minimum of 10 years of service to receive a pension under Rule 6.12 - Rule 5.32 is a general rule - The amount of pension varies for employees retiring after 10 years of service - Rule 6.12 is a specific rule that takes precedence when a special situation arises.
[Paras 11, 12] B Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 5.32 (a), (b) (c) and 16.12 - Pension - Limitation - Delay - Pension claims cannot be time-barred as they are a recurring cause of action - However, arrears can only be claimed for up to 38 months.
