Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 310 - Age of retirement - Rules prescribing age of superannuation at 65 years - Amendment in the Rules - Age limit reduced from 65 to 60 - Determining age of retirement is the responsibility of the Government or employer - Court intervention only if arbitrary age fixed - Uniform criteria for all - Group II Service retirement at age 60 not unfair or arbitrary - Provides sufficient tenure - Amendment not violative of Article 14 - Rule-making power includes power to amend and revise.
