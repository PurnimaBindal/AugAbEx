The Punjab State Co-operative Milk Producers Federation Limited Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1980, Rule 2.8 (a) (ii) pertaining to recruitment, selection, and quashing is discussed in light of various Constitution of India Articles, including Article 16 and 226.
The respondents in question were recruited via direct appointment.
The request is made to quash the selection and issue directions to fill the vacancies on the basis of seniority.
However, as per the rule, vacancies were circulated internally.
Therefore, the question of violation of the quota rule does not arise.
It is held that the selection of respondents 3 to 9 is in conformity with Rule 2.8 (a)(ii) of the Punjab State Co-operative Milk Producers Federation Limited Service (Common Cadre) Rules.

The Punjab State Co-operative Milk Producers Federation Limited Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1980, Rule 2.8 (a) (ii) pertaining to appointment and selection is discussed in light of various Constitution of India Articles, including Article 16 and 226.
The selection is being sought to be quashed on the ground that seven and not four posts were filled up.
However, as per the rule, there is no provision for filling up the posts by promotion.
Therefore, the variation in number and filling up of seven posts does not give cause of action to the petitioners.
