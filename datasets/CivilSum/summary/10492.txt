The petitioner, who applied for appointment on compassionate grounds and was placed on the priority list of the government, is entitled to be considered for the appointment under the policy.
The government instruction on married dependents of the deceased not being entitled to compassionate appointment cannot be made retrospectively applicable.
Additionally, the post was available before the instructions came.
[Para 6]

Only the economic/financial condition of the family is justified and rational to achieve the object of compassionate appointment.
The criteria based on the status of married or unmarried is without any basis and has no nexus with the object of the scheme.
Clause 2(i) of the instructions is enough to serve the purpose, and Clause 2(iv) declared unconstitutional and struck down, as it violates Article 14 of the Constitution and is not in line with the directions of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in Umesh Kumar Nagpal's case.
[Paras 7 and 8]
