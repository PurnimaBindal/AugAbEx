Constitution of India, Article 226 - Cancellation of airport pass and identity card - Decision made by Director General of Civil Aviation and Bureau of Civil Aviation Security in the interest of airport or national security - It is not a vested right for petitioner to hold a pass as it can be cancelled or withdrawn in public interest.
