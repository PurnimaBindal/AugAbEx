Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulation 14(1) along with Circular No. 969/86 dated 17101986 - Suspension allowance - Dearness allowance - As per Regulation 14(1), an employee under suspension is eligible for allowances based on the current rates equivalent to those given to employees of the same grade as the said employee.
The disputed Circular No. 969/86, which froze the allowance rate as of the date of suspension, conflicts with the Regulations and has been annulled.
Therefore, the petitioner has a right to receive dearness allowance at the current rate of payment.
