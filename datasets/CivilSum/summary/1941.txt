Constitution of India, Article 16 - Tea Act, 1953, Section 31(2) - Promotion - Seniority - Respondent No. 1 was selected for the post of Assistant Accountant at Dubai Office of appellant No. 1 - Respondent No1's juniors were promoted to the post of Accountant subject to the seniority right of Respondent No. 1 - When Respondent No1 returned, he was asked to join duty as an Assistant Accountant - After he protested, he was promoted to the post of officiating Accountant and his name was included in the seniority list of Accountants - Learned single Judge held that Respondent No1 was entitled to the benefit of the next below rank since he was on deputation, and after reversion to the parent department, he was entitled to seniority over all his juniors in the promoted post - There is no question of upsetting any settled position as each of Respondent No1's juniors were well aware that their position in seniority list was subject to the seniority of Respondent No. 1.
