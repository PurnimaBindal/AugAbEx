Conduct Discipline and Appeal Rules, Rule 22(2) - Suspension - Criminal proceedings - If an employee is suspended due to ongoing criminal proceedings against them, the competent authority must reinstate them upon the termination of the proceedings.
However, if the authority decides to initiate disciplinary proceedings against the employee, they are allowed to suspend or continue the suspension already ordered without passing a fresh order.
If the charge-sheet already contains an order for continuation of suspension, this is sufficient compliance with Rule 22(2) of Conduct Rules.
