The Uttar Pradesh Palika (Centralised) Service Retirement Benefit Rules, 1981, Rule 3, Sub Rules (2) and (3) state that the only question to consider is whether these Rules can be applied retrospectively to employees who retired from Palika service before the Rules came into effect.
The appellant, who retired before the Rules were implemented, cannot claim the right to pension under these Rules based on the DS Nakara court decision and the fact that the Rules have no retrospective operation.
Therefore, the relief sought by the appellant cannot be granted.
