Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation of services/Temporary appointment - Termination - A petitioner served as a B.Ed.
teacher on a temporary or six-month basis but was terminated after four years of service.
They claim reinstatement and service regularisation based on the Arnod Panchayat Samiti's case, which was decided by the Division Bench of the Rajasthan High Court.
However, as the petitioner was not selected by a selection committee and did not remain in service during the writ petition's pendency, they cannot receive the benefit of the judgement.
