According to Punjab School Education Board (Matriculation Examination) Regulations, 1988, Reguln.
6 and National Open School Society, Rule 10(9), failed candidates have the right to re-appear as private candidates for the Matriculation Examination.
The non-mention of failure on the provisional certificate or mark sheet does not matter if the subject-wise result of failure is mentioned.
The granting of permission to re-appear depends on whether the candidate had failed or not, as determined by the mark sheet according to the rules.
The mark sheets issued by the Controller of Examinations under CBSE Rules should not be ignored for authenticity reasons.
Therefore, the petitioners should be granted permission to re-appear in the exam.
