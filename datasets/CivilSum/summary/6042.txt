The petitioner was suspended due to an investigation in a criminal case.
However, after being acquitted, the respondents have no grounds to maintain the suspension and initiate departmental action.
[Para 6]
If the disciplinary authority wishes to initiate a departmental enquiry after the petitioner's acquittal, they must pass a fresh suspension order.
This is based on the 1980(3) SLR 220 case.
[Para 5]
Despite being suspended in 1984 and acquitted in 1993, charge-sheet was served during the pendency of the writ petition.
The petitioner is entitled to all benefits, including pay, allowances, and increments.
The defence plea cannot be accepted, and the petitioner is reinstated.
[Paras 7 and 8]
