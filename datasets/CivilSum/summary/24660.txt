The Supreme Court upheld the conviction and dismissal of a petitioner from service as per Army Act, 1950 sections 52(a) and 63.
The petitioner had undergone imprisonment and was released on bail, subsequently acquitted by the High Court.
It was ruled that the balance sentence need not be served.
The appellate was only partially allowed.

An inquiry under Rule 22 of Army Rules was carried out before the Court Martial of a petitioner who was charged with theft.
