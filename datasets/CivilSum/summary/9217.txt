According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2-A, 2(k) and Section 10, a claim of subsistence allowance can be considered an Industrial Dispute only if it affects more than one worker.
An individual worker cannot refer to a labour court for such a claim except in cases of discharge, dismissal, termination, or retrenchment of service as defined under Section 2-A Hence, a civil suit may be initiated by a single workman in such a case.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
If a workman claims higher subsistence allowance as per the settlement agreement with management and the dispute does not concern the interpretation of the settlement or any award, a reference under Section 10 is not maintainable.
Therefore, a civil suit is not barred.
