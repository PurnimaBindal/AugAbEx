According to Section 34 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963, Section 45 of the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963, and Rule 28(8) of the West Bengal Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Govt. Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969, termination of a teacher's services requires prior approval of the Board.
However, despite this, the petitioner's services were terminated without such approval, leading to 26 years of ongoing litigation.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to damages in the form of three years' salary and allowances, but is not entitled to reinstatement as a private school teacher.
