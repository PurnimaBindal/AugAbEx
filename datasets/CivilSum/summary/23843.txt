Rajasthan University Teachers and Officers (Section for Appointment) Act, 1974, Sections 3, 5 and 6 - The Selection Committee selects candidates for appointment and also prepares a Reserve List.
Subsequently, any vacancies have to be filled from the Reserve List, and no fresh selections are made as long as the Reserve List is valid.
The argument that Reserve List only applies to vacancies resulting from non-joining of selected candidates is not valid.

Interpretation of Statutes - In matters relating to educational institutions, if there are two interpretations, the courts would usually accept the interpretation of such educational authorities to avoid disrupting the long-established actions and decisions taken by them.
