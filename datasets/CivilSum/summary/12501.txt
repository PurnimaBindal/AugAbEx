Constitution of India, Article 226 - Eligibility for examination of Post Graduation Course in Ophthalmology speciality - The maximum period of training for the award of a Post Graduate Degree is three calendar years as per the recommendations of Post Graduate Medical Education adopted by the Medical Council of India.
The candidate should attend a minimum of 80% of the training period.
The petitioner has completed training for a period of 2-1/2 years, fulfilling the prescribed norm and is eligible to appear in the examination.
The court directed the respondents to declare the result of MD Ophthalmology examination.
