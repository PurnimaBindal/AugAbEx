Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(iii)(v) - Discharge from service - Sepoy - Discharged by Commanding Officer - The competent authority for discharge under clause III(v) of Rule 13(3) is the Brigade/Sub Area Commander - Commanding Officer cannot initiate proceedings for discharge - Discharge without jurisdiction is illegal and void ab initio - Impugned order quashed - Petitioner entitled to be treated in service till completion of 10 years service with consequential benefits.

Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(iii)(v) - Discharge from service - Natural justice - When invoking discharge power under Rule 13(3)(iii)(v) of Army Rules, the concerned person must be given notice and opportunity to explain their position.
