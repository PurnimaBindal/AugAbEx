Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Service Rules Part I, Rule 55(4) - Promotion - Refixation of pay scale - Seniority - Length of service - Petitioners, who were appointed as direct-recruits LDC, were promoted to the position of UDC prior to the promotion of Respondent 7, who was initially appointed as Muharrir and subsequently promoted to the post of LDC.
However, the initial entry of Respondent 7 in service was prior to that of the petitioners, and as a result, Respondent 7 is enjoying a higher scale of pay due to longer length of service.
Consequently, Sub - Rule 4 of Rule 55 of the West Bengal Service Rules is triggered, and the state is required to fix the scale of pay of the petitioners by giving them the benefit of Rule 55(4).
