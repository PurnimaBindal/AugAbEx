Punjab National Bank [Employees] Pension Regulations 1995, Regulation 15 - Pensionary benefits - To avail pensionary benefits under section 15, qualifying service of 10 years is necessary - The petitioner does not have 10 years of service to their credit - Nonetheless, a minimum qualifying service is not arbitrary or discriminatory - Such a regulation does not violate Article 14 of the Constitution of India - The regulations are valid and consistent - [Paras 7 and 8].
