Constitution of India, Articles 226, 74(1), 74(2), 77 - Govt. of India (Transaction of Business) Rules 1961 - Public Interest Litigation (PIL) challenges appointment of the Chairman and Managing Director of Minerals and Metals Trading Corporation over alleged ulterior motives and undue favoritism - Appointment Committee of Cabinet (ACC) consists of the Minister concerned, Home Minister and Prime Minister, but there was no meeting of minds and specific clearance from Central Vigilance Commission was not obtained - The Prime Minister's supremacy in hire and fire of ministers does not extend to appointments requiring approval of ACC - Proposed appointment is quashed and ACC may re-consider if necessary - The scope of PIL and judicial review is explained.
