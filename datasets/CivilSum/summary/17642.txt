Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Unfair means - Disqualification - B.A.
Part I Examination - Petitioner claimed his papers would not contain any alleged printed matter and asserted he would be amongst the top 2/3 students in both the Punjabi subject and overall result if his paper was assessed - Petitioner argued that a charge of use of unfair means should only be established when proof is shown, and the bold stance taken by petitioner proved his honesty, rather than guilt - The punishment prescribed for those found guilty of using any unfair means must be suffered, however it cannot be applied if the student is innocent - Petitioner's claim of innocence convinced the court to set aside his punishment of disqualification from appearing in any University Examination for a period of two years and ordered the respondents to declare petitioner's result of B.A Part I Examination.
