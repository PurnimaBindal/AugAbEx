The Petitioner in the case of Disability Pension was discharged from Army Service due to medical disability.
Despite having served for over six years, the Petitioner was denied the pension on the grounds that the disability was not attributable to or aggravated by military service.
Additionally, the Petitioner did not reach the qualifying service of 15 years, having only served for seven years and ten days.
However, it was found that the Petitioner was physically fit during enlistment in 1965 and his disability was only noticed in 1976.
Therefore, the disability suffered by the Petitioner is attributable to the Army service.
As a result, the Respondents were ordered to release the disability pension to the Petitioner.
