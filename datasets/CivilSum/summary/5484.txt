Under the Employees' State Insurance Act and Factories Act, the ESI Inspector's report revealed that ten people were employed by the Cafe and that a powered grinder was used to make pickles, chutneys, dhals, and other products for sale to customers in the Hotel.
The Insurance Court dismissed the ESI Inspector's report because it did not provide the grinder's dimensions.
However, this refusal was incorrect, and a notice was given to the Cafe from ESI Corporation to pay for the ESI Act, which was valid.
