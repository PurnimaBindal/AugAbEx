Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Services Regulation NIEPA, Regulation 17 - UGC ACt, 1956, Section 25 - Appointment - Deputation - The appointment of the petitioner as Secretary UGC for a tenure of 5 years was on deputation basis.
The terms and conditions of the appointment order clearly indicated that he was treated as on deputation and was relieved by NIEPA (parent department).
It was later discovered that deputation period at maximum can be only three years.
Hence, the tenure of 5 years cannot be taken de-hors the position that his appointment was by way of transfer on deputation.
Additionally, the action of UGC in repatriating back the petitioner when NIEPA did not extend the period of deputation beyond 3 years is considered arbitrary and unreasonable.
[Paras 11 to 14]
