The Government Pleader for Higher Education represents Respondent Nos. 2 and 3.
According to Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, along with the Andhra Pradesh College Service Commission Act, 1985 (as amended in 1990), Section 2 Regularisation of services, appointment irregularities were present for Part time lecturers who were not appointed through any appointing committee or selection commission.
The petitioners were not originally entitled to regularisation of their services, but the Government has decided to regularise irregular appointments made without a selection committee or commission under Government order GOMs.
No. 302.
The petitioners have the right to apply for regularisation of their services under this order.
