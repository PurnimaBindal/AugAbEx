Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of services - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Daily wager employed for 10 years in various branches of the Bank - Bank could not present employment register for two years prior to the termination - Court inferred negatively against the Bank - Workman had worked for more than 240 days in a calendar year prior to the retrenchment - Labour court correctly awarded compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
