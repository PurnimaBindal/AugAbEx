Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of service - Award - Principles of Natural Justice - The Labour Court concluded that the termination of the petitioner's service was null and void because the principle of natural justice was not followed - The Labour Court should have considered the adherence to the principles of natural justice while terminating the petitioner's service - Therefore, the award of the Labour Court was overturned.
