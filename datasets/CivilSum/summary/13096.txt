The Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226, address the regularisation of service.
The petitioner's claim was denied because they did not meet the requirement of three years of continuous service.
However, the petitioner had already served for three years prior to the policy's commencement.
Consequently, the rejection order is flawed, and the respondents are instructed to review the case.
