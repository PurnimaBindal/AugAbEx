Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part-I, Regulation 185-A - Disability pension - Reassessment of disability - A person who has been receiving disability pension based on a disability above 20%, and for 30 years, cannot be reassessed by the CCDA (Pension) Allahabad without a special reason or occasion.
The petitioner's disability was not given a reason to be reassessed by the Board and no reasons were disclosed for diminishing the petitioner's disability to less than 20%.
The order to discontinue the petitioner's disability pension was invalidated.
