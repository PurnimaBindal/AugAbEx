Constitution of India, Article 309 - Pay scales - Recommendations of Pay Commission - While recommendations of pay commissions are not binding upon the Government, the final decision rests with the Government.
[Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Equal pay for equal work - For fixing pay scales, the Government is able to consider various factors, including academic qualifications, duties, and level of responsibilities attached to a post - so long as such considerations are relevant, the decision cannot be interfered with by the Court.
