Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal - Conducting of Enquiry - The Enquiry Officer himself inspected and noted the issue of shortfall of trees, which led to the dismissal of the respondent - The Officer recommended severe punishment of dismissal, but there was no evidence that the respondent was provided with a copy of the Enquiry Report and all the relied upon documents - The High Court reinstated the employee as the Enquiry Officer should not have been a witness to the incident under enquiry and exceeded his limit by stating that the Officer had no right to continue in service - Copy of Enquiry Report and all connected documents relied upon must be supplied to the delinquent - Awarding appropriate punishment is the exclusive jurisdiction of the Punishing/Disciplinary Authority.
