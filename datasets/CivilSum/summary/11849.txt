The petitioner in a case involving the Constitution of India, Article 226 on pay scale, selection grade, promotion, and regular service, argued that the respondents should provide him with the benefit of a Rs. 3000-5300 grade after completing five years of service, and a Rs. 4100-4500 grade after completing 12 years of regular service, due to his regular promotion in 1978.
Though the argument deserved acceptance, the petitioner failed to disclose true facts regarding the promotion of S.D.Os.
Thus, the court directed the respondents to examine the petitioner's request for higher grades starting from the completion of five and twelve years of service, taking into account a previous order and a Full Bench decision on the interpretation of a Government circular from 261989.
