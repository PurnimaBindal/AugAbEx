Constitution of India, Article 226 – Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A – Quantum of punishment – Employee charged with multiple charges including some serious charges – Only minor charges of irregularities are proven – Dismissal from service as a punishment is not necessary and is disproportionate to the misconduct proved – The Labour Court did not contest the punishment – However, the High Court can exercise its powers under Section 11-A to reduce the duration of the punishment – The punishment order is nullified – The petitioner deserves to be reinstated without back wages – Not awarding back wages is sufficient punishment under the present circumstances.
