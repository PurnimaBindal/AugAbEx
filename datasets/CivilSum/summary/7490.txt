Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Maharashi Dayanand University Act, Section 9(14) - Disciplinary proceedings - Appeal - In order to comply with the principles of natural justice at the appellate stage, the appellant must receive a personal hearing and a reasonable opportunity to present their case - The appellate authority must adopt a reasonable procedure to ensure justice is seen to be done and must provide a well-reasoned speaking order that deals with each contention raised and records why it agreed or disagreed - Mere reference to case facts is not enough, the reasons for disagreement must be recorded elaborately.
[Paras 6 and 12]
