The Petitioners have no legal right to claim recognition of their qualifications in the State of Punjab as they are not recognised as equivalent to the JBT course run by the Education Department of Haryana.
It is up to the government to determine the qualifications required for recruitment to the service.
Petition dismissed.
[Paras 5 and 6]

The petitioners are not entitled to a declaration that their JBT (Home Craft) certificates from states other than Punjab be recognised as equivalent to those awarded by the Education Department of Punjab.
In order for a certificate to be recognized, it must have undergone two years of training and be recognized by the state government in which the institution is located.
The petitioners do not meet the second condition.
Petition dismissed.
