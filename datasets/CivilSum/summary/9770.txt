Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 7.3 - Suspension period - If a suspended employee is reinstated, they are entitled to full wages in addition to subsistence allowance and continuity of service for the duration of their suspension unless the punishment authority has already ordered otherwise - If the punishing authority subsequently orders forfeiture of service for the same period, it is illegal and constitutes double jeopardy.
[Paras 12 to 15]
