Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 136 - Recruitment - Selection - Post of Deputy Forest Ranger - The High Court approved the petition stating that there were several irregularities, arbitrariness, and mala fides in the selection process.
The Supreme Court directed a fresh selection to be made in adherence to the rules and guidelines at the time of appeal admission.
An affidavit was filed stating that the selection was finalised as per the court's order, and the State Government was directed to release and publish the names of the selected candidates and take further steps to fill the vacancy as per the select list.
