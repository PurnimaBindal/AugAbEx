The Advocate General Mr. MS Chandel and Additional Advocate General Mr. Ashok Chaudhary represent respondents 1 & 2; Mr. Virender Verma appears as counsel for respondent no. 3.
As per the Himachal Pradesh Non-Government Affiliated Colleges Grant-in-Aid Rules, 1994, Rule 21 and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner was not entitled to be appointed or promoted as Accountant or Superintendent Grade II by the Management Committee without the prior approval of the Director of Education.
Moreover, the college authorities are not obliged to provide grant-in-aid for reimbursement of salary in the pay scale claimed by the petitioner, which shall be limited only in respect of employees whose appointment has been duly approved by the Director of Education.
