Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 42 and 43 - J&K Civil Service Rules, 1996, Article 157-A - Maternity leave - it is improper to deny maternity leave to ad hoc employee as it's available to regular employee.
No reasonable classification made by creating a class within a class - The petitioner must be accorded the benefit of maternity leave like any other female employee without any distinction created between ad hoc and regular staff.
