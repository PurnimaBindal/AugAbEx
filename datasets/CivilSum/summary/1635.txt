Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - Reinstatement - Back wages - High Court has granted reinstatement to the petitioner-respondent with full benefits and back wages.
The Court found that the disciplinary proceedings against him were violative of principles of natural justice as a proper enquiry into the alleged misconduct of unauthorised absence had not been held.
The Court made a distinction between `no opportunity' and `no adequate opportunity', and in this case, the violation was of the latter.
The court did not give the employer the option to start a fresh proceeding or complete the proceeding from the stage the error has inflicted it fatally.
However, backwages were not awarded as it is not a rule of general application.
