Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Gujarat Warehousing Corporation Staff Service Regulations, Regulations 78 & 86 - Suspension - Consequences of suspension - To order suspension is a serious measure that may ruin employee's career and cause damage to family reputation - Unjustified suspension is inhumane to staff, thus, cannot be ordered unless the allegations are serious and might attract a major punishment.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Gujarat Warehousing Corporation Staff Service Regulations, Regulations 78 & 86 - Suspension - Delay in joining posting on transfer - Suspension cannot be warranted for a trivial misconduct - Although petitioner didn't join the transferred place immediately, availed joining leave without permission, and didn't accept the charge but took sick leave, the charge wasn't serious enough to warrant suspension.
[Para]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Gujarat Warehousing Corporation Staff Service Regulations, Regulations 78 & 86 - Suspension - Wages for suspension period - Full salary for suspension period cannot be denied, based only on proved charges - Relevant regulations should decide whether the suspension was justified to determine the wages - Exoneration of employee from an unjustified suspension requires the full payment of wages.
[Para]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Gujarat Warehousing Corporation Staff Service Regulations, Regulations 78 & 86 - Suspension - Revocation of - Wages for suspension period - Payment of wages during suspension period should be determined at the revocation time, not waiting until disciplinary procedures are completed - Action found inappropriate.
