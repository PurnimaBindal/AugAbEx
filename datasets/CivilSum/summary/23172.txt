The definition of "local student" as stated by the Rules for Admission to Smt. NHL Municipal Medical College, Rules 7 and 6(i), creates an unfair distinction between residents of Ahmedabad city who qualify from local institutions, and those from outside the city who have passed the same qualifications within the local limits.
This definition goes against the objective of catering to the needs of the people of Ahmedabad city and violates Article 14 of the Constitution.
While Rule 7 does not need to be struck down entirely, the definition of "local student" must be reinterpreted to include permanent residents of the Ahmedabad Municipality who qualify for admission from institutions situated within the Ahmedabad Urban Development Area.
Additionally, the reservation in admissions based on domicile, university, or institutions is permissible only up to the undergraduate level as per Article 14 of the Constitution.
