Civil Procedure Code, Order 6, Rule 17 - Amendment of plaint - For a suit regarding declaration and permanent injunction, if the charge sheet that the suit is based on is dropped, and the plaintiff's services are terminated for a different reason, a new cause of action is created, which cannot be challenged by amending the original pleading with an entirely different cause of action - Therefore, the request to amend the plaint was rightly declined.
