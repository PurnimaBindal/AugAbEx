Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Selection - Petitioner, who was qualified for the post of Lecturer in Economics against the reserved quota of Ex-serviceman (general category), was issued the wrong interview card for the post of Lecturer in Commerce - No Ex-serviceman (general category) candidate was selected for the Economics post - Petitioner's right to be considered was denied illegally - Respondents ordered to consider petitioner for the Economics post against Ex-serviceman (general category) quota, based on availability.
