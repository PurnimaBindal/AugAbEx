Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Indian Police Service (Cadre) Rules, 1954, Rule 3 - Appointment/Selection -  Mere selection does not guarantee appointment.
However, the state must consider merit when making appointments from a select list, and cannot be arbitrary.
If the government chooses not to fill the posts after the selection, they must have appropriate reasons for doing so.
The selected candidates cannot demand appointment for future vacancies that arise after the selection process is closed.
