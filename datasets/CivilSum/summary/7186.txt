Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(8) - Departmental Enquiry - Natural Justice - Delinquent employee can be assisted by another government employee to present his defence - Refusal of Inquiry Officer to adjourn proceeding when delinquent employee wished to avail assistance of co-employee - Constitutes denial of reasonable opportunity - Inquiry officer's approach highly technical and unsympathetic - Violation of Rule 8(8) leads to inquiry vitiating as delinquent employee's defence is prejudiced - Impugned order of removal from service invalidated.
[Paras 10 and 11]

Departmental Enquiry - Assistance of co-employee - Mere unavailability of co-employee's services does not in itself invalidate inquiry proceedings - Evaluation of whether delinquent employee is prejudiced must be considered on a case-by-case basis.
