Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Assessment Promotion Scheme - Seniority/promotion - The petitioner requested the benefits of the Assessment Promotion Scheme after completing the required 4-year period of service.
However, there was no evidence to suggest that the assessment board met during the year in which the petitioner sought benefits.
It was fair for the authorities to consider 2000 as the regular appointment date for the petitioner, given that the board meeting was held in the following year.
Consequently, the petition was dismissed.
