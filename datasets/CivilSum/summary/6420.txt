Constitution of India namely Articles 16 and 226 relate to Transfer of employees.
The petitioner in this case has been transferred to a school that is about 4 km away from the previous school.
It is being contended that mid-term transfers should not be allowed.
As per the verdict of the Supreme Court, employees should not be transferred during the middle of a session as it can cause disturbance to the education of students.
The court has ordered to keep the order of transfer in abeyance till a decision is taken.
