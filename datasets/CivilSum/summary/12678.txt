The Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1996, Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Reduction of superannuation age - The State has the power to amend service rules at any time, according to the State Government.
The High Court cannot interfere with a policy decision of the State Government under Article 226 unless it opposes public policy.
[Paras 10, 12] B The Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1996, Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Reduction in superannuation age - Since the State Government's service rules govern service conditions for State Government employees, the principle of promissory estoppel does not apply.
The State Government has the prerogative to change service conditions.
