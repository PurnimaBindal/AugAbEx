Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - IPS (Cadre) Rules, Rule 9 - I.P.S.
(Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 8 - Appointment - Cadre Post in Police Service - If no cadre officers or select list officers are available, non-select list officers may be temporarily promoted to vacancies lasting no more than three months - State Government must strictly follow the provisos of regulation 8(2) of Promotion Regulations and Rule 9 of cadre rules - Prior concurrence of the Central Government is necessary if the vacancy lasts longer than three months - Prior approval of the Union Public Service Commission is also mandatory if it continues for more than six months - Appointments that violate these conditions are not lawful and are ad hoc or local arrangements.
