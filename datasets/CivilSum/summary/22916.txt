Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Maharashtra Secondary Board Regulations, 1966, Regulation 19(7)(xvi) - Maharashtra Municipality Act, 1965 - Hinganghat Municipal Council (Regulating the period of Service of Secondary School Teachers) Bye-laws 1980 - Bye-law 4 - Age of Superannuation/Retirement - Conditions of service - The Bye-laws, 1980 stipulated that school teachers retire at the age of 58 instead of 60 - Teachers are governed by the same statutory provisions and the same Education Code - There is only one class of teachers under municipal councils - Hence, it is unfair to introduce disparity within the class of teachers regarding superannuation - Thus, Bye-law 4 has been invalidated for violating Article 16 of the Constitution.
