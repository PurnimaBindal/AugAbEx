The Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act (61 of 1986), Sections 3, 14(1) and (3) state that a company cannot engage child labor.
If a company violates this rule, a show cause notice will be issued to the company and the company will be directed to pay Rs. 20,000 to each child affected.
The order is without fault.
