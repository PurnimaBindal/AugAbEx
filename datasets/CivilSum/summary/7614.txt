Under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, the state is responsible for referring an industrial dispute to the Labour Court for adjudication, and the Labour Court is then required to examine the reference.
In terms of pitting the worker's claim in the industrial dispute against it, a mere delay in providing the demand notice would not qualify to defeat said claim.
However, in the event that the dispute is resolved in the worker's favor, the Labour Court may take into account delay as a factor in determining relief.
