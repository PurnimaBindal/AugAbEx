According to Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and Punjabi University, Ordinance 6, failing students have the right to appear in supplementary examinations, but if they still fail after both the annual and supplementary examinations, the rigour of Ordinance 6 should apply.
Furthermore, if the University fails to hold examinations promptly or to declare results in a timely fashion, candidates should not be made to suffer for it.
B.
The Constitution allows courts to interfere in academic matters with restraint to protect academic standards.
However, in cases where a University has failed to perform its duties under Ordinances, failure to interfere would undermine justice.
C. The cases of petitioners who had taken the BA Part-I examination had not been complied with after nine months, despite their compliance with instructions from a Motion Bench.
The Respondents were directed to declare the examination results within two weeks, and petitioners were entitled to Rs. 1,000/- in costs.
