Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission test - Eligibility - Standard of test - The course of admission clearly requires Mathematics as an essential subject to make candidates proficient in the chosen field - Students must work hard to meet the established standard of the test and attain admission according to their merits - They cannot claim that the test is too difficult, since they have already passed an exam that qualifies them for it - The High Court, in its jurisdiction under Article 226, should refrain from interfering with the admission methodology unless it is shown to be arbitrary - The experts responsible for setting eligibility criteria and exam standards are best suited to determine the admission criteria.
