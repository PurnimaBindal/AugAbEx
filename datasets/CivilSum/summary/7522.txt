Army personnel cannot be terminated or discharged from service on an allegation of misconduct without following proper procedure, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 and Army Rules, Rule 13, Table III(V).
Once absolved of involvement in an offence by a Court of Inquiry, mere suspicion is insufficient grounds for discharge.
A proper enquiry is necessary for legitimate discharge.
[Para 9]
The Court can investigate whether a termination or discharge from service was merely a punishment under the guise of an innocuous order, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 and Army Rules, Rule 13, Table III(V).
A notice and order cannot be a camouflage for a punishment.
[Paras 10 to 12]
