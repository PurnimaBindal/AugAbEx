Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Appointment - Termination - Natural Justice - The petitioner was appointed as the Standing Counsel by the Corporation for a two-year term that could be terminated by the Managing Director at any time, without notice.
Services of the Standing Counsel were terminated due to his involvement in a criminal case.
The Court held that the Corporation, as a client, had the right to terminate the appointment of the advocate for such reasons and was not obligated to provide notice or invitation for an explanation.
The termination was therefore valid.
[Paras 3 and 5]
