Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 21 - Education - Fee Structure - Non-fixation of fee - Delay in admission - A college must have adequate infrastructure, teaching faculty, and proper means to ensure that education of the students is not adversely affected by lack of funds, and must disclose what they can charge from the students as they have to carry out professional courses.
It is always recommended for the college to declare proper fee at the beginning of the academic session to avoid future controversies - Directed to admit the students by accepting provisional fee and make adjustment after final fixation of fee to ensure that the students are not put to any inconvenience or uncertainty during the currency of their professional course.
