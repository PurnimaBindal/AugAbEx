Article 226 of the Constitution of India discusses whether a diploma in Laboratory Technician vocational course from Govt.
Vocational Institute Rohtak/Karnal is equivalent to the Diploma in Laboratory Technician from the Bacteriologist Laboratory, Karnal, which is a question of a technical nature.
Only an expert in the field of concern can decide the matter.
[Para 5]

The requirement for candidates applying for the position of Laboratory Technician advertised by the SSS Board, Haryana is to have a Diploma in Laboratory Technician from the Bacteriologist Laboratory, Karnal, or from an Institute recognized by the Govt. of Haryana.
The petitioners have a Diploma in Laboratory Technician vocational course from Govt.
Vocational Institute Rohtak/Karnal, which is not recognized by the Govt. of Haryana.
Therefore, their applications were not considered.
As per the advertisement, neither the petitioners have the required diploma nor their institute is recognized by the Govt. of Haryana.
Thus, the court cannot direct the Board to consider their applications for the post in question.
