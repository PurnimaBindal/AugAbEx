In accordance with the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(17), a company deducted an amount of ESI from their employees' salaries but did not deposit it with the Department.
Despite this occurrence, prosecution against the company and its Directors was quashed due to the fact that the Directors cannot be deemed the "employer" within the definition of Section 2(17) of ESI Act.
This definition applies to the company only, and thus, proceedings against the company are allowed to proceed.
