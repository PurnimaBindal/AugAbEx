The Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 9A, 10(1) and 12 and Constitution of India, Article 226 address disputes and reference denials.
When the management unilaterally increased the interest on loans, the union put forward a demand which needs to be decided upon by the adjudication machinery.
Disputed questions regarding justification of the demand, availability of options, changes in service conditions or violations of Section 9A must be assessed.
Therefore, directions were given for a reference to be made.
