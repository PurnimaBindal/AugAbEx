Rules 8, 5 and 16 of the Central Administrative Tribunal (Salaries, Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice Chairman and Members) Rules, 1985 - Pension - An individual who retires voluntarily as a Senior District Judge and later joins the Central Administrative Tribunal as a member is not allowed to combine the two services in order to obtain a consolidated pension as a CAT member or add to his judicial service qualifying service until the age of 62 - Rule 8 solely governs the pension of an independent member of the CAT and is comprehensive in nature - Rule 16 does not allow for any additional interpretation - The individual must retire from his judicial service in accordance with Rule 5 - It is not permissible to combine the two services and such a combination is not authorized - The clear language of the rules precludes adding additional pensionary benefits - The pensions are governed solely by Rule 8 - Commission to the CAT is a tenure appointment and not a reemployment under the Union of India.
[Paras 19, 20 & 21]  B Appointment in the Administrative Tribunal after voluntary retirement from Judicial Services is not re-employment in the affairs of Union of Government and so, Pay fixation order, 1986 is not applicable.
