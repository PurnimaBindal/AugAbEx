Civil Procedure Code 1908, Section 9 - Employees Provident Fund (Family Pension Fund and Deposit Insurance Fund) Act 1952, Section 7(a) - Family Pension - Stoppage of - Remarriage - The plaintiff's family pension was stopped due to remarriage, but their suit was decreed in appeal.
The defendant argues that the court cannot determine the employer's liability under the Act.
However, since the issue in the suit pertains to the status of the plaintiff as the deceased's wife and not the determination of money due from the employer, the refusal to pay family pension without proof of remarriage is illegal.
The appeal is dismissed accordingly.
