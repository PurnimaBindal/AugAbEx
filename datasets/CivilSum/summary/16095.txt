Under Section 8(1) of the Punjab Guru Gobind Singh Medical College, Faridkot (Acquisition) and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978, the State Government had the authority to take over the College and absorb its staff while retaining their conditions of service.
A petitioner who was an Associate Professor in the College and had been absorbed into the Medical Education Service Class-I as an Assistant Professor ENT was entitled to higher promotions as per the Service Rules, and his conditions of service had not changed.
Although the Act only secured the 'term of office', not the 'office', the petitioner's petition was dismissed.
