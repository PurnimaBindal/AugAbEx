According to the Andhra Pradesh Legislative Service (Ad hoc) Rules, 1983, Rule 4, the position of "Special Secretary" to the Legislative Assembly is considered a confidential post.
As per Articles 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution, the court cannot intervene in the appointment made by the appointing authority who is the best judge to determine the necessity of the post and select a trusted candidate.
It is beyond the court's ambit to assess the competence of such individuals.
