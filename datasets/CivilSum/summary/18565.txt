According to Jammu & Kashmir Press Subordinate Services Recruitment Rules 1980 and Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, promotion to the post of Katib requires minimum qualifications of 'Sangsaz' with three years of experience.
Despite having all the necessary qualifications, the petitioner was not promoted to the post.
Furthermore, juniors to the petitioner were promoted.
Therefore, it is unfair to ignore the petitioner from promotion.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
