Constitution of India Articles 226/227 - Industrial Dispute Act, Section 11-A - Termination - The respondent, a conductor with Punjab Roadways, was terminated from service after being caught defrauding the management by intentionally issuing tickets of less denomination to passengers as detected by two inspectors.
The respondent raised an industrial dispute and the labour court ordered for the respondent to be reinstated with continuity of service with 50% back-wages.
However, the management preferred a writ petition against the award of the labour court stating that the workman defrauded the management and therefore loses the right to continue in service.
The court held that the workman was given an opportunity to defend and that the enquiry held was fair and just.
It further held that once the workman was found to have committed fraud, he loses the right to continue in service.
The award of the labour court was quashed and the writ petition was allowed.
