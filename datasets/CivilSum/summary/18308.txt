The Delhi Land Reforms Act allows for Voluntary Retirement with payment of excess amount.
However, if the Corporation wishes to recover any excess amount from the retirement, it must follow proper legal procedures.
In this case, the Corporation's action by way of suits pursuant to a legal notice was the correct procedure.
The respondents were not allowed to initiate coercive methods and get notices issued for the recovery of amounts as arrears of land revenue.
The impugned notices were without authority of law and arbitrary, and the Corporation should pursue remedies in suits.
