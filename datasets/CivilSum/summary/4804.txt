Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act and the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 allow for disciplinary proceedings and punishment, including stoppage of salary increments, for conductors.
A challenge under the Payment of Wages Act is sustainable, but it has been held that neither the authority nor ADJ has the jurisdiction to address issues concerning the Act.
This is due to the judgment in Baldev Singh and Chottey Lal Saxena's case, which states that the authority set up under the Payment of Wages Act cannot intervene with decisions made during disciplinary proceedings against an employee.
