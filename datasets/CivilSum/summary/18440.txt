The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 226, were found to be violated in the case of the denial of admission to a Veterinary Nursing Course due to the petitioner's Senior Secondary qualification from the National Institute of Open Schooling not being recognized in Rajasthan, despite being recognized in the guidelines issued by the Board of Secondary Education in that state.
The reasoning of the admission denial was deemed discriminatory and unjust.
