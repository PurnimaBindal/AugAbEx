Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act, 1965, Sections 75, 148 - Rajasthan Ministerial Staff Service Rules, 1957, Rule 24 - Seniority - Seniority is determined on the basis of interview only as marks secured in Stenography test, typing speed and efficiency test out of 100 marks are ignored - Selection Committee drew final results from marks in interview only - Provition of rule 24(2) was not properly considered in impugned orders.
[Para 5] B.
Seniority - Challenge as to - Estoppel - Seniority should be assigned on basis of marks secured in interview as well as in Stenography test, typing speed and efficiency test - Ignoring the marks in these tests was against the rules - Principle of estoppel does not apply against statute and petitioner has the right to challenge the illegal procedure adopted by the Selection Committee.
