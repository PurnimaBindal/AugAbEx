Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Termination - Petitioner, a HAFED chowkidar, had his services terminated.
He approached the Labour Court, but his claim was rejected.
In a writ petition, he prayed for reinstatement with all consequential benefits.
He contends that he remained in service after the date of termination, and the attendance register was tampered with.
The Managing Director was directed to appear in court and admitted to the tampering of the attendance register.
After an enquiry, the management agreed to reinstate the petitioner but without back wages.
The petitioner will get benefit of continuity of service but not back wages.
The petition was allowed.
[Paras 2 and 3]
