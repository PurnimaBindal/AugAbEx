Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Rules, Rules, 13 and 13(iii)(i) - Pension Regulations, Regulation 122(a) - Pension - Retiral Benefits - The petitioner did not request for his discharge at any stage, and there was no termination or discharge on medical grounds of his services under Rule 13 of Army Rules - Discharge orders passed by Army Authorities were erroneous - The prayer for the release of full pension and other retiral dues is justified - Respondents are ordered to pay these dues from three years immediately prior to the filing of the petition.
