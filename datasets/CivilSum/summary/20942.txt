Coal Mine Nationalisation Act, 1973, Section 14 - Resumption of duty - Central Government took over non-cooking coal mines on 31.1.73 and nationalised on 1.5.73 - The Tribunal found that the employees were still employees and were just not allowed to work - The Tribunal's factual finding cannot be questioned - Therefore, the employees' right to resume duty will not be affected by the amended provision of the Act in force since 15.12.86 - The Tribunal was correct in allowing workmen to resume duty from the date of takeover.
