The writ filed by the respondent for the direct payment system in the FCI Depot was allowed as per Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970.
However, no notification under Section 10 was produced to show that employment of contract labour is prohibited in godowns of the appellant.
Therefore, the Court cannot direct the Central Government to issue a notification in that regard in the absence of any notification.
Also, the Air India Statutory Corporation and Section 10 of the Act cannot be applied here.
[Paras 6, 8 and 9]
