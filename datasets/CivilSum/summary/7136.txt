As per the Army Act, 1950, Section 20 and the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, a student of Armed Forces Medical College was discharged due to drug addiction.
The bond between the Medical Cadet and the Armed Forces states that if a Medical Cadet is deemed medically unfit, they must pay an amount of Rs. 1,50,000/- per completed year.
The importance of discipline in Armed Forces cannot be overlooked, and therefore, there should be no interference under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
