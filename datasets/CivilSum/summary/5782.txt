The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Admissions/Education/Reservation in Medical Courses - MBBS Course - Common Entrance Test - Reservation for sportsmen/women - Admission to courses aimed at producing efficient and expert professionals must be based solely on academic excellence - Candidates cannot be admitted through other routes, such as sports - To produce academically sound doctors, a cut-off percentage of marks in the CET is necessary - Minimum eligibility criteria of 50% marks for reserved categories in the CET is upheld, and the plea for a minimum eligibility of 35% marks is rejected.

The Constitution of India, Article 51-A - Reservation in admissions - Relaxation in eligibility criteria - Admission in MBBS Courses - Medical Courses - Common Entrance Test - Minimum eligibility marks - The Constitution of India requires citizens to strive for excellence in all areas, so setting a minimum percentage of marks in the CET for admission to the MBBS Course is necessary to produce good and efficient doctors.
