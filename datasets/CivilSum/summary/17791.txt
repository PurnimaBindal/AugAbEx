Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - State Govt. has discretion to make reservation under provisions - Once made, cannot refuse to make appointment of persons belonging to class in whose favour reservation has been made - Reservation order made under Articles 15(4) and 16(4) can be withdrawn or cancelled by Govt. - Persons belonging to reserved class entitled to be appointed against reserved vacancies/posts as long as order of reservation stands.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - Reservation - Reservation order for physically handicapped persons in the cadre of Sales Tax Officer still stands - While readjusting overall reservation to bring it within 50 per cent of total posts, Govt. dropped reservation for physically handicapped persons - Govt. cannot refuse to reserve or not make appointment against post/vacancy reserved for physically handicapped persons as long as order of reservation stands.
