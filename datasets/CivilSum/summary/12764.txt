The claim for disability pension by the petitioner under the Pension Regulations of the Army, 1961, Regulation 173 was rejected on the basis that the Idiopathic Disorder was not a result of military service, despite serving for over six years.
However, the decision not to grant disability pension to the petitioner, evaluated by the invalidating medical board, assessed 40% disability for two years without a review Medical Board examining the petitioner and reaching a different conclusion, cannot be supported.
Therefore, the petition was allowed.
