As per Sections 7-A, 8-A and 8-B of the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the Act is applicable to Non Government Education Institutions.
However, in light of a Supreme Court decision, it is only applicable to institutions from 181982 to 181988.
The matter regarding the period from 181988 onward is referred back to the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner to make a decision on the question of applicability of the Act to the said institution.
