According to Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, along with Section 20(3)(i) and 20(3)(ii) of the Minimum Wages Act 1948, a penalty must be imposed equal to five times the amount of delayed wages in cases where wages have been awarded.
Under Section 20(3)(i), the workman is entitled to the amount paid less than the minimum wages, and a penalty of up to ten times the amount worked out, and in this case, the petitioner is covered by Clause (i) of sub-section (3) of Section 20, and as a result, the Authority's award is entirely justified.
Ponnambalan's case judgement is cited.
