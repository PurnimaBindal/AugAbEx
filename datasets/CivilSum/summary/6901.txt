The Army Rules of 1954, specifically rules 177, 179(5), and 180, state that in order to maintain natural justice, a delinquent must be given time to prepare and cross-examine witnesses.
Although examination in chief may be recorded in the delinquent's absence, as long as this requirement is met, proceedings for initiating a General Court Martial are not in violation of procedure.
[Para ]
A Court of Inquiry is not always necessary for a General Court Martial, and therefore any irregularities or illegalities in such a court do not necessarily mean that the convening of a GCM is invalid.
[Para 11]
Rule 175(5) of the Army Rules of 1954 does not require that the members of a second Court of Inquiry be the same as those of the first Court, especially if the first Court was cancelled due to members' incompetence.
[Para 12]
The non-supply of statements recorded in cancelled Courts of Inquiry does not affect the validity of disciplinary proceedings, as these statements hold no value.
