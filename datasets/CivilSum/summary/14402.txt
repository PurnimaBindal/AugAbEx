Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay - Promotion - Retirement - The petitioner's two time-bound promotions were cancelled due to a Govt. circular that prohibits promotions given after 1883 if the petitioner did not pass Departmental Accounts examination.
Moreover, the petitioner did not receive any retirement benefit due to the order after retirement, and the pay was reduced as a result of the promotion order's cancellation.
However, the impugned order has been quashed, and the respondent is to issue a show cause notice to the petitioner.
