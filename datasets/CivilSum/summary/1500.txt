According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Services) Act, 1971, Section 4, the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 4, and Punjab Govt. Education Department instructions dated 23.7.1957, higher qualifications do not automatically entitle J.B.T.
teachers to the pay scale of Masters.
After the Security of Service Act and Rules were created, pay scales are determined by the Act and Rules, not by the instructions dated 23.7.1959.
Even teachers of Govt. Schools are entitled to pay scales according to the Rules of the post they are appointed to, irrespective of their qualifications.
Parity between teachers in privately managed aided schools and government schools is only in respect of pay scales and dearness allowance, not increments granted to teachers in government schools under instructions dated 191960.
The benefit of the Master's grade was being given to J.B.T.
teachers on the basis of wrong interpretation of the instructions.
The relief was granted on the basis of the statutory enactment, not on the basis of executive instructions.
Haryana Govt.
Instructions dt. 931990 & Punjab Govt. instructions dated 23.7.1957 clarify the instructions dated 23.7.1957, stating that possessing/acquiring higher qualification does not automatically entitle teachers to claim higher pay scales.
