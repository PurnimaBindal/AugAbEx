Swatantrata Sainik Sammann Pension Scheme, 1980 - Pension - Freedom fighters family pension - The scheme aims to honor and alleviate the sufferings of individuals who sacrificed for the country.
When evaluating these claims, a lenient approach is necessary, and the standard of proof required is not as stringent as in criminal cases.
The petitioner demonstrated his claim of being imprisoned for more than six months with several documents and was granted a Sanman Pattar by the Freedom Fighters Committee.
Despite minor discrepancies regarding the exact date of imprisonment, the court ruled in favor of the petitioner, directing the respondents to grant him a pension under the Scheme from the date of his petition filing.
