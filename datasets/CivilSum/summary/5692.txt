It is an established fact that the Labour Court must proceed with the reference and make a decision on the case's merits, even if the employee fails to appear based on Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F and Industrial Disputes (Punjab) Rules, 1958, Rule 22.
The dispute will be settled by examining the statement of claim and other pleadings and records available.
When the management acknowledges that the workman was in their service, they must prove the claim of resignation or abandonment; otherwise, it will be assumed that the dismissal fell under Section 25-F of the ID Act.
