The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Documents - Enforcing conditions - Relaxation of conditions - Extension of time to fulfil conditions - It is important to follow the conditions published in the advertisement strictly.
However, conditions concerning the completion of documents with the applications can be relaxed if the selecting authority feels it is a case of hardship or for any other good reason.
In such a situation, it is necessary for the authority to publish such extension/relaxation as a general notice so that all candidates can benefit.
It is not obligatory for the authority to relax, nor can any candidate claim it as a right.
Candidates were required to produce a certificate of the weaker section, which they could not obtain in time.
Those who acted diligently to obtain and produce the certificates are entitled to relief while others are not.
[Paras 21, 22, 24, 26, 27 and 28] B.
The Constitution of India, Article 226 and 227 - Discretionary relief - Scope of - Selection - Relaxation of conditions of completion of documents in support of applications - The court can grant relief in appropriate cases in the exercise of its discretion in cases of extreme hardship or injustice, keeping in view the diligence exercised by the candidate.
No hard and fast rule can be formulated in this regard.
It depends on the facts and circumstances of each case.
Candidates who provide the necessary documents after the selecting agency has made its recommendations cannot invoke the writ jurisdiction.
However, those who produced the certificates much earlier than the completion of the selection proceedings must receive relief.
