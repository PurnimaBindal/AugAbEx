Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976 - Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) and other Alied Services - Allocation of marks - Viva voce marks reserved under clause 4 of Appendex III to Rules - Main competitive exam qualification requires obtaining at least 45% marks in all subjects including viva voce - Petitioner challenged allocation of highly excessive viva voce marks after failing to qualify - Commission directed to declare result on basis of performance in written exam alone not sustainable - Petitioner cannot challenge viva voce marks after taking chance in exam - Allocation of 200 marks to viva voce against total 525 marks not excessive per nature of selection.
