Constitution of India, Article 311 - Reduction in rank - If a promotion order was based on an incorrect date of joining, cancelling the order does not constitute a reduction in rank.
State of Punjab v. Jagdip Singh, AIR 1964 SC 521 and Ravinder Nath Tiwari v. Divisional Superintendent of Education, Jabalpur, 1978(1) SLR 484 referenced.
