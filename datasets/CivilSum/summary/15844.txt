According to the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979 and notifications dated 18101979 and 2291981, teachers with higher qualifications are entitled to higher pay scales.
Specifically, S.S. Masters employed in Govt. Schools with Post-Graduate qualifications prior to 111978 or obtained them before that date should receive stepped up pay based on this criteria.
The government has been instructed to adjust their pay as necessary, paying out arrears for the previous 38 months preceding the submission of the writ petition within the next two months.
