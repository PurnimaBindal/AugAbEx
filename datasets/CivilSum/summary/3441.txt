Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Territorial Jurisdiction - Reference - Because the workman faced penal consequences in Calcutta, he was allowed to raise his dispute there, despite being under the control of Bombay office and being transferred there.
[Para 15]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2A, 10(1) - Reference - Appropriate Government - The Government of West Bengal was deemed the appropriate Government to refer the industrial dispute to the Industrial Tribunal since the workman suffered the cessation of relationship between master and servant in Calcutta.
