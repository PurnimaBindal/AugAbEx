Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 311 - Reservation for Schedule Castes and Tribes - The reservation of posts must be made subject-wise, not category-wise in the advertisement - The total number of posts reserved in the advertisement is not considered legal - Posts must be indicated in such a manner to achieve the purpose for which applications are invited.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Principle of Natural Justice - In cases where the services of the appellants are terminated due to a change in the law and not because of the demerits or misconduct of the candidates, it is unnecessary to hear the individuals before their services are terminated - The rule of audi alteram partem does not apply in such cases - No violation of natural justice principles.
