Rule 13 of the Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1958 and Sections 5(1)(d) and 5(2) of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1987 apply to the case in which a suspension order was issued against the petitioner due to a criminal case against him.
The suspension order was not unjustified, and therefore, the petition was dismissed.
[Para 3] B.
The Appointing Authority has discretion over suspending a government servant due to a pending criminal case.
As long as the decision is not mala fide and is based on material, the Court will not interfere, especially considering that suspension does not entail a punishment for the government servant.
Therefore, the suspension order issued in relation to the framing of the charge in the criminal case has a justifiable basis.
