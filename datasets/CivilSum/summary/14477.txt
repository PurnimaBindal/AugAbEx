According to Section 1(3) of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the petitioner-Centre must determine whether they are a Hospital or Industry falling within sub-section (3) of the Act.
The manufacturers who produce artificial limbs use both engineering skill and scientific skill, so they are within the definition of "electrical, mechanical and general engineering products" specified in Schedule 1 of the Act.
Therefore, the provisions of the Act would apply.
