UP Public Services Tribunal Act (17 of 1976), Sections 1(4)(a) to (s), 4 and 6 - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 2(ii)(g), 15 and 22 - Bar of suits under Section 6 of U.P.
Act 1976 - Proceedings under 1936 Act not considered as suits under Section 6 - Authority under Payment of Wages Act cannot be designated as Court and claim petition filed before it is not a suit - Hence, Section 6 of the 1976 Act does not bar such a claim petition.
[Para 8]  B Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - Claim by workman before authority - Adjudication on entitlement - Plea that no claim can be granted without there being adjudication regarding entitlement is not tenable - Matters regarding payment of salary, subsistence allowance, and arrears of DA which the employee has the right to receive do not require any declaration or adjudication by any authority.
