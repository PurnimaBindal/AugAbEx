According to the Army Act, 1950, Section 46, and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 22, an accused serving as a Sepoy in the army has committed an offence by forcing drinks on an army recruit and then opening the buttons of his trouser.
The accused was found guilty and sentenced to three months imprisonment and removal from service.

The Army Act, 1950, Sections 46 and 164(2), and Army Rules, 1954, Rules 22 and 115(2), state that an accused serving as Sepoy in the Army has committed an offence by forcing drinks on an army recruit and then opening the buttons of his trouser with indecent intention.
A Summary Court Martial was held, and the accused pleaded guilty to the charge.
The Rule 22 mandate was complied with, and the conviction and sentence were upheld.
