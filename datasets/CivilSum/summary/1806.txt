Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952), Section 14-A - Prosecution of accused for non-payment of employees contribution - The delay in payment of contribution does not justify quashing of criminal proceedings.

Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952), Section 14-A - Offence by Company - Non-payment of employees contribution by a company - Managing Partner and other partners of establishment charged for the offense - The complaint mentions that all petitioners were in charge of and responsible for the conduct of the company's business - No evidence suggests that petitioners were not partners at any point of time on the due date of the contributions - No basis for seeking the quashing of the criminal proceedings.
