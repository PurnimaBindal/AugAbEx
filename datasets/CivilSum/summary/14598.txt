Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers Class I, PWD (Building and Roads Branch) Rules, 1960, Rule 8(3) - Promotion - A Screening Committee/PPSC must weigh both good and bad records of a petitioner who has been reverted from Xen to S.D.O.
position.
Crossing the Efficiency Bar does not erase previous adverse remarks, but it is a critical moment in an employee's career that must be considered.
The case of petitioner's juniors were promoted in 1987, 1988, and 1989, and the record of the petitioner's promotion as Xen in 1986 had to be taken into consideration.
However, there is no indication that the Screening Committee considered the petitioner's record as Xen after their promotion in 1986.
As a result, the impugned order of reversion was dismissed, and the respondents were directed to reconsider the petitioner's case for promotion.
