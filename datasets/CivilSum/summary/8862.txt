Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Disputed question of fact - The State Govt must determine if there is a dispute between workmen and management before referring it to a Labour Court under Section 10 of the Act.
They cannot investigate a disputed question of fact or law.
The petitioner argues that he is a workman because his role is clerical and he does not supervise others.
The Labour Court must decide the disputed question of fact by recording evidence.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
[Para 4] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Enquiry - Termination - The State Govt cannot investigate whether the enquiry was appropriate or not when the Management terminates the petitioner's job.
The Labour Court must make an inquiry into disputed questions of fact, so the State Govt's only responsibility is to determine if a dispute exists, which they have determined it does.
The petition is granted.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
