Defence Service Regulations, Para 70 - Promotion - Petitioner, who retired as a major in Army, seeks promotion on the grounds that a junior was promoted instead of him and he was not even screened by Selection Board - Respondents contend that Petitioner retired before the screening, and under Clause 70, an officer whose early advancement is in the interest of service can be specially selected for promotion irrespective of seniority - High Court cannot interfere with the specialized knowledge of Selection Boards, but can only review procedural errors or errors of law leading to injustice or violation of principles of natural justice - Dismissal of petition.
[Paras 6, 9 and 10]
