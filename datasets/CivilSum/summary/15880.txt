Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Pension - Retirement - A charge sheet was issued after more than 4 years of retirement alleging that the petitioner had failed to turn up to hand over the complete charge and that he was responsible for shortages of material which had caused loss to Govt. - The petitioner filed a reply but no inquiry was held - There was no evidence produced to prove any charges - In this situation, it was not possible to establish that the charges stood proved - The orders of recovery were held to be unjust, unfair, and a violation of natural justice as no material had ever been brought to the petitioner's notice.

Retiral benefits - Payment of gratuity and leave encashment delayed for more than 17 years - The authority was directed to pay 12% interest.

Retiral benefits - Legal representatives - None of the deceased's sons/daughters contested the benefits except his widow - This implies that none of them needed the money due - The benefits were ordered to be released to the widow alone.
