The court set aside a promotion given to juniors in preference to a senior official based on Constitution of India, Article 16.
The court ordered that the petitioner be considered for promotion from the date when his juniors were promoted, but could not order promotion.
[Paras 4 and 5]  B.
Power of appointment and promotion is given to a particular Authority under the Rules based on Constitution of India, Article 16.
The Authority appointed a Committee for making promotions on the basis of recommendations, but the promotion was set aside as the Rules did not make provision for the appointment of a Committee.
The Authority concerned must make the promotion without being influenced by the advice of the committee.
