Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Filing a petition challenging the award of Labour Court after 2 years indicates lack of vigilance on the part of the petitioner - Delay cannot be overlooked, even if another worker has filed a writ petition against the same award.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Natural justice - Bias and prejudice - In case of bias and prejudice, evidence is required to raise such plea.
If there is no record of findings in this regard, such plea cannot be raised for the first time in writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 4, 5, 6, 9]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Standing Orders of Maruti Udyog, Order 2022 - Misconduct - Assaulting a Senior Officer of the Company outside the factory premises amounts to misconduct.
An employer has the right to take disciplinary action against an employee for such misconduct.
The employer can expect good conduct from its employees at all places, even outside its premises.
