Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Award - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Legal validity - Workman employed as part-time chowkidar - Requests reinstatement with full backwages - It was not feasible to ignore the irregularity of a chowkidar, therefore this situation qualifies for an exception to the general rule, and thus, the compensation awarded is appropriate.
