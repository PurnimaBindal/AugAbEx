Forest Departments Standing Orders 1973 - Work Charge Rules, 1964, Rules 27, 28, 29 - Semi permanent employee - Order of dismissal - Acquittal in criminal case subsequently - Implication of - The fact finding report cannot be equated with inquiry report under disciplinary rules as charge-sheet was not given, opportunity for the respondent to respond was not provided and witness was not allowed to present evidence - The respondents should have waited till criminal case concluded - Punishment based on fact finding report cannot be considered as per law - The impugned order is illegal, arbitrary, and without any foundation - As per GM Tank's case judgment by Apex Court, petitioner must be reinstated with back wages from date of acquittal, continuity of service and granting of related benefits.
