Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Rajasthan Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rule 39 Expl 2 - Examination - Non-appearance - Declared fail - Discharge - If a candidate did not appear due to unavoidable circumstances, is it considered an attempt at the examination?
- The petitioner did not abandon the examination, therefore, it cannot be said that he made an attempt - The order of discharge is not valid.
[Paras 9, 10 and 11]
