Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 324(6) - Representation of the People Act, 1951, Section 159(2) - Election duty of Govt. employees - As per the 1998 amendment in Section 159 of the Representation of People Act, employees from institutions wholly or substantially financed directly or indirectly by the State or Central Government can be assigned for election duty, in addition to Govt. employees.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 324(6) - Representation of the People Act, 1951, Section 159(2) - Election duty of Govt. employees - To avoid assigning seniors below juniors or to those in lower pay scales, duty must be assigned as per pay scales.
However, this is not practically feasible as the pay scale does not accurately indicate the status of an employee.
There is no concept of seniority or status for one day's election duty.
