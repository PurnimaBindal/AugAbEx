In accordance with Clause 3 of the Allotment of Government Residence (General Pool, New Delhi) Rules 1963, an employee who jointly owns a house in Delhi with his brother and has rented out his own house to a tenant with the permission of the Rent Controller under section 21 of the Delhi Rent Act is not eligible for the immediate possession of the property after the expiration of the lease agreement.
Moreover, the employee is not responsible for damages for the use and occupation of Government accommodation.
