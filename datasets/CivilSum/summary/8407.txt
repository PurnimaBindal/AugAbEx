Constitution of India, Articles 136, 141 - Summary dismissal of SLP under Article 136 of Constitution - Supreme Court does not lay down any law as envisaged by Article 141 by such dismissal- No inference drawn from dismissal in limine by non-speaking order that contentions raised in SLP on merits have been rejected by Supreme Court.
