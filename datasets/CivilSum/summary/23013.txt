Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bombay Municipal Corporation Act, 1988, Section 83 - Dismissal/Removal from service - Clause (d) of proviso (2) of Section 83 states that officers earning monthly emoluments exceeding Rs. 400/- could not be dismissed from service by the Commissioner without prior approval of the standing committee - Although the Act does not provide for the punishment of removal, the appellant received a show cause notice for dismissal and was eventually removed from service - The impugned order of punishment, though it used the term removal from service, was actually an order of dismissal from service - As it was not approved by the standing committee, this order was clearly illegal and incorrect.
The court must look beyond the form of the order to understand its true character.
[Paras 2 and 8]
