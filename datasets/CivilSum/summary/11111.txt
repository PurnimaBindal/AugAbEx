Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment Regularisation of service - Policy decision - The Government announced a policy decision to regularize the services of ad hoc appointees who served for a specified period as ad hoc employees.
Termination of the petitioner's services after providing nine years of service without any reservation, without the respondents taking action according to the government's policy decision for the petitioner's service regularization, cannot be a basis for issuing an adverse order against the petitioner for his appointment in service without his name being recommended by the Employment Exchange or without his case being considered through open market competition.
The petitioner had gained sufficient experience that the respondents should have taken note of, and as a result, the requirement through the Employment Exchange should have been waived or applications from the open market should have been invited.
The impugned order terminating the petitioner's services is quashable, and the respondents must consider the petitioner's case for regular service.
