Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bombay Police Act, 1951, Sections 25 and 5(b) - Bombay Police (Punishments and Appeals) Rules, 1956 - Bombay Police Manual, Para 436 - Punishment - Punishment of decrease of one increment from existing pay - Reduction in pay prescribed as one of the penalties which can be imposed by para 36(2)(c) of Bombay Police Manual - Order upheld.

Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Bombay Police Act, 1951, Sections 25 and 5(b) - Bombay Police Manual, Para 454(2) - Disciplinary enquiry - Defence Assistant - Services of a Police Officer chosen by the appellant not allowed as Defence Assistant on the basis that it would disrupt the administration of the concerned Police Station - Appellant failed to indicate alternative choice within given period - Appellant did not make any grievance in reply to second show-cause notice - Point raised for the first time in the High Court - Proceedings not vitiated.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Bombay Police Act, 1951, Sections 25 and 5(b) - Disciplinary enquiry - Charge-sheet - Evidence/Witness - Person not cited in the charge-sheet examined during enquiry - Summoned only to produce a Diary - Failure to mention his name as witness in the charge-sheet, of no consequence - No prejudice caused to appellant.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Bombay Police Act, 1951, Sections 25 and 5(b) - Disciplinary enquiry - Defence witnesses - Natural justice - Some of the defence witnesses cited by the appellant were not examined because their evidence was not considered to be relevant - Adequate opportunity to defend was not denied to appellant.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Bombay Police Act, 1951, Sections 25 and 5(b) - Disciplinary enquiry - Enquiry Officer - Cross-examination of witness by Enquiry Officer himself - No objection to the Enquiry Officer putting some questions - No prejudice caused to appellant - Enquiry not vitiated.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Bombay Police Act, 1951, Sections 25 and 5(b) - Disciplinary enquiry - Misconduct - Charges levelled that the appellant made false allegations and used improper language when he was asked to explain his conduct - No merit in appeal - Charges made out from the allegations - Appeal dismissed.
