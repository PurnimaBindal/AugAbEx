The Constitution of India, Article 226/227 pertains to an industrial dispute that was raised, but the Appropriate Government refused to refer the case for adjudication, citing that it was not suitable for reference to the Industrial Tribunal or Labour Court.
The order was challenged, and it was noted that the order of the appropriate government was correct and valid, as the petitioner failed to fulfill the requirement of recruitment rules and could not be appointed to the post of driver.
Therefore, interference is not warranted.
