Criminal Procedure Code, 1972, Section 433-A Premature release - If an accused was acquitted by a lower court before S. 433-A came into force but was later convicted by an appellate court after it was enforced, they are entitled to the benefit of remission prevalent prior to S. 433-A.

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 433-A (as it came into force on 18121978) - Premature release - A life convict who was sentenced before S. 433-A came into force cannot be considered for premature release until completing 14 years of actual sentence, and Para-516 B of Punjab Jail Manual and Haryana Govt. instructions contained in Memo No. 43/19/83 JJ(2) dated 27-2-1984 do not apply in this case.
