Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - Natural justice - Reference - Second order on reference - The Government may make a reference to the Labour Court without notice to the employer after refusing to make a reference upon the representation or second demand notice of the dispute.
The reference shall not be invalid only because notice was not given to the employer before the reference was made at the first instance.
[Para 4]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - The Government may not decline to make a reference to the Labour Court on the basis that the workman did not work for more than 240 days.
Such matter is decided by the Court based on the evidence of the parties.
