According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 21, and the Constitution of Jammu & Kashmir, Section 124, there are rules pertaining to compassionate appointment.
Clause (ii) and (iii) of Sub-Rule 2 of the Compassionate Appointment Rules, 1994, detail the criteria for those who die in specified circumstances such as militancy related action or enemy action.
However, the death of a Salesman in the Food and Supplies Department in harness is not covered by these clauses.
It is only the Head of the Department who is competent to make compassionate appointments, and Deputy Commissioners do not have this power.
Any appointments made by Deputy Commissioners under these circumstances would violate Art. 14 and 16 of the Constitution and cannot be sustained.
