Constitution of India, Article 133 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14 - The dispute raised in this appeal was resolved by a three Judge Bench in AIR 1998 SC 2073, which ruled that only casual laborers working on open lines, not those working on projects, can receive the benefits - Respondents claim that they are all open line casual laborers, and that the tribunal mischaracterized them as project casual laborers – However, they are allowed to approach the tribunal to correct any factual errors in the judgment - No relief can be granted on this point in the civil appeal.
