Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - Correction of - Petitioner's date of birth was entered in their service book based on a certificate from the church.
They wanted to change it based on another certificate issued by the same church near their retirement.
However, according to government regulations, correction applications must be made within five years of entry into service.
Therefore, the petitioner's claim was rejected under the given circumstances.
