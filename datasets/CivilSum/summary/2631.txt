According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, pay fixation on promotion should not result in a lower pay scale for the promottee.
Additionally, Major General is a higher rank than Brigadier, and therefore entitled to a higher pay scale.
[Para 10]
As per Fundamental Rules, Rule 22(I)(a)(1), when a Brigadier is promoted to the rank of Major General, their pay should be fixed one step above their previous pay as a Brigadier, as the Major General position carries duties of higher responsibilities.
[Para 10]
The Government of India Orders at SAI 2/S/98 state that both paras 9(c) and 12(c) allow for Major Generals to be entitled to a higher pay scale than Brigadiers based on logic.
Therefore, when promoted from Brigadier, a Major General's pay should be fixed one step above the previous pay of the Brigadier rank.
