According to Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 19, if a government servant is convicted of a criminal charge, the Disciplinary Authority can take action against them.
The rule does not state that if the Appellate Court suspends the sentence, the order of dismissal based on conviction will be obliterated and the government servant will be treated as suspended.
The order of dismissal from service still stands, even if the sentence is suspended and the accused is enlarged on bail.
The suspension only allows the accused to avoid execution of sentence for the duration it is in effect, but the conviction remains until it is set aside.
