According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution and Rule 4 of the Punjab Government's National Emergency Rules of 1965, military service should not have prevented the petitioner's promotion in the Vidhan Sabha Secretariat.
Whether or not the 1965 Rules were applicable is not relevant as the petitioner should have been considered for promotion when his juniors were.
