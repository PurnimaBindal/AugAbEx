Kerala Service Rules, Rule 33 - Increment - Petitioners were never regularised in provisional service as HS As, so they cannot be granted an increment after termination of provisional service as HS A. and regular service as primary departmental teachers was followed without regular appointment in the same category of post.
[Paras 3 and 4]  B Refund - Petitioners were not at fault and administrative authority granted them an increment despite a wrong fixation of pay.
Therefore, it is arbitrary to make petitioners liable to refund the amount, and instead, the respondent should refix the pay without any liability for refund.
