Constitution of India, Article 300A - Maharashtra Mathadi, Hamal & Other Manual Workers (Regulation of Employment and Welfare) Act, 1969, Sections 6 and 13 - Payment of dearness allowance - The issue of whether the payment is of a statutory nature is raised - The purpose of dearness allowance is to ensure that workers' purchasing power is not eroded due to a rise in the cost of living - This allowance must be paid to all registered employees, regardless of how it is calculated.
