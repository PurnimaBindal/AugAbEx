According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221, if a constable is absent without discipline, and is unlikely to be a good police offer, the Superintendent of Police can pass an order under this rule to terminate the person.
This order is not intended to be punitive, but earlier decisions suggest that it may have that effect if based on absence from duty.
An appeal has been dismissed on this basis.
