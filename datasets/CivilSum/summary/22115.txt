According to the Coal India Ltd Common Coal Cadre Rules, Clauses 4 and 47 and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, deemed promotion or regularisation cannot be granted without a valid promotion order passed by the competent authority as per Rules.
As a result, no writ of mandamus can be issued to the authorities to regularise a person on a promotion post, and there is no legal right to hold and continue holding such a post.
