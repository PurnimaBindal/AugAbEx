Constitution of India, Article 16 - AP Police Service Rules, 1966, Rule 5 - Appointment - The date of selection is the date when the advertisement calling upon candidates to apply is issued - candidates must have a specific and determinate date to attain the minimum or maximum age for both applying and for the recruiting agency to scrutinise applications.
