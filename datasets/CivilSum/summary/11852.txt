Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 17(1) (A) and Section 15(3) - Appeal - Certifical of deposit of amount of compensation - Employers have a statutory right to appeal which cannot be restricted by additional directions from the Authority under the Act to deposit the compensation amount within 30 days of the order.
The Act only requires that the appeal be accompanied by a certificate of deposit of the awarded amount.
It is not necessary to deposit the amount within 30 days.
An appeal that is accompanied by the certificate of deposit is valid.
