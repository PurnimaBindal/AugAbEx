Evidence Act, Section 114 - Estoppel - Promissory estoppel - Promissory estoppel only applies when there is a promise made by one party and the other party relied on it and changed their position.
If there was no promise, promissory estoppel does not apply.
[Para 6] B. Punjab Civil Service Rules, Rule 718 - Pension/Contractual appointments/Conditions of service - Retainership of Assistant Advocate General is determined by Rule 718 of the Civil Services Rules, but this does not mean that the incumbent is not entitled to a pension.
As a contractual appointment, the State Government can approve additional benefits and facilities.
Once such allowances and facilities are approved by competent authority, the State Government is obligated to implement them.
In the appointment letter of the petitioner, the protection of pension and telephone expenditure at their residence was approved, which the State Government must grant.
