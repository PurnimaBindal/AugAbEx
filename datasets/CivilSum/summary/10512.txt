Constitution of India, Article 14 - Criminal Procedure Code, Section 197 - General Clauses Act, Section 21 - Sanction for prosecution of government servant - Review - Natural justice - Following the use of Section 197 Cr.PC, Section 21 of General Clauses Act cannot be called upon by the Government to withdraw the sanction - No review provisions exist for the order passed by the Government using both administrative and quasi-judicial power, unless following natural justice principles and including a reason for the review - The Government cannot review the administrative and quasi-judicial orders provided all material has been considered and decisions made with due care.
[Paras 11 to 14]
