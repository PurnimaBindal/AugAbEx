Constitution of India Article 226 regarding the date of promotion can be revised.
In this particular case, the petitioner's date of promotion was revised from 24.8.1972 to 2111973 by the respondent.
However, since the petitioner belongs to a reserved category and the third vacancy for the reserve category happened on 111973, it is held that the petitioner cannot claim 24.8.1972 as the deemed date of promotion since no vacancy occurred on that date.
