Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944 - Medical reimbursement - The order refusing medical reimbursement to the petitioner is overturned.
The petitioner had to opt for fixed medical allowance of Rs. 100/- as the area where he was residing was not covered by CGHS.
Those availing fixed medical allowance of Rs. 100/- would be entitled to hospitalization facilities provided under these rules.
The parent office must scrutinize the claims for medical reimbursement for those opting for fixed medical allowance under the rules, as in the case of serving employees.
The respondents are directed to reimburse petitioner's medical expenses during his treatment in DMC, Ludhiana, as per rates fixed by the Central Government under the Rules.
[Paras 11 and 12]
