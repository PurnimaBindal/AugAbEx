Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Indira Vikas Patra Rules, 1986, Rule 8 - Pensionary benefits - The petitioner, who was 73 years old at the time, received Indira Vikas Patra in lieu of the arrears of revised pensionary benefits which were accrued to him, but lost them - IVPs are not named, and can be cashed by anyone in any post office, but verification must occur before payment - The issuing post office will be notified if an IVP is presented for encashment in another post office - The respondents are instructed to pay the petitioner for the lost IVPs after obtaining an indemnity bond, as the IVPs have not yet been encashed by anyone else.
