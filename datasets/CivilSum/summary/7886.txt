Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental and criminal proceedings - If an employee is acquitted in criminal proceedings due to a technicality, their employer cannot take further action against them through departmental proceedings.
[Para 6] B.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental proceedings - If there is a delay in issuing a charge-sheet, it can be explained by the pendency of criminal proceedings pertaining to the same subject matter and the employee's acquittal on a technical ground.
This delay is considered a valid reason for delaying the institution of departmental proceedings.
