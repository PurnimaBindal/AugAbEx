The Bank of India Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations 1976, Regulations 4(j) and 11, are not obligated to hold disciplinary proceedings regarding the dismissal of a petitioner convicted under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947.
The employee's conduct is relevant, and a rule allowing for termination without an investigation does not violate Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India.
Furthermore, the suspension of the sentence does not negate the petitioner's conviction, and the existence of an appeal does not change this.
