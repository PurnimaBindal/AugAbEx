Article 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India state that an executive action must be supported by reasons and cannot be arbitrary, as that goes against the principle of equality in Article 14.
Terminating someone's employment as 'no longer required' without any explanation is an arbitrary termination and can be annulled.
[Paras 7 to 9] B.
When an appointment is made based on a contract, it cannot be terminated before the contractual period ends without proper reasons.
[Para 9] C If an employer has multiple projects and is advertising for new appointments, terminating the services of existing employees as 'no longer required' is arbitrary.
[Para 9] D. If a termination order is based on charges and complaints, it must be preceded by a hearing.
[Para 10] E. The term of a contractual appointment cannot be extended by the court; it ends on the date specified.
[Para 11] F. Concept of superannuation is irrelevant to fixed-term contractual appointments.
[Para 13] G. An employee cannot claim reinstatement after expiry of the contractual term but can be compensated for the remaining duration of the contract.
