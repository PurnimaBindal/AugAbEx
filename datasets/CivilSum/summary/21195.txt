Admission to Dental Medical Courses - Process of admissions - Entrance Test - The authorities are required to set approximate dates for the entrance examination, giving colleges enough time for regulating admissions.
Students may have to complete more than one examination for admission purposes.
Colleges must have sufficient opportunities to select the date of entrance examination, leading to a final date of admission for the college.

Admission to Dental Medical Courses - Process of admissions - Entrance Test - If irregularities are discovered in the entrance test process, the court is not authorized to instruct the authorities to fill seats from the select list.
Instead, a new test must be conducted to provide all applicants with a fair chance.
