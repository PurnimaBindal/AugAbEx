Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Dismissal from service - Inquiry dispensing - Although reasons for dispensing with the inquiry must exist, courts are not to examine them.
However, the reason must have some connection with the proposed action.
Sub-Inspector was dismissed from service upon information that he was linked with terrorists and had agreed to coordinate with them.
Conducting an inquiry was not practical.
The challenged order must not be interfered.
