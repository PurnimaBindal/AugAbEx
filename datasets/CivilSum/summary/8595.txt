Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - Petitioner challenged transfer from Govt. Mohindra College, Patiala to Govt. Ranbir College, Sangrur - Grounds for challenge were repeated transfers over last 3 years and alleged mala fide intent of Respondent No. 3 due to impending superannuation - Held, previous transfers were initiated by petitioner's request and current transfer was due to a couple case - Argument that Respondent No. 3 had ulterior motives is baseless - Petition dismissed.
