Punjab Educational Services, Class III (School Cadre) Rules, 1955, Rules 2 and 4 - Regularisation of Services/Qualifications/Change of conditions of service - The petitioner, who was appointed as a Shastri and OT in 1976, made a representation for regularisation based on the policy decision of Punjab government dated 28101980.
The policy stated that ad hoc employees were to be regularised after completing three years of continuous service.
However, the petitioner refused regularisation, stating that he was not qualified as per the revised qualifications for the post.
Nevertheless, the appointing authority was competent enough to waive the conditions of minimum qualifications, and if the petitioner was lacking in some qualifications, it would be considered as waived at the time of his appointment.
Even though later amendments in the rules prescribed higher qualifications, it would not disentitle the petitioner from claiming the benefit of the policy decision.
Therefore, directions were issued to regularise the petitioner's services.
