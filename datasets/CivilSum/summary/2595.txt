The Civil Courts Act, 1977, Sections 30(3) and 30(4) deal with appointment of Ministerial Officers of Subordinate Courts.
There is a question whether the power of control under Section 30(4) conferred to Chief Justice (CJ) for appointment is dependent on or independent of Section 30(3).
It is also debatable whether the power of appointment vested in District Judge can be regulated by CJ by exercising power under 30(4) in absence of provision of mode and method of recruitment.
Additionally, whether the CJ has power on administrative side to constitute Selection Committees, issue orders, instructions, guidelines and circulars to regulate method of recruitment of ministerial officers is a contentious issue.
To resolve these questions, the matter has been placed to a larger bench for decision.
