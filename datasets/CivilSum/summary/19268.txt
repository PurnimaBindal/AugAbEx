Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Pension - The petitioner received a certificate from his co-prisoners in Central Jail, Lahore, which was verified by the Punjab Govt. to recommend his case for grant of Swatantrata Sainik Pension under the scheme.
Other individuals were also granted pension on the basis of the same certificates.
As a result, the government was instructed to grant the petitioner a pension under the 1980 scheme.
