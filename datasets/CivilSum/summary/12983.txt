As per the Constitution of India, Article 16 and UP Intermediate Education Act (2 of 1921), Sections 16-G, 15, and Regulations framed under the Act, Chapter 3, Regn.2, in case of a post falling vacant under promotion quota, it cannot be filled up by making an appointment on a compassionate ground from promotion quota.
However, the compassionate appointee could be considered against the posts which are meant to be filled by direct recruitment.
[Paras 8 and 10]
