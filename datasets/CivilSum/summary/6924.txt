Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, Schedule 2, Item 6 - Daily wager - Termination of service - Ground of misconduct - Termination order without salary in lieu of notice - Enquiry initiated after termination - According to Paras 14 and 15, subsequent enquiry cannot make termination legal and valid, as termination was violative of principles of natural justice and provisions of Section 25-F stand violated since termination order was not accompanied by salary in lieu of notice and notice was not proper.
