The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 309, 14, and 16, forbid employee classification and discrimination based on involuntary sterilization, special increments, cut-off dates, and sterilization operations.
The government forcibly subjected all workers to sterilization after having two children and provided increments only to those who underwent the operation before a specific date.
The classification is illogical and unrelated to the objective, and the clause violates Article 14 of the constitution, and is therefore void.
