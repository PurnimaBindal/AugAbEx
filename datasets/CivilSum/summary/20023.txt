Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 311 - Reversion - Natural justice - The Appellants, who were Permanent Way Mistries, were suddenly reverted to the post of Gangmen without any opportunity to defend themselves - The Tribunal incorrectly believed that they were ad hoc employees, and upheld their dismissal - However, the Tribunal failed to correctly understand the arguments put forth before it, making a fundamental error - As a result, the orders are set aside, and the case is sent back for reconsideration with regards to the specific contentions presented, in accordance with the law.
