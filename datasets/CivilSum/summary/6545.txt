Constitution of India, Article 226 - Age of Retirement - Enhancement of - Petitioner working as engineer in respondent-corporation - The rules before the report of the Fifth Pay Commission stated the age of retirement as 58 years - The Fifth Pay Commission recommended an enhancement of the retirement age to sixty years for Government servants - An office memorandum forwarded to the respondent-corporation but they did not take any action to amend their rules - Retirement letter was issued to the petitioner at age 58, which was not appropriate - The petitioner has the entitlement to continue in service until he attains the age of 60 years according to the rules.
[Paras 1, 2 and 4]
