The petitioner, promoted as Director, is entitled to an increment of higher responsibility and the same emoluments/pay scale as other promotees, regardless of which stream they came from, in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The petitioner has a vested right to the emoluments attached to the post of Director and cannot have them taken away by a proposed alteration in the rules.
Rule 26A cannot be used to fix the petitioner's initial pay on their promoted post in the pay scale appropriate to the post from which they were promoted.
