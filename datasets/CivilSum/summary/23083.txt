Rule 2(B) of the Bombay Civil Services Classification and Recruitment Rules, 1939 covers inter-se seniority between PSC selectee employees and non-PSC employees for appointments to ministerial posts in Govt. Offices.
Non-PSC selectee employees were appointed on an ad hoc basis without going through the selection process for PSC in 1962 and 1965.
However, in 1974 a Govt.
Resolution declared that direct recruitment to said ministerial posts should be made on competitive examination held by PSC and PSC-selected candidates working in various offices should be treated as senior to non-PSC persons.
The seniority lists were revised to reflect this resolution and the impugned order of the High Court was set aside.
[Paras 8, 10, 11, 14, 16]

Supreme Court directions cannot be considered as obiter dicta.
[Para 15]

Service jurisprudence dictates that appointments of incumbents who did not belong to the stream of regularly and lawfully appointed candidates through the Public Service Commission, should only be regularised when their stream joins the regular stream.
Bombay Civil Services classification and Recruitment Rules, 1939, Rule 2(B) covers inter-se seniority between ad hoc appointees and selected appointees.
