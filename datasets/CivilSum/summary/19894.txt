Constitution of India, Article 16 - Anticipated vacancies/ Appointment/Termination for want of vacancies - Service of the Petitioner (Constables in Haryana Armed Forces) terminated for want of vacancies - Court held that vacancies are always filled in anticipation of future vacancies and provision in budget is made.
Respondents should have observed all procedural formalities before discharging petitioners.
Therefore, they cannot claim that petitioners were discharged for want of vacancies.
