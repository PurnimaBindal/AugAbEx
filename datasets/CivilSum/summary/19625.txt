According to Statute 15(2) and (3) in Volume 1 of the Punjabi University Calendar 1990, it is the responsibility of the Registrar to maintain a seniority list for all employees of the University.
However, if a request is made, the Syndicate is responsible for determining seniority.
Despite this, it is admitted that no seniority list has been maintained in accordance with the Statute and promotions have been made without consideration of seniority.
The Syndicate has been directed to address pending representations and interested parties are prohibited from participating in meetings.
Promotions will be reconsidered once seniority is determined.
