The petitioner in this case is a JBT Teacher who was transferred on her own request and lost her seniority in the District.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14/16/Seniority/Provincialised Cadre states that her seniority must be fixed at the tail end of the seniority list of the provincialised cadre and not in the State cadre.
Directions have been issued to fix her seniority in the provincialised cadre and consider her case for the grant of selection grade.
