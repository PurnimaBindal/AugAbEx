Constitution of India, Article 226 - Re-appointment - Employer and employee relationship - A Bus Conductor was dismissed from their services, but their appeal was allowed by the first appellate authority who ordered their re-appointment as a fresh conductor and clarified that their earlier service period would not be considered.
The relationship will be governed by a fresh contract, and the earlier misconduct will be wiped out.
Respondent No. 2 waived the right to punish the conductor after the re-appointment.
