Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection committee - Post of Primary School Teachers - The qualifications and vacancies of Graduate Trained Teachers and Primary School Teachers were different and therefore, two different Selection Committees were constituted for selecting candidates.
It is not permissible for one Selection Committee to recommend the names for appointment in respect of another category of candidates.
[Para 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Appointment - The decision not to fill up vacancies has to be taken bona fide for appropriate reasons.
If the state fills up the vacancies, the comparative merit of the candidate should be respected.
No discrimination in this regard should be permitted.
[Para 14]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ petition - The question of limitation should not be raised for the first time in writ applications.
It is a mixed question of law and fact.
[Para 20]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - The matter regarding the issuance of a fresh notification for appointments requires re-consideration at the hands of the Tribunal afresh.
The matter is remanded back for decision afresh.
[Paras 30 and 31]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - No person should be appointed for a post for which they are not suitable or if they have not been able to secure the minimum qualification mark.
The select list must be adhered to, and all appointments must be made on merit in terms of the correct select list.
Any appointments made contrary to the well-settled legal principles should be set aside.
