Maharashtra Zilla Parishads District Services (Recruitment) Rules, 1967, Rule 6(4), Item 8 of Appendix IV (Part II) to Rule 5 - Promotion - Vacancies - Implementation of Resolution No. 1070-F dated 22101970 - Whilst the resolution allows for Zilla Parishads to upgrade existing Primary School Teacher posts to Head Master positions, this does not automatically convert the posts - Approval from the Chief Executive Officer is required, and the High Court incorrectly directed the implementation of a decision that was not final regarding the entitlement to Head Master appointments - The Executive Officer is instructed to make a final decision within 3 months.
