Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Admission - Admission to Higher Course of studies under University - The University, under the direction of the court, published in four newspapers that students from `Patrachar Vidyalaya' should not be permitted to enroll for higher courses in the university - Additionally, a circular was issued to all affiliated colleges and institutions - The petitioner did not disclose on their admission application to the college that they passed through `Patrachar Vidyalaya' - It was held that the University could not be held responsible for disallowing the petitioner from pursuing higher studies in college - The University Authority must make a decision regarding the equivalence of certificates.
