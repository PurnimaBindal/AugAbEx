The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, were cited in a claim for regularisation despite non-regularisation because of a perceived lack of medical fitness.
Candidates personally appeared in court and only a speech impediment was cited as a physical disability.
The petitioner did not hide their condition and therefore the principle of estoppel would apply.
The court has ruled in favor of the petitioners' entitlement to regularisation.
