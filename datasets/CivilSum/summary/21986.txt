[Para 14] B Interpretation of statute - Judges must interpret the words, phrases, and provisions of the statute by explaining them and not defining them.
They interpret the statute, and their words should not be interpreted as statutes.
[Para 10] C. Precedent - Court judgments are not to be interpreted as statutes due to the circumstantial flexibility where one additional or different fact may change the conclusion in two cases.
The courts should not rely on decisions without discussing how the factual situations fit into the decision on which they relied.
Disposing of cases blindly by relying on decisions is improper.
