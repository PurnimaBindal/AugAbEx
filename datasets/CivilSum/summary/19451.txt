Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Suspension - Increment - Increments during period of suspension - A petitioner who is placed under suspension as departmental enquiry is contemplated against him must receive a subsistence allowance of 75% of their salary upon the expiry of six months.
Even if a criminal case has been instituted against the petitioner, as long as no order has been passed by the competent authority to withhold or stop the petitioner's increments, they are entitled to receive them.
This includes the annual increment, as stated by the petitioner and outlined in the rules.
