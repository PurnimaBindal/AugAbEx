SK Bhanot, DAG for the respondent, cites Constitution of India, Article 162, regarding the entitlement to pension and gratuity.
Punjab Government had decided to grant pension to teachers of Provincialised Local Body Schools on the basis of length of service and in accordance with the pension rule.
However, no death-cum-retirement gratuity was to be paid.
The petitioner is entitled to pension only from the time when the government extended pensionary benefits to such employees and not from the date of his retirement.
For pension fixation, the entire service rendered by the petitioner including that rendered with Provincialised Local Body School has to be counted.
The respondents are directed to refix the petitioner's pension accordingly and release the arrears.
