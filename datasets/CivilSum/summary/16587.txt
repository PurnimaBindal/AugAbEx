Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - A charge sheet was pending against the petitioner and his juniors were promoted on June 30, 1999 - the petitioner was not considered for promotion to the post of Assistant Executive Engineer.
However, the charges pending against the petitioner were dropped on March 28, 2003 - Accordingly, the petitioner should have been promoted with effect from June 30, 1999.
