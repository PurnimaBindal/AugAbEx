Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226, 21 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(iv) - Trainee - Absence from duty - Termination without notice - Sustainability - Rule 13(3)(iv) does not provide for a show cause notice before termination - However, according to the due process of law as envisaged in Article 21 of the constitution, a right of hearing must necessarily be provided - Therefore, the Commanding Officer cannot terminate the service of a recruit without providing the minimum opportunity of hearing in the form of a show cause notice - Furthermore, in accordance with the instructions dated 28121988, action to terminate can only be taken after the service of a show cause notice, as decided by the High Court in Surinder Singh Sihag's case - As a result, the order has been set aside.
