Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner was deemed healthy upon joining the army but was medically discharged within three years - His medical category was marked as "EEE" with a cause of death listed as "Generalized Seizure-345" - The court attributed the cause of death to military service and not constitutional factors - Hence, the petitioner is eligible to receive disability pension from the date of his discharge.
