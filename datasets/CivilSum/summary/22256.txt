The petitioner in a case involving Army Act, 1950, Section 164 and Constitution of India, Article 226 contended that his writ petition against his dismissal from service and sentencing 2 years in RI by Court Martial was delayed because he had filed a representation to Army authorities against punishment, which was not decided for 10 years.
However, the court found that the delay and laches were too great, and the writ petition was correctly dismissed.
