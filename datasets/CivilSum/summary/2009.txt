According to the Andhra Pradesh Judicial Ministerial Service Rules, Rule 5, Division IV, Category 5 and G.O.M.
No. 182, dated 1771987, those who are promoted or working as Junior Assistants on the basis of additional qualifications are granted additional increments.
The petitioners were first promoted to Junior Assistants, but were later posted as Typists due to their typing knowledge.
They also obtained a law degree.
However, their juniors who were promoted to Junior Assistants received the benefit of additional increments based on the same qualifications.
This denial of benefits to the petitioners would be discriminatory and violate Article 14 since they are posted on equivalent posts to Junior Assistants.
