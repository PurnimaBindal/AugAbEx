The petitioner sought direction to be considered for the post of Professor in Ras-Shastra in the Government Ayurvedic College, Patiala, citing his seniority to the 4th respondent who was promoted to the post instead.
The respondent argued that the petitioner was working in the PG Department where there was no post of Professor in Ras-Shastra, while the 4th respondent, although junior, was working in the Under Graduate Department where such post was available.
However, after an evaluation of the rules governing the service, it was found that there was no distinction between the PG and UG departments in Ayurveda, and they were considered to be one and the same.
As such, since both the petitioner and the 4th respondent worked in the same college and belonged to the same service, and the petitioner was senior to the 4th respondent, the petitioner should be considered for the post of Professor in Ras-Shastra.
