Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ appeal - Latches - Appeal point raised for first time - Delay of six years in filing the writ petition - Final seniority list published in 1982 - Writ petition against seniority list filed after six years - Only a statutory representation can condone the delay - Stale claim not entertained - Judgment set aside, Petition dismissed - Plea of latches - Legal ground - Can be raised for the first time in an appeal.
