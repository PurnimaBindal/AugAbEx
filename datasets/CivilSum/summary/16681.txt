According to the Constitution of India, including Articles 39(e), 41, 43(3), 16 and 21, widows are entitled to receive family pension for two years after the death of their husband.
However, a widow has been denied her legal right to this pension for the past 44 years without any justification.
The authority has been ordered to provide the widow with a provisional pension of Rs. 50,000/- and a monthly family pension of Rs. 200/-.
