Constitution of India, Article 32 - Bifurcation - Staff Artists at All India Radio - While considering Staff Artists as a category it is important to remember their unique status - They are a class of individuals who possess a special talent or skill in their respective art forms - This group of people cannot be compared to regular Government employees in every aspect - The success of All India Radio and Doordarshan depends on the quality of artists they employ to produce their programs - The Scheme includes a transitional period - If there are skilled Artists who do not wish to be classified as Government employees, there is no immediate reason to terminate their contracts - The transitional period can be extended to accommodate artists who are unwilling to become Government servants.
