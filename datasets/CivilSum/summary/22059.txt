Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, Rule 27(c) - Seniority - The Kerala Public Service Commission must determine inter se seniority between departmental candidates and open market candidates in accordance with its letter of effective advice.
A letter of provisional appointment that specifies that advice is only provisional and that only acting appointments were made cannot be considered a letter of effective advice.
Only the Commission's letter preparing the final list can be considered effective advice.
The seniority list prepared by the Commission cannot be rejected.
[Paras 7, 8, 9 and 11] B Natural justice - Seniority - The appointment letter specifies the method of fixing seniority after the final appointment letter is issued and taking into account seniority of other category candidates.
The communication of the provisional appointment letter cannot be denied and the conditions in it cannot be claimed as without notice.
