Employees working in field offices are not entitled to overtime allowance or additional wages for working on Saturdays on field duty as they are already receiving extra benefits such as field holidays and extra casual leaves while working six days a week.
This decision has been rightfully made as their case does not fall under the Municipal Employees Union (Registered) Sirhind and others v. State of Punjab and others, 2000(9) SCC 432 ruling.
