Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - The Labour Court awarded respondent No. 2 a minor punishment of censure, despite an enquiry officer finding her not guilty of misappropriation of funds but guilty of not following instructions and directly corresponding with the Chief Minister.
The misconduct was not serious enough to warrant termination of services.
The petitioner challenged the Labour Court's decision, but the HC declined to interfere once it determined that the Labour Court had exercised its discretion properly - the petition was dismissed.
