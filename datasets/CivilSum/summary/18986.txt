Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjabi University Calendar, Volume II, Clause 24 - Correction of Result - Effect of - Challenging the reduction and revision of his BA III result, the petitioner was admitted based on his old mark sheet as the respondents' action was questioned - Respondents argued that the petitioner was already aware before taking the LLB entrance exam that his result card needed to be corrected - They even sent a letter addressing this issue before the entrance test was held - The law does not protect a person who knowingly disregards correct facts for his gain and later tries to retract - The petitioner cannot urge his case after several years of ignoring the facts - He cannot be permitted to continue with his studies - The petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 11 & 12]
