The Constitution of India's Article 311 outlines the principles of natural justice and removal from service.
A driver, who refused to drive a train and was later removed from services for causing an accident by a less familiar driver, had his removal order set aside by a tribunal on the grounds of violating those principles.
Although the respondent had undisputedly admitted to the charges, the tribunal did not justify the interference.
However, due to the lack of charges against him in his earlier long service, the punishment of removal was converted to compulsory retirement.
