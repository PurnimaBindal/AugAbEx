The Public Service Commission (Composition and Condition of Service of Members and Staff) Regulations, 1957 (Kerala) Rule 4 states that only those who serve in the office are entitled to payment of DA, and not those who have vacated the office.
Therefore, the Petitioner cannot claim the benefit of DA, even if gratuity is payable as per notification.
The contention that denial of payment of DA to the Petitioner is discriminatory and violative of Rule 4 of Regulations and Article 318 of Constitution is not upheld, as Article 318 of the Constitution does not apply to retired individuals like the Petitioner.
Thus, the Petition is dismissed.
