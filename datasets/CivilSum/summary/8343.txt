Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, Section 29(2) - C.C.S.
(Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 21(2) - Customary divorce - Second marriage - The petitioner legally obtained a divorce from their first wife according to community custom before marrying their second wife - The Civil Court confirmed the dissolution of the first marriage - The petitioner was not required to seek permission from the department for the second marriage - The CAT's order to reduce punishment was quashed in light of the Civil Court's order - The petitioner's legal heirs are entitled to pensionary benefits - Time of compulsory retirement is considered time on duty.
