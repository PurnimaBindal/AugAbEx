Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Absence from duty - Despite sending a telegram to request an extension of leave due to illness, the petitioner's absence of 50 days was considered the gravest act, resulting in their dismissal - The Rule 16.2 states that dismissal can only be awarded for the gravest act of misconduct or as a cumulative effect of continued misconduct indicating incorrigibility and complete unfitness for police service - The length of service of the petitioner was not taken into consideration, and the authorities failed to apply their minds- As a result, the order of dismissal was quashed.
