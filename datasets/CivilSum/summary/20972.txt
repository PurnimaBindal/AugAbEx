The unfair means in the examination cannot be presumed just by finding a piece of paper near the candidate in the examination hall.
It should be relevant to the exam and can be attributed to the candidate.
A successful candidate who performs well in all examinations must have a report of malpractice detected in the examination hall signed by the invigilator.
Even though the center superintendent signed it, who was absent from the hall, the piece of paper recovered was not relevant to the exam.
Hence, nothing can be associated with the candidate, and he would be considered to have passed that exam.
