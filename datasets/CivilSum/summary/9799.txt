Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Act, 1971, Section 7 - Re-hearing of appeal - Whether amounts to review - Maintainability - The Commissioner and Secretary to the Government of Haryana has issued an order for the rehearing of the Respondent's appeal by the Director Education Haryana.
However, this direction goes against the Provision of Section 7 of the Act and cannot be sustained as it nullifies the decision of the Civil and Appellate Court.
Therefore, the order by the Commissioner and Secretary is not within their statutory jurisdiction and the petition is dismissed.
