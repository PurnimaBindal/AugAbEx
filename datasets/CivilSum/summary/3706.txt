The Writ court has limited power in admission decisions for PhD programs, as it will not interfere with academic experts unless there is a violation of natural justice principles or statutory laws.
Courts have also imposed restrictions on their discretionary powers in such decisions.
[Paras 11-14] B.
If a person is admitted through an old scheme and a new scheme is introduced, they cannot claim benefits under the new scheme and must follow the rules and regulations of the old scheme.
