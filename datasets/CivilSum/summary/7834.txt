Constitution of India, Article 316(1-A) - Appointment - The respondent was appointed as incharge chairman of public service commission 'till further orders' in the absence of the petitioner Chairman who was taken into custody by the CBI in connection with a criminal case relating to irregularities in Engineering Entrance Examination.
The petitioner Chairman neither placed under suspension nor was a reference made by the President to the Supreme Court.
The absence of the petitioner-chairman came to an end after he was released on bail and reported to the office to resume his duties.
The continuance of respondent cannot be said to be in consonance with Clause (1-A) of Article 316.
Clause 'till further orders' in notification is not in accordance with Article 316(1-A) and therefore deleted.
The petitioner-chairman is entitled to function as a full-fledged chairman, subject to a decision taken by the Governor after a reference is made by the President to the Supreme Court under Article 317(1).
