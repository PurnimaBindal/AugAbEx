Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Degree and diploma - Essential qualifications specifically prescribing preference to degree than diploma - Degree shall be given preference over diploma.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21 and 320 - Selection - Appointment - Essential qualifications - Advertisement must prescribe the essential qualifications and experience - Public Service Commission does not have the power to relax the qualifications - The Commission is obligated to consider and obey the essential elements during the selection process - Appointment made without following the norms and essential requirements is illegal, unless the qualifications are relaxed according to the law - The court must reject such actions of the respondent immediately as it affects the livelihood of eligible candidates.
