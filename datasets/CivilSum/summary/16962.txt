The Hon'ble Division Bench gave directions to provide the petitioner with alternate employment, but they were not complied with.
The respondent justified their inaction on the grounds that the applicant had to deposit their gratuity and leave encashment benefits.
However, the petitioner had already received these benefits and would have used them to fulfill their domestic responsibilities.
Therefore, the respondent's action to recover these amounts was not justified.
The court ordered the respondent to calculate the petitioner's wages from the date of the order and pay them within one month.
