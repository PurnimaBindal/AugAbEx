Administrative Orders can supplement statutory rules, but cannot go against them.
An Administrative Order by the government directing a reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in promotion, though there are no such provisions in the rules for promotion, is valid.
Article 309, along with Articles 16 and 14 of the Constitution, form the basis for these Administrative Instructions and Rules.
[Para 12]
In the absence of provisions in rules for promotional reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, the government may issue Executive Instructions to that effect.
In the case of Indian Audit and Accounts Department (Administrative Officers, Assistant Accounts Officers and Assistant Audit Officers) Recruitment Rules, 1963, the rules framed have no such provision, but executive instructions have been issued.
These instructions are valid, given that they comply with the Constitution's Articles 16, 14, and 309.
