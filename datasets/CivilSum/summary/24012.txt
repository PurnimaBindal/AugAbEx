The Kerala Judicial Service Rules, 1991, Rule 3(4) is valid and not discriminatory, as it provides a ratio for determination of inter se seniority between the incumbents of the Criminal wing and Civil wing of subordinate judiciary.
The integration of two wings of judicial service under a set of Rules framed by the Govt. in exercise of Art. 234 & 235 of the Constitution cannot be violative of Articles 14 and 16.
The Kerala High Court has upheld the Rule after Full Court discussions, and it cannot be struck down as being discriminatory or violative of Art. 14.
