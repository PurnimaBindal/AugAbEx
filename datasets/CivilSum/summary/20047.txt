Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Legitimate expectation - Promotion - Rule providing fixation of seniority from the date the candidate first discharged duty on that post - Absorbed employees of other departments who were already regularised in their parent department sought to be absorbed as temporary/ad hoc and to be regularised in rotational system after absorption - Hardship caused to juniors becoming seniors - The system was deemed illegal - The doctrine of legitimate expectation must be consistent with the operation of statutory rules, orders or the Act - Juniors can bypass seniors if merit is the consideration and legitimate expectation takes a back seat - However, seniors are given priority if seniority without reference to merit is the consideration - A wrong principle of regularisation by which legitimate expectation is dashed contrary to general and special rules - The principle of regularisation in rotation without any regard to the service prior to absorption held illegal.
[Paras 10 and 11]
