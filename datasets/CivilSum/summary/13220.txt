Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Correction in date of birth - Natural justice - An authority can investigate the veracity of an employee's date of birth despite it being on record for a long time if there is sufficient reason to do so, but the employee must be included in the inquiry.
The authority cannot unilaterally determine the date of birth and superannuation of a Govt. servant.
Any ruling made without adhering to basic natural justice principles would be void and ineffective.
