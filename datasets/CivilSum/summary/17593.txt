Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Merger of cadres - Merger of cadres of Junior Clerks, Clerks and Supervisors in different pay scales in the Central Co-operative Banks in Punjab - Seniority to be fixed from the date of initial appointment on merger of cadres resulting in making junior clerks and clerks juniors to those who were appointed as Supervisors earlier to them - This action resulted in promoting unequals as equals and adversely affected promotions of Clerks and Junior Clerks - Merger of cadres cannot put persons in a higher pay scale in a different cadre as juniors to those in a lower pay scale in his own cadre.
