Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Food and Supplies Department Sub-Offices Group C Rules, 1982, Rules 19 and 9 - Reservation - Promotion - Rule 19 and 9 provide a reservation of 20% of posts and roster points for vacancies when promoting to the post of Deputy Superintendent - Until 20% of the posts are filled by SC candidates, the roster cannot be considered exhausted - The authority is directed to complete the reserved roster points.
