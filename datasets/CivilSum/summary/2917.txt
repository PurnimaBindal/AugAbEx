Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Circular No. 10/7/88/88- EPI/8014 issued by Department of Finance (Finance Personnel-I Branch), Government of Punjab on 30.8.1988 - Benefit of house rent allowance in revised pay scales - Refixed pay cannot lead to reduction in house rent allowance - High court bound to follow decision in Civil Appeal "Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Punjab" dated 2141995 (arising out of SLP (C) No. 9149 of 1992) and CWP No. 10243 of 1998 "Hem Raj Gupta and 47 others v. State of Punjab and others" - Same matter cannot be reconsidered.
