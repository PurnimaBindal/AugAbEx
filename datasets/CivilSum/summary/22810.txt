Constitution of India, Article 16 - MP State Industries (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1985, Rule 6(2) - Appointment - The rule does not relate to filling vacancies as they occur, but instead aims to maintain proportionality between promotion and transfer recruits and duty posts or cadre strength.
This means that those recruited by promotion or transfer should not exceed 50% of the total duty posts or cadre strength.
