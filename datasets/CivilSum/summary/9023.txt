The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 outlines the rules concerning seniority, regularisation/ad hoc service.
An employee is considered a member of a service only after they have been appointed regularly.
Ad hoc employees are not entitled to the benefit of their period of ad hoc service towards seniority after regularisation to the same post.
Furthermore, if ad hoc employees' services were regularised based on specific instructions stating that they would not be entitled to seniority benefits on regularisation, they cannot then claim those benefits after taking advantage of those instructions.
This has resulted in the dismissal of the petitioner's case.
