The High Court reversed the Labour Court's order to award back wages to a workman, stating that delay in raising the dispute and the workman's gainful employment were not considered.
That part of the order cannot stand, and the case is remitted to the High Court for the question of back wages only.

According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, a notice period of 30 days for absence from duty must be computed only from the date of service of the notice.
Compliance with this requirement would occur if the employee rejoins duty within the stipulated 30-day time frame starting from the day of notice issuance.
