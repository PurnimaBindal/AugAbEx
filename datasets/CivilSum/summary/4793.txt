Central Civil Services (Redeployment of Surplus Staff) Rules, 1990 - Redeployed surplus Staff - While redeployed surplus staff have the right to seniority by counting past service, this right is not available for the purpose of a higher pay scale - When based on an option, employees are bound by the conditions of the offer of employment - The 1990 Rules are not strictly applicable to staff who were redeployed in 1985.
