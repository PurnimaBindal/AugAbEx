Constitution of India, Article 227 - Writ jurisdiction - An ex-parte award was passed by Labour Court, and the petitioner has raised concerns about the service of the summons.
The Court, in its jurisdiction under Article 227, cannot overlook the fact that a party who has not been served cannot be condemned unheard.
As such, the Labour Court has been directed to re-examine the question of service of notice on the petitioner and withdraw and cancel the ex-parte award if it is found that there was no proper service of notice.
The parties will be afforded an opportunity to present their opinions.
The petition has been granted.
