Medical reimbursement must be provided as per policy, even for treatment received at an unrecognized hospital without prior permission, if the required specialized treatment was not available at a recognized hospital.
The reimbursement should be equal to the rates of recognized hospitals and 75% of any amount above that.
Any denial of total reimbursement based solely on the use of an unrecognized hospital is not valid.
Authorities must reimburse as per policy with 9% interest.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
The provision of medical reimbursement is a beneficial act of the welfare State for its employees, and must be interpreted liberally in favor of employees.
Authorities should not be rigid and stick to technicalities while dealing with human problems.
There can be no rigid calculations when it comes to human beings.
