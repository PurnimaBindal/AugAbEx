State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 49 - State Bank of India Officers Service Rules, Rule 22(1)(b) - Pension - Entitlement for incapacitation - Rule 22(1)(b) allows for eligibility for pension due to incapacitation, but removal from service for misconduct does not qualify as incapacitation - Clause (c) applies to voluntary retirement after 20 years of service but not to removal for misconduct.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination - The mere fact that one employee received a pension after removal from service on the basis of wrong advice is not sufficient grounds to grant the same benefit to others - The same mistake should not be repeated.
