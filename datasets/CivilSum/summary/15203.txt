Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Legislative Assembly (Allowances and Pension of Members) Act, 1975, Section 7-A (as amended) - Constitutional validity of amendments - Section 7-A provides for a clear classification to grant pension based on the tenure of legislative members and is neither illegal nor irrational - Article 14 of the Constitution allows for reasonable classifications, including sub-classifications - Thus, the provisions of Section 7-A are within the limits of the Constitution.
