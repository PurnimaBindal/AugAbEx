Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Termination and retrenchment - The appellants were hired for a definite period, and their appointment was allowed to continue.
It was held that no vested right accrues, and the conditions of their appointment were never changed or altered.
Hence, Section 25-F of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, will not apply to such cases.
The principles of natural justice do not apply to fixed-term contracts when the termination occurs after the contractual period expires.
