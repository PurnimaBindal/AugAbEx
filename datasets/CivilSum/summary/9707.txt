Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3) - Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension - Army Officer discharged from service on medical grounds - Officer's pension claim for disability of both eyes rejected without examination by Medical Advisor (Pensions) held as violative of Article 300-A of Constitution.

Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3) - Disability pension - Officer denied pension on the ground of hereditary disease, which authorities failed to prove the existence of said disease during his induction into service - Officer entitled to receive pension for disability of both eyes.
