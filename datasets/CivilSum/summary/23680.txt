The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 states that compensation in lieu of reinstatement is permissible even after the termination of an employee's services has already been set aside.
However, management losing confidence in the employee and the employee being likely to attain superannuation soon might render an award of reinstatement futile.
A High Court's refusal to sustain the reinstatement is not subject to interference by the Supreme Court, and hence compensation in lieu of reinstatement is allowed.
[Para 3]  B While granting compensation in lieu of reinstatement, an employee's right to back wages cannot be completely denied.
