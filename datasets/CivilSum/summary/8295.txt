Constitution of India, Article 16 - Limitation Act, 1963 - Pay - Limitation - The claimant must be paid their proper pay that is computed correctly.
It is the right of the employee to have their salary fixed during their entire tenure of service, and they have the right to exercise this right at the time of each payment of salary.
This is allowed when they have the entitlement to compute the salary in accordance with the law.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Efficiency Bar - Petitioner's efficiency bar stopped - Speaking Order - The order to stop an employee's efficiency bar should be through a speaking order and sufficient reasons must be given to allow the employee's representation against it.
The decision not to allow an employee to cross efficiency bar must also be made based on the record of the concerned employee.
