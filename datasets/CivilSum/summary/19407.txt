The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, outlines the regularisation of part time employees.
In light of this, keeping an employee as a part time worker for 23 years in a government employment position when a regular post has become available is completely unfair and amounts to worker exploitation.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to regularisation at least when such a post has become available.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]

The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, also outlines the termination process for a part time employee.
It is unreasonable to terminate a part time employee who has provided 23 years of service, especially when a regular post is available.
In this case, the termination was without reason or justification, and before the expiry of even the sanctioned period.
As a result, the termination is presumed to be for extraneous considerations and held to be patently arbitrary and mala fide.
The petitioner is entitled to be reinstated with compensatory costs for harassment, with costs of Rs. 5000/- to be recovered by the State from the officer concerned.
