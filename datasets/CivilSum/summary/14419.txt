Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Fixation of pay - Military pension - Ex-servicemen joining civil services and the fixation of their pay - The deduction of an enhanced military pension while determining the pay of an ex-serviceman is considered arbitrary and illegal by the Supreme Court in UOI v. Vasudevan Pille and others, JT 1995(1) SC 417 - The petitioner's deduction order impacting his pay is nullified, and there is a direction to re-fix his pay.
