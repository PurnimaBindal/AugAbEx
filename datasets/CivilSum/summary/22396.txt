The High Court allowed the writ petition in a case involving the termination of service of "Tiny Deposit Collector" workmen, after finding that there was no acceptable evidence to justify the orders of termination.
However, on appeal, it was held that a wrong permission granted to lead evidence and absence of acceptable evidence are different concepts.
The Division Bench was deemed to have confused the two concepts and there was no finding recorded that permission was wrongly granted.
Therefore, the order was set aside, and the appeal was allowed.
[Para 11]
The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 was explained in terms of the termination of service and granting of permission to lead evidence to an employer to justify its order of termination.
