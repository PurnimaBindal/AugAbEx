According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 14 and 16, Sugar Mill employees are entitled to a regular pay scale.
The Mill promised in the appointment letters that different types of employees would receive a regular pay scale after the Mill started functioning.
The employer cannot deny the employees this promise by arguing that they had already accepted a fixed salary for years.
The arrears of the regular pay scale can only be restricted due to the delay.
[Paras 11, 12 and 14] B.
The employees' entitlement to the regular pay scale cannot be defeated only because of delay and laches, particularly when they were constantly fighting for their claim.
As per the advertisement and conditions of appointment, the employees must receive a regular pay scale based on the Constitution of India, in Articles 14, 16, and 226.
The court limited the arrears to just 38 months.
