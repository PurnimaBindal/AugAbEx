Constitution of India, Article 14 - Maharshi Dayanand University Calendar, Volume I, Statute 39(1) - Natural justice - Hearing - Post-decisional opportunity of hearing - If the urgency of the matter requires, an order can be passed subject to post-decisional opportunity of hearing, but a hearing must take place if the order passed affects civil rights, even if a statute does not require a hearing - The petitioner was expelled from the university for one year on charges of hooting and causing knife wounds to students, resulting in a Section 324/34 IPC case against them - The immediate action taken was necessary, but a post-decisional opportunity should have been granted - The court held that HL Trehan's case AIR 1989 SC 568 had a different factual situation wherein the Supreme court held that a post-decisional opportunity of hearing does not subserve the rules of natural justice - The University has been ordered to provide the petitioner with a hearing and make a suitable decision within one month.
