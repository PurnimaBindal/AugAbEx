Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Examination - Result withheld - The University was obliged to issue roll numbers to petitioners as they were eligible to sit for June, 2000 examination.
The Court deemed it unjustified for the University to withhold the roll numbers of the petitioners, hence granting the interim direction for their issuance - Consequently, the petitioners were successful in having their petition granted and the University was directed to declare their result.
