According to Punjab Town Planners (State Service Class I) Rules, 1972, Rules 8 and 10, for appointment to the post of Assistant Town Planner, a quota of 80:20 is in place for direct recruitment and promotions.
In cases where incumbents are promoted on an ad hoc basis and continue to hold higher posts for a reasonably long period, the resulting vacancies must be treated as regular vacancies and subject to the quota rule.
[Paras 10 and 11]
