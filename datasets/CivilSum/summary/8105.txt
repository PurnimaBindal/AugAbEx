Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28, Schedule IV Item 9 - Classification of Employees - Can the Industrial Court classify employees on a complaint?
- The Industrial Court awarded an employee reinstatement, but she was not given the scale of a highly skilled worker and continued on probation.
The Industrial Court examined all the points and permitted the claim, whereas in the appeal, the parties contended that the Industrial Court could not classify workers.
However, it was held that the Industrial Court had not committed any illegality in accepting the case and correctly found that the respondent was doing work of a highly skilled nature after deliberating on all the facts and pleas brought before it.
