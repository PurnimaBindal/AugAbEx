Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pension - Cut off date - Policy decision - A Government circular dated 1471977 entitles certain retired teachers to pension if they served in erstwhile District Board schools up to 1101957 and retired between 1101957 and 31.10.1966.
Petitioners were given family pension, but the Government had a right to fix a particular date for extending the benefit of pension to retired CPF members.
The husbands of the petitioners were members of CPF and continued to be so until retirement.
The policy decision to fix 18.12.1972 as the date for pension entitlement does not suffer from any arbitrariness and is constitutional.
