Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 45 - Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration Common Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972 - Classification/General Pool Allotment/Out of turn allotment - Retiring Govt. employees and their relations classified into two categories - Those retiring after June 9, 1987 and before February 28, 1989 must share Govt. accommodation for only 6 months and those retiring on or after February 28, 1989 for 3 years - Categorisation is challenged as arbitrary, artificial and unjustified - Instructions are invalidated and petitions allowed - Criticism of Home Secretary UT, Chandigarh follows.
