Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Dismissal from service - Absence from duty - The petitioner was absent for 38 days in 9 months due to sickness and received treatment in ESI dispensary, which was documented - The explanation given by the petitioner was not deemed a confession without an investigation and an apology for absence was also offered - Their service was terminated on 28.11.1981 - The Labour Court ruled that the dismissal was lawful on 11.3.1992 - There would be no point in remanding the case to the Labour Court - The employer was ordered to pay the workman Rs. 1,00,000/- in compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
