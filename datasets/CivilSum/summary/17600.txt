Haryana Kanungo Service (Group C) Rules, 1981, Rules 7 and 11 - Seniority - Appointment - Eligibility - To be eligible for appointment as Kanungo, a person must complete training and pass the examination conducted by the Director of Land Records - Those appointed on compassionate grounds without being eligible must receive seniority from the date of completion of training, not from the date of appointment.
[Paras 6, 7, 8 and 11]
Haryana Kanungo Service (Group C) Rules, 1981, Rules 7 and 11 - Classification - Seniority - Eligibility - Training - Direct recruits and promotees - Promotees are entitled to seniority from the date of their appointment in the cadre of Kanungo, while direct recruits become eligible after completing training - This classification is justified and does not discriminate.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equality - Seniority - Officials cannot claim seniority in violation of rules and Article 14.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Necessary parties - Seniority - Officials who are not party to the petition and would be adversely affected by any order cannot have their seniority determined in their absence.
