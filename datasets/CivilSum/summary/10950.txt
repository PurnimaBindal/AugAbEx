Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173 - Entitlement rule for causality pensionary awards, 1982 - Disability pension - Grant of - Petitioner was discharged from the Army due to the recommendation of the Medical Board, which declared him unfit for Army service.
Claim for disability pension rejected on the ground that disability resulting in discharge was neither attributable nor aggravated by military service.
No adverse remark noted in his service record before his enrolment in Army on the time of medical examination.
The respondents' obligation to put the petitioner to Re-medical Survey Board was not done.
Rejection of petitioner's claim was completely unjustified.
[Paras 3, 4, 5 and 7]
