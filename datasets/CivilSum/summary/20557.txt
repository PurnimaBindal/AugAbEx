Constitution of India, Article 142 - Review - Only the guilty person may initiate an appeal or application - No one else may intervene on their behalf as they are the only aggrieved person - A State seeking review of a review order and remitting a sentence is not maintainable if they have no right to be an aggrieved person, as in the case of a contemner who is an I.A.S.
Officer convicted for contempt of court - An application for remission of sentence by the contemner was rejected, and the review application was subsequently dismissed.
