According to Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, Disability Pension is granted to a petitioner who has been shunted out of military service on the basis of suffering from a disease.
In this case, the petitioner suffered from Schizophrenia and Generalised Seizures, resulting in a composite disability of 60%.
However, it was determined that these diseases were attributable to army service and that no deformity was noticed during the petitioner's initial medical exam.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
