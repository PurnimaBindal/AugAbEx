Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Withheld pending departmental enquiry - On the date the petitioner was overlooked for promotion no departmental enquiry was actually pending against him - A chargesheet must be served for it to be considered pending - In this case, the chargesheet was served on June 13, 1996 which was after the promotion order made on February 14, 1996 - The petitioner's case cannot be considered under the sealed cover method - The court directed for the sealed cover to be opened and for action to be taken as per the recommendations of the Departmental Promotion Committee.
