According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, in regards to admission in education with reservation for Scheduled Castes, a petitioner from UP was given admission in an Engineering College in Haryana based on a Common Entrance Test.
The petitioner had provided a certificate from the District Magistrate of UP with his application, and the prospectus did not require a certificate from the Revenue Authority in Haryana.
As a result, the cancellation of the petitioner's admission was deemed improper.
