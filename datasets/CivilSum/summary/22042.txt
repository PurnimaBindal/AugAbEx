The Tamil Nadu Police Subordinate Service's (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rule 3(b) mandate that non-appearance and non-compliance with a Medical Board order is considered misconduct and that higher discipline is expected from a member of the police force.
Thus, failure to appear before the Medical Board and not comply with the order to appear is sufficient for initiating disciplinary proceedings and a subsequent order of dismissal.
[Paras 6, 9, and 11] B.
The respondent challenged an order of dismissal on the grounds that the disciplinary authority was subordinate in rank to the initiator of the disciplinary proceedings and that the inquiry was vitiated.
However, the disciplinary proceedings against the respondent were well within the law, and the tribunal's view was contrary to the law laid down by the Supreme Court in 1996(2) SCC 145.
The order of dismissal was affirmed.
