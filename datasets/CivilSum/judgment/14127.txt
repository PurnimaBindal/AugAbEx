Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - We have heard learned counsel for the petitioner at some length.
2.
Challenge in this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India is to the award dated 2352001, vide which, the workman was reinstated with continuity of service and full back wages, on the ground that the Presiding Officer, Labour Court has not appreciated the evidence of its right perspective and has granted full back wages without any plausible reason.
3.
Both these grounds are mis-conceived.
Learned Presiding Officer, Labour Court has appreciated the documentary as well as oral evidence.
4.
MW-1 Umed Singh JE in his cross-examination has practically admitted the case of the workman.
Furthermore, in the written statement filed on behalf of the Haryana Urban Development Authority, no objection was even taken that the workman was gainfully employed during the period of his termination till ordering reinstatement and as such he is not entitled to back wages.
In absence of such plea, we find no error in the impugned award.
Dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
