VK Jhanji, J - This shall dispose of Civil Writ Petition No. 9682 of 1991 and be read in continuation of order dated 14.1.1993 passed by RS Mongia, J  2.
Vide order dated 14.1.1993, the order impugned in the writ petition whereby the petitioners were denied the benefit of service as Executive Clerks towards their seniority when they were absorbed as Civilian Clerks, was quashed being a non-speaking order and the respondents were directed to decide the representation of the petitioners afresh by passing a speaking order.
In pursuance of this order, the respondents have passed two orders dated 13.10.1993 and 19101993.
Both these orders have been annexed as Annexures A/2-A and A-2 respectively with Civil Misc. No. 11391 of 1994.
Vide order dated 13.10.1993 (Annexure A/2-A), petitioners namely Sham Lal, Pran Nath Khurana and Om Prakash have been given seniority in the Clerical cadre from the date of their joining in the Executive Clerical cadre.
In consequence thereof, they have been promoted to the rank of Assistant and Deputy Superintendent (Office) on the deemed dates when their juniors were so promoted.
Vide order dated 19101993 (Annexures A-2), petitioners, Sham Lal and Pran Nath Khurana have further been promoted as Superintendent (A-Grade).
However, the petitioners have not been given any arrears of pay and allowances from the deemed dates of promotion on the ground that they actually did not work on the higher posts.
The action of the respondents in not making the payment of arrears of pay and allowances from the deemed dates of promotion has been impugned on the ground that the petitioners are being made to suffer for no fault of theirs, particularly when their rightful claim of promotion, on consideration have been found to be justified.
3.
The grievance made by the petitioners is well-merited.
It has been held by a Division Bench of this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 1486 of 1993 (Om Prakash v. State of Haryana and another decided on 311994,) relying upon two judgments of this Court in Charan Dass Chadha v. State of Punjab, 1980(3) S.L.R.
702 and KK Jagia v. State of Haryana and another, 1972 S.L.R.
578, that once an employee is promoted with effect from a retrospective date, he cannot be deprived of pay and other allowances to which he would have been entitled had he infact been promoted to the said post on the date on which he later on was promoted.
Such like condition, as has been imposed in the present case, for depriving the employees of the arrears of pay etc. was held to be illegal.
Similar view has been taken by the Division Bench of this Court in Sikander Lal v. State of Haryana and another, 1989(2) RSJ.
569.
No judgment to the contrary has been cited by the counsel for the respondents.
4.
Consequently, the writ petition is allowed with costs which are assessed at Rs. 2,000/-.
Respondents are directed to pay the arrears of pay and other allowances to the petitioners from the deemed dates of promotion with interest thereon at the rate of 15 per cent per annum upto the date of payment.
The respondents are further directed to consider the case of Sham Lal, petitioner, for grant of selection grade under the Rules.
Since the petitioners have already retired, the respondents are directed to revise the pension payable to the petitioners after allowing the aforesaid benefits within a period of four months.
5.
Civil Misc. No. 11391 of 1994 stand disposed of.
Order accordingly.
