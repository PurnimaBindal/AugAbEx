HS Bedi, J - This is a case which reflects very adversely on the inaction by the State of Punjab and its officials.
The petitioner had submitted bills claiming reimbursement of expenses incurred by him for the period 1993 - January, 1997.
Admittedly, up till now not a penny has been paid.
Frustrated in his attempt to secure the amount due to him, the petitioner has filed the present petition.
2.
Notice was issued on 8th January, 1998 and Mr. MS Gill, Additional Advocate General, Punjab, accepted the same in Court and the case was ordered to be listed for final hearing today.
3.
Mr. HS Gill, Deputy Advocate General has put in appearance and prays for time to file the written statement.
In view of the facts stated above, more particularly, that the payments in question relate to the years 1993-1997, I am of the opinion that his request is wholly unjustified.
It is accordingly declined.
4.
Mr. Gill has put on record a communication dated 14th January, 1998 from the Director, Health and Family Welfare, Punjab, to the Advocate General, Punjab, in which it has been stated that sanction pertaining to five bills amounting to Rs. 68,507 (11.9.1994 to 18.4.1995), Rs. 98,837/- (5101992 to 28121992), Rs. 25,841/- (261995 to 11101995), Rs. 77,613/- (1511993 to 1691993) and Rs. 42,704/- (25.4.1996 to 31121996) has also been accorded whereas action is being taken with regard to the reimbursement of two bills i.e. Rs. 91,48940 (11.9.1993 to 31.8.1994) and Rs. 50,29470 (1191995 to 1631996).
5.
Mr. AC Sharma, the learned counsel for the petitioner, has, however, pointed out that the bills that he had submitted for medical reimbursement pertained to the year 1993 onwards and, as such, interest on the delayed payment should also be made payable.
This prayer is, however, untenable in the light of the judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in Om Parkash Gargi v. State of Punjab and others, 1997(1) S.L.R.
177 wherein the Court observed as under :  "It is true that but for the benefit of reimbursement of the amount granted by the Government, the petitioner has no right to claim reimbursement.
The question is : Whether on account of delay in reimbursing the amount incurred towards medical expenses, the State should be liable to pay also interest on the delayed payment ?
We are of the view that, it is inexpedient and not proper to direct the State to pay interest for delay in payment of the reimbursement amount.
It requires verification of the amounts spent by the petitioner and similar person.
His right only is to get reimbursement and it does not follow that for the delay in the payment of medical reimbursement, he should also be entitled to interest thereon."
6.
This petition is accordingly disposed of with a direction that the amounts for which sanction has already been accorded shall be released to the petitioner within a period of one month from today by a demand draft made payable at Jammu and that the other bills that have been submitted shall also be finalised and paid to the petitioner in the same manner within a period of three months from today.
The costs of the petition are also determined at Rs. 5,000/- to be paid to the petitioner along with first set of payment.
However, it is made clear that in case some claim of the petitioner pertaining to the aforesaid period remains unsatisfied, he would be at liberty to approach this Court once again.
This petition is disposed of in the above terms.
Dasti order.
