RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The petitioner was suspended on 3111988 because of pendency of criminal case against him under Sections 409, 420, 465, 471, 120-B IPC.
According to the petitioner, on the same allegations as were in the criminal case, he was also served with a charge sheet for departmental enquiry on 311989, to which he filed the reply on 2411989.
He was reinstated in service on 2331989.
The departmental enquiry was started by the department itself because of the pendency of the criminal case.
That trial Court acquitted the petitioner vide judgment dated 2451996.
Petitioner filed this writ petition alleging that despite this acquittal in the criminal case, his case had not been considered for further promotion, with effect from the date his juniors were promoted on 8121997.
It may be observed here that the petitioner was again suspended on 1431997 without serving any charge-sheet on him.
He was again re-instated on 2311998.
The petitioner was served with another charge-sheet on 2681988, to which he has already filed the reply.
2.
On 921999, we had asked the respondents to file a specific affidavit as to why the enquiry against the petitioner for which he was served a charge- sheet on 311989 had not been proceeded with or concluded despite the criminal case having ended in acquittal of the petitioner on 2451996 and further whether any charge-sheet has been served for which the petitioner had been suspended on 1431997 and also reinstated on 2311998.
The respondents have filed an affidavit dated 2431999 in which inter alia it has been averred that it was on the asking of the petitioner that the enquiry had been stayed during the pendency of the criminal trial.
However, we are not much satisfied with the explanation of the respondents as to why the departmental enquiry, if it was sought to be proceeded with, did not re-start after the acquittal of the petitioner by the criminal court of 2451996.
It has been stated in the affidavit that it had been decided on 20.8.1998 to continue with the first departmental enquiry for which the Inquiry Officer had been appointed on the same date but the same has been changed now on 10111999.
3.
According to the learned counsel for the petitioner, the petitioner's case should have been considered when his juniors were considered for promotion as it had not been decided by then that the first departmental enquiry should be continued.
He further submits that the second enquiry which had been started by issuance of the charge-sheet on 26.8.1999 should not come in the way of consideration of the case of the petitioner for his promotion with effect from December, 1997.
4.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties, we are of the view that since the respondents have now decided to continue with the first departmental enquiry, which was started way back in January, 1989 and the decision to continue the same after acquittal of the petitioner having been taken in August 1998, they should conclude the departmental enquiry within a reasonable time and then consider the case of the petitioner for promotion with effect from 8121997, when his juniors were promoted.
We are further of the view that the enquiry which was started by issuance of the second charge-sheet on 2681978 should not come in the way of the petitioner for getting his case considered for promotion with effect from 8121997 as it is a subsequent event than the date with effect from which he is to be considered for promotion.
5.
For the foregoing reasons, we dispose of this writ petition by directing the respondent to conclude first enquiry within four months and action, if any, to be taken on the enquiry report must be taken within two months after the submission of the report by the Inquiry Officer.
Needless to mention that the petitioner would co-operate in the proceedings before the Inquiry Officer.
In case, he does not co-operative, the Inquiry Officer would be at liberty to proceed with the enquiry in accordance with law.
After the aforesaid directions are carried out, the petitioner's case would be considered for promotion within one month thereafter, by taking into consideration the service record upto 8121997, including the final outcome of the enquiry which is to be concluded as aforesaid.
Before parting with the judgment, we may also observe that after the conclusion of the enquiry as aforesaid, the case regarding as to how the suspension period is to be treated and increments to be granted would also be decided.
Copy of this order attested by the Special Secretary of this Court be given to Mr. Rathee, DAG, Haryana for onward transmission to the concerned quarters.
Order accordingly.
