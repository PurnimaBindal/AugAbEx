RS Mongia, J - (Oral) Petitioner, who was born on 2nd September, 1917, was compulsorily retired as Naib Tehsildar on his attaining the age of 55 years, with effect from 18th December, 1972.
In the year 1974, the petitioner was sanctioned pension at the rate of Rs. 116/- p. month and in the year 1977, the monthly pension was revised to Rs. 143/-.
2.
In the year 1978 the petitioner had applied for commutation of his pension under the Punjab Civil Services Rules, under which the retiree has a right to get his monthly pension commutated to the extent of one-third, which is recoverable from the monthly pension of the retiree till the attaining of the age of 70 years.
The petitioner was sanctioned a sum of Rs. 5160-60 in the form of commutation of pension at Rs. 47/- per month and the deductions from the pension of the above-said amount were to start with effect form 16th February, 1980.
A copy of the order has been attached as Annexure P-1 to the petition.
It took some time before the petitioner could be paid the actual commuted pension, and in fact, he was paid Rs. 5044-90 on 19th May, 1980.
3.
Deductions from the monthly pension of the petitioner at the rate of Rs. 47/- per month were commenced with effect from 16th February, 1980.
On 19th March, 1981, instructions (Annexure P-2) were issued by the Secretary to Government, Haryana, Department of Finance, to all the departments in the State, on the subject of liberalisation of pensionery benefits on the recommendations of the Pay Commission.
One of the benefits was restoration of commuted pension, which was in the following terms :-  "Restoration of commuted pension :  The existing pattern for commutation of pension could continue as before.
Where a pensioner commutes a part of his pension and received lump sum in lieu thereof, the would be allowed restoration of the surrendered value of pension in full after completion of 70 years of age."
It may be stated that these instructions were given effect to from 1st December, 1979.
4.
The case of the petitioner is that in spite of the instructions of the Government, dated 19th March, 1981, the deductions from the pension continued even after the petitioner had attained the age of 70 years on 1st September, 1987.
According to the learned counsel, after the petitioner had attained the age of 70 years, no further deductions could be made towards the recovery of commuted pension and the retiree had to be allowed restoration of the surrendered value of pension in full after completion of 70 years of age.
It has been averred by the petitioner and not denied by the respondents that the deductions continued much after 1987 and as stated by the petitioner's counsel at the bar the deductions had continued upto 11th April, 1990.
The petitioner is seeking a direction to the respondents that in view of the instructions (Annexure P-2), any deductions made after 1st September, 1987 (i.e. after the petitioner attained the age of 70 years) are liable to be refunded.
5.
The learned counsel for the respondents, on the other hand, has submitted that as per the instructions of the Government issued, which are dated 9th July, 1985, the commuted pension of the petitioner is to be restored after the petitioner had attained the age of 70 years or had surrendered the full value of the pension whichever is later.
Accordingly the case of the respondents is that his restoration could only be ordered after 122 months as the full recovery of the commuted pension will be effected within 122 months.
6.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, I am of the view that the petitioner's case is covered by the instructions contained in Annexure P-2 and the instructions which were issued later on by the Government on 9th July, 1985, would cover only those cases where the pension was got commuted after the said date.
The petitioner's case would not be covered by the said instructions, as the commutation of pension took place prior to those instructions.
Accordingly, the respondents could not deduct any amount from the pension towards the recovery of the commuted pension after 1st September, 1987, when the petitioner had attained the age of 70 years.
He is entitled to the refund of the amount that has been deducted from his pension after 1st September, 1987.
7.
For the foregoing reasons, I allow this petition and direct the respondents to refund to the petitioner the amount of pension that has been deducted after 1st September, 1987, when he had attained the age of 70 years, within a period of two months from the receipt of this order.
There will be nor order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
