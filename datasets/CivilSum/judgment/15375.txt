Mehtab S Gill, J - The petitioner has filed this petition in the nature of certiorari for quashing the order dated February 6, 1996 (Annexure P-4) passed by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Finance Department, Chandigarh (respondent No. 2).
Further, he has prayed for the release of pay and allowances with interest at the rate of 18% per annum for the higher post of the Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana for the period from April 2, 1992 to February 17, 1993 along with all consequential reliefs.
2.
The petitioner has averred that he was appointed as Assistant Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana vide order dated November 10, 1986.
He was directed to perform the duties of the post of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana vide order dated April 2, 1992 (Annexure P-1), in addition tot the post of Assistant Examiner.
The petitioner joined and discharged his duties as Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana for the period from April 2, 1992 to February 17, 1993.
He has further stated that the post of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana, is a promotional post of the post of Assistant Examiner, which is also in the same office.
The petitioner was promoted on February 18, 1993 as Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana vide Government orders dated February 18, 1993.
The petitioner retired voluntarily on April 30, 1996 from the post of Director, Local Audit, Haryana.
3.
It has been further averred that the petitioner filed representation dated February 19, 1995 (Annexure P-2) to respondent No. 2 requesting for the grant of pay of the higher post of Examiner in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300 for the period from April 2, 1992 to February 17, 1993.
The petitioner filed another representation on January 17, 1996 (Annexure P-3) for considering his claim, butt it was rejected by respondent No. 2 vide his order dated February 6, 1996 (Annexure P-4).
4.
Notice of motion was issued.
5.
Written statement was filed.
6.
I have heard the learned counsel for the petitioner and the learned counsel for the respondents.
7.
Learned counsel for the respondents has stated that vide order dated April 2, 1992 (Annexure P-1), the petitioner was directed to look after the current charge of the post of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana.
These orders were issued under Clause (iv) of Rule 4.22 of the Punjab Civil Services, Rules, Volume I, Part-I (hereinafter referred to as "the Rules").
He has further stated that it is incorrect to state that the petitioner was appointed to hold full charge of the duties of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana.
This could not have been done, as this post is a promotional post of Assistant Examiner, though being in the same office.
8.
Going through the order dated April 2, 1992 (Annexure P-1), it comes out that the petitioner was entrusted with current charge of the post of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana.
9.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has stated that the case of the petitioner falls under clause (i) of Rule 4.22 of the Rules.
Since the petitioner discharged the full functions and duties of the post of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana, in addition to the post of Assistant Examiner, Clause (iv) of Rule 4.22 of the Rules does not have any bearing on the case of the petitioner.
10.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has drawn my attention to a Division Bench Judgement of this Court rendered in the case of Balbir Singh Dalal and others v. State of Haryana and others, 2002(4) SCT 422 (P&H) (Civil Writ Petition No. 10152 of 2000) decided on May 9, 2002, wherein in the relevant rules have been discussed.
The Judgment rendered by a Division Bench of this Court squarely covers the case of the petitioner.
11.
In view of the discussion and the judgment cited above, this writ petition is allowed to the limited extent that the petitioner is entitled to receive higher pay that of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana for the period from April 2, 1992 to February 17, 1993 with all consequential benefits.
Petition partly allowed.
