Ajoy Nath Ray, J - The award of compensation was made upon the principles of grant of compensation to workmen and a sum of Rs. 93,450/- was awarded on the basis that the deceased husband of the widow claimant was earning Rs. 1,000/- a month.
2.
The practically admitted document showed as per the salary certificate of the deceased that the monthly income was Rs. 2,800/-.
The only reason for awarding compensation on a lesser monthly income was that the owner of the vehicle did not come to the box.
3.
In our opinion, this was not a good ground for disbelieving the document upon which there was no shadow of suspension.
4.
We are, therefore, of the opinion that the compensation should have been granted on the basis of income above Rs. 2,000/- as per the Workmen's Compensation Act.
This sum is stipulated to be Rs. 1,86,900/-.
5.
Thus, the award is enhanced to the above principal sum.
Interest will be at the rate of award and from the date awarded.
6.
Payment is to be made within nine weeks from the date hereof; in case of failure to make such payment with such time, interest will be chargeable at the enhanced rate of 18% per annum and in that event, the appellants will be entitled to the costs of the award and also of this appeal assessed at Rs. 17,500/-.
7.
Needless to mention, in case of punctual payment, there will be no enhancement of the rate of interest and no liability to pay costs.
8.
Payment is to be made by Account Payee Cheque to the appellant or the appellant's legal representative.
Sums, if any, paid already, may be deducted from the above ordered sums.
Payment to be made to make full compliance with of this appellate order.
9.
While paying, the respondents shall add to the above ordered sum, a sum of rupees one thousand for funeral expenses which will not carry interest in any event.
10.
The appeal is, thus, disposed of.
Appeal allowed.
