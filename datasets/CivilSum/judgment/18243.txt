Ashutosh Mohunta, J. (Oral) - The petitioners have filed the present writ petition for quashing the impugned notice 2011987, Annexure P-7 by which the service of the petitioners was sought to be terminated.
2.
It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioners that the petitioners have put in more than 18 years of service with the respondents without any break, hence, the notice annexure P-7 is liable to quashed.
Learned counsel further contended that all the petitioners have been granted the first ACP after having rendered 10/20 years of regular satisfactory service vide order dated 3012001 w.e.f.
1.12.1996.
It is submitted that the Assured Career Progression is granted only to regular employees.
A copy of one such letter issued to petitioner Krishan Kumar, petitioner No. 13 is annexed as Annexure P-1/A It is further submitted by the learned counsel that the petitioner No. 5 and petitioner No. 11 have further been promoted to the post of Junior Engineer and are working at the present moment in circle Bhiwani, which also goes to show that they are being treated as regular employees of the respondents.
On the basis of the above, the impugned notice of termination dated 2011987, is sought to be quashed.
3.
On the other hand, learned counsel for the respondents has submitted that the termination notice, Annexure P-7 was issued, as the petitioners were directly recruited and not through Employment Exchange.
It is further submitted that the appointment of the petitioners was in violation of the instructions dated 28.12.1982.
4.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, it is admitted that all the petitioners have rendered more than 18 years of service with the respondents and they have also been granted first ACP after having rendered 10/20 years of service as is clear vide order dated 3012001, Annexure P-1/A Moreover, petitioner No. 5 and petitioner No. 11 have also been promoted to the post of Junior Engineer and are being treated as regular employees and thus, the service of the petitioners are not liable to be terminated.
5.
In the light of the above fact, the writ petition is allowed and the notice of termination dated 2011987, Annexure P-7 is quashed.
Petition allowed.
