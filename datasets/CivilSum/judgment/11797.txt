PK Jain, J - An order dated 7111996 (Annexure P.21) passed by respondent No. 2, whereby transfer orders of 5 Managers and 7 Assistant Managers/Development Officers, issued vide order dated 19.9.1996, have been withdrawn, as a result of which the petitioner stands re-transferred as Manager, Central Co-operative Bank Limited, from Karnal to the Central Co- operative Bank Limited, Kurukshetra, in the same capacity.
This order (Annexure P21) is the subject matter of challenge in this writ petition.
2.
According to the averments in the petition, the petitioner was transferred from Karnal to Kurukshetra on 19.9.1996, which was challenged by him in CWP No. 15114 of 1996, which was dismissed.
On 7111996 the transfer orders dated 19.9.1996 were cancelled and the petitioner was posted at Karnal but again on 911997, the petitioner was shifted to Kurukshetra vide impugned order dated 911997 (Annexure P.21).
It is the case of the petitioner that the petitioner is being harassed and humiliated by the respondents without any reason and he has been transferred six times within a period of 5-1/2 months from 1071996 to 911997.
It has been alleged that the action of the respondents in passing the impugned order is without any administrative exigency or any valid reason and is wholly arbitrary, discriminatory and against the principles of natural justice.
It has been pointed out that the children of the petitioner are studying in colleges at Karnal and that he along with his wife is having heart and asthma problems and they are under the expert doctor's treatment at Karnal.
3.
In its return filed on behalf of respondent No. 2, it is stated that the orders dated 7111996 had to be cancelled and withdrawn on account of two writ petitions (CWP Nos. 18928 of 1996 and 17657 of 1996) filed by Dharam Singh, Manager, Karnal Central Co-operative Bank Ltd, Karnal and Maha Singh, Manager, Sonepat Central Co-operative Bank Ltd, Sonepat.
It has been further pointed out that the petitioner, being a Manager, cannot be posted in his home-town district i.e. Karnal.
The allegations of malice or harassment have been denied.
4.
In his separate return filed by respondent No. 4, it has been pointed out that the petitioner was transferred from Karnal to Kurukshetra vide order dated 19.9.1996 (Annexure P.20) which order was challenged by the petitioner in CWP No. 15114 of 1996, which was dismissed by this Court and as such the petitioner cannot have any grievance if he has been detained at Kurukshetra.
5.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have perused the record.
6.
It cannot be disputed that in transferable post an order of transfer is a normal consequence and personal difficulties are matters for consideration of the department.
It is also true that the order of transfer often causes a lot of difficulties and dislocation in the family set-up of the concerned employee but on that score the order of transfer is not liable to be struck down.
Unless such transfer order is passed mala fide or in violation of the rules of service and guidelines for transfer without any proper justification, the Court would not interfere with the order of transfer.
7.
It is also correct that it is not always possible to establish malice in fact in a straight cut manner.
In an appropriate case, it is possible to draw reasonable inference of mala fide action from the pleadings and antecedent facts and circumstances.
8.
The petitioner has given the history of his various transfers right from 22.8.1992 till the impugned order.
It is enough to say that on 1071996 the petitioner was promoted as a Manager and was transferred in such capacity to the office of respondent No. 2 at Chandigarh.
On 2271996, he was transferred to Karnal.
Since Karnal is the home-district/town of the petitioner, who is an officer of the rank of Manager, was transferred to Kurukshetra by order dated 19.9.1996 (Annexure P.20).
This order was challenged by the petitioner by filing CWP No. 15114 of 1996 in this Court but the same was dismissed.
In other words, the transfer of the petitioner from Karnal to Kurukshetra was upheld by this Court in its writ jurisdiction.
9.
By order dated 19.9.1996 (Annexure P.20) several transfers were made including the petitioner which order had to be withdrawn by order dated 7111996 (Annexure P.21) in view of two writ petitions filed by Dharam Singh, Manager, Central Co-operative Bank Ltd, Karnal, and Maha Singh, Manager, Central Co-operative Bank Ltd, Sonepat.
Resultantly, the petitioner had to go from Karnal to Kurukshetra.
In these circumstances, no malice or mala fide on the part of respondents Nos. 1 to 3, while passing the impugned order, is established.
If the petitioner had been transferred from one place to another in the past, that would not help the petitioner in showing any arbitrary or discriminatory action on the part of respondents Nos. 1 to 3 against the petitioner.
It has already been mentioned above that personal or family difficulties do arise as a consequence of transfer order of an employee from one place to another but the same are to be taken care of by the department in appropriate cases, but the Court would not interfere in a transfer matter on this ground.
10.
For the reasons mentioned above, we do not find any merit in this petition and the same is hereby dismissed.
