JL Gupta, J. (Oral) - The petitioner who was working as a Sub Divisional Engineer, was permitted to voluntarily retire from Government service w.e.f.
November 10, 1992.
The respondents did not release his retiral benefits.
Consequently, he approached this Court through the present petition with a prayer that the respondents be directed to "finalise the case of the petitioner without any further delay and to pay the pension and other pensionary benefits alongwith interest."
2.
No written statement has been filed on behalf of the respondents.
However, Ms. Ritu Bahri, counsel for the respondents states that the pension was released to the petitioner on August 3, 1994.
The benefits of gratuity, General Provident Fund and commuted pension, were released in September 1994.
That as for leave encashment, the learned counsel is not in a position to make any definite statement with regard to its payment or otherwise.
3.
Mr. Inderjit Malhotra, learned counsel for the petitioner submits that all these payments were inordinately delayed and the respondents should be held liable to pay interest.
4.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties, the respondents are directed to release the amount due to the petitioner on account of leave encashment within one month in case it has not been already paid to him.
It is further evident that the payments have been unduly delayed.
No written statement has been filed to indicate the reasons for the delay in payment.
The petitioner having retired in November 1992, should have been paid all the retiral benefits within a period of three months.
This was not done.
Consequently, the petitioner shall be entitled to the payment of interest at the rate of 12 per cent with effect from February 15, 1993 till the date of actual payment of the amount due to him.
The amount of interest shall be calculated and paid within three months.
The writ petition is, accordingly, disposed of.
In the circumstances, there will be no order as to costs.
