GS Singhvi, J - This petition has been filed for issue of a writ of mandamus to the respondents to pay to the petitioners salary of summer vacation.
2.
The petitioners have given details of their service particulars in statement Annexure P-1 which has remained uncontroverted.
They have given out that during summer vacation they had discharged their duties in accordance with the directions given by the departmental authorities and, therefore, they are entitled to be paid salary.
The petitioners have relied on various, judgments of this Court as also of the Supreme Court dated July 23, 1990 in Civil Appeal No. 3535 of 1989 (Zile Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others).
3.
It appears from the record that the petitioners had earlier filed Civil Writ Petition No. 4066 of 1993 but the same was withdrawn by them.
During the pendency of this writ petition, the petitioners filed a review petition in that case and vide order dated 10.3.1995 this Court has disposed of their review petition with an observation that, C.W.P.
No. 4066 of 1993 shall be treated as dismissed as withdrawn with liberty to file afresh writ petition.
4.
Learned Counsel for the petitioners has also drawn my attention to a recent order passed by a Division Bench on 221995 in C.W.P.
No. 14285 of 1993, Ram Bilash Shastri and 27 others v. State of Haryana and others, wherein a direction has been given for payment of the salary of summer vacation to the writ petitioners.
5.
In view of the uncontroverted facts, there is every justification for accepting the claim of the petitioners for payment of salary of summer vacation.
6.
Consequently, the writ petition is allowed and the respondents are directed to pay salary of summer vacation to all the petitioners within a period of three months from the date of presentation of a certified copy of this order, in accordance with the directions given in order dated 221995 passed in C.W.P.
No. 14285 of 1993.
