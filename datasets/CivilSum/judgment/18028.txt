MR Agnihotri, J - This judgment shall dispose of two Civil Writ Petition Nos. 13864 of 1990 (Rakesh Kumar v. State of Punjab and others) and 13383 of 1989 (Ajay Kumar and others v. State of Punjab and others) as common questions of fact and law are involved in the same.
2.
The petitioners applied for admission to the Diploma Courses in Polytechnics located in the State of Punjab against 2 per cent seats reserved for admission in Technical Institutions in the State, for the children of the serving staff of Technical Education Department.
When they were not considered for admission and persons with lesser academic merit were granted admission, they represented to the Secretary and the Director of the Department of Technical Education and Industrial Training, Punjab, for the redressal of their grievance.
Even these representations did not bring the desired effect as the respondents were of the view that reservation was available to the children of serving employees of the Technical Education Wing and that as the administrative control of The Government Junior Technical School, Kapurthala, had been transferred to the Industrial Training Wing in 1988, the petitioners ceased to be eligible against the seats reserved for the wards of the teaching personnel.
3.
Having gone through the writ petition and the return filed by the respondents, I am of the considered view that the plea taken by the respondents is wholly untenable in law.
The transfer of administrative operational control of the educational institutions could not render the petitioners ineligible for admission.
4.
Consequently, I allow these petitions and direct the respondents to regularize the admission of the petitioners as they have already been granted provisional admission and have been permitted to attend the classes by this Court.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
