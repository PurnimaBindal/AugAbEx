MM Aggarwal, J - According to the petitioner, she was working as Steno-typist prior to 1979 in the office of District Industries Centre, Panipat.
She was promoted as Assistant on 26121979 in the scale of Rs. 525- 1050/- She continued drawing special pay of the post of Steno Typist, which was raised from Rs. 25/- to Rs. 50/- on 121981.
The petitioner continued officiating as Steno Typist.
But for her promotion to the post of Assistant, she would have drawn the pay of Rs. 590/- whereas her pay had been fixed at Rs. 5551/- on the higher post of Assistant on 1121981.
This led to a recurring financial loss to the petitioner.
Government of Haryana issued various instructions in 1983 and again in 1993 (Annexures P-2, 3 & 4) to the effect that where an employee was getting special pay and it was subsequently enhanced or refixed on 121981 and if that was more advantageous to him then that should be paid.
The pay of the petitioner was rightly fixed and payment was being made but in the year 1997, the respondents started making some deductions on account of the excess payments made.
The petitioner had made representations and served legal notices.
Instead of fixing the pay properly, letter dated 681999 (Annexure P-9) was issued for effecting recovery.
No opportunity to the petitioner was given before making this order nor she was heard.
That no recovery from her could be effected.
It was prayed that the Order Annexure P-9 being illegal be set aside and the petitioner be given benefit of special increments.
2.
This petition was contested by the respondents by filing written statement and pleadings that the petitioner was originally shown in the office of Project Officer, Rural Industries Project, Hisar as Steno Typist in the pay scale of Rs. 110-225+25.
Then pay sales of various categories were revised from 141979 by way of notification dated 2931980 and pay of the petitioner was fixed at Rs. 480+25 special pay in the revised pay scale of Rs. 400-660+25-special pay.
The petitioner was promoted as Assistant in the scale of Rs. 525-1050/- with effect from 26121979 and her pay was fixed at Rs. 525/- i.e. minimum scale as applicable to an Assistant after taking into account the element of Special Pay of Rs. 25/- per month drawn on the post of Steno Typist.
The next date of increment was allowed to the petitioner on 1121980 raising her pay to Rs. 540/-.
Then special pay was enhanced from Rs. 25/to Rs. 501- per month w.e.f.
1121980 as per instructions of the Government dated 2011983.
The pay of the petitioner was refixed from 121981.
The benefit of special pay so enhanced, had been given time and again.
This was not admissible to the petitioner and had been granted inadvertently.
After verification, it was found by the audit party and objection was raised.
The excess payment made had been directed to be recovered.
No illegal orders had been passed.
3.
Annexure P-1 as produced by the petitioner and referred to by the respondents in the written statement shows that the petitioner was getting Rs. 600+50/- as Steno Typist on 111985 but as Assistant it was Rs. 700/- after jump cross.
Annexure P-3 are the instructions dated 2011983 that in such cases when the special pay in respect of the same post has been enhanced, the rate of special pay to be taken into account for the fixation of pay in the higher post should be drawn immediately before the date of promotion subject to the fulfilment of other conditions governing the fixation of pay in the higher post.
The pay of those Steno Typists promoted to the higher post before 121981 may be refixed w.e.f.
121981 if it is more advantageous to them.
Annexure P-4 are further instructions dated 331993 referring to the earlier instructions in case those provisions were causing hardship to the employees.
It was decided that when higher post does not carry a special pay but the lower post carries a special pay in lieu of higher time scale, the incumbent may be allowed the benefit of such special pay towards fixation of pay in the higher promotion post regardless of the time for which he has drawn it on the lower post subject to it being certified that but for promotions, he would have continued to draw if on the lower post.
Annexure P-5 is the letter from Director of Industries, Haryana to the General Manager, District Industries Centre, Panipat refixing the pay of the petitioner in which as on 1121985 the pay has been shown to be Rs. 620/- whereas on 111986, it is Rs. 1400/- after revision of scales.
When the petitioner was directed to show the comparative table, then according to the petitioner, the pay being drawn to him was Rs. 1480/- on 111986, Rs. 1520/- on 111987, Rs. 1560/- on 111988, Rs. 1600/- on 111989, Rs. 1650% on 1.1.1990 and Rs. 1700/- as on 111991.
As compared to this Annexure P-5 will show the pay at Rs. 1440/- as on 111986, Rs. 1480/- as on 111987, Rs. 1560/- as on 111989, Rs. 1600/- on 1.1.1990 and Rs. 1650/- on 111991.
The difference appears to be of one increment only.
4.
Annexure P-9 is an order dated 20.7.1999 to the effect that necessary action be taken for effecting recovery of balance amount from the petitioner.
In this letter or otherwise no calculations have been shown as to what was the recovery to be effected.
5.
The petitioner had no part in originally getting her pay fixed or getting special pay after she was promoted or when the special pay to the promoted post had been abolished.
She neither made any representation or misrepresentation claiming the special pay or get the salary fixed.
It would be quite appropriate and inequitable to effect recovery of one extra increment if any given in the earlier years after a lapse of 10-15 years.
6.
Under these circumstances, this writ petition is allowed.
Order of recovery is set aside.
This is made clear that this order is in the facts and circumstances of the case and will not be treated as precedent in any other matter pertained to any other employee.
Petition Allowed.
