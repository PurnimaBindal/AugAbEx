GS Singhvi, J - The petitioners, who are working as General Manager/District Managers in the employments of Haryana State Federation of Consumers Cooperation Wholesale Stores Ltd (hereinafter described as `Confed'), have prayed for issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondent to implement its decision regarding revision of pay scales w.e.f.
111996 and pay them all the arrears together with interest.
2.
The petitioners have relied on letter dated 12111998 (Annexure P.1) issued by the Managing Director of the Confed for revision of the pay scales of the employees w.e.f.
111996 and averred that they are entitled to the fixation of pay in the scale of Rs. 6500-10500 w.e.f.
111996.
They have further averred that persons junior to them, namely, Sarvshri Swaran Lal, KT Kokne, RD Verma and Karnail Singh have been granted the benefit of higher pay scales, but they are being paid salary in the scale of Rs. 2000-3500.
3.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondent, it has been averred that the petitioners are not entitled to higher pay scale because they are not senior to Sarvshri Swaran Lal etc.  4.
We have heard learned counsel for the parties.
In our opinion, it is not necessary to delve deep into the issue relating to seniority because by an order passed today in C.W.P.
No. 10460 of 1994--S.K.
Jain v. The State of Haryana and others, 2004(2) S.C.T.
490 we have quashed the seniority list circulated vide order dated 2171994 issued by the Managing Director of the Confed and directed preparation of fresh seniority list keeping in view order dated 13.12.1993 passed in C.W.P.
No. 16464 of 1989 and other connected petitions and we feel that ends of justice would be met by directing the respondent to decide the petitioners' claim for fixation of pay in the higher scale within one month after the preparation of the revised seniority list.
5.
Hence, the writ petition is disposed of with the direction that within one month of the issuance of revised final seniority list in accordance with the direction given in C.W.P.
No. 16464 of 1989, the respondent shall decide the petitioner's claim for fixation of pay in the scale of Rs. 600-10500 w.e.f.
111996 by passing a speaking order and communicate the same to them.
If the petitioners feel aggrieved by such order, then they shall be free to avail appropriate legal remedy.
Order accordingly.
