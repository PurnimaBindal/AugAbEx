GC Garg, J - Lt. Col. Chattar Singh filed this petition seeking a writ of quo warranto with an allegation that Raj Kumar, who has been appointed as a Director of Electronics is a usurper and does not possess the qualification of the post.
2.
Mr. Manchanda, learned Addl.
AG Haryana states that no qualification has been laid down for the post of Director of Electronics though the post is a cadre post.
Once it is conceded that the post of Director of Electronics is a cadre post, the least that is expected of the Govt. is to lay down qualifications for the said post.
It is extremely difficult for us to believe that the post has been created without prescribing the qualification therefor.
Even if no qualifications have been laid down the Govt. is directed to first lay down the qualifications for the post of Director of Electronics by framing the Rules or by way of executive directions as is permissible by law and thereafter make a selection and appoint a person to the post after following due procedure.
In that view of the matter respondent No. 2 shall no longer function as Director of Electronics till such time the rules are framed, qualifications are laid down and a person is selected and appointed as per the rules of the policy laid down by the State Government.
Writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.
