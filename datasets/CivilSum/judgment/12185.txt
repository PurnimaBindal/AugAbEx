Swatanter Kumar, J - In this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, 10 petitioners pray for issuance of a writ, order or directions in the nature of Mandamus directing the respondents to grant an additional increment to the petitioners, on completion of 8/18 years total statutory service by including the service rendered by them on work charge basis, followed by regular service.
2.
Necessary facts are that the petitioners were appointed as Baildars and/or Mali on work charge basis in different years from the period ending upto 1978.
The services of the petitioners were regularised by the respondents on different dates between 31st October, 1995 to 30th August, 2002, details whereof have been given in Annexure P/1 to the writ petition.
3.
On 14th May, 1991, the Government of Haryana issued instructions which were subsequently modified, vide circular dated 781992 in regard to grant of additional increment after completion of 8/18 years of services of the employees.
4.
Some similarly situated employees of the respondents-department filed Civil Writ Petition No. 18429 of 1996 and Civil Writ Petition No. 13703 of 1998 decided on 621997 and 25.1.2001 respectively.
These writ petitions were allowed by different Benches of this Court.
The judgments whereof are annexed to the writ petition as Annexures P/4 and P/5 respectively.
The petitioners place reliance upon the judgment of Full Bench of this Court in the case of Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab, 1988(1) RSJ 433 for the grant of such relief.
Despite opportunity, no written statement was filed on behalf of the State and thus, the matter was heard on 292003 and reserved for judgment.
5.
Facts remain undisputed on record and in any case the case of the petitioners is squarely covered on law and facts by the judgment of Full Bench in the case of Kesar Chand (supra) wherein, it has been clearly held that the services rendered by the employees on work charge basis, particularly in continuity with the regular service, has to be computed towards grant of pension.
It is total length of service which has to be counted, for determining the service rendered.
6.
Still in other case, Varinder Kumar v. State of Haryana, 2002(3) Recent Services Judgments 348 : 2002(2) SCT 799 (P&H), this Court has taken a view that the service rendered by the employees on work charge basis has to be counted as part of total service rendered by the employees for the purpose of grant of the benefits of increments after 8/18 years under the said scheme.
The consistent view of this Court therefore, has been that such service would have to be counted by the respondents for grant of such benefits to the employees.
7.
We must notice that the petitioners did not serve upon the respondents any notice for justice, before filing this writ petition.
8.
The respondents had no opportunity to consider the case of the petitioners in light of their own policy and the judgments of this Court, referred to above.
9.
In the circumstances afore-stated, we allow this writ petition to a limited extent and direct the respondents to consider the case of the petitioners for grant of 8/18 years increments to the petitioners after considering the period rendered by them on work charge basis immediately preceding and in continuation of their service as part of total service.
However, parties are left to bear their own costs.
Petition partly allowed.
