RL Anand, J - Shri Rameshwar son of Shri Lal Chand has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for the issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to grant the arrears of disability pension to the petitioner starting from 2.8.1946 to 2531984 along with interest.
The petitioner has further sought the direction of this Court against the respondents to release the disability pension to him at the rate of 30% disability with effect from 2631984 instead of 20% disability with penal interest at the rate of 18%.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he was enrolled in the Indian Army on 2751942.
He was invalidated out of the service on account of the disability i.e. IAT Left Leg, on 2251944.
The petitioner was granted disability pension by assessing his disability at 20% and it was so recommended by the Medical Board.
The petitioner got the disability pension with effect from 281994 (1944 ?)
to 181946.
In the year 1946 Re-Survey Medical Board was constituted and the disability of the petitioner was again assessed at 20%.
However, the respondents discontinued the disability pension to the petitioner with effect from 2.8.1946.
Resultantly, the petitioner made representations.
In the year 1984 the disability of the petitioner was again assessed at 30% which was reduced to 20% by CCDA, Allahabad.
3.
The grouse of the petitioner is that the disability of the petitioner has been wrongly discontinued with effect from 2.8.1946 and that it has been wrongly reduced to 20% by the CCDA who is not in a position to impose his own opinion without referring the petitioner to the Medical Board.
The petitioner made the representation against the reduction of his disability from 30% to 20% but to no effect.
Hence the present writ petition.
4.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents who filed the reply and denied the allegations.
According to the respondents, the petitioner was discharged from service on 281944 being medically unfit.
He was granted disability pension at the rate of Rs. 6/- per month with effect from 281944 to 31.10.1945 and at the rate of Rs. 15/- per month with effect from 1111945 to 181946.
His disability was accepted at less than 20% with effect from 2.8.1946.
Resultantly, his disability was discontinued with effect from 2846.
Thereafter, there was no trace of the petitioner up to 1982 i.e. for a period of 36 years.
In the year 1982, the petitioner intimated to the respondents authority regarding the aggravation of his disability.
Re-survey Medical Board was constituted which assessed the disability of the petitioner at 30% for a period of 2 years starting from 2631984 which was reduced by the CCDA to 20% and in this manner according to the respondents, the petitioner is only entitled to disability pension at 20% disability.
5.
I have heard Shri Bahadur Singh, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner and Shri Anil Malhotra, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents.
6.
The first point which survives for determination is whether the petitioner is entitled to disability pension starting from 2846 upto 2531984 or not and the answer of this Court is in the negative.
This claim is being rejected on account of inordinate delay.
There was no intimation to the respondents about the whereabouts of the petitioner from 1946 to 1984.
7.
The second point for determination is whether the CCDA is competent to reduce the disability of the petitioner from 30% to 20% without referring the petitioner to the higher Medical Board or not and the answer of this Court will be again in the negative.
8.
In the view of this Court, no sanctity can be given to the views of CCDA.
Before reducing the disability of the petitioner, the petitioner was never given any intimation.
He was never directed to appear before any officer.
In this manner, the opinion of the CCDA is not binding on the petitioner.
9.
In this view of the matter, the petitioner is entitled to the disability pension on the assessed disability of 30% starting from 2731984 onwards up to 27.9.2001 when the petitioner shall appear before the Re-Survey Medical Board constituted by the respondent-authorities.
The arrears of the disability pension as stated above, shall be released to the petitioner within three months from the date of the receipt of the copy of this order failing which the petitioner shall be entitled to get 12% interest.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Dasti on payment.
Petition allowed.
