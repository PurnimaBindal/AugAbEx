GC Mital, J - The petitioner was appointed as Dai on 17111983 on ad hoc basis and she continued to be in service in the same capacity till her services were terminated on 1781988.
2.
In the meantime, selection was conducted by the Subordinate Staff Services Selection Board (for short 'the Board'), and vide selection letter, Annexure P-2 dated 14th July, 1988 she was also amongst the selection candidates.
In spite of her being in service since 1983 and on her having been selected by the Board on 471988, her services were dispensed with on 1781988 on account of the fact that her merit amongst the selection candidates by the Board was lower than the posts available.
3.
In view of Division Bench Judgment of this Court in C.P.W.
No. 72 of 1988 (Piara Singh v. State of Haryana), decided on 2691988, the services of the petitioner could not be terminated.
For the reasons recorded therein, a writ of mandamus is issued directing the respondents to reinstate the petitioner forthwith with continuity of service and all other benefits.
She will have her costs which are quantified at Rs. 500/-.
4.
As a consequence of this order, either the junior-most will have to vacate the post for the petitioner or the Government may create another additional post as it may deem proper.
Petition allowed.
