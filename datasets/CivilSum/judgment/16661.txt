SJ Mukhopadhya, J - This application has been preferred by petitioner against the order contained in Letter No. 151 dated 9-1-2002 whereby and whereunder the 4th respondent (Senior Personnel Officer (MP) C.C.L.
Ranchi) informed the 5th respondent (Staff Officer (Personnel) C.C.L.
Hazaribagh) that the claim of petitioner for compassionate appointment has been rejected.
2.
According to the petitioner his father, Late Kanhu Mahato was an employee of Central Coalfields Limited who died in harness on 3-8-1999.
He applied for compassionate appointment within two months but the respondents have illegally rejected his claim.
3.
The counsel for the respondents referred to Letter No. 151 dated 9-1-2003 and submitted that the petitioner applied for compassionate appointment after more than six months from the date of death of his father.
For the said reason, there being delay, the competent authority has not considered his case for compassionate appointment.
4.
In the case of Roopna Manjhi v.
C.C.L., 2003(1) JCR 324 (Jharkhand), this Court observed that at least one and a half year from the death of the deceased employee should be allowed to the dependents to apply for compassionate appointment.
In the said case, the letter of rejection was set aside and case of Roopna Manjhi was remitted for reconsideration.
Judgment of learned Single Judge was also affirmed by a Division Bench in L.P.A. No. 16 of 2003.
5.
The case of the petitioner being similar, the rejection order contained in Letter No. 151 dated 9-1-2003 is set aside with direction to respondents to reconsider the case of the petitioner for compassionate appointment within a period of three months from the date of receipt/production of a copy of this order.
6.
The writ petition stands disposed of with the aforesaid observations and directions.
Order accordingly.
