- Satya Parkash husband of the petitioner sought voluntary retirement and in pursuance of his request he was retired from service w.e.f.
3181994.
He, however, unfortunately died on 1091994.
The petitioner being the widow of the deceased-retired employee sought the family pension, D.C.R.G.
payable to the retiree on 3181994 and other retiral benefits which was, however, almost delayed.
This delay forced her to seek relief by filing of the present writ petition in this Court on 24.3.1998.
During the pendency of the writ petition, she was allowed family pension and D.C.R.G.
on 9.3.1999.
There is no explanation for the delay caused in making the payment.
2.
We, therefore, direct the State of Haryana to pay interest at the rate of 12% per annum to the petitioner on the amount released to her from 1031995 to 9.3.1999 when the payment was actually made.
3.
We further direct that in case, the petitioner is entitled to the revision of family pension w.e.f.
11196 on revision of scale of pay and pension, the same would also be allowed to her and payment be made to her within three months from today, failing which she shall be entitled to the interest at the rate of 18% per annum on the amount due to her on its revision i.e. 111996 to 112000.
4.
This order shall be treated as a decree under Section 21 of the Legal Services Authority Act, 1987, and shall be meticulously complied with.
5.
Writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
