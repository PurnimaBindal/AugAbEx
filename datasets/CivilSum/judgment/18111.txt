Rakesh Kumar Jain, J - The petitioners have challenged the action of the respondents for not considering the petitioner's eligibility for the post of Staff Nurse under the Ex-Servicemen Category.
2.
The case, as pleaded by the petitioners, is that vide Public Notice dated 662006, respondent No. 2 i.e., Director, Health and Family Welfare Department, Punjab has invited applications to fill up 690 posts of Staff Nurse on contract basis on the fixed salary on the following terns and conditions :-  "1.
Applicant should be resident of State of Punjab.
(Along with Proof).
2.
Applicant should be between 18-35 years of age.
There will be relaxation of 5 years in case of SC/ST.
3.
Punjabi upto Matric Standard is essesntial for Sr.No.
1 to 5.
4.
Candidates will be considered on merit basis in the category for which they have applied e.g. General and Reserve Category.
5.
Reservation will be applicable according to instructions of State Government.
Application along with attested testimonials in accordance with format should be presented by 20.6.2006 either in person or by Post in the office of Dr. Sukhdev Singh, Additional Director, Room No. 219 office of Director Health and Family Welfare (Punjab), Family Welfare Bhawan, Sector 34-A, Chandigarh."
3.
The applications were to be submitted on proforma which is reproduced as under :-  "Format of application  1.
Post of which applied.
2.
Name of Applicant (In Block Letters)  3.
Father's name (in Block Letters)  4.
Date of Birth (Age in years) 5.
Male/Female  6.
Nationality  7.
Marital Status  8.
Whether belong to Reserve Category ?
If yes then proof and certificates be attached.
9.
Character certificate of the last attended institute.
10.
Permanent Address.
11.
Address for Correspondence.
12.
Qualifications.
Sr. No., Educational and others, Year of Passing, University/Board, Max Marks, Marks obtained", % age  4.
Attracted by the Public Notice, both the petitioners submitted their application forms in which petitioner No. 1 applied in General Category and petitioner No. 2 in Scheduled Caste Category.
5.
On 3082006, another advertisement was issued in which it was mentioned that applicants, who have applied in response to the advertisement dated 66206, need not apply again.
6.
In December, 2006, another public notice was issued by the respondents to the effect that original documents submitted by the applicants shall be checked in which different dates were given for different categories as prescribed in Annexure P-14.
7.
The case of the petitioners is that they are the dependents of ex- servicemen whom the District Sainik Welfare Officers, Mansa and Hoshiarpur, issued relevant certificates on 15122006 vide Annexure P-11 and P-12, which were produced before the respondents on 16122006.
However, the documents of the Category of ex-servicemen were examined by the authorities but the petitioners have been ignored on the ground that they have applied after the cut off date mentioned in the advertisement i.e. 20.6.2006.
It was maintained by them that since they have produced the certificates of ex-servicemen issued on 15122006 before the concerned authorities on 16122006, their case should have been considered in the category of ex-servicemen and they should have been given the appointments as per their merits.
8.
Notice of motion was issued.
9.
Reply has been filed by Assistant Director, Directorate of Health and Family Welfare Punjab, Chandigarh on behalf of respondents No. 1 and 2.
It has been urged that advertisement was issued on 662006 in the leading `The Tribune', `Daily Ajit Jalandhar' newspapers on 662006 and subsequently on 3082006 for inviting applications for the post of staff nurse alongwith other kind of posts.
The last date for receipt of application for these posts were 20.6.2006 and 7.9.2006 respectively.
While advertising these posts at condition No. 4 (Annexure P-13), it was made clear that the applicant shall be considered on merit basis in the same reserve category in which they reservation policy of the State Govt. will be applicable.
Since petitioner Nos. 1 and 2 initially applied in the General and Scheduled Caste Category, respectively, in response to advertisement dated 662006 (Annexure P-13) and after going through the advertisement dated December, 2006 (Annexure P-14), they came to know that they are not entitled under the category in which they have applied, the petitioners changed their mind for the reserve category and filled in their applications as dependents of ex-servicemen, which is in contradiction of Condition No. 4 (Annexure P-13).
10.
We have heard both the learned counsel for the parties perused the record.
11.
During the course of hearing, the State counsel produced the photocopy of the application form of petitioner No. 1 Chhinderpal Kaur in which Column No. 8 relates to reserved category but she marked `No' against that category while applying in response to the advertisement dated 662006 in which the last date of submission of application form was 20.6.2006.
Similarly, it has been mentioned in the written statement that petitioner No. 2 had applied in Scheduled Caste category which has not been denied.
Therefore, both the petitioners did not apply till the cut off date i.e. 20.6.2006 under the category of dependents of the ex-servicemen.
Since there is no application before the respondents pertaining to the reserve category of ex-servicemen before the cut off date i.e. 20.6.2006, the plea of the petitioners that they have obtained the certificates of dependant of the ex-servicemen on 15122006 and produced the same before the authorities on 16122006 in response to Annexure P-14, is inconsequential because their application is silent about this category of which they wanted to take the advantage.
12.
It is well settled that sanctity of the cut off date has to be maintained especially when it was categorically provided in condition No. 4 of the terms and conditions of Annexure P-13 that candidates shall be considered on the merit basis in the category in which they have applied i.e. General, Reserve Category, they cannot change the category after the cut off date especially when petitioner No. 1 had applied in General Category in which her percentage was less whereas petitioner No. 2 had applied in the Scheduled Caste category in which she could not make any place.
In view of the aforesaid reasons, we do not find any merit in the case of the petitioners and as such, writ petition is dismissed in limine.
However, there shall be no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
