SP Kurdukar, CJ - Admitted.
Pleadings of the parties are complete.
By consent the writ petition is placed on board and called out for hearing.
2.
It is needless to set out in details the averments made in the petition.
Suffice it to state that the petitioner was appointed as temporary engineer in Class II service and was promoted as Executive Engineer in Class I on 13.7.1971.
Some time in the year 1986 a charge-sheet was served upon the petitioner relating to misconduct of the year 1982-83.
During pending enquiry the petitioner came to be promoted as Superintending Engineer on 1821987.
The enquiry was completed by the Enquiry Officer and he submitted his report in August 1992 exonerating the petitioner.
However, the formal order of dropping the charges against the petitioner came to be passed on 11101993.
On 2171993 six Superintending Engineers came to be promoted to the rank of Chief Engineers.
It is alleged by the petitioner that out of these six promoted Superintending Engineers, five persons were junior to him.
3.
After having been exonerated from charges in August 1992, the petitioner on 2871993 made a representation to the Chief Minister requesting him to consider his claim for promotion.
On 13.10.1993 the petitioner again made another representation to the Minister concerned to consider his claim for promotion to the rank of Chief Engineer.
Pending decision on these two representations, it is common premises that on 821994 a fresh charge-sheet was served upon the petitioner in respect of alleged mis-conduct of the year 1989.
This mis-conduct relates to an award given by the petitioner in the year 1989.
It is common premise that against the said award, FAO is pending in this Hon'ble High Court.
4.
The petitioner on 10101994 submitted his reply to the charge-sheet.
An Enquiry Officer has been appointed and enquiry is likely to be commenced very soon.
5.
Mr. Khehar, learned Senior Counsel appearing for the petitioner informed us that the petitioner is to retire on 3151995.
6.
The principal contention which needs to be considered in this writ petition is as to whether the State Government can ignore the claim of the petitioner for consideration of promotion to the rank of Chief Engineer after he has been exonerated on 11101993 when five persons junior to him were considered and promoted on 2171993 as Chief Engineers.
As of that date i.e. 2171993 there were neither any allegations of mis-conduct nor any charge- sheet was served or any enquiry was pending.
Learned counsel for the petitioner urged that the petitioner was not considered for the post of Chief Engineer when persons junior to him were promoted on 2171993, because at that time departmental enquiry was pending in respect of alleged mis-conduct of the year 1982.
Mr. Khehar urged that the respondent is bound to consider the case of the petitioner for the promotional post of Chief Engineer on the date when the charges against him were dropped and if he was found suitable, he ought to be promoted to the post of Chief Engineer w.e.f.
2171993 when persons junior to him (the petitioner) were promoted.
7.
Mr. Arun Sekhri, Under Secretary to Government of Punjab, Irrigation & Power Department, Punjab, filed the written statement on behalf of the respondent and he admits that six Superintending Engineers were promoted on 2171993 and the petitioner was ignored because departmental proceedings were/are pending against him.
The only reason given by the respondent in the written statement is that since fresh enquiry is pending against the petitioner, he could not be considered for the promotional post of Chief Engineer.
8.
Rule 9 of the Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules 1964, is the relevant rule for the purpose of promotion in the present case.
The petitioner has set out the said Rule at page 7 of the writ petition, the correctness thereof is not disputed before us.
9.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties and in view of the facts narrated hereinbefore, in our opinion, it was obligatory for the respondent- State of Punjab to consider the claim of the petitioner for the post of Chief Engineer when he was exonerated on 11101993.
The petitioner having been found `not guilty of any mis-conduct', it was necessary for the respondent to consider the petitioner's claim for the post of Chief Engineer in accordance with Rules and Regulations, particularly, Rule 9 which is set out at page 7 of the writ petition.
The petitioner's claim for promotion to the post of Chief Engineer having not been considered by the respondent until second charge- sheet was served upon him on 821994, it would not be open to the respondent to take shelter of second charge-sheet dated 821994 to deny the claim of the petitioner for the post of Chief Engineer, which claim was due to him by virtue of his having been exonerated by the disciplinary authority vide order dated 11101993.
It is on these admitted facts, we are of the opinion that the claim of the petitioner ought to have been considered for the post of Chief Engineer some time in October 1993.
Obviously he should have been considered for the post of Chief Engineer from the date his juniors were promoted as Chief Engineers.
10.
We accordingly direct the respondent to constitute a Departmental Promotion Committee (D.P.C.)
and submit the papers of the petitioner for consideration to the said Committee for considering his claim to the post of Chief Engineer.
The Departmental Promotion Committee to complete the entire exercise preferably on or before 3151995 and at any rate before 3171995.
If the petitioner is found suitable by the Departmental Promotion Committee in term of Rule 9, the respondent will pass appropriate order giving benefits of this promotional post of Chief Engineer from the date when his juniors were promoted i.e. 2171993.
This is, however, subject to the decision of the Departmental Promotion Committee.
This Writ Petition to stand disposed of in the above terms.
A copy of the order be given dasti.
