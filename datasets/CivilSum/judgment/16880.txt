RL Anand, J - I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance, have gone through the record of the case.
2.
The dispute in this case lies in a narrow compass.
The petitioner is a Sewadar.
He was appointed in the year 1989.
His services were regularised on 8th October, 1992 on the recommendation of the Manager Gurdwara Shri Dukh Niwaran Sahib, Patiala by the President of Shiromani Gurdwara Prabhandhak Committee, Amritsar and a regular grade was granted to the petitioner.
3.
The grievance of the petitioner is that though a regular grade has been given to him but he has not been granted any increment so far in the regular grade w.e.f.
January 1996 in spite of the fact that there is nothing adverse against his service record.
4.
The prayer of the petitioner is being opposed by the learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents mainly on the ground that it is not clear as to which of the respondents is to grant the benefit of regular increments to the petitioner.
5.
The petitioner is the employee of respondent No. 1 and he is presently posted at Gurdwara Sahib Baba Sukha Singh, Neelpur, Tehshil Rajpura.
Since Gurdwara Sahib Baba Sukha Singh Neelpur (respondent No. 2) is managed and controlled by respondent No. 1, in these circumstances directions are given to respondent Nos. 1 and 2 to grant regular increments to the petitioner as admissible to him, as per the regular grade w.e.f.
January 1996.
The order of this Court shall be complied with by the respondents within three months from the receipt of a copy of this order.
This petition stands disposed of accordingly.
Dasti order.
Petition allowed.
