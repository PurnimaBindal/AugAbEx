SS Nijjar, J - We have heard the learned counsel for the parties at length and perused the record of the case.
2.
Mr. Dhanda has vehemently argued that the petitioner had withdrawn the notice of pre-mature retirement before the period of three months.
He was, therefore, entitled to be re-enlisted on the post of Head Constable.
According to the learned counsel, the petitioner had initially applied for earned leave for 240 days.
Thereafter, he was to be given three months time for voluntary retirement with effect from 01.08.1995.
He would have been on earned leave from 04121994 to 31071995.
3.
Written statement has been filed by the respondents.
It is submitted that voluntary retirement of the petitioner was accepted on 31071995.
He was retired on the expiry of the period of three months notice.
4.
We are of the considered opinion that the claim put forward by the petitioner cannot be accepted.
The voluntary retirement of the petitioner was accepted on 31071995.
A perusal of the letter dated 25.10.1994 shows that the petitioner had stated that on 21.05.1995 he had completed 25 years of service.
He further stated that he voluntarily wants to go on pension.
He had also requested that he be granted the earned leave which may be due and admissible to him.
Accepting the aforesaid request, the learned leave of the petitioner was sanctioned from 04121994 to 31071995.
He was ordered to be retired with effect from 01.08.1995.
We find no infirmity in the order of retirement.
5.
Learned counsel for the petitioner further argued that, in any event, he was entitled to be re-enlisted on the post of Head Constable under Rule 1225 of the Punjab Police Rules.
A bare perusal of the aforesaid Rule shows that it would not be applicable in the case of voluntary retirement.
The petitioner could have been offered the post of Constable under Rule 12.24 of the Punjab Police Rules.
The respondents actually offered the post of Constable to the petitioner, but he refused to accept the same.
In view of the above, we find no merit in the present writ petition.
Dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
