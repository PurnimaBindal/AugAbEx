Swatanter Kumar, J - The petitioner Jai Pal was appointed to the post of Helper in Haryana Roadways Depot through employment exchange on 1121985.
The petitioner was promoted to the next post of Assistant Fitter on 9.7.1987.
According to the petitioner his service record was good and he had performed the duties to the satisfaction of all concerned.
He was charge-sheeted for misbehaviour with the Head Mechanic.
Charge-sheet was served on him on 361994 to which the petitioner gave a reply.
The departmental enquiry remained pending and in the meanwhile 13 other Assistant Fitters were promoted to the post of Fitter on 2871995.
After the petitioner made representations, on 1981997 punishment of stoppage of one annual increment with cumulative effect was imposed upon him.
The petitioner challenged this punishment before the High Court in CWP No. 18281 of 1997.
The writ petition was disposed of directing the respondents to consider the case of the petitioner for promotion.
Again the petitioner was not promoted when six other Assistant Fitters Junior to the petitioner were promoted on 661997.
Still further apprehending that the petitioner would not be promoted again, he filed this writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, for issuance or directions to the respondents to promote him with effect from 661997 with further prayer for quashing the order dated 661997 vide much other six persons junior to the petitioner were promoted as fitters.
The petitioner also prayed for quashing of Annexure P/5 which is the order passed by the respondents on 821999, stating that the petitioner was not entitled to promotion to the post of Fitter as on 2871995.
2.
Upon notice the respondents filed a detailed reply.
The case of the respondents has been precisely stated in paragraph No. 2 of the preliminary submissions, which reads as under :-  "That on the basis of order dated 16.9.98 passed by this Hon'ble Court the case of the petitioner was considered for promotion and found that the persons shown in para No. 5 of the present writ petition stated to be juniors to the petitioners and promoted on 28795 belonged to the category of Backward Class and they were promoted on the basis of Govt. instructions.
The petitioner belongs to General category and he was not entitled to the promotion since from 28795.
This fact could not be mentioned in the written statement while filing the reply of writ petition No. 18281 of 1997 filed by the petitioner.
To clear this aspect the respondent has passed the speaking order dated 8299 and it was clearly mentioned in this order that petitioner is not entitled for promotion from back date i.e. 28795 because the junior fitters namely S/Shri Phool Singh and Bhupinder Singh belonged to Backward Class.
The case of the petitioner was not considered for promotion as on 6.6.97 due to pendency of enquiry under rule 7 of Haryana Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal Rules, 1987 against the petitioner on the charges or misconduct.
The petitioner had also made a confession in writing during the pendency or enquiry.
The competent Authority after taking a lenient view awarded punishment under Rule 8 of Haryana Civil Services (P & A) Rules, 1987 vide order Annexure P-5 attached with the present writ petition.
Thereafter the case of promotion was considered by the Competent Authority and petitioner was promoted to the post of fitter vide order dated 1598 a copy of which is attached as Annexure R-2.
That respondent No. 3 was also promoted as fitter inadvertently who was not possessing the prescribed qualifications as middle pass, hence the respondent No. 3 was reverted to the post of Asstt.
Fitter vide order No. 2170/ECW dt. 3.7.98, a copy of which is attached as Annexure R-3.
3.
From the above narrated facts it is clear that the departmental proceedings were pending against the petitioner on 2871995 and the order of punishment was passed only on 1981997.
The petitioner had pleaded guilty during the departmental enquiry.
The petitioner had filed a civil writ petition No. 18281 of 1997 challenging the order of punishment dated 1891997, Annexure P/10 to that petition, and further that he should be promoted to the rank of fitter with effect from the date his juniors were promoted.
The Division Bench rejected the prayer of the petitioner for quashing Annexure P/10, as such the order of punishment dated 1981997 has also attained finality.
However, the Division Bench directed the respondents to consider the case of the petitioner for promotion as fitter with effect from 2871995 when his juniors were promoted.
The respondents considered the case of the petitioner and rejected his case for promotion to the post of fitter as on 2871995 for valid and proper reasons vide Annexure P/5 to the present writ petition.
It is stated that case of the petitioner was kept pending because a charge-sheet had been served upon him and the department proceedings were pending.
As such the petitioner was not found fit to be promoted on 661997.
Ultimately the petitioner was promoted to the post of fitter on 151998.
The two persons who were promoted above the petitioner on 2871995 belong to Backward Classes while the petitioner belong to general category.
Both these reasons which have been stated in Annexure P/5 cannot be faulted for any reasons.
Thus, we decline to interfere in the order dated 1691998.
However, the question that still survives for consideration is whether the department was justified in ignoring the petitioner for promotion even on 661997.
4.
It is a settled principle of law that during the pendency of the departmental enquiry sealed cover procedure can be adopted or the case of a candidate can be deferred for consideration, but his claim for promotion cannot be denied on that ground alone.
On 2351997 a seniority list was circulated by the respondents vide which the petitioner was known senior to Karam Chand, Lal Chand, Narender Kumar, Rajender Singh, Nem Chand and Siri Kishan.
All these persons have been promoted as Fitters from the post of Assistant Fitter vide order dated 661997 Annexure P/4.
While challenging the validity of Annexure P/4 the petitioner has not taken care to join all these persons as respondents, therefore, we cannot set aside the appointments of these persons even if they were junior to the petitioner and have been promoted.
It was the obligation of the petitioner to implead these persons as respondents to the petition if he prayed before the Court for quashing their appointment as on 661997.
5.
The respondents have admittedly promoted the petitioner now vide order dated 151998.
The order of punishment was passed against the petitioner on 1981997.
Thus, the petitioner's case should have been considered for promotion in accordance with rules immediately thereafter.
In fact once the proceedings have concluded and punishment has been imposed, the petitioner was entitled to be considered for promotion in accordance with rules.
It can no way be justified that the respondents have given promotion to the petitioner with effect from 151998 and not from 661997 or August, 1997 when admittedly the departmental proceedings had concluded.
6.
In view of the above discussion, we are of the considered view that petitioner was not entitled to promotion as on 2871995 as the persons promoted, junior to him, belong to backward classes, but the petitioner was entitled to get promotion with effect from 661997/1981997.
Consequently, we allow this writ petition limited to the extent that the case of the petitioner would be considered for promotion to the post of Fitter with effect from 661997/19.8.997, the date when six other persons junior to the petitioner were promoted as Fitters.
However, there shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
