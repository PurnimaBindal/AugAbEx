GS Singhvi, J - These petitions involve challenge to the promotions/posting orders dated 13.12.1979, 1511984 and 10.2.1984 issued by the Government of Haryana, promoting the private respondents as Sub Divisional Officers/Sub Divisional Engineers.
2.
Petitioner, Mool Chand Chaudhary (CWP No. 1112/84) joined service as Sectional Officer in the Irrigation Department of the Government of Haryana after he had passed Diploma in Civil Engineering.
During the course of service he passed AMIE in August, 1980.
He has challenged the promotions of respondents No. 3 to 5 on the ground that they do not fulfil the conditions of eligibility incorporated in Rule 7 of the Haryana Service of Engineers Class II, Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970 (hereinafter referred to as `1970 Rules').
According to the petitioner these respondents have passed Diploma in Production Engineering which does not find mention in Appendix `B' attached with `1970 Rules' and, therefore, they were ineligible to be promoted as Sub Divisional Officers even on ad hoc basis.
Promotion of respondent No. 6 has been challenged on the ground that he was ineligible to the promoted against source-IV of Rule 6 of `1970 Rules'.
By placing reliance on the order dated 3.10.1983 passed in CWP No. 5630 of 1981, Jagdish Prasad Gupta v. State of Haryana, it has been pleaded that promotion of respondent No. 6 was found to be unjustified by the High Court but he has been again promoted on regular basis by order dated 1511984.
3.
Petitioners, CS Tayal and Mahabir Sharma (CWP No. 1455/84) have questioned the legality of promotions of private respondents on similar grounds by urging that these respondents were not qualified to be considered for promotion.
In CWP No. 1738/85 and CWP No. 2552/85, the petitioners have questioned the promotion of respondents No. 2 to 8 on the ground that the examination passed by the these respondents from the Machanical Engineers Associations (India) is not one of the qualifications enumerated in Appendix `B' and it cannot be treated as equivalent to AMIE  4.
In the separate replies filed by respondents the promotion orders have been justified on the ground that the qualifications possessed by the private respondents namely, Diploma in Production Engineering or B.Sc.
in Production Engineering or the examination conducted by the Mechanical Engineers Association which has been subsequently re-named as Institution of Mechanical Engineers (India) have been treated at par with AMIE Part `A' and `B' and the Institute of Engineers has itself recognised these qualifications for appointment against supervisory posts.
5.
In so far as challenge to the orders dated 18121979 is concerned, writ petition Nos. 1112/84 and 1455/84 deserve to be rejected on the ground of laches as has been held in CWP No. 1210/84 decided on 2571996.
For the detailed reasons which have been incorporated in my order dated 2571996, I hold that challenge to the order dated 13.12.1979 is highly belated and on that ground these two writ petitions deserves to be dismissed qua order dated 13.12.1979.
6.
In so far as the order dated 1511984 is concerned, it has been noticed by me in the order dated 2571996 that during the pendency of the writ petition, the Government has undertaken afresh exercise for making promotions to the post of Sub Divisional Officer in the light of the directions given by the Apex Court and as a result of this exercise order dated 30th November, 1992 has been issued.
In the reply filed by them to CWP No. 1455/84, the respondents No. 1 and 2 have made reference to the order dated 30th November, 1992 (erroneously described as order dated 1121992).
That order has not been challenged in these writ petitions by way of amendment and, therefore, any adjudications on the legality of the order dated 1511984 which already stands superseded by order dated 30th November, 1992 will be futile.
7.
For the aforesaid reasons, the writ petitions are dismissed.
It is, however, made clear that the petitioners shall be free to challenge the order dated 30th November, 1992 and this decision shall not impede their right to challenge the legality of order dated 30th November, 1992.
