2.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance, have gone through the record of this case.
3.
The petitioner Smt. Bimla Devi is seeking regularisation of her services w.e.f.
31st January, 1996 in view of the policy dated 7th March, 1996, Annexure P-1 and the policy dated 18th March, 1996, Annexure P-2, formulated by the State of Haryana from time to time.
The prayer of the petitioner for regularisation has been rejected mainly on the ground that there was a break in service of more than one month at a time in a year.
4.
Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner has invited my attention to a judgment of the Hon'ble Division Bench reported as Smt. Ramai v. Haryana Urban Development Authority, 2000(4) Services Cases Today 992, where a similar point was discussed and decided by the Hon'ble Bench and it was held that if the break in service is on account of medical illness beyond the control of the employee, such employee cannot be discriminated.
Similar is the case in hand.
Following the ratio of the Hon'ble Division Bench, this writ petition is allowed and directions are given to the respondents to regularise the services of the petitioner w.e.f.
31 st January, 1996 as per the policy and she should also be given all the constitutional benefits within three months from the receipt of the copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
