JL Gupta, J - The petitioner, a Major in the Army was convicted by the Additional Sessions Judge, Rohtak for offences under Sections 306 and 498-A of the Indian Penal Code.
In spite of the petitioner having been granted bail and the sentence having been suspended by this Court, the Central Government in exercise of its powers under Section 19 of the Army Act, 1950 dismissed the petitioner from service.
This order was conveyed to the petitioner vide letter dated September 23, 1988.
A copy of the order is appended as Annexure P4 with the writ petition.
The petitioner filed a writ petition challenging the order inter alia on the ground that it could not have been passed when his sentence had been suspended.
A written statement has been filed on behalf of the respondents justifying the action.
2.
During the pendency of the writ petition, the appeal filed by the petitioner viz. Crl.
Appeal No. 109-SB of 1988 against the order of conviction passed by the learned Additional Sessions Judge, Rohtak has been accepted by JS Sekhon, J vide judgment dated October 18, 1989.
Consequently, the very basis on which the impugned action has been taken, has been annulled.
That being so, the order of dismissal cannot be sustained.
It has accordingly to be set aside.
3.
I. therefore, allow this petition and set aside the impugned action of the respondents in dismissing the petitioner from the service.
The petitioner shall be deemed to have continued in service and is entitled to all consequential benefits like arrears of salary and promotions etc.
He will also be entitled to his costs, which are assessed at Rs. 2,000/-.
petition allowed.
