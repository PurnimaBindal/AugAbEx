GS Singhvi, J - The only point which needs adjudication is as to whether the service rendered by the petitioner as Librarian w.e.f.
16.8.1963 should be taken into consideration for the purpose of fixation of his pay in the grade of Rs. 3,700-5,700 w.e.f.
111986 in accordance with the notifications issued by the Government of Punjab on 641989 and 24.5.1989.
2.
The petitioner joined service as Librarian in the DAV College, Hoshiarpur, on the basis of letter (Annexure P-1) dated 2771963 issued by the President, DAV College Managing Committee, Hoshiarpur, after the petitioner had been recommended by a selection committee.
His appointment was approved by the Vice-Chancellor, Panjab University vide his letter dated 1.10.1963.
It appears that DAV College Managing Committee, New Delhi had interfered with the working of the DAV College, Managing Committee, Hoshiarpur, some time in the year 1976-77 and as a part of this interference, the petitioner was transferred as Librarian from DAV College, Hoshiarpur to DAV College, Malout vice Shri SP Narula vide Annexure P-13.
He was transferred back to DAV College, Hoshiarpur vide telegram dated 861977 (Annexures P14).
3.
Pay scales of the post of Librarian were revised from time to time.
Vide notification dated 641989 the Government of Punjab revised the grade of the College cadre Librarians with a condition that the existing Librarians would be given the benefit of senior grade and selection grade on fulfilment of the conditions specified in that notification.
Those who had completed 16 years of service as on 111986 were to be given the grade of Rs. 3,700-5,700. the conditions laid down by the University Grants Commission were relaxed vide notifications dated 941990, 11.4.1990 and 1591992.
The petitioner was given revised grade of Rs. 3,700-5,700 w.e.f.
111986 but payment in the revised rade was stopped after some time on account of non-receipt of approval from the competent departmental authority.
4.
When petitioner's case was forwarded to respondent No. 2, it was rejected on the ground that the petitioner's service should be taken into account only w.e.f.
1061977.
This decision was conveyed to the Principal of the College vide Annexure P-10 and the same has been repeated vide Annexure P-12 dated 15.6.1993.
Respondents have declined to allow selection grade to the petitioner on the ground that the regular service rendered only to one college can be taken into account for the purpose of grant of selection grade and as the petitioner had served DAV College, Hoshiarpur, only from 1061977 his past service cannot be taken into account for the purpose of grant of the benefit of selection grade.
5.
Argument of Shri Tewari, learned counsel for the petitioner, is that the rules framed by the Government of Punjab do not contemplate counting of service rendered only in one college for the purpose of grant of selection grade and, therefore, the decision taken by respondent No. 2 that previous service of the petitioner is not to be counted for the purpose of grant of selection grade is patently illegal and arbitrary.
Shri Tewari further argued that the petitioner had rendered service under the same Managing Committee from 1963 to 31121985 and his forced transfer for a period of six months by the Managing Committee of New Delhi cannot deprive him of his right to be granted selection grade w.e.f.
111986 because total service rendered by the petitioner was more than 22 years as on 111986.
Mrs. Tuli argued that service rendered by the petitioner in different institutions cannot be clubbed together for the purpose of grant of selection grade and, therefore, the decision taken by respondent No. 2 cannot be termed as arbitrary or unreasonable.
6.
A look at the notification dated 641989 (Annexure P-3) together with Appendix `A' shows that selection grade of Rs. 3700-5700 is admissible to the Senior Librarians who have completed 16 years of service subject to good performance appraisal reports.
Neither the notification dated 641989 nor Appendix `A' contemplates service under one college as a condition for grant of selection grade.
It is possible to understand the rationale of requirement of 16 years of continuous service as a Librarian but the plea raised by the respondents, namely, that service rendered by an incumbent in different institutions run by different managements cannot be counted for the purpose of grant of selection grade or their plea that a Librarian must have worked in a particular college for 16 years so as to be entitled to the grant of selection grade does not appear to be reasonable.
Assertions made by the respondents in para 3 of the preliminary objection and para 4 of the main reply are not supported by the rules framed by the Government.
Similarly, the decision contained in Annexures P-10 and P-12, namely, that the service rendered by the petitioner only w.e.f.
1061977 is to be counted for the purpose of grant of selection grade is wholly arbitrary and unjust.
Respondent No. 2 has not referred to any Government instructions requiring 16 years continuous service in a particular college or under a particular managing committee as a condition to the grant of selection grade and, therefore denial of selection grade to the petitioner cannot be upheld.
Arbitrariness of the decision taken by respondent No. 2 is proved from the fact that respondent No. 2 has altogether ignored the fact that petitioner had served under the same employer without any interruption and his transfer/posting from one college to another could not have been made a ground to deny him the benefit of selection grade.
7.
So far as respondent No. 3 is concerned, it has filed a reply and expressed its willingness to give benefit of selection grade to the petitioner subject to the sanction of respondent No. 2.
8.
In the result, the writ petition is allowed.
Decisions contained in Annexures P-10 and P-12 are declared illegal.
The respondents are directed to give benefit of selection grade of Librarian to the petitioner w.e.f.
111986.
The petitioner shall be paid arrears within a period of four months of the submission of a certified copy of this order.
Parties are left to bear their own costs.
Petition allowed.
