GS Singhvi and HS Brar, JJ.
- We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the writ petition.
In our considered view, the writ petition deserves to be disposed of by a short order.
On the basis of admitted facts, it is clear that order Annexure P-7 was passed by respondent No. 1 without making an enquiry in accordance with the principles of natural justice.
It is admitted position that no charge sheet was served upon the petitioner and he was not given any opportunity to submit his explanation in regard to those charges.
Notwithstanding such an enquiry, respondents have held the petitioner guilty of causing loss to respondent No. 1 and ordered the recovery of Rs. 650485 from the salary of the petitioner alongwith interest at the rate of 18% pa upto 31121991 and 24% from 111992 till the entire amount is recovered and also administered a strict warning to him.
2.
In our opinion, the respondents could have taken action against the petitioner only after holding an enquiry and as no such enquiry has been held, order Annexure P-7 is liable to be quashed.
3.
Order Annexure P-7 was affirmed in appeal vide Annexure P-11 and once we have held that the original order Annexure P-7 has been passed in violation of principles of natural justice, the appellate order must also fall flat.
4.
Consequently, the writ petition is allowed.
Orders Annexure P7 and P11 passed by respondents No. 1 and 2 respectively are declared illegal and are hereby quashed.
However, respondent No. 1 would be at liberty to take action in the matter in accordance with law.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
