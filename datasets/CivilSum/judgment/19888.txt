JV Gupta, J - This LPA is directed against the order of learned Single Judge dated 18th of February, 1983 whereby the termination order the writ petitioner was set aside.
2.
It has been stated at the Bar that at the time of admission of the writ petition on 17111977 the operation of the termination order was stayed and later on the writ petition was allowed on 1821983.
Thus the petitioner is continuing in service without any break.
3.
The services of the petitioner were terminated on the ground that he did not possess the requisite qualifications prescribed for the post.
Accordingly to the learned Single Judge, it was not a matter of dispute that prior to the appointment of the writ petitioner he had disclosed his qualifications in the application and was regularly selected through the Subordinate Services Selection Board.
In case the authorities felt at a later stage that he did not fulfil the requisite qualifications, then obviously they had to go into the matter by holding a regular enquiry against him and could pass the impugned order only after hearing him.
It was thus manifest that the petitioner had been punished by the termination order which, on the face of it, was violative of Article 311 of the Constitution of India.
There is no ground to interfere in the L.P.A. at this stage when the petitioner is continuing in service without break.
Consequently, the appeal, fails and is dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
