GS Singhvi, ACJ.
- In this petition, the petitioner has prayed for directing the respondents to allow them to appear in the test being held for recruitment of Supervisors under ICDS Project.
2.
A perusal of the record shows that in pursuance of notice Annexure P1 issued by Director, Social Welfare, Punjab vide which applicants were invited for recruitment of Supervisors from among Angawari Workers, the petitioners submitted their applications to Child Welfare Project Officer, Jhunir, but the same were not considered apparently because the competent authority did not receive them within the stipulated time.
3.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents, it has been averred that the applications filed by petitioner Nos. 6 to 12 and 16 were received along with letter dated 27121993 of Child Development Project Officer, Jhunir i.e. after completion of the process of issuing roll numbers.
According to the respondents, the candidature of the petitioners could not be considered due to the delayed received (receipt ?)
of their applications.
4.
In my opinion, the reason put forward by the respondents for not considering the candidature of the petitioners does not appear to be legally sustainable because they sent their applications through proper channel and they cannot be penalised or put to dis-advantageous position on account of default committed by the departmental authority.
However, at this belated stage, no effective relief can be given to the petitioners because they have neither amended the writ petition so as to challenge the selection made in 1994 nor they have impleaded the selected candidates as party respondents.
For the reason mentioned above, the writ petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
