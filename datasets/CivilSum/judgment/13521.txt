GS Singhvi, J - This petition has been filed for issue of a mandamus to the respondents to reimburse the expenses incurred by the petitioner on open heart by-pass surgery which the petitioner had undergone at Escorts Heart Institute and Research Centre, New Delhi.
2.
From the record it is apparent that the petitioner had submitted original bills as early as in January, 1995 and the matter remained pending with the departmental authorities for a long time.
3.
Learned Deputy Advocate General has made a statement that an amount equivalent to the expenses incurred for similar treatment at the All India Institute of Medical Sciences has been sanctioned on 1641996.
4.
Recently this Court has examined various circulars issued by the Government along with the judgments of this Court and the Supreme Court and has given general directions for reimbursement of the expenses incurred by a Government servant (existing and retired) for their medical tratement in the recognised Hospitals and Institutions.
5.
In our opinion that decision squarely applies to the case of the petitioner and he is entitled to get reimbursement of the expenses incurred by him qua treatment at the Escorts Heart Institute and Research Centre, New Delhi.
6.
We, therefore, direct the respondents to decide the claim made by the petitioner in accordance with the directions given by this court on 12.4.1996 in Civil Writ Petition No. 16570 of 1995.
If payment is not made to the petitioner within the time stipulated in the order dated 12.4.1996 he shall become entitled to the payment of interest at the rate of 15% per annum with effect from April 5, 1996.
7.
The Writ Petition is disposed of in the manner indicated above.
