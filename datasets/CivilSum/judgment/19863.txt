JV Gupta, J - This appeal is directed against the order of the learned Single Judge whereby the order of the State Government declining to make the reference for adjudication of the dispute under the Industrial Disputes Act, was set aside and the State Government was directed to reconsider the matter and to pass a fresh order one way or the other.
2.
The operation of the impugned order was stayed at the time of the motion hearing on September 5, 1983.
3.
During the course of arguments taking into consideration the facts and circumstances of the case, the learned counsel for the respondent agreed that in case his client is paid a sum of Rs. 15,000/-, the writ petition may be dismissed and in that situation he will not have any claim whatsoever against his employer, i.e. the Punjab Tractors Ltd, the appellant.
The learned counsel for the appellant agreed that his client will pay the said amount within three months from this order by way of full and final settlement between the parties.
In view of the said agreement, the appeal is allowed.
The order of the learned Single Judge is set aside and the writ petition is dismissed provided a sum of Rs. 15,000/- is paid to the workman Ajit Singh within three months from this order by way of a Bank Draft.
Petition dismissed.
