VM Jain, J. (Oral) - This order shall dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 760 of 2003, 713 of 2003, 747 of 2003, 1313 of 2003, 1428 of 2003, 728 of 2003, 609 of 2003, 6487 of 2003, 6870 of 2003, 7301 of 2003, 6863 of 2003, 6889 of 2003 and 7305 of 2003, as common question of law and facts are involved in these petitions.
For the purposes of convenience reference may be made to Civil Writ Petition No. 760 of 2003.
2.
The petitioners in this petition are the retired employees of the Khalsa Girls High School, Moga, and the said institution is having 93% grant-in-aid from the Punjab Government.
They retired from service before 111996.
The petitioners are governed by "The Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992" which came into force with effect from 521987.
As per the said scheme, the petitioners became entitled to the grant of retiral benefits including pension etc.
The petitioners have already been paid the pension and gratuity etc. as per the unrevised grades.
However, after revision of pay scales w.e.f.
111996, the petitioners became entitled to revision of their pension etc.
Admittedly, the revised pension etc. has not been paid to them so far.
3.
The case of the petitioners is covered by the Punjab Government Notification dated 2171998/1881998, copy of which is Annexure P-2, according to which, the petitioners are to be given the pension etc. on the basis of revised pay scales.
Since the petitioners were not being paid the pension etc. at the revised rates, therefore, they made representations to the concerned authorities in this regard.
Since no action was taken by the respondents, the petitioners filed the present writ petition, seeking a writ of mandamus for the grant of revised pension etc.  4.
Notice of motion was issued.
Shri KK Bhatnagar, the then Secretary to Government of Punjab Department of Education along with Shri Sohan Lal, Director Public Instructions (Schools), Punjab, appeared in person before a Division Bench of this Court on 2712003.
It was submitted by Shri KK Bhatnagar that the Education Department has taken a policy decision wherein it has been determined that revised pensionary benefits would be payable to the employees of the privately managed aided schools in the State of Punjab, even though the said employees retired (on attaining the age of superannuation) prior to 111996 and that monetary benefits are payable w.e.f.
111996.
It was further submitted by Shri KK Bhatnagar that the only reason for not implementing the policy decision for granting revised retiral benefits to the employees w.e.f.
111996 was the constraint expressed by Finance Department allegedly on account of lack of finances.
It was submitted that the matter was then pending decision with the Council of Ministers and that a final decision in this behalf was likely to be taken within next week or so.
Accordingly the case was adjourned with a direction that the decision taken by the Council of Ministers be placed on the record.
5.
In pursuance of the aforesaid directions, the learned counsel appearing for the State has placed on record a photocopy of the letter dated 23rd of May, 2003, received by the Advocate General, Punjab from the Principal Secretary, to the Government of Punjab, Department of School Education.
It is mentioned therein that the matter with regard to the payment of pension in the revised scale of pay to the employees of Privately Managed (Recognised) Aided Schools came up for discussion in the meeting of the Council of Ministers on 2252003 and that in principle, a decision was taken to continue payment of pensions to the employees who retired before or are to retire on 3152003 and that pensions shall be paid to them in the revised scales of pay w.e.f.
111996.
6.
The learned counsel appearing for the State of Punjab has also produced before us letter dated 2852003, received by the Advocate General, Punjab, from the Joint Secretary Education, Government of Punjab, Department of Education, to the effect that the Council of Ministers had taken decision in its meeting on 2252003 which was favourable to the employees of Privately Managed Government Aided Schools.
It was alleged that the Finance Department could not release funds and ultimately the Council of Ministers took a considered decision on 2252003.
It was alleged that in the said decision taken by the Council of Ministers, it was decided that the existing pensioners retiring upto 3152003 shall be paid the arrears of their pension in three years in 36 equated monthly instalments and that funds for the payment of pension will be provided through supplementary estimates.
It was alleged that the funds will be available only after the supplementary estimates are passed by the Punjab Vidhan Sabha which was likely to meet sometime in November- December, 2003.
7.
As referred to above, the respondents are not disputing the payment of revised pension to the pensioners.
The only ground taken in the aforesaid letter is the financial constraints.
We are unable to agree with the contention raised on behalf of the State of Punjab for non-releasing the arrears of pension to the petitioners by taking shelter behind the financial constraints.
It is well settled that the pension is not bounty to be paid at the sweet-will of the employer.
It is a postponed earned payment which an employee is entitled after retirement for the services already rendered by him.
In our opinion the State of Punjab is not entitled to postpone the payment of the pension to the petitioners in the manner in which it is trying to do.
Apparently, the action of the respondents in not releasing the pension, is arbitrary and illegal.
8.
With regard to the arrears of pension, gratuity etc. to be paid to the retirees, who retired on or after 111996, such retirees are also entitled to the grant of revised pension etc. in view of the law laid down by a Division Bench of this Court, in C.W.P.
12671 of 2003, Nirmal Devi v. The State of Punjab and others, decided on 2712003.
9.
For the reasons mentioned above, the above mentioned writ petitions are allowed and the State of Punjab is directed to pay the entire arrears of pension etc. to the petitioners in all these petitions, latest by 3062003.
No costs.
Petitions allowed
