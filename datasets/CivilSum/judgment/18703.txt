Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - We have heard learned counsel for the parties at some length.
2.
It is the conceded case that the State Government did not pay the arrears of Selection Grade to the petitioner for a considerable period.
No fault is attributable to the petitioner.
In normal course, she would be entitled to interest and even costs of this petition.
Keeping in view the totality of facts and circumstances of the case, we consider it just, fair and equitable if the petitioner is paid an additional sum of Rs. 10,000/- on account of interest and costs.
3.
This writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
Petition allowed.
