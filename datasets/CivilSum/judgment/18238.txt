SK Seth, J - The controversy involved in present writ petition lie in a narrow compass with regard to pay-scale to be given to petitioner upon `Kramonnati'.
2.
Relevant facts are not in dispute.
Petitioner at the relevant point of time was holding the post of Inspector, Industries which is a Class-III executive post.
The designation of the post of Inspector, Industries was changed to Assistant Manager, Industries by order dated 29-4-1998 (Annexure P/3).
The post of Assistant Manager, Industries is a feeder post for promotion to the post of Assistant Director, Industries and other equivalent posts which are Class-II gazetted posts and are governed by MP State Industries (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1985.
By order/circular dated 17-3- 1999/19-4-1999, State Government came out with a scheme for Kramonnati.
Said order/circular has been filed by petitioner and respondent as Annexure P/6 and Annexure R/1 respectively.
In accordance with the said order/circular, petitioner was given Kramonnati by order dated 7-2-2003 (Annexure P/9) in the pay-scale of Rs. 5,000-8,000/-.
Grievance of petitioner is that upon Kramonnati, he was entitled to pay-scale of Rs. 8,000-13,500/-, which is admittedly sanctioned for the post of Assistant Director, Industries and other equivalent posts.
3.
After notice, respondents have filed their reply and justified the grant of pay scale of Rs. 5,000-8,000/- to the petitioner.
4.
I have heard learned counsel for the parties at length and perused the material available on record.
5.
Controversy revolves around Item No. 4 of the circular dated 17-3-1999 which reads as under :  (Vernacular matter omitted ...Ed.) 6.
Learned counsel for petitioner submitted that in the light of instructions contained in Item No. 4 as quoted above, petitioner is entitled to the pay-scale of Rs. 8,000-13,500/- which is the corresponding pay scale sanctioned for the post of Assistant Director, Industries and other equivalent posts.
7.
Per contra, learned counsel appearing for respondents submitted that before Kramonnati, petitioner was drawing the salary in the pay-scale of Rs. 4,500-7,000/- and, therefore, upon Kramonnati, he has been rightly given the pay-scale of Rs. 5,000-8,000/-, as mentioned in Column 3 against Item No. 7 of Column 2.
8.
After having heard learned counsel for parties, I find no merit and substance in the contentions of learned counsel for respondents.
It is an undisputed position that for the post of Assistant Director, Industries, the pay-scale of Rs. 8,000-13,500/- is sanctioned.
Petitioner has been given Kramonnati in the light of circular dated 17-3-1999.
From perusal of Item No. 4 of the Circular, it is clear that upon Kramonnati, the pay is to be fixed in the pay-scale as mentioned in column 3 or in the corresponding pay-scale sanctioned for the post.
(Vernacular matter omitted.).
It is also clear that various pay scales, as mentioned in column 2 of that circular is not exhaustive.
Had it been so, there was no need or occasion to use the word (Vernacular matter omitted.).
The contention of learned counsel for respondents that next higher stage of pay-scale has been given to petitioner and upon regular promotion, he would be entitled to the regular pay scale sanctioned for the post of Assistant Director, Industries, is not only misconceived, but is also contrary to the order/circular dated 17-3-1999 (Annexure P/6).
The concept of Kramonnati is now a well recognized concept under service jurisprudence so as to give succor to a Government servant who for no fault of his own could not get promotion to the next higher post.
No material has been placed on record to show that the pay-scale sanctioned for the post of Assistant Director, Industries is Rs. 5,000-8,000/- and in absence of any relevant material available on record, the inevitable result is that order impugned so far it relates to the petitioner granting the pay-scale of Rs. 5,000-8,000/- upon Kramonnati cannot be sustained and deserves to be quashed.
Accordingly, respondents are directed to issue appropriate orders in terms of the circular dated 17-3-1999 (P/6) so as to give to the petitioner benefit of sanctioned pay-scale of Rs. 8,000-13,500/- for the post of Assistant Director, Industries w.e.f.
the date as mentioned in the impugned order.
9.
In the result, writ petition is allowed to the extent indicated hereinabove.
Respondents are directed to complete the whole exercise within a period of four months from the date of communication of certified copy of the order.
Petitioner shall also be entitled to costs of this writ petition.
Counsel's fee Rs. 1,000/-, if certified.
Petition allowed.
