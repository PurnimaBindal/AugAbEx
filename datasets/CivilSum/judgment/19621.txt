Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - The petitioner retired as a Senior Accountant on July 31, 1991.
He was entitled to the payment of gratuity and also to the payment of salary for 8 months' leave which was due to him at the time of his retirement.
It is averred that a total amount of Rs. 81,700/- was due to the petitioner towards gratuity and an amount of Rs. 51,200/- was due on account of the leave encashment.
The petitioner was thus entitled to the payment of a total amount of Rs. 1,32,900/-.
2.
According to the written statement filed on behalf of respondent No. 2, the petitioner was entitled to the payment of Rs. 80,11650 on account of gratuity and an amount of Rs. 51,200/- towards leave encashment.
In spite of the fact that the liability for the amount of Rs. 1,31,316.50 has been admitted, the payment not been made to the petitioner since his retirement only on account of the fact that the Audit Department had raised an objection with regard to the payment of a paltry sum of a few hundred rupees towards additional dearness allowance.
This dispute has been pending since the year 1985.
3 .
The action of the respondents in withholding the retirement benefits of the petitioner was wholly illegal.
The payment could not have been withheld only on account of an objection raised by the Audit Department in the year 1980.
4.
Consequently, it is directed that the petitioner will be paid all the retiral benefits alongwith interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum w.e.f.
November 1, 1990 till the date of its actual payment.
The petitioner will also be entitled to his costs which are assessed at Rs. 3000/-.
Order accordingly.
