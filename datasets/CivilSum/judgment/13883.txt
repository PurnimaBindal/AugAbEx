KS Garewal, J.-This judgment shall dispose of C.W.P.
No. 9237 of 1988 filed by Jaipal and 6 others (JBT Teachers) and C.W.P.
No. 8937 of 1988 filed by Ran Singh Bhardwaj and 12 others (J.B.T.
teachers).
The petitioners in both petitions have sought higher pay scale in accordance with the qualifications acquired by them and have relied on instructions dated July 23, 1957 (Annexure P-1) and judgment of Supreme Court of India in Avtar Singh Versus Manmohan Singh (Civil Appeal No. 3799 of 1983) decided on September 14, 1984 (Annexure P-2) and clarification thereto vide order dated February 2, 1985 (Annexure P-3).
2.
The case of the petitioners is that they were teachers in different schools in Haryana and had acquired higher qualification while in service.
They have relied upon letter dated July 23, 1957 (Annexure P-1) which laid down the rule that teachers would be entitled to pay scales according to their qualifications irrespective of the posts they were holding.
3.
The petitioners had tabulated the dates of their appointment and dates of passing BA In each case BA degree was obtained after appointment.
For example Jaipal petitioner No.
I in C.W.P.
No. 9237 was appointed on June 8, 1965 and passed BA on June 27, 1978.
Likewise Ran Singh Bhardwaj petitioner No. 1 in C.W.P.
8937 of 1988 was appointed on September 5, 1969, and for reasons not disclosed, he rejoined on January 1, 1979 and passed BA on October 9, 1980.
Obviously all the petitioners had acquired BA degree after joining service.
4.
At the time of arguments photo state copy of the letter dated October 26, 1995 was placed before the court.
Therein Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government of Haryana, Education Department had written to Director, Secondary Education and Director Primary Education Haryana for grant of higher pay scales of JBT teachers in terms of letter dated July 23, 1957 on the basis of having acquired BA qualification upto March 8, 1990.
It was stated that the sanction of the Governor of Haryana had been accorded to the grant of higher pay scale in terms of the letter dated July 23, 1957 and pay scales as sanctioned by the Government from time to time to JBT teachers of Government schools from the dates of acquiring BA qualification by them.
However, it was made clear that only such JBT teachers would be entitled to the benefit of the above mentioned higher scales who had acquired higher qualification of BA before March 8, 1990.
5.
Since the petitioners had acquired qualifications after joining service, though long before the cut off date, they are entitled to the benefit of higher pay scales.
Principles of good governance require that like should be alike.
State should not needlessly ignore valid and just claims of its employees.
Driving its employees to litigate is demoralising for them, it is also expensive as well and detracts from their duties.
Such a policy serves no public purpose.
One rarely comes across a case where the State suo moto accords benefits to its employees where these are due.
Such tendency must be avoided.
Principles of good governance should be followed but in the present case these principles have been flouted.
6.
These writ petitions deserve to be allowed and it is directed that the petitioners shall be granted benefit of higher pay scale from the date they acquired the BA degree.
This direction has been given on the basis of instructions dated July 23, 1957 and Full Bench judgment of this Court in Om Parkash Arya v. State of Haryana, C.W.P.
No. 484 of 1986 decided on May 12, 1989.
7.
Each of the petitioners shall be entitled to payment of Rs. 2000/- as costs.
Petition Mowed.
