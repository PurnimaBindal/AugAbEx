Dalveer Bhandari, J (Oral) - The controversy involved in this case is rather narrow.
The petitioner was admittedly appointed as a teacher in the Municipal Corporation of Delhi and after some time he was promoted as a Head Master.
2.
Resolution No. 666 of Corporation passed on 6th November, 1978, reads as under :  "Resolved that as proposed by the Commissioner in his letter No. 444/C & C dated 26.9.78 and recommended by the Standing Committee vide its Resolution No. 440 dated 12101978, approval to the modification in condition No. 7 of the service condition of teachers, officers and other employees transferred to the Delhi Administrations along with schools w.e.f.
171970 be accorded to the extent that the age limit for retirement of officers and other employees will be 58 years and in case of teachers and class IV servants it will be 60 years."
3.
In view of this resolution, the petitioner was entitled to continue up to the age of 60 years in the Municipal Corporation of Delhi.
But in contravention of the said resolution, the MCD had issued order dated 11101977.
By this order the petitioner had to retire from the service on 31101977 (after he completed 58 years).
4.
It may be pertinent to mention that a similar matter came up before the Division Bench of this Court in Smt. Sheila Puri v. MCD, 1985(9), Delhi Reported Judgments 180.
In this matter the court has taken view that in view of the resolution of the MCD a teacher employed with the MCD is entitled to serve up to the age of 60 years.
5.
The Municipal Corporation of Delhi aggrieved by this order filed a Special Leave Petition before the Supreme Court.
The Supreme Court affirmed the order of the High Court and dismissed the appeal filed by the Municipal Corporation of Delhi with costs.
6.
Mr. Bucchar, learned counsel appearing for the Municipal Corporation of Delhi has placed on record a Resolution No. 802 dated 311984 in which it is mentioned that the benefit of increase in age be given to teachers appointed after 291983.
The MCD cannot be permitted to create discrimination amongst person similarly placed.
This precisely is the ratio of the said judgment of Smt. Sheela Puri of the Division Bench of this court which has been approved by the Supreme Court.
On consideration of the all facts and circumstances and the relevant resolutions of the Municipal Corporation of Delhi, the Writ Petition is allowed with costs which is quantified as Rs. 1500/-.
7.
The petitioner has now even completed 60 years a long time ago, so there is no question of reinstatement in service.
However, the petitioner shall be entitled to financial and other consequential benefits.
The respondent MCD is directed to compute these benefits and give it to the petitioner within three months from today.
