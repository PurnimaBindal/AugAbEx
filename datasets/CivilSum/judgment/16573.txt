Ashutosh Mohunta, J - The petitioner, who was working as a Mate and was promoted to the rank of Tubewell Operator, had served for more than 18 years.
He has filed the present petition for payment of gratuity.
Mr. Jagdish Manchanda, learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the Hon'ble Apex Court in Bakshish Singh v. Union of India, AIR 1994 SC 251 has held that an employee is entitled to gratuity even after service of five years and that the employer is liable to pay the gratuity.
It is submitted that in the present case, the petitioner has served for more than 18 years and therefore, he is entitled to get gratuity.
2.
In view of the judgment in Bakshish Singh's case (supra), the writ petition is allowed and the respondents are directed to pay the entire gratuity to the petitioner alongwith interest at the rate of 9% per annum from the date of the gratuity is due to within a period of four months from today.
Petition allowed.
