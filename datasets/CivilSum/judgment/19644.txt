SS Sodhi, J - The claim here is for regularisation of services in terms of the policy decision of the State Government of February 16, 1987, Annexure P-1.
The Petitioners-Vijay Yadav and Din Mohmod were appointed on ad hoc basis as Computor-cum-Clerks in January and December, 1982.
Both these petitioners were appointed merely as a stop-gap arrangement but have continued in service for a period exceeding two years.
It is on this account that they seek regularisation of their services in terms of the policy decision of the government referred to earlier.
2.
A reference to the policy decision of the Government on February 16, 1987, Annexure P-1, would show that it applies only to posts which have been taken out of the purview of the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana.
The posts held by the petitioners admittedly do not fall in this category.
These posts are to be filled on recommendations made by the Subordinate Services Selection Board.
This being so, the petitioners are clearly not entitled to seek regularisation in terms of these instructions.
3.
Confronted with this situation, the petitioners sought to question the regularisation of services being restricted only to posts which did not fall within the purview of the Subordinate Services Selection Board.
Discrimination and arbitrariness being imputed to this condition.
This is, indeed, an untenable challenge as a correct and reasonable policy of employment must visualise appointments being made after regular selection and not otherwise.
No legal infirmity can, thus, be imputed to this policy decision being confined to posts outside the purview of the Sub-ordinate Services Selection Board.
No occasion, thus, arises for granting to the petitioners the relief claimed.
Order accordingly.
