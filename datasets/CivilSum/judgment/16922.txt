Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - It is conceded before us that the services of the petitioners have been regularised with effect from 171999.
2.
Learned counsel for the petitioners while relying upon the instructions issued by the Government and the letter dated 3131998, Annexure P5 to the petition, submits that the services of the petitioners ought to have been regularised with effect from 1.2.1996 as the posts were sanctioned with effect from that date.
Counsel also relies upon Division Bench Judgments of this Court in the cases of Civil Writ Petition No. 13183 of 1998, Lakhbir Chander and others v. State of Haryana and others, Annexure P10 to the petition and Civil Writ Petition No. 118 of 1999, Sant Lal and others v. State of Haryana and another, 2000(4) SCT 517 (P&H)(DB), Annexure P20 to the writ petition.
3.
In the light of above circumstances, we consider it appropriate to direct the concerned authorities to take a decision with regard to the request of the petitioners, aforementioned within a period of three months from today and communicate the order to the petitioners.
We would expect the State to be fair and take a uniform stand in the cases which fall in the same class.
Writ petition is disposed of.
No order as to costs.
