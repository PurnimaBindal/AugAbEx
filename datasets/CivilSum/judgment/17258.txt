THB Chalapathi, J - The petitioner was appointed as a Constable in the year 1971 in Ropar District and he was sent for Lower School Course at Police Training College, Phillaur in the year 1981 and completed the training successfully.
According to him, he is senior to respondents 2 to 5 since he passed the lower School Course six months prior to respondents 2 to 5.
Therefore, according to the petitioner, the action of the Ist respondent deputing respondents 2 to 5 to Intermediate School Course is illegal and arbitrary.
Admittedly, for a promotion to the post of Assistant Sub Inspector of Police, the Head Constables have to undergo Intermediate School Course at Police Training College, Phillapur.
Further according to the petitioner, the Deputy Inspector General of Police, Patiala, prepared a list of the candidates in the year 1993 to be sent for Intermediate School Course.
In that list, the name of the petitioner was omitted while the names of respondents 2 to 5 have been included.
The petitioner, therefore, approached this Court for issuance of a writ of mandamus directing the Ist respondent (DIG of Police Patiala Range) to depute the petitioner to Intermediate Lower School Course for the Session 1993 commencing from November, 1993.
2.
A written statement has been filed on behalf of respondent 1 in which it is averred that the petitioner was promoted as Head Constable on 231983 while the respondents 2 to 5 were promoted as Head Constables with effect from 1101981 and 1111981.
Therefore respondents 2 to 5 are seniors to the petitioner in the category of Head Constables and no junior of the petitioner in Patiala range was deputed to Intermediate School Course and, therefore, the writ petition is liable to be dismissed.
3.
From the record, it is clear that the petitioner belonged to Ropar District whereas the respondents 2 to 5 continued to work in Patiala District.
There is no dispute of the fact that the seniority lists of the Constables and Head Constables are maintained on district-wise.
Therefore, the petitioner who has been working in Ropar District could not be promoted as Head Constable till 231993 in Ropar District whereas the respondents 2 to 5 who were working at Patiala had been promoted as Head Constables on 1101981 and 1111981 i.e. prior to the promotion of the petitioner.
Though the respondents 2 to 5 were appointed as Constables subsequent to the petitioner, but they earned promotion as Head Constables prior to the petitioner.
Therefore in the category of Head Constables the respondents 2 to 5 are seniors to the petitioner because of their earlier promotion.
4.

There are several other persons senior to the petitioner who are waiting to be deputed to the Intermediate School Course.
The petitioner cannot make any grievance in regard to sending respondents 2 to 5 to the Intermediate School Course since they were promoted as Head Constables earlier to the petitioner because they remained in Patiala District whereas the petitioner continued to work in Ropar District.
From the date of promotion as Head Constable, respondents 2 to 5 are Seniors to the petitioner.
6.
In this view of the matter, I do not find any merit in this writ petition.
The writ petition is, therefore, dismissed.
However, there will be no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
