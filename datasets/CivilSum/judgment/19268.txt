NK Agrawal, J - In this writ petition filed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioners have prayed for issuance of a writ of mandamus directing respondent No. 1 to grant pension to the petitioners under the Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980.
2.
On notice of motion having been issued, respondents have put in appearance and filed written statements.
3.
Sh. H.S.
Giani, learned counsel appearing for Union of India, contends that the persons who are certifiers of the petitioners and have claimed imprisonment along with the petitioners in the Central Jail, Lahore, have issued such certificates (co-prisoner certificates) to numerous people and such certificates have been found issued indiscriminately and have created false claims for grant of Samman Pension under the scheme.
4.
We have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the paper book.
5.
From the perusal of written statement filed on behalf of State of Punjab, it is observed that after verifying the claim of the petitioners, the State of Punjab has recommended the case of the petitioners for grant of pension under the Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme.
6.
In this view of the matter, we direct the Union of India to re-consider the case of the petitioners for grant of pension under the Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 in the light of recommendations of the Punjab Government.
Writ Petition disposed of accordingly.
