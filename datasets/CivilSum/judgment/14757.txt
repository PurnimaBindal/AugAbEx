JV Gupta, CJ - The writ petitioner, Malak Singh, challenged the order dated January 22, 1970, copy Annexure E, whereby his annual increment was withheld for a period of one year without cumulative effect.
The learned Single Judge in the order under appeal, dated November 21, 1973, observed as follows :-  "In the fact of the observations made above the petition deserves to be dismissed, but the learned counsel for the petitioner has brought to my notice a judgment rendered by PS Pattar, J in Lakhbir Singh v. The State of Punjab, C.W.
1826 of 1971 decided on May 9, 1973 in which a contrary view has been taken.
Sitting in Single Bench, I am bound to follow this decision."
Consequently, the writ petition was allowed and the impugned order was quashed.
2.
The writ-petitioner filed the above-said LPA.
which was dismissed on August 8, 1974, as barred by time.
The writ-petitioner went to Supreme Court against the said order when the case was remanded vide order dated November 28, 1989, for deciding the L.P.A. on merits.
3.
The matter now stands concluded by the judgment of the Supreme Court reported as Kulwant Singh Gill v. The State of Punjab, Judgments Today 1990(4) SC 709, wherein it as been held that the stoppage of increment without cumulative effect was a minor penalty and that no regular enquiry was necessary to be held before inflicting the same.
That being so, the writ petition filed by the petitioner was liable to be dismissed.
Consequently, this appeal succeeds and is allowed.
The order of the learned Single Judge is set aside and the writ petition is dismissed with no order as to costs.
Appeal dismissed.
