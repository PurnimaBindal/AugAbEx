Jawahar Lal Gupta, J - These two writ petitions, viz. C.W.P.
No. 9488 of 1988 and 10984 of 1988 can be disposed of by one judgment.
C.W.P.
No. 9488 of 1988 has been filed by Head Constable Prahlad Singh against the orders dated October 10, 1988 and October 11, 1988 (Annexures P.7 and P.8) By the order at Annexure P-7, the petitioner's date of confirmation was changed from January 31, 1983 to January 31, 1985.
By the order at Annexure P-8 the petitioner, who had been deputed to attend the Intermediate course, was recalled from the course.
Both these orders were passed on the representation submitted by Head Constable Sadhu Ram and five others, who are petitioners in C.W.P.
No. 10984 of 1988, who claim to be senior to Prahlad Singh.
This writ petition (9488 of 1989) had been filed on October 26, 1988.
It came up for motion hearing on November 1, 1988.
The Motion Bench passed the following order :-  "Admitted.
The operation of the order, copy Annexure P.8, is stayed meanwhile.
The petitioner will be allowed to complete the training subject to the result of the writ petition at his own risk.
It is, however, made clear that no seniority will be claimed on the basis of the training by him".
In pursuance to this order, the D.I.G.
Hissar issued an order on November 25, 1988, the relevant portion of which reads as under :-  "Ref.
your TMP No. 25824-25/W dated 71188 reg.
CWP No. 9488 of 1988.
One extra seat is hereby allotted for intermediate School Course to accommodate Head Constable Prahlad Singh No. 329/SRS of your district subject to the condition laid down by the Hon'ble High Court/ DTHI 21 OCDTC 1 252145/11/ 98 + 8S + 88SD/ BSRD ?
RC."
2.
Head Constable Sadhu Ram etc. were primarily aggrieved by this order and thus filed CWP No. 10984 of 1988 on December 2, 1988.
They claim to be the senior to Head Constable Prahlad Singh and have sought a direction in the nature of mandamus for being deputed for the intermediate course.

A perusal of the above chart would show that the petitioners in CWP No. 10984 of 1988 were enrolled as Constables and promoted as Head Constables earlier than Prahlad Singh.
Accordingly, they had a right to be considered for confirmation as Head Constable prior to the confirmation of Prahlad Singh.
Originally, Prahlad Singh had been confirmed w.e.f.
January 31, 1983 and was thus deputed for the Intermediate School Course without considering the claim of the persons it senior to him.
They had represented against this order and also filed CWP No. 8613 of 1988.
This writ petition was disposed of by a Division Bench of this Court vide its order dated October 5, 1988.
After noticing the respective contentions of the learned counsel for the parties, the Division Bench directed that the representation dated 2011988 filed by the petitioners (Sadhu Ram etc.) be considered and disposed of within one week.
It was further directed that till the disposal of the representation, Prahlad Singh will not be deputed to Police Training College, Madhuban.
It was in pursuance to this order that the authorities had passed the two orders, as noticed above, changing Prahlad Singh's date of confirmation and recalling him from the course.
5.
A perusal of the factual position shows that Prahlad Singh had been enrolled as a Constable on September 1, 1974 and had passed the Lower School Course in the term ending April, 1980.
He had been promoted as a Head Constable on December 5, 1980.
As against this, Sadhu Ram etc. had been enrolled earlier and had passed the Lower School Course in the year 1978.
They had also been promoted to the rank of Head Constable earlier than Prahlad Singh.
While Sadhu Ram etc. were confirmed w.e.f.
August 31, 1984, Prahlad Singh had been confirmed w.e.f.
January 31, 1985.
I find no infirmity in this action of the respondents.
Prahlad Singh cannot be confirmed w.e.f.
a date earlier than the one on which persons senior to him viz. Head Constable Sadhu Ram etc. had been confirmed.
Their date of confirmation has to be earlier to that of Prahlad Singh.
It has not been shown by producing evidence on record that they were unsuitable for confirmation at any earlier stage.
In this view of the matter, the challenge of Prahlad Singh to the order dated October 10, 1988, by which his date of confirmation was changed from January 31, 1983 to January 31, 1985 cannot be sustained.
Equally lacking in merit is his challenge to the order dated October 11, 1988 by which he was recalled from the Police Training College, Madhuban.
However, the fact remains that Prahlad Singh has already completed the course.
In this situation, two alternatives are available.
Either the action of the department in deputing Prahlad Singh for the Course be quashed or as ordered by the Motion Bench while admitting his petition it be declared that he will not derive any benefit towards seniority etc. by virtue of his having qualified the course prior to Sadhu Ram etc.
Adopting the first alternative would result in wastage of public funds etc. Consequently, I consider it fair to adopt the second course and direct that Prahlad Singh will not derive any advantage towards seniority etc. on account of the fact that he has completed the intermediate course in the term ending March 31, 1989.
Further since Prahlad Singh is apparently junior to Head Constable Sadhu Ram etc. they had a right to be deputed for the course before anyone junior to them could be deputed.
This was not done.
The action of the department was apparently arbitrary.
In order to undo the injustice to them, it is directed that they will be deputed, if not already sent, for the next course commencing on October 1, 1991.
6.
As a result CWP No. 9488 of 1988 is dismissed.
CWP No. 10984 of 1988 is allowed to the extent that the petitioners shall be deputed for the next course commencing on October 1, 1991.
This is of course subject to the condition that they have not already been deputed for the Course.
In the circumstances of the case, the parties are left to bear their own costs.
Petition dismissed.
