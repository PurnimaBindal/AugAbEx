Viney Mittal, J - The petitioners have approached this Court under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for the issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus for directing the respondents to release the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 to them with effect from May 1, 1990 alongwith a direction to release the arrears to them on that basis.
Certain facts may be noticed.
2.
All the petitioners are Matriculates and possess an ITI diploma and were initially appointed as Helpers in the Haryana Roadways.
The case of the petitioners is that they are performing duties in the workshop and are working against the technical posts.
On August 23, 1990, the State Government of Haryana issued a general order whereby it was decided to modify the pay scales of some posts as detailed out in Annexure-A appended to the general order with effect from May 1, 1990.
A perusal to the general order and Annexure-A appended thereto, the technical posts in various departments for which the minimum educational qualification prescribed ITI certificate was modified from various lower scales to Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from May 1, 1990.
Subsequently, a difficulty was felt as to whether a person, who was holder of a technical post but was not a matriculate, would also be entitled to some increase in the pay scale or he would stagnate at his old scale.
However, to overcome this situation, the Government of Haryana issued another communication dated July 20, 1991 vide which the earlier recommendations were modified and it was provided that the technical posts in various departments, for which the minimum educational qualification is only ITI certificate/diploma from polytechnic without insistence on qualification of matriculation, would be entitled to the modified scale of Rs. 1200-2040 from May 1, 1990.
3.
The case of the petitioners is that after the aforesaid clarifications of the departments, the Government of Haryana released the pay scales of Rs. 1200-2040 to all those employees who were holding technical posts and those who are matriculates with ITI certificates/polytechnic diploma in the department of PWD Public Health, but the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 was not released to the Helpers, Fitters, which compelled the aforesaid employees to approach this Court by way of a civil writ petition No. 7928 of 1993.
It was contended by the aforesaid petitioners that they possess the educational qualification of matriculation and also possess technical qualifications having passed ITI certificates and, therefore, they were also entitled to the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040.
A Division Bench of this Court, vide judgment dated December 1, 1993 allowed the aforesaid writ petition and issued directions for fixation of pay of the aforesaid petitioners according to their educational qualification of matriculation with ITI certificate.
A copy of the aforesaid judgment has been appended as Annexure P-3 with the petition.
The petitioners have stated that the Special Leave Petition filed by the State Government was dismissed by the Apex Court vide order dated March 15, 1996 which has been appended as Annexure P-4.
4.
Since the aforesaid scale of Rs. 1200-2040 was not released to the petitioners, therefore, they were also constrained to file a Civil Writ Petition No. 10760 of 1998.
The aforesaid writ petition was disposed of vide order dated October 30, 1998 by a Division Bench of this Court whereby the respondents were directed to dispose of the legal notice of the petitioners by passing a speaking order.
5.
In pursuance to the directions issued by this Court vide order dated October 30, 1998, the respondents considered the claim made by the petitioners and ultimately vide order dated February 12, 1999 rejected the same.
A copy of the aforesaid order dated February 12, 1999 has been appended as Annexure P-5 with the petition.
6.
The petitioners are aggrieved against the aforesaid order Annexure P-5 and have approached this Court through the present writ petition.
7.
On a notice issued to the respondents, they have put in appearance.
A written statement has been filed on behalf of respondents No. 1 and 2.
The claim of the petitioners has been contested.
The stand taken by the respondents in the written statement is that the general order dated August 23, 1990 was merely applicable to such employees for which minimum educational qualification prescribed was matric with ITI certificate or polytechnic diploma.
The respondents have taken the stand that since the qualification for the post against which the petitioners were initially appointed was not matric with ITI/Polytechnic, therefore, they were not entitled to the grade of Rs. 1200-2040 as per the general order dated August 23, 1990.
The respondents have also defended the order dated February 12, 1999 (Annexure P-5 passed by respondent No. 2.
We have heard Shri Rajneesh Narula, the learned counsel for the petitioners and Shri Amol Rattan, the learned Assistant Advocate General appearing for the respondents.
8.
Shri Rajneesh Narula, the learned counsel for the petitioners has submitted that the petitioners were possessing the qualification of matric and also possessed ITI certificates and on the basis of the academic and technical qualifications, the petitioners are entitled to the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from May 1, 1990 ie, the date from which the other persons similarly situated are being paid the said scale.
Shri Narula has relied upon a Division Bench judgment of this Court in Ram Kishan and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1994(3) SCT 609 : (CWP No. 7920 of 1993) decided on December 1, 1993.
Shri Narula submits that on the fact of it, it is apparent that the petitioners are working in the workshop of the Haryana Roadways and on the basis of their qualifications and because of the fact that the posts were of technical nature they were entitled to the benefit of the aforesaid modification of pay scales.
9.
On the other hand, Shri Amol Rattan, the learned Assistant Advocate General has submitted that the prescribed qualifications for the post to which the petitioners were initially appointed is not matriculation with ITI/Polytechnic and, therefore, the mere fact that the petitioners possessed the aforesaid educational and technical qualifications did not entitle them to claim the modified scales of Rs. 1200-2040.
10.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and having given our thoughtful consideration to the entire controversy, we find that the present petition deserves to succeed.
It is not in dispute that the petitioners did possess the qualification of matriculation with ITI certificates and it was only on the basis of the aforesaid qualifications that they were actually appointed in the workshop run by the Haryana Roadways.
The judgment of the Division Bench in Ram Kishan's case (supra) wherein a direction had been issued to the State of Haryana to refix the pay of the petitioners according to their qualification the matriculation with ITI certificates supports the contention raised by the petitioners.
Subsequently, a Single Bench judgment of this Court in Labh Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1995(1) SCT 311 (P&H) : 1995(1) Recent Services Judgments 345, followed the directions issued in Ram Kishan's case (supra).
11.
Thus, in this view of the matter, the present writ petition is allowed and the order dated February 12, 1999 Annexure P-5 is hereby quashed.
The respondents are directed to fix the pay of the petitioners in the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from May 1, 1990 as per the circular dated August 23, 1990.
12.
Accordingly, the respondents shall re-fix the pay of the petitioners as indicated above within a period of four months from the date a certified copy of the order is received by them.
However, the payment of the arrears to the petitioners on the aforesaid basis is restricted to the period of 38 months prior to July 22, 1997 ie the date when they served the legal notice upon the respondents claiming the aforesaid modified pay scales.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
