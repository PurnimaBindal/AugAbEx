MR Agnihotri, J - On 22nd April, 1991, this writ petition was allowed as it was squarely covered by the judgment of this Court in C.W.P.
No. 6863 of 1986 (Shamsher Singh and others v. The State of Punjab and another), dated 18th April, 1988.
Thereafter, it was brought to my notice that service upon the respondents had not been effected and then fresh notices were issued to the respondents for 3rd June, 1991, after recalling my judgment dated 22nd April, 1991.
2.
The respondents have since been served and written statement has also been filed on behalf of the Accountant General (A & E), Union Territory, Chandigarh, respondent No. 2, along with a copy of the letter dated 7th June, 1990, from Shri RN Gupta, IAS Secretary to Government, Punjab Department of Finance, advising the Accountant General (A and E), Punjab, Chandigarh, that "the petitioners who retired before 3131985 are to be allowed the benefit of treating D.A./A.D.A.
as DP with effect from 3131985 and their pensions revised with effect from 3131985."
3.
After going through the pleadings of the parties and having heard the learned counsel, I am of the considered opinion that the view taken by me in the earlier judgment dated 22nd April, 1991, is in accordance with law and consistent with the view taken by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 6863 of 1986 (supra).
4.
Accordingly, I allow this writ petition and direct the respondents to pay to the petitioners and all other similarly situated persons, the same pensionary benefits in terms of the judgment of this Court in Shamsher Singh v. The State of Punjab, CWP No. 6863 of 1986, decided on 18th April, 1988, within a period of three months, failing which the amount becoming due shall be payable with interest at the rate of 18 per cent per annum, from the date of accrual upto the date of actual payment thereof.
However, there shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
