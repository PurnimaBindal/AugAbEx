Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - The petitioners seek migration to Govt.
Dental College, Patiala on the plea that the candidates, who were lower in merit and closely related to highly influential persons, were given migration, whereas they have been ignored for migration.
2.
On the other hand, the stand of the respondents is that the petitioners cannot be allowed migration to Punjab Govt.
Dental College, Patiala as the seats are not available and for the creation of additional seats, approval of Dental Council of India is required to be taken.
3.
Mr. Girish Agnihotri, Advocate, the learned Counsel for the petitioners, brought to the notice of the Court that the petitioners were allowed to attend classes on provisional basis.
On the contrary, Mr. RL Sharma, Advocate, the learned Counsel appearing for the respondents, stated that though the petitioners are attending classes under the orders of this Court, but their migration has not so far been approved nor they have been given regular admission to the said Dental College, Patiala.
However, Mr. Sharma further stated that the petitioners shall be allowed migration in the next ensuing academic Session 1991-92 which is to commence in July, 1991.
Mr. Agnihotri has stated that in the third professional, there are only, seven students and the rest of the seats are available as some of the students have left the course in the mid Session.
4.
Under the facts and circumstances of the case, respondents are directed that in case seats are available in the Dental College, Patiala, the petitioner be allowed migration for the Session 1990-91 and their provisional admission be regularised.
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.
No order as to costs.
Ordered accordingly.
