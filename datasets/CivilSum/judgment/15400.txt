Swatanter Kumar, J - The petitioner was appointed as Lab.
Attendant with the CCS Haryana Agricultural University and was promoted to the post of Lab.
Assistant on 15121990.
Petitioner was granted 2nd Assured Career Progression scale of Rs. 5000-7850/- vide order dated 2142000.
The respondent-University served notice to show cause upon the petitioner on 282001 calling upon him as to why the 2nd Assured Career Progression scale granted to him with effect from 181996 should not be withdrawn for the reasons stated in the notice.
The petitioner submitted a detailed reply.
After considering the same and hearing the petitioner, the respondents vide order dated 8112001 withdrew the 2nd Assured Career Progression benefit granted to the petitioner with effect from 181996.
This order is challenged in this writ petition.
2.
At the very out-set, we may refer to the relevant part of the impugned order declining request of the petitioner, which reads as under :-  "He completed 20 years service on 3395 but was not matriculate.
He passed his matriculation examination on 31.7.96 i.e. after 1196.
The ACP scale is to be granted on the functional pay scale of the direct recruitee fresh entrant post as on 311295.
Since the employee was non-matric and scale of post of non-matriculation, Lab.
Attdt.
was Rs. 775-1025/- only so the benefit of 2nd ACP scale of Rs. 5000-7850 w.e.f.
1896 on the basis of passing matriculation on 31.7.96 cannot be extended to the incumbent w.e.f.
1896.
Thus the 2nd ACP of Rs. 5000-7850 wrongly granted to him w.e.f.
1896 vide this office letter No. 17042-85 dated 2142000 is hereby withdrawn."
3.
This fact is not even disputed before us that the petitioner was not matriculate, which is the prescribed qualification for the post in question.
We are unable to find any error with the above reasoning given by the respondents.
The counsel for the petitioner relied upon a Division Bench judgment of this Court in the case of Suraj Bhan and others v. State of Haryana and another, 2001(2) SCT 708 (P&H) (DB) : 2001(1) R.S.J.
205.
The petitioner cannot derive any advantage from this judgment as the facts of his case are totally different and the plea of discrimination, which was up-held by the Division Bench in that case is not even pleaded in the present writ petition.
It is not a case of promotee or a direct recruitment.
Same cadre are being treated differently.
4.
The learned counsel for the petitioner also relied upon Rule 5 of the Haryana Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1998 which provides for eligibility for grant of Assured Career Progression scale.
There it is specifically provided that the employee has not got any financial up-gradation in terms of grant of pay scale higher than the functional pay scale prescribed for the post.
On the bare reading of this rule, the petitioner can claim no advantage.
5.
We see no merit in this petition.
The same is dismissed in limine with no order as to costs.
However, we clarify that in terms of the judgment of the Hon'ble Apex Court in the case of Sahib Ram v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) SCT 668, the respondents cannot recover the amount already paid to the petitioner as the petitioner had not made any misrepresentation while he was granted the benefit of 2nd Assured Career Progression scale.
The order would be effective prospectively.
Petition dismissed.
