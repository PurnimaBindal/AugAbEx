SS Sodhi, J - The controversy here is with regard to the date of birth of respondent Gurcharan Kaur who held the post of Block Primary Education Officer, Kapurthala.
2.
The relevant facts as they emerge from the material on record are that on October 20, 1954, Gurcharan Kaur was appointed as Untrained Teacher in the erstwhile State of Pepsu.
For the purpose of this appointment, she gave her date of birth as 2-5-1990 (BK) which is equivalent to May 18, 1933.
3.
While in service Gurcharan Kaur passed the Junior Teachers Training Examination of the Punjab Education Department in 1955-56 and according to the certificate of passing it, which was issued on July 19, 1956, her date of birth was again shown to be May 18, 1933.
4.
Later, when the grade of pay of Gurcharan Kaur was revised with effect from the date when she had passed the Junior Teacher's Course that is July 19, 1956, the entry in her service book too gave her date of birth to be May 18, 1933.
It is pertinent to note that this entry, is admittedly signed by Gurcharan Kaur.
5.
On the other hand, Gurcharan Kaur, on her own part, seeks to rely upon the School Leaving Certificate issued to her by the Government High School, Tibba, in District Kapurthala from where she had passed her Middle Examination in 1953.
This certificate shows her date of birth a, recorded in the School register to be 8-11-1995 BK (February 19, 1939).
6.
Next, there is the Matriculation Certificate of the Panjab University issued to her on May 21 1968, where again, her date of birth is recorded as February 19, 1939 (equivalent to 8-11-1995 BK)  7.
It was the case of Gurcharan Kaur that her date of birth had been wrongly changed in her service book from February 19, 1939 to May 18, 1933, it being averred, in this behalf, that in 1981, the Block Education Officer, Kapurthala, while sending a representation regarding her General Provident Fund, wrongly mentioned May 18, 1933, as her date of birth and consequently her date of retirement to be May 31, 1991.
It was then, she claimed that she learnt that her date of birth had been changed from February 19, 1939 to May 18, 1933.
It is said that she thereupon made a representation Annexure P-7, which was recommended by the District Education Office vide Annexure P-8 but it was rejected by the Director Public Instructions (Schools) on Junary 18, 1990.
She then submitted a representation to the Government on July 20, 1990, followed by a notice through her counsel on' March 16, 1991, but to no avail.
8.
The case as set up by Gurcharan Kaur was controverted, on behalf of the State of Punjab.
Great emphasis being placed upon the fact that the entries in her service book showing her date of birth to be May 18, 1933, were duly signed by her and she was, consequently, estopped from challenging them.
The delay on her part in seeking the change in her date of birth and that too on the verge of her retirement was also stressed.
Further, it was denied that any representation had been submitted by her in the year 1968 as a consequence of which corrections were made in her service book recording thereafter her date of birth as February 19, 1939.
9.
During the hearing the writ petition, the learned single Judge had occasion to examine the service book of Gurcharan Kaur.
The observations made with regard to it being "In the service book, an extract from which has been produced as Annexure P-6, the petitioner's date of birth appears to have been originally recorded as 2-5-1990 BK.
This appears to have been later on changed to 5-21990 BK.
On conversion to Christian era, the date of birth is recorded as 16-5-1933 A.D.
This entry appears to have been deleted and changed to 19-2-1939 twice.
However, both the entries depicting the date of birth 19-2-1939 appear to have been deleted and an entry depicting 18-5-1933 A.D. has been recorded.
There are no signatures or initials below the corrections.
On the left hand side entries appear to have been recorded in February 1965.
The first entry relates to the passing of JT Examination from the Education Department, Punjab, during the Session 1955-56 whereunder the date of birth has been recorded as 18-5-1933.
The Second entry records the factum of the petitioner having passed the 8th class examination from High School, Tibba in March, 1953.
Date of birth is recorded as 8-11-1955.
Both these entries appear to have been signed on February 26, 1965.
The date of birth which was originally recorded as 8-11-1995 appears to have been scored out later on.
10.
Keeping in view the entries in the service book and other documentary evidence on record, the learned single Judge quashed the order Annexure P-9 of June 18, 1990, declining to correct the date of birth of Gurcharan Kaur as prayed for by her and declared that her date of birth was February 19, 1939 and that she could not, therefore, be retired from service on the basis of her date of birth being May 18, 1933.
11.
It will be seen that in arriving at the finding that the correct date of birth of Gurcharan Kaur was February 19, 1939, the learned Single Judge had adverted to the School Leaving Certificate issued by the Government High School, Tibba, from where she had passed the Middle Standard Examination in 1953.
The noteworthy feature that impels attention here, is that this certificate had been obtained by Gurcharan Kaur after she had been appointed as Untrained Teacher in 1954.
The School Leaving Certificate is of April 24, 1956.
Similarly, her Matriculation Certificate mentioning her date of birth to be February 19, 1939, is again of the period after she had joined service and the entry in her service book, duly signed by her, showing her date of birth to be May 18, 1933.
12.
The main reliance was upon the entries in the service book, which tend to show that the original date of birth as recorded therein of May 18, 1933, was subsequently changed to February 19, 1939 and then again to May 18, 1933.
13.
Such being the State of the Service Book, there can be no escape from the conclusion that without proper evidence being led, no conclusion can safely be arrived at with regard to what is recorded or scored out therein, it would be essential to know who made the changes, who authorised them and when.
It is apparent, therefore, that complicated questions of fact arise, which cannot be adjudicated upon without affording due opportunity to the parties for adducing evidence.
14.
Reference may here be made to be judgment of this Court in Jiwan Dass v. State of Haryana, 1989(1) RSJ 250, where it was held that this Court being a Court of Record, is not the proper forum to adjudicate on questions of fact pertaining to disputed date of birth which can be decided only after examination and assessing the supporting and corroborative evidence and that the proper forum to decide this matter would be the Civil Court.
15.
Similar was the view expressed by the Division Bench of this Court in Ram Singh Arora v. State of Punjab, 1989(1) RSJ 416.
16.
Mr. PS Patwalia, appearing for respondent Gurcharan Kaur sought to wriggle out this situation by raising the contention that, as the date of birth of Gurcharan Kaur has been changed from February 19, 1939 to May 18, 1933, without any show-cause notice being issued to her, it could not be treated as effective and thus had to be disregarded.
This contention, though attractive on the face of it, cannot, however, stand scrutiny, as the service record shows that, the first entry, in fact, mentioned her date of birth as May 18, 1933.
This was later scored off and changed to February 19, 1939 and then again it was shown to be what the original entry was, namely, May 18, 1933.
This being the situation, no question of the change of date being vitiated on account of no show-cause notice having been served upon Gurcharan Kaur, really arises.
17.
In the context of the totality of the circumstances of the case, the controversy here must appropriately be left for adjudication by the Civil Court, if any party chooses to move it.
We consequently hereby set aside the order of the learned single Judge and accept this appeal.
In the circumstances, however, we make no order as to costs.
It may be clarified here that nothing said in this judgment or in that of the learned single judge shall be construed as expression of opinion with regard to the correct date of birth of Gurcharan Kaur.
