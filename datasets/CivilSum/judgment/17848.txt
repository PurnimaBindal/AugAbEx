JS Sekhon J - The Petitioners are serving as ad hoc teachers in Govt. School under the State of Haryana.
They have been disallowed medical allowance by the State Govt. on the ground that instruction Annexure do not provide so.
2.
The observations of the Apex Court in the case reported as Rattan Lal v. State of Haryana & others, 1985(3) SLR 548 are clear enough on the point that ad hoc teachers employed by the State Govt. are entitled to medical leave benefit etc. and other allowances.
In the circumstances there is no option but to accept this petition and direct the respondents to pay medical allowance to the petitioners.
There is no order as to costs.
Petition accepted.
