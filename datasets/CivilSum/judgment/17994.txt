GR Majithia, J - Common questions of law and facts arise for determination in civil petition Nos. 2620 of 1986; 3179 of 1987 and 517 of 1987, as such these writ petitions are being disposed of by this judgment.
Reference to relevant facts has been made from civil writ petition No. 2620 of 1986.
2.
The petitioners have sought a mandate from this Court to respondent No. 2 to allow them the revised pay scale of Rs. 500-1100 from February 1, 1981 and not with effect from December 31, 1985.
Facts first :-  The petitioners joined the service of respondent No. 1 as Draftsmen in October, 1979/March, 1981 in the unrevised pay scale of Rs. 160-400.
The pay scale was identical with the pay scale which is allowed to the Draftsmen working in the various departments of the Haryana State.
The pay scales of the Haryana Government employees were revised by Notifications dated February 28, 1980 and July 21, 1980.
Respondent No. 1 adopted the same pay scale for common categories on June 12, 1981.
Respondent No. 2 again revised the pay scales of various employees by letter dated July 3, 1981.
The pay scale of the Draftsmen category was revised from Rs. 525-1050 to Rs. 600-1100 with effect from February 1, 1981.
The petitioners filed representations to respondent No. 1 to grant them the same scale of pay as were allowed the their counterparts in the employment of State of Haryana.
During the pendency of the writ petition, respondent No. 1 has given the petitioners same pay scale as is allowed to the Government employees with effect from December 31, 1985 vide order dated July 27, 1987.
The petitioners grouse is that they should have been allowed the scale of pay of Rs. 600-1100 with effect from January 1, 1981 since this scale was allowed to the Government employees from that date.
3.
Written statement has been filed on behalf of the respondents.
4.
Respondent No. 2 maintained that it issued instructions vide letter No. 1/162/IPR (FD)-80, dated July 22, 1981 to all the agencies of the Government including respondent No. 1 that all proposals of revision of pay scales should be routed to the State Finance Department and no direction or resolution be passed by the agencies of the State Government in the matter of revision of pay scales unless the Finance Department has been consulted.
Respondent No. 2 in compliance with the instructions submitted proposals for removal of anomalies in the pay scales of its employees vide letter dated October 16, 1986.
The matter of removal of anomalies of pay scales of the petitioners are also included in that proposal and is under the active consideration of the State Government.
Respondent No. 1 in the proposal has stated that the revised scales of pay of Rs. 600-1100 be allowed to the Draftsmen with effect from February 1, 1981.
5.
Respondent No. 1 in its written statement has taken a preliminary objection that the conditions of service of Government employees are different from the employees of respondent No. 1.
The posts of Government employees are pensionable whereas the posts held by the employees of respondent No. 1 are non-pensionable.
The employees of respondent No1 get Provident Fund contribution from it.
In addition they also get the benefits of bonus as well as conveyance allowance.
It is further pleaded that the representation of the petitioners is under the active consideration of respondent No. 1 and that the case of the petitioners is being processed and pending before the anomalies committee for consideration.
6.
At the time of hearing, learned counsel for the parties could not inform me whether the case of the petitioners for revision of the pay scales with effect from February 1, 1981 (which was pending consideration before the Anomaly Committee) has been disposed of or not.
Expression of opinion on the merits of the claim of the petitioners will prejudice them.
Consequently, I refrain from expressing opinion on the merits of the case.
7.
It will meet the ends of justice if respondent No. 2 is directed to dispose of the proposal submitted by respondent No. 1 for revision of pay scales of the employees like the petitioners working with respondent No. 1 expeditiously.
Respondent No. 1 has stated in its written statement that the proposal is under the active consideration of the Anomaly Committee.
Respondent No. 2 is directed to take final decision in the matter within three months from the date of receipt of copy of this order.
With these observations, the writ petition is disposed of.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Petition disposed of.
