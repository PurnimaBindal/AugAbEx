GS Singhvi, J - In this petitioner the petitioner has prayed for quashing orders Annexures P13 dated 2961984 and P20 dated 1621985 passed by Chief Engineer (Operation), HSEB, Chandigarh (respondent No. 2).
2.
The petitioner joined service as Assistant Lineman in Punjab State Electricity Board on 541957.
During the course of service, he was promoted as Lineman Grade-II and then as Line Superintendent Grade-II.
On its creation, the petitioner-service was allocated to the Haryana State Electricity Board (for short, the Board).
While working as Line Superintendent Grade-II in Panipat Division of the Board, the petitioner had been elected as President of the Employees Union of Panipat Sub-Division (West).
In that capacity, he espoused the cause of the employees before the officers.
This caused annoyance to them and a series of punitive measures were taken against him by suspension etc. on trifle matters.
The details of the action taken by the respondents against the petitioner are as under :-  (a) He was placed under suspension vide order Annexure P1 dated 3171967 issued by Shri Narender Singh Gulia, the then Officer on Special Duty of the Board and a departmental enquiry was initiated against him on the charge of having recorded fictitious attendance of one Shri Nanak Chand, Lineman Grade- II (Workcharged) from 16.7.1967 to 18.7.1967.
He was reinstated vide order Annexure P2 dated 3111967 and the period of suspension was treated as duty for all intents and purposes in terms of Rule 73(b) of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part-I (for short, the Rules), as applicable to the Board.
Thereafter, vide order Annexure P3 dated 21121967, respondent No. 2 administered a warning to him to remain careful in future.
(b) A First Information Report was got registered against him at Police Station, Isarana on 2771973 under Sections 353 and 186 of the Indian Penal Code.
After 4 days, he was suspended vide order dated 3071973.
He was tried in the Court of Judicial Magistrate Ist Class, Panipat, who acquitted him vide judgment dated 9.4.1975 (Annexure P4).
Thereafter, he was reinstated vide order dated 951975 (Annexure P5).
(c) The petitioner was again suspended vide order dated 29.10.1982 passed by Superintending Engineer.
Operation Circle.
Bhiwani.
He challenged the same in C.W.P.
No. 5005 of 1982.
On being noticed by the Court, the concerned authority of the Board asked him to give an undertaking to withdraw the writ petition and assured that his suspension would be revoked.
After submission of the required undertaking, respondent No. 2 passed order Annexure P7 dated 411983 for his reinstatement with retrospective effect.
(d) The petitioner was suspended fourth time vide order Annexure P8 dated 2331983 issued by respondent No. 2.
He challenged that order in C.W.P.
No. 2303 of 1983.
The same was dismissed as withdrawn by a Division Bench on 1611984 in view of the undertaking given by the counsel for the respondents that the enquiry against the petitioner shall be concluded within four months.
In the meanwhile, the petitioner was served with show cause notice Annexure P10 dated 1.7.1983 issued by respondent No. 2 proposing to take action against him under Rule 8 of the Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952 read with Regulation 10 of the Haryana State Electricity Board (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1980 (for short, the Regulations) on the charge of having removed the cable from the site of a consumer, namely, Shri Jhanda Ram and installing the same at another site without issuance of STD or authorisation from the competent authority.
On receipt of the show cause notice, the petitioner made application dated 481983 and sent reminders dated 1391983.
28.10.1983 and 4121983 for supply of documents necessary for filing reply.
However, copies of the documents were not made available to him and he was compelled to file reply dated 311984.
He then filed CWP No. 126 of 1984 with the complaint that the enquiry has not been completed in terms of the undertaking given before the Court.
On receipt of notice of the contempt petition, respondent No. 2 passed order dated 2961984 (Annexure P13) and administered a warning to the petitioner to be more careful in future in the performance of official duties.
Soon thereafter, he passed order Annexure P15 dated 281984 vide which the period of the petitioner's suspension was treated as extraordinary leave.
The petitioner challenged orders dated 2961984 and 281984 in C.W.P.
No. 3702 of 1984 which was disposed of by a Division Bench on 29101984 in the following terms :-  "At the time of hearing of this writ petition, the learned counsel for the respondents-Board stated at the bar that the impugned order Annexure P/15 may be deemed to have been withdrawn and the Board shall pass a fresh order in this matter within four months."
(e) In the back-drop of the statement made by the Board's counsel before the Court, respondent No. 2 issued order Annexure P17 dated 26111984 withdrawing order dated 281984.
Thereafter, he issued notice Annexure P18 dated 5121984 with the following proposals :- (i) Why he may not be denied pay and allowances for the period of suspension except what had already been paid as subsistence allowance ?
(ii) Why the period of suspension may not be treated as a period not spent on duty in terms of Rule 73 of the Rules ?
3.
The petitioner filed a detailed reply Annexure P19 dated 11121984 in which he pleaded that there was no justification to deny him full pay and allowances for the period of suspension or treat the period of suspension as not spent on duty.
After two months, respondent No. 2 passed order dated 1621985 for treating the period of suspension from 2331983 to 231984 as extraordinary leave.
4.
The petitioner has now challenged orders Annexures P13 and P20 on the ground of violation of rules of natural justice and the provisions of Rules 4.7 and 73 of the Rules.
He also levelled allegations of mala fides against respondent Nos. 3 and 4 by asserting that the impugned orders have been passed with the sole object of punishing him for his Union activities.
He has averred that the charge levelled against him is frivolous and the warning has been administered by respondent No. 2 without there being any evidence to substantiate the allegation.
He has assailed Annexure P.20 on an additional ground that the order treating the period of suspension as extraordinary leave amounts to forfeiture of past service and consequential postponement of increment and this could not have been done without holding an enquiry and giving him opportunity of defence.
5.
In the written statement filed on behalf of respondents Nos. 1 and 2, it has been averred that the impugned orders have been passed in consonance with the relevant rules and regulations and after giving due notice and opportunity of hearing to the petitioner.
They have not controverted the averments contained in the writ petition with reference to various proceedings taken against the petitioner, but have averred that the orders under challenge were passed keeping in view the fact that he had been found guilty of misconduct.
6.
Respondent Nos. 3 and 4 have filed separate written statements to controvert the allegation of mala fides levelled against them.
7.
I have heard learned counsel for the parties and carefully perused the record.
Regulation 5 of the Regulations specifies the penalties which can be imposed on an employee.
Warning is one of the minor penalties enumerated in Part-A thereof.
Regulation 4 empowers the competent authority to place an employee under suspension.
The petitioner has not questioned the competence of respondent No. 2 to exercise powers under Regulations 4 and 5.
Therefore, the only question which needs consideration is whether the impugned orders are vitiated due to violation of the rules of natural justice or arbitrary exercise of power by respondent No. 2.
In so far as order Annexure P13 is concerned, I am not inclined to entertain the petitioner's prayer because identical prayer made in C.W.P.
No. 3702 of 1984 was not entertained by the Division Bench, which has as mentioned above, disposed of the petition on 29101984 in view of the statement made by the counsel for the Board that order Annexure P13 may be deemed to have been withdrawn and the Board shall pass a fresh order.
If the petitioner felt that the order of the Division Bench was erroneous, in-as-much as, his prayer for quashing order Annexure P13 had not been considered, then he should have availed remedy either by filing review application before this Court or a Petition for Special Leave to Appeal before the Supreme Court.
He adopted neither of these courses.
Therefore, his challenge to the order of punishment is liable to be rejected as barred by constructive res judicata.
However, I find considerable merit in his plea that order Annexure P20 is vitiated due to violation of the rules of natural justice and is arbitrary to the core.
A cursory reading of reply filed by the petitioner to notice dated 5121984 shows that he had raised several factual and legal issues to contest the proposed denial of service benefits for the period of suspension.
However, without even adverting to those points, respondent No. 2 arbitrarily and in a casual manner ordered that the period of suspension will be treated as extraordinary leave.
While doing so, he was totally unmindful of the fact that his decision would have grave adverse impact on the service career of the petitioner, namely, forfeiture of past service and denial of increments which he would have earned during that period.
Not only this, respondent No. 2 has not assigned any reason for passing such a drastic order ignoring the fact that on three previous occasions, the petitioner had been reinstated with all service benefits.
In the premise aforesaid, I hold that order Annexure P20 is vitiated due to the violation of the basic rules of natural justice.
In the result, the writ petition is partly allowed.
Order Annexure P20 is declared illegal and quashed with the direction that the petitioner shall be given all consequential benefits within three months of presentation of a copy of this order.
However, it is made clear that this order shall not preclude the competent authority from passing fresh order in accordance with law.
Petition partly allowed.
