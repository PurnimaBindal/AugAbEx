VK Bali, J (Oral) - The challenge in this writ petition is to award, annexure P-1, rendered by the Labour Court, Amritsar on 10.2.1982, vide which order of termination of the workman was invalidated and direction was issued to reinstate him with entire back wages.
The petitioner, State of Punjab through the Executive Engineer, P.W.D.
B & R Construction Division, Pathankot, endeavoured to set aside the impugned award on the solitary ground that respondent No. 1 was not a workman as defined under Section 2(a) of the Industrial Disputes Act and also that the P.W.D.
B&R department, Punjab Government, does not fall within the ambit of the definition of 'Industry' as provided under Section 2(J) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
I need not go into the various contentions as have been spelled out in the writ petition as the matter has now been squarely covered against the petitioner by the judgment of the Apex Court in Des Raj v. State of Punjab, 1989(1) RSJ, 258.
The Hon'ble Supreme Court while dealing with Civil Appeal No. 2168 of 1987, which was against the PWD Drainage Division of the Punjab Government held that "the Irrigation Department of government comes within the ambit of Industry when the main functions are subjected to the dominant nature test."
It was further held that "in the event of definition of Industry being changed either by enforcement of new definition or by many other Legislative change, it would be always open for the Irrigation Department to raise the issue again."
In view of the observations of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in case Des Raj and others v. State of Punjab and others (supra), the writ petition has to be dismissed and is accordingly dismissed with no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
