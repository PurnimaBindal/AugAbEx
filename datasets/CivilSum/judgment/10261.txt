RL Anand, J - On 412002 the following order was passed by this Bench :-  "Notice of motion to the respondents.
On the asking of Court Ms. Geeta Mathuria, AAG, Haryana accepts notice on behalf of the respondent No. 1 and Manoj Sood, Advocate on behalf of respondents Nos. 1 and 3.
The petitioners are the project teachers.
The life of the project is up to 3132002.
The initial appointment of the petitioners was for a period of one year which is going to expire on 3112002.
The petitioners are apprehending that after 3112002 they might be on the road side and it will not be in the interest of the project itself which has to continue up to 3132002.
The learned counsel for the petitioners has invited our attention to the order passed by the Hon'ble Supreme Court on 26.7.1991 in SLP Nos. 9865-71 of 1991 and the various orders passed by this High Court in which in similar circumstances protection was given to the project teachers to continue in service till the project is completed.
We are of the considered opinion that if the petitioners are allowed to continue up to 3132002, it will be in the interest of the project itself.
We should not allow the project to collapse mid-way.
Therefore, we dispose of this writ petition with the directions to the respondents not to terminate the services of the petitioners up to 3132002.
Copy Dasti."
2.
This application has been moved for modification/recalling of the above said order.
Notice of the application was given to the opposite counsel.
3.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance have gone through the record of the case.
4.
The learned counsel Mr. Malik has vehemently opposed the application by stating that the work which was being performed by the petitioner is being taken from some JBT Teachers of the Education Department.
He further submits that there is work for the petitioners and, therefore, their services could not be terminated earlier to 3132002.
On the contrary, the learned Advocate General, Haryana submits that no work is being taken from the JBT teachers and that the posts of the petitioners against which they had been working have already been abolished.
5.
This Court is taking a consistent stand in these types of cases.
The petitioners were the Project Teachers.
The term of the petitioners was up to 3112002.
The teaching aspect of the project has already been discontinued by the Society.
In these circumstances, we dispose of the main petition with the following order :  6.
If at any stage the respondent-society wants to employ any Project Teachers prior to 3132002, the petitioners will have the preferential right.
If in any case the project is to be continued after 3132002 and teaching is to be imparted to the primary classes, the petitioners will have the right to apply and their case will be considered according to rules.
Order accordingly.
