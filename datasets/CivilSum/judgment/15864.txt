Amarjeet Chaudhary, ACJ.
- The petitioner through the present Civil Writ Petition has impugned the order dated 2221996, copy annexure P-1 to the writ petition, whereby he has been prematurely retired from service from the post of Sub Divisional Engineer, Public Health in PWD (Public Health Branch), Haryana.
2.
Facts relevant to the controversy involved in this case as pleaded by the petitioner are that he initially joined the service as Junior Engineer on 24.1.1963 who was promoted to the post of Sub Divisional Engineer on 4.11.1981, reverted as Junior Engineer on 1231985 and again promoted as Sub Divisional Engineer in the year 1989-90.
He claims his record to be unblemished and that no adverse remarks have ever been communicated to him except for the year 1988-89 and as per his knowledge, even those adverse remarks were expunged on the representation made by the petitioner.
However, the petitioner pleads that he was awarded punishment of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect vide order dated 15.5.1990 and the said punishment was over in the year 1992.
Secondly the petitioner admits that he was issued a charge sheet under Rule 7 of the Haryana Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1987 on 18111993 to which the petitioner filed reply.
The Inquiry Officer was appointed and he exonerated him in regard to charge No. 4 and held him guilty in charge Nos. 1 to 3.
The petitioner was issued show cause notice on 3081995 proposing stoppage of two annual increments with cumulative effect and recovery of Rs. 1,59,52920 (copy annexure P-3 to the writ petition).
The petitioner claims to have filed reply to the show cause notice and it is after filing of the writ petition, the respondents have imposed the penalty vide order dated 841996 (copy annexure P-4 to the writ petition), received by him on 151996.
3.
The petitioner pleads that other officers have not been penalised and his reply was not considered by the respondents.
The petitioner is due to retire on 3051998 after attaining the age of 58 years.
However, the respondents already retired him pre-maturely vide order dated 2221996, annexure P-1.
The petitioner has claimed that except for 1988-89, no other adverse A.C.R.
was communicated and entire record was good/very good.
However, the adverse remarks for the year 1988-89 were expunged by the Engineer-in-Chief.
Hence he prays the quashing of impugned order dated 2221996, annexure P-1, and allow him to continue in service as if no impugned order has been passed.
4.
On a notice of motion having been issued, the respondents appeared and filed the written statement contesting the claim of the petitioner and specifically taking the plea that the relevant instructions governing the pre-mature retirement or grant of extension to a Government employee to continue in service beyond the age of 55 years are contained in Annexure R-1 dated 391968 and annexure R-2 dated 12121988 and that since the charge sheet under Rule 7 related to the integrity of the petitioner whereby loss to the tune of Rs. 1,59,52920 was caused to the State exchequer, he was rightly retired after recommendations of the Committee of officers which considered his case on 2511996.
5.
The claim of the petitioner that his service record was above board and he was never communicated any adverse remark was also refuted as per specific stand taken in para No. 3 of the written statement to the effect that adverse remarks had been intimated to the petitioner for the period 1.4.1966 to 31.10.1966, 1966-67, 1967-68, 1988-89 and 6/1992 to 3/1993.
It was denied that the petitioner made any representation against his adverse A.C.R.
for the year 1988-89 or these were expunged by the competent authority.
It was averred that the record was not traceable and the petitioner should have placed the same on the record.
It is also the stand of the respondents that the charges levelled in the charge sheet have been proved.
The petitioner did not challenge the said order either by way of appeal or otherwise and he did not file any defence even to the show cause notice.
So the penalty was ultimately imposed vide order dated 841996, annexure P-4.
Thus two requirements of para No. 1 of instructions dated 12121988 stood complied with.
It has been pleaded that impugned order has been passed in public interest and in accordance with the instructions on the issue.
6.
In pursuance of the orders passed by this Court, Annual Confidential Reports of the petitioner and the report of the Officers Committee to which his case for retention in service was referred, was also produced and we have perused the same.
7.
The petitioner has not filed any replication to the written statement filed by the respondents.
8.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the material on record.
The specific averments made by the respondents with regard to adverse A.C.Rs.
for the period 1966-67, 1967-68 and 6/1992 to 3/1993 have been converted nor it has been controverted that the petitioner ever filed reply to the show cause notice on the basis whereof he has been awarded the punishment vide order dated 841996, annexure P-4, nor it has been rebutted that he has not filed any appeal against the order, annexure P-4.
9.
Similarly the petitioner has not challenged the instructions, annexure R- 2, under which even if the ACRs of the Government employee meet the requirement of instructions, annexure R-1, to the effect that 70% or above record is good but if charge sheet has been issued reflecting upon the integrity of the employee, he can be retired prematurely in terms of para 1 of the instructions, annexure R-2, dated 12121988.
Even the averments of the petitioner that he was not conveyed any adverse A.C.R.
except that of 1988-89 has not been proved to be correct in view of para 3 of the written statement which has not been rebutted by the petitioner and even the stand of the petitioner that he had challenged the adverse A.C.R.
for the year 1988-89 which was expunged by the Engineer-in-Chief has been proved to be incorrect as no material has been placed by the petitioner on record particularly when the respondents denied this fact and no replication has been filed by the petitioner.
10.
For the facility, relevant portion of the instructions dated 12121988, annexure R-2, is reproduced as under :-  "It has been decided that in the following kind of cases of disciplinary proceedings, integrity may be considered as doubtful :- (i) the cases in which the charge-sheets have been issued/court cases are pending where the charges are such which cast aspersion on the integrity of the employees."
Undisputedly in the present case the charge sheet issued to the petitioner on 18111993 casts aspersions on the integrity of the petitioner in which he has ultimately been punished also.
So his case is fully covered under para 1 of the instructions supra.
Besides this, we have perused the service record of the petitioner and the minutes of the Officers Committee meeting which considered the case of the petitioner for retention in service beyond 55 years and we have not found any irregularity in the process of reaching the decision by the Committee in recommending the premature retirement of the petitioner which cannot be termed as a punishment.
Though the overall service record of the petitioner may be good but in view of the instructions, annexure R-2, and he having been finally punished vide order, annexure P-4, dated 841996 which order has not been impugned by him, the petitioner cannot succeed in the writ petition.
The learned counsel for the petitioner relied upon Dharam Pal v. State of Haryana, 1996(3) All Instant Judgments page 581 : 1996(1) SCT 812(P&H)(DB), but the said judgment is of no help because the point of instructions, annexure R-2, dated 12121988 which was not there and the said judgment is distinguishable.
We are of the considered view that even one instance of grave misconduct is sufficient to weed out delinquent official against whom charges have been duly proved in departmental enquiry.
In the light of above discussion, there is no merit in writ petition.
The same is accordingly dismissed leaving the parties to bear their own costs.
Petition dismissed.
