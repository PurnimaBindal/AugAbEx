SS Saron, J - The claim of the petitioner in the present petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India is for the issuance of appropriate order directing respondent No. 3 to suitably amend/modify the appendix attached as Annexure P-5 to letter No. 31251/PROM/77/CA-3 dated 1851992 (Annexure P-4) to the extent he has wrongly been shown to be lacking the qualification of Technical Trade (TT) Class II and to promote him retrospectively from the rank of Naik to Havildar with seniority with effect from not later than 1851992.
2.
The facts giving rise to the present petition are that the petitioner is serving in the army in Army Ordnance Corps (AOC) with effect from 20121985.
He claims a legal right to get corrected and up date the maintenance of his service record which has erroneously not been done by respondent No. 3.
It is averred by the petitioner that while serving with the Officer Commanding, 25 Infantry Division Ordnance Unit, respondent No. 4 passed the Technical Trade (TT) Test Gp-A Class II Test (`TT Class II Test' for short) on 2591991.
He appeared in the said TT Class II test along with many others, some of them have been impleaded as proforma respondents No. 7 to 11.
The publication of the necessary orders like DO Part II orders in this regard is a command function based on which respondent No. 3 i.e.
Officer Incharge, Records further publishes the requisite Part II orders which are entered in the record of service.
The petitioner's Unit, vide letter No. 184/227/Adm dated 2311998 confirmed the Publication of D.O.
part II order No. 2/0029/92 dated 26.4.1992, copies of which are appended to the petition as Annexures P-2 and P-3 respectively.
3.
The case of the petitioner is that he is not being promoted from the rank of Naik Havildar and that the reason wrongly being given for this by the respondents is that he is lacking in TT Class II test.
Therefore, he is not being promoted.
4.
Notice in the case was issued to the respondents.
Respondent Nos. 1 to 3 have filed their written statement.
It is the stand of said respondents No. 1 to 3 that no person junior to the petitioner has been promoted till March 1995.
Besides, the proforma respondents are senior to the petitioner and were rightly promoted before him.
It is further stated that the petitioner along with other Naiks was screened for promotion to the rank of Havildar during April 1992 but due to non-availability of vacancy, promotion order was not issued to him.
Besides, the petitioner was also lacking in TT Class II and this fact was brought to the notice of the concerned Naiks so that in future they could clear the required test.
Thereafter the petitioner's case for promotion to the post of Havildar came up w.e.f.
131995 but he could not be promoted due to his adverse ACR record and his juniors whose ACR record was found satisfactory were promoted.
Ultimately in January 1996 the petitioner earned the required ACR record and was promoted accordingly by the respondents.
It is submitted that there is no mala fide intention or arbitrariness on the part of the respondents as alleged by the petitioner.
The petitioner has also filed a replication to the written statement wherein he has reiterated his stand in the petition.
5.
The learned counsel for the parties have been heard and with their assistance I have gone through the records of the case.
6.
The learned counsel for the petitioner has referred to letter dated 1851992 (Annexure P-4) which gives the reasons for his non-promotion to the rank of Havildar.
The name of the petitioner figures at No. 24 of the Appendix (Annexure P-5) to the aforesaid letter dated 1851992.
The reason for not promoting the petitioner is indicated as :- "Lacking TT Class II".
7.
This reason for not promoting him to the rank of Havildar as contended by the petitioner, is without substance as he had duly passed the TT Class II with effect from 1731992 (Annexure P-6).
However, the fact of his passing the TT Class II test was published on 2271993 after lapse of almost one year.
Therefore, the reasons given in the letter dated 1851992 (Annexure (P-4) i.e. "Lacking TT Class II" for not promoting the petitioner are totally baseless.
In support of this contention, the petitioner has referred to Annexures P-6 which is to the effect that the petitioner was "awarded CL-2 GP A" on 1731992.
The petitioner made a statutory complaint dated 491997 which has been considered in terms of letter dated 23.8.1999 Annexure P-1.
In para 3 of this letter, it has been recorded as follows :-  "The complaint has been examined by the COAS in detail along with all other relevant documents, and comments/recommendations of the intermediary authorities.
The NCO came up for promotion to the rank of Hav on 01 Mar 95 but could not be promoted due to not meeting the CR criteria.
The NCO was promoted to Hav w.e.f.
01 Jan 96, once he attained the laid down CR criteria.
Thus, no injustice has been done to the NCO."
8.
The petitioner further contends that it is the admitted case of the respondents in their written statement that the award of TT Class II GP `A' was not recorded in the service documents till the date of issuance of letter dated 1851992.
As per consideration of petitioner's statutory complaint in terms of the letter dated 28.3.1999 Annexure P-1, the reason given for not promoting him is due to not meeting of his confidential report criteria.
Had the petitioner been considered for promotion when he had admittedly passed the TT Class II on 1731992, he would have fulfilled the necessary criteria.
In support of his contention, the petitioner relies upon the observations made in the case reported as Ajeeb Singh Bakshi v. State of Haryana, 1969 SLR 400 to contend that change in criteria for promotion cannot be given retrospective effect.
Besides, he also places strong reliance on the order dated 23.12.1999 passed by this Court in the present case while issuing notice of motion.
9.
On the other hand, learned counsel for the respondents contends that no person junior to the petitioner was promoted in March 1995 and that the proforma respondents, who have been promoted to the rank of Havildar, are all senior to the petitioner.
The main reason for not promoting the petitioner to the rank of Havildar during April 1992 is indicated to be non-availability of vacancies.
Lack of TT Class II was brought to the notice of all the concerned Naiks so that in future they could clear the required test.
It is further contended that the case of the petitioner for promotion to the rank of Havildar came up on 1371995 but he could not be promoted due to adverse ACRs and it is in January 1996 that the petitioner earned the required AC records and was promoted accordingly.
The respondents have contended that no person junior to the petitioner has been promoted as Havildar and in this respect a reference has been made to the table at para 6 of the written statement.
The learned counsel for the respondents has referred to the judgment of Hon'ble Rajasthan High Court in the case of Nathu Singh Shekhawat v. Union of India and others, 2003(4) SCT 455 (Rajasthan) : Military Law Journal 1997 Raj.
103 to contend that a Colonel in the Army was placed in the approved panel of Brigadiers, however, he could not be promoted due to non-availability of vacancy.
In the meanwhile, he attained the age superannuation.
He assailed his non-promotion to the rank of Brigadier.
On the facts it was held that he would not be entitled to any relief.
The counsel for the respondents also contends that the writ petition is liable to be dismissed on account of laches and in support of this contention, he relies on the judgment of the Hon'ble Apex court in PS Sadasivaswami v. State of Tamil Nadu, AIR 1974 SC 2271.
10.
I have considered the respective contentions of the learned counsel for the parties.
The points involved for consideration are whether the non-promotion of the petitioner as Havildar was only due to the reason that he did not pass the TT Class II and whether the change in criteria in 1995 affects his rights.
Besides, whether the petitioner could not be promoted as there was no vacancy.
11.
It is well settled that chances of promotion is not a condition of service and is defeasible and an employee does not have a vested right of promotion but only that of consideration for promotion.
Besides, a person can only be considered for promotion on availability of vacancy.
It is the case of the respondents that at the time of promotion of the petitioner and others in terms of the letter dated 1851992, there was no vacancy available for promotion to the rank of Naik and that no person junior to the petitioner has been promoted.
12.
In order to appreciate this contention, a reference may be made to the following tabulation in para 6 of the written statement :-  No. Rank Trade & name of Official Dt. of E  Dt. of B  Dt. of NK  Dt. of TT-II  Dt. of Hav.

A persual of the above tabulation shows that the petitioner, who is at serial No. 5, has been promoted as Havildar with effect from 111996.
Besides the date of promotion of Naik in respect of the persons who have been promoted earlier to the petitioner is 111989 whereas the date of promotion of the petitioner as Naik is 131989.
In these circumstances, I am of the view that no prejudice has been caused to the petitioner as no person junior to him has been promoted and that the main reason for not promoting the petitioner is due to lack of vacancies and not that he did not clear TT Class II Test.
14.
The learned counsel for the petitioner, however, relies heavily on the order dated 23.12.1999 passed by this Court while issuing notice of motion and emphasises that the order clearly envisages that whatever the ACR criteria was at the relevant time in the year 1992 should have been applied to the petitioner and not any other criteria.
The order dated 23.12.1999 reads as under :-  "Learned counsel for the petitioner has drawn our attention to the recommendation of the Commandant, 14 Field, Ammunition Depot dated 2131997 (Annexure P-14) to contend that in fact the petitioner had the requisite qualification of TT Class II with effect from 1731992 and the POR was published vide AOC Records DO II No. 01/0154/056/C/93 and vide 25 In DOU DO II No. 02/0029/004/92, as has been found recorded in his FS docus.
On the basis of the qualifications as have been mentioned in Annexure P-2 which the petitioner had even on 1731992, he was promoted as Hawaldar in 1996.
Whatever the ACR criteria was at the relevant time in the year 1992 should have been applied to the petitioner and not any later criteria because the alleged illegal supersession was in the year 1992.
The statutory complaint has been rejected on the ground that the petitioner was not making the grade of CR criteria in the year 1995.
The statutory complaint was regarding the alleged illegal supersession in the year 1992 and not 1995.
Notice of motion for 2122000.
Along with notice of motion, a copy of this order be also sent to the respondents."
15.
However, to my mind, the contention of the learned counsel for the petitioner is not tenable as the fact is that there was no vacancy available for promotion of the petitioner.
It is contended by the petitioner in his rejoinder that the contention of the respondents regarding non-availability of vacancy for promotion is devoid of merit as in para 6 of the written statement it is admitted that two persons were promoted as Havildar on 141992.
The relevant extract of para 6 showing the promotions of various persons on 141992 has already been reproduced above.
The two persons i.e. Rakesh Kumar Sharma and Kali Charan, who have been promoted to the rank of Naik on 141992 were admittedly senior to the petitioner as their date of promotion as a Naik is 111989.
The seniority of JCOs, NCOs and others is maintained as per instructions dated September 5, 1992 of the AOC Record Office.
Paras 10, 11 and 12 of the said instructions read as under :-  "Seniority AOC Record Office maintains seniority rolls for Sepoys, Lance Naiks, Havildars, Naib Subedars, Subedars and Subedar Majors by trade/category wise.
The seniority of sepoy is assessed from the date of enrollment (date of birth where date of enrollment is the same).
Seniority after promotion to higher rank is assessed from the date of such promotion.
Wherever ante-date seniority is granted it will be counted accordingly.
Seniority of direct Havildars will be determined as under :- xxx xxx xxx" A persual of the above shows that seniority roll of the aforesaid ranks is maintained as per trade/category wise and that seniority after promotion to higher rank is assessed from the date of such promotion.
16.
In the present case two persons i.e. Rakesh Kumar Sharma and Kali Charan, referred to by the petitioner, were promoted as Havildars on 141992.
They were enrolled as Naiks on 111989, whereas the petitioner was enrolled on 131989.
Therefore, they being senior, no prejudice can be said to have been caused to the petitioner.
The petitioner can possibly have no grouse against their promotion.
The judgment in the case of Ajeeb Singh Bakshi's (supra) referred to by the learned counsel for the petitioner is not applicable to the facts and circumstances of the case in view of the fact that the change in criteria is not a reason for the non-promotion of the petitioner.
The real reason for his non-promotion is non-availability of vacancy for the rank of Havildar and no person junior to the petitioner is shown to have been promoted as Naik before him.
In this view of the matter, the judgment of Rajasthan High Court in the case of Nathu Singh (supra), referred by the learned counsel for the respondents, would apply and even otherwise due to non-availability of the vacancy the petitioner could not be promoted.
It is only when a vacancy became available that he was promoted with effect from 111996.
In this view of the matter, no prejudice has been caused to the petitioner for his non-promotion as Havildar with effect from the date he claims promotion.
The question of delay and laches in filing the present writ petition is not being adverted to as I have already reached the conclusion that non-promotion of the petitioner to the rank of Havildar was with a valid justification and there being no vacancy available would not entitle the petitioner for promotion.
Keeping in view the above facts and circumstances, there is no merit in this writ petition and the same is dismissed with no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
