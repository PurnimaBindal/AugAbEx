THB Chalapathi, J - This appeal is directed against the decree and judgment of the learned Additional District Judge, Amritsar in Civil Appeal No. 166 of 1994 dated 2631996.
2.
The respondent-plaintiff filed the suit for recovery of Rs. 15,000/- as wages due to him.
According to the plaintiff, he joined service in the Health and Family Welfare Department in May, 1987 as Medical Officer.
He put in more than 3 years service, but he was not paid the salary for three days in May, 1990, 5 days in June, 1990 and also the wages for the months of October, 1990 and February, 1991.
He further averred that the increments were also not released from 1989 onwards.
According to him, action of the department in not releasing the wages for aforesaid period and not granting the increments, is illegal and unjustified.
He further alleged that his services ought to have been regularised in view of the notification issued by the Punjab Government.
Therefore, he filed this writ petition claiming arrears of the salary due to him.
3.
The defendants-appellants filed a written statement contending inter alia that the Court has no jurisdiction to entertain the suit and the suit is not maintainable.
It is further averred that the plaintiff was appointed on short term basis for a period of six months by an order dated 2551987 and he joined the Department on 561987 and the plaintiff was irregular in performing his duty.
His ACR for the year 1988-89 shows that he was irregular.
The adverse remarks were conveyed to him on 131990.
Since the plaintiff joined his duty on a fixed pay, his services were terminated on 6121987.
Thereafter he was again appointed for a period of six months upto 561998.
Again he was appointed on 8121988 for a further period of six months which expired on 861989.
After one day break, he was again appointed on 961989.
According to the defendants, since the plaintiff was appointed on short term basis, he is not entitled to any benefit claimed in the suit.
It is also alleged that the plaintiff was declared absent from duty by the Senior Medical Officer, Incharge, PHC, Sarhali (Amritsar).
The pay of the plaintiff for the month of October, 1990 could not be disbursed in time and the same was deposited in his Saving Bank Account No. P-2859 on 3071992 and the salary for the month of February, 1991 onwards was not disbursed for want of sanction of the competent authority and the petitioner wilfully absented himself from his place of duty with effect from 641991.
The plaintiff was appointed on short term basis at a fixed pay of Rs. 940/- in the old scale which is equivalent to Rs. 2,200/- of the revised pay scale.
According to the defendants, the plaintiff is not entitled for regularisation without the recommendations of the Punjab Public Service Commission.
Since the order of appointment was for short term, there was no necessity to issue any order of termination.
Therefore, the suit is liable to be dismissed.
4.
On the basis of the pleadings, the trial judge framed appropriate issues.
The plaintiff examined himself and marked certain documents as Exhibits P-1, P-2 and Exhibit P-7 and on behalf of the defendants DW-1 was examined.
On a consideration of the evidence on record, the learned Sub Judge Ist Class, Amritsar by his judgment dated 541994 decreed the suit for the recovery of wages for 3 days in May, 1990 and 5 days in June, 1990 and interest @ 12% P.A.
on the unpaid salary for the months of October, 1990 and February, 1991 till payment, if not already made to the plaintiff.
Aggrieved by the decree and judgment of the trial Court, the appellants preferred an appeal to the learned Additional District Judge, Amritsar, who by the impugned decree and judgment dated 2631996 dismissed the appeal.
Hence the 2nd appeal by the defendants.
5.
Admittedly, the plaintiff was not paid the salary for three days in May, 1990 and 5 days in June, 1990.
But accordingly to the appellants, the plaintiff was absent during this period and therefore the salary for these days was not paid.
It appears that no notice has been given to the plaintiff for his absence.
Except the evidence of DW-1 there is no other evidence to show that the plaintiff was absent during this period.
There would have been attendance register to show the absence of the plaintiff, but the same was not produced in the Court.
It is also clear from the evidence on record that the salary for the month of October, 1990 has been deposited in the Saving Bank Account of the plaintiff on 3071992 i.e. after the period of more than 1-1/2 years.
Therefore, both the Courts rightly held that the plaintiff is entitled to the interest on this amount for the delayed payment.
It is also the case of the plaintiff that he was not paid the salary for the month of February, 1991 for want of sanction.
But the fact remains that he worked upto February, 1991.
Therefore, the plaintiff is entitled to salary of that period.
Though necessary sanction was not granted during the relevant period, it appears that subsequently the sanction was obtained.
When admittedly the plaintiff worked for this period, he cannot be denied the salary on the ground that no sanction was obtained.
It is the duty of the authority concerned to obtain sanction within time before the expiry of the term of the plaintiff.
6.
In this view of the matter, I do not find any grounds warranting interference with the decrees and judgments of the Courts below.
7.
The appeal, therefore, fails and is, accordingly, dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
