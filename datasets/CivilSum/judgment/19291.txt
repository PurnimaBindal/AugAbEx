RS Mongia, J - This judgment will dispose of this writ petition as well as CWP Nos. 8387, 8389, 8397, 8659, 10310, 10311, 10479, 10478, 10599, 10730, 10840, 10971, 11363, 11364, 12085, 12409, 12788, 12920, 12921, 12949, 13121, 13353, 13596, 13635, 13929, 14023, 14042, 14225, 14226, 14247, 14451, 14499, 14546, 15623, 15809, 15810, 15820, 16202, 16205, 16356, 16455, 16625, 16660, 14249 and 8404, all of the year 1990, as also CWP Nos. 79, 5251, 8560, 11007, 11334, 18031, 18997, 249, 367, 1375, 2470, 2873, 7277, 4006, 2906 and 2886 all of the year 1991 and CWP No. 3313 of 1992, as common question of law and facts is involved in all these writ petitions.
2.
For the purpose of disposal of the above writ petitions, following relevant facts may be noticed:  In the State of Punjab, there was a cadre of Teachers known as Cadre of Master/Mistress.
Their pay scale prior to January 1, 1978, was Rs. 220-500.
In 1978 there was another cadre of Teachers known as Classical and Vernacular Teachers cadre (C and V cadre).
The latter cadre consists of Teachers in the Subjects of Hindi, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Drawing, Art and Craft, Agriculture, P.T.I.
etc.
The pay scale of the C & V cadre was Rs. 170-350.
A Hindi or Punjab Teacher and some other category of Teachers having qualifications of Prabhakar or Giani were given three advance increments on their initial appointments whereas Sanskrit Teacher having Shastri qualification was given five increments on his initial appointment.
3.
With effect from January 1, 1978, the State of Punjab revised the pay scales of cadres of Masters/Mistress from 220-500 to 620-20-700-25-850-30- 1000-40-1200 whereas pay scale of the Teachers of C & V cadre was revised from Rs. 170-350 to Rs. 570-15-600-20-700/25-850/30-1000-40-1080.
The increments which Hindi/Punjabi and Shastri Teachers were enjoying prior to the revision were also kept intact meaning thereby that a Hindi/Punjabi Teacher on January 1, 1978, was to get minimum of Rs. 620/- whereas a Shastri Teacher was to get Rs. 660/- as pay per mensem.
It may be observed here that C & V cadre of Teachers was a lower cadre as compared to the Master/Mistress cadre as the former was a feeder cadre for promotion to the Master/Mistress cadre and 10% posts in the Master/Mistress cadre were to be filled by promotion from C & V cadre.
The Teachers belonging to Master/Mistress cadre made grievance to the State Government that on the revision of the pay scale, the Teacher belonging to a Master/Mistress cadre on January 1, 1978, would get Rs. 620/- pm as pay whereas a Teacher belonging to C & V cadre like Hindi/Punjabi and Shastri Teacher would get the pay of Rs. 620/- and Rs. 660/- pm respectively.
According to them, a Shastri belonging to a lower cadre could not get more pay than a Teacher belonging to Master/Mistress cadre on January 1, 1978.
After consideration of the claim of the persons like the petitioners, the State Government issued instructions on February 18, 1983 (Annexure P-1), which read as under:  ``I am directed to invite a reference to Government of Punjab, Department of Finance, letter No. 30/11/80-FR( ), dated 22nd February, 1980, on the subject noted above and to convey the sanction of the Governor of Punjab to the effect that Masters/Mistress of the Department of Education will be allowed a starting salary of Rs. 660/- pm in the revised scale of Rs. 620-1200 already sanctioned w.e.f.
111978.
2.
Sanction of Governor of Punjab is also accorded to the effect that the pay of those Classical and Vernacular Teachers (Language Teachers) who were in service on 1.12.1977 and who were Giani/Prabhakar and/or Shastri will be fixed in the revised scale at the stage of Rs. 620/- and/or Rs. 660/- as the case may be, if it comes to be fixed at a stage lower than this with effect from 111978 under the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979.''
According to para 1 of the instructions referred to above, on January 1, 1978, a Teacher belonging to Master/Mistress cadre was to get Rs. 660/- in the pay scale of Rs. 620/1200.
4.
The grievances of the petitioners are as follows:  (i) That Teachers who might have to their credit five to ten years of service belonging to Master/Mistress cadre on January 1, 1978, would get Rs. 660/- in the pay scale of Rs. 620-1200 and a person, who is appointed on January 1, 1978, or even later would also get the same pay.
In other words, the service rendered by the persons like the petitioners has been made otiose for the purpose of fixing their pay/scale.
(ii) A Teacher belonging to a lower cadre i.e. C & V cadre like a Shastri Teacher would also get Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978, and he may be having lesser service as compared to Teacher in the Master/Mistress cadre but Teacher belonging to the latter cadre would also get Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978.
(iii) The Teachers belonging to Master/Mistress cadre, who were drawing pay between Rs. 260/- to Rs. 270/- prior to January 1, 1978, their pay was fixed at Rs. 660/-.
In other words, the Teachers, who had put in five years of service on January 1, 1978 their pay was fixed at Rs. 660/-.
By virtue of the instructions dated February 18, 1983, a person who might have been appointed even a day earlier than January 1, 1978, or within one year of January 1, 1978, his pay was also to be fixed at Rs. 660/- w.e.f.
January 1, 1978, and the persons who had more than five years or more service prior to January 1, 1978, were not given any benefit of the letter dated February 18, 1983.
(iv) According to the pay fixation formula appended to Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scale of Pay) Rules, 1979, the pay of certain Teachers was fixed more than Rs. 660/- per mensem on the basis of the pay that they were drawing in the unrevised pay.
The Teachers whose initial pay was to be fixed at Rs. 620/- got the benefit of the instructions of 1983 quoted above to get their pay fixed at Rs. 660/- with the advantage of two increments whereas the other Teachers, whose pay had already been fixed more than Rs. 660/- did not get the benefit of two increments granted by the 1983 instructions.
In support of this contention, reliance was placed on a judgment of the learned Single Judge in Jagdish Chander Walia v. State of Punjab, 1984(1) SLR 441.
5.
Argument in support of the above mentioned grievances was that the action of the respondent-State in not giving benefit of the length of service for fixing the pay scale or giving advantage of length of service in fixing the actual pay in a particular pay scale was violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Further not giving any benefit of 1983 Instructions to those Teachers, who had already got their pay fixed more than Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978 but giving the same benefit to a Teacher whose pay was fixed at Rs. 620/- on January 1, 1978 was again discriminatory and violative of Articles 14/16 of the Constitution of India.
A similar argument was raised that keeping a Shastri Teacher and a Teacher in the Master/Mistress cadre both at Rs. 660/- as the starting salary on January 1, 1978, was discriminatory as Shastri Teacher was in lower cadre as compared to Master/Mistress cadre.
6.
On the other hand, learned counsel for the respondent argued that it is always an incident of revision of pay scales that on the day the new pay scales come into effect, two persons having different length of service may start getting the same pay on that day depending on the little difference in the number of years of service that a particular individual might have put in.
However, if a person has a sufficiently long length of service, his pay would naturally be fixed in the revised pay scale depending upon the pay that was being drawn by that person in the unrevised pay scale and it would be more than the minimum pay in the revised pay scale.
As in the present case, some petitioners got their pay more than Rs. 660/- pm on January 1, 1978.
The revised pay scale of the Teachers belonging to C & V cadre was lower i.e. Rs. 570-1080 as compared to the Master/Mistress cadre which was Rs. 620-1200.
It was only that in cases where Teachers belonging to C & V cadre having Giani or Prabhakar qualifications were given some advance increments that their pay was to be fixed at Rs. 620/- or Rs. 660/- pm depending on the number of increments that they were to be given on the basis of their qualifications.
This was only for such Teachers who had some extra qualifications like Giani/Prabhakar to give them incentive.
So far as 1983 instructions are concerned, the learned counsel submitted that in fact by virtue of these instructions literally the pay scale of Master and Mistress was being revised to start from Rs. 660/- instead of Rs. 620/-.
In effect it was not giving any increments as every Teacher belonging to Master/Mistress cadre was to start at minimum of Rs. 660/-.
7.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, I am of the view that there is no merit in these writ petitions.
Whenever pay scales are revised, it would ipso facto follow that a Teacher who was appointed on the day when the pay scales are revised would get the same pay as a Teacher who might have put in a few years of service in the unrevised pay scale.
That is bound to happen.
On revision of pay scales, whosoever is already working, would naturally get minimum of the revised pay scale and a person who might join that day would also get the same pay.
A person who might have put in quite long service in the unrevised pay scale, his pay would naturally be fixed in the higher pay scale depending upon the formula that may be laid for fixing the pay in the higher pay scale.
Admittedly, some of the petitioners started getting more than Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978 by virtue of the pay that they were drawing in the unrevised pay scale.
Therefore, it cannot be said that there is any discrimination between two set of Teachers on the revision of pay scale on the basis of the length of service.
Moreover, I find that the 1983 Instructions, referred to above, are literally increasing the starting pay from Rs. 620/- to Rs. 660/- and each one belonging to Master/Mistress cadre was to get Rs. 660/- per month without any precondition.
In fact literally the instructions did not mean that the Master/Mistress were getting two advance increments.
This was possibly done to remove any disparity between a Master/Mistress and a Sanskrit Teacher belonging to C & V cadre, whose pay was to be fixed at Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978, in the pay scale of Rs. 570-1080 by giving him three increments.
As observed above, the pay scale of the Master/Mistress cadre came to start at Rs. 660/- and not Rs. 620/- per mensem.
Otherwise, the pay scale of C & V Teachers was lower i.e. Rs. 570-1080 as compared to Rs. 620-1200 of Master/Mistress cadre which literally came to be Rs. 660-1200 on the basis of 1983 instructions.
I do not find that there was any discrimination between two sets of Teachers i.e. C & V cadre and Master/Mistress cadre.
8.
As observed above, I am of the opinion that the starting scale of the Master/Mistress cadre w.e.f.
January 1, 1978, was literally Rs. 660/-.
There was no question of giving any benefit to those who might have got their pay fixed at more than Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978, on the basis of the pay that they were drawing prior to January 1 1978, as literally no increments were being given to those persons whose pay was to be fixed at Rs. 620/- per month as notionally their pay scale was starting at Rs. 660/- per mensem, I do not find that any case of discrimination has been made out.
The authority cited by the learned counsel for the petitioners has no relevance to the points raised in the present writ petition.
9.
Consequently, I find no merit it these writ petitions which are hereby dismissed.
Petitions dismissed.
