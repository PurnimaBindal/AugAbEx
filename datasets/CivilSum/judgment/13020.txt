When this writ petition was filed, in the heading of the petition, at Note No. 3, it was clearly mentioned as follows :-  "Similar case if any : CWP No. 7195 of 2004 in which operation of impugned order has been stayed and next date of hearing is 24.5.2005."
2.
Relying on the aforesaid statement, this Court issued notice of motion.
The respondents have filed written statement in which two preliminary objections have been taken.
Firstly it is stated that petitioners have not come to the Court with clean hands and had made mis-statement of facts with regard to the status of the petitioners.
The petitioners are challenging the order of retrenchment which has been passed after complying with the provisions of Industrial Disputes Act.
Therefore, they have an alternative remedy of approaching the Labour Court.
On the basis of identical preliminary-objections having been raised in CWP No. 7195 of 2004, the same has been dismissed on 252005 and the petitioners therein have been relegated to the remedy under the Industrial Disputes Act.
3.
Faced with this situation, learned counsel for the petitioners argued that the case of the present petitioners is distinguishable from the case of the petitioners in CWP No. 7195 of 2004.
4.
Having made the statement, which we have reproduced above, we are unable to accept the statement made by learned counsel for the petitioners that the present case is distinguishable in any manner from the earlier writ petition.
5.
In view of the above, this writ petition is dismissed in terms of the decision of this Court rendered in CWP No. 7195 of 2004 decided on 252005.
Petition dismissed.
