THB Chalapathi, J - The petitioner is working as Junior Engineer (Mechanical) in the Office of Executive Engineer, P.W.D.
Public Health Division No. 2, Gurgaon.
The State Government while revising the pay scales of the Junior Engineers in 1979 granted the selection grade from 10% to 20% of the cadre of the Junior Engineers working in the Public Works Department.
Accordingly the pay of the petitioner was revised in the selection grade of Rs. 700-1250 on 1721981.
On 141984 the pay of the petitioner was fixed at Rs. 940/- with next date of increment on 141985.
According to the petitioner, the total strength of the Junior Engineers (Mechanical) was 65 upto the year 1977 and the selection grade was given to 20% of the total strength.
Therefore, 13 persons should have been given the selection grade, but only 7 Junior Engineers were granted the selection grade.
He further stated that similarly-situated Junior Engineers in Irrigation Branch filed a writ petition bearing No. CWP 3985 of 1983 and on the basis of the judgment in that case, the Junior Engineers of Irrigation Branch have got their pay revised.
He also stated that there is another writ petition bearing No. CWP 2754 of 1993 which was allowed by this Court and, therefore, the petitioner is seeking a direction to refix his pay in view of the decision in CWP No. 2754 of 1983 decided on 891989 and also refixation of his pay in the selection grade.
2.
In the written statement filed by the defendants it is admitted that the petitioner was working in the time scale of Rs. 200-450 prior to 1979.
It is further stated that there are only 28 sanctioned posts available in the cadre of the Junior Engineers (Mechanical) and therefore only 6 Junior Engineers were to be given selection grade.
Those Junior Engineers who were given selection grade on 141979 were senior to the petitioner.
Therefore, the petitioner was not entitled to selection grade on or before 141979.
The petitioner was given selection grade with effect from 1721981.
It is further admitted that the total strength of the Junior Engineers working in the Department was 65 though only 28 posts of Junior Engineers were sanctioned upto the year 1977.
3.
Annexure P-1 clearly shows that the petitioner has been granted selection grade with effect from 1721981 and his pay has accordingly been fixed in the revised scale of selection grade vide an order 2381988.
But according to the petitioner due to increase in the percentage of selection grade from 10% to 20% in the year 1977, he is entitled to the selection grade with effect from 141979.
But the fact remains that the order granting selection grade confers the benefit on the petitioner.
The petitioner is not entitled to selection grade automatically.
It is for the Government to grant selection grade.
Simply because there was increase in the strength, the petitioner cannot claim to be given selection grade as a matter of right.
Reliance placed by the learned counsel for the petitioner on the judgment of this Court CWP No. 2754 of 1983 dated 8th September, 1989 is misconceived.
In that case, the selection grade was granted to the petitioners therein w.e.f.
Ist April, 1979.
Though the selection grade had been given to them, the same was withdrawn by an order dated 13th June, 1983.
The Court has held that once the benefit has been given w.e.f Ist April, 1979, the same cannot be withdrawn to the detriment of the petitioners and further some of the juniors of the writ petitioners in that writ petition were given selection grade with effect from Ist April, 1979, while the benefit given to the petitioners therein was withdrawn by the letter dated 13th June, 1983.
In those circumstances, this Court held that the said letter dated 1361988 was discriminatory and accordingly declared that the petitioners are entitled to the revised pay scale in the selection grade with effect from Ist April 1979 since the petitioners therein were granted selection grade from that date.
But in the case of the petitioner herein, the selection grade was given to him only w.e.f.
1721981.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to claim that he is entitled to have the selection grade with effect from 141979 and his pay should according to revised.
Further the claim of the petitioner is also to be rejected on the ground of delay and laches.
The writ petition has been filed by the petitioner only in the year 1996.
Though the strength of selection grade posts has been increased from 10% to 20% in the year 1979, the petitioner has been given the selection grade only in December 1981.
The petitioner's pay scale was fixed in the revised scale of pay only in August 1988 (vide order Annexure P-1).
The petitioner kept quiet for nearly 15 years from the date of granting selection grade to him and also for a period of 8 years from the date of office order dated 23rd August, 1988.
The petitioner having accepted the order and failed to seek the relief that he was entitled to selection grade with effect from 141979 within a reasonable period, he disentitled himself from seeking any relief in this writ petition.
I am, therefore, of the opinion that the petition is liable to be dismissed not only on the ground that the petitioner is not entitled to revise pay of the selection grade w.e.f.
Ist April 1979, but also on the ground of delay and laches.
4.
In this view of the matter, I do not find any ground to give any relief as sought for by the petitioner.
The petition is, therefore, dismissed.
No order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
