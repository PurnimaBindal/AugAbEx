VK Bali, J (Oral) - Prayer of the petitioner is for grant of family pension.
Short affidavit Shri ML Agnihotri, Deputy Controller (Finance & Accounts) has been placed on records.
It is stated therein that the mistake earlier made is regretted and the petitioner is eligible for grant of family pension under the Family Pension Scheme, 1964.
It has further been stated that respondents 2 and 4 are being requested to send the case of the petitioner immediately so that it could be finalised and that as soon as the case was received from respondents 2 and 4, it would be sent to the Accountant General, Punjab and would be got finalised at personal level.
In view of the affidavit aforesaid, this petition has become infructuous and is accordingly dismissed.
However, a direction is issued to the respondents to do the entire exercise, as mentioned in the affidavit dated 22.5.1999 and complete the family pension papers of the petitioner as expeditiously as possible and preferably within two months from today.
The pension so fixed including arrears thereof be made over to the petitioner within one month thereafter.
No costs.
Petition dismissed.
