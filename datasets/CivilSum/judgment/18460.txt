HS Bedi, J - CM allowed.
The replication is taken on record.
The petitioner's date of birth has been changed from 10121947 to 10.12.1945 vide order dated 1872003, Annexure P-9, and he has been ordered to be superannuated on that basis.
Several documents have been put on record by the petitioner in the present proceedings to show that in fact his correct date of birth was 10121947.
The stand of the respondents is that the change in date of birth has been made on the basis of the record from the Army where the petitioner had been earlier employed.
However, keeping in view the fact that order, Annexure P-9, prejudicially affecting the petitioner has been made without notice to him, we quash the order aforesaid but give liberty to the respondents to pass a fresh order in accordance with law after giving the petitioner an opportunity of hearing.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
