PK Mohanty, J - The writ petitioner has prayed for a direction to the opposite parties 1 to 3 to approve his appointment in the post of Hindi Teacher of the school in deleting the name of opposite party No. 5 and consequently to direct the opposite parties 1 and 2 to sanction his salary with usual DA, A.DA and arrear dues.
2.
The short fact of the petitioner's case is that he was appointed as a Hindi Teacher in Binoba JP Ucha Bidyapitha, Sirakuli in the district of Mayurbhanj by Resolution No. 65 dated 12-3-1968 of the Managing Committee of the School, a copy of which is Annexure 1.
The petitioner was issued with the appointment letter under Memo No. 171 dated 14-3-1988 and he submitted his joining report on the same day at 10 am This post of Hindi Teacher, according to the petitioner, was initially held by opposite party No. 5-Amit Kumar Singh and on his resignation, the post having fallen vacant, he was appointed.
The petitioner claims that his name has been mentioned by the Headmaster of the school in the renewal recognition form sent to opposite parties 1 and 2, but the same has not been sanctioned and approved.
The petitioner is a Graduate from Ranchi University having Hindi as one of his optional subjects and has also passed "Rastriya Bhasa Kobidha" examination and as such he is qualified to hold the post.
According to the petitioner, the school in question received grant-in-aid on 13-1-1987, as would be revealed from letter No. 5398 dated 21-1-1984 and the petitioner has been appointed much earlier to the School becoming an aided institution by the Managing Committee, but in spite of the fact that he was legally and validly appointed and there was no objection from opp.
party No. 5, who had resigned from the post, opposite parties 2 and 3 are unnecessarily not approving his appointment and making the necessary payments.
3.
The opposite party No. 3 Inspector of Schools, Baripada Education Circle through the Assistant Inspector of Schools has filed a comprehensive counter-affidavit refusing (refuting ?)
the claim of the petitioner.
It is the case of opposite party No. 3 that he was not aware of the appointment of the petitioner till 24-2-1992 and only on 24-2-1992, opposite party No. 4 - Headmaster of the School intimated about the appointment of the petitioner by his letter, a copy of which is Annexure A-3 to the counter-affidavit.
It is further averred that neither the Headmaster intimated the fact of appointment of the petitioner nor the petitioner's name was shown in the recognition form sent in the year 1991-92 as claimed by the petitioner.
A copy of the application form for recognition of the year 1991-92 has been filed and Annexure D-3 to emphasise that the petitioner's name does not find place therein.
The Inspector of Schools asserts that the endorsement of the Headmaster in Column 14 of Annexure 4 that the post was approved vide letter No. 5948 dated 26-2-1989 of the Circle Inspector of Schools, Baripada is not correct, inasmuch as the office order dated 26-2-1961 approving some posts in the petitioner's school has been annexed as Annexure C-2 to show that the petitioner's name did not find place in the said letter as asserted by the petitioner.
According to the Inspector of Schools, the school in question became an aided school with effect from 1-3-1987 and as such, the alleged appointment of the petitioner having been made in the year 1968, the appointment was not in order.
It is asserted in the counter-affidavit that the post of Hindi Teacher was sanctioned under the Central Plan Scheme with effect from 1-4-1991 in the Directorate of Secondary Education Letter No. 21716 dated 28-5-1992 in favour of opposite party No., a copy of which is Annexure 3 and as such, there was no sanctioned post of Hindi Teacher in the petitioner's school till 31-3-1991 and therefore the appointment of the petitioner to a non-existent post could not arise in the year 1988.
4.
In view of the pleadings of the parties and the submissions made at the bar, the question that needs consideration is, as to whether the petitioner was validly and legally appointed to the post of Hindi Teacher on 12-3-1988 and he joined on 14-3-1988 as against a sanctioned post.
The further question that needs consideration is, as to whether the petitioner's appointment was to an existing vacancy on the resignation of opposite party No. 5 as alleged.
5.
The claim of the petitioner that Binoba JP Ucha Bidyapitha, Sirakuli became a recognised and aided educational institution on and from 23-3-1989, cannot be accepted in absence of any materials to that effect and in the face of the specific assertion of the Inspector of Schools that the school was admitted to the grant-in-aid with effect from 1-3-1987.
The assertion of the petitioner that he was appointed against the vacancy caused due to the resignation of opposite party No. 5, who was holding the sanctioned post of Hindi Teacher on 12-3-1988, also cannot be accepted since the post of Hindi Teacher in the Binoba JP Ucha Bidyapitha, Sirakuli along with other 494 schools was created and sanctioned under a Centrally Planned Scheme, intimated by letter dated 28-5-1992 of the Directorate of Secondary Education (Annexure D).
It is further apparent from Annexure D/3 that the appointment of Amrit Kumar Singh (opposite party No. 5) was approved as against the sanctioned post of Hindi Teacher in the school in question.
It has also not been brought on record, as to whether there was any sanctioned post of Hindi Teacher in the school in question earlier to 31-3-1991 and, as to whether the appointment of the petitioner was made in due process and in accordance with the Orissa Education (Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers and Members of the Staff of Aided Educational Institutions) Rules, 1974, the school having become a Recognised Aided Educational Institution with effect from 1-3-1987.
The petitioner has placed no material to show that his appointment was duly intimated to the appropriate authority for approval at any point of time earlier to 24-4-1992 (Annexure A-3) inasmuch as his stand that in the application for renewal of recognition of the school in the prescribed form in the year 1991-92, his name appears as a Hindi Teacher, is relied (belied ?)
by Annexure B/3 the xerox copy of the purported application form to the counter filed by the Inspector of Schools.
The Management of School has not been made a party, however, the Headmaster-cum-Secretary has been impleaded as opposite party No. 4, who has not chosen to appear and file its return, to place on record the factual position of the case.
6.
In that view of the matter, we do not find any merit in the writ application and accordingly, it is rejected.
However, there shall be no order as to cost.
Application dismissed.
