Mehtab S Gill, J - The petitioner has prayed for the issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to grant him the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600 granted to the post of Mechanic Refrigerator on the principle of "Equal pay for equal work" from the date he joined the Animal Husbandry Department as Mechanic Refrigerator with effect from June 25, 1987.
2.
The petitioner has averred that he was appointed as Mechanic Refrigerator through Employment Exchange on ad hoc basis vide order date June 19, 1987 (Annexure P-1).
In the appointment letter dated June 19, 1987 (Annexure P-1), the petitioner was erroneously placed in the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 whereas the pay scale of the post of Mechanic Refrigerator prevailing at the time was Rs. 1400-2600.
The services of the petitioner were regularised vide order dated January 20, 1992 (Annexure P-2).
The services of the petitioner were regularised with effect from December 31, 1990 and he was placed on probation for a period of two years.
The petitioner was granted pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040.
3.
It has been further averred that three sanctioned posts of Mechanic Refrigerator in the Department of Animal Husbandry, Haryana existed.
The two other persons who were working as Mechanic Refrigerator were, namely Shri Gurdev Singh, who has since retired and Shri Kesho Ram Gupta, who has been promoted to the post of Foreman.
These two persons were drawing the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600 which is the pay scale attached to the post of Mechanic Refrigerator.
In the notifications which are attached as Annexure P-3 and P-4 with the writ petition, the pay scale of the Mechanic Refrigerator was revised from Rs. 200-450 to Rs. 525-1050 and further this pay scale was revised to Rs. 1400-2600.
All these three posts of Mechanic Refrigerator in the Animal Husbandry Department, Haryana carry equal responsibilities.
These posts are also inter-transferable and inter-changeable.
After the retirement of Shri Gurdev Singh, the petitioner was transferred to Vaccine Institute, Hisar where Shri Gurdev Singh was working as Mechanic Refrigerator.
A copy of the transfer order dated June 18, 1993 is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P/4-A  4.
Representations were made by the petitioner mentioning therein that he be also placed in the same pay scale as the other two persons, namely, Sarv Shri Gurdev Singh and Kesho Ram Gupta were drawing the same at that time.
These representations are dated July 3, 1990, March 18, 1992 and January 4, 1993, which are annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-5, P-6 and P-7 respectively.
A legal notice dated February 15, 1993, Annexure P-8, was served upon the respondents wherein the petitioner requested that the discrimination in the matter of pay scales be removed and he be granted the regular pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600 attached to the post of Mechanic Refrigerator but to no effect.
5.
Written statement was filed by the respondents.
6.
I have heard the learned Counsel for the petitioner and the respondents.
7.
Learned Counsel for the petitioner has stated that it is a clear violation of the principle of "Equal pay for equal work" and also violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
He has further stated that the posts of Mechanic Refrigerator carry equal responsibility and equal work and they being inter-changeable and transferable, this also shows that equal work has got to be done by all the Mechanic Refrigerator.
Thus, the principle of "equal pay for equal work" should also apply.
8.
Learned Counsel for the respondents has stated that the two posts of Mechanic Refrigerator were sanctioned by the Government vide order dated March 6, 1986 in the pay scale of Rs. 420-700 and later on, this was revised to Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from January 1, 1986.
The scheme for the replacement of chilled semen with frozen semen technology was of a differentiation in the pay scale.
Two temporary posts under the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 were sanctioned under the scheme for the replacement of chilled semen and two regular/permanent posts were sanctioned in the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600.
The petitioner was working against one of the temporary posts, as referred to above.
A copy of the Haryana Government Gazette Notification dated April 29, 1987 is annexed with the written statement as Annexure R-2.
9.
In reply to para 6 of the petition, the respondents have stated that the Director, Animal Husbandry (respondent No. 3) has already taken up the issue of rationalisation of the pay scale of Mechanic Refrigerator.
The Government is already seized of the matter and is assimilating information pertaining to the varied pay scales of Mechanic Refrigerator existing in different departments.
As soon as the Government takes decision in this regard, the petitioner's claim to the scale of Rs. 1400-2600 will be automatically covered.
10.
The argument put forward by the learned Counsel for the respondents could not give satisfactory reply as the position depicted in Appendix-A appended to the Haryana Veterinary Headquarter and Field (Group-C) Services Rules, 1999 notified by the Government on September 29, 1999 is altogether different.
In the Appendix-A, a copy of which is attached as Annexure A-1 to Civil Misc.
Application No. 4944 of 2000, the post of Mechanic Refrigerator is shown at Serial No. 14 under the heading `Technical Posts'.
As per the said entry, there are two permanent posts of Mechanic Refrigerator and there is one temporary post of Mechanic Refrigerator and the total number of posts have been shown to be three.
The pay scale shown for both the permanent and temporary posts of Mechanic Refrigerator therein is Rs. 5000-150-7100-EB-175- 7850.
11.
Since the nature of work is the same and also the fact that the post of Mechanic Refrigerator is a transferable one, I do not see any reason as to why the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600 (unrevised) and subsequently revised to Rs. 5000-7850 has not been granted to the petitioner.
12.
This writ petition is, accordingly, allowed and the respondents are directed to grant the aforesaid pay scale to the petitioner from the date of filing of this writ petition.
13.
So far as the question of grant of interest at the rate of 25% per annum, as claimed in this petition, is concerned, I do not find any merit in granting the same as the matter was under the active consideration of the State Government as borne out from the return filed by the respondents.
Petition allowed.
