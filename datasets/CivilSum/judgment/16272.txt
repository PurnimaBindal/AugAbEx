VK Jhanji, J - This judgment will dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 2291 and 2904 of 1981.
2.
Petitioners were originally appointed as Clerks by respondent No. 3 on 18.12.1963, 1861964, 21.8.1969, 1851970, 3131970, 2061967, 21.8.1969 and 951969 respectively.
The next higher post from the post of Clerk is that of Assistant/Inspector.
The method and mode of recruitment is laid down in Rule 52 of the Punjab Trust Services (Recruitment and Conditions of Service0 Rules, 1978 (hereinafter referred to as the Rules).
The said rule reads as under :-  "5.
METHOD OF RECRUITMENT : (1) Recruitment to various categories of posts in a Service at the time of its initial constitution shall be made by the appointing authority by absorption of persons already holding corresponding posts in the service of a Trust provided they are found fit by an authority appointed by the Government in this behalf for becoming members of the Service after taking into consideration their qualifications and service record.
(2) After filling the vacancies under sub-rule (1) the remaining vacancy or vacancies which may occur thereafter shall be filled up in following manner, namely  (i) Fifty per cent of the vacancies by direct recruitment; and (ii) Fifty per cent of the vacancies by promotion selection : Provided that if no suitable candidate is available by direct recruitment or by promotion, the vacancies may be filled up by transfer or on' deputation.
Provided further that if no qualifications have been laid down in Appendix 'B' for any post to be filled up by promotion, that post shall be filled up by direct recruitment."
3.
A bare reading of the above rule would show that recruitment to various posts including those of Assistants/Inspectors has to be made on 50:50 basis i.e. 50 per cent by way of direct recruitment and 50 per cent by promotion.
On 811979 the respondents advertised 20 posts of Assistants/Inspectors.
When the respondents proceeded in filling up the vacancies by direct recruitment, the petitioners made representations to the effect that recruitment to the post of Assistant/Inspector could be made only on 50:50 basis.
After considering the representations of the petitioners, the respondents promoted the petitioners to the post of Assistant/Inspector on the following dates :-  Petitioner No. 1 1974 Petitioner No. 2 4.10.1979 Petitioner No. 3 25.8.1980 Petitioner No. 4 25.8.1980 Petitioner No. 5 25.8.1980 Petitioner No. 6 28.9.1979 Petitioner No. 7 12.10.1979 Petitioner No. 8 12.10.1979 4.
On 2251981 vide Annexure P-3, the petitioners were ordered to be reverted as Clerks.
Petitioners have challenged the order, Annexure P-3 by way of present writ petition.
5.
Written statement has been filed on behalf of respondent Nos. 1 and 2 in Civil Writ Petition No. 2291 of 1981 whereas no written statement has been filed in Civil Writ Petition No. 2904 of 1981.
6.
Mr. Balram Gupta, Advocate, Counsel for the petitioners has submitted that when the petitioners were reverted, no opportunity of hearing was given to them.
He has further submitted that direct recruits and the promotees had to be promoted on 50:50 basis and the same ratio has to be followed at the time of reversion.
he has also contended that services of none of the direct recruits have been terminated.
7.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties, I find that this petition has to succeed.
It is not disputed that the petitioners are continuing as Assistants as the order of reversion, Annexure P-3 was stayed in Civil Writ Petition No. 2291 of 1981 on 2951981 by this Court.
In Civil Writ Petition No. 2904 of 1981, at the time of motion hearing, order of reversion was not stayed.
It has also not been disputed by the respondents that at the time when petitioners were reverted, direct recruits were allowed to continue in service whereas the petitioners who had been promoted as Assistants have been reverted.
As per rule 52 of the Rules, direct recruits and the promotees have to be appointed on 50:50 basis whereas at the time when the petitioners were reverted, direct recruits were allowed to hold the post and the petitioners were reverted back meaning thereby that the direct recruits were allowed to hold posts more than their quota.
This is clearly in contravention of Rule 52 of the Rules and this action cannot be sustained in law.
As already stated, the reversion of the petitioners was stayed by this Court on 2951981.
On the last date of hearing, it was pointed out that during this period, number of posts of Assistants had become available and filled up by promotion from amongst persons who were junior to the petitioners.
8.
Mr. Rajiv Raina, AAG Punjab had sought time to confirm the fact whether some posts of Assistants had become available and filled up by promotion from amongst person junior to the petitioners.
He states that he is not in a position to inform as to the correct position because despite a number a letter having been written by him to respondent Nos. 1 and 2, no communication has so far been received from them.
9.
Petitioners were promoted as Assistants on regular basis but they were reverted back without any opportunity of being heard having been afforded to them.
Petitioners who were promoted on regular basis, could not be reverted without issuing a show cause notice or without holding any enquiry against them.
Thus, on this score also, the action of the respondents reverting the petitioners, cannot be sustained.
10.
Mr. Rajiv Raina states that as far as petitioner No. 1 is concerned, this writ petition has become infructuous because the order of reversion in his case has since been cancelled.
This fact is not disputed by learned counsel for the petitioners.
11.
In this view of the matter, this writ petition is allowed with costs.
Order, Annexure P-3 is liable to be quashed and the same is hereby quashed.
Petitioners shall be entitled to all the consequential benefits from the date they were promoted as Assistants.
Respondent No. 3 shall pay costs which are quantified at Rs. 1,000/-.
Petition allowed.
