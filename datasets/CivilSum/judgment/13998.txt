Jasbir Singh, J (Oral) - Petitioner has filed this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India with a prayer that a writ of certiorari be issued to quash order No. PF/HSS 5303 dated 991986 (Annexure P/10), vide which promotion to the post of Assistant was declined to him.
He has further prayed that a writ of mandamus be issued to respondent No. 2 to promote him from the date when his junior Shri Kartar Singh was promoted i.e. w.e.f.
1571983.
2.
Admitted facts of the writ petition are that the petitioner was appointed as a Clerk in the office of respondent No. 2 on 1091955.
His name figured at serial No. 18 in the seniority list of Clerks, as circulated by the office of respondent No. 2.
It is the case of the petitioner that his case for confirmation was not considered, though persons, who were appointed after him, were confirmed w.e.f.
2041962.
One permanent post was kept vacant so that the petitioner could be confirmed, as and when order is received from the competent authority.
Petitioner continued to file representations, seeking his confirmation from the date when his juniors were confirmed.
In the meantime, seniority list of the Clerks was again circulated in the year 1986 (Annexure P/9).
Name of the petitioner was not shown in that seniority list.
3.
Vide order dated 2231986, representation moved by the petitioner was accepted and he was deemed to have been confirmed as a Clerk w.e.f.
131962.
4.
Thereafter the petitioner agitated the matter regarding his seniority before competent authority and ultimately, vide order dated 11121985 (Annexure P/6), it was ordered by respondent No. 2 that name of the petitioner be shown at serial No. 84-A in the seniority list and he be shown senior to Shri Kartar Singh.
5.
Against order Annexure P/6, objections were invited, which were disposed of by the competent authority, vide order dated 1351986 and it was ordered that there was no necessity to reopen the seniority matter of the Clerks and it was further directed that the name of the petitioner be shown at serial No. 84-A in the final seniority list circulated on 761983.
6.
In the meantime, Shri Kartar Singh, who was junior to the petitioner, as per final seniority list, was promoted to the next post of Assistant, vide order dated 1571983.
7.
After rectification of his seniority, petitioner claimed promotion to the post of Assistant from the date when Shri Kartar Singh, who was junior to him, was promoted to that post.
Vide order dated 991986 (Annexure P/10), that relief was declined to him on the ground that in the meantime, Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984 (in short the rules) were made effective w.e.f.
1241984 and since, as per those rules, he has not passed the prescribed test, he cannot be promoted to the next post.
The petitioner has challenged this order passed, by filing the present writ petition.
8.
It has been contended by counsel for the petitioner that it was ordered by the competent authority, vide order dated 1351986 (Annexure P.7) that he be shown at serial No. 84-A in the final seniority list circulated on 761983.
By referring to that order, counsel further contends that in the year 1983, when his junior were promoted, he was eligible for promotion, however, that relief was declined to him, because of mistake on the part of the department.
Once this seniority has been rectified, he is entitled to promotion from the date when his juniors were promoted.
He has further stated that in the year 1983, it was not incumbent upon the petitioner to pass any test because Rules, which were made applicable in the year 1984, cannot be made basis for declining any promotion to him.
By referring to above mentioned argument, counsel prays that order Annexure P/10 be quashed and the respondents be directed to promote the petitioner from the date when his junior Shri Kartar Singh was promoted.
9.
This prayer has vehemently been opposed by Mr. Gurinder Singh Bhandari, DAG Punjab appearing on behalf of the State.
He has stated that the matter regarding seniority inter-se between the Clerks, qua the petitioner was finalised by the competent authority only in the year 1986 and as such, petitioner became eligible for promotion in that year only and since he has not passed any test, as per the rules, he could not be promoted at that time.
He prays that the writ petition, being devoid of any force, be dismissed.
10.
After hearing counsel for the parties, this Court is of the opinion that vide order Annexure P/10, relief was wrongly declined to the petitioner.
Final seniority list was circulated by the office of respondent No. 2 on 761983.
Petitioner made a representation against wrong fixation of his seniority.
Matter was pending and ultimately it was decided by the competent authority on 1351996, vide which it was directed that his seniority be fixed at serial No. 48-A in the final seniority list, circulated on 761983.
This clearly means that the order passed in the year 1986 would relate back to the circulation of the final seniority list in the year 1983.
This order has since become final between the parties and in view of the order passed in the year 1986, petitioner, admittedly was senior to Shri Kartar Singh, who was promoted on 1571983.
He being senior, was definitely entitled to get promotion w.e.f.
the date when his juniors were promoted.
11.
Counsel for the respondents has not indicated any other dis- qualification, on the basis of which, he could not have been promoted when Shri Kartar Singh etc., juniors to the petitioner, were promoted.
Only contention raised is that since the matter regarding seniority list was finalised in the year 1986 and in the meantime, rules had come into existence and since the petitioner has not passed the requisite test, he was not eligible for promotion.
12.
This Court feels that this argument is devoid of any force whatsoever.
As per the admitted facts, position is to be seen as on the date when Shri Kartar Singh was promoted on 1571983.
At that time, there was no condition of passing any test, as the rules were introduced, subsequently in the year 1984.
13.
Counsel for the petitioner has placed on record a copy of an order dated 1121987, vide which petitioner was promoted provisionally to the post of Assistant, subject to the decision of this writ petition.
Dispute, now only remains for the period from 1571983 to 1121987.
It has also been brought to the notice of this Court that the petitioner has since retired from the service.
14.
Keeping in view facts and circumstances of this case, as found mentioned above, this writ petition is allowed, order dated 16.9.1986 Annexure P/10 is quashed and respondents are directed to promote the petitioner from the date when his junior Shri Kartar Singh was promoted.
It is further directed that the petitioner shall be entitled for all consequential benefits, as may be available to him, as per law.
The needful be done within four months from the date of receipt of a copy of the order passed.
Petition allowed.
