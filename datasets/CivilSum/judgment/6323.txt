GS Singhvi, J - The main issue raised in these petitions relates to the interpretation of the circulars dated 1121988, 981989, 191989 and the notification dated 1721989 issued by the Government of Punjab.
In view of the similarity of the prayers made in all the petitions they are being decided by a common order.
2.
Brief facts  C.W.P.
No. 5692 of 1990  The petitioners Ramesh Kumari and others are working as Teachers/Masters in various schools under the Education Department of Government of Punjab.
They are aggrieved by the circular dated 191989 by which the Government of Punjab issued certain clarifications regarding the implementation of the instructions contained in the circular dated 1121988.
The petitioners say that by refusing to count the ad hoc service rendered by them prior to regularisation of their services, the respondents have discriminated them inasmuch as they will be deprived of the benefit of proficiency step up on completion of 8 and 18 years of service.
Similarly, the decision of the Government not to count service rendered by them on higher post will result in double jeopardy on reversion from the higher post because they will lose the benefit already earned by promotion to the higher post and they will also be deprived of the benefit of the instructions contained in the circular dated 1121988.
The petitioners have prayed that the instructions contained in the circular dated 191989 be quashed and the respondents be directed to grant them proficiency step up(s) on the basis of the circular dated 1121988 and they be also restrained from making recovery from the pay of the petitioners on the basis of the circular dated 191989.
3.
In the reply filed by the respondents, it has been pleaded that the clarifications contained in the circular dated 191989 became necessary in order to remove the doubts entertained by the competent authorities.
It has also been pleaded that if a person has been promoted to the higher post and he/she is reverted to the lower post after some time, the service rendered on the higher post cannot be counted for the purpose of extending the benefit specified in circular dated 1121988.
The respondents have pleaded that the petitioners cannot retain the benefits unlawfully earned by them on the basis of mistake committed by the departmental authorities by granting higher monetary benefits to them.

Karnail Singh etc. are working as Language Teachers/Masters/DPE in different Government Schools.
On the basis of the instructions issued by the Government of Punjab vide circular dated 23.7.1957 they were given the benefit of higher pay scales in view of their higher qualifications.
In the year 1988, the Government of Punjab implemented the recommendations of the Third Pay Commission and framed the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay Scales) Rules, 1988.
Subsequently, vide notification dated 1721989 pay scales of the teaching staff of the Education Department were further revised and three grade structure was introduced and thereby the teachers belonging to different cadres became entitled to the grant of Senior Scale/Selection Grade on completion of 8/18 years of service.
At the same time, the Government of Punjab issued circular dated 1121988 as per the decision of the President of India for grant to proficiency step up(s) on completion of 8/18 years of service.
The petitioners say that in view of the government notification dated February 17,1989 and the circular dated 1121988 they became eligible for grant of Senior Scale/Selection Grade and proficiency step-up(s), but that benefit was not extended to them and on the basis of the subsequent clarifications issued vice circular dated 191989, the respondents have declined to extend the benefit of Senior Scale/Selection Grade and proficiency step-up(s) to the petitioners.
The petitioners have pleaded that the denial of proficiency step-up to an employee merely because his/her promotion is wholly arbitrary and irrational.
Similarly denial of proficiency step-up to an employee who has refused promotion to the higher post is without any reason or rhyme.
The petitioners have also pleaded that the instructions contained in the circular dated 191989 could not be given retrospective effect so as to deprive the petitioners of the right which they acquired on the basis of the instructions dated 1121988 and the notification dated 1721989.
5.
The respondents have objected to the maintainability of the writ petition by stating that the petitioners have not availed of the departmental remedies.
On merits, they have pleaded that in terms of the instructions contained in the circular dated 191989 the benefit of Senior Scale/Selection Grade cannot be extended to the petitioners by counting their total service.

No. 12134 of 1990  7.
The petitioners Ramesh Chander and others joined service as Teachers in various Government Schools in the State of Punjab.
They were given higher pay scales after acquiring higher qualifications in view of the Punjab Government circular dated 23.7.1957.
The petitioners are aggrieved by the circular dated 191989 whereby those who have been awarded higher pay scales on the basis of the higher qualifications have been denied the benefit of proficiency step up(s).
Similarly, those who have gained one or more increments due to fixation of their pay in the higher scale/selection grade have been denied the benefit.
The benefit of proficiency step up has also been denied to those who have been promoted to the next higher post and even to those who have refused to avail the promotion.
The contention of the petitioners is that the clarifications issued by the Government vide Annexure P4 dated 191989 are against the very spirit of the policy decision of the Government to grant benefit of proficiency step up, namely, to give incentive to the employees whose work and conduct is satisfactory.
The petitioners have pleaded that there can be no justification to deny the benefit of proficiency step up to those who may have been given higher pay scale on account of their higher qualifications or those who may have received promotion in the normal course.
Likewise, the persons who have refused to avail promotion cannot be denied the benefit of proficiency step up.
The implementation of the circular dated 191989 has also been questioned on the ground that the same cannot operate retrospectively.

18.
From the above narration of facts it transpires that the principal grievance of the petitioners is against the denial of proficiency step-up in terms of the amendment dated 1121988 and failure of the respondents to give the benefit of Senior Scale/Selection Grade to the teachers on completion of 8/18 years service.
The basis of the claim made by the petitioners is that the respondents are bound to count their total period of service for the purpose of grant of proficiency step-up and Senior Scale/Selection Grade.
The respondents have relied on the clarification issued by the Government on 191989 in support of their plea that the petitioners are not entitled to the aforesaid benefit.
In some of the cases, the respondents have raised an objection that the petitioners did not represent to the Government before approaching this Court for grant of relief in terms of the amendment dated 1121988.
To me, it appears that the respondents have not properly examined the claim of the petitioners in the light of the decisions rendered by this Court and the Supreme Court on the issue of grant of proficiency step-up and for grant of benefit of Senior Scale/Selection Grade.
By and large it has been accepted that ad hoc service rendered by an employee should be counted for the purpose of grant of proficiency step-up and Senior Scale/Selection Grade.
However, in the cases of the petitioners no definite finding can be recorded about the nature of the service rendered by them prior to December 1, 1988.
Therefore, it would be appropriate to direct the competent departmental authorities to examine the claims of the petitioners for grant of benefits claimed by them in the writ petitions in the light of the service rendered by them and the decisions of this Court as well as the Supreme Court and decide the same by passing a speaking order on the issue of entitlement or otherwise of the benefit of proficiency stepup and Senior Scale/Selection Grade.
19.
Consequently these writ petitions are disposed of with the following directions :-  (i) The competent departmental authorities are directed to give notice to each of the petitioners regarding his/her claim for grant of proficiency stepup and/or Senior Scale/Selection Grade calling upon him/her to submit a detailed representation giving full particulars of his/her service.
If the concerned employee submits such representation then the same be examined by the competent authority in the light of the rules framed by the Government as well as the instructions issued by it and then a reasoned order be passed and communicated to the teacher concerned about his/her right to get proficiency step-up and/or Senior Scale/Selection Grade.
(ii) This exercise shall be undertaken and completed by the respondents within a period of six months of the receipt of a certified copy of this order by the Secretary to Government, Punjab, Department of Education; Director, Public Instructions (Schools), Punjab and Director, Public Instructions (Primary), Punjab.
(iii) If any of the petitioners feels aggrieved by the decision taken in compliance of this order, he/she shall be entitled to institute fresh petition for remedy of his/her grievance.
Orders accordingly.
