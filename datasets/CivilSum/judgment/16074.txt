VK Jhanji, J. (Oral) - In this petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, petitioner is praying for a writ in the nature of certiorari for quashing Annexure P-12 dated 331992 whereby the University has rejected the representation of the petitioner for approving appointment as Lecturer in Physics.
2.
As per case set out in the petition, the petitioner was appointed as Lecturer in Physics by respondent No. 3-D.A.V.
College, Chandigarh (in short, the College).
This was a temporary appointment w.e.f.
3081991 to 10.3.1992.
The college so sought approval of the appointment of petitioner from the university.
This request of the College was declined by the University vide its letter dated 111992 on the ground that the petitioner does not fulfil the requisite qualifications.
Petitioner thereafter made a representation to the University.
The University did not decide the said representation.
Petitioner approached this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 1314 of 1992.
This Court finally on 28.1.1992, directed the University to decide the representation of the petitioner after giving him an opportunity of hearing.
Vide order dated 331992 (Annexure P-12), the representation of the petitioner was rejected by the Registrar, Panjab University Chandigarh, on the ground that such candidates who have qualified JRF test conducted by the UGC/CSIR from December 1989 onwards are eligible for appointment as Lecturer, unless they are exempted from passing the said test in terms of UGC's letter dated 30.1.1990.
The petitioner has now challenged the order, Annexure P-12, dated 331992, here in this writ petition.
3.
Written statement has been filed only on behalf of respondent No. 3.
The University has chosen not to file written statement.
According to written statement of respondent No. 3, the case of the petitioner was sent for approval to the University, but the University found the petitioner, being not qualified and, therefore, respondent No. 3 had no alternative but to terminate the appointment of the petitioner before expiry of due date.
4.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has contended that the grounds on which approval of appointment of the petitioner was declined, cannot be sustained for the reason that the petitioner had passed JRF under the CSIR in December, 1986, and JRF under UGC in August, 1986.
Counsel has further contended that the petitioner was eligible for being appointed to the post of lecturer.
5.
Learned counsel for respondent No. 3 has submitted that the present writ petition has become infructuous as the petitioner was appointed merely for 6-1/2 months and his appointment was purely temporary.
Since the University had not approved his appointment, the College had no alternative, but to relieve the petitioner.
6.
Having heard the learned counsel for the parties, I am of the view that the ground on which approval was not accorded, cannot be sustained for the reason that circular letter No. F/4-12/86 (NET) dated 30.1.1990 (Annexure P-5) does not qualify that only those candidates who had passed JRF under the CSIR and UGC after 1989 would be eligible.
Petitioners had also sought clarification from the UGC on letter dated 30.1.1990.
In response to the query made by the petitioner, the UGC had informed vide letter dated 21/22.1.1992 that since petitioner has qualified his UGC/CSIR examination, he is eligible for the post of lecturer.
It further finds mention in the letter that there is no time limit for JRF passed candidates and they are exempted from re-appearing in this test for all time to come.
In view of circular letter dated 30.1.1990 and letter of the University dated 21/22.1.1992, there is no manner of doubt that the petitioner who had passed the JRF test under the CSIR and UGC in December, 1986 and August, 1986, respectively was eligible for the post of lecturer.
Petitioner had also completed his Ph.D on 21101991 and under circular letter dated 30.1.1990 (Annexure P-5), all those candidates who were awarded Ph.D upto December 1992, are also eligible.
However, the petitioner has not placed it on record or made any averment as to when the PhD degree was awarded to the petitioner.
7.
Consequently, order dated 331992 (Annexure P-12) is quashed and it is declared that the petitioner was eligible to the post of Lecturer in Physics.
Except giving this direction, no other relief can be given to the petitioner for the reason that the College was not at fault and moreover, the appointment was purely temporary.
The writ petition stands disposed of in these terms.
Petition disposed of.
