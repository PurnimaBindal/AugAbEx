Swatanter Kumar, J - By this order we dispose of above mentioned two writ petitions as common question of law and fact arise for consideration in both petitions.
2.
The petitioners in both these writ petitions were appointed from 1981 to 1986 in the department of P.W.D.
Public Health, Haryana.
They joined their service at Gurgaon on different dates during that period.
The posts were treated as technical posts and the qualification for the posts was matric with ITI The petitioners were appointed on work-charge basis.
However, services of all the petitioners were regularised on different dates subsequent thereto.
The details of their joining and regularisation of service have been given in Annexure P/1 to the writ petition.
The pay scales were revised vide notification dated 3031982 w.e.f.
1.4.1979 to remove the anomaly.
Vide order dated 3071978 the persons similarly situated like the petitioners and who were holding technical posts were getting revised pay scales which were allowed.
The petitioners rely upon different writ petitions and judgment of the Court vide which relief was granted to the petitioners therein quite sometime back.
On or about 28.8.1990 the petitioners made a representation to the authorities to remove the anomaly and to grant them pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040/- w.e.f.
111986 instead of Rs. 950-1500/- granted to them.
On the recommendation of the Anomaly Committee, the pay of the petitioner was revised to Rs. 1200-2040/- w.e.f.
151990 and the said scale of Rs. 1200-2040/- was given to the petitioner with effect from that date.
Now the petitioners are claiming the said scale w.e.f.
111986.
3.
In our view the petitioners are not entitled to the relief claimed for in these writ petitions, amongst others for the reasons that the present petition suffers from infirmity of delay and latches.
The petitioners were granted the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040/- after the recommendation of the Anomaly Committee vide letter dated 2381990 w.e.f.
151990.
No reason, whatsoever, has been stated in the petition as to why the petitioners opted to wait such a long period of 13 years before filing the petition.
Unexplained delay of such a considerable time would vest the petitioners with the plea of laches.
We find no justification, whatsoever, to interfere in this writ petition.
4.
Secondly, the petitioners accepted the order dated 2381990 for all this period, received benefits thereunder without protest and demur.
Even by their own conduct, the petitioners would be debarred from claiming such a relief and particularly even the plea of limitation would be an impediment in the way of the petitioners.
In the writ jurisdiction, the Court would normally not tilt towards the plea of limitation, but such a law cannot be given a complete go by particularly in view of the facts and circumstances of the cases.
We may also notice that the writ petitions cited by the petitioners relate to Haryana State Electricity Board which is an independent body.
As back as on 3031982 on the recommendations of the Anomaly Committee, pay scales were revised and common pay scales were established.
Thus, petitioners were put at notice as back as in the year 1982.
Even today there is no challenge in the writ petitions to the said circular.
5.
For the reasons afore-stated, we find no merit in these writ petitions and the same are dismissed in limine.
Petitions dismissed.
