Sat Pal, J - This petition has been filed under Article 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India and in this petition, the petitioner has prayed that the respondents be directed to grant to the petitioner benefit of the period of service rendered by him in Military for the purpose of increment, seniority and pension.
2.
During the pendency of the writ petition, the petitioner filed an application bearing CM No. 24512 of 1997 under Section 151, CPC and in this application, it was stated that the point raised in this petition is squarely covered by a Full Bench judgment of this court in Jang Singh and others v. State of Punjab etc., 1997(3) RSJ 464 : 1997(4) SCT 313(P&H)(FB).
It was, therefore, prayed that the writ petition be listed for an actual date.
Notice of this application was issued to AG Punjab.
3.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties.
I find that the point raised in this petition is in fact covered by the Full Bench judgment of this Court in the case of Jang Singh (supra).
Accordingly, I proceed to dispose of the writ petition.
4.
In para 2 of the writ petition, it is stated that the petitioner served in the Indian Army as Havaldar Instructor from 4th November, 1963 to 21st August, 1979.
In terms of the law laid down by this Court in Jang Singh (supra) the service rendered in the Indian Army can be counted only for the period when the emergency was in operation.
In para 19 of the judgment in the case of Jang Singh (supra) it has been stated that the first emergency was from 26121962 to 10.1.1968 while the other was from 3.12.1971 to 371977.
Accordingly, I hold that the petitioner shall be entitled for counting the period from 491963 to 10.1.1968 and from 3.12.1971 to 371977, for the purpose of increment, seniority and pension in accordance with Rule 4 of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965.
The respondents are directed to compute the benefits in accordance with the observations given hereinabove within four months.
I further direct that the petitioner will be entitled to consequential benefits for refixation of pay.
However, the arrears so calculated shall be confined to 38 months prior to the date of filing the writ petition.
