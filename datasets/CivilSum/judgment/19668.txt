GC Garg, J - The petitioners were initially recruited as clerks in the Industries Department and were later on promoted as Assistants.
The claim of the petitioners is that they are entitled to be promoted to the posts of Deputy Superintendents and Superintendents, in view of the policy of the Government as contained in Annexures P-7 and P-10.
Mr. Nehra appearing for the respondent-State has stated that in view of the policy as contained in Annexures P-7 and P-10 and circular dated 26.7.1989, Annexure R-2, no reservation is permissible where the cadre consists of only two posts.
There is again no dispute that in the cadre (sic) Deputy Superintendents, there are only two (sic) consequently there is no reservation.
Respondent No. 3 who was promoted earlier, has been reverted vide order dated.
381989, Annexure R3.
2.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, there remains no dispute that two posts of Deputy Superintendents are to be filled on the basis of seniority and by completely ignoring the claim of the reserved categories.
This being the factual and admitted position, the respondent State is directed to consider the claim of the petitioners for promotion to the posts of Deputy Superintendents and Superintendents in accordance with law, as expeditiously as possible, preferably within four months from the receipt by them a copy of this order.
There shall, however, be no order as to costs.
This petition is disposed of accordingly.
