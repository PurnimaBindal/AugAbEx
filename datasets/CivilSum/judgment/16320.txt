MR Agnihotri, J - The Petitioner, whose date of birth is 7th April, 1993, was appointed as Sub-Inspector Consolidation (Patwari) in the Erstwhile State of PEPSU on 10th March, 1950.
He was promoted as Kanungo on 17th January, 1955, and thereafter with effect from 1st November, 1956, with the reorganisation of the new State of Punjab, the petitioner served in the State of Punjab, upto 31st October, 1996, and was allocated to the present State of Haryana from 1st November, 1966.
He was compulsorily retired from service on 14th September, 1988, as on the basis of over all assessment of record of service, the Commissioner, Hissar Division, doubted his integrity and awarded him adverse confidential report.
It is against this order that the petitioner has invoked the extraordinary jurisdiction of this Court, mainly on the ground that the learned Commissioner, Hissar Division, was inimically disposed towards him and, therefore, his compulsory retirement was not based on the relevant and correct date of his service record.
2.
In the written statement filed by Commissioner, Hissar Division, it has been clearly stated that the service record of the petitioner was not upto the mark and there was complaints regarding dishonesty and corruption for the years 1969-70, 1970-71 and 1986-87.
The representations made by the petitioner against the adverse remarks had also been rejected.
3.
Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and after going through the material on the record, I do not find any infirmity in the order of retirement of the petitioner which was in public interest.
It has been settled by now by Hon'ble the Supreme Court that it would be in public interest if dead wood is chopped off and employees having unsatisfactory record of service, whose annual confidential reports warrant their pre-mature retirement, are so retired.
4.
Under the circumstances, this writ petition is wholly without any force and is dismissed, with no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
