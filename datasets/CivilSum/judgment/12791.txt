B Rai, J - This writ petition has been filed with a prayer to issue a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to offer appropriate appointment to the petitioner on compassionate grounds in view of the death of the father of the petitioner while in service.
2.
Father of the petitioner was employed in the Divisional Officer of the Life Insurance Corporation of India (hereinafter referred to as the Corporation) Branch No. 1, Jalandhar.
He died on 1281991 while in service.
Petitioner submitted an application on 2821994 for appointment on compassionate grounds.
Prayer made in the application was declined by the respondents vide letter dated 361994.
Thereafter, petitioner made a representation through his counsel on 1771995.
The said representation was dismissed by respondent No. 2 on 1.8.1995.
Hence this petition.
3.
In response to notice of motion, respondents have filed written statement denying the claim of the petitioner to be appointed on compassionate grounds.
4.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have perused the material on record.
5.
Admittedly Pritam Singh father of the petitioner died on 1281991 while in the service of the respondent-Corporation.
At that time, mother of the petitioner was gainfully employed in the Punjab P.W.D.
Mechanical Division, Jalandhar.
It is not disputed that the petitioner had applied for employment on compassionate grounds in the respondent-Corporation.
Keeping in view the instructions contained in Annexure R1 to R3 issued by the respondent- Corporation, representation of the petitioner was rejected.
The instructions regarding appointment of one of the dependants of the deceased employee were issued enabling the bereaved family to overcome the hardship faced by the members of the family of deceased employee.
In para (4) of the instructions contained in Annexure R2, it is specifically provided that where any member of the family is employed, no appointment may be made on compassionate grounds.
In the instant case, admittedly, before the death of the father of the petitioner, his mother was already gainfully employed in the Public Works Department, Mechanical Division, Jalandhar and still she is in service.
Not only this, father of the petitioner had expired on 1281991 whereas the application for appointment on compassionate grounds was made on 2821994, i.e. after more than two years and six months.
The instructions contained in Annexures R1 to R3 provide that appointment on compassionate grounds can only be considered if a request is made by any member of the family of the deceased employee within a period of one year from the date of death of the employee.
In the present case, petitioner did not make any application for grant of employment on compassionate grounds within the period prescribed under the said instructions.
In this regard, reference may be made to L.I.C.
of India v. Mrs. Asha Ramchandra Ambedkar and another, A.I.R. 1994 SC 2148 : 1995(4) SCT.
298 : 1994(2) SCT.
548 as well as three Division Bench decisions of this Court in Smt. Daljit Kaur v. L.I.C.
of India, 1994(3) P.L.R.
268, : 1994(4) SCT 161 : Nirmal Arora v. L.I.C.
of India, 1995(2) PLR 108, : 1995(2) SCT 149 and Tina Bedi v. L.I.C.
of India, 1995(2) PLR 195 : 1995(2) SCT 266 and a decision of Kerala High Court in V. Santra v. L.I.C.
of India, 1994(1) SCT 142.
For the reasons recorded above, we do not find any merit in this petition and the same is dismissed with no orders as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
