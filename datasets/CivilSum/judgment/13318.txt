Bakhshish Kaur, J - Smt. Daropti Rani petitioner was appointed as a Clerk in Animal Husbandry Department on December 20, 1979.
She worked in the Department up to February 12, 1982.
On May 10, 1982, she was again appointed as a Clerk through the Employment Exchange in P.W.D.
(Public Health), Hisar.
2.
By way of filing this petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner has invoked the jurisdiction of this Court for the issuance of a writ in the nature of certiorari quashing the orders Annexure P.3 and P.4 whereby her request regarding regularisation of her services has been declined.
3.
The Government of Haryana has issued instructions that the services of those ad hoc employees who have completed two years of service shall be regularised.
A notification was issued on January 1, 1980 to the effect that the services of those ad hoc employees who have completed minimum two years of service on 31121979 shall be regularised.
On January 3, 1983 another notification was issued that the services of those clerks who had completed two years of service on September 15, 1983 and were on service on the cut off date i.e. September 15, 1983, shall be regularised.
These notifications are Annexure P.1 and P2 respectively.
The services of such clerks who had completed two years service up to September 15, 1983 and were in service on that date, were regularised, but the case of the petitioner was rejected on the ground that there was break of service of three months and that she had not completed two years of service on September 15, 1983.
Hence the writ petition.
4.
Pursuant to the notice issued.
the respondents put in appearance and written statement on behalf of respondent Nos. 2 and 4 was filed.
In their written statement, the respondents have admitted that the petitioner was appointed through Employment Exchange in the Animal Husbandry Department, Hisar on 20121979 and she worked in the Department up to February 12, 1982.
It is also admitted that on 1051982 she was again appointed in this Department.
Likewise, the issuance of the notifications dated 111980 and 3111983, Annexures P.1 and P2 were also admitted.
The claim of the petitioner is, however, resisted by raising a plea that she has got no right to invoke the extraordinary jurisdiction of this Court.
The break of three months as is in the service of the petitioner cannot be condoned.
However, break of service rendered on ad hoc basis up to a period of one month may be condoned.
5.
I have heard Shri RK Malik, learned counsel for the petitioner and Shri Sultan Singh, learned Assistant Advocate General for the State of Haryana.
6.
The notification Annexure P.1 was issued by the State of Haryana on the basis of a policy decision taken by them that the services of the ad hoc employees would be regularised if they fulfil and conditions laid down therein, i.e. if they had completed minimum of two years service on 31121979.
This notification Annexure P.1 was followed by another notification dated January 3, 1983 Annexure P2 and vide this notification, those clerks who had completed two years of service on September 15, 1982 and were on service on that date, were entitled for regularisation of their services.
The relevant portion of the notification Annexure P2 reads as under :-  "(i) Only such ad hoc clerks who have completed two years service on 15th September, 1982 and were in service on 1591982, may be regularised against the posts/vacancies of clerks.
The recommendees of the Subordinate Services Selection Board should be absorbed against the remaining vacancies.
These recommendees of the Board who cannot be absorbed their names be returned to the Board to enable them to recommend their names to other departments for appointment against clear vacancies."
7.
It is an admitted fact that the petitioner had joined the service as clerk on 20121979 on ad hoc basis.
She had worked in the Department up to 12.12.1982.
She was again appointed as clerk on May 10, 1982.
She had completed more than two years service on 1591982.
Immediately after the issuance of notification, she made representation for regularisation of her services on the post of clerk, but the same was rejected by the Executive Engineer PWD (Public Health), Division No. 4, Hisar vide order Annexure P.3.
The Director, Animal Husbandry Department also rejected her representation, vide order Annexure P4 and conveyed the petitioner that she has not completed two years of service, so she cannot be granted the benefit of regularisation.
8.
Mr. RK Malik, learned counsel for the petitioner contended that similar controversy has been decided by a Division Bench of this Court in a case reported as Giani Ram and another v. State of Haryana, 1981(2) SLR 803 and a Full Bench decision of this Court in Jagdish Lal v. State of Punjab, 1988(2) PLR 43 (Pb.
& Hry.).
It has been held that an employee who has completed the required number of years of service, with less than 30 days break, is entitled for regularisation and minimum number of years of service need not necessarily be completed immediately preceding the cut off date i.e. September 15, 1982.
The petitioner in this case joined the service on 20121979.
She was also in service on 1591982, i.e. the date of notification, Annexure P2.
Thus, the petitioner had completed two years of service as on 1591982.
Therefore, her case is fully covered under decision rendered in Jagdish Lal's (supra) case, and the Full Bench also referred to the object of the Government Notification dated March 29, 1985 by observing as under :-  "The very object of deciding to regularise the services of ad hoc employees, as seen from some of the earlier orders was : Whereas by continuation of the ad hoc appointments made as above as an administrative necessity, the ad hoc employees have required necessary experience and their ouster after a considerable period of service would entail hardship to ad hoc employees as a whole and accentuate the problem of unemployment, the President of India is pleaded to decide."
If this was the very object of regularising the service, we are unable to find any reason as to why we should take in that the continuous service of one year immediately preceding April 1, 1985, was alone to be taken into account."
9.
In view of the observations made by their Lordships in Jagdish Lal's (supra) case, it is evident that the case of the petitioner is covered under the notification and the cut off date 1591982 mentioned in the notification, applies to such employees also who have completed the prescribed number of two years service on the cut off date as it is not necessary that two years of service must be that one immediately preceding the said date i.e. 1591982.
The petitioner is, therefore, entitled to the regularisation of her services.
The absence of the petitioner for three months from service as mentioned in Annexure P.3 was not, therefore, relevant to be considered for regularisation of her services.
For the aforesaid reasons, this petition is allowed.
The impugned orders Annexure P.3 and P.4 are set aside, being illegal and unjust.
The respondents are directed to regularise the services of the petitioner within a period of six months from the date of receipt of a copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
