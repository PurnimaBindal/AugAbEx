SS Saron, J.- This order shall dispose of C.W.P.
No. 17183 of 2001 and 17186 of 2001.
2.
The Petitioner Jai Parkash in C.W.P.
No. 17183 of 2001 has sought direction against the respondent for the release of his pension arrears of Rs. 19184/- with interest at the rate of 18% pa as the amount of Pension arrears has been retained on account of non-receipt of share from the Municipal Committee, Ladwa, which already stands deducted from the monthly salary bill each month from the petitioner.
3.
The petitioner Muni Lal in CWP No. 17186 of 2001 has also sought a direction to release Rs. 22043/- with interest which has been retained from the pension of the petitioner because of non-receipt of the share from the Municipal Committee, Ladwa, which stands deducted from the monthly salary bill each month from the petitioner's salary.
4.
Jai Parkash petitioner in CWP No. 17183 of 2001 has averred that he served Municipal Committee, Ladwa, for more than 35 years as Class IV employee (Peon) on regular basis.
He retired as peon on 30.4.1999 while posted at Municipal Committee, Ladwa, at the age of 60 years.
His pension was fixed on 7.12.1999 vide P.P.O.
No. 1993 and Rs. 1654/- was fixed as Pension in the unrevised pay scale.
Copy of the payment order has been appended as Annexure P-1.
It is claimed that a sum of Rs. 19,184/- was retained by the Director Local Bodies and only Rs. 11700/- was paid on the plea that this amount of Rs. 19184/- had not been cleared by the Municipal Committee, Ladwa as the contributory provident fund account was maintained by the Municipal Committee.
The petitioner claimed that this amount is liable to be released in his favour.
5.
Muni Lal petitioner in C.W.P.
17186 of 2001 retired as Octroi Clerk on 3151999 on attaining age of 58 years.
He served for more than 33 years.
Accordingly his pension payment orders were issued vide pension payment order No. Pension-99/28899 dated 28.9.1999.
A copy of the pension payment order is appended as Annexure P-1.
He also claimed amount of Rs. 32,108/- which was calculated as arrears to be paid to him on un-revised scale but out of it only Rs. 10065/- was paid and remaining Rs. 22043/- was retained by the Director Local Bodies, Haryana, on the plea that accounts are yet to be cleared from the Municipal Committee, Ladwa and till the clearance, the amount was being retained.
6.
Separate written statements have been filed by respondent Nos. 1 and 2 and respondent No. 3 respectively.
In the written statement filed by respondent No. 3 in Jai Parkash's case, it is accepted that the petitioner had come into service of Municipal Committee, Ladwa, on 181967 and his pension along with interest upto 3131993 comes to Rs. 14552/-.
This amount uptil the period of Nov 1999 i.e. when his pension was sanctioned comes to Rs. 19,184/-.
This amount was to be sent from the Provident Fund of the petitioner.
At the time of sanction of his pension he had stated that if the Municipal Committee had sent less amount of pension share to Pension Fund or if any other share is pending, it may be deducted and the petitioner shall have no objection to the same.
Apart from this, the petitioner had taken a loan of Rs. 5000/- in June 1992 and this is also a reason for sending less pension share.
7.
In the written statement filed in Muni Lal's case, it is submitted that the petitioner had joined the services of the Municipal Committee on 1211967.
His pension along with interest upto 3131993 comes to Rs. 16,674/-.
This share uptil the period of August, 1999 along with interest amounted to Rs. 20,679/-.
The petitioner had taken advance of Rs. 5000/- in April 1992 and Rs. 2000/- in June 1992.
This amount has to be recovered from the provident fund of the petitioner, which could not be recovered at the time of final payment of Provident Fund.
Therefore, this payment has been deducted from the arrears of pension of the petitioner by the Director, Urban Development, Chandigarh.
An amount of Rs. 1364/- which was deducted in excess has already been sent back to the petitioner in the month of March, 2002.
8.
No replication has been filed to the written statements filed by the respondents.
9.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance gone through the records.
10.
It is not in dispute that the employees of the Municipal Committees in Haryana were earlier governed by the Contributory Provident Fund Scheme.
However, the State Government promulgated the Haryana Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules 1993 (hereinafter referred to as the 1993 Rules).
The pension to the Municipal employees was to be paid from the own contributory fund of the employees.
In this manner all the Municipalities in the State of Haryana were asked to send the total employer's share of contribution upto 3131993 towards pension fund, which was lying in the General Provident Fund Account of the respective employees.
The 1993 Rules are applicable to the employees of the Municipalities who were in service or are appointed on or after 16.4.1992 on whole time regular basis and were working immediately before 16.4.1992 and opt for the 1993 Rules.
The said 1993 Rules are not to apply to the employees who opt out of the same; are members of the All India Services or members of the Haryana Civil Services or the members of Haryana Government serving in the Municipalities; are paid out of the contingencies; are work charged employees; are employed on contract basis, except when the contract provides otherwise; are re-employed after superannuation; and are specifically excluded wholly or partly from the operation of these rules.
Rule 3(h) of the 1993 Rules defines `qualifying service' as follows :-  "qualifying service" means the service rendered under a Municipality for which an employee is paid from the Municipal fund and shall include any service rendered under the Haryana Government and Improvement Trust, or a Corporation immediately before joining the Service : (Emphasis Added) Rule 3(i) defines `Service' as follows :-  "Service" means the service rendered under the control of a municipality."
Rule 4 of the 1993 Rules provided for exercise of option.
Rule 5 provides for the establishment of pension fund.
Rule 6 deals with transfer of amount to pension fund and reads as under :-  "6.
Transfer of amount to Pension fund - (1) The share of money alongwith interest standing in the Contributory Provident Fund Account to the credit of an employee of municipality who opts or who is governed by these rules, shall stand transferred to the pension fund to the extent it was contributed by the concerned municipality during the period of Service of an employee alongwith any loss caused to the contribution of the concerned through withdrawal during the Service which shall be made good by the employee paying the amount alongwith interest due for that period to the pension fund."
Rule 7 deals with operation of pension fund.
Rule 8 deals with maintenance of accounts.
Rule 9 deals with grant of pension.
Rule 10 deals with payment of pension.
11.
The grievance of the respective petitioners in the present petitions is to the wrongful withholding of Rs. 19,184/- in Jai Parkash's case and Rs. 22043/- in Muni Lal's case.
The provisions of Rule 6(1) of the 1993 Rules reproduced above, shows that the share money along with interest standing in the contributory provident fund account to the credit of an employee of the Municipality who opts or who is governed by 1993 Rules shall stand transferred to the pension fund to the extent it was contributed by the concerned municipality during the period of Service of an employee along with any loss caused to the contribution of the concerned through withdrawal during the service which shall be made good by the employee paying the amount along with interest due for that period to the pension fund.
The stand of the respondents is that the amounts in question stand transferred to the pension contribution of the concerned Municipalities through withdrawal during the service shall be made good by the concerned employee, paying the amount along with interest due for that period.
The respondent No. 3 has categorically stated that the petitioner Jai Parkash at the time of his sanction of pension had stated that if the Municipal Committee had sent less amount of pension share to pension fund or any other share is pending, it may be deducted and the petitioner shall have no objection to the same.
Besides, the petitioner had taken a loan of Rs. 5000/- in June 1992 and this was also the reason for sending less pension share.
These averments of the respondent Municipal Committee have not been controverted by the petitioner Jai Parkash.
Similar is the position with regard to petitioner Muni Lal.
The deductions made in his case is in consonance with Rule 6, that is, the loss caused to the pension contribution of the Municipalities has been made through withdrawal from the provident fund of the petitioner Muni Lal.
The respondent No. 3 Municipal Committee, Ladwa, has stated that the share of Muni Lal up till the period of August 1999 along with interest amounted to Rs. 20,679/-.
Besides, the petitioner had taken an advance of Rs. 5000/- in April 1992 and Rs. 2000/- in June 1992.
This amount was to be recovered from the provident fund of the petitioner Muni Lal which could not be recovered at the time of payment of final payment of G.P.F.
It is due to this reason that this amount was deducted from the pension of the petitioner by Director, Urban Development, Haryana, Chandigarh.
An amount of Rs. 1364/- had been deducted in excess which has already been sent back to the petitioner in the month of March 2002 by the aforesaid Directorate.
These averments in the written statement have not been controverted.
Therefore, we are of the view that the deductions as made from the pensions by the Municipal Committee, Ladwa, is in accord with the Rule 6 to the 1993 Rules.
Consequently, there is no merit in these writ petitions and the same are dismissed.
Petitions dismissed
