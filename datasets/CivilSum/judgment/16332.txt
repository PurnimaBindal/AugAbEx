IS Tiwana, J (Oral) - The petitioner impugned the order of the trial Sub Judge dated 13th February, 1989, whereby he has struck off certain issues framed by his predecessor on 14th October, 1987.
The ground is that the impugned order was passed without properly appreciating the pleadings of the parties and without recording any good reason for passing of the same.
According to the learned counsel for the petitioner, a number of triable issues which arose from the pleadings of the parties have been abandoned vide impugned order.
2.
Having gone into the matter with the assistance of the learned counsel for the petitioner and perusing the pleadings of the parties, I am of the view that the above noted stand of the learned counsel is justifiable to a great extent.
The lower Court, as already pointed out, has not given by reason for striking off the issues as framed earlier.
All that has been stated is "I am of the view that certain issues have been wrongly framed or incorporated."
I, therefore, allow this petition and direct that the parties be put on trial on issues as framed vide order dated 14th October, 1987 except the issues mentioned at numbers 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 19.
These issues, to my mind, do not give rise to any triable cause or in other words are not material for the grant of disallowance of the relief as prayed for in the plaint.
Therefore, the impugned order dated 13th February, 1989, is set aside to the extent as indicated above and the parties, through their counsel are directed to be present before the lower Court on 5th February, 1991, for trial of the suit on the issues which now survive.
No costs.
Ordered accordingly.
