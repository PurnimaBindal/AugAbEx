JV Gupta, CJ - This order will also dispose of LPA Nos. 915 to 923 of 1988, as all these appeals arise out of one judgment of the learned Single Judge.
2.
By virtue of the order of the learned Single Judge the new rationalisation policy dated April 25, 1985, Annexure P.1, was quashed and at the same time, certain directions for framing a new policy were given therein.
The only contention raised on behalf of the appellants is that no such direction could be given by the learned Single Judge for framing the new policy.
In support of the contention, reference was made to SP Gupta v. President of India, A.I.R. 1982 Supreme Court 149; K Rajindran v. State of Tamil Nadu, 1982(2) Services Law Reporter 196 and Asif Hameed v. State of J & K, A.I.R. 1989 Supreme Court 1899.
This proposition as such could not be successfully contested on behalf of the respondents.
However it was suggested that the said directions given by the learned Single Judge may be considered as suggestions and guidelines for framing a new policy by the State Government.
3.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, the order of the learned Single Judge quashing the above said policy dated April 25, 1985, is upheld.
However, the said order is modified as regards the directions given by the learned Single Judge for framing a new policy.
With these observations all these appeals fail and are dismissed with no order as to costs.
Appeal dismissed.
