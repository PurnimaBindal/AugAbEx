VK Jhanji, J - This is plaintiff's second appeal against the orders of Additional District Judge, Jalandhar, dated 1381984 dismissing his appeal preferred against judgment and decree of Sub Judge' 1st Class, Jalandhar, dated 1881983 whereby his suit for declaration to the effect that the order dated 1021977 passed by the General Manager, Punjab Roadways, Jalandhar, terminating his services, being illegal, was dismissed.
2.
Briefly stated, the facts are that the plaintiff was working as a Driver in Punjab Roadways, Jalandhar.
His services were terminated vide order dated 1021977.
He challenged the said order by filing a suit on 6.11.1982 for a declaration that the order terminating his services was illegal, ultra vires, against the principles of natural justice, null and void.
He also prayed that he is entitled to all benefits of service.
3.
The trial Court dismissed the suit and on appeal, the first appellate Court dismissed his appeal holding that the plaintiff had abandoned his service.
The present appeal has been filed by the plaintiff challenging the judgment and decree of the Courts below.
4.
It is not disputed that the plaintiff applied for leave from 151976 to 181976 and leave was granted from 651976 to 1561976 as earned leave, and from 16.6.1976 to 971976 on half pay.
He was to join his duty on 1071976 but he applied for another 65 days' leave.
His leave was rejected on 12.71976 but even after rejection of his leave, he did not resume his duties.
In the leave application, the plaintiff did not mention his latest address.
With regard to rejection of his leave intimation was sent to him at his old address and he was directed to resume his duty.
This communication was received back by the department with the remarks of the postal authorities that the plaintiff was not found in the village.
The defendant thereafter published a notice in the newspapers, the daily Urdu and Punjabi Ajit on 30.11.1976 requiring the plaintiff to resume his duties within 15 days thereof.
Since the plaintiff did not choose to join his duty his services were terminated on 1021977.
5.
Learned counsel for the plaintiff has contended that his services could not be teriminated without holding an enquiry or without giving him an opportunity of being heard.
His contention is that it is not a case of abandonment but rather a case of illegal termination of services.
6.
On the other hand learned counsel for the defendant has submitted that the plaintiff did not join his duty despite notice sent to him through post as well as by publication in the newspapers.
7.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties, I find that there is no merit in the appeal.
The facts of this case clearly show that the plaintiff, on the expiry of his leave, failed to join his duty on 1071976.
Instead of joining, he sent another application for grant of leave for 65 days and the said application was rejected on 12.
71976.
The plaintiff never cared to join his duty, when his application for leave was rejected.
When the order rejecting his leave was sent through registered post, the same was received back by the defendant with the remarks of the postal authorities that the plaintiff was not found in the village.
The plaintiff did not join his duty even after notice was published in the newspapers on 30.11.1976 requiring him to join duty within 15 days.
This clearly shows that the plaintiff, of his own sweet will, has been on leave from 1071976 without obtaining previous permission.
From the facts of this case, no other conclusion can be arrived at except that the plaintiff had actually abandoned his post of duty for exceedingly long period without sufficient grounds for his absence.
No useful purpose could have been served to give him any further opportunity.
Even otherwise the suit of the plaintiff is clearly barred by time inasmuch as his services were terminated on 1021977 and the present suit has been filed on 6.11.1982.
Cause of action to the plaintiff had arisen on the day his services were terminated and the suit could be filed within three years from the day when the cause of action had arisen.
It is not the case of the plaintiff that the authority terminating his services was not competent to pass order of termination.
Thus, the order terminating his services cannot be said to be null and void or passed by a person who had no jurisdiction to pass such an order.
8.
As a result thereof, the appeal is dismissed with costs.
Appeal dismissed.
