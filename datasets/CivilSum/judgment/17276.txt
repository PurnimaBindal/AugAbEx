SB Sinha, J - This appeal is directed against a judgment and order dated 27th September, 1993.
2.
The writ-petitioner-respondent applied for appointment as constable.
Admittedly, at the relevant point of time, there was no provision for interview or any other test.
Such a provision admittedly has been incorporated by reason of a notification published in the Calcutta Police Gazette on 4th July, 1995 wherein 100 marks and been accorded, 50 being for reading and writing and 50 for general knowledge and current affairs.
As in the order of 1990 such a provision was not existing, the rules existing at the relevant point of time must be followed.
3.
In this view of the matter, we are of the opinion that there is no infirmity in the judgment under appeal.
This aspect of the matter has been considered in NT Bevin Katti v. Karnataka Public Service Commission reported in AIR 1990 SC 1233 : 1992(2) SCT.
578, which in turn has been followed by the Appeal Court in BL Gupta v. M.C.D.
reported in 1998(9) SCC.
233.
4.
For the reasons aforementioned, there is no merit in this appeal which is accordingly, dismissed with costs.
The counsel fee is assessed at 100 Gms.
5.
However, it is made clear that if one of the petitioners namely the writ petitioner-respondent No. 6 had not appeared in physical fitness test, the same may be taken note of for consideration.
Keeping in view the fact and this appeal has been pending for a long time, the respondents are directed to relax the age bar of the respondents.
Affidavit-in-opposition filed today be kept on record.
Order accordingly.
