RL Anand, J - Mahabir Singh petitioner has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India against the respondent and it has been prayed by the petitioner that the order Annexure P-5, passed by respondent No. 1, and the show cause notice dated 2461999, Annexure P-1, issued by respondent No. 2, be quashed and directions be given to respondents to allow the petitioner to continue in service till the age of superannuation.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he was born on 861949.
He joined the Haryana Police as Constable on 18121971.
In due course he was promoted to different posts on the basis of his eligibility and performance.
He has already completed 25 years of qualifying service on 17121996.
During his service, he had number of commendation certificates.
It is alleged by the petitioner that respondent No. 2 on the basis of some misconception issued a show cause notice to him proposing to retire him compulsorily from service in public interest under sub-rule (2) of Rule 918 of the Punjab Police Rules.
The grounds stated by the department were as follows :-  "1.
He was awarded a punishment of censure in 1948 for carelessness and not taking interest in official work.
2.
He was awarded a punishment of censure in 1992 for not taking any action after due date in case FIR No. 1118/92 and FIR No. 134/92.
3.
He was awarded a punishment of censure in 1996 for taking bribe from accused in case FIR No. 24 dated 2111996 under Sections 451/506/34 IPC.
Besides, above, he has earned following adverse remarks in his ACR during his service :- 4.
141994 to 3131995 (conveyed) General remarks : An official of above average capabilities.
5.
2751995 to 3131996 (conveyed) General remarks : I do not agree with the report of Sh. Mehar Singh, DSP/M Garh.
This ASI is thoroughly corrupt.
One corruption D/E is pending against him.
His ranking is degraded to C. 6.
2251996 to 10.12.1996 (conveyed) General remarks : 1.
Integrity doubtful  2.
He was censured for bribery in Departmental Enq.
for Rs. 3,000/- in case bearing No. 24/96, police stn.
Mahendergarh.
7.
11121996 to 3131997 (conveyed) General remarks : He was warned regarding absence.
Complaint regarding absence was received once."
3.
On receipt of the above accusation, the petitioner filed explanation.
He submitted that charge No. 1 relates to the year 1984 and cannot in any way be taken as an ground for retiring him prematurely after 16 years.
He further stated that with regard to the punishment of censure in 1996 for taking bribe from the accused in case FIR No. 24 dated 2111996, under Sections 451/506/34 I.PC the competent authority has already dropped the proceedings on merits after considering the report of the Enquiry Officer.
The general remarks during the period 141994 to 3131995, which were conveyed to the petitioner, show that he has above average capabilities and, therefore, these remarks cannot be termed as adverse from any angle.
The general remarks for the period 2751995 to 3131996 have already been expunged by the compulsory retirement vide order dated 26.4.1994.
The report for the period 2251996 to 10.12.1996, which was conveyed to the petitioner, has already been dropped by the compulsory retirement on the basis of the report of the Enquiry Officer and in these circumstances, no valid basis for compulsory retirement of the petitioner remain to be considered.
The petitioner has given satisfactory reply to the show cause notice and still the impugned order of compulsory retirement has been passed against the petitioner in an illegal, arbitrary and mala fide manner and, therefore, it cannot be sustained in the eyes of law.
4.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents, who filed the reply and denied the allegations.
The respondents stated that the petitioner was enlisted in the Police Department as Constable on 18121971.
In the ACR for the period from 2251996 to 10.12.1996, the honesty of the petitioner was reported doubtful, which was conveyed to the petitioner on 221998.
Against the said remarks the petitioner has not made any representation, hence the same is binding upon him.
The honesty of the petitioner was reported doubtful in ACR for the period from 2251996 to 10.12.1996 while he was working as Asstt.
Sub-Inspector.
The petitioner was compulsorily retired from service on 2842000 which has been duly approved by the State Government.
In short, the defence of the respondents is that the order of compulsory retirement has been passed according to rules.
5.
In support of his case the petitioner has filed the following documents :-  1.
Annexure P-1, the show cause notice for compulsory retirement dated 2461999.
2.
Annexure P-2, the representation made by the petitioner dated 1671999.
3.
Annexure P-3, the letter dated 31.3.1999 issued by DGP whereby it was intimated to the petitioner that the adverse remarks recorded in the ACR for the period 2751995 to 3131996 have been expunged.
4.
Annexure P-4, the copy of order dated 2021998 issued by the office of SP, Bhiwani.
5.
Annexure P-5, copy of the order passed by DGP, Haryana vide which the petitioner was compulsorily retired.
6.
In rebuttal, the respondents have placed on record Annexure R-1, copy of the letter dated 1122000 issued by the DGP, Haryana to the Financial Commissioner seeking the approval of the letter to retire compulsorily Mahabir Singh, ASI in public interest and Annexure R-2 is the copy of the letter dated 832000 vide which the Home Department gave approval to the proposal of compulsory retirement.
7.
I have heard Mr. Raj Mohan Singh, Advocate on behalf of the petitioner, Mr. Rajbir Sehrawat, DAG, Haryana on behalf of the respondents and with their assistance have gone through the records of this case.
8.
Rule 918(2) of the Punjab Police Rules lays down that the Inspector General of Police may, with the previous approval of the State Government, compulsorily retire any Police Officer, other than an officer belonging to Indian Police Service or Haryana State Police Service who has completed twenty-five years qualifying service, without giving any reasons.
An officer who is so compulsorily retired will not be entitled to claim any special compensation for his retirement.
The reading of the above statutory rule would show that the Inspector-General of Police with the previous approval of the State Government can retire a police officer compulsorily who has not completed 25 years qualifying service, without assigning any reason.
Annexure P-1 is the show cause notice which was issued to the petitioner.
It shows that the Director General of Police issued the notice of compulsory retirement to the petitioner from service in public interest by invoking the provisions of Rule 918(2).
The word "public interest" has not been defined either in the Punjab Police Rules or in the Civil Services Rules, but it will always be a question of fact what is public interest in particular circumstances.
As a layman one can say that all is valid which is in the interest of public.
But before passing any order by taking the benefit of the term "public interest" one has to see whether the order has actually been passed in public interest or some extraneous reasons have been taken into consideration.
Following things had weighed against the petitioner.
First, the petitioner was awarded punishment of censure in the year 1984 for carelessness and not taking interest in official work.
The show cause notice was issued in the year 1999.
It is a stale incident which should not be taken into consideration.
It was then mentioned in the show cause notice that the petitioner was awarded punishment of censure in the year 1992 for not taking any action after due date in case FIR No. 1118 of 1992 and FIR No. 134 of 1992.
Third, the petitioner was awarded punishment of censure in 1996 for taking bribe from accused in FIR No. 24 dated 2111996 under Sections 451/506/34 IPC.
It was also observed by the Punishing Authority that from 141994 to 3151995 there were general remarks against the petitioner that he has above average capabilities.
This also could not be taken into consideration.
One more entry is recorded against the petitioner for the period 2751995 to 3131996.
The Reporting Officer disagreed with the report of DSP Mohindergarh and came to the conclusion that the petitioner was thoroughly corrupt and one corruption departmental enquiry is pending against him and, therefore, his rank was graded C Against these adverse remarks, the petitioner made a representation and the same was accepted as is evident from the document Annexure P-3.
Therefore, this adverse entry of corruption also cannot be taken into consideration.
Then there is one more entry for the period 2251996 to 10.12.1996.
According to this entry, the petitioner was awarded punishment of censure under FIR No. 24 of 1996 for taking bribe of Rs. 3,000/- in Mohindergarh.
This entry has also become meaningless in view of the order dated 2021998 vide which it was remarked that the Enquiry Officer had declared the petitioner not guilty of the charges.
The Superintendent of Police did not agree with the finding of the Enquiry Officer.
He issued a show cause notice for the stoppage of five annual grade increments, but the order of the Punishing Authority was set aside as he did not record any disagreement note.
In other words, this entry for the period 2251996to 10.12.1996 also could not be relied upon by the Punishing Authority before passing the impugned order Annexure P-5.
The reading of the order of compulsory retirement would show that the following four entries were taken note of by the Director General of Police :-  141994 to 3131995  (conveyed) 1.
General remarks : An official of above average capabilities.
251996 to 10.12.1996(conveyed) 1.
Honesty : 1.
Is not beyond doubt General remarks : 2.
He was awarded a punishment of censure under FIR No. 24/96 PC for taking bribe of Rs. 3,000/- in Mohindergarh.
11121996 to 3131997(conveyed) Guilt, if any, and was the attention of concerned officer was drawn towards the same through any other letter.
Is the officer remains present at the Head-quarter after closing of office and during vacations.
: Warning was given regarding absence.
Complaint regarding was received once."
9.
Through the above discussion I have tried to show that the entry for the period 141994 to 3131995 cannot be taken into consideration because the official has been rated above average and the entries for the period 251996 to 10.12.1996 have already been expunged and these entries have lost significance on account of the acceptance of the appeal of the petitioner.
Now we are only left with the last adverse entry for the period from 11121996 to 3131997 vide which the warning was given to the petitioner and there was one complaint against the petitioner with regard to absence.
10.
Now the point for determination would be whether for this absence and for the warning, the petitioner has become a dead wood or has become so incompetent that he has become unfit to discharge the duties of a police officer.
The power of judicial review no doubt is limited but if the High Court under Articles 226/277 of the Constitution comes to the conclusion that the power under Rule 918(2) has been exercised in such a manner which shows non-application of mind on the part of the Punishing Authority, certainly this action can always be successfully challenged by the petitioner.
It is true that the petitioner has completed 25 years qualifying service, still the public servant can always look that he should be allowed to serve till he attains the age of superannuation.
As I have held above that the entries for the period 251996 to 10.12.1996 cannot be taken as adverse to the disadvantage of the petitioner and the entry for 11121996 to 3131997 is of very trivial nature, therefore, I am inclined to hold that the order Annexure P-5 and the order Annexure P-1 cannot be sustained in the eyes of law and these should be quashed.
Ordered accordingly, Directions are given to the respondents to take the petitioner into service immediately on receipt of copy of the order and he should be allowed to serve till the age of superannuation.
The petitioner shall be granted all benefits of service for the period when he remained out of job and he will be considered on duty from the date when he was relieved from the service.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Order accordingly.
