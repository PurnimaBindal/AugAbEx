MM Kumar, J - This petition filed under Article 226 of the Constitution of India prays for issuance of a writ in the certiorari, quashing the Order dated 4111987 (Annexure P-6), whereby respondent No. 1 accepted the representation made by respondent No. 3 and ordered cancellation of Order dated 1571987 (Annexure P-5), by which the petitioner was promoted on the post of Assistant.
2.
Brief facts of the case necessary for disposal of this petition are that the petitioner joined as a Clerk in the respondent Department on 2521969 and was promoted as Steno-typist on 7.1.1972.
He was further promoted as Junior Scale Stenographer in the year 1982 vide Order dated 26.6.1987 with effect from 551982.
Respondent No. 3 was appointed as Steno-typist on 681973 and thereafter he was promoted as Junior Scale Stenographer with effect from 1121986.
The petitioner was further promoted on the post of Assistant on 1571987.
The afore-mentioned promotion of the petitioner was challenged by respondent No. 3 by filing a representation before the Commissioner-cum- Secretary to Government of Haryana, Department of Industries on the ground that under Rule 9 of the Haryana Stores Purchase Department (Group-C) Service Rules, 1982 only Clerks and Steno-typists could be considered for promotion to the post of Assistant and the Junior Scale Stenographers are not eligible to be considered for promotion.
Respondent No. 1, the Commissioner and Secretary, accepted the representation of respondent No. 3 and ordered cancellation of promotion of the petitioner.
The afore-mentioned impugned order dated 4111987 reads as under :-  "2.
The State Govt. observed that the promotion made by your office vide No. E/11/247/II/14101-19 dated 1571987 is not as per provision made in the Service Rules because amendment is made in the Service Rules.
The present provision of Rules is to be continued.
3.
The promotion of Sh. GK Ranga, Head Asstt.
on the post of Superintendent, and Shri PC Jain Asstt.
on the post of Head Asstt.
made are not as per Departmental Service Rules, as Sh. GK Ranga Head Asstt.
does not fulfil the conditions of experience for promotion.
In this connection, the case is still under consideration regarding amendment of Rules and no amendment has been made yet.
4.
As regards the promotion of Sh. Umrao Singh to the post of Asstt., only clerks and stenotypists are eligible for the promotion of Asstt.
as per provision of Service Rules.
The promotion of Sh. Pawan Kumar Junior Scale Stenographer to the post of Assistant is in violation of service Rules, as per present Service Rules only Clerks and Steno-typists can be promoted on the post of Assistant.
In view of above, the Government order the cancellation of promotion Order vide letter dated 1571987 and proposal for relaxation of Rules be separately submitted."
3.
When the matter came up for hearing on 16111987, a Division Bench of this Court directed that statuo-quo be maintained till further orders.
Accordingly the petitioner has continued working on the post of Assistant.
4.
In the written statement, the principal stand taken by the respondent-State is that under Rule (1)(c) of the Rules, for promotion to the post of Assistant, only Clerks and Steno-typists have been made eligible whereas according to Rule 9(1)(d), Junior Scale Stenographers are to be considered for promotion to the post of Senior Scale Stenographers and further on for the post of personal Assistant or Superintendent.
It has further has submitted that the lien of the petitioner on the post of Clerk/Steno-typist stood automatically terminated when he was confirmed on the promoted post of Junior Scale Stenographer vide Order dated 26.6.1987 with effect from 551982.
5.
Mr. Shailender Sharma, learned counsel for the petitioner has argued that 1982 Rules are applicable only to the Haryana Stores Purchase Department (Group C) Services.
According to the learned counsel, the petitioner has been working in the Supplies and Disposal Department which is different from the Haryana Stores Purchase Department.
Learned counsel has drawn my attention to Punjab Civil Service (Promotion of Stenographers and Steno-typists) Rules, 1961 as amended by the State of Haryana on 14111978 vide Annexure P-2 and argued that infact these rules apply to the case of the petitioner.
According to these rules, a Junior Scale Stenographer is entitled to be considered for promotion to the post of Assistant without passing the departmental examination of Assistant Grade.
The learned counsel has further argued that in any case, the petitioner was promoted to the post of Assistant on 1571987, Annexure P-5 and on the representation made by respondent No 3, he was reverted without issuing any show cause notice of hearing to him.
According to learned counsel, a copy of the representation filed by respondent No. 3 should have been supplied to him with an opportunity of hearing and only then order, Annexure P-6 could have been passed.
Mr. SS Brar, the learned State counsel has relied upon Rule 20 of 1982 Rules and argued that the earlier rules of 1956 were repealed to the extent the 1982 Rules are applicable to the posts covered under those rules.
According to learned counsel, earlier 1956 Rules used to apply and now 1982 Rules are applicable to the petitioner.
The learned counsel has maintained that the Supplies and Disposal Department is one and the same Department as Stores and Purchase Department.
With regard to grant of hearing, the learned State counsel has pointed out that since there was violation of the rules, no hearing was required to be afforded to the petitioner.
6.
Having heard the learned counsel for the parties, I am of the considered view that this petition deserves to be allowed.
It is admitted position that on the representation made by respondent No. 3, impugned order dated 4111987 has been passed and the promotion given to the petitioner vide Order dated 1571987 (Annexure P-5) was cancelled by the respondent.
It is also admitted that neither any copy of the representation was supplied to the petitioner nor any hearing was afforded to him before passing the impugned order dated 4111987.
In para 10 of the writ petition, the afore-mentioned averments have been made to which there is no denial.
It is well-settled principle of law that an order adversely affecting an employee can only be passed after granting an opportunity of hearing to him.
For the afore-mentioned proposition, reliance could be placed on the judgments of the Supreme Court in the cases of Mohinder Singh Gill v. Union of India, AIR 1978 SC 851, Menaka Gandhi v. Union of India, AIR 1978 SC 597, Ram Chander v Union of India, 1986(2) SLR 608, R.P.
Bhatt v. Union of India, 1986(1) SLR 470 and SL Mukherjee v. Union of India, 1990(4) SCC 594.
7.
The argument raised by the petitioner that the 1982 rules are not applicable, could have been considered but the necessity of doing so is obviated because the impugned order dated 4111987 (Annexure P-6) has been found to be illegal as the principles of natural justice were not followed.
It shall be open to the petitioner to raise this argument alongwith any other argument before the authority, if such a necessity arises.
8.
In view of the above, the writ petition is allowed.
Order, Annexure P-6 is set aside.
Consequently the petitioner shall be entitled to all the benefits of the post of Assistant on which he was promoted.
However, the respondents shall be at liberty to proceed in accordance with law, if so advised.
The petitioner shall be entitled to raise all the arguments before the authority concerned.
If the authority concerned choses to issue show cause notice to the petitioner and pass an order adverse to him, the order shall not be given effect to for two weeks of the date of communication of the order to the petitioner.
Writ petition stands disposed of in the above terms.
Petition allowed.
