SS Nijjar, J - The petitioner joined the post of Platoon Commander in the Punjab Home Guards on 22.10.1968.
He was promoted as Company Commander on 3031984.
He was put on probation for a period of one year.
After completion of probation period, he was confirmed on the post of Company Commander.
He was allowed to cross the efficiency bar with effect from 121984 at the stage of Rs. 850/-, by order dated 21.12.1984.
By order dated 21101977, two increments of the petitioner were stopped with cumulative effect.
The petitioner made a number of representations against the aforesaid order.
Ultimately, by order dated 25.1.1993, the order of stoppage of two increments was set aside.
However, soon thereafter, by order dated 28121993, the petitioner was prematurely retired.
This prompted the petitioner to file C.W.P.
No. 3168 of 1994, which was decided on 19121997.
The order of pre-mature retirement was quashed.
The petitioner was directed to be reinstated into service with all consequential benefits that may flow therefrom.
After the reinstatement, the petitioner made a representation on 25.9.1999 for refixation of his pay by ignoring the order whereby two annual increments with cumulative effect had been stopped.
The respondents by order dated 21101999 rejected the claim of the petitioner.
Consequently, the petitioner was constrained to file the present writ petition.
2.
The written statement has been filed.
The respondents have controverted the case set up by the petitioner.
It has been submitted that the pay of the petitioner has already been refixed by orders dated 1931993 and 3.12.1996 respectively.
The benefit of two increments has been granted to the petitioner from the due date and the arrears of pay have already been paid to the petitioner on 1011994.
3.
Mr. Chopra has vehemently argued that the averment made by the respondents is wholly incorrect and in fact, the petitioner is only being paid a sum of Rs. 228/-.
In fact, the petitioner is entitled to pay fixation as recommended by the District Commander as on 1061993.
According to the table (Annexure P2), the pay of the petitioner had to be refixed at Rs. 1000/- on 121985 and at Rs. 1040/- on 121986.
On this basis, the arrears of pay had to be worked out.
Learned senior counsel further submits that the pay has been wrongly refixed by not granting the benefit of proficiency step up to the petitioner from the date he was permitted to cross efficiency bar i.e. with effect from 121984.
This benefit has been denied to the petitioner on the basis of entry in the service record of the petitioner commencing from the year 1970- 71.
The respondents have also taken into account entries for the years 1986- 87, 1987-88.
4.
We have considered the submission made by the counsel for the parties.
We do not find much substance in the submission made by Mr. Sran to the effect that even old and stale entries from the year 1970-71 upto the year 1977 could be taken into account while determining the case of the petitioner for the grant of proficiency step up.
The High Court in CWP No. 3168 of 1994 came to the conclusion that ACRs which deal with the period of 1970-71 onwards do not give any remarks justifying the same.
It has further been held that the Court is left guessing as to the nature of the remarks so as to find out, whether these were in fact adverse.
It has further been observed that the entries for the year 1987-88 and 1993-94 were never conveyed to the petitioner.
Between the years 1988 and 1993, the entries were in fact "Good/Very Good."
Admittedly, the petitioner has crossed the efficiency bar with effect from 121984.
We, therefore, see absolutely no justification as to why the petitioner should not be granted benefits of proficiency step up by counting a period of eight years from the date when he crossed the efficiency bar i.e. 121984.
5.
In view of the above, the petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to refix the pay of the petitioner by granting the benefit of proficiency step up in accordance with rules.
The eight years period is to be counted from the date the instructions came into operation.
Let the necessary exercise be done within a period of two months from the date of receipt of certified copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
