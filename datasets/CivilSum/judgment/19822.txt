RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The primary relief claimed by the petitioners in this case is that the petitioners' Institution is entitled to exemption from the applicability of the provisions of Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1959 (hereinafter called the Act), inasmuch as they are following and implementing the Scheme of the affiliating University of the Institution i.e. Guru Nanak Dev University.
The petitioners had filed a representation dated 14th July, 1989 with the respondents, (copy of which has been attached as Annexure P-8) asking for the said relief.
According to the petitioners, no decision has been taken on the representation.
In the written statement filed by the respondents, the stand taken is that the petitioners' institution does not fulfil the requisite conditions for the exemption.
2.
Without going into the merits of the case, I deem it proper at this stage to direct the respondent-Authorities to decide the petitioners representation by passing speaking order.
The petitioners would be at liberty to supplement the representation already submitted, within a fortnight from today, if it is so desired and on receipt of any such supplementary representation, if any, the respondents shall decide the matter within two months thereafter by a speaking order.
If no supplementary representation is received by Ist May, 1991, the respondents shall be at liberty to decide the representation already with them, within two months from Ist May, 1991.
3.
Interim order dated 31st July, 1989 shall continue till the matter is disposed of by the respondent-Authorities.
4.
This writ petition stands disposed of with the above observations.
5.
A copy of this order be given Dasti to the counsel of the parties, if applied.
Order accordingly.
