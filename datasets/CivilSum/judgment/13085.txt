Viney Mittal, J - The petitioner has approached this Court through the present petition filed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
The prayer made in the petition is for the issuance of a writ in the nature of certiorari for quashing the order dated December 9, 2002 vide which the claim of the petitioner for the grant of the benefit of past service has been rejected.
A copy of the aforesaid order dated December 9, 2002 has been appended as Annexure P/13 with the present petition.
2.
The petitioner initially joined the service of the Director Public Instructions (Schools), Punjab as a Clerk on July 27, 1976.
He continued to work as such upto September 8, 1977.
Thereafter he was appointed as a Clerk in the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board on regular basis on April 3, 1979.
On that basis he worked upto April 8, 1983.
3.
In March, 1983, the petitioner applied for the post of clerk in the respondent-Punjab Urban Development Authority through proper channel.
His application was forwarded by the Managing Director of the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board, The petitioner was found to be suitable and as such he was selected for the aforesaid post.
An appointment letter dated March 25, 1983 was issued in this regard.
A copy of the aforesaid appointment letter has been appended as Annexure P/3 with the petition.
On his aforesaid appointment, the petitioner was ordered to be relieved from the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board on March 25, 1983 to join his new assignment at PUDA.
The petitioner submitted the joining report at PUDA as a Clerk on April 11, 1983.
As per the appointment letter, the pay of the petitioner was also protected by the respondent-PUDA.
4.
In a Board meeting held on October 7, 1980, the respondent-PUDA vide agenda item No. 5325 had taken a decision that all the employees joining PUDA would be given the benefit of regular service in the previous Government departments/semi-Government or Public Undertakings on the same post for further promotion.
The case of the petitioner was duly covered under the aforesaid agenda item and as such the petitioner submitted an application dated September 12, 1994 along with the documentary evidence indicating his past service as Clerk in the previous departments.
The request of the petitioner was forwarded by the Administrative Officer (Headquarters) to the competent authority vide communication dated September 16, 1994.
He made another request in the year 1998 requesting the authorities to allow him the benefit of past service.
He also claimed that similar benefits had been given to one Tara Singh.
He served a legal notice dated March 14, 2002 with regard to the aforesaid claim.
No action was taken by the respondents.
Accordingly, the petitioner approached this Court through Civil Writ Petition No. 12968 of 2002.
The aforesaid Civil Writ Petition was disposed of by a Division Bench of this Court vide order dated August 19, 2002.
The respondents were directed to decide the legal notice served by the petitioner.
Thereafter the respondent- PUDA authorities passed the impugned order dated December 5, 2002 with an endorsement dated December 9, 2002 vide which the claim of the petitioner for the grant of benefits of the past service was rejected.
A copy of the aforesaid order dated December 5, 2002 with endorsement dated December 9, 2002 has been appended as Annexure P/13 with the petition.
5.
The petitioner is now aggrieved against the aforesaid order and has approached this Court by way of the present petition.
6.
The respondents have put in appearance and filed a written statement contesting the claim of the petitioner.
It has been averred in the written statement that in view of agenda item No. 7920 in the meeting dated April 10, 1986, the claim of the petitioner for the benefit of the past service could not be accepted and as such had been rightly rejected by the authorities.
The respondents have also maintained that the aforesaid claim has been duly considered by the competent authority and thereafter the order Annexure P/13 has been passed keeping in view the agenda item No. 7920 decided on April 10, 1986.
7.
The petitioner has also filed a replication to the written statement filed by the respondents.
We have heard Shri Vipin Mahajan, the learned counsel appearing for the petitioner and Shri Vivek Chauhan, Advocate for Shri Govind Goel, Advocate, the learned counsel appearing for the respondents and with their assistance have also gone through the record of the case.
8.
The factum of the petitioner joining the Punjab Urban Development Authority vide appointment letter dated March 25, 1983 (Annexure P/3) is not in dispute.
It is also not in dispute that in pursuance of the aforesaid letter, the petitioner has submitted his joining report on April 11, 1983.
The pay of the petitioner had been protected at the time of his appointment taking into consideration his "last pay drawn".
The respondents have merely contested the claim of the petitioner on the basis of the decision taken in agenda item No. 7920 dated April 10, 1986.
9.
Shri Vipin Mahajan, the learned counsel appearing for the petitioner submits that a specific decision was taken by the Board in the meeting dated October 7, 1980 vide agenda item No. 5325 (Annexure P/6) that all the employees who joined the regular service of PUDA would be entitled to be given the benefit of their regular service in the previous Government departments/Semi-Government or Public Undertaking on the same post for further promotion.
On that basis Shri Mahajan maintains that in view of the aforesaid decision alone, the petitioner joined the service of the Punjab Urban Development Authority.
It has been further maintained that any subsequent decision taken in the year 1986 i.e. vide agenda item No. 7920 dated April 10, 1986 was merely prospective in nature and could not have effected the vested rights of persons like the petitioner who had joined prior thereto on the basis of the decision dated October 7, 1980 vide item No. 5325.
10.
On the other hand Shri Vivek Chauhan, the learned counsel for the respondents maintains that in view of the subsequent decision dated April 10, 1986, the claim of the petitioner could not be accepted by the respondents and, therefore, the same had been validly rejected by passing the order Annexure P/13.
11.
We have given our thoughtful consideration to the rival arguments raised by the learned counsel for the parties.
In our considered view the present petition deserves to succeed.
12.
We have perused the agenda item No. 7920, a copy whereof has been appended as Annexure R/1 with the written statement filed by the respondents.
From the perusal thereof, we do not find that the same operates retrospectively in any manner.
When the petitioner had joined the service of the respondent-authority in the year 1983, then at that time, the decision agenda item No. 5325 dated October 7, 1980 was in operation.
The petitioner had joined on the basis of the aforesaid agenda item No. 5325.
It is also not in dispute that a person similar to the petitioner, namely, Tara Singh had been granted the similar benefit.
We do not find that the respondent-authority was justified in any manner to reject the just claim of the petitioner.
13.
Accordingly, we allow the present petition and quash the order dated December 5, 2002 with endorsement dated December 9, 2002 (Annexure P/13).
It is further directed that the petitioner would be entitled to the grant of benefits of his past service in view of the decision vide agenda item No. 5325 dated October 7, 1980.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to all consequential reliefs of the grant of the aforesaid benefit.
The respondents are directed to implement this order within a period of four months from the date a certified copy of this order is received by them.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
