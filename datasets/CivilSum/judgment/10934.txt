VS Aggarwal, J (Oral) - Harnek Singh had filed an application under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
His contention was that he was a causal worker on daily wage with the Punjab State Electricity Board.
He claimed the salary of temporary workman (work charge T Mate) on the principle of `Equal pay for equal work'.
The learned Presiding Officer, Labour Court, Ludhiana before whom the matter was pending, allowed the application and held that the respondent-workman is entitled to the difference of salary and bonus as claimed by him.
2.
The question in controversy requires little further probing because of the decision of this Court in the case of Mangat Hospital v. Presiding Officer, Labour Court, Jalandhar, 1999(1) Recent Services Judgments 6 : 1998(4) SCT 742 (P&H).
It was held that in such like cases where the right has not been adjudicated, Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act cannot be pressed into service.
Similar is the position herein.
As an offshoot of the aforesaid, it must follow that the writ petition necessarily must be allowed.
Accordingly, the impugned order is quashed.
However, nothing said herein would restrain the workman from raising an industrial dispute about which no opinion is being expressed.
Petition allowed.
