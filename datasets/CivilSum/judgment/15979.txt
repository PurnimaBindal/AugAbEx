Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - The petitioner prays for a writ in the nature of Mandamus directing the respondents to refix his pension after taking into consideration the service rendered by him from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963.
A few facts may be noticed.
2.
The petitioner joined service as a Nursing Tutor at the VM Civil Hospital at Jalandhar on April 7, 1950.
He continued to work as such till February 28, 1959 when he went to Australia under the Colombo Plan Scheme.
On return from Australia, the petitioner was granted study leave from May 1, 1961.
He remained at the All India Institute of Medical Science till March, 1963.
The petitioner joined the PGI under the orders of Director, Research and Medical Education, Punjab, on April 1, 1963.
He held different posts at different intervals till January 31, 1980, when he retired on superannuation.
The PGI gave him pension for the period of service from April 1, 1963 to Jan 31, 1980.
The petitioner represented.
However, his request was not accepted.
Ultimately, the petitioner approached this Court through the present writ petition with a prayer that the respondents be directed to refix his pension after taking into consideration the service rendered by him from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963.
3.
The respondent-Institute contested the petitioner's claim.
It was pointed out that "the Institute has written a number of letters (Copies attached Annexures R-1 to R-9) to Director, Health Services Punjab regarding the period of service rendered by the petitioner under the Punjab Government but the Punjab Government has not confirmed whether the petitioner had worked in the Punjab Government from 741950 to 3131963."
It is also averred that the State of Punjab was a necessary party to the case.
4.
The matter came up for consideration on July 20, 1995.
On the request of counsel for the petitioner, the State of Punjab was impleaded as respondent No. 4.
In pursuance to the notice issued by the Court, a written statement has been filed on behalf of the State Government.
The petitioner has also filed various documents to prove that he had actually worked in the State Government from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963.
5.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the State of Punjab, it has been, inter alia, averred that the petitioner had worked as Nursing Tutor "from 741950 to 131959 on contract basis".
On this basis, it has been stated that "the petitioner is not entitled for any pensionary benefits for the service rendered by him at VM Hospital Jalandhar as Nursing Tutor as he worked on contract basis."
6.
Counsel for the parties have been heard.
7.
On behalf of the petitioner, it has been contended by Mr. HS Sangha, Advocate that the documents produced on the record clearly show that the petitioner had joined service with the State Government on April 7, 1950 and had continued to work as such till March 31, 1963.
On behalf of the State, Mr. Parminder Singh has very fairly pointed out that on checking up from the record, the State Government had prepared a duplicate Service Book and the period of service rendered by the petitioner from April 7, 1950 to June 30, 1962 has been duly verified.
He, however, points out that with regard to period from July 1, 1962 to March 31, 1963, the department has not been able to verify the factual position.
Mr. Nehra, learned counsel appearing for the PGI has, however, contended that the benefit of service rendered by the petitioner from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963 should not be given to him in view of the fact that it was a contractual appointment which did not qualify for pension.
8.
The two questions that arise for consideration :-  (i) Did the petitioner serve the State Government from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963 ?
(ii) Does this service qualify for pension ?
REGARDING NO. 1  9.
Admittedly the period of service from April 7, 1950 to June 30, 1962 has been duly verified by the respondent.
As for the period from April 1, 1962 to March 31, 1963, the petitioner has produced on the record a copy of letter dated October 20, 1966 sent by the Chief Medical Officer, Jalandhar to the Director Health Services, Punjab, Chandigarh with a copy to the Director of the PGI By this letter, it was "requested that the copy of the Government sanction for the period 171962 to March 31, 1963 in continuation of his previous study leave, mentioned above may please be supplied to this office so that the service book of the official concerned may be completed".
It, thus, appears that the petitioner was on study leave from April 1, 1962 to March 31, 1963.
The department had sought a copy of the order of sanction given by the State Government.
The occasion for this could have arisen only if the petitioner was serving the department.
Though this letter was produced by the petitioner alongwith various documents, the respondents have not challenged its correctness or filed any affidavit to indicate that the letter had not been issued.
In view of the letter dated October 20, 1966, it is clear that the petitioner had worked in the service of the Health Department in the State of Punjab till March 31, 1963.
10.
In view of this position, it is clear that the petitioner was an employee of the State Government from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963.
The first question is answered accordingly.
REGARDING NO. 2.
11.
The petitioner was appointed as a Nursing Tutor vide order dated January 18, 1950.
A copy of this letter has been produced as Annexure P-31, by the petitioner.
It appears that the petitioner had submitted an application for appointment as Tutor on October 1, 1949.
The appointment was offered to him by the Director of Health Services.
A perusal of this letter does not at all indicate that the appointment was being made purely on contractual basis or that the post of a Tutor was not pensionable.
In spite of this, Mr. Nehra contends that the appointment being contractual, the petitioner is not entitled to count the period of service from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963 towards pension.
For this purpose, the learned counsel has placed reliance on a letter dated November 16, 1968 sent by the petitioner to the Director Health Services.
In this letter, the petitioner had mentioned that he had worked at VM Civil Hospital, Jullundur from April 7, 1950 to March 1, 1959 when he proceeded to Australia.
He had further pointed out that he had joined service as Nursing Superintending at the PGI on April 1, 1963.
In para No. 3 of the letter, the petitioner had stated as under :-  "I was on contract service at Jullundur (DHS) Punjab.
My contract was to be over in 1965 when I was to be given pension right as clearly stated in your letter MC WN 2645459 dated 30121964."
12.
It is on the basis of this statement in the letter that the learned counsel has raised the contention that the appointment being contractual, the petitioner was not entitled to the grant of any pension for the period of his service from 1950 to 1963.
13.
The argument raised by the learned counsel does credit to his ingenuity but is not tenable in law.
Every appointment begins with a contract.
It does not, however, mean that the post is not pensionable.
Secondly, the original letter of appointment is on the record.
As already noticed, the order did not indicate that the appointment was purely contractual or that the petitioner shall not be entitled to any pension.
Thirdly, even the statement made by the petitioner as mentioned above, was only to the effect that the contract of appointment was for a period of 15 years whereafter he was entitled to be given pension.
It did not mean that the post of Nursing Tutor held by him was not pensionable.
It also deserves notice that the learned counsel for the respondents had not been able to refer to any Rule to show that the post did not carry the benefit of pension or that even if the service was assumed to be contractual in the beginning, it would not qualify for pension.
14.
Accordingly, the second question is answered in the negative.
It is held that the period of service from April 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963 qualify for pension.
15.
No other point was urged.
16.
In view of above, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to refix the petitioner's pension after taking into consideration the period of service rendered by him from March 7, 1950 to March 31, 1963.
The needful shall be done within three months from today.
As a consequential relief, the petitioner shall be entitled to arrears of pension from February 1, 1980 onwards.
These arrears shall be paid to him alongwith interest at the rate of 12% from the date of accrual till the date of actual payment.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
