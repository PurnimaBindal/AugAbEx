MR Agnihotri, J - After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and having gone through their pleadings, we find that as the petitioner has passed the Matriculation Examination from the Bihar School Examination Board, Patna, which is established by the State Government of Bihar, The Matriculation Examination stands recognised in view of the policy decision of the Haryana Government circulated on 18th March, 1975, which reads as under :-  "No. 2184- Edu.
(4E)-75/8784  From :  Commissioner & Secretary Haryana Govt.  Education Department.
To  All Heads of Department of State (etc.)  dated, the Chandigarh, 18th March, 1975.
Sub : Recognition to various qualifications for recruitment.
Sir,  It is to inform you to draw your attention towards the above noted subject that regarding the recognition of various qualifications the matter was under consideration of the Government for change in the present system.
Now the Govt. has taken following decisions :-  (i) Those degrees or diplomas etc. which are recognised by Govt. of India, those shall deem to be recognized by Govt. of Haryana also.
(ii) Those degrees or diplomas etc. which have been awarded by recognised Universities and by the Boards established by State Govt. for High /Higher Secondary those, will be recognised ipso facto.
Accordingly, we allow this petition and quash condition No. 2 from the impugned letter dated 18th September 1992, (Annexure P.4) and direct the respondents to consider the petitioner for promotion to the post of Drama Inspector, treating his academic qualifications as equivalent to Matriculation for the purpose of employment and further promotion in service.
