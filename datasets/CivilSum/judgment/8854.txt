THB Chalapathi, J - This writ petition is filed to quash the order of the Labour Court, Patiala dated 16121993.
2.
The Ist respondent was appointed as Class IV employee in the scale of Rs. 300-430 for a period of 89 days on temporary basis and her services have been extended from time to time upto 22.6.1989.
Thereafter she was not given any further appointment.
Therefore, the Ist respondent raised an industrial dispute which was referred to the Labour Court for adjudication.
On the basis of the material placed before it, the Labour Court held that the Ist respondent worked for 265 days and her services were wrongfully terminated, without any notice, charge-sheet or enquiry and therefore, the provisions of Section 25-F are attracted and accordingly ordered reinstatement of the Ist respondent with full back wages.
3.
There is no dispute that the Ist respondent worked for 265 days ie from 28.9.1988 to 22.6.1989.
There is also no dispute that without following the provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act the services of the petitioners have been terminated and, therefore, I do not find any ground warranting interference with the award passed by the Labour Court directing the reinstatement of the Ist respondent.
4.
The learned Counsel for the petitioner argued that the services of the Ist respondent were terminated on 22.6.1989 but she raised the industrial dispute only on 2721992 i.e. after a period of two and a half years and there was no material before the Labour Court that the Ist respondent was not gainfully employed and, therefore, the order of the Labour Court directing the payment of full back wages is liable to be set aside.
5.
In similar circumstances, a Division Bench of this Court in CWP No. 16133 of 1993 decided on 2431994 held that "the workman is entitled to back wages with effect from the date of demand notice and not from the date of termination of his services till the raising of the industrial dispute."
In that case, there was a delay of two years and seven months.
Following the decision of the said Division Bench, I am of the opinion that the Ist respondent is not entitled to full back wages for the period from 22.6.1989 till 2721992, the date of demand notice.
6.
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed partly and the award of the Labour Court is modified directing the reinstatment of the Ist respondent with back wages from the date of raising of the industrial dispute i.e. 2721992.
No order as to costs.
