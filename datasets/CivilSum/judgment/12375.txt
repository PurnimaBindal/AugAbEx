THB Chalapathi, J - The petitioner has filed this writ petition seeking a direction to the Government to count ad hoc service rendered by him with effect from 18.9.1970 to 31121979 as a Family Welfare Extension Educator.
2.
The petitioner has been working as Block Extension Educator.
He was initially appointed on 18.9.1970 on ad hoc basis.
He worked as such till 31121979.
The State Government by a Notification dated 111980, excluded Class III post from the purview of Subordinate Services Selection Board in the case of ad hoc employees who are having a minimum period of 12 years as on 31121979 and regularised the services of certain employees in pursuance of the said notification.
Since the petitioner has been continuously working from 1970 to 31121979, his services were also regularised with effect from 111980 by an office Order 14111980.
The State Government in order to give incentive to Govt. employees facing stagnation framed a policy to release higher scale of pay for those who completed 10 years and 20 years of satisfactory service vide an order dated 281994.
The said benefit was denied to the petitioner on the ground that the ad hoc service rendered by him from 18.9.1970 to 31121979 cannot be counted for the purpose of granting the benefit of higher pay scale.
Therefore, he approached this Court by way of this writ petition.
3.
A written statement has been filed by the respondents admitting that the petitioner has been working as Block Extension Educator in the Primary Health Centre.
It is also admitted that the petitioner has been working as such from 18.9.1970 as an ad hoc employee till his services were regularised with effect from 111980.
It is further contended that the benefit of higher pay scale has been extended only to regular employees and therefore, the service of the petitioner as ad hoc employee cannot be counted for the purpose of granting him the higher pay.
4.
A Full Bench of this Court in Rakesh Kumar Singla v. State of Haryana, 1995(4) R.S.J.
586 : 1995(4) SCT 285 (P&H)(FB) has held that the ad hoc service has to be counted for the purpose of seniority and would also be counted for the purpose of grant of selection grade while computing 12 years' regular service as contemplated in the policy decision notified by the Government of Haryana in its circular letter dated 261989.
5.
A similar question has been raised in the writ petition that came up before a Division Bench of this Court in Surender Singh v. State of Haryana, 1997(4) R.S.J.
409 : 1998(1) SCT 53 (P&H)(DB) wherein it has been held that the ad hoc service rendered which is countable for the purpose of seniority and other service benefits shall be counted for the purpose of regular satisfactory service in the context of circular dated 821994.
The facts of the present case are identical with the case decided by the Division Bench of this Court referred to above.
6.
I, therefore, allow the writ petition and direct the respondents to count the ad hoc service rendered by the petitioner for the purpose of granting him the benefit of higher pay scale as contemplated in the policy decision of the Haryana Government as contained in its Circular letter dated 821989.
It is further directed that the decision of this Court be implemented within three months from the date of the judgment and arrears of pay shall be restricted to be period of 38 months prior to the date of the petition i.e. 121985.
Petition allowed.
