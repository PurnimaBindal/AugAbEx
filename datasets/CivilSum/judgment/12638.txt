Mohinder Pal, J - The petitioner was enrolled in the Army in the Corps of Engineers on May 26, 1982.
He got promotions from time to time and at present he holds the rank of Naib Subedar (a JCO Rank).
The question involved in this writ petition is regarding the extension of service of the petitioner by two years, to which he would normally be entitled on attaining the rank of Naib Subedar.
The extension of two years is due with effect from June 01, 2008 and if the petitioner is given this extension he would continue in service till May 31, 2010.
2.
The respondents have the policy for giving such extensions which is contained in letter dated September 21, 1998, which has been amended from time to time.
The petitioner's case for the grant of extension in service was considered in the light of policy contained in letter dated September 21,1998, and amended vide letter dated November 22, 2005.
As the petitioner was granted three days' rigorous imprisonment under Section 39 (b) of the Army Act on August 25, 1984, for overstaying the leave when he was in the lower rank of Sapper, the extension in service by two years was not granted to him.
3.
It is case of the petitioner that his case for extension of two years in service could not be declined in view of the policy of the respondents as contained in the letter dated June 13, 2007.
4.
Notice of motion was issued in this case on April 08, 2008.
In the reply filed by the respondents in Court today, it has been pleaded that the petitioner is not entitled to the benefit, as contained in letter dated June 13, 2007 (Annexure R-4), which prescribes the procedure and criteria for screening of J.C.Os.
for granting extension of two years, as the provisions of this letter were brought into operation from June 13, 2007.
Since the formalities of Screening Board for grant of extension to the petitioner had already been completed by the time new policy on screening came into being i.e on June 13, 2007, this policy (Annexure R-4) is not applicable in the case of the petitioner because reopening of past cases is restricted in the policy.
5.
We have herd the learned counsel for the parties and have gone through the records of the case.
6.
According to the policy as contained in letter dated June 13, 2007 (Annexure R-4), the J.C.Os who are convicted under Sections 39(a), 39(b) and 45 of the Army Act during service as J.C.Os, will be debarred from extension.
Thus, any punishment in the lower ranks under the above-mentioned offences will not debar an extension in the rank of the JCO The case of the petitioner is covered under the policy (Annexure R-4) as he was awarded punishment under Section 39(b) when he was in the rank of Sapper.
The plea of the respondents that as the case of extension of the petitioner had been declined before June 13, 2007, he is not entitled to the same, is not at all tenable.
The discharge order dated May 08, 2007 (Annexure P-1) is effective from May 31, 2008.
However, much before the said order could be effectuated, which has not been given effect to till date as May 31, 1008 is yet to approach, the respondents themselves clarified their policy dated September 23,1998 by issuing letter dated June 13, 2007 (Annexure R-4).
The petitioner having not yet retired, is covered under the policy (Annexure R-4).
Admittedly, the petitioner has not been punished for any offence under Sections 39(a), 39(b) and 45 of the Army Act during his service as JCO As such, as per the clarification contained in letter dated June 13, 2007 (Annexure R-4), he is entitled to extension.
Similar view was taken by a Division Bench of Delhi High Court in the case of Naib Subedar Brijesh Kumar Sharma v. Union of India and others, (Civil Writ Petition No. 8860 of 2007 decided on January 14, 2008).
7.
For the aforesaid reasons, this writ petition is allowed and the respondents are directed to consider the case of the petitioner for extension of two years in service in terms of the policy dated September 21, 1998 read with the clarification dated June 13, 2007 forthwith as the petitioner is going to retire on May 31, 2008.
A copy of this order be given `Dasti'.
Petition allowed.
