AL Bahri, J - This petition can be disposed of by passing a fair and reasonable order.

The dispute which remains to be decided is for the grant of interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum with effect from the date of retirement till payment of G.P.F.
which was due to the petitioner on his retirement.
3.
Gurmail Singh petitioner retired from Punjab Government service as Drawing teacher on April 30, 1999.
His G.P.F.
arrears were paid to him on April 27, 2000, amounting to Rs. 4,81,423/-.
The contention of the State counsel is that some delay occurred as the petitioner did not submit the necessary documents.
Whatever may be the reason the money remained with the State Government, which belonged to the petitioner and was kept in CPF account and since there is a delay of about one year in making payment of the CPF the State Government is supposed to pay interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum for that period on the amount aforesaid.
Such directions were given by the Ist Lok Adalat in CWP No. 10065 of 1998 (Bharat Bhushan Sood v. State of Haryana and others), decided on December 21, 1999.
Hence this writ petition is disposed of with the direction to the respondents to pay interest on the aforesaid amount with effect from May 1, 1999, till April 27, 2000, within a period of two months.
It is made clear that if any other dispute regarding calculations of the principal amount due is being raised, the petitioner would be at liberty to approach the respondents or the authority concerned.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
The copy of the order be given to the counsel for the parties.
Petition allowed.
