Ashok Bhan, J - This order shall dispose of the present letters patent appeal which arises from an order passed by the learned Single Judge dismissing execution application No. 3882 of 1989 arising from C.W.P.
No. 2253 of 1184 and C.W.P.
No. 12375 of 1990 in which the same relief i.e. the order passed by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 2253 of 1984 be implemented has been sought and that is why they are being disposed of together by this order.
2.
Smt. Kirpaljeet Kaur (hereinafter referred to as the appellant) filed C.W.P.
No. 2253 of 1984, who was working as Technical Assistant in the office of Director Public Instructions, Punjab in the pay scale of Rs. 200-500 seeking equation and parity with the pay scales of Lecturers (junior scale).
This writ petition was allowed by the learned Single Judge on 2891987 and in conclusion, the following directions were issued :-  "Consequently, the writ petition is allowed and a writ of mandamus is issued to the respondent State of Punjab to release to the petitioner the same pay scale, that is Rs. 700-1600, which was given to the Lecturers (junior scale), with effect from the date the same was released to those lecturers alongwith the consequential provision for the grant of selection grade.
The petitioner shall be entitled to the costs of this petition which are quantified at Rs.
1000/-."
Since the respondents did not implement the mandamus issued by this Court, the appellant filed C.O.C.P.
No. 44 of 1988 which was disposed of by MR Agnihotri, J on 4.10.1988, with the following directions :-  "Consequently, the respondents are once again directed to pay to the petitioner the arrears of the pay and allowances for the entire period with effect from 1st January, 1973, within one month."
3.
On the basis of the judgment in the writ petition and the aforesaid contempt petition, the petitioner was released the pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 with effect from 111973 from the date it was released to the Lecturers (junior scale).
4.
The appellant was still not satisfied with the relief.
Her grievance being that she has not been given the full relief as per the directions issued by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 2253 of 1984, and, therefore, filed C.O.C.P.
No. 185 of 1989, claiming that she should be given the senior scale as well as the selection grade as was given to the Lecturers.
In reply to the contempt petition, the following stand was taken by the Secretary to Govt. Punjab, Department of Education :-  "The petitioner has already been released Rs. 700-1600 grade w.e.f.
111973.
She has also been allowed full arrears w.e.f.
111973 amounting to Rs. 21,951.60 and Rs. 11,25295 vide Punjab Govt.
Memo No. 3/84/87-2 Edu.
VII/4230 dated 25989 (Annexure R-1), the petitioner has been allowed the revised scale of Rs. 2100-3700, which is a selection grade available to school Lecturers after 18 years of service, as a measure personal to her.
"with the release of Rs. 700-1600 grade and its conversion to Rs. 2100-3700 which is a Selection Grade, there is thus nothing more to be done to implement the High Court decision passed in Civil Writ Petition No. 2253/1984.
The contempt proceedings are also consequently liable to be dismissed."
5.
Learned Single Judge while disposing of this contempt petition observed that the directions given by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 2253 of 1984 have been fully complied with and stand implemented.
Rule was ordered to be discharged.
6.
Thereafter, the appellant filed CM No. 3882 of 1989 treating it to be an execution application.
This application was dismissed by MR Agnihotri, J on 20.2.1990 against which the present letters patent appeal has been filed.
7.
Counsel for the appellant has failed to show from the High Court Rules and Orders that any such execution application was maintainable specially after the dismissal of the contempt petition in which a finding was recorded that the directions issued by the learned Single Judge in the writ petition stood implemented and fully complied with.
Presuming the execution application was maintainable, even then no appeal against the said orders is maintainable according to the Code of Civil Procedure.
L.P.A. is thus dismissed being not maintainable.
8.
In C.W.P.
No. 12375 of 1990, the appellant has claimed the same relief i.e. implementation of the judgment of the learned Single Judge in C.W.P.
No. 2253 of 1984.
A writ petition seeking the implementation of an order or a direction issued in the earlier writ petition is not maintainable.
Moreover, petitioner had filed a contempt petition in which a finding was recorded that the directions issued by the learned Single Judge in the writ petition have been complied with and stood implemented.
The findings recorded in the contempt petition having become final, the present writ petition seeking the same direction would neither be maintainable in equity nor in law.
We thus find no merit either in the letters patent appeal or in the civil writ petition which are dismissed with costs which are assessed at Rs. 1000/
