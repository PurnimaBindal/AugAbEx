Hemant Gupta, J - The challenge in the present revision petition is to the order dated 30.6.1988 passed by the Authority under the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 and the order in appeal dated 1811989 against the aforesaid order.
2.
The petitioner has filed a petition under Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (hereinafter referred to as the Act), claiming a sum of Rs. 209/- on account of his wages for the period 141985 to 2821986 and bonus for the year 1985-86.
3.
It was found by the learned Authority that two increments of the petitioner were stopped by the order of the disciplinary Authority i.e. General Manager, after a charge-sheet was served upon the petitioner and the reply received.
Therefore, it was found that deductions are not illegal.
The said order was affirmed in appeal as well.
4.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, I am of the opinion that the petition under Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 itself was not maintainable.
It has been so held by this Court in the case of State of Punjab v. Dildar Singh, 2007(1) SCT 202 (P&H) : 2007(1) Service Cases Today 202 and General Manager, Punjab Roaldways, Ropar v. Swaran Singh, 2001(3) SCT 99 : 2001(3) RSJ 41 and The General Manager, Punjab Roadways v. Ajit Singh Conductor, 1999(4) SCT 388 : 2000(1) Recent Services Judgements 276, while relying upon the judgment of Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in State of Punjab and others v. Baldev Singh Conductor, 1999(1) SCT 536 : 1999(1) RSJ 201, that the Authority constituted under the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 has no jurisdiction to interfere in the order passed by the Disciplinary Authority against the employee concerned.
In view of the above, the present revision petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
