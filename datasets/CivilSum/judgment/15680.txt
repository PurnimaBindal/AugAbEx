RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The facts of the case are in a very narrow compass.
2.
There is one post of Director of Public Instructions (Primary) in the State of Punjab.
Petitioner, who was working as Deputy Director, Public Instructions was given additional charge of the post of Director, Public Instructions (Primary) vide order dated August 30, 1996, copy at Annexure P1/I It was mentioned in that order that petitioner wouldn't be given any extra financial benefit for his work and he would work in his own pay scale.
3.
On December 3, 1996, petitioner was posted against the vacant post of Director, Public Instructions (Primary) in his own pay scale.
The order, which is Annexure P1/II reads as under :-  "Sh. ML Sachdeva, Deputy Director, School Administration-I is hereby posted against the vacant post of Director, Public Instructions (Primary), Punjab in his own pay scale.
Sh. ML Sachdeva will draw pay in his own pay scale against the post of Director, Public Instructions (P)."
4.
On 27111997, the petitioner was promoted as Director, Public Instructions (Primary) in the pay scale of the post of Director i.e. Rs. 4500-6700 with effect from September 1, 1996, the date he was given the additional charge of the post of Director, Public Instructions and had been working as such.
It would be apposite to reproduce the order dated November 27, 1997 :-  "Dr. ML Sachdeva, now working as Director of Public Instructions (Schools) Punjab in his own pay scale is hereby promoted as Director, Public Instructions in the pay scale of Rs. 4500-125-5000-200-6100 with effect from 191996, the date from which he has been working as Director of Public Instructions."
5.
On December 21, 1997 an order was issued that petitioner would retire on December 31, 1997 after attaining the age of 58 years plus two years extension.
Another order was issued on May 12, 1998, by the Secretary, Government of Punjab, who had earlier issued order dated November 27, 1997 (reproduced above) that in fact petitioner could not have been retrospectively promoted as Director, Public Instructions with effect from September 1, 1996 and he could only be considered to have been promoted as such with effect from November 20, 1997 on the recommendation of the Personnel and Administrative Department.
Copy of order has been appended as Annexure P.7 which reads as under :-  "On the advice given by the Personnel and Administrative Reforms Department vide its Memo No. 3/49/963 PPI/21327 dated November 20, 1997, the department promoted Dr. ML Sachdeva (Retired) as Director Public Instructions (Schools), Punjab Chandigarh vide its order No. 1/78/96-3 Edu.
4/4421 dated 281197 in the pay scale of Rs. 4500-125-5000-150-5900-200-6700.
Personnel & Administrative Reforms Department has objected that Dr. ML Sachdeva (Retd.)
has been given promotion w.e.f.
20111997 whereas the Administrative Department has promoted him w.e.f.
191996 meaning thereby from the date he was given current duty charge.
2.
After giving the above mentioned order the Personnel and Administrative Reforms Department has written that there is no instruction from the Government to promote the officer from the date of having current duty charge.
It was also added that promotion of Dr. ML Sachdeva be considered from 20111997 on the recommendation of the Personnel and Administration Reforms Department order No. 1/78/96-3 Edu.
4/4421 dated 20.11.97 is being amended and now Dr. ML Sachdeva is promoted from 20111997 instead of 191996."
Primarily the above order has been made the subject matter of challenge in the present writ petition.
The ancillary prayer is that petitioner should be released death-cum-retirement gratuity.
6.
Petitioner argued that though he was entitled to be promoted as Director Public Instructions (Primary) w.e.f.
a date in April 1996 when the incumbent of the post retired, yet without any rhyme or reason he was not given that promotion with effect from the due date.
He further submitted that as per order dated August 30, 1996, Annexure P.1/I he actually held the post of Director Public Instructions (Primary) with effect from September 1, 1996 continuously till his retirement and the respondents correctly passed the order on November 27, 1997 promoting the petitioner as Director Public Instructions (Schools) w.e.f.
September 1, 1996.
7.
Petitioner also argued that he is entitled to the pay of the post of Director Public Instructions right from September 1, 1996.
He submitted that in case it is held that he was only given additional charge or current duty charge w.e.f.
September 1, 1996 vide order dated August 30, 1996 (Annexure P.1/I), there is no dispute that he was given regular charge of the post after the order dated December 3, 1996 (Annexure P1/II) was passed.
It should be this date, in that eventuality, which should be considered as the date of promotion of the petitioner as Director Public Instructions and also for purpose of pay in that post.
In support of his contention, he has cited 1999(2) SCT 286, a judgment of the apex Court.
8.
On the other hand, counsel for the respondents, however, argued that petitioner could not be given retrospective promotion because petitioner could have been considered for promotion only after the recommendation of the Personnel and Administrative Reforms Department and was not entitled as a matter of right to retrospective promotion.
9.
After hearing the petitioner in person and learned counsel for the respondents, we are of the view that there is substance in the arguments of the petitioner and the writ petition is liable to succeed.
10.
From the reading of the order of Annexure P.1/I, it is evident that the petitioner was only given additional charge of the post of Director Public Instructions in his own pay scale.
He had been clearly told that he would not get any extra financial benefit.
However, this order was substituted by order dated December 3, 1996 (Annexure P.1/2) (already reproduced above), vide which the petitioner was posted against the vacant post of Director of Public Instructions (Primary).
No doubt it is mentioned in the order that he would be in his own pay scale, but thereafter the order dated November 27, 1997 (Annexure P-6) came promoting the petitioner w.e.f.
September 1, 1996.
This order has been changed to the effect that the petitioner would be deemed to have been promoted on Novermber 27, 1997 and not from September 1, 1996 on the gound that the Personnel and Administrative Reforms Department had sent its recommendations on that date.
The fact remains that the petitioner had been continuing on the post w.e.f.
the passing of the order at Annexure P1/II dated December 3, 1996 and prior to that w.e.f.
September 1, 1996 holding additional charge of the post.
So we find no justification for the redpondents not to promote the petitionder w.e.f.
December 3, 1996 if not from September 1, 1996 because from September 1, 1996 to December 3, 1996, the petitioner was holding only additional charge.
This is specially so because not only the post was vacant, the petitioner had actually been posted against this post on December 3, 1996.
Supposing, the Personnel and Administrative Reforms Department was to take another year to make the recommedations, would that mean that the petitioner would not get regular promotion till the Personnel and Administrative Reforms Department had made the recommedations.
To our mind, the answer has to be in the negative.
Petitioner did discharge the duties of Director Public Instructions from September 1, 1996 to December 3, 1996 holding the post as additional charge and then on regular basis.
Nothing against the petitioner can be spelled out from the orders that he could not be considered for regular promotion at least from December 3, 1996.
11.
For the foregoing reasons we allow this writ petition and quash the order dated December 21, 1997, Annexure P.7, to the extent it fixes the date of promotion of the petitioner as Director Public Instructions (Primary) as November 20, 1997.
However, as observed above, the petitioner was only given additional charge of the post till December 3, 1996 when he was actually posted against the post vide order dated December 3, 1996, copy at Annexure P1/II.
The date of promotion of the petition for all intents and purposes, including fixation of pay, arrears of pay as Director Public Instructions would be December 3, 1996.
The petitioner would be entitled to be fixation of pay, arrears of pay of and pension on the aforesaid basis.
Let these directions be carried out within a period of three months.
12.
A copy of this order, attested by the Special Secretary of this Court, be given to Mr. Chinna for onward transmission to the concerned quarters.
Petition allowed.
