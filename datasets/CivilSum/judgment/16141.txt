VK Bali, J - The only relief sought in this petition, filed under Articles 226/ 227 of the Constitution of India, is to finalise the seniority of the members of the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, this as per statement of Mr. Khehar, learned counsel appearing for the added respondents, has since been done vide orders dated February 12, 1992, copy whereof is annexed as Annexure R-Addl.
with the written statement, filed by the added respondents.
However Mr. Dhandev Singh, learned counsel appearing for the petitioner contends that what all has been done vide Annexure R-Addl1 is that a roster has been prepared under the provision of Rules 6, 8 and 9 of the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, Public Works Department, B&R Branch, Rules, 1965.
There seems to be some merit in the contention of Mr. Dhandev Singh.
Even though it is true that the seniority has to be fixed after preparing a roster under the provisions of Rules 6, 8 and 9 of 1965 Rules, yet a separate seniority list has to be prepared.
The Government of Punjab is directed to prepare a seniority list of the members of the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II in accordance with Annexure R-Addl1 within three months from today.
It is made clear that if any one is aggrieved of fixation of his seniority, he will have a right to agitate either before the Government or before this Court in accordance with law.
2.
This petition is disposed of in the manner indicated above.
However, there will be no order as to costs.
Order accordingly.
