GR Majithia, J - The workman has challenged the award of the Labour Court to the extent to which it allowed only 50% of the back wages in this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
2.
The services of the workman were terminated.
He got a reference made to the Labour Court.
The Labour Court on evidence found that the termination was illegal.
However, after so holding, it held that the workman is entitled to reinstatement in service with continuity in service but with 50% back wages.
There appears to be no justification for ordering that the workman is only entitled to the back wages to the extent to 50%.
The order cannot be sustained.
Once the termination has been set aside and there is no justifiable reason for refusing reinstatement, the consequent result must follow.
The workman is entitled to reinstatement in service with full back wages.
Civil Writ Petition stands disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed of.
