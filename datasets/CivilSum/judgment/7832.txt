Viney Mittal, J - The petitioners, who were working as Taxation Inspectors in the office of the Deputy Excise and Taxation Commissioner, Panchkula have filed the present petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for the issuance of a writ in the nature of certiorari for quashing the order dated August 30, 2001 vide which the arrears of refixation of the pay of the petitioners in the pay scales of Rs. 1600-2600with effect from January 1, 1986 instead of October 1, 1991 has been denied.
A further prayer has been made for the issuance of a writ of mandamus for directing the respondents to grant the arrears of pay to the petitioners on their refixation of pay with effect from January 1, 1986 or in any case with effect from the date of their joining instead of October 1, 1991 along with consequential benefits.
The petitioners have also prayed for the issuance of a further writ of certiorari also to quash order dated August 10, 2000 to the extent that no arrears would be paid to other Excise and Taxation Inspectors who had not filed the writ petition in this Court earlier, though their pay had been revised with effect from January 1, 1986 instead of October 1, 1991.
2.
Certain facts may be noticed.
3.
The petitioners are working as Excise Inspectors and Taxation Inspectors in the Excise and Taxation Department, Haryana.
Some of the petitioners have further been promoted to the posts of Assistant Excise and Taxation Officers.
The case of the petitioners is that the pay scales of Excise Inspectors and Taxation Inspectors were always higher in comparison to the feeder posts of Assistants/Junior Auditors/Accountants/Statistical Assistants and on the recommendations made by the expert pay revision committees, the pay scales were revised with effect from February 1, 1969 and April 1, 1979 and the pay scales of Excise Inspectors and Taxation Inspectors were always higher in comparison to the feeder posts.
4.
The petitioners further state that with effect from January 1, 1986 when the pay scales were again revised and the pay scales of Assistants/Junior Auditors/Accountants/Statistical Assistants were revised from Rs. 525-1050 to Rs. 1400-2600 and the pay scales of Excise/Taxation Inspectors were also revised from Rs. 600-1100 to Rs. 1400-2600, then both the above categories of posts i.e. feeder posts as well as promotion cadre were placed in the same scale of Rs. 1400-2600.
The Excise and Taxation Inspectors through their Union made a representation to the State Government for providing them pay scales higher to their feeder posts and on the aforesaid representation the State Government of Haryana considered the case of the aforesaid Excise and Taxation Inspectors and the pay scales of the Excise Inspectors were revised from Rs. 1400-2600 with effect from January 1, 1986 to Rs. 1640-2900 but with effect from October 1, 1991 vide letter dated January 20, 1992.
A copy of the aforesaid letter dated January 20, 1992 has been appended as Annexures P/2 with the petition.
Subsequently, the Union of the aforesaid Inspectors again approached the State Government to revise the pay scales of Taxation Inspectors also since the revision of pay scales had not been effected in case of Taxation Inspectors.
The State Government again agreed to the request made by the Union of the Taxation Inspectors and revised the pay scales of the Taxation Inspectors from Rs. 1400-2600 to Rs. 1640 to 2900.
A copy of the aforesaid communication issued by the State Government dated October 13, 1992 has been appended as Annexure P/3 with the petition.
5.
Since the pay scales of the aforesaid Inspectors had been revised with effect from October 1, 1991 instead of January 1, 1986 ie with effect from a prospective date without assigning any reason, therefore, certain Excise/Taxation Inspectors filed a Civil Writ Petition No. 1388 of 1995.
Another Civil Writ Petition No. 1380 of 1998 was filed by the similarly situated Inspectors i.e. Kailash Chander Hooda and others v. State of Haryana and others and vide order dated December 2, 1998 the aforesaid Civil Writ Petition No. 1380 was allowed by a Division Bench on this Court by making the following observations :-  "4.
For the foreging reasons, we allow this writ petition and hold that the petitioners category would be entitled to the revision of the pay scale w.e.f January 1, 1986 and not w.e.f.
October 1, 1991.
Let the calculations be, accordingly made to refix the pay of the petitioners.
However, the arrears, so calculated would be confined to the period of three years and two months prior to the filing of the present writ petition, which was filed on January 31, 1998.
Any other consequences, which flow from the refixation of the pay of the petitioners, would also be given to the petitioners.
Let these directions be carried out within a period of four months.
6.
It is the case of the petitioners that SLP filed by the State of Haryana was dismissed by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India on July 13, 1999.
Even a review petition filed by the State of Haryana was declined and thus the order dated December 2, 1998 granting the revised pay scales of Rs. 1640-2900 with effect from January 1, 1986 instead of October 1, 1991 became final.
Since the directions issued by the Division Bench in the aforesaid writ petition were not carried out, therefore, the matter was taken up by way of a contempt petition by the aforesaid petitioners.
To comply with the aforesaid directions, the Department of Finance, Government of Haryana issued a communication dated August 10, 2000 whereby it was decided to revise the pay scales of Excise/Taxation Inspectors from Rs. 1400-2600 to Rs. 1640-2900 with effect from January 1, 1986 instead of October 1, 1991.
However, while passing the aforesaid general orders it was decided that payment of arrears of 38 months will be restricted to the petitioners only.
However, pay of all the Excise/Taxation Inspectors was to be fixed with effect from January 1, 1986 from the date of their option on notional basis.
7.
On the aforesaid approval granted by the Financial Department, a general order was issued by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government of Haryana, Prohibition, Excise and Taxation Department on August 22, 2000 granting the revision of the pay scales of all Excise/Taxation Inspectors with effect from January 1, 1986 instead of October 1, 1991.
Copy of the communication dated August 10, 2000 issued by the Finance Department is appended as Annexures P/6 with the petition whereas general order issued by the Financial Commissioner-cum-Secretary to Government of Haryana, Excise and Taxation Department has been appended as annexure P.7 with the petition.
8.
The petitioners felt aggrieved against the restriction of the payments of arrears to the persons who had filed the earlier writ petition alone and sent a legal notice to the respondents to consider and grant a similar relief to the petitioners as well keeping in view the judgment of Kailash Chander Hooda's case (supra).
A copy of the legal notice dated August 9, 2000 has been appended as Annexure P/8 with the petition.
Because of the no outcome of the issuance of the aforesaid legal notice, the petitioners approached this Court by filing the a Civil Writ Petition No. 14594 of 2000.
The aforesaid Civil Writ Petition was decided by a Division Bench vide order dated October 31, 2001 whereby it was directed that the legal notice/justice demand notice issued by the petitioners be decided by the competent authority by taking a conscious decision with regard to the claim made by the petitioners.
9.
In accordance with the aforesaid directions dated October 31, 2001, respondent No. 3 Excise and Taxation Commissioner has passed the order dated August 24, 2001 whereby the claim made by the present petitioners has been rejected.
A copy of the aforesaid order dated August 24, 2001 has been appended with the present petition as Annexure P/9.
10.
The petitioners have filed the present petition being aggrieved against the order Annexure P/6 whereby the Finance Department had restricted the grant of payment of arrears of 38 months to such persons who had approached the court earlier and the order Annexure P/9 whereby respondent No. 3 has restricted their claim for the grant of aforesaid arrears which had been granted to the similarly situated persons.
11.
The order Annexure P/9 is being defended by the respondents in the written statement by saying that the claim made by the present petitioners have become time barred and since vide directions contained in the order Annexure P/6, the pay of all the employees belong to the cadre of Taxation Inspectors was to be refixed in the pay scales of Rs. 1640-2900 with effect from January 1, 1986 on the notional basis alone, therefore, the petitioners were not entitled to get the arrears of payment.
12.
We have heard Shri Vikas Chatrath, learned Counsel for the petitioners and Shri Rajbir Sehrawat, learned Deputy Advocate General and with their assistance have also perused the relevant record.
We find that the present writ petition deserves to succeed.
13.
It is admitted case between the parties that when earlier anomaly in the case of Excise/Taxation Inspectors was removed by the State Government and revised pay scales were granted to them with effect from October 1, 1991 instead of January 1, 1986 then certain Excise/Taxation Inspectors filed C.W.P.
No. 1380 of 1998 Kailash Chander Hooda and others v. State of Haryana and others.
The aforesaid writ petition was allowed vide order dated December 2, 1998.
On the basis of the directions contained in the order of the Division Bench in the aforesaid writ petition, a general order was issued by the Finance Department as well as the respondents being Annexures P/6 and P/7 respectively.
14.
As a matter of fact the petitioners, being persons similarly situated, were automatically entitled to the same relief without there being any necessity of their approaching the Court all over again.
However, rather than granting the relief automatically the respondents even ignored the legal notice sent by the petitioners to them on August 9, 2000 (Annexure P/8) so much so the petitioners had to approach this Court through Civil Writ Petition No. 14594 of 2000 and it was only on directions issued by this Court vide order dated October 31, 2001 that the claim made by them was considered.
We have also perused the orders Annexure P/9 passed by respondent No. 3 but do not find that the same is legally sustainable.
15.
From the aforesaid order Annexure P/9 we find that respondent No. 3 has categorised various Excise/Taxation Inspectors into different categories.
The categorisation has been done by respondent No. 3 on the basis of their approaching this Court through different writ petitions.
On that basis respondent No. 3 has held that since in each case the claim has been restricted to 38 months from the date prior to the date of filing the writ petition by a group of Inspectors as ordered by this Court, therefore, in the case of the petitioners the claim has been denied to them by holding that they had not approached this Court.
This approach of respondent No. 3 is totally lopsided and without any basis.
The above classification has been done by the aforesaid respondent without there being any distinction between various groups of persons who had to repeatedly approach this Court through separate writ petitions.
16.
We find that the petitioners are similarly situated persons, as the petitioners in Kailash Chander Hooda's case.
Since even the petitioners had approached this Court by filing an earlier writ petition No. 14594 of 2000 which had been decided by this Court vide order dated October 31, 2001 by issuing a direction to the respondents to consider their claim, therefore from any angle the claim of the petitioners to the arrears of salary for a period of 38 months prior to the aforesaid date i.e. October 31, 2001 cannot be denied in any manner.
Thus, we allow the present writ petition and direct the respondents to calculate the arrears of pay of the petitioners for the period of 38 months from October 31, 2001.
The aforesaid payment shall be made to the petitioners within a period of three months from the date a certified copy of this Court is received by them.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
