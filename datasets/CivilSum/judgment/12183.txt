KC Gupta, J - Heard.
This regular second appeal is directed by the State of Haryana against the judgment and decree dated 2122000 passed by the learned Additional District Judge, Karnal vide which the appeal of the respondent-plaintiff was accepted and the judgment and decree of the Civil Judge (Jr.
Div.)
Karnal dated 1051999 was set aside and the entries in the annual confidential reports of the respondent-plaintiff for the period of 16.5.1992 to 3131993 and from 141993 to 24.8.1993 were expunged and further the respondent-plaintiff was allowed to cross efficiency bar with effect from 1.11.1995 and also that he was entitled to the arrears of pay and allowance from the date of accrual along with interest at the rate of 12% per annum from the date of accrual till payment as the Superintendent of Police while recording the A.C.Rs.
has not given any reason about doubtful integrity and immoral character of the respondent-plaintiff.
2.
The main contention of the learned State counsel is that the Civil Court has no jurisdiction to try such suit.
However, this Court in Prabh Dayal v. Haryana State, 2001(4) SCT 293 has held that if the recording of the annual confidential report is not in accordance with rules, then the civil Court has jurisdiction to entertain the suit.
I have carefully gone through the judgment of the learned Additional District Judge, Karnal and I concur with the reasoning given therein.
There is no illegality or impropriety in the same.
No substantial question of law is involved.
Hence, this regular second appeal is dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
