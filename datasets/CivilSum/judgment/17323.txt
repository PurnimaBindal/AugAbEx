GS Singhvi, J (Oral) - This petition has been filed for issuance of a writ directing the respondents to consider the petitioner for appointment as J.B.T.
Teacher against one of the posts reserved for Ex-Servicemen (General).
2.
It appears from the record that the petitioner as well as respondent No. 4 (who is a dependent of ex-Serviceman) applied for recruitment as J.B.T.
Teacher in pursuance of advertisement No. 4/96 issued by the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana (renamed as Haryana Staff Selection Commission).
They claimed consideration against the posts reserved for Ex- Servicemen (General).
Petitioner's contention is that respondent Nos. 2 and 3 have manipulated the selection of respondent No. 4, ignoring his preferential right to be appointed against the post reserved for Ex-Servicemen in view of the instructions issued by the Government of Haryana vide circular letter dated 21st May, 1979.
3.
In the written statement filed today, the Board has questioned the locus standi of petitioner to be considered for appointment as J.B.T.
Teacher on the ground that he does not possess the essential qualifications.
In support of this assertion, respondent No. 3 has relied on following decisions of this Court :-  (i) C.W.P.
No. 8882 of 1997 (Azad Singh v. State of Haryana ), decided on 871997.
(ii) C.W.P.
No. 8871 of 1997 (Chander Pal Sharma v. State of Haryana ) decided on 2471997.
4.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have perused the orders passed in Azad Singh's case (supra) and Chander Pal Sharma's case (supra) and in our opinion, the petitioner, who has passed the Unit Education Instructor's Course, cannot be treated for recruitment as J.B.T.
Teacher because the qualification possessed by him is not equivalent to J.B.T.
For the detailed reasons recorded in the order passed in Azad Singh's case (supra), which shall be treated as part of this order, the writ petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
