Swatanter Kaur, J (Oral) - The prayer in this petition is for quashing order dated 352000.
Annexure P4, passed by the respondents reducing the pay of the petitioners as well as ordering recovery of the amount already paid.
2.
Upon notice, reply had been filed on behalf of the respondents.
In the reply, it has been stated as follows:  "The foregoing interpretation of the Rules has resulted in issuance of instructions dated 3-5-2000 which are strictly in accordance with the provision of Haryana Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1980.
Further, all the petitioners would be granted an opportunity of show cause before their pay is refixed in view of the instructions dated 3-5-2000.
It is worthwhile to mention here that the similar Writ No. 7181 of 2000 - Darshan Lal Kakkar and others v. State of Haryana and CWP No. 7907 of 2000, Sadhu Ram v. State of Haryana have already been disposed of by the Hon'ble High Court on 17-1-2000."
It is apparent that the impugned order, Annexure P4, has been passed in clear violation of the principles of natural justice and the petitioners have not been afforded any opportunity of being heard.
The respondents have further stated that they would hear the petitioners and pass an order in accordance with law and instructions issued by the Government.
3.
For the reasons aforestated, we set aside Annexure P4 with liberty to the respondents to pass an order afresh in accordance with law and in consonance with the principles of natural justice.
No order as to costs.
Ordered accordingly.
