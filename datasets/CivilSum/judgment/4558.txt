Sat Pal, J. - In this writ petition, the petitioners have challenged the order dated 19th/21st February 1997 (copy annexure P-1) whereby the appeal of Smt. Kamal Mohni, respondent No. 3, herein, has been accepted by respondent No. 1 and she has been ordered to be assigned seniority with effect from 1st February, 1973 amongst the Assistants.
2.
Briefly stated, the facts of the case are that the petitioners joined the Forest Department as Clerks during the period 1964 to 1968 on being regularly selected by the Haryana Subordinate Services Selection Board.
They were confirmed as Clerks during the period April, 1969 to November, 1971.
Petitioners No. 6 and 8 were promoted as Assistants in the year 1971; petitioners Nos. 1 to 4 were promoted as Assistants in the year 1973; petitioner No. 5 in the year 1974; petitioner No. 9 in the year 1976 and the petitioner No. 7 in the year 1979.
Petitioners Nos. 1, 6 and 8 were confirmed as Assistants in the year 1979; petitioners Nos. 2 to 5 were confirmed as Assistants in the year 1981; petitioner No. 9 in the year 1987 and the petitioner No. 7 in the year 1988.
Petitioners Nos. 1 to 5 were promoted as Head Assistants/Deputy Superintendents in the year 1981; petitioners Nos. 6, 7 and 8 in the year 1982 and petitioner No. 9 in the year 1984.
Petitioners Nos. 1 and 2 were confirmed as Head Assistants/Deputy Superintendents in September, 1988 and the petitioners Nos. 3 to 9 were confirmed as Head Assistants/Deputy Superintendents in the year 1996.
3.
Respondent No. 3 joined the Forest Department as a steno-typist on 19th July, 1972.
She was promoted as Stenographer with effect from 1st February, 1973.
It may be noted here that the pay scale of the Stenographers is equivalent to that of the Assistants.
She was confirmed as a Stenographer on 1st November, 1982.
She was further promoted as Head Assistant on 1st August, 1982 and was confirmed on that post on 9th January 1996.
4.
Respondent No. 3 submitted a representation dated 341978 (Copy Annexure R-3/3) to the Chief Conservator of Forests, Haryana, Chandigarh (respondent No. 2 herein) wherein she stated that under rule 3(2) of the Promotion of Stenographers and Steno-typists Rules, 1961 (hereinafter referred to as the 1961 Rules), the incumbents working against the posts of Stenographers were adjustable against the posts of Assistants of the same time scale and their seniority was reckoned from the date of their appointment to the post of Stenographer/Assistant.
She submitted that she had given her option to be posted as Assistant in the direction office but she had not been selected, though her junior, namely Sh. Som Nath, Stenographer had been taken in the direction office.
She submitted that in view of this, her case should be considered sympathetically for absorption in the direction office.
This representation was, however, rejected vide memo letter dated 2231979 (copy annexure R-3T/4).
In this memo, it was, however, stated that the matter with regard to the seniority of Ms. Kanwal Mohini Sapra was under consideration.
5.
After the receipt of the memo dated 22nd March, 1979, respondent No. 3 kept silent.
However, after the lapse of about 16 years, she submitted an appeal dated 10th May, 1995 (copy Annexure R-3/8) to the respondent No. 2.
In this representation, she stated that she was promoted as Stenographer on 1st February, 1973 but has been shown as junior to four officials, namely Sarvshri Prem Lal, Jai Narayan, Babu Lal and Smt. Bimla Devi who were promoted as Assistants on 2nd August, 1973, 3rd August, 1973, 24th July, 1973 and 23rd January, 1974, respectively.
It was further stated by respondent No. 3 in this appeal that her appeal was lying pending in the Head Office since 1978 and it may be considered sympathetically.
This appeal was rejected by respondent No. 2 vide memo dated 10th April, 1996 (copy annexure R-3/T9).
6.
Aggrieved by the order dated 10th April, 1996 (Annexure R-3/T9) respondent No. 3 submitted another appeal dated 24th July, 1996 (copy annexure R-3/T10) to respondent No. 2, wherein she again prayed to give her benefit of seniority from the date of her appointment as Stenographer w.e.f.
1st August, 1973.
Thereafter respondent No. 3 submitted another appeal dated 24101996 (Copy annexure R-3/12) addressed to the Commissioner and Secretary to Government of Haryana, Forest Department, Chandigarh (respondent No. 1).
In this appeal, respondent No. 3 requested respondent No. 1 that in terms of the Government Stenographers Rules of 1961, she should be given seniority from the date of her appointment as Stenographer.
The said appeal was accepted by respondent No. 1 vide order dated 19th February, 1997 (Copy annexure P-1).
In this order, it has been stated that respondent No. 3 has become entitled for seniority from 1st February, 1973 because she was promoted as Stenographer on the said date.
The aforesaid order has been challenged by the petitioners in this writ petition.
7.
From the records, we found that the petitioners were not given any opportunity by the respondent No. 1 before passing the impugned order, dated 19/21st February, 1997.
Neither the learned counsel for the State nor the learned counsel for respondent No. 3 could point out any document which could indicate that any opportunity was given by the respondent No. 1 to the petitioners before passing the impugned orders.
In view of these facts, the learned counsel for the parties submitted that the parties through their counsel should be heard by the Court on the claim of respondent No. 3 for assigning seniority in the cadre of stenographers/Assistants on 1st February, 1973.
Accordingly, we heard the learned counsel for the parties.
8.
Mr. Yadav, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners submitted that the appeal dated 2471996 filed by respondent No. 3 claiming her seniority with effect from 1st February, 1973 was rejected by respondent No. 2 vide order dated 18.9.1996 (copy annexure R-3/11).
He submitted that under the 1961 Rules, there was no provision to file the second appeal.
He, therefore, contended that the second appeal dated 24101996 filed by respondent No. 3 before respondent No. 1 was not maintainable.
He further submitted that vide impugned order, dated 30.4.1997 passed by respondent No. 1, respondent No. 3 has been made senior to the petitioners but no opportunity was given by respondent No. 1 before passing the said order.
9.
Lastly, he submitted that the respondent No. 3 had not worked as an Assistant nor she had handled the work of Assistant for a period of two years and as such, she was not eligible for promotion to the post of Deputy Superintendent/Head Assistant.
He further submitted that the appeal dated 10th May, 1995 (copy annexure R-3/8) filed by respondent No. 3 was belated and as such should have been dismissed on the ground of laches.
10.
Ms. Deepali Puri, learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent No. 3 submitted that the said respondent had submitted representation dated 3rd April, 1978 and in response to this representation, she was informed by respondent No. 2 vide memo dated 22nd March, 1979 (copy annexure R-3T/4), that the matter with regard to her seniority was under consideration.
She submitted that since no reply was received from respondent No. 2, she submitted the appeal on 10th May, 1995 to respondent No. 2 and after the rejection of this appeal, she submitted the second appeal dated 24101996 to respondent No. 1.
She, therefore, contended that respondent No. 3 had been submitting the representations to the authorities from time to time and as such her claim was not belated.
She further submitted that respondent No. 3 had gained experience of work against the post of Assistant for more than two years, when she took over the seat, where complaints were dealt with.
She submitted that in this connection, certificate of experience was duly issued by the competent authority.
She drew our attention to the memo dated 20th December, 1996 (Annexure R-3/13) and submitted that the petitioners were given hearing by respondent No. 1 before passing the impugned order.
11.
We have given our thoughtful consideration to the submissions made by the learned counsel for the parties and perused the records.
It is an admitted fact that respondent No. 3 submitted her first representation dated 3rd April, 1978 (copy annexure R-3/3) wherein she claimed seniority in the cadre of Stenographers/Assistants with effect from 1st February, 1973 and further made a request that she should be absorbed in the direction office.
Her request for absorption in the direction office was rejected by respondent No. 2 vide memo dated 22nd March, 1979 (Annexure R-3T/4).
In this memo, it was also stated that the matter with regard to her seniority was under consideration.
Thereafter, respondent No. 3 did not submit any representation for a period of more than 16 years and it was only on 10th May, 1995 when the said respondent submitted her representation to respondent No. 2.
The representation dated 10th May, 1995, thus was highly belated one and as such, it deserved to be rejected on account of delay and laches.
In fact, this representation was rejected by respondent No. 2 vide memo dated 10th April, 1996 (Annexure R-3/T9).
During this period of 16 years, final seniority list had already been issued and the petitioner throughout this period, had been shown senior to respondent No. 3.
After the rejection of the representation/appeal filed by respondent No. 3 and respondent No. 2, vide memo dated 10th April 1996, no further appeal was maintainable before respondent No. 1 under the rules and even if such an appeal was maintainable, the same ought to have been rejected on the ground of delay and laches.
We, are, therefore, of the opinion that the impugned order passed by respondent No. 1 accepting the appeal of respondent No. 3 cannot be sustained.
Here reference may be made to a judgment of the Supreme Court in the case of High Court of MP v. Mahesh Kumar, JT 1994 (5) SC 620.
In this case, the representation of the writ petitioner was rejected in May 1976 and the writ petition was filed in June 1985, and it was held by the Supreme Court that the writ petition ought to have been dismissed by the High Court on the ground of delay and laches.
For the reasons recorded hereinabove, we accept the writ petition and set aside the impugned order dated 19th/21st February, 1997 (Annnexure P-1) passed by respondent No. 1.
