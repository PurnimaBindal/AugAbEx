SK Jain, J. (Oral) - Raj Kumar Gupta respondent No. 1 herein was appointed as Sectional Officer in Public Works Department (B&R) Punjab, in 1959.
On the reorganisation of erstwhile State of Punjab, his services were allocated to the Haryana State wef.
November 1, 1966.
He was promoted as Sub Divisional Engineer on 30121972.
Approval of the Haryana Public Service Commission, Chandigarh, was sought accordingly.
The Chief Engineer, PWD B&R, Chandigarh, had issued a letter dated 1411974 to the Accountant General, Haryana, Chandigarh for issuance of the plaintiffs' pay slip considering him as regular employee of the department.
The plaintiff has been getting salary of the post of Sub-Divisional Engineer regularly in the pay scale as sanctioned by the Haryana Government from time to time.
Vide order dated 2261989, the State Government declared that Sub-Divisional Engineers were entitled to get the scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/- after completion of 12 years of regular service.
The plaintiff having completed the said period of 12 years of regular service before 151989, was entitled to this revised scale of pay.
But for the reasons best known to the State Government, he was not given the said pay scale, although other Sub-Divisional Engineers who were promoted in the year 1972-73 were granted that scale.
After serving a notice under Section 80 CPC, the plaintiff filed a suit for a decree of declaration to the effect that he was entitled to get his pay fixed in the revised pay scale of Rs, 4,100-5,300/- wef.
151989.
He also claimed a consequential decree for mandatory injunction directing the defendants to pay all the arrears of salary etc. wef.
151989 till the date of final payment.
2.
The suit was contested by the defendants.
Written statement was filed.
It was admitted that the plaintiff joined the service as Sectional Officer in the year 1959 and was promoted as Sub-Divisional Engineer in 1972.
It was further contended that he was promoted purely on ad hoc basis as a stop-gap arrangement against out of rota/quota in the year 1972 and was made regular only on 661980 and prior thereto, he was performing the duties as Sub-Divisional Engineer on ad hoc basis for six months against the vacancies of deputationists or those meant for the direct recruits.
It was further averred that the promotion quota post against which the plaintiff was promoted became available only on 661980 and as such, the plaintiff enjoyed benefit for more than eight years against non-quota post.
He had not completed 12 years of regular service in this manner and was not entitled to revised pay scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/-.
Validity of notice under Section 80 CPC was challenged although its receipt was admitted.
Additional pleas with regard to maintainability, valuation of the suit for the purposes of Court fees and jurisdiction, absence of cause of action and the suit being bad on account of non-joinder of necessary parties and being barred by time were taken.
After taking the replication on record, the learned trial Court had framed the following issues :-  (1) Whether the plaintiff is entitled to get his pay fixed in the revised pay scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/- wef.
151989 as alleged ?
OPD.
(2) Whether the promotion of the plaintiff made in the year 1972 was purely on ad hoc basis as a stop gap arrangement and he was made regular only on 661980 and if so, to what effect ?
OPD.
(3) Whether the suit is not maintainable ?
OPD.
(4) Whether the suit has not been properly valued for the purpose of Court fee and jurisdiction ?
OPD.
(5) Whether the plaintiff has no cause of action ?
OPD.
(6) Whether the suit is bad for non-joinder of necessary parties ?
OPD.
(7) Whether the suit is time barred ?
OPD.
(8) Whether the plaintiff is estopped from filing the suit ?
OPD.
(9) Whether the suit is bad for multifariousness ?
OPD.
(10) Relief.
3.
After the parties led evidence, the learned trial Court found that the promotion of the plaintiff in the year 1972 was not merely a stop-gap arrangement and his regular service was to be counted from the year 1972.
He was entitled to get his pay fixed in the revised pay scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/- with effect from 151989.
Resultantly, the suit of the plaintiff was decreed vide judgment and decree dated 9.10.1991 passed by the Senior Sub Judge, Jind.
4.
The said judgment was challenged on behalf of the respondents in Civil Appeal No. 97/13 of 14111991 which was dismissed by the Additional District Judge, Jind.
5.
It is that judgment and decree of the first appellate Court which has been appealed against by the defendants and which requires my examination of its sustainability.
6.
I have seen the pleadings in the suit, the evidence adduced by the parties in the suit and judgment of both the Courts below.
Following facts are admitted on both ends :-  (i) that the plaintiff had joined the Public Works Department (B & R) in the erstwhile State of Punjab in 1959 as Sectional Officer;  (ii) on the reorganization of the State of Punjab, his services were;  (iii) he was promoted as Sub-Divisional Engineer in June, 1972 and after six months, he was reverted but again vide order Ex. P-1 he was given promotion against regular post after his service record was screened and some of his colleagues were ignored;  (iv) he had been continuously working on the said post since 30121972;  (v) on January 14, 1974 the Chief Engineer had requested the Accountant General, Haryana, for issuance of regular pay scale to the plaintiff.
It was issued in compliance with the Government letter Ex. P3.
From letter Ex. P4, it is evident that Sub-Divisional Engineers who were senior to the plaintiff were promoted in the year 1972-73 and were given the revised pay scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/-.
There is nothing on the record to show that the plaintiff was ever served with a show-cause notice or any other communication for not granting him the revised pay scale;  (vi) Ex. P5 is the representation of the plaintiff made to the Chief Minister on his behalf; and  (vii) notice Ex. P6 was served on the respondents.
7.
The case of the respondents was projected through D.W.1 M K Budia who brought the record of the office of the Engineer-in-Chief, Haryana, Chandigarh, to the effect that the plaintiff was promoted on ad hoc and provisional basis in June, 1972; that, however, in August, 1972 he was reverted to his previous post because of non-availability of the post; that thereafter, he was again promoted in December, 1972 on provisional basis vide order Ex. P 1; that vide order Ex. D2, he was made regular on that post wef.
661980; that he was working on ad hoc basis as Sub-Divisional Engineer on 561980 and, thereafter, he was working on regular basis.
The pay scale of the Engineers was revised vide order dated 261989.
They were entitled to get pay scale of Rs. 3,000-4,500/- after completion of five years of regular service and the scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/- after completion of 12 years of regular service.
The notification in this regard is Ex. P3; and lastly, he stated that the posts against which the plaintiff was adjusted on 661980 was from the quota post only.
8.
In nutshall, the case of the respondents is that the plaintiff having not completed 12 years of regular service, was not entitled to the revised pay scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300/-.
9.
The main thrust of the argument of the learned Assistant Advocate General, Haryana, is that the period of 12 years of regular service is calculated with effect from 961980 and not with effect from 13.12.1972 for the purposes of revised pay scale.
10.
Learned counsel for the plaintiff-respondent has drawn my attention towards letter Ex. P-1 dated 12121972.
Vide this letter, the plaintiff along with others was promoted to the post of Sub-Divisional Engineer.
There is no mention in this letter that this promotion was on ad hoc basis as a stop-gap arrangement.
However, in No. 2 of the said letter, it is mentioned that officers at serial No. 7 and 9 (Raj Kumar Gupta-plaintiff) were promoted on provisional basis subject to the terms and conditions contained in Haryana Government order dated 11121972.
Plaintiff Raj Kumar (PW1) had stated that he was promoted against regular post.
This fact had been admitted by DW1 M K Budia also.
He stated that the plaintiff was working as Sub-Divisional Engineer since December, 1972 continuously.
It is, thus evident that the promotion of the plaintiff was not as a matter of stop gap arrangement.
The Chief Engineer vide letter dated 1411974 had requested Accountant General, Haryana, Chandigarh that regular pay slips be issued to the Officiating Sub Divisional Engineers and admittedly the regular pay slip of the plaintiff was issued.
There is not even an iota of evidence to show that no post out of the quota of promotees was available when the plaintiff was promoted in December, 1972.
It is also not established on record that the post against which he could be promoted became available only in the year 1980.
In view of the above discussion, I am not inclined to interfere with the concurrent findings of fact recorded by the Courts below which are hereby affirmed.
Resultantly, this Regular Second Appeal fails and is hereby dismissed.
The parties are left to bear their own costs.
