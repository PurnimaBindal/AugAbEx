Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - We have heard learned counsel for the petitioner at some length.
2.
It is contended that articles of charges served upon the petitioner in the year 1995 related to the year 1989-1990 and as such they are belated and could not have been logically proved against the petitioner.
Secondly it is contended that order of the disciplinary authority is non-speaking and the penalty imposing recovery of Rs. 65,575/- is excessive.
3.
None of the contentions impress us.
The inquiry against the petitioner was conducted in accordance with rules.
Although the charges were framed against the petitioner after some time, but that per se would not be sufficient ground to quash the charge sheet.
Neither any prejudice has been caused to the petitioner on account of delay nor anything has been pleaded on the writ petition that as a result of delay the petitioner has suffered any prejudice leading to unfair trial.
The order of the disciplinary authority stands merged into the order of the appellate authority dated 2nd July, 2001.
As a matter of fact, the petitioner had been found to be responsible by the inquiry officer, disciplinary authority as well as appellate authority for the negligence which has resulted in financial loss to the government.
The total loss is stated to be more than 5 lacs while the petitioner has been called upon to pay Rs. 65,575/- only.
We do not find the punishment to be excessive.
Petition dismissed.
