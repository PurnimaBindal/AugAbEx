The petitioners approached the Labour Commissioner for enforcement of the orders passed by the Labour Court, Ludhiana and the matter was then referred to the Assistant Labour Commissioner, Ludhiana for recovery certificate and necessary action under the Act.
Since no action was taken by the respondents which necessitated the filing of the present writ petition.
3.
At the time of admission of the writ petition on 481994, a sum of Rs. 4,00,000/- lying deposited in the current account of M/s Parbhat Finance and Investment Ltd. in Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar, was ordered to be attached.
Respondent No. 3 filed written reply and pleaded that vide order dated 2491991 in for Civil Writ Petition No. 9670 of 1991 the recovery of Rs. 75,22,302/- on the petition of Pritam Singh and Ors. v. State of Punjab, from the estate of management firm a sum of Rs. 6,78,04397 paise was found in the bank account of M/s Parbhat and Finance Investment Company of various banks.
The amount was remitted to the Labour Court, Bhatinda, for distribution amongst the claimants through demand drafts.
According to the respondent, fresh recovery can be effected if the assets of the aforesaid management-company are available in the District.
4.
During the course of arguments on the request of the learned counsel for the petitioners, he was allowed to place on record the photostat copies of orders dated 131995 and 2031995, passed in CWP No. 10349 of 1994 as Annexures P.4 and P.5, respectively.
Vide order dated 131995 (An.
P.4), passed by the Division Bench of this Court in CWP No. 10349 of 1994 (Lakhwinder Singh etc. v. State of Punjab) a sum of Rs. 14,48,20525 in the account of M/s Parbhat and Finance & Investment Ltd Amritsar, with Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar, was attached and thereafter vide order dated 2031995 (Ann.
P.5), the Division Bench disposed of CWP No. 10349 of 1994 by giving the following directions :  "Consequently, we direct the Manager of the Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar, to release by way of bank draft a sum of Rs. 8,02,300/- in the name of Assistant Labour Commissioner, Moga, payable at Moga.
This amount is to be released from the account of M/s Parbhat Finance and Investment Ltd., lying with the said Bank.
This amount will be further paid to the awardees in whose favour the Labour Court had given the award.
Mr. Bains, the Labour-cum-Conciliation Officer, Moga, who is to disburse the amount would get the receipts from the awardees in token of payment.
On the receipt, the awardees will affix their photographs which would also be signed by them.
The Manager of the Bank shall remit the amount as aforesaid within one week of the receipt of copy of the order from this Court and the said amount shall be disbursed to the awardees expeditiously."
5.
In the instant case, a sum of Rs. 4,00,000/- has already been ordered to be attached lying deposited in the same bank account of M/s Parbhat Finance & Investment Ltd. in Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar.
As is evident from the copies of the orders Annexures P.4 and P.5 referred to above, an amount to the tune of about Rs. Six lacs is still lying deposited with the Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar.
Consequently, this writ petition is disposed of in terms of the decision rendered in CWP No. 10349 of 1994 and a direction is issued to the Manager, Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar, to remit a sum of Rs. 1,43,000/- by way of bank draft in the name of Assistant Labour Commissioner-cum-Conciliation Officer, Circle No. 4, Gill Road, Ludhiana, payable at Ludhiana.
This amount is to be released from the account of M/s Prabhat Finance and Investment Ltd. lying with the said bank.
This amount shall be further paid to the petitioners by respondent No. 2 against valid receipts.
On the receipts the petitioners shall affix their photographs which would also be signed by them.
The Manager of the Bank shall remit the amount as aforesaid within 15 days of the receipt of a copy of this order from the Court or a certified copy thereof from the petitioners whichever is earlier.
However, respondent No. 2 and the Manager of the Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar, shall be at liberty to seek further directions/clarification, if any, from this Court.
The office shall send the copies of this order immediately to the Manager, Punjab and Sind Bank, Khalsa College Branch, Amritsar, as well as to Assistant Labour Commissioner-cum-Conciliation Officer, No. 4, Gill Road, Ludhiana, for compliance.
Order accordingly.
