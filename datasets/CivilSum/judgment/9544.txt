DV Sehgal, President.
- A demand notice dated 1791992 was issued by the workman-petitioner and he sought reference of the dispute to the Labour Court on the ground that his services were illegally terminated by the Management- respondent No. 2.
The Joint Secretary, Labour Department, Haryana Government, however, vide his order Annexure P3 did not consider his case fit for reference by recording the reasons that it had been found that he had served for only about one month and 23 days.
The workman-petitioner addressed a detailed representation Annexure P4 to the Joint Secretary for re- consideration of his case.
He stated therein that he was employed with respondent No. 2 since 2388 and the factory was closed by respondent No. 2 on 24.7.1992.
This representation, however, did not find favour with the Joint Secretary who refused to change his earlier decision and sent letter dated 27.10.1993 Annexure P5 to the workman.
2.
In our view, the question whether the workman has put in the requisite length of service is a matter exclusively within the ambit of Labour Court to adjudicate upon and could not be inquired into by the State Government and reference could not be declined on that basis.
The position of law in this regard has now been made clear by a Division Bench of the Hon'ble High Court in Punjab Anand Lamp Employees Union v. M/s. Punjab Anand Lamp Industry Limited and another, 1996(4) RSJ 250 : 1996(3) SCT 56 (P&H);.
This position of law is fairly conceded by Sh. SM Sharma, Learned Deputy Advocate General, Haryana appearing for respondent No. 1.
3.
In this view of the matter, we quash the orders of the Joint Secretary, Labour Department, Haryana Government Annexure P3 and P5 and direct him to make a reference under Section 10(i) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 in accordance with the law.
Parties are directed to appear before the Labour Court on 2431999.
4.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed of.
