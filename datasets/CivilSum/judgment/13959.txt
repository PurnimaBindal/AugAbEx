GS Singhvi, J - In this petition, the petitioner has prayed for quashing orders dated 26121997 (Annexure P2) vide which officer-in-charge Records, Army Medical Corps, Lucknow (respondent No. 3) conveyed the decision of CDA (P) Allahabad to discontinue the disability pension payable to him w.e.f.
2061997.
He has further prayed for quashing order dated 10.12.1999 (Annexure P5) vide which the Government of India, Ministry of Defence dismissed the appeal filed by him against Annexure P2.
2.
The petitioner joined Army on 27.6.1967.
On 121976, the Ambulance in which he was travelling met with an accident, as a result of which the petitioner suffered compressed fracture.
Consequently, his medical category was downgraded from AYE to EEE.
He was invalidated out of service on 3111983 and was paid disability pension because the Army Medical Board had assessed his disability to be more than 20%.
After 14 years, respondent No. 3 sent Annexure P2 to the petitioner conveying the decision of CDA (P), Allahabad to discontinue the disability pension being paid to him on the ground that his disability had been re-assessed at 11 to 14%.
The appeal filed by him was dismissed by the Government of India vide Annexure P5.
3.
The petitioner has relied on paras 14 and 17 of the Entitlement Rules of the Casualty Pensioner Awards, 1982 and averred that the decision of the concerned authorities to stop the disability pension payable to him is vitiated due to violation of the rules and arbitrariness.
4.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents, it has been averred that as a result of periodical survey of disability of the petitioner, he was paid disability pension up to 19.6.1997.
According to them, the disability pension payable to the petitioner was discontinued because PCDA (P), Allahabad had accepted his disability at less than 20% for a period of 5 years.
The respondents have further averred that in pursuance of letter dated 722001 of the Ministry of Defence, a fresh Re-survey Medical Board was held on 2912002 and the petitioner's disability was assessed at less than 20% for whole of his life and the finding of the said Re-survey Medical Board had been approved by the Pension Sanctioning Authority vide letter dated 2572002.
5.
We have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the record of the case.
In our opinion, the decision of the respondents to discontinue the disability pension payable to the petitioner is liable to be invalidated in view of the judgment of this Court in Havaldar Ram Phal v. Union of India and others, 2002(4) SCT 952 (P&H) : 2003(2) RSJ 229 and order dated 24.1.1993 passed by the Supreme Court in Civil Appeal No. 164 of 1993-Ex. Sapper Mohinder Singh v. Union of India.
In those cases, it has been held that opinion of the Medical Board on the issue of disability on the Army personnel should be accepted until a fresh Medical Board examines the pensioner and comes to a different conclusion.
6.
Shri Gurpreet Singh, learned Additional Central Government Standing Counsel fairly stated that the petitioner's case is fully covered by the two judgments referred to above.
7.
Hence, the writ petition is allowed.
Orders Annexure P2 and P.5 are quashed.
The respondents are directed to pay disability pension to the petitioner for the period between 2061997 and 1962002.
The arrears payable to the petitioner be released within a period of three months from today failing which the respondents shall pay him interest at the rate of 12% w.e.f.
2061997.
For the period after 2062002, the respondents shall be free to convey the decision taken on the recommendations of the Re-survey Medical Board.
Petition allowed.
