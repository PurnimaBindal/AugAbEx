R S Mongia, J - The grievance made by the petitioner in the present writ petition is that the service rendered by him as a driver on work-charge basis has not been counted and reckoned for the purpose of grant of pension and other pensionary benefits.
2.
The undisputed facts are that the petitioner was employed as a Driver by the Haryana State Electricity Board (hereinafter called the 'Board') on work-charged basis w.e.f.
1.1.1969 and he was brought on regular cadre w.e.f.
10101972.
He retired on 15.10.1982.
For the grant of pension, ten years qualifying service is necessary under the relevant Rules of the Board.
The petitioner was not granted pension which led the petitioner to file the present writ petition.
The reason as to why the petitioner was not entitled to the grant of pension has been mentioned in para 5 of the written statement filed on behalf of the respondent-Board.
The same is reproduced below :  "The contents of para 5 of the writ petition are not disputed to the extent of grant of increments above the Efficiency Bar w.e.f.
1.1.1981.
It is further stated that the petitioner was appointed in work-charged capacity w.e.f.
1.1.1969 and was brought on regular cadre (Civil Post under Civil Services Rules) w.e.f.
10101972.
Under the relevant pension rules, the service in work-charged capacity is not permanent post or in officiating capacity on regular cadre (as distinguished from work-charged), only is to be considered for counting for qualifying service under the Pension Rules.
Thus, the petitioner has been in regular appointment w.e.f.
10101972 to 15.10.1982, which works out to 10 years and 6 days.
However, during this period of regular officiating, the petitioner has been granted extraordinary leave (not countable for pension) for a period from 11121976 to 911977 (30 days), 29280 to 19580 (81 days) & 12.7.82 to 22.7.82 (11 days) total - 122 days.
Thus, the total qualifying service of the petitioner was less than 10 years and he is, therefore, not entitled for the grant of pension under the rules."
3.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that in fact the period of extraordinary leave, which was duly granted, cannot be excluded for counting the qualifying period for pension.
However, he submitted that this point need not detain us inasmuch as in view of the judgment of the Full Bench of this Court in Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab, 1988 Pb.
& Hry.
265, the work-charged period followed by regular appointment has to be counted for the purpose of qualifying service for the grant of pension and other retiral benefits.
He submitted that in view of the judgment of the Full Bench, the period from 1.1.1969 to 9101972, which was spent on work-charged basis, had to be counted towards the qualifying service for pension and even if the period of extraordinary leave was not taken into consideration, the period from 1.1.1969 to 9101972 and thereafter upto 15.10.1982 would be much more than the qualifying service of 10 years.
4.
On behalf of the respondents, it could not be successfully argued as to how the point raised by the learned counsel for the petitioner was untenable.
5.
In view of the dictum laid down in Kesar Chand's case (supra), it cannot be denied that the work-charged period of an employee has to be counted towards qualifying service for grant of retiral benefits.
That being so, the period from 1.1.1969 to 9101972, which the petitioner spent on work-charged basis as a Driver, had to be counted towards the qualifying service for the grant of retiral benefits.
It cannot further be denied that if such period is counted, the petitioner would have more than 10 years qualifying service to his credit for the grant of retiral benefits.
6.
For the foregoing reasons, this petition is allowed and it is held that the petitioner was entitled to get his work-charged period counted for the grant of retiral benefits.
The respondents are directed to grant all the retiral benefits including pension and pay the arrears within a period of three months from the receipt of copy of this order.
If the retiral benefits are not released within the stipulated period, the petitioner would be entitled to the grant of 12% interest per annum on the arrears of pension and other retiral benefits.
I make no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
