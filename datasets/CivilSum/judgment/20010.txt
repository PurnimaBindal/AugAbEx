Lalit Mohan Shrama, J - Heard learned counsel for the parties.
Delay is condoned.
Special leave is granted.
2.
This appeal by the Union of India is directed against the judgment of the Central Administrative Tribunal dealing with the claim of special allowance made by the present respondents.
The respondents are welders employed in Armour Welding in Heavy Vehicles Factory, Avadi, Madras.
In 1969, a decision was taken to grant special allowance to the Armour Welders, calculated at 20% of their pay provided they pass the requisite trade test.
By letter dated 25.9.1975 a decision was taken to calculate the special pay on the basis of the scales recommended by the Third Pay Revision Commission.
This was indicated to be an interim measure pending the adoption of piece rate system for calculating the special pay.
The piece rates system, however, was never adopted.
On 1921991, special allowance was directed to be paid at a flat rate.
This decision which has been applied with effect from 1921991 is not in dispute herein.
The controversy between the parties is confined to the period from 111996 to 1821991.
The respondents' demand was that inasmuch as their pay scales have been revised from 111986, the special allowance should be calculated and paid on the basis of the pay scales enforced from 111986.
Since the said demand was not acceeded to, they moved the Central Administrative Tribunal with an application which has been allowed by the impugned judgment.
3.
The learned Additional Solicitor General appearing for the appellant-Union of India contended that the letter of 1975 referred merely to the scales recommended by the Third Pay Revision Commission and not to the Fourth Pay Revision Commission's recommendations and that therefore the respondents cannot base their demand on the said letter.
He has also emphasised the interim nature of the direction in the said letter and urged that in any event the respondents could not be allowed their special pay at a rate higher than that which has ultimately been given to them in 1991.
4.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties we are of the view that the argument on behalf of the appellant is well founded.
The 1975 letter was in terms confined to the scales recommended by the Third Pay Commission.
Further it was in the nature of an interim measure.
The said letter cannot and does not sustain the claim of the respondents for calculating the special allowance at the rate of 20% of the pay scales introduced with effect from 111986.
On a consideration of the said letter along with other relevant material on record, we accept the case of the appellant that special pay for the period 111986 to 1821991 should not exceed the flat rate given to the respondents on 1921991.
Accordingly, the respondents who have been divided in three categories being highly skilled Grade I, highly skilled Grade II and skilled Armour Welders shall respectively get the special allowance at the flat rates of Rs. 140/-, Rs. 120/ - and Rs. 100/- for the period 111986 and 1821991 also.
The appeal accordingly is allowed but there will be no order as to costs.
Appeal allowed.
