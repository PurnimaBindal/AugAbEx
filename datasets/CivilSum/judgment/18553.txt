SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - The grievance of the petitioners in the present case in that the respondents have directed recovery from the petitioners on the basis that excess Rural Area Allowances have been paid.
This recovery order has been issued on the basis of a letter dated 25.9.2001.
In ground No. (v) of the writ petition, it is specifically stated that the aforesaid order has been passed by the respondents without complying with the rules of natural justice.
2.
Written statement has been filed by the respondents.
But the reply given to paragraph (v) of the grounds of the writ petition is that reply has been given in the foregoing paragraphs.
In the earlier paragraphs, it has been sought to be established by the respondents that Talwara is no longer a rural area.
We are of the considered opinion that the facts and circumstances which have been pointed out in the written statement should have been brought to the notice of the petitioners.
They should have been given an opportunity to put forward their case before the appropriate authority before passing any order causing civil consequences.
Learned counsel for the petitioners has further stated that in fact the case put forward by the petitioners finds support from the observations made by the Executive Engineer, Shah Nehar Extension Civil Division, Hoshiarpur in his Certificate.
The aforesaid Certificate has been attached with the writ petition as Annexure P-11.
3.
In view of the above, this writ petition is allowed.
The impugned order, Annexure P-7 is hereby quashed and set aside.
The respondents are directed to proceed against the petitioners in accordance with law and by observing the rules of natural justice, if so advised.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
