Prasenjit Mandal, J - This writ application is directed against the judgment and order dated 30.11.2004 passed by the Central Administrative Tribunal, Calcutta Bench in OA No. 1197 of 1996 and Miscellaneous Application No. 311 of 1996.
The learned Tribunal has dismissed the OA Case No. 1197 and disposed of the Miscellaneous Application No. 311 of 1996 by the impugned order.
2.
Being aggrieved by and dissatisfied with the impugned order, this writ application has been preferred by the writ applicant.
3.
Facts in brief are that the writ applicant was appointed to the post of Scientist Grade S-I of the Indian Council for Agricultural Research now known as Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibers on 28.9.1976 and then he was posted at Sisal Research Station, Sambalpur, Orissa.
He wanted to make higher studies and his prayer for study leave for a period of one year was granted from 2891981 to 2791982.
Then he prayed for extension of study leave for a further period of one year with effect from 28.9.1982 and the said prayer for leave was granted.
Then he prayed for extension of his study leave up to 31121984.
That time his prayer was refused.
However, he was granted 9 days earned leave from 2891983 to 6101983.
Then he was also granted 451 days extraordinary leave from 7101983 to 31121984.
Then he again prayed for extraordinary leave for one year from 111985 to 31121985.
Such prayer was refused and he was directed to join his duty immediately.
He did not comply with such direction.
Later on, he made a request to allow him to join his duty in the afternoon of 261986 after a gap of one and half years.
However, he was allowed to join his duty on 2361986.
The period of absence from 111986 to 2261986 was considered as being absent unauthorisedly.
The writ applicant was awarded a penalty of censure by the Director General, ICAR, New Delhi for his unauthorized absence.
Then again the writ applicant absented himself unauthorisedly from 21.11.1987 to 15.11.1990.
Disciplinary proceedings were started against him for absence from duty and his period of absence from duty was treated as dies-non.
Then he was transferred to Sisal Research Station, Bamra, Sambalpur.
4.
The writ applicant filed several cases before the learned Tribunal and the High Court at Calcutta.
Lastly, he filed the Original Application No. 1197 of 1996 with the prayer of the following reliefs :  "(i) to pay and disburse forthwith the applicant's current salary as claimed herein;  (ii) to pay and disburse the applicant's arrear sum salaries as claimed herein through Bank Daft or through General Provident Fund."
5.
His application has been dismissed.
Thereafter, the writ applicant has filed the present writ application.
6.
Upon due consideration of the materials on record and the submissions advanced by the learned Advocates of both the sides, we find that it is not in dispute that the writ applicant was appointed Scientist Grade S-I under the respondent No. 1 on 28.9.1976.
It is not in dispute that he worked up to 27.9.1981 and then he went on study leave.
It is also not in dispute that the Respondent Authority granted him study leave from 2891981 to 27.9.1983 from time to time.
It is not in dispute that he again prayed for extension of study leave up to 31121984 and the same was refused.
However, he was granted 9 days earned leave from 2891983 to 6101983.
It is also not in dispute that the writ applicant was granted extraordinary leave for a period of 451 days from 7101983 to 31121984.
Then again the writ applicant prayed for extraordinary leave from 111985 to 31121985 which was not granted.
He was directed to join his duty immediately.
It is not in dispute that the writ applicant did not report for duty.
Thereafter he prayed for allowing him to join only on 261986 that is after a gap of about one and half years and he was allowed to join his duty only on 2361986.
For that unauthorized absence from 111986 to 2261986, he was censured by the respondent No. 1.
7.
The fact of taking unauthorized leave by the writ applicant did not come to an end.
Again he remained absent unauthorisedly from 21.11.1987 to 15.11.1990 and for that reason disciplinary proceedings were started against him and an award of dies-non passed against him for such unauthorized long absence.
Then he filed the OA case No. 723 of 1994 before the Central Administrative Tribunal, Calcutta Bench and the learned Tribunal disposed of the said application by an order dated 281994 with the following observations :  "In view of the above position as there are formal defects in this application.
We give liberty to the applicant to withdraw this application and he may file application/applications for the reliefs prayed for in the instant case if not otherwise barred.
The application is disposed of.
No costs."
8.
Thereafter, the application under Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 was filed by the writ applicant and that application was treated as OA No. 1197 of 1996.
9.
Beside the above period of unauthorized leave the writ applicant absented himself from attending office without intimation since 29121991 up to the date of filing of the application on 3091996.
Nor did he file any application for leave for regularization of the unauthorized absence, before the respondent No. 1.
10.
The writ applicant's contention that he was busy in looking after his cases and writ applications before the learned Tribunal and the High Court at Calcutta is ridiculous.
He has also contended that he was present in the Hon'ble Courts since the month of January, 1992 till the time of filing his reply on 2122005 and he was assisting the proceedings of the matter and so he should not have been deemed to have resigned (vide annexure P-9 at page 142).
We find neither the learned Tribunal nor the High Court at Calcutta had asked him not to attend the office.
So the contention of the writ applicant that he was busy in looking after the cases and writ applications filed by him before the tribunal and the High Court, Calcutta cannot be accepted.
And such period of absence cannot be treated as on duty.
11.
He has also contended that he joined C.M.F.R.I.
on 9.8.1996; but we find that this contention is not supported by any cogent document.
Moreover, as per rival contention of the Respondent Authority C.M.F.R.I., is not at all under the Administrative control of the Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres, Barrackpore.
He did not apply for leave.
Nor did he explain as to why was he unable to attend service.
The conduct of the writ applicant proves that he had abandoned the service.
The writ applicant is not, therefore, entitled to get any pay and allowances, increments, promotion etc. since 29121991.
No departmental proceedings were needed when the writ applicant had abandoned the service.
12.
Because of such unauthorized absence the competent authority was, we hold, within its power to proceed on the basis that the writ applicant should be deemed to have resigned from council's service with effect from the forenoon of 29121991 (vide letter dated 2342003 of the Respondent Authority appearing as P-5 at page 130).
Consequent to such situation and the result of the OA No. 1197 of 1996 and the Miscellaneous Application No. 311 of 1996, the Respondent Authority was also within its competence to issue the letter dated 20122005 to the writ applicant intimating that the writ applicant was not entitled to salary for his unauthorized absence from duty and the said period could not be counted towards his past service (vide annexure P-10 at page 143).
13.
By the Miscellaneous Application No. 311 of 1996 the writ applicant has prayed for fixation of pay, seniority, arrears of pay along with interest, etc. As the writ application had abandoned his service, the question of granting arrears of pay, promotion, interest, etc. does not arise at all.
14.
In view of above discussions, we are of the view that the findings of the Central Administrative Tribunal cannot be said to be perverse or without any basis.
We are of the view that the learned Tribunal has rightly observed that the OA Case is bereft of any merit and consequently the Miscellaneous Application No. 311 of 1996 should also be dismissed.
So the writ applicant is not entitled to get any mandamus relief as sought for in the writ application.
The findings of the learned Tribunal are, therefore, upheld.
15.
So there is nothing to interfere with the impugned judgment and order.
Accordingly, the writ application is dismissed.
16.
Considering the circumstances, there will be no order as to costs.
17.
Urgent xerox certified copy of this order, if applied for, be supplied to the learned Advocates for the parties on compliance of all formalities.
Girish Chandra Gupta, J - I agree.
Petition dismissed.
