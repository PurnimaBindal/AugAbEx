3.
Respondent No. 4 filed objection.
Stand taken is that petitioner was not having requisite height of 5'-6", therefore, he was not eligible for conversion.
Reliance is being placed on regulations 176 and 182.
For facility of reference these regulations are being reproduced below :  "176.
Qualifications for direct appointment as Inspectors Sub- Inspectors as Assistant Sub-Inspectors :- (1) Applications for direct appointment to the rank of Inspector Sub Inspector or Assistant Sub Inspector shall at the discretion of the Inspector General be referred to the Superintendent of the district in which the applicant resides direct for report in Form 25.
(2) The applications should be accompanied by the following certificates :-  (i) Education Certificate in original or a copy attested by a Magistrate or by any other gazetted officer.
(ii) Permanent residentship certificate.
(iii) Testimonial in support of the applicants having taken part in sports activities.
(iv) Age according to the Matriculation Certificate.
The applicant must be between 18 and 25 years of age.
4.
Applications of only such candidates will be considered for selection who conform to the following physical standards fixed by the Government :  (i) Height 5'-6"  (ii) Cheat Unexpanded 32"  Expanded 33-1/2".
5.
The minimum academic qualification for the post of an Assistant Sub Inspector is Matriculation and a candidate for Sub-Inspector or Inspectorship should ordinarily be a graduate.
The Inspector General of Police may in special cases relax this educational qualification.
182.
Recruits age and physical standards of : (1) The physical standards and Age of candidates for enrolment as constable shall be the same as for other ranks as mentioned in rule 176.
(2) The greatest care shall be taken to ensure that the age of every police officer is correctly recorded at the time of his enrolment and appointment.
The record becomes of the utmost importance when the question arises of an officer's right to pension and is accepted as decisive in the absence of full proof."
6.
Above Regulations would not apply to the fact of this case.
So far as Regulation 176 is concerned it is applicable to direct recruitment.
Same would be the position when Regulation 182 is taken note of.
Petitioner came to be appointed as driver by way of conversion.
It was not the case of direct recruitment.
Even otherwise once a decision is taken by competent authority then his predecessor is not within its right to review the order.
Such a view was expressed by the Privy Council in case reported as RT Rangachari v. Secretary of State, AIR 1937 Privy Council 27.
Privy Council has observed that in a case in which after Government officials duly competent and duly authorised in that behalf have arrived honestly at one decision their successors in office after the decision has been acted upon and is in effective operation cannot purport to enter upon a reconsideration of the matter and to arrive at another and totally different decision.
Aforementioned observations would be attracted to the fact of this case.
This petition is accordingly allowed.
Order dated 23.2.1999 is set aside.
Order dated 781996 shall remain in force.
7.
This petition is disposed of in the manner indicated above.
