THB Chalapathi, J (Oral) - With the consent of the parties, the arguments in this writ petition have been heard.
2.
It is brought to my notice that petitioner Nos. 4, 5 and 6 have withdrawn the writ petition and their names have been deleted from the array of the petitioners by order dated 1541999 in C.W.P.
No. 7536 of 1999, and petitioner No. 8 has been given relief by the Government itself.
3.
The petitioners who were working as Water Pump Operator Grade II with effect from 1986, 1987 and 1988 have completed more than 5 years service and have approached this Court by way of this writ petition seeking regularisation of their service.
According to them, even after being regularised as Pump Attendants they are performing similar identical duty as Water Pump Operators and possess the requisite qualification.
It is also not disputed that the post of Water Pump Operator is Class III post.
4.
In Civil Appeal Nos. 1579-1580 of 1998 dated March 18, 1999 (Amrit Lal v. State of Haryana and ors.), the Supreme Court passed the following order:-  "Leave granted.
The appellant was a water pump operator, Grade II (a Class III post) on daily wages prior to his regularisation in a Class IV post.
As per the Government Notification dated 1151994, daily wagers who are working on Class III posts and have completed five years' service on 3131993 should be regularised against the respective Class III posts on terms and conditions which are specified in that Notification.
The appellant, although he was working in a Class III post has been regularised in a Class IV post contrary to this Notification.
5.
The respondents contended that the appellant does not possess the requisite qualifications for the Class III post of Water Pump Operator, Grade II.
The qualification which is prescribed for the post as Water Pump Operator, Grade II is either possessing an ITI certificate or being literate and having minimum experience of five years and field test.
The department has issued letter dated 28.10.1994 and also a further certificate dated 321995 to the effect that the appellant (inter alia) possesses the prescribed qualification and is a matriculate.
In view thereof, we allow the appeals and direct that the appellant should be regularised in a Class III post as a Water Pump Operator, Grade II and be paid accordingly.
The same view has been taken by the Punjab and Haryana High Court in the case of Puran Chand and Ors. v. State of Haryana and others C.W.P.
No. 15051 of 1995 from which special Leave Petitions were filed before this Court.
This Court dismissed the petitions by its order in Special Leave Petition (C), Nos. 24700-24702 of 1996 dated 1981997.
6.
The appeals are accordingly allowed."
7.
In view of the above, I direct the State Government to consider whether petitioners in this writ petition possess the requisite qualification and whether they have completed 5 years service as on 3131993 and to pass a speaking order in regard to regularisation of their services as Water Pump Operators, within two months from the receipt of the copy of this order or production of the same by the petitioners.
8.
A copy of the order be given dasti.
Appeals allowed.
