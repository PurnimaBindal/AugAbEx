KC Puri, J - Challenge in this Civil Writ petition is to Annexure P-17 dated 11121998 and Annexures P-18 and P-19 dated 16121998 whereby the petitioner has been declared junior to private respondent Sandeep Jain.
2.
The brief facts of the case are that the petitioner, private respondent No. 3 and other candidates in response to advertisement dated 10081989 applied for two posts of Section Officers (Accounts).
The petitioner, respondent No. 3 and 58 other candidates were interviewed.
The petitioner and respondent No. 3 were selected.
The petitioner was placed at serial No. 1 and private respondent No. 3 was placed at serial No. 2 in order of merit on the basis of their qualifications, experience and interview by the Selection Committee.
The petitioner and respondent No. 3 joined service as Section- Officers.
Similarly, the posts of Clerks were filled on the basis of interview.
The seniority of the Clerks was also fixed on the basis of selection list prepared by the respondent-Corporation.
Similarly, seniority list of Assistants and Junior Scale Stenographers was prepared in order of merit.
They were selected.
In the tentative seniority list of the petitioner and respondent No. 3, petitioner has been shown at serial No. 1 and respondent No. 3 has been shown at serial No. 2.
The respondent No. 3 submitted a representation dated 22031995, Annexure P-4 claiming seniority over and above the petitioner but the Managing Director, who was one of the members of the Selection Committee dismissed the representation on 26051995, Annexure P-5.
The respondent No. 3 preferred appeal dated 23061995, Annexure P-6, to the Board of Directors of respondent-Corporation and that appeal was also rejected vide order dated 19091995, Annexure P-7.
It is further mentioned that according to the bye- laws of the Corporation known as The Punjab Police Housing Corporation Employees Service Bye Laws, no appeal is provided against the order of rejection of representation.
Although the representation and the appeal preferred by respondent No. 3 against tentative seniority list were rejected by the Managing Director and Chairman of the Corporation respectively yet respondent No. 3 served reminder dated 23.02.1996, Annexure P-8 to the Board of Directors and the same was rejected on 05031996.
Respondent No. 3 again submitted reminder dated 16071996 to the Board of Directors vide Annexure P-9.
The Managing Director, vide endorsement dated 21.08.1996 conveyed the order of Chairman dated 19091995 whereby his appeal against the rejection order dated 21031995 was rejected.
Further promotion from the post of Assistant Collector (F&A) to the post of Deputy Controller (F&A) is being made on the basis of merit-cum-seniority as per bye-laws.
The post of Deputy Controller (F&A) became vacant in the month of July, 1997 on account of repatriation of incumbents holding the said post to their parental department.
Shri KN Sharma, Controller (F&A) also proceeded on leave in the month of December, 1997 and accordingly both the posts of Controller and Deputy Controller (F&A) became vacant.
The petitioner was the senior-most Assistant Controller in the Corporation.
Therefore, he was re-designated as Deputy Controller (F&A) and was given the charge of the post of Deputy Controller (F&A) vide order dated 12121997, Annexure p-10.
3.
It is further pleaded that the Departmental Selection Committee was constituted for filling up the post of Deputy Controller (F&A) by way of promotion and the name of the petitioner was cleared for promotion to the post of Deputy Controller (F&A).
Accordingly, the petitioner was promoted as Deputy Controller vide order dated 01041998, Annexure P-11 on regular basis.
He joined as Deputy Controller on 02041998 and till then working as such.
4.
Respondent No. 3 whose representation/appeal against the tentative seniority list was rejected thrice by the Corporation filed Civil Writ petition No. 5077 of 1998 whereby prayer was made for declaring him senior to the petitioner and sought promotion as Deputy Controller after quashing the promotion order of the petitioner dated 01041998, Annexure P-11.
The following order was passed on 16041998 by a Division Bench in that writ petition:-  "It is well made out from the reading of the petition that the dispute herein is of inter-se seniority between the petitioner and respondent No. 3.
Petitioner earlier represented against the seniority list wherein he was shown junior to respondent No. 3.
The said representation was rejected and against that petitioner has filed appeal which is pending disposal before the Board of Directors.
When respondent No. 3 was promoted to the post of Deputy Controller (Finance and Accounts), petitioner addressed representation Annexure P/12 to respondent No. 2 again.
This representation is based on seniority of the petitioner vis-a-vis respondent No. 3, which has not assumed finality on account of appeal, preferred by the petitioner.
Without going into the merits of this case, at this stage, the Court rather deems it more appropriate that the appeal of the petitioner which was filed on 23061995 is decided first.
Consequently, the Board of Director-appellate authority is directed to decide the appeal of the petitioner as expeditiously as possible and preferably within three weeks of the receipt of copy of this order.
Inasmuch as the representation of the petitioner is based on the rules by which promotion is to be made on the basis of merit-cum-seniority, representation Annexure P/11 be also decided within three months of the receipt of copy of this order despite adverse order, if any that may be passed in the appeal preferred by the petitioner.
Disposed of accordingly.
Copy of the order be given dasti on usual payment.
Sd/- VK Bali, Judge  Sd/- M.L.
Singla, Judge"  16041998  5.
The Board of Directors, in their meeting held on 15051998 decided to declare respondent No. 3 senior to the petitioner and also passed an order reverting the petitioner from the post of Deputy Controller (F&A) to that of Assistant Controller (F&A).
Aggrieved against that order, the petitioner approached this Court by way of Civil Writ Petition No. 7249 of 1998.
In the writ petition, notice of motion was issued on 22051998 and the operation of the order of reversion of the petitioner was stayed.
Ultimately, writ petition was disposed of by a Division Bench of this Court, quashing Annexures P12 to P14 being issued without affording any opportunity to the petitioner.
The petitioner made representation to the Board.
The Board of Directors passed order Annexure P-17 and in accordance with the proceedings of the Board, two impugned orders, Annexures P-18 and P-19 have been issued vide which the petitioner has been held junior to respondent No. 3 and he has been reverted from the post of Deputy Controller (F&A).
The reasoning given in these orders was that the petitioner joined the service after respondent No. 3.
It is pleaded that the petitioner is senior to private respondent No. 3 and on that count, a prayer has been made for setting aside Annexures P-17 to P-19.
6.
This Court vide order dated 23121998 stayed the reversion of the petitioner and that order is still continuing.
7.
Respondent Nos. 1 and 2 contested the claim of the petitioner.
It has been pleaded in their written statement that the Board had taken decision dated 11121998 after taking into consideration the length of service of petitioner and respondent No. 3 as well as proceedings of the Selection Committee.
It is admitted that appointment letters were issued to the petitioner and respondent No. 3 but those were not in order of merit.
The respondent joined service on 01.09.1989 whereas the petitioner joined on 06.09.1989.
In compliance with the order of the High Court, the petitioner and respondent No. 3 were asked to submit their representations regarding fixation of their seniority.
The Board took the decision after considering the written representations of the petitioner and respondent No. 3.
The Board of Directors in their meeting held on 11121998 declared respondent No. 3 senior to the petitioner after taking into consideration the record of the Selection Committee, date of joining of the petitioner and respondent No. 3 and other relevant facts.
In consonance with the said decision, reversion of the petitioner was ordered.
Since the selection was not made in order of merit, therefore, the seniority has been fixed on the basis of continuous length of service.
The petitioner was given an opportunity to present his case.
There was no violation of the principles of natural justice.
8.
Respondent No. 3 also filed separate written statement.
It has been pleaded in the written statement that the contention of the petitioner that the Selection Committee at the time of selection prepared merit and he was placed at serial No. 1 of merit and, therefore, at the time of preparation of inter- se seniority, he was to be placed at serial No. 1, is totally misconceived, unfounded and contrary to the record.
The Board of Directors in their meeting held on 15051998, Annexure P-12, it is evident that the order of merit was not determined by the Selection Committee.
There was no illegality much less any arbitrariness or act of favouritism in taking decision by the Board of Directors, Annexure P-12.
Bye-laws of the Corporation came into operation in the year 1990 and prior to that, he was considered senior-most in the cadre and that after the bye-laws, he was down-graded in the seniority, which could not be done by applying the bye-laws retrospectively.
The appeal of the answering respondent was never considered or rejected by the Board of Directors.
Orders, Annexures P-18 and P-19 were passed by the Board of Directors after duly considering the representations, Annexures P-15 and P-16 made by the petitioner.
The promotion of the petitioner was also illegal and against the rules of natural justice as the authority while considering the case of promotion of the petitioner vis-a-vis respondent No. 3 failed to take note of adverse Annual Confidential Reports conveyed to the petitioner which attained finality on rejection of representation against the Annual Confidential Reports.
The criteria of adjudging suitability for promotion was not only merit-cum-seniority but mainly based on selection and as such respondent No. 3 was having better merit than the petitioner.
The service record of respondent No. 3 was better than the petitioner.
It was denied that the seniority list of the Clerks/Assistants/Steno-typists selected along with the petitioner and respondent No. 3 was in order of merit.
According to the directions given in Civil Writ Petition No. 5077 of 1998 to decide his appeal, order Annexure P-13 determining the final seniority list was issued.
9.
In Civil Writ petition No. 12198 of 2007, the petitioner has challenged order dated 20.07.2007, Annexure P-18 vide which order promoting the petitioner and respondent No. 3 as Joint Controller (F&A) was withdrawn.
10.
Private respondent No. 3 Sandeep Jain also feeling aggrieved against the said order has filed Civil Writ petition No. 11531 of 2007.
11.
Since all these writ petitions relate to inter se seniority of the petitioner and private respondent No. 3 Sandeep Jain, the same are being disposed of with this common order.
12.
We have heard counsel for both sides and have gone through the record of the case.
13.
The main dispute in all the three writ petitions is between Inderpreet Singh Arora and Sandeep Jain regarding their inter se seniority.
In case, their inter se seniority is settled, all the other disputes will stand resolved automatically.
14.
Petitioner Inderpreet Singh Arora and private respondent Sandeep Jain were interviewed for the posts of Section Officers and both of them were selected out of 60 candidates.
Time of ten days was given to both petitioner Inderpreet Singh petitioner and private respondent Sandeep Jain.
The petitioner joined the service on 06.09.1989 whereas Sandeep Jain joined on 01.09.1989.
In the selection list issued by the official respondents, the name of Inderpreet Singh Arora, petitioner has been mentioned at serial No. 1 and the name of Sandeep Jain has been mentioned at serial No. 2.
In the provisional seniority list also, the name of the petitioner is mentioned at serial No. 1 and the name of Sandeep Jain has been mentioned at serial No. 2.
Sandeep Jain claiming himself senior on the basis of his length of service moved representation to the official respondents.
That representation was declined vide order dated 26051995, Annexure P-5.
Sandeep Jain preferred an appeal against order, Annexure P-5, vide which his representation was declined and the same was also dismissed on 19091995.
Considering Inderpreet Singh Arora, Assistant Controller (P&A) as senior, he was designated as Deputy Controller (P&A) vide order dated 12121997.
15.
One another strong circumstance which goes in favour of Inderpreet Singh Arora is the original sheet vide which petitioner, Sandeep Jain and other 58 candidates were interviewed.
Number 1 has been mentioned against the name of Inderpreet Singh Arora and his grading is shown as B++ and number 2 has been mentioned against the name of Sandeep Jain and his grading is also shown as B++.
So, this document clinches the issue.
According to the Interview Committee, Inderpreet Singh Arora was placed at serial No. 1 whereas Sandeep Jain has been placed at serial No. 2.
The stand of the official respondents is that since inderpreet Singh Arora joined on 06.09.1989 and Sandeep Jain on 01.09.1989, therefore, on account of length of service, Sandeep Jain is senior.
16.
We have considered the said submission but do not find any force in that submission.
The said controversy has been decided by the Apex Court in the authority reported as Chairman, Puri Gramya Bank v. Ananda Chandra Dass, 1995(1) SCT 546 : 1995(2) Recent Services Judgments 80 and in authority Suresh Chandra Jha v. State of Bihar and others, 2007(1) SCT 468 : 2007(2) Recent Services Judgments 393.
It has been laid down by the Apex Court in these authorities that order shown in Select List will prevail for the purpose of inter se seniority of employee and date of joining is inconsequential.
In the said cases, appellant ranked higher to respondent in Select List.
Time was granted to both the employees to join the service.
It was held that mere fact that respondent joined earlier to appellant does not make him senior to appellant.
There were no rules of the department in the year 1989 governing the inter se seniority of the employees.
So, mere fact that Sandeep Jain had joined earlier to Inderpreet Singh Arora does not make him senior to the petitioner in view of the authorities, referred to above.
17.
The petitioner has annexed his appointment letter, as Annexure P1, and that of Sandeep Jain as Annexure P2.
Both these appointment letters were issued on 28081989 and period of ten days was given to both of them.
It is also not disputed that both petitioner and respondent Sandeep Jain joined the service within the prescribed period of ten days mentioned in their appointment letters.
The stand of the Corporation from the very beginning was that petitioner is senior to respondent No. 3 Sandeep Jain.
Petitioner was shown at serial No. 1 in the Select List.
He was also shown senior in provisional seniority list.
Representation of Shri Sandeep Jain and his appeal for treating him senior vis-a-vis petitioner were dismissed.
Petitioner was promoted prior to Shri Sandeep Jain as Deputy Controller treating him senior.
However, why the Corporation took U turn in favour of Shri Sandeep Jain has not been explained by the counsel for the Corporation.
18.
Learned counsel for Sandeep Jain has submitted that list showing the petitioner at serial No. 1 does not show him as senior but as the alphabet of Inderpreet Singh Arora is earlier to Sandeep Jain, so on that count, he has been mentioned at serial No. 2.
We have considered that submission but do not find any force in the same.
In the selection list, Inderpreet Singh Arora has been mentioned at serial No. 1 and Sandeep Jain has been mentioned at serial No. 2 and the department had been treating Inderpreet Singh Arora as senior to Sandeep Jain and on that count, he was given promotion earlier to Sandeep Jain.
The petitioner in Civil Writ Petition No. 12198 of 2007 has placed Annexures P-19 to P-22.
From the perusal of these documents also, it is clear that the Selection Committee has recommended the names of four candidates vide Annexure P-19 for the posts of Accounts Clerks and the final seniority list regarding those employees is Annexure P-20.
The said seniority list is in the same order in which their names were recommended.
Ms. Rachna Sharma has joined on 03121989 according to Annexure P-20, whereas Ms. Vaneet Bhambri has joined on 13121989 but Ms. Vaneet Bhambri has been shown senior according to the list of recommendations of Accounts Clerks.
Similarly, recommendation was made for the posts of Assistants, vide Annexure P-21 and their final seniority list.
Annexure P-22, is in the same order of recommendations as per Annexure P-21.
So, this trend of the official respondents also shows that the recommendations are made in order of merit.
As discussed above, the selection list mentioning Inderpreet Singh Arora at serial No. 1 and Sandeep Jain at serial No. 2 clear all the doubts that petitioner is senior to Sandeep Jain.
19.
None of the counsel for the respondents could explain the fact as to why number 1 has been mentioned against the name of Inderpreet Singh Arora and why number 2 has been mentioned against the name of Sandeep Jain respondent in the list used at the time of interview.
So, we have no hesitation in holding that Inderpreet Singh Arora is senior to Sandeep Jain.
Consequently, Annexures P-17 to P-19 stand quashed and the petitioner is held entitled to all the consequential benefits, being senior to Sandeep Jain.
20.
Petitioner Inderpreet Singh Arora in Civil Writ petition No. 12198 of 2007 has challenged order dated 20.07.2007, Annexure P-18.
Sandeep Jain has also challenged the said order in Civil Writ Petition No. 11531 of 2007.
In the said order, it has been mentioned that the order being passed was awaiting the outcome of Civil Writ Petition No. 19364 of 1998.
Since the said writ petition has been decided, so the matter is left open at the discretion of the Corporation as to whether to fill up the post of Joint Controller (F&A) or not.
However, while granting promotion, the Corporation shall consider Inderpreet Singh Arora as senior to Sandeep Jain.
Therefore, all the three Civil Writ Petition Nos. 19364 of 1998, 12198 of 2007 and 11531 of 2007 stand disposed of in the above terms.
Order accordingly.
