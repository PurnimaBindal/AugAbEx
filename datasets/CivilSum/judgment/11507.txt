Viney Mittal, J (Oral) - The petitioner has approached this Court through the present petition filed under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India.
His grievance is that both respondent No. 3 and 4 namely, Dharam Pal and Joginder Singh, who were his juniors had been promoted vide orders dated June 14, 1990 and September 14, 1990, (copies whereof have been appended as Annexures P-5 and P-6, respectively), still the case of the petitioner was not considered alongwith them for promotion.
2.
The petitioner had earlier filed CWP No. 4694 of 1991.
It was claimed that the petitioner was also entitled to be promoted from the year 1970 as Inspector and subsequently, as Deputy Superintendent of Police since January 3, 1978, the date of promotion of his junior Man Singh and that he was also entitled to all the consequential benefits from the aforesaid promotion.
The aforesaid civil writ petition was dismissed by this Court vide order dated January 6, 1992.
A Letters Patent Appeal was filed by the petitioner.
The aforesaid LPA No. 637 of 1992 was also dismissed by this Court vide order dated July 16, 1992.
Subsequently, a review application was filed by the petitioner.
In the review application, the following order was passed :-  "In this application, the petitioner prays for review of our order dt. July 16, 1992, by which the LPA was dismissed.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that it was true that the case of the petitioner for promotion had been considered during the years 1970, 1971, 1973 and 1974 and according to the respondent, his average reports came in the way of his promotion.
His third prayer in the writ petition was for the issuance of a direction to the respondents to consider his case for promotion on the dates on which respondents Nos. 3 and 4 were promoted.
It is seen from the order of the learned judge that this particular aspect was not specifically argued and the case was decided on the basis that on every occasion, that is, in the years 1970, 1971, 1973 and 1974 the case of the petitioner had been considered.
We agree with the view expressed by the learned Judge.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that actually the case of the petitioner was not considered when respondents No. 3 and 4 were promoted.
Though we consider that it is not a fit case for review of our order dt. July 16, 1992 yet we are of the view that the petitioner should be given opportunity to file a fresh petition, if his case was not considered for promotion when respondents No. 3 and 4 were promoted.
In the result, we dismiss the application leaving the liberty to the petitioner to file a fresh writ petition only for seeking a direction to consider his case for promotion when respondents Nos. 3 and 4 were promoted if according to the petitioner his case was not actually considered for promotion on those occasions."
3.
The grievance of the petitioner is that although the department was required to consider the case of the petitioner at the time of passing of the orders Annexures P-5 and P-6 with regard to the promotion of respondents No. 3 and 4, still at that point of time his case was not considered.
4.
The claim of the petitioner has been contested by the respondents.
In the written statement, it has been averred that the case of the petitioner was duly considered for promotion in the years 1970, 1971, 1972, 1973, 1974, 1975, 1976 and 1977, alongwith his juniors, but he was not found fit.
The specific stand of the respondents taken in the written statement may be noticed as follows :-  "In reply to para 7 of the petitioner it is stated that the case of the petitioner for list `F' from which promotions to the rank of Inspr.
are made was considered in the years of 1970, 1971, 1972, 1973, 1974, 1975, 1976 and 1977 alongwith his juniors but he was not found fit.
As regards his consideration for promotion to the rank of D.SP and SP from the dates from which Respondents No. 3 & 4 were promoted, as already stated in reply to para 5 above of this petition, the Respondents No. 3 & 4 were given seniority as a result of acceptance of their representations by Respondent No. 1 and were subsequently given ante-dated promotions in the rank of Inspector/D.SP and further promoted to the rank of Supdt.
of Police due to restoration elevation of their seniority in the rank of Inspector and D.SP The petitioner has, therefore, no claim to consider his case at time of ante-dated promotion of Respondent No. 3 & 4 as Inspr/DSP."
5.
The grievance of the petitioner in the present writ petition is very limited.
He claims that although respondents No. 3 and 4, who were his juniors, were promoted in the year 1990, but their promotion was ante-dated.
On that basis, it is argued by Sh. GL Sadana, learned counsel appearing for the petitioner that the case of the petitioner was required to be considered at the time of passing of the actual order with regard to respondents No. 3 and 4.
6.
On the other hand, Ms. Mamta Singal, learned Assistant Advocate General, Haryana has reiterated the stand taken by the respondents in the written statement.
It has been argued that the case of the petitioner was (not) only considered in the years 1970 to 1977 but was considered again in the year 1990 in pursuance to the directions dated July 26, 1990 issued by this Court in CWP No. 9950 of 1990 filed by the petitioner.
7.
I have given my thoughtful consideration to the rival contentions raised on behalf of learned counsel for the parties.
I find that the plea raised by the petitioner deserves to be accepted.
8.
Although the respondents have taken a stand that the case of the petitioner was duly considered in the year 1970 to 1977 and subsequently, in July 1990 also, but at the same time his case was apparently not considered at the time of passing the orders of promotion in favour of respondents No. 3 and 4 i.e. Annexures P-5 and P-6, respectively.
9.
A perusal of the order dated August 11, 1992 passed in the review application filed by the petitioner, apparently shows that a liberty was granted to the petitioner to approach this Court again for seeking a direction to consider his case for promotion when respondents No. 3 and 4 were promoted, if the case of the petitioner was not actually considered at the time of promotion of the aforesaid officers.
10.
A perusal of the aforesaid order passed in the review application further shows that it was already noticed by the Division Bench that the case of the petitioner was duly considered in the years 1970, 1971, 1973 and 1974.
Inspite of the aforesaid fact the petitioner was given a liberty to approach this Court with regard to a fresh consideration at the time when the case of respondents No. 3 and 4 was considered for promotion.
11.
In this view of the fact, the present writ petition is allowed and respondents No. 1 and 2 are directed to consider the claim of the petitioner for promotion with effect from the date his juniors namely, respondents No. 3 and 4, Dharam Pal and Joginder Singh, respectively, were promoted vide orders Annexures P-5 and P-6, respectively.
The aforesaid process shall be carried out within a period of four months from the date a certified copy of the order is received by respondents No. 1 and 2.
If the claim of the petitioner is found to be meritorious, then the petitioner would also be entitled to claim consequential benefits in accordance with law.
as have been made available to respondents No. 3 and 4.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
