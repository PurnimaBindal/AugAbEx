SS Sudhalkar, J (Oral) :- The petitioner by this petition is claiming quashing of letter dated 25.8.2001 (annexure P/4).
By the said letter the respondents have directed that a notice be issued to the petitioner for retrenching him from service.
2.
The petitioner is admittedly appointed in the year 1998.
The case of the petitioner is not covered by any of the policies of regularisation.
Therefore, he cannot be treated as a regular employee for consideration of the question as to whether he could be retrenched or not.
The respondents along with their written statement have filed letter annexure R/1 dated 1992001, i.e. during the pendency of this writ petition, vide which the petitioner has been retrenched and it is also mentioned in the letter that petitioner should collect retrenchment compensation.
It is also mentioned that the services of the petitioner have been retrenched on the basis of "Last Come First Go" rule prescribed under Section 25(G) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
It is also mentioned in the said letter that no person junior to the petitioner has been retained in service on daily wages.
The only question to be seen is whether the retrenchment of the petitioner is valid or not.
The retrenchment having been taken place after the filing of the writ petition and when the respondents are contending that the provisions of section 25-F have been followed.
I find that nothing remains to be done in this writ petition.
The petitioner may challenge the retrenchment order, if it is not in accordance with law.
With the above observations, this writ petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
