THB Chalapathi, J - The petitioner filed this writ petition for issuance of a writ of mandamus directing the respondents to regularise his service as per the decision of the Supreme Court and also the instructions of the State Government.
2.
The petitioner had been appointed on daily wages in the office of respondent No. 3 on muster rolls without issuing any order of appointment during the years 1988 to 1994.
According to him, he remained in service till 31th May, 1994 and thereafter he has not been given any muster roll.
Some other similarly situated persons approached this Court and their writ petitions were allowed.
The services of the petitioner were not regularised, but the services of the persons junior to him have been regularised.
In order to seek regularisation of their services, the petitioners filed a writ petition bearing No. 1691 of 1989.
By an order dated 1.11.1992 this Court passed the following order:-  "It is pleaded that the petitioners had put in more than two years service prior to the filing of the present writ petition.
If that be so, the petition is allowed only to the extent that respondent shall consider the case of the petitioner for regularization in view of the judgment rendered by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(5) S.P.J.
1 : 1992(3) SCT 201 (SC) and the instructions issued by the State Government in this behalf.
However, till such time this exercise is done, the services of the petitioner(s) shall not be dispensed with.
No order as to costs."
3.
There is no dispute of the fact that the petitioner has been working as Casual Labourer on daily wages.
The Government of Haryana on the basis of the decision of the Supreme Court in State Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) RSJ 235 issued instructions on 2751993 vide Annexure P-1 in regard to regularisation of work charge/casual/daily rate employees.
According to those instructions, the casual and daily rate employees who have completed 5 years of continuous service on 31st March, 1993 and were in service on that date shall be regularised.
On regularisation, they shall be put in the time scale of pay applicable to the lowest Group `D' cadre in the Government and they would be entitled to all other allowances and benefits available to regular Govt. servants of the corresponding grade.
Subsequently, a clarification was issued by the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana in his circular letter dated 18.3.1994 which is marked as Annexure P-4.
According to this letter, the Government has decided that those daily wages employees who have worked for 240 days in a year should be e regularised but there should not be a break in service for more than 30 days at a time in a year.
Thus it is clear that for getting the benefit of regularisation of services in the policy of the Government, the employee must have worked for a period of 240 days in a year without any break of more than 30 days.
4.

Thus, according to the petitioner he worked for more than 240 days during the years 1988 to 1993.
6.
The learned counsel for the petitioner placed reliance on a decision of this Court in Giri Raj and others v. State of Haryana and others, reported in 1997(2) RSJ 506 to which I was a party.
In that case the petitioners have fulfilled the condition of working 240 days in a year.
The break in service for more than 30 days was held not to come in the way of regularisation of the petitioners and in that case the break was not on account of the fault of petitioners therein, but due to banning on muster rolls for that month.
The learned counsel for the petitioner further relied upon a decision of this Court in Ram Dhari v. The Chief Wild Life Warden, Haryana and another, 1996(1) RSJ 246 : 1996(1) SCT 615 (P&H)(DB).
In that case, the petitioner completed more than 240 days during the period of five years from 1988 to 1993.
7.
In this case according to the petitioner he has fulfilled the primary condition that he was in service for 240 days or more in a year.
If this is so, his case has to be considered for regularisation of his services in accordance with the instructions issued by the Haryana Government vide Annexures P-1 and P-4.
The respondents have not filed any written statement controverting the statement of the petitioner.
I, therefore, direct the respondents to consider the case of the petitioner for regularisation of his services in accordance with the instructions if he in fact completed more than 240 days in a year during the period of 141988 to 3131993.
8.
The writ petition is accordingly disposed of with the above direction.
