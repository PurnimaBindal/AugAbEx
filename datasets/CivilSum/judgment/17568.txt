NC Jain, J - The counsel for the petitioner, has argued that his clients are entitled to the benefit of the Supreme Court judgment reported as Wazir Singh v. State of Haryana, JT 1995(7) SC 404.
The counsel for the petitioners is unable to point from the facts as stated in the writ petition that his clients actually acquired higher qualification before the cut off date.
Consequently, we allow this writ petition with the observations that the petitioners would be allowed the benefit of the judgment of Wazir Singh in case they acquired higher qualification before the cut off date which is 931990.
This exercise whether the petitioners acquired higher qualification before the afore-mentioned date be done by the Director, Primary Education, Haryana, 30 Bays Building, Sector 17, Chandigarh or some other Junior officer to be deputed by the said Director within a period of 15 days from the date of production of copy of this order which be given dasti.
