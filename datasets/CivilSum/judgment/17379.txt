RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The petitioner who was working as Headmaster is staking his claim for promotion to the rank of Principal on the basis of his seniority.
He stands at serial No. 374 in the seniority list of Headmasters.
On 22121997, 16 Headmasters were promoted as Principals, last promoted person Rajinder Gupta being at serial No. 363.
It is further the case of the petitioner that one Headmaster (Ram Pal) who was promoted on 22121997 has since expired.
To this averment, the reply is that Shri Ram Pal had been erroneously promoted as Principal and when it came to the notice of the department that he had expired, his appointment order was cancelled on 27.1.1998.
The fact is that there is atleast one post of Principal vacant which was given to Shri Ram Pal even though erroneously.
It has further been mentioned by the petitioner in para 6 of the writ petition that there is no other person between seniority Nos. 364 to 373 as all of them have since retired before 18121997.
This fact has been admitted.
That being so, the petitioner is the senior-most Headmaster to be promoted as a Principal and since, as observed above, atleast one post of Principal is vacant which was given to Shri Ram Pal, the case of the petitioner should be considered for promotion with effect from the due date within a period one month.
Since the petitioner stands retired on 3041998, therefore on promotion of the petitioner all notional benefits flowing therefrom should be given to the petitioner.
Let these directions be carried out within two months.
2.
The petition stands disposed of in the above terms.
3.
A copy of this order attested by the Special Secretary of this Court be given to Mr. Rathee for onward transmission to the concerned quarters.
Petition allowed.
