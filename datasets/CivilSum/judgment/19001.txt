JS Khehar, J - Daljeet Kaur Chadha, respondent No. 4 in the instant writ petition, was to retire on attaining the age of superannuation on 2821999.
The Education Department accordingly issued a notification dated 26.2.1999 retiring her on attaining the age of superannuation on 2821999.
2.
While respondent No. 4 was in the employment of the Punjab Government, she was sent on deputation to the Union Territory of Chandigarh.
While on deputation, she was granted a State award by the Chandigarh Administration on 591998 in recognition of valuable services rendered by her to the community as a teacher of outstanding merit.
At the time of the grant of the award she was working as Principal in the Government Senior Secondary School, Sector 8, Chandigarh.
On the basis of the State award and in recognition of her outstanding merit, she was re-employed w.e.f.
1.3.1999 for one year by an order dated 931999.
The order of re-employment was issued by the Education Secretary, Punjab.
It also had the approval of the Department of Personnel and Administrative Reforms.
The aforesaid order dated 931999 specifically and expressly relied on the instructions of the Education Department dated 9101989, for allowing extension in service to respondent No. 4 Daljeet Kaur Chadha.
3.
Petitioner Dalbir Kaur Kalyan has impugned the order dated 931999 whereby respondent No. 4 has been granted extension in service after retirement.
4.
The entire controversy is based on the validity of the departmental authorities to grant an extension to respondent No. 4 for a period of one year after she attained the age of superannuation.
Learned counsel for the petitioner emphasised that by a clear and categoric determination at the highest level, it has been decided not to permit either re-employment or extension to government employee after crossing the age of superannuation.
On the other hand learned counsel for respondent No. 4 asserted that the extension in service allowed to respondent No. 4 was clearly permissible in terms of the instructions of the Education Department dated 9101989.
5.
The instructions and the orders issued by the State Government in respect of extension and re-employment after attaining the age of retirement have been placed on record by the parties.
We had also required Mr. Randhir Singh, the learned Deputy Advocate General for State of Punjab to file an affidavit with regard to the instructions issued from time to time by the State Government in respect of re-employment and extension in service.
He accordingly filed an affidavit dated 1791999.
The following narration is based on various instructions placed on record as also the affidavit dated 1791999, filed by the learned Deputy Advocate General, Punjab.
6.
The State Government issued consolidated instructions laying down the policy regarding re-employment and extension in service beyond the age of superannuation, on 1721967.
The aforesaid instructions were reconsidered on 411985 by the Deparment of Personnel and Administrative Reforms.
As per the instructions dated 411985 as a matter of policy a complete ban was imposed on re-employment/extension in service beyond the age of superannuation, except certain categories of employees covered by the specific policy decisions taken from time to time.
7.
The first policy instructions as an exception to the ban on re- employment/extension in service beyond superannuation (as per the pleadings before us) is dated 2821985.
This instruction had been issued by the Education Department.
According to the aforesaid instruction, teachers of the Education Deparment in Punjab who are winners of National Awards could be granted two years extension/re-employment in service after superannuation subject to their physical and mental fitness.
The said instruction was followed by another instruction of the Education Department, Punjab, dated 9101989 wherein it was decided to grant one year's extension/re-employment in service after superannuation to teachers of the Education Department, Punjab on their having won a State Award.
8.
The instructions for grant of extension and re-employment were reconsidered by the Department of Personnel and Administrative Reforms, whereupon an instruction dated 23111990 was issued.
This instruction purports to be in continuation of the instruction dated 411985.
A perusal of the said instruction shows that the Government took a decision with immediate effect banning the grant of re-employment and extension in service to Government employees on or after attaining the age of superannuation.
The said instructions, however, made an exception only for police personnel because of the special law and order conditions in Punjab at that time.
On 341991 the Department of Personnel and Administrative Reforms, Punjab while making a reference to the instruction dated 23111990 exempted the following three categories from the ban envisaged by the instructions dated 23111990 :  "1.
Political sufferers & their wives - Letter No. 5167-S-11- 4(1)19273 dated 17th July, 1969.
2.
Teachers holding National/State Awards - Memo No. 19/29-79-2 Edu.
4/2288- 89 dated 9th October, 1989.
3.
Blind Govt.
Servants - Letter No. 7572-GSI-77 dated August 1, 1977."
9.
A collective perusal of the instructions referred to above would lead to the conclusion that in spite of the ban envisaged in the instructions dated 411985 and 23111990, teachers of Education Department who had won National/State Awards were entitled to extension/re-employment after attaining the age of retirement, similarly some other categories were also exempt, they are not being referred to herein as they are not relevant to the present controversy.
10.
On 2521997 on the express desire of the Chief Minister, a memorandum was placed before the Council of Ministers explaining the State Government's policy regarding re-employment/extension in service to government employees after their retirement.
In a meeting dated 2621997, the Council of Ministers after considering the said memorandum decided to completely ban extension in service to government employees in future.
The aforesaid determination of the Council of Ministers is being extracted hereunder :-  "The Council of Ministers, after considering the memorandum dated 2521997 of Personnel and Administrative Reforms, decided that after amending the already issued instructions in no eventuality in future, no extension be given to any employee."
11.
It is clear from the instructions dated 411985 and 23111990 that there was already in existence a ban on extension and re-employment after retirement from service; some categories were, however, exempt from the ban; now from the afroresaid decision of the Council of Ministers, it is clear, that the exempted categories were also included in the ban.
The aforesaid conclusion is being drawn from the extract of the decision of the Council of Ministers reproduced above, by which it was sought to amend ".....the already issued instructions....." The instructions already issued banned extension and re-employment after retirment subject to a few exceptions.
By way of amendment the exceptions were also excluded by stating ".....in future no extension be given to any employee...." According to the affidavit placed on record by the Deputy Advocate General, Punjab dated 1791999 the decision of the Council of Ministers was incorporated and circulated through instructions dated 651997.
The said instructions were issued to all the Heads of the Departments to the effect that in furture no extension/re-employment would be allowed to any employee under any circumstances.
Paragraph 8 of the affidavit which expresses the aforesaid decision is reproduced as under :-  "That in compliance to the above mentioned decision of C.M.M.
instructions dated 651997 were issued to all the Heads of the Departments etc. to the effect that in future no extension will be allowed in service to any employee under any circumstances."
12.
A copy of the instruction dated 651997 has been supplied to us by the learned Deputy Advocate General.
The same is in vernacular.
On its persual we affirmed that its contents were to the same effect as expressed in paragraph 8 of the affidavit reproduced above.
The question for our consideration is limited.
Do the instructions issued by the Education Department, Punjab dated 9101989 subsist after the decision of the Council of Ministers dated 2621997 which was circulated to all Departments through instruction dated 651977 ?
In our considered view the decision of the Council of Ministers dated 2621997 after it was duly incorporated and circulated to all Heads of Departments for implementation through instruction dated 651997, had the effect of amending all earlier instructions by which some categories had been excluded from the general ban.
By the said decision of the Council of Ministers henceforth no extension or re-employment was to be allowed to any employee in any circumstance.
In this view of the matter we hold that the instruction of the Education Department dated 9101989 was negated and impliedly withdrawn after the Council of Ministers decision dated 2621997 specifically asserting that in future no extension be given to any employee after retirement.
The said decision of the Council of Ministers was clearly implemented when the Department of Personnel and Administrative Reforms circulated the said decision through instruction dated 2621997.
13.
From the facts narrated above it is clear that respondent No. 4 Daljeet Kaur Chadha, had been granted re-employment/extension in service after she had retired from service on attaining the age of superannuation, for a period of one year on the basis of the instruction of the Education Department dated 9101989.
The order of extension was issued on 931999.
We have, however, already held that the instruction dated 9101989 negated and impliedly withdrawn by the decision of the Council of Ministers dated 2621997 and the instruction dated 651997.
It is, therefore, clear that extension/re- employment, in service was granted to respondent No. 4 wrongfully on the basis of instructions which had ceased to exist.
Extension granted to Daljeet Kaur Chadha was in violation of the complete ban imposed against re- employment/extension in service.
14.
For the reasons recorded above the instant petition is allowed; the order dated 931999 is quashed.
It is, however, directed that no recoveries be made from respondent No. 4 from the emoluments paid/payable to her in lieu of service rendered on re-employment/extension in service till the date of this judgment.
There shall, however, be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
