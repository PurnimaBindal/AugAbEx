Amarjeet Chaudhary, J (Oral) - The Inspectorate Staff Union of Food and Supplies Department, Punjab Government, through its president, has filed this writ petition for the issuance of a writ in the nature of the Certiorari/Mandamus, Quo-warranto for quashing order dated 14/1571987 of the Director, Food and Supplies and Special Secretary to Government Punjab, respondent No. 2 vide which guidelines were issued for realisation of excess weight of wheat stock stored in godowns.
2.
At the very outset, Shri OP Goel Additional Advocate General, Punjab, has stated at the Bar that the writ petition be dismissed as infructuous since the impugned order, Annexure P-5, has been withdrawn.
The learned Additional Advocate General has further contended that the respondents will proceed against the defaulting officials under due process of law and in accordance with the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970.
However, Mr. HL Sibal, Sr. Advocate, contends that since impugned guidelines, Annexure P-5, have been withdrawn, no action can be taken against the petitioner.
3.
I have considered the respective stand of the learned Counsel for the parties.
After giving consideration to the entire matter.
I am of the view that no action against the petitioner can be taken on the basis of the impugned guidelines.
Annexure P-5 since the same stand withdrawn by the Government.
However, it is made clear that the State will be at liberty to proceed against the defaulting officials under the law in accordance with Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970.
4.
The writ petition a dismissed having become infructuous with no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
