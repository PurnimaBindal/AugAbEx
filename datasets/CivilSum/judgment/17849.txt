AP Chowdhri, J - The petitioner was posted as Patwari in district Karnal.
By order of the Collector dated February 10, 1992, punishment of stoppage of two annual increments with cumulative effect was passed.
The petitioner challenged the said imposition of punishment.
His plea was turned down.
The appeal met with no success.
2.
The case of the petitioner is that punishment of stoppage of increments with cumulative effect cannot be considered a minor penalty and, therefore, the same can be imposed only after a regular departmental enquiry.
Reliance for this proposition is placed by learned counsel for the petitioner on Kulwant Singh Gill v. State of Punjab, 1991(2) SCT 30 : 1991(1) RSJ 413. which was followed in a recent decision of this court in Om Phal v. State of Haryana, 1991(3) R.S.J.
632.
In these authorities, it has been held that stoppage of increments with cumulative effect cannot be considered to be a minor penalty and, therefore, regular departmental proceedings must be taken.
3.
The plea in the written statement filed by the State is that regular departmental proceedings were taken.
3.
We have gone through the report Annexure R-2/1 and we find that no regular departmental proceedings were held against the petitioner.
It is not disputed by Miss Ritu Bahri, learned AAG, appearing for the State that no regular departmental proceedings were held against the petitioner.
The law referred to earlier fully applies to the facts of this case.
We have, therefore, no option but to set aside the imposition of the penalty.
It is, however, open to the authorities to proceed afresh according to law against the petitioner, if so advised.
The writ petition is disposed of in these terms.
Order accordingly.
