Gokal Chand Mittal, C.J.
(Oral) - According to the regulations for educational/professional qualifications 20 marks have been provided; for performance/CR 30 marks have been provided; for written test 30 marks and for oral interview 20 marks, are provided.
Challenge is made to oral interview marks it on the ground that this is excessive and it should not have been more than 125% or 15%.
Reliance is placed on the decisions of Ajay Hasia v. Khalid Majub Sehravardi, 1981(2) SCR 79 and Ashok Kumar Yadav v. State of Haryana, 1985(4) SCC 417.
2.
Where selection is made for promotion within the service the provision for 20% oral test is not excessive and we find support from the decision in D V Bakshi v. Union of India, JT 1993(4) SC 180.
Keeping in view the ratio of aforesaid decision we find that 20% marks for oral test is not excessive and the regulation which fell for consideration before us is not illegal or arbitrary.
3.
Even the record was produced by the respondent before us.
On a reading of the relevant merit of the selected candidates vis-a-vis the petitioner, we find that they lost position in the educational professional qualification and the written test.
Even on merit we find no ground to interfere vis-a-vis inter se merits of the candidates.
Petition dismissed.
