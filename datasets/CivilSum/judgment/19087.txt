THB Chalapathi, J (Oral) - The petitioner who is working as a lecturer in History was dismissed from service by the Director of Secondary Education without holding any enquiry consequent upon his conviction under Section 304, read with S. 34 IPC and under Sections 25/27 of the Arms Act.
2.
Simply because a person is convicted, he cannot be dismissed from service.
Both under Article 311 of the Constitution of India and the service rules, a person can be dismissed from service on the ground of conduct that has led to his conviction on a criminal charge.
In the order of dismissal which is Annexure P-2, there is no reference to the conduct of the petitioner at all that led to the conviction.
Therefore, I am of the view that the order of dismissal is liable to be set aside.
Accordingly, I quash the order Annexure P2 as affirmed in appeal by the Financial Commissioner vide Annexure P5.
The writ petition is accordingly allowed.
The petitioner is directed to be reinstated in service and on his reinstatement, appropriate order may be passed by the respondent in accordance with law on the ground that led to his conviction.
Petition allowed.
