RL Anand, J. (Oral) - Shri Krishan has filed this Civil Writ Petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India seeking a writ of mandamus to consider his claim for regularisation in the light of the policies, Annexures P-1 and P-2, framed by the State Government.
2.
The case of the petitioner was that he was appointed on daily wage basis in the month of July, 1990 and since then he is continuing as such.
In the month of January, 1994, the services of the petitioner were terminated.
He took the matter to the Labour Court and the matter was compromised and the petitioner was taken back into service.
Back wages were not paid with effect from 111994 to 1521994 but his services were treated as continuous during this period.
The Haryana Government formulated two policies dated 7.3.1996 and 18.3.1996 and according to the latest policy, a daily wager who has completed three years of service, is entitled to be regularised.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he is in continuous service for more than three years and, therefore, he is entitled to be regularised.
This stand of the petitioner was disputed.
3.
During the pendency of the writ petition, an application was moved on behalf of the petitioner that the main petition itself may be disposed of in view of the decision given in Writ Petition No. 12367 of 1997, titled Smt. Kavita Gandhi v. State of Haryana and others, on 3091997.
In the alternative, it was submitted by Mr. Malik that directions be given to the State of Haryana to consider the case of the petitioner in the light of the aforesaid judgment dated 3091997 and if the petitioner is entitled to be regularised as per the latest policy of the Government and also as per the decision given in C.W.P.
No. 12367 of 1997 on 3091997, the State shall pass an appropriate order for the benefit of the petitioner.
The submission of Mr. Malik is very fair.
If the case of the petitioner is covered under the latest policy formulated by the State of Haryana as also the decision of the aforesaid Civil Writ Petition No. 12367 of 1997, then he is certainly entitled to be regularised.
4.
In these circumstances, the writ petition stands disposed of with the directions to the State of Haryana to consider the claim of the petitioner in the light of the latest policy formulated by it and also in the light of the judgment dated 3091997 passed in Civil Writ Petition No. 12367 of 1997 and if the petitioner is entitled to be regularised then necessary orders may be passed within two months from the receipt of the copy of this order.
If, unfortunately, the State does not want to give any relief to the petitioner, the State shall communicate in writing to the petitioner about the passing of such order so that the petitioner may have the opportunity to again approach to the Court of competent jurisdiction.
Copy of this order be given Dasti to both the parties.
It has been submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioner that he shall send the copy of order to the writ petitioner and also to the competent authority for taking appropriate action.
