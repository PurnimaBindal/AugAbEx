SK Singh, J - This petition has been filed by one Amardeep Kumar against the order of the Central Administrative Tribunal dated 25th May, 2001 because his candidature for being employed with the Postal Department on the rule of harness has not been accepted.
2.
Plainly, the Tribunal has given three reasons that Amardeep Kumar cannot be accepted as an indigent person so as to invoke the principle of the rule of harness, The three reasons given are that his elder brother is already employed as a Postman with the Postal Department, consequent upon the death of his father his mother is receiving family pension and the family is possessed of agricultural land of about 4 acres 38 decimals.
3.
Insofar as the last factor is concerned, an attempt was made to submit before the Court that the department has not been able to prove that this land is possessed by the family and that, in fact, the father of the petitioner had sold away the land during his life time.
This makes the position no better except that the funds may be available with the family on the sale of the family land.
4.
The Court cannot certify that the Tribunal has committed any error in rejecting the candidature of the petitioner.
Dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
