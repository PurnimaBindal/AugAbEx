ML Singhal, J - Hukam Chand joined the service of erstwhile Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) on 661954 as LDC.
He was confirmed on that post on 1111956.
Subsequently, with the reorganisation of the States on linguistic basis the PWD (Electricity Branch) of Punjab and PEPSU were merged into one single statutory body known as Punjab State Electricity Board (for short PSEB) in the year, 1959.
Plaintiff's seniority was retained in the joint seniority list based on his date of joining i.e. 661954.
In the year, 1967, PSEB was bifurcated into, to constitute separate boards for Punjab and Haryana namely PSEB and Haryana State Electricity Board (for short HSEB).
The services of the plaintiff were transferred to the newly constituted HSEB.
Plaintiff has been in continuous service of HSEB since then.
His original seniority based on the date of joining was retained in HSEB.
On 981962, he got promoted to the rank of UDC.
On 831968 he got promoted to the rank of Assistant.
On 141980, he got promoted to the rank of Head Clerk.
He got these promotions in view of his original seniority based on the date of joining the PWD (Electricity Branch) of the erstwhile State of composite Punjab on 661954 as LDC.
He got confirmed as LDC on 1111956, as UDC on 251967, as Assistant on 151973.
On 1771987, tentative seniority list of the head clerks for the period ending 3181979 was circulated by the Secretary, HSEB and his seniority was down graded.
A few promotions were also made to the rank of circle superintendents and he was superseded.
He filed suit on 23.4.1988 challenging his supersession and the promotions based on the said tentative seniority list.
At the same time, he prayed for the grant of stay against future promotions of his juniors.
He was granted interim stay against future promotions.
HSEB was directed to finalise the seniority list.
Final seniority list was drawn up in which he was shown junior.
Original date of joining was not taken into account.
It was drawn up mala fide, arbitrarily to favour PEPSU employees and was not based on any intelligible criteria.
Officials shown at Sr. Nos. 48, 53, 54, 58, 59, 60, 62, 63, 67, 68, 69 and 70 in the final seniority list are all former PEPSU employees who were taken over by the Punjab PWD Electricity Branch at the time of integration of PEPSU into Punjab.
All of them are juniors to the plaintiff in terms of the date of joining recorded in the column meant for recording the date of joining in the seniority list.
He was confirmed as LDC prior to the said officials.
He got promotion to the rank of UDC/Assistant ahead of the said officials.
He got confirmed as UDC and Assistant ahead of the said officials.
None of the said PEPSU employees including the plaintiff were in position as Head Clerk on the circulated crucial date ie.
3181979, upto which date seniority list was compiled.
Plaintiff became Head Clerk on 141980 as shown in column No. 4 of the seniority list while officials listed at Sr. Nos. 48, 53, 54, 58, 59, 60, 62, 63, 67, 68, 69 and 70 were promoted to the post of Head Clerks in the year, 1983.
Seniority list of Head Clerks as on 3181979 is wrong and void ab-initio.
In the impugned seniority, he has been shown promoted to the post of Head Clerk on 141980.
The impugned seniority list was drawn up by Sh. JS Sethi, Administrative Officer of the office of the Chief Engineer (OP) South Delhi, who was himself PEPSU employee.
Sh. Sethi had monoeuvred in a manner that he was shown senior to Sh. JK Nanda, Sh. JK Nanda challenged Sh. JS Sethi's fake seniority and Sh. Nanda came out victorious and he was subsequently shown at Sr. No. 30 while Sh. Sethi was shown at Sr. No. 31.
In the impugned seniority list are shown the names of those persons who have never worked as Head Clerks as for instance Sh. Sham Sunder-61, Tek Chand-68, and Prabh Dayal-67.
As per rule 9(i) of the provisions of the Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) Rules, 1952 adopted by HSEB, the seniority of a member shall be determined as under :  (a) Prior to confirmation (i.e. in case of persons serving on probation or in an officiating capacity) in the order of date of their appointment or if such dates be the same in respect of two or more persons, in the order of their ages, the older being placed above the younger.
(b) After confirmation, by their respective dates of confirmation provided that there two or more members are confirmed w.e.f.
has same date they will retain the order to confirmation.
2.
In nutshell, plaintiff's stand was that he had joined earlier to all the aforesaid PEPUS employees, he got promotions earlier to them, he had been promoted earlier up to the post of Head Clerk, he was entitled to be shown as senior to them in view of rule 9 (i) of the provisions of the Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) Rules, 1952 and this rule was upheld in seniority claim of Sh. JK Nanda vis-a-vis seniority claim of Sh. JS Sethi.
On these allegations plaintiff filed suit for declaration against the HSEB and another to the effect that he was wrongly placed at Sr. No. 104 in the impugned seniority list whereas he should have been shown at Sr. No. 48 after Balle Ram who is at serial No. 47 in the seniority list and Hawan Lal should be shown at serial No. 49 and so on and that final seniority list of Head Clerks as on 3181979 and issued by HSEB on 2411990 is wrong.
By way of consequential relief, he prayed for a decree for permanent injunction directing the defendants not to make any future promotions of the Head Clerks to the next higher post as per the final seniority list, which has been impugned here till the said seniority list is re-drawn as per his version.

It was however admitted that the plaintiff joined as LDC in Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) on 661954.
(561954 afternoon) and was confirmed as LDC on 1111956.
On the integration of the State, PWD (Electricity Branch) of Punjab and PEPSU were merged into PSEB in the year, 1959.
Combined seniority was circulated by the PSEB in the year, 1959.
On bifurcation of the Composite State of Punjab into the present State of Punjab, the services of the plaintiff were allocated to HSEB as UDC.
It was admitted that the plaintiff was promoted to the rank of UDC on 981962, as Assistant on 831968 and as Head Clerk on 141980 as per seniority list circulated for each category by Electricity Board from time to time.
Plaintiff was confirmed as LDC on 1111956, as UDC on 251967 and as Assistant on 151973.
He was promoted as Head Clerk on the basis of seniority list of CA/ ARA/ Commercial Asstt, circulated vide Chief Engineer "OP Memo dated 1711979.
The list of Head Clerks circulated on 1711987 was based on seniority list of ARA circulated on 481983, on the basis of which plaintiff was promoted as Head Clerk.
Further promotions to the post of Circle Superintendent are also being made on the basis of seniority list circulated vide memo dated 481983.
A civil writ petition was filed by PEPSU employees challenging that the passing of departmental accounts examination was not applicable for their promotion from LDC to UDC where as it was applicable to the employees of Punjab PWD(Electricity Branch) and the employees recruited by PSEB and HSEB.
In LPA No. 838 of 1980 read with LPA No. 511 of 1981, High Court held that the passing of the departmental accounts examination was not applicable to PEPSU employees for promotion from LDC to UDC whereas it was applicable to the employees of Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) and the employees recruited by PSEB and HSEB.
In view of this decision, the PEPSU employees were promoted on the basis of seniority list circulated on the re-organisation of PSEB on 9.9.1959, whereas employees of Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) and the employees recruited by PSEB were promoted after passing departmental accounts examination.
The officials listed at Sr. Nos. 48, 53, 54, 58, 59, 60, 62, 63, 67, 68, 69 and 70 in the seniority list are PEPSU employees.
It was admitted that the plaintiff joined service prior to them and was confirmed on the rank of LDC, UDC, Assistant prior to them because the above mentioned writ petition/LPA was decided after confirmation of the plaintiff and the same was implemented after the promotion of the plaintiff as Head Clerk.
Plaintiff was promoted as Head Clerk on 141980 and the employees mentioned at Sr. Nos. 48, 53, 54, 58, 59, 60, 62, 63, 67, 68, 69 and 70 in the seniority were promoted as Head Clerks in the year, 1983 with deemed date of seniority.
Seniority list of Head Clerks which was modified in view of the decision of the LPA was implemented.
It was correct seniority list.
Plaintiff represented against the seniority list.
His representation was rejected by the competent authority.
Final seniority list circulated is correct and according to rules/instructions.
Promotions are being made in accordance with the final seniority list circulated and on the basis of record and confidential reports of the employees.
Promotions are being made on the basis of seniority-cum- merit.
Plaintiff shall be promoted as and when his turn for promotion comes.

Additional District Judge, Rohtak accepted the appeal.
Judgment and the decree of the trial Court was set aside.

7.
Not satisfied with the judgment and decree of the Additional District Judge, Rohtak dated 17111997, HSEB has come up in appeal to this Court.
8.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have gone through the record.
9.
Hukam Chand plaintiff joined the service on 661954 as LDC in the Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch).
He was confirmed on 1111956 on the post of LDC.
He was promoted to the post of UDC on 981962 He was promoted as Assistant on 831968 and as Head Clerk on 141980.
When he was promoted to these posts, his original seniority as having joined on 661954 as LDC held the field.
He got confirmed as LDC on 1111955, as UDC on 251967 and as Assistant on 151973.
So far as officials listed at Sr. Nos. 48, 53, 54, 58, 59, 60, 62, 63, 67, 68, 69 and 70 in the final seniority list Ex.P-4 are concerned, they are all PEPSU employees.
They joined service later vis-a-vis Hukam Chand Plaintiff as shown against the date of their joining.
10.
Now the question arises whether plaintiff's placement below them in the later seniority was or was not justified.
Learned counsel for the HSEB submitted that the final seniority list Ex.P-4 was drawn up where plaintiff was shown at Sr. No. 104 in accordance with the judgment Ex.D-1 of the Punjab and Haryana High Court in LPA No. 838 of 1980 against the decision of learned Single Judge in CWP No. 880 of 1978.
11.
It was submitted that in the judgment of LPA No. 838 of 1980 delivered on 17.12.1982 Ex. D-1, it was held that the passing of prescribed examination for earning promotion would not be applicable to the PEPSU employees who joined on integration of PEPSU into Punjab the service of Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) as in PEPSU they were not required to pass departmental accounts examination for promotion to the higher rank and this was a condition of their service and this condition of their service could not be varied to their disadvantage.
12.
Learned counsel for the respondent (Hukam Chand-plaintiff) submitted that Hukam Chand should have been placed above Sh. Hawan Lal shown at Sr. No. 48 in the final seniority list Ex, P-4 because Hukam Chand was always ahead of him as LDC, UDC, Assistant and Head Clerk.
Learned counsel for the appellant submitted that seniority of Hawan Lal etc, was drawn up keeping in view that for them passing of departmental accounts examination was not necessary for further promotion while the seniority of Hukam Chand was fixed keeping in view different rules of promotion governing him.
It was submitted that because Hukam Chand was governed by different rules in the matter of promotion vis-a- vis Hawan Lal and others.
Hawan Lal and others were shown senior to him and he was shown at Sr. No. 104.
13.
Plaintiff joined service on 661954 while Hawan Lal joined service on 15.7.1955.
It was submitted that it was inequitable to show the plaintiff at Sr. No. 104 and Hawan Lal at Sr. No. 48 in the seniority list.
It was submitted that when the plaintiff was always ahead of Hawan Lal as LDC, UDC, Assistant and Head Clerk, how is that Hawan Lal was shown at Sr. No. 48 and he was shown at Sr. No. 104.
Up to the rank of Head Clerk, original seniority list held the field vis-a-vis other employees of the Board.
Despite judgment Ex.D-1 having been given effect to Hawan Lal became Head Clerk in the year, 1984 while Hukam Chand was Head Clerk in the year, 1980.
Latters Patent Bench of this Court while delivering judgment Ex.D-1 only held that erstwhile PEPSU clerks borne on circle cadre were not bound to clear departmental Accounts Examination for further promotion immediately before the formation of the new State of Punjab, with effect from November 1, 1956.
They did not become amenable to the Rules by virtue of their integration with the corresponding service in Punjab.
The rules have at no stage been validly made applicable to them.
Under these circumstances they could not be legally ignored for being considered for promotion on the ground that they had not cleared the Departmental Accounts Examination in terms of the Rules.
The view taken by the Board in their impugned memorandum dated December 23, 1976 is un- assailable.
The finding of the learned Single Judge to the contrary cannot be sustained.
The Latters Patent Bench of this Court thus laid down that the PEPSU clerks were not bound to clear the Departmental Accounts Examination for further promotion.
It, however, does not appeal to logic how the plaintiff would lag behind in the matter of seniority vis-a-vis Hawan Lal when plaintiff had joined as LDC on 661954 while Hawan Lal had joined as LDC on 15.7.1955.
While preparing seniority list Ex.P-4 plaintiff was not given opportunity of hearing.
He should have been given opportunity of hearing if his seniority drawn up originally was to be disturbed.
14.
No fault can be found with the judgment/decree of the learned Additional District Judge, Rohtak dated 17111997.
Additional District Judge, Rohtak while setting aside the judgment and decree dated 23121993 passed by the Additional Civil Judge (Sr. Division), Rohtak has called upon that the seniority of the plaintiff be drawn up over-again and in case plaintiff's seniority as fixed originally is to be disturbed, he shall be given an opportunity of hearing.
Defendant Board shall draw up seniority list over- again keeping in view the date of joining of the plaintiff as LDC, date of his promotion to the rank of UDC, then as Assistant and then as Head Clerk vis-a- vis the date of joining of Hawan Lal and others as LDCs, their promotions to the rank of UDCs, Assistants and Head Clerks together with the effect of decision of LPA.
While determining the dates of their promotion this fact shall be taken into account that they were not required to pass Departmental Accounts Examination while plaintiff was required to pass Departmental Accounts Examination.
Date of confirmation shall not be taken into account but the date of joining shall be taken into account while determining inter se seniority of Hukam Chand and Hawan Lal etc. because seniority has to be counted from the date of appointment and not from the date of confirmation.
15.
For the reasons given above, this regular second appeal fails and is dismissed.
No order as to costs.
Appeal dismissed.
