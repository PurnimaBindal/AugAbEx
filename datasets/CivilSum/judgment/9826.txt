RL Anand, J - The present is a Civil Revision and has been directed against the order dated 1791998, passed by the Court of Additional Civil Judge (Sr. Division), who dismissed the execution application of the petitioner Ravinder Pal.
2.
Some facts can be noticed in the following manner.
Shri Ravinder Pal filed a suit for declaration that he is entitled to the benefit of military service for the period when he was serving in the Army during the emergency period.
This suit was instituted against the Amritsar Central Cooperative Bank Limited through its Managing Director.
The suit was dismissed by the trial Court.
The plaintiff filed an appeal before the Additional District Judge, Amritsar who vide judgment and decree dated 3.1.1989 accepted the appeal of the petitioner and decreed the suit and declaration was granted that plaintiff was entitled to the benefit of seniority and increment on the basis of the service rendered by him in the military on the basis of circular letter Ex.P.5.
Meanwhile, the Central Cooperative Bank was bifurcated and Tarn Taran Central Cooperative Bank came into existence and the plaintiff was allocated to that Bank.
3.
The plaintiff joined Amritsar Cooperative Bank on 111980.
The plaintiff joined the Army service on 29121962.
He was released from the Army on 4121973.
The first emergency was imposed in the country on 26101962 and it remained in force up to 1011968.
4.
The sole point for determination in this case is whether the plaintiff- petitioner is entitled to entire military service starting from 29121962 to 4121973 or he is entitled to benefit of military service for the Civil Department for the period when the first emergency remained in force from 26101962 to 1011968.
The executing Court dismissed the execution application of the petitioner by holding that service rendered after emergency could not be taken into consideration for the determination of seniority and in these circumstances, the objections filed by the JD Bank were accepted and the execution filed by the decree-holder-petitioner was dismissed.
Aggrieved by the order 1791998, the petitioner has filed the present petition.
5.
I have heard Shri VP Sharma, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner, Shri Aman Singla, Advocate, appearing on behalf of respondents No. 1 and 2 and Shri DV Sharma, learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent No. 3 and with their assistance have gone through the record of the case.
6.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that as per the Circular Ex.P.5 which was relied upon by the first appellate court, the petitioner is entitled to the entire benefit of military service starting from 29121962 to 4121973.
7.
In support of its contention, the counsel for the petitioner relied upon 1996(6) SLR 215 : 1996(4) SCT 759 (P&H), Jai Ram v. State of Punjab.
8.
On the contrary, learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent No. 3 relied upon a judgment of Hon'ble Supreme Court 1997(1) SLR 697 and submitted that plaintiff was only entitled to the benefit of military service when the emergency was actually in force and this aspect of the case has already been taken notice of by the Amritsar Central Cooperative Bank and, therefore, the execution of the decree-holder-petitioner has rightly been dismissed by the Executing Court.
9.
After considering the rival contentions of the learned counsel for the parties, I am of the considered opinion that the petitioner is only entitled to the benefit of the military service when the emergency was actually in force.
In this case admittedly, the petitioner joined the Bank on 111980.
It is also not disputed that the petitioner was relieved from the army service on 4121973.
It is also not disputed that the petitioner joined the Army on 29121962 after coming into force of the emergency which was with effect from 26101962.
He served the army up to 4121973.
In these circumstances, he will be entitled to the benefit of increment/seniority with effect from 29121962 upto 1011968 and for no other period.
10.
Learned counsel for the petitioner then submitted that the executing Court has only given the benefit of three years to the petitioner.
This aspect of the case is being disputed by the learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent No. 3.
Be that as it may, I hold that the plaintiff- petitioner will be entitled to the military service in the matter of increment and seniority only for a limited period when the emergency was actually in force.
i.e. from 29121962 upto 1011968.
Therefore, the claim of the petitioner should be computed and calculated in this manner.
If the claim of the petitioner still remains unsatisfied at the above parameter, the petitioner may file fresh execution in the Court of competent jurisdiction.
Order accordingly.
