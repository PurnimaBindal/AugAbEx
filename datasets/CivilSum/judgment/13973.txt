Satish Kumar Mittal, J - The petitioner joined his services as Company Secretary in Haryana Seeds Development Corporation Limited (hereinafter referred to as 'the Corporation') on 491977.
In February 1978, he became member of the Employees Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 (hereinafter referred to as 'the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952') and Family Pension Scheme, 1971, (hereinafter referred to as 'the Pension Scheme 1971').
He continuously paid his contributory share in the aforesaid Schemes until 1991 when his services were terminated by the respondent-Corporation.
He challenged the order of his termination by filing CWP No. 6900 of 1991 before this Court.
After his termination, the petitioner was in financial constraints, therefore, he withdrew his entire amount of provident fund as well as his contribution of Rs. 5150/- under the Pension Scheme, 1971.
Subsequently, his order of termination was set aside by this Court vide order dated 2511995.
The respondent corporation challenged that order by filing L.P.A. No. 202 of 1995 which was also dismissed.
Ultimately, on 2311998, the petitioner was reinstated in service of the respondent-Corporation with all consequential benefits and with continuity in service.
Though the petitioner joined his service on 2311998 yet the respondent-Corporation deducted and deposited with the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner the amount of contributions payable under the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952, Pension Scheme, 1971 and Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 from 1011991 to 2311998 because the petitioner was reinstated in service with continuity.
Since the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 had become applicable w.e.f.
16111995, the deductions were made from the salary of the petitioner under the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Pension Scheme, 1971 upto 15.11.1995 and thereafter under the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 from 16111995 to 3152000, on which date the petitioner retired on attaining the age of superannuation.
After his retirement, the petitioner claimed pension under the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 as he was having qualifying service of 23 years.
Since the petitioner was reinstated with continuity of service and the contributions under the aforesaid Schemes were deducted from his salary with retrospective effect, i.e. from 911991, there was no gap in the continuity of service of the petitioner with the Corporation and in the membership of the petitioner under the Provident Fund as well as Pension Schemes from the date of joining till the date of his retirement.
The petitioner also gave an undertaking that he was ready to pay the amount of Rs. 5150/- along with interest in respect of his contribution from 121978 to 911991 under the Pension Scheme, 1971 but the Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (Pension) (respondent No. 2 herein) rejected the claim of the petitioner vide order dated 2412002 (Annexure P-6) on the ground that the petitioner had only 9 years 4 months and 1 day qualifying service.
Therefore, he was not entitled for the monthly pension under the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995.
Against that order, the petitioner filed a detailed representation which was rejected by the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner (Pension) vide his order dated 942002 (Annexure P-8).
Against the said order, the petitioner filed an appeal before the Central Provident Fund Commissioner.
The same was also dismissed vide order dated 762002 (Annexure P-10) passed by the Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (Pension).
The petitioner has challenged the aforesaid orders dated 2412002 (Annexure P-6), 942002 (Annexure P-8) and 762002 (Annexure P-10) in the instant writ petition.
2.
The contention of the petitioner is that he has completed more than 23 years of service and thus became entitled to the benefit of monthly pension as provided in para 10(2) of the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 but the respondents have illegally rejected the claim of the petitioner on the ground that he ceased to be the member of the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 as well as the Pension Scheme, 1971 on his withdrawing the contribution from the said Scheme in the year 1991 after his dismissal.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that since the petitioner was reinstated in service on 2311998 with continuity of service and the respondent-Corporation deducted and deposited the amount of contribution towards the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 as well as Pension Scheme, 1971 for the period 1011991 to 15.11.1995 and towards the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 from 16111995 to 2311998, therefore, there was neither any gap in the continuity of service nor there was any gap in making the contribution towards the aforesaid Schemes.
The only thing was that the petitioner had withdrawn the amount of his contribution towards the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and then Pension Scheme, 1971 at the time when he was in harness and out of job.
For that, he immediately after joining the service moved an application before respondent No. 3 requesting that his membership in the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 be restored and he offered to deposit the amount withdrawn by him under the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Pension Scheme, 1971 with interest, but his request was not accepted by the respondents in an illegal and arbitrary manner.
Learned counsel for the petitioner further submitted that in view of the clarification given by the Central Provident Fund Commissioner in his letter dated 22121995, the claim of the petitioner should have been considered by the respondents.
By the said clarification, it has been clarified that in the event a member of Pension Scheme, 1971 is out of employment on or after 141993 but has not withdrawn the benefit, he continues to be a member of Family Pension Fund and as such he automatically acquires the membership of Pension Fund, 1995 on its introduction from 16111995.
Further, it has been clarified that in the event a member has withdrawn the benefit, in such case he may opt from the date he quits the service by returning the withdrawal benefit with interest element as may be prescribed and avail of the benefits under the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995.
In both cases, no extra contribution will be payable in the given situation.
In view of the said clarification, the respondents should have considered the claim of the petitioner as he was never given any opportunity to exercise his option.
3.
On the other hand, learned counsel for the respondents submitted that since the petitioner did not serve the respondent-Corporation for 10 years as member of the Employees Family Pension Scheme from 1011991 to 3152000, he was not entitled to pension.
It was further submitted that with the withdrawal of the contribution towards the Pension Scheme, 1971, the petitioner ceased to be the member of the said Scheme and he cannot be given the benefit of the previous service in view of the fact that he ceased to be the member of the Scheme and as such he does not fall under the definition of 'Member' under the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 as according to para 6A of the said Scheme, a member of the Employees Pension Fund shall continue to be such member till he attains the age of 58 years or he avails the withdrawal benefit to which he is entitled under para 14 of the Scheme, or dies, or the pension is vested in him in terms of para 12 of the Scheme, whichever is earlier.
Learned counsel for the respondents submitted that according to the explanation attached to the definition of Member in Clause 2(1)(ix) of the Employee Pension Scheme, 1995, which provides that an employee shall cease to be the member of Pension Fund from the date of attaining 58 years of age or from the date of vesting admissible benefits under the Scheme, whichever is earlier.
With the support of the aforesaid two provisions, it is contended by the respondents that with the withdrawal of the amount of the Scheme, the petitioner ceased to be the member of the Pension Scheme 1971, therefore, his previous service cannot be taken into consideration for the purpose of counting the qualifying service and for the grant of monthly pension under the Scheme of 1995.
4.
We have heard the arguments of the learned counsel for both the parties and perused the record of the case.
In our view, the stand taken by the respondents is hypertechnical and against the very purpose and object of the Scheme.
In the instant case, undisputedly the petitioner was member of the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Pension Scheme, 1971 and the amount of contribution was being deposited towards those Schemes till 1991 when services of the petitioner were illegally terminated.
It is also undisputed fact that the termination of the petitioner was set aside by this Court and he was reinstated in service with continuity of service and with all consequential benefits.
It is also undisputed that though the petitioner was reinstated in service on 2311998 but his contribution towards the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Pension Scheme, 1971 up to 15.11.1995 and thereafter towards the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 was deducted and deposited with the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner, i.e. from the date the petitioner joined his service and the date when he retired.
His contribution towards the aforesaid schemes was duly being deducted and deposited.
The only thing is that when the services of the petitioner were illegally terminated, he withdrew the amount of his contribution when he was in financial constraints.
Immediately after his reinstatement, the petitioner offered that his membership in the Scheme be continued and he had offered to deposit the amount with interest which was withdrawn by him towards the Pension Scheme, 1971.
Since the respondent- Corporation accepted the contribution of the petitioner towards the Provident Fund Scheme, 1971, Pension Scheme, 1952 and Employees Pension Scheme, 1995, from 1011991 to 2311998, there should not be any difficulty for not accepting the request of the petitioner for depositing the amount of contribution withdrawn by him in the year 1991.
Admittedly, the petitioner paid the contribution towards the Pension Scheme, 1971 and Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 from 1011991 to 2311998.
Thus for all intents and purposes, he was a member of the Scheme from 141993 to 15.11.1995.
In view of this factual position, the petitioner was entitled for the pension as provided under Clause (c) of para 6 of the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995.
Admittedly, the said option was not provided to the petitioner.
Secondly, Clause 2(iv) of the said Scheme defines the expression "Contributory Service" which means the period of "actual service" rendered by a member for which the contributions to the fund have been received or are receivable.
Under this Scheme, it is also permissible that past service by an existing member of the Scheme from the date of joining the Employees Pension Scheme Fund can be counted provided he was the member of the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 and Pension Scheme, 1971 on the date of commencement of the Scheme.
Undisputedly, the petitioner was member of the previous Scheme and contribution was also made from his salary from 1991 to 1995 when the Scheme was introduced.
Therefore, he cannot be denied the benefit of the past service prior to 1991 on the ground that he withdrew the amount of benefit, particularly when he offered to deposit the said amount with interest with Regional Provident Fund Commissioner.
5.
In our view, the very purpose and object of the Scheme rendering benefit of pension to the employees cannot be defeated by the respondents by adopting a hypertechnical view of the matter, particularly when the Central Provident Fund Commissioner had issued clarification dated 22121995 to the effect that in the event a member has withdrawn the benefit, in such case he may opt from the date he quits the service by returning the withdrawal benefit with interest element as may be prescribed and avail of the benefits under the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995.
We do not see any reason why the petitioner cannot be permitted to deposit the amount of his contribution which he had got refunded in 1991 in harness.
If the petitioner is allowed to deposit the said amount and a direction is issued to the respondents to release the monthly pension to the petitioner under the Scheme, it will meet the ends of justice.
In view of the aforesaid, this writ petition is allowed and the impugned orders dated 2412002 (Annexure P-6), 942002 (Annexure P-8) and 762002 (Annexure P-10) are hereby quashed and respondent No. 2 is directed to accept the amount of contribution withdrawn by the petitioner under the Pension Scheme, 1971 along with interest and thereafter release the benefit of monthly pension to the petitioner under the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 within a period of three months after depositing the aforesaid amount in accordance with the Pension Scheme, 1971.
Petition allowed.
