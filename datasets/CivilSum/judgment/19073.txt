VK Jhanji, J. (Oral) - It is not in dispute that the petitioner retired from service in February, 1994 but retiral benefits such as leave encashment, GP Fund, Pension, Commutation pension, E.P.F.
and payment of D.C.R.G.
has not been released to the petitioner within two years and there months of this retirement and there is delay of one year to four year and 9 months.
2.
Respondent Nos. 1 to 4 have contended that the delay took place on account of indifferent attitude of respondent No. 5 as respondent No. 5 was required to transfer the GPF accounts of the petitioner but the same was not sent.
Be as it may.
Respondent Nos. 1 to 4 had no justification to withhold retiral benefits for sufficiently long period, and, therefore, the petitioner has become entitled to the interest on the delayed payment.
We, therefore, direct respondent Nos. 1 to 4 to pay interest at the rate of 12% per annum on the delayed payment w.e.f.
1.4.1994 till the payment was made.
The amount of interest shall be paid within three months and in case amount is not paid within three months, then the petitioner should be entitled to interest at the rate of 18 per cent per annum till the same is paid.
In case respondent Nos. 1 to 4 feel that delay is on the part of respondent No. 5 then it shall remain open to them to take appropriate proceedings against respondent No. 5 in accordance with law.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to Rs. 2000/- as costs of the writ petition to be paid by respondent Nos. 1 to 4.
The writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
