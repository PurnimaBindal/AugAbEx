RS Mongia, J (Oral) - This judgment of mine will be disposed of CWP Nos. 11832 and 10326 of 1989, as common question of law and fact are involved in both the cases.
However, a brief reference to the facts of each case is required.
2.
C.W.P.
No. 11832 of 1989.
Surinder Singh, petitioner, was initially appointed as a Restorer in the office of Director, Animal Husbandry, Punjab, Chandigarh, on 14th August, 1970 and subsequently he was promoted as a Clerk with effect from 21st March, 1975.
Later on, he was promoted as Senior Clerk with effect from 26th September, 1978 and was further promoted as Assistant-cum-Accountant in the pay scale of Rs. 570-1080 against a regular post of Assistant-cum-Accountant with effect from 1st October, 1986.
The promotion order is Annexure P-1 to the writ petition, which reads "Shri Surinder Singh Senior Clerk is promoted as Accountant-cum-Assistant in the scale of Rs. 570-1080 vice Shri Amar Singh.
It has been further provided in Annexure P-1 that the petitioner would be on probation for a period of one year and if the work and conduct is found unsatisfactory, he may be reverted accordingly.
Vide order, dated 12th June, 1989 (Annexure P. 2), the pay of the petitioner was revised in pursuance of the provisions of the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988 (hereinafter referred to as the Rules).
The revised pay-scale was Rs. 1500- 2640.
The revision of pay-scale was made effective from 1st January, 1986.
The petitioner was reverted to the post of Senior Clerk vide order dated 4th August, 1989 (Annexure P.3), on the ground that he had not passed the Assistant Grade Examination.
It is this order that has been impugned in the writ petition.
3.
C.W.P.
No. 10326 of 1989  Petitioner, Darshan Kaur was promoted as Junior Auditor from the post of Senior Clerk the pay-scale of Rs. 570-1080 vide order dated 12th March, 1988 (Annexure P.1 to the writ petition).
The pay-scale was revised to Rs. 1500-2640 on 1st March, 1989 (Annexure P.2) made effective from 1st January, 1986.
She was also reverted vide order 4th August, 1989 (Annexure P4) on the same ground as Surinder Singh in the other writ petition was reverted i.e. that she has not passed the Assistant Grade Examination.
It is this order which has been challenged by the petitioner in this petition.
4.
The argument of the learned counsel for the petitioners is that there was no condition imposed in the order of promotion to the ranks of Assistant-cum-Accountant/Junior Auditor that they would have to pass the Assistant Grade examination.
According to the learned counsel, when they were promoted to the abovesaid ranks, there were no rules that before being promoted to the said ranks they had to pass the Assistant Grade examination or after being promoted provisionally they had to pass the said examination.
His further argument is that simply because their pay-scale was equated with the pay-scale of an Assistant in the year 1989, though with effect from 1st January, 1986, that would not ipso facto impose a condition, for being promoted to the ranks of Assistant/Accountant/Junior Auditor, that they must pass the Assistant Grade examination.
5.
On the other hand, Mrs. Charu Tuli learned Assistant Advocate General appearing on behalf of the respondents, submitted that since the pay-scales of the Assistant-cum-Accountant and Junior Auditors had been made equal to the pay-scale of Assistant with effect from 1st January, 1986, and the petitioners having been promoted thereafter, they would be required to pass the Assistant Grade examination to hold the promoted posts.
She further submitted that with effect from 24th June, 1988, a new set of Rules was enforced, namely, Punjab Animal Husbandry class III (Ministerial) Service Rules, 1988, which required that the Assistant-cum-Accountant and the Junior Auditors are required to pass the Assistant Grade examination before they can be promoted to the said posts.
6.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, I find considerable force in the submissions made by the learned counsel for the petitioners.
When the petitioners were promoted to the ranks of Assistant-cum-Accountant/Junior Auditors, there was no requirement under any Rules that they had to qualify the Assistant Grade examination.
Simply because, at a later stage, the pay-scales of Assistant-cum-Accountant/Junior Auditors happened to be equal to that of an Assistant, though with retrospective effect would not ipso facto impose the condition that even the Assistant-cum-Accountant/Junior Auditors had to pass the Assistant Grade examination before being promoted to the said ranks.
This will be further evident from the fact that in the year 1988 before the revision of the pay-scales in the year 1989 (when the pay-scales of the Assistant-cum-Accountant and Junior Auditors were made equal to the Assistant).
Assistant Grade examination was made a condition precedent for promotion to the ranks of Assistant-cum-Accountant/Junior Auditor irrespective of the fact whether they had the same pay-scales as that of Assistants or not.
This would go to show that the equality in the pay-scale has nothing to do with the Assistant Grade examination.
The case of the petitioners cannot be covered by the 1988 Rules, as they were published in the Punjab Government Gazette dated 24th June, 1988, and the promotions had been made prior to that.
7.
For the reasons which have been given above, the reversion orders could not be passed on the ground that the petitioners had not qualified the Assistant Grade Examination, as no such condition could be imposed on their promotion under any Rules, including 1988 Rules, which came into force after their promotion.
8.
For the foregoing reasons, the writ petitions are allowed, the reversion order dated 4th August, 1988 (Annexure P.3) in C.W.P.
No. 11832 of 1989 is hereby quashed.
So also the reversion order dated 4th August, 1988 (Annexure P4) in C.W.P.
No. 10326 of 1989 is quashed.
Needless to mention here that by virtue of the quashing of the reversion orders, the petitioners will be entitled to all consequential benefits flowing therefrom.
There will be no order as to costs.
