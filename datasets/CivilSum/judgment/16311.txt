MN Rao, J - This application is for review of the judgment of Seetharam Reddy, J, in WP No. 8370 of 1987 dated 28111989.
As the learned Judge laid down the office, this review petition was directed to be listed for hearing before me by the Hon'ble the Chief Justice.
2.
The petitioner herein obtained first class in MA and also the degree of M Phil.
in Public Administration.
In 1980 he was temporarily appointed as a lecturer in the 2nd respondent-College.
It appears, one of the permanent lecturers working in the college went on long leave of five years.
In respect of that post, an advertisement was issued by the 2nd respondent-College on 30.3.1984 inviting applications from qualified candidates to fill the post.
Seventeen persons including the petitioner therein applied.
A duly constituted committee, in accordance with the provisions of GOMs.
No. 905 dated 2191976 interviewed all the candidates and selected the petitioner.
The appointment order issued in favour of the petitioner mentions that the appointment was for a specific period from 17.7.1984 to 2281985.
It is also not in doubt that the holder of the lien, in respect of which the petitioner was appointed subsequently, resigned from the post.
As the petitioner's services were not regularised, he filed WP No. 3838/86, which was allowed by K Ramaswamy, J, (as he then was) with a direction to the respondents that an opportunity should be given to the petitioner to place all the relevant material in order to enable the authorities to pass appropriate orders regarding regularisation of his services.
In compliance with the direction, an order was passed by the Director of Higher Education on 19.5.1987 in Rc.
No. 2806/K3-3/85 rejecting the claims of the petitioner on the following grounds :  "On careful consideration of the replies received from the petitioner and the Special Officer, Arts and Science College, Kamareddy, it is decided that the approval of appointment of Sri G Vidyasagar beyond lien period i.e. 2281985 and admission to aid is not feasible of compliance for the following reasons :- (1) He was approved by the Committee in the lien vacancy of Sri K Sudershan Reddy w.e.f.
17.7.1984 and the lien period expired by 22885.
(2) Roaster system was not followed while appointing the petitioner and he was appointed only for the lien period of Sri K Sudershan Reddy.
(3) The post was not advertised as regular vacancy and hence the appointment cannot be approved on a permanent basis.
(4) The post was advertised only as a lien vacancy and as such a number of more qualified and eligible applicants were precluded from applying for the post.
(5) The petitioner was not selected for any regular vacancy and his appointment was made for a properly defined term only, namely, the lien period of Sri K Sudershan Reddy.
(6) Regular vacancy arose on 23885 which has to be filled in as per prescribed procedure in force on this date.
Actually filling up of vacancies caused after the issue of GOMs.
No. 527, Edn., dt. 10.12.84 are banned and in D's Proc.
Rc.
No. 7342/K-2/85 dt. 24.12.84 instructions were issued banning the appointments as per the prescribed procedure in GOMs.
No. 905 Edn., dt. 21.9.76.
(7) The services of the petitioner were terminated w.e.f.
5486 vide Special Officer Lr.
No. 197/E/86 dt. 5486.
There is no proof of his having approached the Competent Authority as prescribed under rules made under the Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, if he has any grievance against the orders of the Special Officer."
3.
Challenging the legality of that order, the petitioner once again filed WP No. 837087.
That writ petition was dismissed by Seetharam Reddy, J, after adverting to the contents of the proceedings issued by the Director of Higher Education on 19.5.1987.
While dismissing the writ petition, the learned Judge observed :  "In view of the proceedings of the Director of Higher Education referred to above, it cannot be said that the petitioner can claim to be appointed on a permanent basis.
Hence, the contention raised on his behalf is devoid of merit and substance and is accordingly rejected.
However, since it is the case of the management that for purpose of appointment of this post, a notification by way of advertisement will have to be made calling for applications and that the vacancy is meant for Scheduled Caste candidates and not for open competition, though it is opposed by the learned counsel for the petitioner, till the vacancy is filled up, the petitioner may be continued in his office."
Seeking review of the above order, the present review petition was filed.
4.
Sri M Ramachandra Reddy, learned counsel for the petitioner, contends that when the manner of appointment of the petitioner in accordance with the provisions of GOMs.
No. 905 dated 2191976 is not in doubt, the petitioner's services ought to have been regularised as per the law laid down by the this Court in WP No. 2699 of 1986 and batch dated 141981 (D.G.
Krishnamacharyula v. Government of AP, reported in 1988(2) A.L.T.
237).
This fact was not brought to the notice of learned Judge (Seetharam Reddy, J,) by the counsel appearing for the petitioner at the time of hearing and the inadvertence of the counsel has resulted in the dismissal of the writ petition.
5.
I am inclined to agree with the contention raised by Sri Ramachandra Reddy GOMs.
No. 905 dated 2191976 prescribes the procedure for filling up the pot of lecturers in private aided colleges.
Whether the posts have arisen temporarily or permanently, the manner of appointment must be in accordance with the procedure contemplated in GOMs.
No. 905.
There is no dispute that the appointment of the petitioner was in accordance with GOMs.
No. 905.
The Selection Committee comprised a representative of the University - an expert in the subject; the Principal and the Special Officer of the College, who was the District Collector.
No doubt the post fell vacant as a result of one of the lecturers going on long leave and because of that reason, in the appointment order it was mentioned that the petitioner was appointed for a specified period i.e. from 17.7.1984 to 2221985.
When once it was established that the appointment was in accordance with GOMs.
No. 905, it was held by PA Choudhary, J, in DG Krishnamacharyulu's case :  "....those teachers who have been selected following the procedure of selection should be treated as regularly filling the posts notwithstanding the fact that their appointments were described as temporary ad hoc .....I, therefore, think it unjust to treat these posts as being vacant notwithstanding the fact that there have been substantial compliance with the existing law in the matter of their appointments."
The above judgment has become final.
The authorities, therefore, are bound to give effect to the law declared by this Court.
Unfortunately, the above judgment was not brought to the notice of Seetharam Reddy, J, by the learned counsel appearing for the petitioner at the time of hearing.
This was undoubtedly "an inadvertence" as rightly described by Sri Ramachandra Reddy, who is the present counsel appearing for the petitioner.
Had the above judgment been brought to the notice of the learned Judge, he would not have dismissed the writ petition.
6.
For these reasons, the review petition is allowed; the dismissal order passed by Seetharam Reddy, J, in WP No. 8370/87 dated 28111989 is set aside and the writ petition is allowed with a direction to the respondents to treat the petitioner as a regularly appointed lecturer in the subject of Public Administration with effect from 17.7.1984 and this post shall be excluded from the purview of the College Service Commission.
No costs.
Government Pleader's fee Rs. 350/-.
Petition allowed.
