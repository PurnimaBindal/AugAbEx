Ashok Bhan, J - This petition has been filed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for issuance of a writ of mandamus directing the respondents to grant to the petitioner the proficiency step-up increments on completion of 8/18 years of service after taking into consideration of ad hoc service rendered by her; for grant of an additional increment for non-participation in the strike on 821978 and to allow her to change her option to come to the new pay scales with effect from 111987 under the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988.
2.
Petitioner was appointed as SS Mistress on 19101977 on ad hoc basis.
Her services were regularised with effect from 1101980.
State of Punjab decided to grant proficiency step up increments with effect from 111988 to the employees working in the State on completion of 8 and 18 years of service.
Petitioner became entitled to the proficiency step-up increment on completion of 8 years service in the year 1985 and to the step-up increment on completion of 18 years of service with effect from 18101995, as she was appointed to the service on 19101977.
Petitioner was not granted the proficiency step-up increments on completion of 8/18 years of service on the due dates on the ground that her ad hoc service could not be counted while calculating 8/18 years of service for grant of proficiency step-up increments on completion of 8/18 years of service.
3.
Grievance of the petitioner that her ad hoc service should be counted for calculating 8/18 years of service has been conceded by the respondents in their written statement.
Petitioner has been ordered to be granted the proficiency step-up increments on completion of 8/18 years of service with effect from 111987 and 19101995 respectively, after counting her ad hoc service.
Writ Petition regarding this relief has become infructious in view of the written statement filed by the respondents.
4.
Employees of the State of Punjab had gone on strike on 821978.
Employees who did not participate in the strike were ordered to be rewarded with an additional increment.
Petitioner did not participate in the strike.
She was denied the additional increment on the plea that she was working on ad hoc basis.
According to the respondents, the additional increment for non-participation in the strike on 821978 could only be extended to the regular employees.
5.
Government had sanctioned the grant of an additional increment to the employees who did not participate in the strike on 821978.
Petitioner at that time was working as an ad hoc employee on which post she was later on regularised with effect from 1101980.
Distinction made for grant of an additional increment (for non-participation in the strike) on the basis of being in ad hoc regular service is unrealistic and discriminatory.
The employees' Union had given a call to all its employees, whether ad hoc or regular, to go on strike on 821978.
Government had sanctioned the grant of an additional increment to those employees who did not participate in the strike.
Petitioner did not take part in the strike and she could not be denied the additional increment for non-participation in the strike merely on the ground that she was working on ad hoc basis on the said date.
No discrimination could be made for the purpose of grant of the additional increment between the employees working on regular basis and ad hoc basis.
Petitioner is, thus, held entitled to the grant of the additional increment for non-participation in the strike on 821978.
However, she will not be entitled to any arrears as she has approached the Court at a belated stage.
The benefit shall enure for the future.
6.
Petitioner is entitled to change her option to come to the new revised pay scales with effect from 111987 in view of the Division Bench judgment of this Court reported in Ghuman Singh and others v. The State of Punjab and others, 1996(2) Service Cases Today 259.
7.
Accordingly, the relief regarding change of option is granted to the petitioner in terms of the aforesaid judgment of this Court.
8.
Counsel for the petitioner did not press the claim for grant of higher pay scale on acquiring higher qualifications as the petitioner had acquired the higher qualifications after the cut-off date.
This petition is allowed to the extent indicated above with no order as to costs.
