Amar Bir Singh Gill, J - Heard counsel for the parties.
The petitioner and his wife were earlier working as Lecturers in Shivalik College, Naya Nangal, District Ropar, which was taken over by the Government in 1997.
Thereafter, vide order dated 1352000, the petitioner was shifted to Patiala.
Thereafter, the petitioner was again shifted to Shivalik College Naya Nangal.
The petitioner and his wife both moved a joint a representation for getting both of them adjusted either at Patiala or Ludhiana on compassionate ground.
Both of them were posted at Ludhiana.
The petitioner was adjusted against respondent No. 3 who according to the petitioner was working at Ludhiana for the last 17 years and ultimately, she was shifted to district Ludhiana in another College.
Subsequently, the petitioner was again shifted to Karamsar in place of respondent No. 3 who was shifted to Ludhiana on the ground that her husband is running private business at Ludhiana, as a couple case.
2.
Considering the submissions of the learned counsel for the parties, we find that if the State Government had accepted the request of the petitioner on compassionate ground for posting him and his wife, both being Government employees in accordance with Govt. instructions at one station, there could be no reason to immediately transfer again the petitioner.
It is thus clear that the petitioner is being discriminated by the respondent in the matter of following Government instructions pertaining to posting of couple case employees at one station.
Under the circumstances, we allow the petition, quash the impugned order Annexure P11, transferring the petitioner from Ludhiana to Karamsar and direct that the respondents shall find out a feasible solution so that respondent No. 3 be also accommodated at Ludhiana or at a place near to Ludhiana.
A copy of this order be given Dasti.
Petition allowed.
