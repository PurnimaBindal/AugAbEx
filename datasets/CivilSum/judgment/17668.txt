SP Kurdukar, CJ - Admitted.
Pleadings of the parties are complete.
By consent, writ petition placed on board and called out for hearing.
2.
Between April, 1982 and August, 1984, the petitioner remained posted as Assistant Food and Supplies Officer at Patti.
Sometime in September, 1984, the petitioner was transferred to Amritsar.
According to him he handed over the complete charge to his successor without any deficiency.
On 25.7.1990, a show cause notice was served upon the petitioner.
The memo of charges was also served on the petitioner alleging certain deficiencies in the stock which were noticed in December, 1984 and January, 1985.
The petitioner filed his reply.
On persual of the reply, the disciplinary authority vide its order dated 1021992 (Annexure P3) awarded punishment of stoppage of one increment without cumulative effect and recovery of Rs. 253596.
On 3041992, the petitioner filed an appeal against this order of punishment before the Appellate Authority.
But the appellate authority dismissed the appeal on 23.4.1993 (Annexure P4).
The petitioner also submitted a representation requesting the order of stoppage of increment be given effect from 111991 instead of 111993.
But the said representation according to the petitioner remained undisposed of till date.
It is in these circumstances, the petitioner moved this Court by way of filing the present writ petition.
3.
Respondent Nos. 1 to 3 filed the written statement and have tried to justify the order of punishment.
4.
The principal contention raised by Mr Randhawa, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner is that the order of punishment of stoppage of one increment has adversely affected his (Petitioner) pensionary benefits.
According to the learned counsel, the charge of misconduct relates to the year 1984 and 1985 and the punishment was awarded on 1021992 after a period of 8 years.
The petitioner has now retired from service on 3061993.
The order of punishment of stoppage of one increment is dated 1021992 but came into force with effect from 111993.
Since the petitioner has retired on 3061993, this punishment is bound to affect his pensionary benefits throughout his life.
It is in these circumstances, learned counsel urged that the said punishment be given effect from earlier date so that petitioner's pensionary benefits are not affected.
5.
In our opinion, having regard to the nature of misconduct involved in this case and in the interest of justice, we modify the order of punishment dated 1021992 which has come into force from 111993 to the period in which the petitioner has submitted his reply to the charge-sheet.
If the authorities had disposed of the petitioner's reply earlier, probably this situation would not have arisen.
In the facts and circumstances of the case, we direct the respondents to give effect to the punishment order w.e.f.
111991 instead of 111993.
6.
The respondents are directed to accordingly fix the pension of the petitioner.
The gratuity amount which is held by the respondents to be released within three months from today or pass an appropriate order in circumstances of the case.
Writ petition to stand disposed of in the above terms.
