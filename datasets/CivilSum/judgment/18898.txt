RL Anand, J - Bhim Singh filed the present petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for the issuance of writ in the nature of certiorari seeking the quashment of order Annexure P-2 vide which the disability pension of the petitioner was denied and the petitioner through this writ petition has prayed for the grant of disability pension from the date it was discontinued along with interest.
Further directions were sought by the petitioner that respondents be directed to constitute Re-Survey Medical Board to assess the enhanced disability of the petitioner.
2.
Some of facts can be noticed in the following manner.
The petitioner was enrolled as a Sepoy in the Indian Army on 3111952.
On 23.10.1968 he was discharged from the service due to disablement developed on account of disease namely Psycho Neurosis.
On 30.7.1968 disability of the petitioner was assessed by the Medical Board at 30% and the petitioner was granted disability pension.
Second Re-Survey Medical Board was constituted which held its meeting on 3071970 and the disability of the petitioner was assessed and it was found to be 30%.
The petitioner was paid disability pension even on the finding of the Board dated 3071970.
Third Re-Survey Medical Board was constituted and it held its meeting on 19.5.1973 and the disability of the petitioner was assessed again as 30% and the petitioner was allowed disability pension up to 18.5.1975.
3.
The grouse of the petitioner now starts.
He says that after 19.5.1973 no Re-Survey Medical Board was constituted.
The disability pension of the petitioner has been wrongly discontinued with effect from 18.5.1975.
The petitioner wrote several letters to the respondents for the constitution of the Re-Survey Medical Board as the petitioner alleges that in fact his disability has increased from 30% onwards.
4.
With these above allegations, the petitioner has made two fold prayer; one for the grant of disability pension with effect from 18.5.1975 and second for the reconstitution of the Re-Survey Medical Board to assess his disability.
5.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents who filed the reply and denied the allegations.
According to the respondents, the petitioner did not appear before the Re-Survey Medical Board in the year 1975 as a result of which his disability pension was discontinued with effect from 18.5.1975.
6.
I have heard Shri I.D.
Singla, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner and Shri Anil Malhotra, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents.
7.
The first point for determination is did the respondents-authority constitute a Medical Board in the year 1975 or not ?
Though it is alleged in the written-statement that Re-Survey Medical Board was constituted at Base Hospital, Delhi Cantt and the petitioner was informed vide letter No. CUN/RSMB/548/75 dated 361975 and a free railway warrant was issued yet I do not find any prima facie proof in this regard.
Rather the evidence on record appears to be that of 27.12.1983 (Annexure P-3/A) vide which the respondent- authority had been trying to constitute Re-Survey Medical Board.
8.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that once the disability pension of the petitioner has been ascertained and calculated by three Medical Boards consecutively and that there is no change in the disability assessed by the three Medical Boards, as per Regulation No. 185-A, it has to be inferred that the disability of the petitioner continued for a period of 10 years at the first instance.
9.
There is a merit in the contention raised by the learned counsel for the petitioner.
Rule 185-A, runs as follows :-  "When the disability is accepted at the same percentage on the basis of three consecutive medical boards including the release/invaliding medical boards, the disability will be normally accepted as incapable of improvement and regulated under the provision of Regulation 143 and the award of disability pension/disability element shall then be sanctioned for a period of 10 years in the first instance".
10.
In the chart prepared by the respondents in the written statement the following position becomes clear.
First Board was constituted on 30.7.1968 and the disability percentage of the petitioner was assessed at 30% and the disability pension was allowed at Rs. 48/- per month for the period of two years starting from 30.7.1968 to 2971970.
Second board was constituted on 2081970 and disability was again assessed at 30%.
Disability pension was assessed at 48/- per month.
It again recommended the disability pension for two years starting from 2081970 to 1981972.
The third Board was constituted on 19.5.1973.
Again the disability of the petitioner was assessed at 30%.
Rs. 48/- per month were assessed for disability pension and Rs. 36/- per month for service element.
This disability was again assessed for interim period of two years starting from 19.5.1973 to 18.5.1975.
When there is a consecutive three Boards' opinion about the disability of the petitioner which was assessed at 30%, it has to be inferred that the petitioner remained disabled up to 10 years starting from the date of first disability i.e. 30.7.1968.
Meaning thereby, that the petitioner remained disabled by fiction of Rule up to 2971978.
After 1978, no medical Board was constituted.
11.
In these circumstances, the present petition is allowed with the declaration that the petitioner will be entitled to disability pension for a period of 10 years ie up to 2971978.
The petitioner will give the adjustment of the disability pension which he has already received.
The balance of the disability pension shall be given to the petitioner within three months from the date of the receipt of the copy of this order failing which the respondent-authority shall also pay interest at the rate of 12% from the date of the default.
Further directions are given to the respondents- authority to constitute the Medical Board.
The petitioner would be informed about the constitution of the Medical Board.
The petitioner then will appear before the Re-Survey Medical Board which will re-assess the disability of the petitioner as to whether it has decreased or increased or remained static.
As per the finding of the Board with regard to the disability of the petitioner, further disability pension shall be released to him accordingly.
If the petitioner does not appear before the Re-Survey Medical Board inspite of due notice, it will always open to the respondent-authority to discontinue the disability pension of the petitioner.
There shall be no order as to costs in the present petition.
Petition allowed.
