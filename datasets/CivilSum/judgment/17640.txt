GS Singhvi, J - The petitioner joined service as Overseer on 2581958 in the Irrigation Department of the then State of Punjab.
He became an employee of the State of Haryana w.e.f.
1111966 as a result of re-organisation of the erstwhile State of Punjab.
He was then promoted as Sub Divisional Officer on 2091978 and continued to hold this post till 31.10.1991 when he retired on superannuation.
With effect from 151989 the petitioner's pay was fixed in the scale of Rs. 4100-5300 and as on 151991, the petitioner was drawing salary with basic of Rs. 4350.
2.
When his pension was fixed, the petitioner found that the Government has taken into consideration his average pay as Rs. 1610/ instead of Rs. 2150.
His grievance is that when his last drawn average pay was Rs. 4350, the respondents were duty bound to fix his pension at the rate of Rs. 2150 plus dearness allowance.
3.
The respondents have filed reply contesting the claim of the petitioner.
4.
During the course of hearing, Shri Raina, learned Deputy Advocate General, Haryana, drew the attention of the Court to the Memo No. 9/54/93-4 IE dated 771995 issued by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to the Government of Haryana, Irrigation and Power Department, whereby the Government conveyed its decision to the Engineer-in-Chief, Irrigation Department, Haryana, that the officers who retire or have retired after getting selection grade of Rs. 4100-5300 be granted pension in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300 and not in the lower pay scale, subject to the condition that the Government will be free to revise the issue regarding pensionary benefits after the decision of the pending writ petitions.
5.
In view of the fact that the Government has itself taken a policy decision to grant the pensionary benefits on the basis of pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300, it is necessary to direct that the petitioner's pension and retirement benefits should be revised by taking into consideration his last pay drawn in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300.
6.
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to revise the pension and all others retiral benefits of the petitioner taking into consideration his pay drawn in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300.
Necessary steps should be taken by the respondents for giving the benefit of revised retiral dues including gratuity etc. within two months of the submission of a certified copy of this order.
It is made clear that if the amount of arrears payable to the petitioner is not so paid within two months period, he shall become entitled to be paid interest at the rate of 15% per annum w.e.f.
1111991 and in that event, it will be the responsibility of the defaulting official to make good the loss which may be suffered by the public exchequer.
Costs are made easy.
Petition allowed.
