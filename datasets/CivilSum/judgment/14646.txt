Amarjeet Chaudhary, J (Oral) - The petitioner was selected for the post of Medical Officer (PCMS Class-II) on 20-1-1987 on ad hoc basis on the completion of her M.B.B.S in 1984, internship in 1985 and house job in 1986.
She joined as such on 24.4.1987.
The petitioner was appointed on regular basis to PCMS Class-II Service of the State Government w.e.f.
29.8.1988 on the recommendations of the Punjab Public Service Commission.
In pursuance of the advertisement issued by the respondents for admission to the Post Graduate Degree/Diploma Course to Government Medical Colleges at Amritsar and Patiala, the petitioner applied for the same.
The Post Graduate Course classes were to start w.e.f.
2-5-1990.
Per terms of the advertisement, 60% of the seats in various Post Graduate Degree/Diploma Courses in these two Government Colleges at Amritsar and Patiala, were reserved for Registrars/Demonstrators/members of the PCMS Class-II Service, whereas the remaining 40% seats were for the fresh medical-Graduates who could compete alongwith the in-service candidates on the basis of their merit.
Per terms of the conditions of the advertisement, Members of the PCMS Service were eligible against 60% quota only if they had rendered a minimum three years, rural service.
Ad hoc service rendered by a member of PCMS Service was to be counted towards experience.
The petitioner's first preference was for MD Pediatrics and her second preference was for MD Gynaecology.
The institution-wise, her first preference was for Medical College, Amritsar and second for Medical College, Patiala.
2.
The respondents had fixed the cut off date for eligibility as 31-3-1990.
The grievance of the petitioner is with regard to the fixing of cut off date for eligibility as 31-3-1990.
According to the petitioner, the cut off date should have been fixed as 1-5-1990 because the classes of Post Graduate Courses were to start on 2-5-1990.
3.
To the petitioner's great dismay in the list of candidates who were selected for admission to Post Graduate Courses, she was selected for the Post Graduate Course in Pathalogy against 40% quota.
Her claim for admission to MD Paediatrics was rejected on the ground that she falls short of experience of three years, rural service by 23 days taking the date of eligibility as 31-3-1990.
4.
This writ petition was admitted on June 7, 1990 and the petitioner was given provisional admission to the Post-Graduate Course in Paediatrics in Government College, Amritsar subject to the decision of the petition.
5.
The matter could not be adjudicated upon earlier.
This Court has been informed that the petitioner has completed her studies and her result has not been declared due to the pendency of the writ petition.
I do not consider it necessary to adjudicate upon the controversy in dispute on merits and to take a legalistic view of the matter.
Rather, I choose to take a compassionate view of the matter.
I, therefore, direct the official respondents to declare the result of the petitioner within a fortnight.
6.
The other plea of the petitioner is that she was selected as inservice candidate.
As such, she is entitled to full salary for the entire duration of the course.
7.
The learned Counsel for the petitioner in order to fortify his claim has relied upon the decision of this Court dated September 11, 1991, rendered in CWP No. 822 of 1988 which has attained finality as LPA No. 541 of 1992 filed by the State was also dismissed.
The Counsel has also relied upon the decision rendered in CWP No. 1492 of 1992 decided on July 17, 1992.
8.
The learned Counsel for the respondents has not been able to show any law contrary to the decision relied upon by the learned Counsel for the petitioner.
9.
Accordingly, the petitioner is entitled to full salary for the entire duration of the Course and the respondents are directed to release the arrears thereof within two months from the receipt of a copy of this order.
Civil Writ Petition No. 8527 of 1990 and Civil Misc.
Application No. 2655 of 1993 stand disposed of accordingly.
Parties are left to bear their own costs.
Petition disposed of.
