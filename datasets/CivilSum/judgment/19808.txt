Jawahar Lal Gupta, J - The petitioner, who has already retired, claims that he was due for promotion as Superintending Engineer w.e.f.
March 31, 1971, when a person junior to him viz. Shri AS Sekhon was promoted.
The petitioner submitted representations.
Vide orders dated August 30, 1974, he was promoted to the post of Superintending Engineer.
His claim for promotion with retrospective effect was finally declined vide orders dated June 26, 1981, a copy of which has been appended as Annexure P.1 to this petition.
In the meantime, as stated by the learned counsel for the parties, the petitioner had already retired from service in the year 1978.
Through the present petition, the order at Annexure P.1 has been challenged and the petitioner has claimed promotion w.e.f.
March 31, 1971.
2.
In the written statement filed on behalf of respondent No. 1, it has been inter alia averred in paragraph 23(1) that "the representation of the petitioner was accepted on April 16, 1977 and he was given promotion w.e.f.
March 31, 1971, the date his junior, Sh. AS Sekhon was promoted."
It has also been averred that the petitioner was paid the salary of the post against which he worked.
3.
In view of the above averment in the written statement, it is clear that his claim for retrospective promotion w.e.f.
March 31, 1971 has been accepted by the department.
It is also clear that the petitioner has been paid salary only for the period he had actually performed the duties of the post of Superintending Engineer.
In other words, as submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioner, he has not been paid the salary for the period from March 31, 1971 to August 29, 1974 in the rank of Superintending Engineer.
Once the claim of the petitioner for retrospective promotion has been accepted by the department, it was incumbent on the authorities to refix his pay in the rank of Superintending Engineer on the hypothesis that he had joined the post on March 31, 1971 and to pay him all the arrears of salary.
Further, even the hypothesis that he was working as Superintending Engineer w.e.f.
March 31, 1971.
4.
I accordingly accept this writ petition and quash the order at Annexure P.1.
It is further ordered that the respondents shall refix the petitioner's salary in the rank of Superintending Engineer on the hypothesis that he was promoted as such on March 31, 1971.
The arrears on account of the difference of salary as also on account of refixation of pension and gratuity shall be paid alongwith interest @ 12 per cent within three months from the date of the receipt of the copy of this judgment.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to his costs, which are assessed at Rs. 3,000/-.
Petition accepted.
