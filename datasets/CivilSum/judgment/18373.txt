We have heard learned counsel for the parties and have perused the record.
2.
It is the conceded position that respondent No. 5-Shri Satnam Singh, while working as an Executive Engineer at the relevant time, passed the impugned order dated 5102000 (Annexure P-13) whereby in terms of Clause-2 of the Contract Agreement No. 2 of 1998-99, he imposed a liability upon the petitioner to pay compensation to the tune of Rs. 10,95,604/- being 5% of the agreement amount of Rs. 2,19,12,082/-.
3.
Aggrieved at the afore-mentioned order, the petitioner submitted a representation-cum-appeal in terms of the agreement between the parties.
It is also not disputed that meanwhile respondent No. 5 (Shri Satnam Singh) having been promoted as Superintending Engineer, entertained the said representation-cum-appeal against his own order dated 2872003 (Annexure P- 23) and dismissed the same vide one of the impugned order dated 1862003 (Annexure P-23).
4.
Various facts of the doctrine of audi alteram partem are well known.
Neither one can be a judge in this own cause nor should be judge his own judgments.
Since the representation-cum-appeal was preferred by the petitioner against the order passed by Shri Satnam Singh-respondent No. 5, he with a view to ensure that justice is not only done but must appear to have been done, ought not to have himself decided the same.
Consequently, we allow this writ petition to the extent that the order dated 1862003 (Annexure P-23) is quashed with a direction to respondent Nos. 1 and 2 to entrust the representation-cum-appeal preferred by the petitioner against the order dated 5102000 (annexure P-13) to a Superintending Engineer-cum-Competent Authority, other than respondent No. 5, for deciding the same objectively and in accordance with law.
The representation-cum-appeal of the petitioner shall be decided within a period of three months from the date of receipt of a certified copy of this order.
No order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
