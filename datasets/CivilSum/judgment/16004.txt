TS Doalia, J - Heard counsel.
The grievance of the petitioners is that they were given increments in the year 1978 which are being withdrawn in the year 1991.
According to the learned counsel for the petitioners, this cannot be done.
The learned counsel is placing reliance on Shyam Babu v. Union of India, (1991)2 SCC 521.
In the case before the Supreme Court, it was found that the petitioners were paid higher scale erroneously.
It was further found that the petitioners were not at fault.
it was accordingly held that no recovery could be made.
Apart from the above, it may be seen that some benefit was given in the year 1978.
This could not be withdrawn in the year 1991, because even if this was wrongly given, the recovery could be made within a period of three years.
That period has already over.
Thus, looking from any point of view, the recovery proceedings taken in the year vide orders annexures P/7 and P/9, cannot be sustained.
These orders are according quashed.
Petitions allowed.
