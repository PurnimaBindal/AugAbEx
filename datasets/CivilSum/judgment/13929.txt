JS Khehar, J (Oral) - Notice of motion.
2.
On our asking Mr. MS Sindhu, DAG, Haryana, accepts notice on behalf of respondent Nos. 1 and 2.
3.
Learned counsel for the respondents states that it is not necessary to file a formal reply to the instant writ petition and that the main case can be disposed of today itself.
4.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties.
5.
The petitioner was placed under suspension for the first time by an order dated 1212004.
The petitioner preferred an appeal against the aforesaid order.
However, the Appellate Authority dismissed the appeal preferred by the petitioner on 1282004.
It is in the aforesaid circumstances that the petitioner approached this Court by filing Civil Writ Petition No. 15540 of 2004 impugning the orders dated 1212004 and 1282004.
This court entertained the aforesaid writ petition and stayed the operation of the suspension order passed against the petitioner.
6.
The petitioner was placed under suspension for a second time by an order dated 1062004.
The petitioner preferred an appeal against the aforesaid order dated 1062004.
The Appellate Authority, while entertaining the appeal, has stayed the operation of the aforesaid suspension order dated 1062004.
7.
The petitioner has yet again, for a third time, been placed under suspension by an order dated 13122004.
The aforesaid suspension order was passed after issuing a show cause notice dated 392004.
The following grounds depicted therein were sought to be taken into consideration while placing the petitioner under suspension :-  "1.
That you have kept cash in hand in excess to the permissible limit during the period March 2001 to March 2003.
2.
That out of amount received by your under SGRY Scheme, balance amount of Rs. 2,41,613/- is lying in the account as it is and you have not spent the said amount (Rs. 2,41,613/-) on the development works till date.
3.
That you have executed the development works without getting the estimate done and you have also not got entered the said development works in the M.B.
(Measurement Book)."
As against the third order of suspension dated 13122004 the petitioner is stated to have preferred an appeal which is presently pending before the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government of Haryana, Development and Panchayats Department, Chandigarh.
Despite the filing of the said appeal, no interim order has been passed in favour of the petitioner.
The solitary basis of the petitioner in approaching this Court at the present juncture is for seeking an interim order against the third order of suspension dated 13122004.
8.
Having perused the basis of the instant suspension order (extracted above) as well as the fact that the appeal preferred by the petitioner is pending adjudication before the Appellate Authority, we consider it just and appropriate to stay the operation of the suspension order dated 13122004, till the final disposal of the appeal.
Order accordingly.
Writ petition stands disposed of.
Order dasti on payment of usual charges.
Petition allowed.
