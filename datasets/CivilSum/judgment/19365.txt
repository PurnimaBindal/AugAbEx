Jawahar Lal Gupta, J - The petitioners, most of whom are working as Junior Library Assistants, in the Punjab Agricultural University, Ludhiana, pray for the issue of a writ of mandamus directing the authorities to grant them the pay scale of Rs.700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980 and Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986.
Are they entitled to this relief ?
A few facts may be noticed.
2.
Petitioner No. 1 is working as a Library Assistant.
The other petitioners (except petitioner No. 6 who retired in 1993) are working as Junior Library Assistants.
They entered the service of the University before December 31, 1972.
The petitioners allege that their duties are similar to those being performed by the Librarians in various Government Colleges in the State of Punjab.
The petitioners were granted the pay scales applicable to the College Librarians on different occasions.
Invoking the principle of 'Equal pay for Equal work', the petitioners submit that the action of the respondent- University in not granting them the scale of Rs.700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980 and the scale of Rs2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986 as these have been granted to the College Librarians, is discriminatory and violative of Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) of the Constitution.
3.
The respondent-University contests the claim made by the petitioners.
It points out that the qualification for the post of Junior Library Assistant is Matriculation with a Certificate in Library Science.
The pay scale of the petitioners at the time of their employment was Rs. 110-200.
Later on, it was revised and raised to Rs. 125-300 which had been granted to the non- professional-semi-professional Librarians in the Education Department of the State Government.
This pay scale was further revised and raised to Rs. 700- 1200.
With effect from January 1, 1986, this pay scale was revised to Rs. 1640-2925.
The petitioners were placed in this scale w.e.f.
that date.
4.
On the other hand, the qualifications for the post of a Librarian in a College or a University were Bachelor's degree with one year's diploma in Library Science or Bachelor in Library Science.
The pay scale of post of a University Librarian was Rs. 300-600.
This pay scale was later on revised and raised to Rs. 700-1600 and the prescribed qualification was Post Graduate Degree with one year's diploma in Library Science or Bachelor's degree in Library Science.
With effect from January 1, 1986, this pay scale has been revised and raised to Rs. 2200-4000.
It has been further pointed out that the hierarchy of various cadres in the University Library comprises of "professional Librarians designated as University Librarian, Deputy Librarian and Assistant Librarian.........." The cadre of the "allied non-professional -semi-professional employees" consists "Junior Library Assistant, Restorer and Attendants".
This cadre structure was laid down in the order dated May 27, 1966.
The claim of the petitioners regarding parity with professional Librarians is wholly mis-conceived.
The nature of duties performed by the petitioners are "altogether different from the professional Librarians i.e. Assistant Librarians in the University and College Librarians in the Colleges..." The petitioners who constitute the supporting staff cannot claim parity in the matter of pay scales with the professional Librarians.
Various documents have been produced in support of the pleas in the writ petition.
5.
The petitioners have filed a replication.
It has been averred that the pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 "has been granted to the Government College Librarians......irrespective of their qualifications if they were recruited prior to 31121972".
The petitioners refer to the Agenda Item prepared by the University for the 91st meeting of the Board of Directors held on February 24, 1984 to point out that the pay scale of Rs. 300-600 had been granted to three of the petitioners w.e.f.
April 1, 1978.
It has been further pointed out that even the Punjab University, Chandigarh had granted the scale of Rs. 300-600 to Junior Library Assistants w.e.f.
November 1, 1966 irrespective of the qualifications possessed by the existing staff.
It is also averred that in respect of the Librarians recruited before December 31, 1972, the pay scale of Rs. 700-1200 had been revised to Rs.700-1600 w.e.f.
January 1, 1980 and Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986.
The petitioners having been equated with the College Liberarians are entitled to the grant of these benefits.
6.
Counsel for the parties have been heard.
Mr. Sibal has submitted that the petitioners perform the same duties as the College Librarians.
This fact was recognised by the University when it sanctioned the pay scale of Rs. 700-1200 to the petitioners w.e.f.
April 1, 1978 vide order dated March 9, 1984, a copy of which has been produced as Annexure P-3 with the writ petition.
Thereafter, the State Government had granted the scale of Rs. 700-1600 to the College Librarians w.e.f.
April 1, 1980 vide order dated May 11, 1989, a copy of which has been produced as Annexure P-7 with the writ petition.
The University had prepared a proposal for the grant of a similar relief to the petitioners but no decision was taken.
On the contrary, the petitioners were placed in the scale of Rs. 1640-2925 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986, while these employees who were in the scale of Rs.700-1600 were placed in the scale of Rs. 2200-4000.
According to the learned counsel, the action was violative of the principle of 'Equal Pay for Equal Work' and suffered from the vice of discrimination.
The claim made on behalf of the petitioners was controverted by Mr. JS Khehar who appeared for the respondents.
7.
The two questions that arise for consideration are :-  (1) Are the petitioners at par with the College Liberians ?
(11) Is the action of the University discriminatory and, thus, violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution ?
Reg : (i)  First of all, it is apt to notice the factual position.
The College Librarians were initially in the scale of Rs. 125-300.
At that time, the petitioners were in the scale of Rs. 110-200.
They were placed in the scale of Rs. 125-300 w.e.f.
November 25, 1972.
On March 3, 1976, the pay scale of the College Librarians was revised and raised to Rs. 300-600 w.e.f.
November 1, 1966.
The petitioners continued in the pay scale of Rs. 125-300.
On January 1, 1978, the College Librarians were placed in the scale of Rs. 700-1200, while the petitioners were granted the scale of Rs. 450-800.
It was only on March 9, 1984 that the petitioners were placed in the scale of Rs. 700-1200.
However, the revision was not made effective from January 1, 1978 but only w.e.f.
April 1, 1978.
It is true that w.e.f.
January 1, 1986, both categories of employees were placed in the scale of Rs. 1640-2925 but by order dated May 11, 1989, the College Librarians were retrospectively placed in the scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980 while no such benefit was given to the category of petitioners.
Furthermore, the College Liberarians were placed in the scale of Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986.
The petitioners had continued in the scale of Rs. 1640-2925.
8.
On a perusal of the above facts, it is obvious that the petitioners were not at par with the College Librarians at the time of their entry into service.
They have not been at par even subsequently.
Even when the petitioners were placed in a scale equal to that granted to the College Librarians, it was not w.e.f.
the same date but a later date viz. April 1, 1978.
The two categories of employees did not start equally nor had they continued to be at par with each other.
It also deserves notice that there is nothing on the record to show that the qualifications of a Junior Library Assistant and a College Librarians are the same.
It has also not been shown that there is parity in the nature of duties.
9.
Taking the totality of circumstances into consideration, the first question is answered in the negative.
It is held that the petitioners were never equal to the College Librarians.
The equation in the pay scale which may have occurred for short intervals of time was merely a co-incidence.
It was not an acknowledgement of equality between the two cadres.
Reg : (ii)  10.
The University was created by an act of Legislature.
It is an autonomous body.
It has a right to determine the conditions of service of its employees.
In the exercise of its powers, it has determined the hierarchy.
It has sanctioned the posts of University Librarians, Deputy Liberians and Assistant Liberians.
The pay scale of Rs. 2200-4000 has been sanctioned for the post of Assistant Librarian.
The qualifications etc. as recommended by the University Grants Commission have also been accepted by the University.
A person, for appointment to the post of Assistant Librarian, has to be a Graduate in Library Science.
The University has also sanctioned posts of Junior Library Assistants which is a non-professional/semi-professional cadre.
The employees in this cadre have to assist the persons working as Librarian.
Deputy Librarians and Assistant Librarians.
In the very nature of things, the posts held by the petitioners are in a lower category.
The qualifications prescribed for these posts are also lower viz. Matriculation with a Certificate in Library Science.
The two categories of employees are different.
There is no parity between them.
11.
Furthermore, it is also clear that a decision of the Government regarding revision of pay scale of its employees is not per se binding on the University.
If the University on consideration of the matter finds that it is unable to follow the policy of the Government in respect of its employees, it cannot be said to have acted illegally or unfairly.
Mr. Sibal was at pains to point out that the Panjab University had implemented the decision of the State Government.
On the other hand, Mr. Khehar pointed out that the Guru Nanak Dev University and the Panjab University had not done so.
Be that as it may, the fact remains that if on account of cadre structure, the University has not chosen to follow the decision of the Government, it cannot be forced to do so.
12.
Mr. Sibal submitted that the grant of the pay scale of Rs. 700-1200 to the petitioners by the University was an acknowledgement of the fact that they were at par with the College Librarians and, thus, equated to the post of Assistant Librarian.
The contention is misconceived.
Even if it is assumed that at some stage, the University had given the same scale of pay to the petitioners as the Government had granted to the College Librarians, it cannot be said that the petitioners had been equated with the Assistant Librarians working in the University.
The two were never treated as equals.
They have continued to be unequal.
Thus, the charge of discrimination is mis-conceived.
13.
There is another aspect of the matter.
It is permissible for the employer to differentiate between the employees on the basis of educational qualifications.
The petitioners are admittedly not qualified for appointment to the posts of Assistant Librarians as they do not possess the degree of Bachelor of Library Science or the other qualifications as prescribed by the University Grants Commission.
The mere fact that the State of Punjab has sanctioned the pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 which has later on been revised to Rs. 2200-4000 in respect of the College Librarians appointed prior to December 31, 1972 in spite of the fact that they did not possess the prescribed qualifications, does not mean that even the respondents are bound to do so.
The University being the best judge of its needs was entitled to insist upon certain qualifications and deny a scale to the persons who were not qualified.
In view of the above, the second question is answered in the negative.
It is held that the action of the respondent-University does not suffer from the vice of discrimination and is not violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
As a result, there is no merit in this petition.
It is, consequently, dismissed.
No costs.
