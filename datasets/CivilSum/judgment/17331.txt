Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - The appellant was working as a Junior Engineer in the Punjab Public Works Department, Irrigation Branch.
He had approached this Court with the grievance that his claim for promotion to the post of Sub Divisional Officer was not considered when persons junior to him had been promoted.
The writ petition having been dismissed by the learned Single Judge, he has filed the present Letters Patent Appeal.
2.
Learned Counsel for the parties have been heard.
3.
The learned Single Judge has found as a fact that the appellant had qualified the departmental examination on November 3, 1982.
The cases for promotion were taken up in July 1982.
The process of selection had been completed before the appellant had qualified the departmental examination.
Learned counsel for the appellant has not been able to show that this finding is wrong.
Since the appellant was not eligible when the process had been completed, it cannot be said that the respondent had erred in not considering his claim.
4.
Mr. Garg submits that the actual order of promotion in respect of the persons junior to the appellant had been issued on January 27, 1983.
A copy of this order has been produced as Annexure P-6 on the record.
A persual thereof shows that by this order, the selected persons had been given the posting order.
Process of selection had been completed earlier.
It has not been shown that the appellant was eligible prior to the completion of the process of selection.
Still further it has been conceded that the appellant's claim was considered subsequently and that he had been promoted.
During the pendency of the case, he has already retired from service.
5.
Taking the cumulative effect of the above facts, no ground for interference with the judgment of the learned Single Judge is made out.
Resultantly, the appeal is dismissed.
No costs.
Appeal dismissed.
