Mohamed Anwar, J - Heard arguments of learned counsel on both sides.
2.
Respondent is a Government Company within the meaning of the Section 617 of the Companies Act.
Petitioner is a BE Graduate in Mechanical Engineering.
He joined the service on 3091981 as a Supervisor in its Production and Maintenance Section.
In 1995, he was working in the post of Senior Engineer.
By order dated 23101995 vide Annexure-A of the Company, he and other employees placed in a similar situation were promoted to the respective next higher cadre post indicated therein.
Under this order at Annexure-A, the petitioner was promoted from the post of Senior-Engineer to the post of Works Manager with effect from 23101995.
It was mentioned therein that the said promotion will be treated as "Outside the Cadre Posts" (Temporary Posts) under the `Career Development Plan' for the present.
It was further stated in that promotion order vide Annexure-A that :  "The Officers shall be on `probation' for a period of one year with effect from 23101995.
The Heads of Departments are requested to forward monthly `Probationary Evaluation Reports' in terms of Para 4 of Administration Circular No. 137 dated 11th September 1989 regularly."
3.
By virtue of the said promotion, the petitioner took charge of his promotional post of Works Manager on 23101995 and had been serving as such under the respondent-Company.
It is an undisputed case that no any further order was passed by the Company extending the period of petitioner's probation of one year, envisaged in the order of promotion at Annexure-A The said period of one year's probation thus came expire on 23101996.
The petitioner's probationary period was not declared by the Company as having been satisfactorily completed and he was not formally confirmed in the said promotional post.
He was thus kept in suspense.
Therefore, he went on making representations to the company at Annexure-B dated 1981996, Annexure-G dated 22111997, Annexure-K dated 1061998 and Annexure-M dated 711999, requesting to the effect to treat him as having been confirmed in the said post on and with effect from 23101996 and to extend him all the consequential service benefits accruing as a result of such confirmation.
Upon those representations, the petitioner was issued with office order dated 12/14111997 of the respondent-Company, vide Annexure-F, informing him that during his probation period, he was assessed in his "Probationary Evaluation Report" as far from satisfactory and with respect to other relevant factors like job knowledge, ability and conduct, he was rated `Average' for 9 months and `Below Average' for the last 3 months.
Therefore, the Competent Authority has ordered extension of his probation for a further period of six months with effect from 23101997, and he was advised to improve his performance.
Subsequently, another order dated 851998 of the company, vide Annexure-J, came to be passed confirming the petitioner in the said promoted post of Works Manager in the pay scale of Rs. 2375-4450 with effect from 23101997 with sanction of annual increment of Rs. 75/- with effect from 23101997 raising the pay from "rs. 2375/- to 2450/- in the pay scale of Rs. 2375-4450".
Aggrieved by the said Annexures-F and J, the petitioner has filed this writ petition praying to :  "a) DECLARE that the Petitioner had satisfactorily completed the maximum period of probation of one year on 23101995 in terms of Office order No. KSDL:SN:79 95-96:2124 dated 27.10.1995 (Annexure A) and that he is deemed to have been confirmed in the post of Works Manager from 26.10.1996 automatically by implication and that extension of his probationary period beyond 23101996 under Annexures F and J are without any authority but arbitrary, capricious and discriminatory.
b) QUASH by the issue of a writ of Certiorari or any other Writ/Order/Direction, the following Orders issued by the Respondent : (i) Office Order No. KSDL:PSN:97-98:1820 dated 12/14111997 Respondent (Annexure F);  (ii) Order No. KSDL:PSN:98-99:390 dated 851998 (Annexure J) (iii) Order No. KSDL: PSN: 98-99: 3021 dated 25/2711999 (Annexure N).
(c) DIRECT the respondent by the issue of a writ of Mandamus or any other appropriate Writ, Order or Direction, directing the respondent to treat the petitioner as having been confirmed against the post of Works Manger on 23101996 and extend to him all the consequential benefits like Seniority in the cadre of Officers, Promotion, etc. d) GRANT such other relief or reliefs as this Hon'ble Court deems it fit to grant in the facts and circumstances and in the interests of justice and equity."
4.
The statement of objections for respondent is filed by its learned counsel, supporting its impugned orders at Annexures-F, J and N on the basis of the entries pertaining to the petitioner's performance as a probationer in the promoted post contained in the "Statement of Probationary Evaluation Report" vide Annexure-R2, which was stated to have been prepared by the concerned authorities of the Company in terms of the requirements of Administration Circular No. 137 dated 11th September, 1989, mentioned in Annexure-A, which has been producted at Annexure-R4 along with the statement of objections.
The contentions of Mr. Ashok B Hinchegiri, learned counsel representing respondent-Company, is that the entries at Sr. No. 12 pertaining to petitioner contained in Annexure-R2 Evaluation Report discloses that for the period from 23.9.1996 to 22.10.1996, his Job Knowledge and Conduct were found Below Average and therefore, the extension of petitioner's probationary period as per Annexure-F dated 12/14111997 for a further period of six months with effect from 23101997 was justified in terms of Rule 6 of the Company Rules governing "PROBATIONERS" issued by the Company under its said Circular No. 137 dated September 11, 1989 vide Annexure-R4.
So arguing, he contended that the petition is liable to be rejected, more so, when he has been confirmed in the promoted post with effect from 23101997 by Company's order dated 851998 at Annexure-J.  5.
The contention of learned counsel appearing for petitioner was that when once the period of probation of one year had been completed and expired on 23101996, without this period being further extended by the competent authority in terms of the relevant provisions contained in the said Circular No. 137 at Annexure-R4, the petitioners shall be automatically deemed to have been confirmed in his promoted post of Works Manager with effect from 23101996.
It was further argued that Annexure-F is patently vitiated for want of authority, since, no any valid order of the company was passed extending the petitioner's period of probation from 23101996 till 12/14111997 on which date, Annexure-F order was passed by it extending his probationary period with effect from 23101997 and long after one year's period of petitioner's period expired.
6.
On consideration of the terms of the probation of the petitioner made under Company's order dated 23101995 vide Annexure-A and the relevant provisions in the Company's Rules concerning its probationers contained in Circular No. 137 dated September 11, 1989 vide Annexure-R4, I find sufficient legal weight and force in the contentions of learned counsel for the petitioner.
For the purpose of this matter, Rules 1, 2, 5 and 6 of the Company Rules contained in Annexure-R4 are relevant.
They are reproduced below :  "1.
The initial probationary period of all employees appointed on recruitment/promotion shall be one year (twelve months).
Only in exceptional cases, the Chairman and Managing Director may reduce the probation period.
2.
The initial period of probation can be extended if necessary, and on specified grounds to be communicated in writing to the employees concerned by further periods of 3 or 6 months on each spell, depending upon the extension ordered by the Competent Authority, which shall in no circumstances exceed a total period of 12 months after the expiry of the initial probationary period of one year.
3. and 4. xxx xxx 5.
Deficient ratings in the Probationer Evaluation Report shall be communicated to the Probationers immediately for corrective action.
6.
If an employee is continuously rated `below average' in respect of any all the four traits for three consecutive months and if the probationer fails to show improvement, his services will be terminated at the end of the fourth month of the probationary period without waiting for completion of the entire probationary period.
xx xx xx xx xx xx  7.
As could be seen from the above quoted relevant rules governing probationers of the company, the prescribed minimum initial probationary period of all employees of the Company is of one year, which period was rightly prescribed for the petitioner in the promoted post under the Company's promotion order vide Annexure-A dated 23101995.
Rule 2 thereof contemplates extension of probationary period of the employees of the Company under the circumstances stipulated therein, which has to be communicated in writing to the employee concerned.
It further declares that under no circumstances, the probationary period could be extended exceeding the total period of 12 months after the expiry of the initial probationary period of one year.
Rule 5 envisages that the `Deficient Ratings' in the `Probationer Evaluation Report' shall be communicated to probationers immediately for corrective action.
Rule 6 states that if an employee is continuously rated `below average' in respect of any/all the four traits for three consecutive months and if the probationer fails to show improvement, his services will be terminated at the end of the fourth month of the probationary period without waiting for completion of the entire probationary period.
Indisputably, no action against petitioner under Rule 6 was taken by the company.
Therefore, it does not matter much to pronounce upon the validity or otherwise of the petitioner's claim.
So also, it is an admitted fact that the petitioner was never communicated by the company of any `Deficient Rating', enabling him to improve upon his performance.
In that view of the matter, Rule 5 also is not of much relevance for the present purpose.
It is also not the case of the respondent-Company that any specific order/orders was/were passed by it in terms of Rule 2 extending the petitioner's period of probation from 23101996 on any ground whatever.
There was a complete full and suspense in regard to petitioner's confirmation or otherwise with effect from 23101996 on which date his one year period of probation stood expired, and till the order dated 12/14111997, vide impugned Annexure-F, was served on the petitioner informing him that his probation period was extended for six months with effect from 23101997.
Obviously, the order at Annexure-R4, which did not authorise the company to extend the period of probation of an employee beyond twelve months after the expiry of the initial probationary period of one year which period in case of petitioner, as indicated, stood expired on 23101996, and more so when it was not followed by any prior specific order in writing communicated to him extending his probationary period from 23101996 onwards.
In that view of the matter, Annexure-F was patently illegal and without authority of law, and therefore, it is liable to be quashed, as prayed.
Consequently, since the petitioner has been declared confirmed in his promoted post of Works Manager by the Company under its order dated 851998 vide Annexure-J, it must necessarily follow that this confirmation should be made with retrospective effect from 23101996 and not with effect from 23101997, as stated in Annexure-J. To this extent, the order in Annexure-J also requires modification.
As a result, the petition deserves to be allowed.
8. for the reasons aforesaid, the petition is allowed.
The order No K.S.D.L./PSN/97-98/1820 dated 12/14111997 vide Annexure-F of the respondent-Company is quashed.
The petitioner is hereby declared to have completed his one year's of probation fixed in the Company's order dated 23101995, vide Annexure-A, in his promoted post of Works Manager satisfactorily and, therefore, he is deemed to have been confirmed in the post on and with effect from 23101996.
The Company's impugned order at Annexure-J No. KSDL/PSN/98-99/390 dated 851998 stands modified to this effect.
Consequently, the petitioner shall be entitled to the monetary benefits in his salary flowing from this order and payable to him.
Petition allowed.
