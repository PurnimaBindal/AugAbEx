Swatanter Kumar, J - In this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner prays that the order dated 1242002, Annexure P/14, be quashed being violative of principles of natural justice and arbitrary exercise of power.
Further it is prayed that the respondents be directed to sanction leave of the kind due to the petitioner for the period 771997 to 2492000.
2.
The petitioner was working as an Assistant Geologist with the respondent- department on regular basis since 1771970.
In the year 1990 the petitioner applied for earned leave from 971990 to 28.9.1990 for going out of country to meet his brother and parents in America.
The leave was sanctioned.
The petitioner claims to have got by-pass surgery performed there in July, 1996.
In January, 1997, he again felt pain and applied for leave on 1221997 on which no orders were passed.
The petitioner left India on 1141997 just informing the respondents.
He also submitted application for voluntary retirement on 1571999.
However, in the meanwhile, departmental proceedings were commenced on 10111999 against him.
Services of the petitioner were terminated vide order dated 1242002 and even the request of the petitioner for voluntary retirement was declined.
The petitioner filed this writ petition aggrieved from this action of the respondents.
3.
The learned counsel appearing for the petitioner placed heavy reliance upon Rule 8121(i) of Punjab Civil Services Rules (Volume-I) relating to grant of extra-ordinary leave in special circumstances.
It is contended that the respondents have the power to grant leave for the period of absence and as such the disciplinary proceedings as well as the impugned order dismissing him from service and declining the request of voluntary retirement is liable to be set aside.
4.
We are not impressed with this contention at all.
The conduct of the petitioner disentitles him from claiming any relief in exercise of its equitable jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
Even if it is conceded for the sake of arguments that initially the leave of the petitioner was sanctioned then there is no justification for him to leave India on 1141997 without specific permission or grant of appropriate leave by the concerned authorities.
A Government employee is obliged to act in accordance with service rules governing the grant of leave or otherwise of the Government servant.
5.
It is not disputed before us that no leave of any kind was sanctioned to the petitioner before he left for USA on 1141997.
He did not come back for a considerable period of more than three years and was permitted to join duties on 9102000 subject to conclusion of the departmental proceedings.
Enquiry against him has been conducted in accordance with law and in consonance with the principles of natural justice.
The respondents no way were obliged to allow the request of the petitioner for grant of voluntary retirement.
We are unable to see any arbitrariness or malafide in the action of the respondents.
The petitioner has taken undue advantage of the Government Rules and has successfully frustrated appropriate action against him even at an earlier stage.
No reason, whatsoever, much lease a reasonable cause, has been shown even on record of this case for his unauthorised absence for such a long period.
We find no merit in this petition.
The same is dismissed in limine.
Petition dismissed.
