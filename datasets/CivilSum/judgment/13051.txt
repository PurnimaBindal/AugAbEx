JS Khehar, J (Oral) - The three petitioners were originally inducted into the service of the Faridabad Complex Administration as Octroi Clerks on 2.9.1967, 18.1.1968 and 1.7.1968 respectively.
It is not a matter of dispute that all the petitioners belong to the category of Scheduled Castes Promotion from the feeder categories of Clerks and Octroi Clerks is to the common cadre of Accounts Clerks, House Tax Inspectors and Demolition Squad Inspectors.
Three vacancies in the aforesaid joint promotional cadre became available in 1979.
All the aforesaid three vacancies were to be filled up out of the category of Scheduled Castes in terms of the instructions issued by the State Government.
Be that as it may, one BS Manchanda and another OP Manchanda, who admittedly belonged to the general category, were promoted to the said cadre in 1979 on purely officiating basis.
Veer Bhan respondent No. 3 contested the action of the authorities in promoting the aforesaid BS Manchanda and OP Manchanda to the aforesaid joint promotional cadre without considering his claim.
In fact, he preferred an appeal to the competent authority, which remained pending for some time.
During the pendency of the aforesaid appeal, Veer Bhan respondent No. 3 was also promoted on ad hoc basis to the aforesaid joint promotional cadre on 2381982.
2.
The claim of the petitioners of superiority over the aforesaid BS Manchanda, OP Manchanda and Veer Bhan came to be recognised, by the Faridabad Complex Administration through an order dated 11101982 (Annexure P-2).
By the aforesaid order, BS Manchanda, OP Manchanda and Veer Bhan were reverted to the posts of Octroi Clerk, while petitioner No. 1 Deep Chand was promoted to the post of House Tax Inspector, petitioner No. 2 Peetam Singh was promoted as Demolition Squad Inspector and petitioner No. 3 Shishupal Singh was promoted as House Tax Inspector.
3.
By an order dated 28101982, all the three petitioners herein were informed that the Minister incharge had stayed the implementation of their promotion order dated 11101982.
On account of the aforesaid factual position, the petitioners were required to resume their duties as Octroi Clerks.
As a consequence of the promotions and reversions referred to hereinabove, all the concerned employees preferred appeals to the State Government.
The State Government, while adjudicating upon their appeals, passed an order dated 3.10.1983 (Annexure P-14).
The aforesaid order was impugned by the petitioners by filing Civil Writ Petition No. 2061 of 1983 in this Court.
The aforesaid writ petition came to be disposed of by an order dated 581985.
The order passed by the State Government dated 3.10.1983, was set aside on account of the fact that it was acknowledged on behalf of the respondents that the State Government did not have the authority to entertain an appeal on the issues raised by the concerned employees.
Additionally, this Court, while disposing of the aforesaid writ petition, granted liberty to the Chief Administrator of the Faridabad Complex Administration, who had earlier passed the promotion order dated 11101982, to implement his aforesaid order.
4.
The Faridabad Complex Administration, in furtherance of the liberty granted to it, by an order dated 18.2.1986, confirmed the promotion of the petitioners to the joint promotional cadre w.e.f.
11101982.
All the petitioners were held entitled to the determination of their seniority with reference to the aforesaid date of promotion i.e. 11101982.
Be that as it may, the order dated 18.2.1986 notices two facts, firstly, that their promotion order dated 11101982 had been cancelled by a subsequent order dated 28101982, and secondly, that Veer Bhan had also been considered for promotion to the joint promotional cadre and had been found suitable for regular promotion to the said cadre w.e.f.
31101979.
Accordingly, he was also held to be entitled to seniority with effect from the said date i.e. 31101979.
5.
The grievance of the petitioners (in so far as the order passed by the Faridabad Complex Administration dated 18.2.1986 is concerned) is, therefore, limited to the aforesaid two issues.
6.
In so far as first issue is concerned, the order dated 28101982 has been appended to the writ petition as Annexure P-7.
Its careful perusal reveals that the promotion order of the petitioners dated 11101982 was merely stayed by the Minister Incharge.
There is no determination by the authorities, in so far as the issue of cancellation of the promotion order dated 11101982 is concerned.
In the aforesaid view of the matter, in so far as the instant limited plea is concerned, there can be no doubt that the aforesaid factual position was erroneously referred to in the order dated 18.2.1986.
7.
In so far as the second issue is concerned, the vehement contention of the learned counsel for the petitioners is that there was no justification in granting promotion to respondent No. 3 Veer Bhan w.e.f.
31101979.
In this behalf, it is pointed out, that there were only three vacancies available in the joint promotional cadre at the aforesaid juncture.
The said three posts were reserved to be filled up by way of reservation from amongst the candidates belonging to the Scheduled Castes category.
All the three petitioners who belong to the category of Scheduled Castes, were regularly promoted against the said three vacancies w.e.f.
11101982.
Veer Bhan is stated to be belonging to the general category, and as such it is contended that he was not entitled to be promoted.
Moreover, there was no available vacancy in the joint promotional cadre against which he could have been promoted w.e.f.
31101979.
In this behalf, pointed attention of this Court has been invited to the averments made in paragraph 3 of the writ petition and the response thereto at the hands of respondent No. 2.
The sequence of facts narrated in paragraph 3 of the written statement disclose, that the claim of Veer Bhan was pending adjudication only as against BS Manchanda and OP Manchanda.
The short question involved in the issue raised by Veer Bhan before the competent authority was that he had a superior claim over BS Manchanda and OP Manchanda.
The aforesaid issue paled into significance after both BS Manchanda and OP Manchanda came to be reverted to the posts of Octroi Clerk by the order dated 11101982.
Neither BS Manchanda nor OP Manchanda ever impugned the aforesaid order reverting them from the joint promotional cadre to the posts of Octroi Clerk.
That being so, the claim of Veer Bhan must be deemed to have been frustrated when BS Manchanda and OP Manchanda were reverted from the joint promotional cadre.
Despite the fact that the pleadings in the instant writ petition expressly depict that no promotional avenue was available for general category candidates, there is no express response thereto in the written statement.
In fact, the pleadings in paragraph 3 of the written statement, are totally silent on this issue.
In terms of the rules of procedure specially Order VIII Rule 5 of the Code of Civil Procedure, facts asserted in the petition, which are not expressly denied in the written statement, will have to be admitted as final between the parties.
On the basis of the aforesaid principle, it clearly emerges that no avenue of promotion was available for the general category candidates in the year 1979.
In the aforesaid view of the mater, promotion of respondent No. 3 w.e.f.
31101979 was held to be unjustified.
Accordingly, the grant of seniority to respondent No. 3 with effect from the aforesaid date is likewise unacceptable in law.
8.
For the reasons recorded above, it is natural to accept even the second contention of the learned counsel for the petitioners, namely, to set aside the seniority granted to respondent No. 3 Veer Bhan over and above the petitioners by the order dated 3.10.1983 (Annexure P-14).
9.
It would be relevant to mention that respondent No. 3, despite service, did not enter appearance before this Court.
Even a written statement, has not been filed at his behest, to contest the averments made in the writ petition.
Allowed in the aforesaid terms.
Petition Allowed.
