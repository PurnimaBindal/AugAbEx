Mehtab S Gill, J - The petitioner has prayed for issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to consider and promote her to the post of Senior Clerk and further to the post of Junior Assistant from the date when persons junior to her have been promoted.
2.
It has been averred by the petitioner that she joined the service of the Bank as a Junior Clerk on 17111983.
She has passed her MA in Public Administration and her LL.B (Non Professional).
She has also passed the CAII B Part-I examination.
In addition to this, she has done one year diploma in Office Organisation and Procedure from the Panjab University, Chandigarh.
She had also done the diploma in Office Management and diploma in Accounting from the Panjab University, Chandigarh.
The petitioner is working as Junior Clerk for the last 17 years and till today she has not got even a single promotion.
About 100 persons junior to her have been promoted as Senior Clerks.
Even the persons who had joined as Peons and were junior to her as Junior Clerks have also been promoted as Senior Clerks.
Details of the persons promoted are given in Annexure P/1 attached to this writ petition.
Juniors of the petitioner have been promoted to the second higher post i.e. from Senior Clerks to that of Junior Accounts.
The respondents circulated a tentative seniority list for the post of Junior Clerks as it stood on 28121994 (Annexure P/2).
By an order dated 20121995, 58 Junior Clerks were promoted as Senior Clerks (Annexure P/3).
In this order, persons upto Sr. No. 64 in the seniority list, Annexure P/2 were promoted.
The case of the petitioner was considered and she was not found fit for promotion.
By an order dated 1531996, 17 more persons were promoted, the last person promoted being Tarlochan Singh Salh is at Sr. No. 81 in the seniority list Annexure P/2.
Copy of this order is attached as Annexure P/4 to this writ petition.
Again on 161996, still more Junior Clerks were promoted.
3.
On 671996, the petitioner was conveyed adverse remarks in her ACR for the 1995-96 (Annexure P/5).
It has been further averred that the only thing against the petitioner was that her relations with her colleagues were not cordial.
Representation was submitted by her against these remarks but the petitioner was informed vide memo dated 19.11.1996 stating that her representation had been considered and rejected (Annexure P/6).
About 40 employees of the Bank had given in writing that they had nothing against the petitioner and her relations with them are very cordial (Annexure P/7).
The fact regarding the adverse report given to the petitioner was that there was a Clerk by the name of Mrs. Asha Rana.
The said Mrs. Asha Rana had in fact physically assaulted the petitioner on 28.11.95 when she was on duty for which the petitioner had given a representation on 29.11.95 (Annexure P/8).
Thereafter, the said Asha Rana alongwith one Karam Chand, Senior Assistant in the bank had started harassing the petitioner.
A complaint in writing was given by the petitioner on 16.2.1996 (Annexure P/9).
4.
On 751996, the petitioner was placed under suspension and a charge sheet was served on 19.6.1996 for creating indiscipline in the bank and for misconduct but the allegations were the same as explained above.
On 18798, show cause notice served on the petitioner was withdrawn and she was warned to be careful in future.
The period of suspension from 7596 to 12.5.98 was treated as duty period for all purposes (Annexure P/10).
On 2051998, 8 more Junior Clerks were promoted (Annexure P/11).
The petitioner made a detailed representation/appeal to the Board of Directors to promote her (Annexure P/12).
The Board of Directors rejected the representation/appeal of the petitioner on 821999 (Annexure P/13).
In the year ending 3131999, more adverse remarks were made in the ACR of the petitioner (Annexure P/14).
A detailed representation was submitted by the petitioner against these remarks (Annexure P/15).
On 372000 certain Senior Clerks of the Bank immediately above the petitioner as per seniority list Annexure P/2 were further promoted as Junior Assistants (Annexure P/16).
5.
Notice of motion was issued.
Written statement was filed.
6.
The respondent-Bank stated that in terms of order dated 20121995 (Annexure P/3), persons junior to the petitioner were promoted to the post of Senior Clerks.
The case of the petitioner was considered vide order dated 20121995, Annexure P/3 but she was not found fit for promotion.
There are disparaging remarks in the ACR of the petitioner for the year 1993-94 which were conveyed to her by respondent No. 1 on 1361994.
It is further averred in the written statement that the petitioner was placed under suspension for an act of gross indiscipline and misconduct and as she was under suspension, her ACR for the year 1992-93 could not be recorded.
7.
Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner and the respondent No. 1.
8.
The learned counsel for respondent drew my attention to the adverse remarks in the ACR for the year ending 3131994, which is attached as Annexure R/1/1.
Only the adverse remarks have been mentioned and basicality it comes out that petitioner is not a cooperative person when it comes to co- operation with her colleagues, superiors and subordinates.
Her punctuality in attendance is also irregular and she is not prompt in disposal of her work.
The learned counsel for the respondent has placed a copy of the ACR for the year ending 3131994.
In this ACR the petitioner has been graded in the column of intelligent and industrious as `yes' meaning thereby as `good'.
Her reputation qua the honesty is `average'.
Knowledge of Banking, Book Keeping, Relevant Law, Rules and Procedure again is `yes' meaning thereby as `good'.
Finally, her general assessment and the remarks about her conduct and discipline were found to be `average'.
Taking the totality of this ACR for the year ending 3131994, there is nothing against the integrity of the petitioner.
The only thing which comes out is that she does not seem to be a cooperative person with her colleagues, superiors and subordinates.
9.
In the ACR ending for the year 3131995, there is an improvement in the behaviour of the petitioner where the Authority recording the Annual Confidential Report in the column `relations with colleagues, superiors and subordinates, has found her conduct to be `satisfactory'.
The most important factor to be taken into consideration in this ACR is the column of `her reputation about her honesty' where the Assessing Authority has put it `very good'.
In all other ACRs for the years from ending 31.3.196 to 31.3.1999 there is nothing to show that she is dis-honest.
Her behaviour with her colleagues seems to have definitely improved.
In the ACR for the year ending 3131999 again in the column of `what is her reputation about honesty' the competent authority has written that `she is honest'.
Her conduct with her colleagues, superiors and subordinates is `average; and her ACr is of fairly satisfactory nature.
10.
After going through all the Annual Confidential Reports starting from the year ending 3131994 to 31.3.1999, nothing comes out, of a grave nature on the basis of which she should not have been given promotion.
11.
In view of the above discussion, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to consider and promote the petitioner to the post of Senior Clerks and further to the post of Junior Assistant from the date, persons junior to her were promoted, with all consequential benefits.
The orders dated 18111996 (Annexure P-6) and dated 821999 (Annexure P/13) impugned in this writ petition vide which the representations of the petitioner against the adverse remarks were rejected, are also quashed.
Petition allowed.
