Swatanter Kumar, J - In this petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner prays for issuance or writ in the nature of mandamus and/or any other appropriate writ, to regularise the services of the petitioner, as per policy of the Government.
2.
The petitioner was appointed as baildar under Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch), at Bahadurgarh on daily wages on 121993.
He worked there upto June, 1993 and in July 1993 he joined as electrician helper on daily wages under the Public Health Department at Bahadurgarh Division and since then he has been working at the same post.
The Government of Haryana formulated and issues a policy of regularisation of service of its employees on 731996.
In terms of this policy a person who had completed three years of service as daily wager was entitled to regularisation of service.
However, according to the petitioner, his case was not considered for regularisation at that stage, but on 291996 the case of the petitioner was considered for regularisation and his services were regularised vide order of the same date Annexure P-3 to the writ petition.
Vide order dated 2121997 the services of the petitioner were deregularised and the order dated 291996 was cancelled though the petitioner is carrying on as a daily wager.
Petitioner in order to redress his grievance submitted representation dated 1251997 to the respondents which was duly recommended by the Executive Engineer but of no consequence.
The petitioner then served a demand notice through his counsel for regularisation of his services under the 1996 policy but the same was declined on the ground that the petitioner had not completed three years of service as on 3111996, vide letter dated 912002 giving rise to the present writ petition.
3.
Upon notice, the respondents have filed a detailed written statement wherein it has been stated that petitioner was engaged as baildar on 121993 where he worked till May 14, 1993 and was thereafter engaged as electrician helper on daily wages in Public Health Department from May 15, 1993 and where he is working as such till date.
As both the departments were separate, so the petitioner had not completed three years as on 3111996 and thus his services could not be regularised on that date.
As such, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief including regular pay scale as the operation of the judgment in Civil Writ Petition No. 2237 of 2000 has been stayed by the Hon'ble Supreme Court.
Thus the respondents pray for the dismissal of the writ petition.
4.
As is evident from the above noticed pleadings of the parties, the facts are hardly in dispute.
The petitioner has been in continuous employment under different departments of the State.
His services were regularised vide order dated 291996 but the said order was cancelled by the respondents by passing the following order :-  Office Order "The regularisation order of Shri Manoj Kumar Electrician Helper issued vide this office No. 15138 dated 291996 w.e.f.
121996 are hereby cancelled as the Executive Engineer, PH Divn.
Bahadurgarh while verifying the service in the first entry made in service book and taken into account the service rendered by Shri Manoj Kumar in the office of S.D.O.
G.W.S.
Sub Division No. 13, Gurgaon (Irrigation Branch) which is other deptt.
and not of Public Health Deptt.
as clarified vide his letter No. 1628 dated 2121997 that S.D.O.
G.W.S.
Sub Division No. 13, Gurgaon is under EE Ground Water Services Division Irrigation Branch, Gurgao.
Sd/-  Superintending Engineer".
5.
A bare reading of the above order shows that the service rendered by the petitioner in other Government department was not taken into consideration for the purpose of determining the continuous period of three years as on 3131996.
The petitioner has worked in departments of the State and has been in employment of the State as a daily wager in both the departments.
It hardly lies in the mouth of the State now to contend that the petitioner was not in continuous service for a period of three years as on 3111996.
In all fairness, the State having regularised the services of the petitioner could not have passed the order deregularising the services of the petitioner without serving a notice upon the petitioner to show cause against the proposed action.
Admittedly, the petitioner remained in employment as regular employee of the State from 291996 to 2121997 when in violation of the principle of natural justice order deregularising the services of the petitioner was passed by the respondents.
The impugned order is thus liable to be set aside even on that ground alone.
6.
The petitioner had admittedly joined as a baildar on daily wages on 121993.
Thereafter, he continuously worked from June 1993 in the present department.
It is also not disputed that the petitioner continues to work on that post even as on date.
According to the petitioner he has worked for 240 days in every year following 1993 till 1996.
In terms of the judgment of this Court in the case of Kavita Gandhi, if an employee has worked for 240 days he shall be deemed to have worked for one year.
On this principle, the respondents ought to have regularised the services of the petitioner.
7.
We may also notice that another Division Bench of this Court in a recent judgment in the case of Husain Mohd.
and others v. State of Haryana, 2003(4) S.L.R.
1 has specifically directed the State Government to frame policy for the regularisation of services of adhoc school teachers.
8.
We may also notice that the petitioner may not be entitled to regular pay scale in view of the order of stay passed in petition for Special Leave to Appeal (Civil) No. 19754 of 2002, however, the petitioner is at liberty to raise this claim after the matter is finally disposed of by the Hon'ble Apex Court but in any case the petitioner would be entitled to the minimum wages.
In view of our above discussion, we quash the order Annexure P-4 with liberty to the respondents to pass an order afresh in accordance with law and keeping in view the observations made by us.
Petition allowed.
