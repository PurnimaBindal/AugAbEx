Subhashan Reddy, J - This writ petition is filed questioning the GO Rt.
No. 1450, Education Department, dated 1481989.
Under the said GO, the effect of the earlier GORt.
No. 1266, Education (1) Department, dated 881979 is set at naught.
Under the GO Rt.
No. 1266, dated 881979, the Government has accepted the proposals of the Director of School Education holding that Teachers Training School Course (2 years course) of Karnataka State be treated as equivalent to that of integrance scheme of TSLC Secondary Grade (2 years course) of Andhra Pradesh State (of 1965-67 scheme) for employment in Andhra Pradesh.
The petitioners have obtained the said certificate from Karnataka State after undergoing training for 2 years after their S.S.C.
Admittedly, all the 9 petitioners have been issued with such certificates evidencing the successful completion of the above training in Karnataka State.
If that be so, they will be entitled for employment in the State of Andhra Pradesh in accordance with GORt.
No. 1266, Education (1) Department, dated 881979.
But, now the petitioners are sought to be deprived of by reason of GO Rt.
No. 1450, Education Department dated 1481989.
2.
When the Government has recognised the certificate of Karnataka State pursuant to the earlier Governmental Order in GO Rt.
No. 1266, dated 881979, there is no reason or justification for not accepting the same for employment in the case of the petitioners also.
GORt.
No. 1450, Education Department dated 1481989 cannot act retrospectively.
But the same is applicable only prospectively.
For better clarity, I declare that GORt.
No. 1450, Education Department, dated 1481989, shall be applicable only to such of the candidates who are admitted to the course concerned after 1481989 and not to the persons who were admitted to the said equivalent course before that date i.e. 1481989.
I am fortified with the above view of mine by a judgment of the Supreme Court in Suresh Pal and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1987(2) S.C.C.
445.
The Supreme Court while dealing with the similar matter, has held that certificates obtained when the Government Order was in force, cannot be de-recognised merely because at a later point of time, the earlier policy was given a go-bye and directed the persons who joined the course earlier to the revised policy to be given benefit of.
3.
In the above circumstances, I hold that the petitioner shall be entitled for the benefits of employment as being covered and governed by GORt.
No. 1266, Education (1) Department, dated 881979 and not under GO Rt.
No. 1450, Education Department, dated 1481989.
4.
The writ petition is allowed to the extent indicated above.
No order as to costs.
Petitioner allowed.
