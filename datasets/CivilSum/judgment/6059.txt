KC Puri, J - Under challenge in this appeal is the judgment and decree passed by Shri SK Jain, the then District Judge, Karnal vide which he accepted the appeal preferred against the judgment and decree dated 2941985 delivered by Shri Dharam Pal, the then Sub Judge IIIrd Class, Karnal and dismissed the suit of the plaintiff.
2.
In short, the facts of the case are that plaintiff, now appellant, in this appeal filed a suit for a declaration against the defendants/respondents to the effect that the order dated 7.10.1982 passed by the Commissioner & Secretary, Government of Haryana, Health Department by which his period from 2321980 to 1371980 was treated as absence from duty without leave and the orders dated 1891980 and 891980 passed by the Director, Health Services, Haryana, Chandigarh by which the H.S.E.B had been asked not to release his unpaid dues and he was asked to deposit one month pay in lieu of notice period as illegal, inoperative and not binding on him.
3.
The plaintiff averred that he was on deputation with defendant No. 3 from 1.3.1979 to 22.2.1980 and thereafter he was called back by his parent department vide order of Secretary, Government of Haryana, Health Department dated 1411980 and he was posted as Medical Officer, Primary Health Centre, Grora District Mohindergarh.
He was relieved by the HSEB on 2221980 afternoon and was to join the new assignment on 331980.
He could not join at Grora.
He submitted his joining report alongwith earned leave from 331980 through registered post to the CMO, Narnaul.
After that, he resigned from the service on 1451980.
His resignation was accepted with effect from 1471980 by the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Health Department, Chandigarh vide order dated 13.7.1981 which was communicated to him vide endorsement dated 1081981.
He was not paid the pay and allowances for the period from 2321980 to 1371980.
He availed joining time on his transfer to Grora from 2321980 to 231980 and from 331980 to 1371980.
He remained on leave as his resignation was accepted with effect from 1371980.
He has not been paid the arrears accrued to him due to the revision of pay with effect from 1.4.1979.
He asked the Government through registered post to release aforesaid arrears but the department has not taken any steps to release the same.
4.
It is further averred by the plaintiff that the Director, Health Department, Chandigarh asked him to deposit one month pay in lieu of notice period vide order dated 891981.
He is entitled to recover arrears of pay and allowances to the tune of Rs. 3,300/-.
5.
The defendants contested the suit on various grounds.
They contested the claim of the plaintiff that he was on deputation with the HSEB for the period in question and that he was recalled from deputation to the parent department.
The defendants also contested the claim of the plaintiff that his absence period was treated absence from duty without leave.
It was denied that the plaintiff was entitled to any pay and allowances for the said period.
The plaintiff did not join his duty after being relieved by the HSEB.
They have supported impugned order dated 1271982 as legal and perfectly valid.
6.
From the pleadings of the parties, the learned trial Court framed five issues.
After returning findings on different issues, the learned trial Court decreed the suit vide judgment and decree dated 2941985.
7.
Feeling aggrieved, the defendants filed an appeal which was accepted as noticed above.
8.
The plaintiff has filed the present Regular Second Appeal in this Court, feeling dissatisfied with the impugned judgment and decree dated 911986.
9.
I have heard arguments of counsel for the parties and have gone through the record of the case.
10.
Learned counsel for the appellant has framed the following substantial questions of law :-  1.
Whether the application for earned leave for the period 23280 to 13.7.80 could be treated as accepted when it was kept pending due to pending of enquiry ?
2.
Whether after filing of the enquiry, the leave application is deemed to be accepted from the date of submission ?
3.
Whether by not passing an order on resignation within a period of 30 days can the plaintiff be held liable for the period from the date of submission of resignation i.e. 14580 to 14.7.80 the date of acceptance of resignation ?
11.
So far as the facts of the case are concerned, that are not in dispute.
The plaintiff was on deputation with defendant No. 3 from 1.3.1979 to 22.2.1980 and thereafter he was called back by his parent department vide order dated 1411980 passed by the Secretary, Government of Haryana, Health Department and was posted as Medical Officer, Primary Health Centre, Grora, District Mohindergarh.
He was relieved by the HSEB on 2221980 afternoon and was to join the new assignment on 331980.
The plaintiff, instead of joining his new place of posting has submitted leave application from 331980 to 391980.
That leave application was not accepted and ultimately he submitted his resignation, which was accepted w.e.f.
1471980.
The period of his absence from 331980 to 1371980 has been treated as leave without pay.
The plaintiff wanted that period should be treated as leave of the kind due and not as extraordinary leave without pay.
12.
From the perusal of whole of the file, the only question of fact involved is whether his leave application should have been accepted by the department or not.
To my mind, none of the abovesaid questions of law have arisen in the case, much less the substantial question of law.
The finding of fact returned by the Ist Appellate Court cannot be allowed to be challenged in the second appeal as the High Court can only interfere in case there is substantial question of law.
In view of the above discussion, the appeal is without any merit and the same stands dismissed with costs.
Decree sheet be prepared.
The files of the trial Court be sent back.
The file of this Court be sent to the record room after compliance.
Appeal dismissed.
