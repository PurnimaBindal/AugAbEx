VK Bali, J (Oral) - Puran Singh, Ex-Naib Subedar through present petition filed by him under Article 226 of the Constitution of India seeks writ in the nature of certiorari so as to quash order of the Chief of the Army Staff as contained in Records Office Bombay Engineer Group, Khadki letter dated May 15, 1991, Annexure P-1, rejecting his statutory complaint.
2.
Reading of Annexure P-1 would reveal that statutory complaint filed by petitioner has been rejected by non-speaking and cryptic order by just observing that the matter has been considered and since complaint lacks substance, the same is rejected.
Learned Counsel for the petitioner relies on SN Mukharji v. Union of India, 1991(1) SCT 241 (SC) : 1990(5) SLR 8, Major SS Balaggan v. Union of India and Ors, 1991(1) SLR 742 and Naib Subedar M Velavudha-in v. The Secretary, Ministry of Defence and Ors, 1992(3) SCT 23 (P&H) : 1992(4) SLR 385 to contend that dismissal of a statutory complaint, in the manner referred to above, has violated the principles of natural justice and the decision on statutory complaint being on administrative side had to contain the reasons rejecting the same.
3.
Without joining issue with the learned counsel for the petitioner, learned counsel for Union of India states that statutory complaint of petitioner shall now be decided by passing a speaking order.
In view of what has been said above, order, Annexure P-1, is set aside.
A direction is issued to respondent-Union of India to decide the statutory complaint of petitioner, Annexure P-6, by passing a speaking order within three months from the receipt of copy of order.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
