NK Sodhi, J (Oral) - Petitioner joined the Pepsu State Police Service on 2141949 as a Prosecuting Sub Inspector.
On the merger of Pepsu with the State of Punjab on 1111956 he became an employee of the Punjab Government.
In 1961 he was transferred to the Sports Department as a Volley Ball coach and worked there till 13121963.
On 14121963 he joined the National Institute of Sports, Patiala on deputation for one year and was absorbed there on 3041965.
He retired on 30.4.1983 as Deputy Director of the National Institute of Sports.
It is common case of the parties that this Institute is being run by the Sports Authority of India, New Delhi and is wholly controlled by the Government of India.
2.
After retirement the petitioner is drawing his pension from the National Institute of Sports, Patiala and his pension has been worked out on the basis of the service rendered by him from 14121964 till the date of his retirement.
He made a claim that his previous service rendered with the State of Punjab be also counted for the purpose of granting him pension.
When this claim was rejected he filed the present petition under Article 226 of the Constitution in the year 1984.
3.
During the pendency of the writ petition the Central Government has by a notification dated 1171995 made the provisions of sub-section (3) of Section 14 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 (for short the Act) applicable to the Sports Authority of India.
After the issuance of this notification the petitioner who is an ex-employee of the Sports Authority of India is governed by the provisions of the Administrative Tribunals Act and, therefore, his claim can be considered only by the Central Administrative Tribunal and the jurisdiction of this Court is barred under sub-section (3) of Section 14 of the Act.
In view of the provisions of Section 29 of the Act this writ petition has to be transferred to the Central Administrative Tribunal, Chandigarh.
I order accordingly.
The office is directed to transmit the record to the Central Administrative Tribunal forthwith.
Since it is an old matter and with a view to avoid any further delay in effecting service, the parties through their counsel have been directed to appear before the Register of the Tribunal on March 6, 1997.
Order accordingly.
