SS Saron, J (Oral) - By way of the present petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India the petitioner has prayed for a direction be issued to the respondent to re-fix his pension and grant him retiral benefits after counting the period of military service for the purpose of pension which has already been counted towards pay fixation and seniority.
A further prayer has been made for quashing the letter dated 7.9.2007 (Annexure-P.12) whereby the petitioner has been asked to deposit an amount of Rs. 1,26,290/- towards interest.
Still further prayer has been made for quashing the order dated 1782005 (Annexure-P.9) whereby interest is sought to be charged from the petitioner on refund of gratuity on the basis of letter dated 1471995 (Annexure-P.13).
2.
The brief facts of the case are that the petitioner was commissioned in the Short Service Commission of the Army on 2441966 and he was discharged on 2251976.
Thereafter, the petitioner was paid gratuity of an amount of Rs. 10,000/- for the military service rendered in the Army in October 1976.
The petitioner then joined as Excise and Taxation Officer which is Allied Service of the Punjab Civil Service on 161981 and thereafter he was appointed in the PCS (Executive Branch) on 18.1.1983.
His seniority in the PCS (Executive Branch) was fixed on 18.3.1993.
The pay of the petitioner was fixed on 2171993 by granting him the benefit of military service rendered by him from 2441966 to 2251976 i.e. for a period of 10 years, 0 month and 27 days.
The petitioner while in service deposited the amount of gratuity of Rs. 10,000/- with the State Government on 372002.
Intimation in this regard was given to the State Government on 1172002.
The petitioner retired from service on 3172004.
3.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties and perusing the record the dispute in the present case is now confined only to the payment of interest on he deposit of gratuity of Rs. 10,000/- by the petitioner on 372002.
It may be noticed that the State Government vide order dated 1782005 (Annexure-P.9) has condoned the period of gap in respect of the petitioner after his release from military service, and joining the civil service.
In other words the period from release after military service i.e. 2251976 to the date of his joining as Excise and Taxation Officer being an Allied Service of the PCS on 161981 has been condoned.
In this manner, a period of 5 years, 0 months and 10 days is condoned after relaxing the Part 2 Rule 4.6.A(5) of the Punjab Civil Service Rules.
Besides, the benefit of military service for the period from 2441966 to 21.5.1976 i.e. 10 years, months and 27 days has been given to the petitioner under Part-2 of Punjab Civil Service Rules read with Rule 4(2) of the Demobilized Indian Armed Forces Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies Punjab Civil Services) (Executive Branch) Rules, 1972.
The benefit, however, was given on the condition that the petitioner will deposit the interest with the Finance Department on account of late deposit of gratuity of Rs. 10,000/- received from the Army.
The petitioner was asked to deposit the interest in accordance with the conditions laid down in letter dated 1471995 (Annexure-P.13).
It was observed that it should be from the date of receiving the amount of benefit to the date of depositing it i.e. from October 1976 to 372002.
The interest, it was observed, will be according to the interest levied on GP Fund from time to time.
On the basis of the interest calculated, the State Government vide order dated 792006 (Annexure-P.12) asked the petitioner to deposit a sum of Rs. 1,26,290/- as interest on the delayed payment of gratuity.
4.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the petitioner joined as Excise and Taxation Officer on 161981 and at that time the instructions dated 1471995 (Annexure-P.13) were inapplicable.
It is submitted that the said instructions could not be made applicable with retrospective effect.
A reference has also been made to the order dated 13.4.2001 (Annexure-P.15) wherein in respect of a similarly situated employee, the State Government had asked him to deposit in Government Treasury simple interest @ 9% per annum incurred from April 1970 to 1371995 and from 1471995 onwards upto the share of refund at the rate of interest applicable to GP Fund accumulations from time to time.
5.
In response, Mr. KS Dadwal, learned Additional Advocate General, Punjab for the State has vehemently opposed the said contention.
It is submitted that the petitioner is liable to pay interest on the delayed payment from October 1976 i.e. when the payment was received upto 372002 when it was deposited with the State Government.
A reference has been made to Para 3(1) of the instructions dated 1471995 (Annexure-P.13).
6.
We are of the view that Note-8 appended to Rule 4.3 (A) of the Punjab Civil Service Rules would be applicable.
The same reads as under :  "Note 8.
The sanction regarding the counting of military service should be accorded by the competent authority at the time of appointment of the person concerned and not at the time of his retirement from civil service.
Sanctions accorded in such cases are required specially to mention the amount of gratuity or both recoverable, the number of monthly installments in which the bonus or gratuity is to be recovered and the date from which the recovery is to commence.
If, in any case, the gratuity is not to be recovered, the fact will be specially stated in the sanction.
The gratuity once refunded in order to secure the benefit of counting form military service for civil pension cannot be paid back in any circumstances."
7.
In terms of the above note the sanction regarding the counting of military service is accorded by the competent authority at the time of appointment of the person concerned and not at the time of retirement from civil service.
Besides, the sanction accorded in such cases are required specially to mention the amount of gratuity or both recoverable, the number of monthly installments in which the bonus or gratuity is to be recovered and the date from which the recovery is to commence.
In any case, this has not been done in the case of the petitioner when he joined the service on 161981.
The petitioner, however, is being asked for interest on the delay in deposit of gratuity from the date of its receipt in October 1976 to the date of its deposit on 372002 at the rate of interest applicable to GPF Accumulations in terms of instructions dated 1471995 (Annexure-P.13).
However, it cannot be disputed as a proposition of law that instructions issued by the Government are prospective in operation.
The sole reliance placed by the respondent is on the instructions dated 1471995 (Annexure P.13).
Clause 3(1) of the said instructions envisages that the Government has decided that wherever employees are required to refund the pensionary benefits received by them for the service already rendered by them under the Central or State Government or autonomous bodies in order to avail of the benefit of counting of past service for pension in terms of the provisions of Rules 4.3 etc. of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-II as amended from time to time the rate of interest will be at the rate applicable on GPF accumulations from time to time, for the period from the date of receipt of pensionary benefits to the date of their refund to the Government or autonomous body.
However, the State Government in respect of a similar employee, namely, S Narinder Singh vide order dated 13.4.2001 (Annexure-P.15) has ordered him to refund the gratuity along with simple interest @ 9% per annum accruing thereon for the period he received the gratuity amount i.e. from April 1970 to 1371995 i.e. a day before the issuance of the instructions dated 1471995 (Annexure-P.13).
Thereafter, from 1471995 onwards the rate of interest applicable to G.P.F.
accumulations from time to time has been charged.
Therefore, we are of the view that the petitioner is also liable to be similarly treated as S Narinder Singh whose interest has been calculated in the said manner.
8.
Accordingly, the petitioner would be liable to pay simple interest @ 9% per annum from October 1976 to 1371995 and thereafter from 1471995 upto 372002 at the rate of interest applicable to G.P.F.
accumulations from time to time as in the case of S Narinder Singh whose order dated 13.4.2001 (Annexure-P.15) is on record.
9.
The respondent shall accordingly re-calculate the interest to be deposited in this manner to the petitioner within eight weeks from the receipt of certified- copy of this order and the petitioner shall thereafter deposit the due interest within two weeks on which the case of the petitioner for grant of re-fixation of pension shall be considered.
Consequently, the order dated 1782005 (Annexure-P.9) asking the petitioner to deposit the interest as per letter dated 1471995 from October 1976 to 372002 is quashed.
Besides, the order dated 792006 (Annexure-P.12) asking the petitioner to pay interest amounting to Rs. 1,26,290/- is also quashed.
10.
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.
Petition allowed.
