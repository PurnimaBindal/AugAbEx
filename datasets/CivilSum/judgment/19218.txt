NK Agarwal, J - Smt. Gurdip Kaur and another through present petition filed by them under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, seek issuance of writ in the nature of certiorari so as to quash impugned order dated December 12, 1996, Annexure P-6, passed by the Deputy Commissioner, Ambala, whereby the petitioners have been reverted from the post of Craft Teachers to that of Craft Assistants thereby reducing them in rank and pay.
2.
All that needs to be mentioned for the purpose of disposing of this petition at this stage is that only July 31, 1995 vide order, Annexure P-4, passed by the Deputy Commissioner, Ambala, the petitioners were promoted to the post of Craft Teacher (Tailoring) against vacant posts in the pay scale of 950-1500.
It is true that some objections were raised against the promotion of the petitioners by certain employees, who were appointed in the Panchayat Samitis but it is also equally true that impugned order, Annexure P-6 dated December 12, 1996 was passed without hearing the petitioners in the matter.
We had asked for a specific affidavit on the point as to whether the petitioners were heard in the matter while passing the impugned order on an earlier occasion and pursuant thereto an affidavit has been filed on behalf of the respondents clearly stating therein that the petitioners were not heard in the matter.
It, thus, stands proved that a benefit conferred upon the petitioners by promoting them earlier in point of time was withdrawn without hearing them, thus, violating the principles of natural justice.
On that count alone, the impugned order dated December 12, 1996, Annexure P-6/R-3 deserves to be quashed.
So ordered.
It shall, however, be open to the respondents to determine the matter afresh after hearing the petitioners and all those who may be concerned/affected in the matter.
Disposed of accordingly.
Petition allowed.
