VK Bali, J - In this writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, petitioner Krishan Kumar seeks a writ in the nature of mandamus for setting aside order Annexure P-3 dated 2411992 vide which reference sought by him under Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act was declined on the solitary ground that he had not put in 240 days of service with the respondent management.
The case of the petitioner as pleaded in the petition by referring to a document of the management is that he had served the management from February, 1989 to May 14, 1991.
Be that as it may, it was a disputed question and the decision of it was not within the domain of the Government.
It has been authoritatively held by the Apex Court in "Ram Avtar Sharma v. State of Haryana," AIR 1985 SC 915 which judgment was followed by this Court in "Balwan Singh v. State of Haryana through the Secretary, Labour Department, Chad.," 1989(2) RSJ 459 : 1992(2) SCT 2 (P&H) that the reference declined by the State Government, on the ground that petitioners services were for short period i.e. less than 240 days and that they had been removed from service in the normal course was not correct and it was for the Labour Court to decide the said question.
This case being squarely covered by the decision aforesaid, has to be allowed and is accordingly allowed.
A direction issued to the respondent- State to refer the dispute as made out in the demand notice of the petitioner to the Labour Court within a month from today.

It has come to the notice of this Court that even though the law is well settled on the issue, the Government in number of cases is declining the references on the points which are exclusively in the domain of the Labour Court.
This petition is allowed with costs which are quantified at Rs. 1,000/-.
