GS Singhvi, J - In all these petitions the petitioners have prayed for quashing of the order dated 1121992 (correct date is 30111992) issued by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Irrigation and Power Department, Chandigarh, on the basis of the direction given by the Apex Court on 11111992 in the Contempt Petition No. 79/91 in Civil Appeal No. 3837 of 1990 (Sushil Kumar Arora and others v. State of Haryana and others).
The petitioners have also prayed that the order of the Supreme Court be implemented in accordance with Rule 9 of the Haryana Service of Engineers Class-II Rules, 1970 and the respondents be restrained from effecting recovery from their salary during the pendency of the writ petitions.
The petitioners have further prayed that they be given retrospective promotion on the posts of Sub-Divisional Officers.
2.
The case of the petitioners is that they were recruited as Junior Engineers.
Some of them were promoted as Sub-Divisional Officers on ad hoc basis and then were given regular promotion between the years 1978 to 1980.
Petitioners in C.W.P.
No. 12622 of 1993 and C.W.P.
No. 13791 of 1993 are still working as Junior Engineers and despite the fact that they are eligible for promotion, they have not yet been promoted.
The petitioners claim that while preparing a revised ranking list the respondents have not taken into consideration their regular promotion and have undone their regular promotion without giving them action-oriented notice and an opportunity of hearing.
3.
The writ petitions have been contested by the respondents on the ground that the impugned order dated 30111992 has been passed strictly in compliance of the Supreme Court decision and keeping in view the order passed in C.W.P.
4483 of 1985 (Ranjit Singh v. State of Haryana).
According to the respondents, after a proper determination of the vacancies, the candidature of the eligible persons has been considered as per the seniority and thereafter revised ranking list has been issued on 30111992.
4.
It may have been necessary to discuss the points raised by the parties in detail but in view of the judgment dated 1781995 passed in C.W.P.
No. 3319 of 1993 : 1995(1) SCT 136(P&H) (Sarwan Kumar Munjal v. State of Haryana and another) decided alongwith three other writ petitions, I am of the opinion that it is not necessary to again make a detailed analysis of the various contentions raised in the writ petitions.
5.
While deciding the writ petition of Sarwan Kumar Munjal, this Court has taken note of relevant facts and rules and has held that before issuing the revised ranking list dated 30111992 the government did not act in consonance with the principles of natural justice.
The Court passed the following order in the case of Sarwan Kumar Munjal's case (supra) :-  "In view of the above, it is declared that the ranking list prepared by the Government, excluding the names of the petitioners/changing their year of promotion is nullity qua the petitioners.
Normally the ranking list should have been quashed on the ground of violation of principles of natural justice but having regard to the peculiar facts of this case, I am of the opinion that ends of justice would be served by directing the Government to give a notice and an opportunity of hearing to the petitioners and other affected persons, giving out details of its proposal to change their positions and after considering the objections, if any, filed by the affected persons, determine their positions afresh in consultation with the Haryana Public Service Commission.
If the position of the petitioners or any other affected persons, as indicated in the list dated 30111992, is changed to their/his advantage then such persons must be given all consequential benefits.
The writ petitions are allowed in the manner indicated above.
The parties are left to bear their own costs."
6.
In my opinion, the petitioners are also entitled to be given similar relief as has been given to the petitioners in C.W.P.
No. 3219 of 1993 (Sarwan Kumar Munjal v. State of Haryana and others) and three other writ petitions decided on 1781995 : 1995(1) SCT 136(P&H).
7.
For the detailed reasons mentioned in the order dated 1781995 passed in C.W.P.
No. 3219 of 1993, which shall be treated as part of this order, the writ petitions are allowed.
It is declared that the ranking list prepared by the Government, excluding the names of the petitioners/changing their year of promotion is nullity qua the petitioners.
Normally, the ranking list should have been quashed on the ground of violation of principles of natural justice but having regard to the peculiar facts of the controversy, I am of the opinion that ends of justice would be served by directing the Government to give a notice and an opportunity of hearing to the petitioners and other affected persons, giving out details of its proposal to change their positions and after considering the objections, if any, filed by the affected persons, determine their position afresh in consultation with the Haryana Public Service Commission.
If the position of the petitioners or any other affected person, as indicated in the list dated 30111992, is changed to their/his advantage then such persons must be given all consequential benefits.
8.
Parties are left to bear their own costs.
Petition allowed.
