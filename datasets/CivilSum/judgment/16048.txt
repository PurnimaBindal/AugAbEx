Ashok Bhan, J - This writ petition has been filed on behalf of 11 petitioners with a prayer to quash Annexure P-3, vide which they have been declared to be ineligible to take the final examination of Intermediate School Course.
Petitioners No. 1 to 10 filed CWP 15908 of 1991 with a prayer for a direction to the respondents to depute them for Intermediate School Course Commencing from 1571991.
This writ petition was allowed on 2121991.
Petitioners were not given admission in the Intermediate School Course on the plea that the State of Haryana has filed a Special Leave Petition.
The Special Leave Petition was filed after a delay of 276 days in the Supreme Court of India which was dismissed on 12.7.1994.
In the meantime, petitioner No. 11 filed another writ petition No. 9140 of 1994, which was disposed of on 25.8.1994 by a Division Bench of this Court and a direction was issued to the respondents to depute the petitioner to undergo Intermediate School Course.
After the issuance of this direction, ultimately, all the 11 petitioners were allowed to join the Intermediate School Course on 3091994.
The Course had commenced on 15.6.1994.
2.
Vide the impugned order, Annexure P-3, the Director, Police Training College, Madbuban had informed the petitioners that since they had joined the Course on 3091994, they would not be able to attend 75% of the lectures delivered and, therefore, are rendered ineligible to appear in the final examination of the Course.
This order has teen impugned before us.
3.
From the narration of facts, it is clear that the delay was on the part of the respondents in not granting admission to the petitioners to the Intermediate School Course.
Petitioners had become entitled to be considered and deputed to the Intermediate School Course after the writ petition was allowed.
Petitioners were not granted admission in the Intermediate School Course as directed, but instead delay was caused by the respondents in granting admission to the petitioners.
Ultimately, petitioners were granted admission on 3091994 i.e. long after the commencement of the Course.
Keeping in view the peculiar facts of this case, the percentage of 75 of petitioners' lectures should be taken from the date they were allowed to join the Course i.e. lectures delivered after 3091994 or they should be deemed to have joined the Course on 15.6.1994, when the Course commenced, and deemed to have attended all the lectures between 15.6.1994 to 3091994.
4.
It is directed that the petitioners be permitted to take the final examination of the Intermediate School Course which had commenced on 15.6.1994.
The result of the petitioners be declared by the authorities concerned.
This writ petition is allowed with costs of Rs. 2,000/-.
Petition allowed.
