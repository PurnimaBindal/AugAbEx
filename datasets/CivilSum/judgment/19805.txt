Jawahar Lal Gupta, J - The petitioners are working as Librarians in various private colleges in the State of Punjab.
They claim the scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980.
2.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents the claim of the petitioners was contested.
With C.M.
No. 204-A of 1991 the petitioners have produced the copies of certain orders passed by the respondents.
One of these documents is Annexure P.22, which is a copy of an order dated December 21, 1989.
By this order the Director Public Instructions conveyed the sanction of the Punjab Government for the grant of pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980 and further Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986 to the left out Librarians working in the private aided Colleges subject to certain conditions.
All Librarians recruited before December 31, 1972, have been given the scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980.
Even the Librarians recruited after December 31, 1972 but upto February 18, 1983, who possess the qualifications as prescribed by the University Grants Commission before December 31, 1972 have also been given the revised scale of Rs. 700-1600.
The petitioners were recruited from different dates between 12th June, 1969 to October 6, 1978.
Apparently, all of them fulfil the qualifications mentioned in the letter of December 21, 1989.
They are accordingly entitled to the scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
April 2, 1980 and the scale of Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986.
This position is not controverted by Mr. TS Dhindsa, learned counsel for the respondents.
3.
In view of the order of December 21, 1989, the petitioners are held entitled to the scales of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
April 1, 1980 and Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
January 1, 1986.
Their pay shall be accordingly refixed and consequential reliefs in the nature of arrears of salary etc. shall also be paid to them.
In the circumstances of the case, the parties are left to bear their own costs.
Order accordingly.
