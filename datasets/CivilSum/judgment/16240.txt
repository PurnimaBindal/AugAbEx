BC Verma, CJ.
- Notice of motion to the AG, Haryana.
Mr. RC Setia, Addl.
AG Haryana accepts notice.
Record which perused.
Counsel heard.
The petitioners' particulars of service during whcih they were not paid their salaries for summer vacation are given in paragraph 3 of the writ petition.
Their grievance is that they are not being paid salaries for the aforesaid period during the tenure of their service.
No doubt such a benefit has to be given to them in view of the decision of the Supreme Court in Civil Appeal No. 3535 of 1990 (Zile Singh v. State of Haryana decided on July 23, 1990 and followed by this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 15167 of 1991, decided on October, 10, 1991.
We, therefore, direct that after due verification of the service record of the petitioners, salaries due to them for the intervening summer vacation during their service period shall be paid to them within a period of three months from today.
This writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed of.
