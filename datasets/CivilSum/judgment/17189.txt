GS Singhvi, J - Whether the petitioner is entitled to get interest on the delayed payment of retiral benefits is the only question that arises for consideration in this petition.
2.
The petitioner joined service in the Punjab State Electricity Board on 361964.
After formation of the State of Haryana w.e.f.
1111966 and establishment of the Haryana State Electricity Board (predecessor of respondent No. 1) (for short, `the Board'), his services were allocated to the Board.
During the course of service, he received promotions on various posts and superannuated on 3131998 while holding the post of Chief Engineer.
However, in the garb of show cause notices issued, the payment of following dues were delayed from 9 to 11 months.
The particulars of the delayed payments are as under :  Sr. No. Entitlement Amount Date of receipt Period of delay (a) D.C.R.G.
Rs. 3,50,000/- 29.1.99 10 months (b) Encashment of leave Rs. 2,13,440/- 7199 9 months (c) Commutation of pension Rs. 4,27,861/- 23299 11 months (d) Pay for 3/98 Rs. 24,578/- 7199 10 months Total : Rs. 10,15,879/- The petitioner has averred that for the delayed payment of death-cum- retirement gratuity etc., he is entitled to be compensated by award of interest.
3.
The respondents have relied on the show cause notice served upon the petitioner vide letter No. CH-1/Conf.
4011 dated 1951997 to justify the withholding monetary benefits due to him.
According to them, the amounts due to the petitioner were paid in January and February, 1999 after the show cause notice was dropped vide letter dated 18121998.
4.
We have heard petitioner-Kirat Gopal and Shri IPS Sahni, Law Officer, HVPNL.
It can no longer be doubted that the arrears of pay and retiral dues payable to the petitioner constituted his property within the meaning of Article 300(A) of the Constitution and he could not have been deprived of the same except in accordance with the procedure established by law.
Rule 2.2(b) of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-II (as applicable to the State of Haryana), which appears to have been relied upon by the respondents for not paying the death-cum-retirement gratuity and other dues to the petitioner, could not have been invoked by them for the simple reason that no departmental enquiry was pending against him on the date of his superannuation.
The show cause notices served upon the petitioner requiring him to submit explanation in respect of certain allegations cannot be equated with the charge-sheet, the issuance of which is sine qua non for initiation of enquiry under the Punishment and Appeal Rules - PR Nayak v Union of India, AIR 1972 SC 554.
In the present case, no charge-sheet is shown to have been served upon the petitioner and, therefore, no departmental enquiry could have been treated as pending justifying withholding of the arrears of pay and retiral dues.
In view of this, we have no hesitation to hold that the petitioner deserves to be compensated by award of interest for the delayed payment of death-cum- retirement gratuity etc.  5.
In RS Randhawa v. State of Punjab and others, 1997(3) RSJ 318, a Full Bench of this Court considered the question as to whether a petition can be filed under Article 226 of the Constitution of India for claiming interest on delayed payment of pension and other retiral benefits.
While answering the question in the affirmative, the Full Bench held that the petition is maintainable and a direction can be given for payment of interest at the rate of 12 to 18%.
In Moti Ram Gupta v. State of Haryana and others, 1993(1) RSJ 799 : 1993(3) S.C.T.
38 and R Kapoor v. Director of Inspection Income Tax and another, 1994(6) SC 589 : 1995(1) SCT 233 (SC), interest at the rate of 18% had been awarded for the delayed payment of retiral benefits and other dues and in the facts of this case, we are of the considered opinion that the petitioner should be adequately compensated for the harassment suffered by him on account of the patently illegal action of the respondents.
6.
Hence, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to pay interest at the rate of 18% on the amount of D.C.R.G., leave encashment, commutation of pension and pay for March, 1998.
The interest shall be calculated from the date of petitioner's retirement to the date of actual payment and be paid to him within a period of 2 months from the date of submission of certified copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
