Ashok Bhan, J - Petitioners, who are the employees of the State of Haryana, have filed this petition for issuance of a writ of certiorari for quashing the impugned order dated 2571996, Annexure P-6, regularising their services with effect from 31121990 instead of 1111986 and for issuance of a writ of mandamus directing the respondents to regularise their services in accordance with the instructions dated 1621987, with effect from 1111986 instead of 31121990.
2.
Petitioners were appointed in the Census Department of the Government of Haryana during the Census Operation in the year 1981.
After the completion of the Census Operation, petitioners faced retrenchment and in the year 1983, in order to absorb the surplus employees of the Census Department, the Government took a policy decision and circulated the same vide letter dated 25.2.1983, relevant portion of which is reproduced as under :-  "I am directed to address you on the above cited subject and to say that after the completion of field operations, Regional Tabulation offices were set up in the State for processing the data collected in the 1981 Census schedule.
These offices are being wound up after the processing work is over and as such a number of persons recruited in these offices are likely to be retrenched/have been retrenched.
With a view to avoid hardship to the employees likely to be retrenched, it has been decided by the State Government that due consideration may be given to the experience gained by these personnel while working in the Census Organisation for their absorption in the State Class III and IV services and if possible they may be appointed on ad hoc basis and later steps be taken to absorb them on regular basis with the approval of the SSS Board/Employment Exchange or the State Government, as the case may be."
In compliance with the directions issued by the Chief Secretary to Government of Haryana, the petitioners were appointed as Clerks in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600 plus usual allowances as are admissible to the Haryana Government employees from time to time, on ad hoc basis.
Petitioners requested the respondent to regularise their service from the date of their initial appointment but the respondent kept the matter pending.
State Government took a policy decision to regularise the services of the ad hoc employee and, ultimately, the services of the petitioners were regularised with effect from 31121990 and not from the date of their initial appointment or as per the instructions issued by the State Government from time to time on completion of two years service on ad hoc basis.
State Government had issued instructions dated 1621987 in which it has been stated that the employees who had completed two years of service as on 1111986 are to be regularised.
Claim of the petitioner under these instructions was not considered.
Prayer made in this petition is to regularise the services of the petitioners with effect from 1111986 in terms of the Government instructions dated 1621987.
3.
In the written statement filed, the main plea raised by the respondents is that the Government instructions dated 1621987 are not applicable to the petitioner's case as the posts of the petitioners were under the purview of the Subordinate Services Selection Board (hereinafter referred to as 'the Board'); that in the Government instructions dated 1621987, it has been mentioned that the services of ad hoc employees, other than Teachers working against the posts which had been taken out of the purview of the Board, who had completed two years of service on 1111986 and were in service on that date may be regularised.
4.
Counsel for the petitioners relied upon two Division Bench judgments of this Court in CWP 15444 of 1994 decided on 1111995 and CWP 1009 of 1987 decided on 12101995, where similar pleas raised by the respondent-State have been repelled.
In CWP 15444 decided on 1111995, similar contention raised by the respondents was repelled by observing as under :-  "We fail to understand the contention reflected in this paragraph.
All these documents were relied upon by the respondents in Piara Singh's case (supra) and after considering the same, the court has given direction to regularise the services of such ad hoc employees.
What is sought to be urged on behalf of the respondents by Mr. Kamal Sharma, the learned Addl.
AG Haryana, is that in the year 1991, the Government has issued certain instructions and since Hon'ble Mr. Justice VK Bali has issued a direction to consider the case of the petitioner for regularisation in terms of the instructions, the case of the petitioner was considered in the light of the Government instructions issued in 1991 with effect from January 1, 1991, and thus, the petitioner was not entitled to regularisation with effect from November 1, 1986.
We are unable to accept this contention since the same would run counter to the direction given by this court as well as by the Supreme Court in Piara Singh's case (supra).
We are unable to appreciate the defence of the respondents that the petitioner's case is different and the Government instructions dated February 16, 1987, are not applicable.
No distinction has been brought to our notice.
In view of the pleadings of the parties and the decision of this Court as well as the Supreme Court in Piara Singh's case (supra), in our opinion, the petitioner is entitled to be regularised with effect from November 1, 1986.
The respondents are accordingly directed to regularise the services of the petitioner from November 1, 1986."
5.
Similarly, in CWP 1009 of 1987 decided on 12101995, the respondent- State was directed to regularise the services of those petitioners who had completed two years of service as on 1111986 and who fulfilled the conditions of eligibility specified in the Government instructions dated 1621987 irrespective of the fact as to whether the posts held by them were taken out of the purview of the Board or not.
Following direction was issued :-  "(1) Services of those petitioners who had completed two years of service as on 1111986 and who fulfil the conditions of eligibility specified in the circular dated 15.2.1987/1621987 should be regularised by adjudging their suitability in accordance with the instructions of the Government irrespective of the fact whether the posts held by them were taken out of the purview of the Subordinate Services Selection Board or not."
The point raised in this petition is similar to the one raised in the aforesaid writ petitions.
Following the view taken in CWP 15444 of 1994 decided on 1111995 and CWP 1009 of 1987 decided on 12101995, we direct the respondents to regularise the services of the petitioners who had completed two years of service as on 1111986 and who fulfil the conditions of eligibility specified in the instructive dated 1621987 irrespective of the fact whether the posts held by them were outside the purview of the Board or not.
The writ petition stands allowed with no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
