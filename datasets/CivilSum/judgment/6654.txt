RL Anand, J - Shri KL Agarwal was the Executive Engineer at the time of filling of the writ petition but now this Court has been informed that he has been promoted to the post of Superintending Engineer.
Yet the controversy is not over as the petitioner if becomes successful in the present writ petition can be further considered for promotion from the back date to the post of Chief Engineer.
He could not be promoted as Superintending Engineer at the relevant time when private respondents were promoted to that post.
2.
The petitioner has filed the present writ petition seeking the directions of this Court that a declaration be given that action of respondent No. 1 in promoting respondents No. 2 to 14 prior to the promotion of the petitioner to the post of Superintending Engineer is illegal and the said order may be quashed and further directions be given to the respondent No. 1 to consider the case of the petitioner for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer w.e.f.
the date when juniors to him were promoted to that post.
It was also prayed by the petitioner that a writ of certiorari be issued and his A.C.R.
for the year 1988-89 vide which the performance of the petitioner was treated as below average be quashed.
Also it was prayed by the petitioner that this A.C.R.
for the year 1995-96 be quashed and it may be ranked as good.
3 The case set up by the petitioner is that he was inducted into service of the Haryana State Electricity Board (hereinafter referred to as the Board) as Apprentice Engineer on 236.1967.
Thereafter, he was promoted to the post of Assistant Engineer in the year 1967.
Then he was promoted to the post of Assistant Executive Engineer and then to the post of Executive Engineer in February, 1978.
He has been discharging his duties against the post of Executive Engineer in an efficient manner.
From the post of Executive Engineer one can be promoted to the post of Superintending Engineer.
The conditions of service of the employees of the Board including the engineering staff are regulated by the provisions of Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Recruitment Regulations, 1965.
As per Regulation 6(d), the post of Superintending Engineer can be filled up by way of selection from the post of Executive Engineer on the conditions that the officer must be of outstanding ability and the consideration on the basis of seniority-cum- suitability as Executive Engineer.
The regulation was amended w.e.f.
19.2.1988 and it was decided that the promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer will be on the basis of principle of seniority-cum-merit.
Also according to the promotion policy accepted and applied by the Board it was clearly envisaged that those Executive Engineers who have 70% good and above ACRs during the last 10 years were to be considered suitable for appointment by promotion.
According to the petitioner, he has 70% good and above reports during the last 10 years on the relevant day and, therefore, he had a superior right of promotion than that of respondents No. 2 to 14.
His ACR for the year 1988-89 relates to the period 141988 to 2411989.
It was communicated to him on 31.1995.
He filed the reply/representation against the same on 22.2.1995.
He also gave a reminder on 19.4.1995.
The representation made by the petitioner was exhaustive but for mala fide consideration the same was rejected and the petitioner was informed on 1211996.
Moreover, the remarks recorded against the petitioner in the ACR for the year 1988-89 were cryptic and meaningless.
No definite opinion was given by the Reporting Officer.
These remarks were communicated to him after a lapse of seven years.
There was extraneous consideration as the authorities wanted to ignore him, otherwise he was eligible for consideration for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer.
The report was procured purposely and belatedly so as to condemn the petitioner.
Moreover, the report for this period was written without application of mind and it should be ignored and the petitioner is entitled to a good report for that period.
The petitioner has also given challenge to the ACR for the year 1995-96 which related to the period 1261995 to 311996.
This was communicated to the petitioner on 3171996.
The petitioner made a representation on 1391996.
He gave the reminder for the decision of his representation which was decided on 1711997.
The petitioner submitted that he repeatedly claimed a personal hearing while determining his claim against the ACR for the year 1995-96 but he was not allowed the same.
Therefore, he made further representation in this regard.
A communication was addressed to the petitioner on 2021997 whereby the Member, Administration & Project Finance summoned the petitioner for personal hearing in response to the adverse remarks communicated to him on 331997.
The personal hearing could not materialise because of the transfer of the Member, Administration & Project Finance and consequently another communication dated 1031997 was issued to the petitioner to afford him personal hearing in connection with the ACR.
The petitioner was summoned for personal hearing on 21.31997.
He appeared before the Member, Administration & Project Finance on the appointed day and fully explained the matter to him.
The petitioner came to know that the aforesaid member recorded favourable findings and recommended that the adverse remarks communicated to the petitioner on 3171997 should be expunged.
Otherwise, the work and conduct of the petitioner during this period was assessed on three occasions.
Firstly, from 1261995 to 311996.
This report was recorded by the Superintending Engineer.
Another report was recorded for the period 13101995 to 3131996.
The third report was recorded for the period 4.1.1996 to 1061996.
The Superintending Engineer who recorded the adverse remarks against the petitioner was repeatedly requested in writing by the Chief Engineer to submit his remark relating to the work and conduct of the petitioner during the period he discharged his duties under his supervision and control.
He did not do so because the said Superintending Engineer was inimical to the petitioner.
The adverse remarks by one of the Superintending Engineers against the petitioner were made to harm his interest and service career.
S much so there was non-application of mind.
The employees of the respondent-Board were on strike from 17101995 to 4111995.
The petitioner availed leave on medical ground from 20101995 to 23101995 i.e. for four days as he was under the treatment of HSEB Health Service Dispensary at Dholkote.
Seeing the attitude of the superiors the petitioner addressed representation dated 29101996 and appended two medical slips.
Still an unwarranted report was given that the petitioner has intentionally and deliberately not attended his duties during the period under reference.
The case set up by the petitioner further is that the respondent Board's action in not allowing promotion to him to the post of Superintending Engineer and allowing promotion to respondents No. 2 to 14, who were junior to him, is wholly illegal, unjust, unfair, unconstitutional and the same is arbitrary.
4.
Thus the petitioner through this writ petition has made a claim for promotion from the date when his juniors were promoted and secondly he has made a request that the ACRs for the year 1988-89 and 1995-96 be quashed.
Since the petitioner has qualified for the zone of promotion on the basis of 70% good reports, therefore, directions be given to respondent No. 1 to consider his case for promotion from the date when his juniors i.e. respondents No. 2 to 14 were promoted.

6.
On the basis of the above summary of the ACRs, it was pleaded by respondent No. 1 that the petitioner was not required to be considered for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer for 10 years in view of the State Govt. instructions dated 235.1973 Yet he was considered for promotion by the Board in its meeting held on 25.101996 and again on 231.1997.

14.
I have heard Mr. PS Patwalia, Advocate on behalf of the petitioner, Mr. Ashok Agarwal, Sr. Advocate on behalf of respondent-Board and with their assistance have gone through the record of the case.
15.
Here it is the case of the petitioner that he was senior to respondents No. 2 to 14 and this aspect of the case is not even disputed by the learned counsel for respondent No. 1.
Otherwise also, reference to the document Annexure P-4, the extract of the seniority list of the Executive Engineers would show that the name of the petitioner is at Sr. No. 56 while that of private respondents start from 57 onwards.
Thus seniority aspect is decided in favour of the petitioner.
16.
The relevant ten years for consideration start from 1986-87 to 1995-96 which include two disputed years of 1988-89 and 1995-96.
The learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent No. 1 also submitted during the course of submissions that for the year 1994-95 the petitioner's report was average though the learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that for this year the report of the petitioner was upgraded to good by virtue of the letter quoted above vide which the representation of the petitioner was accepted.
This aspect of the case shall also be examined by me in the subsequent portion of this judgment.
17.
First of all, let me take the undisputed ACRs i.e. of the years 1986-87, 1990-91, 1991-92, 1992-93 and 1993-94.
The number of these years comes to live.
Now I would like to examine the ACR for the year 1994-95.
According to the respondent, it was an average report because the petitioner got his report written by Shri MR Mittal as very good for the period 106.1994 to 3111995 in spite of the fact that he had not worked under him.
Thus it was declared as invalid in February.
Then the concerned officer was asked to send the report.
He reported it as average starting from 14.8.1994 to 13101994.
Meaning thereby that the officer who was called upon to rewrite the ACR did not comment on the working of the petitioner from 104.1994 to 138.1994.
He simply commented from 14.8.1994 to 13101994 only for a period of two months.
A representation was made by the petitioner for the adverse remarks starting from 14.6.1994 to 13101994.
His representation was dated 31.101996.
It was considered and accepted and the adverse remarks for the period 14.6.1994 to 13101994 were upgraded as good by the competent authority.
Meaning thereby that the ACR for the period April, 1994 to 13101994 was upgraded.
For the rest of the period it was already good.
In these circumstances, the report of 1994-95 has to be treated as good as it was always taken as good by the petitioner.
This is the reason that the petitioner has never given challenge to this report of 1994-95 in the present writ petition.
Thus, it becomes the 6th report for the benefit of the petitioner.
18.
Now let us examine the report of 1988-89.
It was rated below average mainly on the ground that the petitioner was in the habit of coming in confrontation with the officers/officials.
It was also commented that the officer should not be posted where public dealings were involved.
The original record shows that the form was filled by the petitioner from 141988 to 2111989 on 25.31991.
Sh. R.S.
Mishra made the assessment.
His remarks were that the work of the official was defective.
He was in the habit of coming in confrontation with the officials/officers.
His way of disposal was not satisfactory.
His relations with others working with him were not satisfactory.
The officer even quarrelled with a lady Peon.
He further commented about the professional ability of the petitioner by saying that the officer should not be given posting where public dealings are involved.
Finally, it is stated that the officer is energetic but his mind/approach is not positive.
Thus his assessment was made below average.
The reading of the original ACR would show that Sh. Mishra gave his observation on 1141991.
But strange enough these remarks were conveyed to the petitioner in the month of January, 1995 as is evident from the document Annexure P5.
The petitioner made the representation against the adverse remarks.
He also gave the reminder.
So much so the Commenting Officer without application of mind said something about the integrity of the petitioner when no adverse remarks were recorded.
Therefore, there is no difficulty in holding that such a stale communication could not be acted upon for examining the case of the petitioner for promotion.
Rather, this shows totally non-application of mind on the part of the initiating authorities.
If the report of 1988-89 is taken out of consideration zone and if the same is expunged and upgraded then this report cannot be considered adverse against the petitioner and the petitioner gets the 7th good report also making his case worth for consideration for promotion.
Thus, I quash the ACR for the year 1988-89.
Even the written statement of the respondent would show that when the representation of the petitioner was rejected against the ACR of 1988-89, there was non-application of mind.
Realising this problem that the petitioner has been dubbed with integrity doubtful the Board had to eat a humble pie by expunging the remarks against the petitioner but has taken a defence by saying that the expunging of the adverse remarks relating to the integrity from the ACR shows that the representation of the petitioner was well considered by the competent authority.
Even in the written statement no good reason has been given why the ACR of 1988-89 was conveyed late to the petitioner.
The only excuse made by respondent No. 1 is that the said ACR was received late after reporting, reviewing and accepting and, therefore, it was conveyed to the petitioner in January, 1995.
19.
With regard to the ACR for the year 1995-96 though the petitioner has tried to make out a case by stating that it was made on account of non- application of mind.
The petitioner was on leave on medical reasons or that the remarks were not justified, yet I am not inclined to quash the same.
We all know that recording of ACR is the subjective satisfaction of the Reporting Officer.
Unless it is inherently arbitrary written with a mala fide intention or with extraneous reasons, the same cannot be set aside by the High Court while invoking the provisions of Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
Therefore, the prayer of the petitioner for quashment of the ACR for the year 1995-96 is rejected.
But even with the rejection of this relief to the petitioner still the case of the petitioner is proved with regard to other relief.
Resultantly, the writ petition is partly allowed.
The adverse remarks in the ACR for the year 1988-89 are hereby quashed and directions are given to respondent No. 1 that these remarks may be treated as good in view of the performance of the petitioner in the previous year.
The action of respondent No. 1 in ignoring the petitioner for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer is hereby declared illegal and direction are given to respondent No. 1 to consider the case of the petitioner for promoting and to promote him to the post of Superintending Engineer from the date when the persons junior to him were promoted.
Of course, the petitioner shall be entitled to all the consequential benefit of which he was deprived on account of the promotions of respondents No. 2 to 14.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Petition partly allowed.
