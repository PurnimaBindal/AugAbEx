AL Bahri, J (Oral) - State of Haryana in the present case is in a peculiar situation where it is difficult to get out.
Civil Writ Petition No. 12230 of 1989 was filled by 15 persons, Jethu Ram and others, who were employed as Peon-Cum-Chowkidars in different schools in the State of Haryana, claiming that work of either Peon or Chowkidar could be taken from them.
Copy of the order passed in the petition aforesaid has now been produced alongwith Civil Miscellaneous Application, which is allowed and Annexure P/1 is taken on the record.
2.
Som Nath and others, 32 petitioners, who are also working as Peon-Cum-Chowkidars or Peon-cum-Mali-cum-Chowkidars, have approached this Court in this writ petition for the similar relief as granted in the case of Jethu Ram etc.
In the present case written statement has been filed by the State of Haryana, inter alia alleging that in the appointment letters the post was described as "Peon-cum-Chowkidar" or Peon-cum-Mali-cum-Chowkidar".
Since the decision in Jethu Ram's case now holds the field, there is no scope for taking a different view in this writ petition.
For the reasons recorded therein, the present writ petition is also allowed in the same terms.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
