RS Mongia, J (Oral) - Petitioners were all working as Tailoring Mistresses, when the present writ petition was filed.
There are other classes of teachers known as J.A.V./J.S.T.
Teachers, Art & Craft Teachers, Classical & Vernacular Teachers.
The pay scales of the various categories have been revised from time to time by the State Govt. Petitioners have given a Chart in the writ petition showing pay scales of teachers at different times :-  Date Tailoring Mistresses J.A.V./J.S.T.
Art & Craft Classical & Vernacular 1954 70-150 70-150 60-120 60-120 1.4.1955 -do- 80-225 -do- -do- 1111966 125-300 125-300 125-300 125-300 1.2.1968 -do- 220-400 -do- -do- 1.11.1971 170-350 -do- 170-350 170-350 It has further been mentioned in the writ petition that when the pay scale of J.A.V./J.S.T.
teachers was raised to Rs. 220-430 somewhere in May, 1974, other categories were given selection grade in the pay scale of Rs. 220-430 to the extent of 15% of the total posts.
2.
Petitioners were given the pay scale of Rs. 170-350 w.e.f.
1.11.1971 and also the selection grade of Rs. 220-430 w.e.f.
22.5.1974.
Both the benefits were sought to be withdrawn from the petitioners on two counts :-  (i) There had never been revision of pay scale of Tailoring Mistresses to the scale of Rs. 170-350.
The initial pay scale of Tailoring Mistresses (Rs. 70-150) was revised to Rs. 125-300 only and it remained in force upto 31121977 and w.e.f.
111978, the said scale of pay was revised to Rs. 480-880.
It has been specifically averred that the pay scale of Rs. 170-350 which was admissible to the Classical & Vernacular Teachers w.e.f.
1.11.1971 was not admissible to Tailoring Mistresses.
It is further the case that the pay scale of Rs. 170-350 was granted to the petitioners on account of clerical mistake.
(ii) The selection grade of Rs. 220-430 had been granted to the petitioners by clerical mistake by drawing a joint seniority list of Tailoring Mistresses and Classical & Vernacular Teachers.
In fact, the seniority list of Classical & Vernacular Teachers is at the District Level whereas that of Tailoring Mistresses is at the State Level.
3.
It may be observed here that w.e.f.
111978, the pay scale of Classicial & Vernacular Teachers was revised from Rs. 170-350 to Rs. 570-1080.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submitted that the petitioners are entitled to the same pay scale as that of Classicial & Vernacular Teachers because once they were made equal to them w.e.f.
1111966, and consequently withdrawing the benefit of revision of pay scale, which was given to the Classical & Vernacular Teachers is wholly illegal and unsustainable.
In support of his contention, he has cited two judgments of this Court in Amarjit Kaur v. The State of Punjab & others 1988(4) SLR 199 and Sukhwinder Kaur v. State of Punjab & Others, 1989(3) SLR 379.
In the former case, it was observed by the learned Single Judge that the petitioner who was a Tailoring Mistress, could not be denied the revised scale of pay which had been granted to Classicial & Vernacular Teachers.
In the latter authority, the pay scale of Rs. 570-1080 granted to the petitioner (a Tailoring Mistress) was sought to be reduced to Rs. 480-880.
It was held by the learned Single Judge that the Tailoring Mistresses were entitled to the revised grade of Rs. 570-1080 as was granted to Classical & Vernacular Teachers.
Learned counsel for the respondents could not successfully differentiate the abovesaid two authorities.
In this view of the matter, I hold that the petitioners were rightly given the same pay scale as that of Classical & Vernacular Teachers and the same could not have been reduced by the respondents.
They are, in fact, entitled to the same pay scale as that of Classical & Vernacular Teachers.
4.
Now coming to the grant of selection grade and the withdrawal thereof by the respondents.
It is not disputed before me that the petitioners had been given the selection grade on the basis of a joint seniority list which was drawn of Classicial & Vernacular Teachers and the Tailoring Mistresses.
15% of the seniormost incumbents in the said seniority list were to be granted the selection grade.
It is further not disputed by Mr. Agnihotri that senior list of Tailoring Mistresses is at State level and that of Classical & Vernacular Teachers is at the District Level.
Though the posts of Tailoring Mistresses and Classical & Vernacular Teachers may be equivalent as far as pay scales are concerned, yet they do not form a joint cadre.
That being so, the petitioners having been given selection grade on the basis of wrong seniority list which was a mistake, the same could always be rectified and the selection grade withdrawn.
However, the petitioners would be entitled, in their own cadre of Tailoring Mistresses, to selection grade (which is given to Classical & Vernacular Teachers) to the extent of 15% posts of Tailoring Mistresses in accordance with their seniority.
If the turn of the petitioners in their own seniority list has come for the grant of selection grade (i.e. they are amongst the seniormost 15%) then, they will keep on getting the selection grade.
However, if their turn has not reached for the grant of selection grade, they would not be entitled to the same.
However, I make it clear that the payment on account of grant of selection grade which has already been given to the petitioners though by mistake may not be recovered at this stage specially when some of the petitioners have already retired.
5.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed to the extent that petitioners would be entitled to the same pay scale as that of Classical & Vernacular Teachers and as far as selection grade is concerned, they will be entitled to the same to the extent of 15% posts in their own cadre depending on their seniority, I make no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
