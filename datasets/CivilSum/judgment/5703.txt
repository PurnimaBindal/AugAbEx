RS Mongia, J - The petitioner was appointed in December, 1979, as Social Study Master in Hindu Senior Secondary School, Khaithal (hereinafter referred to as `the School').
The Management of the School terminated the services of the petitioner in May, 1981.
However, the order of termination was set aside by the Director Secondary Education in December, 1981 and the petitioner was reinstated in service on October 5, 1991.
The Management of the School challenged the order of Director of Secondary Education in this Court, where the Management lost and ultimately the matter went upto the Supreme Court and the Supreme Court also dismissed the Special Leave Petition of the Management on September 24, 1992.
The petitioner filed an Execution Application No. 425 of 1993 (Copy of Annexure P-2) for execution of the order of the learned Single Judge regarding the petitioner's arrears of salary and allowances.
An agreement was reached between the petitioner and the Management of the School on December 3, 1993, which was filed in the Execution Application.
It may be observed here that the petitioner had again been suspended on May 22, 1993, during the pendency of the Execution Application.
The agreement between the parties, dated December 3, 1993, settled the payment of arrears of pay/allowances upto May 22, 1993, when the petitioner was again put under suspension.
2.
The petitioner's services were again terminated on August 19, 1994, which according to the respondents, were terminated after holding a departmental enquiry.
However, the District Education Officer vide order dated August 2, 1995, ordered reinstatement of the petitioner and by virtue of the order the petitioner was reinstated in service on August 23, 1995.
This writ petition was filed primarily with the prayer that the petitioner has not been paid his salary and allowances from May 22, 1993 when he was suspended despite his reinstatement in service by virtue of the order dated August 2, 1995 passed by the District Education Officer.
It was brought to this Court's notice that the respondent-Management of the School had filed an appeal-cum-representation against the order of the District Education Officer, dated August 2, 1995, which was pending before the Director, Secondary Education.
This Court on July 29, 1996, directed the Director Secondary Education to decide the matter between the parties and ultimately the Director Secondary Education disposed of the appeal-cum-representation of the Management on August 29, 1996 while upholding the order dated August 2, 1995 of the District Education Officer.
That order has been placed on the file.
3.
From the facts mentioned above, it would be seen that the petitioner is actually in service of the respondent-School since August 23, 1995, and there cannot be any controversy regarding the payment of his salary for the period August 23, 1995, onwards.
The interim order dated July 12, 1996, was passed which may be reproduced here :-  ``A short affidavit has been filed by Mr. Sheodan Singh, District Education Officer, Kaithal.
A cheque in the sum of Rs. 39180/- which, according to the respondent-State, represents the salary of the petitioner from August 23, 1995, upto June 30, 1996, has been handed over to the counsel for the petitioner.
This is apart from the payment of Rs. 482410 which has already been made by way of a banker's cheque by the management vide order dated May 9, 1996.
Another draft in the name of the Registrar in the sum of Rs. 91968/- has also been brought on behalf of the respondent-State, which, according to the State Counsel, represents the arrears of salary of the petitioner as per order Annexure P-6 w.e.f.
May 23, 1993, to August 23, 1995.
This is apart from the payment of Rs. 9806/- made by the management by way of bank draft in the name of the Registrar of this Court on May 9, 1996.
Learned counsel for the petitioner, however, disputes the calculations.
Let the Registrar deposit the draft of Rs. 91968/- in the account of the High Court.
Adjourned to July 16, 1996.''
It will be seen from the aforesaid order that a cheque in the sum of Rs. 39, 180/- was given by the State to the petitioner which according to the State was the salary for the period August 23, 1995 upto June 30, 1996 which was payable by the State Government as its share to an aided school.
It is not disputed that the share to be borne by the State Government regarding the salary of an incumbent against a sanctioned post is 75%.
Rest of the 25% is payable by the management itself.
Consequently, upto June 30, 1996, the share payable by the State Government to the management regarding the petitioner's salary already stands paid to the petitioner.
Rest of the 25% is payable by the School management.
It is stated by the learned counsel for the petitioner that the petitioner has not been paid salary by the School from July 1, 1996, onwards.
In an endeavour to avoid any further litigation, we direct the State Government to pay the share of the management as well, i.e. 25% of the salary of the petition from August 23, 1995, to June 30, 1996, and deduct the said amount from the maintenance grant to be provided to the School.
It will also keep on paying the maintenance grant for the said post from July 1, 1996, onwards to the extent of 75%.
We direct the State Government to release its 75% share of the salary of the petitioner from July 1, 1996, onwards to February 28, 1997 as also the 25% share to be borne by the management for the said period and may deduct equivalent amount from the maintenance grant to be paid to the management in future.
We make it clear that by deduction of the aforesaid amount from the maintenance grant as observed above, the payment of the salary to the other employees of the School will not be affected.
Out of the aforesaid amount, the State Government would deduct another amount of Rs. 482410 which already stands paid to the petitioner by the management and would pay that amount along with the grant-in aid to the school.
4.
Now the question is for the period May 22, 1993, to August 22, 1995, when the petitioner was put under suspension.
Since the petitioner has been ordered to be reinstated as no prior approval of the District Education Officer was taken regarding termination of his services, the petitioner would be entitled to pay for the aforesaid period also.
The respondent-State pursuant to order dated July 12, 1996, ibid has already deposited Rs. 91968/- in this Court which represents 75% of the pay of the petitioner to be borne by the State Government towards the maintenance grant payable against the post of the petitioner.
Rs. 9806/- have already been paid by the management towards the pay of the petitioner with the Registrar of this Court, which, according to the Management, is towards 25% of the payment of salary for the aforesaid period.
The State Government shall pay to the petitioner the rest of 25% of the salary for the aforesaid period (as per the calculations made by it for payment of Rs. 91968/- for the aforesaid, period towards 75% of the payment) minus Rs. 91968/- already deposited by the management in this Court and onwards also.
The State Government would deduct that amount also from the maintenance grant payable to the respondent-School management.
5.
We make it clear that if for any aforesaid periods, according to the State Government, no grant-in-aid against the petitioner's post was payable, it will be open to them to pass an order in that regard in accordance with law after affording opportunity to the management but this will not come in the way of the State Government to comply with the aforesaid directions immediately.
If the petitioner moves an application to the Registrar, the amount already deposited, i.e. Rs. 91968/- and Rs. 9806/- be released to the petitioner.
The counsel for the management states that management has already applied for approval regarding the termination of the services of the petitioner way back on the October 25, 1996, and no order thereon has been passed.
The District Education Officer, Kaithal, would decide the same in accordance with law.
The petitioner would be paid his salary by the management every month as would be paid to the other employees.
