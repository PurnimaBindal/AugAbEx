Ashok Bhan, J - Petitioner on his selection by Punjab Public Service Commission, was appointed as Sub Divisional Officer on 2881964.
In May, 1971, petitioner was sent on deputation by the State of Punjab to Bhakra Beas Management Board respondent No. 3 (hereinafter referred to as the Board) and posted at Nangal Township where he served upto 1241984 except for a brief period between April, 1976 to March, 1979 during which period he served in Algeria on foreign assignment duly sponsored by the Central Government.
2.
Consequent upon the transfer of completed component of Beas Project Units and thereupon winding up of Irrigation Wing, the Board reverted the petitioner as Executive Engineer and relieved him from 1241984 (afternoon) to his parent State of Punjab in public interest.
Office Order No. 164 dated 1341984 was issued by the Board with copy to the State of Punjab in continuation of his letter dated 2831984 requesting respondent No. 1 to issue posting order of the petitioner in his own State.
Petitioner was awaiting his posting order on his being relieved w.e.f.
1241984 (afternoon) but no posting orders were given to him till 951984.
Vide order dated 951984 (Annexure P/2) petitioner was given his posting order by the State of Punjab as Senior Design Engineer in Thein Dam Construction Directorate vice Shri Harcharan Singh, Executive Engineer.
Petitioner in response to his posting order dated 951984 (Annexure P/2) reported his arrival to the authorities in Thein Dam Construction Directorate but his joining report was not accepted on the ground that the posting orders dated 951984 (Annexure P/2) were under review.
Subsequently, his posting orders were cancelled by respondent No. 1 vide its order dated 271984 copy of which has been placed as Annexure P/3 to the writ petition.
It was further stated in the order Annexure P/3 that posting order of the petitioner as Executive Engineer shall be issued in due course.
3.
Petitioner vide his representation dated 971984 (Annexure P/4) requested the State of Punjab that he was relieved with effect from 1241984 by the Board and thereafter in partial modification of order dated 3041984, the order dated 951984 (Annexure P/2) was issued posting him as Senior Design Engineer but the same was cancelled vide order dated 271984 (Annexure P/3) and that his further posting order should be issued to avoid unnecessary hardship.
No action was taken on this representation.
Petitioner issued several reminders reiterating his request for posting order but no action was taken on those reminders either.
4.
State of Punjab vide its order dated 911985 placed the services of the petitioner as Executive Engineer again at the disposal of the Board vice Shri YP Nayyar Executive Engineer on his (YP Nayyar's) promotion as Superintending Engineer.
Petitioner was not allowed to join as Shri YP Nayyar was not promoted as Superintending Engineer till 1821985.
On the promotion of Shri YP Nayyar as Superintending Engineer on 1821985, petitioner submitted his arrival report to the Board on 2221985 and requested them to issue his further posting orders.
However, no posting orders were issued and finally petitioner was again reverted back to his parent State of Punjab and was given his posting orders on 631985 by the State of Punjab which were received by the petitioner on 1231985.
Petitioner submitted his arrival report immediately on 13.3.1985 (forenoon) and joined his duties.
5.
Petitioner filed representation on 2731985 (Annexure P/2) that for non-issuance of his posting orders without his fault his compulsory waiting period with effect from 1341984 to 1231985 be treated as duty period and that he should be paid his rightful dues and service benefits.
This representation was followed by two other reminders which have been placed as Annexures P/13 and P/14 to the writ petition.
On 3.8.1987 State of Punjab issued a letter Annexure P/15 stating therein that compulsory waiting period of the petitioner with effect from 1341984 to 1231985, that is about 336 days would be regularised by grant of 120 days earned leave, if due, followed by half pay leave, if due, and thereafter extraordinary leave.
Petitioner has come to this Court impugning this order of respondent No. 1 claiming a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to treat the transitory period with effect from 1341984 to 1231985 when no posting orders were given to him, as duty period and that it be held that the petitioner is entitled to all service benefits including salary which has not been paid with interest on the amount which has been held arbitrarily.
6.
It has been argued by the learned counsel for the petitioner that petitioner was ready and willing to perform his duty and kept on waiting for posting orders which were not given to him by respondent No. 1; that he could not be asked to apply for leave of the kind due for the said compulsory waiting period for which the petitioner was not at fault; that petitioner requested for his posting orders repeatedly and he could not be penalised and made to suffer for the fault of the department.
State of Punjab has not been able to put forward any meaningful defence to the writ petition in their written statement.
7.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties at length and find force in the submission of learned counsel for the petitioner.
From the narration of facts, it is evident that petitioner was ready and willing to perform his duties and was seeking his posting orders repeatedly but was kept on compulsory waiting for no fault of his.
Petitioner cannot be penalised and made to suffer for the fault of the department.
Action of respondent No. 1 to ask the petitioner to apply for the leave of the kind due for the compulsory waiting period is without justification and improper and as such the same cannot be sustained in the eyes of law.
Respondents should have taken necessary steps to ensure that no official should remain without posting order, as, such situation is pitiable and unfair to the concerned officer and also creates stagnation and inefficiency in the department.
Due care should be taken so that no officer should remain without posting order or he should not be posted against a post which is not available in the department.
8.
Asking an officer who has been kept on compulsory waiting for posting orders to apply for leave of the kind due for regularising the intervening period for which he was forced to remain without posting orders is not justified.
There is no rule justifying the refusal to pay the salary for the intervening period of compulsory waiting period for the posting orders, the action of the Government asking the petitioner to apply leave of the kind due and that his salary would be paid and period would be regularised against the leave of the kind due is patently illegal, arbitrary, violative of principles of natural justice.
9.
Accordingly, this writ petition is allowed with costs and a writ of mandamus is issued directing respondent No.1 to treat the transitory period with effect from 1341984 to 1231985 when no orders were given to the petitioner, as duty period.
It is further held that petitioner would be entitled to all service benefits including salary etc. pertaining to this period.
Petitioner shall also be entitled to interest at the rate of 12% per annum on the withheld amount from the date it became due till its actual payment.
Costs are assessed at Rs. 500/-.
Petition allowed.
