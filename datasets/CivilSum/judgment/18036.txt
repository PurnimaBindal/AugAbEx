Radhakrishna Rao, J - The petitioner who was a seasonal employee in a co-operative society contested in the election of Pothireddipalem Gram Panchayat as a Sarpanch in the year 1988.
The petitioner was declared as a successful candidate on 10.3.88.
The unsuccessful candidates-respondents 1 and 2 - filed an Election Petition challenging the election of the petitioner on the ground that he is not eligible for contesting as a Sarpanch as he is an employee of a co-operative society.
The Tribunal below considered that objection and interpreting Section 17 of the Andhra Pradesh Gram Panchayats Act stated that there is no distinction between full-time employees or part-time employees and since the employees of the co-operative societies are barred from contesting the elections, the election of the petitioner has been set aside on the technical ground.
As against that, this writ petition has been filed.
2.
The learned counsel for the petitioner contended that the petitioner was only a seasonal employee and worked only for a period of three months in a year and there is no guarantee that he will be re-appointed.
If the elections are held in the non-season, there should be some force in the contention raised by the petitioner.
During that very season when he was working as an employee either seasonal or on daily wages, he contested the elections.
During that relevant time he was an employee of the co-operative society either temporarily or part-time or seasonal.
When no distinction has been made in Section 17 it can be said that the employees of the Co-operative Societies in general are barred from contesting the elections.
The view that has been taken by the lower court in setting aside the election of the petitioner on the ground that he is employee of the cooperative society as contemplated under Section 17 of the Act is correct.
Accordingly, the writ petitioner is dismissed.
No costs.
Petition dismissed.
