TS Doabia, J - (i) That petitioner submitted that he is a handicapped person.
(ii) He is a graduate of 1987.
(iii) He further submits that he was appointed as a teacher by District Education Officer (Poonch).
This appointment was made in Govt. High School Ghani Mendhar.
This happened in the month of May 1989.
(iv) The further fact is that the father of the petitioner died in November 1989.
This death took place after he got the Government job.
(v) That in case his present service tenure is brought to an end, then he cannot be appointed at any other place as he has become overage.
(vi) That the order of appointment in the present case has been passed by by the District Education Officer.
It is stated that the termination order can be passed by the appointing authority only.
Therefore, the order of the termination passed by Block Education Officer is said to be illegal.
2.
The further fact is that the operation of the order of termination was stayed by this Court.
The petitioner continues to be in service for the last more than eight years in pursuance of the order passed by this court.
3.
The order impugned has been passed by Block Education Officer.
He is not the appointing authority of the petitioner.
As such the order is bad on this score.
Order annexure P-G is thus set aside leaving the State to pass fresh order in accordance with law.
It is, however, made clear that if any adverse order is passed that be kept in abeyance for six weeks.
Disposed of accordingly.
