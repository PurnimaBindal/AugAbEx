THB Chalapathi, J - In the course we are adopting, we do not think it necessary to narrate the various contentions raised by the petitioner in this writ petition.
Suffice it to say that disciplinary proceedings were initiated against him by issuing charge-sheet dated March 18, 1993.
Even though he was placed under suspension with effect from December 30, 1992, he was reinstated in service by order dated October 22, 1993.
It is his case that the enquiry into the alleged misconduct is in progress.
Since the charge-sheet was served on the petitioner way back in March 1993, and enquiry has been ordered into the alleged misconduct, we direct the disciplinary authority to pass final order in the proceedings initiated against the petitioner under the Civil Services Rules as expeditiously as possible at any rate within three months from the date of receipt of the copy of this judgment.
While disposing of the proceedings, we direct the disciplinary authority to pass orders regarding the manner in which the period of suspension has to be treated, annual increments during the period of suspension and for the subsequent period after reinstatement as also proficiency step-up claimed by him in this writ petition is to be dealt with.
2.
Writ Petition is disposed of in the above directions.
Petitioner is directed to make available copies of this writ petition with its Annexures and judgment to the disciplinary authority for enabling him to pass the final order.
