RA Sharma, J - Petitioner, who is Tahsildar, has filed this writ petition, challenging the order dated 1971993 of his retirement from service on completion of 58 years of age.
Learned counsel for petitioner has made two submissions in support of writ petition, viz. (i) As substantive appointment of petitioner initially made was to the post of Patwari he has to retire at the age of 60 years, and (ii) in view of recommendations made by learned Single Judge of this court in Girja Prasad Singh v. State of UP and others, 1993(67) FLR 947 (SC), decided on 1271993 to the Government of UP for raising age of retirement of Class III employees from 58 to 60 years, the respondents ought not to have passed this order of retirement at the age of 58 years.
It is not possible to agree with the learned counsel.
2.
Accordingly to the averments made in the writ petition the petitioner was appointed as Patwari in 1953 and was regularised in 1955 as Lekhpal.
In 1973 he was promoted to the post of Naib Tahsildar and in 1991 he was promoted to the post of Tahsildar.
3.
The age of retirement, according to Rule 31 of Lekhpals Service Rules, 1958 in case of Lekhpal governed by conditions laid down in sub-para (3)(a), (b) and (c) of Paragraph 2 of Government Order dated 27.4.1953, is 60 years and in cases of other Lekhpals retirement age is 58 years.
Those Patwaris who were appointed before 27.4.1953 and had either not resigned or if resigned had withdrawn their resignations by 431953, fall within Clauses (a), (b) and (c) sub para (1) of Para 2 of the aforesaid Government Order.
It has not been pleaded by the petitioner that his case falls under any of the paragraphs of sub para (3) of para 2 of the Government Order dated 27.4.1953.
As such he has to retire even as Lekhpal at the age of 58 years.
That apart, he was promoted to the post of Naib Tahsildar in 1973 and this post is Class III post, the retirement age of which has been fixed as 58 years.
The retirement age of Tahsildar is also fixed at 58 years by rules.
The contention of the petitioner that he was promoted in 1973 to the post of Naib Tahsildar and thereafter to the post of Tahsildar in 1991 on temporary basis and as such his promotion to the post of Naib-Tahsildar and thereafter to the post of Tahsildar have to be ignored while considering the question of age of his retirement, cannot be accepted.
It is not possible to believe that the petitioner is continuously working right from 1973 as Naib Tahsildar that on temporary basis.
But presuming that it is so, that will not make any difference, because for all intends and purposes, as to salary and other benefits, there is no difference in a person promoted on officiating basis and one promoted on regular basis to the higher post.
The officiating service and pay or other emoluments drawn on such post counts towards pension and other post-retirement benefits.
This court in Om Parkash Sharma v. Zila Panchayat Raj Officer, WP No. 1229 of 1991 decided on 23111992, relevant extract from which is reproduced before, rejected the above contentions :  "Yet another factor negativing the claim of the petitioner to be entitled to superannuation on attaining 60 years of age prescribed for Group 'D' post is that they admittedly on the date of the notices for retirement were given and the petitions were filed, were holding the posts of A.D.O.
Panchayat.
The contention that the promotion will not ensure for determining the age of superannuation, being on officiating promotion is not tenable because for all intents and purposes as to salary and other benefits, there is no difference in a person promoted on officiating basis and one promoted on regular post to a higher post.
Officiating service and the pay and other emoluments drawn on such post will also count towards pension and other retirement benefits.
It would lend to an incongruous situation if the petitioners on one hand are allowed the retirement benefits of the post held in presenti and on the other are allowed the benefit of retirement age on the basis of inferior post to which they were appointed initially."
4.
The second submission is based on the decision of Learned Single Judge of this Court in Girja Prasad Singh v. State of UP and others, 1993(67) FLR 947 (SC), decided on 1271993, wherein recommendations have been made to the State Government for amending the rules for raising the age of retirement of Class III employees from 58 to 60 years.
This recommendation has not yet been accepted.
Till retirements of Group 'C' cannot claim retirement at the age of 60 years.
5.
This writ petition lacks merit and is accordingly dismissed.
Writ Petition dismissed.
