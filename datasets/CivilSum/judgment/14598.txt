R S Mongia, J - Briefly the facts giving rise to this petition are that the petitioner while working as Sub Divisional Engineer in the P.W.D., B&R Branch (Civil) was promoted as Executive Engineer along with some other Officers by the State Government vide order dated 3rd March, 1986 (Annexure P-2).
This promotion was on purely temporary basis initially for a period of six months or till the name of the petitioner was approved by the Screening Committee/Punjab Public Service Commission, for regular promotion.
The petitioner was reverted from the post of Executive Engineer to the post of Sub Divisional Engineer vide order dated 13th May, 1991 (Copy Annexure P-1).
The reason mentioned for reverting the petitioner in the order is that the Screening Committee Punjab Public Service Commission, Patiala have not approved the name of the petitioner for appointment to Punjab Services of Engineers, Class-I.
It is this order that has been impugned in the present writ petition.
While issuing notice of motion on 29th May, 1991 the Motion Bench had stayed the operation of the impugned order till further orders.
After written statement was filed on behalf of the respondents, the writ petition was admitted for regular hearing.
2.
The reasons for not approving the name of the petitioner for promotion to the rank of Executive Engineer by the Screening Committee/Punjab Public Service Commission as given in the written statement are; that the Screening Committee, constituted under the Statutory Rules to consider the cases of all the eligible P.S.E.
Class II Officers did not find the petitioner suitable for recommendation for promotion, keeping in view his bad service record.
It is further mentioned that the petitioner had been given two adverse reports for the year 1981-82 and 1985-86.
He had also been awarded punishment of censure on 25th July, 1978 and 26th April, 1988.
He had also been warned on 29th February 1980.
One regular inquiry (R.E.
7/88) was pending with the Vigilance Department.
He had also been charge-sheeted on 17th December, 1987.
In view of these inquiries and bad record, he had not been found fit for promotion.
3.
The learned counsel for the petitioner on the other hand submitted that the petitioner had been permitted to cross the efficiency bar vide order dated 13th December, 1984 and was further allowed to cross the second efficiency bar vide order dated 15th February, 1985.
Further it was submitted that the representations against the adverse remarks dated 9th August, 1983 and 13th December, 1984, had not been decided till the date of filing of the writ petition.
The learned counsel went on to submit that as per the written statement, the petitioner was not recommended for promotion because of the following factors :-  (i) Adverse remarks for the years 1981-82 and 1985-86.
(ii) Censure on 25.7.1978.
(iii) Warning on 2921980.
(iv) Censure on 2641988.
(v) Regular Inquiry No. 7/88 Pending with the Vigilance Department.
(vi) Charge-sheet on 17121987.
By way of additional affidavit the petitioner averred that the report for the period from 27th May, 1981 to 15 January, 1982 was graded as 'below average and the petitioner was conveyed the report as follows :-  "You have been graded as a below average Officer."
It was submitted that the representation against the same was rejected by one word 'rejected'.
From April, 1985 to December, 1985, the report is 'very good'.
However, for the period from 1st January, 1986 to 31st March, 1986, the petitioner was conveyed the following remarks :-  "He did not pay proper attention to work as he was running around for promotion during the period under report".
4.
It has been stated in the additional affidavit that vide order dated 23rd April, 1992, the representation against the above-said remarks was accepted and the remarks were expunged.
Translated copy of the order has been attached as Annexure P-8.
It has further been averred that no punishment of censure was awarded to the petitioner on 25th July, 1978 and therefore, no such order conveyed to him.
The punishment of censure awarded on 26th April, 1988 was for the period 1974-75.
5.
The learned counsel for the petitioner also submitted that under the Statutory Rules i.e. the Punjab Service of Engineers Class I, P.W.D.
(Building and Roads Branch) Rules, 1960 (hereinafter called the Rules) the Screening Committee is required to meet at intervals ordinarily not exceeding one year and consider the cases of all eligible officers for promotion to the senior scale of the service, as on the first day of January of that year.
However, the Screening Committee in the present case met in the year 1989 for considering the cases of P.S.E.
Class-II of P.W.D.
Branch for promotion to P.S.E.
Class-I for the years 1980 to 1989.
The petitioner's case for promotion, which was considered for the year 1986, should have been on the basis of the record upto 1st January, 1986 and if the petitioner was not found suitable then for the subsequent years, record upto 1st January, 1987, 1st January, 1988 and so on, should have been considered.
Further the petitioner's record of service as Class I Officer after his promotion with effect from 3rd March, 1986, should have been considered while considering this case for promotion for the years 1987, 1988 and 1989.
For this proposition the learned counsel relied upon a judgment of the Supreme Court in Gurjit Singh Sahota v. The State of Punjab and another, 1975(2) S.L.R.
516.
6.
On the other hand, the respondents' counsel submitted that the petitioner's case for promotion had been duly considered by the Screening Committee and on the basis of the record, he was not found suitable for promotion.
7.
I had sent for the original record, containing the proceedings of the Screening Committee, which had considered the cases of promotion of P.S.E.
Class-II Officers.
While rejecting the case of the petitioner for promotion in the year 1986, the Screening Committee observed as under :-  "There are two adverse reports pertaining to the year 1981-82 and 1985- 86.
He has been awarded punishment of Censure on 25.7.1978.
Another punishment of Censure was awarded on 26488.
The warning was also issued to him on 2921980.
After hearing him and considering the record, the Committee decided not to approve him."
As far as the rejection of the claim of the petitioner for promotion for the years 1987, 1988 and 1989, is concerned, it has been mentioned that for the reasons recorded for not approving the name of the petitioner for 1986, the petitioner was not being found suitable for promotion for all these years.
8.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, and perusing the record of the case, as also taking into consideration the additional affidavit filed by the petitioner, I am of the view that the consideration of the case of the petitioner for promotion to the rank of Executive Engineer was not in accordance with law.
For considering the case of the petitioner for the year 1986, his record upto 1st January, 1986 had only to be taken into consideration as per Rule 8(3) of the Rules.
From the reasons recorded for not finding the petitioner suitable for promotion in the year 1986, it is clear that the record even after 1986 it is clear that the record even after 1986 it is clear that consideration.
In fact, as per the averments made in the written statement, pendency of Enquiry No. 7/88 with the Vigilance Department and charge-sheet, dated 17th December, 1987 were also taken into consideration.
Further it has now been brought to my notice by way of additional affidavit that the adverse remarks for the year 1985-86 (111986 to 3131986) stand expunged vide order dated 23rd April, 1992.
As far as the award of Censure dated 25th August 1978, is concerned, there is specific affidavit of the petitioner that there was no such order of punishment.
An affidavit has been filed on 24th November, 1993 by Shri S S Dhillon, Joint Secretary to Government Punjab, Department of Public Works (B&R) in which it has been stated as under :-  "It is submitted that the fact regarding punishment of 'Censure' awarded to the petitioner on 25th July, 1978 as stated in the written statement filed on 8th August, 1991 in the Hon'ble Court by Sh. Satish Chandra, the then joint Secretary to Government, Punjab Public Works (B&R) Department are based on the proceedings of the Screening Committee.
A copy of the same is enclosed (R-1).
The original record pertaining to orders of 'Censure' is old one.
Efforts are being made to trace the same, as the same is not readily available."
9.
In this view of the matter, it is not clear as to on what basis the Screening Committee had taken this fact into consideration.
According to the learned counsel, the petitioner's case for the year 1986, requires reconsideration by taking into account only record upto 1st January, 1986 and also the fact that the adverse remarks for the year 1985-86 stand expunged and that there is no order on the record to show that the petitioner was awarded punishment of Censure on 25th August, 1978.
The entire record, good and bad has to be weighed by the Screening Committee/Punjab Public Service Commission.
The crossing of the efficiency bar does not obliterate the previous adverse remarks, but it is one of the events in the employee's service that has to be taken into consideration.
10.
Even if the petitioner was to be found not suitable in the year 1986, then for the subsequent years his work as Executive Engineer, to which post he was promoted on 3rd March, 1986 was also to be taken into consideration as laid down by the Apex Court in Gurjit Singh Sahota's case (supra) wherein it was observed as under :-  "We think that the grievance of the appellant to the extent that his record of service in the Class I post should have been considered when his juniors were promoted to Class I posts is legitimate.
It is true that when the appellant was promoted to Class I post on ad hoc basis, his record of service in Class II post did not justify his promotion and that was the reason why, he was ordered to be reverted.
But that is reason why, when his juniors were subsequently promoted to Class I posts, the case of the appellant for promotion to Class I post should not have been considered on the basis of his service in the Class I post, though his ad hoc promotion to that post was subsequently found to be not justified.
If on the basis of the subsequent record of his service, the appellant was entitled to be promoted to Class I post in preference to any one of his four juniors there was no justification for the order reverting him to Class II service."
When the cases of the petitioner's juniors were considered for promotion in the years 1987, 1988 and 1989, the record of the petitioner as Executive Engineer, to which post he was promoted on temporary basis on 3rd March, 1986, had to be taken into consideration.
From the proceedings of the Screening Committee, there is no indication that the record of the petitioner as Executive Engineer after promotion on 3rd March, 1986 was taken consideration.
11.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed and the impugned order of reversion of the petitioner dated 13th May, 1991 (Annexure P-1) so far as it relates to the petitioner, is quashed.
The respondents are directed to consider the case of the petitioner afresh for promotion keeping in view the observations made above I make no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
