SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - The petitioner joined the Education Department on 681964.
He acquired higher qualification on 19.7.1968.
He was promoted to the post of Headmaster on 5.9.1979 and again to the post of Principal on 1921991.
On the basis of the notification dated 18101979, pay of the petitioner was revised w.e.f.
111978.
One Subhash Chander, who was junior to the petitioner, was granted Rs. 800/- w.e.f.
111978, whereas the pay of the petitioner was fixed at Rs. 750/-.
The petitioner, ultimately filed CWP No. 16777 of 1998.
The petition was allowed.
The respondents were directed to fix the pay of the petitioner at par with Subhash Chander.
The pay of the petitioner was ultimately fixed equal to subhash Chander.
Subsequently, the respondents have come to the conclusion that the pay of Subhash Chander had been wrongly fixed.
Now the respondents have re-fixed the pay of the petitioner equal to Subhash Chander and have ordered recovery of the difference, vide order dated 1372001, Annexure P-5.
2.
The petitioner came to this Court on a bona fide claim that his pay could not be fixed less than his junior.
This plea of the petitioner was accepted by this Court in CWP No. 16777 of 1996 and the respondents were directed to fix the party of the petitioner at par with Subhash Chander.
At no stage, the petitioner made any misrepresentation or committed any fraud on the respondents.
The amount paid to the petitioner on account of mistake committed by the respondents can not now be permitted to be recovered from the pay of the petitioner.
A perusal of order, Annexure P-5, shows that it is merely an order of fixation of pay of the petitioner at appropriate stage in accordance with the rules.
Consequently, impugned order, Annexure P-5, does not suffer from any illegal infirmity.
Respondents are, however, directed, not to recover the difference, in the salary already paid to the petitioner.
The petition stands disposed of.
Order accordingly.
