VK Bali, J (Oral) - Ex. Capt.
Gurdev Singh, who is presently posted as Sub-Divisional Engineer (Mechanical) in the office of Engineer-in-Chief P.W.D.
Public Health Branch, Haryana, Chandigarh, through present petition filed by him under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, seeks a writ in the nature of certiorari so as to quash order dated 6.10.90, Annexure P-3 by which his representation permitting him to cross two efficiency bars, with effect from 2311973 and from 141980 was rejected.
He further seeks a direction to the respondents to allow him to cross the efficiency bar and fixation of his pay in accordance therewith.
The facts from which the relief as asked for stems, need to be noticed.
2-3.
The petitioner remained in military service from July 1963 to September 1, 1968 and after his release from the Army, he joined the post of Principal in Industrial Training Department, Haryana from 15.7.1970 and remained in this Department upto 151974.
Thereafter, he joined the Department of PWD Public Health Branch on the post of Sub-Divisional Engineer (Mechanical) as a direct recruit against the reserve quota meant for ex-servicemen.
He was granted the benefit of military service vide orders dated 171988 and was assigned the deemed date of appointment as 7.11.1969 as the service that he had rendered in the Industrial Training Department was also taken into consideration.
The petitioner was also assigned deemed date of appointment as 23.1.1966.
He was to cross the efficiency bar if for the five years preceding the date on which he was due to cross the efficiency bar his service record was not poor.
While assessing the service record, remarks in his confidential report with regard to efficiency, honesty and integrity etc. were also to be taken into consideration.
The case of the petitioner is that he was due to cross the efficiency bar w.e.f.
2311973 but was not allowed to do so on the ground that he was not found fit as his service record was not good.
The matter was again reviewed and once again he was not found fit from the next date of consideration, that is, 2311974.
Petitioner after receipt of the letter not permitting him to cross the efficiency bar made a representation to the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Industrial Training Department in which besides other grounds he also pleaded that no adverse report was ever communicated to him since he left the office of Industrial Training Institute.
He also pleaded that he was being regularly granted his annual grade increments and thus there was no question of his service record being bad.
The Commissioner, however, vide his order dated 16101990, Annexure P/3, rejected the representation, which as indicated in the earlier part of the judgment has been challenged by the petitioner.
4.
The petitioner was conveyed adverse remarks contained in the confidential report for the years 1976-77, 1977-78, and 1979-80.
The positive case of the petitioner is that he made separate representations against all the reports and vide order, Annexure P/5, while considering the representation against the adverse reports for the year 1976-77 and 1977-78 the Commissioner held that the so-called adverse remarks were only of advisory nature and that being so his representation was filed.
In so far as adverse remarks contained in the confidential report for the year 1979-80 are concerned, the same, as per the case of the petitioner were expunged.
The learned counsel appearing for the petitioner, on the strength of the fact as narrated above, contends that in the entire service record of the petitioner there was no blot and remarks contained in the confidential reports of the years 1976-77, 1977-78 and 1979- 80 could not possibly be considered for not permitting him to cross the efficiency bar.
5.
This petition has been contested and in the written statement filed on behalf of a respondent Nos. 1 and 2 preliminary objection has been raised that the petitioner joined as Sub-Divisional Engineer (Mechanical) on 251974 in the scale of Rs. 400-1100 which scale was further revised to Rs. 900-1700 with effect from 1.4.1979 and his pay was fixed at Rs. 1060/- vide order dated 221981.
The average reports carrying adverse remarks for the year 1976-77, 1977-78 and 1979-80 were conveyed to him on 14.6.1982 and as the record of the petitioner for the relevant years was one of general bad work, he was stopped at the efficiency bar vide orders dated 1221987.
Such an action is strictly in accordance with the government instructions dated 29.1.1974 read with Rule 4.3 of Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I.
Even on merits, the claim of the petitioner has been opposed on the same very grounds.
6.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and going through the records, I am of the considered opinion that the points raised by the learned counsel for the petitioner have merit and therefore this petition must succeed.
7.
Order, Annexure P/5, vide which the representation of the petitioner against adverse reports for the year 1976-77 and 1977-78 was dealt with reads as under :-  "After careful examination of the facts, the Government have decided that the representations of Sh. Gurdev Singh, S.D.E.
(Mech.)
in respect of his AC Rs. for the year 1976-77 and 1977-78 be filed because the adverse remarks in question are the advisory nature."
It is thus amply proved that the petitioner did not file representation against the adverse remarks for the year 1976-77, and 1977-78 and whatever remarks were contained therein were advisory in nature and for that reason alone his representation was filed.
In so far as the remarks contained in the confidential report for the year 1979-80 are concerned, concededly the representation filed by the petitioner against such remarks has been accepted and the remarks have been expunged.
8.
Mr. Suresh Monga, learned Deputy Advocate General, Haryana, appearing on behalf of the State, however, still contends that if the remarks contained in the reports for the years 1976-77 and 1977-78 are examined it would be clear that the petitioner was not efficient enough so as to be permitted to cross the efficiency bar.
He further contends that if overall record of petitioner is considered, even then petitioner could be stopped to cross the efficiency bar as the same is average.
The points canvassed by the State counsel, however, in my opinion, have no merit.
The remarks contained in the confidential reports for the years 1976-77 and 1977-78 cannot be read now to deem the same as adverse.
The appropriate authority while dealing with the representations of petitioner clearly opined that the same were of advisory nature and for that reason alone his representations were filed.
This opinion given by the competent authority cannot now be read so as to construe the remarks in the confidential reports mentioned above and in any case the same cannot be termed as adverse.
In so far as the overall record of petitioner is concerned, the positive case of petitioner is that the same was always good and at no stage throughout his career he was ever characterised as inefficient, incompetent or a person of doubtful integrity.
Mr. Monga without refuting the aforesaid assertion as also without showing any other report of petitioner, cannot urge that the overall record of petitioner is average.
For the reasons stated above, this writ petition is allowed and the order, Annexure P/3, is quashed and a direction is issued to the respondents to allow the petitioner to cross the efficiency bar when he was due to cross it, that is from 2311973 when he was in the department of Industrial Training and also from 141980.
Obviously, petitioner would be entitled to all the consequential benefit that he may be entitled to as a result of the setting aside of the order, Annexure P/3.
Let this exercise be done within a period of three months from today.
There shall, however, be no order as to costs.
