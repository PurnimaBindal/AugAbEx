RS Mongia, J - Respondent No. 2, Shri Raj Pal Singh, was appointed as a Lecturer in Physics in the Arya College, Panipat on September 11, 1984.
The Managing Committee on the College (petitioner herein) terminated his services of July 31, 1986.
The matter was taken up before the Director, Higher Education, Haryana (in short `D.H.E.
(H)') under the Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979.
He came to the conclusion that the services of respondent No. 2 had been wrongly terminated and ordered his re- instatement vide order April 20, 1987 (copy Annexure P-9).
The Managing Committee of the College took the matter in appeal before the Secretary to the Government of Haryana, Education Department, who dismissed the appeal on September 11, 1987 (copy of order Annexure P-11).
This writ petition was filed by the Managing Committee challenging the aforesaid orders of D.H.E.
(H) (Annexure P-9) as well as the appellate order dated September 11, 1987.
2.
It may be observed here that respondent No. 2 Shri Raj Pal Singh got appointment as a Lecturer in Physics w.e.f.
August 1, 1987, in Daya Nand College Hisar, i.e., after one year of the termination of his services by the petitioner on July 31, 1986.
The impugned orders as aforesaid have been challenged by the Managing Committee on various grounds in this writ petition.
However, during the course of arguments learned counsel for the petitioner suggested that since respondent No. 2 has been appointed in Daya Nand College, Hisar w.e.f.
August 1, 1987, and he does not wish to come back to Arya College, Panipat, the Managing Committee of the College would withdraw the order of termination dated July 31, 1986.
An offer was made by the petitioner-Managing Committee to respondent No. 2 that for the period August 1, 1986, to July 31, 1987, 50% wages may be accepted and the Managing Committee shall not claim any grant-in-aid from the State Government towards the arrears of wages of respondent No. 2 w.e.f.
August 1, 1986, to July 31, 1987.
Resultantly respondent No. 2 would be deemed to have worked in Arya College, Panipat upto July 31, 1987.
After getting instructions, the counsel for respondent No. 2 has stated that the offer made by the petitioner is acceptable to respondent No. 2.
A photo copy of the order dated August 6, 1996, issued by the General Secretary of the Arya College Managing Committee, Panipat, has been placed on record in which it is mentioned that in the peculiar facts and circumstances of the case and to end the litigation and as a gesture of good will, especially under the circumstances that respondent Raj Pal had joined some other college, therefore, in anticipation of the approval by the Managing Committee of the College the order dated July 31, 1986, is withdrawn and a sum of Rs. 14520/- towards 50% of the wages, allowance etc, for the period August 1, 1986, to July 31, 1987, be paid to respondent No. 2.
A cheque representing the aforesaid amount in the name of respondent No. 2 Rajpal Singh has been given to this counsel which he has accepted in full and final settlement of his dues from the village.
3.
In view of the facts and circumstances mentioned above, this writ petition had been rendered infructuous and the same is disposed of as such.
The learned counsel for the petitioner states at the bar that the approval regarding the order dated 681996 issued by the General Secretary would be given in the ensuing meeting of the Managing Committee and if for some reason, it cannot be take up in that meeting, it will be done in the subsequent meeting.
4.
As a matter of abundant caution it may be observed that if for any reason, the approval to the order of the General Secretary dated August 6, 1996, is not given by the Managing Committee, the parties would be at liberty to get this writ petition revived.
5.
It would be open to respondent No. 2 to make a necessary representation to the authorities concerned for claiming the benefit of continuity of his service w.e.f.
September 11, 1984, which would decide the same in accordance with law.
Let the service book of respondent No. 2 duly completed be handed over by the petitioner to respondent No. 2 and in case the original service book cannot handed over to him, attested photo copy of the same be given to respondent No. 2.
