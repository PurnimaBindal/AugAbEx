Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - Each of the petitioners on the facts and circumstances of this case has an individual cause of action and will be entitled to an individual fee.
Therefore, this petition cannot be entertained on one court fee.
The application for exemption from filing the individual court fee is dismissed.
The petitioners shall pay the court fee within one week from today.
Civil Writ Petition No. 1125 of 2002  2.
The petitioners in this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India claim the grant of additional increments with all consequential benefits on their completion of 8 and 18 years of service respectively in their service cadres.
They also pray that work charge/temporary service, which is uninterrupted, is to be counted for regularisation.
3.
Granting the benefit of the additional increments fall in the domain of the administration.
The petitioners, in accordance with rules, have served a notice upon the respondents, copy of which is annexed with the petition as Annexure P-7 dated July 30, 2001.
Till today, the respondents have not reacted to the said notice in any manner whatsoever compelling the petitioners to file the present writ petition.
In the facts and circumstances of the case and the service records of each of the petitioners, which will have to be looked into, we direct the respondents to pass appropriate speaking order in accordance with law, upon notice annexure P-7 dated July 30, 2001, within a period of two months from the date a certified copy of this order is brought to their notice.
The order so passed shall be communicated to the petitioners.
4.
The application and the writ petition are disposed of.
5.
Copy of this order be given dasti to the petitioners only after the Court fee has been paid.
Petition disposed of.
