RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The petitioner was appointed as a teacher in the joint Punjab on 1511965 and on reorganisation of the State of Punjab, he was allocated to the State of Haryana on 1111966.
On 841991, Director of Secondary Education, Haryana, Chandigarh issued a notice in accordance with the provisions contained in Rule 326 of Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume I Part I read with Rules 5.32-A(c) of the Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II (as applicable to the State of Haryana) and note thereunder that it had been decided to retire the petitioner from service after attaining the age of 55 years in public interest.
It was mentioned in this notice that petitioner would retire on expiry of three months of the said notice.
It is this notice, copy of which has been attached as Annexure P.1 to the writ petition, that has been impugned in the present writ petition.
2.
The Date of Birth of the petitioner is 16th November, 1935 and in normal circumstances he would retire on 30th November, 1993 on attaining the age of 58 years.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the impugned action of pre-maturely retiring the petitioner has been taken on the basis of the Haryana Government Instructions dated 16th August, 1983 according, to which only such Government employees were to be retained in service after the age of 55 years if they had earned 70% good or better category of reports for the last ten years.
The learned counsel further submits that these instructions have been specifically struck down by the Division Bench of this Court in KK Vaid v. State of Haryana, 1990(1) SLR 1.
He has further submitted that according to KK Vaid's case (supra) the average report cannot be considered as adverse to an employee and in fact average means not so good and not so bad.
According to Rule of Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume I Part I read with Rule 5.32-A(C) of Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II, only those persons are to be retired who have really become a dead wood and not those persons who may be just average.
3.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondent, it has been admitted that the action of pre-maturely retiring the petitioner has been taken in accordance with the instructions of the Haryana Government dated 1681983 and since the petitioner's record for the last 10 years was not good or above to the extent of 70% the petitioner had to be retired on attaining the age of 55 years.

4.
The learned counsel for the petitioner is right in submitting that respondents could not take impugned action on the basis of the instructions dated 16-8-1983 as the same had been struck down by this Court in KK Vaid's case (supra) which judgment was delivered on 1-11-1989 Further According to this very judgment, the average report is not to be considered as adverse.
Moreover, I find from the record that the petitioner was showing improvement as from an average report of 1987-88, he had earned good report in 1988-89 and Outstanding report in 1989-90.
It would be rather too harsh on an employee to pre-maturely retire him if he has started showing improvement in the last few years before the impugned action is taken.
Since, the action has been taken by the respondents on the basis of the instructions which already stood struck down, the impugned action cannot be allowed to stand.
Apart from that I have already given reasons that action of the respondents is not sound in law.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed and the impugned order/notice of prematurely retiring the petitioner dated 8-4-1991 Annexure P.1) is here by quashed.
Since the action was taken by the respondents on the basis of the instructions dated 1681983, which already stood struck down in KK Vaid's case (supra), I am of the opinion that petitioner should be entitled to costs as well, which are quantified as Rs. 1,000/-.
Petition allowed
