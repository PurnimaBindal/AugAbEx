DV, Sehgal, President.
- The petitioners were working as Social Study Masters in Senior Secondary School, Wadala Granthian, Distt.
Gurdaspur, in the State of Punjab.
They were getting the scale of Rs. 100-250/- vide letter dated 341969.
The grade was revised to Rs. 220-500/- from 1111966.
It is the case of the petitioners that without any justification, their scale of pay of Rs. 110-250/- from September 1994 was reduced to basic pay of Rs. 80/- without affording any opportunity.
The petitioners have enclosed with the writ petition two judgments of this Court in Ajit Singh Kahlon v. State of Punjab, CWP No. 6152 of 1993 decided on 18.3.1994 and Kundan Singh v. State of Punjab, CWP No. 6995 of 1992 decided on 18.3.1994, where orders were passed by this Court in similar cases of Social Studies Masters that the reduction effected in their pay should be restored and the pay re-fixed after affording proper opportunity.
In Kundan Singh's case, the order so passed by this Court was made on a concession by the counsel for the State of Punjab.
2.
No doubt there is no representation on behalf of the respondent State of Punjab today.
We, however, feel that the matter does not brook any further delay for the reason that all the three petitioners retired from service on 31121994, 31121995 and 3181996 respectively and re-fixation of their pension and revision of other retiral benefits is pending for want of decision of this writ petition.
3.
We, therefore, direct that the reduction in the pay scale of the petitioners from Rs. 110-250/- to Rs. 80/- shall be undone and their pay shall be re-fixed after affording proper opportunity to the petitioners.
On re- fixation of their pay, their salary in the subsequent revised scale shall be re-fixed till the date of their respective retirement.
Their pension, commuted amount of pension, DCR gratuity and amount of unavailed of earned leave, if any, shall also be revised and sanctioned and paid to the petitioners within a period of four months from today.
It is made clear that payment of the difference of arrears which thus accrues due to the petitioners should be made to them within a period of six months from today, failing which the amount of arrears shall carry interest @ 18% P.A.
with effect from 1102000 till the date of actual payment.
4.
The above directions shall be meticulously complied with and shall constitute a decree under Section 21 of the Legal Services Authority Act, 1987.
5.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
A copy of this order be given to both the parties within seven days free of cost.
Petition allowed.
