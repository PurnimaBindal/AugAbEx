VK Bali, J - Petitioner, Brij Mohan Maini, who was employed with the Punjab State Federation of Co-operative House Building Societies Ltd, as Junior Accountant, seeks revision of pay scale for the period he was in the employment of the respondent - Federation through this writ petition filed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
2.
Brief facts reveal that he joined the service of respondent-Federation as Junior Accountant on February 19, 1979 and he was placed in the pay scale of Rs. 160-10-280/15-400 plus other allowances as admissible to Punjab Government employees at the station of his posting.
He resigned from the service and his resignation was accordingly accepted with effect from January 14, 1980.
However, vide resolution No. 7012 dated January 8, 1981 passed by the Housefed, the pay scale of Junior Accountants was revised and the same was made effective from January 1, 1978.
Petitioner, in the wake of the resolution aforesaid claims arrears of pay from February 19,1979 the date of his appointment, till the date he had submitted his resignation and was relieved of his duties i.e. January 14, 1980.
This was declined vide Annexure P-3 by observing that the resolution (Annexure P-2) dated January 8, 1981 would cover the employees of the Housefed who were in service as on January 8, 1981.
Being aggrieved of this order, petitioner approached the Labour Court through reference made by the Government under Section 18(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes, Act, 1947.
The reference was declined by the Labour Court vide order dated November 19, 1984 (Annexure P-7) The Labour Court in the award expressed its opinion against the petitioner negativing his claim on the ground that the pay and allowances of the employees are regulated by Rule 31 of the Punjab State Co-operative Housing Federation Service Rules, 1976 and in terms thereof the scale of pay of various categories of employees are to be as specified in Annexure -1 thereof.
Inasmuch as the said pay scale could be revised either by the amendment of the said Rules or by resolution of the Board of Directors of respondent-Federation, the Board of Directors vide its resolution dated January 8, 1981 decided to revise the pay scale of its employees with effect from January 1, 1978 but the decision was modified in subsequent resolution Ex. W-6 dated May 28, 1981.
This Ex. W-6 is the same as Annexure P-3 with the present petition.
3.
The claim of the petitioner has been opposed and the grounds taken in the written statement with a view to oppose the claim of petitioner are the same which were put forth before the Labour Court and mention of which has been made above.
4.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties and going through the records and the case, I am of the considered view that resolution (Annexure P-3) and the award of the Labour Court (Annexure P-7) deserved to be set aside.
While dealing with a similar matter, I had held in Parveen Jindal and Ors v. The Registrar, Cooperative Societies, Punjab, 1992(1) RSJ 43 : 1994(4) S.C.T.
489, that the persons, who had remained in service during the period with effect from which the scale was revised, would be entitled to revision of their pay scales even if they had resigned or left the job prior to the date, the order revising the pay scales was passed.
While so deciding, I had relied upon a Division Bench judgment of this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 13981 of 1990, decided on January 21, 1991, Vivek Madan v. Punjab Agro Industries Cooperation.
Learned counsel appearing for the petitioner has submitted that this judgment has been upheld by the Division Bench in a Letters Patent Appeal which was filed against the said judgment and also by the Supreme Court.
However, the Supreme Court reduced the rate of interest from 18% to 12%.
The present case is squarely covered by the judgment aforesaid and, therefore, this writ petition has to be allowed.
5.
In view of what has been stated above, a direction is issued to the respondent-Federation to pay to the petitioner the arrears of pay by giving him benefit of revision of pay scale that was revised with effect from January 1, 1978.
To be precise, petitioner shall be entitled to the difference of pay that he was getting and that which was revised from February 19, 1979, the date when he joined service to January 14, 1980, the date when his resignation was accepted and he was relieved.
The direction given in this order be complied with within a period of one month from today.
6.
However, there shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
