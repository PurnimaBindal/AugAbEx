Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - The petitioner was a candidate for the post of Tehsil Welfare Officer in the Department of Social Welfare.
He competed and was placed at No. 13 in order of merit.
He has not been given an appointment on account of non-availability of a post either in the category of Scheduled Castes or in the general category.
The learned counsel for the petitioner has pointed out to me the order passed by the Division in C.W.P.
No. 9006 of 1990 decided on 15-11-90 in which their Lordships have been pleased to give the following direction :-  "According to the return vacancies are not available for adjusting the petitioner and other persons who were selected.
As and when the vacancies arise or the stay orders with respect to the ad hoc teachers are vacated the petitioner and other similarly situated persons will be given appointments in order of merit prepared at the time of selection.
With this order and direction the writ petition stands disposed of"  He states that he would be satisfied if a similar direction is given in the present case.
2.
It is accordingly directed that as and when a vacancy in the general category becomes available, the persons mentioned in the letter at Annexure R-2 with the return of the respondents No. 4 shall be considered in order of merit and if the petitioner's turn comes, he will be given the necessary order of appointment.
3.
With this direction, the writ petition is dissmised of.
Order accordingly.
