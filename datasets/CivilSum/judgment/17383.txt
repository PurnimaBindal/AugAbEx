SS Sudhalkar, J (Oral) - The petitioners were working as drivers on daily wage basis in the office of respondent No. 3 with effect from 5686, 5781 and 10101987 respectively.
They were regularised by the order dated 711994 (copy annexure P/1) with effect from 26111993.
However, they were regularised in group `D' scale meant for class IV employees.
Vide order dated 16101996 (copy annexure P/2) two persons viz. Sita Ram and Avtar Singh were regularised in Group `C' scale.
The petitioners contended that these two persons were junior to them.
The petitioners represented that their juniors were placed in group `C' scale while they have been placed in group `D' scale.
Vide order dated 1711997 (copy annexure P/3) the petitionerss were regularised as group `C employees instead of group `D' with effect from the existence of the vacancies.
However, vide letter dated 21797, the petitioners were informed that it was decided to withdraw the scale given to them vide letter dated 1711997 (copy annexure P/3) and they were required to show cause within 15 days as to why the scale given to them may not be withdrawn.
Copy of one such letter addressed to petitioner No. 1 is at annexure :P/4.
Petitioners replied to the letter vide letter at annexure P/5.
By letter dated 13111997 the pay scale of the petitioners of group `C' was withdrawn stating that it was inadvertently granted.
Copy of one such letter dated 13111997 addressed to petitioner No. 1 is at annexure P/6.
The petitioners contend that no reason has been shown for withdrawal of group `C' scale which was granted to them and they also contend that persons junior to them are still enjoying the said scale with effect from much earlier date.
The petitioners have prayed that they be placed in group `C' scale with effect from the date their juniors were placed in such scale and that all consequential benefits be given to them.
2.
Respondent No. 3 has filed the written statement on behalf of all the respondents.
It is the case of the respondents that as per Government policy dated 2751993 the services of daily wagers were to be regularised with effect from 3131993 in the lowest pay scale of Rs. 750-940 on group `D' post.
Accordingly, the service of the petitioner was so regularised.
It has further been mentioned in paragraph 2 of the preliminary objection as follows:  "As per Govt. notifications dated 11594, services of those daily wagers were to be regularised against Class III posts on compassionate ground like other daily wagers where posts were not available.
Since services of the petitioners were regularised and posts were created in the pay scale of Rs. 750-940 as per Govt. instructions and as such they are entitled for grant of pay scale of Rs.
1200-2040."
3.
Learned counsel for the petitioners argued that the respondents are totally misreading their own instructions.
For appreciating the contention of learned counsel for the petitioners, let us notice the instructions dated 2751993 annexure P/7 (relevant extract) is as under :  "The matter regarding the regularisation of Work-charged/Casual/Daily rated employees was engaging the attention of the Government for some time past.
After careful consideration it has now been decided that the services of the employees of those categories should be regularised as under :- xxxxxxx xxxxxxx Casual/Daily rated employees The Casual and Daily rated employees who have completed 5 years' service on 31st March, 1993 and were in service on 31st March, 1993, shall be regularised.
On regularisation, they shall be put in the time scale of pay applicable to the lowest Group `D' cadre in the driver and they would be entitled to all other allowances and benefits available to regular Govt. servants of the corresponding grade."
4.
These instructions were followed by another set of instructions dated May 11, 1994 which clarified the earlier instructions.
The relevant portion is reproduced below :  "No. GSR/39/Const/Art/309/94- In exercise of the powers conferred, by the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution of India read with the proviso to Clause 6 of Haryana Government, General Administration Department (General Services) Notification No. 523-3-GS-70/2068 dated the 28th January, 1970 and all other powers enabling him in this behalf and in supersession of Haryana Government General Administration (General Services-I) Notification No. GSR/31/Const/Art./209/93 dated the 1st June, 1993 the Governor of Haryana hereby specifies such Class-III posts as have been held for a minimum period of two or five years on the 31st March, 1993, by Class III employees on ad hoc or daily wages basis to be taken out of the purview of the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana and their services shall be regularised if they fulfil the following conditions, namely :- xxxxxxxx xxxxxxxx xxxxxxxx xxxxxxxx 2.
Daily rated employees.
(1):- The Government had issued instructions dated 2751993, for regularisation of services of all the work-charged casual and daily wager employees who had completed 5 years service on 3131993.
In the reference of these instructions the regularised employees were entitled to the lowest Group `D' scale of pay and all other allowances and benefits available to regular Government employees.
But it has come to the notice of Government in some departments daily wagers also working on Class III posts such as Clerks, Steno-typists and drivers etc.
The question whether such daily wagers should be regularised in Group `D' or Group `C' scale has been engaging the attention of the Government for some time past and after careful consideration, it has now been decided that daily wagers who had completed 5 years service on Class-III posts on 31st March, 1993 and were in service on 31st March, 1993 should be regularised against their respective Class-III posts provided they fulfil the requisite qualifications and were originally appointed on Class-III posts and the posts are available.
If the posts are not available, they should be got created from the Finance Department or they should be regularised in group `D' scale on compassionate ground like other daily wagers."
5.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submitted that the petitioners were working as drivers on daily wages which is a Class-III post and their case is covered by the instructions dated 11594, relevant extract of which has already been reproduced above.
According to the counsel for the petitioners, they were entitled to be regularised on Class III posts if the posts were available and if these were not available they should be got created from the Finance Department as it is on the failure of both these eventualities that such persons are to be regularised on compassionate ground in group `D' scale.
Learned counsel cited two Division Bench judgments of this Court in CWP No. 13808 of 1995 (Miss Sunita Dua v. State of Haryana and another) decided on 15996 and our judgment in Suresh Kumar v. State of Haryana and others (C.W.P.
No. 17189 of 1997) decided on May 20, 1998 : 1996(1) SCT 420 (P&H).
These judgments too support the contention of learned counsel for the petitioners.
Learned counsel for the respondent was not able to distinguish on law or fact the aforesaid two authorities.
6.
It would be seen from the reading of the instructions dated 1151994 that a daily wager who has worked as such for five years against a Class III post has to be regularised in Class III post subject to availability of the post.
In case the post is not available, the Finance Department is to be approached for creating a post failing which the person may be regularised in Group `D' post i.e. Class IV post.
Both in Sunita Dua's and Suresh Kumar's cases (supra), the petitioners were working as Typists on daily wages which is a Class III post.
Since they had completed five years of service as such it was held that they were entitled to be regularised in Class III post.
We do not find that the petitioners were wrongly or inadvertently regularised earlier in Class III post.
It has not been spelled out as to what was that inadvertence.
It is not the case of the respondents that no Class II post was available or the Finance Department when asked refused to create any post.
Consequently, we find that the action of the respondent in withdrawing the regularisation of the petitioners against Class III post is against the instructions.
7.
For the foregoing reasons, we allow this writ petition, quash the order of withdrawal of regularisation dated 13111997 annexure P/6 qua petitioner No. 1 and similar orders passed qua the other petitioners.
Petition allowed.
