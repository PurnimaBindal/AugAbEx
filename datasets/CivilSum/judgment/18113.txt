Hemant Gupta, J - The petitioner is aggrieved against the communication dated 13.4.2007, Annexure P-9, whereby the claim of the petitioner for appointment on compassionate ground has been declined.
2.
The father of the petitioner, Shri Krishan Kumar, working as Assistant Line Man with the respondents, died on 1522002.
The mother of the petitioner namely Angoori Devi earlier died on 5.12.1995.
The petitioner and her younger sister Pooja are the surviving heirs of their parents.
3.
The grand father of the petitioner, as guardian of the petitioner, sought appointment of the petitioner on compassionate ground vide application dated 892003.
At the time, the petitioner was minor.
The petitioner qualified her matriculation examination in the year 2006 and immediately thereafter, she sought appointment on compassionate ground on 11.7.2006.
However, the same was declined by the respondents on 13.4.2007 as the request of the petitioner was stated to be hopelessly time barred.
4.
In reply, it has been admitted that the application dated 892003 was made by the guardian of the petitioner, but the petitioner never submitted the documents within the stipulated time.
The petitioner again applied for compassionate appointment on 11.7.2006 after obtaining the matriculation certificate on 7.7.2006.
But the said application was made much beyond the period of three years which elapsed on 14.5.2005 and thus, the request of the petitioner for compassionate appointment has been rightly declined.
5.
Learned counsel for the petitioner relies upon Clause 18 of the Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003 (hereinafter referred to as the `2003 Rules').
The said Clause of the Rules contemplate that these rules shall be relaxed in the cases of the children who have become orphans and the said rules shall remain alive till one child has attained majority/minimum eligible age for entry into Government service.
In view of the said Rules, it is argued that the claim of the petitioner for appointment on compassionate ground could not have been rejected as being time barred.
The Clause 18 of the 2003 Rules reads as under :-  "18.
Relaxation : There shall be no relaxation of any provision of these rules.
However, as a special case, these rules shall be relaxed only in the cases of children who have become orphans, shall remain alive till one child has attained majority/minimum eligible age for entry into Government service."
6.
Though the 2003 Rules contemplate that an application from the eligible member of the deceased employee, for employment on compassionate ground, is required to be submitted within 3 years of death of the employee, but such condition stands relaxed in the cases of the children who have become orphans as it is contemplated that such condition shall remain alive till one child has attained majority.
The petitioner has attained majority on 2072004 and has sought appointment having passed the matriculation examination in the year 2006.
The petitioner became eligible for appointment in terms of the 2003 Rules only in July, 2004 and moved an application for appointment within 3 years.
Therefore, in terms of Clause 18 of the 2003 Rules, the case of the petitioner was alive.
Since the petitioner has attained the age of majority and obtained the minimum qualification prior to commencement of the Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2006 (hereinafter referred to as the `2006 Rules'), therefore, the claim of the petitioner is entitled to be considered for appointment on compassionate ground under the 2003 Rules only.
7.
As a consequence thereof, the communication Annexure P-9 declaring the claim of the petitioner for appointment on compassionate ground as time barred, is wholly unjustified.
The respondents have over looked the Clause 18 of the 2003 Rules which permits the claim of an orphan such as the petitioner, to be alive for a long period than what is provided in substantive provisions of law.
8.
In view of the above, the present writ petition is allowed.
The communication dated 13.4.2007, Annexure P-9 is set aside.
The respondents are directed to appoint the petitioner on compassionate ground in terms of the Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003.
Petition allowed.
