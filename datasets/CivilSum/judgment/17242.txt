VK Bali, J (Oral) - By this order we dispose of two connected Civil Writ Petitions bearing Nos. 15651 and 15652 of 1997.
Concededly, except for the dates of accident and the extent of disability, all the facts are common.
Brief facts have, however, been extracted from Civil Writ Petition No. 15651 of 1997.
2.
On account of accident that the petitioner unfortunately met with on 22.5.1992, he was hospitalised and ultimately it was found that he has 25 per cent disability.
Despite 25 per cent disability, petitioner had been working on a post of Assistant Lineman and performing the duties attached to the said post to the entire satisfaction of his employer.
Concededly, he performed the duties of Assistant Lineman for a period of five years.
All of a sudden on 8101997, petitioner was handed over an order terminating his services, copy Annexure P-1.
It is this order that has been challenged by present petition.
3.
In response to the notice of motion issued by this Court, respondents entered defence and opposed the cause of the petitioner.
4.
However, the basic fact is that the petitioner was performing and continues to perform his duties as Assistant Lineman for a continuous period of five years.
It could not be disputed during the course of arguments that 25 per cent disability suffered by the petitioner has in any manner, affected him in discharging his duties as Assistant Lineman.
Thus, we are of the view the the petitioner could not be given a marching order.
Concededly as mentioned above, petitioner has been performing his duty as Assistant Lineman to the complete satisfaction of the employer.
5.
In view of what has been stated above, we quash the order dated 8101997 Annexure P-1 and permit the petitioner to continue on the job till such time he is exactly found unfit to perform his duties attached to the aforesaid post.
Disposed of accordingly.
Order accordingly
