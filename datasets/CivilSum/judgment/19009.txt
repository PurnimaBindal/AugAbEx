NK Sodhi, J (Oral) - Petitioners are working as Octroi Moharars with the Municipal Committee, Rohtak.
They were appointed on daily wages w.e.f.
the dates mentioned against their names hereunder :-  Balwan Singh  9101974 Hawa Singh 1.2.1981 Kishori Lal 3.6.1978 Chander Sarup 22.2.1973 Attar Singh 2731978 Kanwal Singh 1.9.1980 Bal Krishan 10.10.1979 Jai Parkash 15.10.1976 Azad Singh 2791976 Ram Chander 10111981 2.
They filed this writ petition in the year 1988 claiming regularisation of their services with effect from the dates of their initial appointment.
It is common case of the parties that during the pendency of the writ petition the services of the petitioners have been regularised with effect from 3131993.
They only claim they are now making is that the date of regularisation should be preponed and that their services be regularised with effect from the date when they completed ten years of service.
This claim is being made on the basis of the government instructions dated April 6, 1990 which were then prevalent in the State of Haryana and were adopted by the Municipal Committee-resondent.
A copy of these instructions has been placed on the record as Annexure P-5 along with CM 21146 of 1998.
These instructions in regard to category-III employee provide that "casual or daily rated employees who have completed 10 years or more of service on 30.9.1988 shall be regularised w.e.f.
1101988 on the basis of seniority-cum-suitability."
It is further provided that "In the case of those employees who are required to be continued in service their services would be regularised on the completion of 10 years of service."
Since the dates of initial appointment of the petitioners as also the instructions relied upon have not been disputed, I am of the view that the claim now made by the petitioners merits acceptance.
Petitioners 1, 3 to 5, 8 and 9 admittedly completed ten years of their service as daily wage employees prior to 30th September, 1988 and, therefore, their services need to be regularised w.e.f.
1101988 as provided in the aforesaid instructions.
Since this has not been done, the writ petition is allowed and the respondent-Committee directed to regularise the services of these petitioners w.e.f.
3131993 they need to be regularised with effect from the dates when they completed ten years of service.
This would obviously be prior to 3131993.
The respondent-Committee is further directed to regularise the services of petitioners 2, 6, 7 and 10 with effect from dates when they completed ten years of service on daily wages.
The writ petition stands allowed in the above terms.
There is no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
