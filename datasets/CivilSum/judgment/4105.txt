GS Singhvi, J - These petitions have been filed for quashing the recommendations made by the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana for recruitment of Canal Patwaris and termination of the services of petitioners.
FACTS:  C.W.P.
No. 17736 OF 1997.
2.
The petitioners were appointed as Canal Patwaris in February, 1995 on the recommendations made by the selection committee.
Their appointments were quashed by the High Court while deciding C.W.P.
No. 2323 of 1995 `Bhagat Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
That decision has been substantially upheld by the Supreme Court in Raj Kumar and others v. Shakti Raj and others, JT 1997(2) SC 688 : 1997(2) SCT 76.
In compliance of the directions given by the Supreme Court, the respondent-Board issued public notices and called upon the candidates who had passed the Patwar examinations held between 25th and 28th April, 1992 to appear for the interviews.
After evaluating the performance of the candidates at the written test and the viva voce test, the respondent-Board has notified the select list on 971997.
As the roll numbers of the petitioners do not figure in the select list notified by the respondents, they have filed this petition to quash the select list and also to quash the circular Annexure P-4 along with the orders Annexures P-5 to P-7 terminating their services.
3.
The petitioners have challenged the impugned selection on the following grounds :  (a) The criteria adopted by the Board is arbitrary and unconstitutional, inasmuch as separate marks have been earmarked for academic qualifications, although the candidates have already appeared in the written test.
(b) The award of marks at the viva voce test is vitiated due to arbitrariness and mala fides.
(c) The experience gained by the petitioners on the basis of their previous appointments has been ignored.
C.W.P.
No. 15945 of 1997.
4.
Petitioner Surinder Pal Singh and others were appointed as Canal Patwaris in February, 1995 on the recommendations of the selection committee.
Their appointments were invalidated by the High Court in C.W.P.
No. 2323 of 1995 `Bhagat Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
They were interviewed along with other eligible candidates in compliance of the directions given by the Supreme Court in `Raj Kumar and others v. Shakti Raj and others' (supra) and as the roll numbers of the petitioners do not figure in the select list notified on 971997, they have filed this petition for quashing the recommendations made by the Board on the grounds which are identical to the grounds raised in C.W.P.
No. 17736 of 1997 `Ashok Kumar and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
Some of the grounds are similar to those raised in C.W.P.
No. 14992 of 1997 `Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others' which has already been decided on 3121997.
C.W.P.
No. 15935 of 1997.
5.
The petitioner has challenged the selection made by the Subordinate Services Selection Board and termination of his services on the grounds set out in the writ petition and also on the grounds that he was not interviewed by the Subordinate Services Selection Board, in view of the directions given by the Supreme Court that the candidates who had appeared in the Patwar examination held between 25th and 28th April, 1992 should be interviewed by the Board.
C.W.P.
No. 16122 of 1997.
6.
The petitioners-Bakshish Singh and 2 others have challenged the recommendations made by the Board for recruitment of Canal Patwaris and the consequential termination of their services on the various grounds set out in the writ petition which are similar to the grounds raised in C.W.P.
No. 17736 of 1997 `Ashok Kumar and others v. State of Haryana and others' and C.W.P.
No. 14992 of 1997 `Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
C.W.P.
No. 16018 of 1997.
7.
The petitioners who were appointed as Canal Patwaris in February, 1995 on the recommendations of the selection committee have sought the Court's intervention for quashing of the recommendations made by the Subordinate Services Selection Board and the termination of their services on the various grounds set out in the writ petition, which are similar to the grounds raised in C.W.P.
No. 17736 of 1997 `Ashok Kumar and others v. State of Haryana and others' and C.W.P.
No. 14992 of 197 `Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
C.W.P.
No. 16226 of 1997.
8.
Petitioners-Prabhu Dayal and others were appointed as Canal Patwaris in February, 1995.
They were again interviewed by the Subordinate Services Selection Board in compliance of the directions given by the Supreme Court in Raj Kumar and others v. Shakti Raj and others (supra).
They are aggrieved by the recommendations made by the Subordinate Services Selection Board and the termination of their services on the grounds which are similar to the grounds raised in C.W.P.
No. 17736 of 1997 `Ashok Kumar and others v. State of Haryana and others' and C.W.P.
No. 14992 of 1997 `Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
9.
In all these petitions reference has been made to C.W.P.
No. 14992 of 1997 `Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others', C.W.P.
No. 16018 of 1997 `Sanjeev Kumar and others v. State of Haryana and others', C.W.P.
No. 16226 of 1997 `Prabhu Dayal and others v. State of Haryana and others', C.W.P.
No. 17032 of 1997 `Mohan Lal and others v. State of Haryana and others'.
The petitioners have pleaded that their cases are identical to the above-mentioned writ petitions.
10.
Arguments in these petitions were heard on different dates and the orders were reserved keeping in view the fact that the judgment in C.W.P.
No. 14992 of 1997 `Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others' and other connected petitions was awaited.
Those petitions have been decided on 3121997 and the various grounds of challenge raised by the petitioners have been negatived except their plea that the names of the candidates belonging to the reserve categories should have been included in the select list prepared for general category candidates because they are more meritorious than the general category candidates.
In its order dated 3121997, the Division Bench has directed the appointing authority to make appointments in accordance with the revised merit lists.
11.
For the detailed reasons recorded in the order dated 3121997, which shall be treated as part of this order, the writ petitions are dismissed.
Those of the petitioners, who were not interviewed by the Board because the direction of the Supreme Court was confined to the candidates who had passed the Patwar Examination held between 25th and 28th April, 1982 shall be free to approach the Hon'ble Supreme Court for appropriate directions.
Petitions dismissed.
