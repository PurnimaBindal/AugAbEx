GS Singhvi, J - This is a petition for quashing of the memos dated 12121997 (Annexure P1) and dated 1111999 (Annexure P2) issued by the Deputy Inspector General of Police, Border Range, Amritsar (respondent No. 2) and the Director General of Police, Punjab respectively declining the petitioner's prayer for promotion as Lady Sub Inspector.
2.
A perusal of the record shows that the petitioner joined service of Punjab Police as Constable on being appointed as such in 1976.
She was promoted as Head Constable on 3101984 and then as Assistant Sub Inspector on 391991.
From 161994 to March, 1998, she served on deputation with C.I.D.
During that period, she held the post of Sub Inspector but on reversion from the C.I.D.
she was posted as Assistant Sub Inspector in her parent cadre.
While she was on deputation, she claimed promotion on the post of Sub Inspector on the ground that she had cleared the Upper School Course.
Her request was turned down by respondent No. 2 and a similar representation made by her was rejected by the Director General of Police Punjab.
3.
In the writ petition, it has been averred that persons junior to the petitioner who have been impleaded as respondent Nos. 4 to 6 were promoted as Sub Inspectors on 11.11.1996 without considering her candidature and in this manner, her fundamental right to equality in the matter of employment has been violated.
It has been further averred that a separate cadre of women police was created by the Government of Punjab in 1990 and all the three posts falling to the quota of lady Sub Inspectors have been filled by appointing sports persons ignoring the claim of general category candidates.
4.
We have heard Shri HS Mann, learned counsel for the petitioner.
In our opinion, the writ petition merits summary dismissal.
The reasons for this, conclusion of ours are:  (1) Civil Writ Petition No. 5549 filed the petitioner for grant of relief identical to the one claimed in this petition was got dismissed without liberty to file a fresh petition.
In view of the law laid down by the Supreme Court in Sarguja Transport Service v. State Transport Appellate Tribunal, Gwalior, AIR 1987 SC 88, and State of Uttar Pradesh v. Labh Chand, 1993(4) SLR 391 : 1993(4) SCT 79 (SC) and by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 12084 of 1998, Karan Singh v. State of Haryana decided on 481998, the second writ on the same cause of action is not maintainable.
(2) The petitioner has neither placed on record the order dated 11.11.1996 vide which respondent Nos. 4 to 6 were promoted as Sub Inspectors nor she has made prayer for quashing of that order and without examining the legality of the order of promotion of the non-official respondents, we cannot issue a direction which will directly or indirectly affect their promotion.
In Surinder Singh v. Central Government AIR 1986 SC 2166 ; 1987 R.R.R.
100 (SC), the Supreme Court and in M/s Arora Soap Industries, Jind v. State of Haryana and others, C.W.P.
No. 1109 of 1998 decided on 1891998, this Court have held that in exercise of jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the High Court cannot nullify an order which has not been placed on the record of the case.
(3) As against the promotion of the non-official respondents, the writ petition is highly belated and no explanation has been given by the petitioner for long delay of three years.
Even from the date of rejection of her second representation, the delay is of more than one year and in view of the observations made by the Supreme Court in PS Sadasivaswamy v. State of Tamil Nadu, 1976(1) SLR 53, that claim regarding promotion and seniority must be made before the Court within six months or at best within one year from the date of accrual of cause of action, we do not have any hesitation to decline the petitioner's prayer for directing the official respondents to promote her as Sub Inspector with effect from 11.11.1996.
Hence, the writ petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
