Jawahar Lal Gupta, J - The petitioner, who retired as Lt. Col. (time- scale, from the Indian Army on June 22, 1971, was reemployed against the vacancy of a Major on July 10, 1971.
On re-employment he was paid fixed emoluments @ Rs. 1400/- per month upto December 31, 1972.
On account of the revision in scale of pay, his emoluments were fixed at Rs. 1800/- per month w.e.f.
January 1, 1973.
He continued to draw his salary tiff January 1, 1976.
2.
On July 29, 1978, the Government of India issued revised instructions regarding fixation of pay of re-employed officers.
In paragraph 4 of these instructions provision was made for fixation of initial pay.
In paragraphs 9 and 12 it was provided as under :-  Para 9.
Once the initial pay of a re-employed Officer has been fixed in the manner indicated above, he will be allowed to draw normal increments in the scale of the rank in which he is re-employed provided that the pay and the gross pension (excluding disability element of pension) pension equivalent of other retirement/release benefits taken together do not at any time exceed Rs. 3,00000 per month.
Para 12.
In cases of Officers who were already serving on re-employment on 1176 and who opt to be governed by the provisions of this AI their pay will be refixed.
Increment earned for the period of re-employed service prior to 111976 will be added to the initial pay in terms of para 4 above."
3.
These are the two provisions which fall for consideration in this case.
Mr. PS Kang learned counsel for the petitioner, submits that the petitioner was re-employed on July 10, 1971 and continued in service till June 22, 1980.
According to learned counsel the benefit of increments for the period from January 2, 1976 to June 22, 1980 has been given to the petitioner.
The benefit for the period from July 10, 1971 to January 1, 1976 has been wrongly declined.
Since repeated representations made by the petitioner having been rejected by the authorities, the petitioner has approached this Court through the present writ petition.
None has appeared on behalf of the respondents to controvert the submission made on behalf of the petitioner.
4.
The short question that falls for consideration is as to whether or not the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of increments for the period of service rendered by him on re-employment from July 10, 1971 to January 1, 1976.
In my view the answer to this query is contained in paragraph 12, quoted above.
It is categorically provided that officers, who were serving on re-employment on 111976 and who opt to be governed by the provisions of these Army instructions, would earn increments for the period of re-employed service rendered by them prior to January 1, 1976.
The petitioner had rendered service from July 10, 1971 to January 1, 1976.
Increments for this period had to be given to him.
5.
The Army authorities seems to have taken a view that the limitation mentioned in paragraph 4 would cover paragraph 12 also.
It is on this premises that they appears to have declined the relief to the petitioner.
In my view the view taken by the authorities is not in consonance with the expressed language of paragraph 12.
Under paragraph 4 of the instructions, only the initial pay has to be fixed.
After fixation of the initial pay, the increments for the period of re-employed service prior to January 1, 1976 have to be added to the initial pay.
This has not been done.
As a result the petitioner was put into a substantial financial loss.
6.
The earlier instructions have been issued by the Army authorities on March 26, 1960 vide Annexure P2.
These instructions were superseded by the Government vide its letter dated July 29, 1978 (Annexure P.3), thereby the Government has included the grant of additional increments.
The effort was to emulate the conditions of personnel.
It could never be the intention of the Government to.
reduce the emoluments and pay the officer something less than what he was actually drawing.
That being the intention behind the instructions, it appears to be clear that the petitioner is entitled to the grant of increments for the period.
of re-employed service rendered by him prior to January 1, 1976.
7.
The writ petition is accordingly allowed.
The respondents are directed to refix the petitioner's pay by giving him the benefit of increments from July, 10, 1971 to January 1, 1976.
Arrears of salary falling due on this account shall be paid to the petitioner alongwith interest @ 12 per cent within three months from the date of the receipt of the copy of this judgment.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to his costs, which are assessed at Rs. 3,000/-.
Petition allowed.
