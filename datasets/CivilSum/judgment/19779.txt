RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The petitioners were appointed in the Haryana Roadways prior to the year 1979.
At that time the pay scale of Storemen was Rs. 300-400.
In April, 1979, the pay scale was revised to Rs. 750-940.
It has been alleged in the petition that the minimum qualifications for the post of Storeman as well as Assistant Store Keepers in the Haryana Roadways was Matriculation with 2/3 years experience in handling automobile stores.
The pay scale of the Assistant Store Keeper in April, 1979 was also Rs. 750-940.
However w.e.f.
1-1-1986, the pay scale of the Assistant Store Keeper was revised from Rs. 750-940 to Rs. 950-1500, whereas the pay scale of Storemen was not revised.
The petitioners claim that they are also entitled to the same pay scale w.e.f.
111986.
It is the case of the petitioners that in spite of the representation having been made to that effect, no relief was granted by the respondents.
This led to the filing of the present writ petition.
2.
Before the writ petition was admitted, many opportunities were given to the respondents to file the written statement.
However, no written statement was filed on behalf of the respondents and in fact till date no written statement has been filed.
Consequently, I have no option but to take the averments made in the writ petition as having been admitted.
It has been mentioned in the writ petition that the duties and responsibilities of the Storemen are identical to that of Assistant Store Keeper in the Haryana Roadways and it was in fact taking this fact into consideration that their pay scale was revised to Rs. 750-940 in April, 1979 which was equal to the pay scale of Assistant Store Keepers.
3.
I find force in the contention of the learned Counsel for the petitioners that once the pay scale of the Storemen was made equal to that of Assistant Store Keepers it was for the State Govt. to give reasons and justify as to why w.e.f.
1-1-1986 the pay scale of Assistant Store Keepers was revised to Rs. 950-1500 whereas the pay-scale of the Storemen in the Haryana Roadways was not revised.
As observed above, since no written statement has been filed, it has to be taken that the State Govt. has no justification to offer for treating the Storemen differently than Assistant Store Keepers.

equality clause enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
4.
For the foregoing reasons, I allow this writ petition and direct the respondents to give the same pay scale to the petitioner-Storemen in the Haryana Roadways as was given to the Assistant Store Keepers w.e.f.
1-1.1986.
The arrears shall be paid to the petitioners within 3 months from the receipt of this order.
However, there will be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
