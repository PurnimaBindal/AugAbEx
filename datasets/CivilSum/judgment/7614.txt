SS Nijjar, J - The only relief claimed by the petitioner in this petition under Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India is that the writ of mandamus directing the respondent-State of Haryana to refer the industrial dispute to the appropriate Labour Court be issued.
The demand notice issued to the petitioner has been rejected by respondent No. 1 with the following order :-  "On the above cited subject you are informed that Government has treated no fit case of you for sending to the Labour Court for decision because after verification it has come to the notice that you have raised the demand after 8 years and no reasonable explanation has been given for delay.
Therefore, in view of the Hon'ble Supreme Court decision in case Nedungadi Bank Ltd v. KP Madhwan Kutti, 2000 LLR 340 : 2000(1) SCT 1088 (SC), delayed demand cannot be sent to the Labour Court.
Therefore, keeping in view of the above, your demand notice has been rejected."
2.
We have heard the counsel for the parties.
A perusal of the aforesaid order clearly shows that the respondent No. 1 has actually exceeded in its jurisdiction in adjudicating the dispute on merits.
The judgment of the Supreme Court relied on by the respondents in Nedungadi's case (supra) would not be applicable in the facts and circumstances of the present case.
In fact, the Supreme Court has laid down the law on numerous occasions consistently.
It is for the Labour Court to adjudicate upon the reference.
Mere delay in serving the demand notice would not be sufficient to defeat the claim put forward by the workman in the industrial dispute.
Delay would be a factor to be taken into consideration by the Labour Court in moulding the relief in case the dispute is decided in favour of the workman.
In view of the above, petition is allowed.
Impugned order dated 442002 (Annexure P.3) is quashed.
The matter is directed to be referred before the Labour Court, in accordance with law.
Petition allowed.
