SS Sodhi, J - There is a clear policy decision of the Life Insurance Corporation of India, as contained in its circular letter dated August 13, 1981 (annexure R/1), in terms of which, no agency is to be given to any one, whose spouse is employed in the Central or State Government of in any public sector undertaking.
It was in disregard to this policy decision that an agency was given to the petitioner whose husband is employed as an Engineer with the Punjab State Electricity Board.
This being so, no occasion is provided here for interference in writ proceedings with the order of the respondent- corporation that no fresh business be entertained from the petitioner while the matter of the grant of agency to her in violation of the policy decision is being enquired into.
Dismissed.
