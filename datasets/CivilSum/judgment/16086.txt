S K Jain, J - Darbari Lal plaintiff-appellant herein, was appointed as Assistant Lineman on 16121968 in the Haryana State Electricity Board.
He was promoted to the post of Lineman with effect from 3031982.
He had been working as such at Chhachhrauli.
His promotion order was cancelled on 631984 vide office order No. 106 of that date (Ex. D-2).
He filed Civil Suit No. 147 of 1984 on 341984 against the Haryana State Electricity Board and another for a decree of declaration to the effect that order dated 2031984 thereby reverting him as Lineman to that of Assistant Line Man was null and void.
2.
The suit was contested on behalf of the Board.
Their case is that the plaintiff remained absent from duty without permission in the year 1975-76.
A show-cause notice was issued on 131976 and his services were terminated w.e.f.
181975.
He filed a mercy appeal which was considered by the Board sympathetically and he was re-appointed as Assistant Lineman on ad hoc basis only for six months on 18121976.
He accepted fresh appointment vide letter Ex. D-4.
Through in inadvertent mistake, he was promoted to the post of Lineman and when this mistake came to light, his promotion order was withdrawn.
The parties fought the litigation on the following issues :-  (1) Whether the order dated 2031984 made by the defendant No. 2 in order to revert the plaintiff from the post of Lineman to the post of Assistant Lineman is null and void and not binding upon the plaintiff 9 OPP.
(2) Whether the suit is not maintainable in the present form ?
OPD.
(3) Whether the suit has not been framed according to the necessary party ?
OPD.
(4) Whether the plaintiff estopped by his own act and conduct ?
OPD.
(5) Whether the suit has not been framed according to law ?
OPD.
(6) Whether this Court has no jurisdiction to try this suit ?
OPD.
(6A) Whether the respondents-defendants disobeyed the injunction order dated 341984, if so, to what effect ?
OPD.
(7) Relief.
3.
Learned Sub Judge IInd Class, Jagadhri vide his judgment and decree dated 14.8.1986 dismissed the suit of the plaintiff.
Feeling aggrieved, the plaintiff preferred Civil Appeal No. 94/13 of 1986, which was dismissed by Shri M S Nagra, Additional District Judge, Ambala vide his judgment and decree dated 1881988.
4.
It is that judgment and decree of the first Appellate Court which has been appealed against by plaintiff-appellant in this Regular Second Appeal and which requires my examination of its sustainability.
5.
I have seen the pleadings in the suit, the evidence adduced by the parties in the suit and the judgments of both the Courts below and have also heard the learned counsel for the parties.
6.
Sole argument advanced by the learned counsel for the appellant is that the impugned order was passed without affording a hearing opportunity to the plaintiff.
I do not find any force in this argument.
7.
Perusal of the record would show that final notice vide memo No. 3664 dated 131976 (Ex. D-7) was issued by the defendants to the plaintiff telling him that he was absent from essential duty with effect from 181975 on his own accord.
He was asked to resume duty within ten days failing which his service would be liable to be terminated.
This notice was received.
by him on 331976 vide A.D. receipt Ex. D-6.
Vide office order No. 442 dated 581976 (Ex. D-5), service of the plaintiff were terminated with effect from 181975 as he was wilfully absent from duty.
Vide memorandum No. 20629 dated 18121976 (Ex. D-5), on his application.
The plaintiff was appointed as Assistant Lineman on ad hoc basis on the clear understanding that the appointment was purely temporary for a period not exceeding six months and terminable without any notice.
Endorsement No. 20630 dated 18121976 appended to the abovesaid office order shows that necessary undertaking had been obtained from the plaintiff that he will not be given the benefit of service rendered by him in the Board in the capacity of ALM.
Vide order No. 139 dated 3031982 Ex. D-1 he was promoted to the post of Lineman, although in the third column of office order meant for "date of joining as ALM" the entry is "23.12.1968".
He had been re-employed on 18121976 vide order Ex. D-5 as mentioned above.
This mistake, having come to light, the Board, vide order No. 106/631984 (Ex. D-2), cancelled the above said promotion order.
8.
Admittedly, the promotion order had been passed on the basis of wrong date of joining as ALM and, therefore, cancellation of such an order cannot amount to reduction in rank under Article 311 of the Constitution because such an order of promotion did not confer any legal right on the plaintiff and subsequent order cancelling the said promotion did not amount to reduction in rank.
In holding the above view, a reference can be had to :-  (i) State of Punjab v. Jagdip Singh, AIR 1964 SC 521; and  (ii) Ravinder Nath Tiwari v. Divisional Superitendent of Education, Jabalpur, 1978(1) SLR 484.
9.
In the net result, no fault can be found with the concurrent findings of act recorded by two Courts below which are hereby affirmed.
10.
Resultantly, this appeal fails which be and is hereby dismissed.
No costs.
Appeal dismissed.
