Heard learned counsel for the parties.
2.
This Special Appeal has been filed against the interim order of the learned single Judge dated 4-8-2004 staying the transfer order.
3.
Transfer is an exigency of service and hence this Court should not ordinarily interfere vide Vijay Kumar Chaurasia v. State of UP, 2004(4) SCT 146 (Allahabad) : 2004(3) All WC 1898 : (2004 All LJ 2389).
The aforesaid decision has considered the relevant case law on the point.
4.
It has also been held in Sandeep Kumar Singh v. State of UP, Special Appeal No. 911 of 2004, decided on 5-8-2004, Shesh Nath Singh v. Mukesh Singh, 2005(1) SCT 265 (Allahabad) : 2004 All LJ 3688, Special Appeal No. 860 of 2004, decided on 26-7-2004 and Union of India v. Reghubir Prasad decided on 21-7-2004 that a special appeal is maintainable against an interim order staying a transfer order.
5.
The Single Judge has stated that there have been frequent transfers of the writ petitioner.
In our opinion there is no bar to frequent transfers.
Moreover, the mere fact that the petitioner's children are studying and transfer in mid-session is not a ground for interference with the transfer order.
6.
The appeal is allowed and the order dated 4-8-2004 is set aside.
Appeal allowed.
