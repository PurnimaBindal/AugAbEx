N Paul Vasanthakumar, J - The prayer in the writ petition is to issue a writ of Certified Mandamus, to call for records relating to the impugned order passed by the respondent in proceeding's No. Mu.
Mu.
No. 07/69709/2004 dated 30122004 dated 30122004 and quash the same consequently direct the respondent to fix the pension for the amount of Rs. 1,512/- and other allowances and to direct the respondent to refund the amount to the petitioner, which was already recovered from the petitioner's pension.
2.
The case of the petitioner is that the petitioner retired from service on voluntary retirement scheme on 191988 and thereafter, the petitioner's pension was fixed and by the impugned order dated 1812005 an amount of Rs. 14,429/was ordered to be recovered, which according to the respondent, it is based on the audit objections.
3.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the respondent having fixed the amount of pension from 191988, without any misrepresentation on the part of the petitioner, the respondent is not entitled to order recovery of the said amount as per the Judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the decision reported in the case of Shyam Babu Verma v. Union of India, (1994) 2 SCC 521 and the Judgment of Division Bench of this Court made in WP No. 1944 of 2006, by order dated 30.6.2006. a Division Bench of this Court has also followed the Judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the decision reported in the case of Kerala State Road Transport Corporation v. AG Varghese & Others, (2003) 3 SLT 770.
4.
Following the said judgments of the Hon'ble Supreme Court and having regard to the fixation of pension to the petitioner without any misrepresentation on the part of the petitioner and the impugned order having been passed without issuing any notice, based on the alleged audit objection, the same is unsustainable.
Hence, the impugned order is set aside and the writ Petition is allowed with a direction to the respondent to refund the recovered amount to the petitioner within a period of eight weeks from the date of receipt of a copy of this order.
5.
With the above direction, the writ petition is allowed.
Consequently, the connected M.P. is closed.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
