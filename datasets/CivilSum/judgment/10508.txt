GS Singhvi, J(Oral) - This petition was filed by the petitioner in person on 28.9.1992.
Order sheets dated 991992 to 14.12.1994 show that on each and every date the petitioner was present in person.
This he apparently did with the hope that the Court will be able to hear and decide his claim on the grant of interest on the arrears of his salary etc. at an early date.
The very fact that the Court has fixed as many as six dates of hearing between 2991992 and 14.12.1994 shows the anxiety on the part of the Court to make an expeditious adjudication of the claim made by a retired employee.
Therefore, the mere fact that the petitioner is not present today cannot be a ground for delaying the grant of relief to him if any due.
2.
Even though four opportunities have been given to the respondents to file reply and one of these was subject to the payment of Rs. 500/- as costs, the respondents have not chosen to file reply to the writ petition will have to be decided on the basis of the averments made in the petition itself.
3.
The facts which emerged from the Writ Petition show that the Petitioner retired from he Service of the Government of Punjab with effect from 3131987 while he was holding the post of Executive Engineer, Project Construction, Irrigation Branch, Head Office at Chandigarh.
While he was still in Service, the departmental authorities withheld his salary for the period between 1.9.1984 to 8.3.1985 and 74.
1985 to 1561985.
4.
After his retirement, the Government passed an order dated 2361988 and withheld his salary for the aforesaid periods.
This order was made subject -matter of challenge in CWPNo.
11303 of 1988.
That Writ Petition was allowed by a learned Single Judge on 121990.
The operative portion of the order passed by the learned single Judge reads -  " Consequently, the writ petition is allowed and impugned order dated 23rd June, 1988 (Annexure P.9) is quashed.
The respondents are directed to pay to the petitioner the arrears of salary for the periods in dispute, that is from 1st September, 1984 to 8th March, 1985, and from 7th April, 1985 to 15th June, 1985, within a period of three months.
There shall be no order as to costs."
5.
The state of Punjab filed an LPA against the order of the learned Single Judge and the LPA.
No. 975 of 1990 was dismissed by a Division Bench of this Court on 14.8.1991.
Despite the dismissal of the LPA the respondents did not make payment to the petitioner and this compelled him to file Contempt Petition No1129 of 1991.
After notice of the Contempt Petition was issued, payment of arrears of salary was made to the petitioner.
Taking note of the factum of payment of salary to the petitioner, this Court disposed of the Contempt petition vide its order dated 631992.
6.
Not only the payment of salary was withheld for considerably long period, the respondents withheld the payment of death-cum-retirement gratuity which became due to the petitioner as early as in June, 1987.
The gratuity was ultimately paid to him on 28111988.
This is the statement made by the learned Assistant Advocate General and there is no reason to disbelieve it.
7.
In the present Writ Petition the petitioner has made a claim for payment of compound interest at the rate of 18% P.A.
on the amount of arrears of salary as well as on the amount of gratuity.
He has also prayed for an award of adequate compensation for harassment and agony to which he has been put by the respondents who withheld the payment of salary as well as gratuity.
8.
The only argument advanced by the learned Assistant Advocate-General in opposition to the Writ Petition is that the petitioner could have made a claim for award of interest in CWPNo.
11303 of 1988 and since he did not make such a claim in that petition, he should be deemed to have given up his claim to interest.
She further argued that the amount of salary was not paid to the petitioner in view of the order of punishment dated 2361988.
Learned Assistant Advocate-General further argued that gratuity was withheld because of the pendency of departmental enquiry .
9.
After giving thoughtful consideration to the submissions of the learned Assistant Advocate-General and having perused the record of the case, I am fully convinced that neither of the contentions of the Assistant Advocate -General merits acceptance.
Petitioner's right to get salary for the period between 1.9.1984 to 8.3.1985 and 74.1985 to 1561985 was put under cloud by the order dated 2361988.
Earlier to the passing of that order he was denied payment of salary for the aforesaid period on the ground of pendency of a departmental enquiry for his alleged absence during that period.
However, as soon as this Court allowed his petition, the cloud of order dated 2361988 disappeared.
Thereafter, there remained no justification whatsoever for withholding the payment of salary in terms of the directions issued by this Court.
Till the acceptance of his petition on 121990 the petitioner could not have possibly made a claim for grant of salary because the same had been ordered to be withheld by the order of the government dated 2361988.
Only after the setting aside of the order of punishment the petitioner could possibly make a claim for payment of salary for the period aforesaid.
Therefore, the argument of the learned Assistant Advocate- General that failure of the petitioner to make a claim for award of interest in the previous Writ petition should operate as a bar to the grant of relief in this petition cannot be accepted .
10.
In so far as payment of interest on gratuity is concerned, in my opinion, the petitioner earned a right to get the gratuity on the date of his retirement.
It became his property with effect from 141987.
This right of the petitioner was put in abeyance on account of the disciplinary enquiry initiated by the Government.
During this period the Government indirectly enjoyed the property of the petitioner.
Therefore, there is every justification in the claim of the petitioner that he should be awarded interest on the amount of gratuity for the period it was not paid to him .
11.
In the result, the Writ Petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to pay interest to the petitioner on the amount of gratuity payable to him at the rate of 18% per annum with effect from 141987 till the date of actual payment of gratuity to him.
On the amount of arrears of salary payable in terms of the order dated 121990 passed by this Court in C.W.P.
No.11303 of 1988, the respondents shall pay him interest at the rate of 12% per annum with effect from 121990 to the date of actual payment.
The petitioner shall get costs of Rs1000/-.
Petition allowed.
