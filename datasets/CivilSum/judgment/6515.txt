Swatanter Kumar, J - In this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner's pray for the issuance of a writ or direction, directing the respondents to pay the difference of salary between the daily wagers and regular employees with effect from 121996 till the date the orders of their regularisation were passed i.e. 931998, 24.9.1998 and 851998, copy of which are appended with the petition as Annexures P/1 to P/3 respectively.
All the petitioners joined the respondent-department as daily wagers during the period January 1992 to 1998.
After working for a considerable period, the petitioners filed a Civil Writ Petition being Civil Writ Petition No. 1540 of 1996 praying for regularisation of their services.
The said writ petition was withdrawn by the petitioners, who subsequently filed a fresh writ petition for the same relief with the leave of the court.
Vide order dated 2941997, the respondents were directed to consider the case of the petitioners for regularisation.
The respondents, vide impugned orders dated 931998, 24.9.1998 and 851998 respectively regularised the services of the petitioners with effect from 121996 but ordered that the salary of the regular post shall be payable to the petitioners only from the date of order i.e. 931998, 24.9.1998 and 851998.
2.
Aggrieved from this part of the order, the petitioners have filed the present writ petition.
3.
Upon notice, the respondents contested the petition and filed a written statement.
4.
It is not disputed that the services of the petitioners were regularised after passing the orders of the court.
However, it is stated that as the matter was pending in Giri Raj's case before the Hon'ble Apex Court, which has subsequently been decided, the department was not obliged to pay arrears to the petitioners with effect from 121996.
5.
There can be hardly any controversy which would require detailed discussion by the court.
The respondents at their own initiative and after taking into consideration various judgments of the High Court and the Supreme Court including earlier decision in the case of the petitioners decided to regularise the services of the petitioners, vide order dated 931998, 24.9.1998 and 851998, copy of which appended with the petition as Annexures P/1 to P/3.
In these orders, it is specifically stated "who have completed tenure of 3 years as on 3111996, are hereby regularised with effect from 121996 subject to the following terms and conditions".
Then it has been stated that services of all the petitioners are regularised with effect from 121996.
6.
Firstly, these orders do not state that the petitioners are entitled to get salary/regular scale from the date of the issuance of the orders.
Secondly, it also nowhere states as to why the petitioners would not be entitled to get the salary with effect from 121996 from the date from which their services have been regularised.
It must be noticed that under clause 4, seniority of the petitioners would be reckoned with effect from 121996.
In other words, they are entitled to all the benefits from that date.
Once, the services of the petitioners have been regularised with effect from 121996 and they have been performing the duties and functions similar to the regular cadre from that date and in the impugned orders no reasons whatsoever has been stated for not giving the regular pay scale but from the date in question, we see no reason to deny the relief claimed by the petitioners in this petition, in terms of full bench judgment of this court in the case of Vijay Kumar and others v. State of Punjab and others, 2002(1) SCT 931 (P&H) Civil Writ Petition No. 14050 of 1999, decided on 13122001, wherein, it has been held that the petitioners upon satisfaction of the conditions therein are entitled to get minimum of the basic scale with dearness allowance.
7.
Learned counsel for the respondents have not been able to provide any plausible reason or ground to deny the benefits to the petitioners particularly when their services have been regularised with effect from 121996 i.e. the date from which their seniority shall be reckoned.
8.
For the reasons afore-stated, we allow this petition and direct the respondents to pay minimum of the pay scale to the petitioner in accordance with the rules with effect from 121996.
This petition is accordingly disposed of.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
