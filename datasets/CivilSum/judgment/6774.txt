DV Sehgal, President.
- The workman issued demand notice dated 25111992 challenging the order of termination of his service, which undoubtedly, was passed in the year 1985.
Reference under Section 10(i) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 was declined vide memo dated 16.3.93 Annexure P6 on the ground that the workman had raised the dispute after seven years of termination of his service.
The facts and the documents brought on record of this writ petition make it clear that the workman-petitioner earlier sought remedy by approaching the Civil Court and filing a suit.
The suit remained pending from 1985 till 14.10.92 when it was dismissed on the ground that the Civil Court has no jurisdiction.
In view of the Full Bench decision of the High Court in Ram Chander Morya v. State of Haryana, 1999(1) SCT 141 : I.L.R.
1999(1) Punjab and Haryana 93, no doubt, the outer limit for seeking reference of dispute has been fixed as five years.
It has, however, been observed that in case there are extenuating circumstances like pursuing a different remedy the period spent on seeking such remedy is to be excluded for computing the period of five years.
Mr. SM Sharma, learned DAG Haryana has fairly conceded that this is the final position of law.
In this view of the matter, we quash the order dated 16.3.1993 Annexure P-6 passed by respondent No. 2 and direct him to make the reference of the dispute to the Labour Court.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed of.
