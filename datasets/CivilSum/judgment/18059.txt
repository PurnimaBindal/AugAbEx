MR Agnihotri, J - Reply to the civil misc. has been filed in which it is stated that though according to the Division Bench Judgment the petitioner is entitled to be paid the wages at par with the wages being paid to the persons similarly situated but is since no is similarly situated persons are receiving Rs. 850/- per month as claimed by the petitioner, the National Fertilizers Ltd, Panipat - petitioner employer is not obliged to pay the same wages to the applicant respondent.
Having heard the learned counsel for the parties, I find that the objection is hypertechnical and as such, the applicant respondent No. 2 is entitled to be paid a sum of Rs. 850/- per month, as statutorily fixed minimum wages by the Haryana Government during the pendency of the writ petition.
This shall be consistent with the provisions of Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 as also according to the law laid down by the Division Bench of this Court in 1990(1) Recent Services Judgments 308.
With this direction, the civil misc. stands disposed of.
Ordered accordingly.
