GS Singhvi, J - The petitioners have prayed for quashing the order Annexure P2 dated 1531994 issued by the Government of Punjab granting the amended pay scale to the petitioners w.e.f.
111994 instead of 111986.
The petitioners have also prayed for issuance of a mandamus directing the respondents to grant them the revised pay scale of Rs. 1640-2925/- w.e.f.
111986 along with arrears and interest at the rate of 24% per annum.
2.
The claim of the petitioners to be paid salary in the revised pay scale w.e.f.
111986 is primarily based on the judgment of this Court in C.W.P.
No. 13115 of 1994 Dr. Dharam Paul and others v. State of Punjab and others decided on 3031995.
They have averred that after having accepted the factum of existence of anomaly and having taken decision to remove the same, the government cannot arbitrarily fix the date with effect from which the benefit of revised pay scale is to be given to them.
3.
At the hearing of the case, learned counsel for the petitioners invited our attention to the order dated 25.8.1998 passed by the Supreme Court in Civil Appeal No. 10549 of 1996 State of Punjab and another v. Dr. Dharam Paul and others and urged that in view of the dismissal of appeal filed by the State of Punjab against the judgment of the High Court, the petitioners should be given the benefit of revised pay scale w.e.f.
111986.
The learned Deputy Advocate General frankly and fairly conceded that the case of the petitioners is similar to that of Dr. Dharam Paul and others.
4.
In our opinion, the petitioners deserve to be granted relief in terms of the order passed by the Supreme Court in Dr. Dharam Paul's case (supra).
Therefore, without making an elaborate analysis of the fact and the question of law raised in the writ petition, we allow the writ petition and direct that in terms of the judgment of the Supreme Court in Civil Appeal No. 10549 of 1996, the petitioners should be given the benefit of revised pay scale of Rs. 1640-2925/- w.e.f.
111986.
The petitioners shall be paid arrears within 6 months.
Petition allowed.
