THB Chalapathi, J - The petitioner filed this writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India seeking issuance of a writ of mandamus directing the respondent No. 2 to consider his case for promotion to the post of Senior Assistant/Accounts Clerk with effect from the date his juniors have been promoted with all consequential benefits.
2.
According to the petitioner, he was appointed as a clerk and joined the service in 1963 and he passed S.D.C./Accounts Clerk's Examination in the year 1973 and later he was promoted as S.D.C.
on 28.8.1979 and a seniority list was circulated by respondent No. 2 on 18121986 in which the name of the petitioner was placed at Sr. No. 572 and later he came to know that the persons junior to him who had been placed at Sr. Nos. 573-A, 591, 677, 777 and 979 have been promoted as Accounts Clerk/Senior Assistants with effect from 3081982.
The petitioner made several representations to the respondents to consider his case for promotion, but of no avail.
He further avers that some other employee namely Manohar Lal also made a representation and he was promoted with retrospective effect from 30th August, 1982, the date when his junior was promoted.
Therefore, the petitioner also seeks promotion from retrospective effect.
3.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, I do not find any merit in this writ petition.
Admittedly, the juniors of the petitioner were promoted in 1982 as Accounts Clerks/Senior Assistants.
The petitioner did not make any grievance.
It was only on 2811995 the petitioner made a representation for considering his case for promotion from the date his juniors were promoted.
When that representation was not considered, he approached this Court in the year 1996.
The writ petition is liable to be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
The seniority of the persons who have been earlier promoted cannot be disturbed after a lapse of several years.
In my judgment in CWP No. 14119 of 1995 of even date, I took the view after referring to the decision of the Supreme Court in Roshan Lal v. International Aiport, AIR 1981 SC 97 and Jagdish Lal v. State of Haryana, JT 1997(sic) SC 387 : 1998(1) SCT 26 (SC) that the seniority list or the promotion made earlier cannot be disturbed after long lapse of time.
The alleged juniors of the petitioners have admittedly been promoted in the year 1982, but the petitioner made a representation only in the year 1995 ie after lapse of 13 years.
The writ petition is liable to be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
The writ petition, therefore, fails and is, accordingly, dismissed.
No order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
