JS Narang, J - This judgment would dispose of two writ petitions bearing No. 12403 and 15845 of 1998 as both the petitioners in the said petitions have claimed seniority amongst themselves and also against Sh. Sher Singh, common respondent, to the post of Assistant.
As such, the above said petitions are being disposed of by a common judgment.
In CWP 15845 of 1998, the petitioner Bhiwani Parshad has arrayed Sarvshri Sher Singh and Rattan Lal as respondents whereas in CWP 12403 of 1998, petitioner Rattan Lal has arrayed Sarvshri Sher Singh and Bhiwani Parshad as respondents.
2.
It would be appropriate to take the facts from CWP 15845 of 1998.
In this petition Shri Bhiwani Parshad petitioner has impugned the order dated 3091998, annexed as Annexure P8, vide which he has been reverted to the post of Clerk from Assistant and has been placed on ad hoc basis till his turn matures against the post of Clerk on regular basis against promotional quota.
3.
Sarvshri Bhiwani Parshad, Sher Singh and Rattan Lal joined as Peons on 2981961, 15.9.1964 and 1631970 respectively in the then pay scale.
Gradation list of non-gazetted officers was circulated on 111984 and Sarvshri Bhiwani Parshad, Sher Singh and Rattan Lal were shown at Serial No. 5, 10 and 23 respectively.
The said list is attached as Annexure P1.
It is an admitted fact that there is a provision for appointing Clerks in the promotion quota to the extent of 20% from Class IV employees.
As per the Service Rules of Group-C, only those officials become eligible for promotion against 20% quota who are matriculates and as such could be considered for promotion to the post of Clerks.
Sarvshri Bhiwani Parshad, Sher Singh and Rattan Lal passed their matriculation examination on 2571982, 1381981 and 1121982 respectively.
This fact has not been denied by the State.
It is obvious that all the above said three officials became eligible to be considered for promotion to the post of Clerks after the respective dates of their passing the matriculation examination.
However, the fact cannot be lost sight of fixation of seniority of the officials as had been circulated on 111984.
Sarvshri Sher Singh and Rattan Lal respondents were promoted to the post of Clerk on ad hoc basis against the vacancies of direct recruits quota on 3071982, copy Annexure P3.
Shri Bhiwani Parshad made a representation categorically objecting to the promotion of both the said officials, they being junior to him.
Another office order was issued, which is dated 2981982, vide which Bhiwani Parshad was also promoted as Clerk in the pay scale of Rs. 400-10-490/540-15-600-EB-20-660.
It has been noticed that both the orders categorically provided that the officials were promoted on six months basis or till the candidate from the Subordinate Service Selection Board (hereinafter referred to as the Board) joined, whichever was earlier and the said appointment was purely on ad hoc basis.
4.
Shri Bhiwani Parshad did not raise any objection or claimed change in the date of promotion to the post of Clerk on ad hoc basis.
Subsequently, vide office dated 461996 Shri Sher Singh and Shri Bhiwani Parshad were regularised on the date post of Clerk with effect from the dates mentioned against their names.
In the case of Shri Sher Singh it is 2.8.1982 and in the case of Shri Bhiwani Parshad it is 3181982.
Copy of the office order has been annexed as Annexure P5.
It goes without saying that the name of Shri Rattan Lal, petitioner in CWP 12403 of 1998, did not find mention in the said office order.
It was on 1261996, when another office order was issued vide which Shri Sher Singh and Bhiwani Parshad were promoted as Assistants in the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600 with immediate effect (copy of the order is annexed as Annexure P6), that Shri Rattan Lal represented to the concerned authority seeking his regularisation to the post of Clerk and thereafter promotion to the post of Assistant.
In this regard show cause notice dated 211998 was issued by the concerned authority to Shri Bhiwani Parshad stating as to why he should not be reverted and his regularisation cancelled and Shri Rattan Lal be not promoted to the post of Assistant.
Shri Bhiwani Parshad petitioner sent a detailed reply to the said show cause notice copy of which has been annexed as Annexure P7.
The concerned authority considered the representation made by Shri Rattan Lal and also the reply submitted by Shri Bhiwani Parshad and passed the order dated 3091998 by which Shri Bhiwani Parshad has been reverted from the post of Assistant to the post of Clerk and that too on ad hoc basis.
It is this order which has been impugned by Shri Bhiwani Parshad.
5.
So far as Shri Rattan Lal is concerned, through CWP 12403 of 1999, he has impugned the order dated 461996, Annexure P5 and order dated 1261996 annexed as Annexure P6 vide which Shri Sher Singh and Shri Bhiwani Parshad have been regularised on the post of Clerk and have been promoted to the post of Assistant.
In view of the facts narrated here above suffice it to say, that it shall be in the fitness of things if the matter is considered collectively vis-a-vis Bhiwani Parshad, Sher Singh and Rattan Lal.
6.
In short the grievance of Shri Bhiwani Parshad is that respondent No. 1 by passing the impugned order dated 3091998 has ordered his reversion from the post of Assistant to that of Clerk and that too on ad hoc in order to accommodate respondents No. 2 and 3 Shri Rattan Lal and Shri Sher Singh.
In fact, Bhiwani Parshad had been promoted to the post of Clerk from a Class IV post i.e. Peon.
It is admitted case, as averred in para 2 of the reply submitted by respondent No. 1 that the petitioner was senior amongst the Peons in the department as per seniority list as on 1251981 and he had been promoted on ad hoc basis against direct recruits quota vacancies on 3081982.
While passing the impugned order dated 3091998 annexed as Annexure P8, respondent No. 1 incorrectly concluded to the effect that the petitioner Bhiwani Parshad becomes eligible to the post of Clerk only after Shri Rattan Lal, as such Shri Rattan Lal has the right to be regularised first on the post of Clerk.
As a sequel thereto, respondent No. 1 cancelled the orders regularising services of Bhiwani Parshad petitioner to the post of Clerk vide Endst No. ASS/Ett-10-96/12801-44 dated 561996 and also cancelled the order promoting him to the post of Assistant vide Endst No. ASS/Estt-6-98/13502-616 dated 1261996.
So far as the case of Bhiwani Parshad is concerned, he had definitely passed the matriculation examination on 2571982, and as such became eligible to the considered for the post of Clerk which had fallen vacant on 381982 and thereafter on 1281982.
Shri Bhiwani Parshad had rightly requested to be promoted to the said post and he was promoted to the post of Clerk vide order dated 3081982 in the pay scale off Rs. 400-600 on six months basis.
The petitioner Bhiwani Parshad was subsequently regularised on the post of Clerk vide order dated 561996 with effect from 3181982.
Subsequently, the petitioner Bhiwani Parshad was promoted to the post of Assistant in the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600.
The officer considering the case of Bhiwani Parshad fell into error in coming to the conclusion that when the post fell vacant on 381982, Shri Sher Singh was seniormost, whereas, as per the list disclosed in the written statement by respondent No. 1, name of Shri Bhiwani Parshad is given at Serial No. 5 whereas the names of Shri Sher Singh and that of Rattan Lal are given at Serial No. 10 and 23.
The relevant extract from para 2 of the written statement submitted on behalf of respondent No. 1 reads as under :-  Sr. No. Name in the list Date of passing matriculation examination Date of order of ad hoc promotion against direct quota vacancies 5 Bhiwani Parshad 2971982 (2571982 as per copy of matric certificate) 3081982 10 Sher Singh 1381981 3071982 23 Rattan Lal 1121982 3071982 25 Yog Raj 21.9.1984 05.7.1985 31 Rameshwar Dayal 08.7.1971 18.2.1982 33 Munna Lal 0771981 18.2.1982 38 Dharambir 08.7.1971 18.2.1982 40 Dalip Chand 03.7.1973 18.2.1982 44 Prem Singh 08.7.1971 18.2.1982 7.
For considering the claim of all the three vis-a-vis the post which became available on 381982, the seniority as on 1251981 amongst the peons had to be kept in mind.
On 381982, the claim of Shri Bhiwani Parshad was sustainable against the post which fell vacant on 381982.
No doubt all the three officials had become eligible to be considered for promotion to the post of Clerk but on account of seniority the post should have gone to Bhiwani Parshad and the second vacancy should have gone to Shri Sher Singh.
Therefore, Shri Bhiwani Parshad was entitled to be regularised on the post of Clerk with effect from 381982 and Shri Sher Singh was entitled to be regularised with effect from 1281982, the dates on which the posts became available.
In pursuance thereto, their promotions to the post of Assistants also need to be corrected accordingly.
So far as Shri Rattan Lal is concerned, his claim over and above Shri Bhiwani Parshad and Sher Singh is not sustainable.
8.
The learned counsel for Bhiwani Parshad has brought to our notice a Single Bench judgment of this Court rendered in Ram Kumar Sharma v. State of Haryana, 1996(6) SLR 450 : 1996(4) SCT 715 (P&H), wherein it has been said that seniority does not get altered by the date of passing the qualifying examination or acquiring the eligibility.
Names have to be considered in order of seniority as fixed under the rules or the relevant executive orders.
The date of passing the qualifying examination would not determine the seniority or that the names of selected candidates shall be placed in the order in which they have acquired the requisite qualifications.
We concur with the principle enunciated in Ram Kumar Sharma's case (supra).
We, therefore, set aside the impugned office order dated 3091998 (Annexure P8) qua Bhiwani Parshad which orders his reversion from the post of an Assistant to the post of Clerk.
We also set aside the cancellation of the order dated 561996 (Annexure P5) vide which the services of the petitioner had been regularized upon the post of Clerk w.e.f 3181982.
The petition filed by Bhiwani Parshad is consequently allowed with no order as to costs.
9.
So far as CWP 12403 of 1998 filed by Rattan Lal is concerned, the same shall stand dismissed in view of the above observations.
Petition allowed.
