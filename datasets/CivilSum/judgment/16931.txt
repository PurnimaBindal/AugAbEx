RL Anand, J - I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance have gone through the record of this case.
2.
The point in controversy which requires adjudication by this Court is very short.
Petitioner Mrs. Surinder Kaur was seeking promotion with retrospective effect but the same could not be given to her on account of the charge-sheet.
Later on, in the departmental proceedings she has been exonerated and has been given promotion with retrospective effect.
On these premises the case of the petitioner is that since she has been exonerated and has been given promotion with retrospective effect, therefore, she is entitled to back wages also from the date of her promotion.
In support of her case the learned counsel for the petitioner has relied upon 1995(4) AIR 63.
3.
The submission raised by the learned counsel for the petitioner cannot be accepted for the simple reason that the petitioner was charge-sheeted and she did not serve the department when the departmental proceedings were pending against her.
Relying upon the principle of "no work no pay" the petitioner cannot claim the back wages.
Dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
