Ashutosh Mohunta, J - This petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India is directed against the orders dated 25101991 (Annexure P-1) and 1911993 (Annexure P-2) passed by the Joint Secretary to Government of Haryana, Rural Development and Panchayat Department, whereby the petitioner has been ordered to have retired from service prematurely on completion of 55 years of age.
2.
Briefly, the facts of the present case are that the petitioner, who was working as a Clerk/Accounts Clerks with effect from 2.2.1959 in the office of the Block Development and Panchayat Officer, Hissar (for short `the BDPO') was conveyed the adverse remarks recorded by the BDPO in the Annual Confidential Report (for short `the ACR') for the financial year 1982-83.
The petitioner represented against the communication of the adverse remarks.
The Deputy Commissioner, Hisar, accepted his representation and expunged the adverse remarks conveyed to him vide order dated 2241987 (Annexure P-6).
As a result of the expunction of the adverse remarks, the petitioner was allowed to cross the Efficiency Bar, which was due to him with effect from 141983.
The petitioner was also given adverse remarks in the ACR for the year 1988-89 against which he made a representation on 1531990.
The representation was accepted by the Joint Secretary to Government of Haryana, Rural Development & Panchayat Department, vide order dated 2221991 (Annexure P-10) and the honesty and overall assessment of the petitioner was ordered to be treated as `Good'.
Thereafter, the petitioner was sanctioned the Selection Grade vide order dated 3111991 (Annexure P-11) passed by the Joint Secretary of the Department.
Despite the fact that he was allowed to cross the Efficiency Bar with effect from 141983 and was also granted Selection Grade vide order dated 3111991, to his utter dismay, the petitioner was given three months' notice for premature retirement on completion of 55 years of service vide order dated 25101991 (Annexure P-1), the background for which was that he was given adverse remarks in the ACR for the year 1982-83.
The petitioner represented against his premature retirement.
However, vide order dated 311992 (Annexure P-12) passed by the Joint Secretary, the operation of three months' notice for retirement was stayed.
However, vide order dated 1911993 (Annexure P-2), the stay granted vide order dated 311992 was vacated and it was ordered by the Joint Secretary that the petitioner would stand retired from government service on the expiry of three months' period from the date of the order.
The orders dated 25101991 (Annexure P-1) and 1911993 (Annexure P-2) are under challenge in the present writ petition and prayer has been made on behalf of the petitioner that he be retired from service on attainment of 58 years of age and not earlier to that.
3.
It has been contended by the learned counsel for the petitioner that when the adverse remarks given in the ACR for the year 1982-83 had been expunged by the Deputy Commissioner vide order dated 2241987 (Annexure P-6) and the petitioner had been allowed to cross the efficiency bar with effect from 141983 and also he was granted the Selection Grade vide order dated 121991, there was no occasion for the respondent to retire the petitioner prematurely by giving him three months' notice immediately thereafter vide order dated 25101991.
4.
The writ petition has been contested by the respondent-State by filing a detailed written-statement.
It has been contended on behalf of the State that the integrity of the petitioner had adjudged `doubtful' in the ACR for the year 1982-83 and, therefore, the petitioner did not deserve to be continued in service beyond the age of 55 years.
So far as the expunction of the adverse remarks by the Deputy Commissioner is concerned, the plea taken on behalf of the State is that the Deputy Commissioner did not have the power to expunge the adverse remarks as the appointing authority of the petitioner was the Joint Secretary of the Rural Development and Panchayat Department.
5.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and on going through the case file, I find merit in the contentions raised by the learned counsel for the petitioner.
6.
Undisputedly, after expunction of the adverse remarks for the year 1982-83 by the Deputy Commissioner vide order dated 2241987 (Annexure P-6), the petitioner was allowed to cross the efficiency bar with effect from 141983 and he was also granted the selection grade with effect from 141979 vide order dated 3111991 (Annexure P-11).
If the Deputy Commissioner was not Competent Authority to expunge the adverse remarks conveyed to the petitioner, then the respondent-Department immediately ought to have taken notice of it and would not have allowed the petitioner to cross the efficiency bar, which had earlier been stopped because of the adverse remarks given to him in the ACR for the year 1982-83 and he would not have been granted the selection grade vide order dated 121991 (Annexure P-11).
If the Deputy Commissioner was not the Competent Authority to expunge the adverse remarks passed against the petitioner, the Department concerned ought to have immediately come into action.
All through it remained in slumber and nothing adverse was conveyed to the petitioner after the expunction of the adverse remarks by the Deputy Commissioner vide order dated 2241987 (Annexure P-6).
The petitioner came to know about the deficiency in the expunction of adverse remarks by the Deputy Commissioner as back as in April 1987 only after he was given the premature retirement notice vide order dated 25101991.
It shows that the petitioner had not been given due opportunity of hearing with respect to the adverse remarks recorded in the ACR for the year 1982-83.
All through he remained under the impression after the Deputy Commissioner, Hisar had expunged the adverse remarks vide order dated 2241987 (Annexure P-6) that there was nothing adverse against him in the service record.
Rather the authorities allowed him to cross the efficiency bar vide order dated 2961987 and granted him the selection grade vide order dated 3111991.
At this later stage it was not proper for the authorities to retire the petitioner prematurely at the age of 55 years merely on the ground that adverse remarks were recorded in the ACR for the year 1982-83 against the petitioner.
Moreover, in the letter dated 14101987 (Annexure P-9) the Deputy Commissioner had brought to the notice of the Joint Secretary of the Department that vide his office Memo No. 4422-2ECO-II-78/7189 dated 20.10.1978 and 2ECD-80/6981 dated 7.10.1980 powers had been delegated to the Deputy Commissioners.
There is nothing in the written-statement filed on behalf of the respondent to contradict the stand taken by the Deputy Commissioner in his letter dated 14101987 (Annexure P- 9).
In view of this, I am of the considered opinion that the Deputy Commissioner, Hisar, was the Competent Authority to expunge the adverse remarks recorded against the petitioner in the ACR for the year 1982-83 vide letter dated 2241987 (Annexure P-6).
7.
In the light of the above, I allow the writ petition and set aside the orders dated 25101991 (Annexure P-1) and 1911993 (Annexure P-2) passed by the Joint Secretary to Government of Haryana, Development and Panchayat Department.
It has been conveyed to this Court that the petitioner has got retirement on attainment of 58 years of age by virtue of the stay order dated 1121993 passed by this Court.
It is ordered that the petitioner is entitled to the grant of all the retiral benefits and other consequential reliefs which he is eligible for on his retirement at the age of 58 years.
The petitioner is also allowed costs of the petition, which are assessed at Rs. 5,000/-.
Petition allowed.
