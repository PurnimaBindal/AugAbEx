RL Anand, J - Gurpal Singh, Driver, has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for the issuance of a writ of mandamus directing the respondents to grant the petitioner the benefit of Military service he rendered during the operation of proclamation of emergency i.e. 26101962 to January 8, 1968 and December 3, 1971 to August 1975, towards service increments and seniority in accordance with the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 with all consequential benefits.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he joined the Army on 2421960 as a sepoy and served up to 181975 when he was discharged from army on fulfilling the conditions of employment after serving 15 years 158 days.
After release from Army the petitioner was appointed as Driver in the Pepsu Road Transport Corporation on 821978 on permanent basis and till date the service record of the petitioner is good.
The Punjab Government framed Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 vide which the Government was pleased to give benefits towards service increments and pension to Government re-employed ex-servicemen for the period of service they rendered in the army during the operation of proclamation of emergency on 26101962.
Accordingly this benefit was given to almost all the re-employed ex-servicemen.
The petitioner is also entitled for the benefit of service increments and seniority for the service he rendered in the Army during the operation of proclamation of emergency i.e. October 1962 to January 8, 1968 and December 3, 1971 to August 1975.
The petitioner prayed for the benefit of military service.
Such a benefit has already been granted to similarly situated persons but the petitioner has been deprived of this benefit.
The action on the part of the respondent-authority is discriminatory, without jurisdiction and is liable to be set aside.
With this broad allegations the petitioner has come in the present writ petition.
3.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents who denied the allegations of the petitioner.
4.
I have heard Shri Hardip Singh, counsel for the petitioner and Mrs. SK Bhatia, appearing on behalf of the respondents and with their assistance have gone through the record of this case.
5.
It is a common case of the parties that the petitioner is an employee of PRTC, which has adopted the instructions, issued by the State of Punjab.
As per Sections 2 and 4 of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, ex-servicemen is entitled to the benefit of the military service in the matter of seniority and increments etc.
The impression "military service" was construed by the Hon'ble Full Bench of this Court in a case reported as Jang Singh v. State of Punjab, 1997(3) RSJ 464 : 1997(4) SCT 313 (P&H) (FB) and the Hon'ble Full Bench was pleased to hold that the ex-servicemen will be entitled to the benefit of military service which is covered under Rule 2 which will entitle the person for any benefit for the service rendered during the national emergency period.
The Rule clearly indicates that military service will be only service which have been rendered by such officer/official during proclamation of emergency period and not the other service rendered by them in any wings of the Indian Armed Forces of the country.
6.
The ratio of the Full Bench can be made applicable to the facts in hand.
The first emergency came into operation in this country from 26101962 and it was effective up to 8.1.1968.
The second time the emergency was imposed in this country starting from 3.12.1971 and it remained operative and effective up to 181975.
The petitioner joined the army admittedly on 2421960 as a Sepoy.
From 2421960 up to 25.10.1962 the petitioner will not be entitled to the benefit of military service.
However, he will be entitled to the benefit of the military service from 26101962 to 8.1.1968.
Again the petitioner will not be able to get any benefit of the ex-military service from 911968 upto 2.12.1971.
He will, however, be entitled to the benefit of military service from 3.12.1971 to 1.8.1975.
In this view of the matter, the writ is partly allowed and directions are given to the respondents to grant the benefits of military service to the petitioner in the light of the observations made above.
Petition partly allowed.
