The question before the High Court was whether the respondents were entitled to the higher pay scales in terms of the Circular/letter dated July 23, 1957 issued by the erstwhile State of Punjab.
It was not disputed before the High Court that subsequent to the Re-organisation Act, 1966, the said circular was applicable to the teachers employed in the education department in the State of Haryana.
The respondents claim that they were Junior Basic Training Teachers and were in Group I of Category 'B'.
According to them they improved their qualifications and as such were entitled to pay scales of Group II in category 'B'.
Relying upon the Circular/letter dated July 23, 1957, as interpreted by this Court in Punjab Higher Qualified Teachers' Union (Non-petitioners) and Ors. v. State of Punjab and Ors., (1988(2) SCC 407), the High Court allowed the writ petition filed by the respondents and granted them the pay scales asked for by the respondents.
3.
We have been taken through the judgment of the High Court.
We see no ground to interfere.
We agree with the reasoning and the conclusions reached therein.
The appeal and the SLPs are dismissed.
No costs.
