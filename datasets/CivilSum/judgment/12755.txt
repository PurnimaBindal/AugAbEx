RL Anand, J - Present writ petition filed by Pritam Singh deserves to be dismissed on the short ground that as per the opinion of the medical board, his disability was assessed at 15% to 19% as against the statutory requirment of 20%.
Some facts can be noticed in the following manner :-  The petitioner was enrolled in the Territorial Army on 1911978.
He sought discharge from the Territorial Army on 22.3.1981 and thereafter, he joined the Defence Security as a Sepoy on 24.3.1981.
As per the petitioner, he was discharged from the military service on medical grounds on suffering Essential Hypertension on 3131991.
Disability pension claim was submitted to the CCDA who rejected the same on the ground that the disease suffered by the petitioner is neither attributable nor aggravated by the army service, on 26.8.1992.
The petitioner filed an appeal on 2111992 which was also rejected on 6101993 though the petitioner was conveyed about the rejection of the appeal in the year 1998 when he filed a writ petition in the High Court.
With the above allegations, the petitioner has sought the disability pension by seeking the directions of this Court for the quashment of the orders, Annexures P-2 and P-6.
2.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents and the solitary defence taken up by the respondent-authorities is that the extent of disability suffered by the petitioner is less than 20%.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to the disability pension.
3.
I have heard the counsel for the parties and with their assistance have gone through the record of the case.
4.
During the course of submissions, counsel for the respondents has shown to me the opinion of the Medical Board, which was constituted on 311991.
The disability of petitioner was assessed at 15% to 19% for a period of two years for the disease - Essential Hypertension.
The statutory requirement to claim the disability pension is 20% or more.
Since, the disability suffered by the petitioner is less than 20%, therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any disability pension.
Counsel for the petitioner has not been able to show from any document that the opinion of the Medical Board is false or fabricated.
5.
In this view of the matter, the present writ petition is hereby dismissed.
No costs.
Petition dismissed.
