VK Bali, J (Oral) - The surviving claim of the petitioner is only with regard todate of his retirement and consequent thereupon payment of post- retiral benefits as also interest on delayed payments of various post-retiral benefits.
2.
There is no need to give detailed facts of the case and suffice it to say that the petitioner, after serving for 21 years, made a request for premature voluntary retirement vide his application dated 2681994.
When his request was not acceded to, he filed the present writ but during pendency of the writ petition, concededly, his request has been accepted.
He has been retired w.e.f.
30111994.
But his pension and other post-retiral benefits have been calculated as if he has retired on 191995.
The respondents cannot possibly retire the petitioner w.e.f.
30111994 and pay him post-retiral benefits w.e.f.
191995.
This action of the respondents could not be defended during the course of arguments.
It is, thus, ordered that all post- retiral benefits of the petitioner shall be calculated as if he retired on 30111994.
3.
There is no justification for non-payment of post-retiral dues of the petitioner in time.
The payments, as made on various dates with regard to various post-retiral benefits, have been mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d) of para 1 of the preliminary submissions in written statement dated 22.7.1999.
Nothing at all could be urged during the course of arguments that may show any plausible explanation for delaying the post-retiral benefits of the petitioner.
Consequently, the petitioner is held entitled to interest @ 10% per annum three months after the date he retired, i.e. 30111994, till such time the various payments, as mentioned above, were made over to him.
The arrears on account of interest be calculated and made over to the petitioner within six weeks from today.
Disposed of accordingly.
Orders accordingly.
