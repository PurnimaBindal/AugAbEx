NK Kapoor, J - This is defendants' appeal against the judgment dated 8th December, 1978, passed by the Additional District Judge, Amritsar, vide which the judgment and decree of the Sub Judge IInd Class, Amritsar, dated 27th July, 1976, dismissing the suit of the plaintiff has been set aside.
2.
The plaintiff brought a suit for declaration to the effect that the impugned order dated 1st February, 1974, of the Additional Director, Public Relations Department permitting him to cross the efficiency bar with effect from 10th August, 1973, instead of 10th August, 1971, was illegal, void, ultra vires, unconstitutional and against the Civil Service Rules.
The plaintiff further sought consequential relief for recovery of Rs. 320/- in the shape of loss suffered by him in consequence of the above mentioned order regarding the increments from 10th August, 1971 to 10th October, 1974.
3.
The defendants put in appearance and controverted the pleadings of the plaintiff.
It was contended by the defendants that Director, Public Relations Department, on checking the Annual Confidential Reports of the plaintiff, found his conduct to be unsatisfactory as 50 per cent of his Annual Confidential Reports were low average and so the plaintiff was allowed to cross the efficiency bar with effect from 10th August, 1973.
4.
On the pleadings of the parties, the following issues were framed by the trial Court on Ist October, 1975 :  "1.
Whether Additional Director, Public Relations was competent to pass the impugned order ?
O.P.D.
2.
Whether Director, Public Relations, Punjab, checked the confidential reports of the plaintiff and found his work and conduct unsatisfactory as alleged in para No. 4 of the written statement ?
3.
Whether the plaintiff is entitled to the declaration prayed for ?
4.
Relief."
5.
The trial Court under issue No. 1 found the Additional Director, Public Relations, Punjab, as competent to pass the impugned order.
Under issue No. 2 it was held that the Director, Public Relations, on checking the work and conduct of the plaintiff, found the same to be unsatisfactory and accordingly this issue was decided against the plaintiff.
In view of findings on issues No. 2, issues Nos. 3 and 4 were also decided against the plaintiff.
6.
The lower appellate Court, by a well-considered judgment, reversed the findings of the trial Court on the material issue i.e. issue No. 2 thereby accepted the appeal and reversed the judgment and decree of the trial Court.
7.
Before me, the learned counsel for the appellants has not been able to point out any infirmity in the impugned judgment.
All he has stressed is that the work of the plaintiff was found to be unsatisfactory by the Director, Public Relations, Punjab.
The learned counsel, however, has not been able to refer to any document on record by which it could be inferred that the competent authority applied his mind in this regard.
The Annual Confidential Reports of the plaintiff i.e. Exhibit DW 2/4 for the year 1965-66, Exhibit DW 2/6 for the year 1966-67, Exhibit DW 2/7 for the year 1967-68, Exhibit DW 2/8 for the year 1968-69, Exhibit DW 2/9 for the year 1969-70 and exhibit DW 2/10 for the year 1971-72, referred by the learned counsel for the appellants, rather, support the case of the plaintiff.
In all these reports, the performance of the plaintiff is adjudged as 'average'.
There is no report of adverse remarks against the plaintiff nor there is any material on record by which it could be deduced that his performance was "below average."
8.
Consequently, I find no merit in this appeal and hereby dismiss the same.
No costs.
Appeal dismissed.
