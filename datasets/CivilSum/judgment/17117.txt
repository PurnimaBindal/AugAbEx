RL Anand, J - This writ petition has been filed by Smt. Saroj widow of late Shri Om Parkash son of Shri Dharam Singh under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
She has prayed for the quashment of the orders Annexures P-7, P-8, P-10, P-11, P-12 and P-13 and petitioner Smt. Saroj has further sought the directions of this Court to release the family pension as well as the amount of Army Group Insurance Scheme.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that her husband Om Parkash was enrolled in the J.K.
Rifles on 3051983.
When was posted at Jabalpur, on 961987 he received the orders regarding his posting at Ladhak and the petitioner was directed to report at Transit Camp No. 213 and he was granted six days leave also.
On 1061987 when the husband of the petitioner was on leave he received another intimation from the respondent-authorities that in spite of reporting at Transit Camp No. 213 he should report at Transit Camp No. 229.
After availing six days leave, the husband of the petitioner reported at Camp No. 229 on 1561987.
When he was still there, on 2481987 the husband of the petitioner received an intimation from the village that his father had met with an accident.
Om Parkash then obtained the permission from his Commanding Officer of Camp No. 229 and returned to village where he reached on 2581987.
The luck was not smiling upon Om Parkash.
Unfortunately, on 3181987 when he was returning to Transit Camp No. 229, he met with an accident and he died on the way when he was being taken to Medical College/Hospital, Rohtak.
The military authorities certified that Shri Om Parkash died while he was in service on account of head injury and the death was attributable to the military service.
The widow of the petitioner applied for family pension and also for the benefits under the Group Insurance Scheme but her request was declined on the ground that Shri Om Parkash, the husband of the petitioner, became a deserter because he did not report at Transit Camp No. 213 as ordered by the authorities.
Since he became a deserter he is also not entitled to the benefit of the Group Insurance Scheme.
3.
I have heard Shri I.D.
Singla, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner and Shri Anil Malhotra, appearing on behalf of the respondents.
4.
The short point for determination is that did the husband of the petitioner ever receive the order that he should report at Transit Camp No. 229 or not ?
5.
During the course of submissions, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner had invited my attention to the original orders dated 1061987 issued by Captain Shri RP Singh vide which the husband of the petitioner was directed to report at Transit Camp No. 229 instead of Transit Camp No. 213.
6.
The reading of this order would show that this order was issued in substitution of the earlier order Annexure P15 vide which the husband of the petitioner was directed to report at Transit Camp No. 213.
With the issuance of the order dated 1061987, it has to be inferred that the husband of the petitioner was directed to report at Transit Camp No. 229 and he was not supposed to report at Transit Camp No. 213.
7.
In these circumstances, the stand of the respondent that the husband of the petitioner became a deserter is untenable in the eyes of law.
In this case, the husband of the petitioner had reported at Transit Camp No. 229.
He was given leave to go to his village in order to look after his father.
He returned to his village and when he was again going to report at Transit Camp No. 229 on 3181987, he met with an accident.
The death of Shri Om Parkash has been certified as having taking place when he was in Army service, on account of head injury.
8.
In this view of the matter, the writ is allowed by setting aside the impugned orders and the directions are given to the respondent-authorities to release the family pension, benefit of the Group Insurance Scheme and also the other benefits such as death-cum-retirement gratuity to the petitioner, within three months from the date of the receipt of the copy of this order.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
