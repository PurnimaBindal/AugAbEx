Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - The petitioners in these eight writ petitions have a common grievance.
They challenge the order of the re-fixation of their pay by which their pay has been reduced to the extent of the enhanced pension granted to them.
Learned counsel for the petitioners places firm reliance on the decision of their Lordships of the Supreme Court in Civil Appeal Nos. 3543-3548 of 1990 Union of India and others v. G Vasudevan Pillay and others decided on December 8, 1994 : 1995(2) SCT 842(SC).
Mr. Rathi appearing for the respondents concedes that in view of the decision of the Apex Court, the writ petitions have to be allowed.
2.
Accordingly, it is held that the impugned order, a copy of which has been produced as Annexure P-1, is quashed to the extent the respondents deducted the enhanced military pension from the salary of the petitioners.
The respondents are directed to refix the pay of the petitioners without deducting the amount of enhanced military pension.
The petitions are accordingly, disposed of.
In the circumstances of these cases, there will be no order as to costs.
