Ashutosh Mohunta, J - A reference was made by the Labour Court for deciding the dispute between Kamlajit Singh and Punjab Power Generation Machines Limited, Village and Post Office Channo, District Sangrur.
During the pendency of the case before the Labour Court, Patiala, the Crompton Greaves Limited took over the entire management of Punjab Power Generation Machines Limited.
They filed an application for being impleaded as a party as they had taken over the management.
The application was dismissed by the Presiding Officer, Labour Court, Patiala vide order dated 592000.
2.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has relied on Sub-Divisional Inspector of Post v. Theyyam Joseph, 1996(3) SCT 786 (SC) : 1996(2) S.C.R. 95 as well as The Pepsu Road Transport Corporation v. Presiding Officer, 1991(3) SCT 122 (P&H) : 1991(2) R.S.J.
173 to contend that the Labour Court has the power to implead any party, which is necessary for just and fair decision of the case.
3.
In view of the aforesaid authorities, I allow the present revision and direct that Crompton Greaves Limited be impleaded as a party before the Labour Court, Patiala.
Petition is allowed.
Petition allowed.
