GR Majithia, J - The Petitioners have challenged the promotion policy contained in Office Order No. 687/NGE/G-761 dated August 9, 1974, issued by the Haryana State Electricity Board in this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
2.
The Haryana State Electricity Board (for short, the Board) in the policy decision under challenge provided that the posts of Assistant Engineer II shall be filled up in the following manner :-  (i) Direct recruitment; 65%.
(ii) Promotion from amongst Diploma Holders with Electrical/Mechanical qualifications 20%.
(iii) A.M.I.Es.
(full) 5%.
(iv) Drawing staff, non-Diploma holders, others on merits on the basis of the recommendations of the TA 10%.
"This is subject to the provisions that the officials at item No. (ii) above must have rendered 5 years service in the Board out of which one year must be as Junior Engineer provided further that if the existing Junior Engineers are found unsuitable for promotion to the rank of AEII then appropriate steps under the rules may be taken to revert them so that they are not able to block the promotion chance of those persons Junior to them.
In the case of item No. (iii) above, the officials must have passed the AMIE Examination in full while in the service of the Board and also provided that they shall be eligible for consideration for promotion to the rank of AEII from the date they pass the AMIE examination in full.
In the case of item No. (iv) above, the officials must have rendered 10 years service in the Board.
The mode of promotion to the rank of Maintenance Engineers shall remain unchanged.

3.
Petitioner No. 1 was appointed as Line Superintendent vide order dated July 24, 1961 petitioner No. 2 was appointed as Assistant Lineman vide order dated December 19, 1951.
He was promoted to the rank of Lineman Grade II on May 28, 1956 and Lineman Grade I on April 22, 1959.
He was promoted as Line Superintendent vide order dated June 22, 1962.
Petitioner No. 3 was appointed as Lineman Grade II in the P.W.D.
Electricity Branch on November 13, 1962.
He was promoted as Line Superintendent on January 27, 1961 vide order dated January 19, 1961, but he was reverted as Lineman Grade II on August 12, 1961.
He was again promoted as Lineman Grade I on December 24, 1963.
He was appointed as Line Superintendent vide order dated July 1, 1964.
The petitioners were assigned seniority at serial Nos. 167, 192 and 265 respectively in the seniority list of Line Superintendents.
All the Line Superintendents whether Diploma holders or Non-Diploma holders are members of the same class of service and are governed by the same conditions of service as they constitute one class for the purpose of promotion.
The Board has made reservation for posts for Diploma holders and non-Diploma holders in an arbitrary manner.
Once the Diploma holders and the non-Diploma holders are appointed to the same post, they cannot be discriminated for further promotions.
4.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents, it is stated that petitioners No. 1 and 2 were promoted as Junior Engineers under the amended policy on January 30, 1979 and March 8, 1979, respectively.
Petitioner No. 3 is a non-Diploma holder and no one junior to him in the cadre of Line Superintendent (Non-Diploma holder) has been promoted.
It is admitted that the policy decision under challenge was quashed by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 4013 of 1978 on May 20, 1980.
However, it is submitted that Special Leave to Appeal Petition No. 7045 of 1980 (Haryana State Electricity Board v. Dharamvir Yadav etc.) has already been filed against the decision of this Court in the aforementioned writ petition.
The SLP was admitted on September 15, 1980 and the following directions were issued by the apex Court :-  "The application for stay above-mentioned being called on for hearing before this Court on the 15th day of September, 1980, UPON hearing counsel for the parties above-mentioned THIS COURT DOTH ORDER that pending the hearing and final disposal by this Court of the Appeal above-mentioned (1) the promotions already made which were the subject-matter of dispute in Civil Writ Petition No. 4013 of 1978 on the file of the Punjab and Haryana High Court at Chandigarh shall not be disturbed, but (2) the future operation of the judgment and order dated 20th May, 1980 of the Punjab and Haryana High Court at Chandigarh made in Civil Writ Petition No. 4013 of 1978 be and is hereby suspended, and (3) any promotions which may be made hereafter in the department involved shall abide by the judgment of this Court in the appeal above-mentioned.
AND THIS COURT DOTH FURTHER ORDER that this Order be hereby observed and carried into execution by all concerned."
It is further stated that another SLP No. 6740 of 1980 has been filed against the decision rendered by this Court in L.P.A. No. 618 of 1975 on May 5, 1980.
The same was admitted on September 15, 1980 and is pending in the Supreme Court.
The case of the petitioners for promotion from anterior date will be taken up after the decision of the aforementioned S.L.Ps.
5.
Petitioners No. 1 and 2 have been promoted.
No person junior to petitioner No. 3 has been promoted and as such he has got no subsisting grievance at this stage.
As admitted by the Board, the petitioners' case for promotion from anterior date will be taken up for consideration after the decision of the S.L.Ps.
by the apex Court.
The petitioners are permitted to make an appropriate application before the Board and the same will be disposed of by the latter in accordance with the Rules.
With these observations, the writ petition is disposed of.
