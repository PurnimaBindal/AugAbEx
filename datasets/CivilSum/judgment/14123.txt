Notice to show cause why the petition be not admitted.
3.
Mr. Amol Rattan, Assistant Advocate General appearing for the State of Haryana accepts notice.
4.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties.
5.
The challenge in this writ petition filed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India is to an order dated October 29, 2001 (Annexure P-3) passed by the Special Secretary.
Government of Haryana, Labour Department vide which the said authority declined to make a reference of the workman in furtherance to the demand notice to the Labour Court/Tribunal in terms of Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (hereinafter referred to as `the Act') on the ground that ".....It has been found that you have raised dispute after lapse of a year of your termination of your service and you have not given any cogent reason for such delay...."  6.
The above reason given by the said authority is contrary to the principles of law as enunicated by the Hon'ble Apex Court in the case of Ajaib Singh v. The Sirhind Co-operative Marketing-cum-Processing Service Society Ltd and another, AIR 1999 Supreme Court 1351 : 1999(2) SCT 667 (SC).
7.
In view of the settled law, we have no hesitation in setting aside the said order which we hereby do and direct the said authority to pass an order afresh in accordance with law after hearing the parties.
8.
The petition is disposed of without any order as to costs.
Petition disposed of.
