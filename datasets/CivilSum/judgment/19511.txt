GC Mittal, CJ and Sat Pal, J. - In the present writ petition the petitioners have challenged the decision dated 9th November, 1991, Annexure- P-5 to the writ petition of the respondent-Corporation of challenging the basis of seniority of the petitioners from the basis of date of selection to the date of appointment.
2.
It is not disputed before us that Regulation 16(1) of the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971 is applicable to the employees of the Corporation including the petitioners for the purposes of seniority.
In terms of the aforesaid Regulation the seniority of direct recruits has to be determined in order of merit in which they are selected for such appointment and the persons appointed as a result of earlier selection are to be considered senior to the persons appointed through a similar subsequent selection.
Thus, the date of selection of the merit in the selection is to take precedent for the subsequent selections.
Mr. Bhatia, the learned counsel for the respondent, has contended that the impugned decision has been taken by the respondent-Corporation.
In view of the fact that as per practice prevailing in Punjab Region the seniority list is prepared on the basis of the date of appointment and not on the basis of the date of selection.
We are of the view that in view of the statutory Regulation 16(1) of the Regulations the seniority has to be fixed on the basis of the date of selection as stated herein above and not on the basis of the date of appointment.
3.
In view of the aforesaid reasons, the writ petition is allowed and we direct the respondent-Corporation publish the seniority list of the petitioners on the basis of the date of selection in terms of Regulation 16(1) of the Food Corporation of India Staff Regulations, 1971.
Petition allowed.
