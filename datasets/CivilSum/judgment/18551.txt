SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - We have heard learned counsel for the parties at length.
2.
In reply to para 9 of the writ petition, respondent No. 2 has categorically stated that minimum required qualification was B Pharmacy which the petitioner obtained on 2821994.
Therefore, his experience in manufacture had to be counted from 2821994 onwards.
As per the certificate furnished on 971999, his experience as Assistant Manufacturing Chemist in Injectable and Ophthalmic is made out for 13 months, which relates to the period from 131994 to 3131995.
The required experience under Rule 49 of Drugs and Cosmetic Rules, 1945 was 18 months.
The petitioner had not been given the benefit of 25 months experience by clubbing the experience gained prior to the degree qualification.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that under Rule 49, there is no distinction with regard to the experience whether it is before or after the graduation qualification.
3.
We are unable to accept the submission made by the learned counsel for the petitioner.
Rule 49 has to be read with Rule 76 of the said Rules which provides that for being given a licence of a manufacturing chemist, a person has to be Graduate in Pharmacy or Pharmaceutical Chemistry with atleast 18 months practical experience after the Graduation in the manufacture of drugs to which the licence applies.
We are unable to read Rule 49 isolated from Rule 76 of the said Rules.
4.
In view of the above, we find no merit in this petition which is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
