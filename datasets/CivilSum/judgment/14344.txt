GS Singhvi, J - This petition has been filed for quashing the order dated 1141998 passed by the Government under Rule 8 of the Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987 read with Rule 4 thereof imposing punishment of stoppage of one annual grade increment without cumulative effect upon the petitioner.
2.
The petitioner has challenged the impugned order primarily on the ground that it does not contain reasons and therefore, it is violative of the principles of natural justice.
3.
After hearing Shri SP Singh, we are convinced that the writ jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution does not deserve to be exercised for invalidation of the impugned order because the petitioner has failed to explain the delay of more than 2 years and 3 months in the filing of the writ petition.
In the writ petition not a word has been said why the petitioner kept silence for a long period of over two years before filing the writ petition.
The petitioner does not belong to a class of persons who are unable to seek remedy in a Court of law due to ignorance, poverty or such like circumstances.
Therefore, we do not find any justification to grant indulgence to him by exercising extraordinary jurisdiction of this Court for over-looking the delay of 2 years and 3 months particularly when he has failed to take recourse to the remedy available to him under Rule 14(2) of 1987 Rules.
For the reasons mentioned above, the writ petition is dismissed.
However, liberty is given to the petitioner to avail remedy in a civil Court for invalidation of the impugned order.
Petition dismissed.
