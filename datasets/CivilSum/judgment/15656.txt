GS Singhvi, J - In this petition filed under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioners have prayed for issuance of a writ of mandamus to the respondents to grant them revised pay scales with effect from 111986 instead of 3111989 and also to pay arrears with interest at the rate of 24% per annum.
They have also prayed for directing the respondents to grant revised pay scale of Rs. 950-1800/- with initial start of Rs. 1000/- to petitioner Nos. 12 to 18 from the date they acquired the qualification of matric with ITI diploma.
Still further, they have prayed that the respondents be directed to grant them proficiency step-up with increments.
2.
The facts relevant to the decision of the prayers made by the petitioners are that they joined services of the Municipal Corporation, Ludhiana on different dates and that presently they are working as Electricians, Mechanics, Fitters, Welders, Blacksmits, Helpers, Cleaners and Tyremen.
They were drawing salary in the pay scales of Rs. 950-1800/- and Rs. 770-1400/- as on 111986.
They were allowed revised pay scales of Rs. 1200-2100/-, Rs. 950-1800/- and Rs. 770-1400/- with effect from 3111989.
The particulars of the dates of appointment and the pay scales of the petitioners have been set out in the statement which has been placed on the record as Annexure P-1.
In the writ petiti9on, it has been averred that the petitioners are entitled to the benefit of revised pay scales with effect from 111986 as was done by the State Government in the cases of its employees and as was given to the employees of the Workshop Category of the Punjab Roadways in pursuance of the order dated 1321996 passed by a Division Bench of this Court in C.W.P.
No. 15920 of 1995, Ram Murti and others v. State of Punjab and others.
The petitioners have further averred that vide order Annexure P-5 dated 731994 issued under Rule 3(h) of the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay Scales) Rules, 1988, the State Government has granted the benefit of revised pay scales to the persons employed in different categories under the Municipal Corporations, Municipal Committees, Notified Area Commitees and Improvement Trusts with effect from 3111989 except to those employed as Inspectors, Chief Sanitary Inspectors/Sanitary Inspectors and this action of the government is ex facie discriminatory because similarly situated employees of Punjab Roadways have been allowed revised pay scales with retrspective effect.
Still further, the petitioners have averred that in view of the order dated 1.12.1997 passed by a learned Single Judge in C.W.P.
No. 16973 of 1991, such of them who have passed Matric with ITI diploma are eligible to be given initial start of Rs. 1000/- with effect from the acquisition of qualifications.
They have also referred to the letter Annexure P-8 dated 1121988 issued by the State Government for grant of proficiency step-up after completion of 8 years and 18 years of service and have pleaded that failure of the government to apply those instructions is violative of their fundamental right to equality guaranteed under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
3.
The respondents have not filed written statement and, therefore, we shall proceed on the premise that they do not want to contest the petition on facts.
4.
Shri R.C. Charath invited our attention to the orders passed by the Division Bench in Ram Murti's case (supra); the order dated 21.4.1997 passed by the Supreme Court in S.L.P.
(C) No. 13167 of 1996 - State of Punjab v. Ram Murti and others; the order dated 1.12.1997 passed by a learned Single Judge in C.W.P.
No. 16973 of 1991 - Amarjit Singh and others v. State of Punjab and others and submitted that in view of the law laid down by a Co-ordinate Bench, the petitioners are entitled to grant of revised pay scale with effect from 111986.
Learned Deputy Advocate General could not point out any distinguishing feature between the case of the petitioners and that of Ram Murti etc. Rather, he half-heartedly conceded that in view of the retrospective revision of pay scales granted to similarly situated employees of Punjab Roadways, the petitioners can legitimately claim the benefit of revised pay scales with effect from 111986.
He, however, submitted that other prayers made by the petitioners may be left to be decided by the competent authority of the government.
5.
In view of the above, we allow the writ petition and direct that the petitioners be given revised pay scales of Rs. 1200-2100/-, Rs. 950-1800/- and Rs. 770-1400/- with effect from 111986 instead of 3111989.
The respondents are directed to pass appropriate order in this respect within four months of the submission of certified copy of this order.
However, the arrears payable to the petitioners as a result of revised fixation of their pay shall be confined to a period of three years counted from the date of filing of the writ petition i.e. 8.7.1999.
As regards the petitioners' prayer for grant of higher initial start to petitioner Nos. 12 and 18 and the proficiency step-up increment, we direct the respondents to take appropriate decision within four months from the date of submission of certified copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
