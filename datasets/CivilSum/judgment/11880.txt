TS Doabia, J - This is a petition under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India.
2.
The brief facts are noticed as under :  The petitioner was working as conductor with respondent-Corporation.
He worked from 14th April, 1980 to 4th of September, 1981.
His services were terminated.
He raised an industrial dispute.
The Labour Court granted the relief of re-instatement with 50% back wages.
The respondent No. 1 preferred an appeal before the respondent No. 4.
This appeal was allowed.
3.
During the period when the order passed by the Labour Court and till the order was reversed by the Industrial Court, the petitioner was in service.
4.
It may be seen that the petitioner had completed more than 240 days in one calendar year.
As noticed above the petitioner served the Corporation from 14th of April 1980 to 4th September, 1981.
This fact has not been disputed.
In view of the law laid down by the Supreme Court in the case of State Bank of India v. N Sundra Money, (1976)1 SCC 822 : (AIR 1976 SC 1111); L Robert D'Souza v. Executive Engineer Southern Rlys, (1982)1 SCC 645 : (AIR 1982 SC 854); Mohan Lal v. Management of M/s. Bharat Electronics Ltd, (1981)3 SCC 225 : (AIR 1981 SC 1253); HD Singh v. RBI, AIR 1986 SC 132; Punjab Land Dev.
and Reclamation Corpn.
Ltd., Chandigarh v. Presiding Officer, Labour Court, Chandigarh, (1990)3 SCC 682, the termination is in violation of the provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 as made applicable to the State of Madhya Pradesh under the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Relations Act, 1961.
5.
This petition is allowed to the extent indicated above.
6.
Taking into consideration the above legal position, it would be just and proper to order reinstatement of the petitioner but without back wages.
Petition allowed.
