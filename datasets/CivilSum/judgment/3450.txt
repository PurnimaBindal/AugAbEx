GR Majithia, J - This judgment will dispose of C.W.P.
No. 14476 of 1990 and 2765 of 1991 since identical questions of law and fact arise for determination therein.
2.
Reference to relevant facts has been made from the pleadings in C.W.P.
Nos. 14476 of 1990 :-  The petitioners who are working as Assistants in the office of Engineer-in-Chief, Haryana, P.W.D., B & R Branch, Chandigarh (for short, the Head Office) have challenged the promotion of respondent No. 3 Shri Dharam Paul Mathuria, Assistant in the Head Office as officiating Deputy Superintendent and also the order dated November 7, 1990 issued by the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana Public Works ( Buildings & Roads) Department to effect that the instructions issued by the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana vide letter No. 22/2/87-3GSIII dated August 11, 1988 does not envisage a new roster and the scheme contained in Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter No. 38/90/78-2GSI dated February 9, 1979 continues, in this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
3.
The facts :-  The petitioners are working as Assistants in the Head Office; that they joined the service on December 9, 1963, August 4, 1965 and October 31, 1966 respectively but they were allocated to Haryana on the re-organisation of the State; that respondent No. 3 joined the service on August 6, 1971; that the Governor of Haryana in exercise of powers under the proviso to Article 311 of the Constitution of India framed rules for regulating the recruitment and conditions of service of the persons appointed to the Haryana Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads Branch) called the Haryana Public Works Department ( Buildings and Roads Branch) Headquarter Office, Ministerial Service (Group C) Rules, 1979 (for short, the Rules); that these rules were published in the Haryana Government Gazette Notification dated February 20, 1980; that rule 2(g) of the Rules says that "Service" means Haryana Public Works Department, Buildings and Roads Branch (State Service Group-C, Ministerial Establishment of Subordinate Offices); that rule 3 of the Rules says that the Service shall comprise of the posts shown in Appendix 'A' to the rules; that sub-rule (3) of rule 9 envisages that all promotions, whether from one grade or from another or from one class of service to another class of service, shall be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness; that by Government Order contained in Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter No. 38/20/78/2GSI, dated February 9, 1979, reservation of posts for Schedule Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-Servicemen under the services of Haryana State was made; that in clause (6) of Para 1 of the Order it is provided that in the case of posts to be filled by way of promotion, the benefit of reservation should be given where the basis of promotion is seniority-cum-fitness; that in compliance with the Government's order, roster was prepared regarding posts to be filled up by promotion on the basis of seniority-cum-merit; that by Government's order contained in letter No. 22/2/87-3GS-III, dated 1181988 para 1(6) of Government's letter No. 38/20/78-2GSI dated February 9, 1979 was deleted; that the Engineer-in-Chief, Public Works Department (Buildings & Roads Branch) sought clarification from the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Public Works (Buildings & Roads) Department whether a fresh roster had to be prepared in the light of the Government's letter No. 22/2/87-3GS-III, dated August 11, 1988 and he was informed that the Govt. instructions contained in letter No. 22/2/87-3GSIII, dated August 11, 1988 did not at all envisage a new roster and the scheme contained in Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter No. 38/90/78-2GSI dated February 9, 1979 was to continue; that the petitioner apprehending that the respondent No. 3 is likely to be promoted as officiating Deputy Superintendent in supersession of the petitioner's claim, filed a representation to respondent No. 2 that since the Government's order dated August 11, 1988 applies prospectively and not retrospectively, the roster has to be prepared after that date and not from a prior date; that petitioner No. 1 and others filed C.W.P.
No. 14394 of 1990 in this court, which was disposed of on November 9, 1990 with the following observations :-  "After hearing the learned counsel, we find that the representation dated 441990, copy Annexure P-3 with the writ petition is still pending consideration with the respondent authorities and has not been decided so far.
The learned counsel contends that on one side the representation has been kept pending and while the post of Deputy Superintendent, Head Office is going to be filled within a few days.
No decision one way or the other has since been taken by the respondent authorities as yet.
We, therefore, direct the respondents to decide the representation of the petitioners in accordance with law by passing speaking order expeditiously probably within one month from today.
Till then the post of Deputy Superintendent shall not be filled up.
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly."
That the aforesaid order was passed by the High Court at 1115 am and the petitioner No. 1 personally conveyed the order to respondent No. 2 and submitted a certificate issued by the counsel for the petitioners to him, that respondent No. 2 out of fear or reprisal from the Minister, Public Health Department, issued the order the promotion of respondent No. 3 as Deputy Superintendent on November 9, 1990 without disposing of the representation filed by petitioner No. 1 and others.
4.
Respondent Nos. 1 and 2 in their written statement admitted that in accordance with the rules promotion by selection has to be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness; that they also admitted that the Government order dated February 9, 1979 by which reservation was made for Scheduled Caste, Backward Classes and Ex-servicesmen was not to apply where the basis of promotion was seniority-cum-fitness.
However, it was stated that para 1(6) of the Government's instructions dated February 9, 1979 was deleted vide Government's letter No. 22/2/87/3GSIII, dated August 11, 1988.
It was denied that the letter August 11, 1988 envisaged the preparation of a fresh roster.
The roster had been prepared in the light of Government's letter dated February 9, 1979.
The representation of the petitioner was rejected on November 9, 1990 and thereafter the order of promotion of respondent No. 3 as officiating Deputy Superintendent was passed.
The allegation regarding extraneous pressure from the Minister-in-charge was denied.
5.
Indisputably under the rules, the promotions have to be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fiteness.
The Government's instructions contained in Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated February 9, 1979 regarding reservation of posts for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-servicemen under the service of Haryana State were not applicable where the promotion was to be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness.
This clause in the Chief Secretary to Government letter dated February 9, 1979 was deleted by Government letter No. 22/2/87-3GSIII dated August 11, 1988.
The resultant effect was that the reservation of posts for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-servicemen which was made vide Government's letter dated February 9, 1979 was also made applicable to promotions made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness.
Respondent sought clarification from respondent No. 1 whether in the light of Government's letter dated August 11, 1988 roster had to be prepared for promotional posts in the service mentioned in the rules.
Respondent No. 1 did not express any opinion in this behalf.
The Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana Public Works (Buildings and Roads) Department informed respondent No. 3 vide memo No. 1/2/90-4 B and R (W) dated November 7, 1990; that the Government's instructions issued vide letter No. 22/2/87-3GSIII dated August 11, 1988 did not at all envisage preparation of a new roster and that the scheme contained in Chief Secretary to Government's Haryana letter No. 38/90/78/2GSI dated February 9, 1979 was to continue.
On the basis of this letter respondent No. 2 promoted respondent No. 3 to the post of officiating Deputy Superintendent on the roster point.
6.
Respondent No. 2 was in error in promoting respondent No. 3 on the roster point allegedly prepared under the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated February 9, 1979.
That order did not provide for reservation of posts for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-servicemen under the service of Haryana State where the promotion under the rules was to be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness.
The promotions in the Service under the rules applicable to the petitioners and respondent No. 3 were to be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness.
Consequently, the question of preparation of roster did not arise.
Para 1(6) of the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated February 9, 1979 was deleted by the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated August 11, 1988.
After the deletion of this para reservation was to be made for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-servicemen even where the promotion was to be made on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness.
Respondent No. 3 who is junior to the petitioners claimed promotion on a roster point over the petitioners in the light of the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated August 11, 1988.
In order to give benefit of reservation for promotional posts in the service under the Rules, roster has to be prepared in the light of Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated February 9, 1988.
But the roster had to be made applicable with effect from August 11, 1988 and all the promotions made after this date, reservation for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-servicemen has to be provided for.
The Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Public Works (Buildings and Roads) Department did not correctly understand the scope of the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated August 11, 1988.
He had no right to nullify the effect of this order.
Under this order, para 1(6) of the Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated February 9, 1979 was deleted and reservation for Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes and Ex-servicemen in the promotional posts was made applicable.
Once the reservation has been made applicable, it clearly envisages that the roster had to be prepared.
Respondent No. 2 acting on the advice notionally prepared the roster with regard to the vacancies with effect from February 9, 1979 and ordered the promotion of Respondent No. 3 to the post of officiating Deputy Superintendent.
The action of respondent No. 2 in promoting respondent No. 3 to the post of officiating Deputy Superintendent is wholly contrary to the instructions contained in Haryana Government's letters dated February 9, 1979 and August 11, 1988.
7.
The allegation of the petitioners that respondent No. 2 contravened the order passed by this Court in C.W.P.
No. 14394 of 1990 on November 9, 1990 and promoted respondent No. 3 to the post of officiating Deputy Superintendent without first deciding their representation, as required under the High Court's Order, has substance.
The defence taken by respondent Nos. 1 and 2 that certified copy of the High Court's order was not produced and they did not rely upon the uncertified copy of the order cannot be appreciated.
If respondent No. 2 doubted the genuineness of the certificate issued by the counsel for the petitioners, he ought to have deferred the question of appointment of respondent No. 3 to the next higher post till a certified copy of the High Court's order was produced before him.
It appears that respondent No. 2 was under pressure to promote respondent No. 3 without delay and the haste in which he acted in promoting respondent No. 3 as officiating Deputy Superintendent indicates the extraneous pressure put on him.
But I leave the matter at this since I have otherwise found that the promotion of respondent No. 3 to the post of Officiating Deputy Superintendent on the roster point was wholly illegal.
8.
For the reasons stated above, the writ petitions succeed and are allowed with no order as to costs.
The office order dated November 9, 1990 passed by respondent No. 2, promoting respondent No. 3 to the post of Officiating Deputy Superintendent is quashed.
Respondent No. 2 will prepare a roster for the promotional post in the service to which the Rules are applicable, with effect from August 11, 1988 and the reservation for promotional post after this date will be made keeping in view the instructions contained in Chief Secretary to Government, Haryana letter dated February 9, 1979 but it would be made applicable with effect from August 11, 1988.
Petitions allowed.
