MM Kumar, J - This petition filed under Article 226 of the Constitution challenges order dated 2851984 passed by the respondent-State revising the date of promotion from 2481972 to 2111973 on the post of Head Assistant in the office of Transport Commissioner, Haryana.
A further prayer has been made that the impugned order dated 2851984 (Annexure P-2) be quashed and the petitioner be assigned the date of promotion as 2481972 on the post of Head Assistant.
2.
Brief facts of the case are that the petitioner joined as a Clerk in 1959 through the Subordinate Services Selection Board and was posted in the Transport Department.
The petitioner belongs to the reserved category of Scheduled Caste.
He was promoted on the post of Assistant w.e.f.
871965.
On the re-organisation of the State of Punjab, a new State of Haryana was created w.e.f.
1.11.1966 and he was allocated to Haryana.
On the basis of representation made by the petitioner claiming further promotion to the post of Head Assistant, he was promoted on a reserved post as Head Assistant vide order dated 29.11.1973 in pursuance to the instructions issued on 10121971.
A perusal of his promotion order (Annexure P-1) shows that he was given assumed date of promotion as Head Assistant w.e.f.
2481972 because according to instructions dated 10121971 every third vacancy in a lot of five vacancies was ear-marked for the reserved category of Scheduled Caste.
As a consequence of his promotion with a deemed date of 2481972, he claims to have become senior to Amrit Singh Chadha who was promoted much later and respondent No. 3 KS Mukhra who was promoted on 311973.
The petitioner has asserted that once Amrit Singh Chadha and KS Mukhra were rendered juniors to the petitioner, then they could not be given promotion on the post of Superintendent on 2011978 and 10.3.1978 respectively without first considering the case of the petitioner.
He has stated to have made representation in this regard.
His representation was accepted and he was given assumed date of promotion as Superintendent as 2011978, the date which has been given to Amrit Singh Chadha.
His seniority was also fixed over and above Amrit Singh Chadha.
However, the order giving deemed date of promotion to the petitioner was challenged in appeal by KS Mukhra which resulted into passing of impugned order on 2851984 withdrawing the order giving assumed date of promotion to the petitioner as 2011978.
Shri KS Mukhra was aggrieved by the order as he was reverted from the post of Superintendent to the post of Head Assistant.
Accepting his claim was accepted which was to the effect that after the issuance of instructions dated 10121971, the first vacancy arose on 131972, when Joginder Singh Head Assistant was promoted and the second and third vacancy accrued when Neta Ram and KC Gupta Head Assistants were promoted on 111973 on the post of Superintendent.
It was further explained that against the first vacancy dated 131972 KC Gupta was promoted as Head Assistant, the second vacancy was filled up by promotion of KS Mukhra and on the third vacancy OP Nandwani was promoted as Head Assistant.
The operative part of the impugned order dated 2851984 (Annexure P-2) reads as under :-  "4.
According to the instructions for reservation, the third vacancy of Head Asstt.
after 10.12.71 had to go to the member of Scheduled Caste.
The position of vacancies which occurred after 10121971 emerged as under :- (1) First vacancy of Head Asstt.
become available on 131972 when Sh. Joginder Singh, Head Asstt.
was promoted as Superintendent in Haryana Roadways, Chandigarh.
(2) The second vacancy of Head Assistant became available on 111973 when Sh. Neta Ram Head Asstt.
was promoted as Superintendent Jind.
(3) The third vacancy also became available on 111973 when Sh. KC Gupta, Head Asstt.
was promoted as Supdt.
Rewari.
5.
Against the first vacancy Sh. KC Gupta was promoted as Head Assistant, against the second vacancy, Sh. KS Mukhra was promoted as Head Assistant and against the third vacancy Shri OP Nandwani was promoted as Head Asstt.
Thus in view of these facts Shri Lachhman Dass becomes entitled to the third vacancy of Head Assistant which occurred on 111973 and against which Shri OP Nandwani was promoted.
6.
In view of these circumstances explained, you are requested to kindly revise the orders of your predecessor dated 28/29111973 by which the alleged assumed dated 2481972 of promotion as Head Asstt.
was given to Sh. Lachhman Dass and the subsequent orders thereof.
You are also requested to assign assumed dates to all entitled officials of your department.
The entire process should be completed within 15 days and the Govt. be informed of the action taken within this period positively.
Record of your office is sent herewith."
3.
On account of some clarifications required in the written statement, this Court on 24.3.2004 has directed the respondent-State to file an additional affidavit of a responsible officer.
The affidavit has been filed on 642004 making the factual position crystal clear.
After issuance of instructions on 10121971, the following vacancies had arisen and were filled up accordingly :-  "1.
01031972 The Ist vacancy occurred in the cadre of Head Assistant was with the promotion of Sh. Joginder Singh to the post of Superintendent against which Sh. KC Gupta was promoted as Head Assistant on 01031972.
2.
01011973 Sh. Neta Ram was promoted as Superintendent causing the 2nd vacancy after 10121971.
Against this vacancy Sh. KS Mukhra was promoted as Head Assistant.
3.
01011973 The 3rd vacancy also occurred on 01011973 with the promotion of Sh. KC Gupta as Superintendent.
The petitioner was promoted against this vacancy.
4.
24081972 So far as the vacancy said to have occurred on 24081972 is concerned, it is submitted that vide orders dated 19081971, S/Sh.
ML Narang and KS Mukhra were promoted to the post of Superintendent and Head Assistant respectively before finalization of seniority list.
Likewise, Sh. OP Nandwani was also promoted to the post of Head Assistant vide orders dated 31081971.
Since, the persons senior to the above three officials were ignored, as such corrections were made vide orders dated 22.8.1972 to be effective from 24081972, wherein S/Sh.
ML Narang, KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani who were earlier promoted wrongly, were reverted back to the post of Head Assistant and Assistant respectively and in their place S/Sh.
Sajjan Singh was promoted as Superintendent and Roop Chand Sharma & Amrit Singh Chadha were promoted as Head Assistants.
Hence, no vacancy of Head Assistant became available on 24081972 as claimed by the petitioner.
Actually though S/Sh.
Roop Chand Sharma and Amrit Singh Chadha were promoted on 24081972, however, they were promoted against vacancies caused by reversion of their juniors S/Sh.
KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani, on re-fixation of seniority.
Sh. Mukhra was originally promoted on 19081971 and Sh. OP Nandwani on 31.8.1971.
Hence, the vacancies against which S/Sh.
Roop Chand Sharma and Amrit Singh Chadha were promoted occurred prior to 10121971."
4.
Similar stand has been taken by respondent No. 3 Shri KS Mukhra in a separate written statement with further allegations of misrepresentation of facts.
5.
The above facts make it absolutely clear that the third vacancy on the promotion of Shri KC Gupta as Superintendent has occurred on 111973 against which the petitioner has been promoted.
Therefore, the order giving the deemed date of promotion to the petitioner as 2481972 has to be corrected by order dated 2851984, Annexure P-2 as the promotion of Sarvshri ML Narang, KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani was ordered before finalisation of the seniority list.
Many persons senior to the aforementioned three persons were ignored which resulted into their reversion on the posts of Head Assistant and Assistant respectively.
However, Sajjan Singh was promoted as Superintendent and Roop Chand Sharma and Amrit Singh Chadha were promoted as Head Assistants on 2481972.
Therefore, on 2481972 there was no vacancy as claimed by the petitioner.
6.
Mr. NS Panwar, learned counsel for the petitioner has still preferred to argue that once the vacancy given to Roop Chand Sharma and Amrit Singh Chadha belong to August, 1971 (as caused by Sarvshri KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani), then they could not relate back to August, 1971.
According to the learned counsel, the date of promotion of the aforementioned two persons, namely, Roop Chand Sharma and Amrit Singh Chadha has to be considered as 2481972 and then these two vacancies would be counted for the purposes of instructions issued on 10121971.
Therefore, the petitioner would be entitled to third vacancy and deemed date of promotion of 2481972 as Head Assistant.
7.
Mr. SS Brar, learned State counsel has argued that the factual position has been made absolutely clear by the additional affidavit filed by the respondents and there was no vacancy caused on 2481972 and, therefore, no deemed date of promotion on 2481972 could be given to the petitioner.
Learned State counsel has pointed out that even otherwise hardly any difference would be made as the petitioner as well as the private respondents have retired.
8.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties, I am of the considered view that this petition is liable to be dismissed because on re-shuffling of the seniority list of Assistants in pursuance of the directions issued by this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 2414 of 1971, decided on 1161971, there were reversions right from the cadre of Superintendents to the cadre of Assistants.
Some promotions ordered earlier like ML Narang, KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani were withdrawn.
As a result KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani were reverted and in their place Roop Chand Sharma and Amrit Singh Chadha were promoted as Head Assistants on 2481972.
There was, however, no vacancy on that date and they came to occupy the vacancies caused by KS Mukhra and OP Nandwani which have occurred in August, 1971.
In fact no vacancy had occurred on 2481972.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim any deemed date of promotion of 2481972 as Head Assistant on the basis of instructions dated 10121971 and no interference in the impugned order Annexure P-2 is warranted.
The writ petition is wholly without merit and is liable to be dismissed.
For the reasons stated above, this petition fails and the same is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
