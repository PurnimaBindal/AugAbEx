MR Agnihotri, J - This judgment will dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 3910 and 3911 of 1981, as both the writ petitions have been filed by the General Manager, Northern Railway, challenging the same award of the Central Government Labour Court, Jalandhar, dated 8th September, 1980.
2.
The facts are not disputed.
The claim of the petitioner is that the period from 6th February, 1972 to 19th October, 1975, which has been treated as one of suspension and nothing more than the subsistence allowance has been paid to him, should be counted towards service rendered and increments be allowed for that period, including the arrears of salary therefor.
3.
The learned Labour Court has rightly taken the view that the respondent workman was legally entitled to claim wages for the period in question as, firstly, he has been acquitted by the criminal court, and secondly, the suspension was not on any ground attributable to any lapse on the part of the respondent.
4.
Consequently, the award of the Central Government Labour Court, Jalandhar, is upheld and the writ petitions are dismissed.
Petitions dismissed.
