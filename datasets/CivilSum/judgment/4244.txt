GS Singhvi, J - After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, we are fully convinced that the petitioners are entitled to relief in view of the various decisions of the Supreme Court, i.e.Chaman Lal and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1987(4) S.L.R.
4 and Punjab Higher Qualified Teachers Union v. State of Punjab, 1988(1) S.L.R.
768 and a large number of other Judgments to which reference has been made in the order dated 1491994 passed in CWP No. 15630 of 1993 (Rattan Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others) 1995(1) SCT 711 (P&H) decided along with six other writ petitions.
2.
In its order dated 1491994, the Division Bench his made a reference to almost all the cases decided by this Court on the subject and has held that there is no justification for denying relief to similarly situated persons.
3.
Though learned counsel for the respondents has drawn our attention to an order passed by a Division Bench of this court on 24.11.1993 in CWP No.7435 of 1993 but after perusing the said order, we are fully convinced that the said order cannot stand as bar in granting relief to the petitioners.
It is clear from the perusal of the order dated 24.11.1993 that the Division Bench had not considered to the judgments of the Supreme Court and a large number of decisions rendered by this Court (Co-ordinate Benches).
For the reasons which have been recorded in the order dated 1491994 in CWP No.15630 of 1993 and which shall also be treated as part of this order, this writ petition is allowed.
The petitioners shall be given relief in the same terms in which relief has been given to the petitioner by order dated 1491994.
