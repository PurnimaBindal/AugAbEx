Ashutosh Mohunta, J - The petitioner was a Diploma-holder and joined the service of the State of Punjab in the P.W.D.
(Electricity Branch) in August 1955.
On the constitution of the Punjab State Electricity Board (for short `the Board') in the year 1959, the petitioner became the employee of the Board.
He was promoted as Assistant Engineer, Class-II (Civil) on 511970.
In the meantime, he acquired the qualification of AMIE, which is equivalent to the Degree of Bachelor of Engineering.
The said qualification of AMIE was got entered in the official record.
Thereafter, the petitioner was promoted as Assistant Executive Engineer (for short `AEE') with effect from 1941974.
He was promoted as Executive Engineer on 2161983.
On attaining the age of superannuation, the petitioner was retired from service with effect from 28.2.1993.
Before his retirement, the Board issued a circular dated 2451990 whereby it was ordered that "an Assistant Engineer/Assistant Executive Engineer would be placed in the next higher scale of Rs. 3000-5600 and allowed a minimum pay of Rs. 3500/- in the 10th year ie on completion of 9 (nine) years regular service as Asstt.
Engineer/Asstt.
Executive Engineer.
Similarly, he would be placed in the next higher scale of Rs. 4500-6300 in the 17th (seventeenth) year i.e. on completion of 16 (sixteen) years regular service as Asstt.
Engineer/Asstt.
Executive Engineer."
The grievance of the petitioner is that though he was given the pay scale of Rs. 3000-5600 with a start of Rs. 3500/- on completion of nine years of regular service as Assistant Engineer/Assistant Executive Engineer, but he has been denied the benefit of the next higher scale of Rs. 4500-6300 on completion of 16 years of regular service as such on the pretext that he was not a direct recruit having entered the Service of Engineers after attaining the degree of Bachelor of Engineering.
His representations made to the authorities concerned have been rejected and he received the rejection letter on 2421995 (Annexure P-9).
The petitioner has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India for the quashment of the letter dated 2421995 (P-9) by issuing a writ in the nature of certiorari and also for the grant of higher time bound pay scale, which has been allowed to the others having common seniority and common cadre of Assistant Engineers in pursuance of the circular letter dated 2451990 (Annexure P-4).
2.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have gone through the case file.
3.
The service conditions of the petitioner are governed by Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Civil) Regulations, 1965 (for short `the Regulations').
Regulation 7 thereof deals with the recruitment to the service.
The relevant portion of Regulation 7 is reproduced hereunder for facility of reference:-  Recruitment to the Service.
Recruitment to the Service shall be made by the appointing authority by any of the methods indicated below as may be determined in each case :- (a) In case of posts of Assistant Engineers.
(i) By appointment from Trainee Engineers as provided in Regulation 9.
(ii) By promotion as provided in Regulation 10.
(iii) By transfer of an officer already in the service of a Government of any other State Electricity Board or an Undertaking of Government.
xxx xxx xxx xxx" 4.
Regulation 10 deals with eligibility for appointment by promotion.
In the present case, the petitioner continued getting time bound promotions and pay scales on the basis of his seniority in the cadre from time to time.
However, he has been denied the pay scale of 4500-6300, which he was eligible to get on the completion of 16 years of service as Assistant Engineer/Assistant Executive Engineer/Executive Engineer with effect from 511986 as per letter dated 2451990 (Annexure P-4).
According to letter dated 2451990, the next higher grade is to be allowed as under :-  AE/AEE (i) xx xx xx xx (ii) xx xx xx xx (iii) 4500-125-5000-5900-200-6300.
Second next higher scale in the 17th year ie on completion of 16 years regular service as AE/AEE.
XEN xx xx xx xx 5.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the Board, the primary stand has been taken, that the petitioner has got three promotions in his service career and the benefit extended under letter dated 2451990 (Annexure P-4) was not applicable in his case.
However, the interpretation with regard to the letter dated 2451990 made in the written statement is utterly wrong.
There is no mention in the letter dated 2451990 that an employee who has got three promotions, would not be eligible to get the next higher grade on completion of 16 years of service.
In the present case the petitioner was promoted as Assistant Engineer with effect from 511970 and completed 16 years as such on 511986.
According to letter dated 2451990, an Assistant Engineer/Assistant Executive Engineer on completion of 16 years of service as such, would be eligible to get the next higher scale of Rs. 4500-6300.
There is no mention in the said letter that the pay scale of Rs. 4500-6300 on completion of 16 years of service as Assistant Engineer/Assistant Executive Engineer would only be granted to a person who joined directly as Assistant Engineer on the basis of the Degree of Bachelor of Engineering and not to an promotee.
In Regulation 7 it has only been mentioned that recruitment to the post of Assistant Engineer shall be made from three sources, viz. :  (i) By appointment from Trainee Engineers as provided in Regulation 9.
(ii) By promotion as provided in Regulation 10.
(iii) By transfer of an officer already in the service of a Government or any other State Electricity Board or an Undertaking of Government.
6.
After getting promotion as Assistant Engineer from the post of Section Officer, the petitioner got the same position and status as a person who joined service directly as Assistant Engineer on the basis of his degree of Bachelor of Engineering.
16 years of service is to be counted from the date of joining as Assistant Engineer.
I find support in my view from the authority of the Division Bench of this Court reported as Bhakra Beas Management Board v. Rajinder Singh Patpatia and another, 2001(1) SCT 364 (P&H) : 2002(1) RSJ 32.
The factual position depicted in the present writ petition, has not been denied in the written statement filed on behalf of the Board.
Consequently, I allow the writ petition and quash the order dated 2451995 (Annexure P-9), whereby the petitioner has been denied the higher time scale.
The petitioner shall be granted the higher pay scale of Rs. 4500-6300 with effect from the date on which he completes 16 years of service as Assistant Engineer, as per the letter dated 2451990 (Annexure P-4).
The fiscal benefits which the petitioner becomes eligible to shall be released to him within two months from the date of receipt of a certified copy of this judgment, failing which an interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum shall be charged, which shall be recovered from the officer/official responsible for causing the delay.
Petition allowed.
