CS Rajan, J - The petitioner was denied admission to the part-time B Tech (Electronics and Communication Engineering) course by the third respondent on the basis of a clause, viz., VII(iii)(c) in the prospectus, which is as follows:  "Date of marklist of the Diploma examination will only be considered as the date of acquisition of Diploma and the experience will be counted accordingly".
I think the above clause is absolutely arbitrary and illegal.
The date of the marklist is immaterial as far as the acquisition of the Diploma is concerned.
It is the date of publication of the result that is relevant.
Ext.P1 Diploma shows that the petitioner has passed the above examination held in August, 1996 and he is placed in the First Class.
But the marklist was obtained only on 1821998.
I do not think that the date of issuance of the marklist is material so far as the date of acquisition of the Diploma is concerned.
Therefore, the third respondent is directed to consider the case of the petitioner for admission for the part-time B Tech (Electronics and Communication Engineering) course for the year 1998-1999, provided he is otherwise eligible.
Original Petition is disposed of as above.
