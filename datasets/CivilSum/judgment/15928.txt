RS Mongia, J - The petitioner had joined the Armed Forces on February 11, 1961 and was discharged on September 27, 1972 and thereafter he joined Haryana State Electricity Board against a reserved post meant for Ex-servicemen on January 1, 1975 by way of direct recruitment.
He applied for the benefit of military service rendered during the period of emergency i.e., October 26, 1962 to January 10, 1968.
Under Rule 4 of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 (as applicable to the Haryana State Electricity Board), the petitioner was given the benefit of military service rendered during the period of emergency vide order dated August 20, 1986 (Annexure P2).
By this process his deemed dated appointment would have been October 18, 1969, but he was given the deemed date of appointment as June 19, 1972, as it was the date on which he had acquired the qualification of BA, which was the minimum academic qualification required for joining as direct recruit U.D.C.
The petitioner has the grievance that under the aforesaid 1965 Rules it is not necessary that on the deemed date of appointment, the person must be academically qualified as benefit of military service was an incentive to the person who had rendered service during the period of emergency.
In support of his submission that it is not necessary under the 1965 Rule to hold minimum qualifications for being eligible to be appointed on the deemed dated, he referred to SLP No. 7413-14 of 1991, decided on May 10, 1995 by the Apex Court, wherein it was observed as under:-  "Rule 4 requires that the individual concerned must be qualified to appear for the examination for the posts in question after he is demobilised.
However, he should not have been above the upper age limit fixed for the said posts when he joined the military service.
Reading of Rule 4(a) and (b) and Rule 5 together would suggest that it is not necessary that he should have been qualified to apply for the post when the first opportunity to occupy the said post became available to him while he was in military service."
2.
In this view of the matter, I am of the opinion that the stand taken by the respondents that the petitioner had to be qualified on the deemed date of appointment is not correct and consequently, the order at Annexure P2 has to be set aside and the deemed date of appointment to the petitioner has to be given as October 18, 1969 for the purposes mentioned in Rule 4 of the '1965 Rules.
3.
Consequently, this writ petition is allowed, the order dated August 20, 1986 at Annexure P2 is set aside and it is held that the petitioner be given deemed date of appointment as October 18, 1969 for all intents and purposes and be given all consequential benefits ensuing therefrom.
Let these directions be carried out within a period of four months of the receipt of a copy of this order either from this court or a certified copy thereof from the petitioner.
I make no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
