Sat Pal, J - This petition has been directed against the order dated 3rd April, 1997 passed by the Civil Judge, Senior Division, Sangrur.
By this order, the learned Civil Judge has dismissed the application filed by the plaintiff under Order 18 Rule 17-A CPC for production of additional evidence.
2.
Mr. Garg, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner submits that the petitioner/plaintiff has only to bring on record the letter of appointment and he has not to examine any other witness.
3.
Mr. Ahluwalia, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents, however, submits that the documents sought to be placed on record are not relevant.
4.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and having perused the impugned order, I am of opinion that it would be in the interest of justice that the petitioner is allowed to bring on record and prove the letter of appointment.
The relevancy of this document can be appreciated by the learned trial court after it is brought on record.
5.
In view of the above discussion, the petition is allowed and the impugned order dated 3rd April, 1997 is set aside and the petitioner is allowed only to bring on record the letter of appointment and prove the same on one date which shall be fixed by the learned trial court subject to payment of costs of Rs. 1000/-.
It is, however, made clear that the learned trial court shall give one date on which the petitioner shall produce the said evidence at his own risk.
The parties are directed to appear before the learned trial court on 2nd February, 1998.
With this order, the petition stands disposed of.
Petition allowed.
