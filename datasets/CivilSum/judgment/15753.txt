THB Chalapathi, J (Oral) - The present respondent has filed a suit for declaration that the service rendered by him w.e.f.
22.5.1950 to 2951956 is to be counted for the purpose of pension.
According to the plaintiff- respondent he joined the Wild Life Department on 22.5.1950 and retired on 3181984 and has rendered total service of 34 years on attaining age of superannuation and though he was appointed temporary in 1950, his services were confirmed in 1956 and his pension has been calculated only from the date of his confirmation in service and the period of service rendered by him from 22.5.1950 to 29.2.1956 has not been counted for the purpose of calculating the pension.
2.
The State took the plea that the plaintiff was appointed as a contingent employee in the year 1950 and he was confirmed only in the year 1956 and, therefore, service rendered by him prior to 1956 cannot be taken into consideration for the purpose of calculating pension.
The trial court dismissed the suit.
The learned Additional District Judge, impugned judgment dated 1381998 decreed the suit and directed that the services rendered by the (sic) purpose of pension.
Aggrieved by the said judgment, the State filed this second appeal.
3.
There is no denial of the fact that the service book has been maintained in 1950 itself when the respondent-plaintiff joined the service.
The service register which has been marked as PW2/A clearly shows that plaintiff was appointed on temporary basis and his service has been confirmed w.e.f.
141956.
There is nothing on the record to show that the plaintiff had been paid salary out of contingent fund, therefore, there is no basis that the plaintiff was only a contingent employee prior to 1956 and the service rendered by him from 1950 to 1956 cannot be counted for the purpose of pension.
The learned Additional District Judge relied upon service register of the petitioner and came to the conclusion that the petitioner was initially appointed as temporary employee and thereafter his service were confirmed.
In this view of the matter, I do not find any ground to interfere with the judgment and decree of the lower appellate court.
Second appeal, therefore, fails and is accordingly dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
