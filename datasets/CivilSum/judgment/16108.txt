Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - This judgment will dispose of CWP Nos. 14667, 16082, 8307, 10658 of 1991 and 2216 of 1992.
These petitions have been taken up together with the consent of the counsel for the parties as common questions of law and facts are involved therein.
For the purpose of judgment the facts have been taken from CWP No. 14667 of 1991 'RS Bhullar etc. v. State of Punjab etc.'  2.
The petitioners are working as Junior Auditors in the Food and Supplies Department and were given the pay scale of Rs. 1500-2640 w.e.f.
111986.
The grievance of the petitioners is that Assistants/Accountants working in the various departments were given pay scale of Rs. 1800-3200, whereas the same has been denied to them.
The petitioners further allege that the post of Jr.
Auditor has been considered to be equivalent to that of Assistant/Accountant right from the year 1968.
According to the petitioners the action of the respondents in denying the same pay scale is arbitrary and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
3.
In order to fortify their arguments, counsel for the petitioners have relied upon Division Bench decision of this Court rendered in C.W.P.
No. 7807 of 1991, Hardaljit Singh v. State of Punjab etc. decided on 31101991.
4.
The learned Advocate General, Punjab appearing fort the State has contested the claim of the petitioner and contended that the petitioners were not entitled to any benefit and the ratio of the case Hardaljit Singh (supra) was not applicable to the facts in controversy.
5.
I have considered the submissions of the learned counsel for the parties and persued the paper book.
6.
The department of finance, Punjab Government, had issued a notification dated 951991 in exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to Article 309 read with clause (3) of Article 187 of the Constitution of India.
The Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules 1988 were amended.
The Rules read :-  1.
The rules may be called the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) (Second Amendment) Rules 1991.
2.
They shall be deemed to have come into force unless otherwise provided on and with effect from the Ist day of January, 1986.
II.
In the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988 in the First Schedule as amended by Govt. of Punjab Department of Finance Notification No. 2/29/90-EPI/5294, dated the 15th June, 1990 :  (a) Under the heading 'VII ACCOUNTS PERSONNEL' for serial No. 2 and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be substituted namely :  Sr. No.
Existing scale in rupees Revised scale in rupees Remarks.
2.
Junior Auditor 570-1080 For existing incumbents 1500-2640 The placement in the pay scale of Rs. 1800-3200 shall be made after a minimum of 5 years of service as Junior Auditor on or after the Ist day of January 1986 and the incumbents be designated as Auditors.
For future incumbents 
1500-2640 The recruitment of Junior Auditors in various departments of the State shall be made by direct appointment from amongst the person who are second class graduates or possess equivalent qualification to graduation.
1800-3200 The placement in pay scale of Rs. 1800-3200 shall be made from amongst those employees who qualify the SAS Part-I examination or the Assistant Grade Examination and who have rendered service as Junior Auditors for a minimum period of five years and : 7.
The Rules were the subject matter in Hardaljit Singh's case (supra).
A Division Bench of this Court in that case had modified the said notification to the extent that the Junior Auditor would be given the pay scale which had been given to Assistant-cum-Accountants with the stipulation that the order shall not affect the seniority or promotion in the cadre.
8.
In this view of the matter, since the notification has been amended by the Division Bench in Hardaljit Singh's case (supra) allowing the Junior Auditors the pay scale of Assistants/Accountants, the petitioners also cannot be denied that pay scale as denial thereof will be violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India particularly when the said judgment of the amendment of the said notification has neither been challenged nor quashed.
The writ petitions are disposed of accordingly.
This judgment is to be given effect to within two months from today.
In view of the peculiar circumstances of the case, the parties are left to bear their own costs.
Order accordingly.
