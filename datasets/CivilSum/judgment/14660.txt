RS Mongia, J (Oral) - This judgment of mine will dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 1148 and 2196 of 1986, 1471, 1324, 2123, 4496 and 7498 of 1987, 4441 of 1988, 141, 11177, 11753, 15618 and 12120 of 1989 and 16156 of 1990, as identical questions of law and fact are involved in the same.
2.
It is not necessary to notice the facts of these cases in any detail as it is not disputed that these writ petitions are to be allowed in view of the full bench judgment of this court in Neelam Kumari v. State of Punjab and others, Civil Writ Petition No. 13174 of 1991 decided on September 24, 1992, (1993(1) SCT p. 770).
3.
The petitioners in all these cases have passed J.B.T.
Course in Home Craft/Home Science and Diploma in Art and Crafts from various institutions in the State of Haryana.
Some of the petitioners have.
passed J.B.T.
Course from Rajasthan.
On May 29, 1984, September 20, 1985 and August 16, 1990, Punjab Government issued three notifications de-recognising the J.B.T.
Course (Home Crafts/Home Science) and Diploma in Art and Crafts, which had been obtained from the Institutions of State of Haryana and J.B.T.
Course from Rajasthan, respectively.
In some of these cases, the appointments of the petitioners as teachers in the various schools were not approved/regularised by the State Government on the ground that professional qualifications of the petitioners stood de-recognised by the State of Punjab through the abovementioned notifications.
In some of the cases, the petitioners were not called for interview for appointment on the ground that their professional qualifications stood de-recopised by the State of Punjab through the abovesaid notifications.
This led to the petitioners to file the present writ petitions.
4.
A full bench of this court in Civil Writ Petition No. 13174 of 1991, had an occasion to consider this point and it has been held on the basis of the government letter, dated May 9, 1960 (which has been annexed as Annexure P-11 with Civil Writ Petition No. 1148 of 1986) that once particular professional courses were recognised, the derecognition, would not effect those persons, who had obtained such qualifications prior to the date of de-recognition, meaning thereby that all such petitioners who had obtained these qualifications prior to the date of de-recognition from the State of Haryana would be deemed to be qualified for the purpose of appointment to the various posts in the State of Punjab and the de-recognition would be effective only qua those persons who might have obtained or may obtain the basic professional qualifications after the date of de-recogaition.
5.
In view of the judgment of this court, the learned counsel for the State submits that he is not in a position to oppose the writ petitions.
6.
For the foregoing reasons these writ petitions are allowed and it is held that the petitioners, who are holding professional qualifications, like J.B.T.
(Home Crafts) or Diploma in Art and Crafts from the various institutions in the State of Haryana or J.B.T.
from Rajasthan, which were obtained prior to the date of de-recognition, as mentioned in the earlier paragraph of the judgment, would not be considered as unqualified and their cases would have to be considered for approval/regularisation by treating them as duly qualified.
The respondents are directed to consider the cases of the petitioners for approval/regularisation by considering them duly qualified.
Needless to mention that on the approval being granted or on regularisation of the services the petitioners would be entitled to all consequential benefits flowing therefrom.
Let these directions be carried out within three months from the date of the receipt of the copy of this judgment.
7.
In some of the cases, the petitioners were not called for interview on the ground that they were not having qualifications recognised by the State of Punjab.
It is hereby directed that if any vacancy arises from now onwards or the vacancies which might be already existing are sought to be filled, then such petitioners or any other similarly situated persons, who have got the basic professional qualifications like J.B.T.
(Home Crafts) or Diploma in Art and Crafts from the State of Haryana or J.B.T.
from Rajasthan prior to the date of its de-recognition, that is, May 29, 1984.
September 20, 1985 and August 16, 1990, respectively, would be called for interview.
The Employment Officers of Employment Exchanges would be obliged to forward their names for consideration.
There will no order as to costs.
