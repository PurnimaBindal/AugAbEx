Ashutosh Mohunta, J - The Ajnala Co-operative Sugar Mills Ltd - respondent No. 3 advertised in the newspaper on 12101990, (Annexure P1), for filling various posts like fitter helper, electrical helper, Blacksmith, machinist helper etc.
In pursuance of the advertisement the petitioner also applied for the post of machinist helper.
Interviews were held and the petitioner was selected as a machinist helper vide letter dated 31121990, annexure P2.
In the said letter, it was mentioned that "the grade etc. will be mentioned in the appointment letter which will be issued to you after joining the duty."
2.
Instead of issuing the appointment letter the petitioner was being paid fixed salary of Rs. 1200/- per month from the date of his initial appointment till the date of his regularisation i.e. upto 15.6.1996.
The claim in the present petition is that the petitioner should be paid salary of machinist helper in the grade of Rs. 800-1050 from the date of his initial appointment and not from the date of regularisation which was with effect from June, 1996.
3.
Mr. Rana, learned counsel for the petitioner states that the persons similarly situated filed civil writ petition No. 3608 of 1997 in this Court and a Single Bench of this Court vide order dated 412001 held as follows:  "the services of the petitioners have to be regularised from the respective dates of their initial appointment and they are also entitled to be paid regular scales of pay from the respective dates of their initial appointment as prayed for.
But so far as the arrears are concerned the petitioners will be entitled to the same for a period of 38 months preceding the dates of the earlier writ petitions namely CWP No. 381 of 1995 and CWP No. 2379 of 1995 filed by them respectively."
4.
It was further ordered that the petitioner shall be paid arrears to 38 months preceding the date of earlier writ petitions i.e. civil writ petition 381 and 2379 of 1995 filed by them respectively.
Their services should be regularised accordingly from the date of their initial appointment.
According to the learned counsel, the petitioner is also entitled to the same relief.
5.
Mr. Mahajan, learned counsel for respondents No. 2 and 3 does not dispute the above position.
6.
In view of the above, the present writ petition is also allowed in terms of the decision in civil writ petition No. 3608 of 1997 and it is ordered that the services of the petitioner shall be regularised from the date of his initial appointment.
It is further ordered that the petitioner shall be entitled to the regular pay scales from the date of initial appointment on the post of machinist helper.
The petitioner shall be paid arrears of 38 months preceding the date of earlier writ petition No. 381 of 1995.
The arrears shall be paid to the petitioner within a period of three months from today, otherwise the petitioner would be entitled to the interest @ 9% per annum till the date of payment.
Appeal allowed.
