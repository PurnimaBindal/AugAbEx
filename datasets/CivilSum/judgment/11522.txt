Mehtab S Gill, J - Petitioner has prayed for modifying the award dated March 18, 1993, which is attached as Annexure P-4 with the writ petition.
2.
Petitioner has averred that on April 4, 1979, he reported for duty at 8.00 A.M. Duty Inspector, DK Sharma, did not allow him to go on route from Karnal to Guhla.
Apart from not allowing the petitioner to go on this route, which was his daily route, he was deliberately and purposely not sent on any other route.
3.
False complaint was made against the petitioner by the Duty Inspector.
Petitioner was put under suspension on April 4, 1979.
He was served with a charge-sheet on April 11, 1979 (Annexure P-1).
4.
Petitioner, on April 18, 1979, replied to the charge-sheet.
Shri SN Gaur, Legal Adviser of Haryana Roadways, was appointed as Enquiry Officer.
Only one witness, who came forward to give evidence against the petitioner, was Shri DK Sharma, the Duty Inspector.
5.
Show cause notice was issued to the petitioner on January 6, 1981.
Petitioner submitted his reply.
Respondent no. 2, the General Manager of Haryana Roadways, Karnal terminated the services of the petitioner vide order dated March, 2, 1981 (Annexure P-3).
The termination orders of the petitioner were challenged before the Industrial Tribunal, Faridabad.
The learned Tribunal gave his award on March 18, 1986.
The learned Presiding Officer of the Industrial Tribunal, in his order, stated that the misconduct did not justify the punishment of dismissal of the petitioner from service.
Petitioner was reinstated.
No back wages were awarded to the petitioner.
Two increments of the petitioner with cumulative effect were stopped.
6.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties.
7.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has stated, that the petitioner is entitled to full back wages and the two increments of the petitioner which have been stopped, should be given back to him.
8.
He has drawn by attention to a judgment of this Court rendered in the case of Hari Palace, Ambala City v. The Presiding Officer, Labour Court and another, 1979 ILR 720.
A Constitutional Bench of this Court has stated that full back wages should be given to the claimant on his being reinstated.
9.
Learned counsel for the respondents has drawn my attention to a judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the case of Haryana Urban Development Authority v. Devi Dayal, 2002(3) SCC 473 : 2002(3) SCT 323 (SC).
The Hon'ble Supreme Court has held full back wages can be curtailed.
10.
Petitioner is present in person.
He has stated before me that he was satisfied if 25% of the back wages be awarded to him.
This is a concession being made by the petitioner himself.
11.
The award dated March 18, 1986 passed by the Industrial Tribunal, Faridabad is modified to this extent that the petitioner will be entitled to 25% of his back wages from the date of dismissal till his reinstatement.
The order passed by the learned Industrial Tribunal stopping two increments with cumulative effect is set aside.
12.
With the above observations, award dated March 18, 1986 is modified to this extent only.
Order accordingly.
