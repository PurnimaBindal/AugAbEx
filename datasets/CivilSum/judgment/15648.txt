RL Anand, J - Sohan Lal has filed the present petition seeking the directions of this court commanding the respondents to re-fix his pension after 4 years 11 months and 3 days of military service rendered by him during the emergency from 24.1.1963 to 10.8.1968 towards pension and gratuity and the arrears of pension be paid to the petitioner along with interest @ 18%.
2.
Admittedly, the petitioner is ex-serviceman.
It is also not disputed that emergency was enforced in this country from 26101962 to 1011968.
As per the Haryana rules, a person is entitled to the benefit of military service for the actual period when a person has served the Army.
The prayer of the petitioner has been contested on two grounds; firstly, that the petitioner did not join the civil department/civil service within three years of this discharge.
The petitioner was discharged from military service on 1251979 and he was appointed in the Civil department as Conductor on 5.12.1973.
In these circumstances, the petitioner is not entitled to count the period of military service towards pension, the second objection taken up by the State is that the petitioner was not appointed as a permanent Conductor, in these circumstances, he is not entitled to the benefit of military service.
3.
Both the contentions raised by the counsel for the State are not tenable.
In the opinion of this court, the first contention of the State that the discharged person must join the civil department within three years, is against public interest and it is arbitrary.
We all know that we are living in a poor country.
A discharged person may or may not get an appointment in the civil department within three years.
It depends upon the exigency and when the post suitable to the said discharged person becomes available depending upon his qualification and experience and nature of job.
With regard to the second objection also, my attention has been invited to the Govt.
Notification dated 13.8.1979.
The condition of appointment to a permanent post has been withdrawn by the words "In case of first or any subsequent appointment to any service or post."
4.
The petitioner has made a prayer for the grant of benefit of military service for 4 years 11 months 3 days.
The prayer of the petitioner cannot be granted in its entirety because first emergency in the country was invoked on 26101962 and it continued upto 1011968.
In this view of the matter, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of military service upto 1011968 only and not beyond that.
Thus, the present petition is partly allowed and directions are given to the respondents to allow the benefit of military service to the petitioner starting from 791963, when the petitioner joined the service, upto 1011968.
Petition partly allowed.
