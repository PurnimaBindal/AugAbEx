Jayanta Kumar Biswas, J - The petitioner is questioning the decision of the District Inspector of Schools (S.E.
), Howrah dated December 9, 2004 holding that in view of provisions in government order No. 304(6)-Edn (B)/IM- 1/82 dated June 21, 1983 the date of next increment given to the petitioner on improving his qualification was to be changed.
2.
On June 1, 1966 he joined Uluberia Hazi Ishaque DUS Senior Madrasah as an assistant teacher.
At the time of appointment he possessed a bachelor's degree.
On November 22, 1983 he improved his qualification by obtaining a master's degree.
He prayed for benefits of higher scale.
The post graduate scale of pay was given to him with effect from November 22, 1983.
Prior to that date he had been enjoying the graduate scale of Rs. 440-1170/- and he was getting pay at Rs. 540/-.
The post graduate scale was Rs. 550-1470/-.
In terms of government orders existing at that date his pay (in the post graduate scale of pay) was fixed at 550.
The fixation took effect from November 22, 1983.
3.
Thereafter his scale was fixed from time to time according to the relevant rules revising pay and allowances.
On reaching the age of superannuation he retired from service on September 30, 2000.
Thereafter the authorities issued orders dated October 3, 2000 and July 5, 2001 directing recovery on the ground that for wrong fixation of pay with effect from April 1, 1984 he had overdrawn a sum of Rs. 97,945/-.
Questioning the actions he took out WP No. 7473(W) of 2002 before this Court.
That was disposed of by order dated July 28, 2003 directing the district inspector of schools to give a reasoned decision in the matter.
4.
Consequently that authority gave decision dated April 24, 2004 holding that the petitioner had been given the benefits in accordance with the provisions of the relevant government orders.
He however forwarded the papers to the higher authority for clearance.
The higher authority (deputy director accounts, education) held that in view of the government order dated June 21, 1983 date of increment of the petitioner in the higher scale had not been properly fixed.
Consequently the district inspector of schools gave the impugned decision directing correction of the relevant papers fixing the date of increment in the higher scale according to the government order dated June 21, 1983.
5.
Counsel says that in view of the decision dated December 9, 2004 the authorities will be entitled to deduct the sum to Rs. 97,945/- on account of alleged overdrawal.
The writ petition was admitted by order dated February 28, 2005.
The respondents were directed to file affidavit.
That order was communicated to the respondents who have chosen not to file any affidavit.
The matter was taken up for hearing on December 8, 2006 when more appeared for the respondents.
Today also no one is available to argue their case.
6.
I do not find any reason why the petitioner is apprehending that on the basis of the decision dated December 9, 2004 the respondents will deduct the sum to Rs. 97,945/- on account of overdrawal.
What the authority said is that only the date of increment in the higher scale should be refixed.
There is nothing to show that after fixation of pay in the post graduate scale with effect from November 22, 1983 which was the next date of increment in the scale of Rs. 550-1470/-.
Nothing has been said either about the next date of increment that would have fallen due to the petitioner in the graduate scale which he had been enjoying prior to November 22, 1983.
7.
Be that as it may, the fact, that the date was not properly fixed is apparent, since it is not the case of the petitioner that the next increment was given to him at the date that was to fall due in ordinary course in the higher scale to which he entered with effect from November 22, 1993.
I do not find any reason to accept the contention that the petitioner was entitled to get the benefit of next increment with effect from April 1, 1984.
No such case has been made out in the writ petition.
Counsel says that the authorities will change the date from April 1, 1984 to November 22, 1984.
No such case has been made out as well.
8.
I am, however, of the view that since the petitioner entered into the post graduate scale with effect from November 22, 1983 he would not have been entitled to get the next increment in that scale before any date at which he would have been entitled to the increment in ordinary course as a person entitled to that scale.
I do not find any merit in the contention that the government order dated June 21, 1983 gave more advantage to the persons who were governed by provisions in sub-cls. (ii) and (iii) of cl. (e).
In those cases the date of next increment was to remain unchanged.
9.
Such a provision was not made in sub-cl. (i) of cl. (c).
That means that next increment of a person governed by that sub-clause was to fall due according to the scheme of the scale concerned.
Here the admitted case is that the petitioner was governed by the provision in cl. c (i) of that government order dated June 21, 1983.
It is therefore clear that if there was any mistake in fixing the next date of increment to which he would have been entitled, that requires correction, and consequently if any amount is to be refunded by the petitioner, he would be liable to refund that amount.
10.
For these reasons, I hold that the impugned decision does not call for any interference by the writ Court.
I accordingly dispose of the writ petition ordering that all benefits to which the petitioner became entitled consequent upon his retirement shall be paid to him within four weeks from the date of communication of this order.
The benefits shall be paid with interest at the rate of 10% per annum, since for all the mess leading to non-payment of retiral benefits, to him, he was never at fault, and the authorities had not taken any steps for correcting the situation before his retirement.
These directions shall be complied with by the director of school education, the district inspector of schools and the authority of the institute concerned.
In the facts and circumstances, there shall be no order for cost in the case.
11.
Urgent certified xerox copy of this order shall be supplied to the parties, if applied for, within three days from the date of receipt of the file by the Section concerned.
Petition allowed.
