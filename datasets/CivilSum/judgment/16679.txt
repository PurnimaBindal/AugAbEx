SS Nijjar, J - In this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioners have made a prayer for the issuance of a writ in the nature of Mandamus directing the respondents not to change the date of annual increment of the petitioners on the fixation of their pay in the revised scale of pay with effect from 1st January, 1978 and the petitioners be allowed to draw their annual increment on the completion of their one year service from the date of their appointments and the respondents be further directed to fix the salary of the petitioners accordingly and to give all other consequential benefits such as arrears of pay etc., for which the petitioners are legally entitled.

On the basis of the recommendation of the 2nd Pay Commisison, the pay scales of the Punjab Government employees, including Masters working in the Punjab Education Department, were revised w.e.f.
111978.
The petitioners were appointed in the pay scale of Rs. 220-500 as Masters.
4.
The State of Punjab notified the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979 (hereinafter referred to as "the Rules"), which came into force w.e.f.
111978.
Masters working in the Education Department were granted pay scale of Rs. 620-1200 with effect from 111978.
The petitioners were allowed to draw their annual increment on the completion of their one year service from the dates of their appointment.
They continue to draw the annual increments accordingly.
On 1821983, the State of Punjab issued a letter in which it has been decided that the Master/Mistresses were to be allowed a starting salary of Rs. 660 in the revised scale of Rs. 620-1200 w.e.f.
111978.
Copy of the letter has been attached to the writ petition as Annexure P-1.
Initially, when the pay of the petitioners was fixed, they were granted the benefit of annual increments which they had already earned in the old scale.
By letter dated 1621983, the pay of the petitioners was fixed at Rs. 660 and it was directed that they shall draw their next annual increment on 111979 and not on the completion of one year service.
The dates of the annual increment of the petitioners have been changed.
The petitioners having been appointed from August 1977 to November 1977 are entitled to draw their first increment from August 1978 to November 1978.
But this had been postponed to 111979.
The petitioners have been told that since their emoluments have exceeded Rs. 75/-, their annual increment would fall due on completion of one year service from 1178 in terms of Rule 71(i) of the Rules.
The petitioners claim that the aforesaid rule is being misinterpreted by the respondents.
According to the petitioners, Rule 71(i) will be applicable only in cases where on fixation of pay at the minimum of the time scale and the revised pay, the emolument exceeds Rs. 75/-.
In the case of the petitioners, the minimum scale is Rs. 620/-.
In case the pay of the petitioners is fixed at the minimum of the scale of Rs. 620, their emoluments in the revised pay does not exceed Rs. 75/-.
Masters/Mistresses have been granted a personal benefit that they should be given a starting salary of Rs. 660 per month.
Therefore, Rs. 660 cannot be termed as minimum of the time scale.
By interpretation of the aforesaid Rule, the petitioners have been deprived of the services they have rendered from August, 1977 to January, 1978, for the purposes of annual increments.
It has been further stated that the decision of the writ petition is squarely covered by a judgment of this Court rendered in CWP No. 1454 of 1977 decided on 2681983.
A copy of the aforesaid judgment is attached as Annexure P-2 to the writ petition.
5.
The State of Punjab has not filed any written statement.
Mr. Ladhar, learned counsel appearing for the State of Punjab has prayed for an adjournment in order to enable him to file the written statement.
6.
Having considered the entire matter, I am of the considered opinion that it is not necessary to adjourn the matter.
In CWP No. 1454 of 1977, the respondents had filed a written statement.
In that written statement, the respondents had given a justification as under :-  "Normally, the increment is granted after the completion of one year service unless it is specified, but the grades of the petitioners have been revised from 125-300 to 170-350 with effect from 1.11.71, and situation became abnormal.
It is in accordance with the instructions as laid in letter No. 574-Ed.II(4)-71/dated June 14, 1971 (1.11.71) that the personnel who were drawing pay or salary in their old scale less than the minimum of the revised scales has been fixed, at minimum of the revised grades with their annual increments after one year from the date of revision.
But in other cases where the petitioners were drawing more than the minimum of the revised scale and their pay was intermediate they are fixed at the next higher stage of the revised scale with their date of increment as unchanged."
7.
After considering the aforesaid explanation, it has been held that the date of increment of the petitioners who were drawing pay in the old scale higher than the minimum of the revised scale remains unchanged.
It has also been held that there is nothing in paragraph 2 of letter P.1 to infer that the petitioners whose pay has been fixed at minimum of the revised scale are to be given increment after the expiry of one year from the date of revision.
It has also been held that paragraph 2 of letter (Annexure P-1) regarding the date of increment cannot be sustained.
8.
In view of the above, I am of the opinion that the writ petition has to be allowed and I order accordingly.
The petitioners will be allowed annual increments in the revised pay scale with effect from the same date they were entitled to in the old scale.
Let the arrears, if any, be paid to the petitioners within a period of three months from today.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
