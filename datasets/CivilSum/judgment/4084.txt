GS Singhvi, J - These petitions are being disposed of by a common order because the substantive prayer made by the petitioners is common to all the cases.
First of all, we may briefly notice the facts.
C.W.P.
No. 3736/87 :  2.
The petitioners joined service as Junior Engineers in Public Works Department (Public Health Department), Government of Punjab.
Vide order dated 28121984, they were promoted as Sub-Divisional Engineers on purely temporary basis against the vacancies meant for direct recruitment.
The tenure of their promotion was six months or till the availability of candidates selected by the Punjab Public Service Commission.
After two years and six months, they were reverted to the post of Junior Engineers on the availability of candidates selected by the Public Service Commission.
Their grievance is that the official respondents have not made recruitment to the service in accordance with Rules 6 and 7 of the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class-II PWD (Public Health Rules), 1967 (hereinafter referred to as `the 1967 Rules') and in this manner they have been deprived of their legitimate right to be promoted against the posts of their quota.
The respondents have contested the writ petition by asserting that on the basis of purely temporary promotion, the petitioners did not acquire any legal or vested right to hold the posts of Sub-Divisional Engineers and no illegality has been committed by government in reverting them on the availability of candidates recommended by the Public Service Commission.
In a separate written statement, the respondent No. 19 has raised an objection to the maintainability of the writ petition by contending that the petitioners do not have the right to hold the posts of Sub-Divisional Engineers on the basis of their promotion against the posts earmarked for direct recruitment.
C.W.P.
No. 3785/87:  3.
Petitioners-Surinder Mohan Singh Puri and others joined service as Junior Engineers in P.W.D.
(Public Health Department) between 1970 and 1973.
They passed AMIE between 1981 and 1984.
They were promoted as officiating Sub-Divisional Engineers in the year 1984.
In the writ petition, they have sought regularisation of their service in accordance with the provisions of the 1967 Rules.
The respondents have pleaded that the petitioners are not entitled to be appointed as substantive Sub Divisional Engineers and in any case, they cannot question the appointments of the non-official respondents.
C.W.P.
No. 3926/87 :  4.
Petitioner-Krishan Kumar, who joined service as Junior Engineers in PWD (Public Health Department) was promoted in 1984.
His case is almost identical to the case of the petitioners in Civil Writ Petition No 3736 of 1987.
The only additional factor mentioned in the writ petition is that he was convicted in January 1982 under the Prevention of Corruption Act, but was acquitted by the appellate Court on 771983.
He has challenged his reversion on the ground of arbitrariness, violation of fundamental right to equality enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and the principles of natural justice.
The written statement filed by the official respondents is similar to the one filed in Civil Writ Petition No 3736 of 1987.
C.W.P.
No. 4267/87 :  5.
Petitioner-Chaman Lal Dubey has challenged his reversion from the post of Sub-Divisional Engineer on the ground that without undertaking an exercise for making regular recruitment in accordance with Rules 6 and 7, the respondents cannot revert him.
C.W.P.
No. 4416/87 :  6.
Petitioner-Sohan Lal Jindal, joined as Junior Engineer on 12.1.1968.
After passing AMIE in August 1981, he was promoted as Sub-Divisional Engineer on 14101983.
He has sought regularisation of his service against the quota of Junior Engineers who have passed AMIE  C.W.P.
No. 3403/88 :  7.
Petitioners-RK Chopra and others have invoked writ jurisdiction of the High Court for claiming promotion to the posts of Sub-Divisional Engineers.
They have also prayed for quashing clauses (iii) and (iv) of Appendix-B annexed with the 1967 Rules on the ground that no special quota can be prescribed for recruitment of persons possessing higher qualifications.
C.W.P.
No. 5429/97 :  8.
The Diploma Engineers Associations has approached this Court for issuance of a mandamus to the respondents to make appointment/promotions to the cadre of Sub-Divisional Engineers in accordance with Rule 6 of the 1967 Rules.
Their contention is that by misconstruing the stay order passed by the High Court in Civil Writ Petition No 7415 of 1995, the respondents are not making regular promotions to the posts of Sub-Divisional Engineers and in this manner valuable rights acquired by the persons serving in the lower cadre have been frustrated.
In the written statement, the respondents have expressed their inability to make regular appointments in view of the stay order passed by this Court.
C.W.P.
No. 5876/98 :  9.
The petitioner-Manmohan Singh and others have also filed writ for issuance of a mandamus to the respondents to make promotions on the posts of Sub- Divisional Engineers keeping in view the law laid down by the Supreme Court in RK Sabharwal v. State of Punjab JT 1995(2) SC 351 : 1995(2) SCT 646 (SC).
10.
We have heard S/Shri R.K. Malik, Rajiv Atma Ram, Gopal Mahajan, learned Counsel for the petitioners and Shri GS Grewal, learned Advocate General, Punjab assisted by Shri Rupinder Khosla, learned Deputy Advocate General, Punjab.
11.
The submission of the learned Counsel for the petitioners is that due to the failure of the respondents to undertake an exercise for regular recruitment in accordance with the 1967 Rules, the respondents have deprived the petitioners of their legitimate right to be promoted in their respective quota.
Learned Counsel pointed out that the stay order passed in Civil Writ Petition No 7415 of 1995 must be treated as confined to the category of posts to which petitioners-Rajinder Pal Sharma etc. belong and it cannot be interpreted as imposing total ban on regular recruitment to the service.
Learned Counsel argued that the failure of the official respondents to make regular appointments has resulted in violation of the fundamental as well as legal rights of the petitioners.
The learned Advocate General stated that the meeting of the screening committee was held on 13121985 as per the provisions of Rule 9(3) and 9(4) of the 1967 Rules but recommendations of the screening committee could not be given effect to due to the stay order passed by the Supreme Court on 1721986 in Civil Writ Petition No 4025 of 1981, Bishan Dass Bagga v. State of Punjab, which was vacated on 421992.
He submitted that the government is willing to implement the recommendations of the screening committee but that cannot be done because of the stay order passed in Civil Writ Petition No 7415 of 1995.
The learned Advocate General also invited our attention to the order dated 581994 passed in Civil Writ Petition Nos. 3405, 3406 and 3407 of 1994, Jasbir Singh and others v. State of Punjab and others, and submitted that the government is bound to give effect to those directions subject to other orders of the Court.
12.
For the purpose of proper appreciation of the petitioners' grievance, it will be useful to refer to Rule 6 of the 1967 Rules and the order dated 581994 passed in Civil Writ Petition No 3405 of 1994 and other connected writ petitions.
The same read as under :-  Rule 6 of 1967 Rules : "Recruitment to Service - (1) Recruitment to the service for cadre and ex-cadre posts shall be made in the following manner only from the sources listed below in the proportions and the order indicated against a lot of every 40 vacancies :-  Allotment to each source in lot of 40 vacancies comprising five blocks  Method of Recruitment Proportion I, II III IV V 1.
Direct appointment 26 5 6 5 5 5 2.
Promotion from the members of the Punjab PWD Public Health Sectional Officers Service 8 2 1 2 1 2 3.
Promotion from Draftsmen members of the Draftsmen and Tracers Service 2 1 - - 1 - 4.
Promotion from members of the Punjab PWD Public Health Sectional Officers Service and the Draftsman members of the Draftsmen and Tracers Service, possessing qualification prescribed in Appendix B 4 1 1 1 1 1 Total 40 8 8 8 8 8 Order dated 581994 : (i) The Government should determine the vacancies of promotion quota for the years 1985 & onwards.
(ii) The recommendations of the committee which met on 13121985 be implemented if there is no other impediment in the implementation thereof.
(iii) If for any reason, which must be recorded in writing, recommendations of the committee, which met on 13121985, cannot be implemented, a meeting of the committee should now be convened and it be called upon to make its recommendations for the years 1985, 1986 and onwards.
(iv) The committee shall make its recommendations against year-wise anticipated vacancies for promotion quota and Government should make regular promotions under Rule 9(11) in accordance with the recommendations of the committee after they are approved by the commission.
(v) This exercise should be undertaken and completed within a period of three months.
(vi) If direct recruits selected by the Public Service Commission become available and they are to be appointed against the quota prescribed for direct recruitment, the Government shall be free to replace, revert any existing Sub-Divisional Engineer who may be holding the post on ad hoc basis or who may be having current-duty-charge.
(vii) If in the meanwhile, it becomes imperative for the Government to make any promotion on ad hoc basis, such promotion be made strictly according to seniority subject to suitability.
(viii) In future, no arrangement be made against the promotion quota posts by giving current-duty-charge to the holder of a lower post.
(ix) The Government may resort to ad hoc/officiating promotion and pay to the incumbent of the post, salary prescribed for the post of Sub-Divisional Engineer."
We may also take cognizance of the fact that in CM Nos. 12632 of 1994 and 12733 of 1994 filed in Civil Writ Petition No 8230 of 1988, the Court passed order dated 19.12.1994 directing the consideration of the case of the petitioners provisionally keeping in view the date of their acquiring AMIE Likewise, in CM No. 12786 of 1994 filed in Civil Writ Petition No 4416 of 1987 and CM No. 12610 of 1994 filed in Civil Writ Petition No 4949 of 1987, directions have been issued on 20.12.1994 and 16121994 respectively for consideration of the cases of the petitioners on provisional basis keeping in view their qualifications and permission has been given for appearance of the petitioners in the screening test subject to the result of the writ petition.
13.
We have perused the averments made in these writ petitions.
We have also read the contents of Civil Writ Petition No 7415 of 1995 in which the petitioners, Rajinder Pal Sharma and Amarjit Kumar, have prayed for directing the respondents to maintain the seniority of the Junior Engineers on the basis of length of service in accordance with Rule 11 of the Punjab Department of Public Works, Junior Engineers (Public Health Branch) Class III Service Rules, 1991 and to consider their eligibility for confirmation on the post of Sub-Divisional Engineers against source No. IV as envisaged in Rule 6 of the 1967 Rules from the date of occurrence of vacancies against the said source keeping in view the law laid down by the Supreme Court in SLP No. 2294 of 1993, Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board v. SM Sharma and others.
It is, thus, evident that the petitioners Rajinder Pal Sharma and Amarjit Kumar have prayed for correct fixation of their seniority and grant of promotion from source 4 specified in Rule 6 of the 1967 Rules and they do not have any lis with the government so far as promotion from other sources is concerned and there is no reason why the government should not make regular recruitment to the service from other sources.
In our view, the official respondents have misinterpreted the interim order passed in Civil Writ Petition No 7415 of 1995 and have stalled regular promotions of persons who are eligible for recruitment from source Nos. 2 and 3 which they are otherwise bound to do in terms of the order dated 581994 passed in Jasbir Singh's case (supra).
14.
In is unfortunate that regular promotions to the service have been held up for last over 15 years and a majority of officers in the cadre of Sub- Divisional Engineers are holding the posts on the basis of officiating and ad hoc promotions.
This situation is not only affecting morale of the service but has also adversely affected the working of the department.
15.
No other point has been argued.
16.
For the reasons mentioned above, we allow the writ petitions and direct the official respondents to make regular recruitment to the service under the 1967 Rules from source Nos. 2 and 3 specified in Rule 6 thereof.
The official respondents shall implement the recommendations of the screening committee which met on 13121985 and make regular promotions on the basis of such recommendations within a period of one month from the date of submission of certified copy of this order before the Secretary to Government, Punjab, Public Works Department (Public Health Branch).
17.
Promotions from these sources shall also be made for the subsequent years keeping in view the directions given in Jasbir Singh's case (supra).
That exercise shall also be undertaken and completed within four months from the date of submission of copy of this order.
We also direct the official respondents to determine the inter se seniority of the officers on provisional basis and make promotions to the next higher posts by considering those who fulfil the conditions of eligibility and are found suitable.
The rights of persons who are eligible for promotion from source 4 shall be decided in the light of orders which may be passed in the Civil Writ Petition No 7415 of 1995 and other similar cases.
