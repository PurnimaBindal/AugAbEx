GS Singhvi, J - The petitioner has prayed for issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to regularise the period of his service from 2481995 to 341997 and to release his unpaid salary with interest at the rate of 18% per annum.
2.
The averments made in the writ petition show that the petitioner joined service as ad hoc J.B.T.
Teacher on 27.8.1971.
His services were regularised with effect from 111979.
By an order dated 1571995, the District Education Officer, Hisar transferred Shri Hawa Singh, who was working as J.B.T.
Teacher in Government Primary School, Ban Mandhori to Government Primary School, Kheri Lochabh, vice the petitioner.
He i.e. the petitioner was required to be relieved by the Block Education Officer, Narnaul on the arrival of Shri Hawa Singh but the latter relieved him from duties in the afternoon of 2381995 even before Hawa Singh reported for duty.
He also issued Last Pay Certificate to the petitioner.
On being relieved, the petitioner reported for duty before the Block Education, Officer, Fatehabad, Hisar for being allowed to join duty as J.B.T.
Teacher in Government Primary School, Ban Mandhori but he did not accept his joining report on the ground that there is no separate primary school in Ban Mandhori.
To this effect letter Annexure P-1 dated 2581995 was written by the Block Education Officer, Fatehabad, Hisar.
Immediately thereafter, petitioner submitted representation Annexure P-2 dated 2881995 and requested the Director, Primary Education, Haryana to give direction for his posting.
He repeated this request vide representations dated 4101995, 211996, 2721996, 841996, 27.9.1996 and 241997.
Copies of representations dated 841996 and 241997 have been placed on record as Annexures P-3 and P-4, and photostat copies of representations dated 4101995, 211996, 21721996 and 27.9.1996 were produced by the learned counsel during the course of hearing.
He also placed on record two other representations dated 251997 and 2851997.
On his representation dated 241997, Director, Primary Education, haryana, recorded the following note :-  "This is very serious case.
DPEO/HSR is directed to post him against some vacancy positively on 3/4 and send to me detailed report about this lapse by 7/4 positively.
The official responsible be charge sheeted and further action initiated."
Thereafter, District Primary Education Officer, Hisar, issued order Annexure P-5 dated 441997 adjusting the petitioner in Government Primary School, Mayond Khurd.
However, the petitioner was not paid salary and allowances for the intervening period compelling him to seek intervention of the Court.
3.
In the written statement filed by them through Shri Tara Chand Saini, District Primary Education Officer, Hisar, respondent Nos. 1 to 5 have pleaded that the petitioner did not join duty at his new place of posting and remained wilfully absent from duty from 2481995 to 351997 and, therefore, it is not possible to treat him on duty and to pay salary.
The respondents have also accused him of wilful absence from duty and disobedience of the orders issued by the department.
In paragraph 2 of the written statement, respondents have also made an attempt to raise an issue relating to alleged embezzlement by the petitioner of the amount collected during enrolment drive.
4.
In the replication, petitioner has refuted the allegation of embezzlement and has placed reliance on Annexure P-8 written by the Head Teacher, Government Primary School, Mayond Khurd to the District Primary Education Officer, Fatehabad.
He has also placed on record Annexures P-9, P-9A, P-10 and P-11 to show that a deliberate attempt has been made by some officials of the Education Department to mislead the Court in believing that he had absented from duty.
5.
During the course of arguments, learned Deputy Advocate General reiterated the stand taken by the respondents in the preliminary objections and submitted that the petitioner is not entitled to be paid salary for the intervening period because he did not report for duty.
However, when we confronted him with the fact that the petitioner had made representations dated 2881995, 4101995, 211996, 2721996, 841996, 27.9.1996, 241997, 251997, 2851997 and 23.7.1998 and pointed out to him that respondent No. 2 himself felt satisfied with the genuineness of the claim of the petitioner, the learned Deputy Advocate General could not advance any argument to justify the failure of the concerned authorities of the Education Department to allow him to join duty at the place of transfer.
The promptness with which the petitioner made representations to the Director, Primary Education complaining that he was not being allowed to join duty and his efforts to seek redressal by making representations to the higher departmental authorities are, in our considered view, sufficient to negative the plea of respondents that the petitioner deliberately avoided joining the duty at Ban Mandori.
This conclusion of ours is amply supported by two facts, namely, (i) that the Block Education Officer, Fatehabad (Hisar) wrote letter Annexure P-1 dated 2581995 to the District Education Officer, Hisar seeking the latter's guidance by mentioning that there is no separate Primary School in Ban Mandori; and (ii) that no tangible action was taken by the respondents in spite of the unequivocal direction given by respondent No. 2 vide his note dated 241997.
6.
On the basis of the above, we hold that the respondents have by their acts and omissions deprived the petitioner of his legitimate right to serve the department between 2381995 to 3.4.1997 and to get pay and allowances.
As a logical corollary, we hold that their failure to pay salary and allowances to the petitioner for the intervening period is wholly arbitrary and violative of his fundamental rights to equality and livelihood.
7.
In the result, writ petition is allowed.
Respondents are directed to pay salary and allowances to the petitioner and also give him all consequential benefits in respect of the period during which he was unlawfully kept out of employment by being not allowed to join duty after 2481995.
The arrears along with interest at the rate of 18% per annum shall be paid to him within three months of the submission of certified copy of this order.
In case the respondents fail to carry out this order, petitioner shall be free to bring this fact to the notice of the court by filing a miscellaneous application.
Petition allowed.
