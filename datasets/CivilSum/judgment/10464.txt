Jawahar Lal Gupta, J - The plaintiff-appellant filed a suit for a declaration that the order dated June 13, 1986, by which he was retired at the age of 58 years with effect from June 30, 1986, was violative of the Jind State Civil Service Regulations, 1945, and is, thus, illegal.
He prayed for a declaration that he was entitled to continue in service till June 30, 1990, when he would have attained the age of 62 years.
This suit was dismissed by the learned trial Court.
His appeal having been dismissed by the appellate Court, he has filed the present second appeal.
2.
The appellant was born on July 1, 1928.
He was appointed as a Clerk in the erestwhile state of Jind on Feburary 13, 1946.
Various princely States were merged to form Pepsu in the year 1948.
In 1956, the erstwhile State of Pepsu was merged with the Punjab State.
The State of Punjab was reorganised in the year 1966.
The State of Punjab was reorganised in the year 1966.
Irrespective of the events of merger and reorganisation, the appellant claimed that he continued to be governed by the Regulations, he was entitled to continue in service up to the age of 62 years and could not have been retired before June 30, 1990.
The defendant contested this claim.
The lower appellate Court considered the matter in detail.
Relying upon Division Bench decision of this Court in Krishan Lal Aggarwal v. State of Punjab, 1995(1) SCT 382 (P&H) : 1995(2) RSJ 160, the Court held that the appellant cannot take the benefit of the rules which were in force at the time of his appointment as a Clerk.
He having been promoted as a Naib Tehsildar, would be governed by the rules applicable to that cadre.
Accordingly, it rejected the claim.
3.
Mr. RN Raina, learned counsel for the appellant, had initially argued that case at a considerable length and contended that in view of the two Single Bench decisions of this Court in The State of Haryana and others v. Amar Nath Bansal, 1993(4) SCT 734 (P&H) : 1993(4) RSJ 427, and Ram Sarup Jindal v. The Chief Secretary and another, 1994(3) SCT 258 (P&H) : 1994(3) RSJ 224, the view taken by the lower appellate Court cannot be sustained.
He had even attempted to distinguish the Division Bench judgment in Krishan Lal Aggarwal's case (supra).
However, ultimately the counsel himself pointed out that the matter was concluded against him by the decision of their Lordships of the Supreme Court in State of Haryana and others v. Amar Nath Bansal, JT 1997(1) SC 734.
In this case, the claim made by the employee for continuing in service up to the age of 62 years has been categorically negatived.
Resultantly, the claim made by the appellant cannot be sustained.
The appeal is wholly lacking in merit.
It is consequently dismissed.
