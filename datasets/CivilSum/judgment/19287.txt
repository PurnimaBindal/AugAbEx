NK Sodhi, J. - Mr. Hemant Kumar, Advocate for the respondent Corporation states that the charges against the petitioner have since been dropped and that the retiral benefits due to him would be released within a period of two months, provided the petitioner fills up the requisite forms which he would obtain from the Office of the Deputy Manager (Administration), Regional Office, Food Corporation of India, Chandigarh.
The petitioner is directed to do the needful within two weeks from today and thereafter the Corporation shall take steps to release the retiral benefits to the petitioner within the aforesaid period and pay him the dues with interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum payable with effect from 181990 till the payment is made.
The writ petition stands disposed of in the above terms.
