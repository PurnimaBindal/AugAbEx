Amarjeet Chaudhary, J (Oral) - In this writ petition the petitioner has impugned the order dated 1941993 of Senior Regional Manager, Food Corporation of India, vide which de novo inquiry against the petitioner was ordered to be initiated.
The order of the de novo inquiry has been impugned on the ground that the Senior Regional Manager had no authority to order de novo inquiry after the submission of the report of inquiry conducted earlier.
Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that in writ petition No. 2108 of 1994 (BR Yadav and others v. FCI and others) decided on 3091994, the Court on identical grounds had quashed the order, ordering de novo inquiry.
Learned counsel for the respondents has not been able to repel the arguments of the counsel for the petitioner.
In this view of the matter, the writ petition is allowed in terms of BR Yadav's case (supra) and the order dated 1941993, a copy of which is Annexure P-3 to the writ petition, is quashed.
There will be no order as to costs.
