GS Singhvi, J - These three petitions contain almost identical prayers made by the petitioner for grant of revised pay scales of Rs. 950-1400 with effect from 111986 and to assign them seniority with effect from the date of the promotion to the higher posts in the work charged cadre.
2.
Madan Lal (petitioner in CWP No. 12641 of 1994) was appointed on 991976 as Fitter Coolie on work charged basis by the Executive Engineer (P.W.D.)
Public Health Division No. 1, Karnal.
He was promoted as Fitter (Pay Scale of Rs. 400-600) with effect from 2171983 vide order Annexure P2.
He was shown to be on probation of three months.
When the Government of Haryana revised the pay scales of its employees with effect from 111986 the pay scale of Rs. 400-600 was raised to Rs. 950-1400.
However, the petitioner was not allowed the benefit of this grade even though other similarly situated persons were given the benefit of the revised pay scale.
The petitioner's pay was fixed in the pay scale of Rs. 750-940.
The petitioner has claimed that denial of salary to him in the grade of Rs. 950-1400 amounts to violation of the doctrine of 'equal pay for equal work' and that there is no reason or rhyme in depriving the petitioner of the benefit of pay in the scale of Rs. 950-1400.
The petitioner further says that although he had a right to be regularised on the post of Fitter, the respondents have regularised his service on the lower post and in this manner he has been subjected to discrimination.
Another grievance made by the petitioner is that from the month of May 1994 his pay has been reduced without any reason or rhyme.
3.
The writ petition has been opposed by the respondents, who have pleaded that the petitioner was appointed on work charged basis and was promoted as Fitter Grade-II on work charged basis.
Subsequently, in accordance with the policy formulated by the Government of Haryana for regularisation of the work charged employees, the petitioner's service was brought on the regular cadre vide order dated 1531984 issued by the Superintending Engineer (P.W.D.
), Public Health Circle, Karnal.
The respondents have pleaded that the petitioner has been working as Fitter Coolie and he has no right to be paid salary in the grade of Rs. 950-1400.
The respondents have asserted that the petitioner is not entitled to the fixation of his pay in the grade of Rs. 950-1400 because he has clearly accepted the revised pay scale of the post of Fitter Coolie.
4.
CWP No. 13702 of 1994 has been filed by Jai Bhagwan with the allegation that he joined service on 12.12.1979 as Keyman and he was promoted as Pump Operator Grade-II with effect from 921987 in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600.
He too was placed on probation and although he has successfully completed the period of probation, order of confirmation has not been issued in his favour.
Further statement of the petitioner is that with the revision of pay scales he was given the benefit of the grade of Rs. 950-1400 with deffect from 111986 and was also given the benefit of annual grade increment in February 1988.
However, from 1989 onwards the benefit of grade increment has not been allowed to him.
The petitioner further says that a number of persons junior to him were promoted as Pump Operators Grade-II without considering his case and that from the month of July 1994 he is being paid salary in the grade of Rs. 750- 940 instead of Rs. 950-1400.
Petitioner's claim is that he has right to be paid salary in the revised pay scale of Rs. 950-1400.
Another claim made by the petitioner is that for grant of annual grade increment from 1989 and also for his retrospective promotion as Pump Operator Grade-II.
5.
In reply the respondents have stated that the petitioner was brought in the regular cadre as a Keyman with effect from 111987 vide order Annexure R1 dated 21101988 and that he has no right to be promoted as Pump Operator Grade-II.
The respondents have alleged that the petitioner has been discharging the duties as Keyman and he does not have any legal or Constitutional right to be treated as Pump Operator Grade-II or to be paid salary in the revised pay scale of Rs. 950-1400.
6.
In CWP No. 16714 of 1994 petitioner Shiv Shankar has stated that he was appointed as work charged employee with effect from 1541977 and was promoted as Pump Operator Grade-II with effect from 14.2.1983 in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600.
According to the petitioner, he was not given the benefit of revised pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 and although he has become entitled to be confirmed on the post of Pump Operator Grade-II after the expiry of the period of probation specified in the order of promotion, the respondents have not taken any steps to treat him as a confirmed employee.
According to the petitioner, the respondents have reduced his pay without any notice or opportunity of hearing.
Reply filed by the respondents to this petition is almost identical to the reply filed in other two writ petitions.
According to the respondents, the petitioner does not have any legal or constitutional right to be promoted as Pump Operator.
7.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the record of the case.
8.
For recruitment to the various posts in the work charged establishment of the Public Works Department, the Engineer-in-Chief, Haryana, P.W.D.
Public Health Branch, has issued executive instructions vide Memo, dated 19101981.
It has been circulated by the various other authorities.
One such circular dated 23111981 issued by the Superintending Engineer (PWD), Public Health Circle, Ambala Cantt., has been placed before me.
A look at the schedule annexed with this letter of the Superintending Engineer shows that on the post of Water Pump Operator persons holding the posts of Keymen/Oilmen (five years' experience) and Fitter Coolies (with seven years' experience) are eligible to be promoted.
On the basis of these rules it can be said that each of the petitioners fulfils all the conditions of eligibility which could entitle them to be promoted as Pump Operator Grade-II/Fittter Coolie and there was nothing inherently wrong in the promotion of the petitioners.
9.
There is no denying that the petitioners had been promoted as Pump Operator Grade-II/Fitter Coolie in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600 and out of the three petitioners, Jai Bhagwan's pay was also fixed in the revised pay scale of Rs. 950-1400.
He was also allowed the benefit of one increment in the year 1988.
Other petitioners have not been given the benefit of the grade of Rs. 950-1400.
10.
During the course of hearing Inquired from Shri Raina whether any action oriented notice or opportunity of hearing was given to any of the petitioners before reducing their pay or withdrawing the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 in which the petitioner Jai Bhagwan was already fixed.
Shri Raina very fairly stated that no such notice was given to any of the petitioners.
It is also clear from the record that no order cancelling the promotion of the petitioners as Pump Operator Grade-II/Fitter has been passed by any competent authority.
It can thus be said that the petitioners Madan Lal and Shiv Shanker were holding the posts in the grade of Rs. 400-600 on the date of enforcement of the revised pay scales and the petitioner Jai Bhagwan was promoted as Pump Operator Grade-II in the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 after the enforcement of the revised pay scales.
pay of all the petitioners as also the pay scale of petitioner Jai Bhagwan has been reduced without any notice or inquiry and without any opportunity of hearing to them.
The respondents have also not been able to show as to how the petitioners could be denied the benefit of revised pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 when the orders of their promotions have not been nullified by any competent authority or by a Court of law.
Failure of the respondents to take action in accordance with the principles of natural justice has the logical fact of vitiating the impugned action.
11.
In view of the above conclusion, the writ petitions are allowed.
The respondents are directed to give the benefit of revised pay scale of Rs. 950- 1400 to all the petitioners with effect from 111986 or the date of their promotion.
The petitioners shall also be entitled to the benefit of annual increments.
It is, however, made clear that this order of mine shall not preclude the respondents from taking appropriate action in accordance with the principles of natural justice for revising the pay scales of the petitioners.
However, that can be done only after they are fixed in the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 under the directions of this Court.
If at all the respondents want to take any action adversely affecting the rights/status of the petitioners, such action should be taken after giving reasonable opportunity of hearing to the petitioners.
The competent authority shall also bear in mind that other similarly situated persons have already been given the benefit of revised pay scales.
