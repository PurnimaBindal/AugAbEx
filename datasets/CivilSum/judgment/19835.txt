RS Mongia, J (Oral) - In this writ petition, the primary challenge was against Respondent No. 2-Dr. Mangat Ram Dogra that he should not be interviewed for the post of Associate Professor in the Department of Ophthalmology in the Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh.
It is not necessary to state the grounds of attack against Respondent No.2, inasmuch as I had asked for the result of the interview, from the counsel appearing for Respondent No. 1.
He has placed on record a photocopy of the proceedings of the Selection Committee for the post of Associate Professor of Ophthalmology.
The Selection Committee did not find any one of the three candidates, including Respondent No. 2, suitable for the post in question.
In view of this, the writ petition becomes infructuous and is disposed of as such.
No costs.
Order accordingly.
