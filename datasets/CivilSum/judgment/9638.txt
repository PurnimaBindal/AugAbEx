GR Majithia, J - The petitioners have challenged the order of respondent No. 2 dated November, 1980, whereby the recommendation of the Pay Scales Anomalies Removal Committee were accepted.
2.
Respondent No. 1 directed that the persons employees in the Clerical Cadre in the University in pre-revised scale of Rs. 120-5-145-7-180/12-300 prior to the date of the notification of the revised scales, i.e. June 6, 1980, be allowed the scale of Rs. 420-10-490-525-15600-EB-20-700 as a measure personal, but persons recruited to the cadre on or after that date (i.e. June 6, 1980) should be in the normal scale of Rs. 400-10-490/540-15-600-EB-20-660.
It was further ordered that selection grade of Rs. 480-15-600-EB-20-700-30-760 should be applicable to 20% of all permanent posts of Clerical Cadre whether in the scale of Rs. 1400-10-490/540-15-600-EB-20-660 or in the personal scale of Rs. 420-10-490/525-15-600-EB-20-700.
The incumbents in the pre-revised indentical scale of Rs. 120-5-145/7-180/12-300 and given the scale of Rs. 400-10-490/540-15-600-EB-20-600, on the analogy of revision of pay scale of Clerks appointed before June 6, 1980 would also be entitled to personal scale of Rs. 420-10-490/525-15-600-EB-20-700, but those recruited after that date would be in the scale of Rs. 400-10-490/540-15-600-EB-20-660.
The petitioners maintain that the Clerks who were appointed on or after June 6, 1980 were granted pay scale of Rs. 400-10-490/540-15-600-EB-20-660 with effect from June 6, 1980, and this pay scale of Rs. 400-600 was revised to Rs. 950-1500 with effect from January 1, 1986.
The qualification for the post of Clerk is Matric I Class or HIgher Secondary II Class or BA The duties/responsibilities of Clerks are the same.
All the Clerks working with respondent No. 2 form one cadre and they have one seniority list.
The persons appointed before June 6, 1980 or thereafter form one cadre and have one seniority list.
The petitioners urge that the persons who are similarly situated cannot be granted different pay scales.
3.
The respondents in their written statement have justified their action that the recommendation of the Pay Scales Anomaly Removal Committee of respondent No. 2 have been accepted.
They maintain that the petitioners who were appointed in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600 are not entitled to the revised pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 granted as a measure personal to certain clerks who were recruited in the better pay scale prior to June 6, 1980.
Since the petitioners were recruited in the pay scale of Rs. 400-660, their request for grant for revised pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 cannot be accepted.
4.
The defence taken by the respondents cannot be sustained.
Clerks cannot be classified into two groups.
The Clerks who have been placed in two groups for the purpose of fixation of pay do equal and similar work.
There is no plea by the respondents that the group who is placed in an advantageous position do not perform work and duties of the same nature as are performed by the other group or perform duties which are onerous or are different from the work performed by the petitioners.
The grouping of the clerks is violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
The apex Court in P Savita and others v. Union of India and others, 1985(3) SLR 29, in almost similar circumstances, held as under :-  "For the purposes of the case in hand, it is sufficient to note that the classification between two groups of Senior Draughtsmen is without any basis.
They do the same work, they perform the same duties, and as such the ratio of the decision in Randhir Singh's case applies to this case with greater force.
The order passed by the Government of India on 27th January, 1978, implementing this classification has to be struck down and we do so.
In our opinion, it would be a grant injustice to continue the appellant on the scale of pay of Draughtsman even after promotion as Senior Draughtsman, which is destructive of all incentive and initiative in the service.
In our judgment, the High Court was in error in declining relief to the appellants.
We accordingly set aside the judgment of the High Court and allow this appeal and direct the Union of India to fix the scale of pay of appellants at Rs. 425-15-500-EB-15-560-20-640-EB-20-700.
The appellants will be entitled to costs from the respondent No.
1."
The ratio of this judgment is fully applicable to the facts of the instant case.
For the reasons aforementioned, the writ petition succeeds and is allowed.
The respondents are directed to grant the same pay scale and allowances to the petitioners as are being given to Clerks appointed before June 6, 1980, from the date of appointment.
There will be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
