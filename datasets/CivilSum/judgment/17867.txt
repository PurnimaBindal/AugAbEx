VK Bali, J - Concededly, the petitioner had put in more than two years' service prior to the filing of the present petition.
Learned counsel for the State contends that his case for regularisation shall be considered in view of the instructions of the State as also the judgment rendered by the Supreme Court in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201 : 1992(5) SPJ1, and if he fulfills all the conditions as mentioned in the instructions and the judgment aforesaid, his services shall be regularised.
I order accordingly.
However, till such time the case of the petitioner is considered for regularisation, his services shall not be terminated.
The petition is disposed of No order as to costs.
Order accordingly.
