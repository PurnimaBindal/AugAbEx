NK Sodhi, J - The plainfiff-appellant while working as an Accounts Clerk was involved in a criminal case and convicted on charges of embezzlement by the Additional Sessions Judge, Amritsar.
He was thereafter dismissed from service and the order of dismissal was successfully challenged by him in a civil court and that decree has become final.
The prayer made in the suit out of which the present appeal has arisen is that the plaintiff should be granted promotion from the date when it became due to him on his turn and the persons junior to him had been promoted.
The lower Appellate Court took the view that because of his conduct which led to the conviction he was not entitled to promotion.
The argument of the learned counsel for the plaintiff - appellant is that it was for the department to decide whether the appellant was entitled to promotion or not and that the Court could not take decision in this regard.
There is merit in this contention.
It is correct that as to whether an employee who had been convicted by a Criminal Court should be given promotion or not is a matter for the competent authority to decide after taking into consideration his conduct that led to his conviction and not for the civil court.
Consequently, the appeal is allowed and the judgment and decree of the lower Appellate Court set aside.
The competent authority is directed to consider the case of the plaintiff-appellant and pass an appropriate order as to whether he is entitled to promotion with effect from the dates when it became due to him and his juniors were promoted, in the light of his conduct that led to his conviction.
A decision in this regard shall be taken within three months from the date of receipt of a copy of this order.
No costs.
