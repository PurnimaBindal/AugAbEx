Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - The petitioner through the present writ petition has prayed for issuance of a direction to the respondents to count his entire service for the purpose of pension, gratuity and other retiral benefits commencing from 22101952 to 3131991, the day on which he retired as Assistant Foreman from service under the Bhakra Beas Management Board alongwith interest at the rate of 12% per annum.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he joined as Electrician Chargeman in the Irrigation Department of Punjab State on 22.10.1952 and was promoted as Assistant Foreman on 2581959 and further promoted to the post of Foreman on 1111960.
He continued working as Foreman till 1861963, in the Irrigation Department of the State of Punjab.
3.
On 1961963 he was transferred from Irrigation Department to the Beas Satluj Link Project which was under the Nangal Canal circle of Irrigation Department itself.
He was transferred from Nangal Circle to the Beas Satluj Link as the Foreman on the same day i.e. 1961963 without any break under the same employer.
It is also the case of the petitioner that he continued as the employee of Irrigation Department till 30.10.1966 (it should be 31.10.1966) and initially on 1111966 on re-organisation of State of Punjab, his services were transferred from Beas Satluj Link Project to the newly constituted Beas Construction Board under Section 80 of the Re- organisation Act and he was further transferred from Beas Construction Board to Bhakra Beas Management Board w.e.f.
1.5.1979 without any break and he retired from service as Foreman under the Bhakra Beas Management Board on 3131991.
4.
It is also the case of the petitioner that there was no break in his service right from the very beginning though the management had been changed from time to time and that he has been paid pension and other retiral benefits by the Bhakra Beas Management Board by counting his service period from 1961963 to 3131991 and the remaining period from 22.10.1952 to 1861963 has not been counted.
He made representation to the Superintending Engineer Beas Satluj Link Circle for counting of his entire period from 22101952 to 3131991 but to no effect.
5.
The petitioner has alleged discrimination with him inasmuch as Shri Sucha Singh s/o Shri Ishar Singh was given benefit of entire service from 20359 to 30.6.1991 and he also made a reference that there were so many employees similarly situated.
It is also the case of the petitioner that he was made regular with effect from 111988 and in view of Full Bench decision of this Court in Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab , A.I.R. 1988 Punjab and Haryana 265 (F.B.
), he is entitled to pension even for the period rendered on work charged basis.
6.
The respondents contested the claim of the petitioner by filing separate written statements.
Respondent No. 1 took preliminary objections to the effect that the petitioner was working as work charged employee at Bhakra Nangal Project who was declared as surplus on completion of said project.
However, there was an independent project namely Beas Project and with a view to avoid un-employment of the persons like the petitioner, Bhakra Dam Administration in consultation with the Beas Project Administration evolved a scheme Annexure R-1, which was duly approved by the Governor of Punjab vide letter dated 2941961, Annexure R-2 and as per the said scheme, the re- employment by Beas Project, upon the resignation of the staff rendered surplus by the Bhakra Nangal Project were to be paid all terminal benefits including ex-gratia amount equivalent to retrenchment benefit and thus the service of the petitioner from Bhakra Nangal Project ended on 1861963 who was paid all terminal payments including the ex-gratia amount equivalent to the retrenchment benefits and the petitioner was given alternative appointment afresh vide appointment letter dated 1961963, Annexure R-3.
On re-organisation of the State of Punjab, the services of the petitioner and other similarly situated employees working in connection with the Beas Project came to be transferred by virtue of Section 75 of the Punjab Re- organisation Act to the newly constituted corporate entity known as Beas Project.
However, upon completion of Beas Project the services of the petitioner and others came over to the Bhakra Beas Management Board by virtue of Award of Industrial Tribunal in the year 1974.
Subsequently, the services of the petitioner were regularised by the Bhakra Beas Management Board w.e.f.
111988 who eventually retired from the said service on 3131991 and was given all the retiral benefits with effect from the date of his appointment in Beas Project i.e. 1961963 till the date of retirement.
It is also the stand of Respondent No. 1 that since the petitioner was in employment of Bhakra Nangal Project from 1.8.1957 to 1861963 as a work charged employee whose services were terminated on having become surplus and he having joined Beas Project afresh on work charged basis in accordance with the scheme Annexure R-1 and R-2, no benefit was admissible to him for the services rendered on Bhakra Nangal Project for the purpose of pension etc., particularly when he was paid the terminal benefits for the service rendered under the Bhakra Nangal Project by the said management.
7.
The respondent No. 1 has also taken preliminary objection that the petitioner is guilty of mis-representation of facts as he intermingled the Bhakra Nangal Project and the Beas Project which are separate entities.
The claim of the petitioner has been refuted on merit also.
8.
The respondent No. 2 has also opposed the claim of the petitioner on almost similar grounds as taken by the respondent No. 1.
The petitioner has not filed any replication to either of the written statements filed by the respondents No. 1 and 2.
9.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the paper book.
10.
Undisputedly the petitioner rendered service under the Bhakra Nangal Project upto 1861963 where he was declared as surplus and had tendered his resignation whereafter the petitioner accepted a fresh appointment under the Beas Project in accordance with the scheme floated by the authorities with the approval of the Governor of Punjab, in pursuance whereof the petitioner joined afresh on alternative job on 1961963.
This step of the authorities was a concession to the employees who were to face retrenchment on completion of Bhakra Nangal Project.
Hence alternative job was provided to them in terms of the Scheme Annexure R-1.
Not only the alternative job, but even the terminal benefits were given by the Bhakra Nangal Project authorities to the petitioner for the service rendered upto 1861963.
This fact was not disclosed by the petitioner in his Writ Petition.
11.
Later on the petitioner joined the Beas Project on 1961963 and became employee of the Beas Construction Board in terms of section 80 of the Re- Organisation Act with effect from 1111966 and subsequently on completion of Beas Project he became the employee of the Bhakra Beas Management Board constituted under Section 79 of the Punjab Re-Organisation Act, 1966.
The respondent/authorities on retirement of the petitioner on superannuation with effect from 3131991, paid him all the retiral benefits for the service rendered from 1961963 to 3131991.
Hence there is no illegality in the action of the respondents, inasmuch as the petitioner was entitled to pensionary benefits only for the service rendered under the Beas Project/Beas Construction Board/Bhakra Beas Management Board from 1961963 and not for the period earlier thereto for which he had already been paid terminal benefits.
The petitioner cannot claim the double benefits for the said service.
12.
We have also considered the pleas of the petitioner that in view of Kesar Chand's case (supra) his entire service should have been counted for the pensionary benefits.
This argument is mis-conceived for the simple reason that in Kesar Chand's case (supra), no terminal benefits were given to the employee before regularisation of his services and employer had not changed nor there was resignation from earlier service and acceptance of fresh appointment.
It was in that context that the work charged service followed by the regular one was counted particularly when it was under the same management.
But in the instant case, the petitioner having received the terminal benefits under the Bhakra Nangal Project which was under the State of Punjab (Irrigation Department) and he was given fresh appointment under the new Project with effect from 1961963 after his tendering resignation so he cannot claim the benefit of the said service for which he has been adequately paid by the authorities.
Hence the facts of Kesar Chand's case (supra) are distinguishable from the facts of the present case.
13.
As regards the plea of the petitioner that he has been discriminated vis-a-vis other similarly situated employees like Sucha Singh, the respondents have denied this is the written statement.
Moreover, the petitioner has not stated whether Sucha Singh had also resigned from earlier service, paid terminal benefits and also appointed afresh like him.
In the absence of such a plea and material, the ground of discrimination cannot be accepted.
The petitioner concealed the factum of his having resigned from earlier service, receipt of terminal benefits and fresh appointment.
On pointing out by respondents, he has chosen not to controvert it by filing rejoinder.
So the petitioner cannot succeed on such a ground.
Secondly, in the face of concessional scheme to avoid retrenchment of the surplus staff as contained in Annexures R-1 and R-2, if the authorities have given wrong benefit to a particular employee de hors the scheme, it cannot be a precedent and ground of discrimination.
For the foregoing reasons, we find no merit in the writ petition.
Consequently, the same is dismissed in limine, leaving the parties to bear their own costs.
Petition dismissed.
