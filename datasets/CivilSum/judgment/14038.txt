Mehtab S Gill, J - The petitioner has filed this Writ Petition in the nature of Mandamus for directing the respondents to release his pensionary/retiral benefits like pension, gratuity and commutation of pension along with interest at the rate of 18% per annum from August, 1994.
He has further prayed for issuing a writ in the nature of Certiorari for quashing order dated 1721995, Annexure P-8, issued by respondent No. 3 whereby recovery of Rs. 300/- has been ordered to be effected from his gratuity.
2.
The petitioner has averred that he retired from the post of City Magistrate, Panipat of HCS Cadre on 3171994 on attaining the age of superannuation.
He assumed the charge of City Magistrate, Panipat on 11.10.1993.
He made a representation to the respondents for release of difference of arrears of salary from 3101988 to 11.10.1993.
The respondent No. 1 passed an order dated 7101993 (Annexure P-2) promoting the petitioner as District Revenue Officer with effect from 3101988 along with all consequential benefits.
The respondent No. 1 passed an order on 2651995 to the effect that the petitioner will not be entitled to any arrears of pay etc. prior to his posting before 11.10.1993 in the HCS (Executive Branch) Cadre.
(Annexure P-3).
Going through orders Annexures P-1 and P-2, it is clear that the petitioner was posted in HCS (Executive Branch) Cadre with all consequential benefits.
A representation (Annexure P-4) was made by the petitioner to the respondents on 721994 mentioning therein that he was nominated to HCS along with all consequential benefits.
Another representation dated 2991994 (Annexure P-5) was made by the petitioner.
Respondent No. 3, Deputy Commissioner, Panipat vide his order dated 571994 (Annexure P-7) had given the charge/duties of District Revenue Officer inclusive of Land Acquisition Officer in addition to duties of City Magistrate, Panipat to the petitioner.
The petitioner was holding triple charge.
Because of this reason, he was given special pay of Rs. 400/- on account of additional duties.
3.
Respondent No. 3 issued another letter dated 1721995 (Annexure P-8) to the petitioner wherein he stated that on the recommendations of the Chief Secretary, recovery of Rs. 8,15615 be made from the petitioner.
The reason given was that the petitioner was wrongly granted Rs. 400/- per month instead of Rs. 100/- per month as special pay.
This order was passed without affording any opportunity of hearing to the petitioner.
4.
Notice of motion was issued.
5.
Written statement was filed.
6.
The counsel for the respondents has stated that the representations of the petitioner were considered.
It is admitted case that the petitioner was appointed to the HCS (Executive Branch) Cadre.
It is further admitted that he was posted as City Magistrate, Panipat on 11.10.1993 but the counsel for the respondents has stated that the petitioner was discharging the duties of District Revenue Officer and the duties of Land Acquisition Collector were conferred upon him vide notification dated 11.3.1993 (Annexure P-6).
Vide this notification, the duties of Land Acquisition Collector were given to him by virtue of his holding the charge of District Revenue Officer.
He has further stated that in his capacity as District Revenue Officer, the petitioner was working as Land Acquisition Collector.
A copy of the notification has been attached as Annexure R-1.
Thus, the petitioner is not entitled to special pay of Rs. 400/-.
7.
The counsel for the petitioner has stated that no opportunity was afforded to the petitioner when order dated 1721995, Annexure P-8 was passed.
He has further stated that it is a non-speaking order and cryptic one.
8.
It is the admitted case of the parties that special pay of Rs. 400/- was being given to the petitioner.
It is further admitted case that the special pay was reduced from Rs. 400/- per month to Rs. 100/- per month.
This is clear from Annexure P-8.
9.
The question that arises here is whether special pay if given to an employee can be added for calculating his retiral benefits.
As no opportunity was given to the petitioner to explain his position, the order dated 1721995, Annexure P-8 is bad in law.
10.
The counsel for the petitioner has drawn my attention to a judgment of this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 2239 of 1991, Radhe Krishan Sharma v. State of Haryana and others, 1993(1) SCT 58 (P&H) : 1993(1) Recent Services Judgments 540.
In this authority, this Court has held that special pay forms part of pay and that the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of special pay towards computation of pension.
In this authority, the employee was working on deputation and the Court has held that the special pay has to be added in the last pay drawn towards calculation of his pensionary benefits.
The decision in Radhe Krishna Sharma's case (supra) squarely covers the case in hand.
11.
In the light of above discussion, this writ petition is allowed.
Order dated 1721995, Annexure P-8, is quashed and the amount of Rs. 8,51615 recovered from the petitioner be paid to him within a period of one month from the passing of this order.
The petitioner shall also be granted interest @ 12% per annum from the date when order, Annexure P-8, was passed till the decision of this writ petition.
It is further directed that the pensionary benefits of the Petitioner be revised by adding special pay of Rs. 400/- per month to his last drawn pay.
Petition allowed.
