In case of an employee retiring after having rendered service, it is expected that all the payment of the retiral benefits should be paid on the date of retirement or soon thereafter if for some unforeseen circumstances the payments could not be made on the date of retirement.
4.
In this case, there is absolutely no reason or justification for not making the payments for months together.
We, therefore, direct the respondent to pay to the appellant within 12 weeks from today simple interest at the rate of 18 per cent with effect from the date of her retirement i.e. 31st August, 1997 till the date of payment.
5.
The appeal is allowed to the above extent.
Appeal allowed.
