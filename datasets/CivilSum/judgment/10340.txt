RL Anand, J - Learned counsel for the parties have been heard and with their assistance have gone through the impugned order.
2.
I do not find any merit in the contention raised by the learned counsel for the respondent that the State has allowed the respondent to continue up to the age of 60 years.
I also further do not subscribe to the view of Mr. Sharam that since the plaintiff-respondent has worked up to 3181996 and, therefore, he is entitled to the amount.
In this case the stand of the State is that the plaintiff was to retire at the age of 58 years as on 3181994.
The plaintiff earlier got a stay order which was vacated by the Court of Additional District Judge on 811996.
Meaning thereby that beyond 3181994 the plaintiff had no vested right to work at the post.
It has already been stated by the learned counsel for the respondent that against the order dated 811996 the respondent-plaintiff has filed the revision and it is still pending but the operation of the order dated 811996 has not been stayed or suspended.
In these circumstances, if the plaintiff has worked beyond 3181994 and has received the salary, he is to account for.
Very strange order has been passed by the Court of Additional Civil Judge (Sr. Division), Malerkotla.
3.
The counsel for the respondent has placed reliance upon 1999(2) Service Cases Today 430 and 1993(1) Recent Services Judgments 365 and submitted that if the plaintiff has been allowed to work under the stay order granted by the Court up to the age of 60 yeras, he is entitled to the wages irrespective of the fact whether he is entitled to work up to the age of 60 years or not.
This point has to be adjudicated by the trial Court after framing the issues and not to be decided on miscellaneous application filed by the plaintiff- respondent.
4.
In this view of the matter, I allow this revision, set aside the impugned order dated 1422000 passed by Additional Civil Judge (Sr. Division), Malerkotla, who is directed to dispose of the suit according to law after framing the proper issues in this case.
5.
Nothing stated above shall amount an expression of my opinion on the merits of the suit.
6.
The trial Court is directed to dispose of the suit within three months.
The parties through their counsel are directed to appear before the trial Court on 492000.
Revision allowed.
