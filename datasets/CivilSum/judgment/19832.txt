RS Mongia, J - Petitioner Mangal Singh retired from Punjab Govt. service on 31.12.1986 as Block Development and Panchayat Officer (for short BDPO).
The pay scale of the post of BDPO was Rs. 1200-1700 and the petitioner was drawing Rs. 1700/- per month as his basic pay at the time of his retirement.
A certificate issued by the BDPO dated 22187 has been attached to the above mentioned effect.
2.
Petitioner applied to the District Manager (Telephones), Amritsar on 451987 for a telephone connection under Non-OYT-SS category for retired Central/State Govt. officers.
3.
The Department of Telecommunication, Sanchar Bhawan, New Delhi issued instructions through a circular dated 26111987 (Annexure P-2 to the writ petition) in which it has been mentioned that certain cases of State Govt. Officers had come to the notice of the office where the concerned State Governments had not yet revised the pay scales of their officers in accordance with the recommendations of the Fourth Pay Commission and those officers continued to draw their pay in the old scales.
Since on 1071987 minimum pay requirements were prescribed for being entitled to be considered under the Non-OYT-SS Category for the retired Central/State Government Officers, it has been decided that wherever a State Government had not revised the pay scale of its officers on the lines of the recommendations of the Fourth Pay Commission, the minimum pay required for the State Government Officers for being eligible for Non-OYT-SS Category would continue to be Rs. 1600/- per month (basic pay and special pay) as prescribed under department letter dated 19381 till the pay scales were revised by the concerned State Governments.
4.
The petitioner's case is that in spite of the fact that matter had been clarified by the instructions of the department dated 26111987, the petitioner was declined a telephone connection under the Non-OYT-SS category for retired Central/State Government Officers on the ground that his pay was not Rs. 3700/- or above.
According to the petitioner when he had applied for the telephone connection the pay scales had not been revised and consequently in accordance with the instructions of the department dated 26111987, the petitioner was entitled to a telephone connection as his basic pay at the time of his retirement was more than Rs. 1600/- and he clearly fell within the ambit of the instructions dated 26111987.
5.
No return has been filed on behalf of the respondents.
It may be highlighted that on February 28, 1989, Mrs. Mohinder Gupta, learned standing counsel for Union of India had accepted notice on behalf of the respondents and time was given to file the written statement by March 30, 1989.
On March 30, 1989 again a request was made for granting time to file the written statement.
Two weeks time was given to file the written statement.
On May 16, 1989, since no reply was filed, another opportunity was granted to file the reply on payment of Rs. 100/- as costs.
Though the costs were paid on August 8, 1989, yet further time was sought to file the return.
The case was adjourned to 6.9.1989 subject to payment of Rs. 150/- as costs.
On September 6, 1989, when the case was again taken up for in motion hearing written statement was filed and written statement has been filed till date.
6.
In view of the fact that no return had been filed, the averments made in this writ petition have to be taken as correct and accepted by the respondents.
After going through the writ petition I find that the petitioner had applied for telephone connection on 451987.
By 30111989 when order Annexure P4 was issued by the Commercial Officer of the Telephone Department at Amritsar that the petitioner was not covered under the Non-OYT-SS category, instructions of the Central Government dated 26111987 had already come and it is apparent that the petitioner's cases was covered by those instructions as petitioner's basic pay at the time of retirement was more than Rs. 1600/- per month.
The rejection of the application of the petitioner was, therefore, wrong and without any reference to the instructions of the department itself.
7.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed, the order rejecting the application for grant of a telephone connection under Non-OYT-SS category is set aside and it is directed that the respondent-department will give telephone connection to the petitioner (if it is still required by him) under the Non-OYT-SS category on the payment of charge as were required at the time when his turn would have come for the telephone connection, but for the rejection of this application.
The telephone connection will be given forthwith subject to the payment of charges by the petitioner.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
