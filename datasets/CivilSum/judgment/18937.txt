DV Sehgal, President - Karam Singh petitioner was a guard-man in the army, which post is equivalent to that of a sepoy.
He suffered bullet injury in the right eye during 1965 operation.
He was discharged from service on 10.6.1983.
The extent of injury was assessed by the Medical Board at 40%.
However, since the date of his discharge from the army, he has not been allowed the disability pension.
No doubt, he is getting the service pension.
It has been very fairly conceded before us today by Sh. CM Sharma, Advocate on instructions from the Ministry of Defence that the petitioner is entitled to disability pension to the extent of 40% from the date of his discharge.
2.
We accordingly direct that the petitioner shall be paid the arrears of disability pension of 40% with effect from 11th June, 1983 along with interest @ 12% PA within a period of six months from today.
In future, the petitioner shall be regularly paid the disability pension as mentioned above along with his service pension every month.
3.
The above directions shall be meticulously complied with and shall constitute a decree under Section 21 of the Legal Services Authority Act, 1987.
4.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
A copy of this order be given to both the counsel within seven days free of cost.
Petition allowed.
