RP Sethi and SS Sudhalkar, JJ.
- Heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the record.
It is conceded that the facts and the points of law involved in this case are similar as were adjudicated by a Division Bench of this Court in C.W.P.
No. 2143 of 1994 decided on 2651994.
Keeping in view the facts of the case and earlier judgment of a Division Bench, the petition is disposed of with a direction to the respondents to grant to the petitioner(s) selection grade on the basis of the total strength, including permanent and temporary posts, with all consequential benefits, to the employees in the Education Department, including the petitioners, who are eligible to the grant of selection grade, subject to the condition that all such employees who are held entitled to the grant of such relief, shall, however, not be entitled to the arrears for the period exceeding three years and two months preceding the filing of the petitions/representations or date of orders.
In the cases of all such employees, including those who have filed the representations or those who are entitled to the grant of selection grade but have not filed the petition, the respondents shall determine their rights in the light of the judgments already delivered by this Court from time to time as expeditiously as possible, but not later than six months under any circumstances.
2.
We have the parties to bear their own costs but direct that the claim of the petitioner(s) shall positively be settled and payments made within a period of two months from today.
