Syed Bashir-ud-Din, J. - Parties are not appearing to pursue and prosecute the case after Feb 1992.
It seems that they have lost interest in the case.
2.
Examination of the petition shows that the petitioner, a Junior Agriculture Officer of the State department, has sought admission in one of the disciplines for M.Sc.
Agriculture degree offered vide advertisement notice dated 5391, Annexure P2.
He applied for the course in the category of Socially and educationally backward classes as a beneficiary of SRO 272 of 1981.
Apart from the grounds taken in the petition, obviously about eight years have gone by.
Relief sought in the petition for selection to the said course at this stage has become wholly in-appropriate due to the change in the circumstances brought about by the passage of time.
3.
The petitioner who is seeking admission for Session 1991 cannot be given admission for the session 1999 or a session thereafter when he has not even applied for any subsequent session(s) including the current session, so as to deprive other like circumstanced and equally situated candidates of their consideration for one of the vacancies offered for selection in terms of the rules governing such selection as to present.
This court in Mohd.
Yunis Shah v. State of J&K and others, OWP No. 370 of 1990, decided on 3071998 observed as under :-  "......
It is neither reasonable nor logical to accommodate the unsuccessful candidate who appeared in 1990-91 Session examination to be admitted and adjusted against a seat for which he had not even applied from available seat quota for 1998-99.
The Court cannot deprive others at least equally circumstanced and situated as the petitioner from consideration to the course/discipline of Post Graduate in Medicine or other discipline in Medicine.
(See AIR 1997 SC 788)."
In result, the Writ petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
