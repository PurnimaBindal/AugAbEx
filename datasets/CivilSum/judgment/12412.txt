Sat Pal, J - This writ petition has been filed on behalf of the petitioners under Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India and in this writ petition petitioners have prayed for grant of higher scales of pay on account of improvement of their qualifications.
The particulars with regard to each of the petitioners have not, however, been furnished.
2.
In view of the instructions issued by the Government of Haryana vide letter dated 2351957 a teacher is entitled to higher scales of pay in accordance with improvement of his qualifications.
Similar claims of other teachers have been sustained by this Court in the judgment dated 1181995 passed by Division Bench of this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 6949 of 1995, Kailash Chand and others v. State of Haryana and others.
The decision of the Division Bench in the case of Kailash Chand (supra) is based on an earlier decision of this Court in State of Punjab v. Kirpal Singh Bhatia, 1975(2) SLR 621.
Similar matter has already been considered and decided by this Court in Rattan Singh v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) RSJ 533 : 1995(1) S.C.T.
711.
3.
Accordingly, the writ petition is disposed of with a direction to the respondents to consider the claim of the petitioners for the grant of higher scale of pay in accordance with law.
If the petitioner/petitioners is/are found entitled to grant of any higher scale of pay their pay shall be re-fixed in the scale to which they are found entitled.
However, the payment of arrears of pay shall be confined to 38 months preceding the date of presentation of the petition to this court.
The parties are left to bear their own costs.
4.
It is further directed that the respondents shall decide the case of the petitioners within six months from the date of receipt of a copy of this order.
With this order, the writ petition stands disposed of.
