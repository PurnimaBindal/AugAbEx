The second grievance from the writ petition of the petitioners appears to be that they are claiming equation of their pay scale with that of their counter-parts serving under the Central Government on the basis of circular Annexure P-1 dated 2631987 and are seeking the pay scale of Rs. 1,640-2,900.
4.
The Respondents have filed the written statement contesting the claim of the petitioners inter alia on the grounds that none of the legal or constitutional rights of the petitioners has been infringed and that the nature of duties and responsibilities of instructors and that of the Language Teachers are different and they have also taken the plea that the petitioners cannot be equated in the matter of pay scale with their counter-parts serving in the Education Department where nature of job of the post and qualifications are not on the post and they have pleaded that w.e.f.
141986 both the Instructors and Language Teachers have been placed in the same pay scale of Rs1,400-2,600.
However, the Respondents have admitted that the pay scale of the Language Teachers was removed w.e.f.
141986 and that the representation of the petitioner No.2 and others was forwarded to the Government which was not accepted as per decision of the Govt. contained in the letter dated 2381990 (Annexure R-2).
5.
I have perused the pleadings and considered the arguments advanced at the Bar.
So far as the claim of the petitioners in the body of the writ petition for scale of Rs.1,640-2,900 is concerned, it has not been prayed for in the relief clause and the only substantive relief prayed for is claim for equation of their pay scales with the Instructors serving in the Respondent Department.
Secondly the petitioner No.2 made a representation claiming the scale of Rs. 1,640-2,900 (Annexure P-2) and also prayed for change of designation which is stated to have been considered by the Government and not accepted, as is sought to be contended on the strength of order dated 2381990 (Annexure R- 2).
Admittedly, this order Annexure R-2 was passed in the year 1990 and produced along with the written statement clearly stating that vide this order the claim of the petitioner was rejected but the petitioners neither filed any replication nor challenged the order adversely affecting them by amending the Civil Writ Petition, Since there is no specific prayer in the writ petition claiming the scale of Rs. 1,640-2,900 nor the order adversely affecting them has been challenged, I am not inclined to grant the said relief to the petitioners.
Now coming to the relief of equation of pay scale of petitioners with that of Instructors, the perusal of heading and prayer clause shows that the petitioners have not specified any date from which they are claiming the equation.
The last revision of the pay scale having been allowed w.e.f.
141986 and the cadre of the petitioners having been equated with that of Instructors and having been granted the same pay scale of Rs1,400-2,600, there is no further scope of adjudication on this issue, However.
during the course of arguments, the learned counsel for the petitioners contended that there was discrimination in the matter of pay scale of the petitioners viz- a- viz Instructors for the period as culled out from para 12 of the Civil Writ Petition.
6.
Needless to say that this claim of the petitioners is hopelessly delayed and is hit by doctrine of delay and laches.
The Court comes to the rescue of those who are vigilant and pursue their remedies with due diligence and not of those who sleep over the matter and approach the Court after undue and unexplained long delay.
Admittedly, neither there is any averments in the writ petition nor any representation is placed in the writ claiming the equation of pay scale with Instructors for the period prior to 141986 and the representation Annexure P-2 which is relied upon by the petitioners does not relate to the said relief as it is something different than the relief of equation of pay scale with Instructors.
In this view of the matter, I do not find any force in this claim also at such a belated stage.
For the reasons aforesaid, I do not find any merit in the writ petition and the same is dismissed.
However, the parties are left to bear their own costs.
