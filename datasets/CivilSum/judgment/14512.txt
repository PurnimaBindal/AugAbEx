MS Liberhan and ML Koul, JJ.
- Undisputedly the facts and circumstances of this case are squarely covered by the judgment of Hon'ble Supreme Court in Civil Appeal Nos. 1088 and 1089 of 1995 decided on 1311995 wherein similar order viz. wherein a police officer was discharged on the ground that he was found unlikely to prove an efficient police officer within his probation period was held to be a stigmatic order.
Hon'ble the Supreme Court directed that appropriate course for the State would be to direct an enquiry if they intend to hold and to give an opportunity of hearing and to defend himself i.e. the petitioner and then pass an appropriate order on the basis of the result of the enquiry.
2.
In view of the observations made by Hon'ble the Supreme Court, we are of the considered view that the impugned order Annexure 12 is stigmatic order and the same is therefore set aside.
However, the State may proceed to hold an enquiry in accordance with law and after granting an opportunity of hearing, pass an appropriate order in accordance with law.
The writ petition standS disposed of accordingly.
Ordered accordingly.
