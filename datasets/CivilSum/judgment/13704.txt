PN Nag, J. - Mr. Varma, learned counsel for the Municipal Corporation of Delhi, states that he does not wish to file reply to show cause notice.
2.
No reply has been filed by the other respondents in spite of various opportunities having granted to them for the purpose.
3.
This revision petition is directed against the order dated 3rd June, 1989 passed by Shri VS Aggarwal, Additional District Judge whereby the application of the petitioner seeking restraint order against the respondents from taking possession of the property in question has been dismissed.
That application was filed by the petitioner-plaintiff along with the suit for permanent injunction against he respondents-defendants restraining them from taking possession of the property in question.
4.
The main contention of the petitioner is that he has become the owner by virtue of adverse possession since he has been in possession since 1954.
This matter has yet to be decided by the trial Court.
However, there is no dispute that the petitioner-plaintiff is in possession of the premises in dispute.
In these circumstances, the trial Court should have exercised jurisdiction by having restrained the respondents from interfering with the possession of the petitioner-plaintiff by way of interim order.
At least the petitioner-plaintiff was entitled to remain in possession except dispossessed by due process of law.
5.
In these circumstances, the impugned judgment is set aside and the respondents-defendants are restrained from dispossessing the petitioner-plaintiff from the property in dispute except by due process of law during the pendency of the suit.
6.
Civil revision stands disposed of.
Parties shall appeal before the trial Court on 4th April, 1991.
Revision allowed.
