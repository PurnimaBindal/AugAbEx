SS Sodhi, J - The controversy in appeal here is with regard to the payment interest on arrears of salary.
Pension and gratuity payable to the appellant-Amar Singh.
2.
The appellant served in the Army from July 14, 1942, till his discharge on May, 5, 1947.
He, therefore, joined service as a Clerk under the Punjab Government on June 26, 1947.
While in service, he filed a writ petition in this Court (CWP 162 of 1967) seeking the benefit of military service, which was eventually granted to him when his writ petition was allowed on October 20, 1978.
The Letters Patent Appeal filed against if was dismissed on January 23, 1979.
The petitioner thereafter retired from service on August 31, 1979 on attaining the age of super-annuation.
3.
The effect of the benefit of military service being granted to the appellants was that he thereby become entitled to an additional amount as salary for every month of his service.
All these amounts were calculated and added up and then paid to the appellant as arrears of salary in two lump sums.
He was paid Rs. 5,920/- on February 3, 1984 and Rs. 49,428/- on March 28, 1985.
In other words, the salary that the appellant was entitled to by virtue of his military service and which should have been paid to him every month since his appointment as Clerk in June 1947 till the date of his retirement, was not paid till February and March, 1985.
A substantial loss of interest on the amount due is thus writ large and this is what he now claims in appeal relying upon the precedent provided by the judgment of this Court in VP Gautam v. Union of India, 1983 S.L.R.
346, where compound interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum was held to be payable on the arrears of pension that the petitioner was entitled to till the date of the actual payment of the amount due.
4.
VP Gautam's case (supra) is clearly applicable in the case of the appellant and consequently the appellant is hereby held entitled and is also granted a decree awarding him compound interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum on the arrears of salary with effect from the dates on which the amounts become due till the date of their actual payment.
Similarly, the appellant is also hereby awarded compound interest at the same rate, that is, 12 percent per annum for the period during which the payment of gratuity and pension was delayed.
5.
This appeal is consequently hereby accepted to this extent.
The judgment and decree of the lower appellate court thus stands modified accordingly.
The appellant shall be entitled to his costs in this appeal.
Appeal allowed.
