SS Sodhi and Ashok Bha, JJ.
- What was sought to be challenged in this writ petition is the selection and appointment of 93 peons by the Life Insurance Corporation of India, on the ground that out of these 93 appointments, only 24 had been made from amongst those who had passed their matriculation or 10+2 examination.
The argument being that this number had been fixed without any basis.
This is, indeed, an untenable contention as according to paragraph 9 of the circular of April 11, 1980 containing recruitment instructions, it has been clearly provided that recruitment of candidates who have passed the matriculation examination shall not exceed 25% of the posts to be filled.
This being so, the respondent could not have recommended more than 24 out of 93 from amongst those who had passed matriculation examination.
An objection was next sought to be raised on the ground that the marks prescribed for the interview were excessive.
This is again a contention that cannot be sustained, as by the very nature of the appointment to be made, interview was clearly the appropriate test.
No occasion thus, arises for granting to the petitioner, the relief claimed.
Dismissed.
