Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - The petitioner is seeking direction to the respondents under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, to pay him the arrears of salary for the period of suspension.
2.
In brief, the case of the petitioner is that while posted as Executive Officer, Municipal Committee, Dina Nagar, a criminal case under the Prevention of Corruption Act was registered against him on 13.2.1986.
Consequently, the Director, Local Government, Punjab, placed the petitioner under suspension vide order dated 441986.
In pursuance of the orders of this Court, suspending the order of suspension during the pendency of the writ petition, the Director, Local Government, Punjab, reinstated the petitioner on 1311988 and the petitioner joined duty.
The criminal case against the petitioner also failed and he was acquitted by Special Judge, Gurdaspur, vide his judgment dated 1361988.
Though the petitioner has since been reinstated and also acquitted in the corruption case, he is not being paid the arrears of salary.
Hence this petition.
3.
The case was admitted by the Motion Bench on 611988.
The respondents did not file any written statement despite the fact that a period of more than two years has elapsed after the admission of the writ petition.
4.
Since no written statement has been filed it appears that the respondents have nothing to say in the matter and accept the correctness of the allegations contained in the writ petition.
Consequently, the writ petition is allowed and the respondents arc directed to release the arrears of salary to the petitioner with 10% interest within a period of two months.
There will be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
