RL Anand, J - The petitioner is challenging his reversion from the post of Assistant Sub Inspector to the post of Head Constable.
2.
Stand of the respondents is that the petitioner was promoted as officiating Assistant Sub Inspector on adhoc basis and his promotion was purely fortuitous with a rider that he would not claim seniority over others and could be reverted any time without any show cause notice.
Further it is the stand of the respondents that the petitioner was neither brought in the class of list `D' nor he passed the Intermediate School Course and thus he had no right of promotion and has been rightly reverted to his substantive rank of Head Constable.
In such a situation, principles of natural justice are not required to be complied with by the respondents.
Employees are always reverted to their substantive ranks when appointed earlier on ad hoc basis.
No right to the post is created when promotion is made purely on officiating basis or gratuitous in nature.
We find no merit in this writ petition which is hereby dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
