Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - Written statement has been filed.
2.
The instructions contained in Annexure R2, provide that an employee should have obtained 50 per cent good reports in preceding 10 years to become eligible for promotion or to get selection grade, are arbitrary and are quashed for the reason recorded in the judgment of this Court in KK Vaid v. State of Haryana, 1990(1) SLR 1.
3.
The Government has not been conveying the average reports to its employees and only adverse reports were being conveyed.
Therefore, even average reports were not being considered 'adverse'.
Now the Government has started the practice of considering average reports as 'adverse' to the employees since 1982 and such reports are being conveyed.
4.
Adverting to the facts of the case, herein the petitioner has been getting average reports from 1971 till 1976-77.
From 1977-78 onwards till today all his reports are good.
His junior has been granted selection grade in the year 1985 w.e.f.
1979.
By the year 1985 the petitioner had more than eight good reports and still has been ignored.
5.
The argument of the counsel for the State is that the Selection grade is being granted, probably with effect from 1979 (about the period he is not sure), and the petitioner has to be considered as on 1979.
The argument is fair but in the year 1978-79 the petitioner had good report as also in 1979-80.
Therefore, once an employee gets good report, he becomes eligible for promotion as well as for the grant of selection grade.
Hence, there has been no impediment in the way of the petitioner in getting selection grade w.e.f.
1979.
6.
For the reasons recorded above, the petition is allowed and a direction is issued to the respondents to grant selection grade w.e.f.
the date his junior has been granted.
The arrears of the selection grade be calculated within 5 months from today and payment be made one month thereof.
The petitioner would also be entitled to the interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum on the amount due.
Petition allowed.
