Sat Pal, J. - By this judgment, we are disposing of 41 writ petitions, bearing CWP Nos. 18402 and 18788 of 1996, 890, 1293, 1553, 1710, 2081, 2170, 2393, 2484, 2789, 2840, 3393, 3394, 3404, 3548, 3779, 3977, 4904, 4986, 5487, 5491, 5555, 6690, 6864, 7255, 7727, 7962, 8937, 10246, 10361, 10445, 10586, 10822, 10933, 11066, 11070, 11866, 12127, 12410 and 12419 of 1997 as the facts and the point of law involved in all these cases are similar.
For the purpose of this judgment, the facts of CWP No. 7255 of 1997 have been taken.
2.
The Government of Haryana vide circular dated 1451991 (copy annexure P1) decided to grant one additional increment at 10th and another at 20th year in the time scale as applicable from 1st January, 1986 to all Group `C' and `D' employees in addition to regular increment.
The aforesaid scheme was modified vide circular dated 7th August, 1992 (copy annexure P-2).
By this circular, the Government of Haryana decided that the benefit of additional increment would be available to Group `C' and `D' employees on completion of 8 and 18 years of `regular satisfactory service' in a particular group and the first additional increment was to be granted after 8 years and the second after 18 years of service.
3.
The scheme with regard to grant of additional increment after 8 and 18 years of service contained in the circular, dated 7th August, 1992 was, however, replaced by the Government of Haryana vide Circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994 (Annexure P.3).
In terms of the said instructions, the State Government decided to grant time bound higher standard pay scale to the Haryana Government employees of Group `C' and `D' category in replacement of the earlier scheme of additional increment of 8 and 18 years.
The instructions contained in the circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994 reads as under :  "(i) Every employee of group `C' or `D' category who has not got any promotion or promotional scale/higher time scale/selection grade in his service career and has completed twenty years or more of regular satisfactory service before 111994 will be allowed with effect from 111994 in place of his present pay scale the second higher standard pay scale specified in column 4 of the enclosed Annexure with respect to the pay scale of the post applicable from 111986.
An employee who completes such regular satisfactory service of twenty years after 111994 and has not got any promotion or promotional scale/higher time scale/selection grade in his service career will be allowed the aforesaid second higher standard scale from first day of the month following the month in which he completes such service.
(ii) Every employee of group `C' or `D' category who has not got any promotion or promotional scale/higher time scale/selection grade in his service career and has completed ten years or more but less than twenty years of regular satisfactory service before 111994 will be allowed with effect from 111994 in place of his present pay scale the first higher standard scale specified in column 3 of the enclosed Annexure with respect to the pay scale of the post applicable from 111986.
An employee who completes such regular satisfactory service of ten years after 111994 and has not got any promotion or promotional scale/higher time scale/selection grade in his service career will be allowed the aforesaid first higher standard scale from first day of the month following the month in which he completes such service.
(iii) Every employee of group `C' or `D' category who has completed twenty years or more of regular satisfactory service before 111994 but has got only one promotion or promotional scale/higher time scale/selection grade in his service career will be allowed with effect from 111994 in place of his present pay scale the first higher standard scale specified in column 3 of the enclosed Annexure with respect to the pay scale of the post applicable from first January, 1986.
An employee who completes such regular satisfactory service of twenty years after 111994 but has got only one promotion or promotional scale/higher time scale/selection grade/higher standard scale in his service career will be allowed the aforesaid first higher standard scale with effect from first day of the month following the month in which he completes such service."
3.
The highest standard pay scale to be granted under these instructions will be Rs. 2000-60-2300-75-2900-EB-100-3500.
4.
An employee who foregoes promotion or seeks reversion to a lower post at his own request will render himself ineligible for the benefit of higher standard scale under these instructions.
5.
In case an employee who has got promotion already but the pay scale of the promotion post is either equal or lower than the pay scale of the feeder post, the benefit of the higher standard scale will be granted in such cases.
6.
`Regular Satisfactory Service' means actual continuous service under Haryana Government or erstwhile Punjab Government before re-organisation on regular basis in the present cadre to be reckoned within the same Group and assessed fit for promotion as per procedure prescribed for promotion cases.
Inter-district transfers on a corresponding cadre within the same department, and the service of an employee on different posts in the same cadre carrying the same pay scale will be treated as continuous service in relation to these instructions.
Non-grant of higher standard pay scale on account of unsatisfactory service record will not be considered as a punishment under the Punishment & Appeal Rules.
7.
These instructions do not cover the employees governed by the UGC pay scales.
8.
If an employee finds the existing pay scale beneficial to him, he can retain that pay scale provided he gives an option in writing to do so within a period of two months to his appointing authority through proper channel which will be placed in the service book of the employee after counter-signatures of the appointing authority.
The option once exercised will be final.
9.
In case the time scale and the selection grade of a post have been clubbed together and replaced by one single revised pay scale effective from 111986, the restriction of selection grade imposed in the foregoing paras for grant of higher standard scale will not apply.
10.
Pay Fixation : On grant of higher standard scale under these instructions, the pay of the employee in the higher standard pay scale will be fixed at the stage next above his pay drawn by him in his present pay scale and the date of next increment would be on completion of the normal period.
However, if pay is once fixed in the pay scale of Rs. 2000-3500 it will not be fixed again in the same pay scale.
"PAY" means the pay as defined in Rule 2.44(a)(i) of the Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume I Part I (as applicable to Haryana State).
11.
Special pay admissible, if any, being drawn by an employee on the present post in the present scale will continue to be drawn as a separate element so long as he holds the post carrying special pay.
12.
Since the grant of higher standard pay scale under these instructions is compensation for stagnation and is therefore based on length of service without involving higher responsibilities, this will be treated as a fortuitous circumstance and no benefit of step-up of pay to a senior just on the basis of seniority in the hierarchy will be admissible.
13.
The existing provisions relating to open-ended pay scales for group `C' and `D' categories of employee shall continue to be in force.
14.
The benefit of additional increments already granted to an employee prior to 111994 on completion of 8 and 18 years of service or on 10th and 20th year point of pay scale in terms of earlier instructions will not be withdrawn.
15.
These instructions take effect from 111994.
16.
The contents of this letter may be brought to the notice of all under your control.
17.
The receipt of this letter may please be acknowledged."
(emphasis supplied)  4.
Vide circular, dated 29th December, 1995 the Government of Haryana, Finance Department, issued clarification, inter alia, on the point as to whether the period of ad hoc service is to be counted for the grant of higher standard pay scales.
The said clarification reads as under :  "Point raised Answer Whether or not the period of ad hoc service is to be counted for grant of higher standard pay scale ?
No, no such benefit is admissible as clearly mentioned in paras 2 and 6 of the scheme dated 8th Feburary Keeping in view the clarification contained in the order, dated 29th December, 1995, issued by the Finance Department, the petitioners have been denied the benefit of ad hoc service for grant of higher standard pay scales.
The present writ petitions have been filed by the writ petitioners against the decision of the State Government denying the benefit of ad hoc service to the petitioners for grant of higher standard pay scales.
5.
Notice of motion in all these writ petitions was issued to the respondents.
Written statement has been filed in CWP No. 7255 of 1997.
The learned Assistant Advocate General submitted that he would rely on the said written statement with regard to other connected cases also.
6.
Mr. Ahuja, learned counsel appearing on behalf of petitioners in CWP No. 7255 of 1997 submitted that in terms of instructions contained in circular dated 7th August, 1992 (copy Annexure P-2) the benefit of additional increment was made available to Group `C' and `D' employees on completion of 8 and 18 years of `regular satisfactory service' in a particular group.
He submitted that the question as to whether `regular satisfactory service' shall include the ad hoc service rendered by the employee came up for consideration before a Division Bench of this Court in the case of Ajit Kumar Jain v. State of Punjab and another, 1992(1) SLR 255 : 1992(1) SCT 365 and in that case it was held that the word "regular" meant consistent course of conduct without any breach or break.
It was further held that since the benefit of principle of `Equal pay for equal work' has already been extended even to ad hoc employees, casual labour, temporary employees, there is no scope for holding that a person who had worked on a post for a period of 8 years without any break should be denied the benefit of this principle.
He further submitted that even as per the circular dated 20th August, 1996 (Annexure P-6) issued by the Finance Department of Haryana Government, the period of ad hoc service which was followed by regular appointment under the State Government, shall be taken into consideration while calculating the period of 8/18 years of `regular satisfactory service' in the same group for the purpose of the scheme issued on 781992 (Annexure P-2).
7.
The learned counsel further submitted that the words "regular satisfactory service" as used in the circular dated 7th August, 1992 (copy annexure P-2) have been repeated verbatim in the circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994 (copy annexure P-3) and since "regular satisfactory service" in the circular dated 7th August, 1992 has been held to include ad hoc service rendered by the employee, the State Government cannot be permitted now to interpret differently the words "regular satisfactory service" mentioned in the circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994 (copy annexure P-3) to mean that the ad hoc service cannot be counted while calculating "regular satisfactory service".
He further submitted that even as per the law laid down by a Full Bench of this court in the case of Rakesh Kumar Singla v. The State of Haryana, 1995(3) PLR 411 : 1995(4) SCT 285 the ad hoc service which had been counted for the purpose of seniority, would be counted for the purpose of 12 years regular service for grant of selection grade in accordance with the policy decision notified by the Government of Haryana vide letter dated 2nd June, 1989.
8.
Mr. Manohar Lal, learned counsel appearing in CWP No. 10586 of 1997 and Mr. Laler, learned counsel appearing in CWP No. 7962 of 1997, reiterated the submissions made by Mr. Ahuja.
9.
Mr Ashish Aggarwal learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners in CWP No. 3548 of 1997 besides reiterating the submissions made by Mr. Ahuja, also drew our attention to the order dated 6th Feburary, 1997 (copy Annexure P-7 in CWP No. 3548 of 1997) passed in Banta Ram v. State of Haryana, CWP No. 1842 of 1996 and submitted that in that case, the State Government was directed to count the petitioner's service on work-charged basis towards computing their total service of 8/18 years or 10/20 years for the purpose of giving them benefit of additional increments and higher pay scale.
Mr. GP Singh, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners in CWP No. 3404 of 1997 besides reiterating the submissions made by Mr. Ahuja, submitted that the petitioners in this case were work-charged employees and in view of the law laid down by a Division Bench of this Court in the case of Banta Ram (supra) the entire work charged period of the petitioners should be counted while calculating 10/20 years of "regular satisfactory service" for the purpose of grant of higher standard pay scales.
10.
The learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners in the other writ petitions also reiterated the submissions made by Mr. Ahuja.
11.
Mr. Amol Rattan, learned AAG appearing on behalf of the respondents, submitted that the decision of the State Government contained in the circular dated 7th August, 1992 and also contained in the Circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994 was based on a concession.
He submitted that it was not based on any statutory rules.
He submitted that even if the Government had agreed to count the ad hoc service for the purpose of grant of additional increments on completion of 8/18 years in terms of the circular dated 7th August, 1992, read with the clarification contained in the circular dated 2081996, the Government was not bound to count the period of ad hoc service for the purpose of grant of higher standard pay scales, particularly in view of the clarification contained in the order dated 29121995, issued by the Finance Department of the Haryana Government.
He submitted that a concession could not be claimed as a matter of right.
In support of his submissions, he placed reliance on a judgment of the Supreme Court in KV Rajalakshmiah Shetty and another v. State of Mysore, AIR 1967 SC 993.
The learned counsel further submitted that in the clarification contained in the orders dated 20th August, 1996, it has clearly been stated that this clarification was applicable to the scheme of additional increments to the employees of Group `C' and `D' categories on their completion of 8/18 years of `regular satisfactory service', in the same group, which scheme was issued on 781992 and stood abolished w.e.f.
111994 by introduction of the scheme of higher standard pay scales on 821994 (Annexure P-3).
He contended that this clarification cannot be applied to the scheme of higher standard pay scales granted on completion of 10/20 years `regular satisfactory service'.
12.
We have given our thoughtful consideration to the submissions made by the learned counsel for the parties and have carefully perused the various circulars issued by the Government of Haryana on the subject matter.
The point raised in these writ petitions as to whether the service rendered by the employees on ad hoc basis can be taken into consideration for calculation of the period of 10/20 years for the purpose of grant of higher standard pay scales, is no longer res integra in view of the law laid down by a Full Bench of this Court in the case of Rakesh Kumar Singla (supra).
In this case, the Government of Haryana vide circular dated 261989 had sanctioned higher pay scale to certain employees on completion of five years of regular service.
The question which came up for consideration in that case was that "Does `regular Service' mean continuous service and include even ad hoc service ?"
This question was answered as under :  "Such period of ad hoc service which is countable for the purpose of seniority and other service benefits in the light of the judgments of the Supreme Court in Direct Recruit Class II Engineering Officers Association v. State of Maharashtra, AIR 1990 SC 1607 and State of West Bengal v. Aghore Nath Dey and another, JT 1993(2) SC 598 : 1993(2) SCT 734 and of the Full Bench of this Court in Chambel Singh v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) PLR 152 : 1994(4) SCT 800 shall be counted for the purpose of regular service in the context of the circular dated 261989."
In view of the law laid down by the Full Bench in the case of Rakesh Kumar Singla (supra), we do not find any merit in the contention raised by the learned counsel for the petitioners that `regular satisfactory service' mentioned in para 2 of the circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994, shall include the ad hoc service rendered by the petitioners for the purpose of grant of higher standard pay scales.
As held in the case of Rakesh Kumar Singla (supra) only such period of ad hoc service which is countable for the purpose of seniority and other service benefits in the light of Chambel Singh's case (supra) can be counted for the purpose of `regular satisfactory service' as stated in the circular dated 821994.
13.
It is true that in the circular dated 7th August, 1992 which pertains to grant of additional increments to Group `C' and `D' employees on completion of 8/18 years service, it has been stated that this benefit would be available to those employees on completion of 8/18 years `regular satisfactory service' and vide circular dated 2081996, the State Government clarified that the period of ad hoc service which was followed by a regular appointment shall be taken into consideration while calculating the prescribed length of service under that scheme but this clarification ipso facto cannot be made applicable to the scheme of grant of higher standard pay scales contained in the circular dated 821994.
It may be noted here that the circular dated 2081996 was issued after the issuance of earlier circular dated 581996.
The words "regular satisfactory service" appearing in the circular dated 781992 were substituted by the word "satisfactory service" vide circular dt. 581996, it was after the deletion of the word "regular" that subsequent circular dated 2081996 was issued.
For ready reference, the decision contained in Circular dated 581996 is reproduced herein below :  "FINANCE DEPARTMENT  The matter has been considered in the FD and decided that the benefit of additional increment(s) shall be admissible to all employees of Group C & D on completion of 8 and 18 years of `satisfactory service' in a particular group as against the condition of regular satisfactory service appearing in FD's instructions No. 1/138/92-1PP(FD) dated 7th of August 1992 and also in para 3 of the clarification issued vide FD letter No. 1/91/92-4PR (FD) dated 14th June, 1994.
Sd/- Superintendent Pay Revision for  Financial Commissioner & Secy.
to Govt. Haryana,  Finance Deptt."
14.
It may be noted here that after certain anomalies with regard to the implementation of the scheme contained in the circular dated 8th Feburary, 1994, were brought to the notice of the State Government, the Finance Department of Government of Haryana issued clarification vide circular dated 29th December, 1995 and vide para 9 of this circular, it was clarified that the period of ad hoc service was not to be counted for grant of higher standard pay scales.
In view of this specific clarification also, the clarification contained in Circular dated 2081996 with regard to grant of additional increment(s) on completion 8/18 years of 'regular service', cannot be made applicable to the scheme of grant of higher standard pay scales on completion of 10/20 years of `regular satisfactory service'.
15.
As regards the case of Ajit Kumar Jain (supra) relied upon by the learned counsel for the petitioners, we are of the opinion that this judgment was with regard to grant of additional increments on completion of 8/18 years of service, and as such, is not applicable to the facts of the present case.
Similarly, in the case of Banta Ram (supra), the point raised before the Bench was as to whether the period spent on work charged basis should be counted towards computing 8/18 years service enabling them to have the benefit of one/two additional increments and higher pay scale admissible as per Government instructions dated 1451991 and government instructions contained in the circular dated 7th August, 1992.
In that case, the Bench was not dealing with the scheme contained in the circular dated 821994 with regard to the grant of higher standard pay scales.
In view of this, the observations made by the Division Bench in that case are not applicable to the facts of the present case.
16.
For the reasons recorded hereinabove, we hold that such period of ad hoc service rendered by the writ petitioners which is countable for the purpose of seniority and other service benefits in the light of the judgment of the Full Bench of this Court in Rakesh Kumar Singla's case (supra) shall be counted for the purpose of `regular satisfactory' in the context of circular dated 821994.
Similarly, in the case of work-charged employees, such period of ad hoc service which is countable for the purpose of seniority and other service benefits in relation to the regular employees, shall be counted for the purpose of `regular satisfactory service'.
17.
In view of the statement made in para 12 of the Circular, dated 8th Feburary 1994, we hold that the benefit of higher standard pay scale to a senior on the ground that the pay of his junior has been fixed higher to his pay in terms of the scheme contained in the circular, shall not be admissible to such senior.
18.
With these directions, the writ petitions stand disposed of.
