P Jyothimani, J - This writ petition is filed for a direction against the respondent to reinstate the petitioner with backwages as per the award dated 28022003, passed in ID No. 191/01 on the file of the Labour Court, Madurai.
2.
The case of the petitioner is that when he was dismissed from service, he raised an Industrial Dispute in ID No. 191/01, on the file of the Labour Court, Madurai and the Labour Court by award dated 2822003, has directed the respondent to reinstate the petitioner with backwages.
Thereafter, the award has been notified in the Government Gazette.
In spite of the same.
The award passed by the Labour Court has not been implemented by the respondent, which resulted, the petitioner to approach this Court, by way of this Writ Petition.
3.
A reference to the Industrial Disputes Act shows that when the breach of award has been committed, there is a remedy available under Section 29 of the Act.
Even in respect of the amount of salary due for the period, the petitioner has got a right under Section 33(2) (c) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
4.
In view of the availability of the efficacious alternative remedy, under the Industrial Disputes Act, the Writ Petition is not maintainable and the Writ Petition is dismissed as not maintainable, leaving it open to the petitioner to get remedy before the appropriate forum.
No costs.
Petition dismissed.
