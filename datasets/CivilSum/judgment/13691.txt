GC Mital, J - This order will dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 6235, 7564, 10295, 11695 of 1989 and 1693 of 1990 as common question is involved in all these petitions.
2.
The writ petitioners are erstwhile employees of Bhakra Beas Management Board and are continuing to occupy the official accommodation allotted to them in spite of their retirement.
Proceedings against them are pending either before the Estate Officer or eviction orders have been finalised, which orders are under challenge in a Civil Court at one stage or the other.
At the same time, these writ petitions have also been filed.
3.
The petitioners cannot have two remedies on the same cause of action.
Where the matter is pending before the Estate Officer, the matter would be premature because against order of eviction, if passed, the appeal would be maintainable under the Punjab Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorized Occupants) Act, 1971 (for short the 'Act').
Where the matter is pending before Civil Court the concerned party cannot seek another remedy of filing a writ petition.
4.
It may be noticed that similar cases came up for hearing because two different Division Benches after the eviction order have been passed by the competent authority under the Act and the eviction orders were upheld and the premises were ordered to be vacated.
These cases are Civil Writ Petition No. 13549 of 1989 'Khushi Ram and others v. Bhakra Beas Management Board and others' decided on 6.2.1990 and Civil Writ Petition No. 16941 of 1989 'Dharambir Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others' decided on 1451990.
5.
In these petitions, the writ petitioners have raised the point of jurisdiction besides the other points.
It would be open to the petitioner to raise all points before the authority or Court where proceeding are pending.
6.
With the aforesaid order and direction, these writ petitions are dismissed with no order as to costs.
Civil Misc. 6514 of 1990 also stand disposed of.
Petition dismissed.
