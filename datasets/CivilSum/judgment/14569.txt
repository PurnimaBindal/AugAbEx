GS Singhvi, J (Oral) - Although the learned counsel for the petitioners has thought it proper not to appear in the case.
Yet after taking into consideration the facts of the case, I find no justification for adjourning the case because, in my opinion, the petitioners' claim is fully covered by the decision rendered by this Court on 1491994 in Civil Writ Petition No. 15630 of 1993 (Rattan Singh v. State of Haryana) 1992(1) SCT 457 (P&H) decided along with 6 other cases.
2.
After taking a note of the decision of the Supreme Court in Chaman Lal and others v. State Haryana and another, 1987(4) S.L.R.
4 and other decisions rendered in cases reported as State of Punjab v. Kirpal Singh Bhatia, 1976(1) S.C.R. 529; Punjab Higher Qualified Teachers Union v. State of Punjab and others, 1988(1) S.L.R.
768; Jetha Singh and others v. State of Punjab, 1988(4) S.L.R.
300; Nasib Kaur and others v. State of Punjab, 1988(4) S.L.R.
301; C.W.P.
No. 9999 of 1989, Megh Raj and others v. State of Haryana decided on 26111991; C.W.P.
No. 9568 of 1993, Ram Parkash and another v. State of Haryana and others, decided on July 8, 1994; C.W.P.
No. 16289 of 1993, Shakuntla Devi and others v. State of Haryana and others, decided on July 5, 1994 and C.W.P.
No. 4639 of 1993, Mohindu Kathuria v. State of Haryana and others, decided on 1311994, the Division Bench of this Court has held that there is no justification for withholding the grant of benefit of higher grade to the persons who acquired higher qualifications during their service and the Circular issued by the Government of Haryana in the year, 1990 limiting the benefit of higher grade to those who are appointed in service with such like qualifications was unjust and unfair.
3.
For the detailed reasons, which have been recorded in order dated 1491994 in Civil Writ Petition No. 15630 of 1993 and which shall be treated as part of this order, the writ petition is allowed and it is ordered that the petitioners shall be given the benefits as ordered by the Division Bench in its order.
Costs made easy dated 1491994.
Petition allowed.
