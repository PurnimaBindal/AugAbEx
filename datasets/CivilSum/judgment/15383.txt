Satish Kumar Mittal, J.- Murli Singh Rana-petitioner was inducted in the service of Haryana State Electricity Board (hereinafter referred to as the Board) w.e.f.
1151955.
While he was working as Storekeeper in the office of respondent No. 3, in the year 1985, he proceeded on extra-ordinary leave on medical grounds with effect from 22111985 to 2071987.
On 16.4.1987, he served a three months notice seeking voluntary retirement from the service on attaining the age of 55 years.
Consequently, he was allowed to retire from the service on 2071987.
After his retirement, the service benefits like pension and gratuity were released vide order dated 9111989 on the basis of his last pay drawn at the time of proceeding on leave i.e. 22111985.
Against that order, the petitioner filed representation dated 2351991 (Annexure P-6), in which he claimed that his pension and gratuity had not been rightly calculated.
The respondent-Board should have calculated the last pay drawn of the petitioner on 2071987, when he was retired.
Since the respondents had calculated his pension as well as his gratuity on the basis of his pay on 22111985, two increments which become due on 1111985 and 1111986, were not taken into consideration.
He also submitted that the respondent-Board vide office order No. 384 dated 1981987 revised the pay scales of its employees with effect from 111986 and since the petitioner was retired on 2071987, his pay should have been revised w.e.f.
111986 for the purpose of calculating his pension and gratuity because as per Clause 12 of the said office order, the benefit of revised pay scale was also to be given to those employees who retired on or after 111986.
In the said representation, the petitioner also stated that his extra-ordinary leave on medical grounds from 22111985 to 2071987 was duly sanctioned by the competent authority vide letter dated 2121989.
By a subsequent letter dated 3081989 (Annexure P-4), the respondent-Board has specifically ordered that the aforesaid extra-ordinary leave will be counted towards increment.
Therefore, the petitioner claimed in the representation that he should be given two increments during the period of his leave i.e. on 1111985 and 1111986 and his pension, gratuity and other retiral benefits should be calculated on the basis of last pay drawn after giving him the benefit to two increments and revision of pay scales, as per office order, referred to above.
The said representation of the petitioner was rejected by he respondent-Board vide letter dated 14121992, which was conveyed to the petitioner by respondent No. 3 vide letter dated 23.12.1992 (Annexure P-9) stating therein that the Board did not find his request feasible for acceptance.
No other reason has been given in Annexure P-9.
Thereupon, the petitioner has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for quashing of the aforesaid order dated 23.12.1992 (Annexure P-9) and for issuance of a direction to the respondents to calculate his pension and gratuity on the basis of revised pay scales and after giving him the benefit of two increments.
2.
Pursuant to notice issued by this Court, the respondents filed the written statement refuting the claim made by the petitioner.
Though the factual position, as stated above, has been admitted but it has been inter-alia pleaded that the petitioner cannot be given the benefit of revision of pay scales in view of office order No. 384 dated 1981987, referred to above, as the claim of the official is not covered under Clause 12 of the said office order, rather his case falls under Clause 11.
Clauses 11 and 12 of the aforesaid office order read thus :-  "11.
Calculation of Leave Salary If any employee was on leave before 1186 and his leave continued beyond that date, his leave salary will be calculated at the same rates as before 1186.
In his case the revision of pay scale will have effect only after he has resumed duty.
12.
Applicability to Retired Personnels The benefit of revised scales of pay will also accrue to those employees who retired on or after 1st day of January, 1986."
3.
The case of the petitioner is that his case falls under Clause 12 as he stood retired on 2071987, therefore, he is entitled to the benefit of revision of pay scales, as he was in service on 111986.
On the other hand, the case of the respondent-Board is that since the petitioner was on extra- ordinary leave on medical grounds for the period 22111985 to 2071987 and he did not resume his duty after the leave, as he stood retired on 2071987, therefore, his case falls under Clause 11 of the said office order.
From a bare reading of the aforesaid two Clauses, I am of the opinion that contention of the respondent-Board is not acceptable.
Clause 12 is clearly applicable in the present case as the petitioner was in service on 111986, the date when the revision of pay scales was given effect.
Merely because the petitioner was on extra-ordinary leave on medical grounds, which was duly sanctioned and which was ordered to be counted for the purpose of increments, it cannot be said that the petitioner was not in service on 111986.
Clause 11 of the office order (Annexure P-10) relates to a different situation which is not existing in the present case.
Therefore, the said Clause is not applicable and the respondent-Board was not justified in rejecting the claim of the petitioner.
4.
Regarding the benefit of two annual increments which were to be granted to the petitioner, the respondent-Board took the stand that the said benefit is not admissible to the petitioner under the provisions of Rule 47 of CSR Vol.
I Part-I and notes 1 and 2 added to this Rule, which are reproduced below :-  "Note-1 :- w.e.f.
the Ist day of Nov. 1975, an increment shall be drawn from the 1st day of the month in which it falls due.
Note-2 :- The increment of an employee on leave on the 1st of the month shall be actually drawn from the date of resuming duty on return from leave."
5.
The contention of the respondent-Board is that since the petitioner was on extra-ordinary leave on medical grounds from 22111985 to 2071987 and he did not resume his duty on 2071987, when he was retired, the benefit of two annual increments could not be given to the petitioner.
The said contention of the respondent-Board is also not sustainable, particularly in view of order dated 3081989 (Annexure P-4) vide which it was specifically ordered that the extra-ordinary leave will be counted towards increment.
Since the petitioner stood retired on 2071987, his last pay drawn for the purpose of calculation of pension and gratuity has to be seen on the date of his retirement, i.e. 2071987.
If prior to that date the petitioner is entitled for any annual increments, the same should have been added in his pay and after adding those increments and giving him the benefit of revised pay scales, as stated above, the last pay drawn of the petitioner should have been determined for the purpose of grant of pension and gratuity and other retiral benefits.
The respondent-Board was not justified at all in rejecting the claim of the petitioner and while calculating the pension and gratuity of the petitioner on the basis of the last pay drawn on 22111985, when he was proceeded on leave.
The stand taken by the respondent-Board in the written statement as well as before this Court is wholly unjustified and unreasonable.
The petitioner is, thus, entitled for pension and gratuity on the basis of his last pay drawn on 2071987 and the last pay of the petitioner on that date has to be calculated after giving him the benefit of two annual increments and also giving full effect to the revision of pay scales vide office order No. 384 dated 1981987 (Annexure P-10).
6.
In view of the aforesaid discussion, this writ petition is allowed.
The impugned order dated 23.12.1992 (Annexure P-9) is hereby quashed.
The respondent-Board is directed to first determine the last pay drawn by the petitioner on 2071987, after taking into consideration the benefit of two annual increments and the revision of pay scales, as held above and thereafter calculate the pension and gratuity.
The difference of amount with regard to the pension and gratuity should be released to the petitioner with interest at the rate of 9% per annum within a period of three months from today.
No order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
