RL Anand, J - Petitioner Ramesh Kumar has filed the present writ petition seeking directions of this Court to quash the order Annexure P-5 dated 2681998 vide which his services were de-regularised.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he was appointed as Conductor on contractual basis on 9121993 on a consolidated salary of Rs. 1,400/-.
On 28.7.1994, respondent No. 2 issued a letter to all the General Managers, Haryana Roadways that the Drivers/Conductors who are working in Haryana Roadways on contractual basis at the fixed salary of Rs. 1,800/- and Rs. 1,400/- per month should be regularised if they have completed two years of service against the vacancies available with them.
As per this policy, an employee who has worked more than 240 clear working days in a calendar year will be deemed to have worked for that full year and the break will be ignored.
On the basis of that policy the petitioner along with some others filed C.W.P.
No. 17708 of 1997 for regularisation of their services in view of the instructions issued by the department.
That writ petition was allowed in view of the statement given by the respondents that the claim of the petitioners shall be considered for regularisation.
On the basis of that decision the services of the petitioner were regularised on 551998 wef 131997.
Later on, the petitioner was issued a show cause notice on 12.8.1998 stating that the services of the petitioner were regularised inadvertently and in contravention of the instructions of the Transport Commissioner.
Thereafter, the impugned order dated 2681998 was passed ordering de-regularisation of he services of the petitioner and the regularisation order earlier passed on 551998 was withdrawn.
The case set up by the petitioner now is that he was regularised as per the instructions dated 2331998 rightly and the impugned order dated 2681998 is illegal.
Hence, the present writ petition.
3.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents.
The respondents filed the written statement and took a stand that subsequently it was noticed that in the depot of the petitioner 303 posts of Conductors were sanctioned and those had already been filled up and the services of the petitioner had been regularised in excess of the available sanctioned posts inadvertently.
Therefore, show cause notice was issued and after giving opportunity to the petitioner his services were de-regularised and the order of regularisation was withdrawn.
4.
I have heard Mrs. Daya Chaudhary, Advocate on behalf of the petitioner and Mr. Vijay Dahiya, AAG, Haryana on behalf of the respondents.
5.
It is stated at the Bar by the learned counsel for the respondents that during the pendency of the present writ petition the services of the petitioner have been regularised w.e.f.
1131997.
In this view of the matter, the main prayer of the petitioner goes in the wind as the relief has already been granted to him.
6.
Now the petitioner states that his junior was regularised w.e.f.
15121995 and, therefore, he should also be regularised from that date.
Let this aspect may be examined by the Government within three months from today and if the junior to the petitioner has been regularised from 15121995 or from any date earlier to 1131997, the petitioner shall also be given the same benefit and advantage in the matter of seniority and other benefits which the State Government had already granted to the junior to the petitioner.
This direction has been given keeping in view the principle of equity and equality.
No order as to costs.
order accordingly.
