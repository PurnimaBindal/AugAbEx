Harjit Singh Bedi, J. (Oral) - The petitioner has sought two reliefs in the present case : firstly, impugning the order dated 14th February, 1990 (Annexure P-9) whereby he had been transferred from Dadri I to Mangal Chaudhry and secondly, claiming interest on account of the fact that he had been permitted to cross the efficiency bar belatedly for no fault on his part.
2.
This petition was admitted, on August 3, 1990, i.e. after the petitioner had actually retired from service.
The petitioner's claim to interest on account of the delay in the processing of his efficiency bar is, therefore, the only claim that now remains alive.
3.
In the reply filed by the respondents, the stand taken in response to this claim is that there had been a delay in the decision with regard to the crossing of the first efficiency bar due to the fact that the Deputy Commissioner, Rohtak had not produced the service record of the petitioner in time.
It has also been submitted that the second efficiency bar in the revised scale was allowed to be crossed vide order dated 8th November, 1989, with effect from 141983 and that this delay had also been occasioned due to the fact that the service record was not available with the authority concerned.
It has nevertheless been pointed out that the amount due to the petitioner on account of the delay in disposal of the efficiency bar case had been paid to him on 28th May, 1990.
To my mind, there is no cogent explanation as to why the petitioner was not permitted to cross the efficiency bar on the due date or within a reasonable time thereafter as it was for the authorities to ensure that the record was made available within a reasonable time.
4.
This petition is accordingly allowed and it is directed that the petitioner will be allowed interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum of on the sum of Rs. 729630 from the date of amount fell due to the date of actual payment.
There will, however, be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
