VK Bali, J (Oral) - Petitioner was appointed on ad hoc basis on 18101979 and retired on 15111994.
In this petition filed by him under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, he seeks post-retiral benefits which have been denied to him on the only ground that this services were regularised on 891994 vide order, Annexure P-4 and he did not have qualifying service entitling him to pension and other post-retiral benefits.
2.
Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that if the ad hoc service is followed by regular service, Rule 317-A of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, in terms, would come to the rescue of the petitioner.
Rule aforesaid reads thus :-  "3.17-A: (a) All services interrupted or continuous followed by confirmation shall be treated as qualifying service; the period of break shall be omitted, while working out aggregate service."
3.
If Rule aforesaid might have been applied, petitioner would have rendered service for fifteen years and little less than one month.
It may be highlighted that petitioner's service throughout was uninterrupted.
Learned State counsel could not controvert the contention raised by learned counsel for the petitioner.
4.
In view of what has been said above, this petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to calculate the post-retiral benefits of the petitioner on the basis of Rule 317-A ibid and make over the arrears thereof to the petitioner within four weeks from today.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
