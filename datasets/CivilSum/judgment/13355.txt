ML Singhal, J. (Oral) - Dr. Arvind Kumar Rana joined HCMS-II (Haryana Civil Medical Service) on ad hoc basis as Medical Officer w.e.f.
20.11.1981.
Vide Haryana Govt. notification No. GSR-3/Constitution/Article 320/92 dated 10.1.1992, his services were regularised w.e.f.
1111991.
According to him, he completed successfully the period of probation on 31121992 and he has been discharging his duties diligently and efficiently all through and his work and conduct has been throughout graded as creditable.
His grievance was that he was unjustly refused the grant of annual increments w.e.f.
November 1982 onwards and he became entitled to senior scale of 3000-4500 w.e.f.
151989 i.e. after he had put in 5 years and after he had put in 12 years service, he was entitled to the scale of 4100-5300.
In the plaint, he has mentioned the relevant notifications entitling him to the grade of 3000-4500 after he had put in 5 years of service and 4100-5300 after he had put in 12 years of service.
2.
Defendant-State of Haryana contested his claim urging that he has not entitled to any such benefit as his was merely an ad hoc appointment and an ad hoc appointee is not entitled to any such benefits.
He would become entitled to these benefits after he puts in the requisite length of service after being regularised.
3.
On the pleadings of the parties, the following issues were framed by the learned trial court :-  1.
Whether the ad hoc service of the plaintiff is countable for the purpose of selection grade as alleged ?
OPD 2.
Whether the plaintiff is entitled to mandatory injunction as alleged ?
OPD 3.
Whether the suit is not maintainable is the present form ?
OPD 4.
Whether the plaintiff has no cause of action to file the present suit ?
OPD 5.
Whether the proper court fees has been affixed with the plaint ?
OPD 6.
Whether the suit is bad for mis-joinder and non-joinder of necessary parties ?
OPD 7.
Relief.
Learned Additional Civil Judge, Senior Division, Hissar decreed the plaintiff's suit for declaration declaring that the ad hoc service rendered by him is countable towards the grant of benefit which accrues to the Medical Officers after they had put in 5-12 years respectively in service and the State of Haryana was directed to refix his pay in the scale of 3000-4500 w.e.f.
151989 and 4100-5300 w.e.f.
20.11.1993 with all consequential benefits flowing from his fixation of pay.
4.
State of Haryana went in appeal to the District Court at Hissar against this decree of Additional Civil Judge, Senior Division, Hissar dated 3111997.
Learned Additional District Judge, Hissar dismissed the appeal vide order dated 2051998.
State of Haryana has come up in this Regular Second Appeal thereby challenging the grant of aforesaid benefit to the plaintiff.
5.
I have heard Shri S.R.
Kanwar, Advocate for the State of Haryana and Shri Suman Jain, Advocate for the respondent-plaintiff Dr. Arvind Kumar Rana.
6.
In my opinion, no exception can be taken to the impugned decree granted by the learned Additional Civil Judge, Senior Division, Hissar in favour of the respondent-plaintiff affirmed in appeal by the learned Additional District Judge, Hissar as ad hoc service rendered by him was also rendered to the public.
Public benefited each day by his experience in the medical care and expertise being bestowed on them by him.
Respondent-plaintiff was fulfilling the requirement and possessing all the essential qualifications for the post of medical officer Class-II in the State of Haryana in the year 1981 and that was why he was appointed.
Ad hoc appointment had to be resorted to because the process of regular appointments might have been felt by the govt. to be more cumbersome, time consuming and tardy.
In Rakesh Kumar Singla v. State of Haryana, 1995(4) SCT 285, Hon'ble Full Bench of this Court held that ad hoc service will count towards the grant of monetary benefits, if subsequently, it was regularised.
7.
I do not find any weight in this RSA and accordingly dismiss the same with costs.
Counsel fee Rs. 1100/-  Appeal dismissed.
