JS Narang, J - This judgment would dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 6159 of 1992, 4265 of 1993 and 11021 of 1993 as the common question of law is involved in all the above said petitions, i.e., an anomaly has arisen on account of the pay scales of the lower post having been made equivalent to the pay scales of the higher post which are emanating from the common feeder post.
2.
The facts are being taken from Civil Writ Petition No. 6159 of 1992.
Petitioners were appointed as Fisheries Officers on various dates in the year 1990.
The service conditions of the petitioners are governed by the statutory rules called the Haryana Fisheries Department (State Service Group C) Rules, 1979 (hereinafter called "the rules").

It is clear from the rules that the posts of Fisheries Officers are to be filled from two sources, i.e., 50% by direct recruitment and 50% by way of promotion from the posts of Assistant Fisheries Officers.
A Pay Commission was constituted by the Government of Haryana and as per the recommendations of the Commission, the pay scale for the post of Assistant Fisheries Officers was fixed as Rs. 160-400 and that of Fisheries Officers Rs. 225-550.
The said scales were granted to the said Officers with effect from 1969 and as per the recommendations of the Commission, the same were again revised with effect from 141979 and as a result thereof, the scales of Assistant Fisheries Officers came to be Rs. 525-1050 and that of the Fisheries Officers Rs. 600- 1100.
4.
The pay scales of some of the categories were again revised with effect from 111986 but the scales of the officers in the Fisheries Department were not revised.
The petitioners represented to the Government of Haryana claiming that on account of the revision reflected in First Schedule of the Haryana Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1987, the scales of the Officers lower in post had been made equivalent to the pay scales of the higher post.
As it had been stated categorically that the pay scales, i.e., Rs. 525-1050 (TS) and also the pay scales of Rs. 600-1100 (S.G.) would stand revised to Rs. 1400-40-1600-50-2300-EB-60-2600, the revision took place with effect from 111986 and as a result thereof, the pay scale of the Assistant Fisheries Officers and that of Fisheries Officers become equal to each other.
It is an admitted case that the scale of Assistant Fisheries Officers and that of Fisheries Officers were different and the said difference is spelt out even when the revision took place with effect from 141979.
5.
The Government of Haryana had appointed a committee to remove anomalies and the said committee had recommended that promotional scales of an employee should definitely be different from the scale from which the promotion is made.
This averment contained in para 8(iii) in the petition has not been controverted while submitting reply to para 8.
The State has taken the stand that the pay scales of Assistant Fisheries Officers were lower than those of the Fisheries Officers but has supported the stand that the said pay scales have been equated by way of a conscious decision taken by the State Government.
The petitioners did not receive any reply or any consideration in respect of the representation dated 14121991 made by the petitioners.
Aggrieved by the non-action on the part of the State Government, the petitioners filed the present petition seeking the issuance of writ of mandamus to the respondents for granting the higher pay scales to the petitioners being upon the higher posts emanating from the common feeder post.
6.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has drawn my attention to the judgments of this Court whereby the principle has been enunciated that the promotional post which is definitely higher than the post from which the incumbents are promoted, their pay scales have to be higher than the pay scales of the post of the said feeder cadre.
The argument is that the posts in the promotion cadre must have higher pay scale than the posts in the feeder cadre.
Reference has been made to the three Division Bench Judgments of this Court :  1.
Sunder Lal Jain v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) SLR 215 : 1995(1) SCT 564 (P&H)(DB).
2.
Ranbir Singh and others v. State of Haryana, 1997(1) Judicial Reports (Labour and Services) 177 : 1997(1) SCT 252 (P&H)(DB).
3.
Civil Writ Petition No. 1380 of 1998, KC Hooda v. State of Haryana, decided on 2121998.
(i) and (ii) have been relied upon in Civil Writ Petition No. 1380 of 1998 quoted above and this judgment has also been further followed by a learned Single Judge of this Court while deciding Civil Writ Petition No. 1388 of 1995 which was decided on 11151999.
7.
The stand of Government of Haryana cannot be accepted because the reasonable view has to be taken.
Admittedly, the post of Assistant Fisheries Officer, is lower post than the Fisheries Officers and as on 141979 carried lower and higher pay scales respectively.
It is not understandable as to what weighed with the Government in equalizing the scales of both the posts as the admitted facts are that the post of Fisheries Officer is a promotional post.
Applying the revisions to the scales by importing the principle of equiliser is not sustainable, such act is unjust and unfair.
8.
For the foregoing reasons, I hold that the petitioners' category would be entitled to the revision of the pay scales with effect from 111986 but the effect of the same shall be given in each individual case taking into consideration the relevant dates and the revision shall be made accordingly which shall include the refixation of the pay of the petitioners from the relevant dates.
They shall also be entitled to the consequential benefits which may flow from the re-fixation of the pay of the petitioners.
9.
Consequently, I allow the petition and direct that the State Government shall carry out the entire exercise within a period of three months from the date when the certified copy of this order is received from this Court or is supplied by the petitioner to the Government, whichever is earlier.
10.
So far as the case of the petitioners in Civil Writ Petition No. 11021 of 1993 is concerned, the date of removal of anomaly shall be read as 28.12.1984 instead of 111986.
The rest of the directions, i.e., the period within which the effect is to be given to shall remain the same.
Petition allowed.
