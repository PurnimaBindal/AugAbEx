RS Mongia, J (Oral) - The petitioner was appointed as Senior Auditor in the Audit Wing of the Department of Cooperation in the year 1962.
The hierarchy in the cadre is that a Senior Auditor is promoted to the post of Audit Officer and then to the post of Deputy Chief Auditor and the next higher post is that of Joint Chief Auditor and the still next higher post is that of a Chief Auditor.
The petitioner was promoted to the post of Audit Officer in the year 1970, to the post of Deputy Chief Auditor in the year 1977 and then to the post of Joint Chief Auditor on 30121986.
All posts from Deputy Chief Auditor to the post of Chief Auditor are Class I posts.
It may be observed here that in the Department, there was only one post of Joint Chief Auditor in the Cadre of Punjab State Cooperative Audit Service (Class-I).
2.
There were two posts of Additional Chief Auditors in the Department.
One of the posts of Additional Chief Auditor became permanently available on 1.11.1987 on the superannuation of one Sh. Nirpendra Singh.
An order was passed by the Government on 9121987 that the petitioner would draw his as Joint Chief Auditor against a vacant post of Additional Chief Auditor under Rule 62 of the Punjab Financial Rules Vol.
I with immediate effect.
The order reads as under :-  "The President of India is pleased to order that Sardar Amar Singh, Joint Chief Auditor, Cooperative Societies, will draw his pay as such against a vacant post of Additional Chief Auditor Cooperative Societies under rule 62 of the Punjab Financial Rules, Vol.
I, with immediate effect."
The above said order was endorsed to all concerned on 10121987.
On the same day ie 9121987, another order was issued by the Government promoting one Sh. JP Gupta, Deputy Chief Auditor as Joint Chief Auditor, Cooperative Societies, but 'the promotion was made effective from 541988, the date on which JP Gupta would have completed experience of 4 years as Deputy Chief Auditor required for promotion as Joint Chief Auditor.
However, it was further mentioned in the order that henceforth Shri Gupta would draw his pay as Deputy Chief Auditor in place of Amar Singh-petitioner, who had separately been ordered to draw his pay against the vacant post of Additional Chief Auditor.
3.

Sh. JP Gupta, Deputy Chief Auditor (Working) against the post of Joint Chief Auditor  Housefed, Housing Coop Socs., Milkfed, Milk Socs.

In pursuance of the above order assigning duties, the petitioner informed the Chief Auditor vide Annexure P3 dated 14121987 that he had assumed the charge of duties as assigned to him vide the above referred order.
It is the case of the petitioner that consequent upon his assuming the charge of duties on 14121987 vide Annexure P-3, he started to discharge the duties attached to the post of Additional Chief Auditor.
It is further his case that the duties which had been assigned to him vide order dated 14121987 (quoted above), he had started to discharge all the duties of Additional Chief Auditor, which were earlier discharged by Shri RS Garg, who was holding the post of Additional Chief Auditor, with some minor/insignificant changes.
It has been averred in the writ petition that so far as these minor/insignificant changes were concerned that was because the petitioner was discharging a combination of duties earlier vested is the two Additional Chief Auditors in the Cadre.
It has further been specifically mentioned in para 9 of the petition that all the duties vested in the petitioner as Joint Chief Auditor were being discharged by Shri JP Gupta, and therefore, the petitioner was not discharging any duties of the post of Joint Chief Auditor after December 14,1987.
4.
It may be observed here that vide order dated 24101989 (Annexure P-6) the petitioner was promoted as Additional Chief Auditor.
This order was endorsed to all concerned on 2111989.
Further, vide order dated 5.12.1990 (Annexure P-7) the petitioner was given the current duty charge of the vacant post of Chief Auditor with immediate effect till the post was filled up or he was promoted as such on regular basis.
5.
The service conditions of the petitioner are governed by the statutory rules known as Punjab State Cooperative Audit Service (Class-I) Rules, 1977 (hereinafter called the 'Rules') Rule 7 deals with the method of appointment.
The relevant extract dealing with the method of appointment of Joint Chief Auditor, Additional Chief Auditor and Chief Auditor is reproduced below :-  "7.
Method of Appointment : Appointment to the service shall be made in the following manner :-  xx xx xx xx  (b) in the case of Joint Chief Auditor by promotion from amongst the Deputy Chief Auditor having an experience of working as such for a minimum period of four years.
(c) in the case of the Additional Chief Auditor by promotion from amongst the Joint Chief Auditors having an experience of working as such for a minimum period to two years; (d) in the case of the Chief Auditors :-  (I) by promotion from amongst the Addl.
Chief Auditors having an experience of working as such for a minimum period of two years; or (II) by transfer of a person holding similar or identical post in any department of the State Government in case no suitable candidate is available for appointment by promotion.
2.
In case a person does not have the requisite experience but is still promoted under sub-rule (i) due to the exigencies of service, he will continue to draw pay in his previous pay scale or may in his option be allowed to draw the minimum of the scale of the post to which he is promoted till such time as he completes the prescribed minimum experience when he will be allowed the regular scale of the post in the promoted rank and will earn next increment after he completes one year of service from the date he acquires minimum prescribed experience."
The prayers of the petitioner in the present writ petition are :-  (1) Petitioner had completed two years of service as Joint Chief Auditor on 30121988 and the post of Add.
Chief Auditor was vacant on that date.
Consequently, he should be allowed regular promotion as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988 and not w.e.f.
24101989/2111989.
(2) The petitioner should be granted pay scale of Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988 and not w.e.f.
2111989.
(3) Since the petitioner had been discharging the duties of Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
14121987 he should be declared eligible for promotion to the post of Chief Auditor w.e.f.
14121989.
(4) Petitioner should be considered for regular promotion to the post of Chief Auditor w.e.f.
1.12.1990 as on that date, the post of Chief Auditor had fallen vacant.
6.
As far as the first prayer is concerned it may be observed that when the petitioner had been promoted as Additional Chief Auditor vide order dated 24101989 (Annexure P-6) he had made representation that in fact he should be promoted w.e.f.
30121988 as he had completed the requisite two years experience as Joint Chief Auditor as per rule 7 quoted above.
This was rejected on the ground that petitioner had not performed the duties of Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988 as such he could not be considered for retrospective promotion w.e.f.
30121988.
It was further mentioned that merely because a vacancy was available it did not entitle any officer to claim retrospective promotion.
There is no dispute on the fact that on 30121988 a post of Addl.
Chief Auditor was vacant.
In fact it is the case of petitioner that w.e.f.
14121987 he had been discharging the duties of Additional Chief Auditor exclusively and was not discharging any duty of Joint Chief Auditor.
There is a serious dispute over this fact regarding the performing of duties.
It is the case of the respondents that in fact even after 14121987 petitioner kept on performing the duties of Joint Chief Auditor and alongwith that additionally petitioner was performing some duties of Additional Chief Auditor also.
Be that as it may, there is no doubt that petitioner had completed his experience as Joint Chief Auditor on 30121988 and whether he was performing exclusively the duties of Additional Chief Auditor or additionally alongwith the duties of Joint Chief Auditor would not affect the eligibility of the petitioner to be considered for promotion as Additional Chief Auditor when he had completed the period of requisite experience under the Rules as Joint Chief Auditor.
7.
The question then arises is that if the post of Additional Chief Auditor was vacant on 30121988 and the petitioner was eligible for promotion to the said post and even assuming that he was only discharging some duties of Additional Chief Auditor alongwith the duties of Joint Chief Auditor, would he have any right for asking for promotion w.e.f.
30121988 ?
On facts and circumstances of this case when the post was vacant on 30121988 and in fact the petitioner was also discharging, if not all, some duties of Additional Chief Auditor, there seems to be no justification not to consider the petitioner for regular promotion as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988.
The Government can always justify that though a post is vacant, yet the Government has no plans to fill the same on some justifiable reasons.
The reasons can be like financial restraints or some other justifiable administrative reasons.
However, none has been suggested in the present case as to why the petitioner was not promoted as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988 and the only reason given is that since he had not worked as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988, no retrospective promotion could be given to him and the promotion which was given on 24101989 as Additional Chief Auditor was justified.
What were the changes in circumstances between 30121988 and 24101989 have also not been spelt out by the respondents in their pleadings.
Consequently, I hold that the petitioner in the peculiar facts and circumstances of this case had a right to be considered for regular promotion as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988.
8.
The second prayer would be granted to the petitioner if on consideration he is promoted as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988.
He would be entitled to the pay scale of Additional Chief Auditor from that date.
9.
Now coming to the third prayer that the petitioner should be declared eligible for promotion to the post of Chief Auditor w.e.f.
14121989.
The basis for this prayer is that the petitioner had been discharging exclusively the duties of Additional Chief Auditor which were assigned to him vide order dated 14121987 (Annexure P-2) and he has not been doing any duty of Joint Chief Auditor.
The case of the petitioner is that for the purpose of promotion to the post of Chief Auditor, two years experience of working as Additional Chief Auditor is necessary under Rule 7(d)(i) of the Rules quoted above.
Since, according to the petitioner he had completed two years experience of working as Additional Chief, on 14121989 he should be declared eligible for promotion to the post of Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
that date.
As observed above, there is a very serious dispute on fact whether the petitioner had been discharging exclusively the duties of Additional Chief Auditor or both of Joint Chief Auditor and some duties of Additional Chief Auditor during the period 14121987 to 2111989.
In view of this disputed question of fact I cannot declare the petitioner to be eligible for promotion to the post of Chief Auditor w.e.f.
14121987.
However, if the petitioner is promoted as Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988 as observed while dealing with prayer No. 1, the petitioner would be eligible for promotion to the post of Chief Auditor after two years of that date ie w.e.f.
30121990.
10.
Now coming to the last prayer i.e. prayer No. 4 that petitioner may be considered for the post of Chief Auditor w.e.f.
1.12.1990, I may observe that petitioner has already been given the current duty charge of the post of Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
5.12.1990.
The post had fallen vacant w.e.f.
1.12.1990.
If the petitioner is promoted w.e.f.
30121990 as Additional Chief Auditor, his case for promotion as Chief Auditor would be considered w.e.f.
30121990 and the fact that he is already holding the current duty charge would be taken into consideration.
11.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed to the extent that the petitioner had a right on consideration for promotion to the post of Additional Chief Auditor w.e.f.
30121988.
The respondents are directed to consider the case of the petitioner for such promotion within a period of one month from the receipt of this order.
If promoted, he would be entitled to the scale of that post from the date of such retrospective promotion and would be further entitled for consideration for promotion to the post of Chief Auditor two years after the date of promotion as Additional Chief Auditor.
I leave the parties to bear their own costs.
Petition allowed.
