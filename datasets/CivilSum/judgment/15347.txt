KC Gupta, J - Jaswant Singh respondent (plaintiff) has filed a suit for declaration that the orders dated 13.6.1990, 1461990 and 8.1.1993 passed by the State Transport Controller, Haryana, by virtue of which he had not been considered for promotion as Sub Inspector in the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 and juniors to him had been promoted by superseding him, were illegal and not binding on his rights.
The said suit was decreed by the Civil Judge (Jr. Division) Jagadhari, vide his judgment and decree dated 15111997 by holding that the annual confidential reports for the year 1980-81 Ex. D2 and for the year 1982-83 Ex. D3 cannot be presumed to be adverse as the same were never conveyed to the respondent.
It was further held that the annual confidential reports or the year 1983-84 has also been conveyed to the respondent in the year 1986 after a lapse of two years against which he had filed a representation which has not been decided so far.
2.
Aggrieved by the said judgement and decree, the State of Haryana filed an appeal which was dismissed by the Addl.
District Judge, Jagadhari, vide his judgment and decree dated 1482000 except with the modification that the orders dated 13.6.1990 and 1461990 were held to be time barred and as such could not be challenged before the civil Court.
3.
Still dissatisfied, the appellant-State of Haryana has filed the present appeal.
4.
I have heard counsel for the parties and carefully gone through the file.
5.
There is concurrent finding of fact that the order dated 8.1.1993 is illegal and is not binding on the right of the respondent and he is entitled to be considered for promotion to the post of Sub Inspector from 8.1.1993 when his juniors were promoted and as a consequential relief he is entitled to all the benefits on the grounds that adverse remarks cannot be taken into consideration as the same were not communicated to the respondent and further the adverse remarks for the year 1983-84 were conveyed to him after a gap of two years and his representation was not decided so far which should have been decided within a reasonable time.
It has been observed by the Hon'ble Apex Court in Baidyanath Mahapatra v. State of Orissa, 1989(2) RSJ 519 that adverse entries must be communicated to the Government servant by December of each year and if the same is communicated after several years then it looses its significance.
Consequently, there is no illegality or impropriety in the impugned order.
The concurrent finding of fact cannot be disturbed in the regular second appeal.
No substantial question of law is involved.
Hence, this regular second appeal is dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
