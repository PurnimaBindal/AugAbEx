GS Singhvi, J - The petitioner has prayed for the grant of following substantive relief :  "Issue a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to release the payment on account of General Provident Fund (in short GP Fund), Group Insurance Scheme, payment on account enhanced earned leave, as per Punjab Govt. letter no. 1/10/98-3FPII/7219 dated 13.5.98, payment on account of bonus for the year 1995-96 and further directing the respondents to consider case of the petitioner for grant of two additional increments under proficiency set-up (step-up ?)
scheme; with further direction to the respondents to pay all the consequential arrears accrued upon the grant of said benefits along with interest at the rate of 18% pa." 2.
In paragraph 2 of the reply affidavit filed by him, Shri Dilraj Singh Gill, Executive Engineer, Jalandhar Mechanical Drainage Division, Nangal Township, has made the following statement :  "That the payment of final GPF amounting to Rs. 78,280/- has already been made to the petitioner vide S.D.O.
Workshop Sub-Division, Jalandhar Vr.
No. 16 dated 10.11.98, the payment of DCRG amounting to Rs. 52,599/- has been made vide Vr.
No. 25, dated 1571997, payment on A/C of Revision of pay scale has been made to the petitioner vide Vr.
No. 52 dated 1061998.
The payment of Leave Encashment/balance Leave Encashment has been made to the petitioner viz. Rs. 38,128/- and Rs. 20,532/- has been made vide Vr.
No. 11 dated 26397 and Vr.
No. 17, dated 10111998.
The payment of bonus for the year 1995-96 for Rs. 4758/- has been made vide Vr.
No. 32 dated 3131997.
The payment of GIS worked out to Rs. 420/- deducted from the pay of the petitioner during the period from 12/94 to 8/96 at the rate of Rs. 20/- per month is yet to be released.
The bill has been prepared and sent to Treasury Officer.
The payment to the petitioner shall be made shortly.
In view of the facts explained above, it is respectfully prayed that the payment of all retirement benefits has been released to the petitioner well in time and no interest may be imposed.
The present writ petition may be dismissed."
3.
On 21.1.1999, we had, after hearing the parties, impressed upon the learned Deputy Advocate General to instruct the concerned authorities to release the remaining dues of the petitioner.
4.
Today, Shri Rupinder Khosla made a statement that draft No. 226949 dated 2711999 for a sum of Rs. 33,925/- representing the revised gratuity has been prepared.
He stated that the draft will be immediately sent to the petitioner.
Shri Khosla also stated that the issue relating to the grant of the benefit of proficiency step up to the petitioner will be decided by the competent authority within 2 months.
He also stated that the amount of group insurance could not be released because the petitioner has not completed the required formalities.
5.
Learned counsel for the petitioner urged that the respondents should be directed to pay interest to the petitioner for the delayed payment of dues.
This submission of the learned counsel merits acceptance because failure of the respondents to pay the amount due to the petitioner amounts to depriving him of his property without the authority of law.
6.
In view of the above, we dispose of the writ petition with the following directions:  (i) The draft representing the amount of enhanced gratuity shall be sent to the petitioner within 7 days.
(ii) The petitioner shall be paid interest at the rate of 18% for the delay caused in the payment of dues.
The competent authority shall calculate the amount of interest and pay the same to the petitioner within 2 months of the submission of certified copy of this order.
(iii) The petitioner's claim for grant of the benefit of proficiency step up shall be decided by the competent authority within 2 months from the date of submission of certified copy of this order.
(iv) The amount representing group insurance be paid to the petitioner within 15 days of completion of formalities.
7.
An attested copy of this order be given to the learned Deputy Advocate General for ensuring timely compliance of the above mentioned directions.
Orders accordingly.
