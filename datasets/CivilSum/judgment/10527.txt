MS Liberhan and HK Sandhu, JJ.
- Concedingly the facts and circumstances of this case are squarely covered by the law laid down in Arjun Dev and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1992(2) SCT 119 (DB) (P&H) : 1992(2) Recent Service Judgments 498.
2.
Learned Counsel for the respondents, however, urged that instructions issued on 9390 explained the instructions issued dated 23757 would prevail and cited CWP No. 14736 of 91 decided on 11292 in support of his contention.
3.
Learned counsel for the petitioners controverted the submission of the learned counsel for the respondent.
It was urged that the instructions already issued were explained in the above cited judgment i.e. 1992(2) R.S.J.
498.
Instructions cannot take away the basis of judgment or set aside or undo the effect of judicial judgment directly or indirectly.
The respondent, cannot by administrative fiat set aside judicial pronouncement.
For this proposition reliance has been placed on decision in CWP No. 10346 of 92 wherein it was observed that "the clarification issued by the Government, if any, cannot undo the judgment passed by this Court."
This view is further supported by the judgment of this Court in CWP No. 11995 of 89 decided on 13th October, 1993.
4.
The judgment relied upon by the learned counsel for the respondent in CWP 14736 of 91 decided on 11292 neither decided nor considered the question raised by the counsel for the petitioner with respect to the instructions dated 9390 being invalid on the ground that these amount to setting aside judicial judgment.
The case was decided on its own facts and points raised.
It is further admitted that persons having acquired higher qualification after issuance of instructions dated 9390 were released higher grade on their acquiring qualifications.
Reference was made to various writ petitions in the petition.
5.
In view of the observations made above, the writ petition is allowed and the respondents are directed to release to the petitioners pay scale of master grade with effect from the date they acquired higher qualification.
Relief with respect to back wages is limited to three years and two months as the petitioners have approached this Court belatedly.
The amount may be released within six months.
Writ Petition stands disposed of.
Petition allowed.
