Sat Pal, J. - In this Writ Petition, the petitioner has prayed for quashing the order, dated 10th November, 1994 (Annexure P-10), by which it was held that the petitioner was not entitled to any service benefit for the period from 10th February, 1980 to 29th August, 1983.
The petitioner has further prayed that the respondents be directed to consider the aforesaid period for the purpose of increment and pension.
2.
Mr. Malik, learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner, submitted that the petitioner had earlier filed Civil Writ Petition No. 5178 of 1989, and in response to the notice of motion issued in that case, the respondents had filed written statement, copy of which is Annexure P-1.
In reply to Para 4, it was stated that "his case was examined and the petitioner was asked to give an undertaking that he would not claim any service benefit for the period he remained out of service but he did not come up with this undertaking uptil now.
It is further submitted that the department is still prepared to gave him benefit towards increments and pension if he gives the desired undertaking but be cannot be made senior to regularly selected candidates along with him."
2.
In view of the averments made in Para 4 of the writ petition, the said writ petition was disposed of by a Division Bench of this Court vide order, dated 14th February, 1980 (Annexure P-2).
The order reads as under :-  "We are of the opinion that in view of the averments made in para 4 of the written statement, the petition has been rendered infructuous.
The respondents may, however, pass an appropriate order if the petitioner gives an undertaking as asked for in para 4 of the written statement.
The petition stands disposed of as indicated above."
3.
In pursuance of the aforesaid order, the petitioner gave his undertaking dated 7th August, 1990, by way of affidavit and in this affidavit, it was stated that the petitioner would not claim any service benefits for the period from 10th February, 1980 to 29th August, 1983, provided the department gave him benefit towards increments and pension of the aforesaid period.
Thereafter, the District Primary Education Officer, Sirsa, vide order, dated 12th December, 1990 (Annexure P-4) held that the petitioner would take leave due to him with effect from 10th February, 1980 to 29th August, 1983 and will not claim any service benefit except benefit towards increments and pension in the light of the judgment of the Punjab and Haryana High Court in C.W.P.
No. 5178 of 1989.
4.
In spite of the order, passed by the District Primary Education Officer, Sirsa, later on the respondent-Government of Haryana issued Memo No. 36/56/92-Ed-II (2) dated 11th February, 1993 (Annexure R-3/T) wherein it was stated that the Government had agreed to sanction extraordinary leave from 10th February, 1980 to 19th August, 1983 to the petitioner subject to the condition that the said period would not be counted towards any purpose.
The said order was communicated to the petitioner vide Annexure P-4.
5.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and perusing the record, we are of the opinion that there is no justification in issuing the order, Annexure R-3/T, dated 11th February, 1994, and Annexure P-10, particularly when the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents in C.W.P.
No. 5178 of 1989, the respondents had clearly stated that the Department was prepared to give benefit towards increments and pension if the petitioner gave the desired undertaking that he should not be made senior to regularly selected candidates along with him and the said undertaking was duly given by the petitioner.
6.
In view of the above discussion, the writ petition is allowed and the orders, Annexure P-10 and R-3/T are set aside.
The respondents are directed to give to the petitioner benefit towards increments and pension in terms of the reply of the respondents in Para 4 of the written statement in C.W.P.
No. 5178 of 1989.
Petition allowed.
