GS Singhvi, J - This petition has been filed for quashing the appointment of respondent Nos. 4 to 28 as Drivers and also for directing the official respondents to appoint the petitioners in view of the provisions contained in Section 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (hereinafter referred to as the Act).
2.
The petitioners have averred that they were appointed as Drivers on contract basis in June 1997 after being selected by the competent authority.
Their grievance is that the respondent No. 3 has appointed the private respondents as Drivers ignoring their priority right under Section 25-H of the Act to be re-employed.
3.
Shri RK Malik argued that the failure of the respondent to give preferential consideration to the petitioners for appointment has resulted in the violation of their right guaranteed by Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and Section 25-H of the Act.
4.
We have considered the submission of Shri Malik but are of the opinion that it is not a fit case for exercise of jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution because an equally efficacious alternative remedy is available to the petitioners by raising industrial dispute under Section 10 read with Section 2(k) of the Act.
In the Premier Automobiles Ltd. v. Kamlakar Shantaram Wadke and others, AIR 1975 SC 2238, the Apex Court held that a person seeking enforcement of rights flowing from the provisions of the Act must avail the remedy available to him under the Act.
This view has been reiterated 20 years later in The Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation and another v. Krishna Kant, AIR 1995 SC 1715 : 1994(1) SCT 23 (SC).
5.
For the reason mentioned above, the writ petition is dismissed with liberty to the petitioners to avail the alternative remedy under the Act.
Petition dismissed.
