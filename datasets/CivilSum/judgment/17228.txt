Harjit Singh Bedi, J. (Oral) - The petitioner joined the 4 years BE Computers Course (8 Semesters) in the Institute of Technology and Management, Gurgaon (hereinafter referred to as "the Institute") for the Session 1996-97 against one of the seats reserved for NRI/NRI sponsored candidates, and as required, she deposited a sum of Rs. 52,500/- which was equivalent to 1500 US Dollars as fee.
It is the petitioner's grievance that she had now been called upon to deposit Rs. 1,08,000/- which was equivalent to 3000 US Dollars instead Rs. 52,500/- vide notice Annexure P-1 dated 30th July, 1997, for the academic year 1997-98.
Aggrieved by the notice in question, the petitioner filed a representation before respondent No. 1, the Guru Jambeshwar University, Hisar, complaining that the increase was wholly unjustified and not warranted by the prospectus, but finding no redressal from the authorities, has filed the present writ petition.
2.
On notice of motion, a reply has been filed by the Institue (respondent No. 2) alone and it has been pleaded by way of preliminary objection that the Institute was not a State as postulated by Article 12 of the Constitution of India and as such, the writ petition was not maintainable.
It has also been pleaded that the fee which was sought to be charged from the petitioner had been settled in accordance with the scheme formulated by the Hon'ble Supreme court in Unni Krishnan J.P. and others v. The State of Andhra Pradesh and others, 1993(2) SCT 512 (SC) : AIR 1993 SC 2178.
It has also been explained that as a matter of fact the Prospectus issued for the academic year 1996-97, Annexure R-2/2 provided that NRI/NRI sponsored candidates were to pay a fee equivalent to 5000 US Dollars per annum and that the quantum of fee had also been approved by the Director of Technical Education, Haryana, vide Annexure R/2/1, dated 11th June, 1996.
It has further been pleaded that the Institute had started its classes for the academic year 1996-97 in the premises of the DAV Public School, Gurgaon, and a fee of 1500 US Dollars only had been charged for the first year, on account of the delay in the start of the academic session, but as the Institute had shifted to its own site from the academic year 1997-98 onwards, more funds were required to set up the necessary infrastruscture and, as such, the fee had been charged at the rate of 3000 US Dollars, equivalent to Rs. 1,08,000/-.
In para 6 of the reply, it has been highlihgted that though the Institute could charge upto 5000 US Dollars as fee, but a fraction of that fee had been taken for the first year as a concession to the students for that one academic year for the reasons set out just above, and the equivalent of 3000 US Dollars for the subsequent year.
3.
Mr. Arun Jain, appearing for the petitioner has aruged that as the Institute had charged a sum of Rs. 52,500/- which was equivalent to 1500 US Dollars per annum for the academic year 1996-97 there was no justification whatsoever for increasing the amount to 3000 US Dollars equivalent to Rs. 1,08,000/- for the academic year 1997-98.
To my mind, this argument of the learned counsel is untenable.
As per the sanction given by the Director Technical Education, Haryana, Annexure R-2/1, dated 11th June, 1996 and the Prosepectus Annexure R-2/2 NRI candiates were required to pay the equivalent of 5000 US Dollars per annum and merely because the Institue had charged a lesser amount for the first academic year 1996-97 could not clothe the petitioner with a right to insist that she should be charged the same amount for the ensuing academic years.
As a matter of fact it is evident that though the Institute was entitled to charge 5000 US Dollars as per Annexures R-2/1 and R-2/2, they have in fact been charged only the equivalent of 3000 US Dollars, as per the notice Annexure P-1.
In this view of the matter, there is no merit in the writ petition and the same is dismissed.
No costs.
Petition dismissed.
