HS Bedi, J - The petitioner joined the Indian Air Force in the year 1964 during the Emergency.
The Emergency was lifted on January 10, 1968.
The petitioner was discharged from the service of the Air Force in the normal course on April 2, 1973.
While the petitioner was in service, the then Punjab Government issued the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 (hereinafter called the `Rules'), giving certain benefits to those officials, who had been discharged from military service and had taken civil appointments thereafter.
The petitioner was appointed as a Clerk on adhoc basis on the establishment of the Haryana Government Civil Secretariat, Chandigarh on October 10, 1974 and he served in that capacity till February 5, 1976.
He was selected for regular appointment on February 11, 1976.
The Haryana Government issued notification, Annexure P-6, dated August 13, 1979 whereby an amendment was made incorporating Rule 4-A in the Rules wherein it was provided that the benefits of military service towards increment and seniority would be admissible only on first appointment whereas for the purpose of pension the first and the subsequent appointment could also to be taken into account.
Rule 4-A was, however, struck down as constitutional by a Division Bench of this Court in Raj Kumar Verma v. State of Haryana, 1981(3) SLR 436.
The Haryana Government thereafter issued another notification, Annexure P-6/A dated April 27, 1982 providing that the benefit of military service towards increments, mentioned in Clause (i) of Rule 4 would be admissible only on first appointment under the Government.
The petitioner thereafter moved a representation seeking military service benefits qua seniority, increments and pension etc.
This representation was rejected by the respondents vide order, Annexure P-10, dated November 15, 1996 on the ground that the petitioner was entitled to the aforesaid benefits only on the first appointment i.e. his appointment on adhoc basis from October 10, 1974 to February 5, 1976 and not on the second appointment on regular basis from February 11, 1976.
The order, Annexure P-10, dated November 15, 1996, executive instructions, Annexure P-2, dated May 19, 1975 and the notification, Annexure P-6/A dated April 27, 1982 have been impugned by the petitioner in the present proceedings.
2.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have gone through the paper book.
3.
It will be seen that the provisions confining the benefits of military service qua increments and seniority only had been struck down as unconstitutional by a Division Bench of this Court in Raj Kumar Verma's case (supra).
It appears that notwithstanding the aforesaid judgment, the respondent-State of Haryana had issued another notification, Annexure P-6/A, amending the rules yet again and reincorporating the stipulations earlier struck down and continuing the benefits of military service towards increments and seniority only to the first appointment.
It is, therefore, evident that the amendment made by Annexure P-6/A is hit by the same vice, which had led to the judgment in Raj Kumar Verma's case.
We also find that the petitioner's initial appointment was on adhoc basis and the benefits of military service could not have been taken by him at that stage as held by this Court in Devi Dayal v. State of Punjab, 1995(2) RSJ 580.
In this view of the matter, the petitioner's appointment on regular basis w.e.f.
February 11, 1976 was his first appointment for all intents and purposes.
4.
For the foregoing reasons, we allow the writ petition, quash the order, Annexure P-10, the notification, Annexure P-6/A and the executive instructions Annexure P-2.
The petitioner will have his benefits computed within a period of six months from the date that a certified copy of this order is supplied to the respondents.
Petition allowed.
