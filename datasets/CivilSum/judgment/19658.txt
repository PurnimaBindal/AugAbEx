RS Mongia, J - The petitioner joined the department of Information and Public Relations as a Tracer on 1821964 in the erstwhile State of Punjab.
He is a matriculate and has the qualification of Draftsman (Civil).
After being confirmed as a Tracer on 141986, he was promoted on officiating basis as a Draftsman and with effect from 13121983 he was put on probation on the said post.
He was confirmed as a Draftsman vide order dated 31st March, 1970 with effect from 27th of August, 1969.
The next post which a Draftsman can get by way of promotion is the post of Exhibition Assistant, the conditions of service of which are governed by Punjab Public Relations Department (Class III) Non-Gazetted Service Rules, 1958 (in short the Rules).
Rule 9 of the said rules gives the method of recruitment to the post of Exhibition Assistant.
The said rule is reproduced below : (relevant extract) :-  "9(14).
In the case of Exhibition Assistant :-  (i) by selection from among the Assistants, Art Assistants, Draftsmen and Photographers : or  (2) by transfer or on deputation of a person already in the service of the government of a State or of the Union; or  (3) by direct appointment."
2.
The petitioner was promoted as an Exhibition Assistant from the post of Draftsman vide order dated 18th of March, 1974 in the pay scale of Rs. 200-450.
Later on petitioner was confirmed as an Exhibition Assistant vide order dated 3.3.1987 with effect from 111983.
3.
It would be seen from rule 9 of the rules quoted above that Assistants, Art Assistants.
Draftsmen and Photographers are eligible for promotion to the post of Exhibition Assistant.
It has been averred in the petition and admitted by the respondent that when the petitioner was promoted to the post of Exhibition Assistant from the post of Draftsman, the pay scale of Exhibition Assistant was Rs. 200-450 and the pay scale of Draftsman/Assistant was Rs. 170-300/160-400.
The Public Relations Department was upgraded from B class to A class department with effect from 2321977.
On account of upgradation, the pay scales of various posts in the department were upgraded.
The earlier pay scales of Assistants, i.e., Rs. 160-400 was enhanced to Rs. 225-500.
The Public Relations Department had recommended to the Finance Department, vide its letter dated 111982 that the pay scale of the post of Exhibition Assistant be enhanced to Rs. 300-600.
However, no final decision was taken in the matter with the result that the petitioner though holding the post of Exhibition Assistant kept on drawing pay in the pay scale of Rs. 200-450 whereas the Assistants/Draftsmen who were in the lower pay scale earlier started drawing the pay in the pay of Rs. 225-500.
4.
The pay scales were further revised by the 2nd Pay Commission with effect from 111978.
The pay scale of Assistants was further revised to Rs. 600-1120 and the pay scale of the post of Exhibition Assistant was revised to Rs. 570-1080.
It will be relevant to mention here that the post from which the petitioner had been promoted, i.e., Draftsman was also placed in the pay scale of Rs. 570-1080.
It is the case of the petitioner that he kept on representing to the concerned authorities that the pay scale of the post of Exhibition Assistant should be raised to Rs. 300-600 with effect from 2321977 and consequent revision accordingly with effect from 111978.
In a nutshell the case was that the post of Exhibition Assistant was a promotional post from the post of Draftsman/Assistant and had to be given a higher pay scale than the post of Assistant/Draftsman.
5.
It is further the case of the petitioner that though the Public Relations Department had recommended to the Finance Department the pay scale of Rs. 300-600 for the post of Exhibition Assistant yet before any final decision could be taken by the Anomaly Committee, the Second Pay Commission had already recommended the pay scale of Rs. 570-1080 for the post of Exhibition Assistant on the basis of its earlier scale of Rs. 200-450.
In the meantime, the 3rd Pay Commission had been set up which recommended the pay scale of Rs. 1500-2640 for the post of Exhibition Assistant with effect from 111986 which was at par with the scales of the posts of Assistants/Draftsmen.
However, the government gave Rs. 1800-3200 to Assistant/Draftsmen with effect from 111986 by framing Public Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988.
The pay scale of Exhibition Assistant was kept at Rs. 1500-2640.
6.
Further according to the petitioner to add further misery, Punjab government issued a notification on 9591 (Annexure P-9) by which part B of the 2nd schedule of the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, was amended.
The post of Exhibition Assistant was converted into the post of Draftsman in the pay scale of Rs. 1800-3200 with effect from 951991.
The petitioner has filed the present writ petition primarily claiming two reliefs (1) his pay scale cannot be equal or lower than the post of Draftsman inasmuch as the post of Exhibition Assistant is a promotional post and has to be in a higher pay scale.
According to the prayer, he should have given the pay scale of Rs. 300-600 with effect from 2321977 and revised accordingly up to date, (2) that the notification dated 9th of May, 1991, Annexure R-9, amending the part B of the 2nd schedule of the 1988 rules be quashed to the extent it converts the post of Exhibition Assistant to that of Draftsman.
7.
In support for the grant of first prayer, learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that it is not disputed that the post of Exhibition Assistant was and is a higher post than the post of Draftsman/Assistant.
This is obvious from the fact that under Rule 9 of the rules governing the recruitment to the post of Exhibition Assistant, the posts of Assistants/Draftsmen are the feeder posts from which the post of Exhibition Assistant is filled up.
It is also not disputed on the part of respondents that the petitioner was actually promoted from the post of Draftsman to that of Exhibition Assistant in the year 1974.
This being the position, it would fortori follow that the pay scale of the higher post, i.e., Exhibition Assistant has to be higher than the post of Assistant/Draftsmen.
The incumbent of higher post always performs more onerous duties and shoulders higher responsibilities apart from enjoying higher status.
It would follow that the with higher responsibilities and duties must have higher pay scale than the post from which the person is promoted to the higher post.
It is not understood as to on what basis the pay scale of post of Exhibition Assistant was kept lower than the post of Assistant/Draftsman.
According to me, the Public Relations Department had rightly recommended to the Finance Department that the post of Exhibition Assistant be placed in the pay scale of Rs. 300-600 when the pay scale of Assistants was upgraded from Rs. 160-400 to Rs. 225-500.
It is strange that at that stage, the Assistant started getting pay scale of Rs. 225-500 whereas the petitioner who was holding the higher post of Exhibition Assistant kept on getting the pay in the scale of Rs. 200-400.
The Pay Commission seems to have recommended the pay scale of the post of Exhibition Assistant taking its basic pay scale at Rs. 200-450.
Had the pay scale of the post of Exhibition Assistant fixed at Rs. 300-600, the same would have been revised to Rs. 700-1200 with effect from 1178 when the general revision took place and further this pay scale would have been revised to Rs. 2000-3500, as the pay scale of Rs. 700-1200 was ultimately revised to Rs. 2000-3500 with effect from 111986.
However, in the case of the petitioner his pay scale was revised from Rs. 200-450 to Rs. 570-1080 with effect from 111978 (when the Assistants started getting the pay scale of Rs. 600-1120).
Further the pay scale of the petitioner's post, i.e., Exhibition Assistant, was revised from Rs. 570-1080 to 1500-2640 with effect from 111986 whereas the pay scale of Draftsman/Assistant was revised to Rs. 1800-3200 with effect from 111986.
This is a clear anomaly and in fact amounts to violation of the equality clause enshrined in Articles 16 and 14 of the Constitution of India.
The person holding the higher post was getting a lower pay scale than a person holding a lower post.
Consequently I hold that the petitioner was entitled to a higher pay scale than the post of Draftsman/Assistant right from 2321977 when the pay scales of the posts of Draftsman/Assistant were revised.
According to me the recommendations of the department to give pay scale of Rs. 300-600 to the post of Exhibition Assistant as against pay scale of Rs. 225-500 to the Assistant/Draftsman was quite justified.
It is held that the petitioner would be entitled to this pay scale, i.e., Rs. 300-600 w.e.f.
2321977 and to the revision of this pay scale from time to time.
8.
As far as the second point regarding the validity of the notification dated 9th of May, 1991, Annexure R-9 is concerned, the learned counsel for the petitioner contended that the Rules governing the post of Exhibition Assistant, i.e., the Public Relations Department (Class III) Non-Gazetted Service Rules, 1958 have not been amended and still the post of Exhibition Assistant is higher than the post of Assistant/Draftsman from which the post of Exhibition Assistant is still filled in.
The argument proceeded that without amending the basic service rules governing the conditions of service of the post of Exhibition Assistant, the notification dated 9th of May, 1991, which converts the post of Exhibition Assistant to that of Draftsman would be violative of Article 16 of the Constitution of India inasmuch as the higher post under the rules was being equated with the lower post existing in the same set of rules.
Once that is so it would ipso facto follow that the impugned notification to the extent it converts the post of Exhibition Assistant to the post of Draftsman would be violative of Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
9.
The learned counsel for the respondent submits that the impugned notification deals only with the pay scale and for the purposes of pay scale the post of Exhibition Assistant had been converted to the post of Draftsman and, therefore, no fault can be found in the notification.
10.
After hearing learned counsel for the parties on this point, I am of the view that the notification dated 9th of May, 1991, to the extent it converts the post of Exhibition Assistant to that of Draftsman is violative of Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
Admittedly under the service rules the post of Exhibition Assistant "is still higher than the post of Draftsman/Assistant as it is from these posts that the post of Exhibition Assistant is filled up.
That being so, it would be unconstitutional to equate two unequal posts even for the purposes of pay scales.
As has been observed while dealing the first point the higher post which has higher responsibilities and more onerous duties must get more pay than the post from which that post is filled up.
For the foregoing reasons this writ petition is allowed and the petitioner is held entitled to the pay scale of Rs. 300-600 with effect from 2321977 and would also be entitled to the revision of that pay scale from time to time.
Let the arrears of the pay of the petitioner be calculated and paid to him within four months of the receipt of this order.
It is further held that the notification dated 9th of May, 1991, Annexure P5, to the extent it converts the post of Exhibition Assistant to that the Draftsman is violative of Article 16 of the Constitution of India and consequently quashed to that extent.
There will, be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
