THB Chalapathi, J - These writ petitions have been filed by the Divisional Forest Officer, Hissar.
Since common questions of law and fact arise in these two petitions, these are disposed of by this judgment.
2.
The respondents-workman, filed applications before the authority under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 claiming a sum of Rs. 2,26,575/- as arrears of less wages for the period from 111995 to 31121995 in CWP No. 15029/97 and Rs. 3,23,750/- in CWP No. 15011/97.
Both the applications have been decided by the authority ex parte and the authority directed the Government to pay the said amount with penalty.
In CWP No. 15029/97, the authority directed the Forest Department to pay a sum of Rs. 9,06,300/- which includes the penalty for non-payment of remaining wages and in CWP No. 15011/97, the authority directed the Forest Department to pay a sum of Rs. 11,95,000/- to the workers.
These orders have been passed on 3171996 which is under challenge in CWP No. 15029/27 and on 371996 which is under challenge in CWP No. 15011/97.
After coming to know of these orders, the petitioners filed applications before the authority to set aside the ex parte orders.
The order under challenge in CWP No. 15029/97 reads as follows :-  "Arguments of both the parties were heard on 1411997.
In this case, ex parte proceedings were ordered on 561996 and 26.6.1996 while this present misc. application was being filed on 29101996 i.e. after the period of 30 days.
The reasons given by the respondents now applicants is not at all reliable and hence I am hereby dismissing this misc application for setting aside the ex parte proceedings dated 561996, 26.6.1996 and order dated 3171996 as having no merits.
The respondents now applicants are hereby directed to deposit the decretal amount within one month with this authority.
No order as to costs."
3.
The order under challenge in CWP No. 15011/97 reads as follows :-  "Argument of both the parties were heard on 1411997.
In this case ex parte proceedings were ordered on 851996 while this present misc. application was being filed on 391996 i.e. after the period of 30 days.
The reasons given by the respondent now applicants is not at all reliable and hence, I am hereby dismissing this misc. application dated 391996 for restoration of the case as having no merits.
The respondents (now applicants) are directed to deposit the decretal amount with this authority within one month.
No order as to costs."
4.
A reading of the above orders clearly shows that the authority has not applied his mind to the averments made in the application for setting aside the ex parte order and also did not consider the reasons for filing of the applications after the expiry of 30 days.
Under sub-rule (4) of Rule 29 of the Minimum Wages (Central) Rules, 1950, an order passed under sub-rule (2) or sub-rule (3) may be set aside on sufficient cause being shown by the defaulting party within one month of the date of the said order and the application shall then be reheard after service of notice on the opposite party of the date fixed for rehearing in the manner specified in sub-rule (1).
Relying on this rule, the learned Counsel for the respondents argued that since applications for setting aside the ex parte order have been filed beyond 30 days from the date of the order, these are not maintainable.
Section 29 of the Limitation Act, 1963 provides that the provisions of the Limitation Act including section 5 of the same shall apply to any period of limitation prescribed by any special or local law for any suit, appeal or application in so far as, and to the extent they are not expressly excluded by such special or local law.
There is no dispute that the Payment of Minimum Wages Act does not exclude the operation of the provisions of the Limitation Act.
Therefore, in my view, section 5 of the Limitation Act applies to the proceedings before the authority under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.
Since the authority has not applied his mind, I am of the opinion that the orders passed by the authority (Annexure P-2) in both these writ petitions are liable to be set aside.
5.
I accordingly allow these writ petitions and remand the matter to the authority under the Payment of Wages Act to decide the applications made by the petitioners for setting aside the ex parte orders dated 3171996 and 371996 which are impugned in these two petitions and which are the subject- matter of these petitions.
Authority shall decide the applications on merits and pass appropriate orders on the applications made by the petitioners for setting aside the ex parte orders.
I further direct the stay of ex parte orders dated 3171996 and 371996 respectively till the disposal of the applications filed by the petitioners for setting aside the ex parte orders.
However, there will be no order as to costs.
Parties are directed to appear before the authority under the Payment of Wages Act on 161999.
Petition allowed.
