NC Jain, J - The petitioners are JBT teachers.
All of them have improved their qualification either by passing out intermediate or BA (I) examination and consequently they are entitled to higher pay scale in view of the instructions issued by the erstwhile Punjab State on July 23, 1957 which were adopted by Haryana State.
The case of the petitioner is squarely covered by a decision of this Court in 1995(1) RSJ 533 : 1995(1) SCT 711(P&H), Rattan Singh and others v. The State of Haryana and others.
Following the dictum of law laid down in Rattan Singh's case (supra) the writ petition is allowed.
However, if any petitioner has not improved the qualification before March 9, 1990, he would not be entitled to claim higher pay scale in view of the law laid down in Wazir Singh JBT Teacher v. State of Haryana, 1996(1) SCT 431(SC) : JT 1995(7) SC 404.
The petitioners would be entitled to higher pay scale from the date of their acquiring higher qualifications.
With these observations, the writ petition stands disposed of.
