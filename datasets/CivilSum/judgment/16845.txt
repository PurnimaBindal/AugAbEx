Swatanter Kumar, J - Separate replies on behalf of the State and the Corporation have been filed in Court today.
The same are taken on record.
2.
It is stated in the written statements that all the retiral benefits have been paid to the petitioner and even pension has been released.
Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that the same has been done after a lapse of more than 1-1/2 years.
The delay is unreasonable and the petitioner is entitled to interest and costs both.
3.
On the other hand, each of the Departments blames the other for causing delay.
According to the State Government, the documents were not filed by the Municipal Committee well in time, while according to the Municipal Committee, they had sent complete documents to the State Government and the delay in disbursement has resulted with the State Government.
4.
Be that as it may, we do not consider it necessary to go any further into this controversy.
The fact remains that the petitioner has been paid his retiral benefits after 1-1/2 years of his retirement.
In view of the well enunciated law, the petitioner is entitled to interest and costs which we consolidatedly assess at Rs. 10,000/-.
If any claim of the petitioner still remains unpaid, the petitioner is at liberty to approach the respondentss.
The interest and costs shall be paid to the petitioner within one month from today.
Petition allowed.
