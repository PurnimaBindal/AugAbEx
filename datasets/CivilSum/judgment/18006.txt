Amarjeet Chaudhary, J (Oral) - In this writ petition filed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner, who is the widow of Man Singh, Ex-Mate, P.W.D.
(B and R), Department, Sub-Division, Rewari, has prayed for the grant of family pension.
2.
This case was admitted on February 16, 1990.
No return has been filed by the State.
It appears that the State has nothing to say in the matter and as such I accept the contents of the writ petition as true.
3.
The learned Counsel for the petitioner content that this case is squarely covered by a Full Bench decision of this Court in Kasar Chand v. State of Punjab, AIR 1988 P&H 265.
4.
I have gone through the judgment relied upon by the petitioner's Counsel and I am of the view that the matter in the instant writ petition is covered by the above-said judgment, wherein it was held that in computing pension the service rendered by a person in work-charge capacity is also to be taken in account for determining the qualifying service for pension and gratuity.
Accordingly following the aforesaid decision, I am of the firm view that the petitioner being the widow of Man Singh is entitled to pension and gratuity.
5.
Let the pension be calculated four months from today.
However, the petitioner would be entitled to arrears for a period of three years and two months before the filing of the writ petition i.e. 12.2.1990 and not earlier.
The arrears be paid within two months thereafter.
6.
With this order and direction, the writ petition stands disposed of.
Order accordingly.
