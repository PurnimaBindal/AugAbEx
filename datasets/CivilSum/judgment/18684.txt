Civil Writ Petition No. 2012 of 2002  2.
Notice to show cause why the petition be not admitted.
3.
Mr. Rajbir Sehrawat, Deputy Advocate General appearing for the State of Haryana accepts notice.
4.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties at some length.
5.
The challenge in this petition is to an order dated January 15, 2002, Annexure P-3 to the petition vide which the Manager of the Punjab National Bank denied to release the pension to the petitioner on the ground that she had remarried after the death of her husband and was not entitled to family pension.
6.
Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that the claim of the family pension by the petitioner is against the Haryana Government as her husband was an employee in the Transport department.
Furthermore, she has filed an affidavit of herself including the voters list on record to show that she has not remarried since the death of her husband.
At face of it, it appears that the Manager of the Punjab National Bank, Machhrouli, had no competence to pass the impugned order as the order is required to be passed by the State Government if at all and that too in accordance with rules and principles of natural justice.
Secondly, it had to be ascertained by the authority concerned whether the petitioner had actually re-married or not, no proceeding in this regard appears to have been taken.
7.
For the reasons aforestated, we allow this writ petition, quash the impugned order dated January 15, 2002, Annexure P-3, and direct the State Government to pass appropriate order in accordance with law within a period of three months from today.
Petition allowed.
