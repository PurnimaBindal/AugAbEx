Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - The petitioner had joined the respondent-Haryana State Cooperative Supply and Marketing Federation (for short 'the HAFED') as Field Inspector on 151970 and was appointed as Manager 'B' Grade in the pay scale of Rs. 200-500 on 19.10.1970.
He was promoted 'A' Grade Manager in the pay scale of Rs. 800-1600 with effect from 21.7.1982 and District Manager in the pay scale of Rs. 1200-1860 on 2811985.
He completed his probation period and was accordingly granted annual increment on 1391985.
The benefit of military service rendered by him was also given and his deemed date of appointment as Manager 'B' Grade was 1111986.
2.
On 30.5.1988 when the seniority and the date of appointment were refixed, the petitioner's entire service record alongwith the previous A.C.Rs.
was considered and he was found suitable and fit for the post of Senior District Manager at the age of 54 years and 11 months.
However, after 10 months, again his case for retention in the HAFED beyond the age of 55 years was reconsidered and the Administrative Committee in exercise of the powers vested under rule 15(c) of the Haryana State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1988, in its meeting held on 471989 decided to retire the petitioner in pursuance of the decision of that Administrative Committee, from the service of HAFED with immediate effect.
A cheque for Rs. 10,836/- on account of three months pay in lieu of three months notice was also given.
The petitioner through the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India has impugned the order of retirement dated 471989, copy Annexure P-7 to the writ petition.
3.
The challenge to the impugned order is primarily on the ground that the petitioner had earned from Good to Outstanding reports during his service career and was also awarded prize in the shape of HMT watch in the year 1987 for his outstanding performance as District Manager.
To supports his stand that he had earned good reports, the petitioner had placed a summary of last 10 years A.C.Rs.
as Annexure A with the writ petition.
4.
On the other hand, the stand of the HAFED is that the petitioner was censured twice and he had earned one adverse entry in his A.C.R.
for the year 1979-80 which was communicated to him.
As such, it could not be said that the petitioner had earned good reports.
Mr. HS Gill, learned counsel for the HAFED contended that the impugned order had been passed on the ground that the work and conduct of the petitioner was not good and he had not earned 70 per cent good reports during his past service of 10 years.
5.
I have considered the respective stand of the parties.
6.
A perusal of the summary for the last 10 years service record of the petitioner reveals that petitioner had earned one outstanding, two very good, six good and one average reports.
These reports as such, by no stretch of imagination, can be treated as bard or adverse in any manner.
No major penalty was ever imposed on the petitioner.
More so, though there was some average entry recorded in the year 1979-80 in the service record of the petitioner, but subsequently he was promoted Manager 'A' Grade on 21.7.1982 and District Manager on 2811985.
He also completed his probation period successfully and was granted increment.
Even one HMT watch was also given as prize to him in 1987.
Taking into consideration view that once the petitioner was promoted, allowed to complete probation period and earned increment, the so-called adverse entry loses its significance pertaining to 1979-80.
7.
This Court in KK Vaid v. State of Haryana, 1990(1) S.L.R.
1, respondent held that the service career of an employee cannot be cut short in a mechanical manner and he has a right to remain in service till he attains the age of superannuation.
8.
In view of the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed and order dated 471989, copy Annxure P-7 vide which the petitioner was retired, is quashed.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
