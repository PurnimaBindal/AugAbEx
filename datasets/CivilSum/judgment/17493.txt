Sat Pal, J. (Oral) - The petitioners in this case were originally recruited in the P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) of the State of United Punjab on 19th November, 1958 and 24th December, 1960 as Draftsmen and Sectional Officer respectively.
On the reorganisation of the State of Punjab, the petitioners were allocated to the State of Haryana.
2.
In para 3 of the petition, it has been stated that in May, 1972 both the petitioners passed the Associate Member of the Institute of Engineers (AMIE) Examination which is recognised as equivalent to a Degree in Engineering.
The petitioners, therefore, had prayed in this writ petition for a direction to the respondents to consider the claim of the petitioners for promotion with retrospective effect with effect from the date they passed the AMIE Examination or in the alternative, they should be promoted with effect from the date they received orders of their confirmation.
3.
Mr. Sharma, learned Deputy Advocate General Haryana, has produced letter dated 15th April, 1993 addressed by the Engineer-in-Chief, Irrigation Department, Chandigarh, to the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government Haryana, Irrigation and Power Department.
In this letter, it has been stated that as a result of the Supreme Court's decision dated 11111992, both the petitioners, Sarvshri KL Rajpal and AK Kumaria have already been promoted as SDO on regular basis in the Ranking Year 1973 vide Government orders dated 1121992.
It is further stated that Shri KL Rajpal has already been promoted as Executive Engineer.
The photo copy of the letter has been kept on record.
In view of the submissions made by the learned DAG, this writ petition has become infructuous and the same is disposed of as such.
The parties are, however left to bear their own costs.
