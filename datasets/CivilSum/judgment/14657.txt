RS Mongia, J - The admitted facts of the case are that the petitioner had served the Indian Army with effect from 26101954 to 961967.
After his discharge from the Army, the petitioner joined the Transport Department, Haryana as Service Station Incharge on 27.10.1969.
In due course he was promoted as a Foreman with effect from 13.10.1979 and was further promoted as Works Manager, though in the pay scale of Foreman, with effect from 3171968.
2.
Under the Punjab National Emergency Rules, 1965, the petitioner was given the benefit of his military service vide order dated 8121988 (Annexure P-2).
Consequently his assumed date of appointment as Service Station Incharge was given as 1431965 and his assumed date of promotion as Foreman was given as 2471968.
3.
One Sh. Varinder Singh Joon who had also served the Indian Navy, on his discharge, joined the Transport Department, Haryana as Service Station Incharge on 18.6.1971 and was promoted as Foreman on 13.8.1979 By giving him the benefit of military service his assumed dates of appointment as Service Station Incharge and as Foreman were given as 1331967 and 24.10.1969 respectively.
By giving the assumed date to Sh. Varinder Singh Joon he became senior to one Sh. Sultan Singh, Foreman.
Said Sh. Sultan Singh had been promoted as Works Manager with effect from 21101969.
Sh. Varinder Singh Joon claimed that since he had become senior to Sh. Sultan Singh, he should be promoted prior to said Sh. Sultan Singh.
Since this had not been acceded to, Sh. Varinder Singh Joon filed a Civil Writ Petition No. 5974 of 1985 in this Court which was decided in his favour on January 7, 1988 and the respondents were directed to promote him as Works Manager with effect from the date his junior Sh. Sultan Singh had been so promoted.
4.
It is not disputed and rather it is admitted that after giving the benefit of military service to the petitioner, he became senior to Sh. Varinder Singh Joon and Sh. Sultan Singh as Foreman.
His name was placed at Sr. No. 6 in the seniority list of Foreman, whereas the names of Sh. Varinder Singh Joon and Sh. Sultan Singh occurred at Sr. Nos. 10 and 11 respectively.
The petitioner also claims that since he was senior to Sh. Varinder Singh Joon as Foreman, he should also be promoted as Works Manager prior to the date of promotion of Sh. Varinder Singh Joon or in any case with effect from the same date he was so promoted.
This claim was denied to him by the respondents which led him to file the present petition.
5.
As observed earlier, the facts narrated above are not disputed by the respondents.
For denying the promotion to the petitioner as Works Manager, the reason given in the written statement is that under Punjab Transport Department (State Service Class II) Rules, 1963, for appointment as Works Manager, a person must have Diploma in Automobile Engineering from a recognised University or from a Government Institute with at least 5 years experience in Motor Workshop.
Since the petitioner did not have Diploma in Automobile Engineering, he was not entitled for promotion as Works Manager.
6.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that the requirement of having Diploma in Automobile Engineering for the post of Works Manager was introduced by way of amendment in the Service Rules vide notification dated 2861974 and, therefore, for promotion prior to that date such a qualification would not be required.
He further submitted that in Varinder Singh Joon's case (C.W.P.
No. 5974 of 1985 decided on 7.1.1988) it was held that said Sh. Varinder Singh Joon who was to be promoted with effect from 21101969 (the date when his junior was promoted) he did not require to possess the qualification of Diploma in Automobile Engineering.
This qualification was introduced in June, 1974.
He also submitted that neither Sh. Varinder Singh Joon nor Sh. Sultan Singh had these qualifications and they were promoted as Works Manager by relaxation of these qualifications.
The argument proceeded that even if by any stretch of imagination it is held that these qualifications were required, then the petitioner was also entitled for relaxation as the same were relaxed in favour of his juniors.
7.
In view of the judgment of this Court in Varinder Singh Joon's case, the learned counsel for the respondents could not dispute that the qualification of Diploma in Automobile Engineering would not be applicable for promotion to the post of Works Manager prior to 2861974 when such qualifications were introduced by way of amendment.
That being so, the petitioner is entitled to succeed.
Admittedly, the petitioner was senior as Foreman to Sh. Varinder Singh Joon and Sh. Sultan Singh.
Both of them were promoted as Works Manager with effect from 21101969.
The respondents were not legally right to deny the promotion to the petitioner as Works Manager with effect from 21101969 on the ground that he did not have the qualification of Diploma in Automobile Engineering.
Once the juniors were being promoted as Works Manager from a particular date, the petitioner had a right to be considered for promotion with effect from the same date.
As held by this Court in Varinder Singh Joon's case, no relaxation is required for those persons who are to be considered for promotion prior to 2861974.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed and the respondents are directed to consider and promote the petitioner by taking him as duly qualified for the post of Works Manager with effect from 21101969, the date when his junior Sh. Varinder Singh Joon was promoted.
Needless to mention that once the petitioner is promoted as Works Manager from the said date, he would be entitled to all the consequential benefits like arrears of pay etc. etc. Let these directions be carried out within three months of the receipt of a copy of this order.
I make no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
