Punjab Anand Lamp Employees Union v. M/s Punjab Anand Lamp Industry Ltd. and another, 1996(4) RSJ 250 : 1996(3) SCT 56 (P&H).
D.V.
Sehgal, President.
- Th petitioner served a demand notice dated 6111987 Annexure P-5 to raise an industrial dispute.
Conciliation proceedings took place but failed as reported vide report dated 7.9.1988 Annexure P-6.
The Union of India, Ministry of Labour respondent No. 1 declined to make the reference of the dispute to the Industrial Tribunal on the ground that the dispute had been taken up by the workman very late without any proper reason and that he had also been served with a notice for reclassification.
There is no representation on behalf of the Union of India before us.
However, the matter is being contested by the management-Bhakra Beas Management Board and its Chief Engineer, who were impleaded as respondents No. 2 and 3.
Union of India was proceeded against ex parte before the Division Bench vide order dated 2121990.
2.
We consider that the grounds on which the Government declined reference were not within the scope of its consideration.
As regards delay, it requires to be mentioned that the Member Irrigation of the management on 771980 had questioned the placement of the workman in the post of Chargeman Miscellaneous instead of his being posted as Chargeman Mechanical.
So the matter was very much in dispute within the management as such there was no delay on the part of the workman.
As regards the question whether or not the petitioner had accepted the designation given to him, it is a matter which requires to be determined by the Industrial Tribunal.
The workman has vehemently denied the assertion so made by the management.
The matter in this regard is concluded by two judgments of the Hon'ble High Court i.e. Full Bench judgment in ILR 1999(1) Punjab & Haryana 93 and in Punjab Anand Lamp Employees Union v. M/s Punjab Anand Lamp Industry Ltd. and another, 1996(4) RSJ 250 : 1996(3) SCT 56 (P&H); In view of this accepted position of law, we find that the order of the Government of India, Ministry of Labour, dated 2771989 Annexure P-8 cannot be sustained and is therefore, quashed.
3.
The Union of India respondent No. 1 shall now proceed to consider the case and make a reference in accordance with the provisions of Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 within two months.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed of.
