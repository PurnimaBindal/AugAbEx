SS Saron, J - In the writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner who was Sarpanch of Gram Panchayat Shera, Tehsil and District Panipat seeks quashing of the order dated 761999 (Annexure P-15) passed by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government of Haryana, Development and Panchayat Department, Chandigarh (respondent No. 1) whereby the appeal of Sumer Singh (respondent No. 3) against the order dated 2641999 (Annexure P-13) has been accepted and the petitioner was placed under suspension.
2.
The petitioner was elected as Sarpanch of Gram Panchayat, Village Shera in December, 1994.
The Deputy Commissioner, Panipat (respondent No. 2) issued a show cause notice dated 871997 (Annexure P-2) regarding cutting of the eucalyptus trees without any permission from any higher official.
The petitioner was asked to submit his reply within 10 days.
The petitioner filed his reply dated 14.7.1997 (Annexure P-3).
The Block Development and Panchayat Officer, Madlauda addressed the letter dated 3171997 (Annexure P-4) regarding auction of eucalyptus trees.
It was stated that the auction was conducted on 1981996 for Rs. 70,400/- in the presence of the then Block Development and Panchayat Officer, from the said auction Rs. 25,000/- was deposited by the Sarpanch in the Cooperative Bank on 291996 and the balance amount is shown to be spent on Government High School.
The opponents of the petitioner lodged FIR No. 316 dated 5101997 (Annexure P-5) under Section 409 IPC regarding cutting of trees.
The Deputy Commissioner, Panipat (respondent No. 2) issued another show cause notice dated 1041998 (Annexure P-6) indicating that the aforesaid case has been registered against the petitioner under Section 409 IPC which is a case of moral turpitude.
The petitioner was asked as to why action be not initiated against him under Section 51(1)(a) of the Haryana Panchayati Raj, 1994 (Act - for short).
Another show cause notice dated 771998 (Annexure P-7) was issued against the petitioner on the allegation of registration of aforesaid case (FIR) and proposed action being taken under Section 51(1)(a) of the Act.
The petitioner submitted his reply dated 1671998 (Annexure P-8).
The petitioner was suspended by the Deputy Commissioner, Panipat (respondent No. 2) on 2181998 (Annexure P-9).
Besides, in terms of another order of the same date i.e. 2181998 (Annexure P-10) regular inquiry was entrusted to the Sub Divisional Officer (Civil) Panipat.
The petitioner submitted a representation dated 141999 (Annexure P-11) for his reinstatement.
The same was filed on the basis that he had been under suspension for more than seven months and till the said date no departmental inquiry had been initiated against him.
Besides, the police on 211999 had sent a cancellation report in respect of the aforesaid case, FIR No. 316 dated 5101997 to the higher officials.
Accordingly, the Deputy Commissioner, Panipat, in terms of order dated 2641999 (Annexure P-13) ordered the reinstatement of the petitioner as Sarpanch of Gram Panchayat Shera.
Sumer Singh (respondent No. 3) being aggrieved against the said order dated 2641999 (Annexure P-13), reinstating the petitioner as Sarpanch, preferred an appeal before the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana Development and Panchayats, Department Chandigarh (respondent No. 1).
The latter in terms of order dated 761999 (Annexure P-15) accepted the appeal.
The effect of which was that the petitioner was placed under suspension.
The said order dated 761999, as already noticed, is assailed in this petition.
3.
The writ petition was admitted on 18.8.1999 and it was ordered that the operation of order (Annexure P-15) shall remain stayed till the decision of the petition.
4.
It is appropriate to notice that the tenure of the Gram Panchayat as per term of Section 3 of the Act, is five years.
The said Section reads as under :  "3.
Duration of Gram Panchayats, Panchayats, Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads - (1) Every Gram Panchayats, Panchayati Samitis and Zila Parishads, unless sooner dissolved under any law for the time being in force, shall continue for five years from the date appointed for its first meeting and no longer : Provided that all the Gram Panchayats and Panchayat Samitis existing immediately on the commencement of the Constitution (Seventy-Third Amendment) Act, 1992 shall continue till the expiration of their duration, unless sooner dissolved by a resolution passed to that affect by the Assembly."
5.
Shri JP Dhull, learned Assistant Advocate General counsel for respondents No. 1 and 2 states that the period of five years of the Gram Panchayat in which the petitioner was elected in December, 1994 has expired and fresh Panchayat has been constituted.
In view of the said circumstance, the present writ petition, in my opinion, has been rendered infructuous.
Accordingly, the writ petition is dismissed as having become infructuous.
Petition dismissed.
