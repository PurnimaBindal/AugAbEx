GS Singhvi, J - The only issue which arises for adjudication in this appeal, which is directed against the order dated March 9, 1989 passed by the learned Single Judge in C.W.P.
No. 5822 of 1987, Harbilas Singh v. State of Punjab & others', is whether the confidential report of the respondent for the year 1985-86 could be taken into consideration, as directed by the learned Single Judge, for the purpose of his confirmation on the post of Head Constable.
2.
The facts of the case lie in a narrow compass.
The respondent was appointed as Constable in the Punjab Police (District Ropar) on 22.10.1971.
He was promoted as Head Constable on 141977 but was reverted in October, 1977 due to non-availability of vacancy in the cadre of Head Constables.
He was again promoted as Head Constable on 151978.
With effect from 441984, the respondent was placed under suspension.
He was reinstated on 651984.
The enquiry initiated against the respondent under rule 1638 of the Punjab Police Rules was filed by the Superintendent of Police, Ropar on 16.8.1984.
Another enquiry initiated against the petitioner was filed by an order dated 13.3.1985 of the Superintendent of Police, Ropar.
In the years 1985 and 1986, the Superintendent of Police, Ropar considered the cases of various Head Constables including the respondent for the purpose of confirmation.
Vide his orders dated 2221985, 2171985 and 2511986, the Superintendent of Police confirmed some of the Head Constables while others including the respondent were ignored.
The respondent was confirmed as Head Constable w.e.f.
111987 by an order dated 20.6.1987 passed by the Superintendent of Police Ropar.
3.
In the writ petition filed by him, the respondent challenged the orders passed by the Superintendent of Police on the ground of arbitrariness and mala fides.
He alleged that the adverse remarks contained in his confidential report for the year 1984-85 could not have been taken into consideration for the purpose of denial of confirmation in the year 1986.
4.
After considering the arguments advanced on behalf of the parties, learned Single Judge held that the denial of confirmation to the petitioner (respondent herein) may have been justified for the year 1985 but he could not have been ignored on 2511986*** when others were confirmed.
Accordingly, he directed the respondents (appellants herein) to consider the petitioner for confirmation on the basis of record w.e.f.
2511986.
5.
The only contention urged by Shri Rupinder Khosla, learned Deputy Advocate General, Punjab is that the learned Single Judge has erred in observing that the case of the respondent should have been considered for confirmation as on 2511986 on the basis of the annual confidential report for the year 1985-86 because as on 2511986 could not have been made available.
Learned counsel submitted that the annual confidential report of the respondent upto the year 1984-85 only could have been available as on 2511986 and, therefore, learned Single Judge has erred in directing the appellants to take into consideration the annual confidential report for the year 1985-86.
6.
In our opinion, the argument of the learned Deputy Advocate General merits acceptance.
The annual confidential report for the year 1985-86 could not have been drawn up/prepared upto 2511986 i.e. the day on which the Superintendent of Police, Ropar considered the cases of the Head Constables for the purpose of confirmation.
Therefore, as on 2511986 the Superintendent of Police had rightly considered the record of the respondent including the annual confidential report for the year 1984-85 in which the following remarks had been entered :-  "A really bad hand.
A negligent, corrupt and wrong headed policeman."
7.
This, in our opinion, afforded a valid justification not to confirm the respondent w.e.f.
111985 or 171985 or 111986.
For the reasons mentioned above, the appeal is allowed.
The order dated 9.3.1989 is set aside and the writ petition is dismissed with costs of Rs. 2000/-.
