Amar Dutt, J - Major Singh Gill has filed the present petition under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India for release of pension in the rank of Subedar and grant of pension for 100% disability.
2.
The facts necessary for the decision of the writ petition are that Major Singh Gill was enrolled as sepoy in the Corps of Engineers in Army on 481961.
He claims that he was last promoted as Subedar with effect from 141988.
Although at the time of enrolment, he was found to be medically fit with effect from 20.2.1988, he was placed in medical category `BEE permanent' on account of Hyper Tension.
He was operated for Mid-intestinal and Thoracotomy, whereafter his medical category was upgraded as "AYE".
On 1011989 he was again admitted to Command Hospital, Southern Command Pune, from where he was transferred to Military Hospital (CTC) on 17.1.1989.
His disease was diagnosed as Pulmonary Tuberculosis, Essential Hyper Tension, acute intestinal obstruction (OPTV) (56).
The petitioner claims that he had contacted these diseases while in service due to the service conditions and so when he was invalided out of service by the Release Medical Board held on 3181989 after being placed him in medical category "EEE", the disability was assessed at 100%.
He further complains that in the discharge book, he was shown as Naib Subedar instead of Subedar and on inquiry he came to know that this had been done because of his admission in the hospital.
At the time of discharge, he was granted disability element of Rs. 550/- per month at the rate of 34% with effect from 191989 to 3081990.
He was then asked to appear before the Re-survey Medical Board for reassessment of his disability pension and through letter dated 13.10.1990, he was informed that the invaliding disability of Essential Hypertension alone had been accepted at 20% instead of last percentage said to have been accepted at 100% and the disease was admitted to be aggravated by the Military service.
The disability element was reduced from Rs. 550/- to 275/- per month.
In the year 1992, the petitioner was again asked to undergo fresh Re-survey Medical Board and his disability pension was reassessed vide letter dated 2991992.
It was surprising that vide this letter, the invaliding disability of the petitioner was stated to be Pulmonary Tuberculosis with 50% disability.
The disability element was worked out at Rs. 110/- per month with effect from 2081993 to 2781995.
That disability was again reassessed and communicated through letter dated 2291995 at 20% and finally on 911996 the same was assessed by the Re-survey Medical Board as less than 20% i.e. 11 to 14% only.
It appears that the disability element was withdrawn which provides the basis of first grievance of the petitioner.
3.
The second grouse of the petitioner is that while discharging him from service, the rank of the petitioner has been shown as Naib Subedar in place of Subedar, to which rank he was promoted with effect from 141988.
The petitioner's efforts to seek redressaal of these two grievances by serving a notice for demand of justice on 2662000 having not been acceded to, the petitioner has filed this petition.
4.
In the written statement, the respondents assert that there is no infringement of any Rules, which would entitle the petitioner to file this petition, which is also highly belated and suffers from delay and laches.
According to the respondents, the petitioner had joined the army on 481961 and was allotted Fitter Trade.
He was remustered to Clerk (GB) B-4 on 2551963.
Since he was a non-optee for he revised terms of engagement vide Army Headquarter letter No. 16099/Policy/AC-PS-2(c)/2085/3/D (AG) dated 16121976 and he was enrolled prior to 2511965, his terms of engagement entitled the respondents to retire him after 28 years' pensionable service or 55 years of age, whichever is earlier.
The petitioner was promoted to the rank of acting Subedar on 3031988 and was admitted to the hospital on 1011989 and remained hospitalised till 3181989.
Since he had remained in hospital continuously for more than 60 days and was holding paid acting rank of Subedar, according to the Army Instructions 84/68, which were prevalent at the time of retirement, which reads as under :-  "(g) Absence due to sickness.
(i) JCOs/GR who are injured or fall sick not due to their own fault while serving in peace areas and are admitted to a military or recognised civil hospital while on duty, will be treated as on duty vide Rule 61(a) of leave rules for the Services, Vol. I-Army.
They will retain their paid acting ranks or paid lance appointments for a maximum period of two months.
The period of two months will include any period of sick leave granted on discharge from hospital on the recommendation of the competent medical authority under Rule 61(a) ibid which is covered by their entitlement of annual/accumulated annual leave for the current year.
Acting promotion/lance appointments in their place will be permissible from the 29th day of their admission to hospital."
the petitioner had to lose his acting rank of Subedar to the rank of Naib Subedar.
Accordingly, he has been getting service pension at he rate of Rs. 1057/- per month with effect from 191989 for life vide PCDA (P) Allahabad PPO No. S/60740/89 dated 16.1.1990.
Out of this, the petitioner got commuted an amount of Rs. 475/- and residual pension comes to Rs. 582/- pm The petitioner has never challenged the service pension being paid to him of the rank of Naib Subedar for the last as many as 11 years.
According to the respondents, the petitioner was estopped from his own act and conduct and from raising a false plea.
In relation to the second aspect, it was submitted that in view of Regulation 173 of the Pension Regulation for the Army, Part I, 1961, which reads as under :-  "173.
Unless otherwise specifically provided a disability pension may be granted to individual who is invalidated from service on account of disability which is attributable to a aggravated at military service and is assessed at 20% or more."
the petitioner cannot be granted disability pension once the disability is assessed at less than 20%.
The respondents submit that when the petitioner was discharged, the disability pension was granted at the rate of Rs. 550/- p.m. with effect from 191989 to 3081990 for 100% disability.
He was brought before the Re-survey Medical Board on 20890 and was granted continuation award of disability pension at the rate of Rs. 275/- pm with effect from 31.8.1990 to 19.8.1992 for 50% disability.
He was again brought before the Re-survey Medical Board on 28.8.1992 and in view of the disability being assessed at 20%, the disability pension at the rate of Rs. 110/- pm was granted.
The petitioner had then claimed substantial increase and was brought before the Re-survey Medical Board on 4.1.2.1993 but no relief was given to him.
Finally, a Re-survey Medical Board was convened on 3181995 and as his disability was found to be less than 20% for five years with effect from 2881995.
On 192000, the petitioner was again brought before the Re- survey Medical Board, which reassessed the disability at less than 20% for five years and, therefore, disability pension has been rightly discontinued.
The respondents submit that both the claims of the petitioner are without merit and, therefore, have to be rejected.
5.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and have gone through the records of the case.
6.
Although the petitioner, in this case, has been claiming that he was promoted at Subedar, the stand of the respondents is that he was never given the substantive rank and on 3031988 was only given the rank of paid acting Subedar.
This averment, which is contained in the written statement filed by the respondents, is not controverted by the petitioner nor has he cared to bring on record any document, which would indicate that he was ever holding the rank of substantive Subedar.
It is probably on this account that the petitioner has since the year 1989, in which he was retired been accepting the pension, which was calculated on the basis of his pension as a Naib Subedar.
Not only this, he has also not got benefit of commutation of pension and is being paid a sum of Rs. 582/- pm as residual pension.
In view of this, the petitioner's attempt to project himself as an individual entitled to pension of a Subedar either on legal advice or otherwise cannot but be deprecated.
The respondents have, in the written statement given a clear picture of the circumstances, in which his acting rank had to be withdrawn in terms Army Instructions 84/68 on account of his being hospitalised for more than two months.
In view of this, the complaint of the petitioner against the failure of the respondents to grant him pension of Subedar is not only belated but also not sustainable on merits.
7.
The second grouse, on which the petitioner was seeking intervention of this Court, was the withdrawal of disability pension.
The facts and circumstances, in which the withdrawal was made are not in dispute although the petitioner has cleverly tried to project that he has been subjected to grave injustice.
Since 1993, the disability on account of Pulmonary Tuberculosis has been assessed at less than 20%.
Consequently, in view of the provisions of regulation 173 of the 1961 Regulations, no disability pension could have been granted to him after the date on which the Re-survey Medical Board has assessed his disability at less than the required 20%.
The claim of the petitioner to restoration of disability pension cannot, in these circumstances, be accepted as on 192000 the Re-survey Medical Board convened for the purpose has assessed the disability at 11 to 14% for a period of five years.
8.
For the reasons recorded above, this petition is devoid of merit and has to be dismissed with costs on account of the fact that the petitioner has deliberately tried to mislead the Court with a view to get undue advantage.
These costs are assessed at Rs. 2000/-.
Petition dismissed.
