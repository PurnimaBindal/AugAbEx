GS Singhvi, J - This is a petition for quashing order dated 381998 passed by Central Administrative Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench (for short, `the Tribunal') vide which OA No. 585-HR of 1998 filed by the petitioner for directing the respondents to consider her for appointment of Extra Department Stamp Vendor (for short, `EDSV') was rejected.
2.
The petitioner is said to have worked as EDSV for fixed periods in the years 1996, 1997 and 1998 but her case was not considered for regular appointment and the application filed by her under Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 was dismissed only on the ground that she does not possess the prescribed qualification of matriculation.
3.
In the writ petition, it has been averred that the minimum qualification prescribed for the post of `EDSV' is VIIIth standard and the Tribunal has committed a serious illegality by dismissing her application on the ground that she was not qualified.
4.
Shri CM Sharma, learned counsel for respondent Nos. 2 to 6 candidly and fairly admitted that as per extant rules, the minimum qualification for appointment at EDSV is VIIIth standard and not matriculation.
5.
In view of the above, there is no escape for the conclusion that the impugned order of the Tribunal is vitiated by an error of law and calls for interference by this court.
6.
Hence, the writ petition is allowed.
Order Annexure P-8 is quashed and the case is remanded to the Tribunal for decision on merits.
The petitioner is given liberty to seek amendment of the application by impleading that person as party non-applicant who may have been appointed as EDSV after filing of the original Application.
Copy of the order be given dasti.
Petition allowed.
