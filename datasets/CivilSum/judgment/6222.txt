MR Agnihotri, J - The petitioners have challenged the Selection of respondents Nos. 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the post of General Manager, Punjab Roadways, on the ground that the procedure adopted by the Punjab Public Service Commission for selection was unconstitutional, as all the members of the Public Service Commission did not interview the candidates and interview were held only by some of them, which did not amount to selection by the Public Service Commission within the meaning of Article 320 of the Constitution.
The contention is sought to be supported by drawing a parallel to the functions discharged by the High Court under the Constitution, where hon'ble the Supreme Court has made observations that the High Court means the entire High Court and not one Judge alone.
2.
On the other hand, the Punjab Public Service Commission, in their reply to the notice of motion, has placed reliance on Rules 17(a)(i) of the Rules of Procedure of the Punjab Public Service Commission, which provides that 'the Commission may from time to time decide to constitute Board(s) or Committee(s) of one or more members for holding interviews of candidates for recruitment to various posts or for the purpose of any of its other functions.
The composition of these Board(s) or Committee(s) will be decided by the Chairman'.
It has further been pleaded by the respondents that in Union Public Service Commission and State Public Service Commission, the same procedure of constituting either one Member or two Members Board even for selection of higher rank posts is followed.
In any case, the petitioners having already availed of the opportunity of getting their merit decided and having failed to get themselves selected on merit in competition with others, are today estopped from challenging the manner of selection having fought and lost.
3.
Dismissed in limine.
Petition dismissed.
