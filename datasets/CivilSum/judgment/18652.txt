RL Anand, J - Firstly, the contentions were raised by the petitioner's counsel and subsequently the command of the case was taken by the petitioner himself, who addressed some arguments in order to convince us that the proceedings conducted on 912002 were farce in order to side-line him.
We are not convinced with the submission raised by the petitioner and his counsel.
On 912002 when the Selection Board joined for consideration for the post of Colonel, the name of the petitioner was also taken into consideration but his promotion was deferred for the reason that some disciplinary proceedings were pending against him.
During the course of arguments, our attention has been invited under what circumstances, there can be deferment consideration, which is awarded as follows :-  "Is awarded due to some administrative or technical reasons like non- availability of latest ACR, pending statutory complaints with the Govt. lack of availability of report in criteria appointment, finalisation of pending disciplinary proceedings.
On finalisation of the above reasons, a review board is held as per parameters of the board in which originally exposed and graded `D' and if selected the inter-se-seniority is restored.
Subjudice cases in connection with promotions which are pending in civil Courts will not be graded `D' but assessed actually and graded as such."
2.
Since on 912002, the petitioner was under the clouds, therefore, his name was rightly deferred for consideration.
In these circumstances, we do not see any merit in this petition and the same is hereby dismissed.
3.
Before parting with this order, we are of the considered opinion that through this petition, the petitioner has tried to make an effort for the review of the two orders, vide which we disposed of two writ petitions filed by the petitioner on the same cause of action.
Petition dismissed.
