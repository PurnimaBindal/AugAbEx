G Radhakrishna Rao and M. Ranga Reddy, JJ.
- This writ appeal is filed challenging the order of the learned single Judge in WP No. 9692 of 1992 dated 21192, rejecting the contention of the appellant herein that he has to be continued in service till he attains the age of superannuation as per new date of birth.
The appellant joined the service of the respondent- Devasthanams in the year 1959.
The appellant initiated proceedings in the respondent-Devasthanams in the year 1992.
2.
Observing silence for more than thirty years regarding his date of birth and initiating some proceedings with a view to get his services extended, clearly disentitle the appellant for change of date of birth as claimed by him.
The appellants, therefore, estopped from denying the date of birth furnished at the time of joining the service in the year 1959.
We see no merits in the writ appeal.
The writ appeal is, accordingly, dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
