Civil Writ Petition No. 536 of 1999.
3.
Heard.
The petitioner is claiming admission to the 25 seats in the B.Ed.
Course in the University College of Education, Kurukshetra University, Kurukshetra.
The minimum eligibility qualification for admission to the said course is prescribed in Annexure P-5, in the following terms :  "Candidates who have passed (Vishisht) Shastri-3 years duration with at least 50% marks in aggregate and with the subject of English in three parts and one additional subject in addition to the normal papers of Shastri from Kurukshetra University or an examination recognised as equivalent thereto.
Other terms and conditions of eligibility will remain the same."
4.
The petitioner has passed BA and MA with Sanskrit from the Panjab University vide Annexures P-1 and P-2, respectively.
The short question that arises for consideration is whether the said qualification possessed by the petitioner is equivalent to Vishisht Shastri of the Kurukshetra University.
5.
Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondent-University, has produced the book of Equivalence of Examinations published by the Kurukshetra University.
Serial No. 134 pertains to the examinations of the Panjab University, Chandigarh.
Item No. 71 shows that the Panjab University also has an examination known and described as Shastri (3-Years) Parts-I, II & III with the subject of English, which is recognised by the Kurukshetra University as equivalent to its own examination of Shastri (3-years) with the subject of English.
The petitioner does not fulfil this requirement.
Therefore, we find no merit in the writ petition.
It is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
