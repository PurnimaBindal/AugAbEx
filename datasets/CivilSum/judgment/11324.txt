VK Jhanji, J - In this petition, petitioners who are English teachers from the Industrial Training Institute, are seeking a writ in the nature of mandamus, directing the respondents to release the pay scale of Rs. 620-1200/- with a starting salary of Rs. 660/- per month w.e.f.
111978 as provided to the Masters/Mistresses of the Education Department of the Punjab State.
2.
In brief, the facts as set out in the petition are that petitioner No. 1 retired as English teacher from the Industrial Training Institute, whereas petitioners No. 2 and 3 are still in employment.
3.
As regards petitioner No. 4, I find that in the writ petition, there is no averment as to whether he is in employment or has since retired.
No relief has also been claimed on his behalf in the writ petition and, therefore, the writ petition, as far as respondent No. 4 is concerned shall stand dismissed.
Wherever the word "petitioners" occurs, it shall be confined only to petitioners No. 1 to 3.
4.
According to the petitioners, trained graduates i.e. Masters and Mistresses in the Punjab Education Department, have been granted a starting salary of Rs. 660/- per month in revised pay scale of Rs. 620-1200/ w.e.f.
dated 111978 by virtue of letter (kited 1821983 issued by the Punjab State.
The State provided the revised pay scale of Rs. 620-1200/- to the English teachers in Industrial Training Institutes w.e.f.
111978.
However, the State is not releasing the pay scale to the petitioners with a starting salary of Rs. 660/- wef.
111978, and also not granting the consequential benefits as provided to the trained graduates, i.e. Masters and Mistresses.
In the petition, it is averred that Language Instructors (Stenography), including the petitioners, in various industrial Training Institutes/Centres have been granted the same pay scale and allowances by the State, as granted to the Masters and Mistresses in the Punjab Education Department.
Petitioners have further alleged that qualification of Masters and Mistresses is B Ed, and they teach upto Matriculation, whereas the petitioners who are M.A.B.Ed.
teach the students in Industrial Training Institutes who join after passing matriculation.
The petitioners though brought to the notice of the respondents, the anomalies in their pay scales and injustice done to them for not granting the starting salary of Rs. 660/- per month w.e.f.
111978, yet they have not been granted the revised pay scale.
Petitioners earlier filed a writ petition, C.W.P.
No. 1077 of 1987 before this Court, which was decided on 881989, with the following directions :-  "After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, it is directed that the said representation copy annexure P/4 be now considered and decided by the respondent-authority within two months from today by passing a speaking order.
Moreover the petitioners will be given an opportunity of hearing before deciding the matter.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly."
In pursuance of the order in C.W.P.
No. 1077 of 1987, the representation of the petitioner was decided by the Secretary to Government, Punjab, Department of Technical Education and Industrial Training, vide order dated 3.8.1990.
The operative part of the order is as under :-  "As per the above judgment/order of the Hon'ble High Court, the petitioners were given hearing on 25.9.1989 by the Government.
Petitioners at the time of hearing, repeated their claim as already narrated by them in the Civil Writ Petition.
Their representation dated 81185 has now been examined by the competent authority as ordered by the Hon'ble Court.
It has been found that duties of English teachers working in the Industrial Training Department are not, on all fours with those of masters/mistresses of the Education Department and the courses, syllabi etc., are different.
Moreover the pay scales of English teacher should he compared with language teacher of the ITI's on 'equal work, equal pay' principle and not with different Institutions of the other departments.
As such, the claim, of English Teachers (Petitioners) of the Industrial Training Department for giving an initial start Rs. 660/- in the scale of Rs. 620-1200/ with effect from 1178, cannot be considered to be justified."
Petitioners have further stated that vide Memo No. ITEC/166/24/5/600-39-7 dated 1871989 issued by the Director, Technical Education and Industrial Training Department, Punjab, it was recommended that the English teachers working in the ITIs should be given the same grade as the grade to the Masters/Mistresses is given and the English teachers should be given the grade of Rs. 1640-2925/- at the time of appointment and after service of 8 years the grade shall be 1800-3200 and after 18 years' service the grade shall be 2000-3500.
The petitioners, thus, prayed that once vide Memo.
dated 1871989, the Government have equalized the post of English teachers working in the ITI's with the teachers/Masters/Mistresses working in the Education Department, they are entitled to the starting salary of Rs. 660/- per month w.e.f.
111978.
5.
The State of Punjab through the Additional Director (Industrial Training) Directorate of Technical Education and Industrial Training, Punjab, Chandigarh, has filed its written statement, starting therein that prior to 111978, the English teachers were placed in the grade of 220-500.
On the recommendation of the 2nd Pay Commission, the State Government revised the pay scale of this category of employees to Rs. 570-1080, w.e.f.
111978.
Thereafter, in pursuance of the recommendations of the Senior Officers Committee constituted to consider the representations of the government employees with regard to pay anomalies in the revised scales of pay, the scale of pay of English teachers was revised to Rs. 620-1200, w.e.f.
111978 vide Punjab Govt. letter dated 731984.
The respondent, have further admitted that vide Memo.
dated 1871989, the posts of English teachers have been equalized with that of Masters/Mistresses working in the Punjab Education Department.
6.
Learned counsel for the petitioners contended that the respondents have not pointed out any jurisdiction in not granting the same grade to the petitioners as given to the Masters/Mistresses in the Education Department, particularly when the Government itself has equalised both the posts.
In reply, learned counsel for the State contended that qualifications for the appointment of English teachers and that of Masters/Mistresses in the Education Department are different, and their working hours are also different and, thus, the English teachers cannot be given the same pay scales as were given to the Masters/Mistresses.
7.
Having heard the learned counsel at some length, I am of the view that the writ petition deserves to succeed.
It is not disputed that prior to 111978, the pay scale of English teacher was carrying the scale of Rs. 220-500 which was revised to 570-1080/- w.e.f.
111978, and the same grade was given to the Masters/Mistresses in the Punjab Education Department.
On representation made by the Masters/Mistresses, they were given pay scale of Rs. 620-1200/- w.e.f.
111978, but their starting salary was fixed at Rs. 660/- per month in the pay scale of Rs. 620-1200/- vide letter No. 38/11/80-FR(9) 1474 dated 19.2.1983.
This was for the reason that Hindi, Punjabi and Sanskrit teachers in the Education Department were getting higher pay, compared to Masters/Mistresses, and in order to remove this anomaly, the scales of pay of Masters/Mistresses remained at Rs620-1200/- but they were given starting salary of Rs660/-.
A similar representation was made by the English teachers working in the I.T.Ls pointing out the anomalies, which was considered by the Senior Officers Committee constituted by the State Government, and the pay scale of English teachers was revised to Rs620-1200/- w.e.f.
111978 vide Punjab Govt.'s letter dated 731984, meaning thereby that same pay scales were given to them as given to Masters/Mistresses in the Education Department, but they were not given the starting salary of Rs. 660/- per month.
Moreover, vide Memo dated 1871989, the English teachers working in the ITIs have been given same grade, as given to the Masters/Mistresses in the Education Department.
It is, thus, cleat that all times the English teachers have been treated at par with the Masters/Mistresses of the Education Department, and for that matter, their pay scales have always remained the same, except that they were not given starting salary of Rs. 660/- wcf.
1.
11978.
In my view, petitioners who were treated at par with the Masters/Mistresses, cannot be denied the benefit which was given to the Masters/Mistresses, particularly when the State Government itself decided to equate the pay scales of certain posts with that of another posts and, therefore, it is not open to it to discriminate between the incumbents of two posts for the grant of pay scale at the time of revision of pay scales.
Such an action is wholly violative of Article 16 of the Constitution of India, inasmuch as it would he denying the equality of opportunity in the matter of employment.
In the present case, petitioners possessed better qualification, as compared to one possessed by Masters/Mistresses, who for some time were getting a starting salary of Rs. 660/-.
The mere fact that petitioners are working in the ITIs instead of Education Department is no ground to deny them the same pay scale.
Consequently, this writ petition is allowed and a writ of mandamus is issued to the respondent-State of Punjab to release to the petitioners, the revised pay scale of Rs. 620-1200/- with a starting salary of Rs. 660/- per month w.e.f.
111978, within three months from the receipt of copy of this order.
Petitioners shall also be entitled to all the consequential benefits arising therefrom.
The pension of petitioner No. 1 shall be fixed/revised according to the pay he was entitled to at the time he retired.
There shall be no order as to costs.
