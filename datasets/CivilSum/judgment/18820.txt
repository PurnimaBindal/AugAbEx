RL Anand, J - Shri Raj Kumar son of Shri Ved Parkash, petitioner, has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India against the State of Punjab, Director Public Instructions and Deputy Director, respondents No. 1 to 3, respectively, and it has been prayed by the petitioner that a writ in the nature of mandamus be issued against the respondents directing them to give admission to him in the Elementary Teachers Training (E.T.T.)
diploma Course against the vacant seat as the petitioner was most meritorious.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he is a Backward Class candidate.
He falls in Ghumar caste.
He did his graduation and thereafter he did M.A.
in History from Guru Nanak Dev University as a private candidate.
3.
In the year 1997, the petitioner applied for E.T.T.
Diploma course examination but the examinations were cancelled by the respondents.
Finally, the examinations were held in November, 1997.
In the year 1998, the respondent issued prospectus for conducting the E.T.T.
Diploma Course for which examinations were held in January 1998 for admission for the year 1997-1999.
The petitioner applied for the same and appeared in the test of Diploma Course in January, 1998 under Roll Number 228571.
The result was declared on 241998 and the petitioner was placed in the waiting list.
It is the case of the petitioner that there were 100 seats in this Course in District Gurdaspur, out of which 5 per cent seats were reserved for Backward Classes.
In this manner, five seats were supposed to be filled up from the Backward Classes candidates.
Further break up of five seats is that 2 will go to the male category, two will go to the female category and one seat has to go to the person who is the most meritorious irrespective of the sex.
4.
In short, the case set up by the petitioner is that after giving two seats to the male category and two seats to the female category, he is the most meritorious and, the 5th seat of the quota of Backward Classes should go to him.
5.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents.
They filed the written-statement and denied the allegations.
In para No. 7 of the written statement the position has been cleared by the respondents.
In the male category, 2 seats have go to Roll Nos. 228105 and 227118.
These two candidates secured 10625 and 10525 marks, respectively.
So far as female section is concerned, one seat has been given to Roll No. 226637 who secured 10950 marks and the other seat has gone to Roll Number 227610 who got 10250 marks.
6.
The fifth seat, according to the learned counsel for the petitioner, is still lying un-filled and it was to go to the most meritorious candidate whether belonging to male category or female category.
Since the Roll Number of the petitioner is 228571 and he had secured 104 marks ie more than the candidate bearing Roll No. 227610, who, secured 10250 marks, therefore, his claim is better.
It was also submitted on behalf of the learned counsel for the petitioner that the 5th seat is still lying vacant and it has not been given by the respondent-authorities to anybody, therefore, the directions be given to the respondents to give the 5th seat to the petitioner.
7.
Learned counsel, appearing on behalf of the respondents, submits that the petitioner has no case because he is seeking the admission for the course of 1997-99 and at this juncture, directions should not be given to the petitioner.
8.
I have considered the rival contentions of the parties and am of the opinion that there is a merit in the contentions raised by the learned counsel for the petitioner.
The petitioner was placed at waiting list No. 1 in the male category.
He secured 104 marks.
The last candidate in the female category was accommodated when she got 10250 marks.
In these circumstances, the petitioner has a better claim than that of the candidate of waiting list shown at No. 1, of the female Section, because she got 10350 marks.
9.
Resultantly, this petition is allowed and directions are given to the respondents that in the event of the 5th seat lying vacant of 1997-99 Session, that shall be offered to the petitioner within fifteen days from the receipt of the copy of this order.
There shall be no order as to costs.
It is also clarified that if the aforesaid 5th seat has already been filled- up, the petitioner will not get the seat.
Petition allowed.
