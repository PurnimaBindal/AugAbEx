MM Aggarwal, J - The petitioner who had joined service of the Haryana State as Sub-Divisional Engineer, Irrigation Department on 781970, was given current duty charge of the post of Superintending Engineer on 19121996 and he joined as such on 20121996.
2.
The grievance of the petitioner is that he was not considered for promotion as Superintending Engineer with effect from the date i.e. 19121996 when current duty charge was given to him or his juniors had been promoted.
He made representation and issued demand notice.
Then, a final seniority list was circulated and person junior to the petitioner had been promoted w.e.f.
911996.
Petitioner had, however, retired from service on 31101999.
He is entitled to be promoted as Superintending Engineer w.e.f.
911996 the date his juniors were promoted.
He was otherwise also entitled to all salary and allowances w.e.f.
20121996 i.e. the date on which he was given current duty charge.
The conditions that he will work in his own pay scale while giving him current duty charge of Superintending Engineer were liable to be quashed.
3.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the State, the facts had been admitted.
However, it was averred that petitioner had already retired from service on 31101999; that it had been made specific in the order that he was being given current duty charge and will work in his own pay sale; that even if the person shown junior to the petitioner in the final seniority list had been promoted but they were of different cadre and not that of the petitioner and petitioner could not draw any benefit due to that.
4.
Annexure P-1 is order dated 18121996 whereby petitioner and five other persons who were Executive Engineers of PWD (IB) Branch had been given current duty charge of the post of Superintending Engineer in their own pay scale without conferring any right to claim seniority or other benefits of the current duty charge post; Annexure P-5 is order of the High Court dated 1682000 in Civil Writ Petition No. 18642 of 1998 whereby the Court had ordered that case of the petitioner shall be considered for promotion as Superintending Engineer from the date when the current duty charge was given to him of this post and appropriate order will be made within three months; Annexure P-6 is order dated 2222001 of the respondent whereby representation of the petitioner for promotion on regular basis w.e.f.
20121996 i.e. current duty charge date, had been rejected.
5.
However, a final seniority list had been circulated on 3072001 vide Annexure P-8 wherein the petitioner is shown at Serial No. 28 and some persons who are shown at Serial Nos. 32, 104, 112, 120, 136 and 144 are stated to have been promoted as Superintending Engineers with effect from 911996 vide Annexure P-9.
6.
Representations of the petitioner had been considered and rejected vide order dated 2222001 Annexure P-6.
However, final seniority list had been circulated after that date on 3072001 vide Annexure P-8.
It is the admitted position that persons shown junior in this list had been promoted w.e.f.
911996.
Since petitioner had retired from service, he was not considered.
7.
Due to the changed circumstances, petitioner is entitled to be considered for promotion as Superintending Engineer w.e.f.
911996 the date on which his juniors have been promoted.
8.
It is the admitted position that petitioner had worked on the post of Superintending Engineer from 20121996 till the date of his retirement.
Reliance has been placed on a Division Bench judgment of this Court in C.W.P.
No. 10154 of 2000 (Dr. HS Dahiya v. State of Haryana) decided on l7th July, 2002.
The petitioner in that case had also been given current duty charge and then he retired while holding that charge.
In that case, while relying upon another Division Bench judgment of this Court in C.W.P.
No. 10152 of 2000 (Balbir Singh Dalai & others v. State of Haryana and another) decided on 952002, [2002(8) SLR 462 (P&H)], the Court had held that petitioner would be entitled to salary and allowance of the post on which he had worked as current duty charge.
9.
In the facts and circumstances of this case, the writ petition is allowed.
It is directed that petitioner shall be considered for promotion as Superintending Engineer with from the date i.e. 911996 when his juniors were promoted.
If he is not found fit for promotion on that date, then he shall be given salary and allowance as payable on the post of Superintending Engineer from 20121996 on wards till the date of retirement.
His pay and pension shall be fixed accordingly.
The needful shall be done within a period of six months.
Petition allowed.
