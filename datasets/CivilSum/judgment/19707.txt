RS Mongia, J - After hearing the counsel for the parties, the writ petitioner is directed to pay to the respondent-workman (applicant) the current wages as are being paid to similarly situated persons by notionally taking into account that the petitioner's services were never terminated.
In other words, the wages are to be calculated as if the respondent remained in service throughout.
The arrears of wages shall be paid along with the wages for the month of June, 1992.
It is represented by the learned counsel for the writ petitioner that in fact payment of Rs. 20,048/- has already been made to the respondent (workman).
However, the learned counsel for the respondent says that payment was made in the name of Labour-cum-conciliation Officer.
If that is so, the respondent-workman would be at liberty to move an appropriate application before the Labour-cum-Conciliation Officer, for withdrawing the said amount.
The petitioner would be entitled to deduct that amount from the arrears of wages which is said to have already been paid to the respondent-workman.
With these observations, this Civil Misc. stands disposed of.
Order accordingly.
