Amarjeet Chaudhary, J (Oral)- The petitioners, who are working as Lecturers in pharmacy under respondent No. 2, in various institutions in the State of Haryana, have filed the writ petition seeking a mandamus directing the respondents not to terminate their services till the regular appointments are made on the recommendation of the Haryana Public Service Commission,  2.
The petitioners have also filed Civil Misc.
Application No. 5331-A of 1991 seeking a direction to the respondents not to terminate their services during the pendency of the writ petition.
3.
In view of the order passed in Civil Writ Petition No. 10582 of 1991 in which the Court had directed to maintain status quo regarding service of the petitioner, notice of the CM was also issued to the State.
4.
At the outset of the hearing, the learned DAG Haryana contends that this writ petition is to be dismissed in view of the order passed in Civil Writ Petition No. 10582 of 1991 Satya Pal Shani v. State of Haryana & others, dated 4101991 in which no relief to the petitioner has been granted.
The learned counsel has also produced a copy of the order of the Division Bench passed in the aforesaid case.
5.
The learned petitioners counsel has not been able to repel the contention of the learned DAG Haryana.
6.
I have myself perused the judgment and find that the matter is squarely covered by the decision rendered in Satya Pal Shani's case (supra).
Therefore, I have no option, but to issue the same direction as given in that case, which reads as under :-  "There is no need for retaining the petitioners in service as the classes and courses are over and if in the near future, some posts are made available and necessity for appointment arises, the petitioner shall be considered for appointment on priority basis."
7.
The writ petition as well as the CM stand disposed of in the terms, indicated above.
Order accordingly.
