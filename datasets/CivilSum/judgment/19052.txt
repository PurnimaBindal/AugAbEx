Syed Bashir-ud-Din, J - Heard.
2.
In the petition, Abdul Rashid Wani, Jr. Stenographer in the office of Deputy Commissioner, Anantnag has prayed for mandamus against the respondents to compel them to depute petitioner to Saudi Arabia on deputation alongwith other Staff in connection with the impending Haj Pilgrimage of Hajis to Saudi Arabia, on the ground that his name has been included in the list forwarded by the office of Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir (Convenor State Haj Committee, Srinagar) to Commissioner-cum-Secretary to Government, Revenue Deptt.
Jammu, Annexure-A, and that at the last moment he has some information that he is being dropped as a member of the Staff proceeding on deputation to Saudi Arabia.
3.
Annexure-A, shows that, it is just a letter addressed from the office of Convenor State Haj Committee, (Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir) to Commissioner-Secretary to Government, Haj and Auqaf Department, Jammu, forwarding the application forms of various medical and para-medical staff to be sent to Saudi Arabia, including that of petitioner on deputation.
4.
It nowhere shows that petitioner's name for deputation as part of the Staff deputed to Saudia Arabia in connection with impending Haj Pilgrimage is approved.
There is no document(s) on record to show that his name has been included in the staff.
There is also no document(s) on record to show that his name, if at all included, is deleted or is being deleted.
This apart, merely proposing the petitioner for being deputed as part of the staff to Saudi Arabia in connection with Haj Pilgrimage or deletion or exclusion of his name from the said deputed staff does not violate any legal, service or other right(s) of the petitioner.
5.
For the aforesaid reasons, the Writ petition is dismissed at the threshold alongwith connected C.M.Ps.
Petition dismissed.
