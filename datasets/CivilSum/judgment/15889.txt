NK Agrawal, J - The prayer of the petitioners herein is to refix their seniority on the promotional post vis-a-vis respondents 4 and 5 on the basis of decisions rendered by the Supreme Court in Union of India v. Virpal Singh, JT 1995(7) SC 231 : 1995(4) S.C.T.
695 and Ajit Singh Janjua and others v. State of Punjab and others, JT 1996(2) SC 727 : 1996(2) SCT 278.
In reply filed by the official respondents it has been stated that steps are being taken to implement these two judgments and final orders shall be passed in the matter as early as possible.
It has also been stated during the course of arguments that instructions in the light of the judgment referred to above have already been issued by the Government.
These instructions were issued on 10th of January, 1997.
2.
In view of the reply filed by the official respondents, this petition has become infructuous and is dismissed as such.
It is, however, directed that seniority between the petitioners and private respondents 4 and 5 shall be refixed in accordance with the judgments and instructions referred to above within two months from today.
