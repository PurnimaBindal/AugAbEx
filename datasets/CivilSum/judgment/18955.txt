DV Sehgal, President - The petitioner was Chief Medical Officer in the services of the State of Punjab and got voluntary retirement on 3-1-1997.
He was not released his retiral benefits and he filed the present writ petition on the basis of un-revised scale of pay.
He was released leave encashment partly on 1-5-1998.
Similarly, he was paid arrears of pension on un-revised scale on 31-7-1998.
He was released GP Fund amount in August, 1998.
He was also paid commuted amount on pension on 31-7-1998 and DCR gratuity on 27-8- 1998.
On revision of scale of pay w.e.f.
1-1-1996, revision of pension, commuted amount of pension, DCR gratuity etc. are yet to take place.
2.
We are of the considered view that all the retiral benefits on the unrevised scale of pay should have been released to the petitioner within a period of 6 months of his retirement.
It has not been done so.
Therefore, he is entitled to the interest at the rate of 12% per annum w.e.f.
1-8-1997 till the date of actual payment of the amount on the basis of un-revised scale of pay.
However, payment of difference retiral benefits on revision of scale of pay (w.e.f.
1-1-1996) have either been paid or are still payable.
We direct that payment on revised scale of pay should be made within a period of 3 months from today, failing which the amount so due on revised scale of pay shall carry interest at the rate of 18% w.e.f.
1-4-2000.
3.
It is further brought to our notice that the petitioner so far has not been paid the amount or GIS and amount due on account of sanction of proficiency increment.
We direct that proficiency increment due to the petitioner should be sanctioned and the amount of GIS and arrears of said increment worked out and paid to the petitioner within a period of 3 months from today, failing which this amount shall also carry interest at the rate of 18% per annum w.e.f.
1-4-2000.
4.
This direction shall constitute a decree under section 21 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 and shall be meticulously complied with.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
5.
Copy of this order be supplied within seven days.
Petition allowed.
