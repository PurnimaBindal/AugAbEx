Viney Mittal, J (Oral) - With the consent of learned counsel for the parties, the main appeal itself is taken up on board for final decision.
2.
Learned counsel appearing for the appellant-Insurance Company has challenged the awarding of interest by the Commissioner under the Workmen's Compensation Act relying upon a judgment of the Apex Court in PJ Narayan v. Union of India and others, 2004(1) P.L.R.
3.
On the basis of the aforesaid judgment, it has been contended that the Insurance Company was not liable to pay the interest.
However, from the perusal of the aforesaid judgment, I find that the said judgment is not applicable to the facts and circumstances of the present case at all.
In PJ Narayan's case (supra), the challenge made by the insured was with regard to exclusion of a clause in the insurance policy by the Company that the Insurance Company would not be liable to pay the interest.
The said contention raised on behalf of the insured was rejected by the Apex Court on the ground that the insurance was a matter of contract between the insurance Company and the insured and it is open to the insurance company to refuse to insure.
In the present case there is no stipulation in the insurance policy, that the interest was not to be paid.
3.
In view of the fact that no such exclusion with regard to the interest clause had been made in the insurance policy, the judgment in the PJ Narayan's case (supra), is not applicable.
No other point has been raised by the learned counsel for the appellant.
4.
As a consequence of the above, I do not find any merit in the present appeal.
The same is dismissed.
Appeal dismissed.
