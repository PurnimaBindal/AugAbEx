Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - This appeal has been filed against the order of the Commissioner, Workmen Compensation Act, Bhiwani, dated 13.8.1992 which on an application had awarded a sum of Rs. 54,764/- as compensation with interest at the rate of 6% per annum.
2.
Dissatisfied with the order, applicant Raj Kumar had filed the present appeal who died during the pendency of the appeal and his legal representatives were brought on record.
3.
The order had been assailed on the ground that the Commissioner, under the Act has erred in law in not imposing the penalty to the extent of 50% of the award, as envisaged under Section 4-A(3) of the Act.
4.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the paper book.
5.
The authority under the Workmen Compensation Act had returned a finding that the accident had occurred during the course of employment of the applicant-Raj Kumar and the accident was fully in the knowledge of the respondents but the respondents did not deposit any amount of compensation on account of injury.
The Management was bound to deposit the compensation with the Commissioner under Section 4-A(2) of the Act.
Once the Court reaches the conclusion that the respondents lingered on the matter without any reason, employer is liable to pay penalty to the applicant and interest thereon.
6.
In this view of the matter, the appeal is allowed and the award of the Commissioner, Workmen Compensation Act, is modified accordingly.
The appellants are held entitled to the penalty to the extent of 50% of the award with interest at the rate of 12% per annum from 3.2.1991 till its realisation.
Appeal allowed.
