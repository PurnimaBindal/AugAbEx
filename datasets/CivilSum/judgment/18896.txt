RL Anand, J. (Oral) - This writ petition has been filed by Surinder Kumar Rana against the State of Haryana under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India and he has claimed medical reimbursement to the tune of Rs. 11,026/- qua his wife Smt. Mrignayani.
2.
Smt. Mrignayani was a patient of cancer.
She was referred to PGI, Chandigarh for treatment on 1591998 and she remained admitted in the PGI w.e.f.
1591998 to 22.9.1998.
Subsequently, she died on 941999.
When the patient was admitted in PGI, four tests were conducted upon her i.e. CEA, CT Scan, Bone Scan and Mamography.
Tests No. 3 and 4 i.e.
Bone Scan and Mamography were conducted in Apollo Hospital, New Delhi and CEA and CT Scan were done from the open market.
According to the petitioner, he spent a sum of Rs. 11,026/- upon these tests and he is entitled to reimbursement.
3.
On the contrary, the stand of the State of Haryana is that the petitioner is only entitled to the medical reimbursement as per the rates as applicable in the PGI In this manner, according to the respondents, the petitioner is only entitled to a sum of Rs. 3,350/- as against the demanded amount of Rs. 11,026/-.
4.
The learned counsel for the petitioner has invited my attention to certificate Annexure P4, issued on 3111998 by the PGI, Chandigarh, in which it has been certified that CEA test is not done in the PGI due to the non-availability of reagents.
The learned counsel further submitted that as per the policy decision of the Haryana Government dated 1751984 the actual cost of CT Scan, if prescribed by an Authorised Medical Attendant, is reimbursable to the Government servants and their dependents.
5.
On the other hand, learned counsel for the respondents submitted that the certificate Annexure P4 is dated 3111998 and has been issued after the medical treatment.
6.
I do not subscribe to the argument raised by the learned counsel for the respondents for the simple reason that even on 3111998 CEA test was not being done in the PGI.
In these circumstances, it can be reasonably inferred that in the month of September, 1998 this test was also not available in the PGI In these circumstances, the petitioner is entitled to the full amount of reimbursement with regard to CEA test.
He is also entitled to the full amount of CT Scan as per the policy of the Haryana Government.
So far as the medical reimbursement vis-a-vis Bone Scan and Mamography is concerned, there is no such certificate either issued by the PGI or there is any policy to that effect.
In these circumstances, the petitioner will be entitled to the medical reimbursement qua tests No. 3 and 4 only to the extent of Rs. 400/- and 250/- respectively, as agitated by the respondents.
The net result is, the writ petition is partly allowed.
The petitioner will get actual medical reimbursement vis-a-vis tests No. 1 and 2, but with regard to tests No. 3 and 4 he will get the medical reimbursement at the rate of Rs. 400/- and Rs. 250/- respectively.
The above medical reimbursement shall be paid to the petitioner within one month from the receipt of the certified copy of the order.
Petition partly allowed.
