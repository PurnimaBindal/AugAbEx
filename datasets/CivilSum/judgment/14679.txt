Radhakrishan Rao, J - The petitioner, who worked as Superintendent of the Court of Special Judge, Economic Offences, Hyderabad, seeks a direction to the respondents to sanction 'additional charge allowance' from 1.7.1986 to 15.5.1987 in accordance with the recommendations of the Pay Revision Commissioner, on the ground that after promotion to the post of Superintendent he was in-charge of the post of Head-Clerk of the same Court for the above said period.
2.
No doubt the Pay Revision Commissioner in his report at Page No. 55 of Volume I recommended that any one holding additional charge of a post whether of equal, higher or lower responsibilities, should be paid additional charge allowance.
In this case no order has been given to the petitioner placing him in the additional charge of the post of Head Clerk.
Unless and until a written order has been passed by placing a person in-charge of a post and he discharges such functions in pursuance of the order, he is not entitled for the additional charge allowance.
The mere fact that the post was kept vacant and no other person was posted cannot entitle him to the additional charge allowance.
In the absence of any order from the competent authority placing him in full additional charge of the post of Head Clerk of the same Court and he discharges the functions in pursuance of the any order, he cannot be said to be entitled to the additional charge allowance.
3.
The writ petition is accordingly dismissed.
No costs.
Advocate's fee Rs. 250/-.
Petition dismissed.
