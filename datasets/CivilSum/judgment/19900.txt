RS Mongia, J - The petitioner, who is a Patwari, was working as such in village Sehsola, Tehsil Nuh, District Gurgaon (Haryana) and on attaining the age of 55 years, he was prematurely retired by the Government by giving him three months' notice vide order dated 10th November, 1989, (Annexure P-1 to this Petition).
The petitioner impugns the said order mainly on the grounds that during his whole service career he had never been charge-sheeted, no enquiry of any kind ins pending against him and petitioner had never been conveyed any adverse confidential report.
2.
Written statement has been filed on behalf of the respondent, wherein the above assertions regarding the service record of the petitioner have been denied.
It has averred that in fact there was an enquiry against the petitioner on the basis of charge-sheet, copy of which has been attached as Annexure R-2 with the written statement and it was as a result of that enquiry that an adverse entry was made in the character roll of the petitioner for the year 1981-82 which when translated into English reads "Reputation for honesty is not good".
It has been further averred the Annual Confidential Report for the year 1985-86 was average which was communicated to the petitioner on 23rd June, 1986.
Similarly, for the year 1986-87, the over-all assessment was average and as far as honesty was concerned it was also recorded as average.
Same is the position regarding the A.C.R.
for the year 1988-89.
The last two A.C.Rs.
were duly conveyed to the petitioner vide communication dated 6th October, 1987 and 16th August, 1989, respectively.
(This factual position has been taken from Annexure R-6 with the written statement).
It has been further mentioned in the written statement that no representation was made against these adverse remarks.
3.
To appreciate the contentions of the learned counsel for the petitioner, it would be appropriate to reproduce the extract of the character roll of the petitioner for the last 10 years before passing the impugned order :-  "Year Overall assessment Remarks 1979-80 Good ........ 1980-81 Good ........ 1981-82 Good Integrity not good.
Communicated vide No. 4846/SK/BC, dated 3101985.
1982-83 Average Communicated vide No. 5078/SK/BC, dated 2891983.
1983-84 Good ...... 1984-85 Good ...... 1985-86 Average Communicated vide No. 2897/SK/BC, dated 2361986.
1986-87 Average (Hoensty Average) Communicated vide No. 6134/SK/BC, date 6101987.
1987-88 Good ......... 1988-89 Average (Honesty Average) Communicated vide No. 4203/SK/BC, dated 1681989."
4.
The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that it has been admitted by the respondent that adverse entry for the year 1981-82 was based on the enquiry report, copy of which has been annexed as Annexure R-2/A.
This enquiry had been initiated on a complaint made by one Shri Mehmood Khan, son of Malhar that the petitioner had taken Rs. 10,000/- from him with a view to arrange certain lands but he did not do so and that the petitioner had returned Rs. 5,000/- back to him and when rest of the money was demanded back, the petitioner had refused to pay the same.
Thereupon Panchayat was convened where the petitioner had promised to pay back the amount, but still no payment had been made to the complaint, but later on the matter was settled by a compromise to pay Rs. 3,000/- to the complainant.
The charge was that according to Government instructions, the petitioner could not enter into bargain of land without permission of the Government and as such he had contravened the instructions.
The Sub-Divisional Officer, who held that enquiry, vide his report dated 15th April, 1982 held that Shri Mehmood Khan Patwari had accepted the gratification of Rs. 10,000/- from the complainant and since it had been admitted before him that the petitioner had compromised by paying Rs. 3,000/-, it proves dishonest intention of the Patwari and a disciplinary action was proposed against the petitioner.
There is nothing on the record to show that any disciplinary action was taken against the petitioner.
From the perusal of the complaint and the enquiry report, I am of the considered view that there was no specific charge of bribe against the petitioner.
As stated above, the charge was that he had violated Government instructions by entering into a bargain without the permission of the Government.
I am afraid on these basis adverse remarks could not have been entered.
Since in this case the respondent itself had placed on record the documents on the basis of which adverse remarks were entered for the year 1981-82, I have gone into this fact.
It may also be observed that the immediate Reporting Officer of the petitioner had mentioned against the column of 'integrity' as 'good'.
It is only the Sub-Divisional Officer who had recorded the adverse remarks, already quoted above.
In spite of these, the overall assessment was 'good'.
5.
As far as other remarks for the year 1986-87 and 1988-89 are concerned, the honesty has been mentioned just as 'average', which does not mean that the integrity was doubtful.
6.
From the perusal of the Annual Confidential Reports for the last 10 years, it is quite evident that the petitioner was not a deadwood which had to be chopped off.
In other words, the petitioner was good enough to continue in the service.
In KK Vaid v. State of Haryana, 1990(1) SLR 1, the instructions of the Government that 70% of the reports for the last 10 years are to be good or above for retaining a person beyond 50 years, have already been struck down.
That being the position, I am of the considered view that the impugned order of premature retirement has not been passed with due application of mind and is not in public interest.
7.
For the foregoing reasons, this writ petition is allowed and the impugned order Annexure P-1, dated 30th November, 1989, retiring the petitioner prematurely is quashed.
The petitioner would be deemed to be in service from the date he was prematurely retired, with all consequential benefits.
However, the parties are left to bear their own costs.
Petition allowed.
