RS Mongia, J - The petitioners, who are working as teachers in the privately managed Government aided Schools claim that on acquiring higher qualifications, they should be given higher pay scale in accordance with Government instructions dated July 23, 1957, issued by the erstwhile State of Punjab, which inter alia provided that the teachers on acquiring higher qualifications would be entitled to higher pay scale.
The petitioners, though worked in privately managed Government aided Schools claims parity with the teachers working in Government Schools.
2.
Learned counsel for the petitioners cites State of Haryana and others v. Rajpal Sharma and others, 1996(4) SCT 75(SC) : JT 1996(6) SC 710 in support of his contention that even teachers working in privately managed Government aided Schools could be given the higher pay scale on acquiring higher qualifications as is done in the case of the teachers working in Government Schools provided they have acquired higher qualifications prior to March 9, 1990.
Admittedly, in the present case, all the petitioners acquired higher qualifications prior to the said date.
3.
Learned counsel for the respondent fairly concedes that the ratio of the aforesaid decision of the Apex Court shall apply to case of the petitioners.
Consequently, we allow this writ petition and direct the respondents to release the higher pay scale to the petitioners with effect from the date when they acquired higher qualifications.
However, the arrears so calculated shall be confined to 3 years and 2 months prior to the filing of the present writ petition.
This writ petition was filed on 281995.
This direction shall be carried out by the respondents within three months from receipt of a copy of this order from this Court or a certified copy thereof from the petitioners.
Petition allowed.
