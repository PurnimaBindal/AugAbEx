Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - Notice of motion in this petition was issued by the Division Bench of this Court on 811999.
Despite numerous opportunities, no reply has been filed on behalf of the State.
Learned Counsel appearing for the State submits that he has written to respondent No. 3, Divisional Forest Officer, Social Forestry, Panipat District, Panipat on different occasions but no response has been received from that office.
2.
The petitioner in this petition prays that he was employed with the State of Haryana as a Mali and subsequently he prayed to the respondents that he be promoted to the post of Forest Guard against 20% quota meant for Class IV employees as his juniors were already promoted.
It is very difficult for this Court to decide this matter on merits in the absence of a written statement of the Government, as determination of two above contentions is primarily based on the records of the respondents.
We, in unequivocal terms, deprecate the practice of the Government departments in not furnishing requisite information and records to the Law Officers of the State.
This not only amounts to unnecessary adjournments but also waste the time of Court and prolong the proceedings unnecessarily before the Court.
Written statement has not been filed despite seven adjournments before the Court.
We are constrained to impose costs upon the State as respondent No. 3 is personally responsible for this avoidable delay, the costs imposed by the Court are directed to be recovered from him salary and/or the officers who are responsible for this delay.
3.
In the circumstances aforesaid, we dispose of this petition with the direction to the respondents to treat this petition as a representation and pass appropriate orders within a period of three months from today.
The petitioner would be entitled to the costs of Rs. 2,000/-.
The costs shall be recovered from the salary of the officers, as afore-noticed.
Order accordingly.
