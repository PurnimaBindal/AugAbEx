Bakhshish Kaur, J - Kashmiri Lal Sharma-petitioner by way of filing an application under Section 33-C(2) read with Section 20 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (in short `the Act') claimed Rs. 7,31,652.35 as arrears of revised pension, compensation at the rate of Rs. 200/- per day from 2791984 till date being pecuniary loss sustained by him and exemplary cost of the proceedings amounting to Rs. 55,000/-.
2.
The petitioner who had been in the service of Union of India had retired on 151979 on attaining the age of superannuation.
His application under Section 33-C(2) of the Act was dismissed by the Presiding Officer Labour Court, Jalandhar.
Aggrieved by that order, he has invoked the extra-ordinary provisions of this Court by filing a writ Articles 226/227 of the Constitution for quashing the order Annexure P-1.
3.
It is an admitted fact that an award was passed by the Presiding Officer, Central Government Industrial Tribunal on 2791984 in favour of the petitioner.
His reversion order was set aside.
He was held entitled to arrears of salary as well as promotional benefits.
The petitioner by way of filing an application under Section 33-C(2) of the Act therefore, prayed for the execution of the award by claiming the amounts specified therein.
4.
It is admitted fact that arrears of pension, gratuity etc. have not been paid to the petitioner.
This petition, therefore, can be disposed of with a direction to respondents No. 2 and 3 to pay the benefits, i.e., pension, gratuity etc. as the petitioner is entitled to recover the same.
5.
The impugned order is, therefore, set aside.
The writ petition is allowed with a direction to respondents No. 2 and 3 to pay the arrears of pension, gratuity and other retiral benefits on the last pay drawn by the petitioner at the rate of 12% per annum from the date it became due till the date of its realisation, within a period of three months.
Petition allowed.
