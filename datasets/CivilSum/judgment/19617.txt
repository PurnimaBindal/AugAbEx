Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - In this petition, a prayer has been made for the issue of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to permit the petitioner to take the annual examination of BAMS Part I By an interim direction issued by the Motion Bench on November 25, 1992, the petitioner was allowed to take the examination.
2.
The only prayer made now is that the University should be directed to declare the petitioner's result.
3.
Respondent No. 2 had considered the petitioner as ineligible to take the examination only on account of the fact that the petitioner had not passed two papers of the Pre-Ayurveda Course in the examination held in March, 1992 as also in the Supplementary examination held in September 1992.
It has, however, been conceded before me that the petitioner has since passed these two papers on April 26, 1993.
Once that has happened, the petitioner's case is squarely covered by the provision of Regulation 14 reproduced in paragraph 3 of the petition whereunder it is inter alia provided that "the result of such a candidate for the higher class shall be held in abeyance till he clears the compartment/reappear paper of the lower examination within the admissible chances......."  4.
It is the admitted position that the petitioner has cleared the examination within the admissible chances.
Consequently, his result for BAMS Part I, cannot be withheld.
The respondents are directed to declare the petitioner's result forthwith and to promote him to the next higher class.
The writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
