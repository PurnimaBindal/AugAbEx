Arun B Saharya, CJ.
- The husband of the petitioner is posted in Government of India Undertaking i.e. MTC at Kharar near Chandigarh, on a non- transferable post.
On this ground, the petitioner has challenged her transfer order dated 14.5.1998 Annexure P-3 from Morni to Safidon (District Jind).
2.
The learned counsel for the petitioner has informed us, in response to a specific query, that the petitioner had got married in May, 1980 and that her husband has been posted throughout at Kharar near Chandigarh.
After her marriage, the petitioner joined service and was initially posted in District Gurgaon, vide order dated 28121987 Annexure P-4.
Then on her own request, she was transferred from Gurgaon and posted to Block Gharaunda District Karnal by order dated 12.5.1989 Annexure P-5.
She was transferred from Karnal to Pillukhera District Jind by order dated 14.6.1993 Annexure P-6.
Thereafter, again on her request, she was transferred from Pillukhera District Jind to Kathura District Sonepat by order dated 18.6.1993 Annexure P-7.
For all these years, she was posted at different places away from Chandigarh.
3.
On the petitioner's own request, she was eventually transferred from Kathura to Pinjore by order dated 2021995 Annexure P-8 and then she was transferred to Morni by order dated 251997 Annexure P-1.
Now by impugned order dated 14.5.1998 Annexure P-3, she has been transferred from Morni to Safidon.
4.
It is apparent that the petitioner remained posted at Pinjore for two years by order dated 2021995 Annexure P-8 and for another one year at nearby Morni by order dated 251997, Annexure P-1.
Thus, she remained posted for the last three years near about Chandigarh.
5.
The guidelines issued by the State do not confer or imply legally enforceable right to the posting of any employee at any particular place.
Since the petitioner has been posted near about Chandigarh for the last three years, we find no ground made out for interference with the impugned order, which appears to have been made in normal course of annual general transfer exercise.
The writ petition is, therefore, dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
