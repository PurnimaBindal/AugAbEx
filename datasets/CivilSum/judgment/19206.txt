GS Singhvi, J - The only issue which arises for decision in this petition is whether the petitioner is entitled to be appointed on a Class III post carrying the pay scale of Rs. 950-1500 in accordance with the instructions issued by the Government on 851995, 3181995 and 19.7.1996.
Averments made in the petition show that Shri Jai Parkash, husband of the petitioner died on 16.9.1993 while he was holding the post of Multi-purpose Health Worker (Male).
The pay scale of that post was Rs. 1200-2040.
The petitioner's application for ex-gratia employment was accepted by the respondent but she was offered appointment as a Class IV employee in the pay scale of Rs. 750-940.
2.
After having acquired the knowledge that other similarly situated persons have been provided employment on Class III posts, the petitioner represented to the respondent No. 3 that her case be also considered for employment on a post carrying one scale lower than the pay scale of the post held by her deceased husband.
However, the same has not been decided so far.
3.
In view of the fact that instructions issued by the Government provide for consideration of the case of the dependent of the deceased employee for employment on a post carrying one scale lower than the post held by the deceased employee and in view of the fact that others have been provided employment on Class-III posts we dispose of this petition with a direction to the respondents No. 2 and 3 to consider and decide the application of the petitioner for compassionate appointment on Class-III post carrying the pay scale of Rs. 950-1500 within a period of three months of the submission of the certified copy of this order together with a copy of the writ petition.
