NK Sodhi, J - Petitioner Dr. Avtar Singh who retired as Health Officer, Municipal Corporation, Ludhiana has filed this petition under Article 226 of the Constitution challenging the action of the respondents in not having granted him the scale of pay from time to time to which he was entitled during his service as Health Officer of the Corporation.
2.
The undisputed facts are that the petitioner was appointed as Medical Officer of Health on temporary basis by the Municipal Committee, Ludhiana, as per order dated May 17, 1976.
The appointment which was for 89 days to begin with was extended from time to time till he was appointed on regular basis with effect from February 14, 1978.
The proposal of the Municipal Committee for appointing the petitioner was approved/sanctioned by the State Government.
Since the order appointing the petitioner did not specify the scale of pay to which he would be entitled, he represented to the Municipal Committee to fix his pay and also the scale thereof.
The Accountant of the Municipal Committee recommended that the petitioner be given pay in the scale of Rs. 600-1100 as according to him this was the grade recommended by the pay commission and adopted by the Municipal Committee.
He pointed out that all Health Officers appointed by the Committee in the past had come on deputation from different departments/organizations and were drawing their own pay scale.
This proposal of the Accountant appears to have been accepted by the Municipal Committee and the petitioner was being paid pay in this scale.
The petitioner again represented in the year 1979 that he was entitled to pay in the grade of Rs. 850-1250 as the Municipal Committee itself had advertised the post of Medical Officer in this grade in the year 1973.
He made several representations in this regard.
The Accounts Officer of the Municipal Committee this time, recommended the case of the petitioner and pointed out that grade of Rs. 600-1100 had been revised to Rs. 850-1250 plus 33-1/3 per cent N.P.R.
as per government letter of November 5, 1970 which had been adopted by the Municipal Committee.
Despite this recommendation, the petitioner was not given the revised grade of Rs. 850-1250.
In the meanwhile, the grades were gain revised by the State Government with effect from 111978 as per its circular dated January 20, 1981 which too had been adopted by the Municipal Corporation.
It may be observed that after coming into operation of the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, a Municipal Corporation know by the name of Municipal Corporation, Ludhiana, was constituted for the city of Ludhiana which took over the Municipal Committee.
The petitioner then represented that he be given the grade as revised by the State Government with effect from January 1, 1978.
The case of the petitioner was considered by the Municipal Corporation and was recommended to the State Government in December, 1988.
The recommendation was that he be allowed pay scale of Rs. 850-1250 for the period 1851976 to 31.12.1977 and the grade of Rs. 1400-2100 for the period 111978 onwards.
The respondent-Corporation vide its resolution dated June 19, 1983 had also recommended the grant of revised pay scale to the petitioner but the State Government did not respond and sat over the matter.
Finally, the Municipal Corporation, as per its letter of November 15, 1989 made the following recommendation to the State Government.
"On the whole Dr. Avtar Singh, M.O.H.
is entitled to get his revised pay scale as under :-  1851976 to 3l121977 850-1250 instead of 600-1100.
1178 to 31.12.85 1400-2100 instead of 1200-1775  1186 onwards on the recommendation of IIIrd pay commission."
There was again no response from the State Government and due to its action the petitioner continued to draw his pay in the unrevised scale and ultimately retired from service in the year 1991.
It was then that he filed the present petition for a direction to the respondents to grant him the revised scales of pay to which he was entitled from time to time and in terms of the recommendations made by the Municipal Corporation as per its letter of November 15, 1989 (Annexure P-32 with the petition).
3.
When notice of motion was issued to the respondents, the Municipal Corporation filed its reply admitting the claim of the petitioner but expressed its helplessness for want of sanction by the State Government.
Despite several opportunities granted to the State, it did not file its reply.
The writ petition had to be admitted on May 22, 1992 and was ordered to be heard within three months.
It is a matter of regret that the State Government for reasons best known to it has not chosen to file its reply even by now.
4.
Having heard counsel for the parties and after perusing their pleadings it is clear that the petitioner was not given the revised grades to which he was entitled from time to time and despite the recommendations made by the Municipal Corporation, the State Government slept over the matter as a result whereof the petitioner continued drawing pay in the unrevised grade and thereafter retired.
Not only did the State Government keep quiet on receipt of the recommendations from the Municipal Corporation since the year 1983 but it also adopted the same indifferent attitude even when the matter remained pending in this court and did not choose to file any reply.
The Municipal Corporation has already accepted the claim of the petitioner and had made recommendations to the State Government as observed earlier.
5.
Consequently, I allow the writ petition and direct the State Government to accord formal sanction to the Municipal Corporation, Ludhiana, for the grant of revised grades to the petitioner to which he was entitled from time to time.
This sanction will be accorded within two months from the date of receipt of a copy of this order by the Government failing which the Municipal Corporation, Ludhiana is directed to proceed to grant revised pay scales to the petitioner as per its recommendation made to the State Government vide its letter of November 15, 1989 on the assumption that the same has been sanctioned by the State Government.
The respondent-Corporation is also directed to pay to the petitioner the arrears to which he had become entitled within two months from the date of receipt of a copy of this order along with interest at the rate of 15 per cent per annum from the date when the amount became due to the petitioner.
Keeping in view the indifferent attitude of the State Government for the last about one decade, the petitioner shall be entitled to have his costs from respondent No. 1 which are assessed at Rs. 5000/-.
Petition allowed.
