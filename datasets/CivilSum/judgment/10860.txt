VK Jhanji, J - This order of mine shall dispose of Execution Application No. 23221 of 1997 as well as Civil Writ Petition No. 6355 of 1994.
2.
The grievance of the applicants in the execution application is that the respondents were directed to release the pay scale to the applicants as per their qualifications with effect from the date they acquired higher qualification but the respondents have not paid arrears of salary with effect from the date the applicants acquired higher qualification.
Prayer made in the execution application is that respondents be directed to pay the arrears with effect from the date the applicants acquired higher qualification.
3.
Mr. NK Joshi, Assistant Advocate General, Haryana appearing on behalf of the State contended that the State is not liable to pay arrears of pay to the applicants from the date they acquired higher qualification but the applicants are entitled to only arrears for only 38 months prior to the filing of the writ petition.
He contended that in CM No. 19299 of 1996 in CWP No. 15630 of 1993, (Rattan Singh and others v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) SCT 711 (P&H).
), clarification was sought by the State and Division Bench of this Court clarified that the payment of arrears shall be restricted to 38 months prior to the filing of the writ petition.
Learned counsel has placed on record photo copy of order dated 251997 passed in the said case.
4.
On going through order dated 251997 passed in CM No. 19299 of 1996 in CWP No. 15630 of 1993 in case titled Rattan Singh and others v. Haryana State, I find that it is the consistent view taken by the Apex Court as also this Court that payment of arrears to the applicants has to be restricted to 38 months in case of delayed institution of the claims.
5.
Mr. Joshi has also submitted that after the clarification, necessary payment to the applicant as also all other persons similarity situated, has already been made.
6.
In view of the statement made by learned counsel appearing on behalf of the State, the present application is dismissed as having been satisfied.
It is made clear that in case any of the applicants has not received payment so far, he may make a representation in this behalf of the State and I have no manner of doubt that in case such a representation is received by the State Government, it shall make the payment to the applicant concerned in case payment has already not been made.
It is further made clear that payment of arrears to the applicants shall stand restricted to 38 months prior to the filing of the writ petition.
Petition dismissed.
