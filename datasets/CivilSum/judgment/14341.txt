DV Sehgal, President - We have considered the aspects involved in the present case with the assistance of Shri SM Sharma, learned Deputy Advocate General, Haryana.
In our view the Joint Secretary to Government of Haryana, Labour Department, ought not to have declined the reference on the ground that due to accident, the workman had suffered 80 per cent disability and he had been awarded additional compensation of Rs. 11,794/- besides retiral benefit.
The matter should have been left for the adjudication of the Labour Court.
It is now settled position of law as held by a Division Bench of this Court in Punjab Anand Lamp Employees Union v. Punjab Anand Lamp Industry Ltd and another, 1996(4) RSJ 250 : 1996(3) SCT 56 (P&H);.
Shri SM Sharma, learned D.A.G., Haryana candidly concedes to this legal position.
2.
In view of the above, we quash the impugned order dated 331994 Annexure P-3 by which the reference has been declined by the Joint Secretary to Govt., Haryana, Labour Department, and direct respondent No. 2 to make a reference under Section 10(i) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, within a period of two months from today.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed of.
