Amitava Lala, J - The petitioners have opted for pensionary benefit as available under GA No. 496-Edn(B)/IM-39/91 dated 16th December, 1991 issued by Joint Secretary to the Government of West Bengal within the prescribed period of time i.e. 90 days from the date of issuance of such memorandum.
2.
Such memorandum provides further that in case of such option the employer's share of contribution together with interest and additional interest shall be refunded to the Government forthwith.
3.
It appears that under Memo No. GA/1086 dated 17th July, 1998 the concerned District Inspector of Schools (S.E.)
South 24-Parganas issued a letter to the School by saying that the Assistant Teachers i.e. the petitioners herein have reopted for Pension-cum-Gratuity within the stipulated time as per GO No. 496-Edn dated 16th December, 1991.
On that basis he was requested to refund with interest Government share of PF etc. of the above named staff of the School immediately and to transfer own share to the head of A/C No. 8336 without any further delay.
About 14 days thereafter such order was withdrawn by the authority under the Memo No. 222 Law dated 31st July, 1,998 by saying that contention of this office memo No. GA/1086 dated 17th July, 1998 is hereby withdrawn as the same is not covered by the existing rules in force as embodied in GO No. 496-Edn(B) dated 16th December, 1991 read with GO No. 148-Edn(B) dated 21st May, 1990.
4.
Neither the State authority nor the school authority have come forward with an affidavit inspite of directions given by the Single Bench of this Court as far back as on 21st December, 1998.
Therefore, this court is not aware what is the defence on their part for the purpose of issuance of such letter.
5.
The learned Advocate for the petitioners contended that the refund of the amount pursuant to the Government order is a matter between the school authority and the State, but the petitioners being the teachers opted for such scheme cannot have any role to play.
Under such circumstances they should not be penalised in any manner whatsoever.
6.
1 take such submission as an appropriate submission.
The State authority cannot take the help of their own fault by withdrawing a letter giving a right to the petitioner in respect of such scheme nor the teachers will be blamed for any default on the part of the school authority in this respect.
7.
Under such circumstances, the writ petition stands allowed by quashing the order under Annexure `H' being memo No. 222 Law dated 31st July, 1998.
The school authority is directed to refund all the amounts under the scheme to the appropriate authority of the State immediately upon service of this order.
8.
The authority under the pension scheme is directed to take appropriate steps to release the pensionary benefits if it is pending before it within a period of 4 weeks from date so that the incumbents concerned can get actual pensionary benefit within a period of two weeks thereafter.
9.
This order is passed subject to payment of court fees in respect of the petitioner Nos. 2 to 5, excepting petitioner No. 1, within 7 days from date.
10.
The writ petition is treated on the day's list as `Motion Adjourned' and the same is disposed of with the above observations. .  11.
No order is passed as to costs.
12.
All parties are to act on a xeroxed signed copy of this dictated order on the usual undertaking.
Petition allowed.
