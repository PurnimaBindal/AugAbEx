JS Khehar, J (Oral) - The petitioner was inducted into service on 151992.
His services were terminated on 141996 i.e. after a period of approximately 4 years.
He raised an industrial dispute whereupon the State Government made a reference to the Industrial Tribunal-cum-Labour Court, Panipat.
The claim of the petitioner was for reinstatement and continuity in service as also for full back wages.
The Tribunal by its award dated 18111997 held the petitioner entitled to reinstatement with continuity of service.
He, however, was not considered to be entitled to back wages.
2.
It is in the aforesaid circumstances that the petitioner was taken back into service in March, 1998.
The award of the Tribunal was challenged before this Court by the respondents by filing Civil Writ Petition No. 4420 of 1998.
The said petition was dismissed.
Whereupon the respondents approached Hon'ble Supreme Court.
The Special Leave Petition was also dismissed vide order dated 1111999.
In these circumstances, the award of the Labour Court became final between the parties.
Thus, the petitioner must by fiction of law be considered to be in continuous service w.e.f.
151992.
On the strength of having rendered continuous service w.e.f.
151992, Rajbir petitioner has approached this Court through CWP No. 16854 of 1998 claiming regularisation on the basis of the instructions issued by the State Government dated 731996 and 1831996.
The aforesaid Civil Writ petition was admitted by a Division Bench of this Court on 2111998.
3.
The petitioner has now moved Civil Misc. No. 11306 of 1998 seeking disposal of the writ petition on the premises that the proposition of law involved in the petition filed by him is squarely covered by a decision of this Court in Mehender Singh v. State of Haryana 1998(4) R.S.J.
375 : 1999(1) S.C.T.
769.
Learned counsel for the respondents conceded that the proposition of law on the basis of which the petitioner has claimed regularisation is squarely covered by the aforesaid decision.
4.
In view of the statement made by the learned counsel for the respondents, CWP No. 16854 of 1998 is disposed of with the direction to the respondents that the petitioner shall be deemed to have been appointed on regular basis in terms of the instructions dated 731996 read with instructions dated 1831996.
He shall also be entitled to all consequential benefits accrued to him by virtue of aforesaid regularisation.
The date with effect from which the petitioner is to be granted regularisation, shall, however, be determined by the respondents on the basis of instructions under reference or any other instructions on the subject.
5.
This petition is accordingly allowed with costs which are quantified at Rs. 2,500/-.
Petition allowed.
