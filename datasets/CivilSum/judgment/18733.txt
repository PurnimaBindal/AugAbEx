KC Gupta, J - Bhola Singh, petitioner, who belongs to Scheduled Caste, was working as Chowkidar in Public Health Division of Punjab Mandi Board in the year 1984.
In the year 1994, vide Annexure P-3 dated 2221994, the Punjab Mandi Board took a decision to entrust the Maintenance work to the Market Committees alongwith the staff with effect from 131994 which were `C' Class Committees.
Accordingly, the maintenance work was entrusted to Market Committee, Talwandi Sabo vide letter Annexure P-4 dated 231994 with effect from 1731994.
2.
The petitioner had passed his matriculation examination in IInd Division in March 1988 vide Annexure P-2.
As per Resolution No. 6 dated 861988, the petitioner was promoted to the post of Clerk as two posts of clerk had become vacant on account of their incumbents being promoted.
Accordingly, vide Annexure P-8 dated 3061988, the petitioner was issued promotion order as Clerk against a vacant and sanctioned post on temporary basis and his probation period was fixed for a period of two years.
He joined as such on 3061998 itself vide Annexure P-9.
Subsequently, the Market Committee also passed Resolution No. 8 dated 2232000.
Annexure P-6, whereby it approved the merger of Water works staff in the Committee.
3.
On 3182000 vide Annexure P-12, the Market Committee cancelled Resolution No. 6 vide which the petitioner was promoted, on the plea that he had not been absorbed in the Market Committee and as such could not be promoted.
4.
The petitioner has challenged, by filing the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the said resolution dated 3182000, Annexure P-12, vide which his promotion as Clerk was cancelled without giving him an opportunity of personal hearing in violation of principles of natural justice.
5.
Notice of motion was issued on 12102000.
The respondents appeared and filed written statement.
6.
We have heard counsel for the petitioner, Sh. JS Maanipur, counsel for the respondents, Sh. SP Garg, and carefully gone through the record.
7.
It is an admitted fact that the petitioner was working as a Chowkidar with the Public Health Wing of Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board in the year 1988.
He is Scheduled Caste as he belongs to Ramdasia Caste and had passed the matriculation examination in IInd Division in March 1988 vide Annexure P-2.
In the year 1994 vide Annexure P-3, the work of Public Health in the Market Committees, which were class `C', was transferred to Market Committees alongwith the staff with effect from 131994.
The Assistant Engineer, Punjab Mandi Board, Barnala, wrote letter Annexure P-4 to Market Committee, Talwandi Sabo (respondent No. 2) mentioning that onwards the work of maintenance of Public Health Schemes was transferred to the Market Committees alongwith staff.
The Executive Engineer, Public Health, Punjab Mandi Board had even issued Last Pay Slip of the petitioner, Annexure P-5 upto 2821994.
The petitioner was promoted as Clerk on purely temporary basis against a vacant and sanctioned post by the Market Committee vide resolution No. 6 dated 861998, Annexure P-7.
Thereafter, the Secretary, Market Committee, Talwandi Sabo, wrote letter Annexure P-8 dated 3061998 to the petitioner informing him about his promotion.
The petitioner accordingly joined his duty as Clerk on 3061998 (Forenoon) vide letter Annexure P-9.
Respondent No. 2, Market Committee, subsequently passed resolution No. 8 dated 2232000 vide which the staff of Water Works was merged in the Committee.
It was also stated that a letter had been written to the Mandi Board for taking approval.
However, it is alleged that the approval had not been granted by the Mandi Board.
The fact remains that the Market Committee had treated the petitioner as its own employee for all intents and purposes.
The above mentioned documents show that the Mandi Board had transferred the maintenance work including that public health to the Market Committees which were `C' Class Committees alongwith the staff with effect from 131994 i.e. the Board also wanted to treat their maintenance staff (Public Health) which were transferred to the Market Committees to be the staff of the Market Committees for all intents and purposes.
The Market Committee, Talwandi Sabo (respondent No. 2) even considering the petitioner to be of its own staff, promoted him as Clerk.
The permanent and sanctioned post was available with the Market Committee when the petitioner was promoted as Clerk.
The petitioner has been demoted without giving an opportunity of hearing which is against the principles of natural justice.
Respondent No. 1 had not stated in para 5 of the written statement that the turn of the petitioner had not yet come for promotion as Clerk.
Respondent No. 1 never objected to respondent No. 2 that the petitioner was not to be treated as employee of respondent No. 2.
On the other hand, the intention of respondent No. 1 from the documents appears to be that staff belonging to Public Health, which was transferred to respondent No. 2, was to be treated as staff of respondent No. 2 meaning thereby, that the petitioner was to be treated as employee of respondent No. 2 for all intents and purposes.
It hardly matters that respondent No1 had not given permission for the merger of the posts of the Public Health Works in the office of respondent No. 2.
Tacitly, the permission is presumed to have been given.
The petitioner cannot be denied the salary of the Clerk simply on the ground that he was not the employee of the Committee.
8.
For the foregoing reasons, the writ petition is accepted.
The order dated 2992000, Annexure P-11, whereby respondent No. 2 had cancelled the promotion of the petitioner as Clerk and the Resolution No. 6 dated 3182000, Annexure P-12, are quashed.
Consequently, the petitioner is entitled to work as Clerk and is to be paid his salary for the post of Clerk from the date he was appointed as such.
Petition allowed.
