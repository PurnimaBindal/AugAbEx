PC Phukan, J (Oral) - By this application under Article 226 of the Constitution, the petitioner has prayed for appropriate writ for quashing and setting aside the order dated 2051996 withholding his increments for two years, the order dated 25.10.1996 imposing on him penalty of reduction to lower time scale of pay and the order dated 2061997 removing him from service.
2.
I have heard Mr. H Lalrinthanga, learned counsel for the petitioner as well as Mr. T Vaiphei, learned Assistant Advocate General for the State respondents.
3.
The petitioner was appointed as a Driver under the Govt. of Mizoram vide order dated 9121988 (Annexure-I).
His explanation was called for non- compliance of instruction, vide order dated 1391991 (Annexure-II).
Pending drawal of disciplinary proceedings he was placed under suspension vide order dated 8111991 (Annexure-III).
It was, however, revoked by a subsequent order dated 6.7.1992 (Annexure-IV).
His increments with withheld for two years due on 1121996 and 1121997 vide order dated 2051996 (Annexure-VII) on the ground that he was convicted under Sections 290/510 IPC and was sentenced thereunder to pay a fine of Rs. 60/- and, in default, three days simple imprisonment.
He was warned for attending Officer on 1171996 under influence of liquor vide letter dated 3171996 (Annexure-VIII).
The Central Civil Services Rules, 1964 prescribed punishment for those Govt.
Servants who indulge in drinking during Officer hours.
He was given an opportunity of making representation on the proposed penalty of reduction to lower time scale of pay vide order dated 1691996 (Annexure-X) on the ground that he was convicted under Sections 290/510 IPC and was sentenced thereunder to pay a fine of Rs. 100/- and in default to four days' simple imprisonment.
After giving him an opportunity of personal hearing and due consideration of his representation, the penalty of reduction to lower time scale of pay was imposed on him vide order dated 25.10.1996 (Annexure-XII).
As against this order and the earlier order dated 2091996 (Annexure-VII) imposing punishment on him, the petitioner has approached this court seeking remedy after a long lapse of time.
He filed instant Writ Petition only on 2331999.
4.
The petitioner was again arrested by the Police under Section 34 of the Police Act on 18.2.1997.
He was convicted by a Criminal Court under the said section of law and was sentenced thereunder to pay a fine of Rs. 20/- and in default to two days' simple imprisonment.
He was given an opportunity of making representation on the proposed penalty of removal from service vide order dated 1951997 (Annexure-XV) on the ground that he was convicted under Section 34 of the Police Act and was sentenced thereunder to pay a time of Rs. 20/- and in default to two days simple imprisonment.
After giving him an opportunity of personal hearing and due consideration of his representation, he was removed from service vide order dated 2061997 (Annexure-XVII).
The full text of this order is quoted below :  "ORDER Aizawl, the 20th June, 1997 Whereas Pu Saiawia Driver, SAD has been convicted on a criminal charge under section 34 of the Police Act; And whereas it is considered that the conduct of the said Pu Saiawia which has led to his conviction is such as to render his further retention in public service undesirable; And whereas the said Pu Saiawia Driver was given an opportunity of personal hearing and offer his written explanation; And whereas the said Pu Saiawia Driver has given a written explanation which has been duly considered by the undersigned; Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Rule 19(1) of CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965, the undersigned hereby removes the said Pu Saiawia Driver from service with immediate effect.
Sd/- MC Goswami Secretary to the Govt. of Mizoram Aizawl, the 20th June, 1997."
5.
Now under clause (2) of Article 311 of the Constitution, no Govt. employee shall be dismissed or removed or reduced in rank except after an inquiry.
But the second proviso to this clause says that this clause shall not apply :  "(a) where a person is dismissed or removed or reduced in rank on the ground of conduct which has led to his conviction or a criminal charge; ........." 6.
This clause makes a Govt. employee who was convicted on a criminal charge, liable to removal without any further enquiry.
It include conviction under any law which provides for punishment for a criminal offence, whether by fine or imprisonment.
Conviction for drunkenness attracts this proviso, similarly conviction under Section 34 of the Police Act (Jagadindra v. IG, AIR 1959 Assam 134 Sunil v. State of West Bengal, AIR 1970 Calcutta 334).
In the affidavit-in-opposition filed on behalf of the State respondents, it is stated that having regard to his past conduct, removal of the incorrigible Govt. Servant like him was justified and that his retention in service inspite of repeated misconduct would have sent a wrong signal to other Govt. employees and would have been against public interest.
Taking into account of the entire document of the petitioner, his repeated misconduct, the impact which his misconduct was likely to have on the other Govt. employees, I have no hesitation to say that the petitioner deserved the penalty of removal.
7.
In view of what has been stated above, I am of the opinion that the impugned orders dated 2051996, 29101996 and 2561997 imposing on the petitioner the penalty of withholding increments of reduction to lower time scale of pay and ultimately of removal from service all on the ground of his conduct leading to his conviction on criminal charges do not call for interference from this court in exercise of its writ jurisdiction.
8.
In the result, this Writ Petition is dismissed.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
