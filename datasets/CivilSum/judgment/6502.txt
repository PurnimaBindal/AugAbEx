HS Bedi, J. (Oral) - The services of Madan Mohan, respondent No. 3, who was a workman employed with the Markfed Refined Oil it and Allied Industries, Kapurthala (hereinafter called the 'Markfed') were terminated by the employer.
He raised an industrial dispute and an award was rendered on 131993 and published on 23.7.1993 ordering his reinstatement with full backwages.
The Markfed thereafter filed Civil Writ Petition No. 10999 of 1993, which was dismissed on 3081993.
Against the order of the High Court, the Markfed filed S.L.P.
No. 17092 of 1993 in the Hon'ble Supreme Court and on 31-1-1994, the following interim order was made :  "Issue notice confined only to back wages.
Interim stay of the payment of back wages."
It is the petitioner's case that he as the General Manager of the Markfed passed an order on 421994 reinstating respondent No. 3 on which he submitted his joining report on 821994.
Madan Mohan thereafter filed a contempt petition No. 876 of 1993 claiming that as the award of the Labour Court had not been complied with, the petitioner was guilty of having committed contempt of Court.
This petition was dismissed vide order dated 2431994 holding that the workman had been reinstated, in the meanwhile.
Respondent No. 1 thereafter filed a criminal complaint (Annexure P-1) dated 811994 under Section 29 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 alleging that as the award dated 131993 had not been complied with, the petitioner as the General Manager was liable to suffer prosecution.
The Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kapurthala after examining the complaint, vide his order (Annexure P-4) dated 361994 issued notice to the petitioner under Section 29 ibid and as the petitioner pleaded not guilty, he was charged for the offence.
It is against the aforesaid order as also the complaint (Annexure P-1) that the present petition has been filed.
2.
A reply has been filed on behalf of respondent No. 3 in which it has been stated that the award had not been honoured in its entirety and in support of this plea, a set of calculations, Annexure R-3/1, has been filed to show that the amount paid was for below the amount actually due and as such the persecution was wholly justified.
3.
It will be clear from the set of facts that the award has been substantially complied with inasmuch as the petitioner had been reinstated with continuity of service and certain amounts have been paid as would be clear from Annexure R-3/1.
If at all, there is any dispute with regard to the calculations does not to my mind, in any way bring the matter within a criminal intent.
4.
This petition is accordingly allowed, the complaints (Annexure P-1) and the summoning order (Annexure P-4) are quashed.
To do substantial justice to all concerned a direction is issued to the Labour Court to re-determine the amount due to the petitioner after associating the Markfed as also the petitioner with the calculations.
The parties to appear before the Labour Court concerned on 3052002.
In case, it is found that some amount has been withheld from the respondent No. 3, the Labour Court would be at liberty to consider the question of grant of interest to him as well.
Dasti orders.
Petition allowed.
