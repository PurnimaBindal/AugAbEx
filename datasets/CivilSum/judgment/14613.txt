VK Bali, J - Petitioner, Sube Singh Kodan, presently holding the post of Auditor in Local Fund Accounts, Municipal Committee, Bahadurgarh, seeks a writ in the nature of certiorari so as to quash orders, Annexures P-7 and P-9 and further writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to grant him benefits of military service as per the provisions of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965.
2.
Brief facts, on which the claim aforesaid has been rested, reveal that the petitioner was selected as Junior Auditor by the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana on December 29, 1972 in the Local Fund Accounts, Haryana under the Finance Department.
Prior to the joining of his service, he had served in Army with effect from July 24, 1963 to September 18, 1970 when he was released on medical grounds.
Later on he was appointed as Store Clerk on ad hoc basis in Tourist Department, Haryana, from December 19, 1970 to October 15, 1971 and then in Medical College, Rohtak from March 31, 1972 to September 26, 1972.
He was thereafter appointed as Junior Field Investigator in Statistical Department on June 30, 1972 on which post he remained upto December 27, 1972.
Petitioner had, however, applied for selection to the post of Junior Auditor in August, 1971 and he was selected by the Subordinate Services Selection Board.
On the basis of the Rules known as Punjab National Emergency (Concessions) Rules, 1965, petitioner applied by submitting an application to respondent No. 2 for grant of benefits of military service rendered by him towards seniority, increment and promotion.
In response to the application aforesaid the petitioner was asked to supply certain documents.
Some correspondence ensued thereafter between petitioner and respondent No. 2, but ultimately his claim was rejected vide letter dated July 28, 1987.
He, however, once again requested the respondents for reconsidering the matter vide letter dated May 2, 1991 and his claim once again was rejected vide letter dated August 19, 1991.
It is in the wake of circumstances detailed above that petitioner has come to this Court for the relief indicated in the earlier part of this judgment.
3.
The cause of petitioner has been opposed and in the written statement that has been filed on behalf of the respondents, all that has been pleaded is that as per order, Annexure P-6, petitioner was appointed as Junior Field Investigator in the Statistical Department, Haryana w.e.f.
June 30, 1972 on regular basis.
Under the provisions of Rule 4(i) of the Punjab Govt. National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, he was entitled to claim the benefit of Military service during emergency for increments only on first appointment and not on his subsequent appointment.
4.
The plea raised by the respondents, besides being wholly un-realistic, runs counter to the decision of this Court in Raj Kumar Verma v. State of Haryana, 1981(3) SLR 436 wherein Division Bench of this Court held that "Rule 4-A of Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Haryana Amendment Rules, 1979," limiting the service benefit to only first appointment under the Government, was violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
For the reasons mentioned above, this petition is allowed.
Orders, Annexures P-7 and P-9 are quashed.
A direction is issued to the respondents to grant military service benefits to petitioner as per the provisions of Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965.
Let this exercise be done within two months from today.
There shall, however, be no order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
