GS Singhvi, J - The petition has been filed for issue of a mandamus to the respondents to re-determine the retiral dues payable to the petitioner by taking his last drawn average pay as Rs. 4425/-.
2.
The petitioner joined service as Overseer in the Irrigation Department of the then State of Punjab.
Upon re-organisation of the State of Punjab, his service was allocated to the State of Haryana with effect from November 1, 1966.
The petitioner was promoted as Sub-Divisional Officer on ad hoc basis with effect from 1261974 and this post he held till his retirement on 31101992 on attaining the age of superannuation.
3.
The petitioner was given selection grade of Rs. 4100-5300/- with effect from 151989 and as on the date of retirement, he was drawing pay of Rs. 4475/- per month and his average pay worked out to Rs. 4425/-.
However, when his pension was fixed, the respondents considered the petitioner's pay in the scale of Rs. 2200-4000/- and, thus, fixed his pension at the rate of Rs. 1825/- per month.
After his retirement, order dated 30.11.1992 was passed preparing a revised ranking list of the Sub-Divisional Officers in pursuance of the order of the Supreme Court and therein, the name of the petitioner has been included amongst the promotees of the year, 1974.
The petitioner represented to the respondents for revision of this pensionary benefits on the premise that he was drawing salary in the scale of Rs. 4100-5300/- but no action has been taken by the respondents.
4.
During the course of hearing, Shri Raina, learned Deputy Advocate General, Haryana, drew the attention of the Court to the Memo No. 9/54/93-4 IE dated 771995 issued by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to the Government of Haryana, Irrigation and Power Department, whereby the Government conveyed its decision to the Engineer-in-Chief, Irrigation Department, Haryana, that the officers who retire or have retired after getting selection grade of Rs. 4100-5300/- be granted pension in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300/- and not in the lower pay scale subject to the condition that the Government will be free to revise the issue regarding pensionary benefits after the decision of the pending writ petition.
5.
In view of the fact that the Government has itself taken a policy decision to grant the pensionary benefits on the basis of pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300/- it is necessary to direct that the petitioner's pension and retirement benefits should be revised by taking into consideration his last pay drawn in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300/-.
6.
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to revise the pension and all other retiral benefits of the petitioner taking into consideration his pay drawn in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300/-.
Necessary steps should be taken by the respondents for giving the benefit of revised retiral dues including gratuity etc. within two months of the submission of a certified copy of this order.
It is made clear that if the amount of arrears payable to the petitioner is not so paid within two months' period, he shall become entitled to be paid interest at the rate of 15% per annum w.e.f.
1.11.1992 and in that event, it will be the responsibility of the defaulting official to make good the loss which may be suffered by the public exchequer.
Costs are made easy.
Petition allowed.
