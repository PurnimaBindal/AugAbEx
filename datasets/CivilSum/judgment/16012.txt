GS Singhvi, J - The petitioner joined service of Haryana Police as Assistant Sub-Inspector (Probationer) on 2881974 after selection by the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana .
He was confirmed as ASI Police on 28.8.1977.
He was promoted as Sub Inspector with effect from 16.1.1979 and was confirmed on that post with effect from 1101981.
The petitioner's candidature for placing him on list `F' was not considered in the year 1984 though persons junior to him were brought on list `F'.
This happened because the petitioner had earned adverse remarks in his annual confidential report for the year 1981-82 and the representation made by the petitioner against the adverse remarks was pending.
Vide its order date 23.5.1989, the Government expunged the adverse remarks in the annual confidential report of the year 1981-82 relating to the petitioner.
Thereafter he was brought on list-`F' with effect from 19121986.
He made representations for retrospective placement of his name in list-`F'.
The Government considered the representation and brought the petitioner's name in list-`F' with effect from 1891985.
This did not satisfy the petitioner because the persons junior to him were brought on list-`F' with effect from 2311984, 1841984, 18.1.1985 and 3011985.
2.
The petitioner was promoted as Inspector with effect from 2111985 and in the seniority list of Inspectors he was placed below Shri Gurbax Singh and above Shri Sajjan Singh.
To this effect the respondent No. 2 issued order Annexure P 1 dated 2191990.
On 1271991, the petitioner was placed under suspension vide Annexure P4 on the ground of his involvement in the case relating to Meham Assembly election.
He was under suspension on the date of the filing of the writ petition.
C.W.P.
No. 14632 of 1992 regarding suspension of the petitioner has not so far been decided.
It is listed at Sr. No. 843 for 1431995.
3.
In this petition, the petitioner has questioned the non-consideration of this case for promotion to the post of Deputy Superintendent of Police on account of adverse remarks in his annual confidential report for the period between 1.4.1990 to 17.6.1990.
He has also assailed the promotions accorded to respondents 3 to 7 who are junior to him.
4.
In reply to the writ petition, the respondents Nos. 1 and 2 have not disputed the facts relating to the appointment of the petitioner as Assistant Sub Inspector and his promotion to the post of Sub-Inspector.
They have pleaded that for placement in list `F' a Sub Inspector is required to complete eight years service as non-gazetted officer out of which atleast five years' service should be as Sub-Inspector in terms of rule 13.14(2) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934.
The petitioner had been recruited as Assistant Sub Inspector of Police with effect from 2881974 and he was promoted as Sub Inspector on 16.1.1979.
He was reverted as Assistant Sub Inspector on 16.1.1979 and was against promoted on 941980.
Therefore, he cannot be said to fulfil the conditions of eligibility for inclusion of this name in list- `F'.
Subsequently, his name was brought on list-`F' with effect from 1891985 and he was promoted as Inspector with effect from 2191985.
In regard to the adverse remarks, the respondents have pleaded that representation made by the petitioner was rejected in the first instance but later on the Government accepted his representation vide order dated 23.5.1989 in so far as ACR of 1981-82 is concerned.
However, other adverse remarks in the ACR of the petitioner for the period from 1.4.1990 to 17.6.1990 were available on the date of his promotion, and, therefore, he was not entitled to be promoted.
Respondents have also pleaded that the suspension of the petitioner relates to his involvement in a criminal case registered on 871991 for offences under Sections 302, 307 and 120-B of the Indian Penal Code and during pendency of the case, the petitioner cannot be promoted on the next higher post.
5.
Today, Civil Writ Petition No. 14770 of 1992 filed by the petitioner against the adverse remarks pertaining to the period from 1.4.1990 to 17.6.1990 has been allowed and the Court has quashed the adverse remarks in the ACR of the petitioner.
6.
The only point which survives for consideration is whether the petitioner had a right to be considered for promotion to the post of Deputy Superintendent of Police when his juniors were promoted.
This issue may have required a more elaborate examination by the Court but in view of the fact that suspension of the petitioner has been quashed in Civil Writ Petition No. 14632 of 1992 and adverse remarks made in the ACR have also been quashed today, there can be no doubt that the petitioner has acquired right to be reconsidered for promotion with effect from the date of promotion of his juniors in the cadre of Inspector.
Even if there was some justification with the respondents in not considering the case of the petitioner for promotion in the year 1992 on account of the fact that the petitioner was under suspension, after the quashing of the order of suspension and the adverse remarks, the petitioner has definitely acquired a right to be considered for promotion ignoring his suspension and adverse remarks.
7.
Consequently, the writ petition is allowed.
Respondent Nos. 1 and 2 are directed to consider the case of the petitioner for promotion as Deputy Superintendent of Police with effect from the date his juniors were promoted.
While considering the case of the petitioner, the respondents shall not take into account the fact that the petitioner had remained suspended and also that there were adverse remarks in his ACR for the period from 1.4.1990 to 17.6.1990.
The petitioner shall also not be ignored on the ground of pendency of criminal case.
However, it shall be for the competent authority to decide as to whether it is justified to withhold promotion of the petitioner in view of the nature of the allegations levelled against him.
This exercise shall be undertaken and completed by the respondents within a period of three months from the date of submission of certified copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
