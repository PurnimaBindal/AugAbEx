ML Singhal, J - Ram Gopal was appointed as JBT teacher (JV) on 13.5.1947.
He was granted senior scale of Rs. 250-10-300 since 1111969 vide letter No. 152/IE/ED.II-618/540 dated 5168 by the Government of Haryana.
He retired from service as Senior Vernacular Teacher on 30111978 from Government Co-education High School, Kamiri, District Hisar in the grade of 250-10-300.
At the time of retirement, he was getting basic pay of Rs. 300/- and was getting Rs. 40/- as Dearness Allowance as DA cut had already been abolished by the instructions of the Government of Haryana since 1.1.1977.
He was entitled to the gratuity on the basis of the following formula :-  Basic Pay + DA X 16 months He was entitled to pension per the rules and instructions according to the following formula :-  Basic Pay + D.A.

According to Ram Gopal, defendants adopted wrong formula while computing pension.
While computing pension, the Govt. could not deduct DA out of the salary drawn.
DA component should have been taken into account while computing pension.
Government adopted a wrong formula while ordering deduction of Rs. 1,45650 from the gratuity.
He was entitled to gratuity and pension according to the formula laid down by rules and instructions illustrated above.
He was entitled to amount of Rs. 2,815/- on account of gratuity, which had been wrongly and illegally deducted.

It was submitted by learned counsel for the State of Haryana that respondent-plaintiff was working as Senior Vernacular Teacher with effect from 1.10.52.
Grades of teachers including the plaintiff were revised with effect from 1.12.67 known as Kothari Commission.
Plaintiff-respondent was drawing Rs. 175/- as basic pay pre-revised scale of Rs. 120-5-175 (Selection Grade).
As per Haryana Govt. Gazette notification No. 152/IE/ED-II-68/540 dated 5168 the pay of the respondent-plaintiff was to be fixed taking into consideration basic pay drawn on 30.11.67 plus dearness pay admissible on the basic pay plus ad hoc relief and temporary allowance.
Plaintiff-respondent's pay was fixed as under :-  Basic Pay Rs. 175/- Dearness pay Rs. 45/- Ad hoc relief Rs. 25/- Temporary allowance Rs. 1750 Total pay Rs. 26250 i.e. Rs. 250 + 12.50 in the grade of 125-250 7.
It was mentioned in the annexure of the said notification dated 5168 that rate of deduction of dearness allowance including temporary allowance will be as under :-  (i) Rs. 1850 (if) basic pay less than Rs. 100/- in the existing scale.
(ii) Rs. 35/-, if basic pay exceeds Rs. 100/- in the existing scale.
It was submitted that since the respondent-plaintiff was drawing Rs. 175/- in the pre-revised scale, he was paid dearness allowance of Rs. 146/- minus Rs. 35/- i.e. Rs. 111/- per month.

The dearness allowance was paid in full to the teachers from 1.1.77 and deduction of DA from salary was stopped with effect from 1.1.77.
It was submitted that a sum of Rs. 1,45650 was justifiably deducted from the gratuity payable to the respondent-plaintiff as Rs. 1,45650 had been paid in excess to the plaintiff by way of DA from 11166 to 311276.

In my opinion, the contention of the learned counsel for the State of Haryana is untenable.
As on 30.11.78 i.e. when the respondent-plaintiff retired from the Government service, he was in receipt of full DA.
There was no DA cut in operation.
DA cut had been abolished and full DA had been restored with effect from 1.1.77.
Government adopted the new policy of giving full DA w.e.f.
1.1.77.
It does not stand to reason why gratuity be not paid on the amount of basic pay etc., which the respondent-plaintiff was getting as on 30.11.78 i.e. when he retired.
Government adopted new policy w.e.f.
1.1.77 of taking full DA into account while calculating pension.
Plaintiff was entitled to pension according to the instructions issued by the Government, which was applicable with effect from 1.1.77 and an employee is entitled to pensionary benefits on the basis of salary last drawn.
In case Bachu Laxman v. Union of India, 1985(1) SLR (Gujarat) it was observed that "the employees shall be entitled to pensionary benefits on the basis of salary last drawn."
In GN Prasher v. Secretary Govt. of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, New Delhi, 1984(1) SLR 484 it was held that "full amount of interim relief paid to the employees was to be included in their emoluments for the purposes of calculating their pension."
9.
In my opinion, respondent-plaintiff was entitled to pension and gratuity on the basis of salary last drawn.
Since DA cut had been abolished before the retirement of plaintiff, DA cut could not have been taken into account while calculating gratuity and pension.
Defendant-State of Haryana could not refix the pension and gratuity after taking DA cut into account.
If DA was paid to the respondent-plaintiff from 11169 to 311276 in excess of what was admissible to him, no deduction could have been made while computing the amount of gratuity payable to him as there appears to be nothing that the plaintiff got excess DA by playing fraud on the Government.
In view of what has been said above, there appears to be no merit so far as this appeal is concerned and the same is, accordingly, dismissed.
No costs.
Appeal dismissed.
