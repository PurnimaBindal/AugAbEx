JL Gupta, J. (Oral) :- The petitioner who was commissioned in the Indian Army in February, 1964 was promoted to the rank of Acting Lt.
Colonel on August 5, 1983.
In September, 1985, the Selection Board considered the petitioner's claim for promotion.
Vide letter dated April 16, 1986, a copy of which has been placed on record as Annexure P/1, the petitioner was informed that he had not been placed in an acceptable grade for promotion.
His claim was periodically considered thereafter but on every occasion he was found to be unsuitable.
2.
In February, 1987, the petitioner claims to have learnt that he had been given an adverse confidential report for the year 1983-84.
It is averred that he submitted a representation against the report on February, 14,1987 (Annexure P/3).
This representation was considered by the Chief of the Army Staff and vide order dated August 17, 1987, the petitioner was informed that while rejecting the complaint, the Chief of the Army Staff had directed that "recommendation for promotion by IO/RO/SRO in para.
18(a)(iii) of A.C.R.
1983-84 be expunged.
As such the Officer will now be given special review in due course".
The special review was held thereafter.
Even as a result of this special review, the petitioner was not found suitable for promotion.
In the meantime the petitioner submitted a non-statutory complaint against the adverse report for the year 1983-84.
This complaint was finally accepted by the Chief of the Army Staff vide his order dated November 8, 1988.
A copy of this order has been placed on record as Annexure P/9.
By this order remarks in the ACR for the year 1983-84 as recorded by the RO were expunged.
The primary grievance of the petitioner is that after the expunction of the remarks for the year 1983-84, he had a right to be considered afresh on the basis of his record upto 1984-85.
3.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents, it has been mentioned that the petitioner was not selected on account of his "lack-lustre performance and comparative merit."
In para 15 of the written statement the details of the occasion on which the claim of the petitioner had been considered as also the reports which had been taken for consideration have been furnished.
This may be usefully reproduced.
It is as under :  "(a) Fresh consideration in September, 85 with ACRs upto 84-85.
(b) First Review in July, 86 with ACRs upto 85-86.
(c) Special Review (Fresh) in June, 88 with ACRs upto 84-85.
(d) Special Review (First) in May, 89 with ACRs upto 85-86.
(e) Special Review (Final) in September, 89 with ACRs upto 87-88.
4.
A perusal of the above resume shows that the petitioner's claim for the promotion was for the first time considered in September, 1985 with ACRs upto the year 1984-85.
After the expunction of the remarks in November, 1988 the petitioner's claim appears to have been considered in September, 1989 with ACRs upto the year 1987-88.
The precise claim of the petitioner is that after the expunction of the report for the year 198384 in November, 1988.
His claim had to be considered afresh along with the officers who had been considered in September, 1985.
For this purpose he claims that only the record upto the year 1984-85 as had been originally considered could have been taken into consideration.
5.
Maj. Aggarwal appearing on behalf of the respondents has conceded before me that this has not been done.
In this situation, it is clear that the respondents have not considered the petitioner's claim on the basis of the changed record in accordance with Law.
In my view the respondents were bound to consider the petitioner's claim for promotion vis-a-vis others who had been considered in September, 1985 on the basis of the record upto the year 1984-85.
Otherwise, the expunction of remarks would be of no consequence to the petitioner.
Equality of opportunity as guaranteed under Article 16 has not been afforded to the petitioner.
Claims of various officers were considered in September, 1985 on the basis of their record upto 1984-85.
Petitioner's claim had also to be similarly considered.
6.
After taking into consideration the facts and circumstances of the case and after perusal of the record as shown to me in court by Major Aggarwal, the writ petition is allowed.
It is directed that the respondents shall consider the petitioner's claim for promotion on the bars of the changed record as upto the year 1984-85 vis-a-vis other officers whose claim was considered in September, 1985.
Thereafter his case would be reviewed on all subsequent occasions in the light of the changed record and his suitability for promotion shall be adjudged.
In the event of the petitioner being found suitable for promotion all consequential benefits in the nature of arrears salary etc. shall follow.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to costs which is assessed at Rs. 2,000/-.
Order accordingly.
