Mehtab S Gill, J - The petitioner has prayed for issuing of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing respondent No. 2 to issue appointment letter to the petitioner for her selection as Staff Nurse and further, to direct respondent No. 3 to give her posting station at Ludhiana.
2.
The petitioner served in the Indian Army and was promoted to the rank of Major.
After completing school education, she joined the Military Nursing Service as a Trainee of 31121974.
The petitioner passed the General Nursing Certificate in the year 1977.
She served in various hospitals of the Army, Navy, Air Force throughout India.
3.
In the year 1998, when the petitioner was posted at Jalandhar Cantt, as Captain, she got married.
Her age at the time of marriage was 35 years.
4.
The petitioner has averred that she spent her golden years of youth in the service of the nation and Defence Service Personnel.
As the petitioner was inclined to serve the nation as well as sick soldiers as their families, the petitioner filled up the "marriage bond" on 25111988.
5.
Two children were born to the petitioner, a girl child on 8.11.1989 and a male child on 8101991.
In May, 1992, the petitioner was transferred to Military Hospital, Kirkee.
The age of her children at that time was 2 years 6 months and 7 months respectively.
6.
The petitioner could not leave the service as she has filled the "marriage bond", with the Army and it had not expired.
At the same time, as she had two small children to look after also and she had to look after her ailing parents-in-laws, the petitioner put in her release papers with the Army authorities.
The petitioner was not released immediately, but had to complete her "marriage bond" and was required to serve upto 24111992, the date of completion of second bond.
The petitioner, thus, put in 10 years 10 months and 24 days as regular service and 4 years bond service i.e. total 14 years, 10 months and 24 days of service in the Army.
7.
In response to an advertisement in The Tribune dated January 1, 1998 (Annexure P-1), the petitioner applied for the post of Staff Nurse (female post).
She applied in the category of Ex-servicemen/women and she fulfilled all the necessary qualification as per the advertisement.
8.
The petitioner submitted her application dated 2811998 (Annexure P-2) to the Secretary, Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board (hereinafter called SSS Board).
Another advertisement was given under the heading of "notice regarding screening test for Staff Nurses" and the petitioner applied for the same.
In the notice advertisement (Annexure P-3), it was stated that the screening test will be held on 28121998.
The petitioner was given Roll No. 03502547 (Annexure P-4).
9.
The result of the screening test was declared in the month of July, 1999 and the petitioner was declared successful and was called for interview to be held on 481999.
Photostat of the interview-slip has been annexed as P-5 with the writ petition.
10.
On 19112001, there was a news item in The Tribune (Annexure P-19) that the list of Staff Nurses has been finalised and the name of the petitioner figured in the category of ex-servicemen.
The petitioner visited the office of SSS Board on 5122001 and she was informed that she had been selected.
She had topped the list of Ex-serviceman quota and was allotted to respondent No. 2.
11.
The petitioner visited the office of respondent No. 2 and vide letters memo Nos. 30451 and 30452 dated 5122001 (Annexures P-20 and P-21 respectively), she was asked to undergo medical examination and character verification.
The petitioner submitted her medical report and character verification proforma in the office of respondent No. 2 vide her letter dated 21122001 (Annexure P-22).
12.
No appointment letter was given to the petitioner.
The petitioner sent several respirations to Governor of Punjab and to the then Chief Minister, Punjab and to various other authorities (Annexure P-6 to P-18 respectively).
The petitioner then finally served a legal notice dated 25112002 (Annexure P-24) upon respondent No. 2.
13.
Notice of motion was issued.
The Bench had directed the learned Additional Advocate General, Punjab at that stage to file a short affidavit and to state as to whether the petitioner was selected to the post in question and was offered appointment.
Respondents No. 1 to 3 filed reply on July 21, 2003.
14.
In its reply, the State has stated that the medical fitness certificate of the petitioner issued by the Civil Surgeon, Ludhiana was received in the office of respondent No. 2 on 20.12.2001 and the Character Certificate was received in the office of respondent No. 2 on 752002.
In the meantime, the Punjab GOvernment vide memo dated 1052002, imposed complete ban on recruitment to the posts lying vacant.
15.
We have heard learned counsel for the petitioner and the learned counsel for the respondents.
16.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has stated that the petitioner had served the Indian Army and was promoted to the rank of Major and served the nation for more than 14 years.
She had to look after two children, one aged 2-1/2 years and the other aged 7 months.
She had also to look after her old parents-in-laws.
It was in these circumstances that she had to leave the Army and she applied for the job of Staff Nurse as per the advertisement given by SSS Board.
17.
Learned counsel for the respondents has stated that, as the medical fitness certificate and the character certificate of the petitioner reached at a time when a ban had been imposed on fresh recruitment, the petitioner could not be given the appointment letter.
18.
There is no dispute between the parties to this effect that the petitioner was selected to the post of Staff Nurse, after being interviewed by the SSS Board.
19.
The petitioner has filed an affidavit, (Annexure P-1) wherein she has stated that the other similarly situated candidates have been issued appointment and posting letters.
20.
The medical fitness certificate issued by the Civil Surgeon, Ludhiana was received in the office of respondent No. 2 on 20.12.2001 and the character verification certificate of the petitioner issued by the Deputy Commissioner, Ludhiana was received in the office of respondent No. 2 on 752002.
The ban on fresh recruitment came into effect on 1052002.
21.
The submission or medical fitness certificate and character verification certificate by the petitioner was well within time.
The ban on fresh recruitment came on 1052002.
If we take into consideration the memo of the Punjab Government banning fresh recruitment, still the petitioner has fulfilled all the conditions for being appointed as a Staff Nurse, well within time.
22.
We are of the considered view that the petitioner who had served the Indian Army diligently, and had given the best part of her life to the service of the nation and to the service of the families of the ex-servicemen, should not have been treated in such a shabby manner.
The respondents should have taken care that she being an ex-serviceman, such like conditions of ban in recruitment should not have been taken into consideration, regarding her appointment and posting.
With the above observations, the writ petition is allowed.
Respondent No. 2 is directed to give appointment letter to the petitioner within 14 days from the date of receipt of a copy of this order and also to give her posting orders as per the norms.
Petition allowed.
