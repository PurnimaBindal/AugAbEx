Pritam Pal, J - Petitioner, Sanjay Babu, a workman, has filed this petition under Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India praying for issuance of an appropriate writ, order or direction in the nature of certiorari for setting aside/quashing order No. 34991 dated 1102003 (Annexure P/3) passed by Labour Commissioner, Labour Department, Haryana, respondent No. 2, and to direct him to issue Recovery Certificate under Section 33(C)1 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (hereinafter referred to as "the Act").
2.
The facts culminating to the commencement of this petition may be recapitulated thus :  3.
The petitioner was appointed as a helper with respondent No. 1 in the month of June, 1996.
Thereafter, his services were terminated on 25.9.1997.
Then, he served a demand notice under Section 2-A of the Act on the management (respondent No. 1) seeking for his reinstatement with full back-wages.
Ultimately, the industrial dispute was referred to the Labour Court, Faridabad.
The learned Labour Court passed an award dated 912001 in favour of the petitioner and accordingly he was reinstated in service with full back-wages.
Later on, the said award was challenged by respondent No. 1/management by way of Civil Writ Petition No. 11634 of 2003 in the High Court, which was dismissed vide order dated March 29, 2004.
5.
In the meantime, on August 1, 2003, the petitioner had filed an application under Section 33(C)1 of the Act for issuance of a Recovery Certificate for Rs. 1,08,810/- (back-wages and future wages @ Rs. 1550/- per month).
However, respondent No. 2 Labour Commissioner, did not issue the Recovery Certificate and vide his order dated 1102003 (Annexure P/3) advised the petitioner to file his claim for remaining wages under Section 33(C)2 of the Act in the relevant court.
6.
This is how, feeling aggrieved, the petitioner has come up in this writ petition against the said order Annexure P/3.
7.
After notice of motion, respondent No. 2 filed his written statement, wherein, it was stated that in fact, the petitioner should have moved an application under Section 33(C)1 of the Act for issuance of Recovery Certificate within a period of one year from the date of publication of award passed by the learned Labour Court.
The petitioner had filed his claim application (Annexure P/2) under Section 33(C)1 of the Act only on 182003, whereas the award was published on 3112001.
As such the same was time barred and hence the request of the petitioner was declined vide impugned order, Annexure P/3.
8.
The controversy centers around the question as to whether the Labour Court was justified in rejecting the request dated 182003 (Annexure P/2) of the petitioner being time barred ?
9.
Before we proceed further, it would be appropriate to reproduce provisions contained in sub-section (1) of Section 33(C) of the Act, which reads as under :-  "33-C. Recovery of money due from an employer : (1).
Where any money is due to a workman from an employer under a settlement or an award or under the provisions of Chapter V-A or chapter V-B, the workman himself or any other person authorized by him in writing in this behalf, or, in the case of the death of the workman, his assignee or heirs may, without prejudice to any other mode of recovery, make an application to the appropriate Government for recovery of the money due to him, and if the appropriate Government is satisfied that any money is so due, it shall issue a certificate for that amount to the Collector who shall proceed to recover the same in the same manner as an arrears of land revenue : Provided that every such application shall be made within one year from the date on which the money became due to the workman from the employer : Provided further that any such application may be entertained after the expiry of the said period of one year, if the appropriate Government is satisfied that the applicant had sufficient cause for not making the application within the said period."
A perusal of the aforesaid provisions goes a long-way to show that the request for issuance of Recovery Certificate in the name of the Collector is to be made within a year from the date on which the money became due to the workman from the employer.
However, such request/application may also be entertained after the expiry of said period of one year if the appropriate Government is satisfied that the applicant/workman had sufficient cause for not making such application within the said period.
10.
Here, in the instant case, the impugned order dated 1102003 (Annxure P/3) does not depict anything which could show that respondent No. 2 had applied its mind regarding the filing of application (Annexure P/2) for issuance of Recovery Certificate at a belated stage.
The impugned order (Annexure P/3), which was passed by respondent No. 2, reads as under :-  "Reference your letter dated 01.08.2003 on the above subject.
On the above subject you are advised to file your claim for remaining wages under Section 33(C)2 in the relevant Court."
The aforesaid order is non-speaking and cryptic one which does not mention any reason as to why the application (Annexure P/2) of the petitioner was impliedly rejected.
11.
In fact respondent No. 1/management had also filed an application for setting-aside the award on 342001, which was ultimately dismissed by the Labour Court on 28.5.2003.
It is also apparent from the replication filed by the petitioner that an application dated 10062003 was earlier also made to the Labour Commissioner for issuance of Recovery Certificate for back-wages from the date of his illegal termination i.e., from 25.9.1997 to the date of award dated 912001 @ Rs. 1550/- (last drawn wages).
When no action was taken thereon, second application dated 182003 (Annexure P/2) was moved by him, which too was ultimately rejected by the impugned order (Annexure P/3).
12.
In this view of our foregoing discussion and taking into consideration the fact that proceedings after the publication of award also remained pending before the Labour Court till 28.5.2003, so we hereby condone the delay occurred in filing the application Annexure P/2 under Section 33(C)1 of the Act and consequently by quashing the impugned order dated 1102003 (Annexure P/3) direct respondent No. 2 for issuing the necessary Recovery Certificate as required under Section 33(C)1 of the Act, in accordance with law, within a period of one month from the date of receipt of certified copy of this copy.
In the result, this writ petition succeeds in the terms as indicated above.
Petition allowed.
