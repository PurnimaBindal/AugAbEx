Ashok Bhan, J - Petitioners who are working as Central Sterilization Supply Department Attendants (hereinafter referred to as CSSD Attendants) in Pt. BD Sharma Medical College and Hospital, Rohtak (hereinafter referred to as the respondent Collage) have filed this petition for issuing a writ of mandamus directing the respondent-College to pay scale higher than the post of Packer in the Govt. of Haryana on the plea that post of Packer is a feeder post to that of CSSD Attendants.
2.
Petitioners (No. 1 was appointed a Packer by the respondent-college on 13.8.1975 in the pay scale of Rs. 300-400.
Similarly Petitioner No. 2 was appointed as a Packer on 24111976 in the same pay scale.
Principal of the respondent-College advertised/circulated 14 posts of CSSD Attendants in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600 on 7.11.1984 which were to be filled in from amongst the Class IV employees of the respondent-College itself.
It is averred that for the purpose of appointment/promotion to the post of CSSD Attendants, Operation Theatre Attendants, X-ray Attendants, Library Attendants, Lecture-Theatre Attendants and Laboratory Attendants, the feeder posts are the post of Packers.
3.
Petitioners applied for the post of CSSD Attendants in pursuance to the advertisement issued by the Principal of the respondent-college.
Petitioners were selected and appointed as CSSD Attendants.
Appointment letter of petitioner No. 1 has been attached as Annexure P2.
4.
The pay scales of Packer and CSSD Attendants were revised from 111986.
Pay scale of Packers was Rs. 750-940 and that of CSSD Attendants Rs. 800-1150.
The pay scale of the post of Packer in the Department of Public Health was revised by the Haryana Govt. from the existing scale of Rs. 750- 940 to Rs. 950-1400 vide letter No. 2/14/89-3HB-IV dated 1881989 in pursuance to the decision of this Court in CWP 7 of 1985 decided on 30111988.
5.
The result of the revision of the pay scales of Packers was that the post of Packer which was a Feeder Post of CSSD Attendants carried the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 while the post of CSSD Attendants, Operation Theatre Attendants, X-ray Attendants, Lab.
Attendants, Lecture-Theatre Attendants and Library Attendants were carrying lower pay scale of Rs. 800-1150.
CSSD Attendants as also various other categories of Attendants working in the respondent college filed representation for revision of their pay scales higher than those of the Packers or at least at par with that of the post of Packer.
Lab.
Attendants working in the Department of Health, Govt. of Haryana were brought at par with the post of Packer and were granted the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400.
No action was taken on the representations filed by the CSSD Attendants by the respondent-College, aggrieved against the which the present writ petition has been filed.
The case of petitioners is that CSSD Attendants cannot be given a grade lower than the post to Packer which is the feeder cadre.
There being an amonaly in the pay scale, the same needs to be revised and the CSSD Attendants are entitled to be placed in a higher standard of pay scale than the Packers and in any case they should at least be put up at par with the post of Packer.
6.
In the written statement stand taken by the respondents in that Packer is not a feeder cadre for the post of CSSD Attendants; that the petitioners were selected and appointed directly as CSSD Attendants in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600; that though the candidates for the post of CSSD Attendants were selected from amongst the Class IV employees including Packers who fulfilled the required qualifications purely on the basis of merit determined by the Selection Committee and not on the basis of any seniority of Class IV employees or that of the Packers; that the post of Packer cannot be treated as a feeder post for the CSSD Attendants because had that been the case then the senior-most Packer would have been promoted on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness or merit-cum-seniority but the petitioners were selected as CSSD Attendants purely on the basis of merit i.e. on the basis of educational qualifications, experience and performance in the interview.
It is admitted that the post of Packer now carries a pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 whereas the pay scale of CSSD Attendants, Operation Theatre Attendant, Library Attendant, X-ray Attendant, Lecture-Theatre Attendant is Rs. 850-1150 and the pay scale of Lab.
Attendant is Rs. 950-1500 having qualification of Matric with Science (Physics and Chemistry).
Counsel for the parties have been heard.
7.
There is no denial to the fact that petitioners who were working as Packers were selected as CSSD Attendants.
Pay scale of the Packer was Rs. 300-430 whereas the pay scale of C.S.S.D.
Attendant was Rs. 400-600.
The pay scale of Packer was revised to Rs. 750-900 with effect from 111986 and that of CSSD Attendants to Rs. 800-1150.
The post of CSSD Attendant is higher than that of the Packer as is evident from the letter Annexure P-6.
It is, therefore, obvious that the post of CSSD Attendant is higher post than that of Packer.
It is apparent from the pleadings of the parties that the pay scale of CSSD Attendants was not revised whereas the pay scale of the Packers was revised resulting in giving a lower scale of pay to CSSD Attendants who were working on higher post than the post of Packers which was lower in pay scale as well as the feeder cadre for the post of CSSD Attendants.
8.
The non-revision of the pay scale of CSSD Attendants has created an anomaly as the persons working on a higher post have been put in a lower pay scale than the post of Packer which is lower in status as well as feeder cadre of CSSD Attendant.
It is not denied by the respondents that the CSSD Attendants, Operation Theatre Attendants, X-ray Attendants, Library Attendants and Lecture-Theatre Attendants were in the same pay scale and their feeder posts are that of the post of Packers.
9.
Haryana Govt. issued a circular that from 111986, the pay scale have to be revised to first higher standard and second higher standard to remove stagnation and anomalies.
The pay scale of Lab.
Attendants have already been revised and they have been put at par with the Packers.
Operation Theatre Attendants who were similarly situated with the petitioners filed CWP No. 9042 of 1995 claiming a higher pay scale in the first higher standard i.e. Rs. 1200-2040.
The aforesaid writ petition was allowed by this Court on 21.11.1995 and it was held that the Operation Theatre Attendants are entitled to revised pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from 111994.
It was noticed by the Bench that they were inclined to make available the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from 111986 but since there was no specific demand raised in the earlier representation to that effect and that certain increments had been given to the petitioners in the other pay scale, the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 was restricted from 111994.
The relevant portion of the judgment (in CWP 9042 of 1995) granting pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 to the Operation Theatre Attendants reads as under:-  ``In paragraph 4 of the petition, the petitioner states that earlier the pay scale for the post of Packer was Rs. 750-900 and that of Operation Theatre Attendant it was Rs. 800-1150.
The pay scale of Packer was revised to Rs. 950-1400 but there was no corresponding revision of pay scale for the post of Operation Theatre Attendant.
It is patent from the pleadings of the parties that the pay scale of the Operation Theatre Attendant was not revised and the pay scale of the said post became lower than the post of Packer.
Annexure R-2 is the copy of the instruction issued by the Government of Haryana and Paragraph 5 of the same reads as under: `In case of employee was has got promotion already but the pay scale of the promotion post is either equal to or lower than the pay scale of the feeder post, the benefit of the higher standard scale will be granted in such cases.'
The above said paragraph clinches the issue because the petitioner had already been promoted and because of the lapse stated above the pay scale of the post of Operation Theatre Attendant became lower in pay scale than that of the Packer.
There is no controversy raised that the first higher standard scale qua the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400/- is Rs. 1200-2040/-.
In terms of paragraph 5 of the instructions mentioned above, the petitioner would be entitled to the said pay scale.''
10.
We respectfully agree with the view taken in the aforesaid judgment and follow the same.
Operation Theatre Attendants and other similarly situated Attendants working in the respondent-Medial College have always been equated in terms of pay scales.
Petitioners are, therefore, held entitled to pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 with effect from 111994 with all consequential benefits.
This petition stands allowed in the above terms.
No costs.
