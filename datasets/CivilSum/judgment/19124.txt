THB Chalapathi, J - This writ petition is filed by the petitioner under Article 226 of the Constitution of India seeking a direction to the respondents to consider his case for the inclusion and his name in list 'F'.
2.
The petitioner joined service as a Constable on 16111962 and at the time of filing of the writ petition he was working as an Inspector.
According to the petitioner, respondent No. 3 who joined service as a Constable much later i.e. on 311970 was promoted as Inspector on 361985.
According to the petitioner, he made a representation which was rejected on 13.1.1990.
Therefore, the petitioner filed this writ petition seeking quashing of the orders of the respondents and to declare that he is entitled to be included in list 'F' as per rule 1310 of the Punjab Police Rules.
3.
I am of the opinion that the writ petition is liable to be rejected only on the ground of delay and laches The following table shows the service particulars of the petitioner and the 3rd respondent.
Petitioner Respondent No. 3 1.
Date of Birth 13.5.1941 6.8.1951 2.
Date of entry into service as Constable 16111962 311970 3.
Entry into List 'C' 15.3.1971 22.11.1971 4.
Promotion as Head Constable 20.10.1972 22111971 5.
Confirmation as Head Constable 18.8.1978 2541973 6.
Entry into list 'D' 28121981 141975 7.
Confirmation as ASI 20.3.1978 26.9.1973 8.
Entry into list 'E' 2851982 151976 9.
Entry into list 'F' 22.2.1988 3011985 10.
Promotion as Inspector 491988 361985 As can be seen from the above table the petitioner was confirmed as Head Constable only on 18.8.1978 while respondent No. 3 was confirmed as such on 2541973.
The petitioner was entered into list 'D' on 28121981 while respondent No. 3 was enlisted on 141975.
The petitioner was confirmed as ASI on 20.3.1978 while respondent No. 3 was confirmed as such on 26.9.1973.
The petitioner was enlisted in list 'E' on 2851982 while third respondent was enlisted on 151976.
The petitioner was enlisted to list 'F' on 22.2.1988 while respondent No. 3 was enlisted on 3011985.
It is also clear that the petitioner was promoted as Inspector on 491988 while 3rd respondent was promoted as such on 361985.
Thus on the basis of date of confirmation as Head Constable in the year 1973, the 3rd Respondent was shown senior to the petitioner.
Even at the time when the 3rd respondent was enlisted in list 'E' in May, 1976, the petitioner did not object to the same.
It was only in the year 1989, the petitioner filed a representation which was disposed of in 1990.
The writ petition is also filed nearly after two years from the date of rejection of the representation.
Thus the petitioner is guilty of delay and laches.
It is not just to disturb the seniority of the respondent No. 3 after lapse of several years.
Therefore, the writ petition is liable to be dismissed only on the ground of delay and laches.
4.
The writ petition is accordingly dismissed.
However, there will be no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
