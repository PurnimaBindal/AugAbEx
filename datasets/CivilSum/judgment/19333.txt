GS Singhvi, J - These petitions have been filed for issue of a mandamus to the respondents to consider the cases of the petitioners for promotion to the post of Math/Science Masters against the quota fixed for promotion under the provisions of Punjab State Education Class III (School Cadre) Service Rules, 1978.
2.
Arguments in these cases were heard on different dates.
In order to seek instructions from the department for proper implementation of the rules, the learned Deputy Advocate General had sought time on more than one occasion.
3.
Today an affidavit of Shri H.K.
Dhanju, Assistant Director, in the office of the Director, Punjab Instructions (Schools), Punjab has been filed.
In this affidavit it has been stated that the department has decided to consider and promote the JBT Teachers against 15% quota meant for promotion of this category in the master cadre subject-wise in future.
It has also been stated that a period of six months will be required for undertaking the necessary exercise.
4.
On our asking the learned DAG, Punjab clarified that promotion quota posts will be filled in all future recruitments i.e. recruitments made from today onwards and the JBT Teachers will be given promotion subject-wise.
5.
In view of the affidavit filed on behalf of the respondents, we dispose of these petitions by directing that while making recruitments to the post of Masters in future, the respondents shall strictly adhere to the promotion quota of 15% and consider the eligible candidates subject-wise according to the criteria laid down under the rules.
In respect of the posts which are available as on date, this exercise shall be undertaken and completed within a period of six months from today.
