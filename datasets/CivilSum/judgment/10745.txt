Nirmal Yadav, J - The petitioners have filed this petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India for issuance of writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents not to recover the amount paid to the petitioners on account of fixation of pay by the respondents themselves as there is no misrepresentation or fraud on the part of the petitioners, in view of the law laid down by the Supreme Court in the case of Sahib Ram v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) SCT 668.
2.
The petitioners have been working as Additional Assistant Engineer with Bhakra Beas Management Board (hereinafter referred to as `the Board') with effect from 15.6.1995, 24.8.1984 and 12.1.1995, respectively.
They are governed by the regulation called The BBMB Class III and IV Employees (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Regulation, 1984.
The BBMB adopted the decision of the Punjab State Electricity Board to accept the recommendations of Fourth Pay Commission and Circular No. 341 dated 3041998 for revising the pay scales of its employees.
Under the said circular, options were invited from the employees for revised pay scales within a period of three months and the employees were alloted to keep the old pay scales for a certain period and opt for the revised pay scale after 111996.
The petitioners opted for `revised pay scales' under the aforesaid regulations as provided under the circular.
3.
The pay of petitioner No. 1 was fixed in the second time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700, which was further revised to Rs. 7250-11400 on completion of 16 years of service.
After passing the departmental examination, pay of petitioner No. 1 was fixed at Rs. 10350/- on 20.8.1998 with next date of increment as 151999 in the scale of Rs. 7500-13700.
However, in pursuance of letter of Special Secretary, BBMB, dated 572000 (Annexure P-3), the promotional scale of Rs. 7500-13700/- was withdrawn.
4.
The pay of petitioner No. 2 was fixed in the second time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700/-, which was further revised to 7250-11400 on completion of 16 years service w.e.f.
1.1.1990.
On passing the departmental examination on 2451997, he was placed in the scale of Rs. 2200-4250 (revised to 7500-13700) and his salary was fixed at Rs. 10350/-.
He was given basic pay of Rs. 12100/- after passing the departmental accounts examination in the month of May 1997.
It is pleaded that fixation of pay was duly verified by the Accounts Officer, BBMB, Talwara, but BBMB by misinterpreting para 5 of Circular No. 13/93 by replacing the word `jump' with the word `increment', reduced his pay.
5.
Petitioner No. 3 was allowed second time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700 which was further revised to Rs. 7250-11400/- on completion of 16 years of service on 521995.
He passed the departmental examination on 2562000 and his pay was fixed at Rs. 10350/- with next increment on 122001 raising his pay to Rs. 10700/-.
It is pleaded that respondents vide letter dated 1062003 intimated that due to change of date of increment on passing the departmental examination recovery of Rs. 21276/- is to be made.
6.
The petitioners submitted notices of demand for justice through their counsels to the respondents stating that after passing the departmental examination, the pay of the petitioners was fixed as per letter dated 672001 and sanction was also issued to this effect by the Special Secretary.
The petitioners have not misrepresented the facts before the authorities and, therefore, no recovery with regard to excess payment should be made.
In support, the petitioners have placed reliance on the judgment of the Supreme Court in the case of Sahib Ram v. State of Haryana and others, 1995 Suppl(1) Supreme Court Cases 18.
7.
The respondents have controverted the averments made by the petitioners and submitted that respondents have allowed time bound promotional scale on the pattern of Punjab State Electricity Board with respect to pay scales of Junior Engineers who have passed departmental examination on completion of 16 years regular service.
There are three categories of employees - (i) the Junior Engineers who pass the said examination before completion of 16 years service and become entitled to all the resultant benefits of the pay structure including jumps; (ii) the Junior Engineers who qualify the departmental accounts examination after completion of 16 years service, get second time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700 (unrevised) and are entitled only for placement in the scale of Rs. 2200-4250 on the date of passing of departmental accounts examination and the benefit of pay structural increments.
(iii) the Junior Engineers who fail to qualify the departmental accounts examination and only entitled to the ordinary time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700 (unrevised).
The petitioners fall in the second category because they have qualified the examination after completion of 16 years of service and got the second time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700.
Therefore, they would be entitled to placement in the pay scale of Rs. 2200-4250 on the date of passing of departmental accounts examination and pay structural benefits including jumps are payable to them only from the date of their annual increments.
It is, however, pleaded that due to inadvertence, the petitioners were allowed pay scale of Rs. 2200-4250/- with increments and jumps with effect from the date of passing of departmental accounts examination by them in stead of mere placement in the aforesaid pay scale.
After necessary clarification, their pay has been correctly refixed in the pay scale of Rs. 2200-4250 with effect from the date of their annual increment.
It is pleaded that the petitioners are not entitled to get their pay fixed in the second time bound promotional scale of Rs. 2100-3700 in terms of the official circular dated 1931999 as the same would be available only to those employees who had already availed of jump in the pay scale of Rs. 2100-3700/- on or before 111996 or in between 111996 to 3041998.
The petitioners passed the departmental accounts examination after 3041998.
Therefore, their cases were reviewed in view of the clarification given by Punjab State Electricity Board vide circular No. 15281/Fin/766/ Vol.2, dated 1052002.
8.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the documents on record.
9.
On careful consideration of the rival submissions, it comes out that pay of the petitioners was fixed in accordance with PSEB/Fin.Circular No. 13/93 dated 131993 (Annexure R-3) which was further clarified vide letters dated 2852001, 1052002 and 2442003, Annexure R-3, R-5 and R-7, respectively.
As per the circulars of the Punjab State Electricity Board, which have been adopted by the BBMB, it has been decided that the instructions contained in official circular No. 13/93 will continue to be followed, thereby a Junior Engineer is eligible for the post of Assistant Engineer in the Punjab State Electricity Board Engineering Service if has been granted the scale of Rs. 2100-3700/- after completion of 16 years service and he should be given placement in the scale of Assistant Engineer i.e. 2200-4250/- on his qualifying the departmental accounts examination afterwards without any benefit of promotional increment.
10.
In view of the above instructions, it emerges that the pay of the petitioners was wrongly fixed and, therefore, we are satisfied that there is no infirmity in the impugned orders concluding that the pay of the petitioners was inadvertently fixed.
11.
Despite the aforesaid conclusion, the question which needs to be answered is - whether the salary drawn by the petitioners on account of wrongful fixation of pay can be recovered from them ?
On this issue the learned counsel for the petitioners has placed reliance on the decision rendered by the Supreme Court in Sahib Ram's case (supra), wherein the Apex Court has observed as under :-  "5.
Admittedly the appellant does not possess the required educational qualifications.
Under the circumstances the appellant would not be entitled to the relaxation.
The Principal erred in granting him the relaxation.
Since the date of relaxation the appellant had been paid his salary on revised scale.
However, it is not on account of any misrepresentation made by the appellant that the benefit of higher pay-scale was given to him but by wrong construction made by the Principal for which the appellant cannot be held to be at fault.
Under the circumstances the amount paid till date may not be recovered from the appellant.
The principle of equal pay for equal work would not apply to the scales prescribed by the University Grants Commission.
The appeal is allowed partly without any order as to costs."
The Apex Court also examined the same issue in PH Reddy v.
N.T.R.D., 2002(2) SCT 987, wherein it was held in paragraph 2 that employees, who had been in receipt of a higher amount on account of erroneous fixation, could not be required to reimburse the excess amount paid to them although the authority was well within its rights to correct the error committed by it and to fix the pay of the employee in terms of his entitlement.
In the instant case, it is not disputed that the wrong fixation of pay of the petitioners was not based on any misrepresentation by the petitioners but was based on benefit having wrongly given by the respondents.
In view of the above, the challenge of the petitioners to the impugned orders is declined.
However, the respondents are restrained from effecting of the excess payment made to them.
Disposed of in the above terms.
Petition dismissed.
