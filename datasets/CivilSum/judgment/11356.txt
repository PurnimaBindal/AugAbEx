MR Agnihotri, J - This judgment shall dispose of three Civil Writ Petitions Nos. 681 of 1980 (Raghuvinder Sahai v. The State of Punjab and another), No. 1021 of 1980 (Amrit Paul v. The State of Punjab and others) and No. 16724 of 1990 (Sri Krishan Kapania v. The State of Punjab and others), as common questions of fact and law are involved in all these cases.
2.
In fact, the legal position in all these cases is squarely covered by a Division Bench judgment of this Court, which is a judgment inter parties, in L.P.A. No. 65 of 1975, dated 4th December, 1978, reported as 1979(1) SLR 851 - (Raghuvinder Sahai v. Siri Krishan Kapania and others) reversing the Single Bench decision.
Their Lordships of the Division Bench allowed the appeal and held that the incumbents of the isolated posts like Hindi/Punjabi Typists, Receptionists, Telephone Operators, etc. did not in fact belong to any cadre of the service of the Punjab Vidhan Sabha and there was nothing illegal in retaining the cadre of Clerks separate from the incumbents of the aforesaid posts.
In pursuance of the aforesaid Division Bench judgment, separate seniority lists of Clerks on the one hand and Typists and others on the other, were to be prepared by the Secretary of the Punjab Vidhan Sabha and promotions to the higher posts in the distinct lines, e.g.
Assistants, Senior Assistants, Deputy Superintendent, etc. on the one side and Stenographers, Personal Assistants, etc. on the other, were to be made separately.
3.
Adverting to the facts of C.W.P.
No. 681 of 1980, petitioner Raghuvinder Sahai joined service as a Clerk in Punjab Vidhan Sabha Secretariat on 5th November, 1962, and was confirmed as such on 15th September, 1966.
He was promoted as Assistant on 22nd November, 1973, and was continuing as such when he was reverted to the post of Clerk by Office Order No. 1 of 1980, dated 18th January, 1980 (Annexure P.1).
Operation of the reversion order was stayed by the Division Bench on 4th March, 1980, and the petitioner had been continuing as such since then.
4.
In the written statement, though the factual position has almost been admitted, yet the reversion of the petitioner is sought to be justified on the ground that on 18th January, 1980, two office orders No. 1 of 1980 and No. 2 of 1980 were issued.
The first one was regarding reversion of the petitioner Raghuvinder Sahai from the post of Assistant and the second was for the integration of the cadres of Clerks and Hindi/Punjabi/English Typists and preparation of joint seniority list based on continuous length of service as Clerks/Typists for the purpose of promotion to the posts of Assistants.
In this regard, reproduction of relevant extract from para 11 of the written statement would be useful.
".......
The averment that the order being arbitrary and discriminatory, violates the provisions of Article 14 of the Constitution of India is not legally tenable for the reasons already stated.
However, the statement that 'decision to prepare joint seniority list of Clerks and Hindi/Punjabi/English Typists was simultaneously taken by Hon'ble Speaker on 1811980 vide office order No. 2 of 1980' is admitted as correct.
It is a fact that office Order Nos. 1 and 2 were part of the same process/exercise.
Office order No. 2 could not have been implemented without giving effect to office order No. 1 of 1980."
5.
After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, I find that the impugned order of reversion of Raghuvinder Sahai petitioner dated 18th January, 1980, is wholly untenable in law.
The petitioner was promoted as Assistant with effect from 22nd November, 1973, and had been continuing as such when abruptly the said order of his reversion was passed.
No doubt, the petitioner was officiating as Assistant but the order was not an innocuous order of reversion as a consequence of the assessment of the performance of the petitioner.
Rather, it was considered as a necessary consequence of the order of amalgamation of the cadres of Clerks and Typists and the preparation of a joint seniority list.
As in view of the Division Bench judgment in L.P.A. No. 65 of 1975 (supra), the cadres of Clerks and Typists were to be kept separate, there was no question of preparation of joint seniority list of the two cadres.
The petitioner having been promoted as Assistant in 1973, was entitled to continue as such unless some posts were abolished or Assistants, senior to him had been reverted.
Since none of the two exigencies took place, the reversion of the petitioner is violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, being arbitrary and discriminatory.
6.
Consequently, C.W.P.
No. 681 of 1980 is allowed and the order of reversion of the petitioner dated 18th January, 1980 (Office Order No. 1 of 1980 - Annexure P.1 ), is hereby quashed.
No consequential relief is necessary to be granted to the petitioner as operation of the impugned order of reversion was stayed by the Motion Bench and the petitioner has been continuing as Assistant since then.
Still, it is directed that as a consequence of the acceptance of the writ petition and the quashing of the said impugned order, the petitioner shall be entitled to the arrears of salary and allowances, etc. along with benefits of seniority and promotion on the basis of his continuous length of service as Clerk from 5th November, 1962, an as Assistant from 22nd November, 1973, to which he would have been entitled under the rules, had the impugned order of reversion dated 18th January, 1980, not been passed.
There shall be no order as to costs.
7.
As regards C.W.P.
No. 1021 of 1980 (Amrit Paul v. The State of Punjab and others) in fact the writ petition has become infructuous as it has been filed against Office Order No. 2 of 1980, dated 18th January, 1980, by which a joint seniority list of Clerks and Hindi/Punjabi/English Typists was ordered to be prepared.
In view of the above-referred to Division Bench judgment of this Court in L.P.A. No. 65 of 1975 fresh separate seniority list was necessary to be drawn, which exercise has since been undertaken.
Consequently, C.W.P.
No. 1021 of 1980 is disposed of with the direction that ignoring the impugned seniority list circulated vide Office Order No. 2 of 1980, dated 18th January, 1980, the Secretary, Punjab Vidhan Sabha Secretariat, respondent No. 3, is again directed to implement the Division Bench judgment (supra) and prepare a fresh seniority list in accordance with the law laid down in the said judgment and the service rules governing the conditions of service of the employees of the Punjab Vidhan Sabha Secretariat, and to make promotions to the higher posts in the separate cadres on the basis of separate seniority lists.
Since the impugned Office Order No. 2 of 1980 is quashed, any promotions made during the pendency of this writ petition shall be reshuffled, if necessary, to conform to the principles laid down in the aforesaid Division Bench judgment.
There shall, however, be no order as to costs.
8.
So far as C.W.P.
No. 16724 of 1990 (Siri Krishan Kapania v. The State of Punjab and others) is concerned, it is in the nature of a cross-petition basing the claim for promotion by taking in view the entry as Hindi Typist in the Punjab Vidhan Sabha Secretariat on 17th February, 1961, and deriving the benefit of joint seniority list with clerks on the basis of length of service.
The petitioner in the present case - Siri Krishan Kapania, who was a respondent in L.P.A. No. 65 of 1975 (supra), having already lost the battle, cannot raise the issue over again in this petition, which is wholly devoid of force.
Consequently, the challenge to Office Order No. 14 of 1980, dated 4th September, 1980 (Annexure P.2) and Officer Order No. 48 of 1990, dated 6th December, 1990 (Annexure P.4) is wholly without any merit and the same stands repelled.
Resultantly, C.W.P.
No. 16724 of 1990 is dismissed, with no order as to costs.
Orders accordingly.
