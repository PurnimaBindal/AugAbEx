SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - With the consent of counsel for the parties, the writ petition is taken up for final disposal at the motion stage today itself.
2.
The petitioner joined as a Doctor in Haryana Civil Medical Service (H.C.M.S.)
in September, 1982.
After putting in 20 years of service, she sought voluntary retirement by giving three months notice.
The notice given by the petitioner was accepted and the petitioner was relieved on 16122002.
By letter dated 432003, the petitioner was directed to submit all papers regarding pension and pensionary benefits through the Office of Senior Medical Officer, Community Health Centre, Pehowa to the Office of Civil Surgeon, Kurukshetra for necessary action.
The complete case of the petitioner was submitted by the S.M.O., Pehowa through letter dated 1532003 to the Office of the Civil Surgeon, Kurukshetra.
On 552003, the petitioner served a legal notice on the respondents as the retiral benefits had not been released.
Thereafter, the respondents released the benefits as follows :-  Date of Release Amount  242004 (Leave Encashment) Rs. 1,27,286/- 9.4.2004 (Gratuity) Rs. 2,48,548/- 1672004 (Gratuity) Rs. 27,617/- 1542004 (G.P.F.)
Rs. 7,53,973/- 152004 (Pension Arrears) Rs. 1,44,935/- 3.
The petitioner was denied the benefit of commutation of pension and Group Insurance Scheme on the ground that she has not applied for commutation of pension within six months.
The petitioner submitted a representation against the aforesaid order on 2742004.
She stated that she had applied for commutation of pension within six months period as the complete case pertaining to her pension had been submitted to the competent authority through letter dated 1532003.
Even then the claim of the petitioner has been rejected by the Office of the Accountant General, Haryana by letter dated 2742004.
In the aforesaid order, it has been mentioned that the claim for commutation of pension has to be applied for within six months after retirement.
In the case of the petitioner, it was applied on 27112003 which does not fall within six months.
In this writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner seeks the issuance of a writ in the nature of Certiorari quashing the aforesaid order (Annexure P-5) and for the issuance of a writ in the nature of Mandamus directing the respondents to release the benefit of commutation of pension and Group Insurance Scheme.
4.
The respondents have filed a written statement.
It has been stated that the petitioner did not complete her pension papers within six months.
The delay, if any, has been caused by the petitioner, and therefore, she cannot claim any interest on the delayed payment of the retiral benefits.
5.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the record.
A perusal of the facts narrated above would clearly indicate that the petitioner sought voluntary retirement which was accepted by the respondents on 16122002.
She sent her complete case for pension through letter dated 1532003.
Therefore, the retiral benefits could not be denied to the petitioner.
Although the respondents have mentioned that the petitioner did not apply for commutation till 27112003, the respondents have not put any such letter on the record.
On the other hand, the respondents have attached communication dated 432003, 2742004 and 23.11.2004.
If any such letter dated 27112003 had been received by the respondents, the same would have been placed on record.
Even otherwise, we see no justification for denying the retiral benefits to the petitioner merely on the ground of delay in submission of the pension papers.
The respondents cannot be permitted to raise technical objection to deprive the petitioner of the retiral benefits.
Having considered the entire matter, we are of the opinion that the writ petition deserves to be allowed.
6.
In view of the above, the writ petition is allowed.
The order passed by the Office of Accountant General dated 2742004 (Annexure P-5) is hereby quashed.
Writ of mandamus is issued directing the respondents to release the commuted pension and the amount due in the Group Insurance Scheme.
Let the amount be released to the petitioner within a period of three months of the receipt of a certified copy of this order.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to interest at the rate of 8% per annum from the date she retired till the release of the pensionary benefits.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
