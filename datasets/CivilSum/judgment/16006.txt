VK Jhanji, J. (Oral) - This shall dispose of Civil Writ Petitions No. 12928, 18511, 14562 and 18508 of 1994.
2.
In these writ petitions, petitioners have prayed for issue of an appropriate direction to the respondents to grant them the same salary and allowances as are being paid to the persons holding similar posts in the regular pay scales, with effect from the date of their appointment.
In reply, respondents have submitted that the petitioners were engaged on various posts on daily wages/muster-roll and thus, are not entitled to the same salary and allowances as are being paid to the persons holding similar posts in the regular pay scales.
3.
The stand of the respondents cannot be accepted in view of judgment of the Apex Court in Surinder Singh and another v. Engineer-in-Chief C.P.W.D.
and others, AIR 1986 SC 584 and a Division Bench judgment of this Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 592 of 1994 decided on 281994.
Petitioners, thus, cannot be denied the payment of salary and allowances as are being paid to the persons holding similar posts on regular basis.
No judgment taking a contrary view has been cited by the counsel for the respondents.
4.
Accordingly, these writ petitions are allowed with a direction to the respondents to pay to the petitioners the same salary and allowances as are being paid to the regular employees holding similar posts.
Arrears, if any, shall be restricted to only 36 months upto the date of filing of writ petitions.
No costs.
