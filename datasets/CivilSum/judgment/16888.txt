Mehtab S Gill, J - The petitioner has prayed for the issuance of a writ in the nature of certiorari for quashing the impugned order dated June 6, 1989 (Annexure P-16) passed by respondent No. 1, i.e. the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana, Development and Panchayat Department proposing a 5% cut in his pension on account of loss caused to Government by him.
The petitioner has further prayed for the issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing the respondent Nos. 1 and 2 to promote him as Joint Director or on equivalent post prior to the date of promotion of his juniors, i.e. respondent Nos. 4 and 5.
He has further prayed for the issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus directing respondent Nos. 1 and 2 to release the pensionary benefits to him.
2.
It has been averred in the writ petition that the petitioner and respondent Nos. 4 and 5 had been working as Block Development and Panchayat Officers in the State of Haryana.
According to the Gradation List issued on January 1, 1987, the petitioner was at serial No. 3 whereas respondent Nos. 4 and 5 were at serial Nos. 5 and 6 respectively.
It has been further averred that Ch.
Bhajan Lal was an M.L.A.
from Adampur Constituency in 1978-79 and was inimical towards the petitioner.
On becoming the Chief Minister, Haryana, he suspended the petitioner on July 30, 1979 and a false case under Section 5(i)(d) of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947 (hereinafter referred to as "the Act") was got registered against him.
A copy of the letter dated April 9, 1980 issued by the Secretary to Government, Haryana, Development and Panchayat Department (respondent No. 1) to the Deputy Commissioner, Hisar, is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-1.
An enquiry was held and a charge-sheet was issued to the petitioner.
3.
It has been further averred that the petitioner was acquitted in the Corruption case No. 9 of 1983 registered against him vide F.I.R. No. 144 dated April 20, 1980, Police Station Sadar, Hisar under Section 5(i)(d) punishable under Section 5(2) of the Act and under Section 161, Indian Penal Code, by the Special Judge, Hisar vide his judgment dated March 2, 1985, a copy of which is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-3.
The petitioner was charge- sheeted vide memorandum dated May 12, 1980 (Annexure P-4).
A regular enquiry was also conducted by the Enquiry Officer, who submitted his report on March 3, 1987.
In this enquiry, the petitioner was exonerated of all the charges levelled against him, but the Commissioner and Secretary to Government of Haryana (respondent No. 1) did not agree on one charge at serial No. 3 and issued a show cause notice dated July 2, 1987 (Annexure P-5).
The petitioner replied to the abovesaid show cause notice.
Vide order dated August 6, 1987 (Annexure P-6) of the Commissioner and Secretary to Government Haryana, Development and Panchayat Department, the petitioner was exonerated of all the charges and the period of suspension from July 30, 1979 to February 15, 1983 was treated as duty period for all intents and purposes with regard to the increments, leave and pension etc.  4.
It has been further averred that respondent Nos. 4 and 5 were promoted during the years 1981 and 1983 respectively as District Development and Panchayat Officers and further promoted on October 13, 1987 to the point of Joint Director, Development and Panchayat Officers.
Shri BD Kalia (respondent No. 4) was posted as Principal State Community Development Training Centre at Nilokheri and Shri Hardev Singh (respondent No. 5) was posted as Senior Deputy Director in the office of Director Panchayats, Haryana, Chandigarh.
The copies of the promotion orders of respondent Nos. 4 and 5 dated October 13, 1987 are annexed with the writ petition as Annexures P-7 and P-8 respectively.
The Annual Confidential Report of the petitioner for the year 1985-86 was spoiled.
Copy of the letter communicating the adverse remarks recorded in the Annual Confidential Report of the petitioner is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-9.
The adverse remarks were conveyed to the petitioner.
The petitioner made a representation against the adverse remarks recorded in his Annual Confidential Report on April 29, 1987 and the Government vide its order dated May 26, 1988 conveyed to the petitioner that the adverse remarks recorded against him had been expunged.
Copy of the order dated May 26, 1988 is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-10.
The petitioner made a representation on December 21, 1987 to the Government about his promotion over and above his juniors, namely, respondent Nos. 4 and 5, a copy of which is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-11.
5.
Inspite of deciding the representation, the petitioner was again placed under suspension on April 27, 1988 with effect from May 2, 1988 without any reason.
The Deputy Commissioner, Narnaul wrote a letter dated October 31, 1988 to the Government that the petitioner was not at fault and he was thus, reinstated on November 21, 1988.
The copies of the suspension and the reinstatement orders are annexed with the writ petition as Annexures P-12 and P-13.
It has been further averred that the Selection Grade was granted to the petitioner with effect from March 1, 1977 by the Government of Haryana vide its order dated November 10, 1986, a copy of which is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-14.
The petitioner was served with a notice/Charge-sheet under Rule 8 of the Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987 along with a charge-sheet in which seven charges were levelled against the petitioner.
The petitioner replied to the charge-sheet on August 16, 1988.
After considering the reply, the Commissioner and Secretary to Government, Haryana (respondent No. 1) passed an order dated March 9, 1989 vide which the period of suspension of the petitioner from May 2, 1988 to November 29, 1988 was treated as duty period, a copy of which is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-15.
6.
It has been further averred that the petitioner, in the meanwhile, retired from service and a notice was sent to him on August 4, 1989.
The petitioner replied to the said notice.
In the memorandum dated June 6, 1989 the Government had proposed to make a 5% cut in the pension on account of the loss caused to the Government by the petitioner, a copy of which is annexed with the writ petition as Annexure P-16.
The petitioner sent his reply to the said letter on September 14, 1989 (Annexure P-17).
Notice of motion was issued.
Written statement was filed.
I have heard the learned Counsel for the petitioner and the respondents.
7.
During the pendency of this writ petition, the petitioner died on December 10, 1990 and his son, namely, Rajeev Marwaha stepped into his shoe.
Vide order dated April 19, 2001 of this Court, he being the legal representative, was impleaded as petitioner in place of his deceased father Shri Satya Paul Marwaha in the writ petition.
8.
Learned Counsel for the petitioner has stated that all along the petitioner was victimised by respondent No. 3 and his service record was intentionally and deliberately spoiled.
A false case under Section 5(i)(d) of the Act was registered against the petitioner wherein he was acquitted by the Special Judge, Hisar vide his judgment dated March 2, 1985 (Annexure P-3).
9.
Learned Counsel for the respondents-State has stated that the petitioner had retired on March 31, 1989, and no pension or gratuity could be granted to him for the reason that the disciplinary proceedings were pending against him and ultimately a cut of 5% was imposed on his pension.
He has further stated that as the petitioner was suspended by the Government because a case under Section 5(i)(d) of the Act was registered against him, he could not be given pensionary benefits.
10.
Since the petitioner has been acquitted by the Special Judge, Hisar in the case bearing F.I.R. No. 144 dated April 20, 1980, PS Sadar Hisar under Section 5(i)(d) punishable under Section 5(2) of the Act and under Section 161 IPC and the adverse remarks which were recorded in his Annual Confidential Report for the year 1985-86 have also been expunged, I do not see any reason as to why the pension and the arrears of the petitioner were withheld.
11.
So far as the question of promotion of the petitioner is concerned.
I will not be touching this point as he is no more alive and the matter cannot be dealt with as of today.
12.
For the reasons recorded above, the writ petition is allowed.
The impugned order dated June 6, 1989 (Annexure P-16) is quashed.
The respondents are directed to pay the pensionary benefits and arrears without imposing any cut of 5% in pension of the deceased petitioner, to his son Rajeev Marwaha, who is the only legal representative of the deceased, within a period of three months.
Petition allowed.
