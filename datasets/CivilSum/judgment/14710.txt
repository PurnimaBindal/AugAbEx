Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - Vide orders dated June 19, 1985, the petitioner was posted in Maili Division at Hoshiarpur.
This order was not carried out by the authorities concerned for a long time.
The petitioner was constrained to approach this Court.
He filed C.W.P.
No. 3645 of 1986.
After the issue of notice of motion, the respondents appeared and stated that posting orders have been passed.
As a result the petition was rendered infructuous.
The petitioner was kept `compulsorily waiting' from June 19, 1985 to September 12, 1986.
He claimed pay and allowances for this period.
Vide letter dated September 15, 1987 (Annexure P-4), he was called upon "to apply for the leave of the kind due for the regularisation of the period in question."
Aggrieved by the order of the respondents, the petitioner has approached this Court through the present writ petition.
It has been averred that the petitioner had reported for duty immediately after the passing of the order of his posting on June 19, 1985 and the action of the respondents in calling upon him to apply for the leave was wholly unjustified and unfair as the petitioner could not be made to suffer for the fault of the officials of the Government.
2.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents it has been inter alia averred that "the petitioner neither submitted his arrival report in the office of the Director, Planning and Design, Chandigarh nor in the office of the Executive Engineer, Maili Construction Division, Hoshiarpur where he was supposed to join in accordance with the orders at Annexure P.1" It has been further mentioned that the Chief Engineer has written to the Government "to grant sanction in respect of the compulsory waiting period of the petitioner from 19685 to 12986 as per Annexures R-3/1 and R-3/2 respectively."
3.
I have heard learned counsel for the parties.
The letter at Annexure R-3/1, which appears to have been sent by the Chief Engineer to the Government on April 10, 1989 reads as under :-  "In this connection, it is intimated that vide this Office order No. 6134/3NGE-1/21/47/85, dated 19685, Sh. Santokh Singh, DHD was posted in Maili Const.
Division, Hoshiarpur, but the Director, Plg.
and Design Studies, IB Punjab, did not accept him and referred back the case.
He was advised time and again to implement the orders issued by this office but to no avail.
Ultimately the matter was brought to the notice of Government vide this office letter No. 1497/3NGE-1/86, dated 26286 whereupon Punjab Govt. issued necessary directions to the Chief Engineer/KAD and Director Plg.
and Design Studies vide their Memo No. 15/39/86-5-IPPII/6879, dated 17386 to implement the posting orders of Sh. Santokh Singh, DHD already issued.
But this too has had no effect.
At last Govt. had finally decided vide their Memo No. 15/39/86-51PPII/10168, dated 7.5.86 to adjust Sh. Santokh Singh DHD at Hoshiarpur where he was previously transferred but could not join and it was conveyed to Chief Engineer/KAD, vide this offices No. 6091/3NGE-1/86, dated 12686.
However, even then the Divisional Head Draftsman could not be adjusted by the KAD Administration.
Sh. Santokh Singh, DHD then filed a Civil Writ Petition No. 3645 of 1986 seeking direction for implementing his posting orders earlier issued.
During the pendency of the Writ Petition, Chief Engineer/KAD had passed orders adjusting Shri Santokh Singh, DHD at Hoshiarpur vide his order No. 424/CE/KAD/Misc.
dated 9986 and accordingly the writ petition was disposed of with costs on 22986.
The official was advised to apply for leave for the period from 19685 to 12986 but he did not do so and claimed/requested to treat the period as of compulsory waiting.
The writ petition filed by Sh. Santokh Singh, DHD is pending and is likely to come up for hearing at any time.
Under the circumstances explained above, it is requested that Government approval treating the period from 19685 to 12986 as a compulsory waiting in respect of Sh. Santokh Singh, DHD, may kindly be conveyed at an early date to avoid further complication."
4.
A perusal of the above letter clearly belies the stand taken on behalf of the respondents to the effect that the petitioner had not submitted his arrival report in the office of the Director or in the office of the Executive Engineer.
This letter, which appears to have been issued under the signature of the Administrative Officer, shows that the joining report of the petitioner was not accepted by the Director.
The statement of fact made in the affidavit is obviously against the record.
Further it is equally unfortunate that the matter has been pending with the Government since April, 1989 and the needful has not been done to date.
The petitioner has been forced to approach this Court for the second time for no fault of his.
5.
Once the petitioner has reported for duty, the officer concerned was bound to allow him to join.
His failure to do so cannot be used to prejudicially affect the interests of the petitioner.
The leave earned by the petitioner can be used by him at his choice.
The department cannot force him to apply for leave for the period during which he was actually waiting for his posting orders to be implemented.
The direction contained in the letter of September 15, 1987 was wholly untenable and unwarranted by the circumstances of the case.
It is accordingly quashed.
6.
The period from June 19, 1985 to September 12, 1986 has to be treated as duty for all intents and purposes.
The petitioner is entitled to the payment of salary and allowances for the period.
Consequently, this writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to pay the petitioner arrears of salary for the period in question.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to interest @ 12% from the date of the actual of the salary till the date of actual payment.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to his costs, which are assessed at Rs. 3,000/-.
The needful shall be done within three months from the date of the receipt of a copy of this judgment.
Petition allowed.
