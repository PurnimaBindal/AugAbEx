Amarjeet Chaudhary, J - The petitioners, who are working as Junior Engineers in the P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) have filed this writ petition jointly under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India wherein they have sought for quashing the order dated 2711983 so far as it relates to the promotion of respondent Nos. 3 to 7 as Officiating Sub Divisional Officers and have claimed promotion w.e.f.
2711983, the date from which the persons junior to them were promoted.
2.
In brief, the case of the petitioners is that the joined as Sectional Officers in Punjab P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) before 1960.
In the seniority list corrected upto 31121980, issued by the State of Punjab, their names figure at serial Nos. 422, 432, 455, 456 and 457, whereas in the seniority list corrected upto 31.12.1973, the names of respondent Nos. 3 to 7 appear at serial Nos. 578, 589, 628 and 647.
Extract of both these seniority lists are Annexures P-1 and P-1/A to the writ petition.
3.
It has been contended that the petitioners have 10 years experience as Overseers and had qualified the Revenue Examination on 3rd November, 1982 and also cleared the Professional Examination as required under Sub-rule (2) of the Rule 5 of PSE Class-II (PWD Irrigation) Rules, 1941.
For promotion to the post of Sub-Divisional Officer (Class-II) 35% quota is reserved for overseers.
It is further contended that on January 27, 1983, 12 persons were promoted vide order 2711983, copy Annexure P-6 to the writ petition, out of which five were senior and the remaining seven were junior to them.
Though the petitioners were senior to respondent Nos. 3 to 7 and eligible for being promoted to the post of Sub Division Officer, yet they were not considered and brought on the selection list.
In response to the letter dated 18111982 of the Chief Engineer, Irrigation Works, Punjab, Chandigarh, respondent No. 2, the service data of the petitioner was sent and thereafter they made representations to respondent Nos. 1 and 2 and a registered notice was also served upon respondent No. 1.
Despite all this, the petitioners were not promoted to the post of sub Divisional Officers.
4.
The star argument of the learned Counsel for the petitioners is that on declaration of the result of departmental examination held in June, 1982, on 3111982, the petitioners became eligible for promotion to the post of Sub Divisional Officers, but they were not considered on one pretext or the other.
According to the learned Counsel, the action of the respondents in not considering the case of the petitioners for promotion is arbitrary and against the principles of natural justice.
5.
Respondent Nos. 1 and 2 have filed detailed written statement controverting the pleas raised by the petitioners.
6.
I have considered the respective submissions of the parties and perused the paper-book.
7.
The perusal of the paper-book clearly reveals that the petitioners became eligible for promotion to the rank of Officiating Sub Divisional Officers after qualifying the Departmental Examinations on 3111982.
The case for promotion of officiating Sub Divisional Officer was taken up in July, 1982 after collecting the service data from the field officers.
At the relevant time, the petitioners were not at all eligible for being considered for the post of officiating Sub Divisional Officer.
By the time they became eligible, the entire selection process had completed.
It was only on 3111982, that the petitioners had acquired eligibility for promotion to the rank of officiating Sub Divisional Officers i.e. only five to 9 days prior to the issuance of promotion orders.
At the relevant time the case of eligible candidates had since been considered by the respondent-department.
Subsequently, the respondent had promoted such Junior Engineers whose cases were not considered due to their involvement in disciplinary proceedings or due to late receipt of record/recommendations from the field officers.
As regards the promotions made vide order dated 17/2021984 which relates to Drawing Establishment Officers, the petitioner could not be considered along with those candidates as they belonged to different category.
As such, the petitioners had no right to be considered for promotion with that category.
8.
In para 8 of the written statement, the respondents have admitted in clear terms that the petitioners are eligible for promotion, but owing to non-availability of the vacancies their names for promotion could not be considered at that stage.
In para 12 of the written statement, it has been stated that the petitioners can be considered on the availability of the vacancies.
9.
The other important aspect of the matter is that all alleged share of promotion of Junior Engineers who were promoted in accordance with the provisions of P.S.E.
Class II rules, is 18% out of overall 35% for promotees, whereas the actual posts already filled up at that time from the category of Junior Engineers (Civil) was above 50 per cent and the Government had withdrawn promotion orders issued during 1982 against the quota of Temporary Engineers.
10.
For the foregoing reasons, I do not find any merit in this writ petition and the same is dismissed with the observation that as and when promotions are made, petitioners shall also be considered.
No order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
