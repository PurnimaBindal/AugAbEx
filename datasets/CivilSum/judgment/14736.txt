Ashok Bhan, J - Petitioner filed C.W.P.
No. 2865 of 1990 for regularisation of his services in view of Division Bench judgment of this Court in Piara Singh v. State of Haryana, 1988(4) SLR 739.
On 731990, Division Bench of this Court passed the following order :-  The petitioner is seeking regularisation of his services in view of the judgment in Piara Singh's case, 1988(4) SLR 739.
According to the learned counsel representation Annexure P/1 in this behalf was made to authority.
Under the circumstances the authority will consider the representation and pass appropriate order thereon within six months from today.
Status quo regarding the services of the petitioner shall continue meanwhile.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly."
2.
Petitioner filed the present contempt petition with the allegations that the directions given by this Court on 731990 have not been complied with.
This allegation of the petitioner has been refuted by the respondents and it is stated that services of the petitioner stood terminated on 28.12.1990 i.e. before the order dated 731990 had been passed.
3.
It is further stated that representation filed by the petitioner was decided on 26101990 by passing a speaking order copy of which has been placed as Annexure P/5 to the written statement.
In view of the averments contained in the written statement, there is no merit in this petition.
Rule is discharged.
Petition disposed of.
