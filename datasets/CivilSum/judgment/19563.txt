RS Mongia, J (Oral) - It is not necessary to state any facts in the present case as according to me, the writ petition has become infructuous.
The primary prayer made in the writ petition which was filed in the year 1989 is in the following terms :-  "a writ in the nature of mandamus be issued to the respondents to consider the claim of the petitioner for the post of Director Research, Director Extension Education, and Dean, College of Agriculture."
2.
During the pendency of the writ petition the petitioner has been made Dean, College of Agriculture, Haryana Agricultural University, Hissar with effect from 281992 and therefore the writ petition has become infructuous and I dispose of the same as such without any order as to costs.
3.
The learned counsel for the petitioner however, submits that the tenure of the office of Dean is of three years, but because of the belated action on the part of the University, he would be deprived of tenure of three years as Dean, because he is to superannuate on 3131994.
It will be open to the petitioner to make appropriate representation for extension/re-employment in service and I have no doubt that if any such representation is made, the University will consider the same in accordance with the Rules and Regulations of the University.
Order accordingly.
