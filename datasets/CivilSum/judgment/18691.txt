C.W.P.
No. 2746 of 2002  2.
We dispose of this writ petition with liberty to the petitioner to approach the State Government for redressal of her grievance as the petitioner relied upon certain compelling circumstances which make it difficult for her to move out of the present place of posting.
If such a representation is moved, the State Government shall pass appropriate order in accordance with the guidelines and rules relating to the subject within a period of three months from the date such representation with a copy of this order is brought to their notice.
The petitioner will, however, not be disturbed till the conclusion of the present academic Session, i.e., Ist May, 2002.
3.
The petition is disposed of accordingly.
4.
Copy of the order be given dasti to the learned counsel for the petitioner.
