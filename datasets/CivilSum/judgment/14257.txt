There was an L.P.A. by the petitioner.
That was withdrawn.
Later on, the petitioner filed an application for amendment introducing necessary averments in respect of his case of mala fides.
In the amended writ petition, he has come forward with the following reliefs :-  "a) issue an appropriate writ, order or direction declaring the action of the respondent in transferring the petitioner from Delhi to Chennai as illegal, unconstitutional and violative of petitioner's fundamental rights guaranteed under Articles 14, 15 and 16 of the Constitution; b) issue a writ of certiorari or any other writ, order or direction quashing the impugned order of transfer dated 15121997 issued by the respondents; c) issue writ of mandamus directing the respondents not to implement the transfer order dated 15121997; d) issue a writ of prohibition restraining the respondent from transferring the petitioner from Delhi to Chennai which is highly mala fide and vindictive in nature; e) issue a writ of mandamus also directing the respondent to grant the revised pay scale and time scale promotion and promotion to the next higher post i.e.
Manager, Personal and Administration."
2.
The main grievance of the petitioner was against the order of transfer which provoked him to file the writ petition.
While doing so, he has also claimed the relief with reference to the revision of scale of pay and his case being considered for promotion to the next higher rank.
In view of the fact that even at the stage of admission relief is restricted only to the claim of revision of pay and his consideration for promotion, the petitioner cannot seek to challenge the order of transfer made by the respondents.

24.
The point is taken by the NAFED that it is not amenable to writ jurisdiction as it is not an authority within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution of India.
NAFED Processed Foods Employees Union and others v. Union of India and others, in 2000(1) SCT 751 (Delhi) : CW 6239/98 decided on 19.7.1999, the Hon'ble Mr. Justice Mukul Mudgal took the view that NAFED is amenable to the jurisdiction.
I respectfully agree with the view taken by the learned Judge.
