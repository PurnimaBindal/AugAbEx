RL Anand, J - We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with his assistance have gone through the record of the case.
2.
The learned counsel for the petitioner has vehemently submitted that the impugned order Annexure P-6 is liable to be quashed on the ground of discrimination as the case of Shri RC Mittal, SDO was also on similar footing.
3.
We are not in a position to accept the submission of the learned counsel for the petitioner.
Mr. KK Kathpalia, the present petitioner was directly responsible for the excess payment to the contractor and the finding of the punishing authority is that such payment has been made with a malafide intention in order to cause loss to the Government.
4.
It was then submitted by the counsel for the petitioner that the impugned order Annexure P-6 is non-speaking order.
This submission of the counsel for the petitioner is also not substantiated from the record.

2.
Shri KK Kathpalia, Sub Divisional Officer while submitting his reply to the above chargesheet had denied the allegations levelled against him.
His reply was not found satisfactory.
The Inquiry Officer (Vigilance) was appointed as departmental Inquiry Officer vide Government order dated 21.10.1986 issued vide letter No. 17/41/82-51E, dated 21.10.1986 to hold regular departmental enquiry in the matter.
After considering the enquiry report, a 2nd show cause voice was issued to him for stoppage of two graded annual increments with cumulative effect besides recovery of Rs. 3565850 vide Government No. 17/41/82/31E dated 2281991.
The reply to this notice was submitted by Shri KK Kathpalia, Sub Divisional Officer which was also considered by Government and was not found satisfactory.
It has been found that excess payment has been made to the contractor by Shri KK Kathpalia Sub Divisional Officer.
Therefore, the allegations levelled against Shri KK Kathpalia stand substantiated.
Keeping all the facts and circumstances in view, the Governor of Haryana has decided to impose the punishment of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect besides the recovery of Rs. 35,65850 upon Shri KK Kathpalia, Sub Divisional Officer and his A.C.R.
for the corresponding period is downgraded to "Average"  5.
The reading of the above would show that the punishing authority fully applied its mind before passing the order in question and it was also decided that the wrong payment has been made by the petitioner.
Resultantly, there is no merit in this writ petition and the same is hereby dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
