RS Mongia, J (Oral) - For the reliefs claimed in the present writ petition, the petitioner had also made a representation dated 2871986 to the respondents, copy of which has been attached as Annexure P-2 to the writ petition.
The.
learned counsel appearing on behalf of the respondents states that the said representation would be decided by the District Education Officer.
Patiala, who according to the learned counsel is the appropriate authority, within a period of three months from today.
The learned counsel for the petitioner, however, submits that in fact it is DPI (Schools) Punjab, who is to decide the representation.
2.
I dispose of the writ petition by directing the DPI (Schools) Punjab to decide the representation of the petitioner, Annexure P-2 within a period of three months from the date of receipt of this order, by passing a speaking order.
He would also take into consideration the facts mentioned in Annexures P-4 and P-6 to the writ petition.
The petitioner would be at liberty to supplement the representation, Annexure P-2, within a fortnight from today.
The same would also be taken into consideration alongwith Annexure P2.
The office is directed to send a copy of this order to DPI (Schools) Punjab, Chandigarh forthwith, alongwith copies of Annexure P-2, P-4 and P-6.
If in the opinion of the DPI (Schools), the District Education Officer, Patiala is the appropriate authority to decide the representation of the petitioner then authority to decide the representation of the petitioner, then copy of this order alongwith the representations of the petitioner, Annexures P-2, P-4 and P-6 be transmitted by the DPI (Schools) to the District Education Officer, Patiala for disposal of the representation.
3.
The writ petition stands disposed of in the above terms.
No costs.
Order accordingly.
