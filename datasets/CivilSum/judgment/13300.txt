Mehtab S Gill, J - Petitioner has filed a writ in the nature of certiorari quashing the award dated March 20, 2000 (Annexure P-5) passed by the Presiding Officer, Industrial Tribunal-cum-Labour Court, Hisar.
2.
Petitioner has alleged that he had been working in the department of respondent No. 2 as Beldar since January 1, 1990 on daily wage basis.
He was continuously working there till September 6, 1991 and thereafter, his services were terminated in violation of the mandatory provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (hereinafter referred to as the `Act').
He made a representation, but when his demand was not met, he issued a Demand Notice on August 4, 1994 (Annexure P-1) stating therein that he had completed 240 days and was entitled to compensation.
He further stated that junior persons were retained in service when he was retrenched.
3.
The Labour Court after going through the evidence rejected the claim of the petitioner.
4.
We have heard learned counsel for the petitioner and perused the annexures attached with the petition.
5.
As per details of the working days given by the petitioner-Reghubir Singh, he was working as a daily wage worker from January, 1990 to May, 1991.
He did not work at all in September, 1990, October, 1990, February, 1991 to April, 1991 and from June 1991 onwards.
6.
While calculating the days, it comes out that he did not work continuously for a minimum period of 240 days in the preceding calendar year.
His services cannot be said to be continuous within the meaning of Section 25-B of the Act.
Details of the record shown to the Labour Court show that he did not work for months together.
It is clear that the worker had abandoned his job and it is not a case of retrenchment.
For the reasons stated above, we find no merit in this petition and it is, thus, dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
