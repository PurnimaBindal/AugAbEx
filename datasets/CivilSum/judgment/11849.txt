GS Singhvi, J - This petition has been filed for issue of a direction to the respondents to grant pay scale of Rs. 3000-4500 and Rs. 4100-5300 to the petitioner with effect from 31121984 and 31121991 respectively upon completion of five years and 12 years service as Sub Divisional Officer (Civil).
The case set up by the petitioner is that he was promoted as Sub Divisional Office in December, 1978 and became entitled to the grant of pay scale of Rs. 3000-4500 after completion of five years regular service and Rs. 4100-5300 after completion of 12 years regular service as Sub Divisional Officer and this benefit is admissible to him on the basis of the instructions issued by the Government of Haryana vide circular dated 261989.
The petitioner says that ad hoc promotion given to him vide order dated 12121978 was regularised vide order dated 511984 and he has been assigned the year of promotion of 1978 and this being the year of regular promotion, the respondents are duty bound to give effect to the instructions dated 261989 for grant of selection grade to him.
The respondents have not filed any reply to the writ petition and, therefore, the averments made in the writ petition remain uncontroverted.
The principal argument of Shri Goel is that in view of the regular promotion accorded to the petitioner in the year 1978, the respondents are duty bound to give the benefit of the grade of Rs. 3000-4500 after completion of five years service and the scale of Rs. 4100-5300 after completion of 12 years of regular service.
2.
Ordinarily the aforementioned argument of Shri Goel deserved acceptance but I find that the petitioner has not disclosed true facts before the Court regarding the passing of various orders by the Government in the matter of promotion of Sub Divisional Officers and if those facts are taken note of it is not possible to give a direction to the respondents to give the pay scales prayed for by the petitioner.
3.
In C.W.P.
No. 3219 of 1993 (Sharwan Kumar Munjal v. State of Haryana and another, 1996(1) S.C.T.
136) decided on 1781995 along with three other writ petitions, I have taken note of the fact that order dated 1511984 was superseded on 11121990, 2811991 and again 30111992.
The position of the petitioner has undergone change in these orders.
His year of promotion has also undergone a change.
Those orders have not been placed before the Court to indicate the year in which the petitioner has been accorded promotion.
In this view of the matter, it is not possible to give any direction to the respondents on the supposition that the petitioner is a regular promotee of the year 1978.
4.
However, I find it just and proper to direct that the respondents should examine the claim of the petitioner for grant of higher grades with effect from the dates of completion of five years and twelve years of service keeping in view the order passed in Sarwan Kumar Munjal case (supra) and a decision of the Full Bench of this Court on the interpretation of the circular dated 261989.
5.
In the result, the writ petition is disposed of with a direction that the respondents should consider the writ petition of the petitioner and his representation and decide the same within six months keeping in view the decision of this Court in Sarwan Kumar Munjal's case and the Full Bench decision relating to the interpretation of the circular dated 261989.
If the petitioner so desires, he may make further representation to the Government within one month from today and said representation shall also be taken into consideration by the Government while taking a decision on the claim of the petitioner for grant of salary in the scales of Rs. 3000-4500 and Rs. 4100-5300.
Parties are left to bear their own costs.
Order accordingly.
