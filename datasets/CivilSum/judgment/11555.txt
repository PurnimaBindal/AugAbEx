SS Sudhalkar, J (Oral) - The petitioner joined service of the respondents on 821987.
His services were terminated on 30.6.1988.
He raised an industrial dispute.
The Labour Court by its award dated 3091999 ordered reinstatement of the petitioner with continuity of service, however, without back wages.
2.
The petitioner filed Civil Writ Petition No. 5740 of 2000 against the denial of back wages and by order dated 7122000, Annexure P/2, this Court allowed back wages to the extent of 75%.
3.
In the meanwhile, the petitioner asked for regulation of his services, which was declined.
The representation of the petitioner for regularisation was rejected vide order dated 7112000, annexure P/3.
4.
We have heard the leaned counsel of the parties.
We find that the representation has not been decided in accordance with law.
The petitioner was in service of the respondent from 1987 and after the award of the Labour Court, which awarded continuity of service, his service did continue (or is deemed to be continued) and his services, therefore, can be said to be continuous.
The petitioner was entitled to be considered for regularisation in accordance with the policy of the Government because, he was in service of the Government on the appointed date and even for the period prior to it, as required.
5.
In view of the judgment of this Court in the case of Avtar Singh v. Punjab State Co-operative Supply Marketing Federation Limited (Markfed), reported in 2001(3) S.C.T.
397, and many other judgments of this Court in this regard, we allow this writ petition and direct the respondents to consider the case of the petitioner for regularisation.
Let the needful be done within two months from today.
Petition allowed.
