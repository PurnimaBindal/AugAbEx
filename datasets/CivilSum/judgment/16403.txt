Jawahar Lal Gupta, J. (Oral) - The petitioners who are working as Press Daftries complain that the respondents have arbitrarily denied them the scale of Rs. 400-600 with effect from February 1, 1981.
They, consequently, pray for the issue of a writ of mandamus directing the respondents to place them in the said scale.
A few facts may be noticed.
2.
The petitioners were initially appointed as Learner Binders in the Printing and Stationery Department of the State of Haryana.
These posts were in the pay scale of Rs. 70-95.
Those amongst the petitioners who had joined service prior to April 1, 1979 were placed in this scale.
However, with effect from April 1, 1979, the scale of the post of Learner Binder was raised to Rs. 300-430.
Consequently, those amongst the petitioners who had joined after 1.4.1979 were placed in the revised scale.
The Learner Binders are eligible for promotion to the post of Press Daftri.
This post initially carried the scale of Rs. 80-120.
However, this scale was revised and raised to Rs. 350-500 with effect from April 1, 1979.
Resultantly, all the petitioners on their promotion as Press Daftries were placed in the scale of Rs. 350-500.
3.
There is another cadre of Office Daftries which is a part of the ministerial establishment.
The post of Office Daftri initially carried the scale of Rs. 75-105.
It was further revised and raised to Rs. 350-500 with effect from April 1, 1979.
With effect from February 1, 1981, the pay scale was raised to Rs. 400-600.
The precise complaint of the petitioners is that even though Office Daftries were initially in a lower scale of pay viz. Rs. 75-105 while the petitioners were in the scale of Rs. 80-120.
They have been placed in the scale of Rs. 400-600 while this scale has not been given to the Press Daftries like the petitioners.
They, consequently, pray that a writ of mandamus be issued directing the respondents to grant them the scale of Rs. 400-600 with effect from February 1, 1981 and to correspondingly give them the benefit of the revised scale with effect from January 1, 1986.
4.
In the written statement filed on behalf of the respondents, the factual position has not been disputed.
However, the primary plea taken on behalf of the respondents is that different channels of promotion have been provided for the two categories of employees.
Still further, it has been averred that "it is the prerogative of the State Govt. in the Finance Department to fix/revise/further revise the pay scale of its State Govt.
employees."
The claim of the petitioners for the grant of the scale of Rs. 400-600 was considered and rejected.
On these premises, the respondents claim that the writ petition should be dismissed.
5.
Counsel for the parties have been heard.
The position with regard to the two categories of employees with which we are concerned in the present case has been given by the respondents in Annexure R.2.
The relevant portion can be usefully extracted.
It is as under :-  Annexure - R.2 Sr. No. Name of the post Pre-revised pay scale Revised scale Further revised pay scale  xx xx xx 4.
Office Daftries (Ministerial) 75-105 350-500 400-600 xx xx xx 6.
Press Daftri 80-120 350-500 - 6.
A persual of the above chart would show that Office Daftries were initially in the scale of Rs. 75-105 while the Press Daftries, the cadre to which the petitioners belong, were in the pay scale of Rs. 80-120.
With effect from April 1, 1979, both categories of employees were placed in the scale of Rs. 350-500.
Thereafter, when the pay scales were revised with effect from February 1, 1981, the Office Daftries were placed in the scale of Rs. 400-600 while a similar relief was not given to the petitioners.
No reason for this differential treatment has been disclosed.
It is, thus, apparent that persons who were initially superior but had become equals with effect from April 1, 1979 have been treated unequally w.e.f.
February 1, 1981.
This is clearly discriminatory and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
No rational basis for this differential treatment having been disclosed, the action of the respondents cannot be sustained.
7.
It is undoubtedly true as claimed on behalf of the respondents that it is the prerogative of the State Government to fix the pay scales.
However, no authority, howsoever high , is above law.
The State Government is not entitled to act arbitrarily.
In the present case, the action of the respondents is apparently arbitrary and unfair.
8.
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to place the petitioners in the pay scale of Rs. 400-600 with effect from February 1, 1981.
They will also be given the benefit of the corresponding scale as revised with effect from January 1, 1986.
The needful shall be done within three months from the date of receipt of a certified copy of this order.
The petitioners shall also be entitled to their costs which are fixed at Rs. 2000/-.
