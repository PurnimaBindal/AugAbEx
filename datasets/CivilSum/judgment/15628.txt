RL Anand, J - Avtar Singh Aujla, petitioner has filed the present writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India praying for the disability pension and for quashing the orders dated 31121996 and 1761980 passed by the respondent-authorities and directions have been sought from this court against the respondents for the release of the disability pension according to the rules and regulations.
2.
The petitioner was enrolled in Para Regiment on 21101965 and remained posted there up to 31.10.1972.
He was discharged on medical ground by giving a certificate by holding that the petitioner suffered disability Neurosis (Hysteria) ICD 300.
On 2541989, the petitioner sent a notice to the respondents explaining everything regarding his enrolment and retirement due to the disability suffered by him during his service tenure.
According to the petitioner, he was 100% fit when he was enrolled in the Army in the year 1965, the petitioner was discharged by holding that he has suffered disability at the time of discharge, i.e. EEE.
The petitioner has completed 7 years and 10 days of service.
The petitioner further alleges that on 181989, the respondent-authorities passed the orders by stating that the petitioner was not entitled to disability pension as he does not have the qualifying service of 15 years.
According to the petitioner, he was discharged due to the disability suffered by him otherwise he would have served the department for 20/25 years.
With these broad allegations, the petitioner has claimed the disability pension.
3.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents, who filed the written statement and denied the allegations.
4.
According to the respondents, the petitioner was diagnosed as a case of Nurosis Depressive Reaction.
He was invalidated out of service w.e.f.
14.5.1978 on account of Nurosis (Anxiety State) and was placed in medical category EEE Permanent, on the commendations of a duly constituted Invalidating Medical Board.
There was no casual relation of the deceased to the service factors of the petitioner.
According to the respondents, the disease of the petitioner is neither attributable to nor aggravated by the military service.
With this broad defence, the respondents have prayed for the dismissal of the writ petition.
5.
I have heard the counsel for the parties and with their assistance have gone through the record of this case.
6.
As per the Medical Board, the disability of the petitioner was assessed at 20% Permanent for a period of two years.
The grant of disability pension is governed by Regulation 173 of the Pension Regulations (Army), 1961.
This regulation provides that disability pension may be granted to an individual who is invalidated from service on account of disability which is attributable to or aggravated by military service.
The question whether disability is attributable to or aggravated by military service has to be decided in accordance with Rule 7(b) of Appendix II.
In Appendix II, it has been provided that a disease which has led to an individual's discharge or death will ordinarily be deemed to have arisen in service if no note of it was made at the time of individual's acceptance for military service.
Admittedly, the petitioner was physically fit at the time of his enrolment in the year 1965.
He was invalidated because of Neurosis Depressive Reaction and this was noticed by the Board for the first time when the petitioner was examined by the Board on 1891976.
By that time, the petitioner has already served in the Indian Army for more than six years.
7.
In this view of the matter and keeping in view the provisions of Regulation 173 read with Appendix II, this court is of the considered opinion that the disability with which the petitioner suffered is attributable to the army service.
8.
Reliance can also be placed upon Hoshiar Singh v. Union of India and others, 1996(4) SLR 98 : 1996(4) SCT 233 (P&H) and Santokh Singh Gill v. Union of India and others, 1992(6) SLR 749 : 1990(1) R.R.R.
419.
9.
Resultantly, I allow this writ petition, set aside the orders dated 21121996 and 1761980, rejecting the claim of the petitioner for grant of disability pension, and give directions to the respondents to release the disability pension to the petitioner w.e.f.
the date of his discharge within three months from the receipt of copy of this order, failing which the petitioner shall also be entitled to interest @12% per annum.
The petitioner is directed to appear before the Re-survey Medical Board as and when called upon by the authorities.
10.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
