Jawahar Lal Gupta, J.- The petitioner claims that she was born on 8111995 BK On conversion to the Christian calendar, the date of birth would be 1921939 A.D.
The respondent-State by an error of calculation had recorded the date of birth as 1851933 AD On the petitioner's representation, the correction was made.
However, date of birth has again been wrongly changed on 1851933 and the petitioner is sought to be retired on that basis.
Her representation having been rejected, she has approached this Court through the present petition.
2.
Facts as are relevant for the decision of the controversy may be noticed.
The petitioner passed her Middle Examination from Govt. High School, Tibba, District Kapurthala in the year 1953.
According to the school-leaving-certificate issued on April 24, 1956 her date of birth was recorded in the school register as Eighth Phagan, nineteen hundred and ninety-five (8111995) BK She joined the service in the erstwhile State of Pepsu in the year 1954 after she had passed the Middle class examination.
She avers that according to the rules prevalent in the erstwhile State of Pepsu, the rule regarding the minimum age for entry into Govt. service was not applicable in the case of female teachers.
However, information regarding such cases had to be furnished to the Govt. in the Education Department.
It is the claim of the petitioner that she was appointed as a Teacher when she was well below the age of 18 years.
A copy of the certificate regarding her date of birth in the Govt. High School, Tibba is at Annexure P-1 to the petition.
While in service she qualified the Junior Teachers Training Certificate Course.
In the certificate issued by the Director of Public Instructions, Punjab on July 19, 1956, her date of birth has been recorded as May 18, 1933.
While in service she passed here Matriculation examination in the year 1968.
In the certificate issued by the Panjab University, Chandigarh on May 21, 1968 (Annexure P-4) her date of birth has been recorded as 1921939.
The petitioner claims that while applying for the Matriculation examination, the petitioner had given her date of birth as 8111995 BK which was converted by the then Head Master of the School at Tibba to February 19, 1939 AD She claims to have made a representation dated February 12, 1968 and even in her service book, the date of birth was changed from May 18, 1933 to February 19, 1939.
A photostat copy of the relevant portion of the service book has been appended with the petition as Annexure P-6.
3.
The petitioner avers that in the year 1989 the Block Education Officer, Kapurthala sent a report regarding the General Provident Fund of the petitioner.
In this report, her date of birth was shown to be May 18, 1933 and her date of retirement as May 31, 1991.
On enquiry, she learnt that the date of birth which has been corrected earlier had been again changed to May 18, 1933.
She claims to have made a representation, a copy of which has been appended as Annexure P-7.
This representation was said to have been recommended by the District Education Officer vide Annexure P-8.
However, the Director of Public Instructions (Schools) rejected her representation vide his order of June 18, 1990.
Copy of the order has been produced as Annexure P-9.
She submitted a representation to the Govt. on July 20, 1990 (Annexure P-10) followed by a notice through her counsel on March 16, 1991 (Annexure P-11).
Having failed to get the requisite relief, she has approached this Court through the present writ petition.
Her claim, in a nutshell, is that the department had originally made a mistake while converting her date of birth from Bikrami Samvat calendar to Gregorian Calendar which was rectified on her representation in the year 1968.
However, this correction has been undone subsequently without any notice to the petitioner.
4.
The written statement on behalf of respondent Nos. 1 to 3 has been filed.
Besides raising certain preliminary objections regarding delay etc., it has been stated that the petitioner had signed the entries in her service book wherein her date of birth was mentioned as May 18, 1933.
Accordingly, it is claimed that she was estopped from challenging the entries after a long delay when she was at the verge of her retirement.
It has been further stated that no representation as alleged in the petition was submitted in the year 1968 and that the question of passing any order rectifying her date of birth did not arise.
5.
The petitioner has filed a replication controverting pleas raised in the written statement.
6.
I have heard Mr. Rajiv Atma Ram, learned counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Arihant Jain, learned Assistant Advocate General for the respondents.
Mr. Jain produced the original service book before me during the course of hearing.
In the service book, an extract from which has been produced as Annexure P-6, the petitioner's date of birth appears to have been originally recorded as 251990 BK This appears to have been later on changed to 521990 BK On conversion to Christian era, the date of birth is recorded as 1851933 AD This entry appears to have been deleted and changed to 1921939 twice.
However, both the entries depicting the date of birth 1921939 appear to have been deleted and an entry depicting 1851933 AD has been recorded.
There are no signatures or initials below the corrections.
On the left hand side entries appear to have been recorded in February, 1965.
The first entry relates to the passing of JT Examination from the Education Department, Punjab during the session 1955-56 whereunder the date of birth has been recorded as 1851933.
The second entry records the factum of the petitioner having passed the 8th class examination from High School, Tibba in March, 1953.
Date of birth is recorded as 8111995.
Both these entries appear to have been signed on February 26, 1965.
The date of birth which was originally recorded as 8111995 appears to have been scored out later on.
7.
The other documentary evidence on record consists of the school leaving certificate (Annexure P-1).
In this certificate the date of birth is recorded as 8111995.
This entry appears to have been made at the time of petitioner's entry into school on May 12, 1951.
The second document is the certificate dated July 19, 1956 issued by the Education Department wherein her date of birth is recorded as May 18, 1933.
The third document is the Matriculation certificate (Annexure P-4) issued in the year 1968 in which the petitioner's date of birth is recorded as February 19, 1939.
8.
A perusal of the documentary evidence on record shows that at the time of her entry into Govt. High School, Tibba in the year 1951, the petitioner had given her date of birth as 8111995 BK This fact is clearly borne out from a perusal of Annexure P-1.
This is the oldest entry on the record of the case.
This is part of the official record.
Admittedly, on conversion to the Christian era, the equivalent date of birth would be February 19, 1939.
This date is recorded even in the Matriculation certificate issued in the year 1968.
In between in the year 1965, an entry was made in the service book also showing that the petitioner's date of birth according to her Middle Class certificate was 8111995 BK There is nothing to indicate as to why this entry was deleted.
Another entry appearing in Annexure P-6 viz. the service book is 521990 BK.
From a perusal of the record it appears that the date of birth viz. May 18, 1933 has been arrived at by converting the entry of 521990 BK into the Christian Era.
Even according to the respondents, the date of birth viz. 521990 is borne out from any record.
The entry in the Bikrami calender is 8111995.
On conversion this entry comes to February 19, 1939 of the Christian calendar.
The whole mistake appears to have occurred on account of the recording of 521990 BK as the date of birth.
In my view the actual date of birth is 8111995 BK.
Admittedly, on conversion the correct date of birth comes to 1921939 AD.
This entry appears to have been made twice in the service book but later on delected.
The error appears to have occurred on account of the original entry of 251990 BK which appears to have been changed to 521990 BK.
Since according to the School leaving certificate (Annexure P-1) the correct date is 8111995 BK, her date of birth should have been recorded as 1921939.
This is corroborated even by the Matriculation certificate, a copy of which is at Annexure P-6.
In my view, this is the correct date of birth.
9.
Mr. Jain submits that the entries in the service book were duly signed by the petitioner and as such she is estopped from challenging its correctness.
It is no doubt correct that part II of the service book (Annexure P-6) bears the signatures of the petitioner.
This document appears to have been signed by the petitioner on February 26, 1965.
It is on the same day that her date of birth had been recorded as 8111995.
In this situation, Mr. Rajiv Atma Ram appears to be right in contending that the petitioner had merely certified the entry regarding her date of birth being 8111995 BK.
Further, even the recommendation of the petitioner's representation by District Education Officer contained in Annexure P-8 shows that her claim with regard to her date of birth being 1921939 was correct.
10.
In this situation, I am of the view that the claim of the petitioner regarding her date of birth as made out in this petition is correct and deserves to be upheld.
11.
The petitioner claims that a representation had been submitted on her behalf in the year 1968.
A copy of this representation has been produced as Annexure P-5.
In the replication the petitioner has stated that this representation is shown to have been received at serial number 242 in the receipt register of the District Education Officer, Kapurthala on 12.2.1968.
This fact has not been rebutted by filing any affidavit or producing any record before me.
The entries in the service book do indicate that at some stage the date of birth was changed from 1851933 to 1921939.
When was this entry changed to 18th May, 1933 ?
No explanation, whatsoever, could be given by the learned counsel for the respondents.
In this situation, learned counsel for the petitioner appears to be right in his submission that the date of birth was correctly recorded as 1921939 but was later on changed without any notice to her.
This change in the date of birth vitally affected the petitioner's interests.
As held by the Apex Court in State of Orissa v. Dr. Binapaani Dei and others, AIR 1967 SC 1269, such an action could not have been taken except in accordance with the principles of natural justice.
No opportunity, whatsoever, was admittedly afforded to the petitioner.
Consequently, the change from 1921939 to May 18, 1933 was made in violation of principles of natural justice and is vitiated on that count also.
12.
In view of the above, the writ petition is allowed, the order changing the date of birth from 1921939 to 1851933 AD as also the order at Annexure P-9 are quashed.
It is declared that the petitioner's date of birth is February 19, 1939.
She would consequently be not retired on the basis that her date of birth is May 18, 1933.
The petitioner would also be entitled to her costs which are assessed at Rs. 2000/-.
Petition allowed.
