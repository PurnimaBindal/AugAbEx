M R Agnihotri and KP Bhandari, JJ.
- After hearing the learned counsel for the parties, we find that the case has been remanded to Assistant Registrar, Co-operative Societies, Thanesar, respondent No. 2 for fresh decision in the matter.
It is stated by the learned counsel for the parties that the petitioner is entitled to his back wages and amount has to be quantified according to the award of he Labour Court.
Respondent No. 3-The Haryana State Federation of Consumer's Co-operative Whole-sale Stores Ltd, has also got some amount already ascertained which has to be recovered from the petitioner.
The Assistant Registrar, Co-operative Societies, Thanesar, respondent, No. 2 is directed to adjust this amount alongwith interest at the rate of 12 per cent per annum on the wages payable to the petitioner and after deciding the matter by passing a speaking order, ensure payment of the amount, if due, to the petitioner within fifteen days of the date of order.
The petition thus stands disposed of.
The parties, through their counsel, are directed to appear before the Assistant Registrar, Co-operative Societies, Thanesar, on 26th November, 1990.
The arrest of the petitioner shall remain stayed till the matter is finally settled.
