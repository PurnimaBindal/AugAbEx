SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - We have heard the learned counsel for the petitioners at length and perused the paper-book.
2.
Mr. Sibal, learned Sr.
Counsel appearing for the petitioners has made a reference to the observations made by the Board of Management of the respondent-University in the proceedings of 210th Meeting held on 2432004 and submitted that the decision contained therein has been taken for erroneous considerations.
According to the learned Sr.
Counsel, the Board has wrongly noticed that the petitioners had earlier been granted Rs. 300-600 (Unrevised) on the pattern of State Government, which was personal to them.
Learned Sr.
Counsel further submits that the Board has been wrongly influenced by the fact that the scale of Rs. 700-1600 had been given to the College cadre librarians on merits.
In support of the submission, learned Sr.
Counsel relied on the earlier orders dated 931984 granting the scale of Rs. 700-1200 to the petitioners which was the revised scale of Rs. 300-600.
The order does not anywhere state that the scale of Rs. 300-600 has been given as a measure personal to the petitioners.
The aforesaid scale continues to be paid to the petitioners, although the same has been revised to Rs. 700-1200.
On the other hand, the scale of Rs. 700-1200 has been revised to Rs. 700-1600 in the case of Punjab State College Librarians.
Learned Sr.
Counsel submits that once the grades have been equated, the petitioners cannot now be granted a scale different from the scale given to the Punjab State College Librarians.
Having considered the entire matter, we are of the opinion that the order passed by the Board of Management cannot be said to be either arbitrary or unreasonable.
This Court had directed the Board of Management while disposing of LPA No. 250 of 1996 on 7102002 to reconsider the entire matter and to take a final decision on the grievance put forward by the petitioners.
In the light of the direction issued by this Court, the Board of Management has passed the impugned order.
The claim of the petitioners has been considered at Item No. 7.
The entire decision needs to be reproduced for better appreciation of the claim put forward by the petitioners.
The decision is as under :-  "7.
Item No. B-2 : Revision of pay/scales Qualifications of Junior Library Assistants of the PAU Library.
In compliance with the orders of the Hon'ble Punjab and Haryana High Court in LPA No. 250 of 1996, filed by Shri Hquiqat Singh and others v. PAU, the Board of Management thoroughly considered/examined the issue of revision of pay scale of the appellants and other Library Assistants/Junior Library Assistants employed in the PAU and their claim for grant of pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
141980 and Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
111986 in light of the qualifications possessed by the appellants/prescribed for the posts, nature of their duties, job responsibilities and the notifications issued by the State Government/UGC as well as statutory provisions of the University.
The members of the Board noted the direction of the Hon'ble Punjab and Haryana High Court that they will not be influenced by the fact that the writ petition filed by the appellants has been dismissed by the learned Single Judge.
After going through the materials on record, the Board observed that the cadre of the appellants cannot be compared with that of college cadre librarians who were granted pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 on merits.
The Board also noted that the appellants were granted pay scale of Rs. 300-600 (unrevised) on the pattern of State Government, which was personal to them and the conversion scale of Rs. 700-1200 already granted to the appellants w.e.f.
141980 on the pattern of State Government is in order.
In view of the above, the Board of Management decided not to grant the pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
141980 and Rs. 2200-4000 w.e.f.
111986 to the appellants and other Library Assistants/Junior Library Assistants employed in the PAU."
3.
A perusal of the aforesaid decision shows that the Board was fully alive to all the relevant parameters for equation of difference posts.
It has been observed that the claim of the petitioners has been considered in the light of the qualifications possessed by the petitioners/prescribed for the posts, nature of their duties, job responsibilities and the notification issued by the State Government, UGC as well as statutory provisions of the University.
After duly considering the entire issue, the Board has come to the conclusion that the cadre of the petitioners cannot be equated with the cadre of the librarians working in the College of the State of Punjab.
The Board of Management is an expert body which is fully competent to take a decision on the equation of different cadres/posts.
It would not be appropriate for this Court to substitute its own opinion for the opinion of the expert body in exercise of the jurisdiction under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
We, therefore, find no merit in the writ petition.
4.
In view of the above, the writ petition is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
