Swatanter Kumar, J - The petitioners have approached this Court under Article 226 of the Constitution of India praying for issuance of an appropriate writ, order of directions to the respondents to release the scale of Rs. 480-Rs. 760/- with effect from 121981 and Rs. 1200-Rs. 2040/- with effect from 151990 and they be granted arrears admissible in accordance with law.
3.
The facts relevant for supporting the plea of the petitioners are that the petitioners were appointed and are working as Electricians/Refrigerator Mechanics from the date of their appointments.
Which were during the period 2421976 to 571979.
For appointment to these technical posts, the prescribed qualification is Matriculation with two years ITI diploma certificate.
All the petitioners possess that qualification.
The Government of 3031982 issued a policy decision for revision of pay scales with effect from 121981.
Under this circular issued by the Commissioner and Secretary to the Government of Haryana, Department of Finance, pay scales were revised in accordance with the annexures attached thereto and which provided the original pay scales of Rs. 120 - 250/- under the revised pay scales effective from 1.4.1979 to Rs. 400 - Rs. 600/-.
This scale was further revised vide letter dated 2381990 to Rs. 1200 - 2040/- with effect from 151990.
The petitioners in furtherance to these circulars of the State are claiming this relief in this writ petition, which they failed to get despite serving a demand notice upon the respondents.
According to the respondents, in another civil <>Writ petition No. 12193 of 1990, titled as Amar Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others, the learned Single Judge had granted the relief vide judgment dated 6121991 which has been affirmed by the Letters Patent Bench vide its judgment and order dated 21112000 in LPA No. 950 of 1992.
3.
Upon notice, the respondents filed detailed reply.
It is stated on behalf of the respondents that the petitioners are not entitled to the relief prayed for.
The scale as modified with effect from 121981 for technical posts in various departments for which minimum educational qualification prescribed Matric with two years ITI certificate are not applicable to this department.
It is also stated that the scales of the petitioners are not corresponding to the scales prescribed as the policy is applicable to common category of posts of other departments and not to work-charged employees made regular in PWD (B&R).
4.
At the very out-set we may notice that the qualifications of the petitioners as stated in the petition are not denied by the respondents.
All that is stated is that the petitioners are not entitled to this benefit as the policy does not apply to the concerned department.
It is true that the Government is competent to decide as to which particular category of employees is to work in a department and what would be the scale payable to them.
But the decision of the Government cannot be arbitrary and it must treat the persons charging similar duties and functions, possessing similar qualifications, equally, we are unable to appreciate the contention raised on behalf of the State that the circular does not apply to the department of PWD (B&R) and it is applicable to other departments.
The policy decision annexed as Annexure P/1 to the petition does not admit of any such classification or exception.
The letter has been issued by the department of Finance and is applicable to all departments.
The very language of the circular sufficiently indicates that the contention raised on behalf of the State is without any merit.
In fact in the case of Kuldeep Singh v. State of Haryana, CWP No. 186 of 1987, the State had itself granted benefit of the revised scales to all the technical departments and the writ petition was dismissed as having become infructuous in view of the statement of the counsel appearing for the State on 3071987 (copy annexed to the writ petition as Annexure P/3).
In any case, the controversy in the present case is squarely answered in the judgment of this Court in the case of Amar Singh (supra) which was affirmed by the Letters Patent Bench as noticed above.
We are in respectful agreement with the reasoning given in the above mentioned cases and have no reasons to express any different opinion.
5.
For the reasons afore-indicated, we allow this writ petition and direct the respondents to place the petitioners in the pay scale of Rs. 480 - 760/- with effect from 121981 and scale of Rs. 1200 - Rs. 2040/- with effect, from 111986.
The petitioners would be entitled to the consequential relief of recovery of arrears, which will be restricted to a period of 38 months preceding the date of institution of the writ petition.
However, we leave the parties to bear their own costs.
Petition allowed.
