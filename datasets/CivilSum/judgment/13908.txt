By this judgment, we will dispose of Letters Patent Appeal Nos. 950 of 1992, 1136 of 1994, 810 of 1995, 636 of 1997, 669 of 1997 and CWP No. 15133 of 1992, as the same are based on common question of facts and law.
Facts of the case, however, have been taken from LPA No. 950 of 1992.
2.
Writ petition was filed by the respondents, who were working as Electricians having two years ITI certificate, for grant of pay scale of Rs. 480-760/- with effect firm 121981 and further revised pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040/- with effect from 111986 on the ground that the same benefit was granted to other similarly placed Electricians in all other departments of the Government.
3.
On the basis of pleadings of parties, learned Single Judge held that a case of invidious discrimination was made out and there was no reason forthcoming to deny the relief sought by the petitioners-respondents.
Consequently, learned Single Judge allowed the above noted prayer made in the writ petition, with all consequential reliefs in the nature of arrears of salary etc.  4.
Impugned judgment is challenged primarily on the ground of delay and laches as relief was sought for grant of higher pay scale with effect from 121981, whereas the writ petition was filed in September, 1990.
Reliance is sought to be placed on decision of the Supreme Court reported in AIR 1994 Supreme Court 1006 to contend that the writ petition filed after unreasonable delay should have been dismissed on the ground of laches.
5.
Claim for proper fixation of pay gives rise to recurring cause of action and the bar of limitation in such a case would not arise even in a civil suit.
Payment of arrears, however, would have to be restricted to a period of 38 months, which could be claimed in a civil suit as well.
In taking this view, we are supported by a judgment of the Full Bench of this Court in the case of Saroj Kumar and others v. State of Punjab and others, 1998(3) SCT 664.
6.
Consequently, we uphold the finding that the petitioners-respondents are entitled to the relief sought by them and modify the impugned judgment of the learned Single Judge the only to the extent that arrears of salary shall be paid for the period of only 38 months preceding the date of institution of the writ petition.
Accordingly, the appeal is partly allowed.
Appeal partly allowed.
