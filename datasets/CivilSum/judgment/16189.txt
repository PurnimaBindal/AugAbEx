Ashok Bhan & AL Bahri, JJ.
- Petitioners in this writ petition have challenged the inequitious action of the respondents treating the similarly placed employees discriminately for the purpose of fixation of pay depending on the date of joining the service.
2.
The petitioners are working as Clerks, Kandamen, Restorers and Care-takers in various Market Committees of Punjab constituted under the Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board.
Prior to 111978, the grade of these posts were Rs. 110-250.
The same was revised w.e.f.
111978 to 400-600.
Again with effect from 111986, the pay scale was further revised of the said categories of employees to Rs. 950-1800.
Respondent No. 1 issued order vide memo No. 3/25/M-1/88/16013 dated 25.7.1990 granting pay scale of Rs. 510-800 w.e.f.
111978 and Rs. 1200-2100 w.e.f.
111986 as purely personal grade only to those Clerks, Candamen, Village Supervisors, Care-takers and Rest House Attendants who were working in the Market Committees before 371980.
The resultant effect was that the person who joined service in any of the above categories after 371980, were given the unrevised pay scale.
3.
It is not dispute between the parties that nature of duties and responsibilities of any category of these services working before or after 371980 are the same.
Petitioners filed the present writ petition alleging therein that they are entitled to the same revised pay scales which have been allowed to those who were working before 371980 on the principle of `equal pay for equal work', the nature of duties, responsibilities and service conditions being the same.
4.
On notice of motion being issued, the respondents filed the written statement, the plea taken by the respondents is that the Board could make reasonable classification founded on intelligible differentia and in the present case the cut off date is July 1980 for giving different pay scales to the employees working prior to 371980, which is based on rational grounds.
The persons who joined prior to 371980, were grounded together and were segregated from other group because the revised grades were given to those employees as a personal grade which could not be made applicable to the employees who joined after 371980.
5.
We have heard counsel of the parties and with their consent are disposing of this writ petition finally at the motion stage.
6.
The distinction made by the respondent-Board in giving different pay scales to those two sets of employees is artificial and farcical.
The Supreme Court of India as well as this Court have repeatedly held that if the nature of duties, responsibilities and conditions of service are the same, then the employees have to be given the same pay scale.
The logic in support of the differentia which the respondent Board has pleaded for giving different pay scales cannot be sustained in the eyes of law the same being discriminatory in nature and thus violative of Article 14 of the Constitution of India Accordingly, this writ petition is allowed and the respondent-Board is directed to give the same pay scale to the petitioners and other similarly situated employees who joined the Board after 371980 from the date they joined the service.
The arrears of pay and allowances shall be paid within three months from the receipt of copy of this order.
No costs.
