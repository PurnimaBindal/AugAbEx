SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - We have heard the learned counsel for the parties at length and perused the record of the case.
2.
The petitioner was appointed as Art and Craft teacher after due selection on 1631972.
She was confirmed on the aforesaid post on 1671976.
She was allowed to cross the efficiency bar in the scale of Rs. 570-1080 with effect from 111988 and her pay was fixed at Rs. 880/-.
On 871991, the petitioner was allowed step-up increment and her pay was fixed accordingly.
On 1552001, the petitioner was served with a Show Cause Notice proposing to re-designate her as Sewing teacher and fixation of her pay in the sale of Sewing teacher with retrospective effect.
The petitioner has submitted the reply to the Show Cause Notice on 2852001.
On 27112001, the impugned order has been passed by the respondents withdrawing the scale of Art and Craft Teacher and also re-designated her as Sewing teacher.
3.
It is submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioner that all along the petitioner has been working on the post of Art and Craft Teacher.
She was selected and appointed on the post of Art and Craft teacher.
Now after lapse of almost 30 years, the respondents has woken up and decided to re-designate her as Sewing teacher.
Learned counsel further submitted that the matter in controversy is, in fact, squarely covered by a Division Bench judgment of this Court rendered in CWP No. 6306 of 1991 (Nirmal Kaur v. The State of Punjab and others, decided on 28.01.1992).
In that case also, the designation of the petitioner was sought to be changed from the Art and Craft teacher to that of Sewing teacher after a period of 19 years.
The writ petition mentioned above, was allowed and the impugned order was quashed.
4.
We are of the considered opinion that the ratio of the law laid down in the aforesaid judgment of this Court, is fully applicable to the facts and circumstances of the present case.
Consequently, the present petition is allowed.
The impugned order dated 27112001, Annexure P-11, is hereby quashed.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
