Vinod K Sharma, J - The petitioner has filed this writ petition seeking a writ of mandamus directing the Punjab School Education Board, Mohali and the Secretary, Punjab School Education Board to declare result of the petitioner of 10th class, held in March, 2002.
A direction was also sought for issuance of provisional roll number to the petitioner to appear in the examination of 10+2 class which was to be held on 132006.
2.
The facts leading to the filing of the writ petition are that the petitioner was studying in the Baring School Batala in the year 1999 in 8th Class and secured 356 marks out of 1000 and he was promoted to Class IX.
In the year 2000-01 he was promoted to Class 10.
The school issued the following certificate to the petitioner :-  "TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN"  IT IS CERTIFIED THAT Sawandeep Singh s/o Jarnail Singh was a regular student of 9-B class in the academic year 1999-2000.
He secured 150 marks out of the total of 750 marks conducted by the School and was promoted to 10th standard on condition.
Sd/- Mrs. R Benjamin, Principal" 3.
The petitioner, thereafter, obtained school leaving certificate duly counter-signed by convenor ICSE on 3182000 and thereafter joined SBAS School and appeared in matriculation examination under Roll No. 605429.
The petitioner failed in the said examination.
The petitioner was again admitted in Class 10 on 1.4.2001 and appeared in the examination for class 10 in March, 2002.
The result of the petitioner was displaced (displayed ?)
on internet and the petitioner was shown as pass candidate.
The result of the petitioner of class 10 reads as under :-  "Punjab School Education Board Matriculation Examination, March 2002 Roll Number : 620626 Name : SAWANDEEP SINGH Father's Name : Jarnail Singh Mother's Name : Simerjit Kaur Result : Pass-Third Division Details of Marks Sr. No. Subject Marks obtained.
Theory/Practical/Total  Pass Marks, Total Marks  Compulsory Subjects 1.
Punjabi  067  50  150 2.

Hindi  033  33  100 4.
Mathematics  014F  33  100 5.

Social Studies  033  33  100 Total Marks  219  650  Additional/Elective Subjects  1.
Health & Physical Education  011  041  052  33  100 2.
Agriculture  053  33  100" 4.
On the basis of the result on the internet, the petitioner was admitted to DAV College, Batala and passed his 10+1 examination and thereafter was admitted to Class 10+2 under Roll No. 722.
The petitioner was not issued roll number for appearing in 10+2 Examination as the respondent failed to declare the result of the petitioner for the examination held for Class 10 in March, 2002 as his result was shown as "RL".
5.
The SABS, Bhuler Public School by letter dated 2552005 (Annexure P-7) made a request to Secretary, Punjab School Education Board, Mohali to declare result of the petitioner.
However, the said result was not declared.
6.
The writ petition filed by the petitioner came up for hearing before this Court on 132006 when following order was passed :-  "Heard counsel for the petitioner.
Notice of motion for 16.3.2006.
On the asking of the Court Mr. BS Teji, Advocate, accepts notice of motion.
In the meantime, the respondents are directed to issue the Roll No. to the petitioner for appearing in the +2 exams which are said to be commencing tomorrow i.e. 2.3.2006.
The Roll No. shall be issued to the petitioner purely provisionally and the result shall not be declared without the orders of this Court.
A copy of this order be given dasti under the signatures of the Court Secretary."
In pursuance to the order passed by this Court, the petitioner appeared in 10+2 examination.
7.
On notice, written statement was filed by the Board wherein it was pleaded that the petitioner had failed to produce detailed marks card of Class 9 and further stand was taken that the petitioner did not disclose that he had failed in Class 9 and had secured 150 marks as certified by the Principal of Baring School, Batala.
It was further claimed that the petitioner had manipulated his admission in Class 9 in SBAS Public School, Batala which is affiliated to Punjab School Education Board, Mohali.
It was further the case of respondent Nos. 2 and 3 that on receipt of the documents from the school it was noticed that the petitioner failed to clear his 9th standard examination and therefore, he was ineligible to appear in the matriculation examination.
8.
The stand of the respondents is that according to the Matriculation Examination Regulations requirement for appearing in Class 10 is that a person has to produce a certificate of having passed the lower examination and as he had failed in Class 9, he was, therefore, ineligible.
However, in the short affidavit filed by the Principal Baring School Batala, it was categorically mentioned that the petitioner was promoted to class 10 along with the similarly situated students of the class on the condition that he will switch over to commerce instead of science subjects and further subject to his performance.
The short reply has been filed by the Principal Dasmesh Senior Secondary School, Parowal, Tehsil Batala, wherein it was claimed that in view of the result shown on the internet the petitioner was admitted for 10+2 as per Board instructions.
9.
Mr. Sarabjit Khaira, learned counsel for the petitioner has challenged the action of respondent Nos. 2 and 3 in not declaring the result of 10th class of the petitioner primarily on the ground that the petitioner after having been promoted to Class 10 had undertaken the regular study and in fact appeared in Class 10 Examination conducted by the Board in which he was said to have passed as per the result shown on the internet.
The contention of the learned counsel for the petitioner was that in view of the fact that the petitioner had stood promoted to Class 9, it was not open to respondent Nos. 2 and 3 to treat him as failed as is sought to be projected as no criteria has been laid down for passing of Class 9.
Learned counsel for the petitioner made reference to the Matriculation Examination Regulations and submitted that only requirement was to produce certificate having passed the lower examination which the petitioner did.
10.
Mr. Khaira, learned counsel for the petitioner further submitted that the petitioner having been allowed to appear in the examination, the respondents are now debarred on the principle of promissory estoppel to cancel his result in the plea of eligibility.
11.
Mr. RN Raina, learned counsel appearing for respondent Nos. 2 and 3 vehemently contended that the present writ petition deserves to be dismissed as the petitioner had not disclosed that he had failed in Class 9.
It was further case of the petitioner that the certificate issued by the Baring School, Batala cannot be said to be a certificate showing that the petitioner had passed lower examination as envisaged in the Regulation.
12.
We have heard the arguments raised by the learned counsel for the parties and find force in the pleas raised by the learned counsel for the petition.
13.
It is not in dispute that the petitioner was, in fact, promoted to Class 10 by the school which means that he passed class 9 Examination.
Merely because, the school imposes a condition on a student directing him to take a particular subject in the next class cannot mean that he is to be treated as failed candidate.
In case, the contention of the respondents is accepted then it would lead to absurd result as the candidate would be entitled to appear in Class 10 Examination qua particular subjects, whereas he would be debarred from appearing in other subjects.
This cannot be accepted.
Once the school has promoted a student to next class it would be deemed that he has cleared the examination, especially when there is no Regulation of the Board stipulating as to how the examination of Class 9 is to be held by the school.
14.
Learned counsel for the petitioner, therefore, is right in contending that the petitioner was fully eligible as he was promoted to Class 10 and on the basis of the school leaving certificate issued to him he has joined Class 10 and appeared in the examination as a regular student.
It may further be noticed that the factum of his having passed the examination is not disputed by the respondents.
It is also not disputed that the result of the petitioner was, in fact, declared on the internet on the basis of which he has already passed 10+1 and has appeared in 10+2 examination, though provisionally.
In view of what has been stated above, the writ petition is allowed.
Respondent Nos. 2 and 3 are directed to declare the result of the petitioner of Class X as well as declare his result of 10+2 in which he has appeared under the orders of this Court.
Petition allowed.
