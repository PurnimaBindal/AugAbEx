SS Nijjar, J. (Oral) - The petitioners were working as Group Instructions in ITIs.
Samrala and Patiala.
They retired from service with effect from 28.2.1994 and 3131997 respectively.
The Punjab Government notified Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988 which came into force with effect from 111986.
The petitioners had earlier opted for the revised pay rules, 1988 with effect from 111987.
By order dated 30121999, the petitioners were promoted as Group Instructors with effect from 861986.
Therefore, in view of the changed circumstances, they expressed their desire to revise the option and to be placed under the Rules 1988 with effect from 111986.
This request of the petitioner was rejected.
2.
In similar circumstances, some other employees of the Education Department had filed 1996(2) SCT 257 (P&H) (DB) : Civil Writ Petition No. 18380 of 1994 Nand Lal Sharma and others v. State of Punjab seeking similar relief.
After considering the entire matter, the Division Bench of this Court held that when there are changed circumstances, permission ought to be granted to the employees to give revised option.
It was also observed that since problem is going to arise affecting a large number of people, the respondents ought to give permission for revising the option to other similarly situated persons.
This would obviate filing of the numerous writ petition claiming the same relief.
In spite of the aforesaid observation, the claim of the petitioner is sought to be rejected on the ground that they do not belong to the Education Department.
Since the Revised Pay Rules of 1988 are applicable to all the employees of the State of Punjab in all departments, no discrimination can, therefore, be permitted on the basis of the department in which the employee is working.
The petitioners are entitled to the same relief as granted to the petitioners in Civil Writ Petition No. 18380 of 1994.
3.
Petition is allowed.
A writ of mandamus is issued to the respondents to accept the revised option of the petitioners contained in Annexure P-3 and P- 4.
The petitioners retiral benefits be recalculated and paid in accordance with fresh option.
Let the necessary relief be granted to the petitioners within a period of three months of the receipt of certified copy of this order.
Petition allowed.
