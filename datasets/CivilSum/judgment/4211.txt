GS Singhvi, J - The petitioner has challenged the legality of the order Annexure P-4 dated 1121992 (correct date of the order is dated 30111992) as also the order dated 381994 (Annexure P-6) passed by the respondents.
2.
The petitioner joined service as Sectional Officer/Junior Engineer in the Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) of the erstwhile State of Punjab.
In the process of re-organisation of the then State of Punjab and creation of the State of Haryana, the service of the petitioner was allocated to the State of Haryana with effect from 1111966.
The petitioner was promoted as Assistant Engineer/Sub Divisional Officer w.e.f.
291279 vide order Annexure P-1.
He was regularised vide order dated 15184 issued by the Governor of Haryana under Rule 5 of the Haryana Service of Engineers Class-II, P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970.
Subsequently, some more promotions were made to the posts of Sub Divisional Officers.
These promotions became subject matter of challenge in this Court and then before the Supreme Court.
The Government undertook review of various promotions on the basis of the directions given by the Apex Court.
Final order regarding these promotions came to be issued on 30111992.
In this order, the name of the petitioner does not figure and according to him, respondents 4 and 5 who are similarly situated have been given promotion as Sub Divisional Officers ignoring his claim.
3.
In the meanwhile, while issue regarding promotion to the post of Sub Divisional Officer was being agitated before the Court, the petitioner was given selection grade on completion of 12 years service with effect from 30121991.
For this purpose, order Annexure P-5 was passed by the competent Authority.
Petitioner's pay came to be fixed in the pay scale of Rs. 4100- 5900 with effect from the date of completion of 12 years of service.
After about a year and six months of the issue of order Annexure P-5, the same officer issued Annexure P-6 dated 381994 and withdrew the benefit of selection grade given to the petitioner and refixed his pay in the scale of Rs. 3000-4500 on the ground that the petitioner's case has not been approved by the Haryana Public Service Commission.
4.
The petitioner filed C.W.P.
No. 13054 of 1994 challenging the actions of the respondents.
This writ petition was disposed of by a Division Bench of this Court on 22.9.1994 with a direction that the writ petition be treated as representation of the petitioner and the same be decided within one month.
Despite this direction of the High Court, no order was passed by the respondents to decide the representation of the petitioner and this has led to the filing of the second writ petition by the petitioner.
5.
It is not necessary for me to make a detailed evaluation of the various facts and points mentioned in the writ petition because I am of the opinion that so far as challenge to the order dated 30111992 is concerned, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of the order passed in C.W.P.
No. 3219 of 1993 (Sarwan Kumar Munjal v. State of Haryana and anr.), 1996(1) SCT 136 (P&H) decided on 1781995 along with three other writ petitions.
On the issue of withdrawal of the selection grade, there is no controversy between the parties that no action oriented notice was given to the petitioner nor an opportunity of hearing was afforded to him before passing the order Annexure P-6.
This leads to an irresistible conclusion that the petitioner has been condemned unheard.
6.
I am also take note of the statement made by the learned Deputy Advocate General, Haryana that vide memo No. 9/54/93-4 IE dated 771995 issued by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to the Government of Haryana, Irrigation and Power Department, whereby the Government conveyed its decision to the Engineer-in-Chief, Irrigation Department, Haryana, that the officers who retire or have retired after getting selection grade of Rs. 4100-5300 be granted pension in the pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300 and not in the lower pay scale, subject to the condition that the Government will be free to revise the issue regarding pensionary benefits after the decision of the pending writ petitions.
7.
In view of the above, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondents are directed to consider the case of the petitioner in regard to his claim for placement in the ranking list in the light of the order dated 1781995 passed in Sarwan Kumar Munjal's case (supra).
The order Annexure P-6 dated 381994 is quashed with a direction that the respondents should give benefit of the selection grade to the petitioner and also fix his pension on the basis of the memo dated 771995.
This action shall be taken by the respondents within three months of the submission of a certified copy of this order.
it is, however, made clear that if the Government's stand regarding grant of selection grade to the limited number of the officers is upheld by the High Court, it will be open to the Government to pass fresh order in regard to the entitlement of the petitioner to get selection grade.
It shall also be open to the respondents to refix the pension and other retiral benefits accordingly.
Petition allowed.
