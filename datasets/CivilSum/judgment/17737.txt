NC Jain and HK Sandhu, JJ.
- This order of ours' will dispose of Civil Writ Petition Nos. 4829, 1770 and 3690 of 1993.
In view of the facts having not been disputed, the present cases are covered by the decision given by a Division Bench of this Court in CWP No. 11318 of 1992 (Ms. Rita Sharma v. Secretary), decided on 27.7.1993, which reads as under :-  "After hearing the learned Counsel for the parties, we find that the petitioner was duly selected by the Haryana Subordinate Services Selection Board, but the appointment letter was not issued in her case, whereas persons for below in the merit list who were selected alongwith the petitioner, have since been issued the letter of appointment.
To be specific, it has been admitted in the written statement that the name of the petitioner appeared at No. 53 in the General Category in the merit list whereas the candidate at serial Nos. 56, 62 and 75 have since been appointed as Home Science Mistresses.
The plea of delay made by the respondents, in order to justify the action, cannot take away the right of the petitioner guaranteed by Article 16 of the Constitution."
2.
Consequently, we allow this petition and direct the respondents to issue the letter of appointment as Home Science Mistress to the petitioner forthwith.
3.
The ratio of the aforementioned case can easily be applied to the facts of the instant cases because it is undisputed before us that all the petitioners were higher in merit in the selection list than the persons who have already been given the appointment letters.
4.
Consequently, we allow all these petitions at the motion bearing and direct the respondents to give appointment letters to the petitioners at the earliest in any case within a period of two weeks.
A copy of this Order be given Dasti to the petitioners on payment of copying charges.
No costs.
Petition allowed.
