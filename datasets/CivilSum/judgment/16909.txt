RL Anand, J - Through this writ petition Ms. Nishi Gupta has sought the quashment of order dated 181997 vide which her case for regularisation was rejected.
It has also been prayed by the petitioner that a writ in the nature of mandamus be issued against the respondents directing them to regularise her services with effect from 1.2.1996 by treating the period from 991994 to 9111994 as duty period.
2.
Some facts can be noticed in the following manner :-  The petitioner was appointed as Bill Clerk in Public Health Sub- Division No. XII, Sector 8, Panchkula on 1931990 against a vacant post but on daily wages and since then she is continuously working on this post.
On 3.12.1990 she submitted a representation to respondent No. 2 through respondent No. 3 with a prayer that her services may be regularised.
Her representation could not be decided earlier, as a result of that she filed C.W.P.
No. 16359 of 1990, which was disposed of by giving directions to the respondents to decide the representation.
Now the case of the petitioner is that she is working continuously as Bill Clerk since her joining except the period when she had to proceed on maternity leave from 991994 to 9111994.
The Government of Haryana issued instructions dated 731996 to regularise the services of such daily wagers who had rendered more than five years service before the cut off date i.e. 3111996.
The petitioner again submitted a representation on 18.9.1996, but no decision was taken.
Resultantly, she had to file one more writ petition No. 1907 of 1997 which was disposed of by giving directions to the respondents to decide her representation.
In pursuance of those directions, the case of the petitioner for regularisation was rejected vide order dated 181997.
Hence, the present writ petition.
3.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents.
The main defence of the respondents is that the case of the petition is not covered by the policy because there is a break in her service on account of maternity leave.
4.
We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance have gone through the record of the case.
5.
We are of the considered opinion that the defence taken up by the respondent authorities is totally untenable.
The petitioner is a married woman.
To conceive a child by a married woman is her inherent right which vests with every womanhood.
If the petitioner has conceived a child and has proceeded on maternity leave only for two months, it is not a sin committed by her.
Her proceeding on leave was a natural consequence on account of her conception.
Relying upon the judgment of Hon'ble Supreme Court in Municipal Corporation of Delhi v. Female Workers (Muster Roll) and another, AIR 2000 SC 1274 : 2000(2) SCT 258 (SC) and the judgment dated 7112000 passed in C.W.P.
No. 11658 of 1999, Ms. Savita Bhoria v. State of Haryana & Ors., we set aside the impugned action dated 181997 of the respondent authorities and given a declaration in favour of the petitioner that she is entitled to be regularised subject to her record w.e.f.
1.2.1996 and all incidental and consequential benefits shall be released to her within one month from the receipt of copy of the order.
There shall be no order as to costs.
Order accordingly.
