V K Bali, J(Oral) - The claim here is only for interest on delayed payments regarding post-retiral benefits.
A statement with regard to delay under various heads has been tabulated in paragraph 18(ii) at page 11 of the Writ petition.

Leave encashment -do -  6.2.96 3 months 76,76000 2.
Written statement has been filed on behalf of the respondents wherein there is no denial with regard to the delayed payments referred to above.
It requires to be mentioned here that the petitioner also complains with regard to deduction of Rs. 22,400/- from his D.C.R.G.
on the ground that an amount of Rs. 30,500/- was to be deducted as per Civil Court decree Annexure R/4 and that too from the salary of the petitioner whereas deduction has been made from D.C.R.G.
It is, however, admitted that after adjusting the amount already paid with regard to recovery, the remaining amount that could be deducted is Rs. 22,400/-.
So far as deduction from the D.C.R.G.
is concerned, the Court finds no illegality having been committed by the respondents.
Once it is admitted position that said amount could be deducted, whether it has been done from salary or D.C.R.G.
would not make the least difference.
The plea of the petitioner on this ground, thus, fails.
So far as the delay of payment under the various heads is concerned, the Court is of the view the ends of justice would be met if the petitioner is granted 10 per cent interest from the date three months after his retirement till the date payment of various amounts as mentioned above have been made but for leave encashment which was paid three months after the retirement of the petitioner.
Let arrears due on account of order passed by this Court be calculated within four weeks from today and made over to the petitioner within next two weeks.
It requires to be mentioned here that the State has not come up with any justification for delayed payments of post-retiral benefits.
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.
Order accordingly.
