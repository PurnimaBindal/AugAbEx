MR Agnihotri, J - Smt. Sarbati Devi widow of one Sada Ram, Chowkidar of Punjab P.W.D.
(B&R), has invoked the extraordinary jurisdiction of this Court for the issuance of a writ of mandamus for the purpose of directing the respondents to consider her claim for the grant of pensionary benefits according to the rules, e.g., family pension admissible to the widow of a Government employee, and for the employment of her son in accordance with the policy instructions of the State Government.
2.
In the written statement filed by the respondents, in has been admitted that husband of the petitioner was working as Chowkidar but attempt has been made to non-suit the petitioner by raising a hyper-techincal objection that the deceased was only a work-charged Chowkidar and not a regular one; hence, the respondents were under no obligation to provide employment to the dependents of such an employee.
3.
We find that the stand taken by the respondents is neither legally tenable nor can it be appreciated, as the same is wholly inelegant for the State which should act as a model employer.
By no stretch of imagination and by no canon of interpretation, the benefit of the policy instructions can be restricted so as to exclude the work-charged employees.
4.
Consequently, we allow this writ petition in limine and direct the respondents to release to the petitioner the pensionary benefits to which she is found entitled under the rules, as also to provide employment to her son keeping in view the academic qualification and technical experience, if any, within a period of two months.
The petitioner shall also be entitled to the costs of this petition which are quantified at Rs. 500/-.
Petition allowed.
