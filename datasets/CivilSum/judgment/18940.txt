Bakhshish Kaur, J - Both the cases are being decided by a common order as identical questions of law and facts are involved in these petitions.
2.
All the petitioners are qualified teachers.
They possess the qualifications MA Hindi, B.Ed.
The petitioners by way of filing these petitions seek writ in the nature of Mandamus directing the respondents to treat them at par with their colleagues of Master grade and not to discriminate them in any manner including appointment/promotion to higher posts in the education department.
3.
The facts of these petitions are not disputed.
The controversy having arisen in these petitions has already been laid to rest on account of decision in CWP 3405 of 1985 decided on 581996 reported as Kali Ram v. State of Haryana, 1997(3) SLR 722 : 1997(1) SCT 54 (P&H).
4.
Mr. RK Malik, learned counsel for the petitioner has stated at the bar that during the pendency of the writ petitions, the government has considered the claim of the petitioners for promotion as Lecturer in Hindi, therefore, this petition has become infructuous.
5.
In view of the facts stated that Mr. RK Malik, learned counsel for the petitioner, the writ petition insofar as cause of petitioners, who have done or whose qualification is equivalent to MA in Hindi are concerned, the same is allowed directing the respondents to consider their case of promotional post of Lecturer in Hindi w.e.f.
the date they fulfil the qualifications.
If found fit they should be promoted to the post of Lecturer against the vacant posts without disturbing those who have already been promoted.
In the peculiar facts and circumstances of the case, it is also ordered that the petitioners who have actually not worked on the promotional posts, would not be entitled to arrears of salary if a decision to promote them is taken from the dates they are considered for promotion as per the directions.
With these observations, both the petitions stand disposed of.
Order accordingly.
