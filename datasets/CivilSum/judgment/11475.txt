Re : Writ Petition  Written statement filed on behalf of the respondents today in the Court is taken on the record.
2.
The three writ petitioners have invoked the writ jurisdiction of this Court under Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India for quashing of the orders dated 731989 (Annexure P-3) and dated 2221989 (Annexure P-4) whereby their services as part-time Lecturers were discontinued with immediate effect.
The petitioners have also sought a direction not a terminate their services till they were replaced by regular appointees and also to pay them their salary at par with their counterparts working on regular basis by following the principle of "equal pay for equal work."
3.
The petitioners were appointed as Lecturers on part-time basis in the subjects of Music, English and Political Sciences respectively in the Govt. College, Safidon, District Jind though against regular vacant posts.
Their names were sponsored by the employment exchange and thus, they were selected through an open competition.
While petitioner No. 1 was appointed on 2891988, petitioner No. 2 came to be appointed on 5101988 whereas petitioner No. 3 joined on 4111988.
The petitioners were working on their afore-mentioned posts on part-time basis when the impugned orders, Annexures P-3 and P-4, came to be passed whereby they were directed to be relieved from their duties, with effect from 731989 in the case of petitioner No. 1 and 3 and with effect from 2221989 so far as petitioner No. 2 was concerned.
Aggrieved at these orders that the petitioners have approached this Court.
4.
Since no interim relief was granted by this Court while admitting the writ petition, on 23.2.2005 the case was adjourned to 23.3.2005 to enable the State Counsel to have the latest instructions in the matter as to whether in the interregnum the petitioners were still continuing on part-time basis or they had been replaced by the regularly selected Lecturers.
Shri Rathee, learned counsel appearing for the State of Haryana, states that whereas petitioners No. 2 and 3 were not employed on part time basis after they were relieved vide orders Annexures P-3 and P-4, petitioner No. 1 was, however, re-employed during the subsequent academic sessions upto the year 1993 as per the following details :-  1.

He further states that the vacant posts of Lecturers have since been filled up on regular basis by appointing the candidates recommended by the Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC).
6.
Faced with this situation, Shri Kamal Sharma, learned counsel for the petitioners, in reference to the subsequent events which he has brought on record by way of an affidavit filed by petitioner No. 1 today in the Court, vehemently contends that since petitioner No. 1 has worked for a sufficiently long period from the year 1988 to 1993, he is entitled to continue against a vacant post till the same is filled on regular basis by a candidate recommended by the HPSC and that for the period the petitioners have actually worked on part-time basis, they are entitled to be paid full salary especially when they performed the duties which were more onerous than being performed by their regular counter-parts.
7.
Having heard learned counsel for the parties and after going through the record of this case, I am of the view that no effective relief can be granted to the petitioners as far as challenge to the orders, Annexures P-3 and P-4, is concerned.
8.
The petitioners were appointed on part-time basis for a specified term and two of them having been relieved way back in the year 1989, no effective relief for their reinstatement and/or continuation of part-time basis can be granted.
Similarly, petitioner No. 1 also worked on part-time basis upto 3131993 and keeping in view the stand taken by the respondents that the vacant posts have been filled on regular basis with the candidates recommended by the HPSC, no effective relief regarding his reinstatement on part-time basis and/or continuation in service can be granted.
However, in the light of the principles laid down by a Division Bench of this Court in the case of Polu Ram and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1998(4) RSJ 152 and in the case of Shamsher Singh and others v. State of Haryana, 1998(4) RSJ 708, there appears to be some justification in the claim of petitioner No. 1 for the grant of minimum of the pay-scale for the period he actually worked on part- time basis from the year 1988 till 1993 provided that the duties especially teaching work assigned to him was not less than what is prescribed by the University Grants Commission for a full time Lecturer.
Accordingly, this writ petition is disposed of with the following directions :-  (a) Higher Education Commissioner, Haryana (previously known as the Director, Higher Education) is directed to look into the claim of petitioner No. 1 for the payment of minimum of the pay-scale of a college lecturer for the period he worked on part-time basis from the year 1988 to 1993.
If on verification of the records of the Govt. College, Safidon it is found that petitioner No. 1 had actually worked at par with the full time Lecturers in terms of the delivery of lectures and/or other responsibilities, it is directed that he will be paid minimum of the pay-scale of the college lecturer for the afore- mentioned period; (b) Since petitioners No. 2 and 3 worked for a very small duration and that too way back in the year 1988-89, it will not be expedient to issue any such direction in their case.
(c) The claim of petitioner No. 1 with regard to payment of the arrears of salary as referred to above, shall be considered and disposed of within a period of four months from the date of receipt of a certified copy of this order.
Petition partly allowed.
