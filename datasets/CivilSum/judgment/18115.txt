Nirmal Singh, J - Petitioner came to be appointed as Bearer-cum-Chowkidar vide order No. DLA/904/L-91/66 dated 13th May 1966 in the department of libraries.
He was superannuated on 3182004 at the ae of 58 years.
Grievance of the petitioner is the under Article 226 of the J&K Civil Services Regulations, an employee, who was appointed on the inferior post prior to 10101966, is entitled to be retired on attaining the age of 60 years.
It is stated that the petitioner was holding he post of Bearer-cum-Chowkidar, and the said post is mentioned as Inferior Post in Schedule-II of the J&K Civil Service Regulations Volume II at entry No. 59 and therefore, he was entitled to be superannuated on attaining age of 60 years whereas the respondents retired him at the age of 58 years, which is illegal and contrary to the provisions of Article aforementioned.
2.
In the counter affidavit, the respondents have admitted the factual averments made in the writ petition but they have pleaded that the petitioner never discharged the duty of Chowkidar during his entire service period as per service record and retired as Library Bearer.
3.
I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the record.
4.
The only question which requires to be determined in this petition is whether the petitioner was the member of inferior service and was to be retained in service up to the age of 60 years.
5.
As per Schedule II of the J&K Civil Service Regulations Volume II at entry No. 50, the inferior categories have been defined as under :  1.
Daftaries 2.
Cleaners 3.
Packers 4.
Workshop Attendants of Industrial Training Schools 5.
Cooks of Hostels 6.
Gardeners of Boarding Houses.
7.
Laboratory Bearers (who joined the service before 10101966) 8.
Watermen (who joined the service before 10101966) 9.
Lescars (who have joined service on or before 10101966) 6.
Again in Schedule-II of the J&K Civil Service Regulations Volume-II at entry No. 59, following categories have been defined as inferior service :  1.
Jamadars 2.
Orderlies 3.
Chowkidars 4.
Messengers (who were in service on or before 10101966) 5.
Sweepers and/or Safaiwalas appointed in time scales of pay on regular establishment in service on or before 10101966.
7.
As per Schedule-II of the J&K Civil Service Regulation Volume-II, the post of Laboratory Bearer mentioned at entry No. 50 has been defined as inferior post whereas the post of Chowkidar mentioned in the said schedule at entry No. 59 has also been defined as such.
8.
When this petition was being heard, Mr. Shukla was asked whether the post held by the petitioner has been mentioned in the Schedule-II of the J&K Civil Service Regulation Volume-II.
In reply, he submitted that even though the post of Bearer-cum-Chowkidar has not been mentioned in Schedule II of the J&K Civil Service Regulation Volume-II but the posts of Laboratory Bearer and Chowkidar have been mentione at entries No. 50 and 59 of the aforesaid schedule under the category of Inferior Posts.
It is stated that petitioner was holding the post of Bearer-Cum-Chowkidar, which post has been defined in the Schedule aforementioned differently under two heads i.e.
Laboratory Bearer and Chowkidar; and therefore, the post of Bearer-cum-Chowkidar, which was held by the petitioner at the time of his superannuation, may be treated to be a post of Inferior category.
9.
A perusal of the record shows that the petitioner was appointed as Bearer- cum-Chowkidar vide order dated 13th May 1966.
Stand of the respondents is that the petitioner did not perform the duty of Chowkidar and retired as Library Bearer, therefore, he cannot be treated to have been retired against the post of inferior category.
Such stand of the respondents cannot be accepted.
There is no proof on the file that the competent authority had ever appointed the petitioner as Library Bearer.
Respondents might have been taking the work of Library Bearer from the petitioner but post held by the petitioner prior to 10101966 of Bearer-cum-Chowkidar was to be treated as inferior post under Article 226 of J&K Civil Service Regulation Volume-I Therefore, the petitioner who was holding an inferior post was required to be retired at the age of 60 years.
The respondents have illegally and erroneously retired the petitioner with effect from 3182004.
10.
In view of the above, this petition is allowed.
Order No. 29 of 2004 dated 11th August 2004 impugned in the present petition is set aside.
Respondents are directed to pass fresh order treating the petitioner in service up to 3182006.
Respondents will pay the petitioner full salary by treating him in service up to 3182006.
They will deduct the amount of pension from the salary of the petitioner, which has already been received by him.
Respondents shall accordingly re-fix his pension in accordance with rules within three months from the date, a copy of this order is served upon the respondents by the petitioner.
Disposed of accordingly.
Petition allowed.
