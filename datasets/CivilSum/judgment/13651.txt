NK Sodhi, J - This is an application for the recall of our order dated May 13, 1991, whereby the Writ Petition filed by the petitioner claiming interest on some alleged delayed payments of retiral benefits had been dismissed being without merit.
2.
The petitioner was appointed as an Assistant Engineer on January 15, 1953 in the Public Works Department (Building & Roads) in the then State of Punjab and after serving the Department for more than 33 years he retired from service on 31.12.1986 on attaining the age of superannuation.
He filed CWP 15649 of 1990 in this Court claiming interest from the respondents on the following four counts :  i) He was paid pension with effect from February, 1987 though the amount of pension was less than the amount to which he was entitled to correct amount of pension was later worked out and arrears paid to him on 121990.
He claimed interest of the amount of arrears w.e.f.
January, 1987. ii) Interest on the gratuity of Rs. 50,000/- for the period 111987 to 23111987. iii) Interest on the balance amount of gratuity of Rs. 50,000/- paid to him on 2311991 which was enhanced with the coming into force of the Punjab Civil Services (Revised scales of pay), Rules, 1988. iv) Interest on commuted pension deposited in his account on 16.2.1990.
According to the petitioner, there was a delay of more than three years.
3.
The notice being issued to the respondents, they filed their written statement denying all the allegations of delay in payments and it was asserted that there had been no delay on the part of one department in refusing the petitioner all his retiral benefits.
After the written statement had been filed on 1231991, the matter came up for motion hearing on March 21, 1991.
It appears that the motion Bench was satisfied that there was no delay in regard to the payments for which interest was claimed by the petitioner under counts No. (i), (iii) and (iv) mentioned above and when counsel for the respondents sought time to have instructions in regard to the interest claimed under count No. (ii), the following order was recorded on that day :-  "Counsel for the respondents seeks time to obtain instructions with regard to payment of interest on Rs. 50,000/- gratuity from 1.1.87 to 231187.
To come up on 1141991."
The case then came to be argued on April 22, 1991, before us when a short affidavit was filed in Court on behalf of respondent No. 3.
Counsel for the respondent informed the Court that the needful had been done inasmuch as the gratuity of Rs. 50,000/- had been released and paid to the petitioner on 23111987.
This amount was payable on 111987 but it had been withheld owing to the pendency of Enquiry No. 3/83 by the Vigilance Department against the petitioner.
The clearance certificate from the Vigilance Department was received on 2191987 and thereafter the amount was released on 23111987.
On the basis of this factual position it was asserted that there was no delay whatsoever in releasing the amount.
Counsel for the petitioner then wanted time from the Court to have instructions in the matter and to file a rejoinder, if necessary.
Time was allowed and the case stood adjourned to May 13, 1991.
No rejoinder was filed on behalf of the petitioner and after the counsel for the petitioner confirmed that the amount of gratuity had been received by the petitioner, we found that there was no merit in the writ petition and accordingly dismissed the same.
4.
It was after hearing counsel for the parties and satisfying ourselves that there was no undue delay on the part of the respondents in releasing the retiral benefits to the petitioner as some of the amounts became due to him as a result of revision which took place after his retirement that we dismissed the writ petition on May 13, 1991.
The present application is mis-conceived, uncalled for and the same stands dismissed.
No costs.
Petition dismissed.
