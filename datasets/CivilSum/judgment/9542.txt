THB Chalapathi, J - The petitioner approached this Court seeking issuance of a writ of mandamus to give him the retrospective promotion as Engineer-in- Chief with effect from 1111983.
According to the petitioner, he was senior to respondent No. 2 and instead of promoting him, respondent No. 2 was posted as Engineer-in-Chief on 1111983 until further orders.
Aggrieved by the same, he filed CWP No 5436/83.
The said petition was allowed in part directing the respondents to consider the matter afresh in the light of the judgment.
Against the said judgment, the LPA No. 849/84 was filed which was also dismissed.
Thereafter, the petitioner was promoted by an order dated 1101984 and was posted as Officer on Special Duty in the Grade of Engineer- in-Chief while respondent No. 2 was posted as Engineer-in-Chief on 1101984.
In this writ petition, the petitioner is seeking that he should have been promoted as Engineer-in-Chief with effect from 1111983.
It is not necessary to narrate the subsequent events.
By an order dated 1591995, the Governor of Haryana accorded his sanction for the creation of a temporary post of Engineer-in-Chief in the pay scale of Rs. 2500-125/2-2750 plus a special pay of Rs. 250/- for the period from 1111983 to 3091984 for the petitioner as per the orders of the High Court of Punjab and Haryana High Court, Chandigarh, CWP No. 5436/83.
It is further ordered that the date of promotion of the petitioner as Engineer-in-Chief shall be 1111983 instead of 1101984.
Thus, the main grievance of the petitioner has been removed and he was given promotion with retrospective effect i.e. from the date when he claimed promotion in this writ petition.
2.
The petitioner submits that he should have been treated as Engineer-in- Chief right from 1111983 in the cadre post but not against the post which was created temporarily for the period from 1111983 to 3091984.
This relief is sought only for the self satisfaction of the petitioner since no financial benefits will accrue to him.
There is no dispute of the fact that the petitioner is senior to respondent No. 2.
It is also not disputed that the entire service record of the petitioner was good.
Therefore, in the ordinary course he should have been promoted as Engineer-in-Chief when the post fell vacant on 1111983 but instead of promoting him, respondent No. 2 was kept in charge of the said post.
Since the Governor was pleased to create a supernumerary post by his order dated 1591995 with effect from 1111983 to 3091984, there will not be any financial burden on the State if the petitioner is declared to be entitled to be promoted as Engineer-in-Chief against the cadre post with effect from 1111983.
3.
I accordingly dispose of the writ petition with a direction that for all practical purposes, the petitioner be treated as retired from service as Engineer-in-Chief in the Cadre post with effect from 1111983.
Petition allowed.
