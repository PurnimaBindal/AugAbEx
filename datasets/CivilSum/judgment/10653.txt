G S Singhvi, J - This petition has been filed to quash the order (Annexure P-1) dated 16.2.1990 and not to revert the petitioner from the post of Platoon Commander.
2.
A look at the averments made in the writ petition shows that the petitioner had been promoted as Platoon Commander and had joined at that post in pursuance of the order of promotion but in view of the stay order passed by the High Court in Civil Writ Petition No. 4189 of 1985 and the connected civil misc. applications, the respondent No. 1 thought it proper to pass the impugned order reverting the petitioner.
3.
By a separate order passed today, Civil Writ Petition No. 4189 of 1985 has been dismissed.
With the dismissal of that writ petition and the vacation of the interim stay, the substratum of the order of reversion of the petitioner goes and, therefore, that order can no longer survive.
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed and the order Annexure P-1 is quashed with a direction that the petitioner shall be deemed to have been working on the post of Platoon Commander on the assumption that the order (Annexure P-1) was never passed.
He shall get all consequential benefits.
Petition allowed.
