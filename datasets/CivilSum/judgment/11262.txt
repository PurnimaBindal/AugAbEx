Swatanter Kumar, J - In this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner prays for issuance of an appropriate writ, order or direction, particularly in the nature of mandamus directing the respondents to treat the period 1101994 to 2641995 and from 2101998 to 1.7.1999 as duty period and granting the consequential relief.
2.
The facts giving rise to the present petition are that the petitioner was working as a driver with the State Transport Authority of State of Haryana.
According to the petitioner he submitted joining report after leave on 2101998.
He has not been paid salary for the afore-stated period and compelled by the circumstances the petitioner earlier filed a writ petition challenging the order vide which the petitioner was sought to be retired on medical grounds.
The petitioner also filed a writ petition being CWP No. 15056 of 1999 for releasing the salary for the said period.
This writ petition was disposed of by a Division Bench of this Court directing the Transport Commissioner to decide the representation of the petitioner in that regard.
Vide order dated 3032000 the Transport Commissioner passed the following order rejecting the claim of the petitioner.
The relevant part of the impugned order reads as under :-  "The petitioner was accordingly allowed to continue in service in Haryana Roadways, Jind from where he was also transferred to Haryana Roadways.
Gurgaon, vide order No. 12492/A1/E3 dated 30794.
The petitioner after having been relieved from Haryana Roadways Jind, for Haryana Roadways Gurgaon on 2894 submitted his joining report in the office of General Manager, Haryana Roadways, Gurgaon, only on 30.8.1994 and thereafter the petitioner Sh. Dewan Singh D-181 absented himself from duty wilfully and unauthorisedly immediately after submitting his joining report and continued absent from duty till 4.10.94 when he was relieved from Haryana Roadways Gurgaon to Haryana Roadways, Jind, due to the cancellation of his transfer order.
The petitioner submitted his joining report in Haryana Roadways, Jind on 10.10.94 but here again after submitting his joining report he absented himself from duty wilfully and unauthorisedly till 25.4.95 and resumed his duty only on 2641995.
In this way the petitioner remained absent from duty wilfully and unauthorisedly from 3894 to 25695.
The period of absence from duty from 10894 to 3.10.94 as stated has already been decided by the General Manager, Haryana Roadways, Jind as leave without pay vide his memo No. 5669/ECD dated 451998.
But surprisingly the office of General Manager, Haryana Roadways, Jind where the petitioner had remained absent from duty for more than six months ie from 111094 to 25.4.95, and then again from 2.10.98 to 19.8.99, did not take any action by way of serving charge sheet etc. against Sh. Diwan Singh.
This was not desirable on the part of the office of General Manager, Haryana Roadways, Jind.
However, keeping in view the interest of justice, the period of absence from duty of the petitioner from 111094 to 25.4.95 and from 2.10.98 to 19.8.99 is treated as leave of the kind due.
The period from 4.10.94 to 10.10.94 would be deemed to have been treated as joining time of the petitioner in view of the cancellation of his transfer from Haryana Roadways, Jind to Haryana Roadways, Gurgaon.
The General Manager, Haryana Roadways, Jind would initiate action against those who failed to take action against Diwan Singh for not initiating disciplinary proceedings for his absence from duty for about eight months.
The representation of the petitioner is thus disposed of accordingly."
Aggrieved from the said decision, the petitioner has filed the present writ petition impugning the order dated 3032000 wherein representation of the petitioner was rejected.
3.
Upon notice, the respondents filed a detailed reply.
It is stated that the petitioner was relieved vide the impugned order on 281994.
He submitted his joining report on 30.8.1994 and thereafter the petitioner absented himself from duty wilfully and unauthorisedly.
The petitioner was pressing for cancellation of his transfer order in furtherance to which he had been relieved and thereafter he again submitted his joining report on 10101994 in Haryana Roadways, Jind and absented himself till he joined his duties again on 2641995.
In this fashion, the petitioner absented himself from 381994 to 25.4.1995 as well as from 2101998 to 1981999.
According to the respondents, appropriate action had been directed to be taken against the petitioner.
Even other facts averred by the petitioner are not admitted.
4.
Learned counsel for the petitioner while relying upon the judgment of the Supreme Court in the case of MR Gupta v. Union of India, S.L.R.
1996 SC 669 : 1996(1) SCT 8 (SC) contended that there is no limitation for claiming of salary and as such the present petition needs to be allowed.
5.
There cannot be much of a dispute with regard to the concept of recurring cause of action resulting in claiming arrears of salary and thus, the claim in the writ petition may not be liable to be rejected on the ground of delay itself.
But that it is not the case herein.
The competent authority has passed an order rejecting the representation of the petitioners for valid and proper reasons.
If the petitioner did not join his duties in furtherance to the orders passed by the competent authority, no blame can be attributed to the respondent-authorities.
Every employee is expected to act and work in accordance with the orders passed by the competent authority and not to frustrate such order by his sheer desire.
The petitioner repeatedly defaulted and remained unauthorisedly absent as per the allegations made in the reply.
In the impugned order the authorities have given benefit to the petitioner even of joining time despite the fact that the transfer orders have been cancelled by the High Court itself.
The respondents have specifically averred and so does the impugned order show that the concerned authority did not take disciplinary action against the petitioner for his absence from duty for about eight months.
The authorities have been directed action against the defaulting officers.
6.
The impugned order does not suffer from the vice of arbitrariness or unreasonableness.
The disciplinary authority in normal course of his business must be permitted to exercise effective control upon the concerned employees.
The respondents have already granted the requisite benefit to the petitioner and the period has been treated as leave of the kind due as well as the joining time has been permitted from 4.10.1994 to 10101994.
We see no reason to interfere in the impugned order.
However, in furtherance of the above order whatever amount is payable to the petitioner as per rules should be paid, if due.
The petition is accordingly disposed of with the above directions.
Petition accordingly.
