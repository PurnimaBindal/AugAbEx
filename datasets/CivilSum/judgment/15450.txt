RL Anand, J - Earlier this writ petition was filed by Shri Gian Chand Gupta (deceased), who sought the directions of this Court for the release of retiral benefits on the plea that he retired from the service on 31.8.1999.
Notice of motion was issued to the respondents and they came up with the plea that the deceased was responsible for causing loss to the respondents to the tune of Rs. 9 lakh.
Notice was issued to the deceased after his retirement.
During the pendency of the writ petition Shri Gian Chand Gupta expired and his legal heirs were brought on record.
2.
It is the case of the respondents that the departmental inquiry had not been completed against the deceased employee nor the same was initiated during his life time.
It was initiated by issuing charge-sheet to the petitioner after the receipt of notice of motion.
In these circumstances, the position of law is well settled.
Departmental inquiry, if any, cannot proceed further.
The consequences would be that the legal heirs of the deceased are entitled to the retiral benefits of which they are being deprived since 1999.
3.
The case stands adjourned to 9.7.2002 with the directions to the respondent that on the next date of hearing a bank draft of all the retiral benefits of the deceased employee shall be brought in Court in the name of Smt. Pushpa Gupta, widow of the deceased and while bringing the amount, interest @ 9% per annum w.e.f.
112000 shall also be calculated on the amount due to the deceased.
Order accordingly.
