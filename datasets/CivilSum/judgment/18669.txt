Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - Minor punishment was inflicted upon the petitioner in August, 1994.
Thereafter petitioner's claim for promotion was considered and he has actually been promoted with effect from January, 1995.
2.
The contention raised on behalf of the petitioner is that the action of the respondents in promoting the petitioner with effect from 1995 and not from the due date on which the petitioner was entitled to be promoted is arbitrary and discriminatory.
According to the petitioner, his juniors were promoted with effect from 25.9.1994 and the petitioner was entitled to be considered for promotion on that date itself.
3.
Learned counsel appearing for the State fairly conceded that once the petitioner has been promoted his claim for promotion needs to be considered by the competent authority from the due date.
4.
Prima facie, we are of the considered view that the respondents ought to have considered the claim of the petitioner for promotion to the post of Executive Engineer from the date his juniors were promoted.
Once the respondents have themselves promoted the petitioner with effect from January, 1995 in that event they are obliged to consider the claim of the petitioner for promotion as on due date or the date on which juniors to the petitioner were promoted.
5.
In the circumstances afore-stated, hardly any controversy arises for determination and we dispose of this petition with a direction to the respondents to consider the claim of the petitioner for promotion in accordance with the rules with effect from 25.9.1994.
This writ petition is disposed of accordingly without any order as to costs.
Order accordingly.
