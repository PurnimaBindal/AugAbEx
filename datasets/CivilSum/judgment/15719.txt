SS Goindi, Member.
- By its award dated 211989, the Presiding Officer, Labour Court ordered the reinstatement of Harbir Singh, workman-petitioner with continuity of service but denied him the back-wages on the ground that the workman had not stated that he had been not employed during the period he was out of service.
The workman-petitioner questioned the later part of the award, which went against him by filing the present writ petition.
The workman was reinstated by the Haryana Urban Development Authority-respondent No. 2 when the matter came up before us on April 1, 1999 on our persuasion and with the efforts of Sh. YP Malik, learned counsel for the workman, he agreed to give up 50% of the backwages provided the remaining 50% backwages are paid to him.
This proposal was conveyed by Sh. Ajai Tewari, Advocate to the management-respondent No. 2, which has accepted this proposal vide memo No. ADA-I-99/4349 dated 16.8.1999 addressed to Sh. Ajay Tewari, Advocate.
A part of its text is reproduced below:  "In compliance of proposal put forth by the Lok Adalat it is fair enough when a workman has been allowed continuity in service then he is entitled to back wages also.
On the above ground worthy CA HUDA has approved the proposal of paying only 50% backwages without interest.
If this proposal is acceptable by the workman in the Lok Adalat when the case is fixed for 1781999, then office will release 50% payment without interest."
2.
In the above view of the matter and taking into account the compromise, which has thus come about between the workman and the management, we direct the management-respondent No. 2 to pay the remaining 50% of the backwages to the workman for the period he was out of job from 2521986 to 8.11.1989, as he was reinstated on 9111989, within a period of two months from today.
3.
The writ petition is disposed of accordingly.
Petition disposed.
