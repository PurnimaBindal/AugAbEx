ML Singhal, J - DK Jain was working as Clerk in the Transport Department having joined on 2351964.
One Asa Ram also joined the Transport Department as Clerk on 17.5.1964.
Asa Ram was promoted as Assistant with effect from 2721975.
He (DK Jain) was not considered for promotion by the State Transport Commissioner, Haryana, although there was a regular post of Assistant lying vacant against which he could be considered and promoted.
Instead he was promoted as Assistant vide order dated 3171980.
One Roop Singh was junior to him as he had joined as Clerk in 241970.
He (Roop Singh) was promoted as Assistant on ad-hoc basis with effect from 21.7.1975.
Promotion of Roop Singh was in violation of Governments instructions/Rules.
No reason was given at the time of promoting Roop Singh vis-a-vis DK Jain who was not promoted.
Vide order No. 517 dated 3171980 DK Jain was promoted to the post of Assistant.
In the wake of promotion of DK Jain to the post of Assistant, Roop Singh was reverted from the post of Assistant which he was holding on ad-hoc basis.
DK Jain filed suit for declaration against the State of Haryana to the effect that he was entitled to be promoted as Assistant from 21.7.1975 instead from 3171980 with all benefits of pay and allowances.
It was alleged by DK Jain that there was departmental enquiry against him which was finalised in the year 1977.
He was exonerated of the charge and no punishment was awarded to him.
He was also allowed to cross the efficiency bar and given annual increments.
He thus claimed promotion from 21.7.1975 i.e. when his junior Roop Singh was promoted.
2.
Defendant-State of Haryana contested the suit of DK Jain plaintiff urging that the suit is barred by limitation.
Roop Singh was promoted as Assistant on ad-hoc basis without any benefit towards seniority.
It was admitted that Asa Ram joined as clerk on 17.5.1964 while plaintiff joined as clerk on 2351964.
Asa Ram was promoted as Assistant from 2721975.
It was urged that the availability of the vacant post did not entitle the official to claim promotion.
He could claim promotion only when department takes decision to fill up the post.
It was admitted that Roop Singh was promoted as Assistant from 21.7.1975.
It was also admitted that Roop Singh was junior to the plaintiff.
Plaintiff could not be considered for promotion as he was under suspension from 12.7.1975 and a departmental enquiry was pending against him.
He remained under suspension till 19101977.
Plaintiff was promoted on 3171980 in the wake of reversion of Roop Singh.
It was admitted that the plaintiff was exonerated of the charge against him in the enquiry.

Relief.
Subordinate Judge III Class, Gurgaon vide order dated 1451991 decreed the plaintiff's suit for declaration that he was entitled to be promoted as "Assistant" with effect from 21.7.1975 and that he was entitled to the benefit of pay and allowance of the post of Assistant in view of his finding that the plaintiff was entitled to promotion with effect from 21.7.1975 i.e. when Roop Singh was promoted.
Plaintiff became entitled to promotion when he was exonerated of the charge in the departmental enquiry.
It was as if there was no departmental enquiry against him.
He would have been entitled to promotion if there had been no departmental enquiry against him.
He found all other issues against the state of Haryana.
4.
State of Haryana went in appeal.
District Judge, Gurgaon vide order dated 20.1.1992 dismissed the appeal.
5.
State of Haryana has come up yet in this regular second appeal to this Court.
6.
Roop Singh was far junior to DK Jain-plaintiff (respondent herein).
He was promoted on 21.7.1975 on ad-hoc basis.
On 21.7.1975 DK Jain could not be promoted as there was departmental enquiry against him and he was under suspension.
The moment he was exonerated of the charge which he was facing in the departmental enquiry, he became entitled to be considered for being promoted with effect from 21.7.1975 as if there was never any departmental enquiry against him.
With effect from 21.7.1975, DK Jain shall be considered for being promoted to the post of Assistant.
With his exoneration of the charge framed in the departmental enquiry, DK Jain became entitled to be considered for being promoted to the post of Assistant in preference to Roop Singh.
The post of Assistant fell vacant with effect from 21.7.1975 and DK Jain became entitled to be considered for promotion.
He was not promoted because of the pendency of departmental enquiry against him.
With his exoneration of the charge framed in the departmental enquiry, D K Jain got right to be considered for promotion with effect from 21.7.1975.
Department will consider for promotion with effect from 21.7.1975 i.e. when Roop Singh was promoted.
If he is not found suitable for promotion with effect from 21.7.1975, he will be considered for promotion against the next vacancy that arose after 21.7.1975.
Plaintiff shall be deemed to have been promoted with effect from the date when he was found suitable for promotion either on 21.7.1975 or any date between 3171975 to 3171980 i.e. when vacancy in the cadre of Assistants fell vacant.
Plaintiff shall be entitled to pay fixation against the post of Assistant with effect from the date from which he is found suitable for promotion and, thereafter to annual grade increments of the post of Assistant.
Plaintiff was however not entitled to arrears for a period beyond 38 months preceding the date of suit consequent upon such pay fixation against the post of Assistant because plaintiff's suit would be barred by limitation so far as claim of arrears beyond the period of 38 months preceding the date of institution of the suit i.e. 3031989 is concerned.
7.
Learned Govt. counsel submitted that plaintiff's suit should have been dismissed as he has claimed promotion with effect from 21.7.1975 through suit was instituted in March, 1989, which was barred by limitation.
In support of this submission, he drew my attention to State of Punjab and Others v. Gurdev Singh, Ashok Kumar, AIR 1991 SC 2219 : 1991(3) SCT 93 (SC), where Gurdev Singh and Ashok Kumar had challenged their termination/discharge from service brought about on 2711977 and 15.3.1979 through suits instituted on 1841984 and 1221985 respectively and the Hon'ble Supreme Court observed that suit for declaration by a dismissed employee on the ground that his dismissal was void is governed by Article 120 of the Limitation Act (Article 113 of the New Limitation Act) and the Hon'ble Supreme Court reversed the decisions of the Punjab and Haryana High Court to the contrary.
8.
Learned Government counsel submitted that the cause of action to DK Jain plaintiff arose in the year, 1977 i.e. when he was exonerated of the charge in the departmental enquiry.
9.
In my opinion, each day gave him fresh cause of action for claiming that he was entitled to be considered for promotion with effect from 21.7.1975 i.e. when his junior Roop Singh was promoted in supersession of him.
With the grant of promotion to the plaintiff with effect from 3171980, plaintiff's seniority in the cadre of Assistants remained affected adversely.
He got cause of action each day till this adverse affect on his seniority in the cadre of Assistants was removed.
It was the duty of the State to set the records straight after DK Jain was exonerated of the charge in the departmental enquiry and order his promotion with effect from the date till which he remained un-promoted because of that enquiry.
State of Haryana cannot be allowed to go on with the illegal and void order on the plea of limitation which is its own creation.
10.
For the reasons given above, the decree passed by the learned Sub Judge III Class, affirmed in appeal by the District Judge, Gurgaon is modified as follows :-  Defendant-State will consider DK Jain-plaintiff for promotion with effect from 21.7.1975 i.e. when Roop Singh his junior was promoted.
If he is not found suitable for promotion with effect from 21.7.1975, he will be considered for promotion against the next vacancy that arose after 21.7.1975.
Plaintiff shall be deemed to have been promoted with effect from the date when he is found suitable for promotion either on 21.7.1975 or any day between 21.7.1975 to 3171980 i.e. when vacancy in the cadre of Assistants fell vacant.
Plaintiff shall be entitled to pay fixation against the post of Assistant with effect from the date from which he is found suitable for promotion and thereafter to the annual grade increments of the post of Assistant.
Plaintiff shall, however, not be entitled to arrears for the period beyond 38 months preceding the date of suit i.e. 3031989, consequent upon such pay fixation against the post of Assistant.
Regular second appeal is disposed of accordingly.
Order accordingly.
