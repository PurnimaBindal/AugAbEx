MS Liberhan, J - The contempt petition arises out of an order passed by JV Gupta, J (as his Lordship then was), in Civil Writ Petition No. 6169 of 1987, on 1st of June, 1989, by which the following directions were given :-  (i) The seniority list Annexure P-3 is quashed as regards the petitioner.
(ii) The case of the petitioner for promotion will be considered in accordance with the instructions issued by the Chief Secretary to Government since the year 1982.
(iii) An appropriate order may be passed within three months of this order.
This order was confirmed by the Division Bench on 1391989 in L.P.A. No. 1607 of 1989, as well as by the Hon'ble Supreme Court, in S.L.P.
No. 14780 of 1989, decided on 14121989 vide which a clause was added that the rights of the persons who joined service earlier than the petitioner, will be governed by the law applicable to them.
2.
The petitioner contends that the order dated 1st of June, 1989, has not been complied with.
The petitioner further contends that the date of promotion of the petitioner as Assistant has been rightly fixed as 3091964, but the seniority has not been given accordingly and his seniority as Superintendent Grade II has been wrongly fixed and his case for promotion as Under Secretary which, according to him, is due w.e.f.
6.6.1987, is not being considered.
The petitioner has made representations on 20490, 25690, 5790 and 19790, which have not been considered and disposed of by the respondents.
3.
Learned counsel for the respondents contends that the order dated 1.6.88 has been complied with in letter and spirit.
Annexure P-3 has admittedly been quashed.
There is no dispute that the case of the petitioner for promotion has been considered and two orders have been produced in court which were passed on 2071990, wherein the date of promotion of the petitioner as Assistant has been fixed as 3091964, which the petitioner accepts as rightly fixed.
By the next order of the same date, the petitioner has been assigned the deemed date of promotion as Superintendent Grade II and Superintendent Grade I as 3.8.87 and 10.6.88, respectively.
The petitioner has challenged the fixation of these dates and he states that some juniors, namely, Shri Sewa Singh and Shri Mukhtiar Singh, have been placed senior to him.
He claims seniority as Superintendent Grade II w.e.f.
5680.
4.
In my considered view, these disputed facts, i.e. the fixation of the dates of seniority, cannot be determined in the contempt proceedings.
Contempt proceedings cannot be used as a means and threat to get a claim.
If the date of seniority has been wrongly fixed or there are any irregularities or illegalities in the orders passed, the petitioner is at liberty to seek the relief in appropriate proceedings.
In view of the contents of the impugned orders, it is writ large that the respondents have complied with the orders dated 161989, in letter and spirit, and there is no wilful or intentional disobedience of the orders of the Court.
In view of this no contempt is made out.
Rule is discharged.
Rule discharged.
