RL Anand, J - Satish Kumar son of late Shri Shamsher Singh, has filed the present Civil Writ Petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India for the issuance of an appropriate writ, order or direction quashing the impugned order Annexure P10, vide which the petitioner was dismissed from service.
The writ has been filed against Union of India, Director General of Police, B.S.F.
and Officer Commanding Shri PK Rathore.
2.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he was recruited in B.S.F.
in November, 1992 as a Constable (G.D.) vide No. 92477329 in 19 Bettry 1044 Arty Regiment.
He did his basic trading from STC Udhampur.
According to the petitioner, his father expired on 3121995.
His mother late Smt. Mehma alias Birma, his elder brother, and his sister-in-law expired on 691978, 10111992 and 851985, respectively.
The aforesaid members of his family died on account of tuberculosis.
In the absence of the petitioner, his elder brother was left for looking after the family.
Unfortunately for the petitioner his brother also suffered from Tuberculosis and was under the treatment of General Hospital, Bawani Khera.
On 1451997, when the petitioner was on duty under the Assistant Commandant Shri A.S. Bisht, he got a telephonic message in the office about the seriousness of his brother Shri Rajbir Singh and the Assistant Commandant was kind enough in relieving the petitioner from his duty to proceed on leave after submitting his leave application to the appropriate authority i.e. Deputy Commandant.
The petitioner submitted his application for leave before Deputy Commandant and stayed for 4 days there, awaiting for the acceptance of his leave application but the application was not accepted.
There was no other alternative for the petitioner but to leave the station on 1751997.
He came to his village in order to attend his ailing brother.
On reaching the house he came to know that the condition of his brother was very serious and as such the petitioner had to stay for two months in the house in order to look after his ailing brother as there was no other family member to look after him.
3.
On 2471997, the petitioner received a registered show cause notice vide which he was called upon to appear before Officer Commanding on or before 2471997.
It was not possible for the petitioner to appear before the Commanding Officer on 2471997 due to late delivery of the notice.
On the very next day i.e. on 2571997, the petitioner sent a telegram to respondent No. 3 praying for adjournment of the date for appearance as he got the notice on 2471997.
The petitioner did not receive any response to his telegram.
He waited for some time.
He went to the office of respondent No. 2 on 1481997.
He contacted respondent No. 3 and offered explanation about his absence.
The petitioner told respondent No. 3 that he could not appear on 2471997 because he received the notice very late.
However, respondent No. 3 did not listen to plea of the petitioner.
When the petitioner appeared before respondent No. 3 on 1481997 the petitioner got himself checked in BSF Hospital situated at the Headquarters concerned.
The petitioner was told by respondent No. 3 orally that he would receive a message whenever respondent No. 3 would be interested to hear the petitioner.
The petitioner again waited for some period and when he did not receive any message from respondent No. 3 he again approached to respondent No. 3 on 20111997.
The petitioner was told that he should run away and he would receive the letter in due course of time.
The petitioner became depressed on receiving this reply.
So he got himself medically checked from BSF Hospital situated at the Headquarters on 20111997.
The petitioner approached the higher authorities and explained his position.
A wireless message was issued to the petitioner vide which he was directed to be present before respondent No. 3 who was also directed to allow the petitioner to join the duty and to start departmental action against him.
When the petitioner approached respondent No. 3, he was told that his services had already been terminated and that there was no wireless message received by him from the Headquarters in order to allow the petitioner to join the duty.
The petitioner again made a representation to the higher authorities when he was not allowed by respondent No. 3 to join the duty.
4.
Thereafter, the petitioner received the order on 1931998 vide which the petitioner was dismissed from service with effect from 31121997.
After receiving the order of dismissal the petitioner again sent a reminder of his representation to the DG (Grievance Cell) but the petitioner was informed on 881998 that his representation/appeal has been rejected.
This message of rejection was received by the petitioner on 1781998.
With the above allegations, the petitioner has stated that the action on the part of respondent-authority was a mala fide one and the principles of natural justice have been violated.
He has challenged the order of dismissal through this writ petition.
5.
Notice of the writ petition was given to the respondents who filed the reply and denied the allegations.
According to the respondents, the petitioner has fabricated the documents.
Respondents stated that this Court has got no jurisdiction to entertain this writ petition.
On merits it was admitted that petitioner was enrolled in Border Security Force on 16111992 and his posting in 1044 Arty.
Regt.
was also admitted.
According to the respondents, the total service of the petitioner is three years and 10 months and most of the time either he remained on leave or he over-stayed his leave or he remained absent without leave for 523 days.
The petitioner is a habitual absentee.
Respondents have given different instances in para No. 2 of the written statement where the petitioner had either overstayed or remained absent.
According to the respondents, the petitioner was issued letters dated 2051997, 3051997 and 1061997.
The petitioner did not rely to these letters nor be stated any reasons about his absence.
A show cause notice dated 971997 was issued to the petitioner and he was directed to put forward his defence to the proposed action of his dismissal from service but he did not avail the same.
After following the proper procedure, the petitioner was dismissed from service with effect from 31121997.
The petitioner was an average constable.
He was habitual for over-staying.
The respondents also stated that no request was received by the competent authority as alleged by the petitioner.
No member of the disciplined force can be allowed to leave force without getting the leave sanctioned.
Even if the averments of the petitioner are to be accepted then also he would have reported back on 1771997 but he did not report for duty or informed his regiment about his absence though number of opportunities were given.
The petitioner has taken the service in a very casual manner.
He (had) never his duty seriously.
Action of the petitioner is gravest act of misconduct.
He had not submitted any application for leave, otherwise, there was no occasion for the authority not to accept the leave application.
He became absent without leave from the regiment and despite repeated attempts on the part of the competent authority he has not come forward to join his duty.
The show cause notice was issued to the petitioner on 971997 vide letter No. 1922.
In spite of the receipt of the letter the petitioner did not come forward with any reply.
No reply to the show cause notice dated 971997 was given by the petitioner.
The allegation of the petitioner that he met respondent No. 3 on 1481997 is nothing else except a false statement.
On that day, respondent No. 3 was not present in the regiments.
He was away of attend PW Course Serial 18 at CSWT Indore and rejoined the regiment on 1791997.
Since the petitioner never met respondent No. 3, therefore, there was hardly any occasion on the part of respondent No. 3 to ask the petitioner to run away.
The so-called outdoor slip is a fabricated document.
It is wrong that petitioner met respondent No. 3 on 20111997 when respondent No. 3 was away on earned leave.
The petitioner was dismissed from service with effect from 31121997 and he was informed about this over on 911998.
The dismissal order was sent twice at the home address of the petitioner but the same was received back undelivered with the remarks of postal authority that the addressee was not available.
There was no ill- will on the part of respondent No. 3 qua the petitioner who was a habitual absentee.
The action of the competent authority is legal and strictly in accordance with the provisions of B.S.F.
Act and Rules.
With this broad defence, the respondents have prayed for the dismissal of the writ petition.
6.
In support of his case the petitioner has placed on record death certificate issued by the Sarpanch of Gram Panchayat Dhana Nasan in which he was certified that the father of the petitioner and other family members died on different dates.
Annexure P-2 is the certificate of Medical Officer, General Hospital Bawani Khera certifying that Rajbir Singh son of Shri Shamsher Singh was under tuberculosis treatment with effect from 1011997.
Annexure P-3 is the show cause notice in which it has been clearly spelt out that petitioner became absent without leave with effect from 1751997.
Resultantly, the retention of the petitioner in service was considered undesirable.
The petitioner was also issued the show cause notice as to why the proposed action should not be taken against him.
The petitioner was supposed to give the reply and to appear before the authorities on 2471997.
Annexure P-7 is the copy of the telegram dated 31121997 addressed to 1044 BSF Arty Regiment vide which it was stated that petitioner has been directed to report for duty in the regiment and as desired by the competent authority the action may be taken against him as per BSF Act and Rules.
Annexure P-9 is the representation made by the petitioner against his order of dismissal and annexure P-10 is the order of dismissal of the petitioner from service.
Annexure P-12 is letter vide which the petitioner was informed that his case for reinstatement in BSF has been examined in detail by the Station headquarter and the same has been rejected being devoid of merit.
7.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and with their assistance have gone through the record of this case.
8.
The case set up by the petitioner is that he became absent from duty with effect from 1751997 as there was no alternative for him as he was to go to home in connection with the illness of his brother.
According to the petitioner he was relieved from duty by the Assistant Commandant on receipt of a telephone call from the village of the petitioner about the serious illness of his brother.
The petitioner waited for four days but his application was not accepted by respondent No. 3.
So much so when he was directed to appear before the Commanding Officer on 2471997, he received that information on the same day and he informed respondent No. 3 on 2571997 and sought the adjournment.
Thereafter, the petitioner started alleging that he appeared before respondent No. 3 on 20111997 and he was shunted out.
He approached the higher authorities who directed respondent No. 3 to accept the petitioner on duty.
But when the petitioner appeared before respondent No. 3 on 6.1.1998 he was rebuked and was informed that his services had already been terminated.
So much so he made representation which too has been illegally rejected.
9.
The learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner had tried to make out a case that absence from duty of the petitioner with effect from 1751997 onwards was not wilful.
He was compelled under the circumstances to leave for his village on hearing the serious illness of his brother.
The entire blame, if any, is upon respondent No. 3 who acted in a mala fide manner.
He did not allow the petitioner to say his grievance on 1481997, 20111997 and 6.1.1998.
10.
On the contrary, the stand of the respondent is that petitioner is a member of a disciplined force.
He was a habitual absentee.
He had the tendency of over-staying and as and when he proceeded on leave he become absent from duty without getting the leave sanctioned.
He was informed to appear before the Commanding Officer on 2471997.
The petitioner has fabricated the record in order to show that he tried to approach respondent No. 3 on 20111997 and 1481997 when on these days respondent No. 3 was not in the office.
The petitioner has been dismissed from service according to rules.
11.
After considering the rival contentions of the parties, this Court is of the considered opinion that petitioner has no case.
12.
It is the admitted case of the petitioner that when he left the regiment, no leave was ever sanctioned in his favour.
The absence from duty specially in the disciplined force is a serious misconduct.
It was not the right of the petitioner to become absent from duty without getting the leave sanctioned.
Before taking the action, the petitioner was informed on 971997 to appear before the Commanding Officer on 2471997.
Even if it is assumed for the sake of argument that notice dated 971997 was received by the petitioner on 2471997 still there is no proof on the record that any telegram was sent by the petitioner.
Also there is no proof that petitioner met respondent No. 3 on 1481997 or 20111997 or on 6.1.1998.
Absence of the petitioner with effect from 1751997 onwards is such a big misconduct that it cannot be approved from any standard.
As per the case of the petitioner he applied for four days leave with effect from 1751997.
In such a situation, the was supposed to report for duty on 2151997.
He has not done so.
In fact, the petitioner had been taking the things for granted.
If the petitioner had approached the higher authorities, he did not bring full facts to their notice regarding his conduct.
Any direction by the head office to respondent No. 3, does not condone the wilful act of absence of the petitioner.
In this view of the matter, I do not see any illegality in the impugned order vide which the petitioner was dismissed from service.
13.
Consequently, there is no merit in this writ petition and the same is hereby dismissed with no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
