Swatanter Kumar, J (Oral) - Issue notice to show cause why petition be not admitted.
2.
Mr. Rajbir Sehrawat, Deputy Advocate General, Haryana, accepts notice.
The petitioner was transferred to Karnal on 30th January, 2002 on his own request and keeping in mind his family circumstances.
It is contended on behalf of the petitioner that he has already shifted along with his family at Karnal and joined duties on 5th February, 2002.
Now after a period of 20 days, another order has been passed transferring the petitioner from Karnal to Gulha Cheeka in public interest.
There is apparently no public interest involved in this transfer order as Mr. Arun Kumar has been transferred at his own request, who has already stayed at Karnal for five years.
This situation has been created by the State itself and it must resolve the same by passing an appropriate order and in accordance with its policy.
We hardly see any reason that public exchequer should be burned unnecessarily for such frequent transfer.
3.
Consequently, we disposed of this writ petition with a direction to the Director, Industrial Training & Vocational Education, Haryana-respondent No. 2 to re-consider the matter and pass appropriate orders, keeping in view the above observations within a period of 30 days from the date a certified copy of this order is brought to his notice.
Till then the petitioner will not be disturbed.
4.
Writ petition stands disposed of.
