SK Jain, J - The petitioner has sought a mandate to the respondents to grant him the benefit of military service rendered by him during the period of Emergency, i.e. with effect from October 16, 1965 to January 1, 1968, towards grant of increments, pay fixation, senility and other benefits, in this petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
2.
The petitioner says that he was enrolled during the emergency period and he rendered service with effect from October 16, 1965 to October 23, 1973.
The plea that he rendered military service from October 16, 1965 to January 10, 1968 during the Emergency has not been controverted by the respondents.
An inference can be drawn that the plea was admitted to be correct.
3.
The claim of the petitioner has been disputed by the respondents on the ground that he was released from the Army on compassionate grounds and that the Department sought advice from the Financial Commissioner and Secretary to Government Haryana, Irrigation Department.
The Financial Commissioner vide letter dated June 25, 1990 informed the Engineer-in-Chief, Irrigation Department, Haryana, that the petitioner could not be granted the benefit of military service while he is in the civil service.
4.
The plea of the respondents that the petitioner is not entitled to the benefit of military service as he was released from the Army on compassionate grounds is not sustainable.
The State of Haryana added a proviso to Rule 4 of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 (for short, the rules) vide Notification No. C.S.R.
238-Const/Art309/Amd(3)/76, dated November 5, 1976 and the same reads thus :  "Provided that a person who has been released from the Military service on compassionate grounds shall not be entitled to any concessions under this rule."
The validity of this proviso to Rule 4 of the Rules was challenged in the apex court and it was held in Raj Pal Sharma and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1985(3) SLR 573, that the proviso was violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
5.
In Dhan Singh and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1991(1) SCT 137 (SC), the apex Court held that benefit of Military service was available to those who were enrolled or commissioned during the period of Emergency, i.e. from October 26, 1962 to January 10, 1968.
Concededly the petitioner joined the Military service during the period of Emergency and as such he cannot be denied the benefit of Military service.
For the reasons stated above, the writ petition succeeds.
The respondents are directed to grant the benefit of Military service to the petitioner, in the light of the observations made above, within six moths from the date of receipt of copy of this judgment.
