GR Majithia, J - The petitioner has sought a mandate to the respondents to release her the family pension payable to the widows of the employees who retired from service prior to July 1, 1979 in this writ petition under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
2.
The facts :-  The petitioner's husband was serving the respondents as Road Inspector.
He died in July 1963 while in service.
Respondent No. 1 took a policy decision which was conveyed by Punjab Government Finance letter No. 38/52/80-6FR/11825 dated December 24, 1980 for grant of pension to the widows/children of the employees who retired prior to July 1, 1974.
The petitioner who is a poor widow moved the authorities to release her pension as per the policy decision taken by respondent No. 1 but to no effect.
3.
The correspondence which transpired between the petitioner and the functionaries of the State reveal the callousness of the latter.
The matter has been dragged on so long but without any fruitful result.
The pension is not a bounty.
It is right which can only be deprived of in accordance with law.
No provision has been brought to my notice disentitling the petitioner to claim the pension.
The defence taken by the State is wholly unjustified.
Any agreement between the employee and the employer whereby the former relinquishes his claim to pension cannot be sustained in law being against public policy.
4.
In result, respondents No. 1 to 3 are directed to sanction pension to the petitioner in terms of the policy decision referred supra within one month from the date of receipt of the copy of this judgment and respondent No. 4 is directed to release the same within 15 days from the date of communication of sanction by respondents No. 1 to 3.
The Civil Writ Petition is disposed of accordingly.
No order as to costs.
Petition allowed.
