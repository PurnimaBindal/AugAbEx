THB Chalapathi, J (Oral) - This writ petition is filed challenging the award passed by the Labour Court, Amritsar, on 2341991.
2.
The workman-respondent No. 2 was employed as Chowkidar in the year 1981 and his services were terminated on 30.6.1987.
The respondent-workman raised an industrial dispute which was referred to the Labour Court, Amritsar, for adjudication.
The Labour Court issued notices to the management, but nobody appeared for the Management and, therefore, an ex parte award was passed in favour of the workman directing that he be reinstated with effect from 1st July, 1987 with continuity of service and full back wages.
3.
Aggrieved by the said award, this writ petition has been filed by the State of Punjab.
4.
There is no dispute that the 2nd respondent was employed in the year 1981 and his services were dispensed with w.e.f.
171987.
The petitioner did not adduce any evidence before the Labour Court that the 2nd respondent himself abandoned the work and left the job.
It is also not disputed that the petitioner was served with notices, but the petitioner has chosen to remain ex-parte and did not participate in the proceedings before the Labour Court.
Therefore, the averments made by the workman stand uncontroverted.
l do not, therefore, see any ground warranting interference with the order of the Labour Court reinstating the workman into service.
5.
Coming to the question of back wages, it is to be seen that the services of the petitioner were terminated with effect from 1-7-1987.
The respondent- workman has not raised the dispute till 22-9-1989.
For a period of two years he kept quiet.
There is a delay of more than two years in issuing a Notice of Demand.
In view of the decision of a Division Bench of this Court in CWP No. 16133 of 1993 decided on 2431994, I am of the opinion that the respondent- worhnan be granted back wages only from the date of Demand Notice i.e. with effect from 22.9.1989.
6.
Subject to the modification in regard to the date of payment of back wages i.e. w.e.f.
22-9-1989 till reinstatement, the writ petition is dismissed.
There will be no order as to costs.
Petition dismissed.
