VK Jhanji, J - In this petition petitioner is seeking a writ in the nature of Mandamus, directing the respondents to pay Provident Fund and also pension with effect from 1101957 when the Government took over the Primary Schools and the petitioner became a government servant.
2.
Petitioner joined Provincialised Local Body School, Kuthua Nangal, Amritsar on 581935 and was made permanent on 141937.
He has averred that he started depositing his share in the Provident Fund Account No.1694 w.e.f.
141937.
He continued to work as primary teacher from 581936 to 1101957 in Kathua Nangal, when the Government took control of all Primary Schools including the one where the petitioner was teaching.
Petitioner retired from service on 22.5.1966.
His grievance in the petition is that he has not been paid the money lying deposited in his Provident Fund Account and that he has been paid pension with effect from 18.12.1972 and not from the date when he retired and that too by counting his service only w.e.f.
1101957 and not from 141937 when he was made permanent.
3.
Respondents in their written statement have stated that the Punjab Government vide letter dated 281973 decided to grant pension to teachers of Provincialised Local Body Schools with effect from 18.12.1972 on the basis of their length of service in accordance with pension rules prevalent at the time of their retirement subject to the condition that no death-cum-retirement gratuity would be paid to them and they would not be required to refund the share of GP Fund received by them from the Local Body/Government.
In compliance with these instructions, pension has been sanctioned to the petitioner and therefore, the writ petition is not maintainable.
In regard to payment of money lying in Provident Fund Account, the reply is vague and it is not discernible as to whether payment has been made to the petitioner.
4.
Having heard the learned counsel, I am of the view that the petitioner is not entitled to pension from 22.5.1966 when he retired from service, but is entitled to pension with effect from 18.12.1972 when the Punjab Government vide letter No.6889-3Edu-III-73 20371 dated 281973 decided to extend pensionary benefits to the employees of privincialised local body schools who were over 30 years of age at the time of provincialisation and retiring between 1101957 to 31.10.1966.
A decision was taken that the employees of local body schools (including non-teaching staff) who were above the age of 30 years on 1101957 and who retired from service before 1111966, from the areas falling in the reorganised State of Punjab, be given pension with effect from 18.12.1972 on the basis of their length of service in accordance with the pension rules prevalent at the time of their retirement, subject to the condition that no death-cum-retirement gratuity would be paid to them and they would not be required to refund the share of Provident Fund received by them from the Local Bodies/Government at the time of retirement.
In view of this decision, petitioner is entitled to pension only from 18.12.1972 when the benefit was extended to similarly situated employees.
However, there is a merit in the contention of learned counsel for the petitioner that for finalising the pension case of the petitioner the respondents were to count entire service rendered by the petitioner including the one he rendered with the Provincialised Local Body School.
A Division Bench of this Court in Civil Writ Petition No.9814 of 1993 (Naurata Ram Singla v. State of Punjab etc.) decided on 351994, relying open judgment of Full Bench of this Court in Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab etc., AIR 1988 P & H 265 and other two decisions of this Court, held that benefit of service with the District Board is also to be taken into consideration while allowing pension.
The learned Deputy Advocate General, Punjab, has also not disputed the entitlement of petitioner to receive the amount lying in his provident fund account.
5.
Resultantly, this writ petition is allowed with a direction to the respondents to fix the pension of the petitioner, taking into consideration the entire period of service of the petitioner with the Provincialised Local Body School, and also to make payment of the amount lying in his provident fund account No.1694, within three months from the date of receipt of copy of this order from this Court.
If on re-fixation of pension the petitioner is found entitled to arrears, the payment of the same shall be made to him with interest at the rate of 12 per cent.
No costs.
