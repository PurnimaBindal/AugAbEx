GS Singhvi, J - This petition has been jointly filed by Raghbir Singh, Walaiti Ram, Raj Krishan Sachdeva, Dharam Singh and Hans Raj, all of whom are working as Assistant Store Officers inthe Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh (for short, `the PGI') for issue of a writ of mandamus to the respondents to grant them revised pay scale of Rs. 2,000-3,500 with a special pay of Rs. 100/- pm with effect from 111986.
They have also prayed for grant of interest at the rate of 18% per annum.
2.
Petitioners joined service in the PGI as Clerks/Store Clerks/Assistant Storekeepers in the pay scale of Rs. 60-175.
At the time of their appointment, the Institute was being controlled by the State of Punjab.
All of them were appointed/promoted as Storekeepers/Assistant Storekeepers on 1321965 except Walaiti Ram, who was appointed as Assistant Storekeeper with effect from 19111965.
The PGI became a corporate body under the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh Act, 1967 (for short `the Act').
The Governing Body of the PGI passed a resolution on 1721975 and approved the recommendations of the Standing Finance Committee of the PGI to the effect that in regard to the categories of staff who were drawing Punjab pay and allowances status quo should be maintained and they should be allowed allowances on the Punjab pattern.
The Standing Committee had also decided that all future recruitment/promotions in the service of the PGI should be made in the same scales as are prevalent in the All India Institute of Medical Sciences, New Delhi.
In the year 1967 the PGI enacted the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research Regulations, 1967.
The service conditions of the petitioners are being governed by these regulations.
All the petitioners were confirmed as Storekeepers.
They were promoted as Senior Storekeepers and then as Assistant Store Officers from the various dates mentioned in para 14 of the writ petition.
The pay scale of the post of Senior Storekeeper was revised by the PGI to Rs. 600-1,120 with effect from 111978.
The pay scale of the post of Assistant Store Officer was also revised from time to time.
With effect from 111986 the pay scale of the post of Assistant Store Officer was revised to Rs. 1,640-2,925.
Similarly, the pay scale of the post of Storekeeper was revised to Rs. 1,500-2,640.
3.
In the writ petition, the petitioners have stated that the Punjab Government has revised the pay scale of an identical post with effect from 111986 to Rs. 2,000-3,500 with a special pay of Rs. 100/- pm However, the institute did not grant the benefit of revised pay scale to them even though they had made representations and had served a notice of demand for justice.
They have also made reference to the recommendations made by the Director of the PGI for grant of pay scale of Rs. 1,800-3,200 to the Senior Storekeepers and that of Rs. 2,000-3,500 to the Assistant Store Officers.
4.
Notice of this petition was issued on 861993.
During the pendency of the writ petition before the Division Bench, the learned counsel appearing for the respondent-PGI made a statement that the matter is under the active consideration of the authorities.
The Court gave more than one opportunity to the respondent-PGI to take necessary decision.
However, no final decision was taken by the respondent-Institute and ultimately the Court admitted the writ petition on 1841994.
A period of more than 8 months has elapsed since then but no reply to the writ petition has been filed by either of the respondents.
In the absence of such reply, the averments made in the writ petition will have to be treated as correct.
5.
The only argument advanced by the learned counsel for the petitioners is that when the respondent-Institute had taken a decision to maintain the status quo regarding the service conditions of the employees who were drawing Punjab pay and allowances, there is no rationale or justification to deny benefit of revised pay scales introduced by the Government of Punjab for various categories of posts with effect from 111986.
The learned counsel pointed out that on 291994 the Director of the Institute has issued an order for revision of pay scale of Assistant Store Officers with effect from 111986.
He, however, pleaded that the benefit of special pay of Rs. 100/- has not been given to the persons holding the posts of Assistant Storekeepers.
He further pointed out that benefit of revised pay scale has not been given to the Senior Storekeepers and in this manner the respondent-Institute has perpetuated discrimination.
6.
A perusal of order dated 291994 does show that the respondent-Institute has revised the pay scales of Assistant Store Officers and Store Officers with effect from 111986.
This shows that the Institute has itself taken a decision to remedy the grievance of persons holding the posts of Assistant Store Officers insofar as it related to the grant of revised pay scale on Punjab pattern is concerned.
However, benefit of special pay of Rs. 100/- per month has not been given to those holding the posts of Assistant Store Officers even though such special pay is said to be admissible to the persons employed in the service of the Government of Punjab.
Similarly, no reason has been given by the respondent-Institute for not giving the benefit of revised pay scale of Rs. 1,800-3,200 to those holding the posts of Senior Storekeepers with effect from 111986.
It is, therefore, reasonable to hold that the respondent-Institute has not removed the discrimination in totality qua the Assistant Store Officers and has not given the benefit of revised pay scale to the Senior Storekeepers and this omission on the part of the respondent-Institute is not based on any rational reason.
Logically, it must be held that the petitioners have been discriminated and their fundamental right of equality has been infringed.
7.
In the result, the writ petition is allowed.
The respondent is directed to give benefit of special pay of Rs. 100/- pm to the Assistant Store Officers with effect from 111986 and also to give benefit of revised pay scale of Rs. 1,800-3,200 to the Senior Storekeeper (Dharam Singh) who held this post till 181987, i.e. the date on which he was promoted as Assistant Store Officer.
Necessary action be taken by the respondent-Institute for payment of arrears to the petitioners within a period of four months of the submission of a certified copy of this order.
Parties are left to bear their own costs.
