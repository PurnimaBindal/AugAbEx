TS Doabia, J - The petitioner sought appointment as Surveyor in the Fisheries Department.
He was seeking benefit of the reserved category.
In para "8" of the petition, it has been pleaded that he had better merit and should have been appointed.
2.
Objections have been filed.
3.
The stand taken by the State is that there were other candidates belonging to the same category, to which the petitioner belonged.
The merit of others was higher.
The petitioner was, therefore, not selected.
4.
Faced with the above situation, the learned counsel for the petitioner submits that initially only eight vacancies were notified.
Later on, these have been increased.
He submits that, if this is done, then the selection of petitioner could be made.
5.
Reliance is being placed on the following decisions :-  1.
1996(4) Supreme 411 : 1996(3) SCT 563 titled Prem Singh and ors. v. Haryana State Electricity Board and others.
2.
1996(1) SCT 660 (Ashok Kumar v. The Chairman, Banking Service Recruitment Board).
3.
1996(6) Supreme Court 674 : 1977(1) SCT 66 (Dilip Kumar Tripathy and others v. State of Orissa and others).
4.
AIR 1998 SC 18 : `1997(4) SCT 133 : 1997(4) SCT 395 (Surinder Singh and others v. State of Punjab and another.
I am of the opinion that the above argument would not be of any avail, so far as the petitioner is concerned.
As a matter of fact with the increase in the number of seats, his chances improved.
His zone of consideration was enlarged.
He was not found high in the merit.
This petition as such is found to be without any merit and is dismissed.
Petition dismissed.
