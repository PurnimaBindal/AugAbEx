G S Singhvi, J (Oral) - This petition has been filed essentially for quashing of Annexure P2 by which the petitioner's service was terminated on his attaining the age of 58 years.
However, careful reading of the writ petition shows that the petitioner has made claim for grant of pension as well.
The facts of the case show that the petitioner was appointed on the post of Leader Bhajan Party on fixed remuneration of Rs. 700/- per month, vide order dated 16.4.1979 Annexure P.1.
His service was terminated on 31.7.1987 but he was re-employed with effect from 20th August, 1987.
After he had attained the age of 58 years order Annexure P2 was issued by the Director, Public Relations, Haryana terminating the services of the petitioner.
2.
I have heard the learned counsel for the parties and perused the record of the case.
Shri Chaudhry, learned counsel for the petitioner, has relied on an order dated October 7, 1994 passed by the Division Bench in Civil Writ Petition No. 8222 of 1994 and argued that in view of the order passed by the Division Bench, the petitioner has a right to be treated as continuing in service upto the age of 60 years, and termination of his service is liable to be set aside.
3.
The learned Asstt.
Advocate General could not point out any special feature on the basis of which the case of the petitioner can be distinguished qua the case of Chander Bhan v. The State of Haryana, Civil Writ Petition No. 8222 of 1994 except that a specific prayer for regularisation of service had been made by the petitioner in Chander Bhan's case.
4.
In view of the judgment of the Division Bench holding that post of Leader Bhajan Party is Class IV post and that employee holding that post is entitled to continue in service upto the age of 60 years, this writ petition deserves to be allowed.
5.
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed.
Order Annexure P2 is declared illegal and is, hereby, quashed.
The petitioner shall be deemed to have continued in service upto the age of 60 years ie, on 10th of March, 1994, and he shall be deemed to have retired from service with effect from 3131993.
The petitioner shall be entitled to salary for the period between 11.3.1992 to 31st March, 1994, on his furnishing affidavit/undertaking before the Director Public Relations, Haryana that he was not gainfully employed elsewhere during this period.
6.
It shall also be open to the petitioner to make an application before the competent authority for regularisation of his service on completion of five years of service in terms of the policy/circular of the Haryana Government dated 2751993 by which the services of persons holding daily wage/work charged posts and casual labours were decided to be regularised with effect from 3131993.
If the petitioner makes such applications/representation, the concerned departmental authorities shall positively pass appropriate orders in the matter of regularisation of petitioner's service within a period of two months and while doing so various orders passed by this Court directing regularisation of services of persons who have completed five years service as on 3131993 shall be kept in view.
For the purpose of regularisation of service the petitioner shall be deemed to be continuing in service as on 3131993.
In the event of regularisation of service the petitioner shall get all consequential benefits.
