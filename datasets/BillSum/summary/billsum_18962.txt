Amends the Higher Education Act of 1965 to revise due process standards governing the accreditation process of educational institutions.
Requires accrediting agencies and associations to provide educational institutions with: written notice of deficiencies and a reasonable time period to correct such deficiencies; advance notice of any adverse action and an opportunity for a hearing; and a right to appeal an adverse action to an independent and impartial arbitration panel.
Requires accrediting agencies and associations to: ensure consistent application of accreditation standards to an educational institution's governing board in receivership; and allow public access to meetings involving accreditation recommendations.
Authorizes the Secretary of Education to issue regulations to further define due process requirements for the accreditation process.
