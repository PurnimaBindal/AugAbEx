Eurasia Foundation Act - Directs the Secretary of State to make an annual grant to the Eurasia Foundation, a private, nonprofit corporation whose purposes are to: promote civil society, private enterprise, and sound public administration and policy in the former Soviet Union; strengthen indigenous institutions that foster national development, constructive social change, equitable economic growth, and cooperative international relationships that are fully consistent with US interests in Eurasia; and conduct programs in response to initiatives in the region that would be difficult or impossible for an official US entity.
