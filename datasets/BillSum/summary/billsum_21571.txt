Keep Our Homes Act of 2008 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow: a tax deduction through 2012 for mortgage counseling costs; a tax deduction through 2009 for amounts, up to $5,000, of mortgage indebtedness in excess of the assessed value of a principal residence; a one-time tax credit for a percentage of the purchase price of a principal residence; nonitemizing taxpayers a tax deduction in 2008 or 2009 for real property taxes; and an exclusion from gross income for unemployment compensation received in 2008 or 2009.
