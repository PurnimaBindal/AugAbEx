The American Horse Slaughter Prevention Act - Prohibits a person from: slaughtering a horse for human consumption; importing to, or exporting from, the United States horseflesh or horses for human consumption; selling, bartering, transferring, receiving, or distributing horseflesh or horses for human consumption; or soliciting or knowingly causing any such actions.Sets forth provisions respecting: criminal and civil penalties; enforcement authority of the Secretary of Agriculture; placement of confiscated horses; euthanasia of unplaceable or severely injured or diseased horses; funding of animal rescue facilities; and exemptions.
