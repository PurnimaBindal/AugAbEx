Amends the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to suspend, through June 30, 1999, the duty on certain drug substances used as HIV antiviral drugs: (S)-N-tert-butyl-1,2,3,4-tetrahydro- 3-isoquinoline carboxamide; (S)-N-tert-butyl-1,2,3,4-tetrahydro-3- isoquinoline carboxamide hydrochloride salt; (S)-N-tert-butyl- 1,2,3,4- tetrahydro-3-isoquinoline carboxamide sulfate salt; and (3S)-1,2,3,4-tetrahydroisoquinoline-3-carboxylic acid.
