Commission for American Mathematics Leadership Act - Establishes the Commission for American Mathematics Leadership, which shall in coordination with the National Academy of Sciences (NAS): review existing research on mathematics education leadership, including US mathematics education status relative to international competitors; propose professional development priorities to strengthen mathematics teaching at all US educational levels; formulate an implementation proposal to assure world class achievement in mathematics education by the United States within a decade; and report findings and recommendations to the Congress.
Directs NAS to provide administrative support services to the Commission.
Makes available for Commission activities a specified amount from appropriations for the National Science Foundation.
