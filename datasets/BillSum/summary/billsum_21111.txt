Enhancing Nutrition in Medical Education Act of 2004 - Amends the Public Health Service Act to allow the Secretary of Health and Human Services to award grants to schools of medicine to integrate innovative curricula on nutrition into medical education.
Requires the Secretary to ensure that such curricula focus on preventive health measures, including : education on the causes, treatment, and prevention of obesity; office education and counseling to ensure an appropriate diet for mostly healthy people; prevention and treatment of common nutritional deficiencies; the appropriate and inappropriate use of herbs and supplements; office recognition and treatment of common eating disorders; and identification of special dietary needs, eating disorders, and appropriate routes of referral for medical nutrition therapy.
Requires grantees to: develop innovative curricula; integrate such curricula into the medical education at the school; evaluate the results achieved with the curricula; establish a multidisciplinary planning committee to develop the curricula; and report to the Secretary on the developed curricula and results achieved.
Requires the Secretary to prepare a consolidated report on the curricula and results achieved by grantees and to disseminate such report to schools of medicine.
