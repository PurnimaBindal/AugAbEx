Vietnam Veterans Children's Disability Act - Directs the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to: identify diseases occurring in children of Vietnam veterans or other veterans that have a positive association with parental exposure to an herbicide agent before the date of conception; include myelogenous leukemia among such diseases; provide needed health care for such children's covered diseases or any associated disabilities; and pay a monthly allowance to such children for any disability resulting from a covered disease, to be based on the degree of disability.Authorizes the Secretary to provide vocational training to such children if the achievement of a vocational goal is reasonably feasible.
