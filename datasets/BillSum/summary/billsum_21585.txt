Requires the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to: develop evaluation methods for lead detection products and techniques; conduct long-term research to establish the sources of children's lead exposure; and sponsor related public education and outreach efforts, including telephone hotline and clearinghouse systems.
Requires the Director of the National Institute of Standards and Technology to conduct research on: effective practices, techniques, and new technologies to reduce human exposure to lead; and safety and effectiveness of lead removal and containment products.
Authorizes State grants for compilation of lead exposure data.
