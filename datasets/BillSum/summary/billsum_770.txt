Technology Retraining and Investment Now Act for the 21st Century - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a tax credit for 50 percent of the information and communications technology education and training program expenses of individuals and employees, up to $4,000 in a taxable year.
Increases the allowable amount of such credit to $5,000 for expenses for a program operated: in an empowerment zone, enterprise community, or renewal community; in a school district in which at least 50 percent of the students are eligible for free or reduced-cost lunches; in an federally-declared disaster area; in certain rural areas receiving federal assistance; in an Indian tribal jurisdiction; by an employer with 200 or fewer employees during a specified period; or for a disabled individual.
Defines "information technology education and training program expenses" to include: course work; certification testing; apprenticeship programs registered by the Department of Labor; and other expenses essential to assessing skill acquisition.
Redefines "eligible educational institution" to include a commercial information and communications technology training provider.
Establishes an Information Technology Training Certification Advisory Board to develop: guidelines for computer science, information technology, and related college courses; and a list of the information technology training and certifications that qualify for the tax credit established by this Act.
