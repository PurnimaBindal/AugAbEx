Emergency Supplemental Appropriations for Hurricane Disaster Assistance Act, 2004 - Makes FY 2004 emergency supplemental appropriations for additional hurricane disaster assistance to: the Small Business Administration for the Disaster Loans Program Account; the Department of Defense for "Operation and Maintenance, Defense-Wide;" the Corps of Engineers - Civil Works for "Operation and Maintenance" and "Flood Control and Coastal Emergencies;" the Department of Homeland Security for the US Coast Guard for operating expenses and Emergency Preparedness and Response for "Disaster Relief;" the Department of the Interior for Fish and Wildlife Service and the National Park Service for construction; the Department of Health and Human Services for the Public Health and Social Services Emergency Fund; the Executive Office of the President for unanticipated needs; the Department of Veterans Affairs for the Veterans Health Administration for medical services, administration, and facilities; the Department of Housing and Urban Development for the Community Development Fund; and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration for space flight capabilities.
