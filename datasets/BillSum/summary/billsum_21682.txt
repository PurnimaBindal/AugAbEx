Distributed Power Hybrid Energy Act - Directs the Secretary of Energy to: develop and transmit to Congress a distributed power hybrid systems strategy (involving two or more independent electric energy sources of usually ten megawatts or less each, located close to a residential, commercial, or industrial load center, including reciprocating engines, turbines, microturbines, fuel cells, solar electric systems, wind energy systems, biomass power systems, geothermal power systems, or electrical components of cogeneration systems); and implement and integrate such strategy with other activities of the Department of Energy Office of Distributed Energy Resources.
