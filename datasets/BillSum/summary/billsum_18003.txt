Promoting Health for Future Generations Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase the tax deduction for contributions to health savings accounts (HSAs); allow Medicare and veterans health care beneficiaries to establish and contribute to HSAs; allow individuals age 50 or older (currently, age 55 or older) to make additional HSA contributions and to increase the allowable amount of such contributions; treat Medicare supplemental insurance premiums as a tax deductible medical expense; allow a new tax deduction for high deductible health plan premiums; allow individual contributions to a Medicare Advantage medical savings account (MSA); allow adult children of holders of HSAs and MSAs to inherit such accounts; and allow MSA funds to be used for wellness and fitness programs.
Amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act to allow MSAs to provide preventive care without requiring a deductible for expenses related to such care.
