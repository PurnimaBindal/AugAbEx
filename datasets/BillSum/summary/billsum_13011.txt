Surface Transportation Board Reform Act of 2003 - Declares as primary objectives for US rail transportation policy: ensuring effective competition among rail carriers at origin and destination; maintaining reasonable rates in the absence of such competition; maintaining consistent and efficient rail transportation service to shippers, including the timely provision of railcars requested by them; and ensuring that smaller carload and intermodal shippers are not precluded from assessing rail systems due to volume requirements.
Requires a rail carrier, upon a shipper's request, to establish a rail transportation rate.
Requires the Surface Transportation Board, if a shipper challenges the reasonableness of such a rate, to then determine its reasonableness without regard to specified factors.Sets forth certain requirements with respect to: acceptance of requests for grain service by rail carriers; and the Board's mandate to require terminal facilities owned by a rail carrier providing rail transportation to be used by another rail carrier.
Requires the Board in a proceeding involving the approval of the merger or control of at least two Class I railroads to consider, among other things, at least the: safety and environmental effects of the proposed transaction; and the effect of such transaction on rail passenger transportation.Sets forth certain requirements with respect to: the Board ensuring that commuter authorities are able to provide commuter rail passenger transportation that meets the commuter rail passenger transportation needs of the United States; and water carriers making their tariffs containing the rates established for their transportation or service available electronically to any person.
