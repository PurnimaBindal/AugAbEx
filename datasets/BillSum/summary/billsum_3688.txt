Neighbor to Neighbor Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code respecting charitable contributions to: allow non-itemizing taxpayers a limited charitable deduction; exclude charitable deductions from the income-based limitation on itemized deductions: repeal the special limitation on charitable contributions of capital gain property; extend the excess contribution carryover period; and allow tax-free distributions from individual retirement accounts (IRAs) for qualifying charitable purposes.Repeals the excise tax on the net income of tax-exempt foundations.
