Trickett Wendler, Frank Mongiello, Jordan McLinn, and Matthew Bellina Right to Try Act of 2017 (Sec.2)This bill amends the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to exempt,&nbsp;from&nbsp;specified requirements and restrictions under that Act and other laws, the provision of certain unapproved, investigational drugs to a terminally ill patient who has exhausted approved treatment options and&nbsp;is unable to participate in a clinical trial involving the drugs.
The manufacturer or sponsor of an eligible investigational drug must report annually to the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) on any use of&nbsp;the drug in accordance with these provisions.
The FDA shall post an annual summary report of such use on its website.
The bill limits&nbsp;the liability of&nbsp;a sponsor, manufacturer, prescriber,&nbsp;or dispenser that provides, or declines to provide, an eligible investigational drug to an eligible patient in accordance with the bill.
