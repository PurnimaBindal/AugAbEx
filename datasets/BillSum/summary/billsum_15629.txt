Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise the tax credit for biodiesel used as fuel to include a credit for the production of agri-biodiesel fuel equal to 10 cents for each gallon produced.
Limits: the amount of qualified agri-biodiesel production of a producer to 15 million gallons for any taxable year; and eligible producers to those with a productive capacity not exceeding 60 million gallons.
Revises the small ethanol producer tax credit to: expand the eligibility of small ethanol producers for the credit; exclude the credit from the definition of passive activity credit; and exclude credit amounts from inclusion in gross income.
