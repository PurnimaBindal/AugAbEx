Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow an investment tax credit for five percent of the cost of a qualified truck.
Defines "qualified truck" as a motor vehicle: first placed in service on or after January 1, 2007, and before January 1, 2008; powered by diesel fuel; having a gross vehicle weight of more than 26,000 pounds; and which complies with certain Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) regulations for diesel emissions.
Allows such credit to reduce regular or alternative minimum tax liability.
Permits a taxpayer election to expense the cost of qualified trucks placed in service in 2007.
