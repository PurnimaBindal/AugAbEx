Anti-Spamming Act of 2001 - Amends the Federal criminal code to make it unlawful for anyone to intentionally: and without authorization initiate the transmission of a bulk unsolicited electronic mail (e-mail) message to a protected computer with knowledge that such message falsifies an Internet domain or other identifier; or sell or distribute any computer program that is designed to conceal the source or routing information of such e-mail, has only limited commercial use otherwise, or is marketed for use in concealing such information.
Provides civil penalties.
Allows attorneys' fees and other litigation costs as part of civil relief from such violations.
