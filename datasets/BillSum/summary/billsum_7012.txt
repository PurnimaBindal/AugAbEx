National Centers of Excellence for Regional Smart Growth Planning Act - Directs the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to: designate not fewer than three national centers of excellence for regional smart growth planning, selected from metropolitan planning organizations (MPOs) that have demonstrated regional planning excellence; develop guidelines for the designation and operation of the Centers; and allocate specified funds to each Center to carry out this Act.
Authorizes the Administrator to make grants on a competitive basis to eligible entities for: improving the collection of data and the development of models for smart growth planning; implementing comprehensive regional smart growth planning programs; applying planning outcomes in regional transportation plans; and working with local governments to coordinate land use, transportation, and air quality planning.
Directs the Centers to: collect and distribute to MPOs information and best practices about regional smart growth planning; make research and development recommendations to EPA and other entities to improve the quality and ease of smart growth analysis; work with institutions of higher education and state and federal agencies to improve smart growth analysis; and provide technical assistance to grant recipients.
