Requires a State reserve unit to: withhold the net proceeds from the sale or refinancing of real property in the State against which a lien for overdue child support by the seller or refinancer has arisen; hold a certain amount of such proceeds (anticipated future child support) in trust for the benefit of the child or children for whom the person has a support obligation; and apply such amounts to satisfy such overdue child support, if all other available remedies are insufficient to do so.
