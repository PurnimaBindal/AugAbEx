Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to the tax exemption for structured settlement factoring transactions (i.e., transfers of the right to receive periodic payments under a settlement of a legal claim for damages in exchange for a lump sum payment)&nbsp;to require that&nbsp;court orders approving such transfers include additional requirements, including that: (1)&nbsp;the annual discount rate of the consideration for&nbsp;a transfer does not exceed the prime interest rate plus 5%; the aggregate amount of charges, fees, and other expenses payable by the payee of the lump sum&nbsp;payment&nbsp;do not exceed 2% of the value of the consideration to the payee; the payee is not liable for any penalty or forfeiture if the transfer does not satisfy the additional requirements imposed by this Act; the transferee (the entity receiving the right to the periodic payments)&nbsp;has given written notice of its name, address, and taxpayer identification number to the annuity issuer and the structured settlement obligor and has filed a copy of such notice with the state court or other authority issuing the&nbsp;order approving the transfer; the transfer is fair and reasonable; and the transferee makes certain disclosures to the payee.
