Amends the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to reduce permitted contribution amounts to Federal candidates by multicandidate political committees.
(Sec.
Prohibits a House of Representatives candidate from accepting contributions from a multicandidate political committee in excess of 50 percent of all contributions accepted by such candidate during a reporting period.
(Sec.
Limits contributions which a House of Representatives candidate may accept from other than in-State residents.
(Sec.
Removes contribution limitations for congressional candidates whose opponents intend to spend or spend $100,000 or more from personal sources.
(Sec.
Prohibits leadership committees.
(Sec.
Revises contribution through intermediary provisions.
