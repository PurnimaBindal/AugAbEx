Food Allergy and Anaphylaxis Management Act of 2008 - Requires the Secretary of Health and Human Services to develop and make available to local educational agencies a policy to manage the risk of food allergy and anaphylaxis in schools to be implemented on a voluntary basis only.
Directs that such policy address: the documentation of student allergies and the development of an individual health care plan for each such student; communication with emergency medical services, school staff, parents, and students; reduction of exposure to causative agents of food allergies; food allergy management training; and administration of epinephrine.
Provides that the policy developed by the Secretary and the food allergy management guidelines contained in such policy are voluntary.
