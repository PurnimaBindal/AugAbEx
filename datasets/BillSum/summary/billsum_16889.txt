Justice for Imprisoned Americans Overseas Act of 2012 or Jacob's Law of 2012 - Directs the Secretary of State to submit a semiannnual report to Congress for five years that identifies: US citizens imprisoned in foreign countries whose fundamental due process and human rights are being violated, the due process and human rights violations that are being committed against such US citizens, and the government officials who are responsible for such violations or who are not fulfilling their official responsibility to protect such rights.
Prohibits the Secretary of State from issuing any visa to, and the Secretary of Homeland Security (DHS) from permitting entry to the United States of, any such identified official and his or her immediate family members.
Makes such ban permanent if an identified US citizen dies while in foreign custody.
