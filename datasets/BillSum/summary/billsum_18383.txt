Oral Health Initiative Act of 2008 - Requires the Secretary of Health and Human Services to establish an Oral Health Working Group to: review the effectiveness of, and recommend improvements to, existing federal oral health programs; and develop programs to improve the oral health of, and prevent dental disease in, children, Medicaid-eligible adults, medically-compromised adults, and other vulnerable populations at the highest risk of dental disease.
Sets forth duties for the Group, including to: review existing oral health programs and policies within the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS); identify opportunities for new programs; and make recommendations for improvements to the financing of oral health care.
Requires the Secretary to establish an advisory panel to provide advise and recommendations to the Group.
