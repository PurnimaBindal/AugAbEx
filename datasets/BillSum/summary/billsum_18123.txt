Safety and Accountability in Construction Act - Requires state transportation departments to submit project management plans for federal-aid highway projects to the Secretary of Transportation for approval.
Authorizes the Secretary to contract with a qualified independent engineer to provide independent safety reviews of federally-financed highway projects if certain determinations are made.
Requires the Secretary to establish a national tunnel inspection program (including national standards for the inspection of such tunnels).
Authorizes states to obligate apportioned surface transportation program funds for: the construction and repair of tunnels; and tunnel safety inspections.
