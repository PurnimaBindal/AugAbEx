Reaffirms congressional opposition to trade boycotts of Israel and calls on the President to take stronger steps to end all trade boycotts of Israel.
Amends the Trade Act of 1974 to direct the United States Trade Representative to include in: the annual trade barrier report a list of each country (and specified boycott-related information about such country) that participates in any trade boycott of Israel; and the National Trade Estimate a report on US steps taken at the World Trade Organization (WTO) or other international fora to end the trade boycott of Israel.
