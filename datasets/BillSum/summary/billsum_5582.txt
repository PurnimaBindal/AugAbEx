Strategic Communication Act of 2005 - Directs the Secretary of State to solicit from tax-exempt national security and foreign policy research organizations offers to establish a Center for Strategic Communication, and select one organization to establish such Center.
Includes among Center duties: provision of information and analysis to the Department of State, the Department of Defense (DOD), the Department of Justice, the Department of Homeland Security, and the Director of National Intelligence on US security and foreign policy issues; development of US communications strategies and monitoring techniques; support of government-wide strategic communication through services provided on a cost-recovery basis; contracting with private sector and academic entities; and mobilization of nongovernment initiatives.
