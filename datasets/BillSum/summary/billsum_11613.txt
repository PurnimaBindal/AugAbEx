San Francisco Bay Shipping and Fisheries Enhancement Act of 1999 - Requires: the Secretary of the Army to carry out a navigation project in San Francisco Bay, California, to remove underwater hazards to navigation near Alcatraz Island; the Commandant of the Coast Guard to modify navigation lanes and reroute vessel traffic after completion of the second phase of such project to improve safety and efficiency; and the Maritime Administrator to ensure that operational vessels owned by, or operated under contract to, the Maritime Administration are surveyed for risks for oil spills or other hazards to human health or the environment.
Authorizes appropriations.
