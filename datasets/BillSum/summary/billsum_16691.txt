Roads to Success Act of 2005 - Directs the Secretary of Education to make a grant to a nonprofit educational organization, Roads to Success, to develop a higher education preparation program curriculum for students in grades 7-12.
Requires the curriculum to provide such students with: one class hour of higher education preparation each week; information on higher education opportunities and importance; career awareness development; life skills building; and education planning.
Requires the grantee to award subgrants to up to five state educational agencies to provide such students with higher education preparation programs using the curriculum.
