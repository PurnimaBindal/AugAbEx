Invest in Small Business Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase the exclusion from gross income of gain from the sale or exchange of qualified small business stock from 50 to 75 % of such gain and to reduce the required holding period for such stock from five to four years; allow a 100% exclusion of gain from such stock sold by a business in an empowerment zone; deny a tax exclusion for small business stock held by a 25% controlled corporate group; repeal as an item of tax preference under the alternative minimum tax the exclusion of gain from the sale of small business stock; repeal the 28% income tax rate on the gain from the sale of small business stock which is not excluded from gross income; revise the definition of "qualified small business" for certain tax purposes to mean a C corporation with aggregate gross assets not exceeding $100 million (currently, $50 million); and provide for an annual inflation adjustment to the small business aggregate gross asset amount after 2007.
