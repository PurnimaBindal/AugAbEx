Fab Lab Classroom Modernization Act This bill&nbsp;authorizes the Department of Education to award grants for FY2019-FY2023 to states&nbsp;for local educational agencies&nbsp;to purchase digital-fabrication laboratory equipment or to provide professional development related to incorporating a digital-fabrication laboratory into the curriculum.
A digital-fabrication laboratory is a high-technology workspace&nbsp;that includes&nbsp;equipment such as three-dimensional printers and laser engravers.
