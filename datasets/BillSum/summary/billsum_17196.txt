Forest Service Cost Reduction and Fiscal Accountability Act of 1998 - Directs the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service, to prepare an implementation schedule for a Forest System accounting system to be known as the All Resources Accounting System which shall include: specified program areas; cost allocations; identification of indirect expenditures and general administration costs; and separate reports for Forest System units, State and private forestry, and research.
Directs the Secretary to: limit and eliminate within a certain period indirect expenditures from Forest Service trust funds or permanent appropriations; disclose indirect expenditures and general administration costs in annual budget requests; and prepare a five-year Forest System cost reduction strategic plan.
