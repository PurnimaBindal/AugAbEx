Gulf State Community Renewal Commission Act - Establishes the Gulf State Community Renewal Commission to: examine, evaluate, and report on the rebuilding of both urban and rural communities in areas that were affected by Hurricane Katrina; identify actions necessary for rebuilding such communities and neighborhoods; and create rebuilding models and plans.
Requires the models and plans to address: transportation needs; economic development and job creation; the tourism industry; housing needs; agriculture development; environmental concerns; and natural disaster mitigation.
