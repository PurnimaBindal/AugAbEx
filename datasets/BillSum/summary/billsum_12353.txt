Revises corporate average fuel economy standards (CAFE standards) provisions for non-passenger and passenger automobiles to: provide for increased average fuel economy standards for non-passenger and passenger automobiles; permit separate average fuel economy standards for non-passenger and passenger automobiles based on vehicle attributes related to fuel economy; and establish a corporate average fuel economy credit transferring program within a manufacturer's fleet.
Provides for the establishment of a public education campaign to provide information to US drivers about immediate measures that may be taken to conserve transportation fuel.
