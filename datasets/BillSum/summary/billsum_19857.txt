Transfers to the Administration all functions of the following Federal agencies that relate to administration or enforcement of the food safety laws, as determined by the President: the Food Safety and Inspection Service of the Department of Agriculture; the Center for Food Safety and Applied Nutrition of the Food and Drug Administration (FDA); the Center for Veterinary Medicine of FDA; the National Marine Fisheries Service of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration of the Department of Commerce as it relates to the Seafood Inspection Program; and such others as the President may designate by executive order.
