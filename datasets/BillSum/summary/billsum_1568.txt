Amends the Federal Fire Prevention and Control Act of 1974 to authorize the FFEMA Director to make grants on a competitive basis for protecting the health and safety of the public and firefighting personnel against fire and fire-related hazards.
Requires: ten percent non-Federal matching funds; each grantee to report to the Director on grant uses; and grants to be made to a variety of recipients, including paid and volunteer firefighters and urban, suburban, and rural fire departments.
Authorizes appropriations.
