Taxpayer Fairness Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: impose a 50% tax on bonuses, including retention bonuses, exceeding $400,000 paid in 2009 to employees of financial institutions (including the Federal National Mortgage Association [Fannie Mae] and the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation [Freddie Mac]) that received $5 billion or more in emergency economic assistance from the federal government; and limit the business tax deduction for such bonuses to 50% of the amount of such bonuses.
