Health Care Safety Net Act of 2007 - Amends the Public Health Service Act to authorize appropriations for FY2008-FY2012 for: health centers to meet the health care needs of medically underserved populations; the National Health Service Corps; and rural health care programs.
Requires studies or reports on: school-based health centers; community health centers; and integrated health system models for the delivery of health care services to medically underserved populations.
