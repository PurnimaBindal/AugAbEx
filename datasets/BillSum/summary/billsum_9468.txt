Directs the Secretary of Education to award National Classified School Employees of the Year Awards to public school employees within certain occupational specialties who provide exemplary service to students in pre-kindergarten through higher education.
Requires the Secretary to choose an awardee each year, out of nominations received from each state, from each of the following occupational specialties: paraprofessionals; clerical and administrative services; transportation services; food and nutrition services; custodial and maintenance services; security services; health and student services; technical services; and skilled trades.
