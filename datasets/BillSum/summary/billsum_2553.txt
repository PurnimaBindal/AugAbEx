Zero Gravity, Zero Tax Act of 2005 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: exclude from gross income space-related income from products or articles produced, or services provided, in or from outer space; allow an investment tax credit for the purchase of stock in a space company that has average annual gross receipts not exceeding $100 million and that derives more than 70 percent of its gross receipts from space-based business; and exclude from gross income gain from the sale or exchange of any stock of certain space corporations.
