American Renewable Fuel and Job Creation Act of 2017 This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to modify and extend: the income tax credit for biodiesel and renewable diesel used as fuel, and the excise tax credit for biodiesel fuel mixtures.
The bill: makes the credits available to domestic producers of the fuels rather than the policy under current law of providing a mixture credit to the blender of the fuel, increases the income tax credit for certain small biodiesel producers, and extends the credits through 2020.
