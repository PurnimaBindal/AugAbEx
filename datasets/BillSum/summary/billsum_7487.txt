Safe Commercial Transportation of Horses Act of 1995 - Amends specified Federal law (and entitles it as the Federal Humane Methods of Livestock Slaughter Act) to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to: issue regulations governing the humane commercial transportation of horses for slaughter; enter into related cooperative agreements with Federal and State entities; and make necessary investigations and inspections.
Establishes civil and criminal penalties for violations of such provisions, including interference with enforcement activities.
Authorizes appropriations.
