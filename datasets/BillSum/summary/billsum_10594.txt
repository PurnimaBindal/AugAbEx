Commission on the Abolition of the Transatlantic Slave Trade Act - Establishes the Commission on the Abolition of the Transatlantic Slave Trade.
Sets forth Commission duties, including: planning activities appropriate to commemorate the 200th anniversary of the abolition of the transatlantic slave trade; encouraging civic, historical, educational, religious, economic entities, as well as state and local governments to participate in anniversary activities; facilitating the convening of a joint meeting or joint session of the Congress for related ceremonies and activities; promoting the sponsorship of related conferences and exhibitions; and examining reports and raise public awareness of of modern-day slavery and human trafficking.
Directs the Commission to submit an initial report to Congress by March 31, 2009, and a final report upon termination.
Terminates the Commission on December 31, 2009.
