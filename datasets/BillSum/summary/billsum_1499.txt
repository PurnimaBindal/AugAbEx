Part-time Student Assistance Act - Amends the Higher Education Act of 1965 to provide access and assistance to increase college attendance and completion by part-time students.
Exempts from student aid need formula determinations earnings up to: $9,000 by dependent students; $12,000 by independent students with no dependents (other than spouse); and $12,000 by independent students with dependents (other than spouse).
Provides for a formula for a revised table of income protection allowances.
Exempts earned income credits from the calculation of income.
Revises and expands a program (child care access means parents in school) which provides grants to institutions of higher education for child care assistance for low-income students.
Directs the Secretary of Education to establish a year-round Pell grant pilot program.
Authorizes a demonstration program to create a holistic approach to addressing the needs of part-time students.
Allows such program to: be at up to 150 associates and bachelors degree-granting institutions; and include grants, leveraging funds from non-Federal sources, comprehensive child care, and better tailored remedial course programs.
