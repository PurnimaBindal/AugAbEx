Stop Debt Collection Abuse Act of 2017 This bill amends the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act to&nbsp;apply&nbsp;that Act's restrictions to&nbsp;collectors of debt owed to a federal agency.
A federal agency that is a creditor may not sell or transfer a debt to a debt collector until&nbsp;90 days after the obligation becomes delinquent or defaults.
Specified notice to the consumer of such a sale or transfer is required.
A&nbsp;collector of debt owed to a federal agency may not collect any&nbsp;interest, fee, charge, or expense&nbsp;that is: unreasonable in relation to&nbsp;actual costs, not authorized by a contract between the debt collector and the federal agency,&nbsp;or greater than 10% of the amount collected.
The Government Accountability Office shall study and report to Congress on&nbsp;the use of debt collectors by federal, state, and local government agencies.
