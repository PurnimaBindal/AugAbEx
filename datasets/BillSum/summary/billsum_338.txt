Shelter, Land, and Urban Management (SLUM) Assistance Act of 2009 - Directs the President, through the Secretary of State and the United States Agency for International Development (USAID), to develop a strategy to provide affordable housing and sustainable urban development in developing countries.
Authorizes the President to provide technical assistance and financial support to developing countries for: basic shelter and affordable housing, particularly for residents of impoverished urban areas; safe water, sanitation, and solid waste removal services; real property use and ownership rights; and governmental urban planning.
