Jurisdictional Certainty Over Digital Commerce Act - Reserves solely to the Federal Government the responsibility and authority to regulate digital commercial transactions.
Prohibits: any State or political subdivision from enacting or enforcing any provision regulating such transactions; or the delegation of such responsibility or authority to any State.
Makes such reservation inapplicable to non-digital commercial transactions, and makes this Act inapplicable to Uniform Commercial Code requirements in effect in any State.
