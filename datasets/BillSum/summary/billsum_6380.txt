HHS Women Scientist Employment Opportunity Act - Directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to: establish policies for the Department of Health and Human Services on matters relating to the employment of women scientists; and monitor compliance and take appropriate action if policies have been violated.
Mandates implementation of the recommendations of the Task Force on the Status of NIH (National Institutes of Health) Intramural Women Scientists.
Provides for a study and report on pay equity.
Authorizes appropriations.
