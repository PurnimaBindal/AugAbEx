Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a business tax credit for the production and use of biodiesel fuel.
Specifies the required mixture rate for biodiesel fuel eligible for the credit.
Requires, for purposes of the credit: certification from the producer identifying the product; sale of the fuel for use in a trade or business; and sale of the fuel for use in a vehicle that is not registered for highway use.
Imposes a tax for biodiesel mixture which is not used as fuel, but for which a credit was taken.
