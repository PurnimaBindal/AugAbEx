Authorizes the Secretary of Transportation to convey: the Coast Guard LORAN Station in Nantucket, Massachusetts, to the town of Nantucket; and the Plum Island Boathouse and Lighthouse, Massachusetts, to the city of Newburyport, Massachusetts.
Provides a reversionary interest under each such conveyances if the property ceases to be used for public educational, park, recreational, cultural, historical preservation, or other similar purposes, or as a present or future site for Coast Guard aids to navigation.
Requires the maintenance of navigation functions within each conveyance.
