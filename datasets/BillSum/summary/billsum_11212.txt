Directs the Secretary of Labor to establish a program to facilitate the establishment of State programs to foster increased employee ownership and greater employee participation in business decisionmaking.
Requires the Secretary to establish the Office of Employee Ownership and Participation to support existing State programs and facilitate new State programs, including: making of matching Federal grants; acting as a clearinghouse for information; and facilitating information exchange and promoting State programs.
Authorizes appropriations.
Requires the Director of the Office of Employee Ownership and Participation to report to the Congress.
