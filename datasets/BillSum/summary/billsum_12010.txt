Fusion Energy Research Accountability Act of 1994 - Directs the Secretary of Energy to convene a fusion policy advisory panel to report to the Congress on: a single narrow approach to fusion power; and develop recommendations for a plan for the future of domestic fusion energy research, ensuring attention to alternative fusion concepts.
Directs the Secretary to: transmit to the Congress a comprehensive management plan for fusion energy research based upon the panel's recommendations; establish a mechanism for ongoing independent evaluation of the fusion energy research program; and transmit a status report biennially to the Congress.
