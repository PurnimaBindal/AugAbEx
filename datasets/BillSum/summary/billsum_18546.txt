Restoring Local Control of Airports Act of 2016 This bill reauthorizes through FY2021 and revises the Airport Improvement Program.
Specifically, the bill: eliminates the federal cap on passenger facility charges (local user fees) of $450 per enplanement; and with respect to airports that increase such charges beyond $450 per enplanement, provides for corresponding reductions in program grant funding.
In addition, the bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to reduce the federal airline ticket tax.
