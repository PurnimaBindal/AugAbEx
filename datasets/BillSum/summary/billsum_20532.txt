Technological Resource to Assist Criminal Enforcement (TRACE) Act - Amends the Brady Handgun Violence Prevention Act to require a licensed manufacturer or importer of firearms to: test fire manufactured or imported firearms as specified by the Attorney General; prepare ballistics images of the fired bullet and cartridge casings; make the records available to the Attorney General for entry into a computerized database; and store the fired bullet and cartridge casings.Directs the Attorney General to assist firearm manufacturers and importers in complying with these requirements by: acquiring, installing, and upgrading ballistics equipment and bullet and cartridge casing recovery equipment; hiring or designating sufficient personnel to develop and maintain a ballistics database; providing education about the role of ballistics; and providing for the coordination among law enforcement, regulatory agencies, and the firearm industry to curb firearm-related crime and illegal firearm trafficking.Requires the Attorney General to: establish a computer system through which State and local law enforcement agencies can promptly access ballistics records; require training for all ballistics examiners; conduct mandatory ballistics testing of all firearms obtained by or in the possession of the Department of Justice; and report to Congress on the implementation of this Act.Prohibits the use of ballistics information of individual guns for prosecutorial purposes unless officials have a reasonable belief that a crime has been committed and that ballistics information would assist in the investigation.
