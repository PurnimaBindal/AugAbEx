Holocaust Victims Insurance Relief Act of 2001 - Directs the Archivist of the United States to establish and maintain a Holocaust Insurance Registry to consist of information on holders and issuers (and related liable entities) of Holocaust-era insurance policies that were: in effect after January 30, 1933, and before December 31, 1945; and issued to a policyholder domiciled in any area of Europe that was occupied or controlled by Nazi Germany or any ally or sympathizer during such period.Requires: insurers to file such information in an electronic format with the Secretary of Commerce by a specified deadline; the Secretary to assess a civil penalty for each day an insurer fails to comply; and notify each State's commissioner of insurance of the identity of any insurer that has failed to file such information or to satisfy any penalty.
