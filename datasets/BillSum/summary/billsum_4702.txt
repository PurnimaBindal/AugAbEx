United Nations Accountability Act of 1997 - Prohibits the payment of arrearages of US contributions to the United Nations (UN) until the President certifies to, and a joint resolution is approved by, the Congress that the UN has implemented specified reforms, including: a reformulation of the assessed US payment to the UN; implementation of a code of conduct for UN employees; strengthening of the powers of the Office of Inspector General of the UN; reform of the UN grievance system to permit UN employees to hire outside counsel; implementation of certain reforms to UN procurement procedures; whistleblower protection for UN employees; adoption of a no growth biennial budget; acceptable downsizing; a freeze on UN employee salaries; making the eight member states with the highest contributions to the UN permanent members of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budget Questions; providing access by Budget Committee member states to certain expenditure documents; requiring an annual review and reauthorization of any peacekeeping missions by the UN Security Council; UN reimbursement for Department of Defense contributions to UN peacekeeping missions; satisfaction of US arrearages in contributions to the UN; allowance of all member states to be nominated to the Security Council; abandonment of UN efforts to establish an international tax; and UN noninterference with any people's religion, moral values, culture, or traditions.
