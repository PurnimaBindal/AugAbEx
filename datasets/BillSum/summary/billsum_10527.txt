Helping Those Who Serve Act - Directs the Secretary of Defense to conduct a demonstration project (project) designed to: increase access of members of the Armed Forces (members) returning from deployment abroad, and their families, to accessible, safe, and confidential mental health counseling; reduce the stigma and career repercussions associated with seeking such services; and make high-quality mental health services more user-friendly to such members and their families.
Requires the Secretary to: establish a Special Working Group to develop guidelines and recommendations to ensure the protection of confidentiality for members participating in the project; establish an advisory group to oversee project implementation; submit project reports to the congressional defense committees; and conduct a study of reasons for attrition among military mental health professionals, and make recommendations to increase the recruitment and retention of such individuals.
