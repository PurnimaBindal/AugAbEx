Trafficking Victims Protection Reauthorization Act of 2007 - Amends the Trafficking Victims Protection Act of 2000 to authorize trafficking victims-related appropriations for activities of: the Interagency Task Force to Monitor and Combat Trafficking; the Secretary of Health and Human Services; the Secretary of State; the President; the Secretary of Labor; the Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation; the Secretary of Homeland Security; and the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development.
Makes juvenile victims of trafficking in persons eligible for interim assistance under such Act.
Directs the Attorney General to prepare model legislation for state use to define and prohibit all acts relating to prostitution of children and trafficking in children for the purpose of labor or sexual exploitation.
