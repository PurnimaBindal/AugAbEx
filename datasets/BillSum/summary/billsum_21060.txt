Exxon Valdez Oil Spill Tax Treatment Act - Allows taxpayers who are plaintiffs in the civil action In re Exxon Valdez, No.
89-095-CV (HRH) (Consolidated) (D Alaska), or their heirs or dependents, to: elect to average, for income tax purposes, income received in settlement of such civil action for the period beginning on January 1, 1994, and ending on December 31 of the year in which any settlement income is received; and make contributions of any amount of such settlement income to certain tax-exempt retirement plans in the year such income is received.
