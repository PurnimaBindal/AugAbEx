Rural Law Enforcement Act of 1997 - Amends the Violent Crime Control and Law Enforcement Act of 1994 to establish: at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock the National Center for Rural Law Enforcement; and an Advisory Board to formulate, adopt, and publish guidelines governing the operation of the Center.
Sets forth provisions regarding the Center's: functions (including the promotion, development, and adoption of a voluntary national system of education and training standards and certification); authority (such as hiring experts and consultants); and methods (including consensus building).
Authorizes appropriations.
