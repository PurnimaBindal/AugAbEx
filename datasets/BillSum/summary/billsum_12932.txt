Protecting Gun Rights and Due Process Act This bill amends the federal criminal code and other laws to raise the threshold that must be met&nbsp;with respect to&nbsp;disqualifying&nbsp;an individual, for reasons relating to mental health,&nbsp;from&nbsp;possessing or receiving&nbsp;a firearm.
Specifically, such an individual must have been either adjudicated mentally incompetent or committed to a psychiatric hospital.
In addition, the bill excludes, for purposes of a determination of whether an individual is disqualified from possessing or receiving a firearm: (1)&nbsp;certain state health reports, and specified determinations made by the Department of Veterans Affairs or the&nbsp;Social Security Administration.
