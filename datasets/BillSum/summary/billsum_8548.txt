Merger Enforcement Improvement Act This bill modifies antitrust enforcement requirements regarding mergers (acquisitions by one corporation of the voting securities or assets of another).
Specifically,&nbsp;the bill: adjusts premerger notification filing fees; requires certain filers to report to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) or&nbsp;to the relevant Assistant Attorney General&nbsp;on information that allows the government&nbsp;to assess the competitive impact of a merger; requires the FTC to study overlapping ownership or control by investors in certain markets; and requires the Government Accountability Office to assess the success of certain merger remedies and the impact of mergers and acquisitions on wages, employment, innovation, and new business formation.
