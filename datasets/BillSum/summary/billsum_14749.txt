Coordinate to Educate Act - Authorizes the Secretary of Education to make grants to local educational agencies to develop and implement coordinated services programs.
Requires special consideration to be given to: geographic distribution of awards, including urban, suburban, and rural districts; districts with concentrated pockets of educationally at-risk students; local educational agencies with high proportions of educationally at-risk students; and areas with a large number of single parent or two-parent, working families.
Authorizes appropriations.
