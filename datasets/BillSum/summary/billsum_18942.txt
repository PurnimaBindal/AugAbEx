Medicare Contractor Reform Amendments of 1996 (sic) - Amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act to make specified changes in the way Medicare administers its contracts for administrative operations.
Provides chiefly among such changes for: augmenting the types of entities eligible to serve as carriers under the program; allowing service providers their periodic choice of fiscal intermediaries; repealing certain contract termination, cost reimbursement, and non-cost-effective fiscal intermediary requirements; and requiring competition in cases of certain new contracts and in cases involving poor contract performance.
Waives competition requirements in specified circumstances.
