Northwest Atlantic Fisheries Convention Act of 1995 - Provides for the implementation of the Convention on Future Multilateral Cooperation in the Northwest Atlantic Fisheries, including regarding: appointment of US representatives and alternate representatives as Commissioners and on the Scientific Council; handling of requests for scientific advice; the authorities of the Secretary of State; and cooperation between various agencies, the States, private institutions, and organizations.
Makes certain actions unlawful, including: violating any regulation issued under this Act or any measure legally binding on the United States under the Convention; resisting, impeding, intimidating, or interfering with certain actions; and transporting, selling, or possessing fish taken in violation of these provisions.
Provides for: civil and criminal penalties, permit sanctions, and forfeiture of vessels, cargo, and fish; enforcement by the Coast Guard; and US district court exclusive jurisdiction.
Directs the Secretaries of State and Commerce to jointly establish a consultative committee on issues related to the Convention.
Authorizes appropriations.
