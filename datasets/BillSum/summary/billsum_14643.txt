New Automobile Voucher Act of 2009 - Establishes in the Department of the Treasury the New Automobile Voucher Program.
Directs the Secretary of the Treasury to: authorize the issuance of a redeemable voucher to a dealer for each person or eligible fleet operator who purchases an eligible new automobile; allow a dealer to participate in the program if it agrees to apply such voucher toward the purchase of an eligible new automobile and complies with all applicable requirements; establish a Web-based system to process vouchers; and make payments to dealers who apply vouchers for such purchases.
Defines "eligible new automobile" as a new automobile whose purchase price is less than $50,000.
Sets forth civil penalties for violations of this Act.
