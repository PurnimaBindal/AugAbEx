Child Abuse Reporting Grant Option (CARGO) Act - Authorizes the Attorney General to make grants to eligible States for: hiring and training law enforcement personnel for child abuse cases; and programs to help abused children cope.Requires an eligible State to have in effect laws: requiring that a "mandatory reporter" (defined to include a health care provider, social worker, child care provider, law enforcement officer, officer of a religious organization, or photographic print processor) report to law enforcement authorities each instance of suspected child abuse, except when the information forming the basis of the suspicion is protected by attorney-client or clergy-penitent privilege; providing appropriate punishments for violations of that requirement; and having a statute of limitations for prosecution of criminal offenses relating to child abuse of not less than five years from the later of the date of the offense or the date on which the alleged victim became 18 years old.Sets forth requirements regarding applications, restrictions on the use of funds, allocation of grant amounts, and expenditure records.
