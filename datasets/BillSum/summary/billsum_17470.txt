Cleaner Trucks for America Act of 2008 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase the credit amount for new qualified alternative fuel motor vehicles with gross vehicle weight ratings of more than 26,000 pounds from $40,000 to $80,000; allow an increased tax credit for alternative fuel vehicle refueling properties that dispense compressed natural gas, liquefied natural gas, or liquefied petroleum gas; and allow such credit amounts against the regular and alternative minimum tax.
