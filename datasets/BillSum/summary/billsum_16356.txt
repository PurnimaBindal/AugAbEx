Southwest Cross-border Violence Recognition Act of 2011 - Directs the Secretary of Homeland Security (DHS): within 180 days, to report on metrics to be used to gauge the incidents or occurrences of cross-border violence and on how the resulting findings shall be quantified for periodic reporting; and every 90 days after submitting such report, to report on incidents or occurrences of cross-border violence, incorporating such metrics and including information and crime statistics from federal, state, local, and tribal sources.
Requires such metrics to include: border sheriff and state law enforcement reports on crime, vandalism, theft, burglary, apprehensions, accidents, and capital crimes; reports from local hospitals in border states regarding the number of individuals treated for wounds obtained during acts of cross-border violence; the impact to property values and businesses along the border as a result of factors such as acts of vandalism, theft, burglary, destruction of property, and intimidation; and accounts of cross-border violence reported by US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), US Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and other federal agencies.
