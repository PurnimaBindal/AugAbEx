National Strategy for Combating Terrorist, Underground, and Other Illicit Financing Act (Sec.
This bill directs the President, acting through the Department of the Treasury, to develop a national strategy for combating the financing of terrorism and related forms of illicit finance.
(Sec.
The national strategy shall focus upon selected aspects, including: threats, goals, objectives, and priorities; coordination with domestic and international governmental entities; the role of the private financial sector in prevention of illicit finance; project and budget priorities; the use and role of Treasury attaches; illicit finance and cyber crime; and technology.
