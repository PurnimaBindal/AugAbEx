Bankruptcy Amendments of 1993 - Amends Federal bankruptcy law to raise to $1 million the ceiling on debts for eligibility to file as a chapter 13 debtor (bankrupt consumer repayment of debt under court supervision).
(Currently the ceiling is $100,000 for unsecured debts, or $350,000 for certain secured debts.)
Modifies the guidelines for: chapter 13 bankruptcies; automatic stays; exemptions; discharges; and preferences.
Directs the Administrative Office of the United States Courts to compile and make available to the public statistical information regarding bankruptcy cases.
