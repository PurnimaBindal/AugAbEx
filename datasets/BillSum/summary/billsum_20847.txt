Amends the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 to direct the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) to: develop an independent safety assessment procedure for nuclear facilities; and create a team to inspect the design, construction, maintenance, and operational safety performance of a facility.
Declares that a final NRC decision on whether to extend an operating license, approve an extended power uprate, or continue to operate a facility shall not be made until: the NRC has completed the independent safety assessment of the facility; and the licensee has fully accepted and implemented each NRC-approved finding and recommendation of the assessment report
