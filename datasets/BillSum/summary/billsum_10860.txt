Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: extend through 2018 the tax credit for producing electricity from certain renewable resources, including wind, biomass, geothermal energy, landfill gas, refined coal, and hydropower; include marine and hydrokinetic renewable energy as a renewable resource for purposes of such tax credit; extend through 2018 the energy tax credit for solar, fuel cell, and microturbine property; repeal the dollar per kilowatt limitation for fuel cell property for purposes of the energy tax credit; extend the energy tax credit to public electric utilities; and expand and extend through 2018 the tax credit for residential energy efficient property.
