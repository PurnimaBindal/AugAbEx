Saver's Bonus Act of 2007 [sic] - Directs the Secretary of the Treasury to develop programs to: match deposits made by low-income individuals into certain savings accounts (saver's bonus); deposit tax refunds electronically into savings accounts; and allow individual taxpayers to purchase US savings bonds on their federal income tax returns.
Requires the Commissioner of the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to notify low-income taxpayers of their eligibility for the saver's bonus and of their options under this Act.
