Free Housing Market Enhancement Act - Prohibits providing Federal funds to the Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae), the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac), or any Federal Home Loan bank (Such entities are referred to as government sponsored enterprises, or GSEs.
)Amends the Federal National Mortgage Association Charter Act ( Fannie Mae) and the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation Act (Freddie Mac) to repeal: the State tax exemption; the requirement that the Treasury approve debt issues; Treasury authority to purchase Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac obligations; depositary authority; and the designation of obligations as lawful investments.Amends the Federal Home Loan Bank Act to repeal: the State tax exemption; Treasury authority to purchase bank obligations; depositary authority; and the designation of obligations as lawful investments.Amends the Federal Reserve Act to prohibit Federal Reserve purchase of GSE debt.Repeals the eligibility of GSE obligations for unlimited investment by national banks, federally chartered thrifts, and credit unions.
