Special Inspector General for Hurricane Katrina Recovery Act - Establishes the Office of the Special Inspector General for Hurricane Katrina Recovery.
Places at the head of such Office a Special Inspector General for Hurricane Katrina Recovery, who shall be appointed by the Secretary of Homeland Security.
Provides for the Special Inspector General to report directly to the Secretaries of Homeland Security and Defense.
Requires the Special Inspector General, among other specified duties, to conduct, supervise, and coordinate audits and investigations of the treatment, handling, and expenditure of amounts made available for Hurricane Katrina recovery by the federal government, and of the programs, operations, and contracts carried out utilizing such funds.
Instructs the Special Inspector General, within ten days of being appointed, to: open a 24-hour fraud, waste, and abuse hotline; deploy auditors and investigators to the affected Gulf of Mexico region of the United States; announce a strategic plan for oversight; go to the media of that region with antifraud messages; liaise with Hurricane Katrina recovery federal agencies to identify vulnerabilities; and coordinate interagency oversight elements through the creation of a task force.
Requires the Special Inspector General to: submit specified quarterly and interim reports summarizing the activities of the Special Inspector General and the Hurricane Katrina recovery activities of the federal government; and publish such reports on the websites of the Secretaries of Homeland Security and Defense.
