Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: allow, on a joint return, a deduction equal to a percentage of the qualified earned income of the lower earning spouse; and revise the rules for the deduction of the health insurance costs of self-employed individuals to allow a deduction for 75 percent (for taxable years beginning in 1999 and 2000) of such costs and a deduction for 100 percent (in following years) of such costs.
