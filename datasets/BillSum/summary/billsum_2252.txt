Compassionate Assistance for Rape Emergencies Act - Prohibits any federal funds from being provided to a hospital unless the hospital meets certain conditions related to a woman who is a victim of sexual assault, including that the hospital: provides the woman with accurate and unbiased information about emergency contraception; offers emergency contraception to the woman; provides the woman such contraception at the hospital on her request; and does not deny any such services because of the inability of the woman or her family to pay.
