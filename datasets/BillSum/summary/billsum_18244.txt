Social Security Benefits Improvement Act of 1993 - Amends title II (Old Age, Survivors and Disability Insurance) (OASDI) of the Social Security Act to: increase the amount of outside income which beneficiaries who have attained retirement age may earn without incurring a reduction in benefits; increase survivors' benefits for those widows and widowers whose spouses died before they reached age 65; repeal provisions which require that a widow or widower must have become disabled within seven years of their spouse's death or within seven years of the end of their eligibility for benefits as the surviving parent of a young child in order to be eligible for disability benefits; and increase the OASDI contribution and benefit base beginning in 1994.
