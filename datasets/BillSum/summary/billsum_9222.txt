Small Businesses Add Value for Employees Act of 2008 or the SAVE Act of 2008 - Amends Internal Revenue Code provisions relating to employer-established simple individual retirement accounts (IRAs) to: repeal certain restrictions on rollovers from simple IRAs: allow employers to elect to terminate qualified salary reduction arrangements at any time during the year; repeal the enhanced 25% penalty on premature withdrawal made from simple IRAs within the first two plan years; allow additional nonelective employer contributions to simple IRAs; establish automatic deferral IRAs; and increase the tax credit for small employer pension plan startup costs.
