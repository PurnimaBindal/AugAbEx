Emergency Deficit Reduction Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: impose an additional 54% tax on individual taxpayers whose modified adjusted gross income exceeds $1 million ($2 million in the case of a joint return); repeal the amortization of intangible and drilling and development costs for oil, gas, and geothermal wells; repeal the percentage depletion allowance for oil and gas wells; and deny a tax deduction for income attributable to the domestic production of oil, natural gas, or primary products thereof.
