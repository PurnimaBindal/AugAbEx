Bullying Redress and Verified Enforcement Act or the BRAVE Act - Amends the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (ESEA) to require the employees of local educational agencies (LEAs) who become aware of bullying to report to an LEA-designated individual, within seven business days: the acts that constituted bullying; the protected characteristic of the victim if the bullying included a reference to or was motivated by such an actual or perceived protected characteristic; and the response of the LEA's employees.
Requires the LEA-designated individual, within 60 days after receiving such a report, to inform all the LEA's employees of the acts described and the response of the LEA's employees.
Requires each LEA to annually review its policies on bullying.
Directs each LEA to publish and make available to all of its students and their parents a quarterly report that: summarizes the bullying reported since the previous quarterly report, summarizes the responses to such bullying by the LEA's employees, excludes any personally identifiable information of any student involved, and informs the public of the right to file a complaint with the Office of Civil Rights for the Department of Education regarding the LEA's failure to comply with this Act's requirements.
Directs the Assistant Secretary who heads the Office of Civil Rights to: establish the complaint procedure and publish it on the Department's website; investigate each complaint to determine if the LEA complied with this Act's requirements; withhold ESEA funds from, issue a complaint against, or enter into a compliance agreement with a noncompliant LEA to bring it into compliance; and make information about each complaint available on the Department's website.
Conditions each LEA's receipt of ESEA funds on the LEA: annually certifying to the Secretary of Education in writing that it is in compliance with this Act's requirements, and submitting its four most recent quarterly reports on bullying.
