Effective Terrorists Prosecution Act of 2006 - Amends federal armed forces provisions enacted by the Military Commissions Act of 2006 to, among other things: exclude from military commission (commission) trials statements obtained by coercion; allow a commission military judge to exclude hearsay evidence determined to be unreliable or lacking in probative value; provide for review of commission decisions by the US Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces rather than the Court of Military Commission Review; revise generally provisions concerning the implementation of treaty obligations with respect to the US prosecution of enemy combatants; restore habeas corpus rights for individuals detained by the United States; and provide for expedited judicial review of provisions of the Military Commissions Act of 2006.
