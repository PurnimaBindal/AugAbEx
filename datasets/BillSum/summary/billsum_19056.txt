Turkish Human Rights Act - Prohibits economic support fund (ESF) assistance to the Government of Turkey unless the Secretary of State determines that there has been significant progress by such Government in: permitting international human rights monitoring organizations to report on the human rights situation in Turkey; ceasing to deny human rights to the Kurdish people; taking action to demilitarize Cyprus and provide support for democracy there; ceasing to blockade US and international assistance to Armenia; and ceasing its restrictions on religious freedom.
