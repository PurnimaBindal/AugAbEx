Native American Education Opportunity Act This bill amends the Education Amendments of 1978 to require the Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) to reimburse states for&nbsp;grants made to education savings accounts for eligible students who: (1)&nbsp;attended or will be eligible to attend a BIA-funded school, live on the reservation of a tribe, will not be attending a BIA-funded school or other public elementary or secondary school for the applicable school year, and meet applicable eligibility requirements.
From education savings accounts, parents may purchase goods and services related to students' educational needs.
