Investment Incentives Act of 2002 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase from $1,500 to $10,000 ($3,000 to $20,000 on a joint return) the limitation on capital losses allowed against ordinary income; make the increase to the $5,000 amount allowed as an annual retirement contribution deduction effective immediately, instead of starting in 2008; exclude from gross income $500 ($1,000 on a joint return) of dividends and interest.
