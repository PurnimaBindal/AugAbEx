Amends the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 regarding the Rural Energy for America Program to: extend the Program through FY2017, make nonprofit organizations eligible for assistance, make assistance available to purchase unique components of renewable energy systems, create a tiered loan and grant application process that reflects project size, prohibit requiring a second meter for on-farm residential portions of rural projects connected to the energy grid, permit the combined amount of a grant and a loan guarantee to cover all eligible activity costs, and limit individual grant amounts for feasibility studies.
