Requires the Center to use such funds to develop an education and training program for rural law enforcement agencies for specified purposes, including: the development and delivery of management education and training for employees of such agencies; and the delivery of assistance (in a consulting capacity) to criminal justice agencies in the development and coordination of programs, training, and research relating to crime in rural areas.
Permits the Center to use such funds to enhance that education and training program through specified means, including: educational opportunities for rural law enforcement agencies; and coordination with institutions of higher education to encourage programs of study at those institutions for employees of such agencies.
