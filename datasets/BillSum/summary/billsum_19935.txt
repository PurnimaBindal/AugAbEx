Central American Security Act - Amends the Nicaraguan Adjustment and Central American Relief Act to: extend permanent resident status adjustment provisions to qualifying Salvadoran, Guatemalan, and Honduran nationals; and revise the application filing deadline.Permits certain pending applications filed by Salvadoran or Guatemalan nationals to be converted to an application for status adjustment under the Act.Amends the Act to: authorize the Attorney General to waive certain grounds of inadmissibility; and provide for issuance of immigrant visas to certain spouses and children.States that applicants for relief: shall not be granted a waiver from any security or criminal background investigation required by the Act; and shall submit fingerprints to the appropriate Federal agencies.Provides for the limited reopening of certain orders of deportation, exclusion, or removal by certain Cuban, or Nicaraguan nationals.
