Adoption Report Card Act of 1997 - Amends Part E (Foster Care and Adoption Assistance) of title IV of the Social Security Act to instruct the Secretary of Health and Human Services to: issue an annual report card containing each State's performance rating (including a performance analysis) with respect to protecting children placed for adoption, in foster care, or with a guardian; and develop, for use in preparing such report card, a set of outcome measures for specified categories related to the family conditions of such children.
