This bill&nbsp;amends title XXI (Children's Health Insurance Program [CHIP]) of the Social Security Act to establish a special rule,&nbsp;with respect to&nbsp;the first quarter of FY2018,&nbsp;for the redistribution of unused CHIP allotments to state child health plans&nbsp;experiencing emergency shortfalls.
Specifically, the Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services&nbsp;(CMS) must&nbsp;redistribute&nbsp;unused allotments to each&nbsp;such state in an amount equal to the state's emergency shortfall before the CMS may redistribute the allotments to any state that is experiencing a nonemergency shortfall.
