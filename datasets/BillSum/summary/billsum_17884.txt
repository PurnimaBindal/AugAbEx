Encouraging Investment in Small Business Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code with respect to qualified small business (QSB) stock (section 1202) to: increase the amount of gain excluded from the sale of such stock to 75 percent; reduce the holding period applicable to such sale to three years; exclude such gain from alternative minimum tax consideration; increase the active business working capital requirement to five years; permit a QSB to make specified purchases of its own stock without losing the gain exclusion if made for a business purpose; exclude biotechnology and aquaculture businesses from QSB status; increase the cap on eligible gain for joint returns to $20,000; reduce the capital gains rate for such gains; and increase the related rollover period to 180 days.
