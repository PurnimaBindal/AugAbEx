Rural Telemedicine Development Act of 1993 - Amends the Public Health Service Act to direct the Secretary of Health and Human Services to award grants: to hospitals, hospital networks, and other health care providers to encourage the initial development of rural telemedicine networks to use telecommunications technology in providing health services to rural areas; and for linking such networks together using advanced telemedicine systems and for linking such networks to existing fiber optic telecommunications systems.
Sets forth provisions regarding: application requirements; preferences in awarding grants; and use of funds for initial development and advanced telemedicine grant programs.
Authorizes appropriations.
