Prohibits HCFA from implementing any new evaluation and management (E&M) guidelines under Medicare unless it has: provided for an assessment of the proposed guidelines by physicians; established a plan that contains specific goals for improving participation of physicians; carried out a minimum of four described pilot projects in at least four different HCFA regions to test such guidelines; and found that specified objectives for E&M guidelines will be met in the implementation of such guidelines.
Requires each pilot project to study the effect of E&M guidelines on physician practices and patient and physician satisfaction.
