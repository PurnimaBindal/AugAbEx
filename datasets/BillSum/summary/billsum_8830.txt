Family Farm Energy Relief Act of 2006 - Repeals provisions of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 relating to: the expensing of equipment used in refining of liquid fuels and accelerated depreciation of natural gas distribution and gathering lines; the pass through of tax deductions for environmental compliance costs; the small refiner exception to limitations on the oil depletion tax deduction; and the two-year amortization of geological and geophysical expenditures.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow certain farmers actively engaged in farming: a tax credit for 10% of farm diesel fuel expenses through 2009; an increased agri-biodiesel producer tax credit; and a small biodiesel producer tax credit.
