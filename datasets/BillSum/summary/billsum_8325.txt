Children's Access to Vision Act of 2005 - Authorizes the Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), to make grants to states for providing: comprehensive eye examinations for children, with priority given to children under nine years old; subsequent vision treatment or services to correct vision problems; and educational materials to parents, teachers, and health care practitioners on recognizing signs of visual impairment in children.
Directs the Secretary to: develop criteria to collect data related to vision assessment and the utilization of follow-up services; and coordinate the grant program with appropriate federal and state child services programs.
Requires states to annually submit to the Secretary a program evaluation.
