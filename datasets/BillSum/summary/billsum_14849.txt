Mortgage Disclosure Enhancement Act of 2007 - Amends the Truth In Lending Act to require additional mortgage loan disclosures by a creditor to a consumer, including: ) the percentage of the loan, as compared to the appraised value of the property; the term of the loan; the monthly income of the borrower, as relied upon in making the loan; the annual percentage rate of interest for loans that are fixed, and for loans that are variable; prepayment fees; and balloon payments.
Amends the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act of 1974 to conform with this Act.
