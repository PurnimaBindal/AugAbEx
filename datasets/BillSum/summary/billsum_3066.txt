Emergency Malpractice Liability Insurance Commission (EMLIC) Act - Establishes the Emergency Malpractice Liability Insurance Commission to examine the causes of soaring medical malpractice premiums and propose a comprehensive strategy to alleviate the impact.
Directs the Commission to investigate and determine whether a causal relationship exists between: skyrocketing malpractice insurance premiums; jury awards; decreased accessibility and affordability of health care; and an increase in the number of physicians moving, quitting, or retiring.
