Physician Self-Referral Amendments of 1999 - Amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act to revise limitations on certain physician referrals to: eliminate restrictions on physician referrals based on compensation arrangements; repeal site of service requirements contained in the exceptions to the ownership referral prohibitions; change the definition of group practice; exclude intraocular lenses, eyeglasses, and contact lenses as designated health services; and permit supervision of clinical laboratory services by pathologists who are independent contractors.
