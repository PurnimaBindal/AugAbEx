Public School Construction Partnership Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to provide for the treatment of qualified public educational facility bonds as exempt facility bonds.
Defines a "qualified public educational facility" as any school facility which is: part of a public elementary school or a public secondary school; and owned by a private, for-profit corporation pursuant to a public-private partnership agreement with a State or local educational agency.
Provides for an exception from the State volume cap.Sets forth provisions concerning: time-related spending requirements for public school construction bonds and doubling the arbitrage rebate exception for governmental bonds used to finance education facilities; and the treatment of public school construction bonds as qualified tax-exempt obligations.
