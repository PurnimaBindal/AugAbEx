Crane Tithe Tax Act of 2001 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: repeal the tax on corporations; repeal the current tax rates for individuals and replace such rates with a ten percent tax on earned income; provide amnesty for any tax liability prior to January 1, 2000; repeal all specific exclusions from gross income, all deductions, and all credits; and repeal subtitle B relating to estate, gift, and generation-skipping taxes.
