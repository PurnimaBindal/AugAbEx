NAFTA-Impacted Communities Relief Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: provide for the designation of communities as impacted by the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA) based upon job loss and unemployment resulting from NAFTA; allow employers in such NAFTA-impacted communities a business tax credit for up to 85 percent of wages paid to employees; increase depreciation expensing levels for business assets located in NAFTA-impacted communities; and direct the Secretary of Labor to provide grants to States containing NAFTA-impacted communities for education and job training assistance.
