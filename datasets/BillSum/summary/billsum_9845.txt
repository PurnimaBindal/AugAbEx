Transparency and Accountability for Business Standards Act This bill requires a&nbsp;federal banking agency to&nbsp;publish for notice and comment: (1)&nbsp;the agency's rationale for,&nbsp;and cost-benefit analysis regarding, the adoption of any prudential regulation that is substantively more stringent than a corresponding international prudential standard; and a proposal for, or the agency's rationale for not proposing, the repeal or amendment of any&nbsp;prudential regulation that is effectively superseded by the implementation of an international prudential standard.
A federal banking agency must issue a report regarding any such final rule issued before the bill's enactment and on or after January 1, 2007.
