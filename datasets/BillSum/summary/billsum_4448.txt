Establishes the Nicodemus National Historic Site in Nicodemus, Kansas.
Authorizes the Secretary of the Interior to: provide technical assistance for the preservation of historic structures, the maintenance of the cultural landscape, and local preservation planning; and acquire certain real property in connection with the Site.
Directs the Secretary to: prepare a general management plan for the Site; and submit a copy of the plan to specified congressional committees.
Authorizes appropriations.
