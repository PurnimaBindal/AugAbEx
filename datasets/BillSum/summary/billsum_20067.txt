Lupus Research Amendments of 1994 - Amends the Public Health Service Act to require the Director of the National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases to conduct lupus research and related activities.
Requires the Director to: coordinate such activities with similar activities conducted by other national research institutes and agencies of the National Institutes of Health; and conduct or support research to expand the understanding of the causes of, and to find a cure for, lupus, including research to determine the reasons underlying the elevated prevalence of the disease among African-American and other women.
