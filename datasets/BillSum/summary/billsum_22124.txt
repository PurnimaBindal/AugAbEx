Transnational Criminal Organization Illicit Spotter Prevention and Elimination Act This bill amends the Immigration and Nationality Act to prohibit: transmitting to another person the location, movement, or activities of law enforcement agents while intending to further a federal crime relating to US immigration; destroying, altering, or damaging any physical or electronic device used by the federal government to control the border or any port of entry; or carrying or using a firearm in an alien smuggling crime.
