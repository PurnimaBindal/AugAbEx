Health Care Paperwork Reduction and Fraud Prevention Act of 2001 - Establishes the Commission on Billing Codes and Forms Simplification which shall make recommendations regarding: standardized forms; and reduction in billing codes; and regulatory and appeals process reform.Directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to establish a process under which a physician may request, in writing from a carrier, assistance in addressing questionable codes and procedures under the medicare program.Sets forth provisions concerning: policy development regarding evaluation and management guidelines under Medicare; and Medicare overpayments.
