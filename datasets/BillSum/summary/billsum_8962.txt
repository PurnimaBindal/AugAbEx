Welfare of Our Friends Act of 2017 or the WOOF!
Act This bill amends the Animal Welfare Act to prohibit the Department of Agriculture (USDA) from licensing a dealer of dogs whose previous licenses have been suspended or revoked in the last 10 years if the license is for operating a facility that the dealer previously used.
Further, USDA may not issue or renew a license to: the dog dealer's immediate family members, a person who resides at the same address as the dog dealer, or certain legal entities if such dealer holds an ownership interest in the entity.
