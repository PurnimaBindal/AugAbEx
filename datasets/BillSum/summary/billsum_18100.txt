Authorizes: the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President Pro Tempore of the Senate to arrange for the presentation to Dr. Dorothy Height (President of the National Council of Negro Women) of a congressional gold medal in recognition of her many contributions to the Nation; and the Secretary of the Treasury to strike such medal and to strike and sell bronze duplicates.
Authorizes charging an amount to pay the cost of the gold medal against the United States Mint Public Enterprise Fund.
