Amends the Food Security Act of 1985 to increase marketing loan gain and loan deficiency payment caps for crop years 1999 through 2002.
(Sec.
Amends the Agricultural Market Transition Act to authorize six-month marketing assistance loan extensions.
(Sec.
Amends the Food Security Act of 1985 to eliminate 1999 through 2002 conservation reserve acreage caps.
(Sec.
Revises the wetlands reserve program to: replace the total program acreage cap with an annual cap for 2000 through 2005; permit inclusion of land that has not historically been wetlands, land that is suitable for wetland establishment, or land that would contribute to certain Canadian-US habitat objectives; and authorize cooperative agreements for goods and services.
(Sec.
Declares that Congress finds that any Federal law or regulation prohibiting or restricting agricultural exports should be maintained only if essential to US security.
Directs the President to conduct a related annual assessment of such laws and regulations.
