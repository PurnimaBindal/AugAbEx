SSI Reform Act of 1993 - Amends title XVI (Supplemental Security Income) (SSI) of the Social Security Act to: increase the limit on the amount of income and resources needed to qualify for SSI; increase the amount of SSI benefits payable; eliminate certain resource exclusions and increase certain earned income exclusions; disregard in-kind support and maintenance in determining income; and phase-in an expansion of eligibility based on age.
