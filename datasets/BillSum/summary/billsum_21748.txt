Gifted and Talented Students Education Act of 2001 - Authorizes the Secretary of Education to make grants to State educational agencies to assist local educational agencies to develop or expand gifted and talented education programs through one or more of the following activities: professional development programs; technical assistance; innovative approaches and curricula; emerging technologies, including distance learning; and direct educational services and materials, which may include compacted, modified, or adapted curricula, acceleration, independent study, and dual enrollment.
