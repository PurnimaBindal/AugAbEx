Restoring Democracy to the United States Reports Congress Act of 2004 - Amends the Rules of the House of Representatives to address: the time limit on roll call votes; actual voting required in conference committee meetings; waiver of the germaneness requirement for conference reports; removal of Members from committee meetings; a limit on redistricting after an apportionment of Representatives; availability of legislation on the Internet; bribery on the House floor; hacking into other Members' computer files; capping committee mailing expenses; and a requirement that at least one-third of committee budget be provided to the minority ranking member.
