Nonitemizer Real Property Tax Deduction Act of 2006 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase the standard tax deduction for taxpayers who do not itemize tax deductions by $500 ($1,000 for joint returns) of the real property taxes paid or accrued by such taxpayers in a taxable year; define economic substance for purposes of evaluating tax shelter transactions; and impose a penalty for understatements of tax liability resulting from transactions lacking economic substance.
