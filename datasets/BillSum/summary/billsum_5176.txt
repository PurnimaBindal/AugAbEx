Stop Child Pornographers and Predators Act - Amends the federal criminal code to: prohibit the production, distribution, receipt, or possession of visual depictions of prepubescent children (age 12 or younger) engaging in sexually explicit conduct; prohibit the use of pornographic materials to facilitate sex offenses against minors; ban the distribution in the United States of child pornography produced outside the United States; and increase criminal penalties for sex offenders who have a prior sex conviction involving a minor.
