Cache La Poudre River Corridor Technical Amendments Act of 2001 - Amends the Cache La Poudre River Corridor Act with respect to the boundaries of the Cache La Poudre River Corridor in Colorado.Modifies requirements for the membership and terms of service of the Cache La Poudre Corridor Commission.
Directs the Commission to administer the Corridor.
(Currently, the Commission has only planning authority.
)Extends the authorization of appropriations.
