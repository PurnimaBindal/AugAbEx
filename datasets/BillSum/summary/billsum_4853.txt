Incentives to Educate American Children Act of 2011 or the I Teach Act of 2011- Amends the Internal Revenue Code to permit a refundable tax credit of $1,000 for: teachers in public elementary or secondary schools or public kindergartens in rural areas or areas with high poverty; and teachers certified by the National Board for Professional Teaching Standards.
Increases such credit to $2,000 for a teacher meeting both requirements.
