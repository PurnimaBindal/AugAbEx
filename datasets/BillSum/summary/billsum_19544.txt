Office of Government Ethics Authorization Act of 1996 - Amends the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 to: authorize the Office of Government Ethics (OGE) Director to accept gifts for OGE use; and extend the authorization of appropriations for the OGE.
Amends Federal law to repeal the requirement that Federal buildings display the Code of Ethics for Government Service.
Modifies postemployment restrictions on certain senior (including very senior) personnel.
Modifies the level of pay applicable with respect to certain senior personnel of the executive branch and independent agencies.
