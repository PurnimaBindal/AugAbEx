Skills Standards Certification Evaluation Act of 2008 - Directs the Secretary of Labor and the Secretary of Commerce to: evaluate skills certification programs for employees; rank such programs or determine best practices models for them; and make recommendations on the merits of a unified national approach to such programs, and ways to streamline duplicative and similar programs into a single, widely recognized certification.
Requires the Secretaries to post evaluation results on the Internet and make them available to Congress and to interested parties.
