Family Caregiver Relief Act of 2003 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise and rename the Child Tax Credit as the Family Care Credit.
Includes within such credit (in addition to the child care credit) a $3,000 credit for each qualifying individual for whom an eligible taxpayer is a long-term care provider.
Defines "eligible expenses" as: certain medical care; lodging away from home; adult day care; custodial care; respite care; and other specialized children's services, including day care for children with special needs.
