Grassroots Campaigning and Election Reform Act of 1993 - Amends the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to: prohibit congressional candidates from raising campaign funds from outside their State; prohibit the establishment of political committees by banks, labor organizations, or corporations; reduce the amount a person may contribute to a political committee; limit candidate expenditures of personal funds, family funds, or personal loans in connection with an election campaign; and apply restrictions and requirements to soft money.
