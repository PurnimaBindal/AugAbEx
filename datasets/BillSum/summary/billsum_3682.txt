Financial Literacy for Homeowners Act - Authorizes the Secretary of the Treasury to make grants to state and local governments and nonprofit organizations to implement anti-predatory lending activities, including: consumer education programs; certified home ownership counseling programs; and referral services for homeowners and prospective homeowners.
Directs the Secretary provide for establishment, operation, and publication of a nationwide toll-free telephone number to receive consumer complaints regarding predatory and unscrupulous lending practices relating to home loans.
Establishes the Predatory Lending Advisory Council to: advise the Secretary; and study and report to the Secretary and Congress on the root causes of default and foreclosure of home loans.
