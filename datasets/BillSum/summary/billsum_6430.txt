Modifies information exchange guidelines pertaining to the technology transfer program.
Authorizes appropriations.
Amends the Hydrogen Future Act of 1996 to instruct the Secretary of Energy to: solicit proposals to prove the feasibility of integrating fuel cells into Federal and State facilities for stationary and transportation applications; establish an interagency task force to develop an implementation plan regarding fuel cell integration with hydrogen production systems; enter into cooperative and cost-sharing agreements with Federal and State agencies for demonstration programs to replace commercially available fossil fuel systems with fuel cell systems; and disseminate the ensuing technical information to such agencies.
Authorizes appropriations.
