Cyber Safety for Kids Act of 2007 - Prohibits an operator of a commercial website from knowingly placing material that is harmful to minors on the website unless: any page that is initially viewable does not include any such material; access to the material is restricted to a specific set of individuals through an age verification requirement; and the website's source code contains the content description tag assigned to the website by the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA).
Requires he NTIA to develop a common content description tag that: will warn and inform consumers regarding the presence of material that is harmful to minors; will allow consumers to block or filter website access; and is technologically capable of being embedded in the website's source code.
Requires a website operator, when registering or re-registering a website, to provide to the Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers certain information, including the content description tag.
