United States Specialty Crop Export Opportunities Act of 2007 - Amends the Agricultural Risk Protection Act of 2000 to authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to regulate plants, plant products, biological control organisms, and noxious weeds for export purposes.
Directs the Secretary to: coordinate fruit and vegetable market analyses with the private sector and the Administrator of the Foreign Agricultural Service; and make publicly available on an Internet website the status of all export petitions and an explanation of the sanitary or phytosanitary issues associated with each pending export petition, and information on the import requirements of foreign countries for fruits and vegetables.
Amends the Homeland Security Act to repeal the transfer of agricultural import and entry inspection functions from the Department of Agriculture to the Department of Homeland Security (DHS).
Directs the Secretary to establish: within the Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service the international agricultural inspection program to carry out import and entry agricultural inspections; a federal-state agricultural inspection communications system; the International Trade Inspection Advisory Committee; and a special export credit guarantee program.
Amends the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 to extend: the promotion of agricultural exports to emerging markets program; and the E. (Kika) de la Garza Agricultural Fellowship program.
