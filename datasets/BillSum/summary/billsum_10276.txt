Dwight D Eisenhower Mathematics and Science Education Amendments of 1993 - Amends the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (of which title II part A is the Dwight D Eisenhower Mathematics and Science Education Act) to revise provisions for mathematics and science education grants and programs with respect to: in-State apportionment; elementary and secondary education programs; higher education programs; technical assistance and evaluation; administrative costs; and State and local applications.
