Manufacturing Advisory Council Establishment Act of 2004 - Establishes the President's Council of Advisors on Manufacturing to: advise the President on policy matters affecting the domestic manufacturing sector; develop policies that will reduce manufacturing production costs, promote the international competitiveness of United States Reports-manufactured products, encourage innovation, investment and productivity in the manufacturing sector, and ensure an adequate supply of skilled manufacturing workers; and assist the Secretary of Commerce in securing private sector involvement for Department of Commerce activities.
States that provisions of the Federal Advisory Committee Act relating to the termination of advisory committees shall not apply to the Council.
