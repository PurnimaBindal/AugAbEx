Rural Telecommunications Improvement Act of 1999 - Amends the Communications Act of 1934 to establish within the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) the Office of Rural Advocacy, to be headed by the Rural Advocate of the FCC, to: promote access to advanced telecommunications service for rural populations; and undertake related functions and activities.
Requires: annual reports (and other reports as needed) from the Rural Advocate to Congress, the President, and the FCC; and an initial report from the Advocate to Congress on actions taken to carry out this Act.
