Elementary Mathematics and Science Equipment Act - Authorizes the Secretary of Education to allot funds to State educational agencies to award grants to local educational agencies to provide hands-on instruction equipment and materials to elementary schools to improve mathematics and science education.
Authorizes appropriations.
Sets forth requirements for: State allotments; State and local applications; grant award priorities; participation of private schools; State and Federal responsibilities; and limitation on use of grant funds only for science equipment or materials and mathematical manipulative materials necessary for hands-on instruction.
