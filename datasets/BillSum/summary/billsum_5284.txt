Stop White-collar Assistance to Terrorists Act - Amends the federal criminal code to increase the maximum prison terms for: certain fraud, money laundering, and bribery offenses if committed to facilitate an act of domestic or international terrorism; and providing material support or resources to terrorists.
Directs the US Sentencing Commission to review and, if appropriate, amend sentencing guidelines and policy statements for offenses committed to facilitate terrorism to reflect congressional intent that the penalties for such offenses be increased.
