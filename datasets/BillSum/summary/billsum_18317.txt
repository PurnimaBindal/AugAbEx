Women, Children, and Infant Tsunami Victim Relief Act of 2005 - Directs the Secretary of State to make funding available to the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) for tsunami victims in Indonesia, the Maldives, and Sri Lanka.
States that such assistance shall be used to: ensure safe childbirth and emergency obstetric care and to prevent HIV/AIDS transmission; reestablish maternal health services; prevent and treat cases of violence against women and youth; offer psychological support and counseling to women and youth; and promote access of unaccompanied women and other vulnerable people to vital services.
