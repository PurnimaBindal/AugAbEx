Jobs for Heroes Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to the work opportunity tax credit, to: (1)&nbsp;revise the definition of "qualified veteran" to&nbsp;expand the eligibility of veterans for such credit,&nbsp; allow such credit for the hiring of a qualified member of the Ready Reserve or National Guard, and make such credit permanent.
Revises the tax credit for differential wage&nbsp;payments made by employers on behalf of Members of the Uniformed Services to: (1)&nbsp;extend eligibility for such credit to an employer without regard to the size of such employer's workforce, and make such credit permanent.&nbsp;
