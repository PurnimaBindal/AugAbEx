Amends the National Voter Registration Act of 1993 to: repeal the requirement for States to provide for voter registration in Federal elections by mail; require applicants registering to vote to provide certain additional information; permit a State, at its option, to remove certain registrants from the official list of eligible voters in Federal elections; permit States to require individuals (other than illiterate or disabled individuals) voting in a Federal election to produce a valid photographic identification and provide their signature before receiving a ballot; and repeal the requirement that States permit certain registrants to vote at the polling place for the former address.
