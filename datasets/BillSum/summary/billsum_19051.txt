Prohibits the FCC, without specific authorization by Congress, from: eliminating or reducing such minimum distance separations for third-adjacent channels; or extending the eligibility for low-power FM stations beyond those organizations and entities proposed in MM Docket No.
99-25.
Invalidates any previously issued low-power FM station license that does not comply with such rule modifications.
Directs the FCC to conduct an experimental program to test whether low- power FM stations will result in harmful interference to existing FM radio stations if such stations are not subject to the minimum distance separation requirements.
Requires the FCC to: publish test results and allow an opportunity for public comment; and report test results and FCC recommendations on reducing or eliminating minimum distance standards to specified congressional committees.
