Small Business Tax Relief Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: make permanent the work opportunity tax credit, the new markets tax credit, and the tax credit for increasing research activities; make permanent accelerated depreciation of qualified leasehold improvement and restaurant property and expensing of environmental remediation expenditures; increase the partial tax exclusion of gain from sales of certain small business stock for noncorporate business taxpayers and businesses in an empowerment zone; permit certain small businesses to elect taxable years ending between April and November; increase the allowable number of S corporation shareholders from 100 to 150; and exempt certain small businesses from the 3% withholding requirement applicable to payments made to such businesses by government entities.
