Next Generation Hispanic-Serving Institutions Act - Amends the Higher Education Act of 1965 to revise requirements for Hispanic-serving institutions (HSIs) under title V (Developing Institutions).
Establishes a program of competitive grants to eligible HSIs that offer postbaccalaureate certifications or degrees (part B grants).
Limits a part B grant award's duration to not more than five years.
Prohibits the Secretary of Education from awarding more than one part B grant to an HSI in any one fiscal year.
Authorizes appropriations for: the current part A program of grants to HSIs that offer baccalaureate degrees or are junior or community colleges; and the new part B program of grants to HSIs that offer postbaccalaureate certifications or degrees.
