Requires grant funds to be used to obtain technical assistance in: drafting such legislation; research and development for land use planning programs and requirements relating to the development of State guide plans; conducting workshops, educating and consulting policy makers, and involving citizens in the planning process; and integrating State and regional concerns and land use plans with such Federal plans.
Requires each Federal land management agency to designate, provide information to, and participate in community events as requested by, a coordinator to work with State planning directors on funded projects.
