Small Business Programs Act of 2007 - Amends the Small Business Act (the Act) to reauthorize for FY2008-FY2009 certain small business assistance programs of the Small Business Administration (SBA) authorized under the Act and the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, including: a disaster mitigation pilot program; general and start-up small business loans; certified development company (CDC) financing; disaster loans; the Service Corps of Retired Executives (SCORE); and grants to microloan program intermediaries and technical assistance providers.
Reauthorizes for such fiscal years: the businessLINC grants program; the small business development center (SBDC) program; the women's business center program; the HUBZone (heavily underutilized business zone) program; the Office of Veterans Business Development; the Advisory Committee on Veterans Business Affairs; and the National Women's Business Council.
