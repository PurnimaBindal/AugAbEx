Amends the Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 2000 to revise the administrative authorities of the Dwight D Eisenhower Memorial Commission.
Authorizes the Commission to: enter into contracts for specialized or professional services without regard to certain civil service requirements; accept gifts of real or personal property; appoint an architect; hire and fire staff without regard to competitive service requirements and obtain temporary, intermittent, and part-time services; enter into cooperative agreements with other government and private entities; and accept volunteer services.
Requires the Commission to: appoint an Executive Director; and obtain adminstrative and support services from the General Services Administration.
Authorizes appropriations.
