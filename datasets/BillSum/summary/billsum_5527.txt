James Campbell National Wildlife Refuge Expansion Act of 2005 - Expands the boundary of the James Campbell National Wildlife Refuge (Refuge) in Honolulu, Hawaii, to include approximately 1,100 acres of land.
Authorizes the Secretary of the Interior to: acquire such land; and make minor modifications to the boundary of the Refuge to achieve US Fish and Wildlife Service goals or to acquire property within the Refuge.
Authorizes appropriations.
