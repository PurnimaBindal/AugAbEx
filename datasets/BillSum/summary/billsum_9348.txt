Global Service Fellowship Program Act of 2009 - Directs the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) to: establish and administer a Global Service Fellowship Program to fund fellowships to promote international volunteering opportunities as a means of building bridges across cultures, addressing critical human needs, and promoting mutual understanding; and establish the Office of Volunteers for Prosperity to implement the Global Service Fellowship Program.
Sets forth Program provisions.
Authorizes appropriations.
