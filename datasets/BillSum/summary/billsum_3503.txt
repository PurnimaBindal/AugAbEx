Emergency Medical Services Support Act - Establishes a Federal Interagency Committee on Emergency Medical Services to provide intergovernmental coordination of emergency medical services.
Requires the Committee to: identify community-based emergency medical services' needs; make grant program and streamlining recommendations; and set priorities.Establishes a Federal Interagency Committee on Emergency Medical Services Advisory Council.
Requires the Committee and the Council to each report annually to Congress on the state of community-abased emergency medical services, including recommendations.
