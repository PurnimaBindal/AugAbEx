Revises Federal veterans' programs to: include care for newborn children among medical services provided under the Department of Veterans Affairs health care program for an enrolled woman veteran; require outpatient dental care for all former prisoners of war (currently, only those interned for at least 90 days); set the pay for the Director of Nursing Service at the maximum rate payable to a Senior Executive Service member; prohibit a veteran's honorable service certificate from being awarded on behalf of a veteran convicted of a Federal or State crime; repeal a limitation on the number of non-career members serving in the Department; repeal a requirement for at least five years of continuous Federal service prior to appointment as a Department Deputy Assistant Secretary; allow the Department's operation and maintenance revolving supply fund to be used for medical supplies, equipment, and services for the Department of Defense; and include Spanish or Latino individuals under the definition of a "minority group member" for purposes of the Department's Advisory Committee on Minority Veterans.
