Amends the Clean Air Act to direct the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to establish a competitive grant demonstration program to carry out projects to demonstrate the capture and storage or use of carbon dioxide emitted from the Capitol power plant in Washington, DC, as a result of burning coal.
Requires the Administrator, in providing grants, to take into consideration: the practicability of conversion by the proposed project of carbon dioxide into useful products; the carbon dioxide energy efficiency of the proposed project; and whether the proposed project is able to reduce more than one regulated air pollutant.
Requires entities that receive grants to use technology designed to reduce or eliminate emission of carbon dioxide that has been used: by not less than three other facilities (including a coal-fired power plant); and on a scale of not less than five times the size of the proposed project of the entity at the Capitol power plant.
Requires such entities to carry out a project in consultation and concurrence with the Architect of the Capitol.
Authorizes the Administrator to provide to an entity that receives such a grant an incentive award for sustained operation.
Terminates the program after two years after this Act's enactment.
Authorizes appropriations.
