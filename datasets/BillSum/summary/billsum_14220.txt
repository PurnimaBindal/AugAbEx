Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to: make a supplemental direct commodity payment to eligible producers that previously received such a payment; make grants to states to assist eligible specialty crop producers for losses associated with drought or excessive rainfall during 2009; provide supplemental assistance to eligible producers and first-handlers of the 2009 crop of cottonseed in disaster counties; continue to carry out the 2002 livestock compensation program and provide assistance to any applicant that conducts a livestock operation located in a disaster county or produces fish classified within the family Ictaluridae (catfish); and make payments to eligible producers for livestock losses occurring in a disaster county.
