Conservation Incentives Program Act of 1995 - Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to carry out a conservation incentives program to provide technical assistance, cost-sharing payments, and incentive payments to participating agricultural operators.
Establishes a special rule and funding obligations for livestock operations.
Establishes program and land priorities.
Sets forth: duties of the operators and of the Secretary; and eligible land categories.
Funds (with spending caps) such program through the Commodity Credit Corporation.
