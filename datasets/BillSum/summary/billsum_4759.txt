Head Start Accountability Act of 2005 - Amends the Head Start Act to require additional accountability measures for Head Start agencies, including: agency designation period limits; redesignation priorities; local oversight boards; administrative and financial management standards; delegate agency evaluations and corrective actions; shorter deadlines for agency corrective actions; revised notice and hearing procedures for assistance suspensions, terminations, or reductions; and annual audits.
Prohibits recipients from using Head Start assistance to appeal any decision of the Secretary of Health and Human Services under the Act.
