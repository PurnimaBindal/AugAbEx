Directs the Attorney General to: conduct an evaluation of potential problem officer early warning programs utilized by local governments; develop a model early warning system that is effective, capable of adjusting to changing circumstances, and affordable to local governments of jurisdictions with populations of 50,000 or more; and prepare and disseminate findings and recommendations to the law enforcement community for the establishment of such programs.
Sets forth provisions regarding: reporting requirements; and payment of expenses incurred in conducting the evaluation and developing a model system.
Expresses thhe sense of the Congress that: the Attorney General should make seed money grants of up to $25,000 to local governments for assisting in establishing such programs; and local governments should be eligible if they adopt and enforce policies prohibiting unreasonable or unnecessary force by law enforcement officers and procedures for receiving and investigating citizen complaints.
