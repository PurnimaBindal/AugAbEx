Coverdell Education Savings Accounts Act of 2001 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to education individual retirement accounts (IRAs), to: permit distributions for qualified elementary and secondary education expenses; increase annual contribution limits from $500 to $2,000, with annual inflation adjustments as of tax year 2003; waive the beneficiary age limitation for contributions on behalf of special needs beneficiaries; permit corporations to contribute to education IRAs; permit annual contributions to be made until the filing date (not including extensions) for a tax year; extend the time for return of excess contributions; and provide for coordination with Hope and lifetime learning credit and qualified tuition program provisions.Renames such educational IRAs as Coverdell education savings accounts.Excludes from gross income: employer contributions to such accounts on behalf of an employee or employee family member; and account distributions.
