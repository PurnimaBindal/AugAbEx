Kids Invest and Develop Savings Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase the allowable amount of the tax credit for retirement savings; expand eligibility for such credit to taxpayers with adjusted gross incomes up to $95,000 ($150,000 for joint returns); establish tax-exempt Roth individual retirement accounts (IRAs) for individuals under the age of 25; and allow a tax credit for contributions to such IRAs.
