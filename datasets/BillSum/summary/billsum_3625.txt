San Francisco Bay Estuary Ecosystem Restoration Act of 2002 - Authorizes the Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, to participate in critical restoration projects in the area of the San Francisco Bay estuary, California, and adjacent waters, including all watersheds that drain directly into San Francisco Bay.
Outlines project selection and cost-sharing requirements.Earmarks specified project funds for critical restoration projects: in the San Pablo Bay watershed; and on the Grizzly Island Wildlife Area in the Suisun Marsh.
