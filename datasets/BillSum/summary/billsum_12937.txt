Ocean and Coastal Management Improvement Act of 1993 - Transfers to the Department of the Interior the following offices and assets of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration: the National Ocean Service; the National Marine Fisheries Service; the Office of Oceanic and Atmospheric Research; the fleet of research and survey vessels; and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Corps.
Transfers to the Department of Environmental Protection (sic) components of the National Ocean Service that carry out coastal management and assessment programs.
