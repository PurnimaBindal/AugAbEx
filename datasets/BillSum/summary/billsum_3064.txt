Frederick Douglass Bicentennial Commission Act This bill establishes the Frederick Douglass Bicentennial Commission to:&nbsp; plan, develop, and carry out programs and activities to honor Frederick Douglass for the bicentennial anniversary of his birth; and recommend the federal government entities appropriate to carry out such programs and activities.
Not later than August 1, 2018, the commission must recommend appropriate activities to Congress.
A final report detailing commission activities and expenditures must be submitted not later than June 1, 2019.
