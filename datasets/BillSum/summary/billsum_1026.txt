Health Workforce Advisory Commission Act of 2005 - Directs the Comptroller General to establish the Health Workforce Advisory Commission to analyze health workforce policy issues, including by: reviewing Federal health workforce policies; analyzing and making recommendations to improve the methods used to measure and monitor the health workforce; reviewing the impact of such policies on the ability of the health care system to provide optimal medical and health care services; analyzing and making recommendations pertaining to Federal incentives and programs that promote the education of health professionals; analyzing the role and global implications of internationally trained health professionals and personnel in the US workforce; and making recommendations to Congress concerning health workforce policy issues.
