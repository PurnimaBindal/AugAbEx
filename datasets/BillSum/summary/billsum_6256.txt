Veteran Small Business Tax Credit Act of 2017 This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a new business-related tax credit for the start-up expenses of a veteran-owned small business.
The allowable amount of such credit is 15% of start-up expenditures that do not exceed $80,000.
The credit is allowed to any individual (or the surviving spouse of such individual) who: has served on active duty in the Armed Forces, and has not been discharged or released from the Armed Forces under dishonorable conditions.
