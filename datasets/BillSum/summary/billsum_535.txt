Commission on the Accountability and Review of Federal Agencies Act - Establishes the Commission on the Accountability and Review of Federal Agencies to: evaluate executive agencies and their programs; and submit to Congress a plan recommending agencies and programs that should be realigned or eliminated and proposing implementing legislation.
Requires the Commission to recommend: realignment where a function performed by two or more agencies or programs can be consolidated; realignment or elimination of any agency or program that has wasted federal funds; and elimination of any agency or program that has completed its purpose, become irrelevant, or failed to meet objectives.
