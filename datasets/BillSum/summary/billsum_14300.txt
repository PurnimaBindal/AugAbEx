Promoting Integrity in Medicare Act of 2016 or PIMA of 2016 This bill amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act to: (1)&nbsp;expand&nbsp;Medicare's prohibition on physician self-referrals to include, with specified exceptions,&nbsp;certain advanced imaging, anatomic pathology, radiation therapy, and physical therapy services; and establish increased civil monetary penalties for violations of the self-referral prohibition with respect to those services.
The Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services shall conduct a&nbsp;compliance review&nbsp;with respect to such referrals.
