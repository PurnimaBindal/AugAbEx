Directs the Secretary of Commerce to assess the US economy to determine what goods the United States currently produces, where such goods are produced, which US manufacturing sector is the most competitive in the global economy, and what policies are necessary to maintain or increase the competitiveness of US manufacturing in the global economy.
Requires the Secretary to: develop and submit to Congress a comprehensive national manufacturing strategy; establish and submit to Congress targets for the growth of US manufacturing for each of FY2012-FY2016; survey all federal agencies that provide support to US manufacturers, and report survey results to Congress; report to Congress on progress and trends in US manufacturing since a specified 2004 report by the Secretary concerning manufacturing in America; and biennially survey US entities that maintain manufacturing facilities outside the United States, maintain a database on information collected through each survey, and submit each survey's results to Congress.
