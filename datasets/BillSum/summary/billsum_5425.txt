Fair and Impartial Reform Tax Act of 2011 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: revise income tax rates for individuals and increase such rates for taxpayers with adjusted gross incomes in excess of $500,000, impose limits on and phaseouts of personal exemptions and itemized deductions for high income taxpayers, increase the tax rate on net capital gains and dividend income, and increase the alternative minimum tax (AMT) on the net capital gains of noncorporate taxpayers for taxable years beginning after 2011.
