Northern Forest Stewardship Act - Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to: provide technical assistance to Maine, New Hampshire, New York, and Vermont for forest products marketing cooperatives, for implementing principles of sustainability, and for land conservation; prepare a related study of the Northern Forest region; and cooperate in Northern Forest research.
Provides for interstate coordination strategy.
Directs the Secretary of Labor to provide technical assistance to forest-based industry for labor safety and training activities.
Authorizes appropriations for: land conservation; and other activities under this Act.
Expresses the sense of the Congress: that States should provide landowners with liability exemptions for permitting free public use of their land; and in favor of a funding mechanism to support conservation of nongame fish and wildlife on public and private land.
