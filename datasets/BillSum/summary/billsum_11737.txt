Website for American Taxpayers to Check and Help Deter Out-of-control Government Spending Act or WATCHDOGS Act - Defines: a "contractor entity" as any entity that receives federal funds as a general contractor or subcontractor at any tier in connection with a federal contract; and "covered entity" as any entity that receives federal funds through a grant or loan, with exceptions.
Requires each contractor entity and covered entity to: apply to the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for a federal funds application number; and annually provide OMB with specified information.
Directs OMB to: issue a federal funds application number to each covered entity or contractor entity that applies for such number; develop and establish a publicly accessible searchable database website of information on each covered entity and contractor entity; promulgate regulations to designate, as specified, loan programs not covered by this Act; and provide for information disclosure exemptions in the interest of national defense or national security.
Requires each federal agency to: use the federal funds application number with respect to any document relating to a covered entity or contractor entity receiving federal funds; and make such information relating to covered entities or contractor entities available to OMB as OMB may require.
