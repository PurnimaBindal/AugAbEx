Amends the Internal Revenue Code to impose an excise tax on self-dealing asset transactions between a disqualified person and a medical service organization.
Describes such a transaction as any direct or indirect sale or exchange, or leasing, of any medical asset to: any organization manager; any person who performs substantial professional medical services for the organization pursuant to an employment or other contractual arrangement; any family member of such persons; or any 35-percent controlled entity of such persons.
