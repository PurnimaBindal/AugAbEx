Domestic Violence Connections Campaign Act of 2004 - Directs the Attorney General to award: a grant to the National Domestic Violence Hotline for technology and telecommunication training and assistance for those affiliated with the Hotline in using improved equipment made available through the Connections Campaign; a grant to a university or other research institution to study the Hotline; and a grant to an experienced organization to conduct a campaign to raise public awareness of domestic violence issues and the Hotline.
