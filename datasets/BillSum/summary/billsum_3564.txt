Family Farm Estate Tax Relief Act of 2010 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: exclude from the value of a decedent's gross estate farmland used by an heir for farming purposes; impose a recapture tax on an heir who disposes of such farmland after the decedent's death or who ceases to use such farmland for farming purposes; and increase the limitation on the estate tax exclusion for land subject to a qualified conservation easement to $5 million and the percentage of the value of such land that is excludable.
