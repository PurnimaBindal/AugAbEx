National Women's History Museum Act of 2011 - Directs the Administrator of General Services (GSA) to convey, by quitclaim deed, to the National Women's History Museum, Inc (the Museum) specified property in the District of Columbia, on terms which the Administrator deems appropriate.
Requires the purchase price for the property to be: its market value based on its highest and best use, as determined by an independent appraisal; and paid into the Federal Buildings Fund.
Requires the property to be dedicated for use as a site for a national women's history museum for a 99-year period.
Provides for the reversion of the property to the United States without any obligation for repayment of any amount of the purchase price if: it is not used as a site for a national women's history museum during the 99-year period, and the Museum has not commenced construction of a museum facility on such property in a 5-year period other than for reasons beyond the Museum's control.
