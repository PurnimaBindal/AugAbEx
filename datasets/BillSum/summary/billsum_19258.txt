Agricultural Market Promotion Program Amendments Act of 1993 - Amends the Agricultural Trade Act of 1978 with regard to the agricultural market promotion program to: give priority to, and obligate specified funds for, small and medium beginning exporters; permit assistance for branded promotion only to supplement an entity's own promotional activities; prohibit assistance for tobacco promotion; and incrementally reduce and eliminate over a five-year period assistance for branded and generic promotion in a foreign country.
