Alcohol Ingredient Labeling Act of 1996 - Amends the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to deem a malt beverage, wine, or distilled spirit mislabeled unless it bears a label disclosing: the alcoholic content; the number of drinks (defining "drink" as .6 ounces of alcohol); its ingredients and calories; the common name of each ingredient, including additives; and a toll-free number for help with a drinking problem.
Authorizes appropriations for the toll-free number.
