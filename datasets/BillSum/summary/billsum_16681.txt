Economic Conversion Clearinghouse Act - Establishes within the Department of Commerce the Office of Economic Conversion Information to: serve as a central information clearinghouse on matters relating to economic adjustment and defense conversion programs and activities of the Federal and State governments; and aid persons in applying for economic adjustment and defense conversion assistance under Federal, State, and local laws.
Outlines further Office functions, including: the development of information data bases for use in identifying and applying for such assistance; the establishment of mechanisms to assure easy access to such information data bases, as well as their user-friendliness (including establishing a toll-free number); and the conduct of a regular review of the various agencies and programs in the Federal system involving economic adjustment and defense conversion.
Establishes the Interagency Economic Conversion Information Coordinating Committee to: advise and make appropriate recommendations to the Office; coordinate and facilitate the information gathering and monitoring activities of the Office among Federal departments and agencies; aid the Office in preparing and presenting information in an accessible, user-friendly manner; and assist the Office in making technical assistance personnel available as needed.
Authorizes appropriations.
