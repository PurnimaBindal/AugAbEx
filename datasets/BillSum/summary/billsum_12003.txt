Medicare Hospice Benefit Amendments of 1996 - Amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act with respect to hospice care to: restructure the hospice care benefit period; cover ambulance services, diagnostic tests, and anticancer chemotherapy and radiation therapy services; permit contracting with independent physicians or physician groups for hospice care services; allow waiver of certain staffing requirements for hospice care programs in non-urbanized areas; define coverage denial, with respect to the limitation on the liability of beneficiaries and providers, to mean a determination that an individual is not terminally ill; and extend the period for physician certification of an individual's terminal illness.
