American Working Families Tax Relief Act of 2009 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to make permanent the tax deductions for state and local sales taxes, qualified tuition and related expenses, and mortgage insurance premiums.
Modifies the first-time homebuyer tax credit by: allowing all purchasers of a principal residence, not just first-time homebuyers, to claim such credit; eliminating the requirement to repay credit amounts over a 15-year period; and imposing a recapture requirement for taxpayers who dispose of a residence within 36 months after purchase.
Extends such credit through December 31, 2009.
