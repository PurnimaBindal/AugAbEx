Instructs the Secretary of the Treasury to mint and issue the following coins emblematic of the landing of Pierre LeMoyne Sieur D'Iberville on the shores of present day Biloxi, Mississippi, on February 13, 1699: ten-dollar gold coins; one-dollar silver coins; and half dollar clad coins.
Mandates payment of surcharges collected from coin sales to: the Biloxi Tricentennial Commission, Inc, for planning and implementing the 1999 Tricentennial Celebration in Biloxi, Mississippi; and the Biloxi First, Inc., upon Commission dissolution, for educational projects directly related to local history instruction for Biloxi public school students.
