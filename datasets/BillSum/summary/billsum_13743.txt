Consumer Bounty Act - Directs courts to require defendants to pay a minimum of $10,000 to prevailing plaintiffs in civil actions brought under specified citizen suit provisions of: the Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA); the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977; the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (commonly known as the Clean Water Act); the Safe Drinking Water Act; the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act of 1972; the Noise Control Act of 1972; the Energy Policy and Conservation Act; the Solid Waste Disposal Act; the Clean Air Act; the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act of 1980 (CERCLA); the Powerplant and Industrial Fuel Use Act of 1978; the Emergency Planning and Community Right-To-Know Act of 1986; the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act; and pipeline safety laws.
Makes multiple defendants in such actions jointly and severally liable.
