Currency Optimization, Innovation, and National Savings Act of 2017 This bill suspends the production of one-cent coins, other than collectible coins,&nbsp;for a 10-year period.
The Government Accountability Office shall study the effect of this temporary suspension and report on whether production should remain suspended.
In addition, the bill provides for: (1)&nbsp;modifications to the&nbsp;composition of the five-cent coin; and the replacement, in circulation,&nbsp;of $1 notes with $1 coins.
