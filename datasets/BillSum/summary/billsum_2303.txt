Orphan Highway Restoration Act - Defines "orphan highway" to mean a highway that: formerly was a US numbered highway; no longer is a principal route for traffic passing through a state; and because of decreased importance to statewide transportation, has received only routine maintenance but needs significant restoration.
Directs the Secretary of Transportation to: make grants and provide technical assistance to states and local government units to restore orphan highways; and allocate funds to grant recipients for FY2010-FY2015 based on a calculated formula.
