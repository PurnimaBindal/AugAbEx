Community Savings and Investment Act of 2004 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to establish a separate corporate income tax rate for qualified community lenders of: 15 percent of the amount of taxable income over $250,000 up to $1 million; and the highest corporate tax rate imposed (currently 35%) for income over $1 million.
Defines "qualified community lender" as a local community bank which: achieved a satisfactory record of meeting community credit needs at its most recent Federal bank examination: provided not less than 60 percent of its loans to its local community; meets specified community ownership requirements; and has total assets of not more than $1 billion.
Reduces tax rates on certain subchapter S taxable income attributable to community lender income.
Excludes from gross income distressed community banking income.
Defines "distressed community banking income" as the net income of an FDA-insured bank which is derived from the active conduct of a banking business in a low-income, high unemployment community as defined by the Bank Enterprise Act of 1991.
