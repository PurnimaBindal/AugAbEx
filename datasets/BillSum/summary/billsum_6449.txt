Directs the tribal council of each Tribe to prepare a Tribal Plan for using payments for carrying out projects and programs to promote: economic development; infrastructure development; or the educational, health, recreational, and social welfare objectives of the Tribe and its members.
Prohibits any payment made to either Tribe pursuant to this Act from: resulting in the reduction or denial of any service or program to which the Tribe or any member of the Tribe is otherwise entitled because of federally recognized status; being subject to any Federal or State income tax; or affecting Pick-Sloan Missouri River Basin power rates.
Authorizes appropriations.
