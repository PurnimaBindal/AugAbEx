Affordable Housing Credit Improvement Act of 2016 This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to the low-income housing tax credit, to: expand the credit by increasing the state housing credit ceiling, modify the cost-of-living adjustment required for the state housing credit ceiling, establish a new average income test which may be used to determine if a low-income housing project qualifies for the credit, and establish a minimum credit rate of 4% for certain new or existing buildings.
