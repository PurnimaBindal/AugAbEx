Alternatives to Opioids Prescribing Act This bill requires the Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services (CMS) to carry out a demonstration project to evaluate alternatives to&nbsp;the use of&nbsp;opioids to treat Medicare enrollees&nbsp;in hospital emergency departments.
Under the project, the CMS must provide incentive payments to&nbsp;participating hospitals&nbsp;for decreasing the use of opioids and increasing the use of opioid alternatives.
