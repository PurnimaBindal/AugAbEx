Presidential Allowance Modernization Act - Revises provisions relating to presidential pensions to&nbsp;allow former&nbsp;Presidents&nbsp;an annuity&nbsp;of $200,000&nbsp;and an additional monetary&nbsp;allowance&nbsp;of $200,000 per year.
Reduces such pension by the amount that a President's adjusted gross income in a taxable year exceeds $400,000&nbsp; Increases from $20,000 to $100,000 the annual annuity of a surviving spouse of a former President.
