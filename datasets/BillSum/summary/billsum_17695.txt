Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: allow an enhanced tax deduction for losses sustained from a fraudulent Ponzi-type scheme; extend the carryback period for net operating losses attributable to such schemes; waive certain limitations on the charitable tax deduction for contributions to charities with losses from fraudulent Ponzi-type schemes; and restore the gift tax unified credit for gifts of an interest in a fraudulent Ponzi-type scheme.
Defines "fraudulent Ponzi-type scheme" as any fraudulent investment operation that provides investors with returns that are derived substantially from investments made by other investors rather than from profits.
