American Opportunity Tax Credit Extension Act of 2010 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to the Hope Scholarship tax credit, to: make permanent increases in the amount of such tax credit; allow such credit for the first four years of post-secondary education (currently, first two years); include course materials in the definition of qualified tuition and related expenses for purposes of such credit; increase adjusted gross income levels for purposes of determining eligibility for such credit and the lifetime learning tax credit; and allow such credit as an offset against the alternative minimum tax (AMT).
