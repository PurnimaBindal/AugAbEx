Homeland Security Community Mobilization Act of 2006 - Establishes the Homeland Security Community Mobilization Program, under which the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall make grants to local entities in 10 metropolitan areas (including New Orleans, Louisiana) to carry out pilot projects that provide: emergency response training to prepare low-income adults and youth to provide emergency assistance in metropolitan areas during disasters; job training and financial assistance to families with children in school, awarding priority to families with incomes under the poverty line; community education and school support; juvenile delinquency prevention programs and youth counseling; entrepreneurial business development training for youth; and family health care information and solutions.
