Stopping the Pharmaceutical Industry from Keeping Drugs Expensive (SPIKE) Act of 2017 This bill amends title XI (General Provisions) of the Social Security Act to require manufacturers of&nbsp; drugs with specified percentage increases in their wholesale costs to submit to the Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services (CMS) written justification&nbsp;for&nbsp;certain increases in drug prices.
The CMS shall&nbsp;publish each&nbsp;submission, together with an&nbsp;easily understandable summary, on its website.
Certain proprietary information may be excluded from publication, as specified by the bill.
A manufacturer that does not comply with the bill's requirements&nbsp;shall be&nbsp;subject to civil monetary penalties.
