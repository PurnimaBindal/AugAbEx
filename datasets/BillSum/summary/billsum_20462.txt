Prepare All Kids Act of 2009 - Creates a part I (High Quality Prekindergarten Programs) under title I of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965.
Implements such part by requiring the Secretary of Education to establish a Prekindergarten Incentive Fund from which matching grants shall be awarded to states and, through them, subgrants to qualified prekindergarten providers to establish, expand, or enhance voluntary high quality full-day prekindergarten programs serving children ages three through five.
Directs state grantees to set aside: at least 15% of their grant for quality early childhood development programs for children ages zero through three; and at least 10% of their grant to extend the hours of early childhood programs to create extended day and year programs.
Requires prekindergarten subgrantees to: maintain a maximum class size of 20 children, with at least 1 prekindergarten teacher per classroom; and ensure that the ratio of children to prekindergarten teachers and teacher aides does not exceed 10 to 1.
