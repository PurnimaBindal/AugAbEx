Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Business Travel Cards Act of 2017 (Sec.
This bill amends the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to authorize US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) to issue an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Business Travel Card (ABT Card) to an individual who: is a US citizen who has been approved and is in good standing in an existing Department of Homeland Security (DHS) international trusted traveler program, and is engaged in business in the Asia-Pacific region or is a US government official engaged in Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation business.
The bill: transfers card program authority from the the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Business Travel Cards Act of 2011 (APECBTC Act) to the Homeland Security Act of 2002; and makes the program, which is scheduled to end on September 30, 2018, permanent.
The CBP shall: integrate application procedures for, and issuance, renewal, and revocation of, ABT cards with existing DHS international trusted traveler programs; prescribe and collect a fee for card issuance and renewal, and notify Congress if card program expenditures exceed collected amounts.
The bill establishes in the Treasury an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Business Travel Card Account (Account).
(Sec.
Amounts in the travel card account under the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Business Travel Cards Act of 2011 are transferred to the Account and shall be available for expenses incurred with any card.
(Sec.
The APECBTC Act is repealed.
A card issued pursuant to such Act before the date of enactment of this bill shall remain valid until it expires.
