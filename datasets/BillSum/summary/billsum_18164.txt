Renewable Energy Development Incentives Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code respecting the renewable resource credit to: include alternative resources (solar, biomass, incremental hydropower, and geothermal energy); provide an increased credit for certain co-production facilities, and for qualified facilities on Indian and Alaskan Native Indian lands; provide for credit transferability; require facility compliance with pollution laws; and eliminate the January 1, 2002, placed-in-service date for purposes of qualified facility eligibility.
