Working Families Relief Act This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to increase and expand tax incentives for employer-provided dependent care assistance.
The bill increases the limit on the amount excludible from the gross income of an employee for employer-provided dependent care assistance and requires annual inflation adjustments to such increased limit after 2017.
The bill also establishes tax credits for: small employer dependent care assistance program start-up costs, and employer matching contributions for dependent care assistance programs.
