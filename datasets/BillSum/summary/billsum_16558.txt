Critical Care Assessment and Improvement Act of 2014 - Requires studies on critical care health services by the Institute of Medicine and the Health Resources and Services Administration of the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)&nbsp; Directs HHS to establish the Critical Care Coordinating Working Group within the National Institutes of Health (NIH) to: advance research and research training in the critical care setting across NIH and HHS; coordinate funding opportunities that involve multiple components of HHS; catalyze the development of new funding opportunities; inform investigators about funding opportunities; represent NIH in government-wide efforts to improve the critical care system; coordinate the collection and analysis of information on NIH research relating to critical care and identify gaps in this research; report annually to the Director of NIH on NIH research efforts relating to critical care; and make recommendations on how to strengthen partnerships within NIH and HHS and public and private entities to expand collaborative, cross-cutting research.
Requires HHS to carry out a demonstration project to improve the quality and efficiency of critical care in acute care hospitals.
