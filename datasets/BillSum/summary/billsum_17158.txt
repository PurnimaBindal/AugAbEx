Commission on Military Justice and Fairness Act - Establishes the Commission on Military Justice and Fairness to investigate and report findings and recommendations to the President and the Congress concerning: the existence of adequate safeguards for military personnel who report incidents of sexual misconduct, sexual harassment, or unlawful gender discrimination; the existence of adequate mechanisms for investigating such incidents, including the appropriate training of investigative personnel; the availability of adequate mechanisms for the resolution of complaints involving such conduct, either through administrative hearing or court-martial; and military justice system procedures and related matters with regard to such cases.
