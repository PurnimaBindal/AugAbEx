Health Care Safety Net Amendments Technical Corrections Act of 2003 - Amends the Public Health Service Act and the Health Care Safety Net Amendments of 2002 to make technical and conforming amendments respecting: health centers; rural health outreach; telehealth; mental health services via telehealth; health professional shortage areas; and National Health Service Corps personnel, scholarships, and loan repayment.
Authorizes grants to State professional licensing boards for State cooperation to reduce statutory and regulatory barriers to telemedicine.
