Tax Depreciation, Modernization, and Simplification Act of 2005 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to modify or create new class lives for depreciable business assets that reasonably reflect anticipated useful life and decline in value; eliminate the mid-quarter accounting convention for determining depreciation; allow an election to consolidate, in a mass asset account, the accounting of depreciable properties that each cost less than $10,000; and extend permanently the $100,000 expensing allowance for depreciable business assets.
