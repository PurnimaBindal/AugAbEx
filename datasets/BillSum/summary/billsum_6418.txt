Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise the income and excise tax credits for biodiesel used as fuel to: allow a $100 tax credit for each gallon of biodiesel produced; provide for an increased income tax credit for small biodiesel producers; revise the definitions of "biodiesel" and "small biodiesel producer"; treat renewable diesel in the same manner as biodiesel for income tax purposes; and treat biodiesel as a taxable fuel for excise tax purposes.
Extends the biodiesel income and excise tax credits through December 31, 2014.
