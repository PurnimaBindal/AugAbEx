Space Preservation Act of 2001 - Reaffirms US policy that activities in space should be devoted to peaceful purposes.Directs the President to: ban US space-based weapons and remove from space any existing US weapons; terminate research and development, testing, manufacturing, production, and deployment of all such weapons; and direct the US representatives to the United Nations and other international organizations to work toward negotiating, adopting, and implementing a world agreement banning space-based weapons.
