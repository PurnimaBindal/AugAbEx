S Corporation Modernization Act of 2015&nbsp; Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise the tax treatment of S corporations by: permanently reducing from 10 to 5 years the period during which S corporation built-in gains are subject to tax, repealing mandatory termination of S corporation elections for excessive passive investment income, allowing S corporations to increase passive investment income from 25 to 60% without incurring additional tax, allowing nonresident aliens to be potential current beneficiaries of an electing small business trust (ESBT), allowing individual retirement accounts to be S corporation shareholders, allowing ESBTs to claim expanded charitable tax deductions, and making permanent the rule requiring a basis adjustment to stock of an S corporation making charitable contributions of property.
