Makes funds appropriated to the Department of Defense (DOD) available for costs incurred by the National Guard in conducting international military-to-civilian contacts, civilian-to-civilian contacts, and comparable activities in order to: support objectives of the commander of the combatant command for the theater of operations in which the contacts occur; build international civil-military partnerships and capacity; strengthen cooperation between US and foreign departments and agencies; facilitate intergovernmental collaboration between the US government and foreign governments; and facilitate the exchange of information between the US government and foreign governments on matters relating to defense and security.
