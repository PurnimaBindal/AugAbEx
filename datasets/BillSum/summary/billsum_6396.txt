Bring Jobs Home Act Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: grant business taxpayers a&nbsp;tax credit for up to 20% of insourcing expenses incurred for eliminating a&nbsp;business located outside the United States and&nbsp; relocating it within the United States, and deny a tax deduction for outsourcing expenses incurred in relocating a US business outside the United States.
Requires an increase in the taxpayer's employment of full-time employees&nbsp;in the United States in order to claim the tax credit for insourcing expenses.
