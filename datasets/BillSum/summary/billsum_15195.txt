Distilled Spirits Tax Payment Simplification Act of 1995 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to modify or impose requirements regarding: the transfer of distilled spirits between bonded premises and between importation and bonded premises; operations as a bonded dealer conducted on the bonded premises of a distilled spirits plant; establishment and operation of such a plant by a bonded dealer; election to be treated as a bonded dealer; the time at which the tax on distilled spirits is determined; distilled spirits lost or destroyed in bond or returned to bonded premises; the time for tax payment and payment by electronic transfer; and application to a plant used by a bonded dealer of provisions relating to sales by proprietors of controlled premises.
