Fitness for Life Act - Authorizes the Secretary of Education to make competitive, renewable four-year grants to ten institutions of higher education (IHEs) for the expansion of their master's degree in physical education programs.
Requires IHEs to use the grant funds to: teach students to incorporate technology and innovative teaching practices into physical education instruction and encourage children to be more physically active outside the classroom; develop program curricula that link practice and theory; partner with local educational agencies (LEAs) that serve schools where their students are to serve as full-time student teachers and most of their instruction is to occur; extend their programs into the communities served by their partner LEAs; actively promote their programs to prospective students; and engage in such other activities the Secretary requires.
Directs the Secretary, in developing the grant program, to consult with an IHE that for the past five years has: offered a master's degree in physical education program that emphasizes the incorporation of technology into physical education instruction; and partnered with an LEA that serves schools in which its master's degree candidates serve as student teachers.
