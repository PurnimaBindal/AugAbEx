Consumer Telephone Records Protection Act of 2006 - Amends the federal criminal code to prohibit: the obtaining by fraud or other unauthorized means of confidential phone records information from a telecommunications carrier or IP-enabled voice service provider (covered entity); and the sale of such records by any person, including any employee of a covered entity.
Exempts law enforcement agencies.
Imposes a fine and/or imprisonment for up to five years.
Doubles such penalties for violations occurring in a 12-month period involving more than $100,000 or more than 50 customers of a covered entity.
