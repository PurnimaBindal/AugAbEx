Flight 93 National Memorial Act - Establishes a memorial at the September 11, 2001, crash site of United Airlines Flight 93 in the Stonycreek Township, Somerset County, Pennsylvania, to honor the passengers and crew of Flight 93Establishes the Flight 93 Advisory Commission to: make recommendations for the planning, design, construction, and long-term management of a permanent memorial; and advise the Secretary of the Interior in the development of a management plan for the site.States that the terrorists on United Flight 93 on September 11, 2001 shall not be considered passengers or crew of that flight.
