Alaska Peninsula Wilderness Designation Act of 1993 - Designates the following lands in Alaska as components of the National Wilderness Preservation System: the Aniakchak Wilderness within the Aniakchak National Monument and Preserve; the Alaska Peninsula Wilderness within the Alaska Peninsula National Wildlife Refuge; and the addition of specified lands within the Becharof National Wildlife Refuge to the Becharof Wilderness.
Provides for the relinquishment of Koniag, Inc, Selection Rights (oil and gas rights) to specified lands in Alaska in exchange for Certificates of Value which could be used only with respect to Outer Continental Shelf oil and gas leases.
