Veterans Mental Health and Back-to-Work Act of 2003 - Authorizes the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, in providing veterans' rehabilitative services, to furnish: work skills training and development; employment support; and job development and placement.
Revises provisions establishing the Committee on Care of Severely Chronically Mentally Ill Veterans to: redesignate such Committee as the Committee on Care of Veterans With Serious Mental Illness; and require in its annual report an assessment of how the veterans' rehabilitative services programs are serving veterans with mental illness.
