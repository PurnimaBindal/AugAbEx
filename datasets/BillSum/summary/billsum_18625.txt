Stark Administrative Simplification Act of 2017 This bill establishes alternative protocols and sanctions for inadvertent technical noncompliance with the Stark Rule against self-referral under Medicare.
"Technical noncompliance" means, with respect to a compensation arrangement, that: the agreement is not signed by one or more parties to the arrangement, the arrangement was a holdover arrangement for a period longer than is allowed by the Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services, or documentation of the arrangement&nbsp;contains specified identifying information&nbsp;but is otherwise insufficient to satisfy the writing requirement of an applicable exception.
