Burt Lake Band of Ottawa and Chippewa Indians Reaffirmation Act - Reaffirms federal recognition and the rights and privileges of the Burt Lake Band of Ottawa and Chippewa Indians (Cheboigan or Cheboygan Band, in Michigan).
Entitles such Band to the federal services and benefits provided to recognized Indians.
Provides for lands to be acquired and held in trust for the Band by the Secretary of the Interior.
