Stop AIDS in Prison Act of 2006 - Requires the Bureau of Prisons to develop a comprehensive policy to coordinate HIV/AIDS testing, treatment, and prevention for federal prison inmates.
Requires such policy to include provisions for: testing and counseling of inmates immediately following admission to a prison facility; education regarding HIV/AIDS transmission and encouragement of behavior to reduce the risk; HIV/AIDS testing annually or after exposure to HIV; procedures for inmates to request HIV/AIDS counseling and tests confidentially; comprehensive medical treatment of inmates who test positive and confidential counseling on managing their medical condition and preventing transmission to others; testing, counseling, and referral prior to reentry into the community; and noting but not penalizing an inmate's refusal of HIV/AIDS testing.
Amends federal criminal code provisions relating to HIV/AIDS testing to: make such testing available to all federal prison inmates upon intake and upon release regardless of length of sentence or risk factors; allow inmates to decline such testing.
