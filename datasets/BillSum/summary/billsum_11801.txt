Rescue and Emergency Services Prepared for Our Nation's Defense Act - Establishes the Commission to Assess Weapons of Mass Destruction Domestic Response Capabilities to: assess Federal agency efforts to enhance domestic preparedness for incidents involving weapons of mass destruction and Federal training programs for local emergency responses to such incidents; recommend strategies for the coordination of response efforts; assess the appropriate role of State and local governments in funding local response capabilities; and report to the Congress within six months after its first meeting.
