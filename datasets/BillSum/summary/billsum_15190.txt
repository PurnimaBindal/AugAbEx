Amends the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to establish a program for Getting Our Girls Ready for the 21st Century (Go Girl).
Authorizes the Secretary of Education to make grants to, and contracts and cooperative agreements with, local educational agencies to provide subgrants to elementary and secondary schools for services that: encourage the ongoing interest of girls in science, mathematics, and technology; and prepare girls to pursue undergraduate and graduate degrees and careers in those fields.
