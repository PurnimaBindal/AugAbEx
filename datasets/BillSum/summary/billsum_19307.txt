Liberian Refugee Immigration Fairness Act of 2015 This bill directs the Department of Homeland Security to adjust to permanent resident status a qualifying Liberian national who: has been continuously present in the United States between January 1, 2013, through the date of status adjustment application; or is the spouse, child, or unmarried son or daughter of such an alien.
Adjustment applications must be filed not later than one year after the date of enactment of this bill.
