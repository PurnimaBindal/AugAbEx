Amends the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to: extend the penny stock bar to any noncovered security; and declare it unlawful for any person against whom certain enforcement sanctions are in effect to serve as officer, director, or participant in any offering involving a noncovered security.
(Sec.
Authorizes a court to prohibit violators of SEC rules governing the use of manipulative or deceptive devices from participating in an offering of a noncovered security.
(Sec.
Bars persons subject to specified court orders from serving as officers, directors, or participants in any offering involving a noncovered security.
