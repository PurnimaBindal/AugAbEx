Internet Gambling Regulation and Tax Enforcement Act of 2009 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: impose an Internet gambling license fee on Internet gambling operators and an additional tax on unauthorized bets or wagers; require such operators to file informational returns identifying themselves and the individuals placing bets or wagers with them; require withholding of tax on net Internet gambling winnings and on the winnings of nonresident aliens; and extend the excise tax on wagers to include wagers placed within the United States or any commonwealth, territory, or possession by a US citizen or resident.
