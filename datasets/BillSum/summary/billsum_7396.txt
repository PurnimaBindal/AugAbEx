Exchange Information Disclosure Act - Amends the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to expand reporting requirements related to health care exchanges.
Requires the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS) to publish reports weekly through March 31, 2015, on: consumer interactions with healthcaregov or subsequent sites and any efforts undertaken to remedy problems that impact taxpayers and consumers, calls to the federal customer service call center, including the number of calls received by the call center, problems identified by users, and referrals of those calls; all navigators and certified application counselors that have been trained and certified by health care exchanges; and all agents and brokers who have been trained and certified by the federal health care exchange.
