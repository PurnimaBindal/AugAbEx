Child Product Safety Notification Act - Instructs the Consumer Product Safety Commission to promulgate a final consumer product safety standard that requires manufacturers of durable infant or toddler products to: provide consumers with a postage-paid consumer registration form with each such product; maintain a record of registered consumer contact information to improve the effectiveness of product recalls; permanently place the manufacturer name and contact information, model name and number, and the date of manufacture on each durable infant or toddler product; and implement specified recordkeeping and notification requirements.
