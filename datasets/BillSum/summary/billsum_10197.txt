Higher Education Assistance Improvement Act - Amends the Higher Education Act of 1965 (HEA) to increase funds available for the provision of student financial assistance.Increases the maximum individual Pell grant amount to: $7,500 for academic year 2002-2003; and $7,900 for academic year 2003-2004.Increases amounts authorized to be appropriated for FY 2002 and 2003 under the HEA student assistance provisions: Federal TRIO programs; Gaining Early Awareness and Readiness for Undergraduate Programs (GEAR UP); supplemental education opportunity grants; graduate assistance in areas of national need; and Thurgood Marshall legal educational opportunity program.Expresses the sense of Congress that appropriations for the following higher education programs should be increased by specified amounts for FY 2002 and as necessary for FY 2003: Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership (LEAP); Child Care Access Means Parents in School (CAMPIS); work-study; National Health Service Corps scholarship and loan repayment programs; Perkins loans; and Javits fellowships.Expands eligibility under the Federal Family Education Loan (FFEL), Federal direct loan, and Federal Perkins loan programs by: eliminating certain new borrower restrictions; expanding eligible service; and eliminating delays in service benefit.
Directs the Secretary of Education to repay or cancel certain amounts of loan obligations under those HEA student loan programs.
