National Uniform Food Safety Labeling Act - Amends the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to deem food to be misbranded unless certain labeling information is provided concerning: raw or partially cooked eggs, fish and shellfish, dairy products, or unpasteurized juice in ready-to-eat form; frozen fish and shellfish other than smoked, cured, cooked, or commercially sterilized; raw eggs; country of origin for perishable agricultural commodities or derived products ; freshness dates; food labeled as natural; kosher and kosher-style foods; unit pricing; and grades (where customary) for farm products.
