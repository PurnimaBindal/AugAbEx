Remove Explicit Material Offensive to Victims Expeditiously Act of 2018 or the REMOVE Act This bill requires the Federal Trade Commission to prescribe rules for the creation of a public website where an individual may: obtain information regarding the existence of sexually-intimate imagery depicting such individual, submitted by a registered interactive computer-service provider; and submit a takedown-request form.
If the form meets specified requirements, a designated agent of a provider must review and remove the imagery from the interactive computer service.
