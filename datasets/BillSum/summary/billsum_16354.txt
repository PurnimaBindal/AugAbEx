Health Savings Act of 2014 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to health savings accounts (HSAs), to: allow an additional tax deduction for amounts paid to the HSA of a taxpayer's child or grandchild; allow a rollover of HSA funds to the child, parent, or grandparent of an account holder; increase the maximum HSA contribution limit; allow mandatory distributions from an individual retirement account (IRA) to be paid into an HSA; exempt HSAs from creditor claims in bankruptcy; and expand the definition of an HSA compatible plan to include bronze, silver, and catastrophic plans on an insurance exchange.
