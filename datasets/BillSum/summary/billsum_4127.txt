Affordable Housing Improvements Act - Amends the United States Housing Act of 1937 to authorize the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to transfer unused section 8 rental assistance amounts for use under the HOME investment partnerships and public housing Capital Fund programs.
Limits such funds' availability to18 months.Amends the Cranston-Gonzalez National Affordable Housing Act respecting HOME investment program use of such funds to: require use within the jurisdiction of the recipient public housing agency; and target specified income-based families for rental and homeownership assistance.
