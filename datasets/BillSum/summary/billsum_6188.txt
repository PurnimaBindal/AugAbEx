S Corporation Modernization Act of 2008 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise the tax treatment of S corporations by: reducing from 10 to seven years the period during which S corporation built-in gains are subject to tax; repealing mandatory termination of S corporation elections for excessive passive investment income; allowing S corporations to increase passive investment income from 25 to 60% without incurring additional tax; allowing nonresident aliens to be potential current beneficiaries of an electing small business trust (ESBT); allowing individual retirement accounts (IRAs) to be S corporation shareholders; and allowing ESBTs to claim expanded charitable tax deductions.
