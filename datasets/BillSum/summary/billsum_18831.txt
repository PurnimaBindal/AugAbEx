Health Access and Flexibility Act of 2003 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code with respect to Archer medical savings accounts to: eliminate such accounts' availability to only self-employed individuals and employees of small employers; repeal the 750,000 limitation on the number of such accounts; reduce the permitted minimum deductibles on qualifying high deductible plans to $1,000 (single coverage), and $2,000 (family coverage); increase the monthly (and thus the annual) deduction limitation permitted for account contributions; permit employer and employee contributions to be made to an account; make cost-of-living adjustments applicable to only high deductible plan maximums; and provide for 60-day rollovers from a health flexible spending arrangement to an account.Amends title XIX of the Social Security Act (Medicaid) to authorize a State to provide alternative medical benefits for eligible population groups which shall consist of at least: coverage for medical expenses in a year after a catastrophic deductible has been met; and contribution into a medical freedom account (as defined by this Act).
