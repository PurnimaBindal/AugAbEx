Drug Overdose Reduction Act - Requires the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) to award grants or cooperative agreements to enable public health agencies or community-based organizations to reduce deaths occurring from overdoses of drugs or controlled substances, including by: providing training on the effective response to individuals who have overdosed; or providing overdose prevention, recognition, treatment, or response to individuals in need of such services.
Requires the Director to: annually compile and publish data on the deaths occurring from overdoses of drugs or controlled substances for the preceding year; and develop a plan to reduce the number of such deaths.
