Emergency Warning Act of 2003 - Directs the Secretaries of Commerce and Homeland Security to establish comprehensive national standards and systems for providing effective public warnings.Directs the Secretary of Commerce to: develop and implement benchmarks assessing current all-hazard warning capabilities; conduct research and pilot programs to determine and improve the effectiveness of such warnings; develop specifications for a national backbone for collecting and routing all-hazard warnings; use the full range of communications technologies to provide warnings; provide public warnings through automatic alert televisions and radios; and educate the public about such warnings.Directs the Secretary of Homeland Security to: develop and recommend standards and guidelines for universal all-hazard terminology and protocols, and issue warnings related to acts of terrorism and other public safety threats via a public warning system.
