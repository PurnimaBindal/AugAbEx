Prison Abuse Remedies Act of 2009 - Amends the Civil Rights of Institutionalized Persons Act to: eliminate the requirement of a prior showing of physical injury before a prisoner may bring a claim for mental or emotional injury suffered while in custody; and provide for a 90-day stay of nonfrivolous claims relating to prison conditions to allow prison officials to consider such claims through the administrative process; and exclude from the application of such Act prisoners under the age of 18.
Amends the federal criminal code to: exempt prisoners under the age of 18 from the restrictions imposed by the Prison Litigation Reform Act; and expand the discretionary authority of judges in awarding relief in actions involving prison conditions.
Amends the federal judicial code to: exempt prisoners under the age of 18 from certain restrictions on in forma pauperis proceedings; expand the number of in forma pauperis actions a prisoner may bring; and revise requirements for assessing filing fees and costs against prisoners in such actions.
