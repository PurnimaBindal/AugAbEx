National Timing Resilience and Security Act of 2017 This bill requires the Department of Transportation to provide for the establishment, sustainment, and operation of a complement to and backup&nbsp;for the timing component of the Global Positioning System (GPS).&nbsp;(GPS satellites contain atomic clocks that provide precise time data and allow GPS receivers to synchronize to those clocks.)
The system must:&nbsp; reduce critical dependencies on the GPS network; ensure the availability of uncorrupted and non-degraded timing signals for military and civilian users if GPS timing signals are corrupted or otherwise unavailable; and be land-based, operational&nbsp;in 2 years, and capable of operation for 20 years.
