Amends the Solid Waste Disposal Act to direct the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to require the preparation of a community information statement as part of the permitting process applicable to any new off-site hazardous waste treatment or disposal facility.
Requires such statement to describe: the effects of the proposed facility on the host community; the types of waste expected to enter the facility, the types of releases expected from the facility, and the human health impacts associated with such wastes and releases; the alternatives for mitigating impacts on the community; the demographic characteristics of the community; the presence in the community of an existing solid waste treatment, storage, or disposal facility or site in which a hazardous substance release has occurred, and the extent to which the site has been remediated; and the permit applicant's compliance with State and Federal environmental regulations and laws.
