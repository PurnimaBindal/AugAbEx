NDRF Ship Disposal Act of 1993 - Mandates the disposal of all vessels in the National Defense Reserve Fleet unless: assigned to the Ready Reserve Force; specifically authorized or required by statute to be used for a particular purpose; necessary for national defense purposes (requires annual recertification); or needed for State or Federal agency use (requires annual recertification).
Allows use of 15 vessels being disposed of for the artificial reef program under specified Federal law.
