Directs the Secretaries of Agriculture and the Interior, prior to making permanent road closings on National Forest System or Bureau of Land Management lands, respectively, to: provide affected State and local officials with advance notice showing affected roads and traffic patterns; and provide for public notice and comment.
Requires: the Secretaries to meet annually with State and local officials to describe agency proposals that will or may result in permanent road closures within the coming two fiscal years; and information to be provided at the first meeting respecting the previous five years' road closings.
