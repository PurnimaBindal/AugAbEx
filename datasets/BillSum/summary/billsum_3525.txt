Trade Prosecutor Act - Amends the Trade Act of 1974 to establish in the Office of the US Trade Representative (USTR) a Trade Enforcement Division, headed by a Trade Enforcement Officer, to ensure that US trading partners comply with trade agreements to which the United States is a party.
Establishes within the Division the Office of Trade Assistance for Small Businesses.
Requires the USTR, after submission of a specified report, to annually identify (and report to Congress on): US trade enforcement priorities; enforcement actions and their impact on foreign trade barriers; and priority foreign country trade practices on which the USTR will focus enforcement efforts.
