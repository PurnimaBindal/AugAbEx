Transforming Student Debt to Home Equity Act of 2017 This bill requires the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) and the Federal Housing Finance Agency&nbsp;to jointly establish and implement a pilot demonstration program to provide, to eligible applicants with federal student loan debt, assistance in purchasing eligible properties.
Specifically, the program may provide for: discounts on the appraised value of eligible properties, flexibility in certain underwriting standards, the development of new mortgage products specifically targeted to eligible applicants, the development of a program that uses actuarial information to determine how the repayment of federal student loans may be integrated into a mortgage repayment schedule to allow eligible applicants to accumulate home equity, and other appropriate assistance.
An "eligible property" is a property: that is designed as a dwelling&nbsp;for occupancy by one to four families; that is safe and habitable; the occupancy of which will promote community revitalization; and that is owned by HUD, the Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae), or the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac).
