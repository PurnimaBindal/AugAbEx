Amends the Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (Act) to: revise specified geographic and illness eligibility criteria with respect to claims relating to atmospheric testing and uranium mining; and make funding for the Radiation Exposure Compensation Trust Fund permanent.Requires periodic claims and budget reporting by the General Accounting Office with respect to Department of Justice administration of the Act.Amends the Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act of 2000 to: provide a $50,000 payment plus medical benefits for a covered individual (or survivor) who has received an atmospheric testing or uranium mining payment under the Act; and extend coverage to all covered individuals (currently covered uranium employees).
