Increased Individual Retirement Accounts for All Act of 2001 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase the annual limitation on deductible contributions to individual retirement accounts (IRAs) to $5,000 and provide a cost-of-living adjustment to such amount; provide for increased "catch-up" contributions for individuals aged 50 or older; and until December 31, 2005, allow a nonrefundable credit to certain individuals for elective deferrals and IRA contributions.
