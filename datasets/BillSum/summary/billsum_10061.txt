Amends the Internal Revenue Code to increase the tax credits for: alcohol used as fuel; biodiesel and renewable diesel used as fuel; alternative fuels; alternative motor vehicles; and alternative fuel vehicle refueling property (service stations for dispensing ethanol and other alternative fuels to retail customers).
Denies to major integrated oil companies (companies with average daily worldwide crude oil production of at least 500,000 barrels) : two-year amortization of geological and geophysical expenditures; percentage depletion for oil and gas properties; and tax deductions for intangible drilling and development costs.
Prohibits the Secretary of the Interior from providing crude oil and natural gas producers on federal lands exemptions from royalty payment requirements during periods when the average US price of crude oil is over $55 per barrel and the average price of natural gas is $10 per 1000 cubic feet.
