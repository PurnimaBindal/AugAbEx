Amends the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to require each interstate Internet seller to comply with requirements of this Act with respect to the sale or offer of prescription drugs.
Requires the seller to: post visibly on its web site home page its street address, the States in which it is authorized as a pharmacy, certain prescriber information, and a statement it will dispense prescription drugs only upon a valid prescription; and disclose such information to State licensing boards.Directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to: engage in activities to educate the public about the dangers of purchasing prescription drugs from unlawful Internet sources; and recommend to Congress the coordination of activities of Federal agencies regarding Internet sellers that operate from foreign countries with the activities of such foreign governments.
