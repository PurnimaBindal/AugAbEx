Congressional Commission on Financial Accountability and Preparedness Act of 2009 - Establishes the Congressional Commission on Financial Accountability and Preparedness to review and report to Congress on the actions leading up to the 2008 breakdown in the financial markets and failures in the regulatory system.
Requires the Commission to submit special reports on: any corporate abuse of taxpayer funds the Commission finds occurred; the current state of the regulatory system and its effectiveness in overseeing the participants in the financial system and protecting consumers; and any fiduciary negligence, fraudulent behavior, poor corporate governance, obstructionism, or media manipulation that the Commission determines was engaged in by relevant corporate officers and elected or appointed government officials.
