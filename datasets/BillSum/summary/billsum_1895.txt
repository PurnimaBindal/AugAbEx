Idaho Panhandle National Forest Improvement Act of 2002 - Authorizes the Secretary of Agriculture to sell or exchange certain National Forest System lands in Idaho, and to use the proceeds to acquire or rehabilitate: facilities for a new ranger station in the Silver Valley area of the Panhandle National Forest; and other facilities in such Forest.Authorizes the Secretary to use other funds if sale or exchange proceeds are insufficient for the acquisition or improvement of the ranger station and associated land.
