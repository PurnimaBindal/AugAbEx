Reverse the Raid on Student Aid Act of 2006 - Amends the Higher Education Act of 1965 to cut student loan interest rates to 34% for student borrowers and 425% for parent borrowers under the Federal Family Education Loan (FFEL) and William D Ford Direct Loan programs for loans whose first disbursement is made on or after July 1, 2006.
Maintains the current 68% interest rate, however, for unsubsidized Stafford loans for middle-income borrowers under such programs.
Authorizes and appropriates the funds necessary to award eligible students with Pell Grants up to specified maximum increasing amounts from academic year 2007 to 2012, with increases thereafter gauged to the Consumer Price Index.
Allows students to consolidate loans under the FFEL program while still in school.
Sets limits on funds available for administrative expenses under the FFEL and Direct Loan programs for FY2007-2012.
Eliminates the current FY2007-2011 authorization of appropriations for the administrative costs of such programs.
Repeals the single holder rule which requires borrowers to apply to their current lenders for FFEL consolidation loans.
