Livestock Concentration Report Act - Establishes a Commission on Concentration in the Livestock Industry composed of the Secretary of Agriculture, cattle, hog, and lamb producers, meat packers, corporate representatives, economists, and antitrust experts to review: the adequacy of the study of concentration in the red meat packing industry; the impact of antitrust, coordination, and other laws affecting the meat packing industry; certain farm-to-retail and other price related data; a specified lamb study; and certain hog processing activities.
Requires the Commission to report to the Congress within a specified time, and terminates the Commission within a specified time after such submission.
