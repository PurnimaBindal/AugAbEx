(This measure has not been amended since it was passed by the House on September 19, 2012.
The summary of that version is repeated here.)
VA Major Construction Authorization and Expiring Authorities Extension Act of 2012 - Title I: Construction Authorizations - Authorizes the Secretary of Veterans Affairs (VA) to carry out major medical facility projects (projects) in FY2013 at VA medical centers in: Seattle, Washington; Dallas, Texas; and Miami, Florida.
Authorizes appropriations for such projects.
Provides project funding limitations.
Title II: Extensions of Certain Expiring Authorities - Extends through FY2013 VA default procedures with respect to guaranteed loans to veterans.
Extends through 2013 VA authority: to operate a regional office in the Republic of the Philippines; to provide treatment, rehabilitation, and related services for seriously mentally ill and homeless veterans; to provide expanded services and housing assistance to homeless veterans; for the Advisory Committee on Homeless Veterans; and to use contract physicians to perform VA medical disability examinations.
