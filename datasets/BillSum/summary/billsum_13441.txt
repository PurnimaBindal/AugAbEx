Directs the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to study and report to the Congress on the causes of recent algal blooms off the northwestern coast of Maui, Hawaii, and to research alternatives for the improved management of chemicals present in wastewater treatment and fresh water runoff.
Authorizes the Administrator to establish: a grant program to enable eligible entities to conduct study activities; and demonstration projects to implement best management practices for the control of nonpoint source pollution from erosion and agricultural runoff.
Authorizes appropriations.
