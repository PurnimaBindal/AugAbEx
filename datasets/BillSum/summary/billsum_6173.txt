Cost of Higher Education Review Act of 1997 - Establishes a National Commission on the Cost of Higher Education to study and make recommendations to the President and the Congress regarding: the increase in tuition compared with other commodities and services; innovative methods of reducing or stabilizing tuition; the impact on tuition of specified trends in college and university costs, student financial aid, and government mandates and fiscal policies; mechanisms for a more timely and widespread distribution of data on tuition trends and other costs of operating colleges and universities; and the adequacy of existing Federal and State financial aid programs in meeting the costs of attending colleges and universities.
