Contract With Investors - Amends the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 to repeal the sunset provisions of such Act.Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: accelerate individual income tax rate reductions; accelerate the repeal of estate and generation-skipping transfer taxes; reduce maximum capital gains tax rates for individuals; increase capital loss limitations for individuals; accelerate contribution increases for certain retirement plans; increase the age for mandatory retirement and pension plan distributions; and exclude from gross income dividends received by individuals from domestic, publicly traded C corporations, with exceptions.Expresses the sense of the Senate that Congress should: pass legislation to safeguard workers' pension and retirement accounts; modernize international tax provisions to permit US companies to better compete internationally; repeal outdated regulatory burdens on US investors and companies; enact tort reform; and simplify the Federal tax code and reduce the cost recovery periods for businesses.
