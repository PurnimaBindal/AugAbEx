United States-China Energy Cooperation Act - Authorizes the Secretary of Energy to make grants to encourage cooperation between the United States and China in joint ventures involving: joint energy and climate change policy education programs; and joint research, development, or commercialization of carbon capture and sequestration technology, improved energy efficiency, or renewable energy sources.
Requires joint ventures to be composed of both: Chinese and United States private business entities; Chinese academic persons (who reside and work in China) and US academic persons; or Chinese and US federal, state, or local government entities.
