Responsible Reinvestment Act of 2009 - Makes permanent the repeal of the estate tax.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase to $500,000 the maximum expensing allowance for depreciable business assets; allow first-year expensing of manufacturing and agricultural property; allow a 20% tax deduction for qualified small business income; allow a deduction from self-employment income for health insurance, health savings account contributions, and simplified employee pension plan contributions; and increase the limit on the tax deduction for contributions to a simplified employee pension plan.
