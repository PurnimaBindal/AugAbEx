Mount Pleasant National Scenic Area Act - Establishes in the George Washington National Forest, Virginia, the Mount Pleasant National Scenic Area.
Sets forth provisions regarding: administration of the Area; roads; vegetation management; motorized travel; fire; insects and disease; and water.
Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to develop a management plan for the Area.
Withdraws all federally-owned lands within the Area from disposition under the mining, mineral, and geothermal leasing laws.
