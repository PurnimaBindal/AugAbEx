This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to make permanent provisions included in PL 115-97 (commonly known as the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act) that: reduced the individual tax rates, and modified the taxation of the unearned income of children.
The existing individual tax rates (10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, and 37%) are currently scheduled to expire and revert to higher rates at the end of 2025.
