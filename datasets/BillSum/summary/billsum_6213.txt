SSI Savers Act of 2010 - Amends title XVI (Supplemental Security Income) (SSI) of the Social Security Act to: increase resource limits for aged, blind, or disabled individuals who do not have an eligible spouse; require an inflation adjustment for such individuals, regardless of whether a spouse is eligible; provide a limited exclusion from resources of certain deferred compensation and education savings arrangements; set forth income rules imputing income from certain deferred compensation arrangements; and eliminate the requirement that SSI recipients apply for periodic payments from certain deferred compensation arrangements.
