Economic Relief for Gulf Coast Families and Businesses Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: exclude from gross income gain from the sale or exchange of Hurricane Katrina Zone assets held for more than five years; and allow reduced estate tax rates for Hurricane Katrina victims.
Includes as such assets: Katrina Zone business stock or principal interests issued after August 28, 2005, and acquired by the taxpayer before January 1, 2007; and Katrina Zone business property acquired between such dates, provided its original use in the Zone commences with the taxpayer.
