Environmental Finance Act of 1995 - Directs the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to establish: an Environmental Financial Advisory Board to provide expert advice on issues affecting the costs and financing of environmental activities at the Federal, State, and local levels; and Environmental Finance Centers in institutions of higher education in each of the regions of the EPA.
Authorizes the Centers to: provide training of State and local officials; publish materials relating to financing of environmental infrastructure; conduct conferences and advisory panels on specific environmental finance issues; establish information services; generate case studies and reports; develop surveys of financial issues and needs of State and local governments; identify financial programs and alternative financing mechanisms for training purposes; hold public meetings; and collaborate and exchange information.
Permits the Administrator to make grants to institutions of higher education to carry out the Center program.
Authorizes appropriations.
