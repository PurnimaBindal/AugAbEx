Voter Advocate and Democracy Index Act of 2007 - Amends the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to establish within the Election Assistance Commission an Office of the Voter Advocate to develop and administer a program to: collect specified voter data from states with respect to each election for federal office; develop, refine, and publish a Democracy Index; make grants to eligible entities; and make recommendations to states to improve their performance in the administration of federal elections (as determined based on a state-by-state comparison of the voter data collected under this Act.)
Directs the Office to establish a Democracy Index Pilot Program for calendar year 2008 to make grants to eligible entities for the purpose of creating a Democracy Index.
Requires each state to submit to the Office of the Voter Advocate specified voter data with respect to each election for federal office.
