Clean Renewable Energy for Public Power Act of 2007 - Amends Internal Revenue Code provisions allowing a tax credit for holders of clean renewable energy bonds (CREBs) to: eliminate allocation limitations on such bonds; reduce from 95 to 90% the amount of CREB proceeds required for capital expenditures; redefine "qualified project" and "public power entity" for CREB purposes; repeal the national limitation on CREBs and arbitrage rules applicable to such bonds; and extend through 2013 the authority for issuing CREBs.
