Indian Health Care Improvement Technical Corrections Act of 1996 - Makes technical corrections to the Indian Health Care Improvement Act, including revisions concerning: the deferral of a health professional's active duty service obligation; and the right of recovery against an insurance company for failure to provide reasonable assurances.
Authorizes appropriations through FY 2000 for the: California Contract Health Services Demonstration Program; Medicare and Medicaid Demonstration Program; Gallup Alcohol and Substance Abuse Center; Substance Abuse Counselor Education Demonstration Program; and Home and Community-Based Care Demonstration Program.
