Bringing Urgent Investment to Local Development Act or the BUILD Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to the tax credit for investment in Build America bonds, to: make permanent the authority for issuance of such bonds, phase in an annual reduction in the credit rate for&nbsp;bondholders and issuers&nbsp;between 2014 and 2019, and allow an issuer a refundable credit amount&nbsp;for investment in economic development&nbsp;bonds in cities with&nbsp;specified unemployment and poverty rates and population loss.
