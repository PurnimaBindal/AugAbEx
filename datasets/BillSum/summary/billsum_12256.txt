National Historic Lighthouse Preservation Act of 1997 - Amends the National Historic Preservation Act to direct the Secretary of the Interior, in order to provide a national historic light station program, to: collect and disseminate information concerning such stations; foster educational programs relating to the history, practice, and contribution to society of such stations; sponsor or conduct research and study into the history of such stations; maintain a listing of such stations; and assess the effectiveness of the program regarding the conveyance of such stations.
Directs the Secretary and the Administrator of General Services to establish a process for identifying and selecting an eligible entity to which a station could be conveyed for education, park, recreation, cultural, and historic preservation purposes.
Requires: the Secretary to review all applicants for the conveyance of a station identified as excess to an agency's needs and forward to the Administrator a single approved application for such station; and the Administrator to convey such station, subject to specified conditions that include a requirement that active aids to navigation continue to be operated and maintained by the United States if considered necessary by the Administrator.
Requires: a station to be offered for sale in accordance with terms developed by the Administrator if no applicants are approved for conveyance; and net sale proceeds to be transferred to the National Maritime Heritage Grant Program.
Requires any Federal department or agency to which a station is conveyed to maintain the station in accordance with the National Historic Preservation Act of 1966 and the Secretary's Historic Preservation Standards.
