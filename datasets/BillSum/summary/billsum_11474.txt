National Food Safety Database Act of 2003 - Amends the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to provide for the establishment of an electronic food safety database which shall be made available, through State agreements, to hospitals, nursing homes, schools, child care facilities, and other eligible institutions.
Authorizes related State grants.
Requires such database to maintain information concerning: enforcement actions; recalls; food-borne illness outbreaks; and facility inspections.
Provides for the establishment of a related interagency task force and advisory committee.
