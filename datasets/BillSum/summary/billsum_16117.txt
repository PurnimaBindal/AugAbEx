Health Professionals Substance Abuse Education Act - Amends the Public Health Service Act to require the Secretary of Health and Human Services to provide support and continue grants to specified organizations to train health professionals to: recognize substance abuse in their patients or patients' family members; intervene, treat, or refer for treatment those individuals who are affected by substance abuse; identify and assist children of substance abusing parents; serve as advocates and resources for community-based substance abuse prevention programs; and address the non-therapeutic use of prescription medications.
Directs the Secretary to: encourage community colleges and other academic institutes to offer academic credit for classes offered by the Addiction Technology Transfer Centers; conduct a process and outcome evaluation of the programs and activities carried out under this Act; and establish and administer a substance abuse faculty fellowship program.
Requires the Secretary to establish centers of excellence at US medical centers or universities to: initiate, promote, and implement training, research, and clinical activities related to targeted issues or special areas of focus; and provide opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration in curriculum development, course development, clinical practice, research and translation of research into practice, and policy analysis and formulation.
