Amends title IV (Student Assistance) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 to direct the Secretary of Education to refinance the private education loans of individuals who are participating in a residency program in: family medicine; obstetrics/gynecology; general internal medicine; gerontology; general pediatrics; or general psychiatry.
Sets the interest rate on the refinanced loans at 825%.
Requires the refinanced loans to have the same terms and conditions as a Direct Consolidation Loan, except they are made ineligible for: consolidation with another loan eligible for consolidation under title IV; income-based repayment terms under title IV; or any loan forgiveness or cancellation under title IV or the Public Health Service Act, except in the case of death or permanent and total disability.
