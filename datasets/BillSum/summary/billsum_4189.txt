Foreign and Armed Forces Tax Fairness Act of 2002 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: restore the full exclusion from gross income of the death gratuity payment; permit a suspension of residency rules governing the exclusion of gain from sale of a principal residence for members of the uniformed services or the Foreign Service serving on qualified official extended duty; exclude from gross income qualified military base realignment and closure fringe benefits; extend tax filing delay provisions to military personnel serving in contingency operations; allow as a business or trade deduction the expenses of a member of the reserve component of the US armed forces in connection with such service (available to itemizers and non-itemizers); include ancestors and lineal descendants of past or present members of the armed forces when determining whether a veterans' organization is exempt from tax; and fully exclude from gross income certain dependent care assistance programs sponsored by the Department of Defense.
