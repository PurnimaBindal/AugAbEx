Regulatory Reform Commissions Act of 1995 - Requires the Director of the Office of Management and Budget to establish three commissions to review and report on the regulations issued by the Environmental Protection Agency, the Department of Labor, and the Department of the Interior.
Includes among review standards determinations as to: whether the regulations are within the scope of authority of the underlying statutes, in accordance with original congressional intent, and subject to judicial review; and economic costs and benefits.
