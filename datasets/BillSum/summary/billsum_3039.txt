Common Sense Budget Act of 2007 - Requires certain reductions in amounts appropriated for FY2008 for specified Department of Defense (DOD) and Department of Energy (DOE) programs.
Makes amounts from such reductions available for: modernization of school facilities; the State Children's Health Insurance Program (SCHIP) under title XXI of the Social Security Act; adult and dislocated worker employment and training activities; programs to increase energy efficiency and conservation and increase investment in sustainable and renewable energy alternatives; sustainable development and humanitarian assistance programs to alleviate global poverty, illiteracy, unemployment, disease, and disaster; homeland security safeguard improvements; deficit reduction; and veterans health care.
