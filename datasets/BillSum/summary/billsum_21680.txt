Forest Service Financial Accountability Restoration Act of 1999 - Places specified limits on Forest Service appropriations until the earlier of the following: the Service is no longer an agency at high risk of waste, fraud, and mismanagement due to accounting and financial reporting weaknesses; or the end of the fifth fiscal year subject to such limitations.
Enumerates the criteria under which the Comptroller General shall certify to Congress that the Service is no longer at high risk Sets forth Service backlog reduction requirements.
