Priorities in Education Spending Act - Repeals specified provisions of the: Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965; Early Learning Opportunities Act; Higher Education Act of 1965; Higher Education Amendments of 1998; Education of the Deaf Act of 1986; Higher Education Opportunity Act; Stevenson-Wydler Technology Innovation Act of 1980; Higher Education Amendments of 1992; Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968; Carl D Perkins Career and Technical Education Act of 2006; Special Olympics Sport and Empowerment Act of 2004; Head Start Act; Workforce Investment Act; National Environmental Education Act; and America COMPETES Act.
Prohibits the Secretary of Education from obligating any funds to implement a literacy program for prisoners under the National Literacy Act of 1991.
