Medicaid Expansion Incentive Act of 2017 This bill amends title XIX (Medicaid) of the Social Security Act to provide additional federal Medicaid funding to states&nbsp;participating in&nbsp;Medicaid expansion under the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act.
The amount of additional funding shall be&nbsp;based upon the net reduction in federal funding&nbsp;for nonparticipating states.
The Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services must publish annually,&nbsp;with respect to&nbsp;each nonparticipating state: the amount of federal funding forgone by the state as a result of its nonparticipation, and the number of individuals who would have gained coverage had the state participated.
