Campaign Cost Reduction and Reform Act of 1993 - Amends the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to revise the definition of: qualifying House of Representatives candidate; contribution; expenditure; and independent expenditure.
Limits with regard to an election cycle of an House of Representatives candidate: general and personal funds expenditures; contributions from persons other than individuals, multicandidate political committees (PACs), political party committees, and persons from outside the district; independent expenditures; and individual contributions.
Sets forth penalties for excess contributions.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a tax credit ($100 for an individual, $200 for a joint return) for contributions to House of Representatives candidates with respect to elections in the taxpayer's congressional district of residence.
