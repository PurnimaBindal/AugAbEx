Amends the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to suspend, through December 31, 2000, the duty on: certain calendaring or other rolling machines for rubber; certain shearing machines used to cut metallic tissue; certain machine tools for working wire of iron or steel; certain extruders of a type used for processing rubber; certain machinery for molding, retreading, or otherwise forming uncured, unvulcanized rubber; certain sector mold press machines used for curing or vulcanizing rubber; and certain sawing machines for working cured, vulcanized rubber.
