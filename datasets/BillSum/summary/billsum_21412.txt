Amends the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to establish gender equity requirements and training programs for teachers and other educational personnel (or parents) with respect to: basic program requirements (under chapter 1 of title I for educationally disadvantaged children), innovative projects, schoolwide projects, parental involvement, and program improvement; Even Start progam uses of funds; secondary school programs (for basic skills improvement and dropout prevention and reentry) applications and award of grants; local targeted assistance programs (under chapter 2 educational improvement partnership); Eisenhower Mathematics and Science program school teacher training and national programs; Women's Educational Equity personnel training and career education programs; gifted and talented students personnel training; and computer-based instruction personnel training under the Secretary's fund for innovation in education.
