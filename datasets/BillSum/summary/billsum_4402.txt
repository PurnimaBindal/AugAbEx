Civil War Sesquicentennial Commission Act of 2002 - Establishes a Civil War Sesquicentennial Commission to plan, develop, and carry out programs and activities appropriate to commemorate the sesquicentennial of the Civil War.Requires the Commission to award a grant in each of FY 2003 through 2015 to: the United States Reports Civil War Center at Louisiana State University, Louisiana; and the Civil War Institute at Gettysburg College, Pennsylvania.
