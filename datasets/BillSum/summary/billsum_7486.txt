NOAA Chesapeake Bay Office Reauthorization Act of 2001 - Amends the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Authorization Act of 1992 to: rename the Chesapeake Bay Estuarine Resources Office as the Chesapeake Bay Office (the Office); require that the Office be administered by the Office of the Under Secretary of Commerce for Oceans and Atmosphere; revise the functions of the Office; require a five-year study concerning living resources of the Chesapeake Bay and a multiple species management strategy for the Chesapeake Bay; require a community-based fishery and habitat restoration small grants and technical assistance program in the Chesapeake Bay watershed; establish a coastal prediction center for the Chesapeake Bay; and establish the Chesapeake Bay Environmental Education Program.
