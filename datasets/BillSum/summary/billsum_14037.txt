Fair Elections Act - Establishes the Fair Elections Commission to study and recommend reforms in the laws relating to elections for Congress.
Sets forth as the primary objectives of the Commission: encouraging fair and open congressional elections that provide voters with meaningful information about candidates and issues; eliminating the disproportionate influence of special interest financing of congressional elections; and creating a system in which incumbent Members of Congress do not possess an inherent advantage over challengers.
Sets forth fast-track procedures for consideration of such reform legislation.
Authorizes appropriations.
