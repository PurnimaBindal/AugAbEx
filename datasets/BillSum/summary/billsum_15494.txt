Amends the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to provide duty-free treatment of previously imported goods for which a duty was paid if they are: exported within three years after the date of such previous importation; sold for exportation and exported to individuals for personal use; reimported without having been advanced in value or improved in condition by any process of manufacture or other means while abroad; reimported as personal returns from those individuals, whether or not consolidated with other personal returns prior to reimportation; and reimported by or for the account of the person who exported them from the United States within one year of such exportation.
