Universal Savings Account Act This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow for the establishment of Universal Savings Accounts.
These Accounts shall be tax-exempt and may be opened by any individual who is at least 18 years of age and a US citizen or legal permanent resident.
Contributions to these Accounts must be in cash and may not exceed $5,500 (adjusted annually for inflation) in any calendar year.
Distributions from a Universal Savings Account are excluded from the gross income of the account holder for income tax purposes.
