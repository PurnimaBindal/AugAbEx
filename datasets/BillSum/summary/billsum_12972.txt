Grant's Tomb National Monument Act of 1997 - Redesignates General Grant National Memorial located at Riverside Drive and West 122d Street, New York, New York, as Grant's Tomb National Monument.
Directs the Secretary of the Interior to: maintain and promote the Monument in accordance with provisions applicable to National Park System units; construct a visitors center; and acquire from New York City non-Federal lands located within the Monument.
Requires the Secretary to establish a study commission to: review security and maintenance at the Monument; plan for interpretive programs and the complete restoration of the Monument; monitor and report to the Secretary and the Congress on the progress of repairs to the tomb; and report to the Secretary who must report to the Congress a final plan for projects to be fully completed by April 27, 1999.
