Technology Retraining And Investment Now Act of 2004 (TRAIN Act of 2004) - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow businesses and individuals a tax credit for 50 percent of their information and communications technology education and training expenses up to $8,000 for a taxable year.
Increases the allowable amount of the credit to $10,000 for expenses for a program operated: in an empowerment zone, enterprise community, or renewal community; in a school district in which at least 50 percent of the students are eligible for free or reduced-cost lunches under the National School Lunch Act; in an area designated as a disaster area under the Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act; in a designated rural enterprise community; in an area designated as a Rural Economic Area Partnership Zone; in an area over which an Indian tribal government has jurisdiction; by an employer who has 200 or fewer employees for each business day in each of 20 or more calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year; or for an individual with a disability.
Defines "information technology education and training program expenses" to include: course work: certification testing; apprenticeship programs registered by the Department of Labor; and other expenses that are essential to assessing skill acquisition.
Redefines" eligible educational institution" to include a commercial information and communications technology training provider.
