Amends the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to allow a military reservist who is ordered to active duty for a period of more than 90 days: an exemption during the period of active duty from payment of rent on a primary residence occupied by such member or dependents; and a deferral of mortgage payments on a principal residence.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow lessors of military reservists granted an exemption from rent payments under this Act a refundable tax credit for the exempted lease amounts.
