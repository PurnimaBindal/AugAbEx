Congressional Commission on Civic Service Act - Establishes the Congressional Commission on Civic Service to make recommendations to Congress for: improving the ability of individuals to serve others; training leaders in public service organizations to better utilize individuals committed to national service and volunteerism as they manage human and fiscal resources; identifying solutions to the barriers that make it difficult for some individuals to volunteer or perform national service; and building on the foundation of current service and volunteer opportunities.
Specifies topics to be addressed by the Commission, including deterrents to service, a mandatory service requirement, and the need for a public service academy.
