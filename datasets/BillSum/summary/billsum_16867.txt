Medical Marijuana Research Act of 2016 This bill amends the Controlled Substances Act to: modify requirements regarding the production of marijuana through the National Institute on Drug Abuse Drug Supply Program; establish, with respect to marijuana research,&nbsp;a new federal registration process&nbsp;that is separate from the process for research involving other schedule I drugs; establish a process for registering persons to manufacture or distribute marijuana for medical research purposes; and modify other requirements related to marijuana research and production.
