Congressional Redistricting Formula Act - Requires any congressional redistricting conducted by a state after an apportionment of Representatives to be conducted in accordance with a plan which: meets certain standards; and is enacted in accordance with specified public notice requirements.
Sets standards regarding: equal population for each congressional district in a state; contiguity of territory; consistency with the Voting Rights Act of 1965; nondilution of the voting strength of any person or group, including any political party; avoidance of fragmenting local government units; and compactness of districts.
