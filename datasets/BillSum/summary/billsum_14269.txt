After School Education and Safety Act of 1997 - Authorizes the Secretary of Education to award after-school education and safety program grants to schools to carry out at least two of the following activities: mentoring programs; academic assistance; recreational activities; and technology training.
Allows each school also to carry out any of the following activities: drug, alcohol, and gang prevention activities; health and nutrition counseling; and job skills preparation activities.
Requires the school to provide such grant-assisted activities: only after regular school hours during the school year; in a manner that reflects the specific needs of the population, students, and community to be served; and in a school building or other public facility designated by the school.
Authorizes appropriations.
