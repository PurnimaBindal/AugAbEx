Prevent Iran from Acquiring Nuclear Weapons and Stop War Through Diplomacy Act - Directs the President to appoint a high-level US representative or special envoy who shall: seek to ease tensions and normalize relations between the United States and Iran, lead US diplomatic efforts with regard to Iran, and act as liaison with US and international intelligence agencies.
Directs the Secretary of State to: rescind the no contact policy with Iran, and establish an office in the Department of State to support the work of the representative or special envoy.
