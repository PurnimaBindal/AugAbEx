Childcare Accountability and Responsibility Act of 2012 or the CARE for Kids Act of 2012 - Amends the Child Care and Development Block Grant Act of 1990 to require a national criminal background check for an individual who is a child care staff member, a family child care provider, or an adult who resides in the home of a family child care provider in any state that receives funds from the Child Care and Development Block Grant Program.
Requires that such background check include: a search of the national Sex Offender Registry, the National Crime Information Center, state criminal registries, and state-based abuse and neglect registries and databases; and a Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) fingerprint check.
Makes a child care provider ineligible for Program funds if the criminal background check reveals an adult felony conviction of any such individual for: child abuse or neglect; spousal abuse; a crime against children (including child pornography); a violent crime; or distribution, possession with intent to distribute, or importation of a controlled substance committed within the previous five years.
Allows: a child care provider or an individual subject to a background check to appeal the results to the appropriate designated state agency, and a state to collect a fee from providers for such background checks to defray costs.
