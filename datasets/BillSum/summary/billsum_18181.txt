Jobs and Opportunity Bonus Tax Credit Act of 2014 or the JOB Tax Credit Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow&nbsp;employers who employ not more than 500 full-time employees during the taxable year a business-related tax credit equal to the lesser of: 50% of the job training program&nbsp;expenditures for a full-time&nbsp;employee participating in a qualified training program, or $5,000.
Terminates such credit after 2017.
