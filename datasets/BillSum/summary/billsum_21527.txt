Americans Giving care to Elders (AGE) Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow caregivers a tax credit for up to $6,000 of the eldercare expenses incurred for their parents (or ancestors of such parents).
Amends the Older Americans Act of 1965 to: increase funding for the National Family Caregiver Support Program through FY2011; and establish a National Resource Center on Family Caregiving to provide information on and support for family caregiver support programs.
