Amends the Internal Revenue Code to apply, with limitations, an estate tax credit equivalent to the limited marital deduction to a decedent in a case in which, as of the date of the decedent's death: both the decedent and the surviving spouse were noncitizens of, and not lawful permanent residents of, the United States; and either the decedent or his or her surviving spouse was a qualified international organization employee.
Defines a qualified international organization employee as a full-time employee of an international organization whose principal place of employment with such organization is in the United States.
