Education in Vocational Technology Act - Authorizes the Secretary of Education to make grants to States to assist local educational agencies in: improving the quality of or developing instruction and training in manufacturing and other vocational technologies; and purchasing state-of-the-art equipment for technical vocational education.
Requires State Boards of Vocational Education to distribute at least 90 percent of such amounts to local education agencies, equitably among urban and rural areas.
Authorizes appropriations.
