Federal Surface Transportation Policy and Planning Act of 2009 - Declares it is US policy to develop a national surface transportation system that advances the national interest and defense, interstate and foreign commerce, the efficient and safe interstate mobility of people and goods, and the protection of the environment.
Sets forth certain US policy objectives and goals, including to: reduce national per capita motor vehicle miles traveled annually; reduce national surface transportation-generated carbon dioxide levels by 40% by 2030; reduce national surface transportation (including passenger and freight) delays and congestion at US points of entry; increase the total usage of public transportation, intercity passenger rail services, and non-motorized transportation; and ensure adequate transportation of domestic energy supplies.
Directs the Secretary of Transportation to: develop performance criteria and data collections systems to evaluate the effectiveness of federal surface transportation programs; implement such programs to meet the policy, objectives, goals, and performance criteria established by this Act; and develop and implement a National Surface Transportation Performance Plan.
Authorizes the Secretary to amend the goals established under this Act.
