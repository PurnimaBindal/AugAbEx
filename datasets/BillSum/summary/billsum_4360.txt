Immigration Document Fraud Prevention Act of 1993 - Provides for: the replacement of current green cards with new counterfeit-resistant identification cards (which shall not be considered national identity cards) for all permanent resident aliens eligible to work in the United States; a national program to educate employers about their responsibilities under the Immigration and Nationality Act and the uses of such cards; and a demonstration program to determine the feasibility of a computerized telephone worker verification system for employers.
Increases immigration fraud penalties.
