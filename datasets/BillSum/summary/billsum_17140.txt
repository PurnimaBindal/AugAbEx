Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise the tax credit for first-time homebuyers by: eliminating the first-time homebuyer requirement (thus making such credit available to all homebuyers); eliminating the repayment requirement for such credit except for resales of a principal residence, or failure to occupy such residence, at any time within 36 months of purchase; exempting from the repayment requirement members of the Armed Forces who are ordered to relocate; modifying the maximum amount of such credit and the adjusted gross income thresholds for reductions in the credit amount; and allowing taxpayers to transfer their anticipated credit amount to another individual for specified purposes, including making a downpayment on a portion of a purchase price of a principal residence.
