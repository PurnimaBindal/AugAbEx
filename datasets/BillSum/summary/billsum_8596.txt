Work Opportunity Credit Improvements Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: extend through 2014 the work opportunity tax credit, allow employers to certify certain employees as members of a targeted group for purposes of such credit in lieu of obtaining certification from a state employment security agency, expand the definition of "qualified veteran" for purposes of such credit to include any recently discharged veteran and any veteran receiving specified benefits, and allow such credit for the hiring of high-risk youth who begin work during the period of 2009 through 2014.
Directs the Department of Defense (DOD) or the National Guard, as applicable, to provide individuals discharged or released from the Armed Forces or the National Guard with information and documentation necessary for qualifying for the work opportunity tax credit as a recently discharged veteran.
