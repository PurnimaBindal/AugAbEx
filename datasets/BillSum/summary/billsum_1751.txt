Elder Justice Reauthorization Act This bill&nbsp;amends title XX (Block Grants to States for Social Services) of&nbsp;the Social Security Act to&nbsp;reauthorize through FY2021 grants to states for&nbsp;activities related to the prevention and detection of elder abuse.
In addition, the&nbsp;bill amends the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to reauthorize through FY2021: the National Training Institute for federal and state surveyors of long-term care facilities, and grants to state agencies that perform surveys of certain nursing facilities.
