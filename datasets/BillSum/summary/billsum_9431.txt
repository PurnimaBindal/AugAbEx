National Commission for Science and Mathematics Leadership Act - Establishes the National Commission for Science and Mathematics Leadership.
Directs the National Science Foundation to: pay Commission expenses up to a specified amount; and provide administrative support for the Commission.
Requires the Commission, in coordination with the National Academy of Sciences, to: review the status of science and mathematics training in the United States relative to international competitors; formulate an implementation proposal to assure world class achievement in mathematics eduation in the United States; propose professional development priorities to strengthen science and mathematics teaching at all levels; and report findings and recommendations to specified congressional committees.
