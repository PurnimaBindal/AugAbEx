Kansas Flood Recovery and Economic Opportunity Act of 2007 - Establishes the federal share of assistance for the state of Kansas under the Robert T Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act for the storms and flooding in that state between June 26, 2007, and July 25, 2007, at 100% of eligible costs.
Extends to certain areas in Kansas declared by the President as major disaster areas under the Robert T Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act (FEMA-1711-DR, as in effect on the date of enactment of this Act) by reason of severe storms and flooding beginning on June 26, 2007, provisions of the Internal Revenue Code allowing: suspension of certain limitations on personal casualty losses; an extension of the period for replacing damaged property without recognizing gain; an employee retention tax credit for affected businesses through 2007; bonus depreciation for certain property acquired after June 26, 2007; increased expensing of small business assets and of demolition and cleanup costs; an increase in the tax credit for rehabilitation expenditures; extended net operating loss carryback periods for losses attributable to storms and for public utility property disaster losses; relaxed income verification requirements for tenants in low-income rental projects; and penalty-free withdrawals and loans from individual retirement accounts and other tax-exempt pension plans.
