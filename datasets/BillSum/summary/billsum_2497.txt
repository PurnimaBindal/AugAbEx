Expanding Capacity for Health Outcomes Act or the ECHO Act (Sec.
This bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) to report on technology-enabled collaborative learning and capacity building models, which connect specialists to primary care providers through videoconferencing to facilitate case-based learning, dissemination of best practices, and evaluation of outcomes.
The report must include: an analysis of the use, integration, and impact of such models; a list of such models recently funded by HHS; recommendations to reduce barriers to adoption of such models; opportunities for adoption of such models into HHS programs; and recommendations regarding the role of such models in continuing medical education.
