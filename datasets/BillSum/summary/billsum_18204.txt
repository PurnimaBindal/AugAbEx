HSA Improvement and Expansion Act of 2007 - Amends Internal Revenue Code provisions relating to health savings accounts (HSAs) to: allow HSAs to incorporate flexible spending and health reimbursement arrangements; increase the annual HSA contribution limitation; permit the use of HSAs to purchase health insurance; allow the payment of certain medical expenses incurred before the establishment of an HSA; allow veterans eligible for service-connected disability benefits to establish an HSA; and allow spouses to make increased catch-up contributions to a single HSA.
