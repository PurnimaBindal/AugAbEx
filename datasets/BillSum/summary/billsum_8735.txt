Reciprocal Access to Tibet Act of 2014 - Directs the Secretary of State to submit an annual, publicly-available report to Congress containing: an assessment of the level of access Chinese authorities granted US diplomats, journalists, and tourists to Tibetan areas in China; a list of the persons in senior leadership positions in Tibet Autonomous Region and other specified provinces, prefectures, and autonomous entities; and a list of the persons in senior leadership positions at the national and regional levels as defined by this Act.
Makes certain listed persons ineligible for US entry under specified circumstances.
Expresses the sense of Congress that: reciprocity forms the basis of diplomatic law and the practice of mutual exchanges between countries; consular access should be given on a reciprocal basis; and the Secretary, when granting Chinese diplomats access to parts of the United States, should take into account the extent to which China grants US diplomats access to parts of China, including the level of access to Tibetan areas.
