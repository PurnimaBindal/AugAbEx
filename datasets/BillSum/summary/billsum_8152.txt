Emergency Supplemental Appropriations for Disaster Assistance Because of the Los Angeles Earthquake Act of 1994 - Makes emergency supplemental appropriations for FY 1994 for disaster assistance activities resulting from the January 1994 earthquake in Southern California and other disasters.
Makes additional funds available to: the Small Business Administration for the disaster loans program account; the Department of Education for impact aid and student financial assistance; the Department of Transportation for the Federal Highway Administration; the Department of Veterans Affairs for medical care; the Department of Housing and Urban Development for housing programs and community development; the Federal Emergency Management Agency for disaster relief; and the President for unanticipated needs.
