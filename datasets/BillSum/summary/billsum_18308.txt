Health Quality and Fairness Act of 1998 - Amends title XXVII (Assuring Portability, Availability, and Renewability of Health Insurance Coverage) of the Public Health Service Act to establish a new part (Patient Protection Standards) which sets forth health plan standards concerning: notice; coverage; access to emergency care; the doctor-patient relationship; quality assurance; designation of a primary care provider; grievance and appeals procedures; and understandability of information.
