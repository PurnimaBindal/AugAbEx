Climate Change Investment Act of 2006 - Repeals provisions of the Internal Revenue Code allowing: an election to expense the cost of certain liquid fuel processing refineries; accelerated amortization of geological and geophysical expenditures; a tax credit for enhanced oil recovery costs; a tax credit for the production of low sulfur diesel fuel; a tax credit for producing fuel from a nonconventional source; a tax deduction for capital costs incurred in complying with certain sulfur regulations; a tax deduction for intangible drilling and development costs for oil and gas wells and geothermal wells; and tax deductions for certain oil and gas well expenditures.
Allows a business-related tax credit for investment in a greenhouse gas intensity reduction project approved by the Secretary of Energy.
