Small Business Tax Fairness and Simplification Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: allow self-employed individuals to participate in cafeteria pension plans; allow long-term care insurance under cafeteria plans and flexible spending arrangements; allow accelerated amortization of certain intangible assets (e.g., good will) acquired from a small business; increase the tax exclusion of gain from the sale of certain small business stock; provide a $2,500 standard tax deduction for home business expenses; permit certain small businesses to elect a taxable year ending in a month from April to November; increase the allowable number of S corporation shareholders; and exempt certain small businesses from withholding of tax requirements for payments made by government entities.
