Federal Asset Management Improvement Act of 2001 - Directs the: Administrator of General Services to establish performance measures to determine the effectiveness of Federal property management; head of each executive agency to monitor their agency's performance against such measures and report results to Congress; and Administrator to submit to Congress a Federal property management plan.Amends the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949 to authorize the Administrator to enter into agreements for the creation of one or more public-private partnerships to: lease Federal real property; and develop, rehabilitate, or renovate facilities on such property for use by executive agencies.
Requires the Administrator to prepare and submit to Congress at least 30 days before entering into such an agreement a business plan regarding each agreement.
