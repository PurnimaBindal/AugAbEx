Boot Camp Prison Act of 1993 - Directs the Attorney General to establish within the Bureau of Prisons ten military-style boot camp prisons on closed military installations to provide: a highly regimented schedule of strict discipline, physical training, work, drill, and ceremony characteristic of military basic training; remedial education; counseling as appropriate; and treatment for substance abuse.
Sets forth provisions regarding: capacity of such camps; eligibility requirements for Federal and State prisoners; post-release supervision (including aftercare, with provision for educational and vocational training and drug or other counseling and treatment where appropriate); and termination of the program.
Directs the Attorney General to evaluate the boot camp prisons and report to the Congress on their performance, including an assessment of the rate of recidivism of boot camp prisoners as compared with similar defendants in conventional prisons, the cost effectiveness of boot camp prisons as compared to conventional prisons, and the program's effect on the overcrowding of conventional prisons.
Authorizes appropriations.
