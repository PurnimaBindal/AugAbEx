Child Care Lending Pilot Act of 2009 - Amends the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 to allow proceeds of loans made through the Small Business Administration (SBA) to be used by certified local development companies to assist a small, nonprofit child care business if: the loan is used for a sound business purpose approved by the SBA; each business meets eligibility requirements applicable to for-profit businesses; one or more individuals has personally guaranteed the loan; each business has clear title to the collateral for the loan; and each business has sufficient cash flow to meet loan obligations and reasonable operating expenses.
Prohibits more than 7% of the total number of loans guaranteed in any fiscal year for certified development companies from being awarded under such program.
