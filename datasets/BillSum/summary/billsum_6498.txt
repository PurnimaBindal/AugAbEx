Securities and Exchange Commission Fee Reduction Act of 1996 - Amends the Securities Act of 1933 to: prohibit the minimum required registration fee from exceeding $100 (current law prohibits the minimum fee from being less than $100); and set forth a schedule for general revenue fees and offsetting collection fees in diminishing amounts through FY 2001.
(Sec.
Amends the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to revise the transaction fee schedule for: exchange-traded securities; off-exchange trades of exchange-registered securities; off-exchange trades of last-sale-reported securities.
Declares that such fees are in lieu of, and not in addition to, any fees that the Securities and Exchange Commission is authorized to impose or collect.
