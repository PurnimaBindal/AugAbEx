Congressional Commission on Expanding Social Service Delivery Options Act - Establishes a Congressional Commission on Expanding Social Service Delivery Options.
Directs the Commission to: study all matters relating to increasing beneficiary-selected or beneficiary-directed options for social service delivery (delivery options) in federal social service programs, including certificate, scholarship, voucher, or other forms of indirect delivery; review all relevant existing programs, including initiatives of the Corporation for National and Community Service; and determine and recommend program areas for which it is appropriate and feasible to implement such delivery options fully or partially.
Requires the Commission to promote goals of: expanding consumer and beneficiary choice in such programs; maximizing use of governmental resources in them; and minimizing concerns relating to any precedent under the Constitution regarding participation in them of faith-based providers.
