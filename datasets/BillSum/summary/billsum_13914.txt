Preserving Religious Freedom and a Woman's Access to Contraception Act - Prohibits employers from restricting employees from purchasing any drug or medical device regulated by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA).
Repeals sections of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act and the Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010 to: allow health savings accounts, medical savings accounts, and health flexible spending accounts to be used to pay for non-prescription drugs; and remove the annual limit for salary reduction contributions to a health flexible spending arrangement under a cafeteria plan.
