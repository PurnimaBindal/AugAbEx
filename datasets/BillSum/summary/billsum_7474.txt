Star-Spangled Banner National Historic Trail Study Act of 1999 - Amends the National Trails System Act to require study for potential addition to the national trails system of the Star-Spangled Banner National Historic Trail (the route of the War of 1812 British invasion of Maryland and Washington, DC, and the route of the American defense to victory at Fort McHenry on September 14, 1814).
Requires the study to be done in coordination with certain other congressionally-mandated studies.
Sets a two-year deadline for completion and transmittal to specified congressional committees.
