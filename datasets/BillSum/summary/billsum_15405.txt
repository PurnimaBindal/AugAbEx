Directs the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to: establish and operate centers for traumatic brain injury (TBI) research, education, and clinical activities; ensure the geographic distribution of such centers; and designate no more than five centers.
Requires the: official within the Veterans Health Administration (VHA) responsible for TBI care to establish a peer review panel to assess the scientific and clinical merit of proposals submitted for the designation of such centers; and Under Secretary of Health to ensure the dissemination throughout the VHA of information produced through the research, education, and clinical activities of the centers.
