Clean and Green Renewable Energy Tax Credit Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: extend through 2030 the tax credits for investment in solar energy and qualified fuel cell property; modify the percentage rate for the energy tax credit; extend through 2016 the tax credit for residential energy efficient property expenditures; allow accelerated depreciation of solar energy and fuel cell property; extend through 2013 the tax credit for electricity produced from certain renewable resources; allow an energy tax credit and a residential energy efficient property tax credit for certain equipment which uses wind to generate energy; and extend through 2009 the tax credit for nonbusiness energy property expenditures.
