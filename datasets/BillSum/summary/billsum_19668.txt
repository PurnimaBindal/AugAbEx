Nuclear Non-Proliferation Policy Act of 1993 - Declares that, in order to end nuclear proliferation and reduce current nuclear arsenals and supplies of weapons-usable nuclear materials, it shall be US policy to pursue the following objectives: encourage the Ukraine to ratify the START I treaty and Ukraine and Kazakhstan to vote to accede to the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty as non-nuclear weapon states; encourage Belarus, Ukraine, and Kazakhstan to remove all nuclear weapons from their territory, accept International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) safeguards over nuclear facilities, and implement effective controls on nuclear exports; reach an agreement with the Russian Federation to deactivate weapons to be withdrawn under START I and II, place all fissile material from weapons under bilateral or international controls, and arrange for inspections and data exchanges; prepare for the ratification of START II by seeking the exchange of information; conclude a multilateral comprehensive nuclear test ban treaty by early 1995; ratify START II in the United States and encourage the Russian Federation to do the same; conclude multilateral agreements to reduce nuclear arsenals; reach agreement with the Russian Federation to halt the production of fissile material for weapons purposes and other worldwide agreements respecting such materials and the placement of all nuclear facilities under IAEA safeguards; strengthen IAEA safeguards and nuclear export controls; reduce incentives for countries to pursue the acquisition of nuclear weapons by seeking to reduce regional tensions; support the extension of the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty at the 1995 conference; adopt a US policy of "no first use" of nuclear weapons, reach agreement with other nuclear weapon states to adopt such a policy, and assist any country which is a party to the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty should weapons be initiated against such country; conclude an agreement with the Russian Federation to dismantle all tactical nuclear weapons; and sign the appropriate protocols to the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone Treaty.
