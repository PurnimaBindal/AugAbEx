San Francisco Bay Shipping and Fisheries Enhancement Act of 1997 - Requires: a navigation project in San Francisco Bay, California, to remove underwater hazards to navigation near Alcatraz Island; modification of navigation lanes and rerouting of vessel traffic to improve safety and efficiency; and a survey of vessels owned by, or operated under contract for, the Maritime Administration for risks for oil spills or other hazards to human health or the environment.
Authorizes appropriations.
