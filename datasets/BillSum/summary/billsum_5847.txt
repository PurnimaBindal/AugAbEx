Declares the sense of Congress that nothing in this Act should: directly or indirectly cause a net decrease in total funds received by the Department of Transportation for Operation Lifesaver below the level that would otherwise have been received but for enactment of this Act; or affect regular first-class rates of postage or any other regular rates of postage.
Requires the Comptroller General to report to Congress: an evaluation of the effectiveness and the appropriateness of the authority provided by this Act as a means of fundraising; and a description of the monetary and other resources required of the Postal Service in carrying it out.
