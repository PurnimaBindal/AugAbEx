Lower Rio Grande Valley Water Resources Conservation and Improvement Act of 2002 - Amends the Lower Rio Grande Valley Water Resources Conservation and Improvement Act of 2000 to: authorize the construction of 20 additional specified projects in Texas; require the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Bureau of Reclamation, to undertake the Lower Rio Grande Water Conservation and Improvement Program through cooperative agreements; require that project proposals be reviewed and evaluated under the guidelines set forth in a specified Bureau of Reclamation document; require the Secretary to determine that a non-Federal project sponsor is financially capable of funding the non-Federal share of the project's costs, including operation, maintenance, repair, and replacement, and whether the project meets the guidelines; permit project sponsors to contract with the Secretary to have the Secretary prepare the reports required under the Act (at a Federal cost share not to exceed 50 percent); increase the authorization of appropriations for carrying out the Program; and limit the non-Federal share of the total cost of any project carried out under or with assistance provided under the Act to 50 percent.
