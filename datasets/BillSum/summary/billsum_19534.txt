Hudson-Fulton-Champlain Commemoration Commission Act of 2002 - Establishes the Hudson-Fulton-Champlain Commemoration Commission to plan, develop, and execute programs and activities appropriate to commemorate in 2009 the anniversaries of the voyages of Henry Hudson (the first European to sail up the Hudson River in the vessel Half Moon), Robert Fulton (who navigated the Hudson River in the steamboat Clermont, successfully inaugurating steam navigation on a commercial basis), and Samuel de Champlain (the first European to discover and explore Lake Champlain).
