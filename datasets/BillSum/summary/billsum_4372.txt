Community Access Preservation Act or the CAP Act - Amends the Communications Act of 1934 to require, with a specified waiver authority, that public, educational, and government (PEG) channels be: carried in the same channel capacity as local commercial television channels, or if no such stations are required to be carried, in the same channel capacity as network-affiliated commercial television stations carried on cable; and provided to every cable subscriber without additional service or equipment charges.
Requires a cable operator to: carry PEG signals to subscribers without material degradation and without altering content; and provide facilities adequate to fulfill such requirements.
Requires cable operators in states that have adopted statewide franchising to: make PEG support payments equal to the greater of the cash payment required under state law or the value of historically-provided PEG support; carry PEG signals from the point of origin to subscribers; and provide at least the number of channels for PEG use provided as of May 31, 2005.
