Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a deduction for contributions made by an individual who is younger than the age of 40 to an individual retirement plan designated as a Retirement Years Savings Account (RYS).
Prohibits any rollover contribution to an RYS account unless: such contribution is from another RYS account; or such contribution is from another individual retirement plan (other than an RYS account) and is made before January 1998.
