Student Financial Aid Data Privacy Protection Act - Amends the Higher Education Act of 1965 to direct the Secretary of Education to take actions necessary to maintain confidence in the National Student Loan Data System, at a minimum: ensuring that guaranty agencies, lenders, and schools primarily access it for legitimate program operations; prohibiting nongovernmental researchers or policy analysts from accessing personally identifiable information; creating a disclosure form for actual and potential students describing the contents of, and access to, the system; requiring guaranty agencies, lenders, and schools to inform borrowers of Federal Family Education Loans, Direct Loans, and Perkins Loans that such a loan will be submitted to the system and accessible to such entities; regularly review the system to delete inactive users, monitor use, and ensure that data is not used for marketing purposes; and developing standardized protocols for limiting access.
Requires the Secretary to study and report to Congress on: mechanisms giving borrowers the option of restricting lender access to their system records; and appropriate risk-based protocols for limiting access.
