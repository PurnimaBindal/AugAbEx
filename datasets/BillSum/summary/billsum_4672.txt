Fusion Energy Sciences Act of 2001 - Instructs the Secretary of Energy to: develop a plan for US construction of a magnetic fusion burning plasma experiment; request a plan review for Congress by the National Academy of Sciences; and develop and transmit to Congress a plan for the Fusion Energy Sciences Program and for the magnetic fusion burning plasma experiment.Authorizes the Secretary to develop a plan for US participation in an international burning plasma experiment: whose construction is highly likely; and where Federal participation is cost effective relative to the cost and scientific benefits of a domestic experiment.
