Qualifying Renewable Chemical Production or Investment Tax Credit Act of 2015 This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a business-related tax credit for the production of renewable chemicals.
The bill defines &quot;renewable chemical&quot; as any chemical that: is produced in the United States from renewable biomass; is sold or used for the production of chemical products, polymers, plastics, or formulated products or as chemicals, polymers, plastics, or formulated products; has a biobased content of 95% or higher; is the product of, or reliant upon, biological or thermal conversion of renewable biomass; is not sold or used for the production of any food, feed, or fuel; and is not a combination of certain specified renewable chemicals.
The bill also allows a tax credit for investment in renewable chemical production facilities.
The bill requires the Department of the Treasury to establish a program to allocate renewable chemical tax credit amounts to eligible taxpayers and imposes an aggregate limit on the amount of credits that may be allocated to not more than $500 million during the 5-year period after enactment of this Act.
