Homeowner Disaster Relief Act of 2014 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to exempt any qualified natural disaster distribution from the 10% penalty on premature distributions from tax-exempt retirement plans.
Defines "qualified natural diasaster distribution" as a distribution from a retirement plan to an individual if: such individual sustained an economic loss due to a federally-declared disaster, the principal place of abode of such individual on the&nbsp;disaster declaration date is in the disaster area, and such distribution is made during the one-year period beginning on the disaster declaration date.
