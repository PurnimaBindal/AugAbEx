Medicare Medication Therapy Management Services Coverage Act of 2002 - Amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act to provide for Medicare coverage of medication therapy management services for certain patients at high risk for potential medication problems, including: beneficiaries taking multiple medications; beneficiaries with complex or chronic medical conditions; and individuals receiving medication for the treatment of asthma or diabetes, lipid reducing medication, anti-coagulation medication, or medication for other chronic diseases the Secretary may specify.
