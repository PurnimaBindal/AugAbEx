Appropriates funds for the Account.
Prohibits such appropriation from being considered as direct spending for purposes of pay-as-you-go provisions of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 (Gramm-Rudman-Hollings Act)Reduces the public debt limit by the amount appropriated into the Account.Bars Account receipts and disbursements from being counted as new budget authority, outlays, receipts, or deficit or surplus for purposes of : the Federal Government budget as submitted by the President; the congressional budget; or the Gramm-Rudman-Hollings Act.Requires the Secretary to report to specified congressional committees on the Account.
