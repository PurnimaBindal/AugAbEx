Strengthening the Visa Waiver Program to Secure America Act - Amends the Immigration and Nationality Act regarding the visa waiver program to: require current and new program countries to report on lost or stolen visas in order to participate in the program; set a maximum 2% visa overstay rate for program countries; require a reevaluation of program countries within one year; and include arrival data in the required air entry and exit system and electronic travel authorization system.
