1B Visa Program Modernization Act of 2007 - Amends the Immigration and Nationality Act to increase the annual H-1B nonimmigrant visa (specialty occupation) cap, with a 20% increase for the following year if the previous year's quota is reached.
Revises H-1B provisions with respect to: application fraud and misrepresentation; employer penalties; Department of Labor investigations; Department of Labor and Department of Homeland Security (DHS) information sharing; information provided to an H-1B nonimmigrant upon visa issuance; employment advertising; and prohibiting an employer of fewer than 50 employees in the United States from having more than 50% H-1B nonimmigrant employees.
