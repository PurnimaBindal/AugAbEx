Build, Update, Improve, Lift, and Design Health Centers Act of 2007 or the BUILD Act  Amends the Public Health Service Act to require the Secretary of Health and Human Services to award grants to health centers to: modernize, expand, and replace existing facilities; and construct new facilities at such centers.
Requires the Secretary to establish a loan guarantee program for loans to health centers for capital need projects, including: acquiring, leasing, modernizing, expanding, or replacing existing facilities; constructing new facilities; purchasing or leasing equipment; and the costs of refinancing loans made for any such projects.
