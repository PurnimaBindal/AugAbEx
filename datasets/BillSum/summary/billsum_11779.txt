Emigrant Wilderness Preservation Act of 2001 - Directs the Secretary of Agriculture, with respect to the Emigrant Wilderness in the Stanislaus National Forest, California, to enter into an agreement with a non-Federal entity (non-profit or private entity, or the State of California or political subdivision) to retain, maintain, and operate at private expense 12 specified water impoundment structures.
Authorizes the Secretary, with the consent of California and the other agreeing party, to include up to six additional, similarly-located structures within such agreement.Authorizes appropriations.
