Directs the Secretary of State to designate as a foreign terrorist organization each of the following: the Arellano Felix Organization, the Los Zetas Cartel, the Juarez Cartel, the Beltran Leyva Organization, la Familia Michoacana, the Sinaloa Cartel, and the Gulf Cartel/New Federation.
Directs the Secretary to report on Department of State activities to assist Mexico with drug cartel violence, including programs under the Merida Initiative, training programs, and equipment.
