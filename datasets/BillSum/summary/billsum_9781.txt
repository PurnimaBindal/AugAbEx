Title II: Additional Improvements - Delays from July to November 2000 the deadline for withdrawing the offer, rescinding the withdrawal, of Medicare+Choice plans for 2001.
Amends SSA title XVIII part C to revise data collection requirements under the Medicare+Choice program with regard to: issuance of encounter data submission; post-stabilization guidelines; effectiveness of elections and changes of elections; uniform premium and benefits; and Medicare+Choice program compatibility with employer or union group health plans.
