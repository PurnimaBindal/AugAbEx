Directs Congress to establish a nonpartisan independent Department of State Review Panel, which shall report to Congress on its review of the following matters concerning Department of State organization and operations: current Department structures and possible reorganization; changes to ensure effective diplomacy and implementation of US foreign policy; challenges through the year 2020 and beyond; efficient handling of future risks; planning assumptions; positioning necessary for negotiation and conflict deterrence; information infrastructure and technology; the vulnerability of US technology to nontraditional threats, such as information warfare; and future scenarios requiring a Department response.
