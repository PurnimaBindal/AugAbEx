Chile-NAFTA Accession Act - Authorizes the President to enter into: an agreement for the accession of Chile to the North American Free Trade Agreement (NAFTA); or a bilateral agreement between the United States and Chile that reduces and ultimately eliminates tariffs and other nontariff barriers to trade and provides for the eventual establishment of a free trade area.
Applies congressional fast track procedures (no amendments) to implementing bills for trade agreements entered under this Act.
