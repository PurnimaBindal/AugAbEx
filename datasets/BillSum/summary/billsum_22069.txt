Directs the Attorney General to establish a National Task Force on Violence against Women.
Includes among the duties of the task force: considering the reports of past Federal and State task forces or commissions on violent crime, family violence, and crime victims; developing strategies for Federal, State, and local law enforcement to protect women against violent crime and to prosecute and punish those responsible for such crime; evaluating the adequacy of sentencing, incarceration, and release of violent offenders against women and making recommendations to ensure that such offenders receive appropriate punishment; and evaluating the adequacy of the treatment of victims of violent crime against women within the criminal justice system and making recommendations designed to improve such treatment.
Authorizes appropriations.
