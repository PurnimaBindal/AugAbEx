Biodiesel Tax Incentive Reform and Extension Act of 2015 This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to: extend through March 31, 2016, the income tax credit for biodiesel and renewable diesel used as fuel and the excise tax credit for biodiesel fuel mixtures, allow through 2018 a new income and excise tax credit equal to $100 for each gallon of biodiesel produced, and provide for an increased credit for small biodiesel producers.
