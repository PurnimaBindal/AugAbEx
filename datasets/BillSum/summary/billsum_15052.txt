Directs the Secretary of the Interior to transfer certain lands on the Northern Cheyenne Indian Reservation, Montana, to the Lame Deer High School District No.
6, Rosebud County, Montana, for construction of a high school and related facilities.
Stipulates that such lands shall revert to the Secretary (to held in trust for the Northern Cheyenne Tribe) if the high school is not operating within a specified time or ceases operations.
