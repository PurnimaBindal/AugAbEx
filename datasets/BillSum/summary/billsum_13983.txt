Captive Primate Safety Act - Amends the Lacey Act Amendments of 1981 to add nonhuman primates to the definition of "prohibited wildlife species" for purposes of the prohibition against the sale or purchase of such species in interstate or foreign commerce.
Makes it unlawful for a person to import, export, transport, sell, receive, acquire, or purchase a live animal of any prohibited wildlife species in interstate or foreign commerce (i.e., for pet trade purposes).
Sets forth exceptions to such prohibition, including, under certain conditions, for: transporting a nonhuman primate to or from a veterinarian, transporting a nonhuman primate to a legally designated caregiver as a result of the death of the preceding owner, and transporting a single primate of the genus Cebus that was obtained from and trained by a charitable organization to assist a permanently disabled individual with a severe mobility impairment.
Sets forth civil and criminal penalties for violations of the requirements of this Act.
Extends the authorization of appropriations to carry out such prohibition for FY2011-FY2015.
