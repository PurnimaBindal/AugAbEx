Fentanyl and Heroin Task Force Act This bill amends the Controlled Substances Act to establish the Fentanyl and Heroin Inter-Agency Task Force: to coordinate federal efforts to identify, target, and dismantle organizations that traffic fentanyl or heroin; to identify sources of fentanyl and heroin production and distribution; and to provide best practices, updated information, and support to federal, state, and local law enforcement agencies.
