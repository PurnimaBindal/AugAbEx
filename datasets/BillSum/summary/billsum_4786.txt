Indian Dams Safety Act of 1994 - Directs the Secretary of the Interior to: establish a dam safety maintenance program within the Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) in order to maintain each dam on Indian lands in a satisfactory condition; perform necessary repair and rehabilitation work to bring any unsatisfactory dam to satisfactory condition and to then place the dam under the program's maintenance; and develop a maintenance action plan, with priority given to dams with high or significant risk ratings.
Directs the Secretary to develop a comprehensive list of dams on Indian lands describing conditions of: dam safety; risk hazard; and maintenance deficiencies.
Authorizes the Secretary to: obtain technical assistance from other agencies and departments; and contract with appropriate Indian tribes to carry out the program.
States that the dam safety program (of 1980) and the program established by this Act shall be under BIA direction.
Authorizes appropriations.
