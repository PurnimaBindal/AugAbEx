Military Families Act - Directs the Secretary of Homeland Security or the Attorney General to adjust the status of an eligible alien to that of an alien lawfully admitted for permanent residence if the alien: applies for adjustment (with a time limit for an alien applying as a family member of a deceased Armed Forces member); is admissible to the United States as an immigrant; pays the application fee (unless waived); and is physically present in the United States.
Applies such provision to an alien who is: a parent, spouse, child, son, or daughter (and their spouse, child, son, or daughter, if any) of a living Armed Forces member or of a deceased Armed Forces member who died as a result of service-incurred injury or disease; or a son or daughter of a Filipino parent who was naturalized based upon active duty World War II service in the Philippine Army, Philippine Scouts, or a recognized guerilla unit.
Defines "Armed Forces member" as a person who: is, or was at the time of the person's death, a US citizen or lawfully admitted permanent resident; is serving, or has served honorably on or after October 7, 2001, as a member of the National Guard or the Selected Reserve of the Ready Reserve, or in an active-duty status in the US military; and if separated from service was separated under honorable conditions.
Waives specified grounds of inadmissibility and authorizes the waiver of additional grounds of inadmissibility.
