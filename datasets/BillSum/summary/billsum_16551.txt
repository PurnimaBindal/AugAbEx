Small Business Disaster Loan Reporting Act of 2007 - Directs the Administrator of the Small Business Administration (SBA) to: amend the 2006 hurricane season disaster response plan to apply to major disasters; report monthly to the congressional small business committees on the operation of the SBA disaster loan program; provide daily updates during presidentially declared disaster periods; provide reports regarding federal contracts awarded as a result of a major disaster; and report on how the SBA can improve the processing of applications under the disaster loan program.
