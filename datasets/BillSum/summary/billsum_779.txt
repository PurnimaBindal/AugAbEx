Family Dairy Farmer and Rural Community Rescue Act of 2002 - Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to carry out counter-cyclical income support programs for dairy producers in participating and nonparticipating States through 2011.
(Each State to be a participant unless it elects otherwise.)
Limits individual or entity payments to 230,000 pounds of milk per month.Provides with respect to the participating State program for: specified marketing area target prices; five regional supply management districts; specified payment rates; and establishment in the Treasury of a National Dairy Producers Trust Fund.
