Saddleback Mountain-Arizona Settlement Act of 1995 - Approves and ratifies the Settlement Agreement providing for the transfer of certain lands in Scottsdale, Arizona, by the Resolution Trust Corporation to the Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community (to be held in trust by the Department of the Interior) and the City of Scottsdale.
Directs the Corporation to make such land transfer.
Sets forth land use limitations (public use and development property).
