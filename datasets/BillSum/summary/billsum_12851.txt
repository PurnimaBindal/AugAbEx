Physician Assistants Continuity of Care Act of 2007 - Amends title XVIII (Medicare) of the Social Security Act to permit a physician assistant, when delegated by a physician, to: certify that inpatient psychiatric hospital services or post-hospital extended care services are required; establish and review a plan for home health services; and certify a terminal illness with respect to hospice care.
Covers as hospice care any legally authorized services of a physician assistant performed under a physician's supervision.
