Federal Support Payment Act - Amends the Federal judicial code to: provide for the registration and enforcement of child support orders in States outside the State in which the order was made; require notice of such registration and enforcement to be provided to the original court; and grant State courts original jurisdiction of civil actions brought by a citizen of another State to order a citizen of the State in which the court is located to make payments periodically to (or for the support of) such citizen's child if under the law of such State a State court is authorized to make such an order.
