Small Business Tax Relief Act of 2009 - Amends Internal Revenue Code provisions relating to small business to: increase and make permanent the election to expense small business assets, including computer software, in the current taxable year; reduce graduated tax rates for corporations with taxable incomes of less than $10 million; exempt income attributable to business tax credits from the alternative minimum tax (AMT); allow five-year carrybacks for business tax credits and small business net operating losses; allow an enhanced tax deduction for income attributable to small business domestic production activities; reduce to five years the recognition period for the built-in gains of S corporations; exclude from gross income all gain from the sale of certain small business stock before 2011; and allow a deduction from the income of self-employed individuals for health insurance costs.
