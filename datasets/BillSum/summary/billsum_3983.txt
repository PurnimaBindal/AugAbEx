Technology Administration and National Institute of Standards and Technology Act of 2004 - Authorizes appropriations to the Secretary of Commerce for the: Under Secretary for Technology and the Office of Technology Policy's activities; National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) scientific and technical research and services laboratories activities; Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award program; NIST facilities construction and maintenance; NIST industrial technology services; Teacher Science and Technology Enhancement Institute program; and NIST under the Enterprise Integration Act of 2002.
Requires: the Director of NIST to carry out a Standards Education program to award competitive, merit-reviewed grants to institutions of higher education to support their efforts to develop curricula on the role of standards in the fields of engineering, business, science, and economics.
