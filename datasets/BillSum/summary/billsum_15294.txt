Tax Relief Extension Act of 2003 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to extend provisions concerning: parity in the application of certain limits to mental health benefits; the credit for electricity produced from certain renewable resources; the work opportunity credit; the welfare-to-work credit; the taxable income limit on percentage depletion for oil and natural gas produced from marginal properties; qualified zone academy bonds; the cover over tax on distilled spirits; the deduction for corporate donations of computer technology; the credit for qualified electric vehicles; the deduction for clean-fuel vehicles and certain refueling property; the deduction for certain expenses of school teachers; the availability of medical savings accounts; the expensing of environmental remediation costs; the expansion of the work opportunity tax credit to the New York Liberty Zone; the temporary special rules for taxation of life insurance companies; certain tax incentives for investment in the District of Columbia; the combined employment tax reporting program; transfers of excess pension assets to retiree health accounts; and IRS user fees.
Adds to the definition of taxable vaccines any vaccine against hepatitis A and any trivalent vaccine against influenza.
