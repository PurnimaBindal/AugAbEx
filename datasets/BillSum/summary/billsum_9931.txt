S Corporation Modernization Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to revise the tax treatment of S corporations, including by: reducing from 10 to 7 years the period during which S corporation built-in gains are subject to tax; allowing nonresident aliens to qualify as beneficiaries of an electing small business trust (ESBT); repealing mandatory termination of S corporation elections for excessive passive investment income; allowing S corporations to increase passive investment income from 25 to 60% without incurring additional tax; allowing individual retirement accounts to be S corporation shareholders; and allowing ESBTs to claim expanded charitable tax deductions.
Directs the Comptroller General to study and report to Congress on entity neutrality under the Internal Revenue Code with respect to S corporations, limited liability companies, and other pass-through entities.
