Jumpstarting Our Business Sector Act of 2009 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: exclude from gross income in 2009 and 2010 long-term capital gains and dividend income; reduce for a two year period the employment and self-employment tax rates; reduce corporate income tax rates in 2009 and 2010; and reduce the income tax rate for taxpayers in the lowest income tax bracket in 2009 and 2010.
Rescinds all unobligated balances of the discretionary appropriations made available by division A of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (stimulus funds).
