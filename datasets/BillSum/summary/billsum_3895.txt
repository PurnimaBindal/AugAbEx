Commission on the Removal of Mexican-Americans to Mexico This bill establishes the Commission on the Removal of Mexican-Americans to Mexico, which shall: review the facts and circumstances surrounding the 1929-1941 removal of certain US citizens and permanent legal residents to Mexico and the impact of such actions on such individuals, their families, and the Mexican-American community; review federal, state, and local government directives that required such removal and any other related information; and report its findings, conclusions, and any recommendations for legislative actions.
