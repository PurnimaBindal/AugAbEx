Child Protection Act of 1997 - Amends the Violent Crime Control and Law Enforcement Act of 1994 to direct the Attorney General to establish, publicize, and operate a national telephone service by which individuals may gain access to the Federal Bureau of Investigation database that tracks the movement and whereabouts of sex offenders.
Sets forth requirements regarding: information that may not be disclosed; notice to callers of fees, penalties for misuse of such information, and unavailability of the service to persons under age 18; fees for use of the service; limitations on the number of requests; prohibited uses of such information; and civil penalties imposed by the Attorney General of up to $1,000 per violation.
