Pre-Claims Undermine Seniors' Health Act of 2016 or the PUSH Act of 2016 This bill delays the Medicare demonstration for pre-claim review&nbsp;of home health services and requires the Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services (CMS) to report on such pre-claim review.
Specifically, CMS shall delay or suspend the demonstration by the later of: one year after either the bill's enactment or the&nbsp;demonstration's scheduled start-date, as applicable; or six months after CMS submits its report.&nbsp;
