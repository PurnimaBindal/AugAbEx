Education for Life Act of 1998 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to expand permitted withdrawals from education individual retirement accounts to include: qualified elementary and secondary education expenses (including home schooling expenses); and qualified job training expenses.
Increases from: $500 to $2,500 the maximum amount which may annually be contributed to such an account; and 18 to 55 the age until which contributions may be made to a beneficiary's account.
Permits a deduction (for both itemizers and nonitemizers) for such contributions.
