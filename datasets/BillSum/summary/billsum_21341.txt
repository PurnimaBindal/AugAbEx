Amends the United States Grain Standards Act to extend: authority for inspection, weighing, and supervisory fees, supervisory and administrative cost limitations, and the advisory committee; and authorization of appropriations.
Authorizes with respect to export port locations of grain: private entity performance of official inspection and weighing activities; and use of more than one designated person or entity to perform such activities.
(Makes certain geographic boundary provisions inapplicable to designated agencies at export port locations.)
