Teenage Pregnancy Reduction Act of 1999 - Instructs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to arrange for the evaluation of a wide variety of existing adolescent pregnancy prevention programs, including those that are not Federal grant recipients, in order to determine: program efficacy and contributory factors; and replication feasibility.
Sets forth participation guidelines for Federal and private sector implementation.
Provides one-time incentive grants for effective prevention programs.
Authorizes appropriations for both program evaluation operations and for the incentive grants.
