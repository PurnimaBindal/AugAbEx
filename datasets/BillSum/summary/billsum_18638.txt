Family Economic Stimulus Act of 2008 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: exclude unemployment compensation from gross income in 2007 and 2008; increase the child tax credit to $1,200 in 2008; allow individual taxpayers a $400 tax rebate ($800 in the case of a joint return) in 2008; extend unemployment insurance benefits; and require states to distribute to unemployed individuals information about unemployment insurance eligibility conditions.
