Enhanced Rescission/Receipts Act of 1993 - Grants the President legislative line item veto rescission authority over appropriation bills and targeted tax benefits in revenue bills.
Authorizes the President to rescind all or part of any budget authority if the President determines that such rescission: would reduce the Federal budget deficit; will not impair any essential Government functions; and will not harm the national interest.
Requires the President to notify the Congress of such a rescission by special message not later than 20 calendar days after enactment of appropriations or revenue legislation.
Makes such a rescission effective unless the Congress, during a review period of 20 calendar days, enacts a rescission/receipts disapproval bill.
Describes: information to be included in the President's message; and procedures to govern consideration of rescission/receipts disapproval legislation in the Senate and the House of Representatives.
