Amends the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to reduce the contribution that a multicandidate political committee may make to a congressional candidate.
Prohibits: a congressional candidate from accepting contributions from out-of-State persons that, in total, equal or exceed contributions from in-State residents; cash contributions in Federal elections; independent expenditures within seven days before a congressional election; contributions between multicandidate political committees; and bundling of funds.
Requires: a multicandidate political committee affiliated with another organization to include such organization's entire name in its own name; and a lobbyist who makes a contribution to disclose his or her lobbyist status.
Sets forth reporting requirements for: contributing lobbyists; and out-of-State contributions in House of Representatives elections.
Bans soft money in Federal elections.
