Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a taxpayer certified as a Patriot employer by the Secretary of the Treasury a tax credit for one percent of such employer's taxable income.
Defines a "Patriot employer" as any taxpayer who: maintains its headquarters in the United States; pays at least 60% of the health care premiums of its employees; maintains or increases the number of its full-time workers in the United States relative to its full-time workers outside of the United States: provides its employees with a certain level of compensation and retirement benefits; and provides full differential salary and insurance benefits for all National Guard and Reserve employees called to active duty.
