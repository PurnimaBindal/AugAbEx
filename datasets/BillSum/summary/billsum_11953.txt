Low-Dose Radiation Research Act of 2018 (Sec.
This bill requires the Department of Energy to carry out a research program&nbsp;on low-dose radiation to enhance the scientific understanding of the effects of exposure and to improve risk-assessment and risk-management methods.
"Low-dose radiation" is defined as a dose less than 100 millisieverts.
(The Nuclear Regulatory Commission limits an adult's annual occupational radiation dose to 50 millisieverts.)
