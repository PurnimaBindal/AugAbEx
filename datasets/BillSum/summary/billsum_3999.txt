Repeals the mandate of the Board and the Committee to report biannually to the Congress on national economic trends, taking into account unemployment, investment, and productivity.
Replaces such mandate with a mandate to consult semiannually with Congress, and to report on their plans and the time required to achieve price stability.
Repeals the Full Employment and Balanced Growth Act of 1978 (Humphrey-Hawkins Act).
Amends the Employment Act of 1946 and the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 to reflect the provisions of this Act.
