Small Business Stimulus Act of 2008 - Directs the Administrator of the Small Business Administration (SBA) to reduce in 2008 the rate of certain loan fees imposed under the Small Business Act.
Appropriates, as an emergency requirement, additional funds in FY2008 for: loan subsidies under the SBA Business Loans Program Account; and direct loans and assistance under the SBA Microloan Program.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: increase to $200,000 in 2008 the expensing allowance for depreciable business assets; and extend through 2008 the five-year carryback of certain unused net operating losses.
