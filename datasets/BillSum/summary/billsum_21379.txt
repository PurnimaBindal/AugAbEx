Securities Law Technical Corrections Act of 2007 - Makes technical corrections to the following Acts: the Securities Act of 1933; the Securities Exchange Act of 1934; the Trust Indenture Act of 1939; the Investment Company Act of 1940; and the Investment Advisers Act of 1940.
Amends the following statutes to make conforming amendments for the repeal of the Public Utility Holding Company Act of 1935: the Securities Exchange Act of 1934; the Trust Indenture Act of 1939; the Investment Company Act of 1940; and the Investment Advisers Act of 1940.
