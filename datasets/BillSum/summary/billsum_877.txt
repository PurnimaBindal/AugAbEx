Requires the Secretary of the Interior to: prepare a management plan for the Monument; and submit such plan to the Committee on Resources of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources of the Senate.
Authorizes appropriations to carry out annual operation and maintenance.
Directs the Administrator of General Services to convey Governors Island (with the exclusion of the Monument) to New York, subject to specified rights of access.
Requires any use of the conveyed land to be in compliance with: the New York State Environmental Quality Review Act; the Governors Island Preservation and Design Manual.
