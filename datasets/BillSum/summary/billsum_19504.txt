Amends the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to suspend, through December 31, 2009, the duty on certain: front panels for cathode-ray television picture tubes; 32V funnels for use with curved screen panels; 32V funnels for use with Pure Flat (PF) panels; certain funnels for cathode-ray television picture tubes; pre-assembled glass envelopes; aperture masks made from aluminum-killed, open-coil annealed steel for color picture tubes; three-beam electron guns for cathode ray tubes; one-beam electron guns for projection cathode-ray tubes; and aperture masks.
Reduces, through such date, the duty on front panels for certain cathode-ray television picture tubes.
