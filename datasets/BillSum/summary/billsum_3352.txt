National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program Improvement Act of 2003 - Amends the Public Health Service Act to: revise the basis for calculating the projected lost earnings of a person who sustained a vaccine-related injury; increase the award for a vaccine-related death; allow compensation for expenses for family counseling and establishing guardianship; allow payment of interim attorneys' fees and costs; establish a procedure for paying attorneys' fees; extend from two to six years the statute of limitations for injuries or death from a vaccine set forth in the Vaccine Injury Table; revise the membership and meeting schedule of the Advisory Commission on Childhood Vaccines; and direct the Secretary of Health and Human Services to conduct a public service announcement campaign about the availability of the Program.Amends the Internal Revenue Code to increase the limit on Vaccine Injury Compensation Trust Fund administrative expenses.
