Amends the Revenue Reconciliation Act of 1993 to delay the effective date of the change in the point of imposition of the tax on diesel fuel from January 1, 1994, to: July 1, 1994; or the 60th day after final regulations are prescribed.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow vendors of diesel fuel sold for any nontaxable use to claim tax refunds on behalf of ultimate users.
Provides a similar rule for vendors of gasoline sold to State and local governments.
