TABLE OF CONTENTS: Title I: Termination of Committee on Standards of Official Conduct in the House of Representatives Title II: Establishment of Commission Title III: Discharge of Commission Report from Committee on Rules in the House of Representatives Ethics Reform Act of 1995 - Title I: Termination of Committee on Standards of Official Conduct in the House of Representatives - Amends: rule X of the Rules of the House of Representatives to terminate the Committee on Standards of Official Conduct; and the Ethics Reform Act of 1989 to repeal provisions regarding that Committee.
Makes conforming amendments to the Rules, the Ethics in Government Act of 1978, and other Federal law.
Title II: Establishment of Commission - Establishes within the legislative branch the Independent Commission on Congressional Ethics.
Sets forth provisions regarding membership and staff of, and procedural rules with respect to, the Commission.
Sets forth duties of the Commission, including: recommending to the House appropriate administrative actions to establish or enforce standards of official conduct for Members, officers, and employees of the House; investigating alleged violations and reporting to the House its findings and recommendations; reporting to appropriate Federal or State authorities, with House approval, any substantial evidence of violations; considering requests of any House Member, officer, or employee for an advisory opinion; and providing information and guidance to House Members, officers, and employees regarding laws, rules, regulations, and other standards of conduct applicable to such individuals in their official capacities.
Title III: Discharge of Commission Report from Committee on Rules in the House of Representatives - Sets forth provisions regarding: discharging the Committee from further consideration of a Commission's report or any resolution introduced with respect to a Commission report, recommendation, or advisory opinion; and floor consideration of such a resolution.
