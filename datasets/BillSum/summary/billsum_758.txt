21st Century Housing Act - Amends the Housing Act of 1948 to authorize the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), in carrying out any program through the Federal Housing Administration (FHA) to: appoint and fix the compensation of Administration personnel; and use premium-generated income for information technology upgrades.
Amends the National Housing Act to: extend the maturity term for insured mortgages; revise mortgage insurance eligibility criteria and requirements for premium charges; authorize waiver of certain requirements for new product initiatives; increase the maximum mortgage amount limit for multifamily housing in high-cost areas; increase the FHA single family housing maximum mortgage limit; and increase the capital ratio for the Mutual Mortgage Insurance Fund.
Increases prerequisites for insured mortgages covering condominiums to require that the condomium project have a HUD-insured blanket morgage.
