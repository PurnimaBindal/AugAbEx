Great Falls Preservation and Redevelopment Act of 1993 - Establishes the Great Falls Historic District in Paterson, New Jersey, to be administered by the Secretary of the Interior.
Authorizes the Secretary to enter into cooperative agreements with: nonprofit entities for the purchase of property or easements, emergency stabilization, and the establishment of a coordinated fund to carry out this Act; property owners for the preservation and interpretation of properties of historical or cultural significance; and the State of New Jersey for rescue, firefighting, and law enforcement services in the District.
Authorizes appropriations.
