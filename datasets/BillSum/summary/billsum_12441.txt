Tax Equity for Health Plan Beneficiaries Act of 2007 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: extend the exclusion from gross income for employer-provided health care coverage to certain eligible beneficiaries and their dependent children; revise the definition of "dependent" for purposes of the tax deduction for the health insurance costs of self-employed individuals; and extend voluntary employees' beneficiary association sick and accident benefits to eligible beneficiaries and their dependents.
