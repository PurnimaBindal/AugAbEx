Egg Research and Consumer Information Act Amendments of 1993 - Amends the Egg Research and Consumer Information Act to: increase the maximum assessment rate from ten cents per case of commercial eggs to 20 cents per case; authorize the Egg Board to increase assessments subject to producer referendum; increase the trigger level for assessment exemptions from (producers of) 30,000 laying hens to 75,000 laying hens; and allocate funds for research activities.
