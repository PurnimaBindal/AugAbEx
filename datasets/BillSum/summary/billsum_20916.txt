Security Assessment Feasibility for Equipment Testing and Evaluation of Capabilities for our Homeland Act or the SAFE TECH Act (Sec.
This bill directs the Transportation Security Administration (TSA) to develop a program allowing a vendor to obtain performance testing of transportation security screening technology through a third party as an alternative to the TSA's regular testing process.
(Sec.
The TSA must develop a process for reciprocal recognition of security standards in coordination with the European Civil Aviation Conference.
(Sec.
The Government Accountability Office must report on the third-party testing program established by this bill.
