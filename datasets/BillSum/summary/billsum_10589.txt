Crow Creek Sioux Tribe Infrastructure Development Trust Fund Act of 1996 - Establishes in the Treasury the Crow Creek Sioux Tribe Infrastructure Development Trust Fund, which shall: receive funds from the Missouri River basin Pick-Sloan program until a specified Fund aggregate is attained; and be used for the socioeconomic recovery and cultural preservation of the Crow Creek Sioux Tribe (based upon a Tribe-developed plan).
Authorizes appropriations.
