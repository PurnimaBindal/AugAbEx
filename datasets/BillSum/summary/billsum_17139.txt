Global HIV/AIDS Food Security and Nutrition Support Act of 2007 - Amends the United States Leadership Against HIV/AIDS, Tuberculosis, and Malaria Act of 2003 to direct the Global AIDS Coordinator to: ensure that an assessment of the food security and nutritional status of each patient enrolled in anti-retroviral medication treatment program is carried out; provide food and nutrition support to each HIV-infected individual and his or her household for at least 180 days; coordinate activities with the United States Agency for International Development (USAID), the Secretary of Agriculture, and other relevant US government departments and agencies; develop indicators to measure the effectiveness of nutrition and food security interventions; and support linkages between US colleges and universities with colleges and universities in focus countries in order to provide training and build indigenous capacity to respond to HIV/AIDS and to improve capacity to address nutrition and livelihood needs of HIV/AIDS-affected and impoverished communities.
