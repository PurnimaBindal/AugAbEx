Strategic Withdrawal of Agencies for Meaningful Placement Act of 2018 or the SWAMP Act This bill: repeals the requirement that all offices attached to the seat of government be exercised in the District of Columbia, and not elsewhere; and prohibits new construction or major renovation of certain executive agency headquarters in the Washington Metropolitan area.
The General Services Administration (GSA) must: establish a process to allow an executive agency to request GSA to issue a solicitation for the relocation of its headquarters; allow any state to bid for the relocation of the agency's headquarters; and in consultation with the executive agency, select a state for the relocation of the agency's headquarters using a competitive bidding procedure based on certain considerations.
