Bringing Business Back Act of 2016 This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow income or gain attributable to certain real property to be excluded from gross income for a one-year period in which the income attributable to the real property exceeds the pre-depreciation expenses attributable to the property.
The exclusion applies to real property that has been certified by the state or local zoning authority and any economic development board as: zoned for commercial use, undeveloped and vacant during the two-year period ending on the date of certification, and located within a qualified census tract.
A &quot;qualified census tract&quot; is any census tract that: has an average poverty rate exceeding the national average poverty rate or an unemployment rate above the national unemployment rate; and exhibits another condition of distress, such as deteriorating infrastructure or population decline.
A census tract is also qualified if it is located in a rural community that: has an unemployment rate of at least 6%, and in which at least 50% of the houses were constructed before 1980.
