Veterans Home Loan Refinance Opportunity Act of 2009 - Amends Internal Revenue Code provisions relating to tax-exempt veterans' mortgage bonds to permit: proceeds from such bonds to refinance residences of veterans (currently, bond financing limited to new mortgages); an annual inflation adjustment after 2010 to the amounts of veterans' mortgage bonds that states may issue; and an increase from 1125 % to 150% in the amount by which interest on veterans' mortgages may exceed the yield on a bond issue.
