Eating Healthy at School Act of 2010 - Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to study and report to Congress on: the effectiveness and efficiency of administrative review systems in holding local educational agencies accountable for complying with the requirements of the school lunch and breakfast programs; the design and test of alternative processes and procedures that may improve the effectiveness and efficiency of such systems; and any such alternative processes and procedures that reflect the results of such tests, and consider the practicality of implementation.
Authorizes the Secretary to prescribe the regulations necessary to implement such alternative processes or procedures.
