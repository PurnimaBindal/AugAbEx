Public Libraries Enhancement Act of 2001 - Amends the Library Services and Technology Act to authorize increased appropriations for FY 2002 through 2006 for State allotments.
Directs the Secretary of Education to identify the increased portion of a State's allotment.
Authorizes a State to use such portion as the Federal share for any of the following: new library construction, existing facilities remodeling, and equipment purchase, lease, and installation; acquisition of reference materials; or operation of public libraries during hours of library service to the public.
