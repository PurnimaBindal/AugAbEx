Directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS) annually to publish in the Federal Register a notice soliciting proposals for: modifications to existing safe harbors issued pursuant to the Medicare and Medicaid Patient and Program Protection Act of 1987; additional safe harbors specifying payment practices that shall not be treated as criminal offenses or serve as the basis for an exclusion; and certain interpretive rulings and special fraud alerts.
Prescribes the rulemaking process to follow, including criteria for modifying and establishing safe harbors.
Authorizes any person to present, at any time, a request to the Inspector General of HHS for: a statement (interpretive ruling) of the current interpretation of the meaning of a specific aspect of Social Security Act civil and criminal prohibitions with respect to Medicare and Medicaid; and a notice (special fraud alert) which informs the public of practices considered suspect or of particular concern under specified kickback, bribe, or rebate prohibitions.
Specifies criteria for such rulings and alerts.
