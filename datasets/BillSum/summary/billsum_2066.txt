Establishes a Commission on Structural Alternatives for the Federal Courts of Appeals to: study the present division of the United States into the several judicial circuits and the structure and alignment of the Federal courts of appeals, with particular reference to the ninth circuit; and report its recommendations for changes to the President and the Congress by February 28, 1997.
Authorizes appropriations.
Directs the Senate Judiciary Committee to act on the report within 60 days of its transmission.
