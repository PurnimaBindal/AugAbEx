Amends the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 to declare that any State that permits a retail electric consumers group to choose among competing electric energy suppliers shall also permit any general purpose local government unit (or group of such units acting together) to offer to act as purchasing agent for consumers' group purchasers in order to purchase electric energy on an aggregate basis (community choice aggregation).
Prescribes implementation guidelines.
Requires any State that has deregulated retail sales of electricity to permit local governments to exercise local franchise powers.
Excludes from the purview of this Act: non-participating municipal electric utilities and non-participating rural electric cooperatives; and States that have adopted retail competition prior to the date of enactment of this Act.
Allows subsequent regulations implementing any State law adopting retail competition to pertain to an opt-out aggregation plan as it may be undertaken by a general purpose local government unit.
