Amends the Internal Revenue Code to extend through December 31, 2012, the designation period of areas as renewal communities and tax incentives for investment in such areas, including: the exclusion from gross income for income tax purposes of gain from the sale or exchange of assets (stock or business property) in such areas, the tax deduction for commercial revitalization expenditures in such areas, and an increased expensing allowance for the acquisition of business and investment assets in such areas.
