Empowerment Zone and Renewal Community Enhancement Act of 2006 - Amends Internal Revenue Code provisions relating to tax-preferred empowerment zones, enterprise communities, and renewal communities to: extend the period of designation for empowerment zones and renewal communities through 2015; provide a safe harbor standard for employee residency requirements in empowerment zones; eliminate certain residency requirements for the empowerment zone employment tax credit; grant authority to expand the boundaries of empowerment zones and enterprise communities; allow carryovers of unused expensing allowances for enterprise zone businesses; allow certain empowerment zone businesses to elect to receive payments in lieu of tax benefits; and allow federal guarantees and tax exemptions for enterprise zone facility bonds.
