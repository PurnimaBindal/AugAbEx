Angel Tax Credit Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow a new business-related&nbsp;tax credit for 25% of equity investments of $25,000 or more&nbsp;in a domestic corporation or partnership that: has its headquarters in the United States, has gross revenues for the taxable year of less than $1 million, employs fewer than 25 full-time employees, has been in existence for less than 7 years as of the date of the investment, has more than 50% of its employees performing substantially all of their services in the United States, and is engaged in a high technology trade or business.
Limits the allowable amount of such credit to $250,000 in any taxable year and imposes an overall&nbsp;limitation on such credit of $500 million for each of calendar years 2013 through 2017.
