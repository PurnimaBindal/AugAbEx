Education Savings Account and School Excellence Act of 1999 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code, with respect to education individual retirement accounts (IRAs), to: permit distributions for qualified elementary and secondary education expenses, including home schooling expenses; and increase (through December 31, 2002) the annual contribution limit from $500 to $2,000.
Waives the beneficiary age limitation for contributions on behalf of special needs beneficiaries.
Permits corporations to contribute to education IRAs.
