Modern-Day Marshall Plan for Eurasia and the Middle East Act of 2003 - Declares that it shall be the policy of the United States to promote stable democracies and market economies in Eurasia and the Middle East, and encourage dialogue between US officials and human rights organizations, civic organizers, politicians, and activists to further democratic reform and economic stability in developing countries there, and encourage dialogue between US officials and the private sector regarding the opening of markets and transparency in business in Eurasia and the Middle East.
Authorizes the President to establish and implement a program to provide economic assistance for the developing countries of Eurasia and the Middle East to foster political stability through programs which promote: economic growth, trade, education and infrastructure; health; and democracy building and human rights.
Requires the President to urge other appropriate countries to provide similar assistance.
