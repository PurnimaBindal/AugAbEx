Military Housing Privatization Initiatives Projects Oversight and Accountability Act of 2007 - Requires each military housing privatization initiative project to be carried out under a guaranteed maximum price contract (to be reviewed by an independent third party) and accompanied by a performance schedule.
Requires each Secretary concerned to prescribe regulations for effective oversight and management of projects under the Secretary's jurisdiction.
Establishes requirements for: combining projects in the event of termination of a lease or foreclosure on a project; funding reserves; sequestration and release of funds to be paid under a contract; community meetings to convey project information; certification of construction experience; bonding levels; and communication, review, and the effect of unsatisfactory performance or default (including the selection of a successor contractor).
Requires annual reports on lessons learned regarding the operation of military housing privatization initiative projects.
