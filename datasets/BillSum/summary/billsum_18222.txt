Traveling Exotic Animal and Public Safety Protection Act This bill amends the Animal Welfare Act to establish a prohibition on the use of exotic or wild animals in performances (e.g., circus, ride, carnival, or parade) of a traveling animal act.
The prohibition does not apply to the use of animals in: zoos; aquariums; research facilities; film, television, or advertising, if the performance is not before a public studio audience; or rodeos.
The prohibition also does not apply to domestic animals or farm animals.
