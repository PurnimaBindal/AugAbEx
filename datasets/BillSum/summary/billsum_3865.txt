Managed Care Bill of Rights for Consumers Act of 1996 - Establishes certain requirements for managed care plans, including: coverage for services furnished by a specialist not contracted with a managed care plan; continued services of a specialist without pre-authorization; assurance of equitable coverage with respect to emergency services; availability on a continuous basis of translation bilingual resources in areas where enrollees speak English as a second language; prohibition for payment of financial bonuses to physicians who reduce or limit medically necessary services; submission of a plan outlining a proposal for service to a medically underserved population in an area a plan is seeking to provide services; certification that a plan in an area provides a minimum number of doctors; disclosure of information on certain financial arrangements; geographical accessibility of items and services covered under the plan; and right of an enrollee to seek care from an out of network provider.
Establishes a civil money penalty.
Applies provisions of the Social Security Act to civil money penalties in the same manner as they apply under such Act.
