Advancing Innovation and Reinvigorating Widespread Access to Viable Electromagnetic Spectrum Act or the AIRWAVES Act This bill requires the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) to complete auctions&nbsp;by December 31, 2020, that will&nbsp;grant priority access broadcast licenses for specified frequency spectrum bands.
The FCC and the National Telecommunications and Information Administration are directed to identify frequencies&nbsp;in&nbsp;specified spectrum&nbsp;bands&nbsp;that may be utilized for non-federal unlicensed use and commercial licensed use.
The FCC must allocate 10% of proceeds from each of the spectrum band auctions specified in the bill&nbsp;to&nbsp;expand wireless infrastructure in rural areas that are underserved or unserved.
The FCC shall conduct a study on how unlicensed frequency spectrum bands&nbsp;can be utilized for: the provision of healthcare in rural areas, distance learning, and facilitating innovations in agriculture.
