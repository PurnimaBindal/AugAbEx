Border Law Enforcement Enhancement Act - Authorizes the Secretary of Homeland Security to make grants to sheriff's offices and local and tribal police departments in jurisdictions within 25 miles of the US southern border for: law enforcement operations; detention (including construction and operation of detention facilities) and transfer of illegal aliens to federal authorities; enforcement of substance trafficking laws and other state and federal criminal laws; and hiring, equipping, training, and overtime.
States that nothing in this Act shall be construed to authorize state or local law enforcement agencies to exercise federal immigration law enforcement authority.
