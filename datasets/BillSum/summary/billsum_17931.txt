Mortgage Loan Consumer Protection Act - Amends the Truth in Lending Act, with respect to credit extensions secured by a real property interest, to revise the category of excluded finance charge items.Amends the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act, with respect to the uniform settlement statement, to require: separate lines for "closing costs, "prepaid items," and "all other costs paid at closing"; at least two days' advance availability of the final settlement statement; and disclosure of all lender fees.Provides, with respect to special information booklets, for: harmonization of good faith estimates and settlement statements; and inclusion of refinancing and upfront payment option information.Prohibits markups and unearned fees.Sets forth servicer escrow-related liabilities.Revises damage and cost, and jurisdiction and statute of limitations provisions.
