Protecting America's Competitive Edge Through Tax Incentives Act of 2006 or the PACE-Finance Act - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: make permanent the tax credit for increasing research activities; increase the rate of such credit from 20 to 40%; expand such credit to include expenses of a research consortia; and allow a business tax credit for the cost of employee continuing education in science or engineering.
Directs the Secretary of the Treasury to: study and make recommendations on methods to expand the scope of the tax credit for increasing research activities; conduct an analysis of the US tax system and its effect on innovation investment and related activities; and report on such studies to the President and Congress.
