Authorizes the Secretary of Agriculture to make grants to: municipalities to facilitate the ability of local farmers to grow food crops or raise livestock for local community sale; local nongovernmental farming associations that promote community-based farming or to qualified farmers to provide technical, advisory, and other assistance for a municipally-based agricultural project; and provide startup funding to agricultural producers who must diversify in order to participate in a community-supported agricultural project.
Directs the Secretary to provide marketing assistance to a participating farmer who has received a lease or loan guarantee that has not been terminated to assist the farmer in marketing to community institutions, including schools, child care centers, and senior centers.
