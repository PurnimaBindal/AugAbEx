Contraception and Infertility Research Centers Act of 1993 - Amends the Public Health Service Act to mandate grants or contracts for centers for improving methods of contraception and diagnosing and treating infertility.
Requires each center to: conduct applied research; develop training protocols and conduct training; develop model continuing education programs; and disseminate information to professionals.
Allows funds to be used for: stipends for training program enrollees; and fees to clinical trial subjects.
Authorizes appropriations.
Establishes a program of agreements with health professionals to conduct contraception or infertility research in return for the Government repaying the professionals' educational loans.
