Multiple-Use Conflict Resolution Act of 2005 - Establishes a voluntary grazing permit and lease buyout program for commercial livestock operators on federal land.
Sets forth land priorities if funds are insufficient to meet all buyouts.
Provides for: voluntary donation of grazing permits; county transitional payments; and permanent retirement of grazing allotments which have no valid grazing permits or allotment leases.
States that a permittee or lessee shall maintain a lease for the remainder of its term in instances of voluntary nonuse or less than minimum use.
