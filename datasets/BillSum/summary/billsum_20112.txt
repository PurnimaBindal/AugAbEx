Amends the Internal Revenue Code to exclude up to $250,000 ($500,000 jointly) of gain on the sale of a principal residence if owned and used as the principal residence for periods aggregating at least two years during the five-year period prior to sale or exchange.
Sets forth special rules relating to: jointly held property; a deceased spouse; a cooperative housing tenant-stockholder; partial principal residence use; determination of marital status; acquisition after involuntary conversion; and periods of out-of-residence health care.
