Amends the Solid Waste Disposal Act to authorize the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to: treat Indian tribes as States under such Act; delegate primary enforcement authority for programs under such Act to Indian tribes; and provide grant and contract assistance to tribes to carry out such Act.
Sets forth conditions under which Indian tribes may be treated as States.
Directs the Administrator to report to the Congress on: recommendations for addressing hazardous and solid wastes and underground storage tanks within Indian country; methods to maximize Indian participation in, and administration of, programs under such Act; and the amount of assistance required and how the Administrator intends to provide such assistance to Indian tribes for the administration of such programs.
Requires the Administrator to establish an inventory of: sites within Indian country at which hazardous waste has been stored or disposed; and open dumps within Indian country at which solid waste has been disposed.
Directs the Administrator to assist Indian tribes in upgrading open dumps to comply with applicable requirements.
