Family Planning Amendments Act of 1995- Amends the Public Health Service Act to require family planning grant and contract recipients to provide nondirective counseling and referrals regarding: prenatal care and delivery; infant care, foster care, and adoption; and termination of pregnancy.
Allows a provider who objects, on religious or moral grounds, to providing such counseling and referrals to refer the woman to another provider.
Requires recipients to: comply with State parental notification or consent laws; and distribute only those condoms meeting current quality and labeling requirements and provide information regarding condom use benefits and risks.
Authorizes appropriations.
Authorizes appropriations for grants and contracts concerning: training to provide family planning services; and informational and educational materials regarding family planning and population growth.
