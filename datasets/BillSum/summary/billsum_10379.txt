Capital Construction Fund Qualified Withdrawal Act of 2003 - Amends the Merchant Marine Act and the Internal Revenue Code to permit as qualified withdrawals from fishing capital construction funds money used by retiring fishermen for the following purposes: paying the net proceeds to a person retiring related commercial fishing vessels and permits; making a rollover contribution into an owner's individual retirement plan; making a payment of an industry fee authorized by the fishing capacity reduction program; and acquiring a vessel monitoring system or the aquisition or construction of fishing gear designed to minimize or avoid bycatch.
