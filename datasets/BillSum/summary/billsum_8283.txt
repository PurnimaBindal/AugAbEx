Unlocking Technology Act of 2013 - Amends the prohibition under federal copyright law on the circumvention of a technological measure that controls access to a copyright-protected work to require that such prohibition apply only to circumventions carried out in order to infringe or facilitate infringement of a protected work.
Declares that it shall not be a violation to: circumvent a technological measure if the purpose is to engage in a use that is not an infringement of federal copyright law; or use, manufacture, import, offer to the public, provide, or otherwise traffic in any technology, product, service, device, component, or part primarily designed or produced to facilitate noninfringing uses of protected works by circumventing a technological measure that effectively controls access to such work, unless the intent is to infringe or facilitate infringement of a copyright.
Declares that it is not an infringement to copy or adapt the software or firmware of a user-purchased mobile communications device for the sole purpose of enabling the device to connect to a wireless communications network if: the copying or adapting is initiated by, or with the consent of, the owner of that device or the owner's agent; the owner or agent is in legal possession of the device; and the owner has the consent of, or an agreement with, the authorized operator of such wireless communications network to make use of its network.
(Thus allowing the &quot;unlocking&quot; of mobile devices without requiring an owner to obtain the consent of the initial carrier network before switching to a new carrier.)
Directs the President to ensure that applicable bilateral and multilateral trade agreements are modified to be consistent with this Act.
