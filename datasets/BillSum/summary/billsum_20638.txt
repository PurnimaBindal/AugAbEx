Access to Obstetric Care Act of 1993 - Directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to: provide for demonstration projects to improve access to obstetric services in underserved urban and rural areas for eligible pregnant women under title XIX (Medicaid) of the Social Security Act; develop and make public each year a compendium of State initiatives to address the obstetric access crisis; and provide a grant for the study of obstetrical malpractice claims.
