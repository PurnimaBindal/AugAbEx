Film Disclosure Act of 1993 - Amends the Lanham Act to require that any distributor or network that proposes to exploit a materially altered motion picture bear a label which conspicuously discloses the fact of: the film's material alteration from the form in which it was first released to the public; the nature of such alteration; and any objections raised by the artistic authors with reference to such alteration.
Delineates the compliance procedure for distributors or networks that propose to exploit a materially altered film.
Grants an artistic author the right to seek injunctive relief in US district courts to prevent violation of his or her rights under this Act.
