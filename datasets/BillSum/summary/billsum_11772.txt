Conservation Assistance Loan Act of 2018 This bill establishes a Department of Agriculture (USDA) nonrecourse conservation assistance loan program.
Under the program, farmers who receive loans must meet certain conservation requirements, including: complying with applicable conservation and wetland protection requirements under the Food Security Act of 1985, using a reduced tillage method and nutrient management practices, and planting a cover crop.
(Under a nonrecourse loan, a farmer pledges a commodity as collateral and may deliver the pledged collateral to USDA to repay the loan.)
