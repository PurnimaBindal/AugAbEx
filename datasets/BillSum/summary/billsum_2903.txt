Encouraging Work Act of 2005 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to consolidate and modify provisions of the work opportunity tax credit and the welfare-to-work tax credit and make the consolidated tax credit permanent.
Expands eligibility for the consolidated tax credit by: determining eligibility of ex-felons without regard to family income; and raising the age ceiling for food stamp recipients from 25 to 40.
Includes a "designated community resident" (in lieu of "high risk youth") and "long-term family assistance recipient" as members of the targeted group eligible for the consolidated tax credit.
Provides for an increased tax credit for employment of long-term family assistance recipients.
Repeals the separate welfare-to-work tax credit.
