Home Energy Affordability Tax Relief Act of 2008 or the HEATR Act of 2008 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to allow individual taxpayers an income-based refundable tax credit for residential energy costs.
Limits such credit to the lesser of 33% of such costs or $500.
Eliminates for major integrated oil companies: the tax deduction for intangible drilling and development costs in taxable years in which the average price of crude oil is greater than $3471 per barrel and the average price of natural gas is greater that $434 per 1,000 cubic feet; and the tax credit for enhanced oil recovery costs in taxable years in which the average price of crude oil is greater than $3471 per barrel.
Denies such oil companies and certain producers or refiners of crude oil a foreign tax credit for payments to certain foreign countries or US possessions from which they receive a specified economic benefit as a dual capacity taxpayer.
