Livestock Market Revitalization Act of 1996 - Amends the Packers and Stockyards Act, 1921 to define "captive supply" as livestock acquired by a packer for slaughter, including livestock delivered seven days or more before slaughter.
Directs the Secretary of Agriculture to: make information available to the public concerning livestock slaughtered or marketed in the United States, and captive market transactions; monitor antitrust and anticompetitive activities among packers and stockyards; and collect and disseminate marketing information on a national, regional, and local basis.
