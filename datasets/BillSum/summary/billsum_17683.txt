Catastrophe Savings Accounts Act of 2006 - Amends the Internal Revenue Code to create tax-exempt catastrophe savings accounts (CSAs).
Allows tax-free distributions from CSAs to pay expenses resulting from a presidentially declared major disaster.
Limits CSA balances to: $2,000 (for individuals with homeowner insurance deductibles of not more than $1,000); and the lesser of $15,000 or twice a homeowner's insurance deductible (for individuals with deductibles of more than $1,000).
