Sustainable Urban Development Act of 2010 - Directs the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) to develop a strategy to foster sustainable urban development in developing countries that updates the Making Cities Work Urban Strategy.
Requires such strategy to: review US foreign assistance strategy and programs; assess the feasibility of establishing a USAID senior advisor for urban sustainable development; evaluate options to leverage private sector partnerships; support US government collaboration with other donors; assess the feasibility of establishing a pilot urban strategies initiative; and analyze approaches to improve environmental sustainability in urban areas.
