Raw Sewage Overflow Community Right-to-Know Act - Amends the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to direct owners or operators of publicly owned treatment works to: institute an alert system for sanitary sewer overflows; notify the public of such overflows in areas where human health is potentially affected within 24 hours; immediately notify public health authorities and other affected entities; and provide specified reports to the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) or the State.
Makes the alert systems eligible for State water pollution control revolving fund assistance.
Authorizes appropriations for such fund through FY 2012.
