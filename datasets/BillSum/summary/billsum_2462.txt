Neighbors in Need Act - Amends the Temporary Extended Unemployment Compensation Act of 2002 (TEUCA) to: extend the TEUCA program in States with high unemployment (States that already qualify for the TEUC-X program (under which second tier benefit amounts are deposited in an individual's account) or would if the trigger for such program were changed) through March 31, 2004, with a phase-out period through June 30, 2004; and provide eight additional weeks of TEUC benefits to those who have exhausted theirs before enactment of this Act.
Amends the Social Security Act to require: increases and decreases in the earnings allocated to State accounts when States meet or fail to meet funding goals; and interest-free advances to State accounts in the Unemployment Trust Fund to be restricted to States which meet funding goals.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to: suspend the tax on individual unemployment compensation for 2004 and 2005; allow certified States to elect to collect Federal unemployment taxes, under the Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA); and require States to distribute to unemployed individuals State-specific information packets explaining unemployment insurance eligibility conditions.
