Amends the Commodity Exchange Act to repeal: the definition of "exempt commodity"; and the guidelines governing transactions in exempt commodities (thus subjecting to CFTC regulatory oversight formerly exempt commodity transactions).Directs the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) to maintain a liaison with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC).Subjects formerly exempt swap transactions to: CFTC enforcement jurisdiction; and CFTC proscriptions against manipulation of commodity market prices.Requires eligible trading facilities and systems to comply with CFTC regulations pertaining to: registration, reporting, recordkeeping, and net capital reserves.Expands the prohibition against fraudulent or misleading contracts to include any member of a contract market (currently any member of a registered entity).Amends the Department of Energy Organization Act with respect to the recruitment and retention of qualified personnel at FERC.
Grants FERC jurisdiction over: energy trading markets; derivatives transactions reflecting electric energy or natural gas prices (including futures, options, forwards and swaps); any person that makes a derivatives transaction; and any operator of an electronic forum in which derivatives transaction are made.
