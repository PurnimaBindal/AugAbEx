Medical Liability Improvement Act of 1993 - Sets forth provisions concerning, with respect to medical malpractice liability claims: limitations on attorney's fees; limitations on noneconomic damages; the statute of limitations; periodic payments for future losses; mandatory offsets for damages paid by a collateral source; certain obstetric services; joint and several liability; and preemption of inconsistent State law.
Amends the Internal Revenue Code to require the reporting of certain medical malpractice payments.
