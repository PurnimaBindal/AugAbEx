Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``Indian Health Care Improvement Technical Corrections Act of 1996''.
References.--Whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to or repeal of a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Indian Health Care Improvement Act.
