This Act may be cited as the ``Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor Act Amendments of 1997''.
The Delaware and Lehigh Navigation Canal National Heritage Corridor Act of 1988 (Public Law 200-692; 102 United States Statutes at Large 4552) is amended by striking ``Delaware and Lehigh Navigation Canal National Heritage Corridor'' each place it appears (except section 4(a)) and inserting ``Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor''.
Section 3(b) of the Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor Act of 1988 (Public Law 100-692; 102 United States Statutes at Large 4552) is amended-- (1) by inserting after ``subdivisions'' the following: ``in enhancing economic development within the context of preservation and''; and (2) by striking ``and surrounding the Delaware and Lehigh Navigation Canal in the Commonwealth'' and inserting ``the Corridor''.

Terms.--Section 5 of the Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor Act of 1988 (Public Law 100-692; 102 United States Statutes at Large 4553) is amended by striking subsection (c) and inserting the following: ``(c) Terms.--The following provisions shall apply to a member of the Commission appointed under paragraph (3) or (4) of subsection (b): ``(1) Length of term.--The member shall serve for a term of 3 years.
Carryover.--The member shall serve until a successor is appointed by the Secretary.
Replacement.--If the member resigns or is unable to serve due to incapacity or death, the Secretary shall appoint, not later than 60 days after receiving a nomination of the appointment from the Governor, a new member to serve for the remainder of the term.
Term limits.--A member may serve for not more than 2 full terms starting after the date of enactment of this paragraph.''
Confirmation.--Section 5 of the Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor Act of 1988 (Public Law 100-692; 102 United States Statutes at Large 4553) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(h) Confirmation.--The Secretary shall accept or reject an appointment under paragraph (3) or (4) of subsection (b) not later than 60 days after receiving a nomination of the appointment from the Governor.''.

Cooperative Agreements.--Section 7(h) of the Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor Act of 1988 (Public Law 100-692; 102 United States Statutes at Large 4555) is amended-- (1) in the first sentence, by inserting ``any non-profit organization,'' after ``subdivision of the Commonwealth,''; and (2) in the second sentence, by inserting ``such nonprofit organization,'' after ``such political subdivision,''.
Grants and Loans.--Section 7 of the Delaware and Lehigh National Heritage Corridor Act of 1988 (Public Law 100-692; 102 United States Statutes at Large 4554) is amended-- (1) by redesignating subsection (i) as subsection (j); and (2) by inserting after subsection (h) the following; ``(i) Grants and Loans.--The Commission may administer any grant or loan from amounts-- ``(1) appropriated to the Commission for the purpose of providing a grant or loan; or ``(2) donated or otherwise made available to the Commission for the purpose of providing a grant or loan.''.
