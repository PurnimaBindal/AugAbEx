ELIMINATION OF QUOTA AND PRICE SUPPORT PROGRAMS FOR PEANUTS.
In General.--Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Agriculture and the Commodity Credit Corporation may not provide loans, purchases, payments, or other operations or take any other action to support the price, or adjust or control the production, of peanuts by using the funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation or under the authority of any law.
Marketing Quotas.-- (1) In general.--Part VI of subtitle B of title III of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 (7 United States Code 1357 et sequentes) is repealed.
Conforming amendments.-- (A) Definitions.--Section 301(b) of the Act (7 United States Code 1301(b)) is amended-- (i) in paragraph (3)(A), by striking ``corn, rice, and peanuts'' and inserting ``corn and rice''; (ii) in paragraph (6), by striking subparagraph (C); (iii) in paragraph (10)(A)-- (I) by striking ``wheat, and peanuts'' and inserting ``and wheat''; and (II) by striking ``; 20 per centum in the case of wheat; and 15 per centum in the case of peanuts'' and inserting ``; and 20 percent in the case of wheat''; (iv) in paragraph (13)-- (I) by striking subparagraphs (B) and (C); and (II) in subparagraph (G), by striking ``or peanuts'' both places it appears; and (v) in paragraph (16)(A), by striking ``rice, and peanuts'' and inserting ``and rice''.
Administrative provisions.--Section 361 of the Act (7 United States Code 1361) is amended by striking ``peanuts,''.
Adjustment of quotas.--Section 371 of the Act (7 United States Code 1371) is amended-- (i) in the first sentence of subsection (a), by striking ``peanuts,''; and (ii) in the first sentence of subsection (b), by striking ``peanuts''.
Reports and records.--Section 373 of the Act (7 United States Code 1373) is amended-- (i) in subsection (a), by striking the first sentence and inserting the following new sentence: ``This subsection shall apply to warehousemen, processors, and common carriers of corn, wheat, cotton, rice, or tobacco, and all ginners of cotton, all persons engaged in the business of purchasing corn, wheat, cotton, rice, or tobacco from producers, and all persons engaged in the business of redrying, prizing, or stemming tobacco for producers.
''; and (ii) in subsection (b), by striking ``peanuts,''.
Regulations.--Section 375(a) of the Act (7 United States Code 1375(a)) is amended by striking ``peanuts,''.
Eminent domain.--The first sentence of section 378(c) of the Act (7 United States Code 1378(c)) is amended by striking ``cotton, tobacco, and peanuts,'' and inserting ``cotton and tobacco,''.
Price Support Program.-- (1) Permanent price support.--Section 101(b) of the Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 United States Code 1441 et sequentes) is amended by striking ``and peanuts''.
Temporary price support.--Sections 108, 108A, and 108B of the Act (7 United States Code 1445c through 1445c-3) are repealed.
Conforming amendments.-- (A) Section 301 of the Act (7 United States Code 1447) is amended by inserting after ``nonbasic agricultural commodity'' the following: ``(other than peanuts)''.
Section 408(c) of the Act (7 United States Code 1428(c)) is amended by striking ``peanuts,''.
Section 5(a) of the Commodity Credit Corporation Charter Act (15 United States Code 714c(a)) is amended by inserting after ``agricultural commodities'' the following: ``(other than peanuts)''.
Liability.--A provision of this section or an amendment made by this section shall not affect the liability of any person under any provision of law as in effect before the application of the provision of this section or the amendment in accordance with subsection (d).
Application.--This section and the amendments made by this section shall apply beginning with the 1996 crop of peanuts.
