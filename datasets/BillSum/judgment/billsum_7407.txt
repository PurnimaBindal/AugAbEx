Regulations.--(1) Chapter 701 of title 49, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section: ``Sec.
Regulations ``The Secretary of Transportation, within 6 months after the date of the enactment of this section, shall issue regulations to carry out this chapter that include-- ``(1) guidelines for industry to obtain sufficient insurance coverage for potential damages to third parties; ``(2) procedures for requesting and obtaining licenses to operate a commercial launch vehicle or reentry vehicle; ``(3) procedures for requesting and obtaining operator licenses for launch or reentry; ``(4) procedures for requesting and obtaining launch site or rentry site operator licenses; and ``(5) procedures for the application of government indemnification.''.
