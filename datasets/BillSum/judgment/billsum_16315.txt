This Act may be cited as the ``Timber-Dependent Counties Stabilization Act of 1998''.
SHARING OF FOREST SERVICE TIMBER SALE RECEIPTS.
Payments.-- (1) Fiscal years 1999 through 2003.--In lieu of making the 25-percent payments to States for each of fiscal years 1998 through 2003, the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to each State that is otherwise eligible to receive those payments the special payment amount determined for that State.
Fiscal years after fiscal year 2003.-- (A) In general.--For each fiscal year after fiscal year 2003, the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to each State that is otherwise eligible for the 25- percent payments to States, as elected by the State, either-- (i) the special payment amount determined for that State, in lieu of the 25-percent payments to States otherwise applicable for that State; or (ii) the 25-percent payments to States applicable for that State.
Election.--The election under subparagraph (A) shall be made by the Governor of a State, for all fiscal years after fiscal year 2003, by not later than 5 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
If the Governor of a State fails to make the election by that date, the State is deemed to have elected the payment described in subparagraph (A)(i).
Expenditure by states.--Amounts paid to a State under this subsection shall be expended by the State in the same manner in which 25-percent payments to States are required to be expended.
Definitions.--As used in this section: (1) 25-percent payments to states.--The term ``25-percent payments to States'' means the 25 percent payments authorized by the Act of May 23, 1908 (35 United States Statutes at Large 260, chapter 192; 16 United States Code 500) for the benefit of counties in which national forests are situated, as in effect immediately before the date of the enactment of this section.
Special payment amount.--The term ``special payment amount'' means, for a State, the amount determined by multiplying-- (A) 76 percent; by (B) the annual average of the 25-percent payments to States made to the State during the 5-year period consisting of fiscal years 1986 through 1990, adjusted for each fiscal year after fiscal year 2004 to reflect changes in the consumer price index for urban areas (as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics) that occur after publication of that index for fiscal year 2003.
SHARING OF BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT TIMBER SALE RECEIPTS.
Payments.-- (1) Fiscal years 1999 through 2003.--In lieu of making the 50-percent payments to counties for each of fiscal years 1998 through 2003, the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to each county that is otherwise eligible to receive those payments the special payment amount determined for that county.
Fiscal years after fiscal year 2003.-- (A) In general.--For each fiscal year after fiscal year 2003, the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to each county that is otherwise eligible to receive the 50-percent payments to counties, as elected by the county, either-- (i) the special payment amount, in lieu of the 50-percent payments to counties otherwise applicable and allocable to that county; or (ii) the share of the 50-percent payments to counties otherwise applicable and allocable to the county.
Election.--The election under subparagraph (A) shall be made by the chief executive officer of a county, for all fiscal years after fiscal year 2003, by not later than 5 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
If the chief executive officer of a county fails to make the election by that date, the county is deemed to have elected the payment described in subparagraph (A)(i).
Definitions.--As used in this section: (1) 50-percent payments to counties.--The term ``50-percent payments to counties'' means the sum of the 50-percent share otherwise paid to a county pursuant to title II of the Act of August 28, 1937 (50 United States Statutes at Large 875, chapter 876; 43 United States Code 1181f), and the payments made to counties pursuant to the Act of May 24, 1939 (53 United States Statutes at Large 753, chapter 144; 43 United States Code 1181f-1 et seq.
), as in effect immediately before the date of the enactment of this section.
Special payment amount.--The term ``special payment amount'' means the amount determined by multiplying-- (A) 76 percent; by (B) the annual average of the 50-percent payments to counties made to a county during the 5-year period consisting of fiscal years 1986 through 1990, adjusted for each fiscal year after fiscal year 2004 to reflect changes in the consumer price index for urban areas (as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics) that occur after publication of that index for fiscal year 2003.
Title XIII of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1993 (Public Law 103-66) is amended by striking chapter 4 (107 United States Statutes at Large 681-682).
