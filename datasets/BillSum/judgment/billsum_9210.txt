TITLE I--AGRICULTURE, RURAL DEVELOPMENT AND RELATED AGENCIES Subtitle A--Farmers Home Administration SEC.
RURAL HOUSING INSURANCE FUND PROGRAM ACCOUNT.
Gross Obligations.--For additional gross obligations for direct loans as authorized under title V of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1471 et sequentes), to be available from funds in the Rural Housing Insurance Fund, as follows: (1) $500,000,000 for loans to borrowers made under section 502 of such Act (42 United States Code 1472), as determined by the Secretary.
$50,000,000 for housing repair loans made under section 504 of such Act (42 United States Code 1474).
$250,000,000 for rental housing loans made under section 515 of such Act (42 United States Code 1485).
Cost of Loans.--For an additional amount for the cost, as defined in section 502(5) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 United States Code 661a(5)), including the cost of modifying loans, of direct loans, as follows: (1) $121,750,000 for low-income housing loans under section 502 of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1472).
$20,090,000 for housing repair loans under section 504 of such Act (42 United States Code 1474).
$132,500,000 for rental housing purposes under section 515 of such Act (42 United States Code 1485).
For additional rental assistance agreements entered into under or renewed under section 521(a)(2) of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1490a(a)(2)), $70,000,000.
RURAL DEVELOPMENT INSURANCE FUND PROGRAM ACCOUNT.
Gross Obligations.--For additional gross obligations for the principal amount of direct loans as authorized by sections 308, 309A, 310A, and 310B of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 United States Code 1928, 1929A, 1931, and 1932), to be available from funds in the Rural Development Insurance Fund, as follows: (1) Water and sewer facility loans, $600,000,000.
Community facility loans, $250,000,000.
Cost of Loans.--For an additional amount for the cost, as defined in section 502(5) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 United States Code 661a(5)), including the cost of modifying loans, of direct loans, as follows: (1) $87,360,000 for water and sewer facility loans.
$21,025,000 for community facility loans.
RURAL DEVELOPMENT LOANS PROGRAM ACCOUNT.
Cost of Loans.--For an additional amount for the cost, as defined in section 502(5) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 United States Code 661a(5)), including the cost of modifying loans, of direct loans from the Rural Development Loan Fund established under section 623(a) of the Community Economic Development Act of 1981 (42 United States Code 9812(a)), $50,000,000.
Principal Amount of Loans.--The funds made available under subsection (a) shall be available to subsidize gross obligations for the principal amount of direct loans of not to exceed $100,000,000.
RURAL WATER AND WASTE DISPOSAL GRANTS.
In General.--For additional grants pursuant to paragraphs (2) and (6) of section 306(a) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 United States Code 1926(a)), $500,000,000, to remain available until expended, pursuant to section 306(d) of such Act.
Purposes.--The funds made available under subsection (a) shall not be used for any purpose not specified in section 306(a) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act.
VERY LOW-INCOME HOUSING REPAIR GRANTS.
For additional grants to the very low-income elderly for essential repairs to dwellings pursuant to section 504 of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1474), $50,000,000, to remain available until expended.
SUPERVISORY AND TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE GRANTS.
For additional grants pursuant to section 525 of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1479 and 1490e), $10,000,000, to remain available until expended.
RURAL HOUSING PRESERVATION GRANTS.
For additional grants for rural housing preservation as authorized by section 533 of the Housing and Urban-Rural Recovery Act of 1983 (42 United States Code 1490m), $50,000,000.
In General.--For additional grants authorized under section 310B(c) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 United States Code 1932(c)) to any qualified public or private nonprofit organization, $100,000,0000.
Limitations.--Effective for fiscal year 1991 and thereafter, grants made pursuant to subsection (a) shall not be subject to any dollar limitation unless the limitation is set forth in law.
LOCAL TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE GRANTS.
For additional local technical assistance grants authorized under section 306(a)(11) of the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (7 United States Code 1926(a)(11)), $15,000,000.
Subtitle B--Rural Electrification Administration SEC.
RURAL ELECTRIFICATION LOANS PROGRAM ACCOUNT.
Insured Loans.--For additional insured loans pursuant to section 305 of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 United States Code 935) for rural electrification loans, $700,000,000, to remain available until expended.
Cost of Loans.--For the additional cost, as defined in section 502(5) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 United States Code 661a(5)), including the cost of modifying loans, of direct rural electrification loans authorized by section 305 of the Rural Electrification Act of 1936 (7 United States Code 935), for the additional cost of direct rural electrification loans, $131,000,000.
Subtitle C--Alternative Agricultural Research and Commercialization SEC.
ALTERNATIVE AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH AND COMMERCIALIZATION.
For an additional amount for necessary expenses to carry out the Alternative Agricultural Research and Commercialization Act of 1990 (7 United States Code 5901 et sequentes), $20,000,000.
For additional amounts to enable the Secretary of Energy to make grants under title III of the Energy Conservation and Production Act (42 United States Code 6831 et sequentes)-- (1) $150,000,000 to be used for the weatherization assistance program for low income persons; and (2) $150,000,000 to be used for the Institutional Conservation Program and the State Energy Conservation Program.
TITLE III--VETERANS AFFAIRS AND HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT SEC.
COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING.
In General.--For an additional amount to carry out a community development grants program under title I of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 (42 United States Code 5301 et sequentes), $1,000,000,000, to be obligated for making grants to States and units of local government under such Act.
Limitation.--For purposes of applying the limitation contained in section 105(a)(8) of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 (42 United States Code 5305(a)(8)) to amounts made available under this section, the maximum percentage of funds received by a unit of general local government under this section that may be used for the provision of public services shall be 20 percent SEC.
EPA CONSTRUCTION GRANTS AND STATE REVOLVING LOAN FUND.
For an additional amount to carry out the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 United States Code 1251 et sequentes) and the Water Quality Act of 1987 (33 United States Code 1251 et sequentes), $1,000,000,000, of which-- (1) $500,000,000 shall be made available for grants under title II of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 United States Code 1281 et sequentes); and (2) $500,000,000 shall be made available for State water pollution control revolving funds established under title VI of such Act (33 United States Code 1381 et sequentes).
TITLE IV--COMMERCE, JUSTICE, AND STATE SEC.
Technical Assistance Grants.--For an additional amount for necessary expenses, not otherwise provided for, of the Small Business Administration for making technical assistance grants under the Microloan Demonstration Program established by section 7(m) of the Small Business Act (15 United States Code 636(m)), $3,000,000.
Business Loans Program Account.--For the cost, as defined in section 502(5) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 (2 United States Code 661a(5)), including the cost of modifying loans, of direct loans for carrying out not more than 50 microloan programs under the Microloan Demonstration Program, $2,600,000, to be made available until expended for the subsidy cost of $15,000,000 in direct loans for the Microloan Demonstration Program.
