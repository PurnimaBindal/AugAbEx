This Act may be cited as the ``Immigration Technical Corrections Act of 1997''.
For purposes of this Act: (1) The term ``INA'' means the Immigration and Nationality Act.
The term ``IIRIRA'' means the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 (Public Law 104-208, division C).
Except as otherwise specifically provided in this Act, the amendments made by this Act to a provision shall take effect as if included in the provision at the time the provision was first enacted, or, if the provision has been amended since enactment, as if included in its most recent amendment.
