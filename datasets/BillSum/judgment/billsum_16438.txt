This Act may be cited as the ``Health Insurance Access Amendments of 1997''.
PHASE-DOWN OF APPLICATION OF PREEXISTING CONDITION EXCLUSIONS IN GROUP MARKET.
Section 9801 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, section 701 of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (29 United States Code 1181), and section 2701 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300g) are each amended as follows: (1) Effective with respect to group health plans for plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2000, in subsection (a), strike ``not more than 12 months (or 18 months in the case of a late enrollee) after the enrollment date'' and insert ``not more than 6 months after the enrollment date''.
Effective with respect to group health plans for plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2001, in subsection (a) (as amended by paragraph (1)), strike ``not more than 6 months after the enrollment date'' and insert ``not more than 3 months after the enrollment date''.
Effective with respect to group health plans for plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2002, in subsection (a) (as amended by paragraph (1)), strike ``3 months'' and insert ``one month''.
EXTENDING AVAILABILITY OF COVERAGE IN INDIVIDUAL MARKET WITHOUT APPLICATION OF PREEXISTING CONDITION EXCLUSIONS.
Phase-Down in Eligibility Restrictions.--Section 2741(b)(1)(A) of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300g-41(b)(1)(A)) is amended as follows: (1) Effective with respect to health insurance coverage offered, sold, issued, renewed, in effect, or operated in the individual market on or after January 1, 2000, strike ``18 or more months'' and insert ``12 or more months''.
Effective with respect to health insurance coverage offered, sold, issued, renewed, in effect, or operated in the individual market on or after January 1, 2001, strike ``12 or more months'' (as inserted by paragraph (1)) and insert ``6 or more months''.
Effective with respect to health insurance coverage offered, sold, issued, renewed, in effect, or operated in the individual market on or after January 1, 2002, strike ``6 or more months'' (as inserted by paragraph (2)) and insert ``one month''.
EXPANSION OF GUARANTEED ISSUE PROTECTIONS TO LARGE GROUP MARKET.

Relevant group market.--The term `relevant group market' means-- ``(A) for periods before January 1, 2000, the small group market, ``(B) for the period beginning on January 1, 2000, and ending December 31, 2001, the small group market and the market that would be included in the small group market if `100' were substituted for `50' in section 2791(e)(4), and ``(C) for the period beginning on January 1, 2002, the small and large group market.''.
Application of Information Disclosure to Relevant Group Market.--Section 2713 of such Act (42 United States Code 300gg-13) is amended-- (1) in subsection (a), by striking ``small employer'' the first place it appears and inserting ``relevant employer (as defined in section 2711(b)(1))'', and (2) by striking ``small employer'' and ``small employers'' each succeeding place it appears and inserting ``relevant employer'' and ``relevant employers'', respectively.
