REFERENCES IN FEDERAL LAW.
Competitiveness Policy Council.--Any reference in Federal law to the Competitiveness Policy Council shall be construed to be a reference to the National Competitiveness Commission.
Competitiveness Policy Council Act.--Any reference in Federal law to the Competitiveness Policy Council Act shall be construed to be a reference to the National Competitiveness Commission Act.
