This Act may be cited as the ``Florida Coast Protection Act of 1999''.
ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STATEMENT REQUIREMENTS.
Section 307(c)(3) of the Coastal Zone Management Act of 1972 (16 United States Code 1456(c)(3)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(C) Necessary data and information.--For purposes of subparagraph (B), a State shall not be considered to receive all necessary data and information with respect to a plan for exploration, development, or production before the date on which the State receives a copy of an environmental impact statement under section 102(2)(C) of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 United States Code 4332(2)(C)) that applies to that exploration, development, or production.''.
Section 25 of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 United States Code 1351(a) is amended-- (1) in paragraph (a)(1), by striking ``other than the Gulf of Mexico,'' each place it appears; and (2) by striking subsection (l).
OIL AND GAS DEVELOPMENT AND PRODUCTION.
Section 25(e) of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act of 1972 (43 United States Code 1351(e)) is amended-- (1) by striking ``(e)(1) At least'' and inserting the following: ``(e) Major Federal Action.-- ``(1) Outside the gulf of mexico.-- ``(A) In general.--At least''; (2) by striking ``(2) The Secretary'' and inserting the following: ``(B) Preliminary and final plans.--The Secretary''; and (3) by adding at the end the following: ``(2) In the gulf of mexico.-- ``(A) In general.--The approval of a development and production plan in a covered area (as defined in section 8(p)(1)) shall be considered to be a major Federal action for the purposes of the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (42 United States Code 4321 et seq.).
Time for review following receipt of environmental impact statement.--In the case of a development and production plan in a covered area, the Secretary shall ensure that each affected State for which a development and production plan affects any land use or water use in the coastal zone of the State with a coastal zone management program approved under section 306 of the Coastal Zone Management Act of 1972 (16 United States Code 1455), receives the final environmental impact statement not less than 180 days before determining concurrence or objection to the coastal zone consistency certification that is required to accompany the environmental impact statement under section 307(c)(3)(B) of the Coastal Zone Management Act of 1972 (16 United States Code 1456(c)(3)(B)).''.
LEASING ACTIVITY OFF THE COAST OF FLORIDA.
Section 8 of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 United States Code 1337) is amended-- (1) in subsection (a)(1), by striking ``The Secretary'' and inserting ``Except as provided in subsection (p), the Secretary''; and (2) by adding at the end the following: ``(p) Leasing Activity Off the Coast of Florida.-- ``(1) Definitions.--In this subsection: ``(A) Covered area.--The term `covered area' means-- ``(i) the Eastern Gulf of Mexico Planning Area (as established by the Secretary) which is adjacent to the State of Florida as defined by 43 United States Code 1333(a)(2)(A); ``(ii) the Straits of Florida Planning Area (as established by the Secretary); and ``(iii) the South Atlantic Planning Area (as established by the Secretary) which is adjacent to the State of Florida as defined by 43 United States Code 1333(a)(2)(A); within 100 miles off the coast of Florida.
Preleasing activity.-- ``(i) In general.--The term `preleasing activity' means an activity relating to a lease that is conducted before a lease sale is held.
Inclusions.--The term `preleasing activity' includes-- ``(I) the scheduling of a lease sale; ``(II) the issuance of a request for industry interest; ``(III) the issuance of a call for information or a nomination; ``(IV) the identification of an area for prospective leasing; ``(V) the publication of a draft or final environmental impact statement or a notice of sale; and ``(VI) the performance of any form of rotary drilling in a prospective lease area.
Exclusions.--The term `preleasing activity' does not include an environmental, geologic, geophysical, economic, engineering, or other scientific analysis, study, or evaluation.
Prohibition of preleasing activities and lease sales.--The Secretary shall not conduct any preleasing activity or hold a lease sale under this Act in a covered area.
