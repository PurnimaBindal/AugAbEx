''; (3) in subsection (d), by inserting before the period at the end the following: ``, including operation, maintenance, repair, and replacement''; (4) in subsection (e), by striking ``the criteria established pursuant to this section'' and inserting ``the guidelines referred to in subsection (b)''; (5) by striking subsection (f) and inserting the following: ``(f) Report Preparation; Reimbursement.-- ``(1) In general.--Subject to paragraph (2), project sponsors may choose to enter into 1 or more contracts with the Secretary under which the Secretary shall prepare the reports required under this section.
Federal share.--The Federal share of the cost of report preparation by the Secretary described in paragraph (1) shall not exceed 50 percent of the total cost of that preparation.
''; and (6) in subsection (g), by striking ``$2,000,000'' and inserting ``$8,000,000''.
