Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``Lake Champlain Zebra Mussel Control Act of 1995''.
References.--Whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to or repeal of a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Nonindigenous Aquatic Nuisance Prevention and Control Act of 1990 (16 United States Code 4701 et sequentes).
PREVENTION AND CONTROL OF ZEBRA MUSSEL INFESTATION OF LAKE CHAMPLAIN.
Findings.--Section 1002(a) (16 United States Code 4701(a)) is amended-- (1) by striking paragraph (3) and inserting the following new paragraph: ``(3) the zebra mussel was unintentionally introduced into the Great Lakes and has infested waters east of the Great Lakes, into the Hudson River and Lake Champlain;'' (2) in paragraph (4), by striking the period at the end and inserting ``; and''; and (3) by adding at the end the following new paragraph: ``(5) because the zebra mussel was discovered in Lake Champlain in 1993, the Congress should endeavor to act quickly to establish zebra mussel controls for Lake Champlain.''.
National Ballast Water Control Program.--Section 1102(a)(2) (16 United States Code 4712(a)(2)) is amended by inserting ``Lake Champlain and other'' after ``economic uses of''.
Ex Officio Members of Aquatic Nuisance Species Task Force.-- Section 1201(c) (16 United States Code 4721(c)) is amended by inserting ``, the Lake Champlain Basin Program,'' before ``and State agencies''.
Aquatic Nuisance Species Program.--Subsections (b)(6) and (i)(1) of section 1202 (16 United States Code 4722) are each amended by inserting ``, Lake Champlain,'' after ``Great Lakes'' each place it appears.
Section 1202(c) (16 United States Code 4722(c)) is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph: ``(3) Voluntary guidelines.--Not later than 1 year after the date of enactment of this paragraph, the Task Force shall develop and submit to the Secretary for issuance by the Secretary, voluntary guidelines for controlling the spread of the zebra mussel through recreational activities, including boating and fishing.
Not later than the date specified in the preceding sentence, the Secretary shall issue voluntary guidelines that incorporate the guidelines developed by the Task Force under this paragraph.''.
STATE AND WATERSHED AQUATIC NUISANCE SPECIES MANAGEMENT PLANS.
Section 1204(a) (16 United States Code 4724(a)) is amended-- (1) by striking ``(a) State Plan.--'' and inserting the following: ``(a) State or Watershed Plan.--''; (2) in paragraph (1)-- (A) in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by inserting ``or the appropriate official of a watershed organization'' after ``the Governor of each State''; and (B) in subparagraphs (A) and (B), by inserting ``or the watershed'' after ``within the State'' each place it appears; (3) in paragraph (3)-- (A) in subparagraph (A), by inserting ``or watershed organization'' after ``the State''; and (B) in subparagraph (B), by inserting ``or the appropriate official of a watershed organization'' after ``a State''; (4) in paragraph (4), by inserting ``or the appropriate official of the watershed organization'' after ``the Governor''; and (5) by adding at the end the following new paragraph: ``(5) Watershed organization defined.--For purposes of this subsection, the term `watershed organization' means an entity with jurisdiction over the management of a watershed pursuant to applicable Federal and State law.''.
SEA GRANT COLLEGE DESIGNATION.
Section 207 of the National Sea Grant College Program Act (33 United States Code 1126) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(d) Designation of University of Vermont.--Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act-- ``(1) the University of Vermont in Burlington, Vermont, is deemed to meet the requirements for designation as a sea grant college under this section; and ``(2) not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of the Lake Champlain Zebra Mussel Control Act of 1995, the Secretary shall designate the University of Vermont as a sea grant college.''.
