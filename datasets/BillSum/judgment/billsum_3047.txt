This Act may be cited as the ``Chapter 12 Extension and Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 2000''.
Extension of Chapter 12.--Section 149 of title I of division C of Public Law 105-277, as amended by Public Law 106-5 and Public Law 106-70, is amended-- (1) by striking ``July 1, 2000'' each place it appears and inserting ``June 1, 2001''; and (2) in subsection (a)-- (A) by striking ``September 30, 1999'' and inserting ``June 30, 2000''; and (B) by striking ``October 1, 1999'' and inserting ``July 1, 2000''.
Effective Date.--The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect on July 1, 2000.
Temporary Judgeships.-- (1) Appointments.--The following bankruptcy judges shall be appointed in the manner prescribed in section 152(a)(1) of title 28, United States Code, for the appointment of bankruptcy judges provided for in section 152(a)(2) of such title: (A) One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of California.
Four additional bankruptcy judges for the central district of California.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the district of Delaware.
Two additional bankruptcy judges for the southern district of Florida.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the southern district of Georgia.
Two additional bankruptcy judges for the district of Maryland.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of Michigan.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the southern district of Mississippi.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the district of New Jersey.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of New York.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the northern district of New York.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the southern district of New York.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of North Carolina.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of Pennsylvania.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the middle district of Pennsylvania.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the district of Puerto Rico.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the western district of Tennessee.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of Virginia.
Vacancies.--The first vacancy occurring in the office of a bankruptcy judge in each of the judicial districts set forth in paragraph (1) shall not be filled if the vacancy-- (A) results from the death, retirement, resignation, or removal of a bankruptcy judge; and (B) occurs 5 years or more after the appointment date of a bankruptcy judge appointed under paragraph (1).
Extensions.-- (1) In general.--The temporary office of bankruptcy judges authorized for the northern district of Alabama, the district of Delaware, the district of Puerto Rico, the district of South Carolina, and the eastern district of Tennessee under paragraphs (1), (3), (7), (8), and (9) of section 3(a) of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note) are extended until the first vacancy occurring in the office of a bankruptcy judge in the applicable district resulting from the death, retirement, resignation, or removal of a bankruptcy judge and occurring-- (A) 8 years or more after November 8, 1993, with respect to the northern district of Alabama; (B) 10 years or more after October 28, 1993, with respect to the district of Delaware; (C) 8 years or more after August 29, 1994, with respect to the district of Puerto Rico; (D) 8 years or more after June 27, 1994, with respect to the district of South Carolina; and (E) 8 years or more after November 23, 1993, with respect to the eastern district of Tennessee.
Applicability of other provisions.--Except as provided in paragraph (1), section 3 of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note) shall continue to apply to the temporary office of bankruptcy judges referred to in such paragraph.
