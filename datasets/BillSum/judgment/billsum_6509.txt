LIQUIDATION OR RELIQUIDATION OF CERTAIN LINE ITEMS IN ENTRIES OF TAILORED GARMENTS FROM COSTA RICA.
Liquidation or Reliquidation of Line Items in Entries.-- Notwithstanding sections 514 and 520 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 United States Code 1514 and 1520), or any other provision of law, United States Reports Customs and Border Protection shall, not later than 180 days after the date of the enactment of this Act-- (1) liquidate or reliquidate at the rate of zero percent the goods entered under the entry summary (CBP Form 7501) line items listed in subsection (b) containing tailored garments from Costa Rica; and (2) refund to the importer of record the amount of excess duties previously collected on those line items.
