Limitations.-- ``(1) In general.--Subject to paragraph (2), of the amounts appropriated under subsection (a) for a fiscal year-- ``(A) not more than 25 percent may be used for the activities of the Office of Environmental Education; ``(B) not more than 25 percent may be used for the operation of the environmental education and training program; ``(C) not less than 40 percent shall be used for environmental education grants; and ``(D) 10 percent shall be used for the Foundation for Environmental Education.

The amendments made by this Act shall take effect as of the later of-- (1) October 1, 1996; or (2) the date of enactment of this Act.
