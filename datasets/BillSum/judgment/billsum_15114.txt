Children with disabilities.--Section 8003(d)(1) of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 United States Code 7703(d)(1)) is amended to read as follows: ``(1) In general.--The Secretary shall pay to each eligible local educational agency for a fiscal year the amount equal to the difference between-- ``(A) the amount equal to the product of-- ``(i) the number of children described in subparagraphs (A)(ii), (B), (C), and (D) of subsection (a)(1) who are eligible to receive services under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 United States Code 1400 et seq.

Authorization of Appropriations.--Section 8014 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 United States Code 7714) is repealed.
Rule of Construction.--Title VIII of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 United States Code 7701 et sequentes), as amended by this Act, is further amended by adding at the end the following: ``SEC.
``Nothing in this title shall be interpreted to entitle any individual to assistance under any program, project, or activity of a local education agency, State agency, or other governmental entity funded under this title.''.
The amendments made by this Act shall take effect on October 1, 2003, or the date of the enactment of this Act, whichever occurs later.
