This Act may be cited as the ``International Commerce Enhancement Act''.
Except as otherwise specifically provided in this Act, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed as the amendment or repeal of a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Arms Export Control Act (22 United States Code 2751 et sequentes).
