This Act may be cited as the ``Timber Sale Financial Accountability Act of 1995''.
RELIANCE ON FOREST SERVICE TIMBER SALE RECEIPTS TO FUND TIMBER SALES.
Timber Receipts To Cover Costs.--Section 14 of the National Forest Management Act of 1976 (16 United States Code 472a) is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection: ``(j) Use of Timber Receipts To Cover Sale Costs.-- ``(1) Timber receipts fund.--There is hereby established in the Treasury of the United States a fund to be known as the `National Forest System Timber Receipts Fund' and consisting of receipts from the sale of trees, portions of trees, and forest products located on National Forest System lands.
The Fund shall consist of two accounts, one account for units of the National Forest System located east of the Mississippi River and the other account for units located west of the Mississippi River and in Alaska.
Amounts in the two accounts may not be commingled.
Amounts in the Fund are to be available until expended.
Use of fund.--Amounts in an account of the Fund shall be available, in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts, to the Secretary of Agriculture for the purpose of covering the cost to the United States-- ``(A) for design, engineering, and supervision of the construction of roads needed in connection with timber sales conducted under this section in units of the National Forest System covered by the account; and ``(B) for Forest Service preparation, advertisement, offering, awarding, and supervision of the operation of such timber sales.
Reliance on fund.--The Fund shall be the sole source of amounts for the activities specified in paragraph (2), and amounts otherwise appropriated or made available to the Secretary or the Forest Service in appropriation Acts may not be obligated or expended for such activities.
Effect on other laws.--Except as provided in paragraph (5), the following provisions of law shall not apply to receipts from the sale of trees, portions of trees, and forest products located on National Forest System lands: ``(A) The fifth paragraph under the heading ``FOREST SERVICE'' in the Act of March 4, 1907 (34 United States Statutes at Large 1270; 16 United States Code 499).
The fourth sentence in section 9 of the Act of June 7, 1924 (43 United States Statutes at Large 655; 16 United States Code 499; commonly known as the Clarke-McNary Act).
The sixth paragraph under the heading ``FOREST SERVICE'' in the Act of May 23, 1908 (35 United States Statutes at Large 260; 16 United States Code 500).
Section 13 of the Act of March 1, 1911 (36 United States Statutes at Large 963; 16 United States Code 500; commonly known as the Weeks Act).
The sixth paragraph under the heading ``administrative provisions, forest service'' in title II of the Department of the Interior and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 1993 (Public Law 102-381; 106 United States Statutes at Large 1400; 16 United States Code 500 note).
The fourteenth paragraph under the heading ``FOREST SERVICE'' of the Act of March 4, 1913 (37 United States Statutes at Large 843; 16 United States Code 501).
Excess amounts.--Amounts in the Fund determined by the Secretary to be in excess of the cost of accomplishing the activities specified in paragraph (2) shall be transferred to miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States.
Amounts transferred shall be considered as moneys received from the national forests within the meaning of the sixth paragraph under the heading ``FOREST SERVICE'' in the Act of May 23, 1908 (35 United States Statutes at Large 260; 16 United States Code 500), section 13 of the Act of March 1, 1911 (36 United States Statutes at Large 963; 16 United States Code 500; commonly known as the Weeks Act), and the fourteenth paragraph under the heading ``FOREST SERVICE'' of the Act of March 4, 1913 (37 United States Statutes at Large 843; 16 United States Code 501).''.
Conforming Amendments.--Such section is further amended-- (1) in subsection (h)-- (A) by striking ``in a designated fund'' in the third sentence and inserting ``in the National Forest System Timber Receipts Fund established under subsection (j),''; and (B) by striking the last sentence, including the provisos; and (2) in subsection (i)(2), by striking ``, and such additional sums as may be appropriated for the construction of roads''.
