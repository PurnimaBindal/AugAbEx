&lt;greek-th&gt; x The Congress finds the following:&lt;greek-th&gt; x (1) The use of children in the production of exploitive child modeling, including on Internet websites, in photographs, films, videos, and other visual depictions, is a form of child abuse that can result in physical and psychological harm to the children involved.

&lt;greek-th&gt; x (3) The purpose of exploitive child modeling is to satisfy the demand of pedophiles.

&lt;greek-th&gt; x (6) Over 70 percent of convicted pedophiles have used child pornography or exploitive child modeling depictions to whet their sexual appetites.

&lt;greek-th&gt; x (7) These exploitive exhibitions of children are unacceptable by social standards and lead to a direct harm to the children involved.
