This Act may be cited as the ``Freedom to Export to Cuba Act of 2017''.
REMOVAL OF PROVISIONS RESTRICTING TRADE AND OTHER RELATIONS WITH CUBA.
Authority for Embargo.-- (1) In general.--Section 620(a) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 United States Code 2370(a)) is amended by striking ``(1) No assistance'' and all that follows through ``(2) Except'' and inserting ``Except''.
Conforming amendment.--Section 1709 of the Cuban Democracy Act of 1992 (22 United States Code 6008) is amended by striking ``section 620(a)(2)'' and inserting ``section 620(a)''.
Trading With the Enemy Act.-- (1) In general.--The authorities conferred upon the President by section 5(b) of the Trading With the Enemy Act (50 United States Code 4305(b)), which were being exercised with respect to Cuba on July 1, 1977, as a result of a national emergency declared by the President before that date, and are being exercised on the day before the date of the enactment of this Act, may not be relied upon on or after such date of enactment to continue the imposition of direct restrictions on trade with Cuba.
Regulations.--Any regulation that imposes direct restrictions on trade with Cuba in effect on the day before the date of the enactment of this Act pursuant to the exercise of authorities described in paragraph (1) shall cease to be effective for that purpose on and after such date of enactment.
Exercise of Authorities Under Other Provisions of Law.-- (1) Removal of prohibitions.--Any prohibition on exports to Cuba that is in effect on the day before the date of the enactment of this Act under the Export Administration Act of 1979 (50 United States Code 4601 et sequentes) (as continued in effect pursuant to the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 United States Code 1701 et sequentes)) shall cease to be effective on and after such date of enactment.
Authority for new restrictions.--The President may, on and after the date of the enactment of this Act-- (A) impose export controls with respect to Cuba under section 5, 6(j), 6(l), or 6(m) of the Export Administration Act of 1979 (as continued in effect pursuant to the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 United States Code 1701 et sequentes)); and (B) exercise the authorities the President has under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act with respect to Cuba pursuant to a declaration of national emergency required by that Act that is made on account of an unusual and extraordinary threat to the national security, foreign policy, or economy of the United States, that did not exist before the date of the enactment of this Act.
Cuban Democracy Act.-- (1) In general.--The Cuban Democracy Act of 1992 (22 United States Code 6001 et sequentes) is amended-- (A) by striking section 1704 (22 United States Code 6003); (B) in section 1705(e) (22 United States Code 6004(e))-- (i) by striking paragraph (5); and (ii) by redesignating paragraph (6) as paragraph (5); (C) by striking section 1706 (22 United States Code 6005); and (D) by striking section 1708 (22 United States Code 6007).
Conforming amendment.--Paragraph (3) of section 204(b) of the Cuban Liberty and Democratic Solidarity (LIBERTAD) Act of 1996 (22 United States Code 6064(b)) is amended to read as follows: ``(3) section 1705(d) of the Cuban Democracy Act of 1992 (22 United States Code 6004(d));''.
Cuban Liberty and Democratic Solidarity Act.-- (1) In general.--The Cuban Liberty and Democratic Solidarity (LIBERTAD) Act of 1996 (22 United States Code 6021 et sequentes) is amended-- (A) by striking sections 101 through 108; (B) in section 109(a) (22 United States Code 6039(a)), by striking ``(including section 102 of this Act)''; (C) by striking sections 110 through 116; and (D) by striking title II (22 United States Code 6061 et sequentes).
Conforming amendment.--Section 606 of the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 (Public Law 104-208; 8 United States Code 1255 note) is repealed.
