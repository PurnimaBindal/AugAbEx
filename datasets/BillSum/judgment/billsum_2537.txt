ELIMINATION OF THE NATIONAL EDUCATION STANDARDS AND IMPROVEMENT COUNCIL.
Amendment.--Part B of title II of the Goals 2000: Educate America Act (20 United States Code 5841 et sequentes) is amended to read as follows: ``PART B--NATIONAL STANDARDS ``SEC.
PROHIBITION OF FEDERAL FUNDING FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF NATIONAL STANDARDS.
``No Federal agency shall expend Federal funds for the development or dissemination of model or national content standards, national student performance standards, or national opportunity-to-learn standards.''.
Effective Date.--The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if enacted on January 1, 1995.
