Temporary Judgeships.-- (1) Appointments.--The following bankruptcy judges shall be appointed in the manner prescribed in section 152(a)(1) of title 28, United States Code, for the appointment of bankruptcy judges provided for in section 152(a)(2) of such title: (A) One additional bankruptcy judge for the southern district of Georgia.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the district of Maryland.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the eastern district of Mississippi.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the northern district of Mississippi.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the middle district of New York.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the middle district of Pennsylvania.
One additional bankruptcy judge for the district of Puerto Rico.
Vacancies.--The first vacancy occurring in the office of bankruptcy judge in each of the judicial districts set forth in paragraph (1)-- (i) occurring 5 years or more after the appointment date of the bankruptcy judge appointed under paragraph (1) to such office; and (ii) resulting from the death, retirement, resignation, or removal of a bankruptcy judge; shall not be filled.
Extensions.-- (1) In general.--The temporary office of bankruptcy judges authorized for the northern district of Alabama and the eastern district of Tennessee under paragraphs (1) and (9) of section 3(a) of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note) are extended until the first vacancy occurring in the office of a bankruptcy judge in the applicable district resulting from the death, retirement, resignation, or removal of a bankruptcy judge and occurring 5 years after the date of the enactment of this Act.
Applicability of other provisions.--All other provisions of section 3 of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note) remain applicable to the temporary office of bankruptcy judges referred to in this subsection.
