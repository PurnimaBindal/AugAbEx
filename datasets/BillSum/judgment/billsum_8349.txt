This Act may be cited as the ``Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Improvement Act of 2005''.
AMENDMENTS TO NEOTROPICAL MIGRATORY BIRD CONSERVATION ACT.
Findings.--Section 2(1) of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6101(1)) is amended by inserting ``, but breed in Canada and the United States'' after ``the Caribbean''.
Purposes.--Section 3(2) of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6102(2)) is amended by inserting ``Canada,'' after ``United States,''.
Definition of Caribbean.--Section 4 of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6103) is amended-- (1) by redesignating paragraph (3) as paragraph (4); (2) by striking paragraph (1) and inserting the following: ``(1) Caribbean.--The term `Caribbean' includes Puerto Rico and the United States Virgin Islands.
''; and (3) by inserting after paragraph (2) the following: ``(3) Fund.--The term `Fund' means the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Fund established by section 9(a).''.
Project Proposals.--Section 5(c)(2) of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6104(c)(2)) is amended by inserting ``Canada,'' after ``United States,''.
Cost Sharing.--Section 5(e) of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6104(e)) is amended-- (1) in paragraph (1), by striking ``25 percent'' and inserting ``50 percent''; and (2) in paragraph (2), by striking subparagraph (B) and inserting the following: ``(B) Form of payment.-- ``(i) Projects in the united states and canada.--The non-Federal share required to be paid for a project carried out in the United States or Canada shall be paid in cash.
Projects in latin america and the caribbean.--The non-Federal share required to be paid for a project carried out in Latin America or the Caribbean may be paid in cash or in kind.''.
Report.--Section 8 of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6107) is amended-- (1) by striking ``October 1, 2002,'' and inserting ``2 years after the date of enactment of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Improvement Act of 2005,''; (2) by striking ``this Act, including recommendations'' and inserting ``this Act that includes-- ``(1) recommendations''; (3) by striking the period at the end and inserting ``; and''; and (4) by adding at the end the following: ``(2) a description of the activities of the advisory group convened under section 7(b).''.
Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Fund.-- (1) In general.--Section 9 of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6108) is amended-- (A) by striking the section heading and all that follows through subsection (b) and inserting the following: ``SEC.
NEOTROPICAL MIGRATORY BIRD CONSERVATION FUND.
Establishment.--There is established in the Treasury a separate account to be known as the `Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Fund', which shall consist of amounts deposited in the Fund by the Secretary of the Treasury under subsection (b).
Deposits in the Fund.--The Secretary of the Treasury shall deposit into the Fund-- ``(1) all amounts received by the Secretary in the form of donations under subsection (d); and ``(2) other amounts appropriated to the Fund.
''; and (B) in subsections (c) and (d), by striking ``Account'' each place it appears and inserting ``Fund''.
Administrative expenses.--Section 9(c)(2) of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6108(c)(2)) is amended by striking ``$80,000'' and inserting ``$150,000''.
Transfer.--The Secretary of the Treasury shall transfer to the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Fund amounts that were in the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Account immediately before the date of enactment of this Act.
Authorization of Appropriations.--Section 10 of the Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 United States Code 6109) is amended to read as follows: ``SEC.
In General.--There are authorized to be appropriated to the Fund to carry out this Act-- ``(1) $5,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2006 and 2007; ``(2) $8,000,000 for fiscal year 2008; and ``(3) $10,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2009 and 2010.
Availability.--Amounts made available under this section shall remain available until expended.
Allocation.--Of amounts made available under this section for a fiscal year, not less than 75 percent shall be expended for projects carried out outside the United States.
