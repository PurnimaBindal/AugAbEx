This Act may be cited as the ``Fiscal Year 2010 Federal Aviation Administration Extension Act''.

Ticket Taxes.-- (1) Persons.--Clause (ii) of section 4261(j)(1)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking ``September 30, 2009'' and inserting ``December 31, 2009''.
