This Act may be cited as the ``Senior Investor Protections Enhancement Act of 2009''.
In General.--In this Act, the following definitions shall apply: (1) Senior.--The term ``senior'' means an individual who is 62 years of age or older.
Securities laws.--The term ``securities laws'' means the Securities Act of 1933 (15 United States Code 77b et sequentes), the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 United States Code 78a et sequentes), the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 United States Code 80a et sequentes), and the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (15 United States Code 80b et sequentes).
Application of Senior Definition.-- (1) Securities act of 1933.--Section 2(a) of the Securities Act of 1933 (15 United States Code 77b(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(17) The term `senior' means an individual who is 62 years of age or older.''.
Securities exchange act of 1934.--Section 3(a) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 United States Code 78c(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(65) The term `senior' means an individual who is 62 years of age or older.''.
Investment company act of 1940.--Section 2(a) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 United States Code 80a-2(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(54) The term `senior' means an individual who is 62 years of age or older.''.
Investment advisers act of 1940.--Section 202(a) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (15 United States Code 80b-2(a)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(29) The term `senior' means an individual who is 62 years of age or older.''.
ENHANCED PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS OF SECURITIES ACT OF 1933.
Civil Actions.--Section 20(d)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933 (15 United States Code 77t(d)(2)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``(D) Special rule for seniors.--Notwithstanding subparagraphs (A), (B), and (C), if a person commits a violation described in paragraph (1), and the violation is directed toward, targets, or is committed against a person who, at the time of the violation, is a senior, the Commission, in addition to any other applicable civil penalty, may impose a civil penalty of not more than $50,000 for each such violation.''.

DIRECTIVE TO THE UNITED STATES SENTENCING COMMISSION.
In General.--Pursuant to its authority under section 994(p) of title 28, United States Code, and in accordance with this section, the United States Sentencing Commission shall review and amend the Federal sentencing guidelines and policy statements to ensure that the guideline offense levels and enhancements appropriately punish violations of the securities laws against seniors.
Requirements.--In carrying out this section, the United States Sentencing Commission shall-- (1) ensure that section 2B1.1 and 2C1.1 of the Federal sentencing guidelines (and any successors thereto) apply to and punish offenses in which the victim of a violation of the securities laws is a senior; (2) ensure reasonable consistency with other relevant directives, provisions of the Federal sentencing guidelines, and statutory provisions; (3) make any necessary and conforming changes to the Federal sentencing guidelines, in accordance with the amendments made by this Act; and (4) ensure that the Federal sentencing guidelines adequately meet the purposes of sentencing set forth in section 3553(a)(2) of title 18, United States Code.
