INCORPORATION OF BANKS; CORPORATE POWERS.
Section 12 of the Federal Home Loan Bank Act is amended to read as follows: ``SEC.
INCORPORATION OF BANKS; CORPORATE POWERS.
Organizational Certificates.-- ``(1) Custodianship.--The Finance Board shall be the custodian of the organizational certificates of the Federal home loan banks previously filed with the Federal Home Loan Bank Board (as in existence before the end of the 60-day period beginning on the date of the enactment of the Financial Institutions Reform, Recovery, and Enforcement Act of 1989) or the Finance Board.
Replacement of lost or destroyed certificate.--If the organizational certificate of a Federal home loan bank is lost or destroyed, the board of directors of the bank shall provide the Finance Board with a substitute certificate containing such information as the Finance Board may require.
Amendment in case of merger, combination, or consolidation.--If 2 or more Federal home loan banks merge, combine, or otherwise consolidate, the resulting bank shall provide the Finance Board with an amended organizational certificate containing such information as the Finance Board may require.
Corporate Status.--Each Federal home loan bank shall be a corporation.
Powers.--Each Federal home loan bank shall have the following powers: ``(1) To adopt, alter, and use a corporate seal.
To purchase or lease and hold or dispose of such real estate as may be necessary or convenient for the transaction of the business of the bank.
To sue and be sued, to complain, and to defend, in any court of competent jurisdiction.
To select, employ, and fix the compensation of such officers, employees, attorneys, and agents as the board of directors determines to be necessary for the transaction of the business of the bank.
To define the duties of, and require bonds of, officers, employees, attorneys, and agents of the bank and fix the penalties of any such bonds.
To dismiss at pleasure officers, employees, attorneys, and agents of the bank.
By the board of directors, to prescribe, amend, and repeal bylaws and policies governing the manner in which the affairs of the bank may be administered.
Such incidental powers as are not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act and are customary and usual in corporations generally.
Prohibition on Excessive Compensation.-- ``(1) In general.--The Finance Board shall prohibit the Federal home loan banks from providing compensation to any employee of the bank that is not reasonable and comparable with compensation for employment in other similar businesses (including other financial institutions or major financial services companies) involving similar duties and responsibilities.
Limitation on Finance Board's Authority to Set Compensation.--In carrying out paragraph (1), the Finance Board may not prescribe or set a specific level or range of compensation.''.

Home Mortgage Loan Defined.--Section 2 of the Federal Home Loan Bank Act (12 United States Code 1422) is amended by striking paragraphs (5) and (6) and inserting the following new paragraph: ``(5) Home mortgage loan.-- ``(A) In general.--The term `home mortgage loan' means a loan made by a member or a nonmember borrower upon the security of-- ``(i) a mortgage, deed of trust or other security arrangement upon qualified real estate, in fee simple; or ``(ii) on a qualified leasehold-- ``(I) under a lease which has a period to maturity of not less than 99 years and is renewable; or ``(II) under a lease having a period of not less than 50 years to run from the date the mortgage, deed of trust, or other security arrangement was executed.
Certain loans included.--The term `home mortgage loan' includes-- ``(i) such classes of first liens as are commonly given to secure advances on real estate, under the laws of the State in which the real estate is located, by institutions authorized under this Act to become members of a Federal home loan bank; and ``(ii) the credit instruments, if any, secured by such liens.
Qualified real estate; qualified leasehold.-- The terms `qualified real estate' and `qualified leasehold' mean real estate upon which is located, or which comprises or includes, 1 or more homes or other dwelling units.
Definition of further terms.--The Finance Board may define any term used in this paragraph.''.
