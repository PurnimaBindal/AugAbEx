ESTABLISHMENT OF UNITS OF THE NATIONAL GUARD IN AMERICAN SAMOA.
Title 32 (National Guard) Amendments.-- (1) Inclusion of american samoa in definition of territory.--Section 101(1) of title 32, United States Code, is amended by striking ``includes Guam'' and inserting ``means American Samoa, Guam,''.
Branches and organizations.--Section 103 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``and each Territory''.
Units: location; organization; command.--Section 104 of such title is amended-- (A) in subsections (a) and (c), by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``and each Territory''; and (B) in subsection (d), by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or a Territory''.
Availability of appropriations.--Section 107(b) of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or a Territory''.
Maintenance of other troops.--Section 109 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or a Territory''.
Adjutants general.--Section 314 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' both places it appears and inserting ``and each Territory''.
Detail of regular members.--Section 315 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' each place it appears and inserting ``and each Territory''.
Termination of appointment.--Section 324(b) of such title is amended by striking ``or the District of Columbia, Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``, the District of Columbia, or the Territory''.
Relief from national guard duty when ordered to active duty.--Section 325(a) of such title is amended-- (A) by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' the first two places it appears and inserting ``the Territory,''; and (B) by striking ``, Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' the third place it appears and inserting ``or Territory''.
Composition of courts-martial.--Section 326 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
Convening authority of courts-martial.--Section 327(a) of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
Governor's authority.--Section 328(a) of such title is amended by striking ``, Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
Training generally.--Section 501(b) of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``and each Territory''.
Support of training operations and training missions.--Section 502(f)(2)(B)(i) of such title is amended by striking ``or possessions'' and inserting ``or Territory or possession''.
Participation in field exercises.--Section 503(b) of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
National guard schools and small arms competitions.-- Section 504(b) of such title is amended by striking ``territory'' and inserting ``Territory''.
Attendance at army and air force schools.--Section 505 of such title is amended in the first sentence by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
National guard youth challenge program.--Section 509(l)(1) of such title is amended by striking ``the territories'' and inserting ``each Territory''.
Issue of supplies.--Section 702(a) of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
Purchases of supplies from army or air force.--Section 703 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' both places it appears and inserting ``or Territory''.
Accountability.--Section 704 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``or Territory''.
Property and fiscal officers.--Section 708 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' both places it appears and inserting ``and Territory''.
Employment, use, and status of technicians.--Section 709(a)(3)(C) of such title is amended by striking ``or possessions'' and inserting ``, a Territory, or possession''.
Accountability for property issued to the national guard.--Section 710 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands'' each place it appears and inserting ``or a Territory''.
Disposition of obsolete or condemned property.-- Section 711 of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, and the Virgin Islands'' and inserting ``and Territory''.
Disposition of proceeds of condemned stores issued to national guard.--Section 712(1) of such title is amended by striking ``Guam, or the Virgin Islands,'' and inserting ``or a Territory''.
Settlements for property loss, personal injury, or death.--Section 715(c) of such title is amended by striking ``or Puerto Rico'' and inserting ``, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, or a Territory''.
Title 10 Amendments.-- (1) Militia duty exemptions.--Section 312(a)(2) of such title is amended by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``Guam''.
Detail of army national guard as students, observers, and investigators at educational institutions, industrial plants, and hospitals.--Section 4301(c) of such title is amended by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``Guam''.
Detail of air national guard as students, observers, and investigators at educational institutions, industrial plants, and hospitals.--Section 9301(c) of such title is amended by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``Guam''.
Definition of state for division e.--Section 10001 of such title is amended by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``and Guam''.
Detail for organizing, administering, etc., reserve components.--Section 12310(c)(7) of such title is amended by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``Guam''.
Title 37 Definitions.--Section 101 of title 10, United States Code, is amended-- (1) in paragraph (7), by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``Guam''; and (2) in paragraph (9), by inserting ``American Samoa,'' before ``Guam''.
