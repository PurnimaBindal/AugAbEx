SOCIAL WORK TRAINING PROGRAM.
Subpart 2 of part E of title VII of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 295 et sequentes) is amended-- (1) by redesignating section 770 as section 770A; (2) by inserting after section 769, the following: ``SEC.
SOCIAL WORK TRAINING PROGRAM.
Training Generally.--The Secretary may make grants to, or enter into contracts with, any public or nonprofit private hospital, school offering programs in social work, or to or with a public or private nonprofit entity (which the Secretary has determined is capable of carrying out such grant or contract)-- ``(1) to plan, develop, and operate, or participate in, an approved social work training program (including an approved residency or internship program) for students, interns, residents, or practicing physicians; ``(2) to provide financial assistance (in the form of traineeships and fellowships) to students, interns, residents, practicing physicians, or other individuals, who are in need thereof, who are participants in any such program, and who plan to specialize or work in the practice of social work; ``(3) to plan, develop, and operate a program for the training of individuals who plan to teach in social work training programs; and ``(4) to provide financial assistance (in the form of traineeships and fellowships) to individuals who are participants in any such program and who plan to teach in a social work training program.
Academic Administrative Units.-- ``(1) In general.--The Secretary may make grants to or enter into contracts with schools offering programs in social work to meet the costs of projects to establish, maintain, or improve academic administrative units (which may be departments, divisions, or other units) to provide clinical instruction in social work.
Preference in making awards.--In making awards of grants and contracts under paragraph (1), the Secretary shall give preference to any qualified applicant for such an award that agrees to expend the award for the purpose of-- ``(A) establishing an academic administrative unit for programs in social work; or ``(B) substantially expanding the programs of such a unit.
Duration of Award.--The period during which payments are made to an entity from an award of a grant or contract under subsection (a) may not exceed 5 years.
The provision of such payments shall be subject to annual approval by the Secretary of the payments and subject to the availability of appropriations for the fiscal year involved to make the payments.
Funding.-- ``(1) Authorization of appropriations.--For the purpose of carrying out this section, there is authorized to be appropriated $10,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 2004 through 2006.
Allocation.--Of the amounts appropriated under paragraph (1) for a fiscal year, the Secretary shall make available not less than 20 percent for awards of grants and contracts under subsection (b).
