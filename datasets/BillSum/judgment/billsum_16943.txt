Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``Miscellaneous Tariff Act of 1993''.
Reference.--Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a chapter, subchapter, note, additional United States Reports note, heading, subheading, or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a chapter, subchapter, note, additional United States Reports note, heading, subheading, or other provision of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States.
