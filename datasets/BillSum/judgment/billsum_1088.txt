This Act may be cited as the ``National Trails System Willing Seller Act''.
AUTHORITY TO ACQUIRE LAND FROM WILLING SELLERS FOR CERTAIN TRAILS.
Oregon National Historic Trail.--Section 5(a)(3) of the National Trails System Act (16 United States Code 1244(a)(3)) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``No land or interest in land outside the exterior boundaries of any federally administered area may be acquired by the Federal Government for the trail except with the consent of the owner of the land or interest in land.
