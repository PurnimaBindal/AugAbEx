This Act may be cited as the ``Civil Rights Amendments Act of 1993''.
AMENDMENTS TO CIVIL RIGHTS ACT OF 1964.
Public Accommodations.--(1) Section 201(a) of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 United States Code 2000a(a)) is amended by striking ``religion,'' and inserting ``religion, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Section 202 of such Act (42 United States Code 2000a-1) is amended by striking ``religion,'' and inserting ``religion, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Public Facilities.--Section 301(a) of such Act (42 United States Code 2000b(a)) is amended by striking ``religion,'' and inserting ``religion, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Federally Assisted Programs.--Section 601 of such Act (42 United States Code 2000d) is amended by striking ``color,'' and inserting ``color, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Equal Employment Opportunities.--(1) Sections 703(a), 703(b), 703(c), 703(d), 703(e), 703(h), 703(j), 704(b), 706(g), and 717(a) of such Act (42 United States Code 2000e-2(a), 2000e-2(b), 2000e-2(c), 2000e-2(d), 2000e-2(e), 2000e-2(h), 2000e-2(j), 2000e-3(b), 2000e-5(g), and 2000e- 16(a)) are amended by striking ``sex,'' each place it appears and inserting ``sex, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Section 717(c) of such Act (42 United States Code 2000e-16(c)) is amended by striking ``sex'' and inserting ``sex, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Section 703(h) of such Act (42 United States Code 2000e-2(h)) is amended by striking ``sex'' the first place it appears and inserting ``sex, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
The heading of section 703 of such Act is amended by striking ``sex,'' and inserting ``sex, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Intervention by Attorney General in Civil Rights Cases.-- Section 902 of such Act (42 United States Code 2000h-2) is amended by striking ``sex'' and inserting ``sex, affectional or sexual orientation,''.
Definition; Rules of Interpretation.--Title XI of such Act (42 United States Code 2000h et sequentes) is amended by adding at the end the following new section: ``affectional or sexual orientation ``Sec.
Definition.--For purposes of titles II, III, VI, VII, and IX of this Act, the term `affectional or sexual orientation' means male or female homosexuality, heterosexuality, and bisexuality by orientation or practice, by and between consenting adults.
Rules of Interpretation.--(1) Nothing in this Act shall be construed to permit or require-- ``(A) that a finding of discrimination on the basis of affectional or sexual orientation be based on any statistical differences in the incidence of persons of a particular affectional or sexual orientation in the general population as opposed to the incidence of such persons in the activity concerned; or ``(B) the use of any quota as a remedy for discrimination on the basis of affectional or sexual orientation.
Nothing in this Act shall be construed to require any person to disclose a personal affectional or sexual orientation.''.

Section 805 of such Act (42 United States Code 3605) is amended by striking ``religion,'' each place it appears and inserting ``religion, affectional or sexual orientation (as such term is defined in section 802(p)),''.

Prevention of Intimidation.--Section 901 of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 (42 United States Code 3631) is amended by striking ``religion,'' each place it appears and inserting ``religion, affectional or sexual orientation (as such term is defined in section 802(p)),''.
Definition.--Section 802 of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 (42 United States Code 3602) is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection: ``(p) `Affectional or sexual orientation' means male or female homosexuality, heterosexuality, and bisexuality by orientation or practice, by and between consenting adults.''.
Rules of Interpretation.--(1) Title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 (42 United States Code 3601 et sequentes) is amended by adding at the end the following new section: ``rules of interpretation regarding affectional or sexual orientation ``Sec.
Findings of Discrimination; Quotas.--Nothing in this Act shall be construed to permit or require-- ``(1) that a finding of discrimination on the basis of affectional or sexual orientation be based on any statistical differences in the incidence of persons of a particular affectional or sexual orientation in the general population as opposed to the incidence of such persons in the activity concerned; or ``(2) the use of any quota as a remedy for discrimination on the basis of affectional or sexual orientation.
Protection of Privacy Rights.--Nothing in this Act shall be construed to require any person to disclose a personal affectional or sexual orientation.''.
Title IX of such Act (42 United States Code 3631 et sequentes) is amended by adding at the end the following new section: ``application of rules of interpretation regarding affectional or sexual orientation ``Sec.
The provisions of this title are subject to the rules of interpretation described in section 821 of this Act.
