SHORT TITLE; REFERENCES TO HIGHER EDUCATION ACT OF 1965.
Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``Higher Education Assistance Improvement Act''.
References to Higher Education Act of 1965.--Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or reference is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or reference to, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Higher Education Act of 1965.
