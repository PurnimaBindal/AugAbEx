This Act may be cited as the ``Native American Housing Enhancement Act of 2004''.
Congress finds that-- (1) there exist-- (A) a unique relationship between the Government of the United States and the governments of Indian tribes; and (B) a unique Federal trust responsibility to Indian people; (2) Native Americans experience some of the worst housing conditions in the country, with-- (A) 32.6 percent of Native homes being overcrowded; (B) 33 percent lacking adequate solid waste management systems; (C) 8 percent lacking a safe indoor water supply; and (D) approximately 90,000 Native families who are homeless or underhoused; (3) the poverty rate for Native Americans is twice that of the rest of the population of the United States; (4) the population growth of Native Americans that began in the latter part of the 20th century increased the need for Federal housing services; (5)(A) under the requirements of the Native American Housing Assistance and Self-Determination Act of 1996 (25 United States Code 4101 et sequentes), members of Indian tribes are given preference for housing programs; (B) a primary purpose of the Act is to allow Indian tribes to leverage funds with other Federal and private funds; (C) the Department of Agriculture has been a significant funding source for housing for Indian tribes; and (D) to allow assistance provided under the Act and assistance provided by the Secretary of Agriculture under other law to be combined to meet the severe housing needs of Indian tribes, the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1471 et sequentes) should be amended to allow for the preference referred to in subparagraph (A) by granting an exemption from title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 United States Code 2000d et sequentes) and title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 (42 United States Code 3601 et sequentes) to tribes who comply with the Indian Civil Rights Act (title II of the Civil Rights Act of 1968; 25 United States Code 1301-1303), or who are acting under the Native American Housing Assistance and Self-Determination Act of 1996 (25 United States Code 4131(b)); and (6) section 457 of the Cranston-Gonzales National Affordable Housing Act (42 United States Code 12899f) should be amended to include Indian tribes, tribally designated housing entities, or other agencies that primarily serve Indians as eligible applicants for YouthBuild grants.
TREATMENT OF PROGRAM INCOME.
Section 104(a)(2) of the Native American Housing Assistance and Self-Determination Act of 1996 (25 United States Code 4114(a)(2)) is amended by inserting ``restrict access to or'' after ``not''.
Title V of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 United States Code 1471 et sequentes) is amended by adding at the end the following: ``SEC.
In General.--Federally recognized Indian Tribes who exercise powers of self-government (or their instrumentalities) shall comply with the Indian Civil Rights Act (title II of the Civil Rights Act of 1968; 25 United States Code 1301-1303) when receiving assistance under this title.
Exemption.--Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 United States Code 2000d et sequentes) and title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 (42 United States Code 3601 et sequentes) shall not apply to-- ``(1) tribes covered by the Indian Civil Rights Act (title II of the Civil Rights Act of 1968; 25 United States Code 1301-1303); or ``(2) tribes acting under section 201(b) of the Native American Housing Assistance and Self-Determination Act of 1996 (25 United States Code 4131(b)).''.
ELIGIBILITY OF INDIAN TRIBES FOR YOUTHBUILD GRANTS.
Section 457(2) of the Cranston-Gonzales National Affordable Housing Act (42 United States Code 12899f(2)) is amended-- (1) in subparagraph (F), by striking ``and'' at the end; (2) by redesignating subparagraph (G) as sub-paragraph (H); and (3) by inserting after subparagraph (F) the following: ``(G) an Indian tribe, tribally designated housing entity (as defined in section 4 of the Native American Housing Assistance and Self- Determination Act of 1996 (25 United States Code 4103)), or other agency primarily serving Indians; and''.
FEDERAL GUARANTEES FOR FINANCING FOR TRIBAL HOUSING ACTIVITIES.
Section 601 of the Native American Housing Assistance and Self- Determination Act of 1996 (25 United States Code 4191) is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection: ``(d) Limitation on Percentage.--A guarantee made under this title shall guarantee repayment of 95 percent of the unpaid principal and interest due on the notes or other obligations guaranteed.
