This Act may be cited as the ``Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 2003''.

Authorization for Additional Temporary Bankruptcy Judgeships.-- The following judgeship positions shall be filled in the manner prescribed in section 152(a)(1) of title 28, United States Code, for the appointment of bankruptcy judges provided for in section 152(a)(2) of such title: (1) One additional bankruptcy judgeship for the district of Puerto Rico.

One additional bankruptcy judgeship for the district of Maryland.
One additional bankruptcy judgeship for the northern district of Mississippi.
One additional bankruptcy judgeship for the southern district of Mississippi.
One additional bankruptcy judgeship for the southern district of Georgia.
Vacancies.-- (1) In general.--The first vacancy occurring in the office of bankruptcy judge in each of the judicial districts set forth in subsection (a)-- (A) occurring 5 years or more after the appointment date of the bankruptcy judge appointed under subsection (a) to such office; and (B) resulting from the death, retirement, resignation, or removal of a bankruptcy judge; shall not be filled.
Term expiration.--In the case of a vacancy resulting from the expiration of the term of a bankruptcy judge not described in paragraph (1), that judge shall be eligible for reappointment as a bankruptcy judge in that district.
Extension of Existing Temporary Bankruptcy Judgeships.-- (1) In general.--The temporary bankruptcy judgeships authorized for the northern district of Alabama and the eastern district of Tennessee under paragraphs (1) and (9) of section 3(a) of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note) are extended until the first vacancy occurring in the office of a bankruptcy judge in the applicable district resulting from the death, retirement, resignation, or removal of a bankruptcy judge and occurring 5 years or more after the date of enactment of this Act.
Applicability of other provisions.--All other provisions of section 3 of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note) remain applicable to the temporary bankruptcy judgeships referred to in this subsection.
TRANSFER OF BANKRUPTCY JUDGESHIP SHARED BY THE MIDDLE DISTRICT OF GEORGIA AND THE SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF GEORGIA.
The bankruptcy judgeship presently shared by the southern district of Georgia and the middle district of Georgia shall be converted to a bankruptcy judgeship for the middle district of Georgia.
CONVERSION OF EXISTING TEMPORARY BANKRUPTCY JUDGESHIPS.
District of Delaware.--The temporary bankruptcy judgeship authorized for the district of Delaware pursuant to section 3 of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note), shall be converted to a permanent bankruptcy judgeship.
District of Puerto Rico.--The temporary bankruptcy judgeship authorized for the district of Puerto Rico pursuant to section 3 of the Bankruptcy Judgeship Act of 1992 (28 United States Code 152 note), shall be converted to a permanent bankruptcy judgeship.
