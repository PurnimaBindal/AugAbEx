Section 6903 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection: ``(d) The Secretary of the Interior shall, on October 1, 1993, and each October 1 thereafter, adjust each dollar amount specified in subsections (b) and (c) to reflect changes in the Consumer Price Index published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the Department of Labor, for the 12 months ending the preceding June 30.''.
