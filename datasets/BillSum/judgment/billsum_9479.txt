SHORT TITLE; REFERENCES TO IMMIGRATION AND NATIONALITY ACT.
Short Title.--This Act may be cited as the ``United States Reports Employee, Family Unity, and Legalization Act''.
References to Immigration and Nationality Act.--Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Immigration and Nationality Act.

TASK FORCE ON IMMIGRANT EXPLOITATION.
There shall be established a national task force on the exploitation of and trafficking in immigrants.
The task force shall consist of Federal and State law enforcement officers appointed by the Secretary of Labor and the Secretary of State.
The Secretary of Labor shall be the Chair of the task force.
The purpose of the task force shall be to create a coordinated and aggressive national and State policy to end the exploitation of and trafficking in immigrants.
The task force shall develop and recommend to the President coordinated Federal and State law enforcement efforts for the elimination of employment abuses against immigrant workers.
