This Act may be cited as the ``Matewan Flood Control Excess Project Land Disposal Act of 1999''.
DISPOSITION OF EXCESS PROPERTY.
In General.--The United States shall convey by quit claim deed to the Town of Matewan, West Virginia, all right, title, and interest of the United States in and to four parcels of land deemed excess by the Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of the United States Reports Army Corps of Engineers, to the structural project for flood control constructed by the Corps of Engineers along the Tug Fork River pursuant to section 202 of Public Law 96-367.
