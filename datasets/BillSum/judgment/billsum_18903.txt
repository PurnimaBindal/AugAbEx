PROJECT FOR NAVIGATION, WELLS HARBOR, MAINE.
In General.--The project for navigation, Wells Harbor, Maine, authorized by section 101 of the River and Harbor Act of 1960 (74 United States Statutes at Large 480), is modified to authorize the Secretary of the Army to realign the channel and anchorage areas based on a harbor design capacity of 150 craft.

Relocation.--The Secretary of the Army may relocate the settling basin feature of the project to the outer harbor between the jetties.
Enforcement of Conservation Easement.--The Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of the United States Fish and Wildlife Service, may accept the conveyance of the right, but not the obligation, to enforce a conservation easement to be held by the State of Maine over certain land owned by the town of Wells, Maine, that is adjacent to the Rachel Carson National Wildlife Refuge.
