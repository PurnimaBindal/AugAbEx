This Act may be cited as the ``Social Security Benefit Enhancements for Women Act of 2002''.
REPEAL OF 7-YEAR RESTRICTION ON ELIGIBILITY FOR WIDOW'S AND WIDOWER'S INSURANCE BENEFITS BASED ON DISABILITY.
Widow's Insurance Benefits.-- (1) In general.--Section 202(e) of the Social Security Act (42 United States Code 402(e)) is amended-- (A) in paragraph (1)(B)(ii), by striking ``which began before the end of the period specified in paragraph (4)''; (B) in paragraph (1)(F)(ii), by striking ``(I) in the period specified in paragraph (4) and (II)''; (C) by striking paragraph (4) and by redesignating paragraphs (5) through (9) as paragraphs (4) through (8), respectively; and (D) in paragraph (4)(A)(ii) (as redesignated), by striking ``whichever'' and all that follows through ``begins'' and inserting ``the first day of the seventeenth month before the month in which her application is filed''.
Conforming amendments.-- (A) Section 202(e)(1)(F)(i) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(e)(1)(F)(i)) is amended by striking ``paragraph (5)'' and inserting ``paragraph (4)''.
Section 202(e)(1)(C)(ii)(III) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(e)(2)(C)(ii)(III)) is amended by striking ``paragraph (8)'' and inserting ``paragraph (7)''.
Section 202(e)(2)(A) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(e)(2)(A)) is amended by striking ``paragraph (7)'' and inserting ``paragraph (6)''.
Section 226(e)(1)(A)(i) of such Act (42 United States Code 426(e)(1)(A)(i)) is amended by striking ``202(e)(4),''.
Widower's Insurance Benefits.-- (1) In general.--Section 202(f) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(f)) is amended-- (A) in paragraph (1)(B)(ii), by striking ``which began before the end of the period specified in paragraph (5)''; (B) in paragraph (1)(F)(ii), by striking ``(I) in the period specified in paragraph (5) and (II)''; (C) by striking paragraph (5) and by redesignating paragraphs (6) through (9) as paragraphs (5) through (8), respectively; and (D) in paragraph (5)(A)(ii) (as redesignated), by striking ``whichever'' and all that follows through ``begins'' and inserting ``the first day of the seventeenth month before the month in which his application is filed''.
Conforming amendments.-- (A) Section 202(f)(1)(F)(i) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(f)(1)(F)(i)) is amended by striking ``paragraph (6)'' and inserting ``paragraph (5)''.
Section 202(f)(1)(C)(ii)(III) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(f)(2)(C)(ii)(III)) is amended by striking ``paragraph (8)'' and inserting ``paragraph (7)''.
Section 226(e)(1)(A)(i) of such Act (as amended by subsection (a)(2)) is further amended by striking ``202(f)(1)(B)(ii), and 202(f)(5)'' and inserting ``and 202(f)(1)(B)(ii)''.
Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to benefits for months after November 2002.
EXEMPTION FROM TWO-YEAR WAITING PERIOD FOR DIVORCED SPOUSE'S BENEFITS UPON OTHER SPOUSE'S REMARRIAGE.
Wife's Insurance Benefits.--Section 202(b)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act (42 United States Code 402(b)(5)(A)) is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: ``The criterion for entitlement under clause (ii) shall be deemed met upon the remarriage of the insured individual to someone other than the applicant during the 2-year period referred to in such clause.''.
Husband's Insurance Benefits.--Section 202(c)(5)(A) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(c)(5)(A)) is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: ``The criterion for entitlement under clause (ii) shall be deemed met upon the remarriage of the insured individual to someone other than the applicant during the 2-year period referred to in such clause.''.
Conforming Amendment to Exemption of Insured Individual's Divorced Spouse From Earnings Test as Applied to the Insured Individual.--Section 203(b)(2)(B) of such Act (42 United States Code 403(b)(2)(B)) is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: ``The requirement under such clause (ii) shall be deemed met upon the remarriage of the insured individual to someone other than the individual referred to in paragraph (1) during the 2-year period referred to in such clause.''.
Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to benefits for months after November 2002.
MONTHS ENDING AFTER DECEASED INDIVIDUAL'S DEATH DISREGARDED IN APPLYING EARLY RETIREMENT RULES WITH RESPECT TO DECEASED INDIVIDUAL FOR PURPOSES OF LIMITATION ON WIDOW'S AND WIDOWER'S BENEFITS.
Widow's Insurance Benefits.--Section 202(e)(2)(D)(i) of the Social Security Act (42 United States Code 402(e)(2)(D)(i)) is amended by inserting after ``applicable,'' the following: ``except that, in applying paragraph (7) of subsection (q) for purposes of this clause, any month ending with or after the date of the death of such deceased individual shall be deemed to be excluded under such paragraph (in addition to months otherwise excluded under such paragraph),''.
Widower's Insurance Benefits.--Section 202(f)(3)(D)(i) of such Act (42 United States Code 402(f)(3)(D)(i)) is amended by inserting after ``applicable,'' the following: ``except that, in applying paragraph (7) of subsection (q) for purposes of this clause, any month ending with or after the date of the death of such deceased individual shall be deemed to be excluded under such paragraph (in addition to months otherwise excluded under such paragraph),''.
Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall apply with respect to benefits for months after November 2002.
