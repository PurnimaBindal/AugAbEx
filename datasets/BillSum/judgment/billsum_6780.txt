AMENDMENTS RELATING TO THE CONGRESS.
Official Mail Privileges of Former Speakers.--The provisions of H. Res.
1238, Ninety-first Congress, as enacted into permanent law by the Supplemental Appropriations Act, 1971 (84 United States Statutes at Large 1989), as extended to former Speakers of the House of Representatives by Public Law 93-532 (88 United States Statutes at Large 1723), are deemed to be amended by substituting ``official mail'' for ``franked mail'' each place it appears.
Transportation of Official Records and Papers to House Member's District.--The provisions of H. Res.
1280, Ninety-fifth Congress, and House Resolution 1297, Ninety-fifth Congress, as enacted into permanent law by Public Law 98-51 (97 United States Statutes at Large 269) are deemed to be amended by substituting ``official mail'' for ``franked mail''.
