This Act may be cited as the ``Retiree Continuation Coverage Act of 1995''.
EXTENSION OF COBRA CONTINUATION COVERAGE.
Public Health Service Act.-- (1) Period of coverage.--Section 2202(2)(A) of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300bb-2(2)(A)) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new clause: ``(v) Qualifying event involving substantial reduction or elimination of a retiree group health plan.--In the case of an event described in section 2203(6), the date on which such covered qualified beneficiary becomes entitled to benefits under title XVIII of the Social Security Act.''.
Qualifying event.--Section 2203 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300bb-3) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new paragraph: ``(6) The substantial reduction or elimination of group health coverage as a result of plan changes or termination with respect to a qualified beneficiary described in section 2208(3)(A).''.
Notice.--Section 2206 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300bb-6) is amended-- (A) in paragraph (2), by striking ``or (4)'' and inserting ``(4), or (6)''; and (B) in paragraph (4)(A), by striking ``or (4)'' and inserting ``(4), or (6)''.
Definition.--Section 2208(3) of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300bb-8(3)) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new subparagraph: ``(C) Special rule for retirees.--In the case of a qualifying event described in section 2203(6), the term `qualified beneficiary' includes a covered employee who had retired on or before the date of substantial reduction or elimination of coverage and any other individual who, on the day before such qualifying event, is a beneficiary under the plan-- ``(i) as the spouse of the covered employee; ``(ii) as the dependent child of the covered employee; or ``(iii) as the surviving spouse of the covered employee.''.
Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974.-- (1) Period of coverage.--Section 602(2)(A) of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (29 United States Code 1162(2)(A)) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new clause: ``(vi) Qualifying event involving substantial reduction or elimination of a group health plan covering retirees, spouses and dependents.--In the case of an event described in section 603(7), the date on which such covered qualified beneficiary becomes entitled to benefits under title XVIII of the Social Security Act.''.
Qualifying event.--Section 603 of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (29 United States Code 1163) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new paragraph: ``(7) The substantial reduction or elimination of group health plan coverage as a result of plan changes or termination with respect to a qualified beneficiary described in section 607(3)(C).''.
Notice.--Section 606(a) of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (29 United States Code 1166) is amended-- (A) in paragraph (2), by striking ``or (6)'' and inserting ``(6), or (7)''; and (B) in paragraph (4)(A), by striking ``or (6)'' and inserting ``(6), or (7)''.
Definition.--Section 607(3)(C) of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (29 United States Code 1167(2)) is amended by striking ``603(6)'' and inserting ``603(6) or 603(7)''.
Internal Revenue Code of 1986.-- (1) Period of coverage.--Section 4980B(f)(2)(B)(i) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new subclause: ``(VI) Qualifying event involving substantial reduction or elimination of a retiree group health plan.--In the case of an event described in paragraph (3)(G), the date on which such covered qualified beneficiary becomes entitled to benefits under title XVIII of the Social Security Act.''.
Qualifying event.--Section 4980B(f)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new subparagraph: ``(G) The substantial reduction or elimination of group health coverage as a result of plan changes or termination with respect to a qualified beneficiary described in subsection (g)(1)(D).''.
Notice.--Section 4980B(f)(6) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended-- (A) in subparagraph (B), by striking ``or (F)'' and inserting ``(F), or (G)''; and (B) in subparagraph (D)(i), by striking ``or (F)'' and inserting ``(F), or (G)''.
Definition.--Section 4980B(g)(1)(D) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking ``(f)(3)(F)'' and inserting ``(f)(3)(F) or (f)(3)(G)''.
This Act shall take effect as if enacted on January 1, 1995.
