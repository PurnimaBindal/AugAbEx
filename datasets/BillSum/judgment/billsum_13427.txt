RECOURSE LOANS FOR PROCESSORS OF SUGARCANE AND SUGAR BEETS AND REDUCTION IN LOAN RATES.
Gradual Reduction in Loan Rates.-- (1) Sugarcane processor loans.--Section 156(a) of the Agricultural Market Transition Act (7 United States Code 7272(a)) is amended by striking ``equal to 18 cents per pound for raw cane sugar.''
and inserting the following: ``, per pound for raw cane sugar, equal to the following: ``(1) In the case of raw cane sugar processed from the 1996, 1997, or 1998 crop, $0.18.
In the case of raw cane sugar processed from the 1999 crop, $0.17.
In the case of raw cane sugar processed from the 2000 crop, $0.16.
In the case of raw cane sugar processed from the 2001 crop, $0.15.
In the case of raw cane sugar processed from the 2002 crop, $0.14.''.
Sugar beet processor loans.--Section 156(b) of the Agricultural Market Transition Act (7 United States Code 7272(b)) is amended by striking ``equal to 22.9 cents per pound for refined beet sugar.''
and inserting the following: ``, per pound of refined beet sugar, that reflects-- ``(1) an amount that bears the same relation to the loan rate in effect under subsection (a) for a crop as the weighted average of producer returns for sugar beets bears to the weighted average of producer returns for sugarcane, expressed on a cents per pound basis for refined beet sugar and raw cane sugar, for the most recent 5-year period for which data are available; and ``(2) an amount that covers sugar beet processor fixed marketing expenses.''.
Conversion to Recourse Loans.--Section 156(e) of the Agricultural Market Transition Act (7 United States Code 7272(e)) is amended-- (1) in paragraph (1), by inserting ``only'' after ``this section''; and (2) by striking paragraphs (2) and (3) and inserting the following: ``(2) National loan rates.--Recourse loans under this section shall be made available at all locations nationally at the rates specified in this section, without adjustment to provide regional differentials.''.
Conversion to Private Sector Financing.--Section 156 of the Agricultural Market Transition Act (7 United States Code 7272) is amended-- (1) by redesignating subsection (i) as subsection (j); (2) by inserting after subsection (h) the following: ``(i) Conversion to Private Sector Financing.--Notwithstanding any other provision of law-- ``(1) no processor of any of the 2003 or subsequent crops of sugarcane or sugar beets shall be eligible for a loan under this section with respect to the crops; and ``(2) the Secretary may not make price support available, whether in the form of loans, payments, purchases, or other operations, for any of the 2003 and subsequent crops of sugar beets and sugarcane by using the funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation or other funds available to the Secretary.
''; and (3) in subsection (j) (as redesignated by paragraph (1)) by striking ``subsection (f)'' and inserting ``subsections (f) and (i)''.
Termination of Marketing Quotas and Allotments.-- (1) Termination.--Part VII of subtitle B of title III of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 (7 United States Code 1359aa et seq.)
Conforming amendment.--Section 344(f)(2) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 (7 United States Code 1344(f)(2)) is amended by striking ``sugar cane for sugar, sugar beets for sugar,''.
Other Conforming Amendments.-- (1) Price support for nonbasic agricultural commodities.-- (A) Designated nonbasic agricultural commodities.-- Section 201(a) of the Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 United States Code 1446(a)) is amended by striking ``milk, sugar beets, and sugarcane'' and inserting ``, and milk''.
Other nonbasic agricultural commodities.-- Section 301 of the Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 United States Code 1447) is amended by inserting ``(other than sugarcane and sugar beets)'' after ``title II''.
Powers of commodity credit corporation.--Section 5(a) of the Commodity Credit Corporation Charter Act (15 United States Code 714c(a)) is amended by inserting ``(except for the 2003 and subsequent crops of sugarcane and sugar beets)'' after ``agricultural commodities''.
Section 32 activities.--Section 32 of the Act of August 24, 1935 (7 United States Code 612c), is amended in the second sentence of the first paragraph by inserting ``(other than sugarcane and sugar beets)'' after ``commodity'' the last place it appears.
Assurance of Adequate Supplies of Sugar.--Section 902 of the Food Security Act of 1985 (7 United States Code 1446g note; Public Law 99-198) is amended by striking subsection (a) and inserting the following: ``(a) In General.--Beginning with the quota year for sugar imports that begins after the 1998/1999 quota year, the President shall use all authorities available to the President as may be necessary to enable the Secretary of Agriculture to ensure that adequate supplies of raw cane sugar are made available to the United States market at prices that are not greater than the higher of-- ``(1) the world sugar price (adjusted to a delivered basis); or ``(2) the raw cane sugar loan rate in effect under section 156 of the Agricultural Market Transition Act (7 United States Code 7272), plus interest.
