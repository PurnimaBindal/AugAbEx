This Act may be cited as the ``Seniors as Volunteers in Our Schools Act''.
Except as otherwise specifically provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or a repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (20 United States Code 6301 et sequentes).
