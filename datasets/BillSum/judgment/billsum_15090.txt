MINIMUM TAX ON FOREIGN AND FOREIGN-OWNED CORPORATIONS.
In General.--Subchapter A of chapter 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to determination of tax liability) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new part: ``PART VIII--MINIMUM TAX ON CERTAIN FOREIGN AND FOREIGN-OWNED CORPORATIONS ``Sec.
Minimum tax on certain foreign and foreign-owned corporations.
MINIMUM TAX ON CERTAIN FOREIGN AND FOREIGN-OWNED CORPORATIONS.
Imposition of Tax.--In the case of a corporation to which this section applies, there is hereby imposed (in addition to any other tax imposed by this subtitle) a tax equal to the excess (if any) of-- ``(1) 34 percent of the product of-- ``(A) 5 percent, and ``(B) the gross receipts of the taxpayer from the sale or leasing of property manufactured by the taxpayer or by any foreign person that is a related party of the taxpayer, over ``(2) the aggregate tax imposed under sections 11, 55, and 1201 for such year.
Taxpayers to Which Section Applies.--This section shall apply to a corporation for the taxable year if such corporation is-- ``(1) a domestic corporation which is 25-percent foreign- owned, or ``(2) a foreign corporation engaged in a trade or business within the United States.
Definitions.--For purposes of this section, the term `25- percent foreign-owned', `foreign person', and `related party' have the respective meanings given such terms by section 6038A(c).''
Clerical Amendment.--The table of parts for such subchapter A is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new item: ``Part VIII.
Minimum tax on certain foreign and foreign-owned corporations.''
Effective Date.--The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1992.
