This Act may be cited as the ``Advisory Committee Termination Act of 1994''.
REPEAL OF ADVISORY COMMITTEES.
Department of Agriculture.-- (1) Swine health advisory committee.--Section 11 of the Swine Health Protection Act (7 United States Code 3810), which required the Secretary of Agriculture to appoint a swine health advisory committee or committees, is repealed.
Cascade head scenic-research area advisory council.-- Section 8 of the Act of December 22, 1974 (16 United States Code 541g), which required the Secretary of Agriculture to appoint a Cascade Head Scenic-Research Area Advisory Council, is repealed.
Global climate change technical advisory committee.-- Section 2404 of the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 (7 United States Code 6703), which required the Secretary of Agriculture to appoint a Global Climate Change Technical Advisory Committee, is repealed.
Mono basin national forest scenic area advisory board.--Section 306 of the California Wilderness Act of 1984 (16 United States Code 543e), which established the Mono Basin National Forest Scenic Area Advisory Board, is repealed.
Nez perce national historic trail advisory council.-- Section 5(d) of the National Trails System Act, (16 United States Code 1244(d)), which required the Secretary of Agriculture to establish an advisory council for the Nez Perce National Historic Trail, is amended in the first sentence by inserting before the period at the end ``and the Advisory Council established for the Nez Perce National Historic Trail shall terminate on the effective date of the Advisory Committee Termination Act of 1994.''.
Department of Defense.--Section 3306 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1993 (50 United States Code 98h-1 note), which authorized the Government-Industry Advisory Committee on the Operation and Modernization of the National Defense Stockpile, is repealed.
Department of Education; Improvement and Reform of Schools and Teaching Fund Board.-- (1) Fund for the improvement and reform of schools and teaching act.--The Fund for the Improvement and Reform of Schools and Teaching Act (20 United States Code 4811 et sequentes), which established the Fund Board, is amended-- (A) in section 3231 (20 United States Code 4831)-- (i) in the heading by striking ``board authorized'' and inserting ``director's responsibilities''; (ii) by striking subsection (a) and redesignating subsections (b) through (f) as subsections (a) through (e), respectively; (iii) in subsection (b)-- (I) by amending paragraph (3)(A) to read as follows: ``(A) coordinate the work of the Fund with the work of the Fund for the Improvement of Postsecondary Education,''; (II) by amending paragraph (3)(C) to read as follows: ``(C) identify promising initiatives and solicit proposals,''; (III) by striking paragraph (2); and (IV) by redesignating paragraph (3) as paragraph (2); and (iv) in subsection (c)-- (I) by striking ``priorities rule'' and inserting ``project summary''; and (II) by striking the first two sentences; (B) in section 3233 (20 United States Code 4833), by striking the second sentence; and (C) in section 3243 (20 United States Code 4843)-- (i) by striking paragraph (2); and (ii) by redesignating paragraphs (3) and (4) as paragraphs (2) and (3), respectively.
Technical amendment.--Section 551 of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 United States Code 1107) is amended-- (A) in subsection (a)-- (i) by striking paragraph (2); and (ii) by redesignating paragraph (3) as paragraph (2); (B) by striking subsection (c); and (C) by redesignating subsections (d) through (k) as subsections (c) through (j), respectively.
Department of Energy-- (1) Technical advisory committee on verification of fissile material and nuclear warhead controls.--Section 3151(c) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1991 (Public Law 101-510; 104 United States Statutes at Large (839)), which authorized the Technical Advisory Committee on Verification of Fissile Material and Nuclear Warhead Controls, is repealed.
Technical panel on magnetic fusion.--Section 7 of the Magnetic Fusion Energy Engineering Act of 1980 (42 United States Code 9306), which authorized a technical panel on magnetic fusion, is repealed.
Department of Health and Human Services.-- (1) Advisory council on hazardous substances research and training.--Section 311(a)(5) of the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act of 1980 (42 United States Code 9660(a)(5)), which authorized an advisory council on hazardous substances research and training, is repealed.
Advisory council on trauma care systems.--Section 1202 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 300d-1), which authorized the Advisory Council on Trauma Care Systems, is repealed.
Job opportunities and basic skills training program advisory panel.--Section 203(c)(4) of the Family Support Act of 1988 (42 United States Code 681 note), which authorized an advisory panel for the evaluation of the Job Opportunities and Basic Skills Training (JOBS) Program, is repealed.
Board of tea experts.--Section 4 of the Tea Importation Act (21 United States Code 42), which authorized a board of tea experts, is repealed.
Device good manufacturing advisory committee.--Section 520(f)(3) of the Federal Food, and Cosmetic Act (21 United States Code 360j(f)(3)), which authorized a device good manufacturing practice advisory committee, is repealed.
End stage renal disease data advisory committee.--The second sentence of section 1881(c)(7) of the Social Security Act (42 United States Code 1395rr(c)(7)), which authorized a professional advisory group to assist in formulation of policies and procedures relevant to the management of the end stage renal disease registry, is amended by striking everything after ``purpose of such'' and inserting ``registry and shall determine the appropriate location of the registry.''.
Federal hospital council.--Section 641 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 291k), which authorized the Federal Hospital Council, is repealed.
National arthritis and musculoskeletal and skin diseases advisory board.--Section 442 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 285d-7), which authorized the National Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases Advisory Board, is repealed.
National commission on alcoholism and other alcohol- related problems.--Section 18 of the Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, and Rehabilitation Act Amendments of 1979 (42 United States Code 4541 note), which authorized the National Commission on Alcoholism and Other Alcohol-Related Problems, is repealed.
National deafness and other communication disorders advisory board.--Section 464D of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 285m-4), which authorized the National Deafness and Other Communication Disorders Advisory Board, is repealed.
National diabetes advisory board, national digestive diseases advisory board, and national kidney and urologic diseases advisory board.--Section 430 of the Public Health Service Act (42 United States Code 285c-4), which authorized the National Diabetes Advisory Board, National Digestive Diseases Advisory Board, and National Kidney and Urologic Diseases Advisory Board, is repealed.
Task force on aging research.--Title III of the Home Health Care and Alzheimer's Disease Amendments of 1990 (42 United States Code 242q through 242q-5), which authorized the Task Force on Aging Research, is repealed.
Department of the Interior.-- (1) Chattahoochee river national recreation area advisory commission.--Section 106 of Public Law 95-344 (16 United States Code 460ii- 5), which authorized the Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area Advisory Commission, is repealed.
Gulf islands national seashore advisory commission.-- Section 10 of Public Law 91-660 (16 United States Code 459h-9), which authorized the Gulf Islands National Seashore Advisory Commission, is repealed.
Jefferson national expansion memorial commission.-- Section 7 of the Act of August 24, 1984 (68 United States Statutes at Large 98, chapter 204; 98 United States Statutes at Large 1467; 16 United States Code 450jj-6), which authorized the Jefferson National Expansion Memorial Commission, is repealed.
Potomac heritage national scenic trail advisory council.--The first sentence of section 5(d) of the National Trails System Act (16 United States Code 1244(d)), which required the Secretary of the Interior to establish an advisory council for the Potomac Heritage National Scenic Trail, is amended by inserting ``except the Potomac Heritage Trail'' after ``respective trail''.
Department of Justice.--Section 5002 of title 18, United States Code, which authorized the Advisory Corrections Council, is repealed.
Department of Transportation.-- (1) Commercial motor vehicle safety regulatory review panel.--Section 31134 of title 49, United States Code, as enacted by Public Law 103-472 (formerly section 209 of the Motor Carrier Safety Act of 1984 (49 United States Code App.
2508)), which authorized the Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Regulatory Review Panel, is repealed.
National driver register advisory committee.--Section 209 of the National Driver Register Act of 1982 (23 United States Code 401 note), which authorized the National Driver Register Advisory Committee, is repealed.
National highway safety advisory committee.--Section 404 of title 23, United States Code, which authorized the National Highway Safety Advisory Committee, is repealed.
